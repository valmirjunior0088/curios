# curios-erase

Curios erasure: a finished Core unit lowered through `curios-anf`'s checked builder into the erased IR, with types, proofs and erasable binders gone. It is directed by types, which it reads off the terms through the kernel that certified them (`curios_cert::Reader`), erases each item into a fragment of its own, at each assignment of the sorts it leaves to its caller, keeps a record of what erasing each item read, and refuses a program for what only the erased program shows — an evaluation cycle among recursive values and an initializer that performs an effect, which `curios-anf`'s verifier finds — in an error of its own.

## Design

### Erasure is a crate of its own, and the crate is erasure

**Decision.** Erasure is `curios-erase`'s root and its top-level modules, and no module repeats the crate's name. The crate depends on `curios-cert`, `curios-anf`, `curios-analysis` and `curios-core`, and outside its tests on nothing of `curios-elab`. It qualifies every `curios_core` and every `curios_anf` name, as `curios-emit` qualifies both representations it translates between (`.claude/rules/rust.md`).

**Rationale.** Erasure reads what the kernel certified and writes what `curios-anf` verifies, and nothing of elaboration's state, and a crate boundary is where that is held: a reader of finished terms cannot reach an elaborator's context by sharing its crate. A name qualified by its crate says at every use which representation it belongs to, where the two hold names alike, a `Module`, a `Term` and an `Atom` on each side.

**Rejected.** Erasure as a module of the elaborator, which reached its context, its trace vocabulary and its errors. A module named `into_anf` inside the crate, which spells the disambiguation the crate's name already makes, as `curios-cert`'s README rejects a `kernel::` namespace.

### Erasure reads through the kernel that certified the term

**Decision.** Every type the walk is directed by is read through the kernel that certified the term (`curios_cert::Reader`, entered by `Kernel::read`), whose every entry is a judgment the kernel already makes: reduction to a head as a judgment that takes a value apart reads it, a term's type, which half a value of a type is in, a case put in force and an inductive arm entered as the arm is checked, the binders and the frame bracket, the constraints a declaration's universe context states, the lookups, the budget and the read record. Inside the bracket the kernel records no erased position and no call. The driver hands a unit's erasure the kernel that certified the unit (`certify_module`, then `erase_unit`), and a program's the kernel that judged its final term (`curios-pipeline`'s `judged`), so erasure reads through the environment that walk built: the unit's definitions as it defined them, the units in scope it mounted and the vectors it filed. Its memos are not handed over: each lives one declaration's budget, which erasure restores for each item it erases.

**Rationale.** The reader of a certified term decides by the side that certified it, so a type is read by the reduction that accepted the term by construction, and no reading decides less than that reduction did: a callee typed through a decision only conversion makes, `f(same(n)) == f(n)`, which the fold leaves stuck, has its function type to erasure as to the kernel (`curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests`). The door adds no rule, so no verdict moves and nothing joins the trusted base. A memo carried from certification into erasure would make what erasure spends depend on whether certification judged an item or adopted its verdict, which a recompile changes. A verified pipeline erases the same way, from the checker's own judgment of a well-typed term (Sozeau et al., [Correct and Complete Type Checking and Certified Erasure for Coq, in Coq](https://hal.sorbonne-universite.fr/ENS-PARIS-SACLAY/hal-04077552v3), JACM).

**Rejected.** Reading through the elaborator's engine, as Lean's code generator infers types through `MetaM` ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.Types`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/Types.html)) and Rocq's extraction recomputes them through `Retyping` ([`Retyping`](https://docs.rocq-prover.org/v8.18/api/coq-core/Retyping/index.html)): the reader of a certified term would decide by the side that did not certify it, and could decide less. Erasure generic over the shared `Env`/`Judge` seam, which would have one implementor: the seam is for the analyses both checkers run, and erasure is not one. Waiting for a proof's mark ([A proof is known by its mark](../documentation/roadmap/soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md)): a mark states no callee's, record's or scrutinee's type, so the reader is needed with it or without it, and where it lands erasure reads the mark where it reads a sort. A second walk over a program's term to hold a kernel for erasure, which judged every program twice.

### Erasure reads a head's type off the finished term

**Decision.** Erasure is directed by types and types no term. The walk carries the type each expression was checked against; where an elimination's head has none to carry — a callee, a projected record, a scrutinee, an index a proof's match pins — `Lowering::type_of` reads it: a name's from its assumption, an application's by opening its head's telescope at the arguments, a projection's from the field it selects, and a constructor's, a literal's and a lambda's from the node itself. A `Bool`, `Nat` or sequence match states its scrutinee's type, so none is read. Each reading is charged a step and kept for the item being erased, so a chain of eliminations reads each of its prefixes once. What it asks of the kernel that certified the term is a type's head, by the reduction that accepted it, and a name's or a neutral's type, through the kernel's reading door, whose every entry is a judgment the kernel makes, so no rule that elaborates runs.

**Rationale.** The term is elaborated and certified, so its types are a function of it. Asking the elaborator for them ran the rules that decide what an author may write over a term no author wrote, in a context seeded with no concept and no witness — a lambda with a `use` parameter, applied on the spot, elaborated and was then refused for the type of its own parameter — and typed a head once per head enclosing it, checking every argument again each time. A name is minted once and assumed at one type, so a reading holds wherever the term stands but in an arm, where the kernel binds a variable the case solves to its value and reads every type by value: the table is set aside for each arm (`Lowering::in_arm`), and needs no other invalidation inside an item.

**Rejected.** Registering concepts in the erasure context, or bracketing each surface rule off as privacy was, which leaves every later rule to be remembered; a memo over the elaborator's inference, which keeps those rules running; types carried on Core's eliminations, which the certifier would have to check or erasure would have to believe; erasing an applied lambda as the `let` it reduces to, which is an optimization where erasure is a transcription.

### Erasure walks the certified module at the sorts the kernel judged

**Decision.** Each declaration is read as the kernel holds it, with its universes. One that leaves sorts to its caller erases once for each assignment of `Prop` and `Type` to them, as the kernel judges it at each, each form read off the declaration's terms at its assignment (`ErasedForms`, `Sorts`); one that leaves none erases once. A form is known by its declaration's name and its sorts (`AtSorts`): an instance erases as its head's form at the sorts it states, and a constructed value, a record, a projection and a match read their declaration's row at the sorts their instance states, so a use at `Prop` drops what the kernel classifies as a proof there and links a form erased at the same sorts. A level carries nothing at run time, so a value erases by its fields and a type former to the unit. Two assignments that erase alike are one form, stored once. An item is kept or erased again whole, and a program links each form it reaches, and every form of an item that computes.

**Rationale.** The kernel judges a declaration that leaves a sort open at each assignment, and reads no term at an open sort (`at_every_sort`, `Kernel::check_instance`), so a form read at an assignment is read off terms the kernel judged there, and a generic body may eliminate a value whose sort is the caller's into data, as the kernel lets it at each. At `Prop` an `Option`'s `some` keeps no field, so the options `/std/Bool/holds` builds, at `Option.{Prop}(Holds(b))`, are bare tags. A monomorphizing compiler makes a form for each instance; this makes one for each value of the one dimension the kernel judges at each value, at most 256, a declaration leaving eight sorts at most.

**Rejected.** Walking a copy of the module projected out of its universes, which is not the term the kernel certified, and reads every sort a declaration leaves open as kept wherever it is used, which no judgment of the kernel's makes. One form for a declaration that leaves a sort open, as Rocq's extraction and Lean's compiler make one: their theories forbid a generic body to eliminate a value whose sort may be `Prop` into data but for a subsingleton (Rocq's [Polymorphic Universes](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/V9.1.0/refman/addendum/universe-polymorphism.html), Lean's [`propRecLargeElim`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Error-Explanations/lean___propRecLargeElim)), as GHC restricts what a levity-polymorphic function may do so one compiled form serves every representation (Eisenberg and Peyton Jones, [Levity Polymorphism](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/levity-polymorphism/), PLDI 2017). The kernel here judges each assignment instead, so a generic body may, a caller at `Prop` handing it a stand-in would meet a callee erased at `Type`, and restricting the language to fit one form would refuse programs the kernel accepts. Forms only at the sorts some use states: a unit's erasure would depend on its successors, and the prelude's image could not be made once.

### A structural type's runtime shape is its telescope's

**Decision.** A function has a runtime parameter for each binder of its telescope, and an anonymous tuple a field for each member. A slot whose type is a proof or a type, where the value is written, holds a content-free stand-in and is not computed (`Lowering::kept_operand`): the unit constant, or, for a function a callee may apply, a function of that type's arity returning it. Which of the two is read off the slot's type reduced to its head, but where the type's own head says no instance of it is a function type, as a decided bound's `Holds(c)` says at a fresh `c`, which is then left unevaluated (`never_a_function`). Only a nominal declaration drops: a structure's fields and a constructor's payload are classified once for each assignment of the sorts the declaration leaves to its caller, against its own signature at that assignment with its binders opaque (`constructor_entries`), and every construction, projection and match reads the row at the sorts its instance states. So a product that is to cost one field for a value and its proof is a structure, and a parameter nothing reads is the optimizer's to remove where it sees every call (`curios-cps`'s `simplify`).

**Rationale.** Whether a binder carries anything is read off its type, and a value of a structural type stands at several types, a substitution or a refinement apart. A lambda is checked against its callee's parameter type with the caller's arguments in it, and the callee calls it at the type its own declaration states; a universe stands where a `Type` variable is declared, and a proposition where a variable at a `Sort` is; an arm's refinement reduces a domain that is stuck outside the arm. A domain one of them reads as a proof or a type another reads as a variable, so a shape decided by either reading is one the other does not have: a function called with an argument it has no parameter for, a tuple read at a field it was built without. A telescope's length is the one thing every reading has. A declaration is read by name and sorts, so its row is one fact wherever it is read at them (`curios`'s `tests::erasure::shape_tests`).

**Rejected.** Dropping a parameter or a field by the type at each site, which is the defect. Dropping a declaration's own parameters at a direct call to it, with a wrapper wherever the declaration is used as a value: two calling conventions in erasure, for what the optimizer removes where it sees every call. A coercion wherever a value passes between two types that read a binder apart, which is every flow of a function or a tuple erasure would have to find, and one missed is a wrong answer. A mark on the binder that says what is kept, which is a quantity, and a change to the language.

### Erasure files its own record of what it read

**Decision.** For each item, erasure records what erasing its forms read (`Asked`): of a declaration around it, its signature and its body as the kernel's reads name them, and its kind where erasure reads the definition off its item; of another item's erased forms, how a name is bound in them, or the forms whole where an evaluation ran them (`Part`). A recompile keeps an item's forms where the item is the baseline's and every part read digests as it did: a signature or a body by the certifier's own digest (`curios_cert::part_digest`), over the unit's Core, places and record; a kind, a binding and forms whole by erasure's. Of another unit the record holds the digest each part had when it was read (`AskedItems`), and a part of a unit in scope that moved is asked again by what it digests to now. A store's filing completes each record with the item's identity, the digest of each part read and the digest of its forms (`Made`), and names it by the item, so a recompile takes an item out of a store where every part read digests now as it did. `curios-unit` stores the records in runs of their own kind (`AskedTable`).

**Rationale.** Each stage keeps what its own record says stands ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../documentation/design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)), and what erasure reads it reads through the kernel, which names what it read by its own parts. Digesting a signature or a body by the certifier's function compares of it what the kernel compares, so the record states once what a part of a declaration is, and a kind, which the kernel does not read, is the one part erasure digests of a declaration itself.

**Rejected.** Keeping an item's forms where the kernel's walk over the baseline adopted, or passed unvisited, every declaration the item read: what erasure keeps would follow the order of the kernel's walk and need one over a baseline, and a part the kernel reads alike erasure may not, a kind among them. The elaborator's trace vocabulary moved into a crate both stages read: erasure would ask in parts the kernel does not tell apart, digest them by the elaborator's comparisons, and keep a second statement of what a part of a declaration is.

### Erasure owns its refusals

**Decision.** `curios_erase::Error` holds what erasure refuses a program for — the verifier's two recursion rules, named by the source names erasure minted each identity for, a natural case key past `NatCase`, and its budget's exhaustion at the term it was reading — each located at the term erasure was erasing and worded as erasure's. Anything else the kernel refuses of a term erasure reads is a compiler fault, reported as a term erasure could not read with the kernel's refusal beneath it. A refusal is spelled and placed by what the driver hands it, a `curios_core::Spelling` and the anchors (`Error::reports`), which `curios-pipeline`'s `erasure_refusal` reads off the elaborator's `Reading` and reports beside the kernel's own (`kernel_refusal`).

**Rationale.** The program was certified, so where only erasure stopped the words are erasure's; a refusal the kernel makes of a term it certified says the compiler is wrong, and saying it in the kernel's words would blame the program. Spelling a term for a reader is the report layer's, whose tables include the text stage's, so erasure is handed the two things it reads of them.

**Rejected.** Erasure's refusals as the elaborator's errors, which named erasure's types in the elaborator and worded a certified program's stop as an elaboration's. `Reading` moved below both stages: it holds the text stage's tables and the surface printer, for a consumer that reads two of its fields.
