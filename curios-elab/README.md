# curios-elab

The Curios elaborator: bidirectional elaboration and typing, unification and metavariable solving, witness resolution, universe solving, zonking, and type erasure — the stage between `curios-text`'s lowering and the erased IR of `curios-ersd`. The language rules it enforces are recorded in [DESIGN.md](../documentation/DESIGN.md); entry points and local architecture belong to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### Deep shared terms are cached, not special-cased

**Decision.** The lowerer emits `Rc`-shared DAGs — a string literal's certified UTF-8 derivation shares every per-byte scan-state chain — and the compiler scales to them through generic mechanisms only, none of which recurses on input depth: an elaboration cache for metavariable-free, minted-name-free subterms whose result names only already-defined globals — so it survives, within one item, the `#`-minted definitions reduction and the frame elaborators mint (`Context::get_or_init_elaborated` / `elaboration_cacheable`, split into probe/record halves for the driver); the reduction cache's probe/record pair (`Context::cached_reduced` / `Context::reduce`); eliminator scrutinees reduced on an explicit frame stack inside `reduce`'s trampoline; elaboration's `elaborate → elaborate_apply → check` cycle defunctionalized onto its own frame stack for ground, all-explicit applications, so a data-shaped constructor spine elaborates at native depth bounded by the written program's binder nesting (`elaborate`'s driver and `ElabFrame`); and each term's memoized derivations (`hash`/`reach`/`free_vars`/`has_local_free`/`has_metavar`) carried on the shared `Rc` node (`Node`) and filled by an iterative post-order walk, so a subtree memoizes once across every occurrence, on a bounded native stack. No stage carries `Str`- or literal-specific machinery; eligibility gates and invalidation discipline belong to the `curios-elab` rustdoc of those methods.

**Rationale.** Sharing-oblivious elaboration cost O(N²) work and O(N) native stack in a literal's byte length, and the depth surfaced wherever the checker ran on a bounded thread. The defect was the compiler's — recursive tree walks over DAG-shaped input — so the cure had to be compiler-general: the next proof-carrying literal or generated spine benefits identically, and `/syn`, `/std`, and the emitter stay ordinary library code.

**Rejected.** Emitter-side pre-reduced scan states (duplicates `/std/Str` semantics inside the compiler); a canonical-spine special case in conversion (a nominal `Str` exception to definitional equality); redesigning the `/syn/Str` evidence to sidestep depth (the language may write deep certified data; the compiler must carry it).

**Deferred.** The elaboration cache now survives a fresh `define` — the insert refuses any result naming a not-yet-defined global (the name analogue of the reduction cache's unsolved-metavariable rule, which lets reduction entries survive `define` selectively), so `define_entry` no longer clears it wholesale — but `set_island` still clears it at every top-level item boundary, so the survival is within-item. Extending it across items would key the cache on the item's actual island rather than merely whether privacy checks were live, so `set_island` need not clear; the reduction cache, island-independent, already spans items.

### Undecided conversions park; solving reads materialized, raw-parked spellings

**Decision.** Four rules landed together (2026-08-08, the inference-and-unification campaign), one discipline. `solve` materializes committed solutions in its candidate and in the occurrence's spine entries before any analysis, so a solved metavariable's spine cannot drag out-of-scope names into the scope check (`zonk_solved_term_metas` at the top of `solve`). A structural mismatch whose side is stuck on an unsolved metavariable — an intrinsic over one, a match mentioning one, an elimination headed by such — parks watching the metavariables instead of failing, while a stuck comparison with no metavariable in a blocking position keeps its hard mismatch (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`). Two stuck matches decompose only when both scrutinees are rigid or their motives and case tables are syntactically identical — reduction can carry the rigid-scrutinee side past the layer a flex-scrutinee side is stuck at, and a scrutinee goal minted across that misalignment *wrongly commits*. And a goal that parks does so under its raw spelling, so retries re-reduce in their frame, watch sets include the metavariables only the raw spelling mentions, and the refinement-suppression analysis keeps the solvable unrefined spelling. Beside these, packed literals gain a solving-only decomposition against `append`/`concat` spines (`packed_literal_view`): the shared reduction and peel laws are untouched, and committed solutions make the folded spellings agree by plain reduction, so the kernel needs no matching rule. Survivors of the item drain report as postponements naming their watched metavariables (`Error::PostponedConversion`), distinct from rigid mismatches.

**Rationale.** A corpus measurement of every explicitly supplied implicit argument in `/std` attributed 45 of 50 to exactly these gaps; the remaining incompleteness (arm selection through a flexible scrutinee, refinement-dependent solutions) is deliberate, of the same kind as imitation staying a guess.

**Rejected.** Real pruning of out-of-scope metavariables — its measured consumers all turned out to be the unmaterialized-candidate defect, and restriction-commits for zero consumers fail the demand bar in the checker's most perimeter-heavy file; reinstate when a real program blocks on an equation solvable only by restriction. η-equating metavariable heads, dropped for never having had a consumer. Unifying constructor result indices before payload arguments — repairs only the constructor sites the postponement rule covers anyway. Match-match decomposition with a flexible scrutinee and differing arms — the wrong-commit shape the gate exists to refuse.

### Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution

**Decision.** `elaborate_apply` settles every argument in one telescope walk, and the dependent substitution only ever receives elaborated terms or compiler-born metavariables — the invariant is the code path, not a guard. A checked-only introduction form whose structure is still blocked becomes a `ParkedWork::Checking` obligation whose placeholder metavariable stands in the telescope: the wake machinery retries it the moment a solution lands, and a force tier at the end of the walk — the old settle, kept — checks whatever never woke, so no pending outlives its apply undischarged. Insertion (implicit metavariables and witness goals) happens in the same walk, at domains opened through the elaborated prefix. Three rules keep the parked store honest: a frozen frame is restored by reapplying only identities not already live (a doubled binder gives a metavariable a non-linear identity spine pattern inversion can never invert); a placeholder solved ahead of its retry ends the obligation without re-elaborating (a second elaboration of a term whose lowering-minted holes are already birthed would drop their spines); and a rollback bracket may not contain retries, since it would consume obligations whose solutions it then unwinds.

**Rationale.** The predecessor materialized arguments in a first walk that substituted raw spellings — written hidden arguments, postponed intro forms — into the telescope, so inserted goals and metavariable birth types could record spellings the reducer's polymorphic-variable gate leaves inert, and conversion could never connect them to the rebuilt forms. Guards patched the observable cases one shape at a time; the single walk removes the category.

**Rejected.** A frozen placeholder species the unifier may not solve: new solver surface whose only job is protecting the discipline, where reconciliation and the force tier need none. Keeping the bracketed best-effort `expect` over raw spellings as a fallback pin: measured across the whole corpus, it never discharged an obligation the wake machinery and the force tier did not, so it is deleted rather than retained as a second acceptance path.
