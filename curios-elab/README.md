# curios-elab

The Curios elaborator: bidirectional elaboration and typing, unification and metavariable solving, witness resolution, universe solving, zonking, and type erasure — the stage between `curios-text`'s lowering and the erased IR of `curios-ersd`. The language rules it enforces are recorded in [design/language/](../documentation/design/language); entry points and local architecture belong to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### Deep shared terms are cached, not special-cased

**Decision.** The lowerer emits `Rc`-shared DAGs — a string literal's certified UTF-8 derivation shares every per-byte scan-state chain — and the compiler scales to them through generic mechanisms only, none of which costs *work* per occurrence of a shared node: an elaboration cache for metavariable-free, minted-name-free subterms whose result names only already-defined globals — so it survives, within one item, the `#`-minted definitions reduction and the frame elaborators mint (`Context::get_or_init_elaborated` / `elaboration_cacheable`); the reduction cache's probe/record pair (`Context::cached_reduced` / `Context::reduce`); and each term's memoized derivations (`hash`/`reach`/`free_vars`/`has_local_free`/`has_metavar`) carried on the shared `Rc` node (`Node`) and filled by an iterative post-order walk, so a subtree memoizes once across every occurrence, on a bounded native stack. No stage carries `Str`- or literal-specific machinery; eligibility gates and invalidation discipline belong to the `curios-elab` rustdoc of those methods.

**Rationale.** Sharing-oblivious elaboration cost O(N²) work and O(N) native stack in a literal's byte length. The work is what these caches remove, and it is the half that no stack discipline addresses; the depth is now carried by `curios-utilities`'s `recurse`, which is why the frame machines that once absorbed it are gone. The defect was the compiler's — recursive tree walks over DAG-shaped input — so the cure had to be compiler-general: the next proof-carrying literal or generated spine benefits identically, and `/std` and the emitter stay ordinary library code.

**Rejected.** Emitter-side pre-reduced scan states (duplicates `/std/Str` semantics inside the compiler); a canonical-spine special case in conversion (a nominal `Str` exception to definitional equality); redesigning the `/std/Str` evidence to sidestep depth (the language may write deep certified data; the compiler must carry it).

**Deferred.** The elaboration cache now survives a fresh `define` — the insert refuses any result naming a not-yet-defined global (the name analogue of the reduction cache's unsolved-metavariable rule, which lets reduction entries survive `define` selectively), so `define_entry` no longer clears it wholesale — but `set_island` still clears it at every top-level item boundary, so the survival is within-item. Extending it across items would key the cache on the item's actual island rather than merely whether privacy checks were live, so `set_island` need not clear; the reduction cache, island-independent, already spans items.

### Undecided conversions park; solving reads materialized, raw-parked spellings

**Decision.** Four rules landed together (2026-08-08, the inference-and-unification campaign), one discipline. `solve` materializes committed solutions in its candidate and in the occurrence's spine entries before any analysis, so a solved metavariable's spine cannot drag out-of-scope names into the scope check (`zonk_solved_term_metas` at the top of `solve`). A structural mismatch whose side is stuck on an unsolved metavariable — an intrinsic over one, a match mentioning one, an elimination headed by such — parks watching the metavariables instead of failing, while a stuck comparison with no metavariable in a blocking position keeps its hard mismatch (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`). Two stuck matches decompose only when both scrutinees are rigid or their motives and case tables are syntactically identical — reduction can carry the rigid-scrutinee side past the layer a flex-scrutinee side is stuck at, and a scrutinee goal minted across that misalignment *wrongly commits*. And a goal that parks does so under its raw spelling, so retries re-reduce in their frame, watch sets include the metavariables only the raw spelling mentions, and the refinement-suppression analysis keeps the solvable unrefined spelling. Beside these, packed literals gain a solving-only decomposition against `append`/`concat` spines (`packed_literal_view`): the shared reduction and peel laws are untouched, and committed solutions make the folded spellings agree by plain reduction, so the kernel needs no matching rule. Survivors of the item drain report as postponements naming their watched metavariables (`Error::PostponedConversion`), distinct from rigid mismatches.

**Rationale.** A corpus measurement of every explicitly supplied implicit argument in `/std` attributed almost all of them to exactly these gaps; the remaining incompleteness (arm selection through a flexible scrutinee, refinement-dependent solutions) is deliberate, of the same kind as imitation staying a guess.

**Rejected.** Real pruning of out-of-scope metavariables — its measured consumers all turned out to be the unmaterialized-candidate defect, and restriction-commits for zero consumers fail the demand bar in the checker's most perimeter-heavy file; reinstate when a real program blocks on an equation solvable only by restriction. η-equating metavariable heads, dropped for never having had a consumer. Unifying constructor result indices before payload arguments — repairs only the constructor sites the postponement rule covers anyway. Match-match decomposition with a flexible scrutinee and differing arms — the wrong-commit shape the gate exists to refuse.

### Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution

**Decision.** `elaborate_apply` settles every argument in one telescope walk, and the dependent substitution only ever receives elaborated terms or compiler-born metavariables — the invariant is the code path, not a guard. A checked-only introduction form whose structure is still blocked becomes a `ParkedWork::Checking` obligation whose placeholder metavariable stands in the telescope: the wake machinery retries it the moment a solution lands, and a force tier at the end of the walk — the old settle, kept — checks whatever never woke, so no pending outlives its apply undischarged. Insertion (implicit metavariables and witness goals) happens in the same walk, at domains opened through the elaborated prefix. Three rules keep the parked store honest: a frozen frame is restored by reapplying only identities not already live (a doubled binder gives a metavariable a non-linear identity spine pattern inversion can never invert); a placeholder solved ahead of its retry ends the obligation without re-elaborating (a second elaboration of a term whose lowering-minted holes are already birthed would drop their spines); and a rollback bracket may not contain retries, since it would consume obligations whose solutions it then unwinds.

**Rationale.** The predecessor materialized arguments in a first walk that substituted raw spellings — written hidden arguments, postponed intro forms — into the telescope, so inserted goals and metavariable birth types could record spellings the reducer's polymorphic-variable gate leaves inert, and conversion could never connect them to the rebuilt forms. Guards patched the observable cases one shape at a time; the single walk removes the category.

**Rejected.** A frozen placeholder species the unifier may not solve: new solver surface whose only job is protecting the discipline, where reconciliation and the force tier need none. Keeping the bracketed best-effort `expect` over raw spellings as a fallback pin: measured across the whole corpus, it never discharged an obligation the wake machinery and the force tier did not, so it is deleted rather than retained as a second acceptance path.

### The universe journal is scoped, and its brackets are closures only off the recursion

**Decision.** `ConstraintStore` records a rewrite's pre-image only while a speculative scope is open, and change detection moved off the journal's length onto an `Entropy` tick — the same currency the cache stamp above the store already advances — so the journal can be conditional without a substitution going unnoticed by the caches that depend on seeing one. A scope is opened by `mark` and closed by `release`, and a mark carries the depth to *restore* rather than a count to decrement, so closing an outer scope also closes any inner one a bracket left open. Nothing resets the depth outright while a bracket can be live; the one reset is `abandon_speculation`, at an item boundary a refusal reached by unwinding every bracket, where no rollback can be pending. `Context::with_solutions` and `UniverseSolver::with_speculation` pair the two as scoped closures in the manner of `with_frame`. The three brackets on the witness-resolution cycle — `probe_match`, `commit_match`, `instantiate` — are hand-paired instead, calling `Context::end_solutions` on their exits.

**Rationale.** Recording unconditionally made a declaration's peak footprint superlinear: a substitution *widens* the constraints it lands in, so the journal kept every intermediate width of every constraint it ever rewrote, and nothing dropped them until the declaration ended. Measured on the fixed prelude, the journal was most of the build's peak, and scoping it cut the peak by roughly a factor of four — the difference between fitting and not fitting in a stock container VM, which is where this was found. Restoring rather than decrementing is what makes the depth safe to get wrong in the only direction it can be: too high, where the journal is merely dropped later than it could be. The counter is reset to zero only where no scope can be live, because a reset inside one is the unsafe direction — a reset at a declaration boundary that fell inside a live scope dropped pre-images a pending rollback still needed, leaving the store inconsistent with the solutions log, which presented as a stack overflow from resolution failing to make progress rather than as anything resembling a memory bug.

**Rejected.** Bracketing the resolution cycle with closures too, for uniformity. `instantiate` recurses one level per nested witness premise, and wrapping a whole large body puts every one of its locals in a closure frame while the outer frame survives holding the arguments — roughly doubling the per-level footprint on the deepest recursion in the crate. An unoptimized `--all-features` build overflowed its stack on the fixed prelude. `#[inline(always)]` on the wrappers was tried as a way to keep the closures and discarded: it cannot fold away a closure body, which is a separate function nothing marks as always-inlined, and applying it to the pervasive brackets is actively worse — inlining merges a callee's locals into its caller's frame for that frame's whole extent, so annotating a bracket called from several points inside a deeply recursive function inflates every level of it. The existing closures are safe for the reason these were not: they wrap small inner blocks, not whole recursive bodies. Raising the stack instead was never on the table — recursive lowering working on the default stack is a contract, not a tuning knob. Capping the container's build parallelism, tried first and discarded: the build script alone needed 7.7 GB, so no `-j` could help.

### A refused item is undone and its dependents are withheld

**Decision.** Elaboration recovers per item. An item that fails is reported, undone and poisoned, and the items after it elaborate as if it had never been written; an item that reaches a poisoned name — by mention, by construction, through a registry entry, or through a witness a refused declaration registered before its body failed — is withheld and reports nothing of its own. Undone is everything a later item could read: the item's base-frame bindings, its registry and witness-table entries (each witness key poisoned in place), the parked work and deferred goals it raised, the terms it recorded for the erasure obligations, its universe constraints, and every speculative scope its unwinding left open (`UniverseSolver::abandon_speculation`). Left in place is what nothing reads: the term and universe metavariables it minted, its totality verdict, and the caches, which clear wholesale. A refusal that surfaces after its item elaborated — a deferred witness goal that never found its entry — retracts the item and every elaborated item that reaches it, to a fixpoint over the elaborated forms, since a witness resolved late is an edge no lowered term shows. Every refusal of one run is reported in item order as one batch (`Error::Batch`), the entry's after the items' and a whole-module pass's after the entry's; the survivors are still finalized so their goals are reported beside the refusals, and a refusal beside goals classifies as mixed at the compile boundary. A name the lowering reports broken (`Context::set_broken`) is poisoned before the first item.

**Rationale.** One failure ended the run, so a file with three mistakes cost three compiles to learn about, and an editor could show one squiggle at a time; the per-item baseline the incremental compilation design rests on wants every item's verdict from one run. Silence for a dependent is the kernel's precedent — `curios-cert`'s recheck reports nothing for a declaration whose dependency it refused — and a report for one could only restate the refusal it depends on. Retraction over elaborated forms rather than lowered ones, and to a fixpoint, is what a witness resolved through the deferred store demands: the user's lowered body never names the witness, and the item that raised the goal may stand before the witness's declaration.

**Rejected.** A per-item universe rollback mark instead of clearing at the boundary: it would reinstate the journaling the decision above removed, and a rollback bracket may not contain retries. Undoing the metavariable store: nothing reaches an orphaned metavariable, since every module walk starts from surviving items and follows solutions from there. One line naming the root cause for a withheld dependent: every such line restates a report the reader already has, once per dependent. Reporting `no witness` where a refused witness stood: the reader can see the witness, and the miss is the refusal's consequence. Keeping a refused declaring item's registry entry in its lowered form: `zonk_module` zonks every entry the module carries and refuses an unsolved universe meta in one, so an entry whose item is gone goes with it.
