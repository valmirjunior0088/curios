# curios-elab

The Curios elaborator: bidirectional elaboration and typing, unification and metavariable solving, witness resolution and derivation, the fill and the proof of a bound, universe solving, zonking, and type erasure — the stage between `curios-text`'s lowering and the erased IR of `curios-ersd`. It is the side of the two-checker design that makes the language usable: it invents what an author leaves out — solutions, witnesses, the proof of a bound the facts in scope imply, universe levels — and everything it invents reaches `curios-cert` as an ordinary term, for a kernel that invents nothing to check ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)). The language rules it enforces are recorded in [the design decisions](../documentation/design); entry points and local architecture belong to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### Deep shared terms are cached, not special-cased

**Decision.** The lowerer emits `Rc`-shared DAGs — a string literal's certified UTF-8 derivation shares every per-byte scan-state chain — and the elaborator scales to them through generic mechanisms only, none of which costs work per occurrence of a shared node: an elaboration cache for metavariable-free, local-free subterms whose result names only already-defined globals (`Context::get_or_init_elaborated`, `elaboration_cacheable`); the reduction cache's probe and record pair (`Context::cached_reduced`, `Context::reduce`), which records nothing reduced across a question to conversion that was declined for a commit (`reduce`'s `remember`); the sort each type was classified at, kept while the context stands as it did (`Caches::sorts`), so a type whose graph shares a field is classified once per node; and each term's memoized derivations (`hash`, `reach`, `free_vars`, `has_local_free`, `has_metavar`) carried on the shared `Node` and filled by an iterative post-order walk, so a subtree memoizes once across every occurrence. No stage carries `Str`- or literal-specific machinery. The elaboration cache survives a fresh `define`, since its insert refuses a result naming a global not yet defined, so `define_entry` clears it only on a redefinition, and `set_island` clears it at every top-level item boundary.

**Rationale.** Sharing-oblivious elaboration costs O(N²) work in a literal's byte length, and these caches remove it; the depth is carried by `curios-utilities`'s `recurse`. The defect is the compiler's — recursive tree walks over DAG-shaped input — so the cure is compiler-general, and the next proof-carrying literal or generated spine benefits alike.

**Rejected.** Emitter-side pre-reduced scan states, which duplicate `/std/Str` inside the compiler; a canonical-spine case in conversion, a nominal `Str` exception to definitional equality; redesigning `/std/Str`'s evidence to sidestep depth, when the language may write deep certified data and the compiler must carry it. An elaboration cache spanning items waits on keying it by the item's island.

### Undecided conversions park; solving reads materialized, raw-parked spellings

**Decision.** Four rules make one discipline. `solve` materializes committed solutions in its candidate and in the occurrence's spine before any analysis (`zonk_solved_term_metas`), so a solved metavariable's spine cannot drag out-of-scope names into the scope check. A structural mismatch whose side is stuck on an unsolved metavariable — an intrinsic over one, a match mentioning one, an elimination headed by one — parks watching the metavariables instead of failing, while a stuck comparison with no metavariable in a blocking position keeps its hard mismatch (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`). Two stuck matches decompose only when both scrutinees are rigid or their motives and case tables are syntactically identical, since reduction can carry a rigid scrutinee past the layer a flexible one is stuck at and a goal minted across that misalignment commits wrongly. A goal parks under its raw spelling, so a retry re-reduces in its frame and watches the metavariables only the raw spelling mentions. Packed literals have a solving-only decomposition against `append` and `concat` spines (`packed_literal_view`), leaving the shared reduction and peel laws untouched. Survivors of the item drain report as postponements naming their watched metavariables (`Error::PostponedConversion`), distinct from rigid mismatches.

**Rationale.** A measurement of every explicitly supplied implicit argument in `/std` attributed almost all of them to these gaps; the incompleteness left — arm selection through a flexible scrutinee, refinement-dependent solutions — is deliberate, of the kind imitation staying a guess is.

**Rejected.** Pruning out-of-scope metavariables, whose measured consumers were all the unmaterialized-candidate defect; η-equating metavariable heads, which no program asked for; unifying constructor result indices before payload arguments, which repairs only sites postponement covers; match-match decomposition with a flexible scrutinee and differing arms, the wrong-commit shape.

### Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution

**Decision.** `elaborate_apply` settles every argument in one telescope walk, and the dependent substitution only ever receives elaborated terms or compiler-born metavariables. A form whose structure is still blocked — a tuple literal or a numeral at a type stuck on a metavariable, a lambda at a domain that is — becomes a `ParkedWork::Checking` obligation whose placeholder metavariable stands in the telescope: the wake machinery retries it when a solution lands, and what never wakes is answered where the term that awaits its type completes (`Context::complete`), by the choice the obligation states for itself (`ParkedWork::choice`): a `use` slot nothing solved takes the witness resolution finds for its goal, a literal gives the metavariable it waits on its shape, the numerals that wait on one type take their default together, a lambda whose every domain is annotated is checked at the type its annotations and body state, the conversions parked apart are asked again with spines held, two applications of one definition agreeing by whatever that solves and an application of a metavariable imitated against a type former's node, each having offered what that would solve before any is held, so that two which give one implicit two answers refuse the term, and a lambda with a domain left open is checked last, at the function type its expectation has come to, or, where it has come to none, at the type its annotations and body state, a lambda's body read at a codomain left open so that a choice it owns waits as an argument's does; one choice at a time and the lowest rank first. The item completes so too, before its levels close, and its drain reports what is left. Which term a choice waits for is kept with the metavariable it turns on (`Solutions::birth_with_kind`, `solve`, `park`): a metavariable is awaited from its birth by the innermost term being read that reads its parts against types holding one — a call, a list or a structure literal, a match with an elided motive, an operator, an intrinsic's or a foreign call's operands — and what a committed solution or a parked problem links is one class, awaited whole by the outermost standing term that awaited any of it. A term read with no expectation completes in full, so inference is local to a statement: a metavariable may outlive its statement and a choice may not, and a projection, a match or a call over a head whose type is still open is refused where it is read. A `!` reads its region's monad by imitation where it stands, the region being its expectation. Insertion of implicit metavariables and witness goals happens in the same walk, at domains opened through the elaborated prefix, and a witness goal whose slot a later part of the call can name, a later parameter's type or the result type mentioning its binder, parks there as a `ParkedWork::Witness` obligation: a wake drops one whose slot unification solved and resolves none, so an argument whose type names a dictionary gives it to the call whichever of the two the walk reaches first. A slot nothing after it names has resolution as its one source and is resolved where it stands, as an operator's is and a premise of the scheme resolution takes. A retry decides its problem in exactly the context the problem parked in, standing on the frames the problem holds with the live local frames set aside (`Context::standing_on`); only its obligation fills a placeholder, with the term it rebuilt, conversion waiting on one as it waits on a recursive group's slot; whatever runs an obligation — a wake's retry, a term's completion, the drain — holds it out of the store, so its term is elaborated once; and a rollback bracket may not contain retries.

**Rationale.** A choice is an answer nothing forces, one among several its problem admits. Taken where the walk first reads it, it makes a call admitted or refused by the order of its parameters and a list by the order of its elements; taken where its term has read every part, it is informed by all of them, each rank committing less than the next and able to decide it, and two parts that would give one implicit two answers are told to the author, where either taken alone would be the order they were written in. Substituting raw spellings — written hidden arguments, postponed introduction forms — into the telescope lets inserted goals and birth types record spellings the reducer's polymorphic-variable gate leaves inert, which conversion can never connect to the rebuilt forms; one walk removes the category. Beneath the floor nothing live is visible and a birth telescope keeps one entry per name, so no binder doubles into a non-linear identity spine and no arm's re-typing is skipped or leaked into a problem parked outside it. A placeholder conversion could solve would stand in the tree as a term the program never wrote, the written one unchecked or held to it by a second comparison; with one writer, a call holds its argument as written, and what the call's type states of that argument meets it once it is checked. What is given up is a check only conversion's solution of its own placeholder could have typed.

**Rejected.** Reconciling a solution conversion reached first with the term the obligation rebuilds, which gives a placeholder two writers, every site that runs an obligation a protocol to keep, and the tree conversion's spelling where the author's was written; an alias metavariable conversion may solve, equated with the placeholder once that is filled, which costs a second hole and a parked conversion for every obligation; the bracketed best-effort `expect` over raw spellings as a fallback, which measured across the corpus never discharged an obligation the wake machinery and the obligation's own answer did not; a force tier at the end of each call's walk, which answers a call's obligations in the order of its slots, so that of two literals at one type the first written is synthesized and the second held to it; restoring each frozen name at its innermost type over the live context, which still lets a verdict depend on where the retry was scheduled.

Of where a choice is taken: every choice held to its item's end, as Lean holds a default instance to its region's and Rust an integer's to its function's, under which what reads the type next meets none, so every reader has to be able to wait, and a value stands in the types after it here, where a numeral not yet realized is a placeholder conversion waits on; one constraint system an expression, solved whole, as Swift does, whose search is exponential in the worst case and which a dependent telescope, opening each domain through the arguments before it, would have to replay; which term awaits read by a pass over the parked problems, where a term settles or at every question, which misses a problem that is out of the store being retried; a metavariable handed outward alone where it is linked, which leaves behind what an earlier link held to it, so that `g(id(id(5)), b)` is refused where `g(id(5), b)` compiles; a depth in place of the term that awaits, two terms opened by a retry standing at one depth; a numeral as a fourth form the call walk parks, which leaves one under a nested call, in a list or in a match arm committing where it is read; literals whose type involves no choice settled first, rustc's two passes, where `g((5, 1), (-3, b))` holds two that must share one; an open part of a shape at the level of the metavariable it shapes, which closes a cycle through a maximum where a field holds a sort, and a product held to that metavariable before its fields are read, which sizes a tuple of proofs as a type's; a settled lambda's codomain born outside its binders, under which `id((A: Type, x: A) => x)` stops compiling, or left open only where the body is itself a numeral, a literal or a lambda, which leaves `g((x: Byte) => id(0), (y: Byte) => b)` refused in one order; the first written of two last-resort candidates that differ, which is the order of the parameters again; a lambda with a domain left open read ahead of the conversions held, under which an action lifted into its region's monad, typed by an imitation, leaves the continuation's body read blind; a held conversion that counts only where it converts, which discards an imitation whose argument pair stands between two open types, the key the lift's witness is found by; the item's choices taken by its drain, once its levels have closed, under which an imitation's instance of its family is closed by nothing; an application imitated at once where no term awaits its metavariable, which reads two parts of one expected type in the order they are written; a projection or a match that waits for its head's type, which nothing reaches once a term completes what it awaits and which every later reader of the stand-in would have to wait behind; and a lambda postponed for a domain of its expected type alone, where a continuation whose codomain the call's expected type gives is read after that type has arrived: read before it, a `!` in its body meets a region `Io(?)`, which the region's imitation reads no monad from.

### The universe journal is scoped, and its brackets are paired by hand

**Decision.** `ConstraintStore` records a rewrite's pre-image only while a speculative scope is open, and change detection reads an `Entropy` tick — the currency the cache stamp above the store advances — rather than the journal's length. A scope is opened by `mark` and closed by `release`, and a mark carries the depth to restore rather than a count to decrement, so closing an outer scope closes any inner one a bracket left open; the one reset is `abandon_speculation`, at an item boundary a refusal reached by unwinding every bracket. Every bracket pairs the two by hand: `Context::solution_mark` opens a scope through `UniverseSolver::mark`, and `Context::end_solutions` closes it through `UniverseSolver::release` on each of the bracket's exits — on the witness-resolution cycle (`probe_match`, `commit_match`, `instantiate`), in conversion, in suggestion and in entailment alike.

**Rationale.** An unconditional journal makes a declaration's peak superlinear: a substitution widens the constraints it lands in, so the journal keeps every intermediate width of every constraint it rewrites. Restoring rather than decrementing makes the depth safe to get wrong only toward too high, where the journal is dropped later than it could be; a reset inside a live scope drops pre-images a pending rollback needs, leaving the store inconsistent with the solutions log.

**Rejected.** Closure brackets in the manner of `Context::with_frame`, which close every scope by construction: `instantiate` recurses once per nested witness premise, and a closure around its whole body keeps every local in a closure frame while the outer frame holds the arguments, roughly doubling the per-level footprint on the deepest recursion in the crate, which overflows an unoptimized build's stack on the prelude; `#[inline(always)]` cannot fold a closure body away. Raising the stack, when recursive lowering on the default stack is a contract. Capping build parallelism, which spreads a build script's peak rather than lowering it.

### A refused item is undone and its dependents are withheld

**Decision.** Elaboration recovers per item. An item that fails is reported, undone and poisoned, and the other items elaborate as if it had never been written; an item that reaches a poisoned name — by mention, by construction, through a registry entry, or through a witness a refused or withheld declaration declares — is withheld and reports nothing. Undone is everything a later item could read: the item's base-frame bindings, its registry and witness-table entries (each witness key poisoned in place), its parked work, the terms it recorded for the erasure obligations, its universe constraints, and every speculative scope its unwinding left open. Left in place is what nothing reads: the metavariables it minted, its totality verdict, and the caches, which clear wholesale. An attempt that read a declaration not yet elaborated is void: undone the same way, poisoning nothing, and made again once that declaration has elaborated ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../documentation/design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)), so no refusal surfaces after its item has finished. A name the lowering reports broken (`Context::set_broken`) is poisoned before the first item. A refused or withheld witness has the key its signature is spelled at poisoned, whether or not it had registered. Every refusal of a run is reported in item order as one batch (`Error::Batch`), survivors are finalized so their goals report beside the refusals, and the run reports what it dropped as well as what it refused.

**Rationale.** A file with three mistakes should cost one compile to learn about, and the per-item baseline incremental compilation rests on wants every item's verdict from one run. Silence for a dependent is the kernel's precedent: a report for one could only restate the refusal it depends on. A withheld witness's key is the one thing a later item can still observe of it, and without it a consumer would report `no witness` at a declaration with nothing wrong. A withheld item reports nothing, so what a run dropped cannot be read off its refusals.

**Rejected.** A per-item universe rollback mark, which reinstates the unscoped journal; undoing the metavariable store, which nothing reaches once every walk starts from surviving items; a line per withheld dependent naming the root cause; reporting `no witness` where a refused or withheld witness stood; poisoning a whole concept when a withheld witness's key cannot be read, which silences a genuinely missing witness elsewhere; elaborating a withheld witness's signature to read its key, which its spelling already gives; deriving what a run dropped from the names its module no longer declares, which cannot tell a deliberate omission from a loss; keeping a refused item's registry entry in its lowered form, which nothing zonked and the kernel would refuse for its unsolved universe metas.

### Totality is recorded as each definition is defined

**Decision.** Each definition's totality verdict is recorded as it is defined — a declaration elaborates after what it reads, so everything a definition mentions outside its own group is already classified — and a written type that mentions a definition already known partial is refused before it is elaborated. The verdict every other declaration reads, and the one the definition's stamp carries, is decided over its final terms as its item is published, once ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../documentation/design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). The obligations (T) and (V) of [Totality of the erased program](../documentation/design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md) are decided with it, over the item's positions and the terms its elaboration recorded, and the entry's once the module is zonked. (T) is seeded from two halves, neither containing the other: a syntactic walk over written type positions, seeding the body of every definition whose declared type ends in a sort — how it reaches `/big_nat/Canonical`'s `is_trimmed`, which no annotation records — and the settle records of every term whose own type is a sort, which catch a type passed as an argument, `ignore(@Shape(inf), 5)`, where the walk has no case for an application's parameters. (V) keeps the `Prop`-sorted records and needs nothing else.

**Rationale.** The obligations are decided after the type-level reduction (T) exists to make safe, so a type reaching a partial definition that is not productive spins until the budget dies, refused for a resource no budget would have helped and naming neither the definition nor the reason; the early net makes both shapes report the partial definition. A term is a proof exactly when its type is a proposition, a question elaboration has decided for every term, so recording it is complete where re-deriving it is not; giving the walk a case for application parameters would re-derive the head's telescope, a second reading of a question elaboration has already decided.

### Generated declarations name their owner

**Decision.** Lowering records whether a definition is authored or generated and records a generated definition's owner explicitly; ownership is never inferred from a flattened path's spelling. An entry and the type former that carries it share one finalized context. Generated constructor and concept-method wrappers generalize their own universe schemes, since each may use a subset of, or dependencies beyond, its owner's scheme, and their owner metadata validates provenance without conflating a wrapper's binders with the entry's.

### A refinement is keyed on the guard as written and decided at the read

**Decision.** An arm's equation is recorded under the scrutinee's `shallow_scrutinee` — universe instances erased, solved metavariables materialized, arguments as written — and, for a guard over the arm's own local definitions, also under the spelling with those definitions substituted (`curios_analysis::Unfolding`), found by exact lookup only. A guard is registered in the shape the reducer presents it, operands included, by rewriting and never evaluating: `spine_whnf` opens application layers, and `reduce_intrinsic` under a reducer that returns its operands untouched performs the half of a fold that acts on operands already in hand. No key is reduced to compare it: an occurrence spelled otherwise than its guard is met at the stuck form reduction leaves it as, against the entry's reduced spelling. The reduced spelling is settled as the kernel settles it — on a miss at a stuck form, under the kernel's locals filter, read on the scrutinee as the kernel spells it (`Context::kernel_spelling`), with the entry's frame and every frame inside it withheld — and compared by the probe's own spelling, its dual and its spelling across the successor seam being the shared rule's to answer (`refined_reduct`, `curios-analysis`'s `answers`); a settled spelling is kept beside its entry, in the entry's frame, for as long as nothing can change what its key reduces to (`Frame::scrutinee_spellings`). An equation in force that names a variable an arm refines is restated at the arm's value for as long as the arm stands, the recorded one stepping aside, as the kernel restates one under the solution it checks an arm with (`Context::restate_equations`); and a key, where it is recorded and where it is asked, and a stuck form put to the reduced spellings are read with every refined variable spelled as its value (`Context::spelled_as_refined`). Levels are decided at the read: `Context::scrutinee_reduct` and `Context::proj_reduct` hold the probe and the scrutinized spelling of a hit to one universe instance, every level they differ in committed equal, and decline the hit where the two cannot be (`hold_at_one_instance`, sharing `align_universe_levels` with `identify_universe_levels`), as conversion identifies differing levels rather than erasing them. That read compares every level, as the kernel's key does; past a key, a stuck form is declined against an equation's reduced spelling only on a level conversion compares, one a nominal family is irrelevant in being no difference there (`levels_clash_to_conversion`), and a question that recorded only weak equations committed nothing. A key is never a reduct: under a suppressed frame the reducer hands back the probe as it was spelled.

**Rationale.** Registering a fact must not cost its subject: reducing arguments to record a key would evaluate the accumulation a guard exists to avoid evaluating. A level-exact key cannot exist when an arm is entered, because universe metavariables are solved throughout the body, so the exactness belongs to the comparison, and the comparison commits what the hit rests on: two instances still open at the read would otherwise close wherever their own bounds put them, and apart, the elaborator has refined a term the kernel's key does not answer; and `Type u` embeds a level in a term, so a key read as a quotient by definitional equality would identify `Type 0` with `Type 1`. An equation is restated because the kernel substitutes a case's solution through the arm it checks: the instance is the key both checkers then hold, and a fact written at the case value meets it for one lookup, where a variable left spelled in the key would meet it only by reducing. The kernel needs no rule at the read because it is handed ground instances; its half is [Case equations and their key](../documentation/design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)'s. `reduce::reduction_tests::scrutinee_refinement_does_not_fire_at_another_ground_universe_instance` and its projection twin hold the read, with `scrutinee_refinement_holds_fresh_universe_instances_to_one` and its projection twin for two instances still open, and `curios`'s `tests::universes::instance_tests::a_case_equation_read_at_another_instance_holds_the_two_to_one` for a program both checkers then judge alike; `convert::conversion_tests::value_conversion_does_not_identify_distinct_type_payloads` holds conversion; `reduce::reduction_tests::a_case_equation_follows_the_solution_an_arm_is_checked_under` holds the restating under the name the kernel's test of it has; and `curios`'s `tests::matching::refinement_tests` hold the guard shapes.

**Rejected.** Evaluating arguments at registration, which prices a fact at its subject; declining a hit only where its two instances are both decided and apart, which leaves two still open to close apart after the read; recording the pairs a hit assumed and checking them where the declaration closes, which refuses a program the commitment compiles; re-keying the stores wherever levels move, since `Caches::invalidate_for_universe_rewrite` fires at rewrites while arms are probed between them; recording the kernel's substituted spelling in place of the written one, which loses the arm's own occurrences their cheap hit, or beside it and settled like it, which costs more than an alias found by exact lookup; reducing a key's arguments at each probe that missed, to compare it with a candidate reduced the same way, which priced a miss at a reduction of every key under its head, needed a memo of its own, and met a fact written at a case value only through whatever the refinements live at the probe reduced; keeping a recorded equation beside its restated instance, which doubles the equations in force at each nested match on a variable one names.

### Erasure reads a head's type off the finished term

**Decision.** Erasure is directed by types and types no term. The walk carries the type each expression was checked against; where an elimination's head has none to carry — a callee, a projected record, a scrutinee, an index a proof's match pins — `Lowering::type_of` reads it: a name's from its assumption, an application's by opening its head's telescope at the arguments, a projection's from the field it selects, and a constructor's, a literal's and a lambda's from the node itself. A `Bool`, `Nat` or sequence match states its scrutinee's type, so none is read. Each reading is charged a step and kept for the item being erased, so a chain of eliminations reads each of its prefixes once. What it asks of the engine is a type's head, by the reduction that accepted the term, and nothing in `into_ersd` can call `elaborate`: the walk holds a reader that offers no rule that elaborates ([A context's state is two halves](#a-contexts-state-is-two-halves-and-a-reader-of-finished-terms-cannot-name-the-second)).

**Rationale.** The term is elaborated and certified, so its types are a function of it. Asking the elaborator for them ran the rules that decide what an author may write over a term no author wrote, in a context seeded with no concept and no witness — a lambda with a `use` parameter, applied on the spot, elaborated and was then refused for the type of its own parameter — and typed a head once per head enclosing it, checking every argument again each time. A name is minted once and assumed at one type, so a reading holds wherever the term stands, and the table needs no invalidation inside an item.

**Rejected.** Registering concepts in the erasure context, or bracketing each surface rule off as privacy was, which leaves every later rule to be remembered; a memo over the elaborator's inference, which keeps those rules running; types carried on Core's eliminations, which the certifier would have to check or erasure would have to believe; reading the certifier's own typing walk, which this crate cannot depend on and a build script erasing the prelude never runs; erasing an applied lambda as the `let` it reduces to, which is an optimization where erasure is a transcription.

### A context's state is two halves, and a reader of finished terms cannot name the second

**Decision.** `Context`'s state is two halves, by what each exists for. `Typing` is what any judgment reads and the machine it runs on: the binder supply, the budget (`Spend`), the frames, the registries, the caches over them, the protocol by which reduction asks conversion (`Asking`) and the names the type-directed features emit. `Underway` is what exists because a unit is being elaborated, and each lifetime in it is a value whose type holds the behaviour that touches nothing else: what one attempt mints and solves is an `Attempt`, exchanged whole (`Context::exchange`); what a walk knows of the declarations that have not finished is `Needs`; the witnesses a unit declares before they elaborate are `DeclaredWitnesses`, out of the registries; the binders the elaborator's proofs read are `Credits`; the terms elaboration settled are `CheckedTerms`; and where a judgment stands is `Posture`. `Context` is where the stores coordinate — a write to one that stamps or clears another's cache, a lookup whose miss records a need — and the crate's one door to them: no module outside `context/` names a store. The engine is one: reduction asks conversion, conversion checks a solution by typing it, and typing drives parked work, over `Context`, parameterized by no mode and split by no trait. Erasure, which elaborates nothing, reads through `Retyping`: a context the reader makes for itself, on a budget and the syntax registry, and keeps private, offering of it what a walk over finished terms needs and nothing else — the binder supply, the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget, and the engine's entries by name, which are a type's head, its sort, whether it is a proposition, a neutral's type, an arm's equation and a match's indices. Its reduction asks conversion what the elaborator's asks, at a stuck fold and at a missed equation. No caller hands erasure a context, and no file under `into_ersd/` names `Context`.

**Rationale.** What a judgment can reach follows what the judgment is for. Every lifetime in one struct leaves what is set aside together to each method that sets it aside, so an attempt exchanged a field at a time is whole only until a field is added; a value exchanged whole has no field to forget. A context handed to erasure is fresh for as long as every caller makes one for the call, and the type takes an elaboration's as readily, with its attempt's solutions, its unfinished declarations and its registries in it; a reader that makes its own is handed none. Erasure's input is a `Zonked<Module>`, so that it holds no metavariable is evidence; that nothing it reads through holds one is the same fact stated of the context. And erasure needs the head of every callee, record and scrutinee it eliminates, which may show only where reduction puts a question to conversion — `f(same(n)) == f(n)` is `true` to conversion and stuck to the fold, which pairs atoms by spelling — so the reader reads a type by the reduction both checkers accepted the term by (`curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests`).

**Rejected.** The engine generic over a mode, so that the type erasure holds has no second half: reduction and conversion compiled twice, typing reached through a hook where conversion checks a solution, and every function of the engine made generic to remove a value that is empty. The reader alone, over the context as one struct: erasure cannot name the state, and the elaborator still holds every lifetime in one. A context the caller keeps empty, which is correct for as long as every caller remembers. The stores as the crate's interface, each caller handed the one it reads: most lookups coordinate two, so the door they share stays one type. The reader's reduction plain, asking conversion nothing (`Context::plainly`): a callee typed through a decision only conversion makes leaves erasure no head to read.

### A structural type's runtime shape is its telescope's

**Decision.** A function has a runtime parameter for each binder of its telescope, and an anonymous tuple a field for each member. A slot whose type is a proof or a type, where the value is written, holds a content-free stand-in and is not computed (`Lowering::kept_operand`): the unit constant, or, for a function a callee may apply, a function of that type's arity returning it. Only a nominal declaration drops: a structure's fields and a constructor's payload are classified once, against the declaration's own signature with its binders opaque (`constructor_entries`), and every construction, projection and match reads that row. So a product that is to cost one field for a value and its proof is a structure, and a parameter nothing reads is the optimizer's to remove where it sees every call (`curios-cont`'s `simplify`).

**Rationale.** Whether a binder carries anything is read off its type, and a value of a structural type stands at several types, a substitution or a refinement apart. A lambda is checked against its callee's parameter type with the caller's arguments in it, and the callee calls it at the type its own declaration states; a universe stands where a `Type` variable is declared, and a proposition where a variable at a `Sort` is; an arm's refinement reduces a domain that is stuck outside the arm. A domain one of them reads as a proof or a type another reads as a variable, so a shape decided by either reading is one the other does not have: a function called with an argument it has no parameter for, a tuple read at a field it was built without. A telescope's length is the one thing every reading has. A declaration is read by name, so its row is one fact wherever it is read (`curios`'s `tests::erasure::shape_tests`).

**Rejected.** Dropping a parameter or a field by the type at each site, which is the defect. Dropping a declaration's own parameters at a direct call to it, with a wrapper wherever the declaration is used as a value: two calling conventions in erasure, for what the optimizer removes where it sees every call. A coercion wherever a value passes between two types that read a binder apart, which is every flow of a function or a tuple erasure would have to find, and one missed is a wrong answer. A mark on the binder that says what is kept, which is a quantity, and a change to the language.
