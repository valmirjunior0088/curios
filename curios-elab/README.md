# curios-elab

The Curios elaborator: bidirectional elaboration and typing, unification and metavariable solving, witness resolution and derivation, the fill and the proof of a bound, universe solving, zonking, and type erasure — the stage between `curios-text`'s lowering and the erased IR of `curios-ersd`. It is the side of the two-checker design that makes the language usable: it invents what an author leaves out — solutions, witnesses, the proof of a bound the facts in scope imply, universe levels — and everything it invents reaches `curios-cert` as an ordinary term, for a kernel that invents nothing to check ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)). The language rules it enforces are recorded in [the design decisions](../documentation/design); entry points and local architecture belong to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### Deep shared terms are cached, not special-cased

**Decision.** The lowerer emits `Rc`-shared DAGs — a string literal's certified UTF-8 derivation shares every per-byte scan-state chain — and the elaborator scales to them through generic mechanisms only, none of which costs work per occurrence of a shared node: an elaboration cache for metavariable-free, local-free subterms whose result names only already-defined globals (`Context::get_or_init_elaborated`, `elaboration_cacheable`); the reduction cache's probe and record pair (`Context::cached_reduced`, `Context::reduce`); and each term's memoized derivations (`hash`, `reach`, `free_vars`, `has_local_free`, `has_metavar`) carried on the shared `Node` and filled by an iterative post-order walk, so a subtree memoizes once across every occurrence. No stage carries `Str`- or literal-specific machinery. The elaboration cache survives a fresh `define`, since its insert refuses a result naming a global not yet defined, so `define_entry` clears it only on a redefinition, and `set_island` clears it at every top-level item boundary.

**Rationale.** Sharing-oblivious elaboration costs O(N²) work in a literal's byte length, and these caches remove it; the depth is carried by `curios-utilities`'s `recurse`. The defect is the compiler's — recursive tree walks over DAG-shaped input — so the cure is compiler-general, and the next proof-carrying literal or generated spine benefits alike.

**Rejected.** Emitter-side pre-reduced scan states, which duplicate `/std/Str` inside the compiler; a canonical-spine case in conversion, a nominal `Str` exception to definitional equality; redesigning `/std/Str`'s evidence to sidestep depth, when the language may write deep certified data and the compiler must carry it. An elaboration cache spanning items waits on keying it by the item's island.

### Undecided conversions park; solving reads materialized, raw-parked spellings

**Decision.** Four rules make one discipline. `solve` materializes committed solutions in its candidate and in the occurrence's spine before any analysis (`zonk_solved_term_metas`), so a solved metavariable's spine cannot drag out-of-scope names into the scope check. A structural mismatch whose side is stuck on an unsolved metavariable — an intrinsic over one, a match mentioning one, an elimination headed by one — parks watching the metavariables instead of failing, while a stuck comparison with no metavariable in a blocking position keeps its hard mismatch (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`). Two stuck matches decompose only when both scrutinees are rigid or their motives and case tables are syntactically identical, since reduction can carry a rigid scrutinee past the layer a flexible one is stuck at and a goal minted across that misalignment commits wrongly. A goal parks under its raw spelling, so a retry re-reduces in its frame and watches the metavariables only the raw spelling mentions. Packed literals have a solving-only decomposition against `append` and `concat` spines (`packed_literal_view`), leaving the shared reduction and peel laws untouched. Survivors of the item drain report as postponements naming their watched metavariables (`Error::PostponedConversion`), distinct from rigid mismatches.

**Rationale.** A measurement of every explicitly supplied implicit argument in `/std` attributed almost all of them to these gaps; the incompleteness left — arm selection through a flexible scrutinee, refinement-dependent solutions — is deliberate, of the kind imitation staying a guess is.

**Rejected.** Pruning out-of-scope metavariables, whose measured consumers were all the unmaterialized-candidate defect; η-equating metavariable heads, which no program asked for; unifying constructor result indices before payload arguments, which repairs only sites postponement covers; match-match decomposition with a flexible scrutinee and differing arms, the wrong-commit shape.

### Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution

**Decision.** `elaborate_apply` settles every argument in one telescope walk, and the dependent substitution only ever receives elaborated terms or compiler-born metavariables. A checked-only introduction form whose structure is still blocked becomes a `ParkedWork::Checking` obligation whose placeholder metavariable stands in the telescope: the wake machinery retries it when a solution lands, and a force tier at the end of the walk checks whatever never woke. Insertion of implicit metavariables and witness goals happens in the same walk, at domains opened through the elaborated prefix. A retry decides its problem in exactly the context the problem froze, beneath a floor that hides the live local frames and with the frozen frame restored whole above it (`Context::with_retry_frame`); a placeholder solved ahead of its retry ends the obligation without re-elaborating; and a rollback bracket may not contain retries.

**Rationale.** Substituting raw spellings — written hidden arguments, postponed introduction forms — into the telescope lets inserted goals and birth types record spellings the reducer's polymorphic-variable gate leaves inert, which conversion can never connect to the rebuilt forms; one walk removes the category. Beneath the floor nothing live is visible and a birth telescope keeps one entry per name, so no binder doubles into a non-linear identity spine and no arm's re-typing is skipped or leaked into a problem parked outside it.

**Rejected.** A frozen placeholder species the unifier may not solve, solver surface only protecting the discipline; the bracketed best-effort `expect` over raw spellings as a fallback, which measured across the corpus never discharged an obligation the wake machinery and the force tier did not; restoring each frozen name at its innermost type over the live context, which still lets a verdict depend on where the retry was scheduled.

### The universe journal is scoped, and its brackets are paired by hand

**Decision.** `ConstraintStore` records a rewrite's pre-image only while a speculative scope is open, and change detection reads an `Entropy` tick — the currency the cache stamp above the store advances — rather than the journal's length. A scope is opened by `mark` and closed by `release`, and a mark carries the depth to restore rather than a count to decrement, so closing an outer scope closes any inner one a bracket left open; the one reset is `abandon_speculation`, at an item boundary a refusal reached by unwinding every bracket. Every bracket pairs the two by hand: `Context::solution_mark` opens a scope through `UniverseSolver::mark`, and `Context::end_solutions` closes it through `UniverseSolver::release` on each of the bracket's exits — on the witness-resolution cycle (`probe_match`, `commit_match`, `instantiate`), in conversion, in suggestion and in entailment alike.

**Rationale.** An unconditional journal makes a declaration's peak superlinear: a substitution widens the constraints it lands in, so the journal keeps every intermediate width of every constraint it rewrites. Restoring rather than decrementing makes the depth safe to get wrong only toward too high, where the journal is dropped later than it could be; a reset inside a live scope drops pre-images a pending rollback needs, leaving the store inconsistent with the solutions log.

**Rejected.** Closure brackets in the manner of `Context::with_frame`, which close every scope by construction: `instantiate` recurses once per nested witness premise, and a closure around its whole body keeps every local in a closure frame while the outer frame holds the arguments, roughly doubling the per-level footprint on the deepest recursion in the crate, which overflows an unoptimized build's stack on the prelude; `#[inline(always)]` cannot fold a closure body away. Raising the stack, when recursive lowering on the default stack is a contract. Capping build parallelism, which spreads a build script's peak rather than lowering it.

### A refused item is undone and its dependents are withheld

**Decision.** Elaboration recovers per item. An item that fails is reported, undone and poisoned, and the items after it elaborate as if it had never been written; an item that reaches a poisoned name — by mention, by construction, through a registry entry, or through a witness a refused declaration registered — is withheld and reports nothing. Undone is everything a later item could read: the item's base-frame bindings, its registry and witness-table entries (each witness key poisoned in place), its parked work and deferred goals, the terms it recorded for the erasure obligations, its universe constraints, and every speculative scope its unwinding left open. Left in place is what nothing reads: the metavariables it minted, its totality verdict, and the caches, which clear wholesale. A refusal that surfaces after its item elaborated — a deferred witness goal that never found its entry — retracts the item and every elaborated item reaching it, to a fixpoint over the elaborated forms. A name the lowering reports broken (`Context::set_broken`) is poisoned before the first item. A withheld item that declares a witness has its key poisoned, its signature alone elaborated under the undoing mark to read the key. Every refusal of a run is reported in item order as one batch (`Error::Batch`), survivors are finalized so their goals report beside the refusals, and the run reports what it dropped as well as what it refused.

**Rationale.** A file with three mistakes should cost one compile to learn about, and the per-item baseline incremental compilation rests on wants every item's verdict from one run. Silence for a dependent is the kernel's precedent: a report for one could only restate the refusal it depends on. Retraction over elaborated forms, to a fixpoint, is what a witness resolved through the deferred store demands, since the lowered body never names it. A withheld witness's key is the one thing a later item can still observe of it, and without it a consumer would report `no witness` at a declaration with nothing wrong. A withheld item reports nothing, so what a run dropped cannot be read off its refusals.

**Rejected.** A per-item universe rollback mark, which reinstates the unscoped journal; undoing the metavariable store, which nothing reaches once every walk starts from surviving items; a line per withheld dependent naming the root cause; reporting `no witness` where a refused or withheld witness stood; poisoning a whole concept when a withheld witness's key cannot be read, which silences a genuinely missing witness elsewhere; reading the key off the lowered signature, a second reading of what a key is; deriving what a run dropped from the names its module no longer declares, which cannot tell a deliberate omission from a loss; keeping a refused item's registry entry in its lowered form, which `zonk_module` would refuse for its unsolved universe metas.

### Totality is recorded as each definition is defined

**Decision.** Each definition's totality verdict is recorded as it is defined — items arrive in dependency order, so everything a definition mentions is already classified — and a written type that mentions a definition already known partial is refused before it is elaborated. The post-zonk obligations, (T) and (V) of [Totality of the erased program](../documentation/design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md), still run over everything elaboration produced. (T) is seeded from two halves, neither containing the other: a syntactic walk over written type positions, seeding the body of every definition whose declared type ends in a sort — how it reaches `/big_nat/Canonical`'s `is_trimmed`, which no annotation records — and the settle records of every term whose own type is a sort, which catch a type passed as an argument, `ignore(@Shape(inf), 5)`, where the walk has no case for an application's parameters. (V) keeps the `Prop`-sorted records and needs nothing else.

**Rationale.** The post-zonk gate runs after the type-level reduction (T) exists to make safe, so a type reaching a partial definition that is not productive spins until the budget dies, refused for a resource no budget would have helped and naming neither the definition nor the reason; the early net makes both shapes report the partial definition. A term is a proof exactly when its type is a proposition, a question elaboration has decided for every term, so recording it is complete where re-deriving it is not; giving the walk a case for application parameters would re-derive the head's telescope, a second reading of a question elaboration has already decided.

### Generated declarations name their owner

**Decision.** Lowering records whether a definition is authored or generated and records a generated definition's owner explicitly; ownership is never inferred from a flattened path's spelling. Registry entries and their type-former definitions share one finalized context. Generated constructor and concept-method wrappers generalize their own universe schemes, since each may use a subset of, or dependencies beyond, its owner's scheme, and their owner metadata validates provenance without conflating a wrapper's binders with the registry's.

### A refinement is keyed on the guard as written and decided at the read

**Decision.** An arm's equation is recorded under the scrutinee's `shallow_scrutinee` — universe instances erased, solved metavariables materialized, arguments as written — and, for a guard over the arm's own local definitions, also under the spelling with those definitions substituted (`curios_analysis::Unfolding`), found by exact lookup only. A guard is registered in the shape the reducer presents it, operands included, by rewriting and never evaluating: `spine_whnf` opens application layers, and `reduce_intrinsic` under a reducer that returns its operands untouched performs the half of a fold that acts on operands already in hand. `canonical_scrutinee` is asked only after the written spelling missed. The reduced spelling is settled as the kernel settles it — on a miss at a stuck form, under the kernel's locals filter, with the entry's frame and every frame inside it withheld — and compared through the dual and successor spellings (`refined_reduct`), and a settled answer is final. Levels are decided at the read: `Context::scrutinee_reduct` and `Context::proj_reduct` decline a hit where the probe and the scrutinized spelling disagree on a level both have decided (`levels_clash_on_a_decided_instance`, sharing `align_universe_levels` with `identify_universe_levels`), and conversion identifies differing levels rather than erasing them. A key is never a reduct: under a suppressed frame the reducer hands back the probe as it was spelled.

**Rationale.** Registering a fact must not cost its subject: reducing arguments to record a key would evaluate the accumulation a guard exists to avoid evaluating. A level-exact key cannot exist when an arm is entered, because universe metavariables are solved throughout the body, so the exactness belongs to the comparison; and `Type u` embeds a level in a term, so a key read as a quotient by definitional equality would identify `Type 0` with `Type 1`. The kernel needs no rule at the read because it is handed ground instances; its half is [Case equations and their key](../documentation/design/soundness/elimination/case-equations-and-their-key.md)'s. `reduce::reduction_tests::scrutinee_refinement_does_not_fire_at_another_ground_universe_instance` and its projection twin hold the read, with `scrutinee_refinement_ignores_fresh_universe_instances` and `projection_refinement_ignores_fresh_universe_instances` as controls; `convert::conversion_tests::value_conversion_does_not_identify_distinct_type_payloads` holds conversion; and `curios`'s `tests::matching::refinement_tests` hold the guard shapes.

**Rejected.** Evaluating arguments at registration, which prices a fact at its subject; re-keying the stores wherever levels move, since `Caches::invalidate_for_universe_rewrite` fires at rewrites while arms are probed between them; recording the kernel's substituted spelling in place of the written one, which loses the arm's own occurrences their cheap hit, or beside it and settled like it, which costs more than an alias found by exact lookup.

### Erasure reads a head's type off the finished term

**Decision.** Erasure is directed by types and judges none. The walk carries the type each expression was checked against; where an elimination's head has none to carry — a callee, a projected record, a scrutinee, an index a proof's match pins — `Lowering::type_of` reads it: a name's from its assumption, an application's by opening its head's telescope at the arguments, a projection's from the field it selects, and a constructor's, a literal's and a lambda's from the node itself. A `Bool`, `Nat` or sequence match states its scrutinee's type, so none is read. Each reading is charged a step and kept for the item being erased, so a chain of eliminations reads each of its prefixes once. Nothing in `into_ersd` calls `elaborate`.

**Rationale.** The term is elaborated and certified, so its types are a function of it. Asking the elaborator for them ran the rules that decide what an author may write over a term no author wrote, in a context seeded with no concept and no witness — a lambda with a `use` parameter, applied on the spot, elaborated and was then refused for the type of its own parameter — and typed a head once per head enclosing it, checking every argument again each time. A name is minted once and assumed at one type, so a reading holds wherever the term stands, and the table needs no invalidation inside an item.

**Rejected.** Registering concepts in the erasure context, or bracketing each surface rule off as privacy was, which leaves every later rule to be remembered; a memo over the elaborator's inference, which keeps those rules running; types carried on Core's eliminations, which the certifier would have to check or erasure would have to believe; reading the certifier's own typing walk, which this crate cannot depend on and a build script erasing the prelude never runs; erasing an applied lambda as the `let` it reduces to, which is an optimization where erasure is a transcription.
