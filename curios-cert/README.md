# curios-cert

The Curios certifier, what Lean and Rocq call the kernel: it decides, from a finished term alone, whether the elaborator's output is well typed — reduction, sort, conversion, the typing judgment, nominal elimination, subsumption, the whole-module walk that applies them, the erasure obligations, and the verdict on a universe context it assumes. It is the side of the two-checker design that only checks, inventing nothing — no metavariable, no search, no inference beyond typing what is written — and it shares with the elaborator only the layer both judge through, `curios-core`, `curios-num`, `curios-algebra` and `curios-analysis` ([An independent certifier re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-certifier-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)). This crate owns what a term *means*; the cross-cutting rules it judges belong to the [soundness decisions](../documentation/design/soundness/), and local architecture to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### The trusted base is a crate boundary

**Decision.** The trusted base is this crate's dependency closure, `cargo tree -p curios-cert --edges normal`, rather than the call-closure of checking entry points inside a larger crate. It reaches the shared layer — `curios-analysis`, `curios-algebra`, `curios-core`, `curios-num` — and their foundations: `curios-common`, `curios-abi`, and `curios-archive` with its derive macro `curios-archive-derive`, which serialize and time rather than decide. Beneath those are five third-party roots and what each pulls: `num-bigint` with `num-integer` and `num-traits`, the unbounded arithmetic under `curios-num`; `sha2` and its `digest` family under `curios-common`; `stacker` with `psm` and `libc`, the stack growth every recursive walk stands on; `rkyv` and its family under `curios-archive`, by which a walk's record and the units it reads are stored; and `tracing` with `tracing-core`, `pin-project-lite` and `once_cell`, under `curios-common`'s spans, which record and decide nothing until a recorder listens; the recorder, `curios-profile`, is reached by none of it. Nothing of the elaborator is reached, and the elaborator reaches nothing of this: `curios-elab` takes this crate as a **dev**-dependency only, for the tests that put a fixture to both, so the certifier's verdict is a second one rather than one the elaborator could have called ([An independent certifier re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-certifier-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)).

**Rationale.** A call-closure is enumerated by tracing and drifts silently as code moves; a crate boundary is enforced by the compiler and read off the manifest, and it makes "not trusted" structural: nothing of the elaborator — its metavariable store, refinement layer or caches — can sit inside the base. A foundation crate is on the soundness board for as long as the certifier's types name it.

**Rejected.** Sharing the representation through `curios-common`, a crate for stage-independent utilities; `curios-core` owns the representation and this crate builds on it.

### The judgments flatten onto the root

**Decision.** The root names what another crate reads with no namespace between, so a consumer names `curios_cert::Certifier`, `curios_cert::convert`, `curios_cert::certify_definitions`. The crate name is the disambiguator: the judgments here name the same things the elaborator names its own, and `curios_cert::convert` beside the elaborator's `convert` reads as the second opinion it is.

**Rejected.** A `certifier::` namespace, which spells the disambiguation the crate name already makes.

### The certifier memoizes its own evaluation, and a memo changes only resource verdicts

**Decision.** The certifier carries evaluation memos — weak-head reducts of terms, the sorts of types, the types terms were inferred and checked at, and the verdicts of comparisons decided with no pair of calls left apart inside its own unfolding, each with the pairs it unfolded, outside whose unfolding alone it answers — after Lean's trusted `type_checker`. A hit hands back the answer alone: it opens no binder, so it mints no identity, as it spends no unit. A local-bearing reduct, a sort and a verdict are each filed apart for a judgment's reduction, which may ask the certifier's conversion, and for plain reduction, which asks nothing and under which nothing is typed. An answer read off the scope — a local-bearing term's reduct, the sort of a type naming a local or read under an arm, the typing of a term naming one or made under an arm — lives only as long as what it read: the case equations in force and the type an arm stands each local at, for a sort and a typing the binders it was read under besides, and for a typing what the arms around it established for the call recorder. No typing is kept of a term naming a member of a group whose body is being checked, so every call is recorded where it stands. No hit is charged, and every table is cleared where the budget is restored ([A reduction step costs what it builds](../documentation/design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md)). Switching the memos off may move an exhaustion point and the identities minted after a hit, and nothing else; `memo_parity_in` holds the semantic half, and `Certifier::uncached` exists so it can.

**Rationale.** A metavariable heap or refinement store injects answers a term alone could not produce; a memo replays the certifier's own pure function of the term, the definitions, the equations in force and which reduction is taking it. Refusing it costs a whole-prelude re-check by a large factor, spent re-deriving prelude spines for every recursive group the totality gate walks (`curios-prelude`'s `memo_parity_in` runs that walk both ways, a share of the prelude to a test). Charging a hit what a memo-free evaluator would have spent compounds — a subterm hit twice per level makes the charge exponential in a structure the memos evaluate linearly — and refuses programs the elaborator accepts within the same budget (the `spend` module states the compounding; `curios`'s `certifier_memo_charge_measurements` holds the floors). A free hit only reduces what a judgment spends, so it can turn an exhaustion refusal into an acceptance and never move a semantic refusal. A hit mints nothing for the reason it spends nothing: the identities a remembered computation minted, replayed on every hit, are counted once per path through a shared term where the work is done once per node, and compound as a charged hit's units would — a `let` chain read by value whose every level names the one before it more than once passes any fixed identity space in a few dozen levels, having opened a few binders a level. The elaborator's tables replay nothing, and Lean's kernel answers a remembered reduct or type before it draws a name. Freshness is the one property a judgment reads off an identity, and a counter that never falls keeps it; the `spend` module states what that concedes.

**Rejected.** Replaying a reduct's identities on a hit, so that every identity minted after it lands where a memo-free run's would: it compounds as a charged hit does, and refuses where the elaborator, which replays nothing, accepts within one budget; and the parity it kept was given up at the first typing hit.

### A `let` is bound for typing and read by value

**Decision.** The certifier checks a `let`'s value once, where the `let` stands, and types its tail over a local that stands for the value (`Certifier::bind`), as Lean's kernel and Rocq's do. Everything that reads a term for more than its type reads it by value, each `let`-bound name unfolded where the term is read. Reduction, conversion and a type's classification run over a reading (`ByValue`), which only their doors make, each unfolding the terms it is handed (`Certifier::read_by_value`); a reading holds the certifier borrowed and offers no typing, no binding and no recording, so nothing it holds can come to name a `let`-bound local while it reads, and a walk behind the doors cannot be handed a term typing holds. Typing's own readings — an elimination's scrutinee and result, a recursive group, a `let`'s and a group's tail type, an arm's case equation, a recorded position, a graded call — unfold in the one function that takes the term (`Certifier::by_value`, `Certifier::graded`). The scope holds every local's type by value. An arm binds each variable its case solves the same way, to its solution at the solution's own type, and its body is checked as written ([An arm is checked in a context specialized by index inversion](../documentation/design/theory/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md)). A member of a recursive group that stands in a term is bound the same way in the group's tail, at its folded selection: the group's bodies are checked over the members' locals, which only reduction unfolds, each once it is known to terminate ([below](#a-member-is-read-by-value-once-it-is-known-to-terminate)), and the tail over locals that stand for the members. Binding over a local that stood for nothing assumes again every local whose type names it and binds again every `let` whose value does, in `Certifier::bind` itself, so no binding leaves the scope holding a term that hands reduction a `let`-bound name. Reduction itself still substitutes a `let` it meets, and counts what that builds.

**Rationale.** Substituting a `let` hands typing the tail's tree: a value named twice is typed twice, and under an arm each copy again under that arm's own equation, which no table answers across — a chain of `let`s through arms on an expression was typed once per path, and the certifier refused programs the elaborator accepted in their size ([two checkers given one budget must cost alike](../documentation/design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md)). Reading by value keeps every other rule where it was argued: the reduct tables, the sorts, the equations and the verdicts stay keyed on the spellings a substituted `let` leaves, so a remembered reduct never rests on a binding an arm replaces, and the closed machine still takes a closed computation that runs through a `let`. A group's tail is what the group is in scope of, as a `let`'s is: reduction opens it over the members, a top-level member is defined as its selection, and the elaborator defines a local one so before it elaborates the tail. Typed over members that stood for nothing, a local recursive function computed nowhere in the function that declares it, and a term spelling a member by value never converted with the member's name. The readers run over a receiver rather than over terms marked as read, because a term is by value relative to the scope and an arm's binding moves the scope: a term held across a binding names a `let`-bound local whatever mark it carries, while nothing binds as long as a reading stands. GHC hands zonking a stripped-down `TcM` on the same ground ([`GHC.Tc.Zonk.Monad`](https://hackage-content.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.10.3/src/GHC/Tc/Zonk/Monad.hs)).

**Rejected.** Unfolding a `let`-bound name inside reduction as a definition is unfolded: the reduct tables carry no binder, so an entry taken under an arm's binding would answer after the arm, and a closed computation through a `let` would leave the machine. Lean's kernel unfolds a `let`-bound variable where reduction meets it and keys its caches by the expression alone ([`type_checker.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp)), which holds because a variable's declaration never changes once made; an arm here binds a local again. A summary per `let` of what its value reaches, a second statement of what the obligations read off a position. A type marking a term as read by value, which marks how a term was made and not what it is once an arm binds a local it names. A flag telling a reader whether its term was unfolded, with assertions only a build keeping its checks makes, which held the rule by convention.

**Evidence.** `certifier::infer::tests::binding` and `certifier::infer::eliminate::scope_tests` hold each door to unfolding what it is handed and each of typing's readings to unfolding where it reads, each mutation-checked against handing a term on as written; and `memo_parity_in` holds the memos.

### A later walk reads the certifier's own totality record

**Decision.** `certify_definitions` answers the module it accepted beside a record of what it judged (`Rechecked`, `Accepted`), or, where it refuses any of the module, its verdicts alone, a refused walk leaving no record: the record holds each definition's totality closed over everything it mentions, what judging it read of other items — each type, universe scheme or registry entry consulted and each body asked for — each registry entry's polarity vector and its variance vector, and, of each declaration of another unit it read, the digest of the part read, made by the walk itself from the terms in its scope and its verdict on them (`curios_core::Certification`, `Reads`, `Abroad`). Only this walk makes one; it is filed with the unit it classifies, and `Globals::mount` takes it beside the unit as the walk filed the two (`Filed`), which the environment reads where it stands and copies nothing of. It answers for every name its unit holds, a totality for each definition and both vectors for each family and record, so its classifications are what type totality and proof totality close from, each asked of the record per name where a judgment reaches the name, and a name it misses is a construction bug of the compiler's own, read as one. Core holds no totality of elaboration's: the elaborator keeps its own verdicts with its unit (`curios_elab::Classified`), and no judgment of the certifier reads them. What an environment reads a unit in scope by beside its terms — each binder's proof mark, and each definition's and the sort its value inhabits (`curios_core::Definition::proof`, `value_sort`) — is what the walk that judged the unit stated, by its own reading and reading none a module arrives with, in the module it hands back beside its record, which is the one a unit stores ([The closed machine binds a proof unreduced](../documentation/design/compilation/the-closed-machine-binds-a-proof-unreduced.md)); an item a walk over a baseline does not judge again takes what the baseline's walk stated of it under its own binders' names (`certifier::stating`). A registry entry is accepted with the item that declares it, which holds it and nothing else: its strict positivity, its variance and its sizing are each judged over that declaration's entries alone, on a budget restored for the judgment, and its former is defined as the entry makes it ahead of the entries that may name it, and a family's constructors once the entry is accepted, neither judged apart from the entry. Every other family an entry mentions answers from the vector the certifier filed for it — this walk's, as it accepted the family, or a unit in scope's from its record — and a family of the module no walk has filed a vector for yet, stored after the declaration judged, is read as reaching it again, since the walk registers every family before it judges any (`curios_analysis::Driver::judged`). The vector an entry carries is elaboration's and read by no judgment: a family's polarity is asked of the checker (`curios_analysis::Driver::polarity`), and the certifier answers from the vectors its walks filed. A walk over a baseline adopts that walk's verdict on a declaration by the same record, where the declaration's terms are the baseline's and everything its filed reads name stands in the part read, and asks the reads of only the declarations the record read backwards says an edit could reach (`Filed`, `curios_core::ReadBy`; [Cached verdicts](../documentation/design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). It visits those and the declarations whose terms are not the baseline's, which it finds by comparing the two modules by what they do not share where one is a version of the other, and reads every other declaration where the baseline holds it once the walk has passed its place, as it would read one it adopted there; the record it files is the baseline's, revised where it judged or dropped a declaration (`curios_core::Revision`). A declaration it would judge it first looks for among the records a cabinet holds of the item, which a filing completed with the certifier's own digest of each part read (`complete`), and takes the verdict of one whose every part read digests as it did. `curios-pipeline`'s `the_prelude_reads_along_the_graph_its_definitions_reach` holds the reads to the graph elaboration built.

**Rationale.** What a proof reaches runs out of the environment as readily as out of the module, so the environment's classification decides proof totality for every proof reaching it. Reading the elaborator's verdict there, cross-checked once at filing, makes the transitive half of every carried verdict the elaborator's conclusion; reading the certifier's own removes the input rather than guarding it, and holding none of the elaborator's in Core leaves no input to read. The record is part of the unit and found under the unit's address, which names the compiler whose certifier made it. A polarity vector is the same kind of fact: what strict positivity composes through runs out of the module as a proof's reach does, and a walk that analyses every family in scope again for each unit it judges spends, on one budget, the analysis of a library on a program that names none of it ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../documentation/design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)).

**Rejected.** A totality stamp elaboration writes on each definition, cross-checked against the walk's own verdict where the walk judges it: the certifier reads it nowhere else, so the check reports a disagreement and decides nothing, and the stamp is a field of Core only the elaborator reads; a strengthened cross-check of the stamp, still the only thing between a later walk and another component's verdict; classifying a unit in scope whose record does not answer for every name it holds from its terms, and computing the vectors it files none of, a second way of reading kept for bytes no walk wrote, which [the soundness board](../documentation/design/soundness/the-soundness-board.md)'s claim leaves outside, a record withheld in part among them, and which every environment a test builds by hand need not reach, since it can mount what a walk over its module filed; reading such a record's absent entry in the refusing direction, which would hide a construction bug of the compiler's own as a refusal; checking a record's coverage where the cabinet reads it back, a guard against bytes the claim already leaves outside; an optional record, when an empty one already says so; a digest of the certifier beside the record, which the unit's address already carries; recomputing every vector in scope on each walk, which leaves the trusted base where it is and makes a declaration's verdict follow everything in scope; a verdict held with its declaration, a record laid out by its unit's places so that it covers the unit by construction, which rests what a walk believes of a unit in scope on the code that paired the two, where asking the record of each definition is the certifier's own check and a lookup a definition where a unit is mounted.

**Evidence.** `recheck::scope_tests` holds a later walk to the vectors the certifier filed, against an environment keeping the vector each entry carries, and `recheck::budget_tests` to the budget a judgment is restored on.

### A group's calls are the ones the certifier types

**Decision.** The certifier does not look for a group's recursive calls with a traversal of its own. `check_group` opens a frame for the group, and every application the walk types whose head is a member is recorded as a call and graded at once by `curios-analysis`'s shared `grade`, under the size context the arms around it built: each arm's own case, where it scrutinizes a binder, and nothing its index inversion solved, its payload binders, the nonzero fact a boolean or switch arm establishes — read before the arm assumes its case equation, which would reduce the comparison to the arm's own literal — and each binder of a lambda applied on the spot standing for its argument, a curried spine read whole, its later lists bound by the lambda the body is. A call a `let`'s value makes is typed, and so recorded, once, where the `let` binds it, and every call's arguments are graded by value. A call is recorded off its path where the path ends, its callees the parts of the member it reaches as the shared `part_calls` reads them — the member's leading lambdas, or a field lambda of the record under them, whose parameters follow the member's — so a witness's method applied to a child is a call of the method; a field of a member body's record is checked as the part it is, its lambda binding the part's parameters. A member named anywhere but at the root of a path is a call of each of its parts with no arguments, graded unknown. A spine's head is typed with the steps after it, the argument lists and the fields a path projects between them, so a definition's call the path roots at reads the summary of the part it reaches, `List`'s witness's method among them, and each list takes its share where it is typed. An argument a callee applies, or applies a field of, as its summary states takes what it is handed where it is checked (`Calls::hand`): a lambda's binders their bounds, a record literal's fields each what its own path is applied with, and a member local, bare or applied to its own arguments, the calls of each part each application reaches, as the shared `handed_member` reads them. Once every body is checked, the shared `decide` closes the calls; a group the walk never typed reads `Partial`. A type is typed as written (`infer_type`), and a nominal value's parameters are typed as an occurrence's are, so a call stands nowhere the walk does not see.

**Rationale.** A traversal separate from typing fails open: a position it never visits contributes no edge, and a group with no edges is accepted. The certifier types every position it accepts, so calls taken from its typing fail closed. Separate call inspection is where Rocq's guard checker was found unsound — its `find_uniform_parameters` inspected self-calls and missed the calls between bodies ([rocq#21682](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/issues/21682)) — while Agda's [`TermCheck`](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/language/termination-checking.html) and Idris 2 extract calls from terms after typing, and Lean 4's kernel compiles recursion to recursors. Grading and closure stay shared, so the checkers grade a call alike and differ only in how they find it; the elaborator keeps its discovery walk over the same two functions. Which members a group's bodies read by value is a turn's question, asked before the bodies are typed, so both checkers ask it of that walk ([below](#a-member-is-read-by-value-once-it-is-known-to-terminate)), and the verdict stays the typed calls'.

**Rejected.** Having the certifier check that discovery is complete, which closes the fail-open case in less code but leaves the verdict resting on a traversal the certifier does not own; keeping discovery for the certifier's verdict as the elaborator keeps it for its own.

**Evidence.** `certifier::calls::tests` puts four groups that must descend to the local gate, each mutation-checked against the one piece of recording it depends on, and `recheck::proposition_tests` holds an inline group's verdict reaching the proof around it. A differential against discovery agrees on every group of `/sys`, `/std` and `programs/`: to retake it, have `close_group` also run `curios_analysis::group_totality(self, group)` with the recorder set aside and `curios_common::note!` both verdicts, then read the notes out of the stream `cargo xtask profile prelude` files, at `curios/.artifacts/prelude.pftrace`, and out of each program's `curios --profile <PATH> wonder` stream, attributing each to the `certify_declaration` span it falls inside.

### A member is read by value once it is known to terminate

**Decision.** A recursive group's signatures and bodies read a member by value only once the member is known to terminate. Every signature is checked first, each member standing for nothing. Then, before any body is typed, each member takes its turn in the order the members are written: it is known to terminate where the calls among the members known so far and its own descend, as `curios-analysis`'s discovery walk finds and grades them (`known_to_terminate`), and a member not known is withheld. From its turn on, a known member's local unfolds in reduction to its selection out of the known members (`Calls::member_value`), whose bodies name a withheld member by its local, so an unfolding stops there; a withheld member stands for nothing in the group's signatures and bodies. Each member stays a local, typed at its signature over the locals and the head of every call recorded off it. The verdict is still the one the typed calls close to: a group owes descent where a member is a proof or its type yields a sort, read as the bodies read it, a type waiting on a withheld member counted as one (`yields_a_sort_where`, `blocker`), and is accepted only where every member descends; with a member withheld, each body's refusal is held back until the verdict has named the group. The elaborator takes each member's turn by the same walk as it fills the member's slot, which reads through once the member is known (`curios-elab`'s `Turns`).

**Rationale.** A body needs a sibling's value: `val: T(2) = 3` holds only because `T(2)` reduces to `Nat`, and `f(x: T(0)) -> T(0) => x` restates its binder at a type its signature reads. An equation of a member is a fact once the member terminates, and a turn establishes that before anything unfolds it, so every unfolding either checker makes is of a definition that terminates where its body is well typed, which the group's acceptance waits on. An ill-founded group is withheld rather than unfolded, and one that owes descent is named rather than refused for its budget. A member is known by the order written, so two bodies each reading the other's value at a smaller argument stand: the first is known before the second's body is reached. The order also picks which member of a cycle that does not descend is withheld, the last of it to take its turn, so a body may read the ones before it by value; a type reaching any of them is still refused by type totality. The elaborator meets the bodies one at a time and parks a reading of a member not yet known on its slot, while the certifier takes every turn before typing a body, so a member unfolds there before its own body is typed and both read the same members by value. A turn reads a body's calls before the body is typed, so it reads them by discovery: one taken by the certifier's typed calls would wait on bodies waiting on it, and the two checkers would clear the members of a cycle in different orders. Unfolding a member in reduction alone keeps every term a body is typed at spelled with the members' locals, so a call is recorded wherever it is typed and no remembered typing answers for one. Rocq types a fixpoint's bodies with its names as bare assumptions and unfolds one only on a constructor, Lean keeps a `partial` definition opaque to its kernel, and Agda type-checks a mutual block reading its siblings' definitions before its termination check runs, and may loop there: the turns are a termination check taken first.

**Rejected.** Knowing a member once its body is typed, a body retried while a member it reads is withheld: the certifier's order then follows which bodies type and the elaborator's which slots are filled, and two bodies each reading the other's value at a smaller argument are accepted by the elaborator and refused by the certifier, neither typing until the other is known. Withholding for good a member whose body reads one written after it, which refuses the same pair. Reading a group that owes descent by value in its bodies before its verdict: a member that does not descend is unfolded, and an ill-founded group is refused for its budget where its gate would name it. Reading every group by value with no turn, the obligations validating after: every budget route stays. A member unfolding to its selection out of the whole group, which unfolds a withheld member reached through a known one. Converting a member's local with its group's folded selection of the same member, terminally: conversion stops being transitive, `T(0)` at the local converting with `T(0)` at the selection, which reduces to `Nat`, and not with `Nat` ([A term is one where conversion says so](../documentation/design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)). Binding each member to its folded selection in the bodies, as the tail binds it: typing then meets terms spelled through the group, a selection there re-enters this check, and a call a goal spells or a memo hit answers goes unrecorded.

**Evidence.** `recheck::recursion_tests` holds a member known to terminate read in a body, a lambda typed at a sibling, a member withheld standing for nothing, a group listing itself named for not descending, and a call a goal states recorded in each body that states it, each mutation-checked, with `curios`'s `tests::rules::group` and `tests::recursion` putting the programs to both checkers.

### A type is accepted by typing it, and reduction is total on arbitrary terms

**Decision.** Four mechanisms, each doing a job the others cannot.

- **Reduction is total on arbitrary terms**: `whnf` never asserts on a shape a caller could hand it — an application that does not saturate its lambda, an arm that does not match its payload, a projection out of range — but goes stuck, since `certify_definitions` walks a module from anywhere to the end with each verdict independent, and a reduction that declines to fire admits nothing.
- **A type is accepted by `infer_type`, not by `Sort::of`**: `infer_type` types the term and destructs its type as a sort, Rocq's `infer_type`, Lean's `inferType`, Agda's sort carried on the type, and it types a type as written, never its reduct; `Sort::of` classifies structurally and is a fast path reached only where typing has run, the role Rocq gives `Retyping.get_sort_of`. One part of a term is typed by no rule, an arm no reduction takes, which nothing reads, and it is known three ways: its scrutinee already is a literal or a constructed value of another case (`never_taken`, by the unifier that excuses an absent arm); it is an arm of a nominal elimination whose targets that unifier finds in a definite clash with the scrutinee's indices; or it was refused, and an equation it assumed, its own or one its case restated, computes to a value of another case (`unless_never_taken`). The third is computed for a refused arm alone and withdraws no spent budget.
- **A count is checked at the boundary or held by its node**: an occurrence's parameters and indices, a value's parameters and a constructor tag's uniqueness are checked where the declaration is consulted, since no typing rule reads a length. A count a node states beside the sibling it is a fact about is held where the node is constructed, by the representation rather than by this walk: a `let` tail's binders against the block's bindings (`Let::new`), a `rec` tail's and each member scope's against the members its group holds (`Rec::new`, `RecGroup::closing`), a group item's exports against its members (`RecItem::new`, `RecItem::with_group`), and an arm's plicity vector against its binders (`InductArm::new`). Every builder closes such a scope over the binders it builds the sibling from and every rebuild keeps both counts, so two counts apart are a defect of the stage that built them, stopped there; a module restored from a cabinet passes no constructor, and bytes no walk wrote are outside the claim the soundness board makes.
- **A position is closed and names no local**: whether a term holds a free local, or a bound index no scope in it binds, is a property of the representation that no judgment is the right reader of — a free local is captured by the next binder a comparison mints, and an index with no binder denotes whatever depth a reader happens to meet it at, so neither is the term the module carries by the time a rule looks — and both are therefore refused over every position a term can sit in before any rule runs, the module's by `curios_core::free_locals_of` and `open_positions_of` and the entry's by its own pair. Reduction declines a term that is not closed rather than looking up a variable it cannot resolve (`accelerable`), and `infer`'s own `Var` rule refuses an index as the boundary does; a variable hands out its identity only through `Var::as_free`, so every reader answers an index as the case it is and none can abort ahead of the verdict.

**Rationale.** Defects arrive through the gap between "the certifier reads a field" and "something established the field": level capture, a bypassed large-elimination guard, and walks that abort. Typing a type's reduct is how reduction meets untyped terms by construction — an occurrence whose level floats above its argument's, `List.{v,w}(Waker)`, is typed as its former's `Type v` where its reduct sits at `Type 0` — and the elaborator settles that level at its argument's (`UniverseSolver::finalize`), so a spelling and its reduct land in one sort. Shapes are made safe by typing, a count against a declaration and the identities a position carries by the boundary, and a count against a sibling by the constructor that builds both, and none of them can abort, which closes each class rather than instances of it. An arm of a case split under a scrutinee that already is another case is no such gap: a value is its case under every substitution, so the split is its one arm wherever it stands, and no rule reads another. Typing it refused programs the elaborator accepted: an arm is checked at its goal with the case in the scrutinee's place, an elimination of that scrutinee inside the goal becomes one over a literal, and a proof under its other arm rested on a guard no equation is recorded for. The scrutinee is read as it stands where its arm types, since computing one would evaluate a term that runs for a verdict reached without it. A refused arm is computed for: the elaborator writes a type it derived by putting a value in a variable's place and types none of them, so an arm typed under an equation over the variable is met at a value where its scrutinee names no local, and no equation is recorded for it, or where the equation is one its case closed. Where such a scrutinee computes to the arm's case, what rested on the equation holds by reduction; where it computes to another, the arm is one no reduction takes, and refusing it refused a type the certifier had typed. The two ways an arm taken once is accepted are each sound alone, so their order decides what a verdict costs and nothing else, and a refused arm's walk is taken back whole, the calls and positions it recorded with its scope, and with them the remembered typings, which would answer a later typing of a term without the positions inside it (`Certifier::tried`), so the way that did not apply leaves nothing a later rule reads. An arm whose targets clash with its scrutinee's indices is no such gap either: no value of the scrutinee's type is that case, which is what excuses the arm's absence. Typing it as written refused a type the elaborator wrote at an instance, a `let`'s annotation among them, where a substitution fixed an index and the arm had been typed under a solution the instance does not have.

**Rejected.** **Computing a scrutinee before its arms are typed**, which decides the same arms and runs every closed scrutinee a program holds, which may be the program, where nothing needed the answer. **Computing only within a type**, the typing memo keyed by it: a typing would follow where it was made, and neither an equation a case closed nor an arm the indices rule out is reached. **Recording an equation for a scrutinee that names no local**: the written spelling would answer and one that reduces without meeting it would not, so the instance is refused all the same, and a local-free term's remembered reduct would have to follow the equations in force. **Taking the decided arm where a goal is put at a case**, in the elaborator: a `let` written at a call's result is put at no case.

### A declaration that leaves a sort to its caller is judged at every assignment

**Decision.** A definition, a top-level recursive group and a registry entry whose universe context leaves sorts to a caller are judged once at each assignment of `Prop` and `Type` to them, on the declaration read at the assignment (`at_every_sort`), each under the whole budget, and stored as stated; a refusal names its assignment, and its report restates the type the declaration states, read at it. An occurrence states as many sorts as its declaration leaves, each `Prop` or `Type`, and two instances apart in a sort are two types. A family's own sort is read off the entry as stated, a quality the declaration does not bind is refused by the scan made before any judgment, a group inside a term binds no sort of its own and is read at each assignment with the declaration that holds it, and a declaration leaves at most `UniverseContext::QUALITY_LIMIT`. The rule is [A declaration leaves a sort to its caller](../documentation/design/theory/a-declaration-leaves-a-sort-to-its-caller.md).

**Rationale.** Every rule that reads a sort reads which of the two it is, and reading the declaration at an assignment leaves each of them a literal to read: no reader takes an answer for a sort that states neither, no memo holds one term at two sorts, and what is accepted of a generic declaration is what would be accepted of the declarations written out. A group's verdict is the conjunction of its assignments', filed under the group as stated, which is the spelling a later walk reads it by. Positivity and variance are decided on the entry as stated: each walks and reduces, and neither reads a sort.

**Evidence.** `recheck::sort_tests` holds a declaration well typed at one sort alone refused at the other, for a definition, a group and a registry entry, mutation-checked against a walk cut to its first assignment and to its last.

**Rejected.** Judging at the parameter with an answer for each reader, which is the elaborator's own check: the decision's reason. One budget across the assignments, under which what a declaration costs to accept follows how many sorts it leaves.

### Every candidate put to the certifier is Curios/Core

**Decision.** Every candidate a test puts to the certifier, a module or a term, is written in Curios/Core in the test that puts it and read with nothing in scope (`test_support::Put`), by the reader and its rule for a closing term's type (`curios_surface::to_core_at`) that the product's door reads a `.crsc` and the soundness board a ticket with (`curios_pipeline::read_core_text`), and put to the walk a compilation judges a module by. A module is put to the walk under the registry stand-in (`recheck::test_support::core_text_refusals`). Below the walk, a term is read alone (`test_support::term`), the declarations a judgment reads are declared from their text (`test_support::declared`), and the hypotheses it is judged under are the binders of a leading function literal, opened at fresh locals (`test_support::hypotheses`). A text declares every name it uses, the propositions the registry names among them. What stays Rust is the harness: the certifier's budget and memos, the identities a test about identity names, and the readings taken off what the certifier answered.

**Rationale.** A held attack is a refusal and not a proof of `False`, so it needs no prelude, and a text names what it builds the way a reader writes it, where a candidate spelled in this crate's constructors is read only as Rust and rots with the representation it spells. One reader and one walk for the suites, the board and the product make what the board says of a ticket what a suite would say of its text. The door that composes them for the product is the pipeline's, not this crate's: a door here would put the reader in the trusted base this crate's dependencies enumerate ([above](#the-trusted-base-is-a-crate-boundary)).

**Rejected.** The prelude in scope, which a refusal does not need and this crate's suites cannot reach. A candidate built in `curios-core`'s constructors, which rots with the representation it spells; below the walk too, where an attack is a Rust test of the function it calls and its candidate is still text.

### The universe decisions are the shared layer's

**Decision.** The two decisions over a universe constraint set — the level entailment oracle (`entails`) and satisfiability (`satisfiable`) — sit with the shared analyses ([`curios-analysis`'s README](../curios-analysis/README.md#level-entailment-is-forward-reasoning-to-a-least-model)), which both checkers call: the certifier for `Certifier::level_leq` and the walk's universe verdict, the elaborator for its store's consistency and the relations its weak join keeps. What this crate holds of them is the verdict on a context a walk assumes, closed and satisfiable (`universe_verdict`).

**Rationale.** A decision procedure for one fixed theory, the semilattice with a successor, is a function of a constraint set with one answer, which [the independence decision](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-certifier-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md) shares as arithmetic over the representation: two copies could disagree only by a slip, and the second opinion on levels stands in which constraints each checker collects. Kept with the certifier, their only caller there, they would leave the elaborator deciding the same questions by a procedure of its own.

**Rejected.** The decisions kept with the certifier and stated a second time in the elaborator, a second engine for one rule.

## Measuring the certifier

`cargo xtask profile prelude` makes the fixed prelude under a record stream filed at `curios/.artifacts/prelude.pftrace`, the work of `curios-prelude`'s build script run by a measurement among `curios`'s profile tests, so the stream holds each root's elaboration and its certification. The walk carries three kinds of span:

- **per item:** `certify_declaration`, grouped by `Item::describe` as the elaborator's `declaration` span is, so one item's cost in each checker is found under one key;
- **per stage:** `universe_verdict`; `check_positions` for type totality and proof totality; `check_entrypoint`; `check_induct_decl` and `check_struct_decl`; the shared `positivity_vectors` and `variance_vectors`, once for each declaration that declares a registry entry; and, at a cabinet's address, `certify::candidates`, looking among the records a cabinet holds of an item the walk would judge, and `judgments::complete`, completing a walk's records where its unit is filed. The residue, escape and indexing checks are `recheck_definitions`'s self time;
- **per judgment:** `convert`, with `convert::unfolded_retry` for the one unfolding granted before a structural refusal; `reduce` and `reduce_forced`, the second past its memo so a hit costs no row; `Sort::of`; and the shared analyses' `entails`, `grade`, `decide` and `invert_indices`. Typing is `certify_declaration`'s self time. The `nat::*` and `truth::decide_bool` rows are `curios-core`'s folds over the carriers' algebra.

The command prints `curios profile`'s summary of the stream, which `cargo run --package curios -- profile curios/.artifacts/prelude.pftrace` gives again: read the self figures, which attribute each nanosecond and byte to the innermost span. The stream is an instrumented program's, so durations are inflated and call counts and allocation are the stable figures. An item's cost in the certifier is read beside its cost in the elaborator by summing each checker's per-item span by group, a query of the stream in Perfetto's UI:

```sql
select extract_arg(arg_set_id, 'debug.group') as item,
  sum(iif(name = 'declaration', dur, 0)) / 1e6 as elaborated_ms,
  sum(iif(name = 'certify_declaration', dur, 0)) / 1e6 as certified_ms
from slice where name in ('declaration', 'certify_declaration')
group by item order by certified_ms desc limit 10
```

The columns are the item, its elaboration milliseconds and its certification milliseconds; `Item::describe` names every witness of a module alike, so a module's witnesses share one row.

**Whether the certifier keeps the closed machine is decided from this stream.** The `reduce` and `reduce_forced` rows of the prelude's certification with the machine on are read against the same stream with it off, which needs a walk that can leave the machine out as `Certifier::uncached` leaves the memos out. If the strategy alone certifies `/std` within the budget every item has, the machine leaves the certifier's path; if not, it stays a trusted evaluator.
