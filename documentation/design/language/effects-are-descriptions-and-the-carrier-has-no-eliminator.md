# Effects are descriptions, and the carrier has no eliminator

**Decision.** Every host operation returns `Io(T)` — an opaque intrinsic carrier holding a *description* of a computation that yields a `T`, never the `T` itself. `Io` is built by two intrinsics (`pure`, `bind`), sequenced by `!` through the ordinary `Monad(Io)` witness, and eliminated by nothing at all. A program's tail is an `Io({})`, and the emitted entrypoint boundary is the only place in the language that forces one.

**The invariant a future intrinsic author needs, stated once: an intrinsic that performs a host effect returns an `Io`, and nothing turns an `Io(T)` back into a `T`.** Those two facts are what make every term of non-`Io` type pure *by typing*. The first is enforced per variant in `curios-core`'s roster and again in each checker's arms; the second is enforced by absence — there is no `IntrinsicHead` entry for `Io`, no match case, no projection, and no `unsafe_perform`. Adding an eliminator, in any spelling, is what would undo this whole discipline, and it is the one change this section exists to warn against.

**What it replaced was an analysis, and the analysis could not be finished.** The certifier used to walk a match scrutinee, every definition it reached, and the head of every application it made, to decide whether a spelling denoted one value. It was defeated three times in the accepting direction — most conclusively by `match f(true)` for a *parameter* `f`, since effectfulness of `f(true)` is not a property of `f(true)` but of the environment, and at the moment an arm records its equation the binder has no value to inspect. That is the shape of the whole objection: the question was never answerable from the term. The [Scrutinee refinement](../../soundness/per-term-rules/scrutinee-refinement.md) row carries what survives of it; the walk is deleted with no successor.

**It is also refinement-restoring rather than merely analysis-deleting.** The walk's final form had to refuse an equation for *any* opaque head, pure or not, because nothing distinguished them — the function space admitted `Cell/get`, so `(Bool) -> Bool` said nothing about purity. Now it does, and a parameter-headed scrutinee refines its arm again.

**Descriptions erase to thunks, and that is the whole runtime cost.** `Io(T)` erases to a zero-argument closure; forcing is a zero-argument application. That was measured before the retype was committed to, on a non-syscall `Cell` loop that isolates the thunk and on a `Handle/write` where the syscall dominates it, and the reverted algebraic-effects design was an order of magnitude worse at the same work. Two consequences are structural rather than incidental: every program allocates and forces exactly one closure at its boundary, and `Io/pure`'s operand is erased at the *construction* site rather than inside the thunk — the language is eager, so `pure(e)` has evaluated `e` before the node exists, and delaying it there would make one arm's evaluation order differ from the rest of the roster.

**Placement law: `Io` owns the monad, never the operations.** `/sys` stays a 1:1 mirror of the host store, `/std` owns the taxonomy, and an operation lives with its subject — the one its own store row names, not the type its result wears (`curios-prelude-archive/README.md` states how `/sys` places a row). So `/sys/Io` holds `pure` and `bind` and nothing else, and no host operation moves under it.

**Rejected: effect rows, effect annotations on arrows, or any effect information in types beyond the `Io` wrapper.** Rows need a row sort in `curios-core`, row equality re-derived independently in the kernel, and a second constraint domain in the elaborator comparable to `universe_solver.rs` — inside a conversion checker that already needs a step budget. The precondition for reopening is carried over unchanged from the superseded algebraic-effects design: a written program this design accepts whose author wanted it rejected. None was found then, and none has appeared since.

**Rejected: algebraic effects with signatures, handlers, and a host-side step interpreter.** Implemented end to end on the `effects-system` branch and reverted whole. The reusable finding is in the measurement above — reifying continuations and interpreting descriptions host-side multiplies allocation and boundary crossings for a generality the standard library never cashed in.

**Rejected: monad laws in definitional equality, and a dependent `bind`.** `IoPure` and `IoBind` reduce their operands and rebuild; neither law fires during conversion. A motive-carrying sequencing would be a new decision if a consumer ever appears, not a generalization forced by this one.
