# An arm is checked in a context specialized by index inversion

**Decision.** An arm of a nominal elimination is checked with the most-general solution of `actual indices ~ case targets` substituted — into the body and the expectation, and into the type of every local it mentions, re-assumed innermost and retracted with the arm — the Goguen–McBride–McKinna specialization step stated directly. Both unification directions run the shared unifier in `curios-analysis/src/invert.rs`: pinning an arm binder to the rigid actual it must equal is the call as the elaborator makes it, and refining an outer variable to the target it is forced to equal is the same call with its sides swapped — which lands the guards exactly right, the occurs check refusing the parameter cycle (`b := b + 1` through a family parameter) and the top guard leaving the variable-variable case to the first direction. A variable scrutinee is additionally substituted by the case's constructed value, in nominal, `Bool`, and `Switch` arms alike; definitional K licenses the substitution, as it already licenses the deletion rule. An *expression* scrutinee — one substitution cannot name — stands as a case equation instead: within the arm, the scrutinee expression is the case's value definitionally, held as bracket-scoped assumption state exactly like the arm's binders and consulted by the kernel's reducer at stuck heads. That is scoped state rather than a store, so the kernel still decides from the term alone; [Case equations inside an arm](../../soundness/what-the-kernel-consults/case-equations-inside-an-arm.md) is what it assumes. Each arm must then inhabit the motive at *its own* constructor's index targets, an absent arm must be *impossible* at the scrutinee's indices by the same unifier, and the large-elimination guard's side condition is decided rather than approximated — [Large-elimination guard](../../soundness/per-term-rules/large-elimination-guard.md) records the rule.

**The result of an elimination may be stated at the ambient goal rather than as a family.** Once the context is specialized per arm, the only obligation a closed motive still carries is its own typing under fresh binders, and that is the obligation a hypothesis whose type mentions the scrutinee cannot meet — the reason the elaborator used to generalize such hypotheses into a Π-motive applied back to the originals, the convoy pattern synthesized. `MatchResult::Ambient` states the expected type once, where it was written, and each arm inhabits it under that case's substitution, the head and each variable index standing for the case's value and targets; the kernel's rule is `documentation/soundness/per-term-rules/an-elimination-at-an-ambient-goal.md`. The form is built over a variable scrutinee; carried by substitution to an expression scrutinee, the goal's syntactic occurrences of that expression stand for the case, the with-abstraction reading, which is a fixed operation on the spelling rather than a case equation. It is never built for a fold, whose induction hypothesis is typed at the family opened at the tail. A written motive, an elimination in inference position, and an expression scrutinee keep the family.

**Rationale.** Unifying a scrutinee's actual indices against a case's targets is one implementation run by both checkers: it is a total function of post-zonk terms on both sides, so a second copy would be a second run of the same function rather than a second opinion. What each checker owns is *applying* the solution, and this is where the two had differed. The elaborator applies it through its refinement store (`refine_head` for the scrutinee and its variable indices, inversion for the arm binders). The kernel holds no store, and until this landed it applied nothing — opening the motive at the case's targets teaches the *goal* the case's equations, and the ambient locals, the body's occurrences of outer variables, and the scrutinee variable itself all stayed unrefined. The convoy pattern, `refine_head`, and both inversion rungs are all machinery for routing the missing half through the half that existed.

**Inversion decides nothing at a `Prop`-valued position, and that is a soundness condition rather than a refinement.** Injectivity and tag disjointness are claims about values a program can tell apart; proof irrelevance says a proposition's inhabitants cannot be told apart. Where the two meet — an index position whose type is a proposition — inversion must therefore derive nothing at all: not a clash, and not the equations a decomposition would manufacture. Both directions of ignoring this were closed inhabitants of `False`, found by attacking the perimeter adversarially rather than by reading it. Reading `Two/a()` against `Two/b()` as a clash makes an elimination look vacuous at a type conversion has just proved inhabited, and `match w end` then inhabits `False`; decomposing `Tag/t(a)` against `Tag/t(7)` manufactures the *relevant* equation `a := 7` from a premise `Tag/t(0)` satisfies equally, which is the singleton rung of the large-elimination guard reading a payload as recovered when nothing recovers it. The rule is decided from the declaration's own `result_sort`, so it needs no capability the `Env`/`Judge` seam did not already have, and it moves strictly toward refusal: an arm excused as impossible becomes mandatory, and a binder forced only at such a position stays unsolved. Measured against the whole corpus before it landed, *no* nominal elimination anywhere has a `Prop`-typed index position, so the condition costs nothing that exists. `Eq(@A, x, y)` is the only family whose positions can become propositional at all, and only under instantiation, where nothing eliminates it. `curios/src/tests/perimeter.rs` holds both witnesses, and [Index inversion and K](../../soundness/per-term-rules/index-inversion-and-k.md) is the entry.

**Two forcings of one binder that clash make the case impossible.** A target may mention a binder twice — `refl(@z) : (z, z)` — and then no position clashes on its own: against `Eq(false, true)` each position solves `z`, to `false` and to `true`. The deletion rule reconciles a binder forced twice, keeping one solution where the two convert; where they definitely clash the case is unreachable, since each forcing was reached by injective steps and the arm needs the two to be one value, and an absent arm is excused exactly as two distinct constructors met in one position excuse it. The clash is asked of the same walk, with nothing flexible, so the paragraph above governs it unchanged: the tag test is licensed by the family's sort and a literal clash is the peel's. It is asked only after conversion has refused at a type that was in scope, which a `Prop`-typed binder never reaches — irrelevance has identified its forcings and the rule has already deleted one. Short of a definite clash the pair is dropped and the arm stays mandatory: a variable may yet be the constructor it meets, an opaque function may send two arguments to one value, and a disagreement only arithmetic sees — `x * 2 + 1` against `y * 2` — is a fold verdict the peel does not call a clash. This is what makes `Eq(false, true)` empty by inversion as `False` is by reduction, which [A bound is stated in a decided proposition and discharged by reduction](a-bound-is-stated-in-a-decided-proposition-and-discharged-by-reduction.md) states; [Index inversion and K](../../soundness/per-term-rules/index-inversion-and-k.md) holds the evidence.

**A case equation is an elaboration convenience, not a certification contract.** Its replay is coupled to the reduction strategy: it fires only where the reducer meets the recorded spelling whole, and β-substitution manufactures respellings — `classify` of a `Bytes/get` form where the equation names `classify(c)` — that no finite key set covers, which is why the registry's entry-probe extension was tried and reverted. A definition whose typing depends on one has the explicit convoy as its strategy-independent spelling, transporting the witness along an `Eq`-stated definitional equation so each arm receives it at its own constructor — `/std/Str/Valid/decode_head_raw` is the worked example.

**Rejected.** Widening the elaborator's convoy so an arm-used hypothesis rides into the motive. Measured, it cleared the transitivity law of `/std`'s since-retired inductive order and broke obligation (V) for what is now `/std/Str/Valid/drop`, because a convoy *re-binds* the hypothesis and the size-change walk then read the re-bound name as a fresh binder nothing is below. That reading was the walk's defect rather than the convoy's — it now grades a redex as its contractum, see `documentation/soundness/whole-module-passes/record_totality-t.md` — but the widening was also unnecessary: specializing in the kernel touches no emitted term, so (V) never sees a change, and the items the widening broke are among the ones this rule cleared. Also rejected: first-match-wins row priority with catch-all defaults. Arms enumerate constructors without priority, nested patterns compile through the pattern matrix by full enumeration, and an omitted arm is legal only where inversion proves it impossible, so arm order never changes meaning and coverage is a checked property rather than a runtime default.
