# A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand

**Decision.** A unit lowered over a baseline, the unit an earlier text of the same sources lowered to, keeps a declaration's lowering and does not lower it where the declaration's text is the one the baseline lowered and every name that lowering resolved through its scope resolves as it did (`curios_text::into_core_unit_over`). What a declaration lowered to beside its items is recorded with it (`LoweredDeclaration`): the trace of its lookups, each name as it was written with what it resolved to, in order (`curios_core::Trace`), and every binding resolving them read, found or not — a label of the scope of the module body it stands in, a label of a module's table and interface at each step of a path, a module's exports where a glob read them, and the mount a name was written under (`Consulted`); what the unit-wide passes read of each of its items, which is every name the item references, where each definition's name was written, the names its declared type mentions and what it is an alias of; its lints, placed in its text; and the definitions it recorded an import scope for. A baseline's record is found by the declaration's anchor and the stamp of its text, and believed once the two texts compare equal. Its trace is asked again through the doors the lowering asked it through, so a kept declaration leaves the unit's tables as lowering it would have, and a resolution that differs, or that the scope now refuses, lowers the declaration from its text. A `use` is resolved by every lowering, being the scope the traces are asked of, and what its resolution read is recorded with its module.

**Asked only where an edit could have reached.** Where the units in scope are the ones the baseline was lowered against, what the edit moved is found from it: a module whose file's text the baseline does not hold is touched, and so is one whose `use` declarations read a binding that moved; a binding moved where its module's entry for its label is not the baseline's, read off the modules whose tables are not (`Moved`). Every declaration of a touched module is asked, and of any other module only the declarations whose lookups read a binding that moved, found through the records read backwards (`LookupReaders`); every other declaration is kept with nothing compared or asked, its text being its file's and every binding it read the entry it was. What asking a kept declaration's lookups would have marked, the `use` sites it read through and the mounts it wrote under, is marked from the bindings its record says it read. Where a unit in scope moved, every declaration is asked. Witness names, the exposure audit and the `unused-declaration` lint read the recorded facts and no term. What a recompile lowered again and what it dropped are read off what its walk did, with nothing compared with the baseline (`PreparedText::lowered_anew`, `dropped`), and the unit it stores is the one lowering the whole text stores, byte for byte.

**Carried, not walked.** A unit lowered over a baseline is a version of it. Every table the lowering keeps by declaration is a persistent map — the lowered items, each at a spaced place (`curios_core::Items`); what each declaration lowered to beside them, by the place of its first item (`Records`); the declarations each module body holds (`Bodies`), and the place of each by its text; the modules' tables and export views, the audiences, aliases, mints, import scopes and anchors; what each `use` read, each body's lints, and what the `unused-declaration` lint's roots reach (`Reachability`) — and the lowering revises the baseline's by what its walk lowered and dropped (`revise`). A module none of whose text was touched, none of whose declarations reads a binding that moved or is a witness keyed by a name lowered again, and none of whose declarations is one no lowering may keep, is passed unentered with every module under it (`Baseline::entered`), its declarations neither visited nor decoded. The unit-wide passes follow the same rule: witness naming names the witnesses lowered now and the kept ones whose key read a name that moved; the exposure audit asks the definitions lowered now and the re-exports and formers that changed, and every definition where an alias or an audience moved; the lint searches back to a root from the items that changed; a module's interface and export view are read again where its heads or a binding it re-exports moved; and what a file writes is read off the declarations its module bodies hold (`PreparedText::texts`).

**Rationale.**

- **A lowering has two inputs, and both are read without lowering.** Every counter a lowering mints from starts at zero for each declaration ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)), and a declaration reads its scope through two doors alone: a name written as a path, and a bare name no binder of its own binds. Its text and the answers to its lookups are everything its lowered form follows.
- **The text alone does not say what a name means.** An edit to a `use` above a declaration touches no character of it and changes which declaration a name in it is. A trace records each dependency with the answer it gave, as a verifying trace does ([Build systems à la carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2018/03/build-systems-a-la-carte.pdf)), and the lookup is the grain: a declaration is lowered again where a name it wrote resolves elsewhere, and at no other edit to its scope.
- **Asked again through the same doors**, because a resolution marks the import it came through as used and the mount it was spelled under as reached: a trace replayed leaves the `unused-import` lint and the reached mounts exact.
- **A binding read is part of what was read.** A name's answer moves with the entries its resolution read and with nothing else, so a record of those entries says, read backwards, which declarations an edit to the module tables reaches; and a label is bound once in a scope, so the `use` site a lookup resolved through, and the mount it wrote under, are what that record says it read, marked without resolving anything again ([A compile costs what it reads](a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md)).
- **Facts are taken where an item is lowered**, while its terms are open. A recursive group is stored closed over its members, and opening one to read the names it mentions costs what lowering it does; the passes that run over a whole unit ask about names and audiences, never about terms.
- **A witness is named anew, never lowered anew.** A kept witness takes the next name of its module as a lowered one does and is named by its signature afterwards, so a witness written above another moves no lowering.
- **A stamp finds and the text decides**: a stamp is thirty-two bits, enough to place a position and too few to believe a lowering by.
- **A module passed is the baseline's whole.** Its text stands, its scope stands and nothing its declarations read moved, so lowering it again writes what the baseline holds; visiting it to keep it costs a step for each declaration of the unit, the unit's size paid for an edit of one declaration ([A compile costs what it reads](a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md)).
- **An aggregate is kept by its changes.** The audit, the lint, the interfaces and a file's texts are functions of the declarations' recorded facts, so what a version changed of those facts is what changes them, as incremental view maintenance keeps a query ([DBSP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16684)).

**Rejected.**

- **A lowering kept by its text alone**, or a file's by a digest of the file: it keeps the lowering that named the other declaration.
- **The scope a declaration was lowered in, compared whole**: every declaration of a module is lowered again when one import is added.
- **Lowering every declaration and comparing each with the baseline's**: the lowering is paid to learn it was not needed, and the comparison walks both sides of every declaration of the unit.
- **A lowering kept a file at a time**, a file that changed lowered whole and its declarations compared with the baseline's: the comparison this removes, paid for the file an author is in.
- **The facts read off a kept item's terms**: a group is opened again for each pass that reads it.
- **Uses recorded with each declaration**, the imports it marked and the mounts it reached: a second account of what the trace already says, kept in step by hand.
- **Each kept declaration visited and kept by its record**: a step for each declaration of the unit, whatever the edit.
- **The unit-wide passes run whole after the walk**: a second walk of the unit, paid for every edit.
