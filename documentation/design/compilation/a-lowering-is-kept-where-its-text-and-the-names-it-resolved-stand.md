# A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand

**Decision.** A unit lowered over a baseline, the unit an earlier text of the same sources lowered to, keeps a declaration's lowering and does not lower it where the declaration's text is the one the baseline lowered and every name that lowering resolved through its scope resolves as it did (`curios_text::into_core_unit_over`). What a declaration lowered to beside its items is recorded with it (`LoweredDeclaration`): the trace of its lookups, each name as it was written with what it resolved to, in order (`curios_core::Trace`); what the unit-wide passes read of each of its items, which is every name the item references, where each definition's name was written, the names its declared type mentions and what it is an alias of; its lints, placed in its text; and the definitions it recorded an import scope for. A baseline's record is found by the declaration's anchor and the stamp of its text, and believed once the two texts compare equal. Its trace is asked again through the doors the lowering asked it through, so a kept declaration leaves the unit's tables as lowering it would have, and a resolution that differs, or that the scope now refuses, lowers the declaration from its text. A `use` is resolved by every lowering, being the scope the traces are asked of. Witness names, the exposure audit and the `unused-declaration` lint read the recorded facts and no term. A recompile compares with the baseline only the declarations it lowered again (`PreparedText::kept_from`), and the unit it stores is the one lowering the whole text stores, byte for byte.

**Rationale.**

- **A lowering has two inputs, and both are read without lowering.** Every counter a lowering mints from starts at zero for each declaration ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)), and a declaration reads its scope through two doors alone: a name written as a path, and a bare name no binder of its own binds. Its text and the answers to its lookups are everything its lowered form follows.
- **The text alone does not say what a name means.** An edit to a `use` above a declaration touches no character of it and changes which declaration a name in it is. A trace records each dependency with the answer it gave, as a verifying trace does ([Build systems à la carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2018/03/build-systems-a-la-carte.pdf)), and the lookup is the grain: a declaration is lowered again where a name it wrote resolves elsewhere, and at no other edit to its scope.
- **Asked again through the same doors**, because a resolution marks the import it came through as used and the mount it was spelled under as reached: a trace replayed leaves the `unused-import` lint and the reached mounts exact, and nothing of them is recorded.
- **Facts are taken where an item is lowered**, while its terms are open. A recursive group is stored closed over its members, and opening one to read the names it mentions costs what lowering it does; the passes that run over a whole unit ask about names and audiences, never about terms.
- **A witness is named anew, never lowered anew.** A kept witness takes the next name of its module as a lowered one does and is named by its signature afterwards, so a witness written above another moves no lowering.
- **A stamp finds and the text decides**: a stamp is thirty-two bits, enough to place a position and too few to believe a lowering by.

**Rejected.**

- **A lowering kept by its text alone**, or a file's by a digest of the file: it keeps the lowering that named the other declaration.
- **The scope a declaration was lowered in, compared whole**: every declaration of a module is lowered again when one import is added.
- **Lowering every declaration and comparing each with the baseline's**: the lowering is paid to learn it was not needed, and the comparison walks both sides of every declaration of the unit.
- **A lowering kept a file at a time**, a file that changed lowered whole and its declarations compared with the baseline's: the comparison this removes, paid for the file an author is in.
- **The facts read off a kept item's terms**: a group is opened again for each pass that reads it.
- **Uses recorded with each declaration**, the imports it marked and the mounts it reached: a second account of what the trace already says, kept in step by hand.
