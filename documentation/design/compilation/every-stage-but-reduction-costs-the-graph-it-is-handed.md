# Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed

**Decision.** A term is a graph: a reduct names one node many times over, and so does anything built from a value used twice. Reduction is the one stage a well-typed program can drive to arbitrary cost, and the budget counts it ([A reduction step costs what it builds](../soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md)). Every other stage costs the graph it is handed and never its tree, so a compilation ends — accepted, or refused for its budget — in time proportional to what was written plus what the budget counted. Work over a term is one of four kinds, each with its rule:

- **A read visits a node once.** Free variables, a needle sought, a binder's use: a walk that only reads remembers the nodes it has entered, or passes over a subterm a fact its node carries proves it has nothing to find in — no loose index, no local, no recursive group. A fact a node carries is made of its children's at the cost of what it adds: its free variables are its largest carrying child's set with the other children's names put into it, the smaller into the larger, so a sum naming one more local a level pays a path a level ([`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nodes-free-variables-are-its-childrens-shared)), and its rank is its children's summaries joined the same way, so an atom holding an atom pays for what it adds around it ([`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nodes-rank-is-its-childrens-joined)). The walk the elaborator finds a group's calls with reads under each arm's context, so it remembers no node across arms: it passes over a term that holds no group, and over a `let`'s value it walked where the `let` binds it, whose copies are those calls again.
- **A rebuild rebuilds a node once per depth and hands back a graph.** Substitution, shifting, capture and the zonks remember a node more than one owner holds, by the node and the binder depth it stands at; a rebuild that changes nothing hands back the node it was given. A pass's terms share one memo — a settlement's zonk, both totality obligations' — and a metavariable's spine is rebuilt only where its solution reads a binder of its birth scope. The normalization a report shows its terms through keeps what one display has normalized.
- **A judgment does not pay its scope.** The elaborator's frames are a persistent scope: a pushed frame refers to the frames beneath it and carries what they hold, so a question a step asks of its scope is a persistent map's lookup, and a held scope is a pointer. A metavariable's spine is the persistent vector of its birth scope's locals, minted and passed over without being walked, and spelled out only where an unsolved metavariable is read at another scope. A local definition is read by value once, where it is made, as is and with its universes erased, and its body is stored with its committed solutions substituted; a solved metavariable is substituted before a binder closes over the term holding it. A walk passes over what it cannot change: a capture, a subterm whose locals are all older than the binders it closes, which the newest local a node keeps tells, and within a spine a run of entries, whose newest local the spine keeps of each run; a materialization, a subterm that holds no metavariable; a reading of a local by value or as an arm refined it, a metavariable's occurrence, whose spine is the scope it was born in; and a node's facts are read off its spine's, never off its entries.
- **A declaration is read a fact at a time.** An instantiated declaration is read by facet — its sort, its arity, one constructor, one field — and only the facet asked is instantiated.
- **A judgment is remembered for as long as what it read stands.** A reduct, a stuck operation's classification, a sort, a type, a check, a comparison's verdict, a settled spelling: each is filed by its term and lives no longer than what it rests on — the declaration, the case equations in force, the binders it was read under, what the arms established — going at each boundary of the scope that can reach it, as [A reduction step costs what it builds](../soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) states. A hit is free and nothing outlives a declaration. Each table's key and life are stated in the module that owns it: the kernel's in `curios-cert`'s `memos`, argued in [`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts) and [Conversion recurrence](../soundness/conversion-recurrence.md); the elaborator's in `curios-elab`'s `caches` and `frames`. A table whose key holds a graph beside anything else tells two keys apart by their hash before it compares a field of either (`curios-utilities`'s `Keyed`), so a probe walks no key but the one it asks for, and what a judgment reads is the same in every run. A key that names no local and no metavariable is filed and asked as its canonical node, consed through one table that lives as long as the tables it keys (`curios-core`'s `Sharing`): two equal terms built apart — a type written once and built again at each level of a value checked against it — are compared node by node at every probe that finds one, so a nesting built a level at a time would ask its depth at every level, where a canonical key is found by its identity. A probe is consed as a key is, and the table remembers every node it met, so a chain built apart is walked once however many of its levels are asked. A checker's key that names a local or a metavariable is filed as it stands: the answer goes with the scope, which a table of canonical nodes living past it would keep, and a metavariable's spine is as wide as the scope it was born in. A closed weak-head value — a sort, a type former, a tuple, a structure or a variant — is its own answer (`curios_core::weak_head_value`): every reducer hands it back as the node it was given before any table is asked of it, since filing it would cons it as a key and a probe would find only the node. A host's reducer charges nothing for one at its door, as a hit is free; within the closed machine, reaching one is a transition, which costs its step.
- **An emission is bounded.** A print is a tree by nature, so a Core render builds a bounded number of child documents for each distinct node of its term and elides the rest; the bound and what it rejects are [the printer's](../tools/a-printer-states-each-fact-once-where-it-is-bound.md).

A `let` is where a program writes most of its sharing, and the kernel binds it: the value is checked once, where it stands, and the tail is typed over a local that stands for it. Whatever reads a term for more than its type reads it by value, each `let`-bound name unfolded ([`curios-cert`'s decision](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-let-is-bound-for-typing-and-read-by-value)). The elaborator keeps a `let` as a local definition, and reads it by value as the kernel does: once, where it is made.

What holds the rule is counted, not timed. Each crate that owns walks runs them over one doubling term in `every_walk_answers_a_doubling_term_in_its_own_size`; `curios`'s `tests::towers` compiles each tower — a chain of `let`s each naming the one before it twice — at sixty lines, has a tower's `core-elab` rung print and a mismatch over one report; and `tower_measurements` prints what each costs each checker in budget units and in node looks, the count of times a term handed out its node, sampled per declaration as `term::looks` beside `budget::consumed`. `curios`'s `tests::growth` compiles each shape an author grows a program along — length, width, depth, scope and size — past where a stage once broke on it, and `growth_slopes` compiles each at three sizes through every stage, counting each stage's instructions, node looks, allocations and bytes: two per doubling is linear and four quadratic, and a held growth's every slope stays under three.

**Rationale.**

- **The budget's premise is this rule.** "Every other stage is bounded by what elaboration produced" holds only if a stage is bounded by the graph, since what elaboration produces is one. A walk per path spends nothing the budget counts, so the budget cannot refuse it, and the compilation neither ends nor is refused.
- **Two checkers given one budget must cost alike.** A tower the elaborator walked in its size and the kernel walked per path was refused by the kernel alone, for a budget the program never spent.
- **A scope a judgment reads is held, not copied, as the peers hold theirs.** [Lean 4's](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/MetavarContext.lean) metavariable holds "the local context containing the free variables that the mvar is permitted to depend upon" and builds the application to them on demand (`mkMVarApp`), over a `LocalContext` of a `PersistentHashMap` and a `PersistentArray`. Rocq paid the other way: identity evar instances, "an explicit substitution that has the size of the context", were "the source of the next large wave of performance issues" ([#12526](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/issues/12526)) until [#16442](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/pull/16442) made "bunches of identity instances … a O(1) constructor".
- **A growth is counted at each stage, as the peers count theirs.** Swift's [scale-test](https://forums.swift.org/t/ongoing-work-related-to-compilation-performance/7085) fits the growth of compiler counters across sizes, and GHC's [performance tests](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/issues/12758) count allocations, read here through the counters `curios-profile` keeps.
- **A table per judgment, with its life stated, is what the trusted peers keep.** Lean 4's kernel keeps inferred types, weak-head forms and plain sets of the pairs conversion decided ([`type_checker.h`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.h)); its `replace` remembers a shared node by offset ([`replace_fn.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/replace_fn.cpp)); and both it and Rocq's kernel bind a `let` where they type one ([`type_checker.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp), [`typeops.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/typeops.ml)).
- **A key is canonical where it is filed, as Rocq's kernel keys its cache.** Rocq hash-conses a term in an implicit environment to key what its kernel has typed ([`hConstr.mli`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/hConstr.mli): "This allows using it as a cache key while typechecking").
- **The problem is a known one, and so is its remedy.** [Selsam, Hudon and de Moura](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01685) state it — "traversing a term requires time proportional to the tree size of the term as opposed to its graph size" — and their remedy, a stored hash, pointer-accelerated equality and a remembered traversal, is the representation `Term` has. rustc met the shape walker by walker ([#54540](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/54540), [#72412](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/72412), [#83031](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/83031), [#140004](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/140004)) and keeps a thirty-line tower in its suite ([`issue-72408-nested-closures-exponential.rs`](https://rust.googlesource.com/rust/+/HEAD/tests/ui/closures/issue-72408-nested-closures-exponential.rs)); [Accattoli and Dal Lago](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.01233) show a count of reduction steps is a reasonable cost model only over shared terms.

**Rejected.**

- **A spine over the bound variables alone**, `let`-defined locals left out as [elaboration-zoo](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/elaboration-zoo)'s pruning mask leaves them: an `Io` block's binders are lambdas', so its scope grows all the same.
- **A spine linear in the scope, as [smalltt](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/smalltt) keeps it**, "usually linear in the size of the local scope": a scope as long as a program costs it at every metavariable minted there.
- **Interning every node where it is built.** It costs every node a judgment builds, where a key is consed where a table files or asks it, and it does nothing for the size of what reduction builds ([smalltt](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/smalltt): "hash consing alone is inadequate for eliminating size explosions").
- **A probe that adopts nothing.** A term is consed its children first, so a probe of a chain built apart walks to its foot before it learns its head was never filed, and what it missed it walks again at the next level: a nesting asked a level at a time costs its square again.
- **Consing a declaration once, where a checker is handed it.** The copies a table meets are built while the declaration is judged — a type inferred at each level of a value, a reduct rebuilt over the value of its operand — and not written into it.
- **Charging a walk to the budget.** A walk that costs its graph is bounded by the nodes the budget charged when they were built. What a walk enters follows how its graphs share their nodes, which nothing holds the same between a term elaborated here and one restored from a stored unit, so a charge by the node is a second price list, and one the program does not determine. A walk that outgrew its graph was a loop repeating it — a table probed at every level of a spine, a comparison meeting its own goal again — and the loop is what is removed.
- **One fixed key for every table**, so that two runs probe alike. What a probe reads would repeat and still follow which keys the fixed one sets side by side, a fact of the hasher and none of the program; and a table whose keys a program writes keeps the keys a process draws.
- **A memo argued per walk.** One shape was reported walk by walk; a rule per kind of work is what a new walk is held to.
- **A closure over the pairs conversion accepted.** Its result would depend on the order goals were met in.
