# A declaration is a function of what it reads

**Decision.** What a declaration compiles to is determined by its own text and by what it reads of other declarations, never by where either is written, by which of them was elaborated first, or by how many came before. The lowering hands a unit's items on as they are written, and sorts nothing. They are elaborated on demand (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/demand.rs`): each after every item its lowered form reaches, and after every declaration it turns out to read while it elaborates, the witness a goal resolves to. A read of a declaration that has not elaborated voids the attempt, whatever is read of it — its name, its body, a witness's key, or a registry entry, which is registered as lowered before the unit's first item: the read is recorded (`Context::need`), what the attempt wrote is undone, the declaration it needed is elaborated, and the attempt is made again. An attempt is never suspended to run another inside it, and never waits on one. Declarations that need one another are refused together, once, at the first of them as written, naming every member. A top-level group whose members do not all need one another is refused as well, at its first member as written, naming the parts it falls into. A witness is known before it elaborates by the key its signature is spelled at ([A witness is named by what it witnesses](a-witness-is-named-by-what-it-witnesses.md)), so whether a witness stands at a key is answered from every witness the unit declares before any of them is elaborated.

Nothing but those reads flows into a declaration. The declaration is the unit of work: an item with every item the lowering generates from it, a type former with its constructors. Its identities — binders, metavariables, universe levels — count from zero for it, in its lowering (`curios_core::Minted`) and above that in its elaboration, and it elaborates in a state of its own: its metavariables with their solutions, its universe solver and its caches are replaced where an attempt at it begins (`Context::attempt`). Each of its items is published as it finishes, zonked, into what every other declaration reads — the bindings of its names, its registry entries, the terms it recorded — which is the form a recompile that keeps the item reads of its baseline, so what is read of it holds nothing of the state it was elaborated in. A declaration that wrote a goal is published by its types alone and keeps that state, in which the goal is reported once every declaration of the unit has finished.

A declaration's signature is published with its body: a declaration that reads another's type reads it once that declaration's elaboration has finished. A body another declaration needs is read, never withheld, but for a proof's. A definition a declaration writes at a proposition, or at a function into one, is published marked a proof (`curios_core::Definition::proof`), and from then on its name unfolds to nothing in either checker: what a later declaration reads of it is its statement. Each checker writes the mark with the marks of the item's binders, by its own reading, for its own run: the elaborator as it publishes the item (`state_proof_binders`, `curios-elab/src/proof_binders.rs`), and the certifier again for every module it judges, reading none a module arrives with (`curios-cert/src/certifier/stating.rs`). Each reads it where a name enters its environment, a unit in scope's as its own, and a unit stores the certifier's. A constructor of a proposition and a law's projection are terms the compiler writes, and stay what they unfold to. A definition taking no parameter whose type is written as a sort is published with the sort its value inhabits (`curios_core::Definition::value_sort`), written beside the mark, by each checker as the mark is, and read, its value unwalked, by every judgment that asks a binder typed by it its sort, its quality or whether it binds a proof.

A proof the elaborator writes applies lemmas its declaration never names, the prover's vocabulary (`SyntaxRegistry::entailment`), so no read says which of them a declaration has. In the unit that declares the vocabulary, the walk asks for its declarations first, which brings in every declaration they read, with no lemma of the unit on offer, and offers all of them from then on (`Demand::all`, `Context::proof_may_apply`): a declaration the vocabulary reads is proved without it, and every other finds the whole of it elaborated. A unit stores which declarations were settled before the offer (`Traces::before`). Compiled over a baseline, where the edit reaches none of them, each declaration stands on its side of the offer; where it reaches one, the vocabulary is asked for as here, each declaration it comes to asked by its trace after the declarations its questions are about, so what the vocabulary reads through a kept declaration is asked for with it, and one that falls out of what the vocabulary reads is elaborated again ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)). Which lemmas a declaration's proofs have is thereby a function of what the vocabulary reads, and of nowhere anything is written.

What is decided of a declaration over its final terms is decided as it is published, once, each judgment on a budget restored for it (`settle_published`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`): the polarity vectors of the registry entries it declares, that the variance vectors they carry claim no more than their telescopes grant, each definition's totality, and its two erasure obligations. No pass over a unit decides any of them again, and a recompile writes none of a reused declaration's. Erasure restores its budget for each item, so a unit takes a declaration under every budget the declaration takes alone, whatever else the unit or its scope holds; a unit in scope is read where it stands and spends nothing.

The certifier judges a declaration the same way (`verdicts_within`, `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`): an item's check, its classification and its erased positions, then the registry entries it declares — their strict positivity, their variance and their sizing — each on a budget restored for it and over those entries alone. A family they mention answers from the polarity vector the certifier filed for it, this walk's as it accepted the family or a unit in scope's from the record its walk left (`curios_core::Certification`). The vector an entry carries is elaboration's, and no judgment of the certifier reads it.

A unit's items are stored in one order, read off what they name: each item at its depth, one more than the deepest item of the unit its elaborated form reaches, and among items of one depth by name, so every item after the items it reaches (`curios_core::Order`, `Module::ordered`). The module is put in that order once, where the elaborator makes it, whole or over a baseline, and every later walk takes the items as they come: the certifier, which refuses an item that names one stored after it, erasure, and a program's link, which is the order its top-level statements evaluate in. What each item reaches is taken where the item is made and kept with the unit, in the elaborator's record (`Traces`), so no walk reads an item's terms to place it again. Over a baseline the order is the baseline's revised by the items the recompile made: each takes the depth what it reaches gives it, an item whose depth moves passes the change on to the items that reach it, in increasing depth, stopping where a depth comes out as it was, and only an item whose depth or name moved is placed again (`Order::revised`). A witness is stored where its name puts it, wherever its module writes it ([A witness is named by what it witnesses](a-witness-is-named-by-what-it-witnesses.md)); tests are scheduled in the order they are written.

**Rationale.**

- **It is the correctness of a build, stated for declarations.** Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones call a build correct when "the result of recomputing the corresponding task matches the value stored in the result" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), definition 3.1). A declaration recompiled alone over its finished unit is that recomputation, so [a stored unit is a baseline](a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md) only as far as this holds, and so is any schedule that elaborates two declarations at once.
- **The surface already promises it.** A witness may follow the code that resolves through it ([Witness resolution](../../syntax.md#witness-resolution)), so where a declaration is written is no part of what it means; a program accepted in one order of its declarations and refused in another keeps that promise by accident. rust-analyzer's account of [three architectures](https://rust-analyzer.github.io/blog/2020/07/20/three-architectures-for-responsive-ide.html) keeps the snapshot-after-headers design for languages with a "declaration before use" rule, and answers the rest by demand, where "we compensate for the deficit of laziness with incrementality".
- **An answer that follows what has elaborated is an answer about the schedule.** A goal retried after every later item resolves once its consumer's levels have closed, so the consumer keeps the levels it happened to be left with; a monad check that reads the table finds no monad where the witness is written lower down; and a goal that never resolves is reported after its item has finished, which means taking finished items back. Each is one question asked at the wrong time, and none of them remains once the question is answered before any declaration that asks it is kept.
- **A signature is not final before its body.** `finalize_definition` settles a written signature's universe context with the constraints its body raised (`finalize_universe_metas(interface, internal)`), and the surface has no syntax for a level, so a signature read before its body has finished could still move.
- **A proof's body is read by its own check alone.** Conversion holds two proofs of one proposition equal before it reduces either, and no match takes a proof into data by reducing it ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../theory/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)), so no judgment asks anything a lemma's body would answer. Unfolded where a reduction happens to reach its name, the body is work no verdict reads, an induction run again wherever a type meets the lemma, and a read that ties every declaration naming the lemma to how it is proved. Withheld, the lemma is a constant of its statement: a declaration that asked what a lemma unfolds to is kept over a baseline where the lemma has since been proved another way (`curios-elab/src/same.rs`), and the certifier's walk adopts its verdict on it (`curios-cert/src/baseline.rs`).
- **Whether a definition is a proof is decided once, where it is published.** Its type says so, and reading that off a type is a judgment: `Sorted(xs)` is a proposition by what `Sorted` declares, `P` by its binder, an alias by what it reduces to. A unit in scope is read where it stands and spends nothing, and the lookup that hands a body out sits on every unfolding's path, so the answer is kept on the definition as its totality is, by the declaration that has the type's sort in hand. It is the mark a binder states ([The closed machine binds a proof unreduced](the-closed-machine-binds-a-proof-unreduced.md)), on the one binder a term does not hold, and the certifier states it again as it states a binder's: a judgment reads what a name unfolds to, so what decides that in the certifier is the certifier's own reading of the type, never what a module arriving says.
- **What sort a type alias's value inhabits is decided once, where it is published.** Each judgment that asks a binder typed by a closed alias one fact of it — whether it is a proof, its sort, whether erasure keeps it — read the alias's value whole to learn it, so every reader of a row paid the row's width, for an answer that is a function of the definition alone. So the answer is kept on the definition beside the mark, read off the value at the definition's own levels and never off the annotation, which may name a larger universe, and asked through a door of its own (`Question::Sort`), as totality and the variance vectors are. The certifier states it again as it states the mark, of the value it judges, at every assignment of the definition's sorts, and a definition it states none of is read by its reduct. Erasure reads every level as one (`curios_core::project_erased_universes`), so it takes the quality alone, a proposition's value erased unwalked. An alias that takes parameters is read by its reduct, which reduction builds and the budget counts.
- **A void attempt costs one elaboration and needs nothing new.** Undoing an attempt is what refusing an item already does (`ItemMark`), and an attempt made again enters nothing: one context serves every attempt, where elaborating the needed declaration inside the attempt that needs it would need a context per declaration, and a worker that waits for one needs a deadlock detector. Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones name it *restarting*: "whenever a task calls fetch on an out-of-date key dep, abort the task, and switch to building the dependency dep", which is "not minimal in the sense that a task may start, do some meaningful work, and then abort" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), section 4.1.2). Bazel's Skyframe is the industrial case: it hands a function `null` for a dependency that is not built, "the function itself is expected to return `null`", and "the dependent node is evaluated, and then the original node builder is invoked again" ([Skyframe](https://bazel.build/reference/skyframe)).
- **The record voids the attempt, not the error.** A read of an unfinished declaration returns an error that cuts the attempt short, and elaboration turns errors into decisions in dozens of places — a candidate tried and dropped, a diagnosis that is simply absent. The need is recorded where the read happens and asked for once the attempt ends, so no such site can make an attempt that read an incomplete answer count.
- **A cycle is a fact about the program, and is reported as one.** Declarations that each need another have no order to elaborate in under any schedule, so the refusal names them and the way out — one group — where the certifier would name an identity the reader cannot find in their own program.
- **A top-level group is a cycle and nothing else.** A declaration reads any other wherever it is written, so `and` at the top ties only declarations that need one another, and a member the others never reach would be held with them for nothing: monomorphic in their universe levels, a recursive member carrying every one of them, so a signature that names it takes a parameter for a level only another member uses; and judged with their descent. With both refusals a top-level group is exactly a cycle of what its members reach, read once their terms and registry entries are elaborated, so a witness's resolutions count, and before their levels close. A local group is not held to it, since it also orders the values a recursive binding forces by need.
- **A counter two declarations share is a read neither wrote.** A binder's identity is a position in a counter, and what a lowered declaration hands its elaboration is keyed by those positions — the names it left unbound, each by the binder it lowered to, and a seed per level by its id — so with one counter for a unit an edit to one declaration renumbers every declaration after it, and every table keyed by their identities with it. No order reads how far a counter had run: an atom is ranked by its shape and then by the age of each identity it holds (`curios-core`'s `Term::rank`), which no identity minted before it moves. Counted from zero for each declaration, in its lowering and above that in its elaboration, an identity says nothing of any other declaration, and an edit to one renumbers no other.
- **A state replaced whole cannot carry what a list forgets.** Solutions, the universe solver and the caches are replaced where an attempt begins, never cleared field by field: a field added later would flow from one declaration to the next unless its author remembered the list.
- **What is published is what a baseline holds.** A finished item is bound in the context from its zonked form, the form a recompile that keeps the item reads where its baseline holds it, so a declaration elaborated beside it in a whole compile and one elaborated over it in a recompile read the same terms, and a hole it left unsolved is refused where it stands.
- **A declaration's items share one state because they share one written declaration.** The lowering makes a family's constructors from the entry it writes, so their types are the entry's written ones, and a written type is one level wherever it is copied; a constructor is finalized at the instance of the family it belongs to. So they are attempted together, and no other declaration is elaborated between two of them.
- **A goal is reported in the state that knows it, over the unit that is written.** A declaration that wrote a `?` keeps the state it elaborated in (`Attempted`), and its goals are reported in that state once every declaration of the unit has finished, so the candidates a report offers are drawn from the whole unit, a definition written below the goal among them, whichever declaration elaborated first. Until then it is published by its types alone, a constant: what reads it elaborates and reports its own goals, where withholding it would hide them until the first was filled. A type former, a concept and a witness are read by more than a type, so one that holds a goal is taken out, and what reads it is withheld.
- **A lemma no declaration names is a read no walk can order by.** Offered by position, to the declarations written after it, a lemma makes where a declaration sits part of what its proofs are built from, and an item the lowering moves behind one of its dependencies gains the lemmas written between the two. Offered as each proof wants it, a lemma is asked for by a declaration it may itself read through a witness, which closes a cycle found only once both have begun. Asked for before everything else and offered whole from then on, the vocabulary waits on nothing that could wait on it.
- **What a declaration reads is found by elaborating it.** A sort made before elaboration orders by what is written, and a witness a goal resolves to, a lemma a proof applies and a body a derivation writes are written nowhere: to stand in for them the sort grows edges that over-approximate, deadlock on the library's own cycles and are dropped a node at a time. The walk needs none of it, since a read of a declaration that has not elaborated asks for it, and a cycle is refused once, by the one place that sees every edge.
- **An item's place is a facet of what it reaches.** Read off its own name and the depths of what it reaches, an item's key moves only where a depth it reads moves, so a recompile gives the order again from what it made, stopping where a key comes out as it was, as every other facet is kept ([A compile costs what it reads](a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md)), and a depth that moves is passed to the items that read it, transitively, as a mark is. Depth is longest-path layering ([Layered graph drawing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Layered_graph_drawing)), made in one pass over a module whole, and the update is the one incremental timing analysis makes of arrival times, which are longest paths too: recomputed downstream of a change, and stopped at a node whose latest input stands ([US 7512919](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/7512919)).
- **An order every stage sorts for itself is an order none of them owns.** Sorted by the lowering over the names an item writes, by the certifier again over the elaborated terms and by erasure a third time, elaboration adding the edges a witness and a lemma make, each sort breaks its ties on the order it is handed, and where a declaration is written reaches the items a unit stores and the module a program compiles to. One order, a function of what the items declare and reach, is the same for the same declarations wherever they are written, and a walk that takes it sorts nothing and can only refuse what it finds out of order.
- **A budget two declarations share is a read neither wrote.** A pass that runs over a unit after its items spends one budget on all of them, so whether a declaration's verdict fits follows what the others cost: sixteen families that name nothing of one another are refused under a budget each is taken under alone, the refusal names whichever the budget ran out on, and a program that declares nothing has to afford the key of every witness in its scope. Decided as the declaration is published, on a budget of its own, a verdict is the declaration's. And a facet written once has one value: a pass wrote every reused declaration's vectors and stamp again on each recompile, where nothing a reused declaration read had moved.
- **A judgment over the scope is a read of everything in it.** Strict positivity decided for every declaration in scope at once spends one budget on all of them: a program that declares nothing has to afford the analysis of every family of the library, a unit of sixteen families sixteen times one, and the refusal names whichever family the budget ran out on. Variance answers for a family in scope from a vector held to that family's declaration when its unit was certified, and a polarity vector filed by the walk that computed it is the same answer to the same question ([Cached verdicts](../soundness/cached-verdicts.md)), so judging a declaration reads the vectors of the families it names and nothing else of the scope. The peers' kernels judge a declaration as it is added: Lean's reference says "Before new inductive types or definitions are added to the environment by the command or term elaborators, they must be checked by the certifier" ([Elaboration and Compilation](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Elaboration-and-Compilation/)), and Agda checks "that the datatypes in the mutual block containing the given declarations are strictly positive", a block at a time, and adds "information about positivity and recursivity of records to the signature" ([`Agda.TypeChecking.Positivity`](https://hackage-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.6.0/docs/Agda-TypeChecking-Positivity.html)).
- **A verdict is a closure, so a declaration asks only what it names.** A definition is partial where anything it mentions is, and every declaration a published one mentions has finished, so its erasure obligations read the verdicts of the definitions their positions write and walk into none of them.
- **Evidence.** `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::order`, `tests::budget`, `tests::identity` and `tests::incremental_*_tests` hold a unit to the module, the record, the erased forms and the budgets its declarations have wherever they are written and whatever is written before them; `curios-elab`'s `context::tests::need` and `curios-cert`'s `recheck::scope_tests` and `recheck::budget_tests` hold each checker's half.

**Rejected.**

- **The certifier's own record of an alias's value sort, beside the elaborator's**, as a family's polarity vector is filed: two fields where one serves both checkers, each stating it for its own run and a unit storing the certifier's.
- **Deferring a goal with no entry and retrying it after every later item.** It is the schedule answering: the goal resolves when a witness happens to register, after its consumer has closed.
- **Ordering uses after their witnesses in the lowering**, with more or harder edges: which witness a use resolves to is a typing fact the lowering cannot know, so the edges over-approximate and deadlock.
- **Requiring a witness before its use**, as Lean and Rocq do: `/std`'s order does not follow its witnesses, and the surface says it need not.
- **A name of another unit read by what it elaborated to**, where a name of the unit is read by its lowered form: two readings, so whether a witness spells its key would follow which unit a definition it names is written in, and the lowering would be handed what elaboration made.
- **Splitting a top-level group that is no cycle into its cycles**, as Lean partitions a mutual block of definitions ([`partitionPreDefs`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Elab/PreDefinition/Main.lean)): one written item would become several the author did not write, where the converse is refused with its way out named. **A universe context for each member**, the group opened at each member's own instance: it relieves exactly the members a cycle never holds, at the cost of the group's representation and of how both checkers compare two instances of one.
- **Elaborating the unfinished declaration inside the attempt that needs it**, Shake's suspension: it needs a context per declaration, and under several workers it leaves the facet another worker holds, which waits.
- **Waiting on the task that holds a declaration**: a worker that waits needs a deadlock detector, which is what rustc's parallel front end lists open: of its threads it says "there are some known bugs, including deadlocks" ([Faster compilation with the parallel front-end in nightly](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2023/11/09/parallel-rustc/)).
- **Clearing a shared context between declarations**, field by field: a field added later flows from one declaration to the next unless its author remembers the list, where a state replaced whole cannot carry one.
- **Renumbering a declaration's identities as its elaboration begins**, in place of minting them per declaration: every table keyed by a lowered identity — unbound names, a level's origin — would follow the renaming.
- **One state for a unit that holds a goal**, so its goals are reported from one place: two declarations' identities are the same numbers, and a declaration elaborated after a goal would follow the declarations before it again.
- **Reporting a goal as its declaration finishes**: what fits it would be drawn from the declarations that had elaborated by then, which is the order they were asked for in.
- **Withholding what reads a declaration that holds a goal**: a reader's own goals and refusals would stay unreported until the first goal was filled.
- **Keeping the lowering's order as what a proof's lemmas are read by**, with the sort computed for that alone: nothing moves, and an edit elsewhere still decides which lemmas an item has.
- **A lemma applied by the declarations written after it**: where a declaration is written is no part of what it means, and the order of `/std`'s `mod` lines would decide which of its bounds are proved, proofs in `/std/Str` applying `/std/Eq/sym`, which is written far below them.
- **A lemma applied by every declaration it does not reach, the vocabulary's own among them**: neither of two unrelated lemmas reaches the other, and a proof of each written with the other is a cycle through two declarations that name nothing of one another.
- **A lemma withheld from the declarations the vocabulary's written names reach, and asked for by any other as its proof wants it**: a witness a lemma resolves to is reached by no name the lemma writes, so a proof in that witness would ask for the lemma while the lemma waits on the witness, a cycle found only once both had begun.
- **Giving a proof no lemma of the unit being compiled**: order-free, and `/std`'s own bounds past its `Nat` lemmas would need written proofs, or the lemmas a unit of their own.
- **An order kept by where a declaration is written**, as the tie among items that do not name one another: a unit would store another module for the same declarations moved, and [a stored unit says what its items mean, not where they were written](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-stored-unit-says-what-its-items-mean-not-where-they-were-written-or-how-they-were-come-by).
- **An order read off a record of what elaboration read**: what the elaborated terms name is what orders them, and is taken off the item where it is made; a question asked and answered absent, or a candidate tried and dropped, names nothing the item holds.
- **The tie broken by the least name among the items waiting on nothing**, the order a unit was stored in before: an item's place is then the sort's choice over the whole unit, so an edit to what one item reaches can move items it never names, and finding which means running the sort again, or running it forward from the first place it moved until its run agrees with the baseline's, over items the edit never reached.
- **An order kept by an algorithm that maintains one under edits**, Pearce and Kelly's ([A dynamic topological sort algorithm for directed acyclic graphs](https://whileydave.com/publications/pk07_jea/)), Haeupler and others' ([Incremental cycle detection, topological ordering, and strong component maintenance](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.2397)) or Jane Street Incremental's heights, which only rise ([`adjust_heights_heap.ml`](https://ocaml.org/p/incremental/v0.17.0/doc/src/incremental/adjust_heights_heap.ml.html)): each keeps some order, and which one follows the edits made, so a recompile would store another order than a whole compile of the same text.
- **No order stored**, each walk ordering what it visits, as Unison stores a definition by its hash alone ([The big idea](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/)): an order every stage sorts for itself is an order none of them owns.
- **Lean's signature rule**, that a signature alone fixes a declaration's universe parameters: it needs level syntax the surface does not have.
- **Publishing a lemma's statement before its proof**, as the peers that check in parallel do — Lean 4.19 elaborates theorem bodies in parallel ([lean4#7084](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/pull/7084)), and Rocq processes only opaque proofs asynchronously, needing the theorem's statement without looking at its proof ([Asynchronous and Parallel Proof Processing](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/v9.1/refman/addendum/parallel-proof-processing.html)): a proof's body is withheld from what reads the lemma, and its statement's levels still settle with the constraints its body raises.
- **Reading whether a definition is a proof off its type where its body is asked for**, with nothing stored: run as the judgment it is, the reading spends on whichever declaration asked first, for every lemma of every unit in scope a compile touches; read by spelling alone, it is a third reader of propositions the two checkers must share exactly, and a statement that is computed goes on unfolding.
- **A mark for each checker**, the certifier's in the record its walk files: the rule that a walk believes its own record exists for a verdict that admits something, and a body withheld admits nothing; two marks that part refuse a declaration that reads the lemma, far from the lemma.
- **The elaborator alone withholding a proof's body**: the certifier would go on asking for every body its reductions reach, and adopt nothing across an edit to one.
- **A keyword for a definition that does not unfold**, Lean's `theorem` and Rocq's `Qed`: under a strict `Prop` nothing observes a proof that unfolds, so the choice has one option.
- **Publishing a signature speculatively and rolling back dependents** when the body disagrees: the answer would be deterministic and the work would not, and a dependent's diagnostics would be computed against a signature that never existed.
- **Passes over a unit after its items**, for the vectors, the totality verdicts and the erasure obligations: one budget for every declaration, each facet written again by every recompile, and one refusal for the unit where each declaration has its own.
- **Recomputing the polarity vectors a declaration reaches**, believing no filed one: the trusted base is unchanged, each verdict records more reads, and variance and positivity keep two answers to one question.
- **Restoring the budget per family inside one judgment of positivity over the scope**: the verdict is then the declaration's, and every compile still walks every family of every unit before it.
- **One budget for everything decided of a declaration**, its elaboration and the judgments of its final terms together: a declaration that elaborates under a budget would be refused under it for what deciding its vectors and its obligations adds.
