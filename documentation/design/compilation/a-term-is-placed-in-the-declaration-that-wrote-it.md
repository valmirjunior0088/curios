# A term is placed in the declaration that wrote it

**Decision.** A position in a Core term is an extent of the source declaration that wrote it: an anchor, two offsets from where the anchor's text begins, and a stamp of that text (`curios_core::Extent`). It names no file and no line, and holds no source. An anchor is one allocation for what it anchors, so a position is a pointer and three numbers, and an image holds each anchor once, behind a shared pointer. A declaration is anchored by the path of the first name it declares, a module's `satisfy` declarations by the module, and the term a program closes with by itself (`Anchored`). Where each text of each anchor lies is the lowering's to say for the unit it lowered, and is kept in that unit's text part (`Anchors`): the parser hands out each top-level item's own text, from its first word to the end of its last, and the lowering sites it as the declaration begins. Past the lowering no stage holds a `Span`. An error carries the extent of the term it is about, and the doors that turn an error into a report resolve it, against the unit's anchors and then its scope's (`curios_elab::Error::reports`). An extent resolves to its range in the text that carries its stamp; where no text of its anchor does, to the whole of the anchor's text where it has one; and otherwise to nothing. A report whose error holds no extent, or one that resolves to nothing, is placed at the name of the declaration it arose in, which the same table holds for every definition a name was written for (`Anchors::label`); only a report with neither goes unplaced.

**Rationale.**

- **A declaration an edit moves is the declaration it was.** Held as offsets into a file, a position makes a declaration lower to another form wherever an edit above it moves it, leaves a declaration a recompile keeps pointing at lines that have since moved, and has a unit a session keeps hold every text it was ever lowered from. Held in the declaration's own text, what a declaration lowers, elaborates and stores to does not follow where it stands, and a kept term is placed on the line the present text holds it at. rustc "encodes local spans relative to the span of an item" for the same reason ([rust-lang/rust#84762](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/84762)), and Roslyn's green node "tracks its *width* but not its *absolute position*" ([Persistence, facades and Roslyn's red-green trees](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/archive/blogs/ericlippert/persistence-facades-and-roslyns-red-green-trees)).
- **A term kept past its text is placed at its declaration, never on a line it does not lie on.** A declaration whose text changes without changing what it lowers to is kept, and a term may hold a position copied from another declaration's signature; either outlives the text its offsets were taken in. The stamp travels with the extent because the term does: a mark on the anchor could not tell a term made from the present text from one kept beside it.
- **A report is the only reader a position has.** Elaboration, the kernel and erasure cannot resolve one, so nothing they make can follow where a declaration is written. The two messages that state a position in their own words, a goal batch rendered whole and a written goal a postponed conversion watched, are rendered where the report is made.
- **An anonymous declaration is found by its text.** A witness's name is read off its signature once every item is lowered, after its terms are built, so it is lowered under its module's anchor and told from the module's other witnesses by its text's stamp.
- **A term is no wider for it**, and copying one no longer touches the count of a shared source.

**Rejected.**

- **A source held as one allocation for a path and a text**, so that two positions' sources compare by pointer wherever a text stands: every term still holds a text, a declaration an edit moves is still another form, and the archive's reader needs an `unsafe` adapter to keep the invariant.
- **Positions moved onto the new text where a declaration is kept**: a walk of every kept declaration of an edited file, for a form that should not have said where it was written.
- **The elaborator handed the anchors**, to resolve a position where it raises an error: an error would keep its span, and elaboration could read where a declaration is written.
- **The stamp held by the anchor**, an anchor interned for each text of each declaration: one entry of a table nothing frees for every keystroke.
- **A witness's terms rewritten under its name once it has one**: every node of every witness built twice, for a name only a report reads.
- **An item known by its place in its file**: an item inserted above renumbers every one below it.
- **An anchor written at every position of an image**, as the path it anchors, or **a stamp as wide as a digest**: an image holds a position for every term a declaration writes, and a stamp tells apart only the texts of one anchor.
