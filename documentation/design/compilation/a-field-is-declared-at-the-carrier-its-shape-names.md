# A field is declared at the carrier its shape names

**Decision.** Erasure records the erased carrier shape of every kept constructor payload and product entry, the Ersd door resolves it to a slot carrier and lays the row out, and the Wasm emitter declares the struct field at it. A heap type is keyed by an identity — a variant family or a product schema — never by an arity. Slot zero of a family is its tag, stored `i8` and read with `struct.get_u`; a scalar payload is a raw `i32` or `f64`; a list, a closure and a nominal row are declared at their own heap types; everything else is `(ref null any)`. Families and schemas share one vocabulary, `curios_cont::ValueExpr::Row` and `Intrinsic::RowGet` over a `RowId`, and `Tuple`/`TupleGet` is kept for the structural rows that name no schema — a multi-result host call's pack, the rebuilds parameter splitting performs — whose final `$tuple/N` types a read finds by testing each arity that could hold the field ([`curios-emit`'s README](../../../curios-emit/README.md)). Slots are grouped by carrier: a carrier owns a range as wide as the constructor holding the most fields of it.

A carrier that is only sometimes an immediate has no heap type. A small packed value — `Bytes` of at most 3 bytes — has one runtime form, the i31, every producer normalizing on the way out, hoisted constants included; its field records `FieldShape::Bytes`, never `Immediate`. A `Nat` or `Int` field is an i31 or a `$big`, and an immediate-encoded family's bare constructor rides beside allocating siblings; neither can be declared at one type. A family-typed reference slot is admitted only where laying the row out typed does not widen it.

The walk that names a field's shape states a binder's register. A binder whose producer names no carrier — a function parameter, a call's result, a field or payload read, a sequence element — records `RegisterShape::Immediate` or `Flt` where every value of its type rides that register's population, read at the type the binder is checked at, and nothing for a product, a family or anything unstated: a binder is held as a reference whatever row it holds. A copy carries the statement, a binder a pass mints states none, and a rewrite handing a binder a producer that names its own carrier drops it, so the Ersd verifier refuses a statement wherever a producer names one. What a stated carrier may feed is the representation's to decide ([`curios-cont`'s README](../../../curios-cont/README.md#representation-is-decided-wherever-every-party-is-visible)).

**Rationale.**

- **Arity keying makes a field unsayable.** One `$tuple/N` serving every product and constructor of that width makes the join over a field's stores the top type by construction, and Binaryen's `TypeRefining` narrows nothing. Keying by identity gives each row a type only its own writers touch.
- **One vocabulary, because a second is paid for silently.** A profitability gate asking whether a body holds a `TupleGet` answers no for every variant once family reads are spelled otherwise, and declines every constructor specialization — the specializer's gate on `state_monad` is the case.
- **Grouping by carrier costs width only where constructors disagree.** Positional slots join disagreeing constructors to the top type again, and disjoint ranges widen every row for little more typing.
- **One form for a small packed value keeps an immediate honest.** A mixed immediate/rope pair for one value is unrepresentable rather than a case every equality and hash must remember, for a normalization that costs a bounds check on operations that already branch. Its standing price is that no position holding such a value can be declared at the rope base, so a reference enters the base through a test of each final shape ([`curios-emit`'s README](../../../curios-emit/README.md#a-rope-is-entered-through-its-final-shapes)): declaring the base would give back what the immediate buys `spines` (`map_wall_spines_slope`).
- **Typing a reference slot trades bytes for instructions**, so the row is laid out both ways and the typed one kept iff the widths agree. By `family_slot_probe`, sixteen of the prelude's families qualify and eight do not, `/std/Map/internal/Node` among the eight: typing its children widens its row from four slots to five, a quarter again the live memory of the corpus's hottest allocated row, to replace casts already exact against a final type — declined, since live bytes convert to time under an all-live collector ([`curios-native`'s README](../../../curios-native/README.md)).
- **A binder states its register for the reason a field states its shape**: no type survives erasure, and a reference crossing a boundary nothing sees whole, an escaping function's parameter or a closure call's result, is otherwise a float no later stage can prove is one. Stating it only where the producer names none keeps each fact said once ([A printer states each fact once, where it is bound](../tools/a-printer-states-each-fact-once-where-it-is-bound.md)).
- **Measured** by `closure_index_dispatch_measurements`: typing family slots pays where a hot loop reads a typed payload and costs a little where a family types its tag and nothing else, as `parse_digits`'s do; extending the rule to products and closures measures flat, because specialization and parameter splitting scalarize every product the corpus declares.

**Rejected.**

- **A recorded width where an identity is needed**, `FieldShape::Product(usize)` and a bare `Family`: enough to count a census, not to spend one, and false of a keyed product. Carrying the identity registers an inductive's family before its constructors are walked, so a recursive type finds its own identity.
- **Both small forms legal, the immediate a fast path**, which every equality, hash and structural test would carry forever.
- **A third vocabulary pair** for products beside `Row` and `Tuple`.
- **A register stated on every binder**, an operation's result included, which says again what the operation says and could say it otherwise; and **a binder's full shape**, which would register a row for every product-typed value read and tells a binder nothing, a binder being a reference whatever it holds.
- **Function signatures typed by shape** in the same stroke: an escaping function's signature stays uniformly `anyref`, rope payloads stay uniform, and a closure field is typed because an escaping function value keeps the arity its declared type states. A known function's parameters and results are held at the carriers their flows decide, which is the representation's to settle rather than a shape's ([`curios-cont`'s README](../../../curios-cont/README.md#representation-is-decided-wherever-every-party-is-visible)).
