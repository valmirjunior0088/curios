# An unread call is dropped by what it hands

**Decision.** Whether a call whose result nothing reads may simply not happen is decided at the call, from the callee and what the call hands it. `curios-anf`'s summary (`summary.rs`) states what a function's body does apart from applying the values it was handed, its parameters and its captures, beside the values among those it applies; a function proved total whose body does nothing observable besides crosses into CPS as droppable given those values, `curios_cps::Droppable::Given`, and any other as `Droppable::Never`. A call may go where each value named is a parameter of the callee and the call hands it a function droppable whatever it is handed (`Module::may_drop_call`), which `eliminate_dead_calls` and `sink_computations` both ask. A copy that hands a function for a value named discharges it there (`Droppable::mapped`): the inliner, copying a loop that captures a parameter of the function it inlines, hands the loop what the call handed. A claim follows every substitution of what it names, as each site naming the value does: `rewrite_atoms` maps the claims through what a round knows, and `shrink` maps those naming a value it substitutes, which its census indexes — as when it moves a fold into its one call and puts the stepper in place of the parameter its loop captures. A rewrite that changes what a call of a function does states the claim anew: returned-closure uncurrying (`uncurry_returns`) makes a member's call apply the closure it returned, which no claim made of building it covers, so the member is `Droppable::Never`, and a collapsed nest (`uncurry_nests`) runs its innermost level's body, which the levels above only bound, so it takes that level's claim. A recursion contification makes a loop of has no call left to read a claim at: the loop carries its function's termination instead (`Continuation::terminates`, set from `Function::terminates`, the certifier's verdict the lowering reads), and `eliminate_dead_loops` sends a jump into a loop nothing reads the answer of to its exit where the loop's body, as the optimizer has left it, observes nothing — every call in it one `may_drop_call` admits, no closure call, host row, cell or channel — under the rule a claim states, a trap among what it may do.

**Rationale.**

- **A higher-order function does what it is handed.** A fold applies its stepper, so judged alone it applies an unknown and is never droppable, and an unread fold handed a pure stepper ran to the end; what it does is its own work and the stepper's, which only the call knows. That is effect polymorphism, which Koka states in a function's type, its effect row a variable the argument's effect fills ([*Koka: Programming with Row Polymorphic Effect Types*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2061), MSFP 2014); here the summary states it of a function's body, with the values it applies as the variables.
- **The conclusion still crosses alone.** CPS keeps no lattice and no notion of purity: what crosses is the conjunction of the certifier's termination verdict and the summary, now naming the values it waits on, and a call reads it off its own arguments.
- **A name no call can hand refuses.** A value named that is not a parameter of the callee, a capture no copy has handed a function yet or a parameter a pass renamed without the claim, keeps the call, so a pass that copies a function or renames what it is handed without mapping the claim loses precision and never soundness. A claim kept over a body that does more than it did is a dropped effect: an `Io` description's builder is total and does nothing, and once uncurried its call runs the description, as a channel's wake of a waiter is run.
- **A handed function must be droppable whatever it is handed**, since what the callee hands it is the callee's to choose, and nothing at the call says.
- **A loop is judged on its body, its termination carried.** A claim names what a function applies of what it was handed, so a walk that applies a field of it — `/std/Map/internal/Node/first_in`, applying its `Key`'s `hash` — gets none, and once the walk is a loop its calls are jumps no claim is read at; inlined where the field is known, the loop's body is a known call `may_drop_call` admits. LLVM splits a loop the same way: `mustprogress` states termination and is carried from a function to its loops, a mark a pass that cannot keep may drop since only its presence is relied on, and `LoopDeletion` judges the loop's side effects itself.

**Rejected.**

- **A flag on each call, decided where the lowering knows the arguments**: inlining a fold copies the call its body makes to its loop, whose arguments name no stepper, so the flag the copy carries is the fold's, unknown.
- **CPS deriving purity again after inlining**: a second derivation of what a call does, over a second representation, which buys the optimizer no program. Counted at `33a949e1e`, with a temporary reading in `curios-cps` asked beside the claim at `eliminate_dead_calls`, `eliminate_dead_loops` and `sink_computations` on every round — each function's effects read off its own body, a construction, an operation, a jump or a switch observing nothing, a closure call, host row, cell, channel or halt observing, and a known call doing what its callee does with what it hands, bottom-up over the components of the graph of the functions each body names and met with `Function::terminates` — over the 28 programs of `programs/` (`curios wonder --stage wasm`) and the whole test program of `/std` (`curios test` over a copy of `curios-prelude/std` and an empty store): no reader met a call or a loop the claim admits and the reading refuses. The reading admits more — 4 unread calls of `/std/Map/internal/Node/find`, 38 loops whose answer is read, and placement candidates that move nothing — and with it deciding every corpus program's WebAssembly is byte-equal, the optimized test program of `/std` holds as many nodes of each kind, its 320 tests pass, and a corpus compilation retires 1.19% more user-space instructions (median of three). Where the claim is narrower — a field of a handed witness applied, as `/std/Try/bind` applies `Monad/pure`; a host row's wrapper whose closure names the function that calls the wrapper again, read as a recursion; a callee's captures outside the summary's handed set; a handed function that applies what it is handed — the optimizer leaves the same program. A dead loop's body is read in CPS, node by node, and each call in it by its claim, which keeps no lattice of its own.
- **One mapping walking every claim at each substitution**: `shrink` substitutes a value at each reduction, so it would walk every function's claim at each; its census already indexes what names each value, and indexes what each claim names beside it.
- **Dead-call elimination run again after inlining**, before contification: what kept the loop a moved fold defines was its claim, still naming the parameter the move substituted, not the order of the passes.
- **Only what a function applies of its parameters**: the loops `/std` writes inside a fold capture its stepper rather than take it, so the loop a call keeps would stay unknown.
