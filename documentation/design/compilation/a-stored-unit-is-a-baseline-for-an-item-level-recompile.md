# A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile

**Decision.** A unit the store holds is a baseline, not a hit or a miss. `Cache::get` returning a unit whose record agrees is the empty case: reuse everything. When the record disagrees, the slot still holds a unit compiled from an earlier text of the same sources, and the fold compiles the unit over it (`Cache::baseline`, `curios-pipeline/src/recompile.rs`), for a question and for a build alike. Each stage keeps what its own record says stands, and redoes the rest:

- **The lowering** keeps each declaration whose text and whose resolved names stand ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)), and lowers the others. A declaration lowered again is elaborated again: nothing compares two lowerings.
- **The elaborator** finds what the edit reached from the edit, through its traces read backwards (`curios_elab::Readers`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/demand.rs`), and decides each declaration the edit reached once every declaration it read is decided: elaborated again where it must be or a part it read came out otherwise, kept otherwise. A declaration the edit cannot have reached is kept and asked nothing. A kept declaration is read where the baseline holds it, as a unit in scope is read, and its item is the baseline's own. A declaration to be elaborated again is first looked for among the records the store holds of elaborations of what it was lowered to (`curios_elab::Candidating`, `Demand::adopted`): one whose every question is answered now as it was, and every part of which it read digests as it did, each of a declaration decided or of another unit, is taken whole, its items read out of the store, and the declaration is elaborated otherwise.
- **The kernel** is handed the module with the baseline whole, adopts the baseline's verdict on each declaration that stands by its own record, and judges the rest ([Cached verdicts](../soundness/cached-verdicts.md)).
- **Erasure** keeps the erased form of a declaration the elaborator kept where every question its erasure put is answered as it was, asking only the items its traces read backwards say an edit could reach and keeping every other unasked, and erases the rest (`curios_elab::ErasedBaseline`, `curios-elab/src/into_anf/over.rs`): an item's erased form is its own ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#an-erased-form-is-its-items)). An item to be erased is first looked for among the records the store holds of erasures of the item it is (`curios_elab::ErasedUnit::complete`): one every part and binding of which it read digests as it did is taken, its erased form read out of the store.

A question is what a declaration read through one of its context's doors: a name's type with its scheme, what the name unfolds to, its kind, whether it is total, the vector it filed, a registry entry, the witness standing at a key, whether one is declared there, whether the unit's lemmas are on offer to a proof the elaborator writes, and, of erasure, how a name is bound in the erased form of the item that declares it, and that form whole where the item asking was evaluated over it ([A top-level value is computed where its unit is compiled](a-top-level-value-is-computed-where-its-unit-is-compiled.md)). Each door notes its question where it is asked, answered or not, once for each attempt and in the order first asked (`curios-elab/src/context/traced.rs`); what a declaration asks of a name it declares is not noted. A trace is its stage's own and is stored in the unit's baseline half ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-stored-in-parts-by-who-reads-each)).

A question about a part of a declaration — its signature, its body, an entry it keys — is answered as it was where that declaration was itself kept, and for one elaborated again where the part came out equal as a unit stores it, each binder under the name it was written with and no position read (`curios-elab/src/same.rs`). What a proof unfolds to is nothing, so that part of one moves with its mark and never with its body ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). A question the unit answers as a whole carries its answer, and is asked of the unit again: the witness found at a key, whether one is declared there, whether lemmas were on offer. A name neither the unit nor its baseline declares is another unit's or no one's. What was read of it stands where the unit that holds it is the one the baseline was compiled after, which a cache says of each unit in scope as it offers the baseline (`curios_pipeline::After`); of any other, each stage recorded the digest of the part it read where its compile ended (`curios_elab::Foreign`, and the certifier's `curios_core::Abroad`), and the part stands where it digests now to what was recorded. A part is digested as it is compared within a unit (`curios-elab/src/same.rs`), consed alone and archived, so two digest alike exactly where they are one as a unit stores them. Nothing is recorded of the compiler's own units, which no baseline is compiled after another of.

What the elaborator's walk finds the edit reached by is its seeds: each declaration it cannot keep by its trace — lowered again, new to the unit, a witness not alone at its key, or one reaching a refusal (`Demand::recorded`) — and each declaration that read an answer that moved: a name the unit gained or lost, read where it was absent or present; a part of a unit in scope whose digest moved; a key at which the unit declares other witnesses than its baseline did, or one a moved unit in scope now answers otherwise. A declaration that read a seed is marked, and one that read a marked one, through the declarations each name was read by; a marked declaration counts the marked declarations it read that are undecided, and is decided when the count reaches nothing, each part of a declaration elaborated again compared with the baseline's to tell its readers whether what they read moved. A declaration elaborated again that reads something new asks for it, as any attempt does. Every declaration no seed reaches is kept before any is elaborated after the offer of the unit's lemmas, so a read no question records — a lemma a proof applies, a method a derivation writes — finds it standing. A unit's readers are read off its traces the first time a recompile over it asks, kept with it, and handed to the unit compiled over it with the edges of each declaration elaborated again or taken away replaced ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-handed-over-never-copied)).

Whether the unit's lemmas are on offer is answered by the side of the offer a declaration was settled on, which a unit stores (`Traces::before`). Where the edit reaches none of the declarations settled before the offer, the prover's vocabulary reads what it read, and each declaration stands on its side. Where it reaches one, the vocabulary is asked for as a whole compile asks for it, each declaration it comes to asked by its trace, and one settled before the offer by the baseline and no longer is elaborated again. A declaration is asked by its trace there, and where a marked one is asked for ahead of what it waits on: its questions are asked again in the order they were asked, each after the declaration it is about has been asked for, and the first answered another way ends the asking, the declaration then elaborated as any other.

The archived `/std` is the baseline of the package named `std`, offered through the same `Cache` seam. A unit compiled over a baseline is filed as any other, since it is stored as the index a whole compile of the same text stores, naming the same objects; a question's is filed where the disk holds every text its fold read ([A command compiles only as far as its answer needs, and a project keeps what it compiled](../tools/a-command-compiles-only-as-far-as-its-answer-needs-and-a-project-keeps-what-it-compiled.md)).

**Rationale.**

- **An editor asks about a file on every edit**, and a whole-unit answer costs a whole compile of the unit — for the standard library, every declaration it holds, per keystroke. Every mechanism existed for whole units: a predecessor is read where it stands and never re-checked, the kernel passes over a declaration its environment holds, a stored item is position-free, and a `Unit` holds both the lowered and the elaborated module.
- **A trace holds every read a result can follow, and a name does not.** Every fact a declaration's elaboration consumes from outside itself arrives through a door, so the trace of the attempt that stood is what the result is a function of. The names an elaborated item reaches hold less and more. Less, since what was read and left no name — a body unfolded while a candidate was tried, a key at which no witness stands, a lemma offered and not applied — is in no term. More, since a name says nothing of which part was read: a declaration that names another for its type alone stands when the other's body is respelled, and a closure over names is transitive because nothing says where a declaration elaborated again came out the same. Respelling `/std/Bool/not`'s body elaborates again 182 of the library's 2,723 names, the declarations that unfolded it and none behind them: `curios-pipeline`'s `std_recompile_census`, counted at `23c593615`.
- **Found from the edit, decided after what it read.** Asking every kept declaration's trace costs a question for everything each read, so what an edit costs follows the unit ([A compile costs what it reads](a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md)); read backwards, the traces say who read each part, so the walk visits what the edit reached and no other. A marked declaration is decided once every marked declaration it read is, so whether a part it read moved is known for each, and one none of whose parts moved reads what it read, which is all a declaration is a function of ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). Counting, as Zig's incremental compiler counts a unit's outdated dependencies, gives that order from the traces of each compile, and nothing is stored to say it.
- **What no seed reaches is kept first.** Some reads record no question, since they read what a whole compile has settled before they can be made: a proof the elaborator writes applies the lemmas on offer, every one elaborated before the offer, and a derivation applies the method it writes through. Over a baseline, what such a read finds is kept or decided ahead of it only where every declaration no seed reaches is kept before any declaration is elaborated after the offer.
- **The side of the offer is stored, not asked.** Whether lemmas were on offer is a question every declaration that proves something asks, so asking it of each is asking every declaration; the declarations settled before the offer are the vocabulary's closure, which moves only where the edit reaches one of them.
- **Asked again as it was asked.** A read of a declaration's part waited for the declaration, so asking a trace again asks for each declaration its elaboration waited for, in the order it did, and a cycle the walk meets that way is one a whole compile meets. A question that waited for nothing is asked of the walk as it stands, and asks for no declaration: whether a witness is declared at a key, and whether lemmas are on offer, which is noted ahead of the lemma and is the whole of the question where none are. A declaration settled on the other side of the offer from its trace is elaborated again, so what the prover's vocabulary reads is decided by the walk and by no graph ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)).
- **A part is compared as it is stored.** A binder's name moves no judgment and is in what a unit stores, the declaration's own and a dependent's that was handed its signature; compared up to its binders' names, a renamed parameter would leave its dependents kept under the old ones, and the unit would not be the one a whole compile stores. A declaration elaborated again keeps the item it was elaborated to, whose positions are the new text's, and its readers stand by comparison: the baseline's item is taken for a declaration that was not elaborated at all.
- **A kept witness is asked what registering would have asked.** A witness registers under its key as its signature elaborates, and the second at one key is refused; a kept one registers nothing, so the walk keeps it only where it is the first the unit declares at its key and none stands there.
- **Evidence holds the claim**: `curios-pipeline/src/tests/incremental_tests.rs` compiles each fixture whole and over a baseline and requires the elaborated modules to agree structurally, item by item and on every registry table and the tests (`assert_modules_agree`), and the two units to be stored as the same index, naming the same objects, their traces with them (`assert_stored_alike`), which is what lets one be filed in the other's place; it holds which declarations are elaborated again over a body edit that leaves a signature standing, one that reaches a declaration that unfolded it, a renamed parameter, and a witness declared where a declaration was told none stood, and an edit that closes a cycle through a declaration whose baseline trace read nothing of the others; `curios-elab`'s `traces::tests` hold the readers to the edges each trace adds and a declaration made again to the edges it replaces. `curios-pipeline`'s `std_recompile_reproduction` holds the standard library to the same bytes for a leaf and a hub, `std_vocabulary_reach_reproduction` for a declaration the prover's vocabulary comes to read, and `std_replay_census` elaborates each of the library's declarations again alone and reports the ones that come out another way, ask otherwise, or take another declaration with them. The predicate is equality: a declaration is lowered and elaborated in identities and a state of its own, so one elaborated over a baseline is the one a whole compile elaborates, down to the spelling of a solved implicit and of a proof the elaborator wrote ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)).
- **Found by what it was made from.** An edit undone, a branch checked out again and a bisection bring back texts the store compiled before, and a declaration lowered as it was then, reading what it read then, elaborates to what it elaborated to then, and an item erases to what it erased to ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). So a filing completes each stage's trace of each declaration into its record — what it was made from, the digest of each part it read, what it made — and names it by what it was made from (`curios_elab::TraceRecord`), and a store keeps every record filed for an input until no slot has named it for five days: "once we are storing the complete result it makes sense to record many constructive traces per key" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), section 4.2.3). What a record read is held by digest, since the declaration it read is the one the walk holds now, not the one it read then. The record is the trace the baseline keeps a declaration by, completed, so one record of a declaration is stored for each stage, and the index names it as it names the trace. A part is digested as it is compared, under no position and no provenance, so a declaration kept carries the record its baseline filed, and the index stays the one a whole compile files. A record's reads are checked against what is decided and never by asking for what they read: a record of another text may read a declaration that now reads the one being decided, and asking for it would close a cycle no whole compile meets.
- **No trace is part of the trusted base.** Which of the baseline's verdicts stand is the certifier's decision, from the reads its own walk filed, and the elaborator's trace decides only what is elaborated and erased again: a declaration it wrongly keeps is judged again where what the kernel read for it has moved, and is stored as the baseline elaborated it, and a declaration taken from a record holds items other than the baseline's, which the kernel judges or finds a verdict of among its own records, by its own digests ([Cached verdicts](../soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). What a declaration is judged under does not follow what else the module holds: the elaborator, the kernel and erasure each spend a budget restored for each judgment of it ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). A resource verdict still follows the baseline in one way, since an adopted verdict is the one its baseline reached under whatever budget that was, where a whole compile judges it under its own; the differential test excludes resource verdicts and says so.

**Rejected.**

- **The reverse closure of what was lowered again, over the names an item reaches**: transitive, since nothing says where a declaration came out the same, and blind to a read that left no name, which it covers only by redoing everything behind an edit.
- **A record and a rule for each stage**, the lowering's by text and interfaces, the elaborator's by its reads, erasure's by a closure: one rule kept in agreement by being read three times.
- **A closure or a trace the fold hands the certifier**: the trusted base would rest on a record the certifier did not make. Its reads are no substitute for the elaborator's either: they are what its judgment of the finished terms read.
- **Asking for each declaration a record read**, as a trace is asked again: a record filed for another text may read a declaration that reads the one being decided now, a cycle no whole compile has.
- **A candidate beside each stage's trace**, an object of its own holding a copy of the trace: two records of one facet, the trace stored twice, and a map beside the traces kept only to keep the second named.
- **A lowering found by what it was made from**: an edit undone lowers the edited file's declarations again and nothing else, the lowering keeping every other by its text and the names it resolved, so a record of its lookups completed as the other stages' are would save a lowering only where a checkout moves many files.
- **Completed records in the prelude's image**: a compile looks for records among its own address's packs, where the image's objects are not, and the image is part of every executable; the first filing over the image completes the records it carries.
- **Comparing two lowerings to keep an elaboration.** An edit that leaves a declaration's meaning alone costs that declaration's elaboration, once, and what it comes to is compared by whatever read it.
- **Taking the baseline's item where a declaration elaborated again came out equal**: its positions are an earlier text's.
- **Keeping a declaration no seed reaches only once the walk comes to it in the order it is written**: a declaration elaborated again ahead of it, reading it through a read that records no question, finds it missing and elaborates otherwise than a whole compile.
- **Asking each declaration whether lemmas were on offer**, where the edit reached none of the vocabulary: a question for every declaration that proves something, for an answer that cannot have moved.
- **Asking for the declaration of every question a trace holds.** A question that waited for nothing would then wait for a declaration, and one being asked for further up closes a cycle no whole compile has.
- **Noting a lemma's signature ahead of whether lemmas were on offer**: a declaration that comes into what the prover's vocabulary reads would ask for the vocabulary it is read by.
- **A build compiling a moved unit whole**: the unit is stored as the same index either way, and a build then pays for every declaration where a question pays for what moved.
- **Suffix re-elaboration from the first change**, Lean's per-command model: the emitted order is a Kahn sort over the unit, so an early edit re-elaborates most of the library.
- **Identifying the standard library by its tree's directory**, which makes the answer depend on the binary's checkout; **a manifest key or root kind for it**, forgeable, and a tier no mount needs; **recognizing the prelude in `curios-wonder` or in `curios-package`'s placement**, which cannot reach the archive.
- **Deriving the record from the spans in the image**: inputs are recorded at the seam they pass through, never believed from a structure that happens to hold them.
- **Per-declaration store slots**, redundant beside the unit slot and its traces; **the recompile as the server's private state**, a third spelling of the fold that `test`, `document` and dependents would not share.
- **Withholding a unit compiled over a baseline from the store**: every question after a saved edit recompiles what moved until a build files the unit, and a question never files the package `std`. What is filed is decided by the one `Cache` implementation that answers `baseline`, on the disk alone.
- **A digest-keyed parse memo**: keyed by path and replaced on change, the table is bounded by the file count.
- **Reading an elaboration's refusals as whether it kept every item**, two different questions: a run whose only fault is an unparsed item refuses nothing and still drops that item's dependents.
