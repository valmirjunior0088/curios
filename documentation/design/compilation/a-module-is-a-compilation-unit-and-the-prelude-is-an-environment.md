# A module is a compilation unit, and the prelude is an environment

**Decision.** A `Module` holds the items its own compilation produced. What precedes it reaches each stage as an *environment* the stage reads, never as items spliced into the front of the module — `Globals` at the certifier, `Established` at elaboration, `Erasures` at erasure, `Scoped` at the lowerer — so no stage carries an index, a delimiter or a caller contract about where one region of a module ends and the next begins. A compilation is a set of units folded over a dependency order, each stage taking its predecessors borrowed (`curios-unit`'s `Predecessors`) rather than one merged map, and an archive is a unit's serialized form, one per root, as a `.vo`, an `.olean` and a `.hi` are. `/sys` and `/std` are two units in that order, first in every product's scope. `compile_entrypoint` cannot tell which unit is `/std`: `curios-pipeline`'s `standard` module alone names the fixed prelude, withholding the archived `/std` for a package named `std` that is the fold's first unit. Every other unit arrives from a manifest that declared it; no flag mounts one. A declaration's root — what privilege and coherence are decided by — is the mount whose prefix owns its name, found by longest match, never a value carried beside the name, and two units claiming one prefix are refused. The program asked for is the last unit of the fold: its declarations are compiled as the unit they are, handed back by the cache where nothing they were compiled from moved and compiled over the baseline it offers where something did, and the term that closes the program is no part of that unit, so it is elaborated, judged and erased over it on every compile (`enter`, `curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`).

**Rationale.**

- **Every stage has the environment anyway**: erasure and elaboration read their predecessors where they stand, erasure beside what their items erased to, and the certifier defines their items into a kernel. Splicing makes each stage also depend on those items sitting at the front of the module — a claim each could state and none check — which takes a delimiter index, a `checked_from` offset, provenance queries recovering what a merged registry discards, and four entry points where there is one judgment. Coq's `Require`, GHC's `.hi` interfaces and Lean's replay from the environment its imports provide do the same.
- **A root is a prefix, not a stamp**, which is what makes the orphan rule work between two ordinary units: were every ordinary root one enum variant, two packages would compare equal exactly where the rule is needed. No stage derives a root from a name's spelling.
- **A scope with no declaration behind it is one nothing can state the dependencies of**, which is why only a manifest mounts a unit.
- **A program's own declarations are the ones its author edits most.** Compiled as a unit they are kept between commands as any unit's are, so a question about a program redoes the declarations an edit reached and the term that closes it. `programs/walk_mirror_indexed.crs`, as the one executable of a package beside it, asked about again with nothing edited elaborates 0.06 M allocations, judges 0.05 M and erases 0.08 M, all of it the final term and the link; with one function's body respelled it elaborates 0.08 M, judges 0.07 M and erases 0.08 M (`curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics` asked of the file three times, the third after `text_of`'s body is edited, each stream folded by `curios profile`; the rows `elaborate_and_zonk` or `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over`, `recheck_module` and `erase_unit`; counted at `23c593615`).
- **The final term rides in the walk of the declarations where they are compiled**, so a program holding a refused declaration reports every refusal with what its final term says, in one batch. Where the unit is restored nothing of it is elaborated, and the term is elaborated alone, over the units in scope and the one restored.

**Rejected.**

- **A root as an index into the compilation's root list**, rustc's `cnum_map` reinvented: an identity meaningful only in the compilation that assigned it, stamped into an artifact another reads. `curios_core::validate_stored_identities` refuses any such identity at every seam a unit is written, and what a cached one may be believed on is [Cached verdicts](../soundness/cached-verdicts.md)'s.
- **Resolving a mount collision by precedence**: Swift did, and then needed `::` module selectors to say what had become unsayable.
- **Checking the positional claim instead of removing it**, sound and the wrong direction, since a wart that stops hurting stops getting removed; **carrying the boundary on `Module`**, which makes it the elaborator's claim — the kernel raises its own binder counter past each local as it enters the context rather than believe a floor a caller computes.
- **The entry left a program beside the units**: nothing of its declarations is kept, and each question lowers, elaborates, judges and erases every one.
- **The final term kept as one more declaration.** Each stage keeps a declaration by its name, and the term has none; and it is judged at one of three types by what its program is compiled as, a program, a test program or a proof. What keeping it would save is the term's own cost.
