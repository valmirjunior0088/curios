# A compile costs what it reads

**Decision.** What a compilation does follows what its declarations read, at every stage it runs. An edit redoes, stage by stage, the declarations that read what it changed and no other: the minimality of a build, with early cutoff, at the grain of a declaration. What it costs is what it reached, and nothing it could not have reached costs it anything. And a compile pays for the declarations of its scope it reads, not for the scope, which is the same rule read from the other side, a scope being an input like any other. A unit's boundary is no limit of the rule, and neither is the program asked for.

- **A declaration has a facet for each stage, and a file has a scan.** A declaration's lowered form; what it elaborates to, with the registry entries, vectors and totality it publishes and, where it is a witness, its key; the certifier's verdict on it; and its erased form. A file's scan is what its items declare, each with where it is written ([A file is parsed where a declaration of it is lowered](a-file-is-parsed-where-a-declaration-of-it-is-lowered.md)). Each is written once, and another declaration reads it through its facets alone ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)).
- **A facet is held, never copied.** A unit is an index of its declarations' facets and is handed over as that index, so what a recompile keeps is the baseline's own, and what a session keeps, a store restores and a fold hands on is one unit ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-handed-over-never-copied)).
- **A recompile makes a version of its baseline.** Every table a unit holds by declaration — its lowered items and the lowering's tables, its elaborated items and where it holds each name, its traces, its erased forms, its certification, and every index read off them — is a persistent map: a version shares every entry its baseline holds, replacing one costs a path, and two versions are compared by what they do not share, which is how the certifier finds the items it must judge and the store the facets it must write ([`curios-utilities`' README](../../../curios-utilities/README.md#a-persistent-maps-shape-is-a-function-of-the-keys-it-holds), [`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-place-is-a-key-in-hand-and-a-rank-on-disk)).
- **A reader records what it read, in the order it read it, found apart from absent.** The lowering records its lookups ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)), the elaborator and erasure the questions they put through a context's doors ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)), and the certifier what its own walk read ([Cached verdicts](../soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). A record is its reader's own and is stored with the facet it made.
- **A facet is reused where what it was made from stands**: its own input unchanged, an item's text for a lowered form, the lowered form for an elaboration, the elaborated declaration for a verdict and for an erased form, and every read of its record answered as it was — known without asking where nothing it read can have moved, and asked again in the order it was made where something may have. A facet made again and found the same dirties nothing.
- **A recompile costs what its edit reached.** What an edit reaches is its seeds — the declarations whose text moved, the names whose binding moved, the parts of a unit in scope that moved, the answers a unit gives whole that moved — and, from each facet made again and found different in a part, the readers of that part. Each stage finds them from the edit, through its own record read backwards, and decides a declaration after everything it read: a declaration the edit cannot have reached is neither redone, asked, decoded, copied, sorted, seeded nor stored. Six costs are bounded by something else, and nothing beyond them: a file's text is read and compared, and digested with where each declaration of it lies where its unit is filed, a file being the grain of reading; an edited file is parsed whole, a file being the grain of parsing; a declaration that reads, transitively, what the edit touched is marked, an integer and a step for each record read backwards, with nothing asked of it; a process inverts a record once, the first time a recompile asks who read what; a unit's index holds an entry for each declaration, which a filing writes and a fresh process reads; and a filing keeps its unit's packs geometric, merging the smallest into one where a new pack breaks the progression of their sizes, so a declaration's stored form is written again a logarithmic number of times over its life.
- **The rule does not ask which unit an answer is in.** A read of another unit is recorded with the digest of the part read, a unit is addressed by who it is and by no unit before it, and one after an edited dependency is compiled over its own baseline ([Cached verdicts](../soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). The program asked for is a unit as the ones before it are ([A module is a compilation unit, and the prelude is an environment](a-module-is-a-compilation-unit-and-the-prelude-is-an-environment.md)), and what a top-level value computes is its item's erased form, computed where its unit is compiled ([A top-level value is computed where its unit is compiled](a-top-level-value-is-computed-where-its-unit-is-compiled.md)).
- **A scope and a baseline are read where they stand, at every stage.** Elaboration's scope, the kernel's environment and erasure's view each read a unit's declarations by the places it keeps, a report spells a name of a unit in scope by that unit's own paths, made where the report is, and a stored unit is restored in parts, by who reads each ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-stored-in-parts-by-who-reads-each)): nothing of a scope or a baseline is copied, replayed, validated or keyed again.

**Rationale.**

- **The goal has a definition.** "A build system is minimal if it executes tasks at most once per build and only if they transitively depend on inputs that changed since the previous build", and under early cutoff, "When it executes a task and the result is unchanged from the previous build, it is unnecessary to execute the dependent tasks" (Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones, [Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), definition 2.1 and section 2.3). A record of reads decides nothing while anything but reads reaches a declaration, which is what [A declaration is a function of what it reads](a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md) settles first.
- **A trace of what was read decides what is rebuilt, asked again in the order it was read.** A verifying trace is "to record the values/hashes of dependencies used last time, and if something has changed, the key is dirty and must be rebuilt" (the same paper, section 4.2.2), and of rustc's try-mark-green, "it is important that we visit each node in `reads(Q)` in same order as they occurred in the original compilation" ([Incremental compilation](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/queries/incremental-compilation.html)). Skyframe and salsa cut off the same way: a node rebuilt to its old value resurrects what it had invalidated ([Skyframe](https://bazel.build/reference/skyframe)), and "even though the inputs changed, the output didn't" ([The red-green algorithm](https://salsa-rs.github.io/salsa/reference/algorithm.html)).
- **A lookup is a read whatever it finds.** Kotlin's incremental compiler keeps "the names (not full declarations) that were looked up during its compilation" ([The Dark Secrets of Fast Compilation for Kotlin](https://blog.jetbrains.com/kotlin/2020/09/the-dark-secrets-of-fast-compilation-for-kotlin/)), and Swift's dependency sets "are tracked by instrumenting lookups in the compiler" ([Dependency analysis](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift/blob/main/docs/DependencyAnalysis.md)). The orphan rule and a witness's key spelled before elaboration are what let a goal's read be one key, found or absent, where GHC "visits the interface file of every orphan module below the module being compiled" ([Separate compilation](https://ghc.gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/doc/users_guide/separate_compilation.html)).
- **Every stage is keyed, not one.** Sixty's build library "keeps track of what dependencies a task used when it was executed (much like Shake) in a table, i.e. what keys it fetched and what the values were", over a key for each parsed module, each resolved module and each definition's type ([Query-based compiler architectures](https://ollef.github.io/blog/posts/query-based-compilers.html)).
- **A read of another unit carries what it read.** GHC's `Usage` records, for a module of the same package, the "Entities we depend on, sorted by occurrence name and fingerprinted", and "if we don't use anything from X it won't appear in our Usage" ([`GHC.Unit.Module.Deps`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.10.1/docs/src/GHC.Unit.Module.Deps.html)). And what is known not to have changed is not asked: of salsa's durability, "we can very easily determine that the tracked function result is still valid, avoiding the need to traverse the input edges one by one" ([The red-green algorithm](https://salsa-rs.github.io/salsa/reference/algorithm.html)); a unit that contains what it contained is such an input, and every read of it stands with it.
- **What an edit did not reach is not asked.** The goal an editor's checker is held to is "execution times proportional to the size of a change to a project, rather than to the project size" (Zwaan, van Antwerpen and Visser, [Incremental Type-Checking for Free](https://doi.org/10.1145/3563303), OOPSLA 2022). A record read backwards says who read a part, so what an edit could reach is found from the edit; and a reader decided after everything it read reads nothing stale, since "the reads that rerun have to do so in control order, otherwise they could use stale information" (Anderson, Blelloch, Baweja and Acar, [Efficient Parallel Self-Adjusting Computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06712), SPAA 2021). Zig's incremental compiler keeps that order by counting, a potentially outdated unit holding "the number of PO dependencies of this AnalUnit" and becoming "a candidate for re-analysis" once that value "drops to 0" ([`Zcu.zig`](https://github.com/ziglang/zig/blob/master/src/Zcu.zig)): the order is computed from the records each compile, and nothing is stored to say it.
- **A declaration is the grain, in a proof assistant too.** iCoq "tracks fine-grained dependencies between Coq definitions, propositions, and proofs, and only checks those proofs affected by changes between two revisions" ([Çelik, Palmskog and Gligoric](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~gligoric/papers/abstracts/CelikETAL17iCoq.txt), ASE 2017), and Leino and Wüstholz cache a verifier's results per procedure ([Fine-grained Caching of Verification Results](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/fine-grained-caching-verification-results/), CAV 2015). The peers split a signature from a body by declaring the body opaque, Rocq's `.vos` being "similar to `foo.vo` except that all opaque proofs are skipped in the compilation process" ([Compiled interfaces](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/V9.0.0/refman/practical-tools/coq-commands.html)); here a proof's body is opaque by its type, and which other bodies a declaration unfolded is recorded, never declared.
- **A version shares what it kept.** A unit is already an index of its declarations' facets handed over as that index, and Isabelle's document model holds versions the same way, "Document-content is persistent by default" and "content of common sub-structures of related document versions is shared to some extent" (Wenzel and Wolff, [Isabelle/PIDE as Platform for Educational Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.4835)); `imbl`'s `OrdMap::diff` "will avoid visiting nodes which are shared between the two sets"; an aggregate kept by its deltas is what incremental view maintenance makes of a query ([DBSP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16684)).
- **Measured, a recompile costs what its edit reached.** Counted in allocations at `cdb0c246a`, a filing at `5e9984bfc`, with a release compiler built with `--features profile`. A one-shot run is `curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics <copy>/<file>` over a copy of `curios-std/src` holding no `.curios`, so the archived unit is the baseline, folded by `curios profile`; a session is `curios-wonder`'s protocol ([Measuring a question's latency](../../../curios-wonder/README.md#measuring-a-questions-latency)) over such a copy, initialized with no `workspaceFolders` and opened on `Nat.crs` with the leaf on disk, then given two unsaved keystrokes, each appending a declaration, the second counted. The leaf appends `pub let _census_probe(a: Nat) -> Nat = a;` to `Nat.crs`; the hub respells `xor(b, true)` as `xor(true, b)` in `/std/Bool/not`. A leaf costs 0.83 M, the declaration it adds asking four questions and no other declaration asked anything; filing it, the address's first, writes every object, 2.11 M, a pack of 54.6 MB and a slot of 4.78 MB, and over a store holding the unit 0.12 M, a pack of 44 KB. A hub costs 120 M: 87.1 M its 188 declarations elaborated again, 26.7 M the 140 judged, 4.5 M erasure. A keystroke is one compile of 45 K; over a package of modules of forty-eight declarations each, a sum over `Nat`, a value read off it and a proof by computation, opened on its first module, it counts 40 K, 41 K and 42 K at one, two and four times as many modules. An edit undone elaborates, erases and judges nothing (`curios-pipeline`'s `std_undo_reproduction`).
- **A store is kept geometric.** A filing that writes only what it made leaves a unit's objects in as many packs as it was filed times, and a restore probes every one; merging the smallest packs wherever their sizes stop doubling keeps a logarithmic number of them and writes each object a logarithmic number of times, as `git repack --geometric` keeps a repository pushed to at every change, choosing "the smallest set of packfiles such that as many of the larger packfiles may be left intact" ([Highlights from Git 2.33](https://github.blog/open-source/git/highlights-from-git-2-33/)). It is size-tiered compaction, whose write amplification is the lower of the two an LSM tree has ([Wei and others, EDBT 2025](https://cs-people.bu.edu/mathan/publications/edbt25-wei.pdf)), paid where the filing is rather than left to a collection that runs once a day, since a day of saves is hundreds of packs.
- **A scope is read as it is asked for.** rustc's metadata is "A list of lazily-decoded values, with the added capability of random access" ([`rustc_metadata::rmeta`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_metadata/rmeta/mod.rs.html)), and rkyv, which the store is written with, reads an archive with "just a pointer offset and a cast" ([Zero-copy deserialization](https://rkyv.org/zero-copy-deserialization.html)).

**Rejected.**

- **salsa.** At 0.28.5, which describes itself as experimental, with no 1.0; its database model would own the term representation, and its invalidation would sit beside the store's trust argument rather than under it. The graph here has thousands of nodes and obligations — budgets, deterministic identities, charged caches — that a small component states directly.
- **A digest compared inside a process.** Where a baseline is in hand it holds the facet itself, and a term's comparison ends at a pointer or a cached hash; a digest is what a record of another unit holds.
- **A unit addressed by the closure of what it depends on.** The closure needs an order no fold gives twice, and a unit after an edited dependency is then compiled whole.
- **Asking every record whole on every recompile**: what a recompile keeps then costs a question for everything each kept declaration read, so what an edit costs follows the unit it is written in, where the minimality above bounds only what it redoes.
- **A unit assembled from every declaration, kept or made**, and a pass of word-sized comparisons over the unit as its end state, which is free at `/std`'s size and a walk of the unit all the same.
- **Reuse below a declaration.** A declaration made again is the one a whole compile makes, and reusing part of an earlier elaboration under a changed read is exact only up to the first read answered otherwise, which needs the elaborator's state at that read, as Lean 4 keeps a snapshot after a definition's header ([`Lean.Elab.DefView`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Elab/DefView.html)).
- **A stored order of declarations**, Excel's calc chain. A kept declaration's reads are settled before it is examined, in the order its record gives, so only a declaration made again can meet one unfinished, and a whole compile has no baseline to learn an order from.
