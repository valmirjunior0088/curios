# A diagnostic spells what its reader can write

**Decision.** A term a report shows its reader — a goal's answer and scope, a mismatch's two sides, a candidate fit — is spelled so that, written where the reader stands, it elaborates back to the term shown. The term is translated back to the surface tree where the reader stands and printed by the printer that formats source (`curios-surface`'s `from_core`): the inverse of lowering and elaboration, so what is shown is a form the grammar produces — a match as its patterns, a projection by its field's label, a literal's hidden entries under their marks. A name takes the shortest spelling resolution would take back to it there: a declaration of the reader's module by its label, a path into a child module, an import in scope as it was written, else the shortest absolute path whose audience includes the reader — the facade a re-export offers, never the internal root. The absolute paths a unit gives a name are listed once for the unit, where a report first asks over it, from the direct interface and export view it stores, and stored by none (`curios_module::PreparedText::spellings`); a report reads the lists of the units in scope in order, and which paths the reader's unit may name, and which it reaches relatively, is decided where a name is spelled (`curios-surface`'s `spell`), which reverses `curios-module`'s resolver and is held to it: over the library, every spelling it gives resolves to its declaration from where the reader stands, and no shorter one does.

- **What else a report spells a name of its scope by is each unit's own too, its lexicon** (`curios_core::Lexicon`): the globals it declares, which a name is shortened against where no reader stands, its nominal families' parameter marks, its structures' field marks and labels, and its constructors' payload marks. A unit's is made once, of the declarations it is about and no other, and kept with the unit (`curios_unit::Unit::lexicon`); a report makes its own unit's and reads the others in order, so it builds no table over its scope and reads no declaration of it that it does not print.
- **A translation is whole or it is none.** Where a term holds a form no program spells where its reader stands — a name the reader reaches by none, a witness resolution would not put back, a hidden field with no label read off a value, a string past the length a report prints one whole — or is past the Core print's bound ([A printer states each fact once, where it is bound](a-printer-states-each-fact-once-where-it-is-bound.md)), the report shows the whole term as the compiler holds it, through Core's own printer, which writes a name in full, and says so in a line: a different program is worse than an unpasteable one, and a print that is half source is read as source. A side a report reduces for display takes no unfolding of a call that reaches an operation of `/sys` its reader writes by no name, `/std`'s dispatch of `Flt/rounded/add` on its rounding among them: the call stays as it was written, which reads back, and every other reduction is taken. The driver hands the elaborator what a unit's reader writes where it makes the context (`curios_elab::Context::set_reader`), since the sides are reduced as the report is built and the reading a report is spelled by is made after. `wonder --stage` dumps and the certifier's refusals keep the faithful spelling: they are read about the compiler. `curios-pipeline`'s paste-back tests compile a program, write each goal's reported answer over the goal and compile again, over each form found to break it and over every public declaration of `/std`; its spelling tests hold each name's spelling to the resolver, from four places a reader stands; and its sweep writes beside every `let` of `/std` the type and the body a report prints for it, with a proof by `Eq/refl()` that what they declare is the `let`, and puts the library to both checkers.
- **What a report leaves out for elaboration to fill is settled by the procedure that fills it**, standing where the reader stands, before the refusal leaves the elaborator (`curios-elab`'s `written`). The pass walks each term a report shows, entering its binders and an arm's refinements as elaboration does, and puts each witness to resolution there: one resolution answers with a dictionary that converts with it becomes the open slot elaboration would mint for it, and every other stays. A hidden member whose type is a proposition — a call's `@` argument, a constructor's, a literal's hidden field — is put there to what fills an omitted one, the reduct's one inhabitant and then the procedure that proves a bound from the facts in scope, with the declaration read at levels of its own, as the name the reader writes is: one it proves becomes the hole `@_` lowers to, a held place. The proof the term holds is that procedure's, written over the bound's reduct with the definitions it opened, which need not read back, and any proof of a proposition is as good as another. A concept method projected off a dictionary is written as the program calls it: as its operator, where the field has one, resolution restores the dictionary and every proof the operator drops is proved again there by the procedure that proves a bound from the facts in scope, and otherwise as its call by its path over the concept's parameters and the dictionary, an open slot where resolution restores it and written where it does not. An operation that holds the proof of its bound, a division's of its divisor or an index's of its range, is put to the same procedure over the bound its row states: a proof it proves again becomes a held place, and the operation is written by its operator or its bare call, as a program whose bound discharges writes it. Any other proof is stated, a call taking it after its operands under `@` and an operator, which has no place for one, giving way to its function's call, `Nat/div(n, d, @proof)`; a proof elaboration never found is a place the program left to it, and is written as one. A refusal holds the frames it was raised under until it is settled, where it is final and its declaration not yet undone, and from then holds the witness binders it was raised under and no frame, so no elaboration state outlives it; a goal is settled where its report is shaped, on the frames it was born under, and a refusal a sandbox discards is never settled. A candidate a refusal shows for a metavariable, with the check that refused it, is written on the frames the metavariable was born under: the argument left out there is where its reader would write it, and a proof a guard of the refusal's own arm would fill again is no proof there. The translation decides nothing about resolution: it leaves out an open slot and a held place, prints a witness binder of a function type unnamed, and declines a witness still standing, a global one or a binder's reference, the reader's own among them. A report made where no elaborator stands, an erasure refusal, settles nothing, and declines where a witness is shown.
- **A refusal holds what it is about as identities and stands at a place.** A declaration it names is a `Global` and a site a typed value, spelled where the report is read as any name is; a `Global` has no `Display`, and what is read about the compiler asks for the faithful spelling by name. Its place is the narrowest written term it is about, and where it is about none, the label of the declaration it arose in, which the lowering keeps for every declaration a name was written for (`curios_core::Anchors`); the line naming that declaration spells it as any name. A refusal about two declarations holds the second as a note, a place with a line of its own (`Report::notes`): a cycle's other members, the witness a duplicate stands against, both claims of a name. A name that resolves to nothing is one refusal, raised by one resolver as one value (`curios_core::Unresolved`): what was written, the step that failed, why it found nothing, and what it is offered — the nearest name in scope where the step failed, one that differs in case alone or else one within `max(length, 3) / 3` of it by the restricted Damerau–Levenshtein distance (`curios_common::nearest`), and the paths the unit may write that carry the name. Written in a declaration it refuses that declaration alone, a path as a bare name, and in a `use` it refuses the unit. A root the unit may not name is such a failure for a reason of its own, and the compiler holds no notion of a facade: that `/std` exports what `/sys` declares is how the library is written.
- **A hidden member the compiler could not fill is refused under the name of the procedure that fills it, and its place words only whose it is and how it is written.** Which procedure fills a member is read off its declaration, its mark and its type's sort: a `use` member is resolution's and refused as `no witness`, an `@` member at a proposition is the bound procedure's — the reduct's one inhabitant, then the facts in scope — and refused as `undischarged bound`, and any other `@` member is unification's and refused as `uninferred argument`, or `uninferred field` in a literal. The headline, what was asked and what the facts said are the fault's, alike at a call, a literal, a constructor, an operator and a witness's telescope; the place adds the line naming whose slot it is — the bound `ok` of `List/get`, the bound of the `/` operator — and the remedy it is written by: `List/get(@...)`, `Positive { @... }`, the method call an operator stands for. The elaborator raises the two of its own as two refusals (`curios_elab::Error::UndischargedBound`, `curios_elab::Error::UninferredImplicit`), so no arm heads one fault with the other's word.
- **A mismatch at a name a binder hides says so, at the binder.** A local hides what the module's scope binds under its name, so the type a reader sees at the name is the local's, spelled past it by its path where the module's is meant: the report holds the binder as a further place, saying the name is bound there and what it hides. The lowering, which resolves the name, files each such use beside what the declaration minted (`curios_core::Mints::hides`), its read of the module's scope recorded as resolution's is, so a name a module gains lowers the declaration again; and no `use` is used by it, the use reaching the local.
- **An unsolved written goal's report may offer up to three verified candidates** (`? ≈`), each at depth one: a scope binder or a constructor the goal's indices admit, a function whose instantiated output unifies with the goal, with holes where its arguments are not determined, pooled from the goal's scope, the entry module, the globals it references and the bindings its `use` declarations import — never the whole prelude. Every attempt runs sandboxed in the solution-rollback bracket. A hole-free constructor fit must also survive an oracle-mode check in the goal's scope and under the guards it was written under, since inversion refuses positions it cannot decide, while an application fit is definitive by the conversion that found it, so a complete suggestion compiles when pasted. A goal that is a bound is put, in the same sandbox, to the procedure that proves a bound from the facts in scope ([A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md)): its proof is a complete fit, and its refusal is reported beside the goal. A suggestion is text the author's paste is checked from, so the trusted base is never consulted.
- **A form the grammar does not have is refused by name, with the form it has**, worded where the parser has read what discriminates the mistake ([`curios-surface`'s README](../../../curios-surface/README.md#a-refusal-is-worded-at-the-commitment-the-alternative-owes)); a mistake decided where types are known names what is written instead where that is exact: `{}` where `()` stands for a type, a call of its own for arguments past a list whose result is a function.

**Rationale.**

- **A report is read to be acted on, and the action is writing something at that position.** A printer answering a narrower question than the reader's departs from it: a suffix unique among all symbols rather than one resolution reaches from the reader's module (`Ord` offered where only `/std/Ord` resolves), imports merged across the unit though `use` binds from where it is written, a resolved witness shown as an internal identity, a projection as `(w).0`. Each is a fact only the stage that owns it can invert: the text stage holds the paths each global can be written by, the grammar the form a term is printed in, and the elaborator what resolution answers where the reader stands, under the binders and arms in scope there.
- **What a paste is promised is what the procedure that judges it was asked.** A printer that predicts what elaboration restores guarantees nothing: Idris's resugaring says so of itself, Rocq's strict implicits are a classification of the function that sees no scope, and Agda's lost instance arguments were put back on the elaborator's side of the command. A suggestion compiles when the checker that found it is the one that judges it, as GHC's hole fits and the suggestions here do. A witness settled by resolution, where the refusal is final, makes every report the second kind, and keeps none of elaboration's state past it.
- **The gap between stating an obligation and answering it is smallest where beginners live** — compute-both-sides equalities, index-forced constructors — and closing it composes pieces elaboration already runs: the resolution probe, the shared inversion unifier and the re-validation oracle. Depth one keeps the cost bounded, the verdicts definite and the output deterministic.
- **A reader tells a fault by its headline, and a headline chosen by place tells them a place.** One unfilled bound was headed `uninferred argument` at a call, `unfilled field` at a literal and `undischarged bound` at an operator, while `uninferred argument` also headed a type argument nothing determined — three names for one fault, and one name for two. The first refusal a newcomer meets is a call's bound, so the fault the language is about reached them under the name of another. `no witness` already read alike at a call and an operator, by the procedure that fills a `use` member.

**Rejected.**

- **A written-or-resolved flag on each argument**: terms are hash-consed, so a flag outside equality would let two differently flagged nodes collapse, and it answers the wrong question — whether resolution restores the argument in the reader's scope, not how it arrived.
- **A model of resolution beside the printer**, deciding from the heads a witness and its goal are written with whether resolution restores it: it reduces nothing and sees no arm's refinement, so it declines what resolution restores — a binder at an argument that computes, one at another application of the goal's former — and leaves out what resolution would not, under an arm that solved a binder's argument or a binder whose concept is written through an alias; and it keeps in every lexicon the definitions and witness tables only it reads.
- **A second printer of the surface language, kept in Core beside the one that formats source.** Nothing holds it to the grammar: a stuck match printed as `Nat.fold p: n2 => Nat; | 0n => …` and a field as `(s).2` are each found by the reader they mislead. A surface tree guarantees output that parses, the sweep over the library holds that it elaborates to the same term, and no checker depends on the surface crate, whose translation reaches a report through the hook its reporter hands in (`curios_core::Notation`).
- **A node or a token for what a report leaves out**: what a report shows is the report's to decide where it is made, and a translation that is whole or none leaves the tree holding only what the grammar produces.
- **A translation above the elaborator that asks it for types, or the elaboration context kept with a report for display**, as Lean's `MessageDataContext` keeps one: a report is then a function of elaboration state that has to outlive the refusal, where it is a function of its term, settled before the refusal leaves, and its units' tables; Lean's own check that a printed instance argument is restored is off, the context lacking the local instances. **A reduction handed to the printer**, a reducer of open terms shared with a report: it answers no resolution, and puts a checker's reduction in a report's reading. **A printer that learns a head's structure from the binders it opens** reaches a variable and not `g(n).name`, so a projection names its structure ([A hidden member takes no position](../theory/a-hidden-member-takes-no-position.md)).
- **Absolute names everywhere**, writable noise; **extending the elaborator's `denoise` fold**, which cannot see binders introduced inside the term it rewrites.
- **A name held as text in a refusal**, printed as the internal path it was built from; **a place attached by whoever raises**, which a refusal no term is being elaborated for has none to attach; **a refusal for each way a name fails to resolve**, in each crate that resolves one, a failed path ending the unit where a bare name ends its declaration; and **the plain Levenshtein distance**, which prices a swap at two and offers nothing for the commonest typo of a short name.
- **Recursive suggestion search**, filling a suggestion's holes by further search: the motivating induction step needs a function invented from a unification problem outside the Miller fragment, and a useful search over applications needs hint indexing and a search policy the workspace does not have. Resolution's bounded backtracking and conversion's deterministic budget would be its loop control, and the depth-one tiers its leaf steps.
- **The tables a report spells by built over the scope for each report**, every unit's symbols counted again, its marks and its witnesses' types copied out of every definition it holds: a refused program then reads the whole of a library it named two declarations of, for each report it is given.
- **A unit's paths listed by its lowering and stored with it**, made and filed by every compilation for a reader only a refused one has; and **one table of the whole scope for each report**, which lists every unit's paths again for each compilation that reports, where a unit's are a function of the unit alone ([Privacy is scoped to a subtree](../surface/privacy-is-scoped-to-a-subtree.md)).
- **One neutral headline by place** — `unfilled argument`, `unfilled field`, `unfilled operand` — with the fault left to the line beneath: uniform, and the headline then names no fault, `no witness` an exception to it. **A headline moved at the call alone**, where its member is a proposition: the literal, the operator and the next callee added keep a word of their own.
- **Prelude-wide candidate pools without an index**, cost without precision; **suggestions for a solved goal**, noise beside its `? =` answer.
