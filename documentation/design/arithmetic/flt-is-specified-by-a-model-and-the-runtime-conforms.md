# `Flt` is specified by a model, and the runtime conforms

**Decision.** `Flt` is IEEE 754-2019 binary64, every one of its 2⁶⁴ bit patterns a distinct value, and its semantics is a model in the repository — `curios_num::Floating`, unbounded integers that compute each operation exactly and round once — rather than whatever the compiler's host computes. Every `Flt` operation folds at the type level by that model, every stage's constant folder shares it, float literals are narrowed by it, and the runtime is held to it: where Wasm's `f64.*` leave the answer open — which NaN an operation produces — the model pins it, and the emitter checks every NaN-producing instruction's result against it. The NaN rule:

- an invalid operation with no NaN operand answers the default NaN, positive and quiet with no payload;
- an operation with NaN operands answers the greatest of their quieted patterns, read unsigned;
- a difference is the sum with its subtrahend negated;
- `neg`, `abs`, `copysign` and the byte conversions touch no bit but what they name.

The primitives are exactly IEEE's compute-exactly-round-once operations — `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div`, `sqrt`, `fma`, the roundings to an integral value and the conversions from `Nat` and `Int` — each carrying its rounding direction as static data: ties to even at `/sys/Flt/<op>`, the other four at `/sys/Flt/<direction>/<op>`. The rest is `/std`: `Flt/rounded/<op>(r, …)` dispatches on a `Flt/Rounding`, `Flt/signals/<op>(r, …)` computes the exceptions IEEE raises from the exact value read through `/std/Dyadic`, `Flt/Env` threads a direction and the raised exceptions as a monad, and every rounding `/std` performs goes through `/std/Flt/exact`'s `round`, the model's rounding written in Curios line for line. Term identity is bitwise, so `0.0` and `-0.0` are distinct and two NaNs are one term exactly when they are one pattern. Conversion decides of a stuck `Flt` term only what the model fixes at every pattern, each a declaration the generated law grid holds to the model ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)): a rounding direction is part of an operation's identity, so a sum toward zero and the same sum toward positive are two operations; `add`, `mul`, `min` and `max` commute, and `fma`'s two factors with its addend kept in place; a difference is its sum; a rounding to an integral value is fixed by every direction; the byte conversions undo each other from both sides; and a composition of `neg`, `abs` and `copysign`, each of which moves the sign bit and reads no other, is written one way, as a magnitude under a sign bit that is clear, set, another value's, or that flipped. No `Flt` operation is declared a ring's or an order's. The readings of a value state decided bounds — `Finite(a)` on `to_int`, `mantissa` and `exponent`, `NonNeg(a)` on `to_nat` — and the conversions into `Flt` are total by correct rounding. Three differentials hold the model and the runtime to each other: the model against the host's arithmetic, against an exact rational oracle, and against the engine that executes the emitted `f64.*`.

Conversion decides what the model fixes at every bit pattern, NaNs included, each identity declared to the law grid and held to the model over its patterns: an operation's rounding direction is part of what it is, so a sum in one direction and the swapped sum in another are two terms; `add`, `mul`, `min` and `max` commute in one direction, and `fma`'s two factors; a difference is the sum with its subtrahend negated; a rounding to an integral value is fixed by every direction's; the byte conversions undo each other both ways; and a composition of `neg`, `abs` and `copysign` is written one way, a magnitude under a sign bit that is clear, set, another value's, or that one flipped, so `neg(neg(x))` is `x`, `abs(neg(x))` is `abs(x)` and `neg(copysign(x, y))` is `copysign(x, neg(y))`. The associativity of `min` and `max` holds of the model and is not declared, a law over a multiset of leaves that nothing consumes.

**Rationale.**

- **The narrowings need a bound that can be decided**, and a decided proposition over a comparison that never reduces makes a literal call unwritable; the fold buys exactly that call, as it does for any intrinsic whose value a type or a proof can depend on.
- **IEEE equality is a fold, not a convertibility rule.** Folding `Flt/eql(0.0, -0.0)` to `true` creates no convertibility: `Eq` still needs `refl`, conversion on literals is bitwise, and refinement rewrites the scrutinee, never an operand — the line Rocq's primitive floats draw. The hazard is a fold the running program can disagree with, and IEEE and Wasm leave exactly one such thing to the implementation, a computed NaN's sign and payload, on which x86 and aarch64 differ. A model that pins it and a runtime that computes it remove the disagreement.
- **The NaN rule keeps laws.** IEEE 754-2019 §6.2.3 leaves which input payload a result carries open; the greatest quieted pattern is symmetric, so `add`, `mul`, `min`, `max` and `fma`'s factors stay commutative on every pattern, and the negated sum makes `sub(a, b)` and `add(a, neg(b))` one computation. Holding the runtime to it costs one self-comparison per operation and a helper call only on a NaN.
- **Directions are data and exceptions are values.** IEEE requires a constant rounding attribute per block (§4.1) and recommends dynamic modes (§4.2); a static tag folds and emits as the default does — hardware for ties to even, an integer soft-float helper for the directed ones and `fma` — and a dynamic mode is a `/std` value dispatched on. Exceptions are a function of the operands (§7.1 leaves their scope to the language), so they need no second output from any fold and no hidden runtime state.
- **The machine becomes evidence.** The model is a few hundred lines of integer code in the trust category of the `Nat` and `Int` folds, with no floating-point unit anywhere in the trusted path, `curios-js` included; the host's hardware is the oracle for testing the model, and an engine that disagrees is wrong.

**Rejected.**

- **One NaN**, the convergence of Lean's `Float32.Model`, Rocq's `spec_float`, Agda and Isabelle's `some_nan`: IEEE §6.2 requires both signaling and quiet NaNs, and it loses a payload on `to_le_bytes ∘ of_le_bytes`.
- **Canonical-only NaN results**, which drop the payload propagation §6.2.3 recommends; **the first operand's payload**, as x86 answers, which makes `add` commutative of numbers and not of the carrier.
- **A ring or an order law declared for `Flt`**: associativity and distributivity fail by rounding, `f + 0.0 = f` at `-0.0`, `f * 1.0 = f` and `min(a, a) = a` at a signaling NaN, `f - f = 0.0` at an infinity and `f == f` at a NaN, each a control in the law grid; and **a difference built as its sum at the `/sys` row**, which leaves every stage below Core without a subtraction for a peephole to restore and prints a written `a - b` as a sum, where the fold makes the two one term in Core alone.
- **Hardware folds with bit-strict semantics**, true on the compiling machine and possibly false on the running one, and **an opaque `Flt`**, which leaves the narrowings no decidable domain.
- **A model over the host's `f32` behind a decline-on-NaN gate**, or **NaN bits left unspecified**: both tie a corner of the semantics to an implementation.
- **Totalizing the narrowings**, by clamping as Lean's `toUInt32` or by `Option` alone as Agda's `primFloatRound`: junk, or a bound reverted to discipline. The `try_` forms stay beside the bounded ones.
- **`to_nat`'s domain as `-1.0 < a`**, its true domain, which the name `NonNeg` outweighs, nothing observable being given up; **a bound on `Nat/to_flt` for exactness**, when rounding is the embedding's canonical extension.
- **A run-time direction operand on each primitive**, a branch the static tag decides at compile time; **exceptions as a second result of every fold**, doubling each primitive's output for what `/std` derives exactly.
- **A rounding primitive** in place of the Curios rounding, opaque to the [binary64 conversion proofs](../../roadmap/arithmetic/07-binary64-conversion-proofs.md); **error-free transformations** in place of the directed helpers, wrong near underflow and overflow; **decoding a float's bits in `/std`**, whose infinity and NaN arm no comparison bound rules out.
- **Folding by reducing the Curios twin** instead of trusting the Rust model, which spends the budget on every closed float and waits on the [exact `Rat`](../../roadmap/arithmetic/04-exact-rationals.md) and those proofs.
