# The carriers' algebra stays in conversion

**Decision.** Conversion decides the carriers' algebra. The laws `curios-algebra` states for `Nat`, `Int`, `Bool`, the words and the conversions between them are definitional equalities both checkers take, and the context's hypotheses stay out of them. Three places hold what the theory is: `curios-algebra`'s law table, each law a generated row of `curios`'s `tests::laws`; `curios-core`'s `linear` module, what conversion decides of a comparison; and `tests::laws::audit`, the conditions a theory in conversion must meet. The algebra is divided among crates by what each part reads, and the dependencies point one way:

- `curios-num`: the concrete carriers and their scalar semantics.
- `curios-algebra`, which depends on `curios-num` alone and names no term: the mathematics over abstract atoms — combinations and their cancellation, distribution, Euclid's recombination, comparison facts, bounds and domination, the Boolean laws and the truth table, the words' normal form and measures, the round trips between carriers, the canonical linear form of a comparison — with the vocabulary of operations and the law table, and what each result means and how strong it is ([its README](../../../curios-algebra/README.md)).
- `curios-core`: which operation each intrinsic is (`Intrinsic::algebra`) and which terms are one atom (`atoms`), reading terms into the algebra's views and rebuilding terms from its results, with reduction demand and charging and the folds that apply the results.
- `curios-analysis`: `convert_intrinsics`, the chain both checkers run when two intrinsics meet, returning an outcome or obligations typed by `Intrinsic::signature` and calling no judgment; and inversion, which reads the algebra only through Core's `peel_intrinsic`, whose `Deduction` cannot carry a residual that is only sufficient.
- Each checker: the elaborator substitutes solved metavariables before the chain and offers its packed-literal view; the certifier discharges the chain's obligations under its own conversion history and budget.

A procedure that searches, such as [the one that proves a bound from the facts in scope](a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md), lives on the elaborator's side of the certifier's dependency closure, reads the views Core publishes and hands the checkers ordinary terms; the algebra holds no search.

**Rationale.**

- **A decided bound discharges by reduction** because the comparison it reflects reduces ([A bound is stated in a decided proposition and discharged by reduction](a-bound-is-stated-in-a-decided-proposition-and-discharged-by-reduction.md)). With the laws as casts, a bound over `x + 0` or `len(a ++ b)` would need a cast wherever it is used, and since a cast along a hypothetical proof stays stuck, every type a law reaches would hold a stuck term.
- **A proof the elaborator writes stays a sum of facts** checked by cancellation, rather than a chain of lemmas restating the laws.
- **This is Coq Modulo Theory's premise**: a decidable theory in conversion with the hypotheses kept out (Strub, 2010), whose metatheory with strong elimination is Jouannaud and Strub's (2017). It needs conversion to stay symmetric, transitive and closed under substitution with the theory in it, and constructors to stay free modulo the theory; `tests::laws::audit` holds each condition at every declared law through both checkers, and freeness at every intrinsic case inversion distinguishes — evidence about the implemented fragment, not a metatheorem.
- **One owner makes each rule readable, testable and single.** The mathematics is tested over bare atoms and concrete values with no checker involved; one chain serves both checkers, so a demand added to it is asked by both; a result's strength is in its type, so inversion cannot deduce what is only sufficient; and a law declared once is generated at every carrier that declares it, so a law true at two carriers and decided at one fails at the other.
- **The algebra reports the work it would do before doing it** — `distribution_size`, `evaluation_size` — and Core charges it, so exhaustion takes the refusing path and never manufactures a verdict.
- **What it costs.** The theory is trusted code both checkers share, so a wrong law is unsound everywhere — it admits by [the board's shared route](../soundness/the-soundness-board.md), and a wrong disequality also excuses an arm at inversion — and every new law must be decided alike by both, so it enters through a declaration and the rows generated from it, never through an arm one checker holds ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)). The theory is small, decidable and held by generated evidence; the cost of casts would fall on every program that states a bound.

**Rejected.**

- **The laws as casts**, conversion kept syntactic — β, δ, ι, η, irrelevance and the closed folds — with each carrier law a `/std` lemma a program transports along: every bound over an open term written by hand, every law a type reaches a stuck term, every written proof restating its laws.
- **The strategies kept apart in the two checkers**, for a second opinion: both copies run rules of `curios-core` and `curios-algebra` anyway, a second copy of a pure function is a second run, and copies drift — the `<`/`<=` seam meeting at `Int` and not at `Nat`.
- **Every result an equality, a clash or a bare residual**, which leaves inversion's restriction to whoever registers a rule.
- **Eager canonicalization**, rewriting sums and comparisons as they are built, which changes demand, spelling and cost at once; the canonical linear view is read where a comparison is asked for, and rebuilds no term and keys nothing.
- **A carrier's rows stated by hand**, a convention the seam slips past, and **an alignment per carrier**, two procedures deciding one relation.
