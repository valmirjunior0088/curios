# A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents

**Decision.** An equation over the intrinsic carriers is taken at one of three places, chosen by what the law would have to do there, and each answers `Equal` or declines, never a disequality it did not already have:

- a **fold**, when the reduct is a term the fold can build outright, charged before it is built — `shl(x, k) = 2ᵏ · x` at a literal `k`, a comparison whose sides are apart modulo the gcd of their coefficients folding to `false`, `map(xs, f) = xs` where `f`'s body reduces to its binder;
- the **peel**, when it equates two stuck values no rewrite could state without deriving a proof — two `get`s reading one position of one root through any windows, a window of a window against the window it names;
- a **probe-side** decision both converters ask for by name, when deciding needs a reducer and rewriting would respell a term a guard was keyed on — two `Bool` terms that agree at every assignment of their atoms, up to eight atoms.

A stuck term is spelled one way. `gt` and `ge` are spelled as their `lt` and `le` mirrors from the moment they enter Core: the `/sys` rows for `Nat`, `Byte`, `Int` and `Flt` build `a > b` as `b < a`, so no representation below the rows has a `Gt` or `Ge` head. A sum is merged and a difference cancelled by the fold on every demand, while a product of two symbolic sums is its own weak-head form, distributed only where a comparison or a converter meeting a stuck product asks (`Nat::normalize`, and `int_normalize` at `Int`). Where no row can build the one spelling, the judgment aligns spellings probe-side: a negated comparison, the `<`/`<=` seam read through the linear views, and the symmetric heads through `peel_symmetric`; and the refinement probe retries a miss under the guard's dual and successor spellings (`probe_spellings`), the four ways one order fact is written on either side of the seam.

A held law is a declaration's generated row. The law's family is declared for the operation at every carrier its rule decides; `tests::laws::generated` states the instance at each, puts it to both checkers as an `Eq/refl()` proof and holds it at closed values computed through `curios-num`, and `tests::laws::audit` holds it reversed, chained with every law sharing a side, at compound terms and through a solved metavariable. A law no family states is written in `tests::laws::written` at every carrier its rule reaches. A control that is not a law stands beside it, a value grid in `curios-core` holds the rule to ground truth, one mutation per family is caught and named by its perimeter entry ([Open fold laws and the sum normal form](../soundness/conversion/open-fold-laws-and-the-sum-normal-form.md), [Intrinsic fold laws and the free-monoid peel](../soundness/conversion/intrinsic-fold-laws-and-the-free-monoid-peel.md)), and a count or a cap is the theory's, never the host's.

**Rationale.**

- **A reducer may not invent a proof.** Rewriting `get(slice(xs, s, l), i)` to `get(xs, s + i)` owes `s + i < len(xs)`, which no reduct can carry; a comparison builds no term, so the peel owes nothing, and irrelevance leaves the bounds unread.
- **A fold may not respell.** A guard's refinement is keyed on its written spelling, and a fold that normalizes a `&&`/`||` tree whole pays for the whole tree at every leaf; a decision made where two trees are compared needs no normal form.
- **What a rule builds is priced.** A left shift's result is the one size its operands do not bound, so its rule sits where a reducer charges it.
- **One spelling at the head is safe only at the boundary.** With both spellings at the head, `a >= b` and `b <= a` are two terms neither conversion nor refinement relates, so a guard `0 <= a` does not discharge `Int/NonNeg(a)`, which says `Int/ge(a, 0)`; a mirror applied in the reducer alone meets a case equation recorded on the guard as written. A spelling that never exists cannot be keyed on. The mirror is antisymmetry, and at `Flt` every ordered comparison against a NaN is false on both sides. A written `>=` renders as `<=` with its operands swapped.
- **Distribution is the normal form's one quadratic step.** Distributed eagerly, the products grow as the square of the summands that survive — on a web `rₙ = rₙ₋₁·(1 + rₙ₋₂)`, to decide what the first summand settles, and the `multiply::products` sample counts them; left stuck, the form is linear and the peel decides on a small term. Merging and cancelling are linear and everything downstream assumes them, so only the product waits. Nothing definitional is lost: a converter normalizes a stuck product on either side before the peel, `compare_nat` normalizes before cancelling, and a refinement keyed on a product distributes it as its arguments reduce. `Nat::normalize` charges each product before building it.

**Rejected.**

- **A normal form for `Bool`.** A reduced ordered BDD is the respelling fold the records reject, and probe-side it is the truth table under the same cap; algebraic normal form over GF(2) makes an `n`-way `||` `2ⁿ - 1` monomials.
- **Reducing a tautology to `true`**, which takes the table into the fold; `Bool/holds_of_eq` over `Eq/refl()` proves it in one written step.
- **Reducing through a window with a library lemma**, which makes the trusted reducer's output name a `/std` declaration and fail where the slot is unfilled, as while the library compiles itself.
- **Asking conversion whether a mapped function is the identity**, which only equates, so `len` and `get` would not see through the map, and which the elaborator's worklist cannot ask without a sandboxed sub-conversion.
- **A right shift through a division node**: `NatDiv` carries a proof its divisor is nonzero that a reducer may not invent.
- **Teaching conversion the mirror**, or keeping `Gt`/`Ge` heads below the rows: a case in the trusted comparison, or a head no producer reaches and no test exercises, where a mirrored row is one line.
- **Rewriting the decided propositions to the canonical spelling**, which leaves one spelling a user must match.
- **The mirror's rule at the operands of `==` and `!=`**, which respells `rem == 1` as `1 == rem` past the refinement `Str/step` stands on; the peel equates a swapped pair without respelling.
- **Deferring sums and differences as well as products**, which loses `x − x = 0`, `(x + y) − y = x` and `(x + y) − x = y` and needs a repair for each of what merging in the fold gives free; **a `Reducer::demand()` read by the folds**, which taxes every caller of a shared demand; **a cheaper monomial representation**, which leaves the count of products the square of the survivors.
