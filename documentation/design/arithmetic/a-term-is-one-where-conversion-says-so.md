# A term is one where conversion says so

**Decision.** No verdict and no solution follows how a term is spelled, the order it holds its operands in, or the arm it stands in.

- **A term at a carrier is layers of the algebra over opaque atoms, classified once, from its leaves to its root.** What a layer is, and which operands it reads, is `Intrinsic::algebra`'s to say and no reader's: an intrinsic the table declares an operation, at a carrier whose values hold no type, is a node, and anything else is an opaque atom. The table declares every intrinsic a reader reads, so no second list says what is read through. The algebra's readers pair atoms by identity, and where they decide nothing of a pair it is classified (`curios-analysis`'s `classify`) and read again as the classification spells it. `curios-algebra` and the `atoms` table do not change: identity is still settled before a handle is handed out.
- **Two opaque atoms are one where the checker's own conversion says so.** The conversion chain hands a pair's opaque atoms back, each checker says which of them convert, and each is spelled as its class's representative.
- **A node is one with another where its own reader says so.** A node's operands are classified first and the node is spelled over them, then held to the earlier nodes of its operation by the shared readers alone, no judgment asked, taking the first one's spelling. So an operation one reader reads and the reader above it takes whole is one atom of the reader above wherever its own reader holds it one, and a checker is asked nothing about what a node's operands are. Every node is held but one standing under a node of its own operation where the law table declares that operation associative, whose reader flattens it. A node is read only against the earlier nodes that stand over its atoms, and an operation over truth values against every earlier one of its kind, the decision diagram holding two formulas one over two sets of atoms. A node the table leaves an operand out of, a quotient over the proof of its divisor, is its checker's: it is handed out as an atom once the operands its meaning reads are classified.
- **A node is held by its reader's identity.** A node is read against the earlier nodes its reader may hold it one with and no others: of its operation and over its atoms, a literal being a constant of its carrier's form and no atom. The reader of a carrier's sum normal form, which reads a sum, a product and a successor floor at `Nat` and a sum and a product at `Int`, holds two nodes one exactly where they are one polynomial over their atoms, and a product the fold leaves stuck is one polynomial with the sum it distributes to; so such a node is held by its carrier, its atoms and its image, the node's value in a prime field under one assignment of residues to its atoms, which two spellings of one polynomial share and two polynomials share with the chance a collision has. Two nodes of one bucket are read in the normal form their reader reads, a product the fold left stuck distributed on either side as the chain distributes one before its peels, charged as every normalization gate is and written back nowhere: the first node of a bucket keeps its spelling, and a later node that is one polynomial with it is spelled as it (`curios-analysis`'s `classify`).
- **An atom's identity is its carrier's, stated once.** `curios-core`'s `same_atom` and `Atoms::handle` say what an atom of a carrier is known by, and every reader takes its handles and its direct comparisons from them. At a carrier whose values hold no type — `Nat`, `Int`, `Bool`, `Byte`, `Flt`, a packed sequence — a universe instance is no part of it, in both checkers. A list's element and a product's factor are known by the term as written. A truth value that is a `Nat` or `Int` comparison is known by what it states, its linear view aligned and read at a polarity, as conversion reads two comparisons: `x < y`, `x + 1 <= y` and `Nat/to_int(x) < Nat/to_int(y)` are one atom of `Bool`, as `x - y <= z` and `x <= y + z` are, a bare truncated difference moved across its comparison where the adjunction is exact, and `y <= x` and `y < x + 1` that atom negated (`Atoms::signed`).
- **An atom is ordered by its shape, then by the age of the identities it holds.** The order the readers put atoms in, and a fold its factors (`curios-core`'s `Term::rank`), reads a term's structure with each binder, metavariable and universe or quality metavariable a placeholder, beside the positions each holds, a summary a node makes of its children's ([`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nodes-rank-is-its-childrens-joined)), and then compares two terms of one shape by their identities in the order of their ages, each by the positions it holds. An identity is read by comparison and never by its value, which counts every identity its declaration minted before it, so no order, and no spelling written from one, follows a display built and discarded, a run a memo answered or made again, or a probe that opened a binder.
- **A commutative operation pairs its operands.** For an operation the law table declares commutative, one operand of each side is paired by identity, in either position, and the other two are a sufficient residual. Operands nothing pairs are unpaired, which the kernel reads as unequal; they are never compared the first with the first and the second with the second. An ordered comparison, a difference and a concatenation keep that comparison, since position is what their operands mean.
- **A metavariable is solved where its equation has one solution, and never picked.** The elaborator solves the one unsolved metavariable an equation over `Nat` or `Int` is linear in to the quotient exact division gives, and puts that quotient to conversion. An equation with more than one solution — `?x * ?y` against `a * b` — stays undecided and is reported as a conversion that could not be decided, naming the implicits never solved.
- **A mismatch says what was not compared.** Where a mismatch ends on operands nothing paired, its report says the two sides were not compared, which is another thing to tell an author than that they differ.
- **An equation answers the terms conversion holds equal to its scrutinee, at the solution its arm is checked under**, in both checkers, and stays a lookup: which spellings a key is held under, what the shared rule `answers` reads and how an arm's solution restates an equation are [Case equations and their key](../soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)'s.
- **A stuck fold is taken again over what the classification holds one.** Once every equation in force has been asked, a stuck operation the algebra declares is classified, its opaque atoms by its checker and its nodes by their readers, and its fold is taken over one spelling to a class, kept only where the result is no longer that operation. The classification itself folds nothing but a magnitude's orientation: any other fold is a reducer's to take. A quotient and a remainder are atoms of two kinds, which Euclid's recombination pairs by their dividend and divisor and nothing asks about each other, so the dividend and the divisor of each are classified before the division is handed out. Two quotients, or two remainders, are one where their dividends and their divisors are, and no checker is asked: the operand the table leaves out of each is a proof, which its value does not read. What the classification of a stuck operation found is filed by the operation in each checker, for as long as what it read stands — the declaration where no class its checker decided entered it, the equations in force and the types the locals stand at where one did — and the stuck fold above extends it: its first operand's classification is where the walk of the whole stands once that operand is walked, a later operand is walked against it, a node walked already met by its identity, and small operands before a larger one's classification are placed before it where every key they share is decided as the walk decides it (`curios-analysis`'s `Classification`).
- **A magnitude is oriented over the classes.** `abs` and `sign` hold an operand and its negation one by orienting it, its monomials in the order of their atoms' ranks and the first leading, which is a choice no reader makes again (`curios-algebra`'s `Operation::oriented`). Where a pair is read again as its classes spell it, a magnitude whose operand moved is spelled by its own fold over the spelled operand, so a magnitude over two spellings of one class is led by one monomial on both sides, `sign`'s negation drawn out of it as its law says.
- **A question a judgment's reduction asks is one conversion, answered by plain reduction.** Where a judgment's reduction asks its checker's conversion, how a classing is bounded, how two calls apart in a universe instance are read and why an answer the solver can change is not kept are [A question reduction asks is answered by plain reduction](../soundness/a-question-reduction-asks-is-answered-by-plain-reduction.md)'s.
- **An elimination reads its scrutinee as conversion does.** Where a match's scrutinee, reduced and forced, would leave the dispatch stuck, a `Bool` connective is read by the decision diagram and the match takes the arm of the literal it is at every assignment of its atoms, and a structural `Nat` match on a sum with no floor distributes its products of two symbolic sums and takes the successor arm where a floor appears. One rule both reducers call says what a scrutinee is read as, `curios-analysis`'s `read_scrutinee`: it answers a value only where the match then takes an arm, so no stuck form is respelled, and both reductions take it, as both take a fold. Where the diagram decides nothing and some two of the connective's atoms may be one, a judgment's reduction classes them by its checker's conversion, once for both literals, under a stuck fold's two conditions, and reads the connective again over the classes.

**Rationale.**

- **Conversion is transitive only where atom identity contains it.** `curios-algebra` decides over handles by normal forms, which is transitive over handles. Two terms that convert and are handed two handles — one call under two proofs of its bound, two functions whose bodies commute a sum, two stuck `match`es whose arms do — break that over terms: the first converts with a second and the second with a third by one residual each, and the first with the third leaves two atoms unpaired on each side. The relation wanted is the one Coq Modulo Theory defines, a term's cap compared in the theory and its aliens by conversion, the whole transitive (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017), and the identity is the one Mathlib's `AtomM` keeps, an atom being a class of terms up to definitional equality.
- **A union of theories is decided from each theory's own procedure, from the leaves up.** The word problem of a union of equational theories over disjoint signatures is decided by abstracting a term's alien subterms from its leaves to its root, removing a collapse and identifying two variables equated to equivalent pure terms, and the combination is polynomial past the theories' own cost (Baader and Tinelli, [*Deciding the Word Problem in the Union of Equational Theories*](https://homepage.cs.uiowa.edu/~tinelli/papers/BaaTin-RR-98.pdf), after Pigozzi, 1974); canonizers of disjoint theories combine where solvers do not (Rueß and Shankar, [*Combining Shostak Theories*](https://www.csl.sri.com/people/shankar/rta02.pdf), 2002). The carriers' readers are such procedures, and an operation of one standing under another's is an alien of it: classified first, by its own reader, it is one atom of the reader above by construction. Read as an opaque atom instead, it is known by its spelling, so `Nat/and(x, y) - Nat/and(y, x)` is no `0`, a hypothesis over it is refused at its other spelling in one step and accepted through an annotation, and each of conversion, a stuck fold, an arm's equation and the bound prover misses it its own way.
- **A truth value's atom is what it states.** Two `Bool` terms are decided over independent atoms, and conversion holds `y < x + 1` to state what `y <= x` states, the negation of `x < y`; known by its spelling, a comparison makes `(x < y) && (y < x + 1)` three atoms, so it converts with neither `false` nor `(x < y) && (y <= x)` though each link of the chain does. A normalization under which a constraint and its negation are one label read at two polarities is what a decision diagram over the atoms of a theory asks of the theory (Chaki, Gurfinkel and Strichman, [*Decision Diagrams for Linear Arithmetic*](https://ofers.dds.technion.ac.il/publications/fmcad09.pdf), 2009), and over the integers the view's alignment is that normalization at one polarity. Implication between two comparisons, which such diagrams also read, relates two atoms and identifies none, so it stays outside what an atom is.
- **A level is no part of a value that holds no type.** Core has no elimination from a type or a level into a number, a truth value, a byte, a float or a packed sequence, so two terms of such a carrier that are one up to universe instances denote one value ([Case equations and their key](../soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)). Stated by each reader for itself, the rule held for a number's summands and not for a truth value's leaves or for the operands a fold compares; stated once for a carrier, every reader holds it, and the generated law grid holds every reader to it over calls written apart. A list holds values of any type, a type among them, so a level can be part of one. Two calls of one name are then apart in nothing a value reads where their arguments convert, and a checker handed the two whole would compare their levels first, the elaborator joining what the kernel is later handed apart; so the calls are asked about by their arguments, in both checkers.
- **Two checkers agree only where conversion is transitive.** The elaborator takes an annotation as a step and the kernel compares the first type with the last, so a relation that is not transitive lets one path through and refuses the other, and the refusal reaches the user as the kernel's.
- **The order a term holds two operands in is a spelling or a rank.** A sum keeps its summands as they were written, and a product's factors and an equality's sides stand in the order of their ranks, which moves with the order binders are declared in. A positional congruence turned either into a verdict, and where an operand was a metavariable into a solution, so what a call meant followed a declaration order.
- **A counter's value is a count of earlier work.** A binder is minted from a counter its declaration starts at zero, and how far the counter had run when it was minted follows every walk that minted before it. A rank that read the value would move with that work, an implicit solved through a product spelled one way or the other by whether a memo answered a closed run. Compared by age, which no identity minted since moves, and spelled by first meeting, which needs no counter at all, the order is a function of the term, and for locals standing in one term age orders them by where their binders stand, a scope being opened from the body of the one enclosing it. Lean's `Expr.quickLt` "first compares the hashcodes", a free variable hashed as its unique id ([`Lean/Expr.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Expr.lean)), and Z3 met the same reading at scale — "AST ids feed heuristic tie-breaking throughout the solver", one instance taking 17 s on one platform and 1633 s on another — and fixed the order its ids are assigned in ([Z3Prover/z3#10165](https://github.com/Z3Prover/z3/pull/10165)), a discipline every site creating one must keep, where a reading that compares keeps nothing.
- **The chain still calls no judgment.** The elaborator's comparison enqueues and may solve, and the kernel's runs inside the pairs its goal is unfolding, so the chain hands the atoms back and is entered again with the partition: a function of a pair and a partition, as `curios-algebra` is one of handles. Each checker classes under its own state — the kernel inside the pairs of the goal the atoms belong to, the elaborator classing only what converts with nothing committed.
- **A solution that is the only one is not a pick.** Conversion holds two sums of products equal where their normal forms agree, so over the equation's atoms the equation is one in a ring of polynomials, which has no zero divisors: an equation linear in one unknown has at most one solution. GHC's `ghc-typelits-natnormalise` derives unifiers over its sum-of-products form the same way. The solver proposes and conversion decides, so it stands outside the trusted base, where a search belongs.
- **The laws and the guards have to compose.** The algebra makes `n + m` and `m + n` one term, and a guard makes its scrutinee `true` in its arm. Answering the guard by spelling left `m + n < k` stuck under `match n + m < k`: conversion held the two comparisons equal outside the arm and refused them inside it, where one had become `true`. An arm's equation is a claim about a term, and which terms are that term is conversion's to say.
- **A spine is classified once.** A reduction folds a spine from its leaves and takes each stuck fold again, so a fold that classified everything beneath it from nothing classified each level again at every level above: the square of a sum's length, and the cube of a tower's whose lines name the line before, in walks no budget counts. One that extends what its operands' folds filed walks what is new at it, and decides what one walk of the whole would, since the walk meets an operand before anything after it and in its own order. An e-class analysis is kept the same way, each class's datum the join of what its nodes make from their children's (Willsey and others, [*egg: Fast and Extensible Equality Saturation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03082), 2021), and the classifications are persistent maps so that extending one shares what it extends (after Conchon and Filliâtre, [*A Persistent Union-Find Data Structure*](https://usr.lmf.cnrs.fr/~jcf/publis/puf-wml07.pdf), 2007).
- **A canonizer is taken over the representatives.** The canonical form of a term of a combination of theories is each theory's canonizer applied once the term's subterms are replaced by their representatives, Rueß and Shankar's `S⟨⟨a⟩⟩ = σ(S[a])` ([*Combining Shostak Theories*](https://www.csl.sri.com/people/shankar/rta02.pdf), 2002). The readers canonize what they read once the classes have respelled it. A magnitude's canonizer is its fold, which reads the order of its operand's atoms, and two spellings of one atom hash apart, so read without its fold a pair of magnitudes over one class would keep two orientations, and the congruence would compare an operand with its negation: two functions over one difference, the one spelling a `match` with its motive written and the other taking it from where it stands, one to the elaborator and refused by the kernel at some orders of their binders.
- **A reader of sums holds two nodes one only where their normal forms agree, and an image tells two polynomials apart without building either.** The fold leaves a product of two symbolic sums stuck ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)), so two nodes that are one polynomial over their atoms are two spellings until one is distributed, and a classification that never distributed held `pow(P, y)` and `pow(D, y)` two atoms of the product reader, `P` a product of two sums and `D` its distribution, so `pow(P, y) * pow(P, z)` and `pow(D, y) * pow(D, z)` were refused where `pow(P, y)` and `pow(D, y)` alone were held by the congruence. Whether two such nodes are one polynomial is polynomial identity testing, which no deterministic procedure is known to decide in polynomial time, one deciding it proving circuit lower bounds (Kabanets and Impagliazzo, [*Derandomizing polynomial identity tests means proving circuit lower bounds*](https://www.cs.sfu.ca/~kabanets/Research/poly.html), 2004), and every expanded form is exponential in the term a web of products is; a value at a point is a ring homomorphism, so two spellings of one polynomial share it and two polynomials share it with the chance a collision has (the [Schwartz–Zippel lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwartz%E2%80%93Zippel_lemma)), which is the direction a cost filter may fail toward, as the atoms' hashes and `atoms.rs`'s collision rule already do. Maple's `testeq` ([Monagan, *Improving testeq*](https://www.cecm.sfu.ca/CAG/abstracts/13012003.html)) and random interpretation (Gulwani and Necula, [*Global value numbering using random interpretation*](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/%7Enecula/Papers/rand_popl04.pdf), 2004) decide equality by such a value and accept a chance of a false equality; here the image only keeps two nodes from being read, and what the readers hold one is decided of the normal forms, as the chain decides it. An index that rules a pair out at sample positions before the full test is the shape of Schulz's fingerprint index ([E's development notes](https://wwwlehre.dhbw-stuttgart.de/~sschulz/WORK/E_DOWNLOAD/V_1.5/E/DOC/DONE)) and of the cvec-matching that extracts candidate equalities from an e-graph for a validator ([Ruler](https://github.com/uwplse/ruler)).
- **Conversion is a congruence only where a fold reads atoms as it does.** `f(a + b)` and `f(b + a)` convert. A fold that pairs atoms by identity leaves `f(a + b) == f(b + a)` stuck, so a term and the same term with one atom respelled do not convert, and a `match` on the comparison does not reduce.
- **A match converts with the arm its scrutinee converts to.** Conversion is a congruence, so a match whose scrutinee converts with a literal or a successor converts with the match on that value, which is its arm; a reduction that stops at the scrutinee leaves the match stuck, and the match against its arm is refused where two steps of conversion hold them one, which is transitivity lost between the two checkers' walks ([Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md)). Coq Modulo Theory's elimination matches modulo its theory for this reason, a constructor `C(ū)` simplifying a term that is no constructor where the theory holds the two one (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017). The folds stop short of the value where reading it would cost every demand on a term — a connective's right operand left as written, a product of two sums left undistributed ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)) — so the reading is an elimination's, one value asked once, where it is taken apart.

**Rejected.**

- **Refusing wherever two operands are unpaired, with no classing.** It makes a verdict stable and leaves conversion non-transitive, and it refuses programs without accepting any.
- **A node put to the checker**, two operations of one kind asked about as two atoms are: comparing them classes their operands in turn, so the cost doubles with each level of nesting, and a wrong equation between two nested terms waits on its budget to be refused.
- **Every node the readers read through handed out as an atom as well.** The questions put over `/std` grow several times over before anything new is decided.
- **Every node held**, a node under its own associative operation among them: each inner conjunction of a long one is then put to the decision diagram against the others, where the diagram reads the whole conjunction as one formula.
- **Only a node standing under a node of another operation held.** `Nat/and(Nat/and(x, y), Nat/and(y, x))` holds two nodes under their own operation, which its reader does not flatten.
- **A node read against every earlier node of its operation.** A sum of two hundred operations is then read pair by pair at every fold above it, and does not answer.
- **Comparisons classed across operations**, `x + 1 <= y` held to `x < y` in the classification's buckets. It reaches no negation, which a reader of formulas reads as one atom at the other polarity, and the carrier's identity reaches every reader of a `Bool` atom where the buckets reach the classing.
- **A node's own fold taken by the classification**, wherever an operand of it moved, past a magnitude's: every other fold a program needs is one a reducer takes again at its stuck fold, the readers reading again what the classes respell, and a fold the classification took would decide, inside a question, what a question's plain reduction leaves stuck. A magnitude's orientation is neither: the classes it needs are the pair's, which no reduction sees, and over representatives its fold only reorients, a collapse among the atoms of one magnitude being the reducer's own refold.
- **Two magnitudes held one where their operands are one or each other's negation**, a residual of two alternatives: no outcome hands back one of two residuals, the elaborator would have to try the first without committing, and a `sign` reaches the linear reader as `sign(x)` beside `+0 - sign(y)`, which a rule over two magnitudes does not meet. **A reader of magnitudes in the chain** states the orientation a second time beside the fold, and the two drift.
- **A residual followed through the readers in turn**, where a reader pairs all but one operand of two nodes: the operands are spelled already, so two that were one are one term and are paired, and following the two that are left decides nothing.
- **A list, kept by hand, of which reader reads through which operation.** It is a second statement of what the readers do, and it is wrong the first time a reader changes: a sum that is a factor of a stuck product is an atom of the product, which a list written from the linear view's name gets wrong.
- **One handle table threaded through every reader.** Every reader, the fold laws and the peels among them, would take a table it has no handles to keep in, and the classification already is the table: what holds a reader to an atom's identity is the generated grid.
- **A sorted spelling at the fold**, a commutative operation's operands held in one order so that two spellings are one term: a product's order is a rank and a sum keeps what was written, and the fold laws would have to hold under every order a later fold leaves.
- **A binder ranked by its level stored on its identity**: about 170 sites in the two checkers and the shared analyses mint binders and would each have to know their depth, a walk that opens binders it does not register in its checker's scope gives two of them one level, and a metavariable has no level. **Each minted identity made neutral where it is wasted** — every cache hit minting again what its computation minted, every display deferred, every probe accounted — is a list a later walk forgets, and a replay counts identities per path where a memo counts work per node, past the identity space on the graphs a memo serves. **Atoms ranked by first encounter**, as Mathlib's `AtomM` keeps them ([`Mathlib/Util/AtomM.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Util/AtomM.lean)): a normal form a fold writes would follow the order its atoms were written in. **A table's identities renumbered densely before it ranks**: every reader made two-phase, its atoms collected before any monomial is built. **Each identity spelled by where a walk first meets it and the ages listed after the shape in that order**: a spelling of the whole atom, which an atom around it spells again, the square of a spine's depth. **A tie walked over the two terms** to the first identity held apart: a rank that reads the terms at every comparison, where the summaries hold the answer.
- **A method on the driver that the chain calls mid-way.** The chain would call a judgment; handing the atoms back keeps it a function a test states by hand.
- **Forcing an atom's arguments before the readers read it**, as a first tier or alone: it reaches no difference in a proof argument or under a binder, and it is a second mechanism for a question the checker's conversion already answers.
- **A relevance mark on an argument**, so that identity could skip a proof: a second statement of what the parameter's type says, carried through every representation.
- **Normalising atoms under binders**, which misses a proof argument, costs a full normal form, and reduces among loose indices.
- **A congruence-closure store**, over the terms of a goal as Lean's `grind` keeps one, or over an arm's equations: it decides the consequences of two equations, which is hypotheses in conversion, and a different kernel.
- **Normal forms by evaluation with rules on neutral terms** (Allais, McBride and Boutillier, 2013), complete for the monoid and fusion laws of lists: it has no commutativity, and it is a rewrite of both conversions.
- **Picking a solution for an implicit with several.** A product keeps no written order to pick by, and any pick makes a program's meaning follow a declaration order.
- **Putting each pairing of two unpaired operands to the elaborator's sandbox and committing the one that alone converts.** It follows the tree a sum happens to be written as, finds no solution that is not already an operand — `x + 1` — and searches where the equation can be solved.
- **Solving a metavariable at a power.** At `Int` a square has two roots. At `Nat` the solution is the only one, but it is an exact root and no division, and nothing yet needs it; the roadmap keeps it.
- **Canonical forms**, a spelling per class so that identity is the relation: `Bool` has no normal form the decisions accept, irrelevance is typed, and an atom's arguments would need full normal forms.
- **Completing a fold or a lookup only where conversion compares.** A scrutinee inside reduction would still be decided by spelling.
- **Putting a stuck scrutinee to the checker's whole conversion against each literal.** It runs every reader of the chain twice where the question is the diagram's alone, and asks a judgment where the theory answers.
- **Excusing a dead arm as impossible**, where its case contradicts a guard around it: a new source of coverage, where refusing the proof in it is the recording rule both checkers already had.
- **Keeping a classed fold that decides no more.** A representative is picked by the order a walk met the atoms in, so the reduct would be a respelling no author wrote.
- **Taking a stuck fold again only where reduction is asked from outside**, one walk for a reduction: an operand's reduct would then follow whether it was reached as an operand or at the root.
- **One classification a scope that every fold writes into**: a representative would be whatever the scope classified first, so a reduct's spelling would follow the order terms were reduced in.
- **Representatives by rank, so two classifications join in any order**: it moves the representative this decision picks and every spelling written from one, where joining in the walk's order decides as the walk does.
- **A classification kept on the node, as its hash is**: it outlives the declaration, so a hit would let a neighbour pay for the readers' charged reads.
- **A classification copied at each level**: a read the budget does not count, the square over a spine, which is the walk again.
- **A node spelled as its normal form at the top of its spine**, distributed where it stands under no layer of the form: a product is spelled distributed under a power and as written under a sum, one class two spellings, and `(a + b) * (c + d) + n - (b + a) * (d + c)` no longer cancels to `n`; and the root pair the chain declined to distribute, a literal on its other side, is distributed, which runs a web of products out of its budget.
- **Every two nodes of one bucket read in their normal forms, the bucket keyed by operation and atoms alone**: every level of a web of products is a sum over one set of atoms, so each is distributed against each earlier one.
