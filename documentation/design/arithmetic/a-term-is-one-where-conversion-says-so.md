# A term is one where conversion says so

**Decision.** No verdict and no solution follows how a term is spelled, the order it holds its operands in, or the arm it stands in.

- **A term at a carrier is layers of the algebra over opaque atoms, classified once, from its leaves to its root.** What a layer is, and which operands it reads, is `Intrinsic::algebra`'s to say and no reader's: an intrinsic the table declares an operation, at a carrier whose values hold no type, is a node, and anything else is an opaque atom. The table declares every intrinsic a reader reads, so no second list says what is read through. The algebra's readers pair atoms by identity, and where they decide nothing of a pair it is classified (`curios-analysis`'s `classify`) and read again as the classification spells it. `curios-algebra` and the `atoms` table do not change: identity is still settled before a handle is handed out.
- **Two opaque atoms are one where the checker's own conversion says so.** The conversion chain hands a pair's opaque atoms back, each checker says which of them convert, and each is spelled as its class's representative.
- **A node is one with another where its own reader says so.** A node's operands are classified first and the node is spelled over them, then held to the earlier nodes of its operation by the shared readers alone, no judgment asked, taking the first one's spelling. So an operation one reader reads and the reader above it takes whole is one atom of the reader above wherever its own reader holds it one, and a checker is asked nothing about what a node's operands are. Every node is held but one standing under a node of its own operation where the law table declares that operation associative, whose reader flattens it. A node is read only against the earlier nodes that stand over its atoms, and an operation over truth values against every earlier one of its kind, the truth table holding two formulas one over two sets of atoms. A node the table leaves an operand out of, a quotient over the proof of its divisor, is its checker's: it is handed out as an atom once the operands its meaning reads are classified.
- **An atom's identity is its carrier's, stated once.** `curios-core`'s `same_atom` and `Atoms::handle` say what an atom of a carrier is known by, and every reader takes its handles and its direct comparisons from them. At a carrier whose values hold no type — `Nat`, `Int`, `Bool`, `Byte`, `Flt`, a packed sequence — a universe instance is no part of it, in both checkers. A list's element and a product's factor are known by the term as written.
- **Two calls of one name apart in a universe instance are asked about by their arguments.** A classing reads two applications of one head, up to its instances, as possibly one, and where the instance is all the heads differ in it puts each pair of arguments to the checker and holds the two one where every pair converts.
- **A classing asked for inside a classing is held inside the two atoms being compared.** Comparing two atoms may class in turn, and that classing asks only about atoms that stand inside one of the two and are neither, by one rule in both checkers (`curios-analysis`'s `Classes::of`).
- **A commutative operation pairs its operands.** For an operation the law table declares commutative, one operand of each side is paired by identity, in either position, and the other two are a sufficient residual. Operands nothing pairs are unpaired, which the kernel reads as unequal; they are never compared the first with the first and the second with the second. An ordered comparison, a difference and a concatenation keep that comparison, since position is what their operands mean.
- **A metavariable is solved where its equation has one solution, and never picked.** The elaborator solves the one unsolved metavariable an equation over `Nat` or `Int` is linear in to the quotient exact division gives, and puts that quotient to conversion. An equation with more than one solution — `?x * ?y` against `a * b` — stays undecided and is reported as a conversion that could not be decided, naming the implicits never solved.
- **A mismatch says what was not compared.** Where a mismatch ends on operands nothing paired, or on two `Bool` terms whose atoms pass the truth table's cap, its report says the two sides were not compared, which is another thing to tell an author than that they differ.
- **An equation answers the terms conversion holds equal to its scrutinee.** An arm's equation is recorded under its scrutinee as written, and one rule both reducers call says what it answers, `curios-analysis`'s `answers`: the scrutinee itself; a term the carriers' readers hold equal to it, or to its negation, the case negated; and, where the rule hands the question back, a term the checker's own conversion holds equal to it — the atoms of two operations of one kind classed and the pair read once more, or a stuck application, projection or `match` compared with the scrutinee's reduced spelling under the view that spelling was settled in. It is a lookup still: no consequence of two equations, no hypothesis, and an `==` guard does not equate its sides.
- **An equation follows the solution its arm is checked under**, in both checkers. Where an arm solves a variable, each equation in force that names it is restated at the solution, and the recorded one steps aside until the arm is left. The kernel substitutes the solution through the arm; the elaborator keeps the variable spelled, so it reads a key and a stuck form with every refined variable spelled as its value. An equation a solution leaves naming no local is recorded by neither checker, and a proof resting on its guard, in an arm no value reaches, is refused where it is written.
- **A stuck fold is taken again over what the classification holds one.** Once every equation in force has been asked, a stuck operation the algebra declares is classified, its opaque atoms by its checker and its nodes by their readers, and its fold is taken over one spelling to a class, kept only where the result is no longer that operation. The classification itself folds nothing: a fold is a reducer's to take. A quotient and a remainder are atoms of two kinds, which Euclid's recombination pairs by their dividend and divisor and nothing asks about each other, so the dividend and the divisor of each are classified before the division is handed out.
- **There are two reductions.** A judgment's reduction asks its checker's conversion at those two points and nowhere else. Plain reduction asks nothing: it answers every question a judgment's reduction puts, and every shared analysis reads a term by it (`Env::force`). Each keeps its own answers, and one read under an equation lives as long as the equation.
- **An answer the solver can change is not kept.** The elaborator's question is answered no where a yes would commit a solution or a level constraint, and it joins no two levels to reach one: two instances apart in a level the store does not read as one are compared by their value, reduced and unfolded as the kernel reads two instances it finds apart, and are one where that makes them one. A weak equation, which requires nothing of two levels a nominal family is irrelevant in, is no commitment. No reduct is remembered across a question so declined, or answered no past two levels it left apart, an equation's reduced spelling settled across one is settled again once either solver has moved, and a scope that put a question and is rolled back with level constraints withdrawn clears what was reduced inside it. The kernel is handed ground instances and has no solver.

**Rationale.**

- **Conversion is transitive only where atom identity contains it.** `curios-algebra` decides over handles by normal forms, which is transitive over handles. Two terms that convert and are handed two handles — one call under two proofs of its bound, two functions whose bodies commute a sum, two stuck `match`es whose arms do — break that over terms: the first converts with a second and the second with a third by one residual each, and the first with the third leaves two atoms unpaired on each side. The relation wanted is the one Coq Modulo Theory defines, a term's cap compared in the theory and its aliens by conversion, the whole transitive (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017), and the identity is the one Mathlib's `AtomM` keeps, an atom being a class of terms up to definitional equality.
- **A union of theories is decided from each theory's own procedure, from the leaves up.** The word problem of a union of equational theories over disjoint signatures is decided by abstracting a term's alien subterms from its leaves to its root, removing a collapse and identifying two variables equated to equivalent pure terms, and the combination is polynomial past the theories' own cost (Baader and Tinelli, [*Deciding the Word Problem in the Union of Equational Theories*](https://homepage.cs.uiowa.edu/~tinelli/papers/BaaTin-RR-98.pdf), after Pigozzi, 1974); canonizers of disjoint theories combine where solvers do not (Rueß and Shankar, [*Combining Shostak Theories*](https://www.csl.sri.com/people/shankar/rta02.pdf), 2002). The carriers' readers are such procedures, and an operation of one standing under another's is an alien of it: classified first, by its own reader, it is one atom of the reader above by construction. Read as an opaque atom instead, it is known by its spelling, so `Nat/and(x, y) - Nat/and(y, x)` is no `0`, a hypothesis over it is refused at its other spelling in one step and accepted through an annotation, and each of conversion, a stuck fold, an arm's equation and the bound prover misses it its own way.
- **A level is no part of a value that holds no type.** Core has no elimination from a type or a level into a number, a truth value, a byte, a float or a packed sequence, so two terms of such a carrier that are one up to universe instances denote one value ([Case equations and their key](../soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)). Stated by each reader for itself, the rule held for a number's summands and not for a truth value's leaves or for the operands a fold compares; stated once for a carrier, every reader holds it, and the generated law grid holds every reader to it over calls written apart. A list holds values of any type, a type among them, so a level can be part of one. Two calls of one name are then apart in nothing a value reads where their arguments convert, and a checker handed the two whole would compare their levels first, the elaborator joining what the kernel is later handed apart; so the calls are asked about by their arguments, in both checkers.
- **Classings nest no deeper than a term does.** Each pair a classing asks about is smaller than the pair it was asked for, so the nesting ends. Unbounded it need not: two calls of a recursive function that differ in an argument unfold to two stuck `match`es whose arms hold the same two calls one step on, the factors of a product nothing pairs, so comparing one pair classes the next and nothing ends it but the stack. Those atoms come of an unfolding and stand inside nothing that was asked, so they stay two atoms, in the refusing direction.
- **Two checkers agree only where conversion is transitive.** The elaborator takes an annotation as a step and the kernel compares the first type with the last, so a relation that is not transitive lets one path through and refuses the other, and the refusal reaches the user as the kernel's.
- **The order a term holds two operands in is a spelling or a hash.** A sum keeps its summands as they were written, and a product's factors and an equality's sides stand in the order of their structural hashes, which moves with the order binders are declared in. A positional congruence turned either into a verdict, and where an operand was a metavariable into a solution, so what a call meant followed a declaration order.
- **The chain still calls no judgment.** The elaborator's comparison enqueues and may solve, and the kernel's runs inside the pairs its goal is unfolding, so the chain hands the atoms back and is entered again with the partition: a function of a pair and a partition, as `curios-algebra` is one of handles. Each checker classes under its own state — the kernel inside the pairs of the goal the atoms belong to, the elaborator classing only what converts with nothing committed.
- **A solution that is the only one is not a pick.** Conversion holds two sums of products equal where their normal forms agree, so over the equation's atoms the equation is one in a ring of polynomials, which has no zero divisors: an equation linear in one unknown has at most one solution. GHC's `ghc-typelits-natnormalise` derives unifiers over its sum-of-products form the same way. The solver proposes and conversion decides, so it stands outside the trusted base, where a search belongs.
- **The laws and the guards have to compose.** The algebra makes `n + m` and `m + n` one term, and a guard makes its scrutinee `true` in its arm. Answering the guard by spelling left `m + n < k` stuck under `match n + m < k`: conversion held the two comparisons equal outside the arm and refused them inside it, where one had become `true`. An arm's equation is a claim about a term, and which terms are that term is conversion's to say.
- **Conversion is a congruence only where a fold reads atoms as it does.** `f(a + b)` and `f(b + a)` convert. A fold that pairs atoms by identity leaves `f(a + b) == f(b + a)` stuck, so a term and the same term with one atom respelled do not convert, and a `match` on the comparison does not reduce.
- **The two checkers agree only where they read one rule.** Each had a lookup of its own, written by hand, and they drifted: the kernel substituted an arm's solution through a body whose equations still named the variable, and the elaborator reduced a missed key's arguments where nothing in the kernel did, so each refused a guard's fact the other accepted. The rule is a function in the shared layer, as the chain is, and each checker reaches it from the same probes: the written key, and the stuck form reduction leaves.
- **The store stays a lookup.** An equation is extended only along conversion, to terms already equal to its scrutinee. It decides no consequence of two equations and takes no hypothesis, so what keeps hypotheses out of conversion still holds ([A bound is stated in a decided proposition and discharged by reduction](a-bound-is-stated-in-a-decided-proposition-and-discharged-by-reduction.md)).
- **Whether a term is total is read on no verdict of conversion's.** Conversion's proof irrelevance is sound because a program is accepted only once totality has passed ([Totality of the erased program](../soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)), which holds only while deciding whether a term is total invokes no conversion. The analyses that decide it reduce through `Env::force` alone, so `Env::force` is plain reduction in both checkers, and plain reduction has to exist whatever answers a question. Which terms owe totality is the judgment's to say, by the reduction typing itself used, so none it took for a proof or a type escapes.
- **A question is answered by plain reduction**, which costs no mechanism the point above does not already need. It is one conversion the budget bounds, over the relation the board already argues for, so its soundness is a corollary of entries that stand; it terminates wherever conversion terminates; and an answer is a function of the term, the definitions, the equations in force and which of the two reductions read it. Coq Modulo Theory reduces modulo a relation defined before reduction is (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017), which is this shape; the smart case defines normalisation and convertibility under an arm's equations mutually (Altenkirch, 2011).
- **An equation reaches a term that names no local of its own.** Read up to conversion, the equation of `g(p)` answers `g(x)` for another proof `x` of one proposition, so nothing read under an equation is the declaration's to keep: the kernel files a sort and an erased half read under one with the scope, as it files a typing ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts)).
- **What a lookup reads, it does not reduce.** Both terms reach the rule reduced. The kernel's memo replays the identities a reduction minted on every hit, so a forcing repeated at every stuck form is counted at every nesting level.
- **A remembered answer is a function of what it is filed under.** The elaborator files a reduct by its term, the equations in force and the reduction that took it, and a question's answer rests on the solver's state as well. Remembered across a question declined for a commit, a stuck reduct would make acceptance follow the order terms were reduced in; remembered past a constraint a scope withdrew, a decided one would be an equation the kernel does not have.
- **A level a value does not read keeps no two terms apart.** Every written use of a universe-polymorphic name mints a level that stays open until its declaration closes ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md)), and a concept that states a proposition among its fields hands that proposition's level to each of its witnesses, so two written quotients are two instances of one witness. Conversion's shortcut holds two spellings apart in levels alone one by joining the levels, which a question may not do; answered no there, two atoms each holding a quotient are two atoms until their declaration closes, though each quotient reduces to one operation at every level. Compared by value, the pair is decided as Rocq's conversion decides it, where two applications of one constant that fail on a universe comparison have "the terms are unfolded" ([reference manual, *Universe polymorphism*](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/master/refman/addendum/universe-polymorphism.html)), and as Lean's `unfoldBothDefEq` does, "First try to solve unification by unifying arguments" and "If it fails, unfold both and continue" ([`Lean/Meta/ExprDefEq.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Meta/ExprDefEq.lean)). A yes so reached holds whatever the two levels close at, so it is kept; a no can become a yes once they are one, so it is not. Two projections of one recursive group reproduce their levels under every unfolding, so no value decides them and they stay two.

**Rejected.**

- **Refusing wherever two operands are unpaired, with no classing.** It makes a verdict stable and leaves conversion non-transitive, and it refuses programs without accepting any.
- **Classing nothing inside a classing.** It ends the nesting, and refuses `f(g(a + b) * h(c + d)) * f(g(c + a) * h(b + d))` against its commutation, two atoms to a side each holding two to class, where the same equation one atom to a side is held.
- **Leaving apart two calls of a function whose calls are being compared.** An atom reaches a classing forced, as the stuck `match` its call unfolds to, and names no function.
- **Charging the nesting and letting the budget end it.** A comparison that unfolds without end spends its budget over minutes, so a wrong equation would wait that long to be refused.
- **A node put to the checker**, two operations of one kind asked about as two atoms are: comparing them classes their operands in turn, so the cost doubles with each level of nesting, and a wrong equation between two nested terms waits on its budget to be refused.
- **Every node the readers read through handed out as an atom as well.** The questions put over `/std` grow several times over before anything new is decided.
- **Every node held**, a node under its own associative operation among them: each inner conjunction of a long one is then put to the truth table against the others, where the table reads the whole conjunction as one formula.
- **Only a node standing under a node of another operation held.** `Nat/and(Nat/and(x, y), Nat/and(y, x))` holds two nodes under their own operation, which its reader does not flatten.
- **A node read against every earlier node of its operation.** A sum of two hundred operations is then read pair by pair at every fold above it, and does not answer.
- **A node's own fold taken by the classification**, wherever an operand of it moved: every fold a program needs is one a reducer takes again at its stuck fold, and a fold the classification took would decide, inside a question, what a question's plain reduction leaves stuck.
- **A residual followed through the readers in turn**, where a reader pairs all but one operand of two nodes: the operands are spelled already, so two that were one are one term and are paired, and following the two that are left decides nothing.
- **A list, kept by hand, of which reader reads through which operation.** It is a second statement of what the readers do, and it is wrong the first time a reader changes: a sum that is a factor of a stuck product is an atom of the product, which a list written from the linear view's name gets wrong.
- **One handle table threaded through every reader.** Every reader, the fold laws and the peels among them, would take a table it has no handles to keep in, and the classification already is the table: what holds a reader to an atom's identity is the generated grid.
- **The elaborator joining the levels of two calls a classing meets apart.** An answer the solver can change is not kept, the kernel has no solver, and where a call is commuted or repeated the levels close apart, so the kernel refuses what the elaborator accepted.
- **Parking a pair while a level metavariable stands in it.** It accepts only the pairs whose sides close as one term.
- **A vector for a recursive member**, so that two instances apart in a level it does not read are a weak equation: a member reproduces its levels under every unfolding, and the universe decision rejects it.
- **A sorted spelling at the fold**, a commutative operation's operands held in one order so that two spellings are one term: a product's order is a hash and a sum keeps what was written, and the fold laws would have to hold under every order a later fold leaves.
- **A method on the driver that the chain calls mid-way.** The chain would call a judgment; handing the atoms back keeps it a function a test states by hand.
- **Forcing an atom's arguments before the readers read it**, as a first tier or alone: it reaches no difference in a proof argument or under a binder, and it is a second mechanism for a question the checker's conversion already answers.
- **A relevance mark on an argument**, so that identity could skip a proof: a second statement of what the parameter's type says, carried through every representation.
- **Normalising atoms under binders**, which misses a proof argument, costs a full normal form, and reduces among loose indices.
- **A congruence-closure store**, over the terms of a goal as Lean's `grind` keeps one, or over an arm's equations: it decides the consequences of two equations, which is hypotheses in conversion, and a different kernel.
- **Normal forms by evaluation with rules on neutral terms** (Allais, McBride and Boutillier, 2013), complete for the monoid and fusion laws of lists: it has no commutativity, and it is a rewrite of both conversions.
- **Picking a solution for an implicit with several.** A product keeps no written order to pick by, and any pick makes a program's meaning follow a declaration order.
- **Putting each pairing of two unpaired operands to the elaborator's sandbox and committing the one that alone converts.** It follows the tree a sum happens to be written as, finds no solution that is not already an operand — `x + 1` — and searches where the equation can be solved.
- **Solving a metavariable at a power.** At `Int` a square has two roots. At `Nat` the solution is the only one, but it is an exact root and no division, and nothing yet needs it; the roadmap keeps it.
- **Canonical forms**, a spelling per class so that identity is the relation: `Bool` has no normal form the decisions accept, irrelevance is typed, and an atom's arguments would need full normal forms.
- **Completing a fold or a lookup only where conversion compares.** A scrutinee inside reduction would still be decided by spelling.
- **Excusing a dead arm as impossible**, where its case contradicts a guard around it: a new source of coverage, where refusing the proof in it is the recording rule both checkers already had.
- **Reduction and conversion defined mutually, bounded by the budget alone.** It is complete to any depth with no argument for its soundness to stand on, and a question is a comparison of its own, inside no unfolding, so two recursive calls a comparison leaves apart inside their own unfolding would be unfolded again by every question asked there, until the budget ran out.
- **A fixpoint with cycles detected**, a lookup already standing for a term declining. A reduct taken while one declined is less reduced than the same term reduced outside it, so every memo and settled spelling would need to know, a verdict would follow the order terms were reduced in, and the regress above is no cycle it would see, each round opening fresh binders.
- **A third reduction**, answering a question inside a question: the same argument once more, for a program nothing has yet written.
- **Keeping a classed fold that decides no more.** A representative is picked by the order a walk met the atoms in, so the reduct would be a respelling no author wrote.
- **Keeping a local-free type's sort for the declaration under an equation**, on the argument that no equation is about a term over binders the classification opens: true of an equation read by spelling, and false of one read up to conversion.
- **Reducing a missed key's arguments in the elaborator**, where the kernel restates. The two lookups then differ in what reaches the shared rule, an allowance caps work the kernel does not do, and a fact written at a case value is met only through whatever the refinements live at the probe reduce.
- **Keeping a recorded equation beside its restated instance.** The equations in force double at each nested match on a variable one names.
- **Answering no wherever a yes would join two levels**, the shortcut's commitment discarded with its verdict: two written quotients are then two atoms, and a hypothesis is refused at a goal that is its own type one unfolding on.
- **Joining the two levels weakly where the sides are numbers.** A weak equation requires nothing, so where a declaration's closing drops one the kernel is handed two instances of a recursive member apart in a level it compares; and the rule is keyed on a type, which leaves the same pair two under any other operation.
- **Clearing the elaborator's reducts wherever a rollback moved a level**, whatever was asked inside. A witness probe rolls back after every trial and no rule of reduction reads a level, so a scope that put no question has nothing to clear.
