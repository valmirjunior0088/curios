# A term is one where conversion says so

**Decision.** No verdict and no solution follows how a term is spelled, the order it holds its operands in, or the arm it stands in.

- **Two atoms are one where the checker's own conversion says so.** The algebra's readers pair atoms by identity. Where they decide nothing of a pair, the conversion chain hands the pair's atoms back, each checker says which of them convert, and the pair is read again with each atom spelled as its class's representative. `curios-algebra` and the `atoms` table do not change: identity is still settled before a handle is handed out.
- **A commutative operation pairs its operands.** For an operation the law table declares commutative, one operand of each side is paired by identity, in either position, and the other two are a sufficient residual. Operands nothing pairs are unpaired, which the kernel reads as unequal; they are never compared the first with the first and the second with the second. An ordered comparison, a difference and a concatenation keep that comparison, since position is what their operands mean.
- **A metavariable is solved where its equation has one solution, and never picked.** The elaborator solves the one unsolved metavariable an equation over `Nat` or `Int` is linear in to the quotient exact division gives, and puts that quotient to conversion. An equation with more than one solution — `?x * ?y` against `a * b` — stays undecided and is reported as a conversion that could not be decided, naming the implicits never solved.
- **A mismatch says what was not compared.** Where a mismatch ends on operands nothing paired, or on two `Bool` terms whose atoms pass the truth table's cap, its report says the two sides were not compared, which is another thing to tell an author than that they differ.
- **An equation answers the terms conversion holds equal to its scrutinee.** An arm's equation is recorded under its scrutinee as written, and one rule both reducers call says what it answers, `curios-analysis`'s `answers`: the scrutinee itself; a term the carriers' readers hold equal to it, or to its negation, the case negated; and, where the rule hands the question back, a term the checker's own conversion holds equal to it — the atoms of two operations of one kind classed and the pair read once more, or a stuck application, projection or `match` compared with the scrutinee's reduced spelling under the view that spelling was settled in. It is a lookup still: no consequence of two equations, no hypothesis, and an `==` guard does not equate its sides.
- **An equation follows the solution its arm is checked under**, in both checkers. Where an arm solves a variable, each equation in force that names it is restated at the solution, and the recorded one steps aside until the arm is left. The kernel substitutes the solution through the arm; the elaborator keeps the variable spelled, so it reads a key and a stuck form with every refined variable spelled as its value. An equation a solution leaves naming no local is recorded by neither checker, and a proof resting on its guard, in an arm no value reaches, is refused where it is written.
- **A stuck fold is taken again over the atoms conversion holds one.** Once every equation in force has been asked, a stuck operation whose fold pairs atoms has them classed by its checker, and the fold is taken over one spelling to a class, kept only where the result is no longer that operation.
- **There are two reductions.** A judgment's reduction asks its checker's conversion at those two points and nowhere else. Plain reduction asks nothing: it answers every question a judgment's reduction puts, and every shared analysis reads a term by it (`Env::force`). Each keeps its own answers, and one read under an equation lives as long as the equation.
- **An answer the solver can change is not kept.** The elaborator's question is answered no where a yes would commit a solution or a level constraint; a weak equation, which requires nothing of two levels a nominal family is irrelevant in, is no commitment. No reduct is remembered across a question so declined, an equation's reduced spelling settled across one is settled again once either solver has moved, and a scope that put a question and is rolled back with level constraints withdrawn clears what was reduced inside it. The kernel is handed ground instances and has no solver.

**Rationale.**

- **Conversion is transitive only where atom identity contains it.** `curios-algebra` decides over handles by normal forms, which is transitive over handles. Two terms that convert and are handed two handles — one call under two proofs of its bound, two functions whose bodies commute a sum, two stuck `match`es whose arms do — break that over terms: the first converts with a second and the second with a third by one residual each, and the first with the third leaves two atoms unpaired on each side. The relation wanted is the one Coq Modulo Theory defines, a term's cap compared in the theory and its aliens by conversion, the whole transitive (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017), and the identity is the one Mathlib's `AtomM` keeps, an atom being a class of terms up to definitional equality.
- **Two checkers agree only where conversion is transitive.** The elaborator takes an annotation as a step and the kernel compares the first type with the last, so a relation that is not transitive lets one path through and refuses the other, and the refusal reaches the user as the kernel's.
- **The order a term holds two operands in is a spelling or a hash.** A sum keeps its summands as they were written, and a product's factors and an equality's sides stand in the order of their structural hashes, which moves with the order binders are declared in. A positional congruence turned either into a verdict, and where an operand was a metavariable into a solution, so what a call meant followed a declaration order.
- **The chain still calls no judgment.** The elaborator's comparison enqueues and may solve, and the kernel's runs under the history of the goal it is inside, so the chain hands the atoms back and is entered again with the partition: a function of a pair and a partition, as `curios-algebra` is one of handles. Each checker classes under its own state — the kernel leaving apart two atoms whose comparison is already in progress, the elaborator classing only what converts with nothing committed.
- **A solution that is the only one is not a pick.** Conversion holds two sums of products equal where their normal forms agree, so over the equation's atoms the equation is one in a ring of polynomials, which has no zero divisors: an equation linear in one unknown has at most one solution. GHC's `ghc-typelits-natnormalise` derives unifiers over its sum-of-products form the same way. The solver proposes and conversion decides, so it stands outside the trusted base, where a search belongs.
- **The laws and the guards have to compose.** The algebra makes `n + m` and `m + n` one term, and a guard makes its scrutinee `true` in its arm. Answering the guard by spelling left `m + n < k` stuck under `match n + m < k`: conversion held the two comparisons equal outside the arm and refused them inside it, where one had become `true`. An arm's equation is a claim about a term, and which terms are that term is conversion's to say.
- **Conversion is a congruence only where a fold reads atoms as it does.** `f(a + b)` and `f(b + a)` convert. A fold that pairs atoms by identity leaves `f(a + b) == f(b + a)` stuck, so a term and the same term with one atom respelled do not convert, and a `match` on the comparison does not reduce.
- **The two checkers agree only where they read one rule.** Each had a lookup of its own, written by hand, and they drifted: the kernel substituted an arm's solution through a body whose equations still named the variable, and the elaborator reduced a missed key's arguments where nothing in the kernel did, so each refused a guard's fact the other accepted. The rule is a function in the shared layer, as the chain is, and each checker reaches it from the same probes: the written key, and the stuck form reduction leaves.
- **The store stays a lookup.** An equation is extended only along conversion, to terms already equal to its scrutinee. It decides no consequence of two equations and takes no hypothesis, so what keeps hypotheses out of conversion still holds ([A bound is stated in a decided proposition and discharged by reduction](a-bound-is-stated-in-a-decided-proposition-and-discharged-by-reduction.md)).
- **Whether a term is total is read on no verdict of conversion's.** Conversion's proof irrelevance and its recurrence rule are sound because a program is accepted only once totality has passed ([Totality of the erased program](../soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)), which holds only while deciding whether a term is total invokes no conversion. The analyses that decide it reduce through `Env::force` alone, so `Env::force` is plain reduction in both checkers, and plain reduction has to exist whatever answers a question. Which terms owe totality is the judgment's to say, by the reduction typing itself used, so none it took for a proof or a type escapes.
- **A question is answered by plain reduction**, which costs no mechanism the point above does not already need. It is one conversion the budget bounds, over the relation the board already argues for, so its soundness is a corollary of entries that stand; it terminates wherever conversion terminates; and an answer is a function of the term, the definitions, the equations in force and which of the two reductions read it. Coq Modulo Theory reduces modulo a relation defined before reduction is (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017), which is this shape; the smart case defines normalisation and convertibility under an arm's equations mutually (Altenkirch, 2011).
- **An equation reaches a term that names no local of its own.** Read up to conversion, the equation of `g(p)` answers `g(x)` for another proof `x` of one proposition, so nothing read under an equation is the declaration's to keep: the kernel files a sort and an erased half read under one with the scope, as it files a typing ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts)).
- **What a lookup reads, it does not reduce.** Both terms reach the rule reduced. The kernel's memo replays the identities a reduction minted on every hit, so a forcing repeated at every stuck form is counted at every nesting level.
- **A remembered answer is a function of what it is filed under.** The elaborator files a reduct by its term, the equations in force and the reduction that took it, and a question's answer rests on the solver's state as well. Remembered across a question declined for a commit, a stuck reduct would make acceptance follow the order terms were reduced in; remembered past a constraint a scope withdrew, a decided one would be an equation the kernel does not have.

**Rejected.**

- **Refusing wherever two operands are unpaired, with no classing.** It makes a verdict stable and leaves conversion non-transitive, and it refuses programs without accepting any.
- **A method on the driver that the chain calls mid-way.** The chain would call a judgment; handing the atoms back keeps it a function a test states by hand.
- **Forcing an atom's arguments before the readers read it**, as a first tier or alone: it reaches no difference in a proof argument or under a binder, and it is a second mechanism for a question the checker's conversion already answers.
- **A relevance mark on an argument**, so that identity could skip a proof: a second statement of what the parameter's type says, carried through every representation.
- **Normalising atoms under binders**, which misses a proof argument, costs a full normal form, and reduces among loose indices.
- **A congruence-closure store**, over the terms of a goal as Lean's `grind` keeps one, or over an arm's equations: it decides the consequences of two equations, which is hypotheses in conversion, and a different kernel.
- **Normal forms by evaluation with rules on neutral terms** (Allais, McBride and Boutillier, 2013), complete for the monoid and fusion laws of lists: it has no commutativity, and it is a rewrite of both conversions.
- **Picking a solution for an implicit with several.** A product keeps no written order to pick by, and any pick makes a program's meaning follow a declaration order.
- **Putting each pairing of two unpaired operands to the elaborator's sandbox and committing the one that alone converts.** It follows the tree a sum happens to be written as, finds no solution that is not already an operand — `x + 1` — and searches where the equation can be solved.
- **Solving a metavariable at a power.** At `Int` a square has two roots. At `Nat` the solution is the only one, but it is an exact root and no division, and nothing yet needs it; the roadmap keeps it.
- **Canonical forms**, a spelling per class so that identity is the relation: `Bool` has no normal form the decisions accept, irrelevance is typed, and an atom's arguments would need full normal forms.
- **Completing a fold or a lookup only where conversion compares.** A scrutinee inside reduction would still be decided by spelling.
- **Excusing a dead arm as impossible**, where its case contradicts a guard around it: a new source of coverage, where refusing the proof in it is the recording rule both checkers already had.
- **Reduction and conversion defined mutually, bounded by the budget alone.** It is complete to any depth with no argument for its soundness to stand on, and a question starts a history of its own, so a comparison of two recursive calls that the recurrence rule closes would regress until the budget ran out.
- **A fixpoint with cycles detected**, a lookup already standing for a term declining. A reduct taken while one declined is less reduced than the same term reduced outside it, so every memo and settled spelling would need to know, a verdict would follow the order terms were reduced in, and the regress above is no cycle it would see, each round opening fresh binders.
- **A third reduction**, answering a question inside a question: the same argument once more, for a program nothing has yet written.
- **Keeping a classed fold that decides no more.** A representative is picked by the order a walk met the atoms in, so the reduct would be a respelling no author wrote.
- **Keeping a local-free type's sort for the declaration under an equation**, on the argument that no equation is about a term over binders the classification opens: true of an equation read by spelling, and false of one read up to conversion.
- **Reducing a missed key's arguments in the elaborator**, where the kernel restates. The two lookups then differ in what reaches the shared rule, an allowance caps work the kernel does not do, and a fact written at a case value is met only through whatever the refinements live at the probe reduce.
- **Keeping a recorded equation beside its restated instance.** The equations in force double at each nested match on a variable one names.
- **Clearing the elaborator's reducts wherever a rollback moved a level**, whatever was asked inside. A witness probe rolls back after every trial and no rule of reduction reads a level, so a scope that put no question has nothing to clear.
