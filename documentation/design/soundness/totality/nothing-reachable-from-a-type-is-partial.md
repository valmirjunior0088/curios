# Nothing reachable from a type is partial

**Assumes.** Obligation (T) of [Totality of the erased program](../totality-of-the-erased-program.md): every definition reachable from a term in a type position is total, decided per recursive group by size-change termination over the calls the kernel types ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-groups-calls-are-the-ones-the-kernel-types)).

**Status.** **probed**, at the engine's accepting side conditions.

- **The arithmetic rung licenses a decrease on two facts, both attacked.** Zero must be ruled out — by a `Nat` dispatch, or by a boolean arm from a comparison against a literal, spelled bare, with its operands flipped and behind a definition only unfolding exposes — and the right operand must be a literal at or above the least value that shrinks. `tests::soundness::an_arithmetic_decrease_needs_a_literal_operand_that_shrinks` holds both halves, with `a_division_by_a_literal_above_one_licenses_the_decrease` accepting; `a_boolean_guard_that_does_not_exclude_zero_licenses_no_decrease` and `a_nonzero_fact_does_not_escape_the_arm_that_established_it` hold the fact's extent to its arm.
- **The local gate's sort test reduces.** `check_group` refuses a group that does not descend when a member is erased: its declared type is a proposition, or yields a sort, decided by reducing at every step and answering yes on a reduction that fails, the refusing direction. `tests::soundness::a_sort_reached_through_an_alias_still_needs_descent`, with `an_aliased_sort_that_descends_is_still_accepted` as the control.
- **An application of a constructor payload reads as the payload.** A recursive call through `below(m, lt)`, where `below` is the function-typed payload an arm bound out of `intro(x, below)`, descends: the size order reads a value as a finite tree, a function-typed payload is a branching node whose children are its applications, and (T) and (V) are what make the payload finite wherever the verdict is consulted. The head is looked up in a set the arm enters and leaves like its refinement, so a parameter applied to anything stays unread. `tests::soundness::a_call_through_a_constructor_payload_descends`, with `a_call_through_a_parameter_bound_function_does_not_descend`; `curios-analysis`'s `tests/driven.rs::an_application_of_a_constructor_payload_descends_only_from_its_arm` and `totality/tests.rs::an_application_of_a_constructor_payload_grades_below_the_constructor`.
- **A redex is graded as its contractum.** A lambda at a spine's head and a `let` bind a name to a term in hand, and the walk reads the body with each binder standing for that term, so a convoy keeps its descent visible; the arguments are walked on their own first, so a binder the body never uses drops no call. `tests/driven.rs::a_lambda_applied_to_the_arm_payload_descends` and `a_let_alias_of_the_arm_payload_descends`, and `tests::soundness::a_hand_written_convoy_keeps_its_descent_visible` and `a_let_alias_keeps_its_descent_visible`.
- **A projection into another group is a call into it.** Two groups calling each other through a projection would each close to no call and classify total while the pair diverges: `tests::soundness::a_proof_looping_through_a_projected_inner_group_is_rejected`, with `a_proof_projecting_an_inner_group_that_does_not_call_back_is_accepted`.
