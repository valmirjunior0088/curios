# Nothing at a proposition is partial

**Assumes.** Obligation (V) of [Totality of the erased program](../totality-of-the-erased-program.md): every definition reachable from a term at a `Prop`-sorted type is total, decided in the certifier from the kernel's own typing.

**Evidence.** Probed.

- **The refusal has two routes, both probed.** The position walk asks first whether a position reaches a definition known partial, which blames a global, and failing that whether the term is partial in itself, carrying a non-descending `rec` group or an `Exit`. The anonymous route is reached from no surface spelling, each being refused during elaboration: `recheck::proposition_tests::an_exit_inside_a_proof_is_refused_with_no_definition_to_blame`, with `a_proof_carrying_the_unit_value_is_accepted` as the control. An `Exit` isolates the route, since a non-descending `rec` at a proof type is refused by `check_group`'s own gate first, while an exit meets no gate and types at the unit.
- **A member selection is its group with a tail.** Core has one recursion form, so the rule that checks a group is the rule that checks an occurrence of it: every member's declared type verified, every body checked against it, descent required where a member is erased, each member assumed under a fresh binder identity with the bodies opened over those, so a member occurrence inside a body is an ordinary local gated by the scope. A node carrying its own copy of the group, answering the member's declared type outright, is not well formed standing alone. `recheck::recursion_tests::a_recursive_member_is_certified_only_with_its_group`, with `a_member_of_a_legal_group_is_still_accepted` as the control. `kernel::sort::tests::a_groups_claim_about_its_member_does_not_decide_that_members_sort` pins the one arm reading a group's claim unchecked: the classifier reduces first, so it reports the member body's own sort.
- **Every `Prop`-typed term is inspected, however it arrived.** A metavariable solution reaches the module through unification or resolution rather than settling, and a partial proof supplied as the witness of a `Prop`-sorted concept is refused by (V): `tests::soundness::a_partial_proof_cannot_arrive_through_witness_resolution`. The kernel seeds from its own typing walk ([Recorded positions and sort-hood](recorded-positions-and-sort-hood.md)).
