# The certifier's totality record

**Assumes.** A totality classification a walk reads for a definition already in scope was reached by the certifier's own walk over the unit holding it, closed over everything the definition mentions, and filed with that unit — `curios_core::Certification`, which only `curios_cert::certify_module` makes.

**Evidence.** Probed, at each way a later walk can be handed a classification. (T) and (V) close over what an erased position mentions, which runs out of the environment as readily as out of the module, so a `Total` on a partial definition in scope would let a proof reaching it pass (V).

- **A record is read only where it covers its unit.** `Globals::of` and `Globals::mount` seed the non-total set from a covering record and otherwise hold the unit's items unclassified, for `partial_definitions` to classify from their terms; nothing on that route reads elaboration's stamp. `recheck::totality_tests::a_carried_totality_stamp_is_ignored_where_the_certifiers_record_classifies` mounts an honest record beside a library whose stamp lies; `a_unit_mounted_with_an_empty_record_is_classified_by_the_reading_walk` mounts it with an empty record; `a_record_that_does_not_cover_its_unit_is_not_read` with a forged record naming the lying definition `Total` and nothing else.
- **A record travels with its unit.** It is a field of the `Unit`, made only by certifying an `Uncertified` one, filed and found as the unit is ([Cached verdicts](../admission/cached-verdicts.md)). `curios-pipeline`'s `certification_tests::a_compiled_unit_files_the_certifiers_record_of_every_definition`, `curios-verdicts`'s `a_reused_unit_brings_its_certification_back`, `an_item_level_recompile_keeps_the_baselines_record_for_what_it_reuses`, and `the_restored_prelude_carries_a_record_covering_every_definition` hold each route.
- **A stamp is read only as a disagreement.** The kernel compares elaboration's stamp on an item it judges with its own closed verdict: `a_totality_stamp_contradicted_only_by_the_closure_is_refused`, with `an_honest_stamp_on_a_definition_reaching_a_partial_one_is_accepted` as the control. The erased representation's termination flag, which pruning, rebasing and the Cont lowerer rest on, is marked from the same records and never by erasure: `curios-elab`'s `into_ersd::tests::erasure_marks_no_function_total_whatever_its_stamp` and `a_record_marks_the_functions_its_total_definitions_erased_to`, and `curios-pipeline`'s `certification_tests::a_sealed_program_marks_its_functions_from_the_certifiers_records`.
- **A recorded `Partial` may be the budget's; a recorded `Total` cannot be.** A read the budget refuses answers unread, so a group whose descent shows only behind an unfolding may classify `Partial` under a small budget, and the record carries that to later compiles — the refusing direction, withholding a use. A typing the budget refuses refuses the item rather than dropping a call, and a refused read removes a decrease and never an edge, so running out cannot manufacture `Total`.
