# An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts

**Decision.** The elaborator makes the language usable, and the certifier only checks. The elaborator invents: it infers what an author leaves out, solves metavariables, resolves witnesses and writes derived ones, fills a bound reduction decides and proves one the facts in scope imply, and settles universe levels. Everything it invents reaches the certifier as an ordinary term, and erasure comes after, on obligations the certifier checks. The certifier decides from a finished term alone whether it is well typed and invents nothing: no metavariable, no search, no inference beyond typing what is written. What elaboration records beside a term, a totality stamp or a concept's name, can make it refuse and never accept. It judges every declaration it is handed, and refuses a name declared twice: by one module, or under a name its environment holds, whatever is declared there. The two requirements are opposite by design — the elaborator accepts what an author means, the certifier only what a term proves — as a proof assistant's tactics expand into the terms its kernel checks, the separation the de Bruijn criterion asks for (Barendregt and Wiedijk, 2005).

Where both must judge alike they share one layer, and a rule in it is one rule for both:

- `curios-core`: what a term *is* — the representation, the intrinsic folds, the closed machine, the linear views comparisons are read through, and the rule every probe keeps.
- `curios-num`: the scalar semantics of the numeric and packed carriers.
- `curios-algebra`: the carriers' algebra over abstract atoms.
- `curios-analysis`: the rules both run behind the `Env`/`Judge` seam — index inversion, the singleton determination walk with the instance a pinned constructor builds, which locals a case's solution re-types, the reading of a term through local definitions, strict positivity, size-change totality's grading and closure, which spellings of a scrutinee carry an arm's case equation, the erasure-shape queries, and the chain conversion runs when two intrinsics meet.

Each checker supplies its own reduction, unfolding and fresh binders through `Env`. Each depends on the shared layer and, outside tests, neither on the other, so the kernel cannot consult a metavariable store, a refinement layer, a parked goal or a cached elaboration: independence is a property of the crate graph, which [`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-trusted-base-is-a-crate-boundary) enumerates. A closed, metavariable-free term with no refinement in scope is evaluated by the shared closed machine, `curios-core`'s `reduce_closed`, entered from both checkers' reduction paths; every other term takes each checker's own recursive strategy.

What the certifier depends on is *certifier-grade*:

1. it decides by a total function its module documentation states;
2. every way it accepts is a rule that is sound alone, so the order rules are tried in decides what a verdict costs and never which way it goes;
3. a rule that does not apply leaves nothing behind, no state a later rule reads;
4. a budget, a cap or a cache changes a verdict only into a refusal, and a cache's hit is what recomputation answers;
5. it fails closed on a shape it does not anticipate;
6. a grid or a differential holds it to a statement independent of it.

Search — a procedure whose acceptance rests on a choice among candidates it cannot justify one by one — is the elaborator's, and stays outside the certifier's closure, which [`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-trusted-base-is-a-crate-boundary) enumerates. What a judgment reaches of the shared layer is named in one module of `curios-cert`: a function of the layer enters a verdict's reach by a line added there, and no other module of the crate imports one.

**Rationale.**

- **A second checker changes the cost of being wrong about a rule.** A rule the elaborator gets wrong is caught unless the kernel gets it wrong the same way, and the two are written against different information: weaker than a proof, much stronger than one implementation reviewed twice. It re-grades nothing in [the soundness board](the-soundness-board.md).
- **The kernel is where each rule is written down.** In the elaborator a rule is spread through implicit insertion, metavariable invention, postponement and resolution; the kernel states it once, in a form that reads as the rule.
- **A shared rule is one the two cannot disagree on, and that costs the second opinion.** A second implementation of a pure function of the same input is a second run, not a second opinion. So the checkers share what a term is and what arithmetic over the representation yields, which two copies could only disagree on through a transcription slip, and each judges for itself typing, how far an open operand reduces before a fold sees it, conversion's recurrence and fallbacks, refinement lookup, level entailment, which calls a recursive group makes, and which positions (T) and (V) are seeded from. The evidence for a shared rule is the fixtures that attack it, and the defects found lie there — the `Prop`-valued index route in the shared unifier, the singleton walk, the size-change walk's coverage gap — while the typing judgment, decided twice, yields the fewest.
- **The closed machine is a trusted evaluator.** A closed term under no refinement has one result under any order that reaches one, as `2 + 2` does, so sharing its evaluator gives up no second opinion and keeps the checkers from pricing one program differently ([A reduction step costs what it builds](a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md)). That makes the value well defined, not the machine right: a defect in it is one neither checker catches, as one in Rocq's virtual machine is, so a differential fixture holds it to both strategies.
- **The grade says what a shared rule must be, where sharing says only that there is one copy.** A chain of sufficient rules is sound when each is, whatever order it is tried in, so the grade forbids the two ways a chain goes wrong — a rule unsound alone, which a grid attacks rule by rule, and a verdict that turns on order, which a debug build holds by asking every alternative of a list and holding the first answer to the rest — and what makes search search, acceptance by a choice nothing checks. An optimization is where a kernel's defects gather: Rocq's list of critical bugs keeps one of its longest sections for its conversion machines.
- **A name means one thing.** What an environment holds under a name was judged by the walk that built it, and a module is judged against its environment, never handed it: a declaration under a held name is a second meaning for the name or a copy of the first, and the kernel has no need to tell which, as Lean's and Rocq's do not. Passing over the copy would rest a certified module on a comparison of two declarations, kept in the trusted base for a module no compilation hands the kernel: a unit's names are disjoint from its scope's, a unit compiled over a baseline adopts that baseline's verdicts by the certifier's record and is handed nothing of it as environment ([Cached verdicts](cached-verdicts.md)), and each root of the prelude is walked against the roots before it. A judgment is likewise about what a name means when it is made: a second declaration under the name, written over the first or declaring a second nominal beside it, leaves an accepted declaration stated in terms of one the module no longer holds, or one of the two never analysed. So the walk refuses every name a module declares twice before it judges anything of it, and judges nothing of a module that redeclares, since every later pass files by name. And the kernel's environment is written once: every way of writing it refuses a name it holds, so nothing a judgment or a remembered reduct read of a name is rewritten under it.
- **A verdict's reach is stated where a test can hold it.** A procedure listed in a document enters a judgment without its row and nothing fails; a function imported through one module is a line of a file, and a test over the kernel's sources fails where another module imports one for itself.
- **Where the elaborator judges rather than invents, a disagreement is a defect either way.** Accepting what the certifier refuses reports the refusal in the kernel's terms rather than the author's; refusing what it accepts withholds a convenience the certifier would have checked.
- **Incompleteness is the kernel's safe direction.** A rule that refuses too much is a visible disagreement and one that accepts too much is silent, so a kernel check that cannot decide refuses.
- **The two are held to each other.** `curios-prelude`'s build certifies the whole prelude and panics on the first refusal; `kernel_memo_parity`, `both_checkers_decide_non_informativeness_alike` and `both_checkers_decide_universe_context_validity_alike` compare verdicts; `curios`'s `tests::laws` puts each checker by itself to every rule of conversion under its laws, near misses among the rows ([Conversion is one relation in both checkers](conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md)); `curios-prelude-archive`'s `kernel_disagreements` is the per-class inventory.

**Rejected.**

- **The kernel inside the elaborator's crate.** Its membership would be traced rather than enforced; a crate boundary makes "not trusted" structural, so no builder, printer or convenience can sit inside the base.
- **A fresh, metavariable-free IR for the kernel.** The translation into it would be trusted and unchecked, and every construct would have two representations. What a kernel input must not contain — a `Metavar` and the `Transient` forms lowering creates for elaboration — is refused where the kernel meets it, as `curios_cert::Error::NotCore`.
- **Sharing the reduction driver** behind a trait for lookup and metavariables. It saves a few hundred lines and makes a mistake in the scrutinee stack, `rec` forcing or the eta side condition a mistake in both checkers.
- **Recognizing fold shapes and accelerating them** instead of a closed machine. Recognition is syntactic where closedness is semantic, each recognized fold would be its own trusted entry, and only folds would benefit; the kernels that stayed general, Rocq's `cClosure` and `vm_compute` and Agda's call-by-need machine, meet the same cost with a machine.
- **Blessing the types that hurt**, Lean's native strings: it fixes strings alone and moves a UTF-8 validator into the trusted base, where the machine accelerates a user's fold as it does the prelude's.
- **Compiling closed terms through the back end**, the `native_compute` analogue: the kernel sits below a pipeline that must not reach the runtime, the browser has no Wasmtime, and acceptance would depend on the whole back end.
- **Per-call-site reduction strategies**, Rocq's `cbv`, `lazy` and `vm_compute`: the machine is what evaluating a closed term is, chosen by a cached O(1) gate rather than an annotation.
- **A ban on first-answer chains and retries in trusted code.** It states a means: it excludes the carriers' algebra as it stands, and admits a single unsound rule.
- **A row per procedure in a README**, graded against the clauses. Nothing fails when a procedure enters a verdict's reach without its row, which is the inventory [the soundness board](the-soundness-board.md) rejects for rules.
- **Running a chain in every rotation** to hold its order. A chain's steps rewrite the pair the next one reads, so most of one has no other order; and for the alternatives that do, every rotation answering alike is every pair of answers agreeing, which one pass over the list asks.
- **Asking every alternative in a release build**, the strongest answer winning. It would hold the order by construction and charge every comparison for it, where a debug build asks the same question of every pair the suite and the standard library produce.
- **A second copy of the shared layer for the kernel.** A copy fails where its original does: what the certifier needs of what it shares is simple code, not a second one.
- **A trusted decision procedure in the kernel.** A certificate is checked; the search that found it is not trusted.
- **Compiling matches to eliminators and recursion to recursors**, as Lean's elaborator does, which would take inversion and termination out of the certifier. `Match` is an intrinsic of Core and partial recursion is allowed at run time: both are the language's, not its elaboration's.
- **A lint against wildcard arms in the trusted crates**, to hold the fifth clause by exhaustive matches. It is a lint configuration with a design decision of its own to make.
- **Passing over a declaration the environment holds term for term**, as the environment's own. It certifies a module in which a name means what the environment holds while every stage below compiles what the module holds, unless a comparison in the trusted base says the two are one; and nothing hands the kernel such a module, a recompile's kept declarations reaching it as a baseline and not as its environment.
- **Judging a declaration under a held name instead of refusing it.** A declaration can be well formed and still be a second meaning for its name: an inhabited proposition under the name of the empty one is accepted where it is judged.
- **Comparing a registry entry whole**, its polarity vector and its variance vector with it, where a walk asks whether a declaration is its baseline's. The vectors an entry carries are elaboration's record and no judgment of the certifier reads one: a walk holds in their place the vectors the certifier's own walk filed. Compared, the two checkers' vectors would be held to each other, and a declaration judged again wherever a budget shaped one of them. An entry is a baseline's by what the certifier reads of it, its terms.
- **Naming a declaration by its content**, as Stateless HOL names a constant by its string and its definition and Unison a definition by its hash. A name could then mean nothing else, and a `Global` would be another thing in every term and every stored unit.
- **The kernel checking mount disjointness itself.** It brings mounts into `Globals`, which holds names, to say of two units what their names already say: mounting a unit that holds a name another holds is a fault the environment asserts.
- **Comparing digests the unit carries.** A digest the kernel did not compute is an input it would believe.
