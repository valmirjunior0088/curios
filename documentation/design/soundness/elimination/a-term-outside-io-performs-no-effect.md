# A term outside `Io` performs no effect

**Assumes.** A term whose type is not an `Io` performs nothing, so a match scrutinee denotes one value and within the arm is the case's, and nothing at a type position performs an effect.

**Evidence.** Probed at the scrutinee consequence and argued at the general premise, which is a typing fact rather than an analysis: every host operation returns `Io(T)`, `Io` has no eliminator, and the only consumer of an `Io(A)` is a `bind` returning `Io(B)` ([Effects are descriptions, and the carrier has no eliminator](../../effects/effects-are-descriptions-and-the-carrier-has-no-eliminator.md)). `tests::board::an_effect_cannot_inhabit_a_pure_arrow` refuses an effect a caller supplies through an ordinary function parameter, the shape no syntactic search can follow, and `a_stuck_application_scrutinee_still_refines` is the control that a stuck-headed scrutinee still teaches its arm. Guest storage obeys it: `Cell/fill(c, true) : Io(Bool)` cannot be a Boolean scrutinee, and forcing `Cell/poll` and matching its result is the positive control.

**The premise is asserted over the surface an eliminator could enter through.** `curios-text`'s `the_sys_io_roster_offers_no_eliminator` pins `/sys/Io`'s exports at `{Io, pure, bind}` and walks every `/sys` `let` for a function taking an `Io` to a non-`Io`, mutation-checked both ways. A foreign row cannot introduce one, since every store row's result is wrapped in `Io` at one site and `WireType` cannot name an `Io`, and no `.crs` can define one without having one. The general form needs a notion of effect carrier: `/std/Async`'s `lift : (Io(A)) -> Async(A)` consumes an `Io` legitimately, `Async` being a description too, and `block_on` and `State/run` are sanctioned runners. A runner for the host carrier would turn `proc/exit(@False, 0)` into a proof of `False`, since `exit` is typed at `Io(A)` for any `A` on the strength of this premise alone.
