# Case equations and their key

**Assumes.** Within an arm the scrutinee is definitionally the case's value, and the equation reaches nothing outside the arm and no term but the scrutinee, read at the solution each arm inside it is checked under.

**Evidence.** Argued at the equation's justification and probed at both consultation points, each spelling of the key, the settlement's obligation and the scope. The equation is assumed by the elimination rule that justifies it, and `whnf` consults it at every stuck head and before taking a term apart, so it is the second source besides the definition store from which a reduct can come: an equation escaping its arm, or answering a term it was not about, is a false definitional equation with the consequences of a wrong fold.

- **Two consultation points, one equation.** Asking before a term is taken apart lifts only the requirement that the reducer afford a reduction first — a guard over an accumulation would otherwise fold the very subject it exists not to evaluate. `kernel::whnf::equations_tests`'s `a_case_equation_answers_a_term_the_budget_cannot_reduce` and `the_two_consultation_points_answer_one_equation_alike`, each mutation-checked against removing its own point.
- **The key is the scrutinee, compared structurally with its levels.** Structural equality under-approximates definitional equality and costs nothing to justify. A key projecting universe levels away would identify `Type 0` with `Type 1`, since `Type u` embeds a level in a term and a definition carrying it into a payload reduces to different values at two instances: `recheck::universes_tests::a_case_equation_does_not_refine_an_occurrence_at_another_universe_instance`, with `a_case_equation_still_refines_the_occurrence_it_scrutinized` as the control, mutation-checked. No surface program reaches the pair — a level carried into a payload is body-only, so it is minimized rather than generalized (`tests::universes::a_body_carried_level_is_minimized_rather_than_generalized`).
- **The equation is recorded under three spellings**, each identifying terms already definitionally equal: the scrutinee as written; where it is a dispatch, the spelling its head resolves to (`whnf::resolved_spelling`, opening layers by the elaborator's bound, heads only, forcing no operand); and its reduct, computed at most once and only after a probe has missed. The resolved spelling keeps the checkers answering a dead arm alike, since a dispatched guard met only as a reduct is met after the decision procedure has folded it: `a_dispatched_guard_answers_its_dual_before_the_procedure_folds_it` and `resolving_a_dispatched_guard_forces_none_of_its_operands`, mutation-checked, and `curios`'s `tests::matching::the_dead_arm_of_a_dispatched_guard_proves_its_dual`.
- **Settling a reduct reconstructs the view it would have had earlier.** A settlement withholds the equation being settled and every equation inside it for the duration of its reduction, so the reduct neither settles to its own case value nor rests on an inner equation that retracts first (`Scope::hide_refinements_from`; `a_case_equation_answers_a_spelling_only_reduction_reaches`, `settling_a_reduced_spelling_withholds_the_equations_inside_it`, mutation-checked). `Scope::could_reduce_to` settles only a candidate whose free locals are a subset of the key's, since reduction can drop a local and never introduce one; it is a cost filter failing toward refusal (`a_reduct_that_drops_a_local_is_still_reached`).
- **An equation follows the solution an arm is checked under.** The kernel records no solution: it substitutes a case's through the arm's body, its expectation and the locals it re-types, so an equation an outer arm assumed over a variable the case solves is keyed on a term the arm no longer holds, and the kernel would refuse a guard's fact the elaborator, which keeps the variable spelled, accepts. Each such equation is assumed again at the solution within the arm's bracket, the recorded one stepping aside until the arm retracts, so an equation naming the variables of nested arms stands once (`Scope::restate`, at `assume_case_value`). The restatement is the hypothesis the arm already had, at the instance the arm is checked at, and it passes the recording rule, so one left naming no local is not recorded. `kernel::whnf::equations_tests`'s `a_case_equation_follows_the_solution_an_arm_is_checked_under` and `a_restated_equation_naming_no_local_is_not_recorded`, mutation-checked at the restatement, the stepping aside and the retraction, and `curios`'s `tests::matching::a_guards_fact_holds_under_a_match_on_a_variable_it_names` with `tests::respelling`'s substitution rows through both checkers.
- **The elaborator reads the recording rule where the kernel does.** The kernel records no equation under a spelling that names no local, and it reads a scrutinee with every arm's solution substituted. The elaborator keeps a refined variable spelled, so it judges the rule on the scrutinee with its solved metavariables materialized, its local definitions substituted and its refined variables spelled as their values (`Context::kernel_spelling`): where an equation is recorded, and again where an arm refines a variable, withholding within that arm every equation the refinement leaves naming no local (`Context::withhold_closed_equations`), the entry that withholds it travelling with the refinements a metavariable is born under. Such a scrutinee computes, so in an arm a value reaches the fact holds by reduction; where it computes to another case the arm is dead, and a proof resting on the guard is refused where it is written, with the note an unnested dead arm gets, where the kernel would have refused what the elaborator accepted. `curios-elab`'s `context::tests::an_equation_a_refinement_closes_is_withheld_for_the_arm` and `a_withheld_equation_stays_withheld_where_its_refinements_are_installed_again`, and `curios`'s `tests::matching::recording_tests` from `a_guard_an_inner_case_contradicts_answers_nothing_in_that_arm` on, mutation-checked against withholding nothing.
- **A local-free term is never refined.** A local-free scrutinee reduces to its case value rather than sticking, so it is skipped — `curios_analysis::records_case_equation`, the one rule both checkers record by — and that skip is what keeps the evaluation memo, which holds local-free terms only, sound. A resolved spelling that drops its locals is never recorded (`a_resolved_spelling_that_drops_its_locals_is_never_recorded`), and `refined_reduct` refuses to probe a term with no local free (`a_local_free_term_is_never_refined`, asserting both sides of the arm); both mutation-checked.
- **Scoping is structural.** The local telescope and the equation stack are one component because they retract as one: a `Mark` checkpoints both, `Scope::retract` truncates both, and `Kernel::scoped` closes everything a walk opened on the failing path as on the succeeding one.
- **Two shared readers match summands up to universe instances.** `Nat::cancel_common` and `compare_nat`'s shared-inner shortcut compare under `project_erased_universes`, so `n <= n` decides for an `n` mentioning a polymorphic name; so do `Nat::same` and `int_same`, through which a word's numbers and two applications of one `Nat`- or `Int`-valued operation compare. This rests on the carrier: Core has no elimination from a type or a level into a number, so two summands differing only in their instances denote one number. `summands_cancel_across_a_universe_instance_and_not_across_an_argument`, `a_product_is_one_number_at_every_universe_instance_of_its_factors` and `spine::position_tests::a_word_number_is_one_number_across_a_universe_instance` hold it; an intrinsic reading a level or a sort as a count would turn them into witnesses.
