# An elimination at an ambient goal

**Assumes.** An arm inhabits the goal as written in the enclosing context once that goal is specialized by the most-general unifier of its case.

**Status.** **probed**, in both checkers, at the rule's two heads and at its refusals.

- **The rule.** `MatchResult::Ambient` states the result once, where it was written ([An arm is checked in a context specialized by index inversion](../../types/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md)). `check_motive` infers the goal's type in the ambient context and reads its sort for the large-elimination guard; `check_arm` takes the goal at the case's targets and value, `MatchResult::at` being the substitution every consumer shares; `infer` reads the elimination's type as the goal. Soundness is the specialization rule's: substitution preserves typing, the unifier is most general so every instance of the case factors through it, and definitional K licenses the deletion rule. Which locals are re-assumed, and at what type, is `curios-analysis`'s `retyped`: every local whose type mentions a variable the case's solution solves, read through local definitions, in every arm and either result form.
- **An expression head is read by occurrence.** Substitution carries a variable's form to an expression — a `let` value substituted into its tail, a contracted redex — and for an expression head the goal's syntactic occurrences of it stand for the case, the with-abstraction reading, a fixed operation on the spelling; an occurrence the spelling does not show is met only through the arm's case equation ([Case equations and their key](case-equations-and-their-key.md)). `an_ambient_result_over_an_expression_replaces_its_occurrences` puts both halves to the kernel.
- **A fold that reads its hypothesis keeps a family.** The hypothesis is the fold at the tail, typed at the result at the tail, which an ambient goal cannot give once the head is substituted away; the kernel refuses one with `curios_cert::Error::AmbientFold`. A case split reads no hypothesis, and its reduct never contains the fold at the tail, so it takes the ambient form.
- **Evidence.** `curios-cert/src/kernel/infer/eliminate/ambient_tests.rs`: an ambient elimination whose goal mentions a hypothesis typed by the scrutinee, accepted beside the closed family stating the same thing, refused as not a motive; a lying goal refused; an expression head read by occurrence; an ambient fold reading its hypothesis refused, mutation-checked; an ambient case split checked at each case. `curios/src/tests/matching/motive_tests.rs`'s `a_hypothesis_typed_by_the_scrutinee_needs_no_convoy` and `tests::soundness::a_proof_over_a_dependent_hypothesis_descends` are the elaborator's side, and `tests::perimeter::index_tests::an_unreachable_arm_retypes_no_local` holds the shared re-typing.
