# Totality of the erased program

**Decision.** Everything erasure deletes must be total, and what it retains may diverge. Erasure deletes types and `Prop`-sorted proofs, so two obligations over one partiality relation on zonked Core enforce it: **type totality**, that a term in a type position holds no recursion of its own declaration that does not descend — a group written in it, a host row that diverges, or a definition of the item being judged whose recursion does not descend — and **proof totality**, that a term at a `Prop`-sorted type reaches, as the checkers reduce it, no definition not known to terminate. Recursion is unrestricted and implicit at the type and value layers alike — a definition may mention itself, and a mutual group is declared with `and` — and totality is decided per recursive group by size-change termination; a group that fails is classified `Unfounded` rather than rejected, with every definition of its item and every definition enclosing such a group, and keeps its meaning wherever erasure retains a value, while a definition reaching one, or calling a host row that diverges, is `Partial`. **Both checkers unfold only what terminates**: a member is read by value in its group's signatures and bodies once its turn finds it known to terminate, and past its item's judgment a definition filed `Unfounded` is defined with no value in both checkers, in its unit and in every unit reading its record, a refused item's included, as a proof is, so a type naming it stands where the name does. Each checker seeds both obligations from its own record of the positions it typed — the certifier classifies every position while its binders are still assumed and takes a group's calls from the applications it types — and the two share only grading a call and closing a group's calls. Both rest on one principle, size-change termination as `curios-analysis`'s `decide` applies it, Lee, Jones and Ben-Amram's theorem (POPL 2001) in the shape Abel's `foetus` gave it for dependent types: a recursive group whose call matrices, closed transitively under composition, carry a strict decrease on the diagonal of every idempotent matrix from a member to itself cannot recurse forever, given that the size order the matrices grade in is well founded on the values the calls are made at. **The size order reads what reduction took apart and nothing conversion concluded**: an arm's case stands for a binder it scrutinizes, its payloads are that case's children, an arm's binders stand below the value of an expression it scrutinizes, which reduction took apart as it takes apart a binder's, and an equation the arm's index inversion solved refines the arm for typing alone. A closure that outgrows its limit claims nothing, and the group is `Partial`.

An expression in an erased value position — an argument to an erased parameter, an erased field, an operand slot instantiated at a proposition or a type, the value a `let` binds at one, locally or at the top level — is not evaluated: its refusals do not occur and it costs nothing. A binding is classified by its type as every other slot is, so `let p = lemma(n); f(p)` and `f(lemma(n))` are one program. A top-level function computes nothing when written, whatever it returns; what still evaluates regardless of its result's sort is a direct call of a function whose result erases, standing where erasure keeps a value. The evaluator a type runs keeps the sentence for a proof: [the closed machine binds a proof unreduced](../compilation/the-closed-machine-binds-a-proof-unreduced.md).

**Rationale.**

- **A divergent type breaks type formation and a divergent proof proves anything, while a program that loops is a program.** Drawing the line at erasure keeps general recursion where it is used and removes it where it is a logical hole. The prelude's partial definitions — `/std/Toml`, `/std/Io`, `/std/tcp`, `/std/Json`, `/std/Flt` and the rest of the productive-but-not-descending corpus — reach no type and no proof, and `/std/Fmt`'s parser is a fold over its format's characters, since `format_type_with` over `parse(s)` is the result type of `render`, `print` and `print_err`.
- **Three routes to `False` need both obligations, and closing one closes neither other.** A partial definition at a `Type`-sorted carrier releases a `Prop` through a projection or an arm binder. An exit typed at a proposition would be dropped by erasure and leave a forged invariant, which [an exit yielding an `Io`](../theory/effects-are-descriptions-and-the-carrier-has-no-eliminator.md) closes by its type. A type-level recursive definition ties the negative knot positivity forbids: `let Bad : Type = Sink(Bad)` is no `induct`, so strict positivity never sees it, and its exploit holds no recursion proof totality could see.
- **The fold leaves the knot untied, so type totality asks only what the declaration writes.** A total type-level function applied to a loop, `Shape(inf)` with `let inf : F = F/more(inf)`, is a stuck application once `inf` unfolds to nothing, never `Sink(Shape(inf))`, and a family instantiated at the loop, `induct Trap(f : F) | mk(x : Shape(f)) end` at `inf`, holds a payload no match takes apart; positivity reads a match stuck on a loop as every arm it could reduce to. What no fold withholds is the declaration being judged, whose verdict comes only after its elaboration: `let Bad : Type = Sink(Bad)` owes descent at its own declaration, and so do a member's signatures, spelled over its group's own selections. So a type may name a loop it never applies — `Vec(Io(Nat), List/length(List/map(xs, spin)))`, a witness's `Answers` field beside a method that loops — and is read as the checkers read it, and a refusal standing on a folded definition names it.
- **A proof is read as the checkers reduce it, which the fold makes an end.** Reduction ends because it unfolds only what terminates, and a proof convertible to one that names nothing partial proves its proposition by a total term, which irrelevance makes the same proof: a bound discharged through the count `List/length([spin(1), …])` carries `spin` in its arguments, and the count reduces to `3`. One walk of `curios-analysis` reads it for both checkers (`reached_as_reduced`), each over its own driver and record, and only where the proof names a definition not known to terminate: a closed part is forced and read again; a match's scrutinee is read as a part of its own before the match reduces, since the step that reads past a proposition's closed scrutinee rests on the scrutinee being total; and a part reading a binder the proof writes is read by the names it writes. A proof's arguments are parts of it: a total `ex_falso(@e: Empty)` applied to a loop folded at `Empty` would prove `False`.
- **The peers fold a loop as Curios does.** Lean's `partial def` is an opaque constant, its mutual block opaque together; Agda's `NON_TERMINATING` functions and Idris's partial ones are not unfolded while a type is checked; Zombie lets a type mention a programmatic term that a logical one uses only as a value. Curios reads the verdict off the body by size-change, a member at its turn, so the rule that holds a loop folded inside its group's judgment is the one stated everywhere, with no pragma and no inhabitation asked.
- **The premise is a containment, not an equality.** Erasure deletes more than types and proofs: the signature-mask skip, the erasable operand, a proof-valued callee that is no direct reference, a `Prop` family's constructor and an erasable scrutinee drop whole constructs with ordinary arguments, and it skips dead code. What the obligations need is that everything deleted lies within a term type totality or proof totality covers, since reachability and the partiality walk descend through whole terms.
- **Consistency, not canonicity.** A closed `s : /std/Str` may be bottom, but its `valid` field is `Prop`-sorted, so proof totality forces every constructed `Str` to carry a total proof: divergence shows as divergence, never as a valid-looking wrong answer.
- **Not evaluating an erased position loses nothing observable.** No term outside `Io` performs an effect, so `let _ = /std/Proc/exit(@{}, 3); …` builds a description and the program goes on, and proof totality makes every proof total while a type computes every unfolding of a definition that terminates, each folded one left standing, which erasure evaluates none of, so the expression would have returned. What is left is a refusal, and removing one is the permitted direction: the run time yields Core's value or a refusal.
- **One rule uses an obligation before it is checked, and forms a fixpoint with it, not a circle.** Proof irrelevance accepts without inspecting either term, which is correct because every `Prop` inhabitant is total — proof totality's job — while elaboration uses it with the obligation unchecked. Conversion takes no other step on trust: where two recursive definitions meet it [assumes no goal](conversion-recurrence.md). Whether a term is total is decided with no conversion invoked: the analyses that decide it — totality's grading and descent, positivity — force through `Driver::force` alone, which is plain reduction in both checkers and asks nothing. So acceptance validates the use after the fact: a program is accepted only if the obligations pass, and then every such step was applied to total terms. The model is missing, not the reasoning: the fixpoint argues from the implementation's dependency structure, and a proof that definitional irrelevance is sound needs a model in which `Prop` is a subsingleton and only total terms are interpreted.
- **A conversion verdict is no fact about a value.** An arm's index inversion concludes its equations through conversion — the one opinion a shared analysis borrows of its driver, which `curios-analysis`'s `Judge` exists to keep visible — and at a `Prop`-sorted type conversion is proof irrelevance, which every proof of a proposition satisfies. A target holding a proposition's constructor is therefore one spelling of the scrutinee among all of them, so a payload read below a binder through such a solution grades below a value no reduction took apart, and a call that hands the same proof back descends for ever. The equation still specializes the arm, which is all typing asks of it. The line is the one `Driver::force` already draws by reducing plainly, and it fails for a binder that binds data as readily as for one that binds a proof, so it is stated of the order rather than of the binder's sort.
- **A proposition's own case is structure, at a least derivation.** The order is well founded on the values irrelevance leaves when a proof is ranked by the least height of a derivation of its proposition, and data by its tree, each proof it holds at that rank. A match on a proof yields the one proof of its result whichever arm is taken, and the one match of a proof into data reads its constructor's payloads off the indices, so a proof's case may be read as the last step of a least derivation, below which every payload — data, a proof, or an application of a function-typed one, which sizing keeps below its family — ranks strictly. A call that hands back a proof of the proposition it matched therefore stands in an arm no least derivation takes, and the arm one does take is never excused, since inversion concludes a clash only between values conversion tells apart (`curios-analysis`'s `unify_node`). What the ranks cannot hold is a constructor conversion supplied rather than reduction, which no least derivation need end in: the index solutions the bullet above keeps out of the order.
- **A field is below its record.** By eta a record is the node of its fields, so a projection `b.i`, and a chain of them, is strictly below `b` in the order above whatever binds `b` — an arm's payload, a parameter, a binder a summary bounds — and a field of a proof record ranks below the record as each premise of its least derivation does; a projection of a record literal is its field (`curios-analysis`'s `Shape::Smaller`). A field applied is read no further: a record is no family whose function-typed fields sizing keeps below it, as an inductive's payloads are.
- **Decidability is budget-relative**, as reduction's is ([A reduction step costs what it builds](a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md)): the same on every machine. Totality spends the budget in three places: descent's bounded weak-head fallback — how a decrease written with an operator, a resolved witness, exposes the arithmetic it is graded on — proof totality's sort decision, asked once per distinct recorded type, and its reading of a proof as reduced, which read the proof by the first definition it names where the budget is spent.
- **A call in a callback is graded where its callee applies it.** A lambda handed to a definition runs where the definition applies the parameter it stands for, so each definition publishes, beside its totality and for each of its parts — its leading lambdas, and each field lambda of the record they answer, over the definition's parameters and then the lambda's — how the part applies each parameter it uses only by applying it, applying a field of it, or handing it on unchanged, at its own place to a member of its group that takes a parameter there, called or itself handed, applied to its own arguments, where its callee applies it, or to another definition whose summary applies what it is handed, whose applications through the call are its own: the matrices grading each application of the parameter, and of each field path of it, `e.0(x, y)`, against its parameters, closed over its group's calls where it is a member of one (`curios-analysis`'s `group_applications`, and `definition_applications` for a definition of no group, walked only where a parameter's type is one a body applies or projects), which each checker computes over what it judged and files in its own record. A list operation applies its function to each element of its list, so `/sys/List/map` and `/sys/List/fold` apply theirs to an element below the list, and a fold's beside an accumulator below nothing; `/sys/List/get` hands out one of the list's elements, so its answer is below the list as a case of the list hands one out. A definition's parameter a field lambda applies is applied wherever that field is called, at arguments no row of the leading lambdas states, so their summary states nothing of it and the field's does. A field is called on a record its leading lambdas answered, as a path into a member's field calls the member's leading lambdas too, so a field's summary states what they apply of the parameter where they answer the record beside what the field applies itself. A call is graded by the summary of the part its path reaches, `List`'s witness applied, its method projected and applied reading the method's. A call handing a lambda to a parameter applied so gives the lambda's binders the relation every application establishes at their place (`binder_bounds`); one handing it a record literal hands each field what its own path is applied with; and one handing it a member of the caller's group, named bare or applied to its own parameters, is a call of each part each application reaches, the member's own arguments graded as a call's are and the field lambda's as the application grades them (`handed_member`); and one handing it another definition applied to every argument its leading lambdas take is a call of that definition's part each application of a field reaches, graded the same way, whose summary states what each of those arguments is handed in turn (`handed_within`). So a witness handed on inside another's application, `List`'s witness applied to `Option`'s applied to a member, is graded where each applies what it is handed, and a witness that hands itself to a container's publishes how it applies its own premises. `/std/List/any` applies `pred` to an element below `l`, so a tree's recursion through it over its children descends, and `/std/List/eql` applies its witness's method to elements below its lists, so a written witness comparing a tree's children with it descends, and so does one comparing lists of lists of them through `List`'s own witness twice. A parameter used any other way publishes nothing, and what it is handed is graded as fresh, as the argument of an applied definition is where the field applied is no part with a summary, or one its application does not fill or whose summary does not see every use of the parameter. Rocq's guard condition reads through a callee's fixpoint the same way, where the callee's function parameter is uniform.
- **A call is read off a path.** A recursive group's call graph has a node for each part of a member: its leading lambdas, and where the body under them is a record literal — a structure's or a tuple's, nested ones followed — each field that is a lambda, whose parameters are the member's followed by the lambda's (`curios-analysis`'s `group_parts`). A call is its root, a member, with the steps from it — the member's own arguments, a field, the field's arguments — and its callees are the parts the steps reach (`part_calls`): a field is followed where the arguments before it fill the part it projects out of, and the path stops at the first field that is not, the call being of the part reached so far; a path that follows a field calls the member's leading lambdas too, since strict evaluation answers the record, its other fields with it, before the field is read; and a path that reaches no field lambda — the member named bare or applied, handed to another definition, or a record literal held at a field — lets the record escape, so it calls every part under where it stands, graded on the arguments it handed and claiming nothing past them. So `(W.0)(l)`, a witness's method applied to a child, is a call of the method below its argument, and a recursion through a record of functions descends where the field's function does, while `W` handed to a definition that reads a field of it is a call of every method, graded as unrelated. A part is read off the body as it stands, whatever declared the record, so a structure of handlers is graded as a witness is; the term is graded as elaborated, no method lifted into a definition of its own and no name minted for one.

**Rejected.**

- **Structural recursion on one argument**, which rejects `/std/List/sort_by`'s `merge` (descending on either of two lists by arm) and the groups of `/std/Cli` and `/std/Tui` that close only across their two members, `/std/Cli/check`'s `problems` and `beneath` among them, none of which a fold can express since a fold cannot short-circuit. `problems` is reached by a type — `/std/Cli/WellFormed` is `Clear(check/problems(command, version))`, a proposition every `Cli` holds a proof of — so the rule would leave a declared command line's type unformed. Size-change without match-refinement expansion fails `merge` too, and most of `/std`'s recursive groups with it, on infeasible call paths.
- **Reading an arm's index solutions as structure**, so that a call descends on what inversion refined an outer variable to: the equation is conversion's and the refinement is typing's, no group in `/std` descends on one, and a guard keyed to the sort of the binder solved reaches none of it, since the proof a solution carries sits under a data binder's target.
- **Reading a proposition's own case as no structure**, the restriction of structural recursion Agda made under its impredicative `Prop` and reverted because programs recurse on proofs ([agda/agda#6639](https://github.com/agda/agda/pull/6639), undone by [#6936](https://github.com/agda/agda/pull/6936)). The recursion it was made for applies a payload that quantifies over every proposition at the family holding it, which a sized proposition cannot do ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../theory/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)), so here it would buy nothing: `/std/List/Permutation`'s `sym` recurses on the payloads of its proof's own case, and a recursion along an accessibility predicate, the one well-founded recursion a proof needs, has nothing else to descend on. A predicate declared at `Type` keeps such a recursion where erasure retains its tree.
- **A `rec` keyword marking recursive definitions.** Whether a definition recurses is a fact about its body and a top-level forward reference already reorders, so it tells the compiler nothing, and its one possible effect — keeping a marked definition's applications folded in conversion while a `let` unfolds — makes what a program elaborates depend on a spelling. Conversion keeps every global head's applications folded alike.
- **Type totality's aggressive reading**, everything reachable from a type: it refused a type naming a loop it never applies, followed the spelling, the same type formed by instantiation being accepted, and kept `/std/Io/all` from counting its answers, while the fold leaves it nothing to refuse. Closing it precisely instead, by an interprocedural "scrutinized at the type level" fixpoint, would have been a second positivity analysis.
- **Withholding a loop at its own name alone**, which a sibling's body and the item's signatures spell past; **asking a group's verdict where `force` unfolds a call**, which reads a finished declaration's verdict again inside another's budget; and **folding every partial definition**, closure and all, which folds the witness a pair of actions resolves to and leaves `Joint/Answers` unread. **Folding at a finer grain than the item**: a witness whose method loops folds its sort-valued fields with it, which no program needs read yet.
- **Proof totality passing over a proof's arguments**, which a total `ex_falso` applied to a loop folded at an empty type turns into `False`: a loop folded at an empty type is no postulate a model holds, Lean's reason to ask a `partial def`'s type inhabited. **Spelling a discharge's count reduced at its one site**, which leaves every other proof that carries a loop it discards refused. **Forcing a part under a binder the proof writes**, opened fresh: a recursion that descends on the binder unfolds once for every arm the walk opens, without end short of the budget, and no proof is yet refused for a loop it discards there.
- **Firing the step that reads past a closed scrutinee only where the scrutinee reaches nothing not known to terminate**, which has reduction ask a verdict at every cast; **type totality still reading what a match scrutinizes**, which misses a scrutinee a total function's unfolding exposes; and **typing or refusing an arm the indices rule out**, a change to the language that leaves a dependent goal unpreserved on data all the same. The step fires only where a match states the proposition it eliminates ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../theory/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)).
- **Gating the whole language on termination**, or **rejecting partial definitions outright**, which deletes `/std/Io`'s driver, `/std/Json/decode` and every productive program for a property none of them threaten.
- **Syntax for the checker**, such as an induction hypothesis on `Cases::Induct`, which helps none of the proofs that matter.
- **Evaluating an erased position that may reach a host operation**, a transitive effect analysis that still cannot preserve divergence.
- **Typing a never-returning operation at an empty `Type`-sorted carrier**, which an elimination launders back into an erased position; the opaque `Io` has no eliminator to launder through.
- **Leaving a bound proof to the optimizer**, which keeps a call nothing below Core knows is total.
- **A rule the reader states for each higher-order function**, as Isabelle's congruence rules (`fundef_cong`) and Lean's `wf_preprocess` lemmas, which rewrite a call to one carrying each element's membership, are: the callee's body is published, so how it applies a parameter is read off it once rather than asserted beside it.
- **Lifting a recursive method into a definition beside its witness**, the shape Lean and Rocq leave their users to write: elaboration would mint names and write terms no source wrote, and a method that hands its witness on to a container's still reaches the witness, so the record is read either way. **Marking a derived witness partial**, as Lean's derived `BEq` on a nested inductive is: no type could reach anything a derivation writes.
