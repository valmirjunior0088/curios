# Conversion recurrence

**Decision.** Conversion assumes no goal: a comparison met again inside itself is never taken as held. Where two recursive definitions meet, three rules decide them, the same in both checkers and tried in one order, and a pair they do not hold is apart.

- **Two instances of one recursive group are a level question**, decided before anything is unfolded. The certifier decides two projections of one group that agree modulo levels by their level vectors under the item's hypotheses (`rec_instances`, through `Term::level_differences`, which keeps every level's position). A family's former is the one member whose refusal is no verdict: its two instances reproduce nothing under unfolding, so they are left to the nodes they build ([A nominal type's levels compare by variance](a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md)). The elaborator sends a family's former applied in full on both sides to its two nodes as well, and identifies every other pair's levels at the applied head or where the two are compared bare (`level_question`), parking a pair only an unsolved metavariable keeps from being a level question. Two calls of one member are then their spines.
- **The same member of two recursive groups is decided by the groups compared as the binders they are**, before either checker unfolds one. Two groups of one shape are one where, member for member, their types convert and then their bodies at those types, every member of both opened at one shared binder: the congruence of a binder, as two lambdas whose bodies convert under one binder are one function. No member is unfolded while two groups are compared, so nothing recurs; two calls of a pair it holds are compared by their spines. Groups are matched by position, which is what makes one index of two a pair, and only where neither binds a universe of its own. A pair it does not hold is left to the unfolding, which is taken once.
- **A pair of calls that re-sticks is unfolded against itself once.** Two folded calls no rule before this one decides are opened one definitional step, both of them, and compared again, and their two heads are a pair being unfolded for as long as that comparison stands. The same two heads met again inside it are apart where both calls re-stick: each one's unfolding, reduced, is a neutral form, no folded call, that still mentions its group, the test forcing keeps a call folded by asked of one step (`restuck`, in each checker's reducer). There a second unfolding poses what the first posed one binder further in, so it decides nothing the first did not. What would hold such a pair is an induction, which a lemma states. Where one call's unfolding takes its match apart — to a head constructor, to a reduct free of its group, or to another folded call — the pair is unfolded again, since it computes there as the first unfolding did: `first_in([T/node(k), ..r])` against `first(T/node(k))`, two members of one group, unfold to `first(T/node(k))` against `first_in(k)`, the same two heads, and the first of those takes `T/node(k)` apart. The test is read off the pair met again, never counted between two meetings. A recursion whose unfolding keeps taking its match apart without re-sticking is the budget's, as a partial one is. The rule withholds an unfolding, so it accepts nothing, and what it refuses that more unfoldings would hold is two re-stuck calls a second round reconciles. A side opened against a term with nothing to open is no pair, and two bare members forced against each other are one as two calls are, apart where they are met again: a bare member is opened at no argument, so its unfolding takes nothing apart. The certifier's pairs are the path of its walk. The elaborator's are carried with each problem, as its binders are: its walk parks what it cannot decide, so a problem retried is retried inside what it was posed inside, and a comparison a run opens for a part of the problem in hand — an argument, two groups as binders, a classing's atoms — is posed inside that problem's pairs. There a pair met again waits, parked, where an unsolved metavariable is what keeps a rule from deciding its two heads, that they are one member or one as binders, or keeps the spines of one member's two calls from agreeing, which they agree by only where nothing is solved until the term that awaits them holds them: refused, it would be refused by the order its metavariables were solved in.
- **A problem a run of the elaborator poses twice is decided once.** Its walk is a worklist every problem of which must hold, so a problem posed again adds nothing the run does not already owe, and the run remembers what it has posed: a problem's type, its two sides, the relation it is asked at and the unfoldings it stands inside. That is what lets it take two equal graphs built apart in their size, as the certifier's remembered verdicts let the certifier. It assumes nothing, because a problem is never the one posed inside its own unfolding: every step that opens a recursive definition, a head opened alone against a term with nothing to open among them, is part of what a problem stands inside, and no chain of problems closes on itself without one.
- **A classing compares inside the pairs of the goal it belongs to.** Where the intrinsic chain hands a pair's atoms back, the certifier compares two of them as it compares an operand of the congruence, inside whatever the goal is inside the unfolding of, and the elaborator poses each two atoms in a conversion of its own, inside the pairs of the problem in hand. A pair neither side of which is an operation its atoms are read out of hands back no atoms at all, since the only pair to class would be the goal, posed again. The pairs are all that bound a classing asked for inside another's question ([A question reduction asks is answered by plain reduction](a-question-reduction-asks-is-answered-by-plain-reduction.md)).
- **A question reduction asks is a comparison of its own**, inside no unfolding: it takes nothing from the goal reduction was serving and leaves it nothing, and it is answered by plain reduction, which asks none, so reduction asking conversion asking reduction ends at one conversion the budget bounds ([A question reduction asks is answered by plain reduction](a-question-reduction-asks-is-answered-by-plain-reduction.md)).
- **A verdict that left a pair apart is not remembered, and one is remembered with the pairs it unfolded.** The certifier remembers a comparison's verdict by its type and its two sides, for as long as what it read of the scope stands ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-certifier-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts)), and only where deciding it left no pair apart inside its own unfolding: `Pairs::apart` counts every pair left apart, and `compare` files a verdict where the count stood still across it. A pair left apart may be one where it is met outside its own unfolding, so a refusal reached across one is a fact of that path alone. A remembered verdict carries the pairs its derivation unfolded, and answers wherever its goal is met outside the unfolding of every one of them: there a derivation unfolds each as that one did and reaches what it reached, and inside one it would meet that pair apart. A comparison a remembered verdict answered rests on the pairs that verdict carries, as on the pairs it unfolded itself. The table is cleared wherever the case equations in force change, and with the declaration, whose universe constraints are assumed once. The sets are plain, with no closure taken over accepted pairs, as Lean's kernel keeps its own and for the reason it gives: a closure's result would depend on the order goals were met in.
- **A folded call is a recursive member's, or a definition's that reduction kept.** Both reducers keep a recursive member's call folded where its unfolding sticks again, and a call of a definition where what it unfolds to is a stuck `match`: the first call a reduction unfolded since it last took an arm, a field or an answer, at the root and at every operand a fold forces, so `digit(k, c)` stays `digit(k, c)` and not the private helper its body calls. A definition whose unfolding names it again is unfolded, since its recursion is no group's and nothing would end a pair of its calls unfolded against itself. A kept call is its own forced form, and a reader of what it unfolds to unfolds it at its own door: conversion where it meets another shape, every rule that reads it as an atom having had the pair; entailment, where a bound's reduct enters it; the readers of what a stuck form waits on; the refinements' reduced spellings; the form every shared analysis reads a type in (`Driver::force`); and a `Bool` formula's leaf (`Reducer::unfold_kept`), so a leaf set is the value's.

**Rationale.**

- **An assumed goal is an induction hypothesis, and conversion checks no induction.** A goal taken as held where it is met again holds where the second meeting stands at a smaller argument of a total recursion and under the hypotheses of the first, and is a circle otherwise. Whether a recursion descends is asked of a definition, by totality ([Totality of the erased program](totality-of-the-erased-program.md)); a comparison is handed two terms and asks it of neither. So the assumption rests on what makes two meetings one goal, and no key is right for it. One that holds no binder's type takes a goal for its twin under binders of another type, where a lookup under a binder reads the type its position gave it. One that holds the whole scope meets no goal again where a round of unfolding opens a binder, which is every recursion over data, so it decides only what recurs with no binder between: a type that is its own component, which totality refuses.
- **Two groups under shared binders are a congruence.** A group binds its members as a lambda binds its parameter, and two bodies that convert under one binder are one function of it whatever the binder stands for. Nothing is unfolded, so the rule needs no measure and no key, and it holds the two spellings of one recursion that an unfolding reproduces without end.
- **The first unfolding computes, and a second would prove only where both calls re-stick.** One unfolding of both sides is what lets two calls meet that differ in an argument the body discards or computes away. The same pair met again re-stuck on both sides poses the first's question one binder further in, and an answer for it there is the induction again. A call whose unfolding takes its match apart is computing, and computation is what the first unfolding was granted for: two members of one group that hand each other a constructor's fields meet again at every step of the walk, and apart there they would be apart however plainly they compute to one term. Rocq unfolds a fixpoint only where its decreasing argument starts with a constructor; the same condition, asked of a call met again, is what bounds the second unfolding here.
- **Apart is an answer.** A pair left apart is refused at once, where the budget would refuse it in the time the budget takes and as no verdict of the comparison. A near miss is then a row of the law audit, which holds no row a checker does not answer ([Conversion is one relation in both checkers](conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md)).
- **A call reduction keeps is the spelling the problem wrote.** A pair a stuck term is taken apart into holds its atoms as reduction spells them, so a solution, a report's side and a refinement's key hold the call where reduction kept it ([A solution is the rigid side as written](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-solution-is-the-rigid-side-as-written)), and a reduct naming a private helper never reaches a reader who writes none. The carriers' readers know an atom by its spelling, so the spelling kept is one reduction gives at the root and at an operand alike ([A term is one where conversion says so](../arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)), and the layers above the atoms keep the one spelling their folds give ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)). Lean keeps such a call folded in its elaborator alone (smart unfolding), and its kernel and elaborator then disagree where one unfolds and the other does not; here both reducers keep it.
- **One set of rules is what makes the two checkers agree here.** An equation one checker holds by an assumption the other does not make is a program one accepts and the other spends its budget on.

**Rejected.**

- **One key in both checkers.** The certifier's, in the elaborator, holds nothing the three rules do not and leaves the same equations to the budget. The elaborator's, in the certifier, needs every binder's type in the key and a condition over every cycle that the recursion descends, which is totality's judgment made by conversion.
- **A cyclic proof with a trace condition**, as CycleQ checks one. It is sound, and it makes conversion a search whose verdict follows which earlier goal a later one met, held by a certifier that then decides an induction.
- **Never unfolding a stuck call**, as Rocq's conversion does not. Two calls apart in an argument their body discards are one by the single unfolding, and the standard library rests on it.
- **A stuck node handed back whole as written, every operand that reaches a stuck form kept as written, or every call kept.** The folds read their layers off the terms, so a node handed back whole hides the layers a fold above it reads, and the readers know an atom by its spelling, so a spelling no reduction gives — a motive's β-redex, a call whose unfolding is another call or a projection — is a second atom beside the one it stands for; each refused the standard library.
- **The innermost definition's call kept, as Lean keeps the call whose `match` is stuck.** For `digit(k, c)` it is `at_place(k, c / 4, c % 4)`, a helper `/std/Map/digits` keeps private, which a solution would then name.
- **A definition's call keyed as a stuck form in the classification**, so two definitions' calls with one unfolding are classed by conversion. It lets every such call be one with every application, and a derived `Eql` over an indexed family, whose `&&` holds its right operand as written, put so many pairs to the certifier's conversion that the budget ran out; a `Bool` leaf read through the call meets them with nothing classed.
- **A written subterm looked up by its reduct where a solution is committed.** It reads a reduct back to a spelling, the model of reduction beside the printer the solution decision turns down.
- **Pairing members as they are met**, ΠΣ's partial bijection, so that two groups of two shapes can be one. Position is what both checkers open a group by and what Rocq's kernel compares two fixpoints by, and no program asks for more.
- **No answer for a pair met again**, as a spent budget is none: a row of the law audit states a verdict.
- **The budget alone.** It ends a regress where a verdict is at hand.
- **Whether forcing moves a call**, as the condition a pair met again is unfolded on. Forcing runs a call's whole chain of unfoldings and keeps it folded wherever the chain re-sticks, so `first(T/node(k))`, which takes its match apart once and re-sticks in `first_in(k)`, would read as making no progress, and the pair would be apart.
- **A count of unfoldings or of reduction steps between two meetings.** A remembered reduction answers in no steps, so a count would follow which memo was hit first.
- **A problem remembered as written, the unfoldings it stands inside left out.** A recursive value that is its own body, opened alone against any term, poses the problem it was opened from, and skipped as posed already it is held: the assumption again, by a path no pair records.
- **A verdict reached across a pair left apart, remembered.** It would answer for the pair where it is met outside its own unfolding, which is where one unfolding may hold it.
- **A remembered verdict answering whatever is being unfolded where it is asked.** A verdict reached by unfolding a pair would answer inside that pair's own unfolding, where a derivation meets the pair apart, so whether a goal held would follow whether its inner pair was met first from outside.
- **Remembering and answering verdicts only where no pair is being unfolded.** It refuses nothing more, and it gives up every hit inside an unfolding, where two equal graphs built apart are as often compared.
