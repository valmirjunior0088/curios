# Strict positivity

**Assumes.** Every `induct` and `struct` declaration is strictly positive modulo polarity ([Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../types/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md)).

**Status.** **probed** at shapes chosen to make the lattice's accepting side wrong (`curios`'s `tests::positivity::refusal_tests`, with `composition_tests` as the accepting controls, and `tests::board`'s `a_non_strict_occurrence_behind_a_record_is_still_refused`), at the positions the walk skips (`tests::positivity::index_tests`), and at both coverage modes; all hold.

- **The accepting diagonals are `Unused` and `Strict` alone**, so a merely-positive occurrence is refused as *positively, but not strictly*, which keeps Coquand–Paulin's construction out of an impredicative `Prop`. The attacked spellings: the direct and double negative, each bare, through an `induct` parameter, a `struct` parameter and a `let`-defined alias; the negative under `List`; one behind a type-level `match`, which is unfolded rather than read opaquely; and a higher-kinded parameter, `induct Bad(F : (Type) -> Type) | mk(x : F(Bad(F)))`, where the analysis answers *a position the checker cannot see through* rather than `Unused`. A declaration reaching itself through a type-former parameter is refused, and one not recursing through it admitted.
- **The skipped positions cannot be reached.** A self-referencing index domain fails kinding before the pass runs, and reaching one through a second declaration requires inhabiting it, binding the witness in a payload the walk sees. An occurrence in an index target stays admitted: an index is not stored, and eliminating refines the motive to it.
- **The kernel believes no archived vector.** It analyzes the whole program at complete coverage, where an out-of-set name is `Mixed`; `tests/driven.rs::an_out_of_set_vector_is_believed_only_under_partial_coverage` holds the two modes to opposite verdicts on one input, and the partial read is backstopped by the certifier re-deriving every vector.
- **A reduction the driver refuses stays refusing.** A payload type the driver cannot unfold is followed at `Mixed` (`tests/driven.rs::a_payload_type_the_driver_cannot_reduce_is_refused_not_admitted`), and a set refused only after such a reduction is refused for the budget, naming the declaration whose walk ran out, rather than reported as not positive (`a_strict_payload_the_driver_cannot_reduce_is_refused_for_the_budget`). The pass runs on a restored budget of its own (`curios-cert`'s `recheck::budget_tests::positivity_is_judged_on_its_own_budget`).
- **A nominal former reads alike in both spellings.** A type constructor reaches the walk as an unfolded `InductType` or a folded application of its group's member, and `Env::force` takes the β step on the exposed value as well as on a projection, so a declaration's verdict does not move with what else the module declares — `/std/Toml`'s recursion through `Map`, `Option` and `Node` reads `Strict` throughout. `curios-elab`'s `reduce::reduction_tests::an_application_whose_group_dissolved_to_its_member_still_unfolds` and `curios-cert`'s `kernel::whnf::rules_tests` twin hold it.
