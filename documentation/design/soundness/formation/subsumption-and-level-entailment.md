# Subsumption and level entailment

**Assumes.** A term typing at `A` may be admitted at `B` whenever `A ≤ B`, decided under the item's assumed constraints ([Subsumption is a relation, not a traversal order](../../types/subsumption-is-a-relation-not-a-traversal-order.md)).

**Status.** **probed**, at the oracle and from source; the level rung is reached from source only at levels the elaborator chose, so at stated levels, offsets and hypotheses its evidence is fixtures against the checkers.

- **Only the domain can be wrong in the admitting direction.** Domains are compared by conversion and codomains cumulatively; a covariant domain would hand a function taking small arguments a large one. `kernel::infer::sort_tests::a_function_types_domain_is_invariant` requires both directions refused, `a_function_types_codomain_is_cumulative` holds the other half, and `the_head_rules_still_decide_a_bare_sort` is the control; mutation-checked, and comparing domains cumulatively fails one invariance assertion and leaves the other green, which is why they are a pair. `tests::board::subsumption_tests` puts the same three propositions to the compiler under the same names: a name checked against a function type forms the Π being subsumed, as `Holds` handed to `Eq/subst` does.
- **The fast path is contained in the oracle.** The oracle reasons forward from the upper level to the least model its hypotheses force, starting from exactly the facts the fast path reads, and firing only raises it, so the oracle accepts whatever the fast path does. `entail::tests::the_structural_fast_path_never_outruns_the_oracle` sweeps that over every level of two parameters at small constants and offsets under several hypothesis sets, and asserts that the two predicates do disagree somewhere; mutation-checked.
- **Every accepted goal holds in the naturals.** `entail::tests::every_goal_the_oracle_accepts_holds_in_every_small_instance` checks each accepted goal, under hypothesis sets reaching every rule and a loop, against every assignment of two parameters to `0..=3` satisfying the hypotheses. `a_hypothesis_does_not_gain_an_offset`, `a_constant_is_not_bounded_by_a_bare_parameter` and `a_premise_carrying_atoms_bounds_a_constant_at_its_floor` pin the clauses whose loss would admit, and `a_looping_hypothesis_set_is_refused_rather_than_chased` the bound past which the oracle refuses.
- **The level refusal Girard's paradox needs is reached from source**, in the elaborator: `tests::universes::a_type_quantifying_over_types_cannot_be_instantiated_at_itself` and `the_self_application_girards_paradox_needs_is_refused`, with `the_same_quantifier_instantiates_at_a_type_below_it` as the control. The elaborator refuses while the module is built, so the kernel's verdict on it is unrecorded.
