# Intrinsic signatures

**Assumes.** Every intrinsic's declared operand and result types are the ones its carrier has.

**Evidence.** Probed against the kernel directly, a row at a time, and auditable only below Core. Typing hands each intrinsic a type off `Intrinsic::signature`, and the roster is the definition of what an intrinsic means, so no surface program can force a wrong row: the spelling and the signature are read from the same table, both checkers walk the one signature, and `/sys`'s declarations restate it, which the prelude build checks against the table. What a wrong row does disagree with is everything else that states the same carrier — the fold, whose operands and result are Rust values of one type each, and the algebra's declaration of the operation — and that is how one would admit: an application typed at a carrier its reduct is no value of, admitted where its fold has no value under laws that take the bound for granted, or read by a law stated over another carrier ([the board's shared route](../the-soundness-board.md)). `curios-cert`'s `kernel::infer::signature_tests` holds the roster to each at closed operands, with `Holds` defined as `/sys` defines it, so a bound is discharged by its decision reducing rather than assumed.

- **A fold lands in the carrier its row states.** `every_fold_lands_in_the_carrier_its_row_states` takes one application of every row a fold answers, at the edge of the domain its bound states — the last index, a window ending where the run does, `255` into a byte, the negative zero into `Nat` — and asserts that the row types it, that the fold gives a value, and that the kernel types the value at the type the row stated. Mutation-checked a row at a time: a comparison producing `Nat`, an `Int` shift counted by an `Int`, and a bound tightened by one each fail it.
- **A bound is its fold's domain, at the edge.** `a_bounded_row_is_refused_one_step_past_its_folds_domain` takes every row that states a bound one step past that edge, where the kernel refuses it by the bound's mismatch and the fold reports or declines. A bound loosened by one — `nat < 257`, `index <= len` — admits its sample and fails.
- **A literal is typed at the carrier its payload is a value of.** The grid types a fold's value by its literal's row, so a literal's row wrong in step with an operation's would pass it; `a_literal_is_typed_at_the_carrier_its_payload_is_a_value_of` states each, and its former at level zero.
- **An operation is declared to the algebra at the carrier its row types.** `Intrinsic::algebra` writes the carriers a second time inside the trusted base, and the bounds oracle, the peels, the negations and the round trips read it per operation of a carrier, so an operation declared at a carrier its row does not type is a law applied to values it was never true of. `an_operation_is_declared_to_the_algebra_at_the_carrier_its_row_types` reads every declaration against its row; `IntShr` declared at the naturals, where a right shift never exceeds what it shifts, fails it.
- **A row no fold answers produces a description.** The effect carrier's rows — its two constructors, and the cell and channel operations — compute nothing, and the one way a wrong row among them admits is by handing out what an `Io` yields, which a diverging host call inhabits at any type ([A term outside `Io` performs no effect](../elimination/a-term-outside-io-performs-no-effect.md)). `every_row_no_fold_answers_produces_an_inert_description` types each at an `Io` and finds each its own reduct; `IoBind` producing its bare result fails it.
- **A parameterized former reports its own sort**, at its element's level and never at its element's sort, or a list of proofs would infer at `Prop` ([What a proposition may carry](what-a-proposition-may-carry.md)) and a list of types would be as small as the types it lists. `a_parameterized_former_sits_at_its_elements_level_and_never_in_prop` states it of all four formers, mutation-checked at `sort_of_intrinsic` in both directions.

The grids are written a row at a time, so a row added to the roster, or a declaration to the algebra, owes them a sample and goes unreached until it has one. Each row is reached at one closed instantiation, which is what its carriers need: what value a fold gives across its operands is [Open fold laws and the sum normal form](../conversion/open-fold-laws-and-the-sum-normal-form.md)'s and [The binary64 model and its NaN rule](../conversion/the-binary64-model-and-its-nan-rule.md)'s. What stays auditable only is the third place the carriers are written, `curios-elab`'s `erase_intrinsic`, which erases each operand against a type restated by hand and compared with the roster by nothing; a disagreement there is a miscompilation rather than an admission ([the board's first boundary](../the-soundness-board.md)).
