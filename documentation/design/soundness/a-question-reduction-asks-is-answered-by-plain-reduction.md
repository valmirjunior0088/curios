# A question reduction asks is answered by plain reduction

**Decision.** Each checker has two reductions. A judgment's reduction asks its checker's conversion at three points and nowhere else: which atoms of a pair or of a stuck fold are one, whether a stuck form is an equation's scrutinee, and which two atoms of a connective the decision diagram leaves undecided may be one ([A term is one where conversion says so](../arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md), [Case equations and their key](case-equations-and-their-key.md)). Plain reduction asks nothing: it answers every question a judgment's reduction puts, and every shared analysis reads a term by it (`Env::force`). Each keeps its own answers, and one read under an equation lives as long as the equation.

- **A classing is bounded as every comparison is.** Comparing two atoms may class in turn, at any depth, and each question is the checker's own conversion inside the pairs of the goal the atoms belong to, so two calls of a recursive function that differ in an argument are unfolded against each other once and are apart met again inside that unfolding ([Conversion recurrence](conversion-recurrence.md)). Nothing else limits what a classing asks, so a question's verdict is a function of its two atoms, in both checkers.
- **Two calls of one name apart in a universe instance are asked about by their arguments.** A classing reads two applications of one head, up to its instances, as possibly one, and where the instance is all the heads differ in it puts each pair of arguments to the checker and holds the two one where every pair converts.
- **An answer the solver can change is not kept.** The elaborator's question is answered no where a yes would commit a solution or a level constraint, and it joins no two levels to reach one: two instances apart in a level the store does not read as one are compared by their value, reduced and unfolded as the kernel reads two instances it finds apart, and are one where that makes them one. A weak equation, which requires nothing of two levels a nominal family is irrelevant in, is no commitment. No reduct is remembered across a question so declined, or answered no past two levels it left apart, an equation's reduced spelling settled across one is settled again once either solver has moved, and a scope that put a question and is rolled back with level constraints withdrawn clears what was reduced inside it. The kernel is handed ground instances and has no solver.

**Rationale.**

- **Whether a term is total is read on no verdict of conversion's.** Conversion's proof irrelevance is sound because a program is accepted only once totality has passed ([Totality of the erased program](totality-of-the-erased-program.md)), which holds only while deciding whether a term is total invokes no conversion. The analyses that decide it reduce through `Env::force` alone, so `Env::force` is plain reduction in both checkers, and plain reduction has to exist whatever answers a question. Which terms owe totality is the judgment's to say, by the reduction typing itself used, so none it took for a proof or a type escapes.
- **A question is answered by plain reduction**, which costs no mechanism the point above does not already need. It is one conversion the budget bounds, over the relation the board already argues for, so its soundness is a corollary of entries that stand; it terminates wherever conversion terminates; and an answer is a function of the term, the definitions, the equations in force and which of the two reductions read it. Coq Modulo Theory reduces modulo a relation defined before reduction is (Jouannaud and Strub, 2017), which is this shape; the smart case defines normalisation and convertibility under an arm's equations mutually (Altenkirch, 2011).
- **A classing needs no bound of its own.** A question a classing asks is a comparison, and the regress classings can fall into is the one the pair rule ends: two calls of a recursive function that differ in an argument unfold to two stuck `match`es whose arms hold the same two calls one step on, the factors of a product nothing pairs, and both checkers keep such a call folded where its unfolding is stuck and still names its group, so the classing that meets the two compares them as folded calls, which are unfolded against each other once. A bound read off where an atom stands misses an atom met forced: a call written in a `match`'s arm is met as its unfolding, which stands inside nothing written, so two spellings of one value under it stay two atoms. And it makes a verdict follow whether a classing is asking, which every table of verdicts in both checkers would have to be keyed by.
- **A remembered answer is a function of what it is filed under.** The elaborator files a reduct by its term, the equations in force and the reduction that took it, and a question's answer rests on the solver's state as well. Remembered across a question declined for a commit, a stuck reduct would make acceptance follow the order terms were reduced in; remembered past a constraint a scope withdrew, a decided one would be an equation the kernel does not have.
- **A level a value does not read keeps no two terms apart.** Every written use of a universe-polymorphic name mints a level that stays open until its declaration closes ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md)), and a concept that states a proposition among its fields hands that proposition's level to each of its witnesses, so two written quotients are two instances of one witness. Conversion's shortcut holds two spellings apart in levels alone one by joining the levels, which a question may not do; answered no there, two atoms each holding a quotient are two atoms until their declaration closes, though each quotient reduces to one operation at every level. Compared by value, the pair is decided as Rocq's conversion decides it, where two applications of one constant that fail on a universe comparison have "the terms are unfolded" ([reference manual, *Universe polymorphism*](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/master/refman/addendum/universe-polymorphism.html)), and as Lean's `unfoldBothDefEq` does, "First try to solve unification by unifying arguments" and "If it fails, unfold both and continue" ([`Lean/Meta/ExprDefEq.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Meta/ExprDefEq.lean)). A yes so reached holds whatever the two levels close at, so it is kept; a no can become a yes once they are one, so it is not. Two projections of one recursive group reproduce their levels under every unfolding, so no value decides them and they stay two.

**Rejected.**

- **Classing nothing inside a classing.** It ends the nesting, and refuses `f(g(a + b) * h(c + d)) * f(g(c + a) * h(b + d))` against its commutation, two atoms to a side each holding two to class, where the same equation one atom to a side is held.
- **Holding a classing inside the two atoms being compared**, an atom classed inside a classing only where it stands inside one of the two and is neither. The walk hands an atom out forced, so a call written in a stuck `match`'s arm is met as its unfolding and stands inside nothing written, and two spellings of one value under it stay two atoms; a verdict follows whether a classing is asking, which a table of verdicts keyed by the pair alone files as the pair's; and the regress it ends is the pair rule's.
- **Charging the nesting and letting the budget end it.** A comparison that unfolds without end spends its budget over minutes, so a wrong equation would wait that long to be refused.
- **The elaborator joining the levels of two calls a classing meets apart.** An answer the solver can change is not kept, the kernel has no solver, and where a call is commuted or repeated the levels close apart, so the kernel refuses what the elaborator accepted.
- **Parking a pair while a level metavariable stands in it.** It accepts only the pairs whose sides close as one term.
- **A vector for a recursive member**, so that two instances apart in a level it does not read are a weak equation: a member reproduces its levels under every unfolding, and the universe decision rejects it.
- **Reading a stuck scrutinee in a judgment's reduction alone.** The diagram and the distribution ask no checker, as a fold asks none, and plain reduction takes what the readers decide, an equation's answer among it: kept from plain reduction, the rule would leave stuck, for every shared analysis, a match both checkers' judgments take apart.
- **Reduction and conversion defined mutually, bounded by the budget alone.** It is complete to any depth with no argument for its soundness to stand on, and a question is a comparison of its own, inside no unfolding, so two recursive calls a comparison leaves apart inside their own unfolding would be unfolded again by every question asked there, until the budget ran out.
- **A fixpoint with cycles detected**, a lookup already standing for a term declining. A reduct taken while one declined is less reduced than the same term reduced outside it, so every memo and settled spelling would need to know, a verdict would follow the order terms were reduced in, and the regress above is no cycle it would see, each round opening fresh binders.
- **A third reduction**, answering a question inside a question: the same argument once more, for a program nothing has yet written.
- **Answering no wherever a yes would join two levels**, the shortcut's commitment discarded with its verdict: two written quotients are then two atoms, and a hypothesis is refused at a goal that is its own type one unfolding on.
- **Joining the two levels weakly where the sides are numbers.** A weak equation requires nothing, so where a declaration's closing drops one the kernel is handed two instances of a recursive member apart in a level it compares; and the rule is keyed on a type, which leaves the same pair two under any other operation.
- **Clearing the elaborator's reducts wherever a rollback moved a level**, whatever was asked inside. A witness probe rolls back after every trial and no rule of reduction reads a level, so a scope that put no question has nothing to clear.
