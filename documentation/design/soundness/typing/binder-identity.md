# Binder identity

**Assumes.** No identity the kernel mints aliases an existing one, so opening a binder instantiates exactly the intended occurrences.

**Status.** **argued**, with one positive control. Every rule of the soundness board is stated over terms whose binders are distinct, and the kernel mints binders of its own — comparing under a telescope, eta-expanding, opening a motive or an arm — into an identity space the lowerer, the elaborator and the archived prelude have minted into. The kernel raises its own counter past each local as it enters the context rather than believing a floor a caller computes, and `curios-core`'s `names` module states why a floor may be derived: the entropy seed only raises. `kernel::convert::recursion_tests::conversion_separates_a_constant_from_the_identity_at_every_colliding_index` is the control, mutation-checked: two lambdas differing only in whether their body names the binder become one term the moment an opened binder aliases a free local.

The kernel's `let` substitutes where the elaborator's binds, so a local definition is never an identity the kernel carries.
