# The bounds oracle and the division family

**Assumes.** Every bound `nat_bound` reports — its arms `curios-algebra`'s `Operation::upper_bound`, one per declared operation, reached through `Intrinsic::algebra` — holds of every value its term can take, and every operand `nat_dominators` lists is never exceeded by the value.

**Evidence.** Probed by four gates, three in `curios-core`'s `reduce::intrinsic::nat_tests` and one in `curios`. This is the one fold family that reaches conversion and carries executable backing; the other open folds are [Open fold laws and the sum normal form](open-fold-laws-and-the-sum-normal-form.md)'s.

- **The division family sees through an open dividend by unconditional laws.** `Nat/div` and `Nat/rem` take the floor law `(i + f) / n = f/n + (i + f%n) / n` and its remainder twin, which hold whatever `i` is, and a split dividing a scaled symbol out whole when every remaining summand is bounded and the bounds together stay below `n`, so none can carry. A zero dividend is `0` with remainder `0`, a divisor of `1` returns the dividend, and a dividend identical to its nonzero divisor is `1`, resting on the proof the operation already demands. Nothing conditional is admissible: `(a + b)/n = a/n + b/n` is false at `a = b = 1`, `n = 2`, and one such equation is all congruence needs.
- **Euclid's identity recombines what the split reads apart.** Wherever a sum holds `k` remainders `x % d` beside `k` copies of `d · (x / d)`, the normal form reads the pair as `k · x` — `curios-algebra`'s `euclid`, driven by `Nat::recombine` and, for truncated division over ℤ, `int_recombine`. It holds for every `x` and nonzero `d`. The multiple is matched in the distributed form the product fold leaves, a quotient pairs with a remainder on its dividend and divisor and never on its proof, and a monomial is compared as a multiset of factors since its factor order is a hash that sees the proof; over ℤ only copies agreeing in sign are taken, so the rewrite always shrinks a sum.
- **A bound exists where the result is bounded in every operand the value is not antitone in**, which licenses reading past a subtrahend, a divisor and a shift amount. `NatShl` has no arm, since a left shift's result is the one size its operands do not bound and `nat_bound` takes no reducer to charge; a literal count reaches the oracle as the product `2ᵏ · x`. A `Byte` is `0..=255` by its carrier, and `Byte` is no wire type, so [the foreign wire contract](../formation/foreign-wire-contract.md) keeps an embedder from supplying one outside the range; `x % n < n` holds by definition. An under-report is a false definitional equation and an over-report only withholds the rule, so the gate asserts `value <= bound`.
- **A comparison reads a bound against a literal, strictly and exactly**: a bound below the literal is the strict verdict, one meeting it the non-strict one, so `Byte/to_nat(b) <= 255` decides as `Byte/to_nat(b) < 256` does (`compare_tests`'s `nat_decides_a_bound_met_exactly_as_non_strict`). **An operand a value never exceeds** — the minuend, the dividend, either operand of `and`, the shifted value, and the divisor a remainder stays below — is compared in the value's place, transitivity through one operand, one strict subterm down (`nat_decides_a_value_against_an_operand_it_never_exceeds`, `dominators_upper_bound_every_closed_instantiation`).
- **The gates.** `bound_upper_bounds_every_closed_instantiation` checks the oracle never under-reports, one block per shape written by hand, so an arm added without a block leaves the gate passing while checking nothing. `euclid_split_is_a_euclidean_division` checks the specification — remainder below divisor, `n·quotient + remainder` equal to the dividend at every sampled instantiation, compared on values. `an_uncertain_summand_leaves_the_division_neutral` checks the refusals, an indivisible coefficient and an unbounded summand. `curios`'s `a_literal_divisor_sees_through_a_symbolic_dividend` states the equations as `Eq/refl()` proofs; `every_open_fold_law_preserves_the_value_at_every_closed_instantiation` and `tests::laws` pin the recombination with three controls — the multiple without its remainder, the wrong multiple, a remainder of another dividend. The law table declares one family here, `x / 1 = x`; the rest rest on a bound or a proof operand a family cannot carry and stay rows written in `tests::laws::written`.
