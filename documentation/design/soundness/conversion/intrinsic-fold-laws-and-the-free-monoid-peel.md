# Intrinsic fold laws and the free-monoid peel

**Assumes.** A `Deduction::Equal` or `Conclusion` a peel answers is a genuine equation, and a `Deduction::Impossible` a genuine disequality, on the carrier's values, at every reader.

**Status.** **probed** by value grids that hold each verdict to its own obligation at every closed instantiation — an `Equal` must hold everywhere, an `Impossible` fail everywhere, an `Equivalent`'s residuals be equi-satisfiable with the pair they replace, `Undecided` promise nothing — each asserting its own verdict tally, so a grid that decided nothing fails. The peels are functions of `curios-core`, run for both checkers by `curios-analysis`'s one chain, so a wrong `Equal` admits by [the perimeter's shared route](../the-soundness-perimeter.md) and a wrong `Impossible` by a second: it reaches inversion and excuses an omitted arm ([Coverage](../elimination/coverage.md)). The equations the folds compute are [Open fold laws and the sum normal form](open-fold-laws-and-the-sum-normal-form.md)'s.

- **`Nat` cancellation.** `Nat::cancel_common` (`curios-algebra`'s `Combination::cancel_common`) strips the floor and summands two operands share, on `Nat` under `+` being a cancellative commutative monoid; it decides a commuted sum equal and a surviving positive floor against the identity a clash. It removes one occurrence per match, pairs summands by identity so incompleteness declines, and returns a pass that cancelled nothing identically. `peel_nat_terms` is reached by a pair either side of which is headed by a successor floor or a sum spine, so a floorless reassociation meets too; a floorless pair sharing no summand declines rather than re-entering the same congruence. `every_nat_peel_verdict_holds_at_every_closed_instantiation`, mutation-checked against cancelling as a set (`x + x + 1 ~ x + 1` at `x = 1`). `Int` is peeled as a group by `peel_int_pair`, `a ⋈ b` iff `a - b ⋈ 0`, held at instantiations including negatives by `every_int_peel_verdict_holds_at_every_closed_instantiation`.
- **The `Bin` and `List` peels.** `every_bin_peel_verdict_holds_at_every_closed_instantiation` and `every_list_peel_verdict_holds_at_every_closed_instantiation` cover symbolic chunks cancelling with a clash past them, window fusion across a shared seam, the empty-window drop, append as concatenation, and a near miss beside each; the window laws' domain is the precondition's own, `s + l <= len(b)`, and a window is a start and a count, so fusion needs no ordering check. A bare side is read at the equality fold as the one-chunk spine it is (`free_monoid_tests`'s `a_spine_against_a_bare_variable_clashes_on_a_positive_residual`). `[a + b]` and `[b + a]` are one list, so a literal-head clash is `Bin`'s alone. A pair spelled otherwise than its segment list regroups as `Equivalent` to its flat spelling, and a flat pair whose leading chunks differ declines (`spine`'s `peel_bin_regroups_a_nested_concat_whose_leading_chunks_differ` and its twins; `curios`'s `a_symbolic_run_is_the_same_value_however_it_is_grouped`). One arm serves both grains, through `element_of_run` and `Generator`. Mutations caught: fusing without the seam check, and `Bin`'s head clash applied to `List`.
- **A fill is decided against the cons it equals.** `replicate(n + 1, a)` begins with `a` whatever `n` is, so `peel_bin` emits that generator and leaves the shorter fill a symbolic chunk; a count with no floor may be empty, so the fill stays an opaque chunk exposing no generator and declines. The eliminator's `uncons` reads the successor through the same `Nat::peel_succ`. `peel_bin_decides_a_fill_of_a_successor_against_the_cons_it_equals` and `peel_bin_declines_a_fill_whose_count_is_not_a_successor`; mutation-checked against reading an unknown count as empty, which decides `replicate(n, c)` equal to `x[]`.
- **Atoms are compared by identity, their arguments forced where a pair decided nothing.** `force_atoms` forces each atom's arguments under the rigid heads they are stuck on, never under a binder, keeps each head verbatim, and orders the sums and products inside them by structural hash; both are denotation-preserving. It is asked by name in `convert_intrinsics` and `connectives_agree`, once a pair as it stood decided nothing, and a pair still undecided goes on to the congruence on its original spelling. `tests::laws::written` states the rows at both binder orders.
- **Products and comparisons pair by identity, in conversion only.** A product's factor order and a comparison's side order are structural hashes, and an unsolved metavariable's is its own number. `peel_monomial` pairs two products' factors under one coefficient, and `peel_comparison` two comparisons' sides, through `pair_factors`: one multiset is `Equal`, one leftover on each side is `Conclusion::Sufficient`, which solves a metavariable against its partner, and nothing else — never `Impossible`, and not in `peel_intrinsic`, since `x · f = x · g` gives no `f = g`. `peel_monomial_pairs_factors_by_identity_not_by_position`, `peel_comparison_pairs_sides_by_identity_not_by_position`, the `numeric` corpus's `products` module, and `a_metavariable_is_solved_through_every_law_that_can_solve_it`, each mutation-checked.
- **Connectives.** `&&` and `||` are semilattices, so `peel_bool` decides equal leaf sets `Equal` and anything else `Undecided`, after `normalize_bool` forces each tree once per comparison. `peel_symmetric` decides a swapped `==`, `!=`, `xor` and the bitwise `and`, `or` and `xor` on ℕ `Equal`, as a peel because respelling operands at the fold would move past a guard's refinement key. A law across connectives — De Morgan, absorption, distribution — is `decide_bool`'s truth table over `curios-algebra`'s `Formula`, a comparison and its dual one atom at two polarities: agreement at every assignment is `Equal`, and anything else is nothing, since independent atoms over-approximate the values they reach together. It declines past `BOOL_ATOM_CAP`, eight atoms, charges before evaluating, and leaves `Flt` out. `truth_tests`, `every_truth_table_decision_holds_at_every_closed_instantiation`, and `a_bool_tree_converts_with_the_bare_term_it_equals_at_every_assignment` in both checkers; mutation-checked against reading a dual at its own polarity.
- **A position through a window or a concatenation is a comparison.** `get(slice(xs, s, l), i)` is `get(xs, s + i)`, a window of a window the window it names, and `get(xs ++ ys, len(xs) + i)` is `get(ys, i)`; rewriting would owe a bound no reduct can carry, while a comparison owes none. `rooted` reads a base through its windows to a root and an absolute start, `same_position` reads an operand of a concatenation at the measures before it, and `peel_position` and the window arm of `curios-algebra`'s `strip_common_prefix` decide `Equal` or `Undecided`, never `Impossible`. `peel_position_decides_a_position_through_a_window_equal`, `position_tests`, and `every_position_verdict_holds_at_every_closed_instantiation`; mutation-checked against dropping the inner start and the operand's offset.
- **Comparisons align through their linear views, probe-side.** `align_comparisons` reads a negated comparison as its dual and two `Nat` or `Int` comparisons through `LinearViews::align`, `<` as the `<=` of its successor over ℤ, so `x + 1 <= y` and `x < y` are one proposition — on the total order, the group and the embedding ℕ → ℤ, which is why `Flt`, whose ordered comparisons against a NaN are false both ways, is left out. `a_floored_le_meets_the_lt_it_spells` and `two_comparisons_whose_views_differ_are_respelled_alike`.
- **A round trip cancels through the rule that inverts it.** `Intrinsic::undone`, over `curios-algebra`'s `round_trip`: `Nat/to_byte` and `Int/to_nat` state their domains, so widening back is the number again, and a widened value lies in the narrowing's domain, so it narrows back whatever the proof; `Flt/of_le_bytes(Flt/to_le_bytes(f))` is `f`, one way only as declared; `Bytes` and `Bits` regroup both ways. ℕ → ℤ is a semiring homomorphism: `int_of_nat` widens summand by summand, `int_preimage` reads a non-negative combination of widened naturals back, and `compare_int` decides such a pair on the preimages. `compare_tests`'s `conversion_folds_preserve_the_number`, `every_open_fold_law_preserves_the_value_at_every_closed_instantiation`, and `tests::laws`'s carriers, with the controls the embedding stops short of.
- **A threshold is the theory's.** A right shift answers a count past the value's width without narrowing it to a host index (`reduce::intrinsic::shift_tests::a_right_shift_by_a_count_past_the_host_word_folds_to_the_arithmetic`).
- **Each family is mutation-checked.** `curios-algebra`'s table declares commutativity, associativity at the words, distribution at `Bool`, the dual, the successor seam, cancellation and the inverse pair, and `tests::laws::generated` states every instance through both checkers and at closed values. Withdrawing each fails the prelude itself — commutativity `/std/Int/eq_of_eql`, flattening `/std/Map`'s use of `Bytes/cons_inj_tail`, the truth table the `Bool` distribution rows, the dual `/std/Int/abs`, the seam `/std/Char/to_ascii_lower`, cancellation `/std/Nat/Le/add_r`, the round trip `/std/Int/view`.
