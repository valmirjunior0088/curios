# Definitional proof irrelevance

**Assumes.** Any two inhabitants of a proposition are interchangeable ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../../theory/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)).

**Evidence.** Argued, by the fixpoint on (V) below; its side condition, [the large-elimination guard](../elimination/large-elimination-guard.md), is probed.

- **Where it fires.** Both checkers try it before any other rule wherever neither side is flexible. In the kernel a struct literal's fields and a constructor's payload compare at the declaration's telescope, so a proof there reaches the rule at its proposition and it fires on every proof-carrying value built from a different proof of one fact — `Str/concat` associating, two `Vec`s counted by different proofs; where a child is compared untyped — a stuck elimination's scrutinee, a tuple literal's component in its arm — the rule is asked of the type a lookup gives the two sides, a neutral's, a stuck elimination's or a constructor's value's ([Eta and untyped child positions](eta-and-untyped-child-positions.md)). Every firing is at a computed proposition, the validity-predicate shape irrelevance exists for, and none has syntactically identical sides. Both checkers carry `fires_at_a_computed_proposition`, pinning that the classifier reads a stuck `match` by its motive, with the identical term at a `Type` motive as its control; mutation-checked against restricting the rule to nominal propositions.
- **The rule and the obligations form a fixpoint, not a circle.** Irrelevance accepts without inspecting either term, which is correct because every `Prop` inhabitant is total — (V)'s job — and elaboration uses the rule while the obligations are unchecked; [Conversion recurrence](conversion-recurrence.md) stands the same way on (T). Conversion consults sort classification, which reaches reduction and neutral synthesis, and whether a term is total is decided with no conversion invoked, so acceptance validates the use after the fact: a program is accepted only if (V) passes, and then every irrelevance step was applied to total terms. A partial proof arriving by witness resolution rather than by settling is refused by (V) (`tests::soundness::a_partial_proof_cannot_arrive_through_witness_resolution`).
- **Whether a term is total is read by plain reduction, and which terms owe it is the judgment's to say.** A judgment's reduction asks its checker's conversion at a missed equation and at a stuck fold, so reduction is not free of conversion, and the obligations reduce in two places. The analyses that decide whether a term is total — totality's grading and descent, positivity — force through `Env::force` alone, which is plain reduction in both checkers and asks nothing: they are generic over `curios-analysis`'s `Env` and reach no other reducer, so what they read rests on no verdict of conversion's and (V) passing means what it meant. Which positions owe the obligation is decided where the judgment records one (`curios-cert`'s `obligation::erased_half`), by the judgment's own reduction, so a term typing took for a proof or a type by a question is classified by that question and none escapes; classified by plain reduction instead, a proposition only a question reveals would be compared irrelevantly and never checked. That classification rests on verdicts, and the fixpoint closes by their order. Take the first irrelevance step applied to a proof that is not total: every verdict before it is correct, the proof was typed and its position classified before conversion met it, so it was classified a proof and (V) passed over it. `a_shared_analysis_reads_a_term_by_reduction_that_asks_nothing` in `kernel::whnf::equations_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `reduce::reduction_tests`, mutation-checked against an `Env::force` that reduces as a judgment does. No test holds the order: it is read off the kernel's walk, which types a term before it converts it.
- **(V) establishes absence of partiality, not normalization, and needs no more.** Abel and Coquand's `Omega(h)`, under a hypothesis equating every two propositions, reduces forever inside an arm whose case equation makes the cast's proof `refl`, yet is not partial and threatens no consistency — only the checker's budget, which refuses. A proof is therefore never reduced to decide what irrelevance decides (`tests::board::a_proof_is_not_reduced_to_compare_it_with_another`).
- **The model is missing, not the reasoning.** The fixpoint argues from the implementation's dependency structure; a proof that definitional irrelevance is sound needs a model in which `Prop` is a subsingleton and only total terms are interpreted.
