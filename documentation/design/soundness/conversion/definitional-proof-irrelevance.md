# Definitional proof irrelevance

**Assumes.** Any two inhabitants of a proposition are interchangeable ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../../types/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)).

**Evidence.** Argued, by the fixpoint on (V) below; its side condition, [the large-elimination guard](../elimination/large-elimination-guard.md), is probed.

- **Where it fires.** Both checkers try it before any other rule wherever neither side is flexible. In the kernel a struct literal's fields and a constructor's payload compare at the declaration's telescope, so a proof there reaches the rule at its proposition and it fires on every proof-carrying value built from a different proof of one fact — `Str/concat` associating, two `Vec`s counted by different proofs; an opaque head's argument is compared untyped and forfeits it ([Eta and untyped child positions](eta-and-untyped-child-positions.md)). Every firing is at a computed proposition, the validity-predicate shape irrelevance exists for, and none has syntactically identical sides. Both checkers carry `fires_at_a_computed_proposition`, pinning that the classifier reads a stuck `match` by its motive, with the identical term at a `Type` motive as its control; mutation-checked against restricting the rule to nominal propositions.
- **The rule and the obligations form a fixpoint, not a circle.** Irrelevance accepts without inspecting either term, which is correct because every `Prop` inhabitant is total — (V)'s job — and elaboration uses the rule while the obligations are unchecked; [Conversion recurrence](conversion-recurrence.md) stands the same way on (T). Conversion consults sort classification, which reaches only reduction and neutral synthesis, and (V)'s pipeline never invokes conversion, so acceptance validates the use after the fact: a program is accepted only if (V) passes, and then every irrelevance step was applied to total terms. A partial proof arriving by witness resolution rather than by settling is refused by (V) (`tests::soundness::a_partial_proof_cannot_arrive_through_witness_resolution`).
- **(V) establishes absence of partiality, not normalization, and needs no more.** Abel and Coquand's `Omega(h)`, under a hypothesis equating every two propositions, reduces forever inside an arm whose case equation makes the cast's proof `refl`, yet is not partial and threatens no consistency — only the checker's budget, which refuses. A proof is therefore never reduced to decide what irrelevance decides (`tests::board::a_proof_is_not_reduced_to_compare_it_with_another`).
- **The model is missing, not the reasoning.** The fixpoint argues from the implementation's dependency structure; a proof that definitional irrelevance is sound needs a model in which `Prop` is a subsingleton and only total terms are interpreted.
