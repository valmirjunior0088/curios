# The evaluation memo

**Assumes.** A remembered reduct, sort, inferred type, check or verdict is the kernel's own pure function of the definition store and of what it read of the scope, and replaying one changes what a judgment spends but never what it decides.

**Evidence.** Argued, in the component's five invariants, and probed at the interlock with a live case equation and at the clears of the local-bearing tables. A wrong reduct admits by [the board's shared route](../the-soundness-board.md).

- **Keys are valid by construction.** `invalidate` runs whenever a name is overwritten. A whnf entry for a local-free term has a scope-independent key; one for a local-bearing term — a function of the case equations in force too, and of which reduction took it — lives in tables cleared wherever that set changes, an equation assumed, retracted, withheld or restored. An arm whose scrutinee names no local records no equation, so it changes nothing in force and clears nothing (`kernel::whnf::equations_tests::an_arm_that_records_no_equation_leaves_what_was_remembered`). Which table a term belongs to is decided inside the component.
- **Reduction reads by value.** The kernel types a `let`'s tail over a local that stands for the value (`Kernel::bind`), so a value named twice is typed once. Where reduction or conversion is entered from typing, the term is read with each such name unfolded, once, and beneath that entry every term is a subterm or a reduct of one that was. A type is classified the same way, and a position's erased half filed under its type by value. So every reduct, every case equation, every sort and every verdict is keyed on the spelling a substituted `let` leaves, and none rests on a `let`'s binding, which an arm replaces for its extent and no key of those tables carries. A typing table is keyed on the term as typing walks it, a name included: its answers are filed with the binders they were read under, a `let`'s among them, and a local bound again clears them as a re-typed one does. `kernel::infer::binding_tests::a_remembered_reduct_does_not_rest_on_a_lets_binding` and `reduction_reads_a_let_bound_name_by_value`, each mutation-checked; a debug assertion in `step_var` refuses a `let`-bound local that reaches reduction by name, and `/std` certifies under it.
- **A judgment that reads the scope lives as long as what it read.** `Sort::of`'s answer is remembered per type with no loose index, and beside it which erased half a position at that type belongs to, which is the sort read once more ([Recorded positions and sort-hood](../totality/recorded-positions-and-sort-hood.md)). A local-free type's is kept for the declaration where no case equation is in force, and with the scoped answers under one: classifying it opens binders and reduces what stands beneath them, and an equation answers a term the kernel's conversion holds equal to its scrutinee, which a term naming only a proof the classification opened can be — `g(x)` under the equation of `g(p)`, two proofs of one proposition (`kernel::sort::tests::a_local_free_types_sort_does_not_outlive_the_equations_it_was_read_under` and `a_position_at_a_local_free_type_is_classified_under_its_own_arm`, both orders, each mutation-checked). A type naming a local has its answer in tables cleared with the local-bearing reducts and wherever an arm re-assumes a local at its specialized type — a neutral's sort is read off its binder (`Scope::assume` reports the re-typing). There a stale answer is wrong where a stale reduct is merely unreachable, since a name handed in from outside can be assumed again at another type once its first binder is closed; so each scoped answer is filed with the binders it was read under, a `Prefix`, and is taken only while `Scope::stands` says they are all still the openings they were. A hit spends nothing and mints nothing, as an inferred type's does: a record's sort asks each field's, so a type whose tree is exponential in its depth is classified once per node. `kernel::sort::tests::a_shared_type_is_classified_once_per_node`, agreeing with `Kernel::uncached`; `a_remembered_sort_does_not_outlive_the_equations_it_was_taken_under`, `a_remembered_sort_does_not_outlive_the_type_its_local_stands_at` and `a_remembered_sort_does_not_outlive_its_binder`, each mutation-checked against the clear or the question it holds, the last with `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::a_motives_binders_are_opened_at_the_types_its_family_gives_them`; `a_position_is_classified_under_its_own_arm`, which holds the erased half to the same life; and `kernel::whnf::memo_tests::restoring_the_budget_forgets_a_remembered_sort`.
- **They are consulted at every reduction level.** `whnf_within` probes on entry and stores on exit, and `Memos::whnf` gates the lookup exactly as `Memos::store_whnf` gates the store.
- **What a judgment's reduction remembers and what plain reduction remembers are held apart.** A judgment's reduction asks the kernel's conversion at a missed equation and at a stuck fold, and plain reduction asks nothing ([Definitional proof irrelevance](definitional-proof-irrelevance.md)), so a local-bearing term has a reduct under each. Each reduction has tables of its own, chosen on the lookup as on the store, and an equation's reduced spelling is settled once for each. A local-free term's reduct needs no second table: reducing one meets no local-bearing stuck form, which is the interlock below, and nothing is asked of a term that names no local. A sort and a comparison's verdict are read through reducts, so each is filed by the reduction that read it as well, a local-free type's and a local-free pair's included: classifying or comparing one opens binders, and what is reduced beneath names them. The typing tables and the erased halves are filed once: nothing is typed under plain reduction, a question being a conversion and an analysis only reducing, and `Kernel::record_checked` asserts it. A span of plain reduction that narrows the equations in force clears only what plain reduction remembered, since nothing else runs inside it. `kernel::whnf::equations_tests::a_reduct_is_remembered_by_the_reduction_that_took_it` runs both orders through both entry points and `a_verdict_is_remembered_by_the_reduction_that_reached_it` both orders of a comparison, each agreeing with `Kernel::uncached`, and `kernel::sort::tests::a_sort_is_remembered_by_the_reduction_that_read_it` holds the sorts; mutation-checked against each table read or written whichever reduction asks.
- **Every table lives exactly as long as a budget does**, cleared wherever it is restored, so a hit is free, an entry is bounded by the budget that built it, and nothing another declaration left behind decides what one spends: `kernel::whnf::memo_tests::what_a_declaration_spends_does_not_depend_on_what_was_reduced_before_it` fails once any reduct table is kept across `Kernel::restore_budget` ([A reduction step costs what it builds](../a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md)).
- **A store cannot charge.** `Memos` hands back a `Replay`, and applying it — minting its identities, spending nothing — is `Spend::charge_nothing`'s job. A reduct's minted identities are replayed on a hit; a free hit can only reduce what a judgment spends, so it can turn an exhaustion refusal into an acceptance and never move a semantic refusal.
- **The typing tables replay nothing, and refuse a term that can hold a call.** `types` remembers `infer`'s answer and `checked` each term beside the type it checked at, for terms with no loose index: for the declaration where every term is local-free and no case equation is in force, and otherwise as a sort read off the scope is — cleared where an equation moves or a local is re-typed, filed beside a `Prefix`, taken while it stands. A local-free term typed under an equation is filed with the scoped answers, as its sort is: the table declined such a term outright before it kept anything read off the scope. The check has a table of its own because the rules that introduce a function and a record and descend a `let` never reach `infer`, so a tuple whose two fields are one node is checked against its record once per path whatever `types` holds. Neither table keeps or answers a term naming a member of a group whose body is being checked: a recursive call is an application of that local, recorded where it is typed and graded against the member whose body states it, so a hit loses the call of every body but the first — `kernel::calls::tests::a_call_is_recorded_in_every_body_that_states_it`, mutation-checked, a group of two members stating one body, accepted with the refusal off. What else a typing writes survives a hit. Its position is recorded at every occurrence, and the positions inside it when it was first typed, in the same item. A group that does not descend, typed inside it, is counted again (`Calls::recall_partial`). And a group typed inside it closed to its verdict under what the arms around it had established for the recorder, so the scoped typings are cleared wherever that changes, entered or retracted: most arms assume an equation beside it, the default arm of a `switch` rules zero out for its scrutinee and assumes none — `a_remembered_type_does_not_outlive_what_an_arm_established`, mutation-checked on both sides. They type a graph whose tree can be exponential in its depth once per node, and replaying its minted identities would count per path, so a refusal naming a kernel-minted binder may name it differently with the memos off; freshness holds because the counter never falls. `kernel::infer::typing_tests::a_shared_closed_term_is_typed_once_per_node` and `a_shared_term_over_a_local_is_typed_once_per_node`, agreeing with `Kernel::uncached`; `a_shared_term_under_an_arm_is_typed_once_per_node`; `a_shared_tuple_is_checked_once_per_node`; `a_remembered_type_does_not_outlive_the_equations_it_was_taken_under`, inferring and checking, and `a_remembered_type_does_not_outlive_its_binder`, each mutation-checked; `a_shared_closed_term_mints_once_per_node`; and `kernel::whnf::memo_tests::restoring_the_budget_forgets_a_remembered_type_and_a_remembered_check`.
- **A comparison's verdict is remembered where it assumed nothing.** `converted` keeps the verdict of each comparison by its type and its two sides, under the lives a typing has and the binders it was reached under, both ways, and only for a goal decided with no goal in progress assumed ([Conversion recurrence](conversion-recurrence.md)). Two terms that are equal graphs built apart reach each pair of their nodes along every path, and remembered by pair each is compared once; a refusal for the budget is no verdict and is never filed. `kernel::convert::conversion_tests::two_towers_built_apart_are_compared_once_per_pair_of_nodes`, agreeing with `Kernel::uncached`; `a_remembered_verdict_does_not_outlive_the_equations_it_was_reached_under` and `a_remembered_verdict_does_not_outlive_its_binder`, each mutation-checked; and `kernel::convert::recursion_tests::a_verdict_that_assumed_a_goal_in_progress_is_not_remembered`, which also holds the table to the budget's life.
- **The interlock with case equations.** The argument needs that reducing a local-free term never meets a local-bearing stuck form, so no memoized reduct depends on an equation later retracted; `Scope::refine` states it, and the reduction rules are read against it — substitution, delta into a closed body, iota binding a closed payload, and the eta probe's fresh binders — with a bodiless global the one local-free term that can stick, which `Scope::refine` declines to record. `kernel::whnf::equations_tests::a_case_equation_reaches_the_reduct_and_not_the_memos` and `a_remembered_reduct_does_not_outlive_the_equations_it_was_taken_under` agree with `Kernel::uncached` before, inside and after an arm, mutation-checked against dropping the clears; `kernel_memo_parity` runs the whole prelude with the memos on and off. The settlement bracket's clears rest on the same argument and on that parity alone.
