# Eta and untyped child positions

**Assumes.** Eta at Π, at Σ and at a nominal struct preserves equality, so a type whose every inhabitant those rules and irrelevance equate has one; the type a lookup reads is the term's type, for a neutral off its head, for a stuck elimination off the result typing checked it against, and for a constructor's value off its declaration; a binder an elimination opens has the type its constructor or its family states; and comparing a child at `Type` where no type is available forfeits rules rather than adding them.

**Evidence.** Argued and probed at both rules, their composition, the lookup and the binders an elimination opens; the caller-side fence named last is argued.

- **Both eta rules are probed in both directions.** Each accepts its expansion and refuses a near miss — an expansion dropping its binder, one swapping its components (`kernel::whnf::rules_tests`, with `eta_contracts_a_function_that_only_forwards` and `eta_declines_when_the_head_mentions_the_binder`). Their composition with irrelevance is unreachable at Π, since Π into a proposition is a proposition and irrelevance, tried first, discharges the goal whole (`a_function_into_a_proposition_is_discharged_before_eta`); at Σ a record stays relevant while one component is, and eta hands the proof component to irrelevance (`hands_a_records_proof_component_to_irrelevance`, with `still_compares_a_records_relevant_component`).
- **Eta by a literal needs no type.** A lambda, a tuple literal and a struct literal against a neutral inhabitant fire eta by their own shape wherever the goal's type did not (`function_eta`, `tuple_eta`, `struct_eta`): the lambda's telescope is opened at the domains it is annotated with and its body compared with the neutral applied, and a tuple's fields are compared with the neutral's projections. The elaborator fires eta by the literal at every goal whose type is not another former, so without the rule a pair it accepts under a child compared at `Type` is refused by the kernel alone, and conversion is no congruence in the trusted checker. `kernel::convert::conversion_tests` holds the rule at `Type` in both directions, each with its near miss, and the restriction to a neutral (`eta_by_the_lambda_converges_a_function_with_a_neutral_at_no_type`, `eta_by_the_literal_converges_a_pair_with_a_neutral_at_no_type`, `the_unit_literal_converges_with_a_neutral`, `eta_by_a_literal_is_taken_against_a_neutral_alone`), mutation-checked against removing the rule and against answering without comparing. `tests::board::eta_tests`'s `a_literals_eta_holds_where_no_type_directs_it` is the programs, and `tests::laws`' contexts state every eta seed under every child a former holds.
- **A type with one inhabitant decides its goal.** At the empty Σ any two terms convert without being read: the type has one inhabitant, and conversion is asked about two terms of the goal's type, the invariant irrelevance discharges a proposition on. The kernel reaches it as Σ eta at an empty telescope (`eta_tuple`), ahead of every structural rule, and the elaborator decides the same goal at the same place, beside irrelevance (`decided_by_its_type`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`). It composes with the eta that reaches it — two terms at a record of units, by their projections at their fields' types, and two at a function into a unit. A nominal struct has no eta by its type: its literal opens one, against a neutral (`struct_eta`) and field by field against another, and two neutrals have none to open. What eta would decide between them is read off the type instead (`one_inhabitant`, in each checker): two neutrals convert at a struct every field of which has one inhabitant — a proposition, the empty Σ, a Σ or a struct of such, a function into one — which is the struct's eta taken on both sides and each field's own rule, so any two terms convert there, by the reading or by a literal. Nothing more is decided by expanding two neutrals: where a field has a second inhabitant, their projections are equal only where their heads are, which the structural rules decide. The reading ends at a struct that reaches itself, one met again while it is judged answering no at whatever parameters — which refuses a struct nested in its own parameter with it, the refusing direction; every other type is forced as eta by the goal's type forces it, which ends because nothing a type reaches is partial ([Nothing reachable from a type is partial](../totality/nothing-reachable-from-a-type-is-partial.md)), and is bounded by the budget as that walk is. It is keyed on the goal type's own shape, which a binder's stand-in cannot produce. `any_two_terms_converge_at_a_type_with_no_field` and `any_two_terms_converge_at_a_struct_with_one_inhabitant`, in `kernel::convert::conversion_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests`, hold the rule, its composition and the refusals beside it in each checker — a relevant field at whatever depth, and a struct that reaches itself — mutation-checked against removing the rule at each type, against taking it at a struct whatever its fields, and against judging a struct met again. `tests::board::eta_tests`'s `any_two_terms_convert_at_a_type_with_no_field` and `any_two_terms_convert_at_a_struct_with_one_inhabitant` are the programs, with the refusals beside them, and `tests::laws` states two neutrals at each such type under every child a former holds.
- **Eta by the goal's type is fired ahead of structure in both checkers.** At a function type both sides are applied and at a record type both are projected whatever their shapes, the kernel in `compare` and the elaborator ahead of its structural dispatch; a lambda and a tuple literal take the same expansion in their own arms. Reached only between two sides no structural arm claims, the rule would leave two stuck eliminations at a record of units compared arm against arm and held apart, each converting with the literal. `eta_by_the_goals_type_is_fired_whatever_the_two_sides_shapes` holds it in each checker's suite, with the near miss at a record that has a relevant field, mutation-checked in the elaborator against leaving the rule to the dispatch's last arm.
- **What a type directs between two neutrals is read off a looked-up type where a child has none.** Where a goal's type is a sort, both sides are ones a lookup types and the two looked-up types convert, the two sides convert where that type has one inhabitant (`by_their_own_type`, in each checker's `convert.rs`). It is the typed rule with its type looked up: conversion is asked about two terms of one type, which is checked here by comparing the two, and the lookup reads a type by a lookup, a substitution and a reduction, never a conversion. It is what makes two eliminations of two proofs of one proposition one term, their scrutinees being a child no type is handed for; without it two calls of one definition that differ in a proof part wherever reduction unfolds them before the pair is posed, and a verdict follows a spelling. Neither side is expanded, and nothing is forfeited by that: eta between two neutrals decides nothing the type's shape does not. Expanding is what cannot be done there — at a looked-up record it projects both sides, the projections' heads are compared with no type, the lookup types them again, and the goal it poses is one already in progress, which the recurrence rule assumes, so any two variables at a record would convert. Reading the type poses no goal about the two sides, so nothing recurs. The elaborator reads each side and each looked-up type through the solutions already committed, so the verdict does not follow whether an implicit was written or solved. `two_neutrals_converge_where_a_lookup_gives_them_a_type_with_one_inhabitant`, in `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests`, holds the goals the rule decides — at a proposition, the empty record, a record of units, a function into a unit, a struct with no field and one of a unit, a recursive definition's call that unfolds to one, and two eliminations of two proofs — and the three it must not, two variables at a record with a relevant field, two proofs of two propositions and two numbers; mutation-checked against the rule removed, against the two types left uncompared, in the kernel against eta taken at the looked-up type, under which the two variables at the record converge, and in the elaborator against a looked-up type read as written. `tests::board::spine_tests`'s `two_calls_of_one_definition_convert_wherever_they_sit` and `tests::board::eta_tests`'s `two_neutrals_convert_where_a_lookup_gives_them_a_type_with_one_inhabitant` are the programs, the second with its refusal beside it.
- **A lookup types every side a position can hold without inferring.** A neutral's type is read off its head (`synth_neutral`), and two more sides are read as it is, by a lookup and a substitution (`looked_up`, in each checker's `convert.rs`). A stuck elimination's type is its result at its scrutinee: its ambient goal as written, or its motive opened at the indices its scrutinee's type carries and then at the scrutinee. It is the claim `Sort::of` already believes of a type-valued elimination, on the same ground: typing checked the arms against the result and the motive under its real binders, and conversion is asked about terms typing has established. A constructor's value's type is the family at the value's parameters and at the index targets its constructor states for its payload. With them a stuck elimination at a proposition meets a variable at it and an elimination of another scrutinee, and a proof a constructor builds meets one a variable names. What a lookup does not type is a lambda and a tuple literal, which open their own eta against a neutral and are compared by their parts against each other. `a_stuck_elimination_is_typed_by_its_result_at_its_scrutinee` and `a_constructors_value_is_typed_by_its_declaration`, in `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests`, hold each reading with its refusals — an elimination at a relevant type, a constructor of a relevant family, and a proof against a variable at the family's other index — mutation-checked against each reading removed, against a family read at its scrutinee alone, and against the index targets left out. `tests::board::eta_tests` has the programs under the same names, each with its refusal beside it, and `tests::laws` states the three under every child a former holds.
- **A binder an elimination opens is opened at the type its position gives it.** An arm's binders are opened at its constructor's telescope over the scrutinee's parameters, a cons arm's at its carrier's domains and the hypothesis at the result at the tail (`Carrier::cons_domains`, `curios-core`), and a motive's at the family's index domains and then the scrutinee at the family over them, as `check_motive` opens them (`ground_cases`, `motive_binders`; the elaborator's `compare_match`). The types are read off the left elimination's scrutinee by the lookup above, once the two scrutinees are compared, which is what makes one type theirs. So two proofs an arm binds are one where the arm's body is compared, as they are where the arm was typed, and an arm's body needs no type of its own: two neutrals in it are decided by the type a lookup gives them, a literal by its own eta, and two literals by their parts. The kernel assumes such a binder in its scoped context. The elaborator's conversion is a worklist that parks what it cannot decide, so there a problem carries the binders it was posed under (`Binders`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`) and a goal it surrenders is retried under them: no verdict follows whether a problem parked. `an_arms_binders_are_opened_at_the_types_their_constructor_gives_them` and `a_motives_binders_are_opened_at_the_types_its_family_gives_them` hold it in each checker's suite, with the same pair over a relevant family beside each, mutation-checked against the binders left untyped; `curios-elab`'s `convert::solve_tests::a_goal_surrendered_under_a_binder_is_retried_under_it` holds the retry, mutation-checked against a goal parked under no binder.
- **What is compared at `Type` forfeits nothing a type directs.** A stuck elimination's scrutinee, its motive and its arms' bodies, and a projection's or an instance's head are compared at `Type`, where what a type directs is read off a lookup, a literal opens its own eta and two literals are compared by their parts. A head that names a type carries the telescope its arguments inhabit, so `compare_arguments` types them: a variable (`a_spine_argument_compares_at_the_heads_domain`), a universe instance of one, a `rec` projection, a stuck elimination, and an application or a record projection of such a head, which the kernel's lookup answers as the elaborator's does (`kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::a_spines_arguments_are_typed_under_a_curried_and_a_projected_head`). The arguments are compared only once the two heads are, which is what makes one telescope theirs. A struct literal's fields and a constructor's payload compare at their declaration's telescope in both checkers (`compare_fields_at`; `a_proof_field_does_not_distinguish_two_literals`, `a_struct_field_at_a_proposition_is_not_read`, `a_constructor_payload_at_a_proposition_is_not_read`), and a nominal struct's eta is not forfeited (`a_nominal_structs_eta_is_not_forfeited_there`). A struct's and a constructor's parameters the kernel compares at the declaration's outer telescope (`params_at`) and the elaborator at `Type`, where the lookup reads the same. Both checkers compare two applications of one definition by their spines before unfolding either, on the pair as posed and so ahead of eta by the goal's type, through a curried spine, and whatever spells the two sides (`one_definition_by_its_spines`, `compare_same_global_apply`; `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::two_calls_of_one_definition_convert_by_their_spines_at_any_type`, `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests::two_calls_of_one_definition_convert_by_their_spines_whatever_spells_them`), which keeps two calls folded where their spines agree; a proof in an argument that does sink into a stuck scrutinee is equated there by the type a lookup gives it, so the verdict follows neither rule's place (`tests::board::spine_tests`, from `a_definition_applied_to_two_proofs_converts_before_unfolding` on).
- **The stand-in is what a binder keeps where no lookup types its elimination's scrutinee, and it is sound because of its value.** No checked term reaches it: a scrutinee is a neutral or reduces. There two scopes are opened at shared binders each assumed at `Type`, and that type reaches `Sort::of`, which conversion asks before every goal. `Type` is the least informative answer: irrelevance fires on `Sort::Prop` alone and eta dispatches on the goal's own shape, so both accepting rules are keyed on answers the stand-in cannot produce. `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::a_binders_stand_in_type_decides_a_goal_the_way_a_relevant_type_does` is the grid over every type a binder could carry, and the second halves of `a_motives_binders_are_opened_at_the_types_its_family_gives_them` and `an_arms_binders_are_opened_at_the_types_their_constructor_gives_them` reach it through an elimination whose scrutinee has no type.
- **A literal's eta is refused where the goal states another type.** A lambda, a tuple literal or a struct literal against a neutral at a type former that is not the literal's own is two terms of two types, and the rule is refused there in both checkers, by one classifier of a former (`Subterm::is_type_former`, `curios-core`): `structural` receives the goal's type for this alone (`another_former`, `another_struct`), and the elaborator's `eta_expand_func`, `eta_expand_tuple` and `eta_expand_struct` answer the same. Without it the neutral restriction alone lets a literal with no field, whose walk compares nothing, convert with any neutral at any type. `eta_by_a_literal_is_refused_at_a_type_former_that_is_not_its_own`, in `kernel::convert::conversion_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests`, holds the refusal for each literal, and that the rule is still taken at the literal's own type and at a sort, mutation-checked against each refusal removed and against a sort counted a former.
- **Two properties rest on the callers.** Where the goal's type is a sort — what a child with no typed context is compared at — or a neutral, nothing states the literal's type, and a neutral is equated with a lambda, a tuple literal or a struct literal by the neutral restriction, which excludes non-neutrals but not neutrals of the wrong type; what makes the types agree there is that every grounded pair is the corresponding children of two parents already shown convertible. And under the stand-in a binder whose real type is no sort is classified `Type 0` where the typed opening refuses it; `check_motive` typing a motive under its real binders first is what keeps such a binder away, and the typed opening, which every checked elimination takes, does not meet the case. No fixture holds that fence.
- **Annotations are a conjunct.** Two stuck eliminations compare their motives or ambient goals beside their heads and arms, so an annotation can only refuse.
