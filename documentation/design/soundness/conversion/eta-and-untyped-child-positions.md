# Eta and untyped child positions

**Assumes.** Eta at Π, at Σ and at a nominal struct with no field preserves equality, and comparing a child at `Type` where no type is available forfeits rules rather than adding them.

**Evidence.** Argued and probed at both rules, their composition and the stand-in a grounded comparison opens its binders with; the caller-side fence named last is argued.

- **Both eta rules are probed in both directions.** Each accepts its expansion and refuses a near miss — an expansion dropping its binder, one swapping its components (`kernel::whnf::rules_tests`, with `eta_contracts_a_function_that_only_forwards` and `eta_declines_when_the_head_mentions_the_binder`). Their composition with irrelevance is unreachable at Π, since Π into a proposition is a proposition and irrelevance, tried first, discharges the goal whole (`a_function_into_a_proposition_is_discharged_before_eta`); at Σ a record stays relevant while one component is, and eta hands the proof component to irrelevance (`hands_a_records_proof_component_to_irrelevance`, with `still_compares_a_records_relevant_component`).
- **Eta by a literal needs no type.** A lambda, a tuple literal and a struct literal against a neutral inhabitant fire eta by their own shape wherever the goal's type did not (`function_eta`, `tuple_eta`, `struct_eta`): the lambda's telescope is opened at the domains it is annotated with and its body compared with the neutral applied, and a tuple's fields are compared with the neutral's projections. The elaborator fires eta by the literal at every goal whose type is not another former, so without the rule a pair it accepts under a child compared at `Type` is refused by the kernel alone, and conversion is no congruence in the trusted checker. `kernel::convert::conversion_tests` holds the rule at `Type` in both directions, each with its near miss, and the restriction to a neutral (`eta_by_the_lambda_converges_a_function_with_a_neutral_at_no_type`, `eta_by_the_literal_converges_a_pair_with_a_neutral_at_no_type`, `the_unit_literal_converges_with_a_neutral`, `eta_by_a_literal_is_taken_against_a_neutral_alone`), mutation-checked against removing the rule and against answering without comparing. `tests::board::eta_tests`'s `a_literals_eta_holds_where_no_type_directs_it` is the programs, and `tests::laws`' contexts state every eta seed under every child a former holds.
- **A type with no field decides its goal.** At the empty Σ and at a nominal struct that declares no field, any two terms convert without being read: the type has one inhabitant, and conversion is asked about two terms of the goal's type, the invariant irrelevance discharges a proposition on. The kernel reaches it as Σ eta at an empty telescope (`eta_tuple`) and by the declaration's field count at a struct (`compare`), ahead of every structural rule; the elaborator decides the same goal at the same place, beside irrelevance (`field_less`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), where a rule reached only between two sides no structural rule claims holds a stuck match equal to a variable and two stuck matches apart. It composes with the eta that reaches it — two neutrals at a record of units, by their projections at their fields' types, and two at a function into a unit — and it is keyed on the goal type's own shape, which a binder's stand-in cannot produce. A struct that has a field is no unit, whatever the field's type: neither checker compares two neutrals at one by their projections. `any_two_terms_converge_at_a_type_with_no_field`, in `kernel::convert::conversion_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests`, holds the rule, its composition and the three refusals beside it in each checker, mutation-checked against removing the rule at each type and against taking it at a struct with fields. `tests::board::eta_tests`'s `any_two_terms_convert_at_a_type_with_no_field` is the programs, with two refusals beside it, and `tests::laws` states two neutrals at each such type under every child a former holds.
- **Eta by the goal's type is fired ahead of structure in both checkers.** At a function type both sides are applied and at a record type both are projected whatever their shapes, the kernel in `compare` and the elaborator ahead of its structural dispatch; a lambda and a tuple literal take the same expansion in their own arms. Reached only between two sides no structural arm claims, the rule would leave two stuck eliminations at a record of units compared arm against arm and held apart, each converting with the literal. `eta_by_the_goals_type_is_fired_whatever_the_two_sides_shapes` holds it in each checker's suite, with the near miss at a record that has a relevant field, mutation-checked in the elaborator against leaving the rule to the dispatch's last arm.
- **What is compared untyped forfeits what only a type directs.** A stuck elimination's scrutinee, its motive and arms under their opaque binders, and a projection's or an instance's head are compared at `Type`, forfeiting goal-level irrelevance and eta between two neutrals there, unit eta with it — the refusing direction. A head that names a type carries the telescope its arguments inhabit, so `compare_arguments` types them: a variable (`a_spine_argument_compares_at_the_heads_domain`), a universe instance of one, a `rec` projection, and an application or a record projection of such a head, which the kernel's lookup answers as the elaborator's does (`kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::a_spines_arguments_are_typed_under_a_curried_and_a_projected_head`, mutation-checked against reading the type under the first three alone). The arguments are compared only once the two heads are, which is what makes one telescope theirs. A struct literal's fields and a constructor's payload compare at their declaration's telescope in both checkers (`compare_fields_at`; `a_proof_field_does_not_distinguish_two_literals`, `a_struct_field_at_a_proposition_is_not_read`, `a_constructor_payload_at_a_proposition_is_not_read`), and a nominal struct's eta is not forfeited (`a_nominal_structs_eta_is_not_forfeited_there`). Both checkers compare two applications of one definition by their spines before unfolding either, on the pair as posed and so ahead of eta by the goal's type, through a curried spine, and whatever spells the two sides (`one_definition_by_its_spines`, `compare_same_global_apply`; `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::two_calls_of_one_definition_convert_by_their_spines_at_any_type`, `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests::two_calls_of_one_definition_convert_by_their_spines_whatever_spells_them`, each mutation-checked), so a proof in an argument does not sink into a stuck scrutinee where the pair is posed as two calls (`tests::board::spine_tests`, from `a_definition_applied_to_two_proofs_converts_before_unfolding` on).
- **The stand-in is sound because of its value.** Opening two scopes at shared opaque binders assumes each at `Type`, and that type reaches `Sort::of`, which conversion asks before every goal. `Type` is the least informative answer: irrelevance fires on `Sort::Prop` alone and eta dispatches on the goal's own shape, so both accepting rules are keyed on answers the stand-in cannot produce. `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::a_binders_stand_in_type_decides_a_goal_the_way_a_relevant_type_does` is the grid over every type a binder could carry, and `a_grounded_motive_binder_carries_the_stand_in_rather_than_its_real_type` pins the counterfactual, mutation-checked against assuming the binders at `Prop`.
- **A literal's eta is refused where the goal states another type.** A lambda, a tuple literal or a struct literal against a neutral at a type former that is not the literal's own is two terms of two types, and the rule is refused there in both checkers, by one classifier of a former (`Subterm::is_type_former`, `curios-core`): `structural` receives the goal's type for this alone (`another_former`, `another_struct`), and the elaborator's `eta_expand_func`, `eta_expand_tuple` and `eta_expand_struct` answer the same. Without it the neutral restriction alone lets a literal with no field, whose walk compares nothing, convert with any neutral at any type. `eta_by_a_literal_is_refused_at_a_type_former_that_is_not_its_own`, in `kernel::convert::conversion_tests` and in `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests`, holds the refusal for each literal, and that the rule is still taken at the literal's own type and at a sort, mutation-checked against each refusal removed and against a sort counted a former.
- **Two properties rest on the callers.** Where the goal's type is a sort — what a child with no typed context is compared at — or a neutral, nothing states the literal's type, and a neutral is equated with a lambda, a tuple literal or a struct literal by the neutral restriction, which excludes non-neutrals but not neutrals of the wrong type; what makes the types agree there is that every grounded pair is the corresponding children of two parents already shown convertible. And a binder whose real type is no sort would be refused by the typed opening but classified `Type 0` by the stand-in; `check_motive` typing a motive under its real binders first is what keeps such a binder away. No fixture holds that fence.
- **Annotations are a conjunct.** Two stuck eliminations compare their motives or ambient goals beside their heads and arms, so an annotation can only refuse.
