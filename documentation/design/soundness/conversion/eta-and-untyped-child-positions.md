# Eta and untyped child positions

**Assumes.** Eta at Π and Σ preserves equality, and comparing a child at `Type` where no type is available forfeits rules rather than adding them.

**Status.** **argued** and **probed** at both rules, their composition and the stand-in a grounded comparison opens its binders with; the caller-side fence named last is argued.

- **Both eta rules are probed in both directions.** Each accepts its expansion and refuses a near miss — an expansion dropping its binder, one swapping its components (`kernel::whnf::rules_tests`, with `eta_contracts_a_function_that_only_forwards` and `eta_declines_when_the_head_mentions_the_binder`). Their composition with irrelevance is unreachable at Π, since Π into a proposition is a proposition and irrelevance, tried first, discharges the goal whole (`a_function_into_a_proposition_is_discharged_before_eta`); at Σ a record stays relevant while one component is, and eta hands the proof component to irrelevance (`hands_a_records_proof_component_to_irrelevance`, with `still_compares_a_records_relevant_component`).
- **What is compared untyped forfeits.** A stuck elimination's scrutinee, its motive and arms under their opaque binders, and a projection's or an instance's head are compared at `Type`, forfeiting type-directed eta and goal-level irrelevance there — the refusing direction. A variable head carries the telescope its arguments inhabit, so `compare_arguments` types them (`a_spine_argument_compares_at_the_heads_domain`), as it does a universe instance of a variable and a `rec` projection. A struct literal's fields and a constructor's payload compare at their declaration's telescope in both checkers (`compare_fields_at`; `a_proof_field_does_not_distinguish_two_literals`, `a_struct_field_at_a_proposition_is_not_read`, `a_constructor_payload_at_a_proposition_is_not_read`), and a nominal struct's eta is not forfeited (`a_nominal_structs_eta_is_not_forfeited_there`). Both checkers compare two applications of one definition by their spines before unfolding either (`one_definition_by_its_spines`, `compare_same_global_apply`), so a proof in an argument never sinks into a stuck scrutinee (`tests::board::eta_tests`, from `a_definition_applied_to_two_proofs_converts_before_unfolding` on).
- **The stand-in is sound because of its value.** Opening two scopes at shared opaque binders assumes each at `Type`, and that type reaches `Sort::of`, which conversion asks before every goal. `Type` is the least informative answer: irrelevance fires on `Sort::Prop` alone and eta dispatches on the goal's own shape, so both accepting rules are keyed on answers the stand-in cannot produce. `kernel::convert::irrelevance_tests::a_binders_stand_in_type_decides_a_goal_the_way_a_relevant_type_does` is the grid over every type a binder could carry, and `a_grounded_motive_binder_carries_the_stand_in_rather_than_its_real_type` pins the counterfactual, mutation-checked against assuming the binders at `Prop`.
- **Two properties rest on the callers.** A neutral is equated with a struct literal by the neutral restriction, which excludes non-neutrals but not neutrals of the wrong type; what makes the types agree is that every grounded pair is the corresponding children of two parents already shown convertible. And a binder whose real type is no sort would be refused by the typed opening but classified `Type 0` by the stand-in; `check_motive` typing a motive under its real binders first is what keeps such a binder away. No fixture holds that fence.
- **Annotations are a conjunct.** Two stuck eliminations compare their motives or ambient goals beside their heads and arms, so an annotation can only refuse.
