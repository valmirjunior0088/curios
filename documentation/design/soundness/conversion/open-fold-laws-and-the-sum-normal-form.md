# Open fold laws and the sum normal form

**Assumes.** Every open fold — a reduction rule firing on a symbolic operand — is a valid equation on the carrier's values.

**Status.** **probed** over values: `curios-core`'s `reduce::intrinsic::laws_tests::every_open_fold_law_preserves_the_value_at_every_closed_instantiation` pins each open fold to its stated reduct, so a law that stops firing fails, and holds original and reduct to one value at every closed instantiation; `curios`'s `tests::laws` states every law the normalizer takes as an `Eq/refl()` proof both checkers see, and every law it declines, read back through the refl-fit report, so taking one moves a row. Folds live in `curios-core`, so a wrong one admits by [the perimeter's shared route](../the-soundness-perimeter.md) in the sharpest way, conversion reducing both sides before comparing. One arm serves both packed grains. Verdicts on stuck pairs are [Intrinsic fold laws and the free-monoid peel](intrinsic-fold-laws-and-the-free-monoid-peel.md)'s, and the division family's [The bounds oracle and the division family](the-bounds-oracle-and-the-division-family.md)'s.

- **A sum is a linear combination, and a product of two symbolic sums reaches it on demand** ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)). `Nat::linear` merges like factors by adding coefficients, keyed up to universe instances as `cancel_common` keys, and `Nat::from_linear` writes them back through `Nat::scaled`, so `x + x` is `2 · x` and `(x + y) · 2` is `2 · x + 2 · y`: merging is `c · f + d · f = (c + d) · f`, true for every `f`. A monomial is a literal coefficient over a factor list ordered by a fixed-keyed structural hash, stable so two factors hashing alike merely fail to canonicalize; `Nat::multiply` distributes in full through `curios-algebra`'s `distribute`, so commutation, association and distribution of symbols are one rule. `Nat::summands` reads a sum in the order `from_linear` writes it, so read-then-rebuild is the identity and cancellation settles. `NatMul` distributes past a literal or a single summand and leaves `(a + b) · (c + d)` stuck; `Nat::normalize` distributes it where a value is asked for by name.
- **Multiplication's unit, annihilation and nested literal factors** — `x · 1 = x`, `x · 0 = 0`, `2 · (3 · x) = 6 · x` on either side — are the three cases of `Nat::scaled`, which puts the coefficient on the left, so `x · 2` and `2 · x` are one normal form.
- **Subtraction.** `0 - x = 0` is truncation, and `(a - b) - c = a - (b + c)` holds for truncated subtraction, taken right-nested so a later literal subtrahend meets `a`'s floor.
- **`Int` has the sum normal form over ℤ.** Integer coefficients make a subtraction a negative coefficient, so no `IntSub` survives reduction and every difference cancels in full: `i - i` is `0`, `(i + j) - j` is `i`. `int_normalize` distributes a stuck product on demand as `Nat::normalize` does, and `int_cancel_common` moves the difference of two sides to the two sides by sign, so `compare_int` decides `i + a < i + b` as `a < b`. The open-fold grid carries these at instantiations including a negative, and `tests::laws` states commutation, association, cancellation and distribution.
- **`Bool` and ℕ's bitwise lattice have their units, absorbers, idempotence and self-cancellation**, one function beside each fold run by `then_laws` on what the two-literal fold left neutral, identity read by plain structural equality. `xor` cancels a shared operand through one nesting, so `not(not(b))` is `b`. Complementary operands — a term beside its `xor(_, true)`, or a comparison beside its dual — decide a conjunction `false`, a disjunction `true`, an equality `false` and an inequality `true`: the complement law of the Boolean algebra on the value type `Bool`, by cases on its constructors, not excluded middle on propositions. The equality folds read both operands (`curios-cert`'s `an_equality_reads_its_right_operand`), while a connective leaves its right operand as written under a stuck left.
- **A shift is a coefficient.** `shl(x, k) = 2ᵏ · x` on the unbounded carriers, which the run time computes too ([Nat and Int are an i31 until they outgrow it](../../arithmetic/nat-and-int-are-an-i31-until-they-outgrow-it.md)); `then_coefficient` charges `2ᵏ` before building it (`cost_tests`'s `a_shift_coefficient_is_refused_before_it_is_built`). A symbolic count is a product by a power held as an atom — `then_power` reads `shl(v, k₀ + f)` as `2ᶠ · v · shl(1, k₀)` — and a right shift by a floored count splits in two steps, `shr(v, k₀ + f) = shr(shr(v, k₀), f)`, since a division node would owe a nonzero proof no reducer may invent. The exponent law and a literal-count right shift's quotient are refused rows.
- **Divisibility decides equality.** After normalizing and cancelling, each side of a comparison is its constant modulo the gcd `g` of every coefficient on both sides, so constants apart modulo `g` meet at no value: `2 · x + 1 == 2 · y` is `false`. `Comparison` spells that set `Ne`, read by `==` and `!=` only and intersected with a verdict already reached; it is a fold verdict, so inversion is handed no new impossibility. `compare_tests`'s `nat_decides_floors_apart_modulo_the_coefficients_gcd_unequal` and its `int_` twin, with congruent and gcd-one near misses; mutation-checked against dropping the modulus.
- **The free monoid's laws.** The full-window collapse, the empty window, both cons peels of `get` and `slice`, the borrow, literal-factor distribution, and the `len` and `map` homomorphisms over an append and a concatenation. A value is read as a flat list of operands — a concatenation or an append — and `seam_window` measures each as `len` does, cancelling off the distance still to cover, so `slice([..xs, ..ys], len(xs), len(ys)) = ys`, `get([..p, k], len(p)) = k`, and a window past every operand but the last lies inside the last, the caller's bound handed on after cancelling the consumed prefix; a measure carrying a summand the distance lacks declines. The measure is computed off the spine, agreeing over four groupings of one run per carrier with the fused literal as ground truth, and `an_unmeasurable_operand_is_not_skipped` holds the declining side (`curios`'s `tests::aggregates::a_length_and_a_window_do_not_depend_on_grouping`). `len(slice(b, s, n)) = n` since `/sys/slice` states `s + n <= len(b)`, and a window moves inside a `map`. `get` and `slice` through a window are declined as reductions, their outer bound being a proof no term in hand carries. Window fusion needs no ordering check, since a window is a start and a count, and a call that would break it cannot be written past `slice`'s precondition (`tests::aggregates::bin_slice_is_a_monoid_citizen`).
- **`map` by the identity, and `fold`.** `map(xs, f) = xs` when `f`'s body weak-head reduces to its binder, `(v) => v + 0` included — β and the folds, not extensionality, opened on a fresh identity charged as the eta probe is (`free_monoid_tests`'s `a_map_by_a_function_whose_body_reduces_to_its_binder_is_the_list`, mutation-checked against collapsing a constant function). `List/fold` reduces over the shape by the three equations defining a left fold over the generators — cons, concatenation, snoc — and a window or an opaque value stays neutral.
- **Each family is mutation-checked.** `curios-algebra`'s table declares the lattice identities, the complement and nested cancellation at `Bool`, and the length homomorphism, and `tests::laws::generated` states every instance through both checkers and at closed values. Withdrawing each fails a row — the unit at ℕ's `or`, the absorber at `and`, idempotence, self-cancellation at `xor` — or the prelude, the append's length failing `/std/Bytes/no_empty`; the complement and nested cancellation are decided twice, by the fold and by the truth table, so the reducing grid is what catches them.
