# The binary64 model and its NaN rule

**Assumes.** `curios_num::Floating` computes IEEE 754-2019 binary64 over all 2⁶⁴ bit patterns, each a distinct value, and the engine executing the emitted `f64.*` agrees with it bit for bit, including which NaN an operation answers.

**Status.** **probed** by three differentials — the model against the host, against an exact rational oracle, and against the engine — and **argued** at two points: that every instruction able to produce a NaN is followed by the check, and mantissa coverage above the low sixteen bits. Every `Flt` operation folds by calling the model, so this entry admits by [the perimeter's shared route](../the-soundness-perimeter.md), the shared function being `curios-num`'s; its second opinions are the host, an exact oracle and the engine ([`Flt` is specified by a model](../../arithmetic/flt-is-specified-by-a-model-and-the-runtime-conforms.md)).

- **Against the host**, an oracle and never the definition: `curios-num`'s `floating::tests` runs `neg`, `abs`, `sqrt` and round-to-integral in the host's four directions over every one of the 2048 exponent fields at fourteen mantissa corners (`every_exponent_agrees_with_the_host_at_the_mantissa_corners`) and, ignored, over each field's whole low-sixteen-bit range (`an_exhaustive_low_mantissa_sweep_agrees_with_the_host`); the binary operations and `fma` run over the IEEE corner grid, an exponent-difference sweep where cancellation decides, and a seeded sample. The exponent field selects zero, subnormal, normal, infinity or NaN and the subnormal grid, so sweeping it covers every case at every scale; the mantissa is covered by corners — its ends, the carry boundary at `2^51`, the low bits `round` reads. The host agrees on every number and on a NaN only by NaN-ness, except for `neg`, `abs` and `copysign`, whose bits IEEE fixes.
- **Against an exact oracle**, past the default direction: `every_direction_rounds_the_edge_grid_the_way_it_names`, `every_direction_rounds_a_seeded_sample_the_way_it_names` and `every_direction_converts_an_integer_the_way_it_names` hold each direction to the inequality defining it, computed over rationals, which reads no line of the model's `round`.
- **The NaN rule** — the default NaN `+0x7ff8_0000_0000_0000` for an invalid operation, otherwise the greatest quieted operand pattern read unsigned, a difference as the negated sum, and `neg`, `abs`, `copysign` and the byte conversions touching only what they name — is `a_nan_result_is_the_greatest_quieted_nan_operand_or_the_default`'s table over NaNs of both signs and kinds, with and without payloads, in every direction.
- **Against the engine**, `curios`'s `tests::numeric::unbounded_tests` compiles each row closed, so it folds, and with a runtime-zero perturbation, so it executes, and demands identical output: `folded_and_executed_scalar_ops_agree` and `folded_and_executed_nans_agree`, printing each NaN's bytes. The rows assemble their NaNs from bytes with payloads and a runtime-tainted byte, since a computed NaN passes on an engine whose default matches the model whether or not the check is emitted. On x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu, with the check's NaN arm replaced by the hardware result, the first row to fail is `add(signaling, quiet)`: the engine answers its first operand quieted where the model answers the greater pattern.
- **The directed helpers and `fma`**, `into_wasm/flt_emitter.rs`'s integer soft-float, are held to the model's fold over the edge grid in every direction with operands tainted at run time (`tests::numeric::rounding_tests::every_direction_executes_as_the_model_rounds`), and `/std`'s own rounding against `Floating::of_dyadic` (`the_exact_rounding_executes_as_the_model_rounds`).
- **The check is argued by cases.** Every arithmetic instruction, `min`, `max`, `sqrt`, round-to-integral and `flt/rem` goes through `emit_flt_checked`; `neg`, `abs` and `copysign` are bit operations in Wasm as in IEEE; the byte conversions reinterpret; the comparisons produce no float; the conversions from `Nat` and `Int` produce no NaN; the directed helpers compute the rule through `flt/nan`. A future NaN-producing instruction is lowered through the check or not admitted, and nothing checks that mechanically.
- **The narrowings answer exactly on their stated domains.** `Flt/to_nat` and `Flt/to_int` answer the exact integer on `NonNeg` and `Finite`, and `Flt/mantissa` and `Flt/exponent` a finite value's exact `mantissa · 2^exponent` (`tests::numeric::unbounded_tests::folded_and_executed_decompositions_agree`); outside the domain the fold declines and the operation stays stuck.
- **The declared laws hold on the model.** `curios-algebra`'s table declares at `Flt` only that `==` and `!=` commute and that decoding the eight bytes encoding wrote is the float given; `tests::laws::semantics` holds each generated instance over a pattern grid — both zeros, both infinities, the extreme subnormals, the least normal, the greatest finite value, a quiet and a signaling NaN with payloads of each sign — compared by bit pattern, and `tests::laws::generated` puts the rows to both checkers.
