# A nominal type's levels compare by variance

**Assumes.** Two instances of an `induct` or a `struct` apart only in a level its declaration is irrelevant in have the same inhabitants. A level is irrelevant when no constructor payload, index target, field or index type mentions it once reduced, except at a position of another family that is itself irrelevant; the two instances' constructor telescopes, targets included, then convert entry by entry ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it came from](../../theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-came-from.md)).

**Evidence.** Argued from what an instance holds, and probed at each place a vector is read and at each rule that could tell two instances apart. Conversion compares nothing at an irrelevant position, so a vector calling a level irrelevant that an instance does hold is a false definitional equation between two types with different inhabitants: over `Box.{u} | box(T: Type u)`, a `Type 0` stored at one instance is read back at another.

- **What is compared.** A nominal node, a type or a value of it, compares its levels at the positions its family's vector calls invariant (`Kernel::instances_eq`) and its arguments whatever the levels, at the left side's instance. A position a vector does not reach is invariant, which is the equality every level was compared at before a vector existed. `recheck::universes_tests`' `two_instances_apart_in_an_irrelevant_level_convert` and `an_invariant_level_is_compared`, and `kernel::convert::variance_tests`' `an_irrelevant_levels_arguments_are_compared_at_either_instance`; mutation-checked against the arm on equality, against a default of irrelevant, and against an arm that skips its arguments once the levels differ.
- **A value compares at its family's variance, not at nothing.** Unfoldings are compared untyped, so two constructor applications are not known to stand at one supertype, and a rule that compared no level on a value would equate `box.{0}(Nat)` with `box.{1}(Nat)`. `a_value_compares_at_its_familys_variance`, mutation-checked against a constructor arm that compares no level.
- **A module's carried vectors are recomputed before its verdicts stand.** The item walk reads the vector an entry carries, as it reads every registry entry, and its verdicts stand because `recheck` then recomputes the module's vectors and refuses a module carrying an irrelevance the recomputation denies, an entry past the declaration's own count included. A carried invariance it would have granted costs an acceptance and admits nothing, and the elaborator ships one: a family read while a payload of it was still unsolved keeps that reading. `a_carried_irrelevance_the_recomputation_denies_refuses_the_module`, mutation-checked against the reconciliation removed, under which the walk's acceptance of the coercion is all that is left; `a_carried_invariance_the_recomputation_would_grant_is_compared_and_not_refused`, mutation-checked against a reconciliation that refuses any difference; `curios-analysis`'s `tests/variance.rs::a_carried_vector_is_recomputed_rather_than_believed`.
- **A unit in scope's vector is the certifier's record.** It was held to its own declarations when its unit was certified, the walk reads it on that word, and the reconciliation reads it the same way where a module's family reaches it: recomputing it at every certification would buy no verdict the walk does not already rest on. `recheck::universes_tests::a_family_of_a_unit_in_scope_answers_from_the_vector_it_carries`, mutation-checked against a reconciliation that recomputes the scope's families, and `curios-analysis`'s `tests/variance.rs::a_family_outside_the_set_answers_from_the_vector_it_carries`.
- **What the analysis cannot see through marks every level it carries.** It reads each type's reduct. A `Type` marks its level and a nominal node the levels at its family's invariant positions; an instance that does not unfold marks all of its own, a `rec` group every level it mentions, a metavariable every level of the declaration, and a reduction the budget refuses every level its term carries. `curios-analysis`'s `tests/variance.rs`, whose refusing rules are mutation-checked one by one.
- **An index target is read**, since a level may reach nothing else: `Fam`'s constructor puts another family's invariant level in its index and in no payload, and two instances apart in it have different inhabitants at one index. `a_level_only_an_index_target_mentions_is_invariant` in both suites, mutation-checked against a walk that skips the targets.
- **A parameter's type and the result sort are not read**, and need not be: they bound a level from below, and the arguments themselves are compared. A concept's level that only its carrier's type mentions is irrelevant, while the one its methods bind at is not: `Monad`'s vector is `=*`, the level of `M`'s codomain unread and the level `bind` takes its payloads at compared. `a_concepts_method_level_is_compared`, mutation-checked against a walk that skips a struct's fields.
- **A mention a reduction removes is no mention.** `Vec`'s level reaches its proof field through a definition that unfolds to an equation over an intrinsic, and `Map/Key`'s through a method that reduces to a projection of the witness; the two instances' field types differ as written and convert by unfolding, which `compare` reaches once the spines of one definition at two instances decide nothing. `a_level_a_reduction_removes_is_carried_as_irrelevant`, mutation-checked against a walk that reads an instance as written, and `a_definition_applied_at_two_instances_converts_by_unfolding`, which holds a rule older than this one and is not mutation-checked.
- **No rule concludes a difference from a level.** The large-elimination guard reads a family's declared sort and its constructors' telescopes, which an irrelevant level leaves convertible, and index inversion's constructor rule reads the family, its sort, the tag and the payloads and never an instance, so no arm is excused by two levels conversion calls one. `recheck::elimination_tests`' `an_irrelevant_level_changes_no_elimination`, mutation-checked against a guard that does not fire, and `an_arm_is_not_excused_by_an_irrelevant_level`, mutation-checked against a constructor rule that clashes on unequal instances, under which the vacuous elimination is certified. Where a level does decide, it refuses: [the case-equation key](../elimination/case-equations-and-their-key.md) keeps its levels, and two projections of one group at two instances are a verdict on every one of their levels (`rec_instances`), a family's former excepted, whose levels are its node's.
- **A former's levels are its node's.** An `induct`'s former is a projection of its group, and two projections of one group apart in a level are refused before either is forced, because a recursive function's two instances reproduce themselves under every unfolding. A former reproduces nothing: its member is lambdas over the node it builds and names no member at its head, so `compare` does not take that refusal for one (`projects_a_former`) and the pair goes on to its nodes, which compare by variance. A family with no parameter is its node once forced. Two bare or partly applied formers compared at a function type are applied to the same fresh binders before either head is read, and the two families agree at every argument, which is what eta states. So a pair has one verdict whether it arrives as two instances of the family's name or already reduced to the projections. `kernel::convert::variance_tests`' `two_bare_formers_apart_in_an_irrelevant_level_convert_at_their_type` and `a_family_with_no_parameter_converts_by_its_name_at_two_instances`, each over a former defined as a projection and as the term itself, as written and reduced first, with the same family carried invariant as its control; mutation-checked against the arm on equality and against a `compare` that takes the refusal, under which the reduced pair alone is refused.
- **A vector that moves is read again.** A family's vector composes through the families it holds, so an edit to one of them can move it; such a family mentions the edited declaration, is elaborated again with the items that read its entry, and carries what a whole compile does. `curios-pipeline`'s `incremental_tests::an_edit_that_moves_a_familys_variance_recompiles_the_families_that_hold_it`.
- **The model is pCuIC's** ([Timany and Sozeau, *Cumulative Inductive Types In Coq*](https://cs.au.dk/~timany/publications/files/2018_FSCD_cumind.pdf)), which relates fully applied instances by their indices and constructor arguments and leaves parameters out. It leaves `Prop` families out for its own complexity; nothing here is argued from it for one beyond the telescopes converting, which the elimination fixtures above hold.
