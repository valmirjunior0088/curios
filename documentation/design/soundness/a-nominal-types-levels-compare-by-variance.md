# A nominal type's levels compare by variance

**Decision.** Two instances of an `induct` or a `struct` apart only in a level its declaration is irrelevant in have the same inhabitants. A level is irrelevant when no constructor payload, index target, field or index type mentions it once reduced, except at a position of another family that is itself irrelevant; the two instances' constructor telescopes, targets included, then convert entry by entry ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md)).

- **What is compared.** A nominal node, a type or a value of it, compares its levels at the positions its family's vector calls invariant (`Certifier::instances_eq`) and its arguments whatever the levels, at the left side's instance. A position a vector does not reach is invariant, which is the equality every level was compared at before a vector existed.
- **A value compares at its family's variance, not at nothing.** Unfoldings are compared untyped, so two constructor applications are not known to stand at one supertype, and a rule that compared no level on a value would equate `box.{0}(Nat)` with `box.{1}(Nat)`.
- **The certifier compares by the vector it computed.** As it accepts a family's entry, `recheck` computes the family's vector and files it, for the declarations judged after and in the record a later walk reads, as it files a polarity vector ([An independent certifier re-checks what the elaborator accepts](an-independent-certifier-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)); every comparison of two instances reads that one, and a family it has filed none for is invariant. The vector an entry carries is the elaborator's and is read by no judgment of the certifier. It is held to the computed one all the same, and an irrelevance it claims past it refuses the entry, an entry past the declaration's own count included, because the elaborator of a later unit compares by what an entry carries. A carried invariance the certifier would have granted is denied nothing, and the elaborator ships one: a family read while a payload of it was still unsolved keeps that reading.
- **A unit in scope's vector is the certifier's record of it.** A walk reads the vector its unit's record files, and computes one before it judges anything for an entry the record files none for, as for a unit mounted with no record.
- **A walk the budget cuts short files nothing.** It reads a term whose reduction was refused as holding every level it carries, so a vector computed past one may be the budget's: the entry is refused for the budget and answers invariant from then on.
- **What the analysis cannot see through marks every level it carries.** It reads each type's reduct. A `match` the reduct is stuck on is read as conversion compares two of it: its head, goal, stated instance and a list's element type as written, and each arm opened against fresh binders and forced, a mention an arm's reduction removes being no mention. A `Type` marks its level and a nominal node the levels at its family's invariant positions; an instance that does not unfold marks all of its own, a recursive member met again on its own path or left folded by reduction the levels the least fixpoint of its group's unfolding holds where the group terminates, and every level it carries where it may not, a metavariable every level of the declaration, and a reduction the budget refuses every level its term carries.
- **An index target is read**, since a level may reach nothing else: `Fam`'s constructor puts another family's invariant level in its index and in no payload, and two instances apart in it have different inhabitants at one index.
- **A parameter's type and the result sort are not read**, and need not be: they bound a level from below, and the arguments themselves are compared. A concept's level that only its carrier's type mentions is irrelevant, while the one its methods bind at is not: `Monad`'s vector is `=*`, the level of `M`'s codomain unread and the level `bind` takes its payloads at compared.
- **A mention a reduction removes is no mention.** `Vec`'s level reaches its proof field through a definition that unfolds to an equation over an intrinsic, and `Map/Key`'s through a method that reduces to a projection of the witness; the two instances' field types differ as written and convert by unfolding, which `compare` reaches once the spines of one definition at two instances decide nothing.
- **A recursive definition is as transparent as any.** Two instances of one group unfold to terms apart only where the group's unfolding holds a level, again and again, so the least fixpoint of what its members' unfoldings hold decides them without unfolding: the pair is generalized over the positions it differs at, a level of its own at each, and a position the fixpoint does not hold is no difference (`held_pairs`, over `Term::generalized_levels`). A level stands for a position, which the generalized pair names, since a ground level has no head to tell its positions apart by. Both checkers compare two projections so, the certifier in `rec_instances` and the elaborator where it identifies two instances' levels. A group that may not terminate holds every position, its unfolding being one neither checker makes.
- **No rule concludes a difference from a level.** The large-elimination guard reads a family's declared sort and its constructors' telescopes, which an irrelevant level leaves convertible, and index inversion's constructor rule reads the family, its sort, the tag and the payloads and never an instance, so no arm is excused by two levels conversion calls one. Where a level does decide, it refuses: [the case-equation key](case-equations-and-their-key.md) keeps its levels, and two projections of one group at two instances are a verdict on every one of their levels (`rec_instances`), a family's former excepted, whose levels are its node's.
- **A former's levels are its node's.** A former is made of its entry as lambdas over the node it builds, and names no group, so two of its instances apart in a level unfold to their nodes, which compare by variance. A family with no parameter is its node once forced. Two bare or partly applied formers compared at a function type are applied to the same fresh binders before either head is read, and the two families agree at every argument, which is what eta states. So a pair has one verdict whether it arrives as two instances of the family's name or already reduced to the lambdas.
- **A definition's vector is the elaborator's alone.** For a definition that answers a type, the elaborator files which of its levels its value holds with the unit, beside the definition rather than in it (`curios_elab::Vectors`), and reads it to hold two instances one without unfolding either; for a definition whose value is a struct or tuple literal it files the same of each field, read where a projection of it meets another. Core holds none, and the certifier reads none: it compares two instances of a definition by their spines and levels and unfolds where that decides nothing, and forces a projection to its field before it compares heads, so a vector claiming an irrelevance its body denies costs a certifier refusal and admits nothing.
- **A vector that moves is read again.** A family's vector composes through the families it holds, so an edit to one of them can move it; such a family mentions the edited declaration, is elaborated again with the items that read its entry, and carries what a whole compile does.

**Rationale.**

- **A false irrelevance is a false definitional equation.** Conversion compares nothing at an irrelevant position, so a vector calling a level irrelevant that an instance does hold equates two types with different inhabitants: over `Box.{u} | box(T: Type u)`, a `Type 0` stored at one instance is read back at another.
- **The model is pCuIC's** ([Timany and Sozeau, *Cumulative Inductive Types In Coq*](https://cs.au.dk/~timany/publications/files/2018_FSCD_cumind.pdf)), which relates fully applied instances by their indices and constructor arguments and leaves parameters out. It leaves `Prop` families out for its own complexity, and nothing here is argued from it for one beyond the telescopes converting.

**Rejected.**

- **Reading past a stuck `match`'s annotations**, its goal and a list's element type, as strict positivity reads past them: conversion compares a goal, so an irrelevance claimed there would be a definitional equation conversion denies.
- **Covariant nominal levels**, `Value.{u} ≤ Value.{v}`, which let a `struct`'s producer be lifted and reach neither a tuple type nor a list, and need subsumption to reach nominal types in both checkers.
- **Inferring variance by checking two fresh instances against each other**, as Coq 8.7 did, which blew up on the HoTT library; it is inferred by occurrence, as Rocq infers it now, compositionally, so a `Tree(A)` holding `List(Tree(A))` stays irrelevant.
- **MetaCoq's rule that a fully applied constructor compares no level**: the certifier compares unfoldings untyped, so "compared at the same supertype" does not hold here, and a value compares at its family's variance.
- **Template polymorphism**, dropping parameter-only levels from a nominal's context and computing its sort from its arguments: pCuIC shows irrelevance subsumes it, and Rocq's own account shows it lost as soon as a definition wraps the type.
- **A vector the certifier reads**, unfolding being its rule already, and **a vector filed on a recursive group**: two projections of one group reproduce themselves under every unfolding, so what they hold is computed where they are compared, from the pair, by the fixpoint of the group's unfolding, and a filed one would answer for a group its holder no longer states. A member's vector is a definition's, filed for its name and read off its value, which is the group.
- **Every level of two projections of one group compared**: a declaration holding a recursive proposition would keep every level the group mentions, which the family it stands for holds none of.
- **Carrying a family's recomputed vector**, the most irrelevance its final telescopes allow: a cold build and a warm one that reuses the family would read it differently.
- **Parameter cumulativity**, `Vec(Type u, n) ≤ Vec(Type v, n)`, which neither Rocq nor pCuIC relates.
