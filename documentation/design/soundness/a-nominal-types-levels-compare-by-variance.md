# A nominal type's levels compare by variance

**Decision.** Two instances of an `induct` or a `struct` apart only in a level its declaration is irrelevant in have the same inhabitants. A level is irrelevant when no constructor payload, index target, field or index type mentions it once reduced, except at a position of another family that is itself irrelevant; the two instances' constructor telescopes, targets included, then convert entry by entry ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md)). Conversion compares nothing at an irrelevant position, so a vector calling a level irrelevant that an instance does hold is a false definitional equation between two types with different inhabitants: over `Box.{u} | box(T: Type u)`, a `Type 0` stored at one instance is read back at another.

- **What is compared.** A nominal node, a type or a value of it, compares its levels at the positions its family's vector calls invariant (`Kernel::instances_eq`) and its arguments whatever the levels, at the left side's instance. A position a vector does not reach is invariant, which is the equality every level was compared at before a vector existed.
- **A value compares at its family's variance, not at nothing.** Unfoldings are compared untyped, so two constructor applications are not known to stand at one supertype, and a rule that compared no level on a value would equate `box.{0}(Nat)` with `box.{1}(Nat)`.
- **The certifier compares by the vector it computed.** As it accepts a family's entry, `recheck` computes the family's vector and files it, for the declarations judged after and in the record a later walk reads, as it files a polarity vector ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)); every comparison of two instances reads that one, and a family it has filed none for is invariant. The vector an entry carries is the elaborator's and is read by no judgment of the certifier. It is held to the computed one all the same, and an irrelevance it claims past it refuses the entry, an entry past the declaration's own count included, because the elaborator of a later unit compares by what an entry carries. A carried invariance the certifier would have granted is denied nothing, and the elaborator ships one: a family read while a payload of it was still unsolved keeps that reading.
- **A unit in scope's vector is the certifier's record of it.** A walk reads the vector its unit's record files, and computes one before it judges anything for an entry the record files none for, as for a unit mounted with no record.
- **A walk the budget cuts short files nothing.** It reads a term whose reduction was refused as holding every level it carries, so a vector computed past one may be the budget's: the entry is refused for the budget and answers invariant from then on.
- **What the analysis cannot see through marks every level it carries.** It reads each type's reduct. A `Type` marks its level and a nominal node the levels at its family's invariant positions; an instance that does not unfold marks all of its own, a `rec` group every level it mentions, a metavariable every level of the declaration, and a reduction the budget refuses every level its term carries.
- **An index target is read**, since a level may reach nothing else: `Fam`'s constructor puts another family's invariant level in its index and in no payload, and two instances apart in it have different inhabitants at one index.
- **A parameter's type and the result sort are not read**, and need not be: they bound a level from below, and the arguments themselves are compared. A concept's level that only its carrier's type mentions is irrelevant, while the one its methods bind at is not: `Monad`'s vector is `=*`, the level of `M`'s codomain unread and the level `bind` takes its payloads at compared.
- **A mention a reduction removes is no mention.** `Vec`'s level reaches its proof field through a definition that unfolds to an equation over an intrinsic, and `Map/Key`'s through a method that reduces to a projection of the witness; the two instances' field types differ as written and convert by unfolding, which `compare` reaches once the spines of one definition at two instances decide nothing.
- **No rule concludes a difference from a level.** The large-elimination guard reads a family's declared sort and its constructors' telescopes, which an irrelevant level leaves convertible, and index inversion's constructor rule reads the family, its sort, the tag and the payloads and never an instance, so no arm is excused by two levels conversion calls one. Where a level does decide, it refuses: [the case-equation key](case-equations-and-their-key.md) keeps its levels, and two projections of one group at two instances are a verdict on every one of their levels (`rec_instances`), a family's former excepted, whose levels are its node's.
- **A former's levels are its node's.** An `induct`'s former is a projection of its group, and two projections of one group apart in a level are refused before either is forced, because a recursive function's two instances reproduce themselves under every unfolding. A former reproduces nothing: its member is lambdas over the node it builds and names no member at its head, so `compare` does not take that refusal for one (`projects_a_former`) and the pair goes on to its nodes, which compare by variance. A family with no parameter is its node once forced. Two bare or partly applied formers compared at a function type are applied to the same fresh binders before either head is read, and the two families agree at every argument, which is what eta states. So a pair has one verdict whether it arrives as two instances of the family's name or already reduced to the projections.
- **A definition's vector is the elaborator's alone.** A definition that answers a type carries which of its levels its value holds, and the elaborator reads it to hold two instances one without unfolding either. The kernel reads none: it compares two instances of a definition by their spines and levels and unfolds where that decides nothing, so a vector claiming an irrelevance its body denies costs a kernel refusal and admits nothing.
- **A vector that moves is read again.** A family's vector composes through the families it holds, so an edit to one of them can move it; such a family mentions the edited declaration, is elaborated again with the items that read its entry, and carries what a whole compile does.
- **The model is pCuIC's** ([Timany and Sozeau, *Cumulative Inductive Types In Coq*](https://cs.au.dk/~timany/publications/files/2018_FSCD_cumind.pdf)), which relates fully applied instances by their indices and constructor arguments and leaves parameters out. It leaves `Prop` families out for its own complexity, and nothing here is argued from it for one beyond the telescopes converting.
