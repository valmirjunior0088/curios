# Judging only what is not in scope

**Assumes.** A declared name the environment already answers for was judged by the walk that built it, so no item of the unit being judged arrives under a name already in scope.

**Status.** **argued** for the premise, which is discharged outside the trusted base, and **probed** at the consequence. The certifier's walk decides what to judge by name — `fresh` is `!globals.in_scope(name)`, gating the per-item typing judgment, the residue passes, and `check_induct_decl` and `check_struct_decl` — so an item arriving under a name the environment holds is not judged at all; `Globals::in_scope` states the assumption.

**What holds the premise is the mount discipline.** Mount sets are pairwise disjoint, checked in `curios-text`'s `into_core` before discovery, so two units cannot declare one name; a unit claiming a prefix the prelude mounts collides and is refused ([A module is a compilation unit](../../architecture/a-module-is-a-compilation-unit-and-the-prelude-is-an-environment.md)), and the elaborator's registries refuse a duplicate rather than overwriting one. The kernel's skip rests on that refusal, and nothing in `curios-cert` checks it.

**The consequence is probed, both ways a collision resolves.** `recheck::scope_tests::a_definition_under_a_name_already_in_scope_is_replaced_rather_than_judged` declares `shadowed` at `Nat` in the environment and at `Bool` in the module, beside `reader : Nat = shadowed`: standing alone the module is refused as a `Mismatch`, and against the environment it is certified, `reader` typed against the environment's `shadowed` while the module's own item is never typed and erasure compiles the body it holds. `a_declaration_under_a_name_already_in_scope_is_live_but_unchecked` is the other resolution: `Kernel::declare_induct` runs over the module's declarations after `Kernel::seed`, so a colliding entry overwrites the environment's for every rule that reads it while `check_induct_decl` skips it, and an `Oversized` payload refused standing alone is certified. Both are mutation-checked against forcing `fresh` true. Positivity is not gated: `positivity_vectors` runs over `Declarations::extending`, preferring the module's entry, so a non-positive colliding declaration is refused as a fresh one is — the declaration fixture's second half. What the gate drops for a colliding entry is its size condition, its residue passes and its universe context's satisfiability.
