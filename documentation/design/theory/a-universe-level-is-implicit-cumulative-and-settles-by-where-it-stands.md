# A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands

**Decision.** Surface `Type` is nullary, and Core gives every occurrence an inferred level in an infinite cumulative hierarchy, `Type u : Type (u + 1)`. A written `Prop` takes a level the same way, `Prop u : Type (u + 1)`, and what is said here of a `Type`'s level is said of a `Prop`'s ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)). A declaration generalizes the levels its constraints leave free and instantiates them freshly at each external use; a recursive group is monomorphic in its own levels. A level is committed, equated with another or settled at a value, only where nothing later can tell: at a position conversion compares nothing at, or at a terminal sort, which cumulativity lifts. `UniverseSolver::finalize` closes a declaration's levels by the positions each stands at:

- a level the signature holds where conversion compares it, in a domain or in a result that is no sort, at a position no family or definition is irrelevant in, is the declaration's parameter with its bounds, neither minimized nor settled, a `Type` written there among them;
- a level the body of a type-valued definition holds the same way is its parameter too, the level of the result sort alone taking its least value;
- a family's level is its callers' where a parameter is typed at it, where its sort stands at it or where an instance holds it, and every other level of its telescopes is minimized;
- an occurrence's level that nothing holds settles at its least value, its principal lower bound or zero;
- a chosen level bounded above only by one other chosen level is that level, so a concept's method level is its family's domain;
- a level neither the type nor the body mentions settles, after the occurrences;
- a witness's levels close with the declaration that resolves it: a goal is resolved while its consumer is open, wherever its witness is written, and is pinned to what the goal fixes, the consumer's closing settling the rest; one a parked constraint wakes after its consumer has closed is closed where it resolves;
- a result sort is determined only where the declaration's terminal is a sort.

A position is read off the term as it is written, by the alignment conversion reads two terms by: a part is aligned with itself one level up, so every level it carries is one pair, set apart by where it stands. It is followed by class, so which member of an identified pair survives decides nothing. Every universe metavariable carries a provenance — *chosen*, a `Type` written in an input position; *occurrence*, the instance level a use of a universe-polymorphic name mints; *inferred*, every other — which names the member that represents a class, the greater, and says how a level no position holds closes: an inferred one is minimized and an occurrence's settles. A function type's sort and a tuple type's are the join of their parts' sorts as typing read each, a Π into a proposition and a record of propositions being one, which is where the kernel types them.

Two instances of an `induct` or a `struct` compare their levels by variance: a level that no constructor payload, index target, field or index type mentions once reduced, except through a position itself irrelevant, is irrelevant and required of nothing; every other level is invariant, and none is covariant ([A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md)). A family's vector is the one read as its group finalizes, a telescope still holding a metavariable there reading every level invariant, and its unit carries that reading. A definition that answers a type carries a vector as well, read off its body where its levels have closed, and any other definition is read as written; a recursive member carries none. The vector is the elaborator's alone: the kernel compares two instances of a definition by their spines and levels and unfolds where that decides nothing.

Where the elaborator meets two instances apart at a level a vector calls irrelevant, a family's or a definition's applied in full, it requires nothing and records a weak equation. A function short of its application is neither committed nor called one term — a bare or partly applied instance, a bare former, two lambdas apart in a binder's annotation — and the pair goes on to reduction and to eta at its function type, each pair it left recorded weak once the two sides are held one. The declaration's closing joins every weak pair the solver can hold that decides no level the declaration holds, all together and then one at a time in the order they were met, and drops the rest. A metavariable solved with a family, imitated under an application or read off a problem's rigid side, takes an occurrence of the family of its own, paired with the one it was read off the same way, and every metavariable's solution is committed as the check against its type rebuilt it.

**Rationale.**

- **Implicit levels remove `Type : Type`'s inconsistency** without universe syntax or runtime cost, and Girard's paradox is spellable and refused. An abbreviation takes every level its body holds, so under `let U: Type = (X: Type, f: (Pow(Pow(X))) -> X) -> Pow(Pow(X))` the paradox's carrier is instantiated at an instance of itself below it, and the self-application that needs the two to be one is refused, `this Type would need to be strictly below itself` (`curios`'s `tests::universes`). A self-reference such as `Box/wrap(Box)` stratifies into two levels, which is the hierarchy working.
- **Two honest shapes meet the same refusal with no syntax to escape by**: a local definition applied to itself at two levels, and a group member called above the group's level. The diagnostic gives the span the constraint came from, a level numbering local to the message, and the two remedies, hoisting the definition or splitting the group.
- **A level settled where a caller could have chosen it leaves a scheme that captures fewer programs, and a level has no syntax to say otherwise.** Harper and Pollack call a definition's scheme principal when it "must capture all valid types" of its definiens ([*Type checking with universes*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(90)90108-T), TCS 89, 1991). Sozeau and Tabareau aim "to ensure that we make the least commitment and generate most general constraints", and say of minimizing that "this might only endanger generality, not consistency" ([*Universe Polymorphism in Coq*](https://sozeau.gitlabpages.inria.fr/www/research/publications/Universe_Polymorphism_in_Coq.pdf), ITP 2014). Rocq's minimization "is applied only to fresh universe variables", "User-named universes and the anonymous universe implicitly attached to an explicit `Type` are considered rigid for unification and are never minimized", and of its own example the manual says "the more general definition would keep the fresh level" ([reference manual, *Universe polymorphism*](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/master/refman/addendum/universe-polymorphism.html)); Iris's std++ unsets the collapse of an unbounded universe in its telescopes ([stdpp#19](https://gitlab.mpi-sws.org/iris/stdpp/issues/19)). At a position conversion compares, a settled level is one a later use tells from the one it needed: a tuple type's field, a former's argument and a level a family is invariant in are compared for equality, and no rule lifts a producer settled at zero to the type its caller answers at. At an irrelevant position or a terminal sort nothing tells, and the least level takes nothing from a caller.
- **The positions are read as written, by the reading conversion uses.** What a declaration keeps is what conversion would be asked to equate between two instances of it, so one statement says which positions those are, at the cost of what is written and of no reduction. It holds a level a reduct would have dropped, in an argument a definition's value never reads; that costs a parameter and commits nothing. A family passed as an argument is a function short of its application there, since a parameter is compared at its function type, where both checkers apply the two sides before reading either.
- **A definition is transparent, so two instances of it are compared as their bodies would be.** "The general principle guiding us is that the use of constants should be transparent, in the sense that the system should behave exactly the same when using a constant or its body", and equating two instances' universes can be "too restrictive, as unfolding would only add a (trivial) constraint" (Sozeau and Tabareau, §3 and §4.2). Of their two options, "a static analysis that tells us for each constant which constraints are to be generated for a self-unification with different instances" or "unifications that add no constraints", a definition's vector is the first narrowed to irrelevance, which a conservative walk decides with no normal form past the body's head. Agda computes a polarity for every definition and compares nothing at an argument a definition is constant in ([`TypeChecking/Conversion.hs`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/TypeChecking/Conversion.hs)); Rocq's first-order rule and Lean's `tryHeuristic` shortcut the same pair and fall back to unfolding. A vector claiming an irrelevance its body denies costs a kernel refusal and admits nothing.
- **An abbreviation behaves as its body does.** `{A: Type, a: A}` keeps its level for a caller written out or named, and as a `struct` of the same content does. A body restates the sort it answers at wherever it says what it answers, in a match's motive, and cumulativity lifts the result there as at the signature's terminal, so the result sort's own level is none of the levels a body holds.
- **A family's level no instance can differ in is no parameter.** A level of a telescope that no parameter is typed at, the sort does not stand at and no instance holds is minimized as a body's is, so no occurrence of the family mints a level for it and no signature naming the family carries one.
- **An occurrence's instance is gone from its reduct.** Both checkers size a tuple type or a Π from its parts as they are written, so an occurrence's level generalized as interface is a level the enclosing type sits at for nothing its reduct holds, and a written level only overstates its reduct's, so settling it where nothing holds it admits nothing false. Rocq's minimization touches only "the fresh universes generated for each global application" and keeps a rigid universe over a flexible one; Agda under `--cumulativity` sends a level bounded only below to the join of its bounds; Lean's `Pure (f : Type u → Type v)` ties `pure {α : Type u}` to its family. Settled so, a scheme carries only the parameters its positions need; `curios-prelude-archive`'s `universe_parameter_census` prints each `/std` definition's count.
- **A level nothing holds takes its least value whether or not anything ever bounded it.** The store discharges `0 ≤ u` where it is added and where a substitution leaves it, and no checker tells a level so bounded from one nothing bounded. A witness at the least level answers a goal at any other, the position being one conversion compares nothing at.
- **A former sized as typing read its parts sits where the kernel types it.** Sized from its parts' reducts, a former sits below a part whose written type is above its reduct's, a closed match typed by its motive, a call typed by its codomain, an occurrence at a level its value drops, and the kernel refuses the sort the elaborator declared. The two checkers land in one sort by sizing alike, and a level left to settle settles for economy alone.
- **A goal is resolved while its declaration is open**, whether its witness is written before the declaration or after it: a witness that has not elaborated is elaborated first, and the declaration's attempt is made again ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)), so what a declaration offers its callers does not follow where its witnesses are written. The goal is pinned to what it fixes and no further: a method's level is the larger of its action's and its region's, which neither order of reading has seen when the witness resolves, so the rest is the declaration's to settle, and a declaration that names a former bare under a witness keeps that former's level for its callers.
- **A level only a parameter's type mentions changes nothing an instance holds.** Equating such levels makes `!` hold its region at the level of a nominal action it binds — `small(n)!` over `Result(Str, Nat)` inside a region over `Result(Str, Type)` — where `Result/bind` passes. An irrelevant level leaves two instances' constructor telescopes convertible entry by entry, a mention a reduction removes being no mention, and telescopes are what the large-elimination guard reads, so the guard decides nothing new. Rocq infers the same variance (`list@{u}` is `*u`), and MetaCoq's PCUIC specifies it (`Variance.Irrelevant`).
- **A pair met at an irrelevant position is joined where it can be and required nowhere.** Required, it is the refusal above. With nothing kept, nothing ties the level: a former's level floats above its argument's, and one family's occurrences land apart in one signature. Weak, a declaration that elaborates with the equations required and holds none of their levels elaborates to the same levels, and where a pair cannot hold, dropping it decides nothing a checker reads: the two instances convert as they stand. Rocq records the same pair as a weak constraint by default ("when comparing universes in an irrelevant position") and says of doing without that it "may produce arbitrary numbers of universes"; it unifies "when neither is smaller than the other and one is flexible", and joining a pair one side of which already bounds the other is what keeps a former's level at its argument's.
- **A weak equation decides no level the declaration holds.** It is recorded at a position nothing reads, so it has no standing to decide a level something does. Joined all the same, a small action sequenced into a region holds the region's payload to the action's level, the two instances of the monad meeting where it is irrelevant, and two arms answering at their own constructors' levels make those levels one through the declared result's. The pair that is dropped leaves an occurrence that settles from its own bounds, at the join of the held levels below it.
- **A family a metavariable is solved with is an occurrence of its own.** A type's instance is the one its problem compares; a family is a function, and what stands at a level it does not hold is each application's to choose. Built at the instance it was read off, a solution holds its binders to that one occurrence's levels: `Monad/bind` written out makes the monad of a small action a monad of small payloads alone, and refuses a region `!`, which is handed its monad, sequences.
- **A solution is the term its check rebuilt.** The check compares a lambda's annotation with the domain it is checked at by subsumption and rebuilds the lambda at that domain, where the kernel compares the two by conversion. Committed as it came, a candidate keeps its annotation: `M`, solved from an action over `Io` at one instance to `(T: Type 0) => Io(T)`, stands where `Try/bind` takes `(Type 1) -> Type 1`, and whether the kernel accepts the program follows which side of the problem solved `M`.
- **A family read while a payload of it is unsolved keeps that reading.** The later items of its own unit were compared under it, so a build that reuses the family must read what a build that elaborates it read.

**Rejected.**

- **`Type : Type`**, **universe syntax** — variables, level expressions, explicit arguments — and **specializing runtime code by universe instance**.
- **Settling a level by where it came from alone**, an occurrence at its recorded floor and a written `Type` outside an input position at its least value wherever they stand: a result is settled at a level conversion compares, a signature's input through a bound of its body, and a function, a producer or an alias over a family that carries a type is held to one level.
- **Declining a commitment the store refuses**, which is what Rocq and Lean do. It accepts a bare former and an ordinary function passed as a family, whose refusing bound is in the store first, and not `!`, whose bound arrives after; it decides consistency at each commitment, which `UniverseSolver::add_constraint` is built not to; and it leaves a verdict following the order of a declaration's evidence.
- **A bound tagged by where it came from**, an occurrence's own argument or a witness's side condition: a bound a substitution discharges leaves no origin behind, and a producer and an alias lose their level with no witness in sight. **A record of whether a constant ever bounded an occurrence** is the same state, kept true through every substitution and alias for a distinction no checker reads.
- **Reading a bound only once the level it names has settled.** An `Io` over a payload at zero stops following its payload down, which is what settling an occurrence at its argument is for.
- **Reading a signature by its parts' reducts**, with the walk that computes a vector. It holds exactly what reduction leaves, and it costs what the signature's computations cost to run, a bound over a closed scan being run to learn that it names no level; and it is a second statement, beside the alignment's, of which positions conversion compares.
- **Leaving a `Type` in a value to be minimized**, a constructor's payload or a literal's component, so that no definition carries a level of its own into a value. A parameter's own sort already reaches one through an implicit argument, `Any/any(S)` storing `Type u` for an `S: Type u`, so the rule would close nothing and leave the property an exception.
- **Holding only an occurrence's level in a type-valued body**, a `Type` written there staying minimized. It leaves an abbreviation behaving unlike its body, and unlike a `struct` of the same content.
- **A vector the kernel reads**, unfolding being its rule already, and **a vector on a recursive member**: two projections of one group reproduce themselves under every unfolding, and both checkers compare their every level.
- **Tying a body's occurrence of a family to the parameter's**: the source states no such tie, and one read off a name would hold two unrelated occurrences together.
- **Rocq's atomic-bound condition**: Curios's levels are algebraic, and occurrences bounded below by a maximum would float.
- **Generic method levels with harder disjunction solving**: every witness's scheme would carry maxima a use can only guess through.
- **Equating an occurrence's level with its argument's at application**, incomplete where one level bounds several arguments.
- **Requiring a witness before its use**, as Lean and Rocq do, **taking a witness's scheme from its head**, as GHC's first pass over its instances does, or **fixing a scheme from the signature**, as Lean does for a theorem's header: a Curios witness's scheme depends on its body, `/std`'s order does not follow its witnesses, and without level syntax a body could not narrow a signature's scheme. What a head does give is its key, which is read before any body.
- **McBride's displacement** (Favonia, Angiuli and Mullanix, POPL 2023), which sets cumulativity aside and would replace the solver and the kernel's entailment.
- **Sizing by the written spelling in both checkers**, **resolving `/sys` formers to their intrinsics**, or **declaring the reduct the truth**: the first keeps every floating level, the second misses aliases and nominal occurrences, the third leaves the kernel accepting a declared type it never typed.
- **Making a goal ground where its witness has not elaborated**, with every level a settlement lands on, or **holding a scheme open until its goals resolve**: under the first a declaration written before its witness sits at its least levels and is refused at a larger type its twin written after the witness accepts; under the second later items would take the levels rather than instances of them.
- **Covariant nominal levels**, `Value.{u} ≤ Value.{v}`, which let a `struct`'s producer be lifted and reach neither a tuple type nor a list, and need subsumption to reach nominal types in both checkers.
- **Inferring variance by checking two fresh instances against each other**, as Coq 8.7 did, which blew up on the HoTT library; it is inferred by occurrence, as Rocq infers it now, compositionally, so a `Tree(A)` holding `List(Tree(A))` stays irrelevant.
- **MetaCoq's rule that a fully applied constructor compares no level**: the kernel compares unfoldings untyped, so "compared at the same supertype" does not hold here, and a value compares at its family's variance.
- **Template polymorphism**, dropping parameter-only levels from a nominal's context and computing its sort from its arguments: pCuIC shows irrelevance subsumes it, and Rocq's own account shows it lost as soon as a definition wraps the type.
- **Parameter cumulativity**, `Vec(Type u, n) ≤ Vec(Type v, n)`, which neither Rocq nor pCuIC relates; **subsumption without conversion**, relating instances one way only, which still refuses a region's `M(A)` against an action's type wherever conversion compares them; and **explicit lifting**, as Lean's `ULift`, which a program with no level syntax cannot write.
- **Comparing an irrelevant level at nothing, with no weak equation kept**, which is Rocq with its weak constraints off: settling such a level at its least value removes the parameters that offer nothing, and neither the slack above a former's argument nor one family's occurrences landing apart.
- **Joining every weak equation the store can hold**, whatever it decides: a level a signature holds is then decided by a position nothing reads.
- **Declining the level shortcuts on any nominal instance**, leaving the pair to the structural arms: the shortcuts exist so that two spellings of one computation are identified without running it.
- **Resolving the witness after the action**, as `Monad/bind` written out does, which refuses the other direction, and **closing every witness with a declaration, one woken after its consumer closed included**: a goal resolved after its consumer has closed has no declaration left.
- **Carrying a family's recomputed vector**, the most irrelevance its final telescopes allow: a cold build and a warm one that reuses the family would read it differently.
- **Committing a family at the instance it was solved from**, imitated or read off a problem's rigid side.
- **Committing only a lambda as rebuilt**, where a lambda beneath a tuple, a struct literal or a constructor meets its field's type the same way, and **equating a lambda's annotation with its domain in the check**, which refuses a decided candidate from the lower side.
