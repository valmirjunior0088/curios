# `Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K

**Decision.** Beside the cumulative `Type` hierarchy, Curios has a strict `Prop` with definitional proof irrelevance, levelled as `Type` is: `Prop u : Type (u + 1)`, written `Prop` and inferred, a proposition is admitted at the `Type` of its level through subsumption, and a proposition reaches data two ways: by a match with no arm, every constructor ruled out at the scrutinee's indices, and by a match on a family that declares one constructor whose every payload its index targets pin, as `Eq`'s `refl` is. A constructor that holds a payload no index pins, a proof among them, is taken apart only into a proposition. A proposition is sized as a type is: a function type into one sits at the join of its domains' levels and its own, a `Prop`-sorted domain counting at its level, and a declared proposition bounds every payload it holds, a proof at the level of what it proves and a payload an index pins like any other. `Eq : Prop` under irrelevance makes the theory definitionally K, permanently: proofs are certificates to be erased, not data to compute with. The checkers decide a sound, budgeted fragment of that theory. Both decide a comparison at a `Prop`-sorted type by irrelevance before either side is reduced, wherever neither side is flexible. Both know an application of a declaration that lands in `Prop` for a proposition by that declaration, before the type is reduced, so asking whether a proof stands somewhere runs nothing the type states. A match that takes a proposition into data reads nothing of the proof, and fires with the proof unreduced: the scrutinee's family instance is read off binders and declarations, its indices state the constructor's payload, and where it is the instance the constructor builds at that payload, by the checker's own conversion, the match is its arm at that payload; otherwise it stands. So `Eq/subst(P, p, v)` along a hypothesis `p: Eq()(a + b, b + a)` is `v`. The elaborator writes a payload an arm names as the index that pins it wherever the arm's equations hold the two one, and a closed match whose arm names none is its arm's body outright. A proof a declaration writes is read by its own check alone: once its item is published its name unfolds to nothing in either checker ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)).

**Rationale.**

- **Irrelevance makes proofs erasable by construction.** Any two proofs of a proposition are equal, so no program depends on which it received, and the large-elimination guard keeps that erasure sound. K licenses index inversion's deletion rule and the substitution of a variable scrutinee by its case value; inversion is shared, so that rule, an acceptance, sits in the trusted base.
- **Reducing a proof to learn it equals another proof is work whose answer was known.** A proof is a lemma's whole derivation, and a checker that reduces proofs first spends its budget there.
- **A proposition is sized because a payload's application is read as a descent.** The size order grades an application of a constructor's payload below the constructor ([Totality of the erased program](../soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)), which holds of a payload that cannot be applied at the family holding it. Unsized, `induct Bad: Prop | c(f: (A: Prop) -> (A) -> A) end` holds one that can: `f(Bad)(b)` is a `Bad`, at the identity it is `b`, and the recursion on it proves `False`. A level above what the payload ranges over is what `Type` already asks of a family. Abel and Coquand's cast that unfolds without end (LMCS 16(2:14), 2020) needs the same instantiation, a proposition quantifying over every proposition applied at one built from itself, so it does not type either.
- **The level is inferred, so a signature states none.** A written `Prop` takes a level as a written `Type` does, a declaration's result level settles at the least its payloads allow, and a proposition over a type variable sits at that variable's level.
- **A match into data learns nothing.** With no arm there is no value to have been handed, and with one constructor whose payloads are its indices the arm is told what the scrutinee's type already said. A payload no index pins would be handed on as it was received, a proof included, and a recursion along one has a type reduce the lemma that built it: Lean's position, where Carneiro (*The Type Theory of Lean*, 2019, §3.1) shows definitional equality undecidable and any checker for it non-transitive. A recursion along a relation is one on a bound ([`curios-std`'s README](../../../curios-std/README.md#a-measure-descends-on-a-bound)).
- **The rule is read off the declaration.** A family declares one constructor or it does not, so every match on it is judged alike, and each checker's guard is a count and the walk over the constructor's targets that both share.
- **The cast agrees with conversion and with the erased program.** Conversion holds `P(a + b)` and `P(b + a)` one type, and erasure takes a proof match's single arm without evaluating the proof (`tests::erasure::value_tests`), so a cast that stood on a hypothesis was the identity to every judgment but reduction. Firing on the indices has reduction say what both already do.
- **No checker reduces a proof to take it into data.** A proof is a lemma's whole derivation: reduced to reach its constructor, a cast along a lemma proved by induction costs the induction wherever a type runs it, and the lemma's body can never be withheld from what reads its statement. The indices of its type state everything the constructor would.
- **A closed match fires outright.** A closed, total proof of a family with one constructor is that constructor, so its indices are the constructor's and nothing is left to ask. The closed machine holds no type to ask it of, and takes the same step by the match's shape alone: one arm, no catch-all, no payload named.

**Rejected.**

- **An impredicative `Prop`**, a function type into a proposition being one whatever it quantifies over, as Lean's and Rocq's are: with a payload's application read as a descent it proves `False`, and with a cast between two propositions it unfolds without end. Narrowing the descent to a payload whose declared type ends in its family, as Rocq's guard does, closes the first and leaves the second to the budget.
- **One small `Prop`**, a statement over a large type left outside it: a predicate over a type variable, membership in a list or an accessibility relation, could not be a proposition at a large carrier.
- **Exempting a proof payload from sizing**, on the ground that irrelevance erases it: a small family then holds a proof of a large proposition, which is the payload the hole is built from.
- **Univalence**, forever: under K all proofs of an identity are equal, so a univalent equality with content cannot be added.
- **Classifying every type by its reduct**: a closed `Holds(d)` is decided, and a text scanned to learn that `Str/Valid(b)` is a proposition, to be told what the head's declaration states; the irrelevance gate would then run a proposition to learn that no proof of it need be.
- **Reading `Type` off a declaration as well**: a proposition is admitted where a `Type` is declared, so the declaration does not say its application is none.
- **Reading a recursive group's claim about its member**: nothing has checked it where it is read, so such an application is classified by what it reduces to.
- **Reducing the proof where the indices do not decide**, which is what such a match did before it fired on them: the proof of an equation between two open ends is a stuck lemma, so reducing it decides nothing the indices do not, at the cost above.
- **Refusing an arm that names its pinned payload**, so that every such match is its arm's body by its shape: the eliminator types an arm at an abstract payload, and where the scrutinee's two indices do not unify, as `some(x)` and `some(y)` do not, nothing holds the payload to either, so the arm cannot be written over them. Such an arm stays as written, and where the match fires its payload is what the scrutinee's indices state, which the closed machine is handed by its host.
- **Recording the scrutinee's family instance on the `match`**, as Rocq's `CaseInvert` does: the kernel reduces a `match` in a type position before typing it, so the record would be believed before it is checked.
- **A proof payload admitted into data**, a one-constructor proposition whose unpinned payloads are proofs, as Lean's subsingleton elimination has it: an accessibility predicate then eliminates into data, with the hazard above.
- **Counting the constructors the scrutinee's indices leave**, so that a family with several is matched into data where all but one are ruled out: a verdict of each match site and of what inversion decides there, where every system that lets a proposition reach data states its condition on the declaration. Several constructors with orthogonal targets, eliminated by matching on the indices (Gilbert, Cockx, Sozeau and Tabareau, POPL 2019), is the declaration's form of it, and nothing here consumes it.
- **A loop detector in reduction**, where the step budget already refuses deterministically.
