# A hidden member takes no position

**Decision.** A hidden member of a telescope — `@` or `use` — takes no position among the members an author writes, and is written only to be named, on data as on a function. A function's parameters, a constructor's payload and a structure's fields are each a telescope whose every entry stands under its mark, and every site that meets one is one of three walks. Where a telescope is declared, each member is entered by its mark. Where it is filled — a call, a constructor application, a structure literal — the plain members are written in order, the hidden ones between two of them are written from the first of their run, and one left out is filled by its mark: an `@` member by unification, by reduction to truth or from the facts in scope, a `use` member by resolution. Where it is opened — a lambda's binders, an arm's — the written binders are aligned the same way and a hidden member left out is bound all the same, so a bound it states is a fact there. A position written to read a value, `.N` or a pattern's positional field, counts the plain members, and a hidden field is read by its label. An invariant is therefore stated once, on the constructor or the structure it constrains: `/std/Io/Chunk`'s `chunk(bytes: Bytes, @some: Holds(0 < Bytes/len(bytes)))` is built as `chunk(b)` and matched as `chunk(bytes)`, and a `/std/Str/At`, which declares its bound and its boundary hidden, is built from its offset wherever the two follow from what is in scope, and has both as facts wherever a position is. A constructor's payload and a structure's field take `@`, and a concept's field `use`; a mark no site takes yet is refused by that site's own rule ([Plicity on a telescope's members](../../roadmap/03-surface/02-plicity-on-a-telescopes-members.md)).

Core states a member's mark in its telescope's entry and nowhere beside it. What an author wrote rides on the written forms — an application's arguments, an arm's binders, a literal's entries, a written position — which elaboration aligns once and rebuilds in normal form: a value holding every slot in order, an arm binding every payload, a projection naming its slot. A normal form reads as the form written in full, so an elaborated term is elaborated again unchanged. The kernel holds an argument's and an arm binder's mark to its slot's where it types them, and conversion reads no written mark.

**Rationale.**

- **A pattern is the form that builds the value.** What a call leaves out, an arm and a pattern leave out, so one alignment (`curios-elab`'s `align`) serves every site and is learned once. Lean's patterns are "a subset of the terms" ([Pattern matching](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Terms/Pattern-Matching/)), Rocq omits implicit arguments in patterns as in terms ([`match.rst`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/doc/sphinx/language/extensions/match.rst)), and Agda inserts a wildcard for each hidden pattern left out by the function its application arguments are aligned by ([`Rules.LHS.Implicit`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/TypeChecking/Rules/LHS/Implicit.hs)).
- **An invariant is checked where the value is built and assumed where it is read.** A proof field written at every literal, or a hidden payload written at every arm, states the invariant at each use where the declaration states it once. Liquid Haskell turns a refined constructor into a smart constructor ([Refined datatypes](https://ucsd-progsys.github.io/liquidhaskell-tutorial/Tutorial_05_Datatypes.html)), and Why3 assumes a type's invariant at function entry ([Syntax reference](https://www.why3.org/doc/syntaxref.html)); here the bound is an ordinary hidden member, filled by the routes that fill a call's and in scope by the rule that puts a hypothesis's proof fields there ([A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md)).
- **A mark lives on its binder.** A vector kept beside a telescope can drift from it, and each rebuild must carry both; an entry's own mark cannot drift and is kept by construction. Agda's telescope is `Tele (Dom Type)` ([`Agda.Syntax.Internal`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/Syntax/Internal.hs)), and Lean's `lam` and `forallE` each carry a `binderInfo` ([`Lean.Expr`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Expr.lean)).
- **The written form and the normal form are two forms.** A literal leaves hidden fields out and a written position counts plain members, where a value holds every slot and a projection names one. Elaboration resolves the first to the second once, as it resolves a label, so nothing below it reads a position, and a value with a hidden slot is a metavariable's solution as any value is.
- **A spread never copies a hidden field.** What the field states is stated of the fields the new value has. Copied under an override it would be a proof of another proposition, so it is filled anew, and the author who means the base's own writes it.
- **A hidden field takes no part in a derived witness**, as a hidden payload takes none: it is no member a literal writes, so `Spell` leaves it out and the text read back infers it.

**Rejected.**

- **Padding a group's rows in lowering**, so that rows of one constructor may write different hidden members: the match matrix is laid out before any signature is known, and the padding would be discarded where [typed patterns](../../roadmap/03-surface/01-typed-patterns.md) aligns each row.
- **One sealed pair of a telescope and its marks**, behind one door: the correspondence is asserted where it could be unspellable, and a nested arity still needs two vectors. **A telescope generic in its annotation**: a tuple type could then spell no mark, at the price of a type parameter through every function the two checkers share between a tuple's fields and a structure's. **A mark on the binder's label**: a label is no part of a scope's identity, and a rebuild that re-mints its binders drops one.
- **Counting plain members in a projection with no written form.** An elaborated term is elaborated again, and a slot would be read as a position.
- **A struct pattern held to a written mark.** A hidden member takes no position there and a named one is read by its label, so no pattern has a mark to write.
- **A spread that falls back to the base's hidden field** where filling it anew fails: one literal elaborated two ways by whether a search succeeded.
