# A value costs when it is kept, not when it is named

**Decision.** An immutable aggregate whose fields travel through compiler-controlled control flow does not pay for a heap object. Two capabilities in `curios-cont` deliver that, on one recorded representation: continuation scalar replacement turns a join-point parameter that every flow reaches as an exact-arity tuple into that many field parameters, loop headers and backedges the central case rather than an exclusion; and window virtualization carries a rope through a walk as `(base, offset, length)`, turning each suffix slice into offset arithmetic behind a `WindowExtent` guard that keeps the eager bounds trap at the original evaluation point. Both splits are recorded as `FieldGroup`s on the module, and the verifier holds every recorded group to its continuation's parameter list the way it already holds arities — a pass that reshapes a recorded parameter list without maintaining the record fails loudly instead of silently disagreeing with the split.

**Admission composes one backward fact with one forward one.** The demand lattice answers how a received value is used, interprocedurally: an argument defers to the parameter that receives it, so `Projected` on a join parameter means every use on every path is a field read or an eligible transfer. The origin lattice answers what reaches it: `Exact(arity)` means every incoming flow is a construction of one arity or an alias of one, with boundaries stated by injection — resume parameters, escaping functions' parameters, the entry — so a surviving bottom means unreached, never assumed. Windows need no origin exactness at all, because any rope opens as its own whole window with one length read; their admission is a region walk over uses alone, and a region with any hostile use is declined whole.

**Materialization is where a whole-value use survives, and it is exact.** A split product rebuilds at the continuation's head and stands in for the old parameter everywhere; projection forwarding and dead-binding elimination erase the rebuild precisely where nothing needed the object, and where something did, the rebuild is the materialization the cost contract prescribes. A window would materialize through the existing slice helper, which reproduces the empty, whole and proper representation choices exactly — and deferring a node's forcing from slice time to first read is value-identical, because the read helper's own force is memoized.

**The envelope, measured.** The emitted `/std/Str/fold` body carries no accumulator tuple and no suffix view or slice-helper call; the digit walk runs fifteen percent under its pre-campaign floor and the multi-byte walk twenty-three; the walk-mirror family compiles the idiomatic and hand-flattened accumulator spellings to identical counts, which is this decision's promise made checkable. The figures live beside their probes: `curios`'s `tests::codegen` ladder, census and mirror instruments.

**The limits, named.** A variant-width flow — one parameter reached by constructor tuples of different arities, as the UTF-8 scan state is — is not split here, and the caller-side scan rebuild it retains is priced at one construction per multi-byte character in the ladder; removing it was left as a successor capability, and [A variant travels as the fields of its widest constructor](a-variant-travels-as-the-fields-of-its-widest-constructor.md) is that successor, which also supplied the measured known-call candidate this decision made the reinstate condition for its retired worker milestone. Resume parameters belong to the return protocol, raw carriers to `represent.rs` — a split says how many values travel, the carrier lattice says how each is held, and a continuation's `Open` offer is why a split `Nat` member reaches a raw register while a worker signature does not.

**Rationale.** Erasure has already exposed the runtime fields, and CPS still knows continuations and direct callees; WebAssembly emission loses those facts, and Binaryen — the WasmGC mainstream's answer — sees the module only after Curios signatures and helper calls have hidden the flows, and does not run in the browser compiler path at all, where `curios-js` ships emission straight to the engine. The recorded representation follows the uniform lesson of GHC's unboxed tuples in Core, Lean's explicit boxing in its IR, and MLton changing SSA so flattening could be expressed as data: the analyses may be as clever as they like, but the decision survives only where every pass can see it.

**Rejected — relying on Binaryen.** Too late to unlock Curios-level rewrites, blind across helper calls and signatures, and absent from the browser path.

**Rejected — source-level unboxed types.** Representation stays an optimizer fact; a surface annotation would let a spelling promise what the analysis is supposed to earn.

**Rejected — reference-counted reset and reuse.** Reusing a uniquely owned dying object solves persistent reconstruction, not the absence of a materialized identity, and Curios targets tracing WebAssembly GC.

**Rejected — per-tag call-pattern specialization for the scan state.** The specializer already matches the reconstruction and declines it on a budget that is honest about its mechanism: cloning the callee once per tag prices a per-character allocation at code growth, measured at extent 37 against a limit of 24 by `step_specialization_extent`.

**Rejected — threading bare fields by convention.** The return protocol's width-two floor is the shape of that cost, paid once already: an unrecorded decision is re-decided every round or policed by rules, where a recorded one removes the choice.
