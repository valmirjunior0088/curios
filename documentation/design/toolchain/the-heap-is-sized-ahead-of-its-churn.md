# The heap is sized ahead of its churn

**Decision.** `curios-runtime`'s shared engine sets `gc_heap_initial_size` to sixteen mebibytes, on the wasmtime 47.0.3 pin that introduced the knob. Nothing else changes: the collector stays the semi-space copier `Collector::Auto` resolves to under the `gc-copying` feature, and the launcher and product boundaries stay where they were. The constant is engine-wide because the knob is — wasmtime bakes the tunable into the `.cwasm` compatibility stamp, so an artifact precompiled under the size can only ever run under it, and the workspace's single-pin invariant extends over the knob with no new mechanism.

**Rationale.** The engine grows its heap only when a single allocation still does not fit after a collection, so under death-birth churn the heap parks within a doubling of the live set: the survivors nearly fill the semi-space half, the room left per cycle is small, and the collector recopies the whole live set continually. Both churn-class workloads measured about two thirds of their churn in that recopying — `chain` parked at 1 MiB over a ~320 KB chain, collecting 1.6 times per round; `spines` parked at 8 MiB over its growing map, ten times per thousand inserts, superlinear per insert — and pre-growing the heap removed the share on both. Sixteen mebibytes is the smallest measured size on the good side of that knee for both members, and the cold-page tax that argues for smaller registered only far above it, on a hot loop, at 256 MiB. Commit is lazy: `hello_world`'s resident set does not move under the reservation. The figures and their retake recipes live with `chain_collection_decomposition` and `spines_collection_decomposition` in `curios/src/tests/codegen/churn.rs`.

**What it was measured to buy.** Three arms on one x86-64 box, same method, best of three — wasmtime 46 stock, 47 without the knob, 47 with it. The pin alone costs the allocation-heavy rows several percent (`chain` +10%, `spines` +7%, `churn` +5%) while `lcg` improves by 4%; the size then buys 2.7× on `chain` (831 → 311 ms at K = 1600) and 1.55× on `spines` (737 → 475 ms at N = 75 000), moves the allocation-free `churn` not at all — the empirical pin on its exit from the churn class — and gives `trees` only 3%, because an all-live tree must outgrow any constant.

**The limits, named.** A constant is a floor, not the policy's final form: the right heap for a small-live churner is tens of mebibytes while an all-live tree wants hundreds, and no engine hook chooses per program. The engine road continues upstream, at the growth policy and what an old generation costs to keep, and [The survivors are what cost](../../roadmap/collector-economics-spec.md) records what is known for whoever picks it up. The remaining third of churn cost, the birth path itself, is compiler territory this decision does not claim.

**Rejected — the compiler-side levers, on the census's numbers.** The death-birth census (`death_birth_census`, beside the aggregate-flow census) found the population pervasive — 509 of 1 168 constructions beside a co-resident dying value of matching width — and entirely cross-frame: the dying value always arrives as a parameter, so no intra-function reuse mechanism reaches it. Static array-scoped reuse was refused because the linearly threaded builder population it targets (136 of 256 rope extends) is not copy-bound — the rope already amortizes a builder to one node per extend and a single force-time fill; reinstate on observed repeated force-fills, the alternating append-and-read hazard the rope documents. Construction coalescing and constructor-tail rewrites were refused because their shapes measure at zero — no constructed-width pairs exist corpus-wide, the walks are accumulator-first, and a tail-modulo-cons write needs the mutable tuple field the representation deliberately lacks; reinstate on either count rising from zero.

**Rejected — reuse machinery as such.** Recorded at the campaign's gate so they are not relitigated. A per-object reference count on an engine-owned heap frees nothing and is purchased solely as a uniqueness test: dup/drop traffic on every duplication, payload fields losing immutability, and an undercount silently corrupting a shared value, a permanent discipline on every future `curios-cont` pass. A sticky one-bit sharing flag shares the mut-ification and the misplaced-dup soundness class. Regions and arenas are inexpressible on WasmGC, where nothing frees wholesale. Owning allocation in linear memory is a different compilation strategy, forfeiting the engine collector and the browser story to buy the Perceus precondition. Perceus stays the comparator, never the mechanism.

**Rejected — a maximal pre-grow, and an environment switch.** Oversizing handed half of `chain`'s win back to cold pages while merely tying on cache-scattered walks, so bigger is not safer. An environment variable choosing the size was rejected for `curios-profile`'s reason — the measurement that chose the constant is specified at its call site, and an out-of-band override could only disagree with it — and for a harder one here: the tunable stamps the artifact, so a per-environment size would quietly fork `.cwasm` compatibility.

**Precedent.** Delegating churn to the host collector is the WasmGC mainstream's strategy — V8's porting guidance, dart2wasm and Kotlin/Wasm shipping no reuse machinery — and the browser half of this toolchain already runs under V8's generational collector. This decision closes the native half's gap to the extent a constant can, with GHC's generational economics as the recorded horizon.
