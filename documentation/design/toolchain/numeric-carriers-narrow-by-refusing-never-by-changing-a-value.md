# Numeric carriers narrow by refusing, never by changing a value

**Decision.** `Nat` and `Int` are unbounded everywhere above the emitter — the free-monoid `Nat` carries no width, `nat_bound` computes in `curios-num`'s unbounded `Natural`, and the erased stages carry whatever the theory produced — and ride a 31-bit `i31ref` at runtime. An operation whose meaning needs a width is therefore not one of these: rotation and leading-zero counts were `Nat` and `Int` operations that narrowed through `to_u32` to answer, which made them 32-bit notions wearing an unbounded type and left them *stuck* above `2³²` — partial in the theory. They are gone from the surface; a carrier that *has* a width is where they belong, and `Byte` is the first to take them: `/std/Byte`'s `and`, `or`, `xor`, `not`, `shl`, `shr`, `rotl` and `rotr` are ordinary definitions over `to_nat` and `of_nat`, none of them a row in the trusted base, each restating the eight bits because nothing below the theory narrows to them — a masked result everywhere, a *guarded* count on the left shift, which would otherwise build its whole product before the mask and meet the envelope on the way to a zero, and a count taken modulo the width on the rotations, where the remainder carries its own bound in the term and no guard is needed. An arbitrary-width one is still `/std/Bits`, whose width is its length. A narrowing may *refuse* a value it cannot represent; it may never change one. So the emitter panics on a result that leaves the i31 envelope rather than truncating it — naming the carrier and `BigNat` or `BigInt` as where larger values live, per [a refusal is a panic the emitter renders](a-refusal-is-a-panic-the-emitter-renders.md), and a stage's constant folder must decline rather than wrap past its own carrier. Values beyond a carrier belong to `/std/BigNat` and `/std/BigInt`, not to a wider primitive.

**Rationale.** The stratification is what lets the theory be unbounded and the backend be fast, and refusal is the only narrowing discipline that keeps the two consistent: a proof stated over Core's `Nat` remains true of the compiled program, because that program either computes the proved answer or computes none. Wrapping breaks exactly that, making a proved theorem false of the running program — which is why the trap is not a wart to be optimized away but the mechanism holding the layering together. There is one width and one boundary, and both are the target's: `ENVELOPE_BITS` is named in `curios-cont` beside the operations it makes partial, and nothing above that crate knows a number at all.

**Where the refusal lives.** One boundary, at materialization, and `curios-cont` owns it: an out-of-envelope value refuses on every path a constant can take — boxed through `emit_data`, held raw in a register through `emit_let_pure`, and by exclusion from constant hoisting, since a trap is not a constant instruction and has to stay at its execution point. `nat_fits_envelope` and `int_fits_envelope` are the one test all three consult.

**There were three boundaries, and the middle one named no target.** The erased carriers were `u32` and `i32`, so a folder refused at 32 bits while the runtime refused at 31, and the band between them was observable: `1073741824 + 1073741824` is `2³¹`, a legitimate `u32`, so a fold carrying it through `to_str` printed it while the same expression with a runtime operand trapped. Those were two programs whose difference was a width no machine had, and both halves had to be asserted separately to pin it. The erased carriers are unbounded now and the split is gone with them — a folder computes what Core computes, so it agrees with the theory by construction rather than by differential.

**What unbounded carriers cost is that folding allocates, and that is a resource question rather than a semantic one.** A fixed width made every non-trapping fold allocation-free for free; without it, multiplication doubles a magnitude and a left shift grows one without bound, so a thirty-step chain can ask a folder for a numeral no machine holds. The type level was never exposed to this because `curios-core` charges every reduction step against a budget. The erased stages answer the same way, in the bound each already owns: `curios-ersd` folds under `MAX_FOLD_BITS`, its evaluator's reify pool read in the unit a numeral grows in, and `curios-cont` under the envelope, since a wider result could not be materialized whatever it was. Past it the fold **declines** rather than refusing, and the difference matters — a decline is invisible, because a program means the same thing whether or not a fold fires, where a refusal is a fact about the program. `curios-num`'s `scalar` states no width at all: a growing operation takes its allowance as a parameter, because the allowance is a fact about the caller's resources rather than about what the operation means.

**What refusal costs is associativity, and a transformation that reorders must pay it.** Wrapping is a total group operation, which is why a compiler for a wrapping carrier may reassociate freely; refusing makes the operation *partial*, and a partial operation can be associative wherever both sides are defined while differing in **where** it is defined. So an algebraic law licenses value-equality only: a pass that changes the *order* operands are combined in owes a second check — that no partial result of the new order falls outside the carrier when the total is inside it. `curios-ersd`'s monoid rebase is the one pass that reorders, and it is licensed on exactly that, not on associativity alone. The rows that have it are `NatAdd` (non-negative operands, so every partial sum is at most the total) and `NatOr`/`IntOr` (total inside the envelope); the rows that do not are multiplication, whose zero annihilator makes the total smaller than a partial, and signed addition, which cancels — `((1 * 0) * 2¹⁶) * 2¹⁶` computes `0` and its reversal traps. Both were registered, and both turned a program that computed into one that trapped until the licence was stated.

**What the layers are not allowed to do is disagree about a value.** A `scalar` that reduced a shift count modulo 32 as Wasm does folded `Nat/shl(1, 40)` to `256` while Core computed `2⁴⁰`, and an emitter guard that tested a product `i32` had already truncated let it through: a *changed* value rather than a representable one meeting a limit, which is what the first paragraph forbids. **Core is the oracle**: an erased stage must produce Core's value, decline to produce one, or refuse — never a third value. `curios`'s `a_shift_past_the_carrier_width_answers_the_arithmetic` holds the computing half — `⌊v / 2^k⌋` is zero past the width, not `v >> (k mod 32)` — `overflowing_computations_trap_at_the_backend_boundary` the emitter's refusal, and `a_shift_past_the_widened_intermediate_traps_folded_and_executed` the fold's, which compiles both the tainted and the folded tables — a fixture built on `runtime_traps` alone exercises the emitter and never the fold, and cannot witness the disagreement this paragraph forbids. The clamping and widening that once made a bounded shift agree with Core are gone with the bound: an unbounded shift is the arithmetic, and the only judgement left is whether the answer is worth building.

**Rejected.** Boxing the overflow — `i31ref` with a wide struct fallback — which preserves the range but pays a two-way test at every unbox to buy one bit, `2³¹` to `2³²−1`, that `BigNat` already covers. Declaring the carrier, making `Nat` a 31-bit wrapping natural as OCaml's `int` and Rocq's `Uint63` do, which deletes every check and the 64-bit multiply widening but writes a backend-derived width into a theory deliberately built unbounded, and makes `n + 1 > n` false of the type theorems are stated over. Making `Nat` arbitrary-precision with a small-value fast path, as Lean does, which duplicates `/std/BigNat` and moves a bignum implementation onto the trusted side of the perimeter — where [the soundness perimeter](../../soundness) grades intrinsic signatures *auditable only* and holds their fold laws by value grids rather than proofs — while `BigNat` over `Bits` is proved.
