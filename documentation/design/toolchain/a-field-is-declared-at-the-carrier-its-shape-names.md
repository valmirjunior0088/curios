# A field is declared at the carrier its shape names

**Decision.** Erasure records the erased carrier shape of every kept constructor payload and product entry; the Ersd door resolves that shape to a slot carrier and lays the row out; the Wasm emitter declares the struct field at it. A heap type is keyed by an *identity* — a variant family or a product schema — never by an arity, because that is what makes a field's carrier a fact anyone downstream can read. Slot zero of a family is its tag, stored `i8` and read with `struct.get_u`; a scalar payload is a raw `i32`/`f32`; a list, a closure and a nominal row are declared at their own heap types; everything else stays `(ref null any)`.

**Arity keying is what made a field unsayable.** One `$tuple/N` serves every product and every constructor of that width across the whole module, so the join over any field's stores is the top type *by construction* — not because the compiler failed to notice something, but because the type is shared by unrelated values. No analysis can improve on it and no closed-world pass can refine it: Binaryen's `TypeRefining` narrowed nothing while this held. Keying by identity gives each row a type only its own writers touch, and only then does a recorded shape have somewhere to go.

**One vocabulary, not one per kind.** A variant family and a product schema are the same thing — a nominal row with typed slots — and differ only in whether slot zero is a tag, which the slots themselves now say. So the IR carries `CpsValueExpr::Row`/`CpsIntrinsic::RowGet` over a `CpsRowId` for both, and keeps `Tuple`/`TupleGet` for the *structural* rows that name no schema: the pack a multi-result host call or cell operation builds, and the rebuilds parameter splitting performs. Adding a third pair beside the other two was the alternative and was rejected before it was written, because the cost of a second vocabulary had already been paid once and measured: the specializer's profitability gates asked whether a body contained a `TupleGet`, answered no for every variant when family reads changed spelling, and silently declined every constructor specialization — a 5.8× regression on `state_monad` that a third arm invites again at every match over projections.

**Slots are grouped by carrier, not by field position.** A carrier owns a slot range as wide as the constructor holding the most fields of it, so two constructors agreeing on a carrier share slots and only a *disagreement* costs width. Positional assignment — field `i` on slot `i + 1` — costs nothing and types almost nothing, because a slot shared by disagreeing constructors joins to the top type again. Over the standard library the rule settles 22 slots against positional's 11, for ten slots more across the entire roster, and only three families widen at all. Giving every constructor a disjoint range types four more slots than that and costs eighteen, so it was declined without being built. `slot_layout_probe`'s successor, `family_slot_probe` in `curios`'s codegen tests, holds the figures.

**A carrier that is *sometimes* an immediate has no heap type, and that is one law rather than three exceptions.** A small `Bytes` rides the i31 since the map wall, so a packed field's population is half immediates and half ropes. A family under the `Immediate` encoding has a bare constructor riding the i31 beside siblings that allocate. Neither can be declared at any type, at any width — the same always-never-sometimes line that `FieldShape::Immediate` already drew for the encoding decision. The rope-base `is_subtype` libcalls that survive in emitted code are this law's standing price rather than an open cost: 61 of `spines`' pre-Binaryen 66 base casts target `$rope/bin`, and typing them means giving back the 6.1× the immediate bought.

**A family-typed reference slot is admitted only where the row does not widen.** This is the one case where typing buys instructions with *bytes*, and the criterion is exact rather than a heuristic: lay the row out both ways, keep the typed one iff the widths agree. Seven families qualify; `/std/Map/Node` is the only one that does not, and it is the case this whole line of work was originally aimed at — a fork's children as `(ref null $node)` costs four slots to six, half again as much live memory on the corpus's hottest allocated row, to replace casts that are *already* exact compares against a final type. Weighed against [the heap is sized ahead of its churn](the-heap-is-sized-ahead-of-its-churn.md) and the `trees` finding that live bytes convert to time under an all-live collector, it is declined. A product needs no such test: one writer can never widen a row.

**What it measured.** Typing the slots of family-keyed rows: `spines` −7.7%, `trees` −5 to −7%, `chain` −2.8%, against `parse_digits` +2% — and that split is the mechanism's own explanation, since `parse_digits` is the one program whose families type a tag and nothing else, so it pays the tag's packing without collecting a payload's carrier. Generalizing the same rule to products, closures and free family slots then measured **flat**, for a reason the static counts gave before the stopwatch did: in the unoptimized wasm each product row appears exactly once, as its own declaration, and `struct.new` on one appears zero times. Curios' own specialization and parameter splitting scalarize every product this corpus declares, so only variant families survive to the shipped module and those were already typed. The null result is recorded with its cause in `closure_index_dispatch_measurements`, because a null result that explains itself is what stops the next campaign re-deriving it.

**Rejected — recording a width where an identity was needed.** `FieldShape` first recorded `Product(usize)` and a bare `Family`, which was enough to *count* a census and not enough to spend one. It also became actively wrong the moment products were keyed, since a product value stops being a `$tuple/N`; a recorded shape must be true rather than useful. Carrying the identity meant threading erasure's lowering through field classification, with an inductive's family registered *before* its constructors are walked so that a self-referential declaration finds its own identity instead of recursing — which is the ordinary case for a recursive type, not an edge one.

**Boundaries.** Function signatures stay uniformly `anyref`; a typed calling convention is a successor this decision does not schedule. Rope payloads stay uniform, the minted-parallel-carrier shape having been priced and declined earlier. The `$envr/N` cast at closure dispatch is unchanged where the closure arrives from a parameter rather than a field — typing a closure *field* is sound because a function value that escapes keeps the arity its declared type states, which holds because every pass that could raise one either clones the function or is already gated on non-escape.
