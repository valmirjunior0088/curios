# A measured figure is counted where it can be, and gated only where it repeats

**Decision.** A measurement reads its quantity as a count the work determines wherever one can stand for it — budget units, node looks, allocations, then retired instructions — and as a time only where no count stands for it, on a machine its record names in the state it was taken in. What a measured run depends on beyond its work is fixed for it: the seed the compiler's maps hash with, the machine's state, which is recorded, and the order its variants run in, which interleaves them. Every figure is read over repetitions and every reading is kept. A check that runs at every change asserts only a figure that repeats exactly over every run of one commit; a figure that varies — every time, and a count that moves — is recorded and compared and never asserted, and no tolerance is set on one. Two records of a figure are compared by one method: its readings taken in groups, the unit compared a group's median, and a difference called only where the two records' group medians separate as a rank test over them requires — with five groups each, entirely, which is two spans disjoint — and otherwise said to be no difference proven.

**Rationale.**

- **A count transports, and a time does not.** A count is read off the work and comes out alike on a loaded machine and a quiet one, and on another machine; a time needs a quiet machine and holds for the one it was read on. rustc-perf leads with `instructions:u` because [wall time's noise exceeds the changes it looks for](https://internals.rust-lang.org/t/what-is-perf-rust-lang-org-measuring-and-why-is-instructions-u-the-default/9815), and Lean measures elaboration in [heartbeats, a count of small allocations](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/4.36.0/IO/Timing/), so that its limits hold on any hardware; Curios's budget units and node looks are counts of that kind.
- **A gate on a figure that varies is busywork or blindness.** GHC held noisy metrics in its testsuite behind acceptance windows, and [its own ticket](https://mail.haskell.org/pipermail/ghc-tickets/2018-November/072634.html) records numbers bumped by hand, failures from noise, regressions missed behind wide windows, a baseline drifting a commit at a time, local runs passing trivially, and an escape written into commit messages. Firefox's alerts on noisy figures need [sheriffs triaging them every day](https://firefox-source-docs.mozilla.org/testing/perfdocs/perf-sheriffing.html) and [thresholds set loose enough that noise passes them](https://wiki.mozilla.org/TestEngineering/Performance/Sheriffing/Noise_FAQ). A figure that repeats exactly fails only where the work changed.
- **What is not the work moves the reading.** [Mytkowicz et al.](https://unpaywall.org/10.1145%2F1508244.1508275) showed that the size of the environment and the link order bias a timing enough to reverse a conclusion; CPython's benchmarks [disable hash randomization](https://www.python.org/pipermail/speed/2016-April/000330.html), and rustc hashes with a function that draws no seed; [Lean sees one commit's instructions move](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/270676-lean4/topic/Highly.20nondeterministic.20instruction.20count.20in.20radar.3F.html) with threads and caches keyed on addresses. A reading kept whole shows what moved it; a figure taken once hides it.
- **A comparison that cannot answer "cannot tell" answers wrongly.** Readings taken one after another share the machine's state and are serially correlated, so a test over them as independent over-claims; a group's median, its group interleaving the variants, is what comes nearest independence, and five of them a record can be ranked, a separation complete in five against five being a rank test's p of about 0.008. A threshold written by hand is a belief about the noise where the record measures it.

**Rejected.**

- **Tolerance windows in the suite**, GHC's: every cost the rationale names.
- **A threshold tuned against the history of noise**, as Firefox's and Lean's radar's are, **or a fixed percentage**, as Criterion's ±2%: a judgement of ours where the readings measure the noise themselves.
- **A rank test over every reading**, Go's `benchstat`'s, and Student's t over them: each treats correlated readings as independent, and the second assumes a normal spread a wall clock does not have.
- **A figure read once**, counted or timed: a count moves with a seed, a thread and an address, and one reading cannot show it.
