# One crate is the authority for one external concern

**Decision.** Among the product crates, every external dependency with a surface worth governing has one crate as its authority, and no crate above it spells the dependency's types or macros: `curios-archive` for rkyv, `curios-num` for `num-bigint` and `num-traits`, `curios-package` for TOML, `curios-common` for `stacker` and `sha2`, `curios-tracing` for `tracing`, which its `profile!`, `sample!` and `note!` expand to and whose subscriber interface the recorder in `curios-profile` implements through it, `curios-native` with `curios-cranelift` for `wasmtime`, and `curios` for `tungstenite` and `httparse`, the socket its test serve is driven over and the requests its serves read. An external source of data is held the same way: `curios-unicode` is the authority for the Unicode Character Database, whose files its build script alone reads, at the version the task files pin and a step fetches, and every reader of a character's properties — `curios-common`'s caret, and `/std` through the tables `/sys` bakes from it — asks it. A dependency's version is stated once: one that two or more manifests take is a row of the root manifest's `[workspace.dependencies]`, and one a single manifest takes keeps its pin there; a row says nothing of who may spell the dependency's types, which is its authority's. The tools — `xtask`, `xboard`, `xbench` and build scripts — take what they need; `xtask` alone takes anything from the network, the tools its steps run and the data its steps and the builds read, so it is the one crate that names `ureq`, and `tar` and `flate2`, which unpack what it fetches, and no build script fetches. Rust owns the native host — Binaryen optimization, Wasmtime precompilation and execution, bundling, the CLI and operating-system services — each behind its authority crate.

**Rationale.**

- **Authority is what a crate seals, not where a version is written.** A row states a version once and says nothing about who may spell the dependency's types, and neither did a pin in one manifest, since any crate could write the version again. What keeps a foreign surface in one crate is that the crate seals it, and review holds the rest: a dependency named where another crate seals it is a finding (`.claude/rules/findings.md`), whatever row it took.
- **What makes a facade possible is sealing.** A dependency whose surface is a mechanism — rkyv's derives and `With` adapters, `tracing`'s macros — is absorbed by one crate trivially. One whose surface is a type is wrapped in a type of the owning crate's and kept private: `curios-num`'s `Natural` holds a private `BigUint` behind a `pub(crate)` constructor, so no one above it says `BigUint`. A dependency's faces share its one authority, and what implements a face is a crate apart where not every consumer may reach it: `tracing`'s macros are what every stage writes and its subscriber interface is what one recorder implements, both through `curios-tracing`, which holds nothing else, so the trusted base, the prelude's build and the launcher take the macros and never reach the recorder `curios-profile` holds.
- **A capability that must not reach every consumer is a crate.** `curios` needs Cranelift and the launcher must not have it. Cargo unifies features across a workspace build, so a feature of the launcher's crate is on in every workspace check and off in the launcher's own build, a configuration nothing else compiles; a crate beside it holds what needs the backend, and the launcher's crate has one configuration.
- **Reimplementing the host in Curios would add risk without teaching the language anything**; the language is defined by the stages from parsing through Wasm generation.

**Rejected.**

- **Each crate that takes a dependency naming its own pin**: two crates taking one dependency stated its version twice, which nothing held equal, and a pin in one manifest decided nothing about who may spell the dependency's types, which sealing decides.
- **Re-exporting the foreign type from its owner**, which seals nothing: every crate above it still spells the foreign type.
- **Exempting a dependency whose type spans many crates, or whose features differ by consumer**: `num-bigint` and Wasmtime are such cases, and sealing and a second crate on a shared row cover them.
