# Surface: findings

Small fixes and possible bugs, worked as one pass. Each entry names where, what is wrong, the fix, its check and its size.

- **A source that opens with a byte-order mark is refused with an invisible character as the fault.** A file beginning with U+FEFF reports `expected a term, obtained '﻿'` at `1:1`, the quoted character rendering as nothing; [the reference](../../syntax.md) says nothing of a mark, and editors on some platforms write one. Fix: skip a leading U+FEFF where a source is read, as most compilers do, or refuse it by name. Check: a fixture with a leading mark compiles, or reports the mark by name. Size: quick.
- **`ParserError` names commitment twice and misstates its public surface** (`curios-parse/src/error.rs`). Its `fatal` field is read by the crate-private `is_uncaught`, which every combinator in `repeat.rs` and `parser.rs` consults, and by the public `is_committed`, which nothing in the workspace calls; every document calls the fact commitment. The type's documentation says the error is opaque outside the crate except for `format`, while `report` (`curios-pipeline`, `curios`) and `tagged` (`curios-text`'s `module.rs`) are read outside it, and `offset` is public and read nowhere outside. Fix: one crate-private predicate, `is_committed`, over a field named `committed`; `offset` crate-private; the documentation names the public surface. Check: `curios-parse`'s tests unchanged. Size: quick.
