# Standard library: findings

- **A terminal's size is spelled two ways.** `/std/Tty/Size` names its fields `cols` and `rows` (`curios-std/src/Tty/Size.crs`), and every size `/std/Tui` takes or answers is `w` and `h` — a `View(w, h)`, a `Frame(w, h)`, a pane's `(w, h)`, `Tui/drive`'s — so a program draws its view at `size.cols` by `size.rows`, the one quantity under two pairs of names, where [`/std` is laid out by tier, subject and kind](../../../curios-std/README.md#std-is-laid-out-by-tier-subject-and-kind) gives one meaning one spelling. Uncertain: which spelling stays, a choice for the user. `cols` and `rows` are the terminal's own words and the host's (`curios-abi`'s `TtySize`); `w` and `h` are the type index of every view, and fit a drawing that is no terminal. Fix, once chosen: rename the losing pair through `/std/Tty`, `/std/Tui` and their tests, the host row's fields kept where `curios-abi` names them. Check: `curios-std`'s `tests::tui` and `curios`'s `tests::tui`. Size: one commit.
