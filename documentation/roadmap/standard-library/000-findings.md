# Standard library: findings

- **`Str`'s `pending` states its range once per count** (`Str.crs`). **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether its uses still close by reduction over `Nat/pow`. It spells the range at literal weights for one, two and three continuation bytes, where `Nat/pow(0x40, r)` could state it once. Its documentation defends the literal weights, and the nine uses that reduce it in proofs are what a rewrite puts at risk. A probe answers for the two that take it apart: with `pending` stated once over `Nat/pow(0x40, r)` behind a guard `r <= 2`, `concluded` and `advanced` check as they are written, since each splits on the count before it reads the range and a power at a literal exponent reduces. The other seven, in `Str.crs` and `Str/Valid.crs`, are unprobed, and a count that stays symbolic would not reduce, `Nat/pow` being a recursion by squaring. Fix: state it over `Nat/pow` only if every use still closes by reduction; otherwise leave it. Check: `/std` builds. Size: quick.
- **Three handle wrappers are built by anyone holding a `Handle`.** `Command/Pipe/of_handle`, `Command/Child/of_handle` and `tcp/Socket/of_handle` are public because each type is declared in a module of its own and built by its parent or a sibling — `Command/spawn` builds a `Child` and its pipes, `tcp/Listener` an accepted `Socket` — and a private representation is visible only beneath the module that declares it. So a program wraps a file's or a terminal's handle as a `Socket` or a `Pipe` and reads it through the wrapper's `Async` witnesses, where the type says the handle came from a connection or a spawn. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether the answer is the library's or the language's — each type declared in the module whose subtree holds every builder, as `/std/Async/Probe` is, or a visibility that reaches the parent's subtree, which the surface does not have.
- **What the merge sort and its neighbours cost is not measured.** The machine they landed on was shared, so no figure is stated for any of four changes: `List/sort_by` as a merge sort (`curios-std/src/List/sort_by.crs`), where the sort it replaced stopped at five thousand elements; `Bytes/ord` and `Bits/ord` as walks over both runs (`curios-std/src/Bytes/ord.crs`, `Bits.crs`), which `Str`'s comparisons go through, against the index walks they replaced; a program's start under a stack of one gibibyte (`curios-runtime/src/engine.rs`); and the prelude's build with the sort's proofs in it. Fix: take them on a quiet machine. The sort's protocol: a program builds `n` naturals into a list by appending, in one of three orders — ascending from zero, descending to zero, and the sequence `s ↦ (1103515245 · s + 12345) mod 2³¹` from 42 with each term reduced modulo 1,000,003 — sorts it and prints its length and first element, beside a control that prints the same of the unsorted list; each runs through `cargo run --release --package curios -- run -` at `n` of 100,000 and 1,000,000, the sort and its control interleaved over three readings, reported as a median with its span. The others are read against their parents: the comparison's against the commit before the walks changed, the start's by `hello_world`'s wall time and resident set, the build's by `curios-prelude-archive`'s build profile. Check: the figures, and a decision that rests on one stating it. Size: quick.
- **A step over text costs a type several times a step over bytes.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** what the units are spent on. Over 32 letters at `85da54b97`, `Parse/text/take_while` costs each checker about 48 900 units where `Parse/bytes/take_while` costs about 9 900, and `Parse/text/literal` about 74 900 against 18 400, where a reader that takes no step costs about 8 500 (`parse_primitive_measurements`, `curios/src/tests/reduction.rs`, counted); a `Str` literal's validity, a fold the closed machine runs over the same bytes, costs 69 units a character (`str_literal_cost_measurements`, beside it). A text walk decodes a character, builds the position past it and descends on a proof of accessibility a step (`Str/span` and `Str/follows` over `Str/At/forward`), and nothing measured says which of the three the units are, or whether any is work the erased program does not do. Fix: once that is known. Check: `parse_primitive_measurements`, read again. Size: unknown.
