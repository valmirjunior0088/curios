# Standard library: findings

- **A dispatch on one subject is written as a chain of comparisons.** Nine headless matches compare one value with `==` against two literals or more, beside ranges and other conditions: `/std/Tui/Input/decode`'s `control`, `sent`'s finals, `csi` and `escape`, `/std/Html/parse`'s tag opening, the CSI reader of `/std/Tui/tests/render`, `/std/time/iso8601`'s duration unit, and `/std/Flt`'s `to_str` and `to_hex_str`. Guards are tried in the order they are written, so a literal compared twice is accepted where a dispatch refuses it as unreachable, and [An arm is taken by its patterns, then its guards](../../design/surface/an-arm-is-taken-by-its-patterns-then-its-guards.md) writes a range as a guarded arm after a dispatch's literal arms; no literal of the nine stands after a range that holds it, so the order the chain tries them in is the dispatch's. Fix: each a match on its subject, its literals arms, its ranges and other conditions guarded `_` arms after them (`| _, Nat/in_range(n, 0x20, 0x7E) => …`), and a conjunction's literal its arm with the rest a guard (`| 0, p.intermediate == 0 => …`). Check: `curios test` over `Tui`, `Html`, `time` and `Flt`; the shapes of `control`, the tag opening and the duration unit compile and run as dispatches at `7281dca52`. Size: one commit.
