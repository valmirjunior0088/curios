# Standard library: findings

- **`/std` reads a hexadecimal digit in three places, two of them restating the third.** `Char/of_hex_digit` (`curios-prelude/std/Char.crs`) reads a digit in either case from a `Char`, and JSON, HTML and `Flt/grammar` call it; `Toml/scan/hex_val` (`curios-prelude/std/Toml/scan.crs`) restates its three ranges over a code point, which TOML's escapes and its hexadecimal integers call, and `http/Url`'s `hex_value` (`curios-prelude/std/http/Url.crs`) restates them over a byte, handing back the bound `v < 16` its percent-decoding needs. A fourth case, or a fix to one, is made where its author remembers. Fix: `hex_val` goes, its callers reading `Char/of_hex_digit` — TOML's escape holds a `Char` already, and its integers' `magnitude` (`curios-prelude/std/Toml/numbers.crs`), which holds the character it reads, hands its digit readers the `Char` rather than its code; `hex_value` reads a byte through `Char/of_ascii` and `Char/of_hex_digit`, its bound decided by `Bool/holds` as today. Check: the TOML, JSON and URL suites; `/std`'s build. Size: one commit.
- **A key past three bytes costs a map's insert more than twice a short key's.** `Hash/hash` of a `Nat` of `2^20` or more, of an `Int` as large and of a `Str` past three bytes is a `Bytes` on the heap, built an append a byte, where a shorter one rides the `i31` ([A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md)); a branch reads its digit through `$bytes/read`, and a leaf's key is encoded again where it is compared. Timed at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`), each workload a program in the shape of `spines` built by the release compiler, seven interleaved rounds, the slope between 200,000 and 1,000,000 inserts, median and span over the rounds: `spines`' keys 226.6 ns [221.7, 231.8] an insert, the same keys times a thousand and seven 548.8 [544.8, 563.3], and `"key/"` followed by the number 772.8 [769.6, 787.5]. The routes, none timed: a key read through a prepared form `Key` supplies, a digit reader with a law to `Hash/hash`, which changes a public concept; the emitter keeping a recursion's unchanged `Bytes` argument resolved, as a loop keeps the run it last resolved; or `Hash/hash` building one flat run. Settled by timing each over the map, text keys beside the numbers, and taking the one that costs the fewest declarations.
- **Uncertain: a union, a filter, an intersection and a difference of maps insert entry by entry, where one walk of both tries would build them.** Each is a `Map/fold` over one map's entries inserting into the map it builds (`curios-prelude/std/Map.crs`): `union(a, b)` inserts each of `b`'s entries into `a`, each a walk from `a`'s root that, where a twig is empty or a leaf holds another key, encodes a key it meets to learn where the two part; `filter(m, pred)` builds from the empty map what is already a trie of the shape it builds; and `intersection(a, b)` and `difference(a, b)` are filters of `a` asking `has(b, k)` at each entry, a walk of `b` from its root an entry. `rekey` keeps inserting, its trie's shape being another dictionary's. Okasaki and Gill's merge of two big-endian Patricia trees descends both at once in time linear in the two sizes (*Fast Mergeable Integer Maps*, 1998; Haskell's [`Data.IntMap`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/containers/docs/Data-IntMap.html)); a branch here holds the position it forks at and no prefix, so a merge reads a twig's prefix off its first key's encoding, as `Node/insert` reads where a key parts from a branch, and two branches at one position merge twig by twig. The laws are stated (`Map/try_get/of_union`, `of_filter`, `of_intersection`, `of_difference`), and extensionality (`Map/try_get/injective`) makes any two implementations that meet them one map. Not yet known: whether a program spends its time there — `Map/union`'s one consumer in `/std` is `Set/union`, and no workload of `xbench` or program of `programs/` calls either. Settled by a program a union's insertions dominate, then a merge with its soundness built in its own recursion, as `Node/insert` builds its outcome's, and a filter that empties a branch's twigs without inserting, the four laws proved of them and the program timed against the fold; or by dropping this entry. Size: unknown.
