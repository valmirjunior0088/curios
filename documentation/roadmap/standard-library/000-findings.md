# Standard library: findings

- **What the merge sort and its neighbours cost is not measured.** The machine they landed on was shared, so no figure is stated for any of four changes: `List/sort_by` as a merge sort (`curios-std/src/List/sort_by.crs`), where the sort it replaced stopped at five thousand elements; `Bytes/ord` and `Bits/ord` as walks over both runs (`curios-std/src/Bytes/ord.crs`, `Bits.crs`), which `Str`'s comparisons go through, against the index walks they replaced; a program's start under a stack of one gibibyte (`curios-runtime/src/engine.rs`); and the prelude's build with the sort's proofs in it. Fix: take them on a quiet machine. The sort's protocol: a program builds `n` naturals into a list by appending, in one of three orders — ascending from zero, descending to zero, and the sequence `s ↦ (1103515245 · s + 12345) mod 2³¹` from 42 with each term reduced modulo 1,000,003 — sorts it and prints its length and first element, beside a control that prints the same of the unsorted list; each runs through `cargo run --release --package curios -- run -` at `n` of 100,000 and 1,000,000, the sort and its control interleaved over three readings, reported as a median with its span. The others are read against their parents: the comparison's against the commit before the walks changed, the start's by `hello_world`'s wall time and resident set, the build's by `curios-prelude-archive`'s build profile. Check: the figures, and a decision that rests on one stating it. Size: quick.
- **A step over text costs a type several times a step over bytes, and half of it is the position past built again.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** what of that the erased program is spared. Over 32 letters at `85da54b97`, `Parse/text/take_while` costs each checker about 48 900 units where `Parse/bytes/take_while` costs about 9 900, and `Parse/text/literal` about 74 900 against 18 400, where a reader that takes no step costs about 8 500 (`parse_primitive_measurements`, `curios/src/tests/reduction.rs`, counted); a `Str` literal's validity, a fold the closed machine runs over the same bytes, costs 69 units a character (`str_literal_cost_measurements`, beside it). A text walk decodes a character, builds the position past it and descends on a proof of accessibility a step (`Str/span` and `Str/follows` over `Str/At/forward`). Counted at `7d28c0209`, the units the elaborator spends on a claim, proved by `Eq/refl()`, that a walk over a literal of `n` letters counts `n`, read at 32 and at 64 letters and divided by the 32 between them, each the `budget::consumed` samples of the claim's declaration in the stream a profiling build files for `wonder diagnostics`: 1,192 a character for `Str/span(s, At/start(s), (_c) => true).past.offset`, 1,097 for a count by `At/forward` over `At/try_next`, 960 for one over `At/skip` behind `at.offset < Bytes/len(s.bytes)`, 356 for `Nat/descend` counting up to `n` over the measure `n - k`, and 120 for the structural `plain(k + 1) = 1 + plain(k)`. So decoding is about 140 of a character's units and the span's own answer about 95; the descent, its comparison with it, is at most 356; and about 600 is `skip` building the position past, the rest cut off (`Str/after`), its first character's width read (`hop`) and a position of the whole text built from the offset (`of_after`). Not known: how much of that 600 the erased program is spared, a position's bound and a cut's validity being proofs it does not hold, and whether a walk could hand on the rest it has cut. Fix: once that is known. Check: `parse_primitive_measurements`, read again. Size: unknown.
- **A listener drops the status of making its address reusable, where its documentation promises the address is.** **Against stated reasoning, and the user's to decide.** `bind_listen` (`curios-std/src/tcp/Listener.crs`) calls `socket_set_reuseaddr(h, true)` and binds its status to `_`, and `the_failures_std_absorbs_by_design_are_absorbed` (`curios/src/tests/host/outcome_tests.rs`) states that as designed: "a listener's `SO_REUSEADDR` is a courtesy the bind does not depend on", beside a kill and a socket's finish. Against it stand `listen`'s own documentation, "the address reusable so a restart does not wait out the previous socket", which a dropped failure leaves unkept with nothing said to the caller, and [the decision](../../design/runtime/a-host-operation-is-a-foreign-declaration-whose-types-are-its-whole-contract.md), which has `/std` read every reply and rejects a failure read as a success; `Child/kill` says at its site why its status is dropped, and this site says nothing. Read at `7d3487f2f`, the test's sentence at `66b39ba87`. Fix, if the reasoning is judged to fail: the status is read as `socket_bind`'s is, the socket closed and the failure raised, and the test's sentence loses its first clause; otherwise the site says why the status is dropped and `listen`'s documentation says the address is made reusable where the host allows. Check: a test under the scripted host, the row answering a failure. Size: quick.
