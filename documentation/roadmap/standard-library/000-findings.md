# Standard library: findings

- **A key past three bytes costs a map's insert more than twice a short key's.** `Hash/hash` of a `Nat` of `2^20` or more, of an `Int` as large and of a `Str` past three bytes is a `Bytes` on the heap, built an append a byte, where a shorter one rides the `i31` ([A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md)); a branch reads its digit through `$bytes/read`, and a leaf's key is encoded again where it is compared. Timed at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`), each workload a program in the shape of `spines` built by the release compiler, seven interleaved rounds, the slope between 200,000 and 1,000,000 inserts, median and span over the rounds: `spines`' keys 226.6 ns [221.7, 231.8] an insert, the same keys times a thousand and seven 548.8 [544.8, 563.3], and `"key/"` followed by the number 772.8 [769.6, 787.5]. The routes, none timed: a key read through a prepared form `Key` supplies, a digit reader with a law to `Hash/hash`, which changes a public concept; the emitter keeping a recursion's unchanged `Bytes` argument resolved, as a loop keeps the run it last resolved; or `Hash/hash` building one flat run. Settled by timing each over the map, text keys beside the numbers, and taking the one that costs the fewest declarations.
- **Uncertain: a union, a filter, an intersection and a difference of maps insert entry by entry, where one walk of both tries would build them.** Each is a `Map/fold` over one map's entries inserting into the map it builds (`curios-prelude-build/std/Map.crs`): `union(a, b)` inserts each of `b`'s entries into `a`, each a walk from `a`'s root that, where a twig is empty or a leaf holds another key, encodes a key it meets to learn where the two part; `filter(m, pred)` builds from the empty map what is already a trie of the shape it builds; and `intersection(a, b)` and `difference(a, b)` are filters of `a` asking `has(b, k)` at each entry, a walk of `b` from its root an entry. `rekey` keeps inserting, its trie's shape being another dictionary's. Okasaki and Gill's merge of two big-endian Patricia trees descends both at once in time linear in the two sizes (*Fast Mergeable Integer Maps*, 1998; Haskell's [`Data.IntMap`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/containers/docs/Data-IntMap.html)); a branch here holds the position it forks at and no prefix, so a merge reads a twig's prefix off its first key's encoding, as `Node/insert` reads where a key parts from a branch, and two branches at one position merge twig by twig. The laws are stated (`Map/try_get/of_union`, `of_filter`, `of_intersection`, `of_difference`), and extensionality (`Map/try_get/injective`) makes any two implementations that meet them one map. Not yet known: whether a program spends its time there — `Map/union`'s one consumer in `/std` is `Set/union`, and no workload of `xbench` or program of `programs/` calls either. Settled by a program a union's insertions dominate, then a merge with its soundness built in its own recursion, as `Node/insert` builds its outcome's, and a filter that empties a branch's twigs without inserting, the four laws proved of them and the program timed against the fold; or by dropping this entry. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: `Vec`'s functions that only read may restate `List`'s over a vector's list.** `List/get(r.list, 2)` over `r: Vec(Nat, 3)` compiles, its bound proved from `r.counted`, and `List/get(r.list, 3)` is refused naming the same facts, so `Vec/get` restates `List/get` (`curios-prelude-build/std/Vec.crs`); what `Vec` alone holds is the refinement and the functions that produce a new count. Not yet known: which of the other readers, `first` and `fold` among them, `List`'s serve as well, and whether a destructuring of a vector's list, which waits on coverage reading the facts in scope ([Surface: findings](../surface/000-findings.md)), changes the answer. Settled by each reader set beside its `List` twin over `.list`. Size: unknown.
- **`Io/all` and `Try/all` answer a `List` that states no length, though it is the length of the list they were given.** A caller reading the answers of a list written out by position takes an `Option` for each, or matches a case the list cannot be in, where `all(@A: Type, actions: List(Io(A))) -> Io(Vec(A, List/len(actions)))` lets `Vec/get` read each answer with its bound discharged where the call is. The definition elaborates — `promised` and `awaited` building the count as they recurse, `Eq/subst` carrying it to the result — but the signature is refused at every caller that maps an action that loops, `/std/Tui`'s `running` and a fail-fast `Try/all` among them, by the reach the theory findings file's entry "A type position reaches every definition it names" states. Settled by that signature for `Io/all` and `Try/all` once that entry is, with a test reading `Io/all` over a list written out through `Vec/get`. Size: one commit, after the theory entry's.
- **State that outlives its user is a capacity-one channel taken and put back at each site, an invariant only prose holds, while three decisions say no rewritable cell exists.** `Io/Reader`'s `Held`, `Tui/Session`'s `Kept`, a scope's count of live children and its deferred releases, and the cell each side of a `/std/Channel` wakes its waiters by (`Woken`) are each a queue of one slot that every user takes, reads and puts back, defaulting an empty slot and dropping the push's outcome. The `Reader` decision of `curios-prelude-build/README.md` states what keeps this sound, "nothing between a take and a put suspending", and nothing holds it: a counter kept this way and bumped twice from 10 reads 12, and reads 1 once one bump yields between its take and its put, the other having read the empty slot as its default and the late put having met a full one, neither reported. `documentation/design/standard-library/a-wait-is-a-suspension-and-only-the-host-waits.md`, the `Reader` decision and `curios-emit/README.md` each reject a rewritable cell as a second mutation vocabulary, on the reasoning of `a8ab05555` that "a mutable one-slot box adds no capability beyond a capacity-one channel", which is the capability these sites use; the principle that commit's decision rested on, "ownership follows values", gave way a site at a time (`f71958527`, `f8fb123e8`, `3344af44b`, `232336e3e`), since a branch cancelled where it waits loses what it holds and nothing holds a value to one use. Settled by stating the rule the sound sites already follow, that what is shared changes in one step that cannot wait, and holding it by a type: one place in `/std/internal` over the queue, made with its value and changed only by a pure function of it, each site written over it, and the decisions' rejections restated as of a second primitive. Not yet known: whether the place is better a primitive of its own, which a prototype measured against the ring decides, and whether `Io/read_all`, `Io/copy` and a resent `write`, which hold their progress across a wait, should keep it in one. Size: unknown, past two commits.
- **Uncertain: a write dropped where it waits after a short write leaves its handle open to more writes, which then follow the cut bytes as though nothing was cut.** `Io/write` over a handle loops a short write until the bytes are sent (`curios-prelude-build/std/internal/Handle.crs`), and a `select` or `timeout` that drops it at one of its waits leaves the peer holding part of a message; the next write on the handle sends after it, and nothing says the stream is cut, where `Write.write`'s documentation is the only guard. Peers meet it two ways: by refusing the resource after a cut, as a Java thread interrupted in I/O on an `InterruptibleChannel` closes the channel ([`InterruptibleChannel`](https://docs.oracle.com/javase/jp/8/docs/api/java/nio/channels/InterruptibleChannel.html)) and Go code treats a timed-out write as fatal to the write half ([golang/go#46977](https://golang.org/issue/46977)), and by shielding a region from cancellation until it ends, as Trio's `CancelScope(shield=True)`, Kotlin's `NonCancellable` and Eio's `Cancel.protect` do ([AnyIO](https://anyio.readthedocs.io/en/stable/cancellation.html), [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/api/kotlinx.coroutines/kotlinx-coroutines-core/kotlinx.coroutines/-non-cancellable/)). Not yet known, a design question: whether a write's suspension, whose cancel runs where it is dropped, marks its handle's write half broken so the next write refuses naming the cut, or whether `Io` offers a shield under which a dropped branch runs its shielded part to its end before it is dropped, a deadline then answering late. Size: unknown.
