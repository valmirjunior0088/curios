# Standard library: findings

- **`/std/Map` and `/std/Set` combine two of a type by a union and nothing else.** `Map/union` and `Set/union` (`curios-prelude/std/Map.crs`, `curios-prelude/std/Set.crs`) are the one operation over two; the entries of one map whose keys the other has, or lacks, are a `Map/filter` over `Map/has` a program writes itself, with no law stating what it answers. Fix: `Map/intersection(a, b)` and `Map/difference(a, b)` as that filter over `a`, each keeping `a`'s values as a filter does, and `Set/intersection` and `Set/difference` over them; their laws are corollaries of `Map/try_get/of_filter`: `try_get(intersection(a, b), k)` is `try_get(a, k)` where `b` has `k` and none otherwise, `difference` the converse, and `Set/has` of each the conjunction and the difference of the two `has`. A walk of both tries is [A union walks two tries once](200-a-union-walks-two-tries-once.md)'s, held to these laws. Check: the laws proved beside `Map/try_get` and `Set/has`, and a test of each operation, the `Set` forms among them, in `/std/Map/tests`, folded and executed. Size: one commit.
