# Standard library: findings

- **A key past three bytes costs a map's insert more than twice a short key's.** `Hash/hash` of a `Nat` of `2^20` or more, of an `Int` as large and of a `Str` past three bytes is a `Bytes` on the heap, built an append a byte, where a shorter one rides the `i31` ([A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md)); a branch reads its digit through `$bytes/read`, and a leaf's key is encoded again where it is compared. Timed at `5bccb9ebd`, each workload a program in the shape of `spines` built by the release compiler, seven interleaved rounds, the slope between 200,000 and 1,000,000 inserts, median and span over the rounds: `spines`' keys 226.6 ns [221.7, 231.8] an insert, the same keys times a thousand and seven 548.8 [544.8, 563.3], and `"key/"` followed by the number 772.8 [769.6, 787.5]. The routes, none timed: a key read through a prepared form `Key` supplies, a digit reader with a law to `Hash/hash`, which changes a public concept; the emitter keeping a recursion's unchanged `Bytes` argument resolved, as a loop keeps the run it last resolved; or `Hash/hash` building one flat run. Settled by timing each over the map, text keys beside the numbers, and taking the one that costs the fewest declarations.
