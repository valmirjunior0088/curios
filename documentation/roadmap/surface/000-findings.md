# Surface: findings

- **`()`, the tuple of no fields, is no pattern, so `/std/Program`'s witness at `{}` cannot take its argument apart.** `execute(_) = Io/pure(())` (`curios-prelude/std/Program.crs`) ignores the `()` it is handed, and no pattern states it: `parse_tuple_pattern` and `parse_tuple_match_pattern` (`curios-surface/src/parse/patterns.rs`) read a field and a comma, or one labeled field, though each is the mirror of the tuple literal, which reads `()`. So `let () = u;`, `(()) => 1`, `f((): {})`, `let (a, ()) = p;` and `match u | () => 1 end` are syntax errors, where `documentation/language.md` gives the binders of a `let`, a lambda and a function's parameters tuple patterns, and `()` is the tuple of no fields. The rejected "pattern for `()`" of [An arm is taken by its patterns, then its guards](../../design/surface/an-arm-is-taken-by-its-patterns-then-its-guards.md) is a way for Core to hold a match with no head, not a pattern a reader writes, so it bears on neither parser. Fix: `()` in both parsers, its lowering holding its binder at `{}`, since a tuple pattern with fields is held to a tuple by its projections and one with none by nothing, so `let () = 3;` stays refused. Check: `let () = u;`, `(()) => 1` and `match u | () => 1 end` accepted by both checkers, `let () = 3;` refused, and each printed back as written. One commit.
- **A field's definition sugar takes a name at each parameter, where a lambda takes a pattern, so `execute((a, b)) = …` and `execute(()) = …` are refused.** `label(params) = value` in a tuple, a structure literal or a witness reads each parameter with `parse_func_param`, a binder (`parse_tuple_field_prefix`, `curios-surface/src/parse/tuples.rs`, which `parse_witness_field` in `top_level.rs` reads), while a lambda reads `parse_func_pattern_param`, a pattern, and the sugar lowers to a lambda. So `(g((a, b)) = a + b)`, `S { g((a, b)) = a + b }` and a witness's `execute((a, b)) = …` over `{Nat, Nat}` are syntax errors, and `/std/Program`'s witness at `{}` cannot write `execute(()) = Io/pure(())` in place of `execute(_)` even once `()` is a pattern (above). `documentation/language.md` says the sugar binds as a lambda does and then lists a binder, and `parse_func_pattern_param`'s comment calls the sugar single-name-only; neither states a reason. Fix: the sugar reads a lambda's pattern parameter, `language.md`'s sentence states the patterns, and `Program.crs`'s witness writes `execute(())`. Check: `(g((a, b)) = a + b)` and the witness's `execute((a, b))` accepted by both checkers, and `Program.crs`'s tests. One commit, after the entry above.
- **A witness entry whose parameter list does not parse is refused at its label, as what the body's list expects next.** `execute(x y) = …` reports ``expected: `..`, obtained: `execute` `` at `execute`, and a later entry ``expected: `}` `` there: `parse_witness_field` (`curios-surface/src/parse/top_level.rs`) reads `parse_tuple_field_prefix`, which fails recoverably so that a tuple's positional field can backtrack, and a witness entry, which has no positional form, backtracks to the body's list instead. [A diagnostic spells what its reader can write](../../design/tools/a-diagnostic-spells-what-its-reader-can-write.md) words a refusal where the parser has read what discriminates the mistake. Fix: a witness entry commits once its label and `(` are read, so the refusal stands at the parameter the list cannot read. Check: a `curios-surface` refusal test of `execute(x y) = …` reporting at `y`. One commit.
