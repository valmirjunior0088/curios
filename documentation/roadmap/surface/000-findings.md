# Surface: findings

- **Uncertain: a range of literals as a pattern.** A dispatch on one natural over ranges is written as a match with no head whose arms are `Nat/in_range` guards, its subject repeated in each: the byte classes of `/std/Str/utf8`, `/std/Str/Valid` and `/std/Tui/Input/decode`, the hex digits of `/std/Char`, `/std/http/read`, `/std/http/Url`, `/std/Toml/numbers` and `/std/Toml/scan`, a control sequence's final byte in `/std/Tui/tests/render`, and the classifier of each `programs/walk_mirror_*.crs`. A pattern names one literal ([Natural-number dispatch](../../language.md#natural-number-dispatch)). Not known: whether `| 0xC2..=0xDF =>` reads better than the guard it replaces; what the matrix compiler splits on for one, a `Switch` holding single keys; and what its arm learns, which `/std/Str/utf8`'s proofs read as `Bool/Holds(Nat/in_range(c, lo, hi))` today. Settled by: `/std/Str/utf8` and `/std/http/read` written in a candidate spelling, with the Core a range comes to and the fact its arm holds stated beside them, and a decision on whether the spelling is wanted. Size unknown until then.
- **Uncertain: a `let` whose pattern can fail, with arms for what it refuses.** A chain of steps that each fail to an exit of their own is a staircase of two-arm matches, one level deeper at each step: `parse` in `/std/Cli/parse` (`curios-prelude/std/Cli/parse.crs:242`, four steps) and the two scope walks of `/std/Toml/build` (`curios-prelude/std/Toml/build.crs:170` and `:187`, three steps each). Guards flatten such a chain only where its steps exit alike, as the status line of `/std/http/syntax` does; a step with an exit of its own needs an arm of its own, which Rust writes `let … else` and Swift `guard let … else`. Not known: whether three sites pay for a form, how it reads beside a `!` region that already stops at the first failure, and what the binders after a refused step are in scope for. Settled by: the three sites written in a candidate spelling, `let some(scope) = … | none() => …;`, and as a `!` chain over `/std/Result`, and a decision on which, if either, reads better than the staircase. Size unknown until then.
