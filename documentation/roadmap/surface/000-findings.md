The report printer writes a held place by that form, and lowering reads the form and no spelling. It reaches the tree, the printer, the lowering, the formatter and `editors/grammar` with its committed `src/`. Check: a fixture for each of the two programs, `a_plain_argument_is_never_a_placeholder` restated, and the grammar's tests. Size: one pass over the surface, supervised, taken once the typed-patterns branch has landed: both reshape how the tree holds `_`.
- **A lambda's binder spelled as a type in scope is reported as a mismatch that names neither the binder nor what it hides.** Against `(@n: Nat, x: Nat) -> Nat`, `(@Nat, x) => let y: Nat = x; y` reports `type mismatch`, `inferred: Nat`, `expected: Type`, its caret under the annotation of the `let`, probed at `3258d8744`: after `@` a lambda reads a binder ([Plicity is part of function identity](../../design/theory/plicity-is-part-of-function-identity.md)), so `@Nat` binds a variable that hides `/std/Nat`, and the report shows that variable's type under the spelling the reader takes for the type itself.
