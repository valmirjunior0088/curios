# A witness reads its own superclasses as the table's

[Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md) holds that "a superclass is the table's, whatever a field's type names", and that it reads alike through the table and through a witness (`a_superclass_reads_alike_through_the_table_and_through_a_witness`). Inside a lone witness's body, read through the witness's own entry, it does not: a law stated over the witness's superclasses, or over a method the witness writes without itself, is refused where its proof recurses through the entry, written or derived, while the same law in a member of an `and` group is accepted.

## What is wrong today

Each is probed at `2cbcf388a`.

- **A written key recursing through its own entry is refused.** Over `induct Tree: pub Type | leaf(Nat) | node(Tree, Tree) end` with `Eql`, `Ord` and `Hash` derived, a written `satisfy Hash/Key(Tree)` whose `node` arm hands `Hash/Key/then` the part `Hash/Key/mono(l, s)` is refused there: expected `Ord/ord(@Tree, use x0.1, x1, x2)`, the entry's own superclass slot, against the registered `Ord(Tree)`'s comparison. A written key recurses today only through a lemma of its own.
- **A derived key over such a family is refused alike**, directly and through another key's type: `satisfy Hash/Key(Tree) { .. }` at `Hash/Key/then`'s `part`, and over `node(Box(Tree))`, under `Box`'s derived key, at `Hash/Key/last`'s.
- **A method that names no member is not read through the entry either.** `satisfy Ord(Two) { ord = reversed, lt(x, y) = Ordering/is_lt(Ord/ord(x, y)), …, is_lt(_, _) = Eq/refl(), … }` is refused at `is_lt`: the entry's `ord` is not `reversed` there.
- **The lone witness reads its entry as no group's member is read.** `elaborate_module_let` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) assumes the witness's local at its type and reads nothing through it, where `elaborate_members` defines each member's local as its slot, read through once the member's turn finds it known to terminate (`Turns`, `curios-elab/src/totality.rs`), a conversion that meets a slot not yet released waiting on it, as `curios_analysis::known_to_terminate` states both checkers read a member. So `satisfy Law(Tree) { … } and Law(Forest) { … }` over a mutual `Tree` and `Forest`, each law recursing through its own entry and its sibling's over a superclass `Base`, is accepted, where the same law over `Tree` alone is refused. The certifier reads both alike, a member known to terminate unfolding from its turn on (`check_group`, `curios-cert/src/certifier/module.rs`): the lone witness's Curios/Core recursing through its own entry is certified today, and its twin whose recursion does not descend refused at the stuck projection.
- **A read through a member not known to terminate is refused in words the program never wrote.** The `and` group above, `Law(Forest)`'s `same(f)` calling `Law/same(f)` at `f` itself, is refused at `Law(Tree)`'s `Law/same(f)` as `type mismatch`, expected `x0.0.0(x1)`, and nothing says that `Law(Forest)`'s recursion does not descend, so that its entry reads as nothing: `Turns` withholds the slot, and the drain reports the conversion blocked on it as any mismatch. A lone witness meets the same words once it reads its entry as a group's member does.
- **A rejection rests on the gap.** [Equality is decided where a decider exists and is `Eq` everywhere, joined by one law](../../design/theory/equality-is-decided-where-a-decider-exists-and-is-eq-everywhere-joined-by-one-law.md) rejects an order's tie stated decided because "inside the witness of a family nested through a container that superclass is the one of the witness under definition, so the container's `==` written in the body and the one in the law's type do not convert there".

## Goal

A witness that resolves through its own entry is a group of one while its body elaborates: its entry is its slot, read through once its turn finds it known to terminate, as every member of a group is read in both checkers. A law stated over a superclass converts with the registered witness's method wherever the body reads it through the entry, and a key derives over a family that holds itself, directly or through another key's type. No rule of the certifier changes.

## Settled

- The rule is the turns': one reading of a member under way, a lone witness's and a group member's, the elaborator's and the certifier's alike. A member not known to terminate stands for nothing, its superclass slots included.
- Rejected, a field rule, under which a projection of the entry at a field whose term names no member reads as that term whatever the recursion: beyond the turns it admits only a witness whose recursion does not descend, which no lawful witness is, at the cost of a rule the certifier must admit, argued under `documentation/design/soundness/`, and a reading of a lone witness apart from its group's.
- Rejected, a derived body recursing through a local function of its own, as Lean's deriving handlers define the bare functions in a `mutual` block and build the instances from them: it leaves the written key refused, gives derivation two recursion shapes and refuses a payload nested through another key's type. A derived witness keeps one shape: every derived law recurses through its own entry.
- `own_methods_read` keeps its reader: a body that reads its own methods only where they name no member names itself nowhere and is no knot at run time, which the turns do not decide.
- A superclass slot's resolution never passes through the entry: a concept is not its own superclass, and a premise is strictly smaller than its witness's head, so the loop GHC's superclass invariant refuses cannot be written. A slot that names a member is withheld with it.

## Stages

1. **A read through a member not known to terminate names it.** A conversion parked blocked on a withheld slot that fails is reported as a read through that member, naming it and that its recursion is not known to descend. Check: the `and` group above refused naming `Law(Forest)` and its recursion, beside its twin whose recursion descends, accepted, in `curios`'s `tests::concepts`; mutation-checked.
2. **The lone witness is read as a group of one.** Its local is defined as its slot, its turn taken over its body, and what it parked retried once the slot is filled, before `own_methods_read`. Check: the written key over `Tree` and the written `Ord(Two)` above accepted, in `curios/src/tests/derive/key.rs` and `curios/src/tests/derive/ord.rs`; `satisfy Hash/Key(Tree) { .. }` over the `Tree` above and over a family whose payload is a `Box(Tree)`, each walking a map in `Ord` order, in `curios/src/tests/derive/key.rs`; the lone `Law` twin whose recursion does not descend refused, naming the member; each mutation-checked; `/std` built, each record its images move named.
