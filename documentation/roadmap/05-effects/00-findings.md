# Effects: findings

Small fixes and possible bugs, worked as one pass. Each entry names where, what is wrong, the fix, its check and its size.

- **`/std/Async/Channel`'s nonblocking faces merge the outcomes the concurrency decision keeps apart.** [Only a fiber waits](../../design/effects/only-a-fiber-waits.md) has a channel's nonblocking push and take report `Push` (taken, full or closed) and `Take(A)` (an item, empty or ended), and rejects `Bool` and `Option(A)` for them, which merge outcomes a later observation cannot recover atomically. `/sys/Channel`'s operations keep that rule; `try_send -> Io(Bool)` and `try_recv -> Io(Option(A))`, the only nonblocking operations a `Sender` or a `Receiver` offers, merge the outcomes again. A program can name the outcomes, but only where they are of no use to it: the module's `pub use /sys/{Channel};` mounts the whole of `/sys/Channel` as `Channel/Channel`, so `Channel/Channel/Push` resolves where `Channel/Push` does not, and `Channel/Channel/new` and `Channel/Channel/push` hand a program a raw channel that answers `taken` and then `full` — one that `send`, `recv` and the two waits refuse, since they take the sealed ends. The face that parks has no three-way answer, and the face with one cannot park. The blocking faces keep the distinction in their own terms: `send` answers `false` only when closed, and `recv` `none` only when ended. No caller needs what the merge loses today: `Signal/notify` ignores `try_send`'s answer and never closes its channel, `Signal/offer` polls with `try_recv` on that same channel, `Tui/run`'s `burst` stops on either `none` and its loop's `recv` then tells an ended queue apart, and `tests::scheduler` asserts `false` on a full channel and `none` on drained ones. Fix: `try_send` and `try_recv` answer `Push` and `Take(A)`, which `/std/Async/Channel` re-exports by name, and the callers match the outcome they read, `Signal/offer` mapping a `Take` to the `Option` an offer commits on. `Push` carries no value back, where [tokio's `TrySendError`](https://docs.rs/tokio/latest/tokio/sync/mpsc/error/enum.TrySendError.html) returns the one it refused, since a caller here still holds what it offered. The re-export narrows to the type and the two outcomes, as `/std/Bytes` re-exports `let Bytes`, so the raw operations stop being a second public face; if the raw channel is meant to be public, the decision says so and `Async/Wait` takes one. The alternative to all of it, that the decision governs `/sys` alone and a library face may merge, is a revision of the decision that says so. Check: `tests::scheduler` asserts `full` apart from `closed`, and `empty` apart from `ended`, and a fixture that `Channel/Channel/new` resolves to nothing. Size: quick.
