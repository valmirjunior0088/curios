# Effects: findings

Small fixes and possible bugs, worked as one pass. Each entry names where, what is wrong, the fix, its check and its size.

- **`/std/Async/Channel`'s nonblocking faces merge the outcomes the concurrency decision keeps apart.** [Only a fiber waits](../../design/effects/only-a-fiber-waits.md) has a channel's nonblocking push and take report `Push` (taken, full or closed) and `Take(A)` (an item, empty or ended), and rejects `Bool` and `Option(A)` for them, which merge outcomes a later observation cannot recover atomically. `/sys/Channel`'s operations keep that rule; `try_send -> Io(Bool)` and `try_recv -> Io(Option(A))`, the only nonblocking operations a program reaches, merge the outcomes again, and `Sender` and `Receiver` seal the channel, so no program can name `Push` or `Take` (`binding not found: Push`). The blocking faces keep the distinction in their own terms: `send` answers `false` only when closed, and `recv` `none` only when ended. No caller needs what the merge loses today: `Signal/notify` ignores `try_send`'s answer and never closes its channel, `Signal/offer` polls with `try_recv` on that same channel, `Tui/run`'s `burst` stops on either `none` and its loop's `recv` then tells an ended queue apart, and `tests::scheduler` asserts `false` on a full channel and `none` on drained ones. Fix: `try_send` and `try_recv` answer `Push` and `Take(A)`, which `/std/Async/Channel` re-exports from `/sys/Channel`, and the callers match the outcome they read; the alternative, that the decision governs `/sys` alone and a library face may merge, is a revision of the decision that says so. Check: `tests::scheduler` asserts `full` apart from `closed`, and `empty` apart from `ended`. Size: quick.
