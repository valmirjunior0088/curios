# Runtime: findings

- **A TLS stream that ends without its closure alert reads as the end of the stream.** `tls_read_outcome` answers `rustls`'s `UnexpectedEof` as the stream's end (`curios-runtime/src/os_host.rs`), "since a length-framed protocol notices a truncation itself". RFC 9112 §9.8 counts a reply framed by the connection's close as whole only where the alert arrived, so a reply cut short by whoever holds the wire is handed on as whole. Fix: `tls_read_outcome` answers a missing alert as `tls`, once `/std/http`'s client reads a reply by its framing and stops where it ends ([HTTP framing](../08-standard-library/01-http-framing.md)'s last stage): a client that reads every reply to the close would fail on each peer that skips the alert. Check: over a loopback peer that ends its stream with no alert, a read answers `tls`. Size: quick.
