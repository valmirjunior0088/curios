# Runtime: findings

- **A guest recursion a few thousand calls deep traps, and nothing sets or states the depth a program may rely on.** `shared_engine` (`curios-runtime/src/engine.rs`) sets no `max_wasm_stack`, so a program runs on [Wasmtime's default](https://docs.wasmtime.dev/api/wasmtime/struct.Config.html#method.max_wasm_stack) of 512 KiB, and a call that is no tail call takes a frame of it. Counted at `bf21a9065`: `List/range(0, 5000)` answers and `List/range(0, 6000)` stops on `wasm trap: call stack exhausted`; `List/take` and `List/zip` stop at 8,000 elements; `List/sort` sorts 4,500 descending elements and stops at 5,000; and `Async/join_all` over fibers that each `sleep` runs 2,000 and stops at 8,000. Each is a recursion as deep as its input, the shape `/std` writes wherever a proof follows the recursion: a list built under a cons, an `Io` walk that binds its tail. `curios-ersd`'s monoid rebase (`optimize/rebase.rs`) makes such a recursion a loop where the deferred step is a registered monoid, which `curios`'s `tests::runtime::loop_tests::arena_deferred_context_recursion_is_stack_safe_at_depth` holds at 60,000, and it registers no row that builds a sequence; [A lowering names the elimination it performs](../../design/compilation/a-lowering-names-the-elimination-it-performs.md) says a sequence recursion left unrebased overflows "at sixty thousand elements", ten times the depth counted here, a figure no test retakes. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** which layer answers. Fix: the launcher gives the guest a stack it chose, on a thread that has it, and the reference states the depth a program may rely on, which covers every shape, an `Io` walk and a tree's recursion among them; or the rebase gains the row for a recursion under a cons, the transformation OCaml calls [tail modulo cons](https://ocaml.org/manual/5.3/tail_mod_cons.html) and Koka generalizes as [tail recursion modulo context](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/tail-recursion-modulo-context-an-equational-approach-2/), which makes a list's builder a loop and leaves the other shapes as deep as they are. Check: a corpus test holds `List/range`, `List/zip` and `Async/join_all` at 100,000, and the decision's figure is one a test retakes. Size: unknown.
