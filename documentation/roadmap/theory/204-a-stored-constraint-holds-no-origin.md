# A stored constraint holds no origin

Working specification for keeping where a level constraint was raised with the solver that raised it, and out of the scheme a declaration stores. A report about a level conflict is made while one declaration is elaborated, from what its constraints say of where each was raised; the same record is archived with every scheme, read by no report afterwards, and kept out of a stored form field by field wherever a declaration is closed. With the record the live solver's alone, a constraint an argument's check raises can say whose parameter it was raised for, which is what a conflict that argument closes does not say today.

## What this builds on

- **One type serves the live solver and the stored scheme.** `UniverseConstraint` (`curios-core/src/universe.rs`) is `lower`, `upper` and an `origin`, archived. It is the element of `UniverseContext::constraints`, which every declaration that closes over levels stores, and of the elaborator's store (`ConstraintStore`, `curios-elab/src/universe_solver/constraints.rs`). `UniverseConstraintOrigin` holds an `extent`, a `kind`, a `declaration`, a `binder` and what a use `instantiated`.
- **A conflict's path is the elaborator's.** Consistency is decided where a declaration's levels settle and not where a constraint is added (`UniverseSolver::add_constraint`, which answers two constants alone). The path of a conflict is assembled by the solver from its own constraints' indices (`UniverseSolver::inconsistency_from_path`), with the origin of each level they name, and reaches a report as `Error::UniverseInconsistency` (`curios-elab/src/error.rs`), which stands at the first place the path knows, notes each use whose scheme's bound is on it, and words itself by the kinds on it (`inconsistent_sort`, `names_a_proposition`, `instantiated_on`).
- **The kernel reads the inequality.** `curios-cert` reads a constraint's two sides, `lower` and `upper`, (`satisfy.rs`, `level_model.rs`, `entail.rs`) and no origin.
- **Nothing reads a stored origin.** A scheme's constraint enters a solver again through `UniverseSolver::instantiate_at` alone, which writes its kind, its extent and what it instantiated anew and carries its `declaration` and `binder` over. Those two are written for an inductive's parameter (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) and read by `UniverseError`'s own `Display`, which no report prints: the elaborator words a conflict itself.
- **What is live is cleared by hand.** `UniverseContext::unplaced` takes the extent out of each constraint where a declaration is consed, at five sites (`curios-core/src/module.rs`, `structure.rs`, `inductive.rs`, `concept.rs`); `UniverseSolver::generalize` takes out what was instantiated; and `UniverseConstraint` compares and hashes by hand, past its origin, since provenance is no part of an inequality's identity.
- **A written level's seed stores an origin too.** `UniverseSeed` (`curios-core/src/universe.rs`) is archived with a lowering and holds an optional `UniverseConstraintOrigin`: the kind and the place of the `Type` that was written (`fresh_universe`, `curios-text/src/into_core/context.rs`), which the declaration's elaboration reads where a conflict names that level (`UniverseSolver::origin`). So the origin's type reaches a stored form by a second road, and this one is read.
- **An argument's check knows its site and tells no one.** `curios-elab/src/elaborate/apply.rs` checks a written argument at its parameter's domain and builds an `ArgumentSite` once the check has failed, `opened_link` costing the rest of the telescope, for the one refusal that takes it (`Error::at_argument`, a lambda against no function type). The conversion that check asks is handed an extent (`subsume_outcome`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) and builds a constraint's origin from it at two places, a sort held under a sort and a level taken beside another.

## The gap

Under `let coerce(A: Type, F: (Type) -> Type) -> Type = F(A);` and `let tie(@T: Type, x: T) -> Type = T;`, the declaration `let bad(G: (Type) -> Type) -> Type = coerce(tie(G), G);` is refused with `this Type would need to be strictly below itself` and `required constraint: max(?u1+1,?u2+1) ≤ ?u1`, standing at the argument `tie(G)`. `coerce` closes at `(A: Type.{u}, F: (Type.{u}) -> Type.{v}) -> Type.{v}`: the bound the call breaks is one level its two parameters share, which its scheme states as no constraint, so nothing a use of `coerce` restates is on the cycle. What is on it is the argument held under its parameter's sort, whose origin says where it was raised and nothing of whose parameter it was raised for, and the reader is not told that `coerce` holds its first argument's level to its second's domain's.

The site cannot ride on the error as `ArgumentSite` does for a lambda. The conflict is raised where the declaration's levels are finalized (`Context::finalize_universe_metas`), after the argument's check has returned, so a site attached where a check fails meets no conflict. It stands with the constraint, and the constraint's origin is a type every image stores: a site added there is a third field to clear by hand where a scheme is closed, and one clear missed archives a callee's identity or restates one declaration's argument on another's conflict.

## Decisions

- **A stored constraint is its inequality alone.** Core's archived `UniverseConstraint` holds the two sides of `lower ≤ upper` and nothing beside them, and where a constraint was raised is held by the solver that raised it, beside the constraint in its own store. Settled on 2026-10-13, over a field on the archived origin and over the same field left out of the archive by `curios_archive::Skip`: the first adds to what is cleared by hand, and the second keeps a scheme generalized and used within one compile relying on the clear in `generalize`.
- **The site stands with the constraint.** An argument's check hands the conversion its site, and the origin the conversion builds holds it; it is neither attached to the error nor recovered from the term when a report is made.
- **The work is staged**: the origin out of the stored form, then the site, then the report.

## Open questions

- **How the check hands the conversion its site.** The conversion is reached through the argument's elaboration, many frames below the loop that knows the callee. Three things bound the answer. The checking mode's expected type is the second half of the elaboration cache's key (`Mode`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate.rs`; `ElaborationKey`, `curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`), so a site carried in the mode enters a key or is left out of one by hand. A subsumption that parks is retried later by its parked origin's extent (`ParkedWork::Conversion`, `curios-elab/src/typing.rs`), after whatever the argument's check held has been let go. And a binder's hint is an interned `Symbol` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`), so a parameter's name is held without a copy. Recommended, and not settled: the context holds the argument under check for the one hop from the loop over a call's arguments to that argument's own `expect`, which reads it where it is asked at the same extent; from there the site is handed on as the extent is today, to the conversion and to the work a parked conversion keeps. What is held is the callee's identity, the mark, the position and the hint, and its cost where no constraint is raised, a callee's identity copied once for each application, is the figure the second stage reads. Not read: whether an argument's elaboration answered from the cache raises its constraints again, and under which site.
- **What becomes of `declaration` and `binder`.** Nothing a reader sees is built from them. They go with `UniverseError`'s wording of a conflict, or the site's shape takes them in: a bound raised for an inductive's parameter and one raised for a callee's are each a bound raised for a named binder.
- **Where the origin's type lives.** It stays in `curios-core`, unarchived, while a conflict travels through Core's `UniverseError` and `ReduceError`. Moving it to `curios-elab` is a further step this spec does not take.
- **The restatements that pass no occurrence.** `instantiate_at` is called with none at five sites, a recursive group's member taken at an instance (`curios-elab/src/elaborate.rs`) and a concept's scheme restated for a goal (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`) among them, so a conflict their bounds close names no declaration. Each has the declaration it restates at hand, a nominal declaration, a concept or a group, and a place only where a term is being elaborated. Whether this spec gives them their occurrence, as a fourth stage, is not settled.

## Stages

1. **A scheme stores inequalities alone.** `UniverseConstraint` loses its origin and derives its equality and hash; `UniverseContext::unplaced` and its five calls go; the solver's store holds each constraint beside its origin; `generalize` hands out constraints alone; `instantiate_at` writes an origin whole. A seed keeps the kind and the place of what was written under a type of its own, so `UniverseConstraintOrigin` is archived nowhere. The stored form of every unit changes with it, so what a store tells a unit's layout by is read and moved in this stage. Check: every report pinned under `curios/src/tests/universes/`, `curios/src/tests/board/` and `curios-pipeline/src/tests/` is the text it was; `wonder stage core-elab curios-std/src/lib.crs` prints what it printed and `/std`'s erased forms are the bytes they were; `curios-cert`'s tests pass untouched; and the size of the image is recorded.
2. **A constraint an argument's check raises holds its site.** The origin gains the site; the argument's check hands it on as the first open question settles; the conversion writes it at its two places. Check: a solver test that a constraint raised with a site keeps it through a speculation's mark and release, and that a scheme generalized over it holds none, which the types of the first stage make the only thing it can hold; and the budget `/std`'s elaboration spends is the count it was, its time read beside it as `.claude/rules/documentation.md` states a timed figure.
3. **The report says whose parameter.** `Error::UniverseInconsistency` words each site its path holds as the slot the term was checked as, by the line a lambda against no function type already takes, stated once for both. Check: the program above as a fixture beside `a_conflict_a_schemes_bound_closes_names_the_declaration_instantiated`, its report naming `coerce` and `A` and standing at the argument; a second whose argument's subsumption parks before it is decided, reporting the same line; and every other pinned report unchanged.

## Rejected

- **A site field on the archived origin.** It is the shape `instantiated` has, and it is cleared by hand in two places already; a third field is a third thing to clear, and the type lets a callee's identity into an image.
- **The same field, with what is live left out of the archive by `curios_archive::Skip`.** An image can then hold none of it, and a scheme generalized and instantiated within one compile never passes through the archive.
- **The site attached to the error where the argument's check fails.** Tried on top of `242c6009f`: the conflict is raised where the declaration's levels are finalized, so the error that passes the check is never the conflict.
- **The site recovered from the declaration's term when the report is made.** It reads a fact back off source positions, which the terms a desugaring writes can share, and reaches only the place the report stands at.
- **A table of sites beside the solver's store.** Two structures that every mark and release must move together, where an entry of the store that holds both moves as one.

## Completion and retirement

The solver's rustdoc states that a constraint's origin is live and a scheme's constraint has none; why, and what was rejected, goes to a decision under `documentation/design/theory/`; the fixture and the solver test hold the behaviour. The line in `documentation/roadmap.md` becomes a checked summary linking that decision, and this file is deleted.
