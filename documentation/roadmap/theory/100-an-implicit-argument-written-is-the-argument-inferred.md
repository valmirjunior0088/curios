# An implicit argument written is the argument inferred

[The surface language](../../syntax.md#names-calls-and-projections) accepts `join(values)`, `join(@Nat, values)` and `join(@_, use custom_show, values)` as one call: an implicit argument written is the argument inferred. Writing one refuses programs that leaving it out accepts, through two defects that each refuse such a program on their own — a case equation answers a stuck call of a universe-polymorphic recursive function by its spelling alone, and the elaborator sizes a declaration's field by its reduct where the kernel types it as written — and one representation that hands both their trigger: a type former is two terms, its name where it is written and its node where unification reached it.

**Next step:** stages 1 and 2 are ready fixes, independent of each other and of stage 3; stage 3 is a ready delivery in three commits.

## What is wrong

Probed at `0fc2fc1bd` with the release compiler, `every` a recursive `(@T: Type, l: List(T), pred: (T) -> Bool) -> Bool` and `checked` taking `Holds(every(l, small))`:

- **A guard spelled apart from its statement is not its statement.** `match every(@Point, l, small) | true => checked(l, @True/qed()) | false => 1 end` is refused, `True` inferred where `Holds(every(@Point, l, small))` is expected, and so is each combination of a written guard and an inferred statement, or the converse, at a structure, at `Nat` and at `Str`; the two written alike are accepted, and so is a callee that does not recurse. The respellings no implicit takes part in are refused the same way: the guard `every(@Pt, l, small)` under `let Pt: Type = Point`, `every(@Point, l, (p) => small(p))`, and `every(@Point, l, tiny)` under `let tiny(p: Point) -> Bool = small(p)`, each against a statement `every(@Point, l, small)`. Over a monomorphic `every_pt(l: List(Point), pred: (Point) -> Bool)` the eta and the wrapper respellings are accepted. A `Nat` match does the same: `match count(@Nat, l) | 0 => checked(l, @Eq/refl()) | _ => 1 end` against `Eq()(count(l), 0)` is refused, where the guard `count(@Nat, l) == 0` against `count(l) == 0` is accepted. `/std/Tui/Glyph/of_cluster` is refused as a report prints it (`REFUSED`, `curios-pipeline/src/standard/tests.rs`): its guard writes the implicit `List/all` takes, and the field its fact proves states it inferred.
- **A field stands at a level its reduct drops.** `pub struct G: Type { chars: List(Nat), @ok: Holds(every(@Nat, chars, small)) }` elaborates and the kernel refuses it, "a declaration domain at level `u` exceeds its family's `0`", at `chars`; with the implicit left out it certifies. Over `pub let Wrap(T: Type) -> Type = List(T)`, with `chars: Wrap(Nat)` and `every` taking `l: Wrap(T)`, the struct is refused with the implicit written and certifies with it left out.

**A guard's fallback asks for a commitment it may not make.** An equation is recorded under the scrutinee as written, and past an exact key the readers hand back a stuck form that is no operation of the algebra whole (`curios_analysis::Answered::Whole`), for each checker to compare with the equation's settled spelling by its own conversion ([Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)). The elaborator compares with nothing committed (`asked_scrutinee`, `same_uncommitted`, `curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`), which answers no wherever a yes would commit a level equation. Every occurrence of a universe-polymorphic call mints an instance of its own, and two projections of one recursive group are compared at every level, so two calls of `every` are never one there, whatever their arguments; the two written alike meet at the exact key, which holds the instances at one (`hold_at_one_instance`). The kernel compares the two whole at the levels they closed at, and where the elaborator left the instances apart they stay apart: `of_cluster`'s field stands at an instance of its own and the guard at another. A classing asks such a pair by its arguments, the heads being one up to their instances ("Two calls of one name apart in a universe instance are asked about by their arguments", [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md); `curios-analysis`'s `classify`), which is why the `==` guard above is accepted: it is an operation, and its two calls are atoms the classing pairs.

**The elaborator sizes a field by its reduct, and the kernel as written.** `telescope_sizing` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) reads each field's sort by `Sort::of`, which reduces first, where `check_signature` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/module.rs`) types the field as written; [A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../../design/theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md) holds the two to one reading: "A former sized as typing read its parts sits where the kernel types it." `/sys/List/List.{Type; c}(Nat)` stands at `Type c` and reduces to `ListType(NatType)` at `Type 0`. A written `@Nat` makes the field's `List(Nat)` meet the callee's `List(T)` as two calls of one global, which joins the field's instance to the callee's; the callee's is held where conversion compares it and generalized as `G`'s parameter, so the field stands at it. An inferred `T` is solved through the two reduced nodes, which hold no level, and the field's instance settles at `0`. `check_is_sort` hands back the sort typing read, `Type c`, and the sizing does not read it.

**A type former is two terms.** Written, `Point` is the global it names, `Var(/Point)`, and `Nat` is `/sys/Nat/Nat`, a definition whose body is the node; a recursive family unfolded by name is its group's projection, a third spelling conversion reads through (`Term::applied_former`, `bare_former`, `curried_former`). Reached by unification, a former is whatever the side it was solved against held, and reduction hands out the node wherever an intrinsic holds a type: weak-head reduction reduces the operand of `ListType`, `CellType`, `ChannelType` and `IoType` and the element type of a list literal (`reduce_intrinsic`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic.rs`), where a nominal node's parameters stay as written. So an implicit solved through `List(T)` is `StructType { name: /Point, .. }` or `NatType`, and one solved through `Option(T)`, a structure's parameter or `x: T` the name. A literal's type, a struct literal's and a constructor's, and every type `/sys` states (`projection`, `curios-text/src/sys_module/projected.rs`) are nodes. The printer writes both as `Point` and `Nat` (`former_eta`, `intrinsic_form`, `curios-core/src/print.rs`). Each structural reading in both checkers — an exact key, a plain reduction, which asks no conversion, a remembered reduct — tells the two apart.

## What is known

- **Stages 1 and 2 close every refusal above with the spelling as it is.** In a scratch copy at `0fc2fc1bd`, with each checker's whole-form fallback asking a pair of calls apart in their instance alone by their arguments, and the elaborator sizing each field by the sort typing read beside its reduct's, every probe above was accepted in both checkers, `/std` elaborated and certified, and `curios-pipeline`'s library tests passed but the read-back that holds `of_cluster` refused, which now reads back; a guard over another predicate, another list or another element type, and a statement in the guard's false arm, stayed refused. While `/std` elaborated, the arguments' route answered no lookup the whole comparison had refused, so the library rests on none of it. Measured at `0fc2fc1bd`, each at one path: the sizing moved no image; the fallback left `/sys`'s images, the certifier's record, the read record and the documentation record byte-identical, and moved `std.pack` from 48,341,990 to 48,342,470 bytes and `std.rkyv`'s digest at its size, through what a comparison that commits no level no longer declines to remember. Counted, the two together moved `profile-prelude`'s `budget::consumed` from 62,349,532 to 62,355,028 in elaboration, the same in each of three interleaved runs, and left certification's at 21,611,279.
- **The node spelling, prototyped.** At `0fc2fc1bd`, a written former applied in full elaborated as its node from the start and each committed solution's former applications respelled: every probe above that names a former was accepted, `of_cluster` read back, the prelude elaborated and certified, and `curios-pipeline`'s library tests passed but two, the read-back of `of_cluster` against `REFUSED` and the hint that the unit type is written `{}`, which `Io(())` no longer reaches. Counted, `profile-prelude`'s `budget::consumed` went from 62,349,532 to 57,710,738 in elaboration and from 21,611,279 to 20,054,499 in certification, and `term::looks` fell 8.6% and 11.7%; `std.pack` went from 48,341,990 to 48,080,785 bytes, `reads.rkyv` from 917,714 to 837,692 and `sys.pack` from 856,046 to 855,458, `sys.rkyv`'s and `std.rkyv`'s digests moved at their sizes, and the certifier's and the documentation's records came out byte-identical. A written former rewritten to its node after its elaboration drifted 41 declarations' schemes, a former's instance dropped and its levels orphaned, and the kernel refused `/std/List/sort_by/flush`'s read-back; elaborated as the node from the start it did not. A name's body is read only where it is a declared former's, since a read is a dependency the incremental record keeps, and reading every applied global's broke six of `curios-pipeline`'s incremental and stored-candidate tests.
- **A former passed unapplied is applied by substitution.** `Monad(M: (Type) -> Type)` and `Lift` take a former short of its application: a witness's `Monad(Io)` and a higher-kinded call's `lift(@Option, o)` pass its name, and an imitation, the one every `!` makes to read its region's monad, passes `(x) => T(x)` over the node it mirrors (`imitate_flex_apply`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`). A method's type instantiated there spells `M(A)` as the name applied to `A`, or as a β-redex over the lambda, wherever the node is meant.
- **A solution keeps the spelling its problem wrote** ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-solution-is-the-rigid-side-as-written)), and the printer already reads a node and a former's one-binder η-expansion back to the name (`former_eta`), as a witness key reads the η-expansion's body (`HeadKey::of_whnf`).
- **Prior art.** GHC keeps one spelling of a saturated former: "saturated applications of FunTyCon must use FunTy", while "unsaturated FunTyCons do appear as TyConApps" ([`GHC.Core.TyCo.Rep`](https://hackage-content-origin.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.14.1/docs/GHC-Core-TyCo-Rep.html)), held by smart constructors that substitution builds through, `mkAppTy` in `subst_ty` keeping a `TyConApp` always visibly so, so that structural equality and the type-keyed maps meet one term. Isabelle expands an abbreviation in its check phase and contracts it in uncheck, the printer's side. Lean, Rocq and Agda have no node to choose: an inductive and its constructors are constants with no value to unfold (Lean's `InductiveVal` and `ConstructorVal` carry none, [*Type Checking in Lean 4*](https://ammkrn.github.io/type_checking_in_lean4/declarations/declarations.html); Rocq's `Ind` and `Construct`), and Agda's `BUILTIN` binds a primitive concept to a declared name, so a literal has the name's type ([built-ins](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/language/built-ins.html)). Rocq's keyed unification takes a subterm whose head is the lemma's key and "the arguments are then unified up to full reduction" ([rewriting](https://docs.rocq-prover.org/V8.13+beta1/refman/proofs/writing-proofs/rewriting.html)), the reading stage 1 gives the fallback.

## Decisions

- **The fallback asks by the arguments, in both checkers.** Where the readers hand back a stuck call whole and the equation's settled spelling is a call of the same head apart in its universe instance alone, each checker asks its conversion of each pair of arguments, by the one statement the classing reads, published from `curios-analysis`. At a scrutinee whose carrier holds no type, a universe instance is no part of the value, as for the classing's atoms; at one that holds a type, the elaborator first holds the two instances at one, as a key's hit holds them, so the kernel is handed one level where it compares.
- **A field is sized as typing read it**: by the sort `check_is_sort` hands back, the kernel's reading, in place of its reduct's.
- **A type former applied in full is its node, in both checkers' terms.** Elaboration resolves a written former applied in full to the node its entry builds: an intrinsic former before it is elaborated, so its operands are checked against the node's own signature and no level of its name is minted, and a nominal one from its elaborated application, at the instance and over the arguments that application settled. No former's body is read for it.
- **A former short of its full application is its η-form**, the lambdas over its node that its entry builds, at an occurrence of its own; and core takes the one β-step where an application of an η-form is built over its whole spine, in `Term::rebuilt` and `From<Subterm>`, so a substitution and an imitation build the node as elaboration does. No elaborated term then holds a former's name, and conversion's readings through a former's projection are left nothing to read. One recognizer of an η-form serves the β-step and the printer, which reads it back to the name.

**Rejected.**

- **The name**, every node read back to the former that holds it wherever one is made: reduction makes a node at every intrinsic's type operand and every literal, so the rule never closes under reduction; reading a reduct back to the name it came from is the model of reduction beside the printer that [A diagnostic spells what its reader can write](../../design/tools/a-diagnostic-spells-what-its-reader-can-write.md) rejects; and the name holds a level its node does not, stage 2's trigger.
- **Each committed solution's former applications respelled**, the prototype's closure: a method's instantiated type and a reduct keep the name, which an exact key and a plain reduction still tell apart from the node, and stage 1 answers only where conversion is asked.
- **A key that reads through a former's name**, as GHC's type maps expand synonyms (`trieMapView`) and Lean's discrimination trees reduce at reducible transparency: [Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md) keys structurally in both checkers, and with one spelling there is nothing to read through.
- **No node**, a former a constant with no value, as in Lean, Rocq and Agda: a node carries its family's name and parameters so conversion compares two with no lookup, and every reader of a nominal node and of an intrinsic type, some 210 and 770 sites, would be rewritten for an invariant elaboration can state once.
- **A node short of its parameters**, as GHC's `TyConApp` takes partial application: every reader of a node then meets one that is no type, and an intrinsic former has no partial form.
- **Stopping reduction at a type former's operand**: it closes the `List` route alone.

## Stages

1. **The fallback asks by the arguments.** Both checkers, the shared statement in `curios-analysis`, and `curios-analysis/tests/driven.rs`. Check: the guard probes above, written as rows of `curios`'s respelling grid, held in both checkers, and the struct field's case beside them; `/std/Tui/Glyph/of_cluster` reads back, leaving `REFUSED` empty; a pair of calls whose arguments do not convert stays unanswered. No representation changes; `std.pack` and `std.rkyv`'s digest may move, and the certifier's record must not.
2. **A field is sized as typing read it.** Check: both structs above certify, each with its implicit written and left out, as tests in `curios`'s suite. No image moves.
3. **The node**, in three commits.
   - **An η-form applied is its node.** The recognizer in `curios-core`, of any arity, the β-step where a term is built, and the printer reading through it. Check: core's term and print tests; `/std`'s images may move, imitation's candidates being η-forms already.
   - **Elaboration builds the node and the η-form.** Each kind of former — nominal, intrinsic, recursive, indexed in two calls — applied in full and short of it; which `/sys` names are intrinsic formers read off core's table of intrinsics, never spelled in `curios-elab`; every site building a former by a registry name building its node; the unit-type hint checked where an intrinsic former's operand is. Check: each guard above written and inferred in every combination at each kind of former, a higher-kinded call and a concept over a former among them, in both checkers.
   - **What no term reaches goes.** A test of `/std`'s image that no term names a type former; conversion's readings through a former's projection removed where nothing reaches them; the decision written as a theory decision of its own, `documentation/design/theory/a-type-former-applied-in-full-is-its-node.md`, since it binds the elaborator, the kernel, core's representation and the printer alike, and `curios-elab`'s README's solution rule pointing at it.

   A representation change: `sys.pack`, `std.pack`, `reads.rkyv` and `sys.rkyv`'s and `std.rkyv`'s digests may move; the certifier's and the documentation's records and every program's run must not.

**Measurement**, for stage 3 against the tree it lands on: `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration` and `certification`, `budget::consumed` and `term::looks` summed over the fold (counted), against the prototype's figures above; `elaborate_and_zonk_with_prelude` and `recheck_module` as medians over three runs of each spelling interleaved, with their spans (timed, on a quiet machine); each image's size, and its digest at one path; and a program typed against many `/sys` operations, timed the same way.

**Retirement.** Once stage 3 lands, the decisions live where they are owned — stage 1's in [Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md), stage 2's in the universe decision's sizing rule, which already states it, stage 3's in its own decision — and this specification is deleted.
