# An implicit argument written is the argument inferred

[The surface language](../../syntax.md#names-calls-and-projections) accepts `join(values)`, `join(@Nat, values)` and `join(@_, use custom_show, values)` as one call: an implicit argument written is the argument inferred. A type former is two terms, its name where it is written and its node where unification reached it, so a written implicit and an inferred one are two terms to every structural reading in both checkers.

**Next step:** a ready delivery in three commits.

## What is wrong

**A type former is two terms.** Written, `Point` is the global it names, `Var(/Point)`, and `Nat` is `/sys/Nat/Nat`, a definition whose body is the node; a recursive family unfolded by name is its group's projection, a third spelling conversion reads through (`Term::applied_former`, `bare_former`, `curried_former`). Reached by unification, a former is whatever the side it was solved against held, and reduction hands out the node wherever an intrinsic holds a type: weak-head reduction reduces the operand of `ListType`, `CellType`, `ChannelType` and `IoType` and the element type of a list literal (`reduce_intrinsic`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic.rs`), where a nominal node's parameters stay as written. So an implicit solved through `List(T)` is `StructType { name: /Point, .. }` or `NatType`, and one solved through `Option(T)`, a structure's parameter or `x: T` the name. A literal's type, a struct literal's and a constructor's, and every type `/sys` states (`projection`, `curios-text/src/sys_module/projected.rs`) are nodes. The printer writes both as `Point` and `Nat` (`former_eta`, `intrinsic_form`, `curios-core/src/print.rs`).

Each structural reading in both checkers tells the two apart. An exact key misses, and a case equation's fallback answers by conversion ([Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)); a plain reduction, which asks no conversion, and a remembered reduct do not. And the name holds a level its node does not: `/sys/List/List.{Type; c}(Nat)` stands at `Type c` where `ListType(NatType)` stands at `Type 0`, a level a declaration's conversion can hold and its sizing then reads ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../../design/theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md)).

## What is known

- **The node spelling, prototyped.** At `0fc2fc1bd`, a written former applied in full elaborated as its node from the start and each committed solution's former applications respelled: every guard and statement over a former written and inferred in every combination, at a structure, at `Nat` and at `Str`, was accepted, `/std/Tui/Glyph/of_cluster` read back, the prelude elaborated and certified, and `curios-pipeline`'s library tests passed but two, the read-back of `of_cluster` against the list of refused `let`s and the hint that the unit type is written `{}`, which `Io(())` no longer reaches. Counted, `profile-prelude`'s `budget::consumed` went from 62,349,532 to 57,710,738 in elaboration and from 21,611,279 to 20,054,499 in certification, and `term::looks` fell 8.6% and 11.7%; `std.pack` went from 48,341,990 to 48,080,785 bytes, `reads.rkyv` from 917,714 to 837,692 and `sys.pack` from 856,046 to 855,458, `sys.rkyv`'s and `std.rkyv`'s digests moved at their sizes, and the certifier's and the documentation's records came out byte-identical. A written former rewritten to its node after its elaboration drifted 41 declarations' schemes, a former's instance dropped and its levels orphaned, and the kernel refused `/std/List/sort_by/flush`'s read-back; elaborated as the node from the start it did not. A name's body is read only where it is a declared former's, since a read is a dependency the incremental record keeps, and reading every applied global's broke six of `curios-pipeline`'s incremental and stored-candidate tests.
- **A former passed unapplied is applied by substitution.** `Monad(M: (Type) -> Type)` and `Lift` take a former short of its application: a witness's `Monad(Io)` and a higher-kinded call's `lift(@Option, o)` pass its name, and an imitation, the one every `!` makes to read its region's monad, passes `(x) => T(x)` over the node it mirrors (`imitate_flex_apply`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`). A method's type instantiated there spells `M(A)` as the name applied to `A`, or as a β-redex over the lambda, wherever the node is meant.
- **The two checkers size an intrinsic node alike**, each reading its operand's sort off the operand's reduct (`Sort::of` in each), so a node holds no level a declaration's sizing could set apart.
- **A solution keeps the spelling its problem wrote** ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-solution-is-the-rigid-side-as-written)), and the printer already reads a node and a former's one-binder η-expansion back to the name (`former_eta`), as a witness key reads the η-expansion's body (`HeadKey::of_whnf`).
- **Prior art.** GHC keeps one spelling of a saturated former: "saturated applications of FunTyCon must use FunTy", while "unsaturated FunTyCons do appear as TyConApps" ([`GHC.Core.TyCo.Rep`](https://hackage-content-origin.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.14.1/docs/GHC-Core-TyCo-Rep.html)), held by smart constructors that substitution builds through, `mkAppTy` in `subst_ty` keeping a `TyConApp` always visibly so, so that structural equality and the type-keyed maps meet one term. Isabelle expands an abbreviation in its check phase and contracts it in uncheck, the printer's side. Lean, Rocq and Agda have no node to choose: an inductive and its constructors are constants with no value to unfold (Lean's `InductiveVal` and `ConstructorVal` carry none, [*Type Checking in Lean 4*](https://ammkrn.github.io/type_checking_in_lean4/declarations/declarations.html); Rocq's `Ind` and `Construct`), and Agda's `BUILTIN` binds a primitive concept to a declared name, so a literal has the name's type ([built-ins](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/language/built-ins.html)).

## Decisions

- **A type former applied in full is its node, in both checkers' terms.** Elaboration resolves a written former applied in full to the node its entry builds: an intrinsic former before it is elaborated, so its operands are checked against the node's own signature and no level of its name is minted, and a nominal one from its elaborated application, at the instance and over the arguments that application settled. No former's body is read for it.
- **A former short of its full application is its η-form**, the lambdas over its node that its entry builds, at an occurrence of its own; and core takes the one β-step where an application of an η-form is built over its whole spine, in `Term::rebuilt` and `From<Subterm>`, so a substitution and an imitation build the node as elaboration does. No elaborated term then holds a former's name, and conversion's readings through a former's projection are left nothing to read. One recognizer of an η-form serves the β-step and the printer, which reads it back to the name.

**Rejected.**

- **The name**, every node read back to the former that holds it wherever one is made: reduction makes a node at every intrinsic's type operand and every literal, so the rule never closes under reduction; reading a reduct back to the name it came from is the model of reduction beside the printer that [A diagnostic spells what its reader can write](../../design/tools/a-diagnostic-spells-what-its-reader-can-write.md) rejects; and the name holds a level its node does not.
- **Each committed solution's former applications respelled**, the prototype's closure: a method's instantiated type and a reduct keep the name, which an exact key and a plain reduction still tell apart from the node, and the equation's fallback answers only where conversion is asked.
- **A key that reads through a former's name**, as GHC's type maps expand synonyms (`trieMapView`) and Lean's discrimination trees reduce at reducible transparency: [Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md) keys structurally in both checkers, and with one spelling there is nothing to read through.
- **No node**, a former a constant with no value, as in Lean, Rocq and Agda: a node carries its family's name and parameters so conversion compares two with no lookup, and every reader of a nominal node and of an intrinsic type, some 210 and 770 sites, would be rewritten for an invariant elaboration can state once.
- **A node short of its parameters**, as GHC's `TyConApp` takes partial application: every reader of a node then meets one that is no type, and an intrinsic former has no partial form.
- **Stopping reduction at a type former's operand**: it closes the `List` route alone.

## Stages

1. **An η-form applied is its node.** The recognizer in `curios-core`, of any arity, the β-step where a term is built, and the printer reading through it. Check: core's term and print tests; `/std`'s images may move, imitation's candidates being η-forms already.
2. **Elaboration builds the node and the η-form.** Each kind of former — nominal, intrinsic, recursive, indexed in two calls — applied in full and short of it; which `/sys` names are intrinsic formers read off core's table of intrinsics, never spelled in `curios-elab`; every site building a former by a registry name building its node; the unit-type hint checked where an intrinsic former's operand is. Check: a guard and its statement written and inferred in every combination at each kind of former, a higher-kinded call and a concept over a former among them, in both checkers, as rows of `curios`'s respelling grid.
3. **What no term reaches goes.** A test of `/std`'s image that no term names a type former; conversion's readings through a former's projection removed where nothing reaches them; the decision written as a theory decision of its own, `documentation/design/theory/a-type-former-applied-in-full-is-its-node.md`, since it binds the elaborator, the kernel, core's representation and the printer alike, and `curios-elab`'s README's solution rule pointing at it.

A representation change: `sys.pack`, `std.pack`, `reads.rkyv` and `sys.rkyv`'s and `std.rkyv`'s digests may move; the certifier's and the documentation's records and every program's run must not.

**Measurement**, against the tree the stages land on: `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration` and `certification`, `budget::consumed` and `term::looks` summed over the fold (counted), against the prototype's figures above; `elaborate_and_zonk_with_prelude` and `recheck_module` as medians over three runs of each spelling interleaved, with their spans (timed, on a quiet machine); each image's size, and its digest at one path; and a program typed against many `/sys` operations, timed the same way.

**Retirement.** Once stage 3 lands, the decision lives in its own document, and this specification is deleted.
