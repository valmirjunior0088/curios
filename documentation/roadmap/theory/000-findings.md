No program shows it yet: `let r: Nat = (let f = (x) => x; f(3));` is refused, but `(x) => 5` in its place is refused alike, a `let`'s value fixing its binders' types itself. - **Conversion sets apart two members of one group met again inside their own unfolding, though their arguments have moved, so it is not transitive across a group.** For two calls of different members of a recursive group, `curios-elab`'s conversion takes one symmetric delta step and holds the pair of heads, and a pair of the same two heads met again inside that unfolding is apart, "since unfolded again it poses itself again" (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), whatever its arguments now are. With `first(t)` and `first_in(l)` one group, `first_in` answering `first(h)` for a head that holds anything and `first(T/node(k))` answering `first_in(k)`, `first_in([T/node(k), ..r])` and `first(T/node(k))` are refused as one, closed too at `k = []`, where both reduce to `0`, while each converts with `first_in(k)`; declared apart rather than as a group, the two convert. The rule stops a pair that would unfold forever, and it keys the pair by its heads alone. In `/std/Map/internal/Node` before `first_in` was declared on its own, a lemma's branch arm met such a pair under its case equations, the elaborator admitted it and the certifier refused it (expected `first_in([branch(at, twigs), ..r])`, found `first(branch(at, twigs))`), so the two checkers also decide it apart. Not yet known: whether this rule is what refuses the closed pair too, whose report shows the two sides only as their reducts, `Eq(@Nat)(0, 0)` against itself at two universe metavariables; what a pair should be held by, its heads with their arguments, so a pair is apart only where it recurs whole and the conversion budget bounds one whose arguments grow, or the step's progress, an unfolding that took a match apart; and how the certifier decides each pair, which `cargo xboard probe` on the pair written as Core text settles. Check: the open and the closed pair above admitted by both checkers, and `curios`'s `tests::runaways` unchanged. Size: unknown.
