# Theory: findings

- **A level `Fmt/render` leaves free in a `Try` body is refused at the unit that calls it, with no position.** [A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../../design/theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md) has a declaration generalize the levels its constraints leave free and instantiate them freshly at each external use. A library function `read(root: Path) -> Try(Io, Str, {})` whose body is `let _ = Try/map_failure(fs/is_file(root), (_) => "failed")!;`, then `let _ = Try/pure(Fmt/render("incomplete ticket"))!;`, then `Try/pure(())` passes `wonder diagnostics` on its own, and the package is refused once `exe.crs` calls it: `invalid inferred universe state: module body: level ?u1 escapes its universe parameter context: it still holds an unsolved universe metavariable`, raised by the escape check in `curios-elab/src/zonk.rs` with no source position. Reproduced at `release/0.16.13` and at `b5a6d9df5`. The level is the one `Try/pure`'s implicit takes from the render's computed type, `format_type_with(Str, parse(s))`: swapping the two lines, dropping the host action, writing `let _ = Fmt/render("…");` without the `Try/pure` and the `!`, or `Str/concat` in the render's place each passes; an annotation `let _: Str = …` does not; a function declared `-> Str` whose body is the render passes, which is how the paper's harness avoids it. What is wrong: `read`'s finalization leaves the level neither settled nor among its parameters, so the caller's instantiation meets it and the diagnostic blames the module body. The fix: settle it where `read` is finalized, as a level nothing later can tell, and have the escape diagnostic name the term it stands in. Its check: the three-file package above compiles, and the universe solver's tests hold a `Try/pure` of a computed-type value under a `!` after a host action. Size: two commits at most, the second for the position in the diagnostic, where the leak is one `finalize` reaches; otherwise lifted into a specification of this area.
