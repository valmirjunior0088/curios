# Theory: findings

- **Reading the levels a recursive group's unfolding holds climbs a group again for every walk that opens it at binders of its own, so a chain of derived witnesses costs twice the climbs a link.** Over a chain of `n` families, each holding the next, each with a derived `Spell` witness, compared through a definition that spells as it does, `group_held` (`curios-analysis/src/variance.rs`) climbs 2, 6, 14, 30, 62 and 126 groups at `n` from 1 to 6, `2^(n+1) - 2`, at `2d608d7ba`, and elaboration takes 0.2 s at `n = 4`, 11 s at 8 and over 60 s at 16. Traced on 2026-11-20 by writing out each group climbed at `n = 4`: the 30 are 8 up to a renaming of the locals they name, the rest one of those opened by another walk at fresh binders (`Free(Mint { .. })`), while a climb's readings are kept by the group as it stands (`Climbs::settled`), so each is a new key. Fix: the climbs keyed by a group closed over the locals it names, in the order it names them, with their types, so a group opened at other binders reads the reading already taken, and the levels each group holds are unchanged. Check: a chain growth in `curios`'s `tests::growth`, linear; the derived-witness test of `tests::universes::instance` within its budget; `cargo xtask workspace lint`, which reads every group of `/std`. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: the elaborator restates the equations in force innermost frame first, where the certifier restates them in the order it recorded them, so the two number an arm's restated instances in opposite orders.** `Context::restate_equations` (`curios-elab/src/context/scope.rs`) walks `Frames::scrutinee_equations`, innermost frame first, numbering each instance it registers in the arm's frame in that order, where `Scope::restate` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/scope.rs`) pushes them outermost first. A frame's equations are heights of their own, and an instance's own spelling sees the ones registered before it, so under two guards an arm on their variable restates together, each checker's own spelling of one instance sees the other where the other checker's does not. A probe in the arm reads both past their own heights, settled again with each other in view, so the rows over `first` and `second` among `curios/src/tests/respelling.rs`'s nested cells hold in both nestings; what is not known is whether a settlement nested at a height between the two instances, one equation short, reaches a verdict in one checker the other does not. Settled by such a program, then the elaborator's instances numbered in recorded order, outermost frame first, with it as their test; or by dropping this entry where none can be built. Size: unknown.
