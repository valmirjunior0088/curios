# Theory: findings

- **The trait every shared analysis asks its checker through is named `Env`, while the crate's `Driver` names one consumer's narrower seam.** `curios-analysis`'s `Env` (`judge.rs`) is what index inversion, positivity, size-change totality and variance may ask of the checker running them: its reduction, its scope, its declarations and registry, and the refinements in force. An environment is one of the four, and the word every document around the trait uses for what implements it is *driver*: "a shared analysis serves two drivers" (`.claude/rules/checking.md`), an analysis needing no more than `Env` "borrows no opinion from its driver", "errors belong to the driver" (`judge.rs`). The word is held in the same crate by `conversion.rs`'s `Driver: Reducer`, what a checker supplies to `convert_intrinsics` beyond its reduction — how it reads an intrinsic before a peel, the elaborator's solving views, and the registry — one consumer's seam, as `Judge: Env` is `invert`'s. The names the user settled on 2026-11-19: `Env` renamed `Driver`, its type parameter `E` renamed `D` and the locals named for it `driver` — the 195 `env`s of `curios-analysis`, in 11 files, each an analysis's parameter, a walk's field or a test's fill of one — and the conversion chain's trait renamed `IntrinsicDriver`, named for the shared chain it drives; `Judge` keeps its name, a driver that lends one judgment. Fix: the rename, and every README, decision and specification that names either restated, among them `curios-analysis`'s README heading "What a checker supplies for itself is `Env`". Size: one commit, a rename and nothing else.
- **Uncertain: reading two instances of a recursive group apart in levels climbs every other group a walk meets again in every round and every nested walk, so one comparison of a derived witness's instances costs minutes.** `group_held` (`curios-analysis/src/variance.rs`), by which `held_pairs` reads two projections of one group — the elaborator wherever two instances meet, their levels unsolved metavariables included (`identify_universe_levels`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`), and the certifier at `rec_instances` — climbs the group's least fixpoint a round at a time, each round a `Walk` of its own whose table of the other groups it meets (`Walk::groups`) starts empty, and a member of another group met in a walk is read by that group's own fixpoint (`Walk::group_marks`), climbed afresh inside it. Re-shown on 2026-11-19 at `5b3028ed8`: a derived `Spell` witness of a recursive `Tree(A)` compared through a definition that spells as it does — `shown(t) = Spell/spell(t)` and `Eq()(shown(t), Spell/spell(t))` by `Eq/refl()`, fifteen lines — checks in 742 s and 9.5 GB, where the witness compared with itself checks in 0.2 s; under gdb the one `identify_universe_levels` that asks it calls `group_held` once, and the walks under that call call it more than 19,800 times more, each from `Walk::call`. The fix the user settled on 2026-11-19, one table of every other group's held levels for the whole reading, was prototyped the same day and leaves the program past 120 s, and gdb names why: the climbs stand 113 deep at the 1500th, and they alternate between two groups — `/std/Fmt`'s local `go`, one allocation at every other level, and a nameless one-member group minted anew between each. `go`'s walk forces a term whose reduct is a call of the nameless group; that group's walk forces a call of `go`; and `go`, climbed further up the stack, has no row yet and is no nested walk's estimate, so it is climbed anew, meeting a nameless group of its own, without end. The groups a reading meets are strongly connected through reduction where no name connects them, so the premise that they form a DAG fails, and a table cannot help: what recurs is a climb in progress. The fix this points to: a group met while it is climbed further up the stack reads that climb's current estimate, as a walk reads its own group's — the nested least fixpoint over a monotone estimate, the outer climb's next round climbing the inner again under its new estimate — and a row is filed only for a climb no estimate fed. What settles it: that, prototyped against the program checked in under a second, the held positions the variance tests pin unchanged, and a chain of groups each met by the next linear in `curios`'s `tests::growth`. Size: unknown.
