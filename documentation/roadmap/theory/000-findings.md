# Theory: findings

- **Conversion sets apart two members of one group met again inside their own unfolding, though their arguments have moved, so it is not transitive across a group.** For two calls of different members of a recursive group, `curios-elab`'s conversion takes one symmetric delta step and holds the pair of heads, and a pair of the same two heads met again inside that unfolding is apart, "since unfolded again it poses itself again" (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), whatever its arguments now are. With `first(t)` and `first_in(l)` one group, `first_in` answering `first(h)` for a head that holds anything and `first(T/node(k))` answering `first_in(k)`, `first_in([T/node(k), ..r])` and `first(T/node(k))` are refused as one, closed too at `k = []`, where both reduce to `0`, while each converts with `first_in(k)`; declared apart rather than as a group, the two convert. The rule stops a pair that would unfold forever, and it keys the pair by its heads alone. In `/std/Map/internal/Node` before `first_in` was declared on its own, a lemma's branch arm met such a pair under its case equations, the elaborator admitted it and the certifier refused it (expected `first_in([branch(at, twigs), ..r])`, found `first(branch(at, twigs))`), so the two checkers also decide it apart. Probed on 2026-11-18, after the member reading of `d55b89f15`: both pairs are still refused, and each side converts alone with what the two share — `first_in([T/node([])])` and `first(T/node([]))` each with `0`, `first_in([T/node(k), ..r])` and `first(T/node(k))` each with `first_in(k)` — so the closed pair is refused by this rule too, and its report misleads: it shows the two sides as their reducts, `Eq.{Type; ?u1}(@Nat)(0, 0)` against `Eq.{Type; ?u0}(@Nat)(0, 0)`, two universe metavariables nothing keeps apart, where what was refused is the pair of members, which a report naming the two calls as written would say. Not yet known: what a pair is held by, which the user settled on 2026-11-18 as the step's progress: the same two heads met again are apart only where no unfolding between their two meetings took a match apart at a constructor, as Rocq unfolds a fixpoint only where its decreasing argument starts with one; an unfolding that leaves its match stuck poses the first meeting's question one binder further in, which is all [Conversion recurrence](../../design/soundness/conversion-recurrence.md) argues apart, and one that fires a match computes. A pair held by its heads with their arguments was weighed and set aside: an open recursion's arguments move every round, so nothing recurs whole and the budget alone would refuse, which the decision rejects; and how the certifier decides each pair, which `cargo xboard probe` on the pair written as Core text settles. Check: the open and the closed pair above admitted by both checkers, and `curios`'s `tests::runaways` unchanged. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: conversion's reading of two projections of one group forces every member's body, so a group whose body computes runs that computation at each such comparison.** `held_pairs` reads the positions two projections apart in levels alone differ at through `group_held` (`curios-analysis/src/variance.rs`), the least fixpoint of the members' unfoldings, each body opened at fresh binders and walked as `held_levels` walks, forcing each closed part it reads. A derived `Spell` witness of a recursive family is such a group, and its body renders through `Spell/call`, `Fmt/render` over a format string, which forcing runs on what an arm binds: in a trial of 2026-11-18 that read a member's vector through `group_held`, `tests::derive::spell::an_indexed_family_spells_each_constructor` and three like it ran past twenty seconds and a gigabyte where they elaborate in under one, and in one that forced the member's application, past two minutes and five gigabytes. A member's vector is read with nothing reduced instead (`group_levels`, `curios-elab/src/universes/variance.rs`), so the vector does not meet it. Probed on 2026-11-18 at `d55b89f15`'s reading: a derived `Spell` witness of a universe-polymorphic `Tree`, used twice at one instance and at two families whose levels differ, compared through what each spells, checks in 0.18 s, so neither shape meets two instances apart in levels alone, the levels being settled before any pair of instances is compared.
