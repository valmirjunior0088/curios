# Theory: findings

- **The settle sweep loses the wake of what it pins.** `settle_synthesizable` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) takes every parked problem out of the store for the length of its loop, and settling a form runs `expect`, whose `retry_parked` then meets an empty store: `wake_parked` (`curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`) clears signals it has no goal to deliver to. The problems are reparked afterwards with watch sets recomputed from what is still unsolved, so a goal that watched the metavariable the settle pinned is woken by nothing and waits for the drain's final sweep, while a goal that watched the settled form's placeholder is retried first. Nothing is refused by it, since re-validation postpones a candidate it cannot judge yet: `Eq/refl()` at `Eq()((x: Nat) => g(0), (x: Nat) => g(0))` meets its implicit against the lambda one round before the equation that types it, and waits the round. Fix: a sweep keeps the signals of what it solves, the store holding what the loop is not working on or `wake_parked` leaving a signal no parked goal could yet receive. It changes the order parked work wakes in across `/std`, so what it moves is taken first. Check: that program's profile carries no `postponed: re-validation could not judge the candidate yet` note from `curios_elab::solve`, and `/std` elaborates to what it did. Size: small.
- **A parked conversion watches its two sides and not its type.** `Solutions::park` (`curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`) computes a conversion goal's watch set from the metavariables of its two sides, so a goal postponed because the type it is compared at is unsolved — an implicit against a lambda at `?A` — is woken when a side's metavariable is solved and not when `?A` is, and waits for the drain's final sweep. Nothing is refused by it. Fix: the watch set takes the unsolved metavariables of the goal's type as well. It wakes more goals per solution, so what it costs across `/std` is taken first. Check: a fixture in `curios-elab`'s `context::tests` parking such a goal and waking it by its type. Size: quick.
- **Uncertain: the one candidate re-validation rejects across `/std`.** At `85da54b97`, elaborating `/std` rejects one candidate in `solve`'s re-validation (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), counted from the `curios_elab::solve` notes in `curios-prelude-archive`'s build profile: while `/std/Str/At/skip` is elaborated, a metavariable's candidate is typed `Holds(1 <= Bytes/len(s.0) - at.0)` where `False` is expected. Not known: what proposes a fact in scope where a refutation is asked for, and whether the rejection is a search doing its work or a candidate built wrong. Fix: once that is known. Check: the count, read again. Size: unknown.
- **`Term::match_ambient` is a public constructor only tests call** (`curios-core/src/term.rs`): the elaborator builds `MatchResult::Ambient` directly, and every caller is a `curios-cert` test. Fix: move it to `curios_core::test_support`, or have the elaborator build through it. Check: `curios-cert`'s tests build with the feature on. Size: quick.
- **A numeral is refused at a type stuck on an unsolved metavariable, where one at a bare metavariable takes its default.** `elaborate_num_lit` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`) commits to the shape default when its expected type reduces to an unsolved metavariable, and takes any other reduct as the type to realize at, so a projection of an unsolved witness is read as a type no numeral has: over `f1(@A: Type, use Sized(A), x: Sized/Carrier(@A), pin: A)`, `f1(5, true)` reports a type mismatch between `Nat` and `(?).0`, where `f1(@Bool, 5, true)` compiles and a `Nat`-typed variable in the numeral's place waits for the witness and compiles. The default itself is taken wherever a numeral is read before its type is known, so what a call accepts follows the order of its parts: over `id(@A: Type, a: A) -> A`, `let x: Byte = id(5);` reports `Nat` against `Byte`; over `g(@A: Type, x: A, pin: A)` and `b: Byte`, `g(5, b)` is refused where `g(b, 5)` compiles; and `h([5], b)` over `h(@A: Type, xs: List(A), pin: A)` is refused as `g(5, b)` is. The call walk parks a function literal, a tuple and a list whose domain is blocked and checks each once it is not (`elaborate_apply_awaiting`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/apply.rs`), and a numeral is none of the three; `elaborate_num_lit`'s reason for realizing at once, that waiting strands what needs the type, does not weigh those parked forms. Fix: an expectation stuck on a metavariable is treated as the bare one is, the closing `expect` then parking the comparison. It keeps the default where it stands, so under a `Byte` carrier `f1(5, true)` is a mismatch where `f1(@Bool, 5, true)` compiles. **Not decided:** whether a numeral waits as the parked forms do, realized at what its domain becomes and at its default only where nothing pins it, which admits every call above and changes where every numeral argument of `/std` commits its type, unmeasured. Check: `f1(5, true)` compiles, and a carrier the default does not meet is still refused. Size: quick as stated; unknown for the numeral that waits.
- **A call's spine solves no implicit where the function it applies is universe-polymorphic.** `compare_same_global_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) decides two applications of one definition by their spines ahead of reduction, and declines a head that carries an instance where either side holds a metavariable, by a stated restriction: the solving stays with the heads it was measured on. So over `let make(@c: Bool, h: Wrap(c)) -> Nat = 0;`, with `let Wrap(c: Bool) -> Type = match c | true => List(Type) | false => Nat end;` and `let given: Wrap(true) = [Nat];`, `make(given)` is refused, `cannot decide a postponed conversion` between `List(Type)` and `Wrap(?)`, `never solved: the implicit argument 'c' of '/make'`, and with `List(Nat)` in the arm and `[1]` given, which leaves `Wrap` no level, it is accepted at `make(@true, given)`: whether a call infers its implicit follows a level the definition's body holds and the argument never reads. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** where the approximation belongs. Lifting the restriction is not the fix: tried at `8a3227187`, `/std` no longer elaborates, `/std/List/sort_by/push_layered` refused as a mismatch between `Layered(@T, second)(rest)` and `Layered(@T, second)([Option/none, ..rest])`, an implicit solved where two spines agreed and the declaration's other argument gives `rest`; which application's spine solved it was not traced. A monomorphic head commits the same way today: over `let rest(xs: List(Nat)) -> List(Nat) = match xs | [] => [] | [_, ..t] => t end;`, `induct Tagged: (List(Nat)) -> Type | tag(xs: List(Nat)): (xs) end`, `both(@xs: List(Nat), a: Tagged(rest(xs)), b: Tagged(xs))` and `first: Tagged(rest([1, 2]))`, `both(first, Tagged/tag([9, 2]))` reports `Tagged([9, 2])` against `Tagged([1, 2])`, and compiles with `b` declared ahead of `a`. Two spines that agree solve an implicit where they are met, and a function that is not injective has other solutions, so the verdict follows the order of the parameters under either head. Fix direction: the approximation as the last resort of an item's drain, after every settle (`drain_parked`, `curios-elab/src/typing.rs`), over the raw spellings a parked problem keeps, under a monomorphic head and an instance alike; it then solves only what would otherwise be refused. Not known: what moving the monomorphic case there changes across `/std`, and whether anything that cannot park needs the solution sooner. It leaves a witness slot to resolution, as the entry below on a witness goal states. Check: the two programs as fixtures in `curios-elab`'s `convert/solve_tests.rs`, `both` accepted in either order of its parameters, and the image `/std` elaborates to compared before and after. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a level conflict that a callee's scheme closes is reported with no position and no callee.** `UniverseSolver::instantiate_at` (`curios-elab/src/universe_solver.rs`) restates a scheme's constraints at an occurrence with no span, its comment leaving the position to the term being elaborated, and a caller refused by a bound its callee's scheme carried was told `this Type would need to be strictly below itself` with the constraint, under `while elaborating` alone: no line, and no mention of the callee. Not known: a program that reaches it, the one that did being a declaration whose scheme kept the order of a call's arguments, which it keeps no longer; and whether the conflict is raised at the call and loses its position, or where the declaration's levels close, past every term. Fix: a constraint an occurrence instantiates keeps that occurrence's span and the name it instantiated, and the report states both. Check: a scheme with a bound its caller breaks, reported at the call with the callee named. Size: small; `instantiate` and its callers take the occurrence.
- **An equation's sides are run twice.** A claim `Eq()(lhs, rhs)` proved by `Eq/refl()` costs twice what the same fact costs as a decision, `Holds(…)` proved by `True/qed()`: over a parse of 32 letters bound by a declaration of its own, 145 790 units against 73 614 at `85da54b97`, each the least budget the program is accepted under (`parse_claim` and `over_read` in `curios/src/tests/reduction.rs` build the pair). The closed machine evaluates an argument before it applies the function, a family's among them, so reducing the expected type (`lift_on_check`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`) runs both sides to form a type nothing has asked the indices of, and conversion then runs the sides it is handed as written; the kernel pays the same. Leaving a family's operands as written halves the cost and changes the spelling conversion reads, which [the closed machine binds a proof unreduced](../../design/compilation/the-closed-machine-binds-a-proof-unreduced.md) rejects. Fix: what a closed run answers kept past the run, by the closed term, so the second asking is a lookup. Check: the two claims cost each checker alike. Size: medium.
- **A fixture's comment gives as the mechanism the raw substitution the walk does not make.** The comment over `postponed_lambda_projecting_by_label_elaborates` (`curios/src/tests/inference/postponement_tests.rs`) says a postponed argument keeps its raw surface spelling where `elaborate_apply` opens the rest of the telescope, that the spelling is load-bearing, and that the result `expect` runs best-effort through it before its authoritative pass. The walk opens the telescope through the argument's placeholder, and `curios-elab/README.md` (Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution) rejects that best-effort pass by name. Fix: the comment says what the fixture holds: a label projection in a postponed lambda's body is resolved on the checked form, and no raw body reaches the call's result type. Check: the comment read beside `elaborate_apply_awaiting` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/apply.rs`). Size: quick.
- **A witness goal whose key is stuck on a metavariable under a match is refused where one headed by a metavariable waits.** `resolve_witness` (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`) parks a goal while a parameter of its concept application is flexible, and reads that with `flex_head`, which answers for an unsolved metavariable and for an application of one. A key that is a reduction waiting on one — a match or a projection stuck on it, which `stuck_on_metavar` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) reads as the same fact — goes on to the table with no head to key by, and the goal is refused where its slot is minted. Over `let Pick(b: Bool) -> Type = match b | true => Nat | false => Str end;`, `induct Flag: (Bool) -> Type | flag(b: Bool): (b) end` and `let same_at(@b: Bool, use Key(Pick(b)), at: Flag(b), key: Pick(b)) -> Bool = Key/same(key, key);`, with `Key(Nat)` registered, `same_at(Flag/flag(true), 2)` reports `no witness of Key(Pick(?)) found`, one argument before the one that gives `b`. `same_at(@true, Flag/flag(true), 2)` compiles, and so does the first call where the premise is declared after `at`: what a call needs written follows the order of its callee's parameters. Fix: a parameter stuck on an unsolved metavariable gates the goal as a flexible head does. The wait leaves the slot open while the arguments after it are checked, so the fix also settles what may solve it: where an argument's type names a dictionary under a definition that drops its premise — `let Shelf(b: Bool, use Key(Pick(b))) -> Type = List(Pick(b));`, a value at `Shelf(true, use never)` — the spine approximation (`compare_same_global_apply`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) would solve the slot to `never`, where the same call with `@true` written takes the registered dictionary and converts. The approximation leaves a witness slot unsolved, a solved slot being an answer only where its type's former keeps the dictionary apart ([Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md)). Both halves were tried at `8a3227187` and not landed. With the gate alone, `on_shelf(@b: Bool, use Key(Pick(b)), at: Shelf(b), key: Pick(b))` over that `Shelf` and `shelved: Shelf(true, use never)` answers `false` at `on_shelf(shelved, 2)` beside `true` at `on_shelf(@true, shelved, 2)`, two dictionaries in two spellings of one call. With the approximation also passing over an open slot at a `use` position and handing the pair on to reduction with what its other positions solved, both answer `true`, `same_at(Flag/flag(true), 2)` compiles, and the image `/std` elaborates to is the same bytes; a slot a type former holds is still solved where the reduced types are compared. Not known: what the whole suite says of the pair, its run having been stopped before its tests. Check: the three calls as fixtures in `curios/src/tests/concepts/premise_tests.rs`, each accepted, and the two spellings of the call over `Shelf` running under one dictionary. Size: small.
- **In the elaborator, a match that takes a proposition into data reduces its proof where its scrutinee holds an unsolved metavariable.** `stated` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`) answers nothing for a scrutinee, or a scrutinee's type, that still holds one, and the match takes the ordinary rule, which reduces the proof to its constructor. Nothing is refused by it, and no term the kernel is handed holds one. Fix: leave such a match parked on the metavariable, as a match whose scrutinee's type is stuck on one is. Check: a count of the ordinary rule taking a proposition's sole arm over `/std` at zero, taken in a scratch copy by a line appended there.
