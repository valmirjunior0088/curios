# Theory: findings

- **A parked conversion watches its two sides and not its type.** `Solutions::park` (`curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`) computes a conversion goal's watch set from the metavariables of its two sides, so a goal postponed because the type it is compared at is unsolved — an implicit against a lambda at `?A` — is woken when a side's metavariable is solved and not when `?A` is, and waits for the drain's final sweep. Nothing is refused by it. Fix: the watch set takes the unsolved metavariables of the goal's type as well. It wakes more goals per solution, so what it costs across `/std` is taken first. Check: a fixture in `curios-elab`'s `context::tests` parking such a goal and waking it by its type. Size: quick.

Not known: what proposes a fact in scope where a refutation is asked for, and whether the rejection is a search doing its work or a candidate built wrong. Fix: once that is known. Check: the count, read again. Size: unknown.

Not known: a program that reaches it, the one that did being a declaration whose scheme kept the order of a call's arguments, which it keeps no longer; and whether the conflict is raised at the call and loses its position, or where the declaration's levels close, past every term. Fix: a constraint an occurrence instantiates keeps that occurrence's span and the name it instantiated, and the report states both. Check: a scheme with a bound its caller breaks, reported at the call with the callee named. Size: small; `instantiate` and its callers take the occurrence.

Nothing is refused by it, and no term the kernel is handed holds one. Fix: leave such a match parked on the metavariable, as a match whose scrutinee's type is stuck on one is.
