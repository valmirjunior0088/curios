# Theory: findings

- **The certifier refuses by its budget two instances of a recursive witness's method apart in a level no unfolding holds, where the elaborator identifies the two.** A hand-written `Spell` witness of a recursive `Tree(A)` whose `node` arm concatenates its subtrees' spellings, compared through a definition that spells as it does (`shown(t) = Spell/spell(t)` and `Eq()(shown(t), Spell/spell(t))` by `Eq/refl()`), elaborates and is refused by the certifier at `/same`, its budget spent (3.5 s at `--budget 200000`, at `75dcef914`). The elaborated `same` states three levels under `u <= v` and `u <= w` alone, its sides `shown@[w](@A, use x0, t)` and `(witness@[v](@A, use x0)).0(t)` (`--stage core-elab`): the elaborator held them one by identifying `w` and `v` at a position the witness's value does not hold, which commits nothing (`identify_universe_levels`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`). The certifier compares two calls of one definition before unfolding only where the head is a global, a call of one, or a group's projection (`one_definition_by_its_spines`, `rec_instances`, `curios-cert/src/certifier/convert.rs`), a global's instances by `universes_eq`, and the levels a value does not hold only at a group's projection and a family's node (`held_pairs`, `instances_eq`). A method read off a call of the witness is none of these, its two instances differ in a level, and both sides unfold, each unfolding opening fresh binders, so no pair meets again. So the two checkers' conversion is not one relation here ([Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../../design/soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md)). Fix: the certifier compares a call along its path, a field of a call of one definition as the call itself (`Subterm::inward`), and two instances of one definition at the levels its value holds, as the elaborator identifies them, read through the analysis both drivers share; the decision states it. Check: a certifier test of both witnesses, the program above checking in under a second, a near miss apart at a held level still refused, `curios-analysis`'s driven suites, `cargo xtask workspace lint`. Size: two commits.
- **Reading the levels a recursive group's unfolding holds climbs a group again for every walk that opens it at binders of its own, so a chain of derived witnesses costs twice the climbs a link.** Over a chain of `n` families, each holding the next, each with a derived `Spell` witness, compared through a definition that spells as it does, `group_held` (`curios-analysis/src/variance.rs`) climbs 2, 6, 14, 30, 62 and 126 groups at `n` from 1 to 6, `2^(n+1) - 2`, at `2d608d7ba`, and elaboration takes 0.2 s at `n = 4`, 11 s at 8 and over 60 s at 16. Traced on 2026-11-20 by writing out each group climbed at `n = 4`: the 30 are 8 up to a renaming of the locals they name, the rest one of those opened by another walk at fresh binders (`Free(Mint { .. })`), while a climb's readings are kept by the group as it stands (`Climbs::settled`), so each is a new key. Fix: the climbs keyed by a group closed over the locals it names, in the order it names them, with their types, so a group opened at other binders reads the reading already taken, and the levels each group holds are unchanged. Check: a chain growth in `curios`'s `tests::growth`, linear; the derived-witness test of `tests::universes::instance` within its budget; `cargo xtask workspace lint`, which reads every group of `/std`. Size: one commit.
