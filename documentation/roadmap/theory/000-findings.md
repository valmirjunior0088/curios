# Theory: findings

- **Reading the levels a recursive group's unfolding holds climbs a group again for every walk that opens it at binders of its own, so a chain of derived witnesses costs twice the climbs a link.** Over a chain of `n` families, each holding the next, each with a derived `Spell` witness, compared through a definition that spells as it does, `group_held` (`curios-analysis/src/variance.rs`) climbs 2, 6, 14, 30, 62 and 126 groups at `n` from 1 to 6, `2^(n+1) - 2`, at `2d608d7ba`, and elaboration takes 0.2 s at `n = 4`, 11 s at 8 and over 60 s at 16. Traced on 2026-11-20 by writing out each group climbed at `n = 4`: the 30 are 8 up to a renaming of the locals they name, the rest one of those opened by another walk at fresh binders (`Free(Mint { .. })`), while a climb's readings are kept by the group as it stands (`Climbs::settled`), so each is a new key. Fix: the climbs keyed by a group closed over the locals it names, in the order it names them, with their types, so a group opened at other binders reads the reading already taken, and the levels each group holds are unchanged. Check: a chain growth in `curios`'s `tests::growth`, linear; the derived-witness test of `tests::universes::instance` within its budget; `cargo xtask workspace lint`, which reads every group of `/std`. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: the elaborator restates the equations in force innermost frame first, where the certifier restates them in the order it recorded them, so the two number an arm's restated instances in opposite orders.** `Context::restate_equations` (`curios-elab/src/context/scope.rs`) walks `Frames::scrutinee_equations`, innermost frame first, numbering each instance it registers in the arm's frame in that order, where `Scope::restate` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/scope.rs`) pushes them outermost first. A frame's equations are heights of their own, and an instance's own spelling sees the ones registered before it, so under two guards an arm on their variable restates together, each checker's own spelling of one instance sees the other where the other checker's does not. A probe in the arm reads both past their own heights, settled again with each other in view, so the rows over `first` and `second` among `curios/src/tests/respelling.rs`'s nested cells hold in both nestings; what is not known is whether a settlement nested at a height between the two instances, one equation short, reaches a verdict in one checker the other does not. Settled by such a program, then the elaborator's instances numbered in recorded order, outermost frame first, with it as their test; or by dropping this entry where none can be built. Size: unknown.
- **A type position reaches every definition it names, where a checker unfolds only what reduction forces, so a type naming a loop it never applies is refused.** Obligation (T) of `documentation/design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md` refuses a type that reaches a definition not known to terminate, reading the reach by mention (`curios-elab/src/totality/reach.rs`), and a definition that mentions a partial one is partial itself. Two sites show it refusing what no reduction forces. With `let spin(n: Nat) -> Io(Nat) = Io/bind(Io/yield(), (()) => spin(n));`, the parameter type `Vec(Io(Nat), List/len(List/map([1, 2, 3], spin)))` is refused ("it reaches `spin`"), though `List/len` over a map reduces to its list's length "whatever `f` does" (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic.rs`), and the same type formed by instantiating `counted(work: List(Io(Nat)), v: Vec(Io(Nat), List/len(work)))` at `List/map([1, 2, 3], spin)` is accepted, so the verdict follows the spelling; a result counted by its list, `all(@A: Type, actions: List(Io(A))) -> Io(Vec(A, List/len(actions)))`, is refused by it at every caller that maps an action that loops — a send, a receive, a read — `/std/Tui`'s `running` and a fail-fast `Try/all` among them. And over `concept Joint(T: Type): Type { Answers: Type, run(T) -> Io(Answers) }` witnessed at `{Io(A), Io(B)}` with `Answers = {A, B}` and `run(t) = Io/both(t.0, t.1)`, a call of `let all_of(@T: Type, use Joint(T), actions: T) -> Io(Joint/Answers(@T))` is refused ("it reaches `/witness~/Joint({_, _})`"), where the field the type reads is `{A, B}`. Of the decision's two reasons for the aggressive reading, one no longer holds: the precise reading "buys nothing (V) does not already refuse", and it buys these. The other, that a total type-level function applied to a partial value reties the knot (`Shape(inf)`), stands against reading what a type scrutinizes, not against holding a loop folded. Settled by the reading [Lean](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/4.26.0/Definitions/Definitions/) (a `partial def` is an opaque constant, never δ-reduced), [Agda](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/language/termination-checking.html) (a `NON_TERMINATING` function does not reduce during type checking) and [Idris](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.07978) (a partial function is not evaluated during type checking) share: neither checker unfolds a definition whose own recursion does not descend, an inline group's included, so `Shape(inf)` and `Bad` stay folded and no knot is tied; (T) narrows to the declaration being judged, whose verdict comes only after its elaboration; and a definition that merely mentions such a root unfolds as any other. Lean asks an opaque constant's type to be inhabited because a proof could use it, which is (V)'s job here. Not yet known: whether (V) reaches through a proof's type arguments, as a bound discharged over a mapped list asks it to; and what the decision's "the expression would have returned" says of an erased type naming a folded loop. Size: unknown, past two commits — both checkers' reduction, the verdict the analysis publishes, the decision and the knot tests of `curios/src/tests/rules/totality_partiality.rs`.
