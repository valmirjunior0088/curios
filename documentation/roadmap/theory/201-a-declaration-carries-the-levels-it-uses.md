# A declaration carries the levels it uses

A declaration's universe parameters are the levels its signature holds where conversion compares two instances of it, read by the one alignment conversion reads two terms by ([A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it stands](../../design/theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-stands.md)). Two readings hold a level no two instances can be told apart by: a field read through a definition, and a top-level group's member that the members holding a level never reach back.

## What is wrong

**A field read through a definition holds every level of the definition.** The alignment reads a projection's head as it reads any definition applied in full, by that definition's vector, and a record-valued definition's vector holds every level its literal mentions, the literal's own instance among them. A concept with a sort-valued member is invariant in that member's level, so each of its witnesses is polymorphic there. `/std/ops/Div` and `Rem` state `Ok(A) -> Prop`, and an operator elaborates `n / 256` to `(witness@Div(Nat).{u}).1(n, 256, …)`, which holds `u` in every signature it stands in where the field it projects, `Nat`'s `div`, holds no level: `let one(n: Nat) -> Eq()(n / 256, n / 256) = Eq/refl();` takes two parameters, and the same equation over `n / 256 + m / 16` four. Over `/std` at `34f5e7600`, 15 parameters of 12 definitions are held there and nowhere else, each a `Nat`, `Int` or `Bytes` lemma over `/` or `%` (`Nat/Gcd/euclid`, `Nat/DivMod/unique`, `Int/shl/unique`, …). Counted: `universe_parameter_census` printed at the commit and again with stage 1's reading built in a copy of the tree, the two joined by name; only those 12 lines differ, each witness instance now at `.{0}`, and `/std` still certifies. Conversion reads the pair as the signature does, so two such projections apart in that level alone are identified by an equation where both levels are open; the kernel forces both sides to the field before it compares their heads. A method named by its path elaborates to its wrapper applied to the witness, and the alignment reads that dictionary argument whole: over `concept Sized(T: Type): Type { Carrier: Type }` and `satisfy Sized(Nat) { Carrier = Str }`, `pub let named(x: Sized/Carrier(@Nat)) -> Nat = 0;` takes one parameter where the type is `Str`. No `/std` signature holds a level that way.

**A top-level group's member carries the levels of members it does not reach.** A group is monomorphic in its levels, two instances of one of its members being a level question in both checkers ([Conversion recurrence](../../design/soundness/conversion-recurrence.md)), and a recursive member carries no vector. So `let alone(n: Nat) -> Nat = n and generic(A: Type, n: Nat) -> Nat = alone(n);` hands `alone` the level `generic`'s parameter is typed at, and `let about(n: Nat) -> Eq()(alone(n), alone(n)) = Eq/refl();` takes it as a parameter. A family's member carries such a level too, `Pause.{u}` in `induct Pause: Type | yield_now() and Step(A: Type): Type | done(A) | step(pause: Pause) end`, and since the family's vector calls it irrelevant, every occurrence settles at the least level: no signature carries it, and each occurrence mints one to settle. Of `/std`'s 270 top-level recursive items at `34f5e7600`, a declaration that names itself among them, one is not a cycle: `/std/Html`'s `render` beside `render_tree` and `render_all`, which never reach it, at no level. Counted over the archive: an edge from one member to another where the other is among what the first's definition or registry entry reaches, the edges `Module::ordered` reads, and a group a cycle where every member reaches every other. Outside the archive, `curios-std/src/tests/big_nat/ord.crs` groups `raw_to_eq` with `ord_head_eq`, which never reaches it.

## Settled

- **A position is read as written, by the one alignment the signature and conversion share.** Nothing is reduced where a signature is read. What a field holds is filed with its definition where the definition's levels close, as a definition's vector is, and read wherever the alignment meets that field. A field is read as its definition is, as written, beneath the field's own binders: a record-valued definition answers no type, so its fields are a value's parts. A reading at the signature alone, the field taken by one unfolding there, would be the second statement of which positions conversion compares that the universe decision rejects.
- **Members of one cycle share their levels.** A member that reaches another takes that member's levels under any scheme, so in a cycle a level is every member's: `/std`'s parser groups (`Json/decode`, `Html/parse`, `Toml/values`) keep the level of the parser one member names. A level the members of a cycle hold for one another is one conversion tells their instances apart by, under the rule that two instances of a group are a level question; what would release it is a vector on a recursive member read by both checkers or level-polymorphic recursion, each a change to the trusted base that no program here asks for. A context per member, the group opened at each member's own instance, removes a level from exactly the members a group that is one cycle never holds, at the cost of `RecGroup`'s representation and `rec_instances` in both checkers.
- **A top-level group that is no cycle is refused, not split.** A top-level declaration reads any other wherever it is written, so at the top `and` does one thing, tie declarations that need one another. Splitting such a group silently turns one written item into several, where the converse case is refused and the report names the way out. The report reads: `'render', 'render_tree' and 'render_all' are grouped, and do not all need one another`, then that a group ties declarations that need one another, each reading another of them before it can finish, and a declaration reads any other wherever it is written, then `declare each part on its own: 'render' alone, and 'render_tree' and 'render_all' as one group`. A local group, which also orders the values a knot forces by need, is not held to it.
- **A dictionary a family takes as an argument is held.** `Parse`'s node keeps its `use Input(I)` argument and `Parse` is invariant in `Input`'s proposition level, so a parser at one level is no parser at another, and the level a `/std` parser's signature takes through `/std/Parse/text`'s witness stays.
- **A function's body is read as written.** An application stuck on a binder keeps the motives its body states, so two instances apart in a level those name are two terms; a value's levels read by its reduct would release nothing a stuck call does not show.
- **Stage 3 waits for a consumer**: no `/std` signature names a method by its path at a level.

## Stages

1. **A field holds what its value holds.** A definition whose value beneath its own binders is a struct or tuple literal carries, per field, which of its levels that field's value holds, beside `Definition::variances` and archived with it, since the witnesses a program's operators project are `/std`'s; the store's schema tag moves with the layout. `Families` answers it, and `Term::level_differences` reads a projection of such a definition applied in full by that field's vector, then the definition's arguments. Conversion then records a weak equation where it identified, and the universe decision's sentence on irrelevant positions names a field of a definition applied in full beside a family and a definition. Check: `one` and `two` take no parameter; `universe_parameter_census` moves the 15 parameters above and nothing else; `/std` certifies; two projections apart in a level their field drops convert in both checkers, and two apart in a level it holds are compared, as fixtures in `curios-elab`'s `convert/variance_tests.rs` and `curios-cert`'s `kernel/convert/variance_tests.rs`.
2. **A top-level group is one cycle.** A top-level group whose members do not all reach one another is refused, naming its parts, beside the refusal of two that need one another ungrouped; the two refusals together make a top-level group exactly a cycle of what its members reach. The check reads each member's type, body and registry entry once they are elaborated, before the group's levels close, so a witness group's resolutions count. `/std/Html`'s `render` and `ord.crs`'s `ord_head_eq` are declared apart. Check: `alone`'s group, `Pause`'s and a witness group whose second member resolves through nothing of the first are refused with the report above, each accepted once split, and `documentation/syntax.md`'s Recursive groups states the rule.
3. **A method applied to its dictionary is read as the field it projects**, as a former applied in full is read as the node it builds, so an operator and a method named by its path are one reading. Check: `named` takes no parameter. Waits for a consumer.

## Retirement

When the three stages land, the universe decision states the field reading, `documentation/syntax.md` and [A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md) the rule for a top-level group, and this specification is deleted.
