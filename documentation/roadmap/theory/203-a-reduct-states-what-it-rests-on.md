# A reduct states what it rests on

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves what would let a reduction table keep a reduct across a boundary it is cleared at today. It is not an implementation plan.

## What is missing

**The reducts are dropped whole at every boundary.** `Caches` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) clears the reduction tables wherever the environment a reduct was taken in changes, a guard registered, a frame left, a settlement's two sides, a suppression's, a retry's, and wherever a rollback, a redefinition or a rewrite of levels may reach one. Over `/std`'s elaboration, 62.3 million units, at least 6.3 million reduce a term again to the reduct a clear had just dropped: 3.45 million after a settlement's boundary, 0.94 after a guard's registration, 0.75 after a frame's exit, 0.43 after a retry's, 0.35 after a rewrite of levels, 0.34 after a rollback; in the declarations that spend most it is 40 to 70 in the hundred (`/std/Hash/nat/of_lt`, `/std/Str/Valid/encoded`, `/std/Flt/signals/fma`). Counted by keeping each dropped reduct aside with the clear that dropped it and filing the units a second reduction of its term to the same reduct took, once; a reduct that came back another was rightly dropped, 2,020 of them. The finding these were filed in, at `be25bfdbe`, names no commit for them, so they are the first figures to take again where this is refined.

**What an elaborated term spells follows when they were dropped.** Filing a settlement's reducts under the environment it runs in, the standing ones put back when it ends, took `/std` to 61.0 million units and the proof of `/std/time/Timestamp/Utc/of_timestamp` as a report prints it from 18 million to 12. It also moved four declarations' terms, `/std/List/sort_by/merge_leads` to one holding six recursive groups inline where it held `merge` by name; leaving out the clears of a retry whose context is the live one moves four, and those of a witness's closing four. So a clear does more than its comment says. A reduct rests on what conversion could answer when it was taken, which grows as solutions and level equations land, and nothing records which reducts rest on it; and a solution is spelled from whichever reduct the table held.

**Closing a witness's universe instance clears every cache and forgets every settled spelling**, where only what was written since its levels were minted can name them. `Context::close_universe_instance` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`), which `resolve_witness` runs where the scheme it took minted levels, ends in `Caches::invalidate_for_universe_rewrite` and `Frames::forget_spellings`: once for every operator over a universe-polymorphic concept, each of which then meets its guards unsettled and its reducts gone. With neither run there, a function of 79 operators under two guards (`infix_under_a_guard_takes_its_witness_where_it_stands`, `curios/src/tests/operators.rs`) answers inside 30,000 units where it takes 80,000, and the printed proof of `of_timestamp` inside 750,000 where it takes 18 million of a declaration's 30; over `/std`'s elaboration the saving is 1 in the hundred, the library holding little arithmetic under guards. Removing the two calls is not sound: the reduct table's second door (`Caches::reduction_erased`) matches two spellings by their level metavariables, which an assignment changes. While the consuming declaration is open the closing is `UniverseSolver::pin_instance` alone, which assigns the levels minted for that one resolution, so what could name them is what was written since they were minted. The two calls removed move four declarations' terms (`/std/Async/attempt`, `/std/Nat/Coprime/quotients`, `/std/tcp/Listener/served`, `/std/Int/shl/unique`). Measured in a copy of the tree at `022931b7f`; the selective form is not built.

## Refinement

What a reduct rests on beyond its term and the environment it was taken in, stated so that a table could keep one across a boundary, as a settled spelling states it of itself (`Spelling`, `curios-elab/src/context/frames.rs`). Whether forgetting only what was written since a resolution's levels were minted leaves `/std`'s image as it stands: a mark taken where `instantiate` (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`) mints the levels, and at a pin the reducts, the elaborations and the settled spellings written past it forgotten, the rest left, the function above then answering inside 40,000 units. Every step that keeps a reduct longer moves the image, so each lands with the declarations it moves named. Until this is stated, [A retry decides its problem in the context it froze](101-a-retry-decides-its-problem-in-the-context-it-froze.md) tells the caches what a retry tells them today.
