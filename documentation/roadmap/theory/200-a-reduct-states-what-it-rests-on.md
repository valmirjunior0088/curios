# A reduct states what it rests on

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves what would let a reduction table keep a reduct, and a classing table a verdict, across a boundary it is cleared at today. It is not an implementation plan.

## Refinement

What a classing's verdict rests on, said more finely than the two write stamps say it, so that one stands across a write that does not reach it; checked by the `classes` row of `runaway_measurements`, the program's units at 62 and at 125, and the counts over `/std`.

What a reduct rests on beyond its term and the environment it was taken in, stated so that a table could keep one across a boundary, as a settled spelling states it of itself (`Spelling`, `curios-elab/src/context/frames.rs`). Whether forgetting only what was written since a resolution's levels were minted leaves `/std`'s image as it stands: a mark taken where `instantiate` (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`) mints the levels, and at a pin the reducts, the elaborations and the settled spellings written past it forgotten, the rest left, the function above then answering inside 40,000 units. Every step that keeps a reduct longer moves the image, so each lands with the declarations it moves named. Until this is stated, a judgment standing on held frames tells the caches what a context rebuilt for it told them (`Context::standing_on`, `curios-elab/src/context.rs`).
