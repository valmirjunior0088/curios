# A term is admitted whichever of its parts is read first

Working specification for who takes a choice the elaborator has to make where nothing forces an answer: the solution a flex application suggests. It is held to [Subsumption is a relation, not a traversal order](../../design/theory/subsumption-is-a-relation-not-a-traversal-order.md), which has a metavariable met at a sort take a level of its own, so that the order of a call's arguments, of a list's elements, or of an argument and the expectation decides nothing, and to [A literal is realized by its expected type](../../design/surface/a-literal-is-realized-by-its-expected-type.md). The code keeps that rule for a sort's level, for a `use` slot's witness, for a numeral's default, for a tuple literal's shape, for a lambda's written type and for what two spines would solve, and for no other choice: every other one is taken where the walk first reads it.

## What this builds on

- **A term awaits what it reads its parts against, and completes.** A call, a list literal, a structure literal, a match, an operator and an intrinsic's or a foreign call's operand walk are each read inside `Context::awaiting` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`). A metavariable born meanwhile is awaited by that term, a call adopting the holes its head's spine leaves open, and a solution or a parked problem that links two joins the classes they are held in, a class being awaited whole (`Solutions::birth_with_kind`, `solve` and `park`, `curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`, the three doors every birth, solution and parked problem passes). Where the term has read its parts, `Context::complete` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) takes the choices left among the problems it awaits, each stated by the problem itself (`ParkedWork::choice`), one at a time and the lowest rank first; the item completes as the outermost term does, before its levels close (`Context::sweep_parked`), and its drain reports what is left. A `use` slot nothing solved takes the witness resolution finds for its goal (`answer_witness`, `curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`): a goal whose slot a term awaits parks where the walk reaches it (`attempt_witness_goal`), a wake resolves nothing and drops a goal whose slot unification solved (`retry_witness`), and a completing term answers every slot it awaits in one pass, as an item does before its levels close (`Context::sweep_parked`, `curios-elab/src/typing.rs`). A tuple literal parked on a bare metavariable gives it its shape (`Context::shape`): a non-dependent product of one open type a field, at which the literal is checked, a literal among its fields shaped and a lambda whose every domain is annotated typed in their turn (`Context::structure`), before the product is held to the metavariable, so that every other literal waiting on it is checked against what the first left open. The numerals that wait on one type take one default (`numeral_default`), a numeral whose type a term awaits having parked (`elaborate_num_lit`). A lambda parked on a bare metavariable whose every domain is annotated is checked at the type its annotations and body state (`Context::settle`). The conversions the term awaits are then asked again with spines held (`Context::hold_awaited_spines`): each first offers what that would solve, nothing committed, two that give one metavariable two answers refuse the term (`answers_differ`, `Error::AmbiguousSpines`), and each is then held and leaves the store, what it left undecided waiting in its place (`HeldConversion`). And last a lambda with a domain left open is checked: at the function type its expectation has come to, or, where it has come to none, at the type its annotations and body state, the domain standing as a metavariable named after its binder and awaited by the term completing. A lambda's body is read at a codomain left open, born under its binders and awaited the same way (`elaborate_func_infer`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`), so a choice the body owns waits for that term. A literal that waits leaves its type open behind it, and three readers answer through that: an open type is sized by the level its own sort states (`Sort::of`, `curios-elab/src/convert/sort.rs`), so a former over it carries that level to whatever it is handed to; a neutral headed by a metavariable is typed by the type the metavariable was born at (`synth_neutral`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`), so an operator's bound is a proposition before its witness is resolved; and a folded recursive call with a stuck argument blocks a conversion as it blocks a check (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`, `curios-elab/src/convert/occurrence.rs`).
- **Two spines decide a pair only where they agree with nothing solved.** `compare_same_global_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) accepts two applications of one definition whose spines convert as they stand, as the kernel does. A pair whose spines hold a metavariable and do not is decided by what it reduces to, as a run of its own, which solves what it forces; where that leaves it undecided the pair parks whole, under its raw spelling, so that its two calls are there for the term that awaits it. A type former's pair is no such one: its node keeps its arguments, and each waits as what it is, so a goal under `Eq` is reported by the argument it has to make true. `hold_spines` asks a parked conversion again with spines held (`Context::holding_spines`): every such pair inside it, at any depth and under an instance head as under a monomorphic one, agrees by whatever that solves, a `use` slot still open passed over.
- **An application of a metavariable is imitated only where it is asked for.** `imitate_flex_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) commits `?m := (x) => T(b, x)` for `?m(a)` met against `T(b, i)`, the right-biased partial application, where `Context::imitates` holds: a completing term is holding spines, or a `!` is reading its region's monad (`Context::imitating`, around `region_monad`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`). Anywhere else the pair blocks as it is written and waits for the term that awaits it, so a part read later that states the function answers it. An imitation commits its head and pairs its arguments with the node's, and a pair between two open metavariables waits: a held conversion that solved anything keeps it, converted or not, and what it left stands in its place.
- **Conversion solves no metavariable to another.** Two unsolved metavariables met against each other park as a pair (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`, "flex–flex does no intersection"; [Flex–flex problems with distinct heads](202-flex-flex-intersection.md)), and a candidate that holds an unsolved metavariable commits where that one's context is contained in the solved one's, or goes past it only by binders the candidate binds itself at that occurrence (`Context::metavar_bound_by_candidate`), which is what a settled lambda's codomain is in the function type the lambda states. So what holds an inner term's metavariable to an enclosing term's is a solution or a parked conversion.
- **A `use` slot unification solves keeps its dictionary's name.** Met against a witness's name the slot is committed as written (`Convert::solve_at_birth`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), where a metavariable of any other origin is committed reduced, so a slot an argument's type solves holds the global that type names and no copy of its methods.

## The gap

Every program this section held compiles in either order of its parts, each a fixture. What is left is the code the missing owner made necessary, which the first stage removes.

## Decisions

A choice is an answer nothing forces: one of several a problem admits. What unification forces is the same whichever problem is met first; a choice is not, so it is taken only where everything that could bear on it has been read, and the decisions below say where that is, who knows it, and in what order choices are taken.

**A metavariable is awaited by a term, and the store keeps by which.** A term awaits where it reads its parts against types that hold a metavariable: a call, its implicits and the holes its head's spine leaves open; a list literal, its element type; a structure literal, its parameters; a match with an elided motive; an operator, its operand type; an intrinsic's or a foreign call's operands. A metavariable is awaited from its birth by the innermost such term being read, and by none outside one. The relation moves where a link is made and nowhere else: a committed solution holds what it mentions to what it solves, and a parked problem holds together what it watches. What links hold together is a class, and a class is awaited whole: where a link joins two, by the outermost standing term that awaited either, one no term awaited included. So a chain of links holds its ends: a numeral under two calls, whose type one parked pair holds to the middle call's implicit and another holds that to the outer call's, waits for the outer call. A term that has completed leaves what it still held awaited by none. `Solutions::birth_with_kind`, `solve` and `park` write it, with a journal `unwind_to` restores, so whether a term awaits a metavariable is read off its class and by no pass over the store. A parked problem is awaited as what it watches is, and where it is retried it runs awaited by that term while the term stands, and by none once it has completed. GHC keeps a unification variable's level the same way, promoting where the solution that links two is made.

**A term read with no expectation completes in full.** The expectation is the one way what encloses a term informs it: the turnaround links what the term awaits to what the enclosing term does, and what is linked moves outward as above. So a term that is inferred — a `let`'s value with no annotation, a scrutinee, a head — has taken every choice it owns before the next line reads it, and inference is local to a statement, as Java's, Kotlin's and Swift's is. A metavariable may outlive its statement and be solved by a later one; a choice may not.

**A term completes by one loop, taking one choice at a time.** Where a term has read its parts and met its expectation, it retries what a solution woke, takes one choice among the problems it awaits, and starts again, until none is left to take. A choice is a parked problem's own answer, and its rank is stated once on `ParkedWork`, in place of a shape matched by each consumer; the lowest rank is taken first, since each of the first five commits less than the next and may decide it, and the last reads a body, which is read blind without what the others give:

1. a `use` slot whose key is rigid takes the registered witness;
2. a tuple literal parked on a bare metavariable gives it its shape, a non-dependent product of one open type a field, labelled as the literal is: the literal is checked at the product, which is then held to the metavariable, so a second literal parked on it is checked against the first's shape and each fills what the other left open;
3. the numerals parked on one metavariable take their default together;
4. a lambda parked on a bare metavariable whose every domain is annotated is checked at the type its annotations and body state, which is written and no guess;
5. the conversions parked apart are asked again with spines held, every one the term awaits having offered what that would solve: two applications of one definition are held to spines that agree, and an application of a metavariable against a type former's node is imitated;
6. a lambda with a domain left open is checked at the function type its expectation has come to, or, where it has come to none, at the type its annotations and body state.

The item is the outermost such term: it completes where its definition is finalized, before its levels close, since a witness's scheme and an imitation's instance of its family mint levels that are the item's only until then; its drain takes what was parked since and reports what is left. Lean's pass has the same shape: resume, one default, resume.

**An open part is a type left to inference.** Each open part of a shape is born at a sort of its own and awaited by the term completing: a proposition's where the metavariable shaped is one, every field of a record of propositions being one, and its quality open otherwise. Its level is bounded from below by the field it is given and from above by the product it stands in, as a field's is where a product is synthesized from its fields; and a type over an open one is sized by the level that one's sort states, so the bound reaches whatever the product is handed to. A literal's fields are read before its product is held to the metavariable it shapes, a literal among them shaped and a lambda whose every domain is annotated typed first: a product is a proposition where every field is one, which the fields alone say. A field typed only after, a lambda with a domain left open, which a literal beside it may give, leaves the product read as a type's there, so where the metavariable was declared a `Type` the item's drain reads the product again (`Context::hold_shapes`). A sort among the fields is met by a metavariable and takes a level of its own, as one that is an argument does.

**A numeral waits for the term that awaits its type.** Checked at a type stuck on an unsolved metavariable that some term awaits, it parks as a checking problem and is realized where a solution wakes it. The numerals parked on one metavariable take a default each of them realizes at: `Int` where one is signed, `Nat` where one is an unsigned number, and `Char` only where every one is a character. Where no term awaits the type the default is taken at once, as today, and the comparison with a type stuck on a metavariable parks. An operator awaits its operand type as any term does.

**A lambda settled by a term is that term's.** Its unannotated domains are born awaited by the term completing it, so a numeral in its body that its domain reaches waits for that term and no further, and a lambda bound through a call has its type where the call completes: `z(3)` compiles, and a function type with hidden binders handed to `z` by a later line is refused, as it is for a lambda bound with no call. Its body is read at a codomain left open, awaited as its domains are, so a choice the body owns waits as one in an argument does: a numeral, a literal or a lambda that is the body, and one inside a call, an operator or a match that is. The codomain is born under the lambda's binders, so the type the body states may name them, and the function type the lambda states holds it under a binder of its own: the solver commits such a candidate, since whatever the codomain comes to arrives through that occurrence and names nothing the function type does not bind. A parameter nothing types is reported as itself: a conversion or a witness goal the item's drain is left with yields to that report where it waits on the parameter's type, itself or through the others left with it, since the codomain is held to the body's type by one parked conversion and the body's to the parameter's by another.

**A `use` slot is answered where its term completes.** The registered witness is the slot's own answer, taken where nothing solved the slot: a later argument whose type holds a dictionary names it whichever of the two the walk reaches first. A wake resolves nothing; it drops a goal whose slot unification has solved. The slots a term awaits are answered in one pass, ahead of anything they wake: two of them a parked conversion holds together are each resolution's to name, where a retry between them would hand the second the first's answer, and which of the two resolution named would follow the order they were parked in. A slot a definition drops its premise under is left to resolution as it is today, the spine approximation passing over one that is open. A dictionary's superclass is no such slot: it is the table's entry at its key, which no other field's type may name another for ([Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md)), so it is resolved where it stands (`resolve_witness_goal`). Nor is a slot no later part of its term can name: a call's whose binder no later parameter's type and no result type mentions (`Cursor::binder_used`), an operator's, whose operands are both read before it is posed, and a premise of the scheme resolution takes, which is part of that answer. Resolution is the one source such a slot has, so it is resolved where it stands too, and waits only on a key still open. What that saves is the frame: a goal parked for its term is answered under the frame it froze (`Context::with_retry_frame`), which under a guard is restored by settling the guard's scrutinee again, once a goal.

**The last resorts agree, or the call is refused.** Two spines that agree are one solution among those a function that is not injective has, and an imitation one among the functions a flex application may stand for, so neither is a rule of conversion's: each is tried over the raw spellings a parked problem keeps, where a term completes, under a monomorphic head and an instance alike. Every problem a term awaits offers its candidate before any is committed; where two candidates for one metavariable differ the call is refused, naming the implicit and both, and where they agree it is solved. Conversion keeps the congruence: two spines that agree as they stand, with no metavariable solved, are equal, as the kernel has it, and a pair that would agree only by solving one is decided by reduction, which solves what it forces: a definition that unfolds to a type former gives its arguments back, and no choice is taken for them. Where reduction leaves such a pair undecided it waits whole, under its raw spelling, and not as what reduction left of it: `Shelf(b)` unfolds to `List(Pick(b))`, and `Pick(b)` against `Nat` holds neither call. Two applications of a type former wait as their arguments do, the node having kept them. Holding is a mode conversion is asked in and no walk of one pair, so a pair inside the one parked is held as the outermost is, and it ends where an oracle begins. A `use` slot still open is passed over where spines are held, as it was: a slot a definition drops its premise under is resolution's. An imitation commits its head and pairs its arguments with the node's, and a pair between two open metavariables waits, conversion solving neither to the other: a conversion held keeps what it solved and leaves that pair in its place, the imitation being the key a witness beside it is found by. A `!` goes on reading its region's monad by imitation directly, the region being its expectation.

## Rejected

- **The relation read where a term settles, by a pass over every parked problem.** It costs a pass a settling term, and misses a problem that is out of the store being retried, whose nested terms settle meanwhile. GHC floated an equality out of its implication to the same end and replaced it with a promotion where the solution is made.
- **The relation read by a scan of the store at every question**: it answers rightly, costs a pass over the parked problems wherever a numeral asks, and misses a pair while that pair is out of the store being retried.
- **A metavariable handed outward alone where it is linked.** What an earlier link held to it stays behind: a numeral under two calls is held to the middle call's implicit by one parked pair, a second pair moves that implicit to the outer call, and the numeral takes its default where the middle call completes, so `g(id(id(5)), b)` is refused where `g(id(5), b)` compiles.
- **A depth in place of the term that awaits.** A problem retried under a term opens terms of its own beside the ones still being read where the wake found it, and two of them at one depth are two terms.
- **A term that hands outward whenever it was checked at a type still open.** It waits where nothing can arrive, and does not wait where something can: a term inferred, whose metavariable a binder's type holds to an enclosing call's.
- **Every choice held to its item's end**, as Lean holds a default instance to its region's and Rust an integer's to its function's. What reads the type next — a match, a projection, a call of `z` — meets none, so every reader has to be able to wait, which is what the parked projection and the parked match are there to survive; and a value stands in the types after it here, where a numeral not yet realized is a placeholder conversion waits on.
- **One constraint system an expression, solved whole**, as Swift does: its search is exponential in the worst case, Swift bounds it to a statement and has a closure's statements read one way, and a dependent telescope opens each domain through the arguments before it, which the walk does as it goes and a collected system would have to replay.
- **An open part at the level of the metavariable it shapes.** Re-validating the product gives each part still open a level above the shared one, which closes a cycle through a maximum that the entry's level closing (`UniverseSolver::default`) does not settle where a field holds a sort; and it saves nothing, a part's own level being internal and closing at its field's.
- **A product held to the metavariable it shapes before its fields are read.** It is sized as a type's whatever it comes to hold: a tuple of proofs is refused where a proposition is declared, and admitted where a type is, for the kernel to refuse.
- **A settled lambda's codomain born where its domains are, outside its binders.** It names none of them, so a body whose type does is refused: `id((A: Type, x: A) => x)` stops compiling.
- **A codomain left open only where the body is itself a numeral, a literal or a lambda.** A call, an operator or a match that is the body still completes in full, its choices with it: `g((x: Byte) => id(0), (y: Byte) => b)` stays refused in one order.
- **A numeral as a fourth form the call walk parks.** A numeral under a nested call, in a list, a structure literal or a match arm still commits where it is read.
- **Literals whose type involves no choice settled first**, rustc's two passes: `g((5, 1), (-3, b))` holds two that must share one.
- **A lambda with a domain left open read ahead of the conversions held.** An action lifted into its region's monad is typed by an imitation, which is what gives the continuation its parameter: read first, the body is read blind, and `/std/File/with_async` is refused at the `!` that lifts.
- **A held conversion that counts only where it converts.** An imitation's argument pair between two open types leaves it undecided, and the imitation discarded with it is the key the lift's witness is found by: `State/get()!` under a region its `State` lifts into is refused.
- **The item's choices taken by its drain, once its levels have closed.** An imitation mints an instance of its family, which nothing closes then: a generic value named bare at a declared type, `let z: Option(Nat) = mk;`, is refused on a level.
- **An application imitated at once where no term awaits its metavariable**, as a numeral defaults at once there. Two parts of one expected type are met in the order they are written, so a function the second states arrives after the imitation the first took.
- **The first written of two last-resort candidates that differ.** It is the order of the parameters again; and lifting the spine rule's restriction on an instance head where the rule stands has `/std/List/sort_by/push_layered` refused, an implicit solved where two spines agreed that the declaration's other argument gives otherwise.

## Non-goals

Solving one metavariable to another ([Flex–flex problems with distinct heads](202-flex-flex-intersection.md)), and which metavariables a parked problem watches. Each has an entry of its own. A reader that waits for a type a later line gives: a projection, a match or a call over a head whose type is open is refused where it is read, once a term completes what it awaits.

## Completion and retirement

- Every program under *The gap* compiles in either order of its parts.
- A call takes one dictionary and infers one implicit whichever of its parameters is declared first.
- One function completes a term, `ParkedWork` states the rank of each problem's answer, and no consumer of the store recognizes a problem by its shape.

Before this specification is deleted, `curios-elab`'s README states the completion of a term in its decision on postponement, the two decisions it is held to and [Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md) state the rule for a numeral, for a literal and for a `use` slot with their rejected alternatives, [the reference](../../syntax.md#numeric-literals) says where a numeral is unconstrained and that a statement is the unit of inference, the roadmap entry is a checked summary, and no reference to this filename remains.
