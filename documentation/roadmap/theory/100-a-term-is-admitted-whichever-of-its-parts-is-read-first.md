# A term is admitted whichever of its parts is read first

Working specification for who takes a choice the elaborator has to make where nothing forces an answer: what a lambda's body comes to where no expectation reaches it, a `use` slot's registered witness, the solution two agreeing spines or a flex application suggest. It is held to [Subsumption is a relation, not a traversal order](../../design/theory/subsumption-is-a-relation-not-a-traversal-order.md), which has a metavariable met at a sort take a level of its own, so that the order of a call's arguments, of a list's elements, or of an argument and the expectation decides nothing, and to [A literal is realized by its expected type](../../design/surface/a-literal-is-realized-by-its-expected-type.md). The code keeps that rule for a sort's level, for a numeral's default, for a tuple literal's shape and for a lambda's written type, and for no other choice: every other one is taken where the walk first reads it.

## What this builds on

- **A term awaits what it reads its parts against, and completes.** A call, a list literal, a structure literal, a match, an operator and an intrinsic's or a foreign call's operand walk are each read inside `Context::awaiting` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`). A metavariable born meanwhile is awaited by that term, a call adopting the holes its head's spine leaves open, and a solution or a parked problem that links two joins the classes they are held in, a class being awaited whole (`Solutions::birth_with_kind`, `solve` and `park`, `curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`, the three doors every birth, solution and parked problem passes). Where the term has read its parts, `Context::complete` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) takes the choices left among the problems it awaits, each stated by the problem itself (`ParkedWork::choice`), one at a time and the lowest rank first; the item's drain is the completion of what no term awaits. It takes three ranks today. A tuple literal parked on a bare metavariable gives it its shape (`Context::shape`): a non-dependent product of one open type a field, at which the literal is checked, a literal among its fields shaped and a lambda whose every domain is annotated typed in their turn (`Context::structure`), before the product is held to the metavariable, so that every other literal waiting on it is checked against what the first left open. The numerals that wait on one type take one default (`numeral_default`), a numeral whose type a term awaits having parked (`elaborate_num_lit`). And a lambda is checked: at the function type its expectation has come to, a domain of it still open, or, where it has come to none, at the type its annotations and body state (`Context::settle`), one whose every domain is annotated ahead of one with a domain left open, which stands as a metavariable named after its binder and awaited by the term completing. A literal that waits leaves its type open behind it, and three readers answer through that: an open type is sized by the level its own sort states (`Sort::of`, `curios-elab/src/convert/sort.rs`), so a former over it carries that level to whatever it is handed to; a neutral headed by a metavariable is typed by the type the metavariable was born at (`synth_neutral`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`), so an operator's bound is a proposition before its witness is resolved; and a folded recursive call with a stuck argument blocks a conversion as it blocks a check (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`, `curios-elab/src/convert/occurrence.rs`). Every other choice is taken where the walk reads it.
- **Conversion solves no metavariable to another.** Two unsolved metavariables met against each other park as a pair (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`, "flex–flex does no intersection"; [Flex–flex problems with distinct heads](202-flex-flex-intersection.md)), and a candidate that holds an unsolved metavariable commits where that one's context is contained in the solved one's. So what holds an inner term's metavariable to an enclosing term's is a solution or a parked conversion.

## The gap

The programs of the first paragraph below are over `let g(@A: Type, x: A, pin: A) -> Nat = 0;` and `let b: Byte = 7;`, read with a lambda settled by its term; the rest are read at `ca95d7595`.

**A settled lambda's body takes its choices where it is read.** A lambda no expectation reaches is checked at the type its annotations and body state, and its body is read with no expectation (`elaborate_func_infer`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`). A numeral, a literal or a lambda that is the body takes its choice there, ahead of the lambda beside it that would have given it: `g((x: Byte) => 0, (y: Byte) => b)`, `g((x: Byte) => (0, x), (y: Byte) => (b, y))` and `g((x: Byte) => (y: Byte) => 0, (x: Byte) => (y: Byte) => b)` are each refused, `inferred: Byte` against `expected: Nat`, where the other order compiles.

**A `use` slot takes the dictionary its key had when the walk reached it.** `elaborate_apply` attempts a slot where it stands (`insert_auto_argument`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/apply.rs`), so a premise whose concept arguments are already known resolves before a later argument's type can name another dictionary. With `struct Slot(K: Type, use Key(K), V: Type)`, a value `b: Slot(Nat, use other, Str)` and `holds(@K: Type, use Key(K), @V: Type, at: Slot(K, V), key: K)`, `holds(b, 3)` takes `b`'s dictionary, while `holds(@Nat, b, 3)`, a premise over a closed type and a key pinned by an earlier argument each resolve the registered one and refuse `b` as a type mismatch (`a_key_type_fixed_before_the_slot_takes_the_registered_dictionary`, `curios/src/tests/concepts/premise_tests.rs`).

**Two spines that agree solve an implicit where they are first met.** `compare_same_global_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) decides two applications of one definition by their spines ahead of reduction, and a function that is not injective has other solutions, so the verdict follows the order of the parameters. Over `let rest(xs: List(Nat)) -> List(Nat) = match xs | [] => [] | [_, ..t] => t end;`, `induct Tagged: (List(Nat)) -> Type | tag(xs: List(Nat)): (xs) end`, `both(@xs: List(Nat), a: Tagged(rest(xs)), b: Tagged(xs))` and `first: Tagged(rest([1, 2]))`, `both(first, Tagged/tag([9, 2]))` reports `Tagged([9, 2])` against `Tagged([1, 2])`, and compiles with `b` declared ahead of `a`. And the rule declines a head that carries an instance where either side holds a metavariable, the solving staying with the heads it was measured on, so whether a call infers its implicit follows a level the definition's body holds and the argument never reads: over `let make(@c: Bool, h: Wrap(c)) -> Nat = 0;`, with `let Wrap(c: Bool) -> Type = match c | true => List(Type) | false => Nat end;` and `let given: Wrap(true) = [Nat];`, `make(given)` is refused, `never solved: the implicit argument 'c' of '/make'`, and with `List(Nat)` in the arm and `[1]` given it is accepted.

**A flex application is imitated where it is first met.** `imitate_flex_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) commits `?m := (x) => T(b, x)` for `?m(a)` met against `T(b, i)`, the right-biased partial application, and a function of another shape is a solution too. Over `struct Two(A: Type, B: Type): pub Type { a: A, b: B }`, `struct Holder(M: (Type) -> Type): pub Type { n: Nat }`, `h: Holder((X: Type) => Two(X, Bool))`, `x: Two(Nat, Bool)` and `first(@M: (Type) -> Type, @A: Type, x: M(A), w: Holder(M))`, `first(x, h)` reports `Holder((X) => Two(X, Bool))` against `Holder(Two(Nat))`, and compiles with `w` declared ahead of `x`.

## Decisions

A choice is an answer nothing forces: one of several a problem admits. What unification forces is the same whichever problem is met first; a choice is not, so it is taken only where everything that could bear on it has been read, and the decisions below say where that is, who knows it, and in what order choices are taken.

**A metavariable is awaited by a term, and the store keeps by which.** A term awaits where it reads its parts against types that hold a metavariable: a call, its implicits and the holes its head's spine leaves open; a list literal, its element type; a structure literal, its parameters; a match with an elided motive; an operator, its operand type; an intrinsic's or a foreign call's operands. A metavariable is awaited from its birth by the innermost such term being read, and by none outside one. The relation moves where a link is made and nowhere else: a committed solution holds what it mentions to what it solves, and a parked problem holds together what it watches. What links hold together is a class, and a class is awaited whole: where a link joins two, by the outermost standing term that awaited either, one no term awaited included. So a chain of links holds its ends: a numeral under two calls, whose type one parked pair holds to the middle call's implicit and another holds that to the outer call's, waits for the outer call. A term that has completed leaves what it still held awaited by none. `Solutions::birth_with_kind`, `solve` and `park` write it, with a journal `unwind_to` restores, so whether a term awaits a metavariable is read off its class and by no pass over the store. A parked problem is awaited as what it watches is, and where it is retried it runs awaited by that term while the term stands, and by none once it has completed. GHC keeps a unification variable's level the same way, promoting where the solution that links two is made.

**A term read with no expectation completes in full.** The expectation is the one way what encloses a term informs it: the turnaround links what the term awaits to what the enclosing term does, and what is linked moves outward as above. So a term that is inferred — a `let`'s value with no annotation, a scrutinee, a head — has taken every choice it owns before the next line reads it, and inference is local to a statement, as Java's, Kotlin's and Swift's is. A metavariable may outlive its statement and be solved by a later one; a choice may not.

**A term completes by one loop, taking one choice at a time.** Where a term has read its parts and met its expectation, it retries what a solution woke, takes one choice among the problems it awaits, and starts again, until none is left to take. A choice is a parked problem's own answer, and its rank is stated once on `ParkedWork`, in place of a shape matched by each consumer; the lowest rank is taken first, since each commits less than the next and may decide it:

1. a `use` slot whose key is rigid takes the registered witness;
2. a tuple literal parked on a bare metavariable gives it its shape, a non-dependent product of one open type a field, labelled as the literal is: the literal is checked at the product, which is then held to the metavariable, so a second literal parked on it is checked against the first's shape and each fills what the other left open;
3. the numerals parked on one metavariable take their default together;
4. a lambda parked on a bare metavariable is checked at the type its annotations and body state, one whose every domain is annotated ahead of one with a domain left open;
5. two applications of one definition parked apart are held to spines that agree;
6. a flex application parked against a nominal type is imitated.

The item is the outermost such loop, and its drain takes what no term awaited and reports what is left. Lean's pass has the same shape: resume, one default, resume.

**An open part is a type left to inference.** Each open part of a shape is born at a sort of its own and awaited by the term completing: a proposition's where the metavariable shaped is one, every field of a record of propositions being one, and its quality open otherwise. Its level is bounded from below by the field it is given and from above by the product it stands in, as a field's is where a product is synthesized from its fields; and a type over an open one is sized by the level that one's sort states, so the bound reaches whatever the product is handed to. A literal's fields are read before its product is held to the metavariable it shapes, a literal among them shaped and a lambda whose every domain is annotated typed first: a product is a proposition where every field is one, which the fields alone say. A field typed only after, a lambda with a domain left open, which a literal beside it may give, leaves the product read as a type's there, so where the metavariable was declared a `Type` the item's drain reads the product again (`Context::hold_shapes`). A sort among the fields is met by a metavariable and takes a level of its own, as one that is an argument does.

**A numeral waits for the term that awaits its type.** Checked at a type stuck on an unsolved metavariable that some term awaits, it parks as a checking problem and is realized where a solution wakes it. The numerals parked on one metavariable take a default each of them realizes at: `Int` where one is signed, `Nat` where one is an unsigned number, and `Char` only where every one is a character. Where no term awaits the type the default is taken at once, as today, and the comparison with a type stuck on a metavariable parks. An operator awaits its operand type as any term does.

**A lambda settled by a term is that term's.** Its unannotated domains are born awaited by the term completing it, so a numeral in its body that its domain reaches waits for that term and no further, and a lambda bound through a call has its type where the call completes: `z(3)` compiles, and a function type with hidden binders handed to `z` by a later line is refused, as it is for a lambda bound with no call. Its body is read at a codomain left open, awaited as its domains are, so a numeral, a literal or a lambda that is the body waits as one in an argument does.

**A `use` slot is answered where its term completes.** The registered witness is the slot's own answer, taken where nothing solved the slot: a later argument whose type holds a dictionary names it whichever of the two the walk reaches first. A wake resolves nothing; it drops a goal whose slot unification has solved. A slot a definition drops its premise under is left to resolution as it is today, the spine approximation passing over one that is open.

**The last resorts agree, or the call is refused.** Two spines that agree are one solution among those a function that is not injective has, and an imitation one among the functions a flex application may stand for, so neither is a rule of conversion's: each is tried over the raw spellings a parked problem keeps, where a term completes, under a monomorphic head and an instance alike. Every problem a term awaits offers its candidate before any is committed; where two candidates for one metavariable differ the call is refused, naming the implicit and both, and where they agree it is solved. Conversion keeps the congruence: two spines that hold no metavariable and agree are equal, as the kernel has it. A `!` goes on reading its region's monad by imitation directly, the region being its expectation.

## Open questions

**How a settled lambda's body is read at a codomain left open.** A codomain metavariable born under the lambda's binders may name them, and is one the solver commits no candidate over while it is unsolved: `Context::metavar_restriction` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`) re-expresses an embedded metavariable through the free names its occurrence carries, and a binder of the candidate's own is none of them, so `id((x) => x)`, whose body's type is its domain's metavariable, is left postponed. Born where the lambda's domains are, outside its binders, the codomain names none, and a body whose type does is refused where it is admitted today. Read at a codomain only where the body is itself a numeral, a literal or a lambda, a call in tail position still completes in full, its numerals with it.

**What moving the last resorts changes across `/std`.** The spine approximation commits a solution in 112 of the 8,773 pairs it reaches, 193 solutions in 54 declarations, `/std/List/sort_by/push_layered` among them, and in none of the 1,922 it reaches inside an oracle, where nothing may park (counted at `ca95d7595`). Imitation is not counted yet. Each of the two stages lists its declarations first and names every one whose elaborated form moves.

**Whether a parked projection and a parked match are still reached** once a match completes its own arms. Over `/std` neither is reached (counted at `ca95d7595`); two fixtures of `curios/src/tests/inference/postponement_tests.rs` reach them by design.

## Stages

Each lands alone, on its own check.

1. **A settled lambda's body is read at a codomain left open.** The first open question is answered first. Check: the three programs of *The gap* in either order, `id((x) => x)` applied on the next line, and a codomain that names a binder kept, `id((A: Type, x: A) => x)`.
2. **A `use` slot is answered where its term completes.** Check: `holds(b, 3)`, `holds(@Nat, b, 3)` and a key pinned by an earlier argument run under one dictionary, as a fixture beside `a_key_type_fixed_before_the_slot_takes_the_registered_dictionary`, whose refusal ends; `f1(5, true)` compiles; the image compared.
3. **The spine approximation is a term's last resort.** Check: `make(given)` compiles and `both` is accepted in either order of its parameters, as fixtures in `curios-elab`'s `convert/solve_tests.rs` and as programs; two candidates that differ are refused; the board's `spine_tests.rs` stands as it is; the image compared.
4. **Imitation is a term's last resort.** Counted over `/std` first. Check: `first(x, h)` compiles in either order of its parameters, as a fixture in `convert/imitation_tests.rs` and as a program; the image compared.
5. **What the missing owner made necessary goes.** The parked projection and the parked match where nothing reaches them, each counted at zero over `/std` and the test suite before it is removed, and the result-directed arms of `blocked_on_metavar`.
6. **Retirement**, as *Completion and retirement* states.

## Verification

- Every program under *The gap* is a fixture in both orders, run and not only elaborated, so that the kernel has judged what the elaborator accepted.
- **The image compared.** `curios-prelude-archive`'s build writes `/std`'s image as `std.rkyv` under its `OUT_DIR`, and `curios-prelude`'s writes `certification.rkyv` and `reads.rkyv` under its own. A release build of the commit before a stage and one of the stage are compared part by part, and the certifier's two files whole. A stage that changes only the order work is retried in moves what the elaborator asked; it may also move which of two convertible spellings an implicit is solved to, a commutative operation's operands being written in the order they were met, as `/std/Nat/Scale/of`'s are, and a definition by its name or by what it unfolds to, as `keys` is in `/std/Map/Node/collected`, whose erased form follows; and a level in the elaborated module, since of two level identifications that cannot both hold the one met first is kept (`of_two_weak_equations_that_cannot_both_hold_the_first_met_is_kept`, `curios-elab/src/universe_solver/weak_tests.rs`). - **What fires, counted.** In a copy of the commit, a `curios_profile::note!` under the target `curios_elab::probe` stands at each site with its fields: `park_projection` and `park_match`, each use; `insert_auto_argument`'s witness arm, whether the goal held an unsolved metavariable and whether the slot is solved on return; `compare_same_global_apply` past its head test, whether the spines converged, how many solutions were committed meanwhile and whether parking was suppressed. `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` files the stream; its `V` rows are counted by their fields, each attributed to the `group` of the last `declaration` span of `curios_elab::elaborate::module` started before it. ## Rejected

- **The relation read where a term settles, by a pass over every parked problem.** It costs a pass a settling term, and misses a problem that is out of the store being retried, whose nested terms settle meanwhile. GHC floated an equality out of its implication to the same end and replaced it with a promotion where the solution is made.
- **The relation read by a scan of the store at every question**: it answers rightly, costs a pass over the parked problems wherever a numeral asks, and misses a pair while that pair is out of the store being retried.
- **A metavariable handed outward alone where it is linked.** What an earlier link held to it stays behind: a numeral under two calls is held to the middle call's implicit by one parked pair, a second pair moves that implicit to the outer call, and the numeral takes its default where the middle call completes, so `g(id(id(5)), b)` is refused where `g(id(5), b)` compiles.
- **A depth in place of the term that awaits.** A problem retried under a term opens terms of its own beside the ones still being read where the wake found it, and two of them at one depth are two terms.
- **A term that hands outward whenever it was checked at a type still open.** It waits where nothing can arrive, and does not wait where something can: a term inferred, whose metavariable a binder's type holds to an enclosing call's.
- **Every choice held to its item's end**, as Lean holds a default instance to its region's and Rust an integer's to its function's. What reads the type next — a match, a projection, a call of `z` — meets none, so every reader has to be able to wait, which is what the parked projection and the parked match are there to survive; and a value stands in the types after it here, where a numeral not yet realized is a placeholder conversion waits on.
- **One constraint system an expression, solved whole**, as Swift does: its search is exponential in the worst case, Swift bounds it to a statement and has a closure's statements read one way, and a dependent telescope opens each domain through the arguments before it, which the walk does as it goes and a collected system would have to replay.
- **An open part at the level of the metavariable it shapes.** Re-validating the product gives each part still open a level above the shared one, which closes a cycle through a maximum that the entry's level closing (`UniverseSolver::default`) does not settle where a field holds a sort; and it saves nothing, a part's own level being internal and closing at its field's.
- **A product held to the metavariable it shapes before its fields are read.** It is sized as a type's whatever it comes to hold: a tuple of proofs is refused where a proposition is declared, and admitted where a type is, for the kernel to refuse.
- **A numeral as a fourth form the call walk parks.** A numeral under a nested call, in a list, a structure literal or a match arm still commits where it is read.
- **Literals whose type involves no choice settled first**, rustc's two passes: `g((5, 1), (-3, b))` holds two that must share one.
- **The first written of two last-resort candidates that differ.** It is the order of the parameters again; and lifting the spine rule's restriction on an instance head where the rule stands has `/std/List/sort_by/push_layered` refused, an implicit solved where two spines agreed that the declaration's other argument gives otherwise.

## Non-goals

Solving one metavariable to another ([Flex–flex problems with distinct heads](202-flex-flex-intersection.md)), and which metavariables a parked problem watches. Each has an entry of its own. A reader that waits for a type a later line gives: a projection, a match or a call over a head whose type is open is refused where it is read, once a term completes what it awaits.

## Completion and retirement

- Every program under *The gap* compiles in either order of its parts.
- A call takes one dictionary and infers one implicit whichever of its parameters is declared first.
- One function completes a term, `ParkedWork` states the rank of each problem's answer, and no consumer of the store recognizes a problem by its shape.

Before this specification is deleted, `curios-elab`'s README states the completion of a term in its decision on postponement, the two decisions it is held to and [Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md) state the rule for a numeral, for a literal and for a `use` slot with their rejected alternatives, [the reference](../../syntax.md#numeric-literals) says where a numeral is unconstrained and that a statement is the unit of inference, the roadmap entry is a checked summary, and no reference to this filename remains.
