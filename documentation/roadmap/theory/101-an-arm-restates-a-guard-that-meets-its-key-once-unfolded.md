# An arm restates a guard that meets its key once unfolded

An arm on an expression restates each equation in force whose settlement meets the arm's key whole, in its spelling or in the type or definition of a local it reads (`curios_analysis::meets_one_of`, read by `curios-cert`'s `Scope::restate` and `curios-elab`'s `Context::equations_read_by`). So under a guard `small(x)` whose body matches on `g(x)`, an arm on `g(x)` restates nothing, and the guard's spelling, settled outside the arm, does not read `g(x)` at its case there. Restating every equation whose settlement reads a local the arm's key names reaches it, and ran `Int/DivMod/unique`, a proof `/std` held until `Int` division became Euclid's, out of the certifier's reduction budget, re-settling nearly every guard of a proof at every arm inside it. No program of `/std` is known to meet the shape, and no workaround of it was found there on 2026-11-20.
