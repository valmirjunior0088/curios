# A type is read through what it computes

Adopted: a type that holds itself through a container whose hidden proof is computed from its payload is admitted where its functor is polynomial, and a declaration that holds a computed proposition keeps the universe levels it would keep holding a family in its place. `Vec(T, n)` stands in a recursive type, a list stands beside a proposition about its length, and `/std/Map` holds its soundness as the recursive proposition its proofs read, with no family restating it beside bridges each way and no level its family form does not have.

Working specification for strict positivity and variance reading a declaration's types through what they compute, where today a recursive member files no variance vector, so the elaborator compares two instances of one at every level, a `match` a type-level definition is stuck on being read through its arms already, positivity reading a datum past its type parameters and a recursive member by the fixpoint of its unfolding, and variance reading a recursive group by the fixpoint of its unfolding, two of its projections compared at the levels it holds. [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) and [A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md) state the two rules; positivity's acceptance test, that no declaration reaches itself through a non-strict path, and variance's, that two instances apart in an irrelevant level have the same inhabitants, are unchanged, and only how precisely each walk finds what a type holds moves. Its first consumer is [A map forks sixteen ways](../standard-library/100-a-map-forks-sixteen-ways.md), whose second stage waits on the delivery left here.

Its next step is stage 1, a delivery.

## What this builds on

- **Positivity reads a stuck `match` through its arms, and at `Mixed` what else it cannot see through.** `curios-analysis/src/positivity.rs` forces a type-level definition to weak-head normal form (`Walk::forced`). A `match` the form is stuck on is read as the arms it reduces to and its annotations for the declarations they name alone (`Walk::stuck`, over `curios-analysis/src/reading.rs`), and a datum past the declaration's type parameters (`Reading::Datum`, over `curios-analysis/src/data.rs`), as [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) states. Every other form it is stuck on is read through `Walk::opaque`, which walks every child at `Mixed`: an application or an intrinsic operation, its type operands among its children (`Walk::intrinsic`); and a recursive member the path is already inside is read by the least fixpoint of its group's unfolding (`Walk::meet`, over `least_fixpoint` in `curios-analysis/src/reading.rs`). A family's index arguments are walked at `Mixed`. `Mixed` is a result, never a stop.
- **Variance marks every level of what it cannot see through.** `curios-analysis/src/variance.rs` reads a type's reduct and marks a level where conversion would compare it; a stuck `match` is read as conversion compares two of it, its arms opened and forced ([A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md)), and a `rec` group marks every level it mentions (`Walk::everything`). The same walk reads a definition that answers a type, its body applied at fresh binders (`definition_variances`, `curios-elab/src/universes/variance.rs`, through `held_levels`), into the vector the elaborator files and compares two instances of the definition by, a recursive member filing none; the certifier reads none, compares two instances of a definition by their spines and levels and unfolds where that decides nothing.
- **Conversion compares what variance reads.** Both checkers compare a stuck `match`'s head, goal and arms (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`, `curios-cert/src/certifier/convert.rs`), and two projections of one `rec` group at every level (`rec_instances` in the certifier; `align_universe_levels` in `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`, over `Term::level_differences`, which pairs two terms' levels position by position), since each reproduces itself under every unfolding; the variance decision rejects a vector on a recursive member for that reason. [Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../../design/soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md) holds the elaborator, which compares two instances of a definition by its vector before unfolding it, to the certifier, which compares them after, and `curios`'s `tests::laws` audits it.
- **Both checkers run both analyses through `Env`** (`curios-analysis/src/judge.rs`), which answers reduction, a definition's body, a local's type, a family's entry and vectors, and whether a family was judged; each checker files its own vectors.
- **What the walks' caution closes is on the board.** [Two families declared apart that mention each other …](../../../xboard/tickets/formation/2026_11_05_two_families_declared_apart_that_mention_each_other_are_each_analysed_with_the_other_as_a_sink_and_the_negative_cycle_through_the_two_is_admitted/metadata.toml) reaches `False` through a definition placed before both families, and [A family of a unit with no vector on record …](../../../xboard/tickets/admission/2026_11_05_a_family_of_a_unit_with_no_vector_on_record_digests_alike_whatever_its_vector_so_a_verdict_a_store_filed_composes_through_one_that_moved/metadata.toml) through a vector a stored verdict did not digest. Both are fixed, and both are why a definition is read where it is used and never through a positivity vector filed for it.
- **How peers read it.**
  - Agda admits `c : ∀ n → Vec D n → D` and a record beside `length xs ≡ n`, because "by default Agda analyses how functions use their arguments" ([Positivity checking](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/stable/language/positivity-checking.html)): its occurrence graph reads a definition's clause bodies and never their types, skips a lambda's domain, reads an index at `Mixed`, and reads a definition outside the mutual block through occurrences filed for it (`defArgOccurrences`, [`OccurrenceAnalysis`](https://agda.github.io/agda/src/Agda.TypeChecking.Positivity.OccurrenceAnalysis.html)). The analysis "can be slow, especially for big mutual blocks", and its source records an imprecision where a mutual name is applied to an argument (issue 1984).
  - Lean's kernel specializes a nested occurrence into an auxiliary mutual block, and refuses `inductive Foo (f: Type) | Bar: (foos: Vector (Foo f) 3) -> Foo f` with "invalid nested inductive datatype 'Eq', nested inductive datatypes parameters cannot contain local variables" ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113489-new-members/topic/Nested.20inductive.20datatypes.20with.20vectors.html)); the suggested workaround drops the length.
  - Rocq's nested positivity requires the nested family's instantiated constructors to satisfy it and the defined type to occur in no index ([reference manual, *Positivity condition*](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/v9.1/refman/language/core/inductive.html)), so a constructor taking `length l = n` beside `l : list T` is refused ([an example](https://vonavi.github.io/coq-examples/examples/using-length-of-list-x-as-an-argument-for-a-constructor-of-x-in-coq.html)).
  - F* has a binder state its polarity, `@@@strictly_positive` and `@@@unused`, and unfolds a definition unless it is irreducible ([*Inductive type definitions*](https://fstar-lang.org/tutorial/book/part2/part2_inductive_type_families.html)); Idris 2 "does not (yet!) track whether a function's arguments are used in a strictly positive manner", and its users reach for `assert_total` ([Seamless, correct, and generic programming over serialised data](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.13441)).
  - Strictly positive types are containers, and a container morphism is exactly a polymorphic function, a natural transformation (Abbott, Altenkirch and Ghani, [*Containers: constructing strictly positive types*](https://people.cs.nott.ac.uk/psztxa/publ/cont-tcs.pdf), TCS 2005): a polymorphic predicate over a container's payload reads its shape alone, which is why a proof about a list's length leaves the list's functor polynomial.

## The gap

Read at the commit that compares a group's projections at the levels its unfolding holds: `Ok` and `Balanced`, a recursive group and a recursive member, file no definition vector (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`'s `elaborate_module_rec` and its single-member witness group file `Vectors::default()`), so the elaborator compares two instances of either by name at every level before unfolding them, committing their levels equal, where the certifier, unfolding the same pair to two projections of the group, finds them one at a level only a parameter's type states. The elaborator is the stricter of the two, never the looser: a pair the certifier would accept may be refused, none is admitted.

`/std/Map` meets it: it holds its soundness as `Rooted` over `Node/Shaped`, a family that restates `Node/Sound` with no function applied to the trie, bridged by `Node/shaped`, `Node/read`, `grounded` and `rooted` (`curios-prelude/std/Map.crs`, `curios-prelude/std/Map/internal/Node.crs`), because `/std/Toml` holds itself through a map and because the family form is what keeps `Map`'s levels irrelevant (`variances = [Irrelevant, Irrelevant]`).

## Decisions

Decided on 2026-11-17. A stuck `match` is read through its arms in both analyses, positivity reads an annotation for the declarations it names alone, positivity reads a datum past the declaration's type parameters, and positivity reads a folded recursive call and a member met again on its own path by the least fixpoint of its unfolding, as [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) and [A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md) state; the decisions below build on them.

- **A recursive member files the vector its unfolding holds.** Each member of a group files a definition vector read off its body as a definition's is (`definition_variances`, `curios-elab/src/universes/variance.rs`), a member met again on its own path or a folded call read by the least fixpoint of its group's unfolding, so the elaborator compares two instances of the member by it before unfolding, as it compares any definition's, and the certifier, unfolding them, reaches the projections it compares at the held positions: one relation. The unit's record holds a vector per name, each member of a group its own.
- **Nothing new per family.** A group's held levels live for one judgment and a positivity summary for a walk; the vectors each checker files are unchanged in kind, a member's being a definition's. `Env` is unchanged.

## Rejected

- **One walker for both analyses**, generic over what it records: positivity and variance record different things at the same nodes — variance reads a value's parameters and every level of a `match`'s goal and head, positivity a goal's declarations alone and a head only where an arm reads what it supplies — and the readings they share are pieces.
