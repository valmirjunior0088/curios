# A type is read through what it computes

Adopted: a type that holds itself through a container whose hidden proof is computed from its payload is admitted where its functor is polynomial, and a declaration that holds a computed proposition keeps the universe levels it would keep holding a family in its place. `Vec(T, n)` stands in a recursive type, a list stands beside a proposition about its length, and `/std/Map` holds its soundness as the recursive proposition its proofs read, with no family restating it beside bridges each way and no level its family form does not have.

Working specification for strict positivity and variance reading a declaration's types through what they compute, where today both read what they cannot see through at its most conservative: a `match` a type-level definition is stuck on, a value that computes no type, and a recursive member met again on its own path. [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) and [A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md) state the two rules; positivity's acceptance test, that no declaration reaches itself through a non-strict path, and variance's, that two instances apart in an irrelevant level have the same inhabitants, are unchanged, and only how precisely each walk finds what a type holds moves. Its first consumer is [A map forks sixteen ways](../standard-library/100-a-map-forks-sixteen-ways.md), whose second stage waits on all four deliveries here.

Its next step is stage 1, a delivery.

## What this builds on

- **Positivity reads at `Mixed` what it cannot see through.** `curios-analysis/src/positivity.rs` forces a type-level definition to weak-head normal form (`Walk::forced`) and reads the form it is stuck on through `Walk::opaque`, which walks every child at `Mixed`: a `match` stuck on a variable, its head, goal and arms alike, the arms closed over their binders; an application or an intrinsic operation, its type operands among its children (`Walk::intrinsic`); and a `rec` member the path is already inside, left folded so the descent ends (`Walk::walk_within`), whose group's member bodies are among the children. A family's index arguments are walked at `Mixed`. `Mixed` is a result, never a stop.
- **Variance marks every level of what it cannot see through.** `curios-analysis/src/variance.rs` reads a type's reduct and marks a level where conversion would compare it; a stuck form is read through its children closed (`Walk::children`), so an instance in an arm, which no forcing reaches under the arm's binders, marks every level it carries, and a `rec` group marks every level it mentions (`Walk::everything`). The same walk reads a definition that answers a type, its body applied at fresh binders (`definition_variances`, `curios-elab/src/universes/variance.rs`, through `held_levels`), into the vector the elaborator files and compares two instances of the definition by; the certifier reads none, compares two instances of a definition by their spines and levels and unfolds where that decides nothing.
- **Conversion compares what variance reads.** Both checkers compare a stuck `match`'s head, goal and arms (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`, `curios-cert/src/certifier/convert.rs`), and two projections of one `rec` group at every level (`rec_instances` in the certifier; `align_universe_levels` in `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`, over `Term::level_differences`, which pairs two terms' levels position by position), since each reproduces itself under every unfolding; the variance decision rejects a vector on a recursive member for that reason. [Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../../design/soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md) holds the elaborator, which compares two instances of a definition by its vector before unfolding it, to the certifier, which compares them after, and `curios`'s `tests::laws` audits it.
- **Both checkers run both analyses through `Env`** (`curios-analysis/src/judge.rs`), which answers reduction, a definition's body, a local's type, a family's entry and vectors, and whether a family was judged; each checker files its own vectors.
- **What a `match` states.** `Match` holds a head, a goal and its `Cases`; `Cases::Induct` may state the instance definitional K fires on (`stated`), and `Carrier::List` states its element type (`curios-core/src/term/shape.rs`). `curios-analysis/src/totality.rs`'s `walk_arms` opens arms against fresh binders.
- **What the walks' caution closes is on the board.** [Two families declared apart that mention each other …](../../../xboard/tickets/formation/2026_11_05_two_families_declared_apart_that_mention_each_other_are_each_analysed_with_the_other_as_a_sink_and_the_negative_cycle_through_the_two_is_admitted/metadata.toml) reaches `False` through a definition placed before both families, and [A family of a unit with no vector on record …](../../../xboard/tickets/admission/2026_11_05_a_family_of_a_unit_with_no_vector_on_record_digests_alike_whatever_its_vector_so_a_verdict_a_store_filed_composes_through_one_that_moved/metadata.toml) through a vector a stored verdict did not digest. Both are fixed, and both are why a definition is read where it is used and never through a positivity vector filed for it.
- **How peers read it.**
  - Agda admits `c : ∀ n → Vec D n → D` and a record beside `length xs ≡ n`, because "by default Agda analyses how functions use their arguments" ([Positivity checking](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/stable/language/positivity-checking.html)): its occurrence graph reads a definition's clause bodies and never their types, skips a lambda's domain, reads an index at `Mixed`, and reads a definition outside the mutual block through occurrences filed for it (`defArgOccurrences`, [`OccurrenceAnalysis`](https://agda.github.io/agda/src/Agda.TypeChecking.Positivity.OccurrenceAnalysis.html)). The analysis "can be slow, especially for big mutual blocks", and its source records an imprecision where a mutual name is applied to an argument (issue 1984).
  - Lean's kernel specializes a nested occurrence into an auxiliary mutual block, and refuses `inductive Foo (f: Type) | Bar: (foos: Vector (Foo f) 3) -> Foo f` with "invalid nested inductive datatype 'Eq', nested inductive datatypes parameters cannot contain local variables" ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113489-new-members/topic/Nested.20inductive.20datatypes.20with.20vectors.html)); the suggested workaround drops the length.
  - Rocq's nested positivity requires the nested family's instantiated constructors to satisfy it and the defined type to occur in no index ([reference manual, *Positivity condition*](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/v9.1/refman/language/core/inductive.html)), so a constructor taking `length l = n` beside `l : list T` is refused ([an example](https://vonavi.github.io/coq-examples/examples/using-length-of-list-x-as-an-argument-for-a-constructor-of-x-in-coq.html)).
  - F* has a binder state its polarity, `@@@strictly_positive` and `@@@unused`, and unfolds a definition unless it is irreducible ([*Inductive type definitions*](https://fstar-lang.org/tutorial/book/part2/part2_inductive_type_families.html)); Idris 2 "does not (yet!) track whether a function's arguments are used in a strictly positive manner", and its users reach for `assert_total` ([Seamless, correct, and generic programming over serialised data](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.13441)).
  - Strictly positive types are containers, and a container morphism is exactly a polymorphic function, a natural transformation (Abbott, Altenkirch and Ghani, [*Containers: constructing strictly positive types*](https://people.cs.nott.ac.uk/psztxa/publ/cont-tcs.pdf), TCS 2005): a polymorphic predicate over a container's payload reads its shape alone, which is why a proof about a list's length leaves the list's functor polynomial.

## The gap

Probed at `7ac1b7d64`; each refusal is the elaborator's "occurs in itself in a position the checker cannot see through", and each declaration is a polynomial functor, a container and a predicate over its shape:

- `struct Box(T: Type): Type { items: List(T), k: Nat, @ok: Eq()(List/len(items), k) }` beside `induct N: Type | tip() | node(kids: Box(N)) end` is refused: `T` stands only as `#ListLen`'s element type, in an index of `Eq`. The same fact stated as a family indexed by the list, `induct Long(T: Type): (List(T), Nat) -> Prop`, is admitted, and so is `Eq()(List/len(@T, []), k)`, which reduces. `/std/Vec`'s entry files `polarities = [Mixed, Mixed]` for the same reason, and `induct Rose: Type | tip() | node(k: Nat, kids: Vec(Rose, k)) end` is refused.
- `let Good(@V: Type, r: Option(List(V))) -> Prop = match r | none() => Bool/True | some(l) => Bool/Holds(List/len(l) < 9) end;` held by `struct M(V: Type): Type { root: Option(List(V)), @ok: Good(root) }`: a type holding itself through `M` is refused. `M` itself files `variances = [Irrelevant, Invariant]` over two levels (`u <= v`), where `F`, holding the family `Short(V)(root)` that states the same, files one level, `[Irrelevant]`: the arm is read closed, so `List/len`'s instance in it marks its level. A `match` whose arms name no instance, `Q` over `Stuck(root)`, files one level and is strict.
- `let Balanced(@V: Type, t: Tree(V)) -> Prop = match t | tip(_) => Bool/True | fork(l, r) => {Balanced(l), Balanced(r)} end;` held by a struct as `M` holds `Good`: the member is met again on its own path and read at `Mixed`.
- `Ok` over `Rose(V)` and `All` over `List(Rose(V))`, a group whose list `match` states `elem = /Rose(V)`, held by `struct Held(V: Type): Type { t: Rose(V), @ok: Ok(t) }`: a type holding itself through `Held` is refused, and `Held` files `variances = [Irrelevant, Invariant]` over two levels.

`curios`'s `an_occurrence_behind_a_stuck_type_level_match_is_rejected` (`tests/rules/positivity_computed_type.rs`) pins the second refusal with both arms strict. `/std/Map` meets all three: it holds its soundness as `Rooted` over `Node/Shaped`, a family that restates `Node/Sound` with no function applied to the trie, bridged by `Node/shaped`, `Node/read`, `grounded` and `rooted` (`curios-prelude/std/Map.crs`, `curios-prelude/std/Map/internal/Node.crs`), because `/std/Toml` holds itself through a map and because the family form is what keeps `Map`'s levels irrelevant (`variances = [Irrelevant, Irrelevant]`).

## Decisions

Decided on 2026-11-17.

- **A stuck `match` is read through its arms, in both analyses.** Every instance reduces the `match` to one arm whose binders its head supplies, or, where definitional K selects the sole arm of a proposition, its stated instance. So both walks open each arm, a default among them, against fresh binders, and force what it holds. Positivity reads each arm at the polarity the `match` stands at, and the head and a stated instance at `Mixed` where some arm reads one of its binders, and as annotations where none does: they then only select an arm, and the arms' join bounds every selection. Variance reads the head, the goal, a list's element type and a stated instance as it reads every term, since conversion compares each.
- **Positivity reads an annotation for its declarations alone.** A goal and a list's element type stand in no reduct: an instance that reduces the `match` drops both, so no parameter of the declaration reaches a type through them, and each is read at `Unused` there. A declaration named there is read at `Mixed`, as today: Core's arm tags are bare labels, so a `match` does not say which family it takes apart, and an annotation naming a family — a list's element type, a recursive member's type — is the only witness that a type computes by taking apart a value of a family being declared, which the walk must still see. Variance reads annotations as it reads every term, because conversion compares them, and a level an instance holds only there is one two instances are told apart by.
- **A datum carries no type parameter, for positivity.** A type is data where its weak-head normal form is an intrinsic carrier whose element types are data, a tuple or function type whose parts are data, or an occurrence of a family outside the declaration analysed whose every payload, read at the arguments the occurrence gives, is data; never a sort, and never a variable, at its head or at any part read. A family met again while it is being classified is assumed data, the greatest fixpoint, since a value is a finite tree of payloads. A datum is a value the walk reads at a data type, the type stated by where the value stands or by the value itself: an index of a family occurrence, at the family's index telescope; a value parameter of a family or record occurrence, at its parameter telescope; the head of a `match` whose cases state a data carrier, a `Bool`, a `Nat`, `Bytes` or a list of data; a `match` whose goal is data. An intrinsic operation is no position of its own: it stands in one of these, `#ListLen` in `/std/Vec` in an index of `Eq` at `Nat`, and the walk has no syntax table to read its signature by. Every occurrence of a type parameter of the declaration analysed — a parameter whose type is a sort — inside a datum is read at `Unused`; every declaration inside it, and every value parameter, is read as anywhere else. It rests on a property the decision states: a datum is the same value at every instance of the declaration's type parameters, because no part of it is a type and no Curios term computes on one — no form inspects a type and no reduction reads a type operand — which is the naturality containers give a polymorphic function. A declaration of the set analysed is read where it stands, since a datum may eliminate a value of it.
- **A recursive member met on its own path is read by the least fixpoint of its unfolding, for positivity.** Its summary is the polarity at which each target stands in its body: the member's own parameters, the declaration's parameters a group closes over, and every declaration it names; its type is read as an annotation, every declaration it names at `Mixed`, which is how a member that takes apart a value of a family being declared is seen. The summaries of a group's members are computed together, seeded at `Unused` and joined upward, each body walked under the others' current summaries, and a meet composes each argument through its parameter's polarity and records every other target at the meet's polarity composed with its own. The fixpoint bounds every finite unfolding, the argument the decision makes for a declaration, and needs no termination. A summary lives for one walk: a group may close over that walk's own binders.
- **Two instances of a recursive group compare at the positions the least fixpoint of its unfolding holds.** The levels a member's unfolding holds are those the variance walk marks in its body, opened at fresh binders, a member met again on the path contributing what the group's fixpoint holds, least fixpoint over the group's members; its type is not read, as no parameter's type is. The variance walk reads a member met on its own path by that fixpoint. Two projections of one group apart only in levels are compared position by position: the group is generalized over the positions `Term::level_differences` pairs the two at, a level of its own at each, the fixpoint is taken over the generalized members, and a pair is compared where its position's level is held and set apart where it is not — the certifier's `rec_instances` and the elaborator's `align_universe_levels` alike, so that a definition is as transparent to level comparison recursive as it is not, and the elaborator's vector and the certifier's unfolding decide one relation. Two instances apart only at positions no unfolding holds unfold to terms apart only there, again and again, so they are one; the comparison unfolds nothing. A level stands for a position, not a head, since a ground level has none. Computed where compared, by each checker, through its own reduction and its own reads, and kept for the declaration being judged; a refused reduction holds every position, as a refused walk does today.
- **Nothing is filed per definition, and nothing new per family.** A positivity summary lives for a walk, a classification for one analysis, a group's held levels for one judgment; the vectors each checker files are unchanged in kind. `Env` is unchanged.
- **The readings are pieces the walks share, and each walk stays its own.** Opening a `match`'s arms against fresh binders, the terms that supply their binders, and the fixpoint over a group's members are stated once in `curios-analysis` and called by positivity and variance, totality's `walk_arms` keeping its own opener, which refines the scrutinee arm by arm; generalizing a pair over the positions their levels differ at is `curios-core`'s, beside `Term::level_differences`, which aligns the pair by the same walk. What each walk records at a node stays in that walk.

## Stages

1. **A stuck `match` is read through its arms.** The shared opener, positivity's reading with no annotation read and the head read only where an arm reads a binder, and variance's reading with the arms opened and forced, which a definition's vector (`definition_variances`) reads through as a family's does. Check, in both checkers (`curios/src/tests/rules/positivity_computed_type.rs`, `curios-cert`'s `recheck::positivity_tests`, `recheck::sort_tests`, `recheck::assignment_tests` and `recheck::variance_tests`, `curios-analysis/tests/driven/positivity.rs`, `curios-analysis/tests/variance.rs`): `an_occurrence_behind_a_stuck_type_level_match_is_rejected` restated as admitted, beside a control whose one arm holds the occurrence left of an arrow, refused; `an_occurrence_a_sort_left_to_a_caller_would_uncover_is_refused` refused still, and `a_parameter_a_sort_left_to_a_caller_would_uncover_is_read_at_mixed` renamed for the `Neg` it reads now; a head carrying a type into an arm, `match r | some(X) => (X) -> Bool/False end` over an argument naming the declaration, refused; a list `match` whose element type names a type parameter or a family outside the set and whose arms are strict, admitted, and one whose element type names the declaration analysed, refused; a `Bool` parameter a stuck `match` only selects on read at `Unused`; `M` over `Good` filing one level, irrelevant, as `F` does, and a control whose arm holds a `Type` at its level keeping it.
2. **A datum carries no type parameter.** The classification of data, memoized for one analysis, and the reading at each position a datum's type is stated by. Check: `Box` admitted, `Rose` over `Vec(Rose, k)` admitted, `Vec`'s entry filing `[Strict, Mixed]`, `M` over `Good` admitted, a predicate over a list written with a callback, `Bool/Holds(List/any(items, (x) => …))`, whose binder names the type parameter, admitted; refused, as Core text, a datum a recursive definition placed before the family computes by taking apart a value of it; read at `Mixed` still, each asserted on the filed vector and mutation-checked against the rule reading it at `Unused`: a type parameter in an index at a variable type, which is no datum, one in an index at a family whose payload is a sort, which is no datum, and a value parameter inside a datum.
3. **A recursive member is read by the fixpoint of its unfolding.** The summary over every target, computed for the group, kept for the walk. Check: the struct over `Balanced` admitted; `Held` over `Ok`/`All` admitted; refused: `struct S(X: Type): Type { n: Nat, f: (let R(k: Nat) -> Type = match k | 0 => X | _ => (R(k - 1)) -> Void end; Option(R(n))) }` beside `induct G: Type | g(s: S(G)) end`, a group closed over the parameter whose first meet is a family's argument, so it is unfolded once before its cycle closes; a definition wrapping its own call left of an arrow, its parameter's polarity climbing to `Mixed`; as Core text, a recursive definition placed before two families whose body names the second at depth one under an arrow; the formation ticket's definition-before-both witness; a family holding a generic predicate over a list of itself, `induct T | node(kids: List(T), @ok: AllSmall(kids))`, whose element type names it once the predicate unfolds; admitted, the same predicate held through a container, `struct Bag(X) { kids: List(X), @ok: AllSmall(kids) }` beside `T | node(b: Bag(T))`.
4. **A recursive group compares at the positions its unfolding holds.** The variance walk's fixpoint over a group, the generalization of a pair over its differing positions in `curios-core`, and `rec_instances` and `align_universe_levels` comparing at the held positions. Check: `Held` and the struct over `Balanced` filing one level, irrelevant, and the definitions `Ok` and `Balanced` filing irrelevant vectors; two projections of one group apart only at positions its unfolding holds none of converting in both checkers, a differential case beside the two statements of the rule, with a control whose arm holds a `Type` at the differing level refused, and one apart at a ground `Type 0` and `Type 1` held there refused; a definition's two instances deciding alike before and after unfolding in `curios`'s `tests::laws`; `/std/Map`'s and `/std/Toml`'s vectors unchanged; `curios`'s `tests::runaways` answering inside its stated budgets.
5. **A hunt round.** Its charters cross the readings with `Prop`, definitional K, a sort left to a caller, large elimination, a group closed over a walk's binders, Core text's definitions named before their families, and two instances of a group at two levels.

## Rejected

- **A datum told by its application's declared type**, a new `Env` query answering a global's type and a classification filed per family as its vector is: the type is already stated by where a datum stands or by what it reduces to, and a filed classification is a stored fact every verdict over it must digest, the second ticket's lesson.
- **A datum whose term arguments are read as before**, a dictionary or a callback naming a type parameter at `Mixed`: the whole datum is one value at every instance, and the reading would refuse a polymorphic predicate written with a callback.
- **A datum read past its declarations too**: a datum may eliminate a value of the declaration analysed, which the walk must still see.
- **A declaration an instance substituted for a type parameter read as that parameter**, in a datum or an annotation: no Core form tells it from a declaration a definition takes apart, the second being what the walk must see; a family holding a generic predicate over a list of itself stays refused, and the parameterized container that holds the predicate is admitted.
- **A type variable as data**: a value of one can be the parameter itself once a proof equates the variable with a sort, admitted only because no binder that could stand for that sort fits under the parameter's level, which is no part of the argument.
- **A summary over a member's own parameters alone**: `S` above admits `G`, though `S(G)` at `n = 1` holds `Option((G) -> Void)`; a group reached by its global name is unfolded twice before its cycle closes, which hides the loss.
- **Recursive members as nodes of the occurrence relation**, Agda's graph, their vectors joined by the declarations' fixpoint: a group closed over a walk's own binders has no key that outlives the walk, and a summary kept for the walk needs none.
- **A vector filed per definition**, Agda's `defArgOccurrences`, read wherever the definition is used: the sink the formation ticket's second witness walks through.
- **Polarity written in the surface**, Agda's `@++` and F*'s `@@@strictly_positive` and `@@@unused`: the positivity decision rejects it, the property being computed.
- **Lean's specialization of a nested occurrence into a mutual block** and **Rocq's nested positivity**: neither reaches a computed proof, Lean refusing `Vector (Foo f) 3` and Rocq an occurrence in an index, which is where a length's element type stands.
- **One walker for both analyses**, generic over what it records: positivity and variance record different things at the same nodes — variance reads a value's parameters and every level of a `match`'s goal and head, positivity a goal's declarations alone and a head only where an arm reads what it supplies — and the readings they share are pieces.
- **Variance reading past a `match`'s annotations as positivity does**: conversion compares a goal, so an irrelevance claimed there would be a definitional equation conversion denies.
- **Every level of a recursive group compared, as today**: a declaration holding a recursive proposition would keep every level the group mentions, which the family form it replaces holds none of, so the map would gain invariant levels and a parameter in every signature naming it.
- **`Mixed` as a stop** at a stuck form: the positivity decision rejects it, and every reading here reads deeper, never less.
