# A retry decides its problem in the context it froze

Working specification for how what waits keeps the context it waits in: a parked problem, and a metavariable. It is held to the elaborator's [Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution](../../../curios-elab/README.md#postponement-is-a-parked-obligation-never-a-raw-substitution), which has a retry decide its problem in exactly the context the problem froze and a term admitted whichever of its parts is read first, and to what [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) and [A diagnostic spells what its reader can write](../../design/tools/a-diagnostic-spells-what-its-reader-can-write.md) state of a metavariable, that a solution, a bound's fill and a goal's candidate are judged under the guards it was born under. Each of the two keeps a copy of its context, and a judgment that is not made where its context stands is made in the copy, rebuilt. Both copies lose which frame a guard stood in, and two programs and a goal are refused for it. This specification has a context held and not copied, by a parked problem and by a birth alike, and one function run a judgment in a context that is not the live one. Two defects met on the same ground are settled with it: a `let` block's local named by what leaves the block, and the report of a candidate rejected where its metavariable was born.

## What this builds on

- **A context is a stack of frames held as eight stacks.** `Frames` (`curios-elab/src/context/frames.rs`) keeps a frame's assumptions, their universe contexts, its definitions, its refinements of a variable, of a projection and of a stuck application, the reduced spelling each of the last was settled to, and why its arm is dead, each in a vector indexed by frame, and beside them the locals and the witness binders in binding order, each one list with a mark where a frame begins. `enter` pushes and `leave` pops. Three brackets narrow what a lookup reads without moving a frame: suppression withholds the refinements below a depth, a settlement those from a frame inwards, and a retry everything below a floor but the definitions.
- **Two copies of it, flat in the same list.** `Context::park` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`) files a problem with what `Frames::freeze` hands it, a `FrozenFrame`: the locals past the floor with their types, the definitions of every frame above the base, the refinements of every frame past the floor, the innermost dead arm, and the witness binders. A metavariable's record (`MetaEntry`, `curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`) holds its telescope, its refinements, its witness binders and its dead arm, each taken where it is born, and no definition. Both hold the refinements as one list a kind with no frame in it (`Refinements`), and both hand that list to `Context::install_refinements`, which registers every entry in the frame that stands.
- **Three ways into a context that is not the live one.** `Context::with_retry_frame`, of thirteen callers, enters a frame, hides every frame below it from every lookup but a definition's (`Frames::hide_frames_below_here`), and has `restore_frame` assume each frozen local again, define each frozen definition that no longer stands, and register the frozen refinements. `Context::with_refinements` judges at a birth: where the birth's refinements are the ones a lookup reads it runs where it stands, and otherwise it suppresses every refinement registered so far and registers the birth's in one frame above them. The locals, the definitions and the witness binders it reads are the live ones, and where a candidate is checked again or two answers are compared its telescope is assumed in a frame over them (`Convert::solve`, `answers_differ`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`). It has six callers: `Context::with_oracle`, a bound's discharge twice, and the solver three times. And `suggest_candidates` (`curios-elab/src/suggest.rs`) assumes a goal's telescope and registers its birth's refinements in one frame, where the item is zonked.
- **A guard's reduced spelling rests on the frames outside its own.** `settle` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`) reduces a stuck-application key with its entry's frame and every frame inside it withheld, and `Frame::scrutinee_spellings` remembers the result in the entry's frame. So which frame an entry stands in is part of what it means: an entry in the frame of an outer one is settled without it. The kernel holds each equation at a place of its own and settles it with that equation and every one inside it withheld (`Scope::hide_refinements_from`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/scope.rs`).
- **Three of the four kinds of parked work stand for a metavariable minted where they park**: a check for its placeholder (`Context::fresh_placeholder`), and a witness goal and a bound's discharge for their slots (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/apply.rs`). What such a problem froze is what its metavariable was born under, kept twice. A parked conversion stands for none.
- **A birth's context is not always one that stood.** A restriction births its stand-in over the binders of another metavariable's telescope it can spell, under the refinements the two births share (`Context::restrict_metavar`, `Refinements::shared_with`), and a lambda's domain is born at a scope taken before the lambda's binders are assumed (`Context::domain_scope`), with the refinements and the witness binders that stand where it is minted.
- **A `let` block is one frame, and what leaves it keeps the block's names.** `elaborate_let` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`) assumes and defines each binding into one frame and answers its tail's type as reduction left it, and `elaborate_rec` does so of a group; the frame then leaves, and with it what each name stood for. And the scope check of `Convert::solve` fails a candidate that names a local its metavariable's occurrence does not, whether or not a `let` bound that local. A block's label is a definition, and a metavariable born in the block takes it as one more binder: `Frames::identity_snapshot` cuts a telescope from every local, and the spine names the label as an argument, the renaming a solution is inverted through. A block's term is closed over its labels where the block ends, and its type is not. ## The gap

The programs are read at `34dfa9bc4`, over `let p(x: Nat) -> Bool = x % 2 == 0;`, `let q(c: Bool, y: Nat) -> Nat = match c | true => y | false => 0 end;`, `let need(y: Nat, z: Nat, @ok: Holds(y <= z)) -> Nat = y;`, `let late(@A: Type, @B: Type, f: (A) -> B, a: A) -> B = f(a);`, `struct Le(z: Nat): Type { low: Nat, @ok: Holds(low <= z) }`, `let same(@z: Nat, @w: Le(z), e: Eq(@Le(z))(w, w)) -> Nat = w.low;`, `let mk(@z: Nat, @w: Le(z)) -> Eq(@Le(z))(w, w) = Eq/refl();`, `let both(@A: Type, @v: A, a: Eq(@A)(v, v), b: Eq(@A)(v, v)) -> Nat = 0;` and `let tag(z: Nat) -> Eq()(z, z) = Eq/refl();`.

**A problem retried under two guards loses what the inner one says under the outer.** `match p(x) | true => match q(p(x), y) <= z | true => need(y, z) | false => 0 end | false => 0 end` compiles: the inner guard, read under the outer, is `y <= z`. With `late((v) => need(y, z), 0)` in place of `need(y, z)`, the lambda waits for its parameter's type and its body is read on a retry, and the declaration is refused: `implicit argument 'ok' of 'need' was not inferred`, with the inner guard listed among the facts as `match x % 2 == 0 | true => y | false => 0 end <= z`, and `a proof was found and did not check, which is the compiler's fault`. The two guards stand in one frame of the retry, and the inner one is settled with that frame withheld, the outer with it. It is refused the same where the retry runs in another arm than the lambda's, `late((v) => need(y, z), match b | true => y | false => z end)`, and compiles under one guard, `match y <= z | true => late((v) => need(y, z), 0) | false => 0 end`.

**A solution judged from another arm than its metavariable's loses the same.** In the inner arm of the two guards and after `let r: Eq(@Le(z))(Le { low = y }, Le { low = y }) = Eq/refl();`, the call `same(r)` compiles and `same(match b | true => r | false => r end)` is refused, a `type mismatch` between `r`'s type and `Eq(@Le(?))(?, ?)`. It is refused the same with `match p(k)` in place of `match b`, and compiles under one guard, under two where the inner one is `y <= z` itself, and with `@z` and `@Le { low = y }` written.

**A goal written under the two guards is offered no proof.** `let h: Holds(y <= z) = ?;` in that arm is reported with `a proof was found and did not check, which is the compiler's fault: please report it`, where `True/qed()` written in its place compiles; under one guard, and under two where the inner one is `y <= z` itself, the report offers `True/qed()`. A goal's candidates are sought with its birth's refinements registered in one frame (`suggest_candidates`).

A recursive block does the same, `let g(k: Nat) -> Nat = match k | 0 => 0 | j + 1 => g(j) end; tag(g(1))`. And `both((let n = 2; mk(@n)), s)` is refused, a `type mismatch` between `Eq(@Le(2))(?, ?)` and `Eq(@?)(?, ?)`, where `both(s, (let n = 2; mk(@n)))` compiles: read first, the block offers `Le(n)` for the implicit `A`, which is born outside it, and the scope check fails the candidate for naming `n`. Each compiles with `2` written for `n`.

**And it leaves through the metavariables born in the block**, each of which holds it as a binder and names it in its spine. Through one already solved: over `let wrap(@A: Type, a: A) -> Eq(@A)(a, a) = Eq/refl();` and `p: Le(2)`, `let _r = (let x = 2; let q: Le(x) = p; wrap(q)); 0` is refused by the kernel, `unbound name`, `A` having been solved to `Le(x)`, and a substitution of the block's type does not reach it. **The context rebuilt is nearly always the one that stands.** Of the 4,922 retries counted above, 157 run in another context than their problem's: 86 under other locals, 65 under the same locals and other guards, and 6 under another refinement of a variable. The other 4,765 hide the frames they stand on, assume and register again what those frames hold, and settle again what those frames had settled.

## Decisions

**A context is held, and never copied.** A frame is one value holding everything the eight stacks hold of it, its locals and its witness binders in binding order among them, numbered where it is entered, and the context is a stack of them. A frame may hold a name twice, an arm assuming its own payload binder again at the type its case's solution gives it (`retype_locals`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs`), and the later one is read. What waits takes the frames above the base as they stand, shared and not copied: a parked problem where it parks, and a metavariable where it is born. A frame written to while something holds it is copied for the writer first, the copy keeping the frame's number, so a holder has what stood and nothing written since. The base frame is the elaboration's and is never held: what waits with no local frame standing holds none.

**One function runs a judgment in a context that is not the live one.** The live frames above the base are set aside, the held ones stand in their place, the work runs in a frame of its own above them, and the live ones come back. Nothing is assumed, defined or registered again, so the frame an entry stands in is the frame it was registered in, and what the work writes is written to its own frame and goes with it. What parks or is born meanwhile holds the frames stood on and that frame. A retry is this function, a judgment at a birth made from elsewhere is, and a goal's report is; nothing else enters a context.

**A retry stands on the frames its problem holds**, whether or not they are the live ones, and a problem left blocked is filed again with the frames it had. A parked check, a witness goal and a bound's discharge hold what the metavariable they stand for holds, the same frames.

**What is judged of a metavariable is judged where it was born.** Where the frames it was born under still stand, outermost first, and the live context holds no refinement they do not, the judgment runs where it stands: the live context is then the birth's with binders and definitions added, and what is judged names none of them. Otherwise it stands on the birth's frames. So a candidate checked again, two answers compared, a bound discharged, the solver's reading of a candidate and a goal's candidates read the guards of the birth in the frames they stood in, its definitions, and its witness binders.

**A birth keeps its telescope and its frames.** The telescope stays a list, what a solution may name and what a restriction prunes. The refinements, the witness binders and the dead arm are read off the frames, and whether one birth's refinements are within another's is asked of the two births' frames, entry by entry as it is asked today. A restriction's stand-in holds the frames both births hold, outermost first for as far as they are the same frames, each as the earlier of the two held it, and one a block's end births holds the frames its metavariable holds beneath the block's own. A lambda's domain holds the frames that stood where its scope was taken.

**A definition is its frames'.** A judgment on held frames reads the definitions of those frames and of the base. One made in the live context since is not in view, and none is read there today.

**What asks every frame asks the frames set aside too.** Whether an application is a guard's key is asked of every frame today, the hidden and the suppressed among them, so that a solution committed where a guard is not in view stays a term the guard answers (`Frames::is_scrutinee_key`); and so are the gates in front of that question and of the closed machine (`has_scrutinee_refinements`, `scrutinee_head_refined`, `any_refinements_registered`). Each is asked of the frames set aside as well, and nothing else is. A key is kept neutral where a frame set aside registers it and none that stands does: a guard the judgment's own frames register is its own, and unfolds where it does not answer as it does with nothing set aside, which the opening of that guard under a settlement rests on (the bound `/std/Char/to_ascii_lower` discharges from its guard's two halves).

**What a frame remembers stays with the frame, and a count forgets it.** A guard's settled spelling is the frame's, so the live context and every judgment that stands on the frame read one memo and settle a key once between them. It stays what the key reduces to there: a spelling rests on the frames below its own, and a frame stands on the same ones wherever it is held. Each spelling is stamped with a count of the events that change what a key reduces to in every frame at once, beside the count of solutions and the universe solver's state it is stamped with already (`Spelling`), and a read under a later count misses. The ten functions of `Context` that clear every spelling today advance that count, so a frame only a parked problem or a birth holds forgets as the live ones do, and no walk is made.

**A settlement's withholding is its stack's, and suppression goes.** The withholding is a depth in the frames that stood where it began: standing on held frames sets it aside with them and gives it back. Suppression has one caller, the judgment at a birth that standing on held frames replaces.

**The caches are told what they are told today.** Standing on held frames clears the reducts and the elaborations at its two ends where the frames set aside or stood on hold a refinement, and stamps the write; a judgment made where it stands tells them nothing. What a reduct rests on beyond its term is not stated ([A reduct states what it rests on](203-a-reduct-states-what-it-rests-on.md)), and until it is no table keeps one longer than it does now.

**The copies go, and two of the three ways in.** `FrozenFrame`, `Frames::freeze`, `restore_frame`, the floor and what reads it; `Refinements`, `Frames::refinement_snapshot`, `install_refinements`, `with_refinements`' second half and the suppression it enters; the frame `suggest_candidates` builds; and a birth's refinements, witness binders and dead arm have no reader left.
