# A retry decides its problem in the context it froze

Working specification for how a parked problem keeps the context it parked under. It is held to the elaborator's [Postponement is a parked obligation, never a raw substitution](../../../curios-elab/README.md#postponement-is-a-parked-obligation-never-a-raw-substitution), which has a retry decide its problem in exactly the context the problem froze. The code keeps that rule for the locals a problem parked under, their order and their types, for the witness binders among them and for the reason its arm is never taken. It breaks it for the refinements, which a retry holds in one frame where the problem parked under several; for a local's universe context, which a retry holds empty; and for what each guard's key reduces to, which a retry settles again.

## What this builds on

- **A context is a stack of frames held as eight stacks.** `Frames` (`curios-elab/src/context/frames.rs`) keeps a frame's assumptions, their universe contexts, its definitions, its refinements of a variable, of a projection and of a stuck application, the reduced spelling each of the last was settled to, and why its arm is dead, each in a vector indexed by frame, and beside them the locals and the witness binders in binding order, each one list with a mark where a frame begins. `enter` pushes and `leave` pops. Three brackets narrow what a lookup reads without moving a frame: suppression withholds the refinements below a depth, a settlement those from a frame inwards, and a retry everything below a floor but the definitions.
- **A park copies the context out.** `Context::park` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`) files a problem with what `Frames::freeze` hands it, a `FrozenFrame`: the locals past the floor with their types, the definitions of every frame above the base, the refinements of every frame past the floor flattened to one list a kind (`Refinements`), the innermost dead arm, and the witness binders. Nothing else of a frame is taken.
- **A retry rebuilds it.** `Context::with_retry_frame` enters a frame, hides every frame below it (`Frames::hide_frames_below_here`), and has `restore_frame` assume each frozen local again, define each frozen definition that no longer stands, and register each frozen refinement again in that one frame (`install_refinements`); the work runs, the floor is given back and the frame leaves. The caches are told at each step: the reducts and the elaborations are cleared where a hidden frame holds a refinement, at every refinement registered, where the floor is given back, and where the frame leaves.
- **One function reads a frozen frame.** `restore_frame` reads its five fields, and a report asks whether it carries refinements (`FrozenFrame::carries_refinements`). Everything else hands it on whole: the parked problems and what a completing term asks of them (`ParkedProblem`, `ParkedChoice`, `ParkedConversion`, `curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`), and the functions that retry one.
- **A guard's reduced spelling rests on the frames outside its own.** `settle` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`) reduces a stuck-application key with its entry's frame and every frame inside it withheld, and `Frames::scrutinee_spellings` remembers the result in the entry's frame. So which frame an entry stands in is part of what it means: an entry in the frame of an outer one is settled without it.
- **A birth copies the context too.** A metavariable's record (`MetaEntry`, `curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`) holds its telescope, its refinements, its witness binders and its dead arm, each taken where it is born, the refinements flattened as a frozen frame's are. `Context::with_refinements` judges under them, at once where they are the ones a lookup reads and otherwise by suppressing every refinement and registering the birth's again in one frame. Six callers: the re-validation of a candidate, a bound's discharge twice, and the solver three times, where it reads a candidate as its metavariable's birth does.
- **How often, and how much.** Over `/std`'s elaboration at `022931b7f`, counted: 3,859 problems park and 3,834 retries run, 3,815 of them in the very context their problem froze, 1,663 of those with a refinement in it; of the elaboration's 7,709 settlements, 2,557 are made directly inside one of those 1,663. At `4fe2620dc`, counted, where 4,133 park: 291 of them with no local frame standing; a park copies 2.6 frames, 6.1 locals, 1.1 definitions and 1.3 refinements on average, and the innermost local frame it parks under holds 2.7 entries on average and 40 at most.
- **Peers hold the context and stand on it.** Lean 4 records with each metavariable the local context it may depend on (`MetavarDecl.lctx`, [`MetavarContext`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/MetavarContext.html)), a [`LocalContext`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/LocalContext.html) held in a persistent map and a persistent array, and a postponed elaboration carries the context saved for it (`SavedContext`, [`TermElabM`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Elab/Term/TermElabM.html)). Agda keeps a value with the environment and scope it was made in and swaps them in to read it (`enterClosure`, [`Agda.TypeChecking.Monad.Closure`](https://hackage-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.6.0/docs/src/Agda.TypeChecking.Monad.Closure.html)). Neither rebuilds one.
- **What [A guard is a hypothesis its arm binds](../soundness/201-a-guard-is-a-hypothesis-its-arm-binds.md) would take with it.** Under that specification, not refined yet, an expression's guard is a local its arm binds: the store of stuck-application refinements goes, and its settled spellings with it. A flat copy then loses nothing of a guard, and the refusal of *The gap* is gone with no change here. What is left of this specification is what a frame holds besides: a local's universe context, the definitions, and whatever a frame comes to hold next.

## The gap

The programs are read at `022931b7f`, over `let p(x: Nat) -> Bool = x % 2 == 0;`, `let q(c: Bool, y: Nat) -> Nat = match c | true => y | false => 0 end;`, `let need(y: Nat, z: Nat, @ok: Holds(y <= z)) -> Nat = y;` and `let late(@A: Type, @B: Type, f: (A) -> B, a: A) -> B = f(a);`.

**A problem retried under two guards loses what the inner one says under the outer.** `match p(x) | true => match q(p(x), y) <= z | true => need(y, z) | false => 0 end | false => 0 end` compiles: the inner guard, read under the outer, is `y <= z`. With `late((v) => need(y, z), 0)` in place of `need(y, z)`, the lambda waits for its parameter's type and its body is read on a retry, and the declaration is refused: `implicit argument 'ok' of 'need' was not inferred`, with the inner guard listed among the facts as `match x % 2 == 0 | true => y | false => 0 end <= z`, and `a proof was found and did not check, which is the compiler's fault`. The two guards stand in one frame of the retry, and the inner one is settled with that frame withheld, the outer with it. It is refused the same where the retry runs in another arm than the lambda's, `late((v) => need(y, z), match b | true => y | false => z end)`, and compiles under one guard, `match y <= z | true => late((v) => need(y, z), 0) | false => 0 end`.

**A retry holds its locals at no universe instance.** `restore_frame` assumes a frozen local through `Context::assume`, which gives it an empty universe context, where the local had the one its binder was given; `Frames::freeze` says so of the base frame's names, which it leaves out for that reason. No program is known to be refused by it.

**The context rebuilt is nearly always the one that stands.** Of the 3,834 retries counted above, 19 run in another context than their problem's. The other 3,815 hide the frames they stand on, assume and register again what those frames hold, and settle again what those frames had settled.

## Decisions

**A problem holds the frames it parks under.** A frame is one value holding everything the eight stacks hold of it, and the context is a stack of them. A park takes the frames above the base as they stand, shared and not copied; a frame written to while a problem holds it is copied for the writer first, so a problem holds what it parked under and nothing written since. The base frame is the elaboration's and is never held: a problem parked with no local frame standing holds none.

**A retry stands on them.** The live frames above the base are set aside, the held ones stand in their place, the work runs, and the live ones come back. Nothing is assumed, defined or registered again, so the frame an entry stands in is the frame it was registered in, and a retry whose held frames are the live ones sets nothing aside. A problem that parks during a retry holds the frames the retry stands on, and one left blocked is filed again with the frames it had.

**A retry sees what its problem saw, and no more.** Its locals stand at the universe contexts their binders gave them. Its definitions are its frames' and the base's: one made in the live context since the park is not in view. A write a retry makes to a held frame is to a copy of its own, which goes when the retry ends, as its frame's writes go today.

**What a frame remembers stays with the frame.** A guard's settled spelling is the frame's, so the live context and every retry that stands on the frame read one memo and settle a key once between them. It stays what the key reduces to there: a spelling rests on the frames below its own, and a frame stands on the same ones wherever it is held.

**A bracket is its stack's.** Suppression and a settlement's withholding are depths in the frames that stood where they began. A retry that sets those frames aside sets the two depths aside with them and gives them back when it ends.

**The caches are told what a retry tells them today.** A retry whose frames, set aside or stood on, hold a refinement clears the reducts and the elaborations at its two ends and stamps the write, whether or not the frames it stands on are the live ones. What a reduct rests on beyond its term is not stated ([A reduct states what it rests on](203-a-reduct-states-what-it-rests-on.md)), and until it is no table keeps one longer than it does now.

**The floor goes.** `retry_floor`, `hide_frames_below_here` and what reads them, `restore_frame`, and the five lists of a frozen frame have no reader left. `install_refinements` keeps the one that judges under a birth.

## Open questions

**Whether a birth holds its frames.** A metavariable's record is four copies of where it was born, and `with_refinements` registers the flat one again, so a solution judged in another arm than its metavariable's meets the guards of its birth side by side. No program is known to be refused for it: a candidate read there comes out less reduced, which the solver takes. Standing on a birth's frames would also change which locals a re-validation sees, from the live ones to the birth's. Check: a program over two nested guards whose implicit is solved from outside the inner arm, refused where the same implicit solved inside it is taken; and the count, over `/std`, of the calls of `with_refinements` that take its slow half with more than one frame of refinements in the birth.

**Whether a retry reads a definition made after its park.** Today a retry sees every definition of the context it runs in. Counted before the rule changes: the retries over `/std` and the suites whose work reads, through `Frames::definition_body` or `definition_kind`, a name none of its problem's frames defines.

**How the locals are kept.** `Frames::locals` hands out one slice of the binding order, read by the dependent match's generalizer (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs`) and by the facts a bound is proved from (`curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`), and the telescope a birth takes is cut from it. Kept a frame at a time they are swapped with their frames; kept as one list they are a ninth thing to set aside. Settled in the first stage, by what leaves the image as it is.

## Stages

Each lands alone, on its own check.

1. **A frame is one value.** `Frames` holds a stack of frames behind the methods it has, each frame what the eight stacks held of it; nothing is shared, a park copies and a retry rebuilds as they do. Check: `/std`'s image the same in every part, with `certification.rkyv` and `reads.rkyv`, and the elaborator's tests.
2. **A park holds its frames and a retry stands on them.** The count of the second open question is taken first. Check: the two programs of *The gap* that are refused compile and the others still do, as fixtures run and not only elaborated; the fixtures of `curios-elab/src/context/tests.rs` that state what a retry sees, each restated where its rule is restated; the image compared, each declaration that moves named with what moved it.
3. **What a birth holds**, if the first open question finds a program. Otherwise the stage is cut, and the four copies a birth takes are filed as a cost.
4. **Retirement**, as *Completion and retirement* states.

## Verification

- **The programs of *The gap*** are fixtures in both forms, the call read in place and the call read on a retry, under one guard, under two, and with the retry in another arm.
- **The image compared.** `curios-prelude-archive`'s build writes `/std`'s image as `std.rkyv` under its `OUT_DIR`, and `curios-prelude`'s writes `certification.rkyv` and `reads.rkyv` under its own. A release build of the commit before a stage and one of the stage, both at one path, are compared part by part (`curios-unit`'s `segments`, `halves` and `apart_parts`), and the elaborated module item by item under its declaration's name. At `4fe2620dc`, counted, with a retry run in a plain frame wherever the context it would rebuild is the one that stands and the caches told as today: three declarations' terms move, `/std/List/balanced`, `/std/Str/Valid/interrupted` and `/std/Flt/of_decimal_in`, and the erased forms do not. The first two are the frames kept apart: they move alike where the copy keeps its refinements a frame each and registers each in a frame of its own. The third is a proof that holds a copy of a `let`'s value again, as it does at `022931b7f`, where its printed form is refused when read back (`curios-pipeline/src/standard/tests.rs`).
- **The retries counted.** In a copy of the commit, `with_retry_frame` compares the frozen frame with what a lookup reads before it hides anything, list by list in order: the locals past the floor, the witness binders, each of the three refinement stores past the floor, the dead arm by identity, and each frozen definition still defined with its body and its kind, with no suppression and no withholding in force. A `curios_profile::sample!` files the verdict and whether the frozen frame holds a refinement, and `Frames::freeze` files the sizes it copies. `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` files the stream; a settlement is inside a retry where a `reduce::settle` span is entered while a `ctx::retry_frame` span is open.
- `cargo xtask workspace lint` at every stage, with the stage's fixtures by name, and `cargo xtask workspace test` once the last has landed.
- Each stage names the figures it may have moved: the budget every declaration of `/std` consumes. At `4fe2620dc`, counted, a retry on the frames that stand with the caches told as today takes the elaboration from 62,266,095 units to 62,047,105 and its settlements from 7,722 to 6,013.

## Rejected

- **The copy kept, with its refinements a frame each.** Registered again in a frame apiece it admits the programs of *The gap*, and costs more than the flat copy does, a frame entered and left for every one; and it stays a second statement of what a frame holds, which the next thing a frame holds has to be added to.
- **The copy kept, and skipped where it equals what stands.** It spares the 3,815 and rebuilds for the 19, so the rule is kept by a comparison where it holds and broken as before where it does not.
- **A retry that tells the caches nothing where it stands on the live frames.** It keeps the reducts across the retry: 189,120 units of `/std`'s 62 million at `4fe2620dc`, counted, and four more declarations' terms move for it. A reduct kept longer is the finding's question, not this rule's.
- **A chain of bindings, each a node, in place of a stack of frames.** Every lookup changes for it, and a park holds 2.6 frames whose innermost holds 2.7 entries: there is nothing for a finer grain to share.
- **A snapshot a frame, taken at the park.** It copies what sharing leaves alone, and a spelling settled in a retry is the snapshot's, not the frame's.
- **Fewer parks instead.** A problem that waits for nothing is decided where it stands, and a `use` slot nothing can name is (`curios-elab`'s decision on postponement); a lambda that waits for its parameter's type has to wait.
- **A problem closed over its context**, as GHC files a wanted constraint under the givens and skolems of its implication and solves it by walking the tree ([`GHC.Tc.Types.Constraint`](https://hackage-content-origin.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.14.1/docs/GHC-Tc-Types-Constraint.html)). A retried check elaborates a term, which resolves names and binds under the scope itself.

## Completion and retirement

- Every program under *The gap* compiles in both forms.
- A parked problem holds no copy of its context, and nothing in `Frames` or `Context` assumes, defines or registers on a retry's behalf.
- The elaborator's `README.md` states under *Postponement is a parked obligation* that a problem holds the frames it parked under and a retry stands on them, with the copy among what was rejected; `frames.rs`'s module documentation states what a frame is and who may hold one.
- `documentation/roadmap.md`'s line is a checked summary, this file is deleted, and no reference to its name remains.
