# A family indexed by a family that holds it

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves induction-induction: a family whose parameters or indices name a family that holds it, as a typed syntax declares a context and the types over it together. It is not an implementation plan: it waits for a program that needs one.

## What is missing

**Both checkers refuse it.** A family's parameters and indices, and a record's or a concept's parameters, name no declaration that holds it ([Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md)), so `induct Ctx: Type | empty() | ext(g: Ctx, a: Ty(g)) and Ty(g: Ctx): Type | unit() end` is refused at `Ty`. The elaborator refuses a declaration of its group (`indexed_by_its_group`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) and the certifier one of its own item or of an item that reaches it back (`indexed_by_its_cycle`, `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`), each by `IndexedByItsGroup`, since Core text can declare the two families apart.

## What is known

- **What the refusal keeps.** Positivity walks no index binder type, which is sound only because nothing a declaration's own cycle declares stands there: `B : (f : (A) -> False) -> Type` beside an `A` holding a `B` carries a negative occurrence of `A` where the walk does not look. Admitting a family indexed by a family that holds it means walking those types, or stating why what a family is indexed by needs no walk.
- **What the peers do.** Lean types a mutual block's signatures without the block's types ("each type constructor's signature must be able to be elaborated without reference to the other inductive types", [its reference](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/The-Type-System/Inductive-Types/) on mutual inductive types). Rocq types a block's arities before its types are in scope, and a pull request adding induction-induction ([rocq#12464](https://github.com/coq/coq/pull/12464)) was closed unmerged, stating "No guarantees about consistency, decidability of typechecking or subject reduction are made". Agda admits it through `mutual`, and its maintainers call its termination checker's success with it "mere coincidence" ([agda#6654](https://github.com/agda/agda/issues/6654)); whether ignoring the recursive calls in an inductive-inductive type's parameters is sound is open there ([agda#2527](https://github.com/agda/agda/issues/2527)), as are definitions that stand in their own types ([agda#1556](https://github.com/agda/agda/issues/1556)).
- **What the theory gives.** Forsberg and Setzer give inductive-inductive definitions a set-theoretic model ([*Inductive-inductive definitions*](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/id/eprint/53108)), and every model of extensional type theory with indexed W-types supports the finitary ones ([Kaposi, Kovács and Lafont, TYPES 2019](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/opus/volltexte/2020/13070/)), so a finitary one adds no consistency strength over the inductive families Curios has. Neither covers a strict, definitionally irrelevant `Prop` with K, the model [Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md) already names as missing.
- **What was tried while both checkers admitted it.** A hunt at `910ab8f34` drove a cast route through `W : Prop` and `S : (W) -> Prop | mk(v : W): (v)`, `W` holding an `S`, and found it refused by descent (`NotDescending`); no proof of `False` was found through one. Declared apart as Core text, the pair was refused by positivity where the family that holds the other is walked first, and admitted where it is walked second, an order no program writes.

## Refinement

- **The consumer.** A program that needs one, typed syntax being the usual: what it declares, and why an indexed family over data declared apart does not serve it.
- **The decision.** A theory decision admitting it for the finitary case, stating the walk over what a family is indexed by or why none is needed, how elimination and size-change read a family indexed by a family of its cycle, and the rule this specification lifts, restated in the positivity decision.
- **Check**: the consumer's program runs; `recheck::positivity_tests` and `tests::rules::positivity_index_tests`' `a_family_is_indexed_by_nothing_that_holds_it` restated to what is admitted and what is still refused, the negative occurrence in an index domain among the latter; a hunt round whose charters cross it with `Prop`, K, large elimination, index inversion and size-change.
