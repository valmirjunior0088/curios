# A guard is a hypothesis its arm binds

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves the form in which an arm holds what its case says of an expression scrutinee: a hypothesis the arm binds and the elaborator proves from, where the kernel holds it today as an equation its reduction answers by. It is not an implementation plan, and no design is chosen. It waits on the count named under *Refinement*, which says how much of what a proof takes from an equation today would have to be written.

## What is missing

**The kernel decides which terms an arm's equation answers, and the elaborator decides it again.** [Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md) has `whnf` consult an arm's equation at every stuck head and before a term is taken apart, so the trusted base holds what that takes: the three spellings an equation is recorded under, the reduced one settled on a miss, the carriers' readers and the kernel's own conversion asked from inside reduction, the equations restated under an arm's solution and withheld while one is settled, a second reduction that asks nothing, and the lives of every memo that reads the scope. The elaborator holds each again, spelled as the kernel spells it (`Context::kernel_spelling`, `restate_equations` and `withhold_closed_equations`, `curios-elab/src/context.rs`; `hold_at_one_instance`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`), since a term one checker answers and the other does not is a program accepted and then refused. Two of the seven tickets on [the soundness board](../../design/soundness/the-soundness-board.md) are this mechanism's: an arm of a fold typed with its scrutinee at the arm's case, and a guard's reduced spelling settled under one case of a split and answered outside it.

## What is known

- **What the equations are used for, over the prelude.** Counted at `ca95d7595` over the certification of `/sys` and `/std`, 2,964 items: 3,045 arms assume an equation and 81 more are restated under an inner arm's solution, ten of those left naming no local; an equation answers 1,522 times. Of those answers 1,506 fall inside a term checked at a proposition. The other 16 are guards as well: a comparison at `Nat`, a `Bool` projection of a tuple, `/std/http/Status/carries_content(code)`.
- **Which spelling answers.** 976 of the 1,522 by the scrutinee as written or as its dispatch resolves, 321 by its dual or its spelling across the successor, 138 by its settled reduct, 76 by the carriers' readers and 11 by the kernel's conversion, with none by atoms classed. The last three kinds, 225 answers, are what 1,507 settlements and 45,810 stuck forms probed past the written spelling are for.
- **How the counts are taken.** A scratch copy of the tree, with a line printed in `curios-cert`: where `Scope::refine` records an equation or skips one, where `Scope::restate` restates one and whether it still names a local, at each answer of `whnf_within` and `refined_reduct` by the probe that gave it, at each `Kernel::settle_refinement`, and at each stuck form `refined_reduct` takes past its written probes. Whether an answer falls inside a proof is a thread-local depth, raised in `check` while the position `Kernel::record_checked` hands back is a proof's. `curios-prelude`'s build script is run by itself, `OUT_DIR` set, and the lines tallied.
- **A guard is already a fact to the elaborator.** [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) reads the guards of the arms around a bound beside the hypotheses, and writes its certificate as an ordinary term over them. A hypothesis is named in that term; a guard is not, and its fact is `True/qed()` at the guard's type, which checks by the kernel's equation.
- **Peers keep the equation as evidence in the term the checker is handed.** Zombie's case rule binds `x : a = true` in each branch, its elaborator uses the equations by congruence closure and its core holds explicit casts (Sjöberg and Weirich, [Programming up to Congruence](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~sweirich/papers/popl15-congruence.pdf), §8.1). GHC's Core passes evidence for a type equality as System F passes types, and so needs no typing rule of its own for a match that refines one ([System F with type equality coercions](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/system-f-with-type-equality-coercions/)). Lean's `match h : e` puts `h : e = p` in scope of an alternative ([Pattern Matching](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Terms/Pattern-Matching)). Agda translates a `with` to an auxiliary function and gives the equation by `with … in eq` ([With-Abstraction](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/language/with-abstraction.html)). Burke's thesis specifies smart case with equations in contexts, finds its restriction on them unstable under substitution and its normalization open, and ends at a core whose equations belong to global definitions ([Local Rewriting in Dependent Type Theory](https://github.com/NathanielB123/fyp/blob/main/submitted.pdf), chapters 5 and 6).
- **What it reverses.** [Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md) whole, and of [An arm is checked in a context specialized by index inversion](../../design/theory/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md) the clause that has an expression scrutinee stand as a case equation, with its rejection of an elaborator that writes the convoy. A variable scrutinee is substituted, in both checkers, and is not touched.
- **What a hypothesis does not give.** A type that reduces in an arm because its scrutinee does: the elaborator would write the transport, or the reader the convoy, where conversion finds it today.

## Refinement

- **What an arm binds**, and at which cases: `Holds` of a `Bool` guard and of its negation, or an equation between the scrutinee and the case's value at every carrier.
- **How a proof names a guard's fact.** Not known: how many of the 1,506 answers are the guard's own fact at a proof's leaf, which a bound hypothesis states outright, and how many reduce a type inside a proof, which need a transport written. Check: the count taken again with the two told apart.
- **What the 16 answers outside a proof become.** Each is read and placed: a fact after all, or a type computed from a guard.
- **Whether it lands in steps.** An arm could bind its hypothesis while the kernel still holds the equation, so that what still rests on the equation is counted before the store is removed.
- **Check.** `/std` certifies with no equation recorded, and the kernel still types every instance of a type it typed, as [An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md) holds it to: `curios-prelude-archive`'s `the_kernel_types_a_declared_type_at_each_value_of_a_parameter` reports none, and the programs of `curios`'s `tests::matching::instance_tests` run.
