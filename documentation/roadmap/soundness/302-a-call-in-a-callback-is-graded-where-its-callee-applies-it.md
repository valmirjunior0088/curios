# A call in a callback is graded where its callee applies it

This specification describes what remains of a capability that has landed in part. A recursion through a callback a definition applies is graded where the definition applies it ([Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)): a tree's `has_zero` recursing through `List/any(children, (c) => has_zero(c))` is total (`curios`'s `a_call_in_a_callback_descends_where_its_callee_applies_it_below`), and so is one through `/sys/List/map`, `/sys/List/fold` or a definition of no group. What is still graded as fresh is listed below, a projection of a binder first, and a derived witness on a type whose children sit in a container last.

## What is missing

- **A projection of a binder.** The grader reads a projection's shape off its subject's expansion, and a binder has none, so `p.0` is below nothing, wherever `p` is bound: `size(p.0)`, `p` the tuple an arm takes out of `pair({Expr, Expr})`, is refused as not known to terminate, and `/std/Json/encode`'s `obj` arm, which calls `encode(pair.1)` with `pair` an element of `ps`, stays partial once `pair` is bounded below `ps`. By eta a record is the node of its fields, and a proof record's least derivation is its introduction over its fields', so each field ranks strictly below the record in the order the totality decision states.
- **A callback handed on to another function.** A parameter handed to another summarized definition is a use the summary does not follow, so `any_kept`, which hands its `pred` to `List/any`, publishes nothing for it (`a_callback_its_callee_hands_on_is_graded_as_unrelated`), and `List`'s witness, whose method hands its `use` parameter to `List/eql`, would publish nothing for that parameter. Composing the callee's summary at the call inside the definition being summarized follows it.
- **A call through a callee's field.** A derived `Eql` on `induct Rose | rose(Nat, List(Rose))` compares the children through `/std/List/witness~/std/ops/Eql(List)(Rose, W).0(x4, x6)`: `List`'s witness applied, projected to its method and applied. A summary states how a definition's leading lambdas apply its parameters, and the method is a lambda field of the record those lambdas answer, so nothing is filed of what it applies.

## Stages

1. **A projection of a binder.** The grader reads `b.i`, and a chain of projections of `b`, strictly below `b` for any binder `b`, and a projection of a record literal as its field; an application of a projected field stays unread. Check: `/std/Json/encode` is total as a fixture put to both checkers, beside a payload tuple's field, a parameter's structure field and a proof record's field, each descending, with refusals that project a record not below, `size((e, e).0)`.
2. **A parameter handed on.** The summary walk composes a summarized callee's applications where a parameter is handed to it, so the definition's summary states what it reaches through the callee. Check: `a_callback_its_callee_hands_on_is_graded_as_unrelated` restated as descending, with a refusal where the callee applies the parameter at something not below.
3. **A call through a callee's field.** A summary is kept for each part of a definition, as for its leading lambdas, and a path rooted at a definition is graded by the summary of the part it reaches. Check: a derived `Eql` on `Rose`, reached by a type, passes both checkers, with a refusal where a container's method applies the witness at its own argument; the record's format raised where a summary's archived form moves.

## Retirement

Each stage lands with the totality decision restated where it moves the rule. Then this specification is deleted, and the soundness findings' entry on a call read off a path names the premises it added: a projection below its record, and a summary composed through a callee's.
