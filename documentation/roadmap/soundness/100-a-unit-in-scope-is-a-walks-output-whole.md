# A unit in scope is a walk's output whole

Broken rule: [The soundness board](../../design/soundness/the-soundness-board.md) leaves bytes no walk wrote into the store outside the claim, yet calls withholding — a record a walk filed, written back with part of it gone, which no walk wrote either — the power over a believed record that stays inside it; and the certifier keeps a second way of reading a unit in scope, for a record that does not answer for every name its unit holds, which only that withholding and environments built by hand reach. The repair is the boundary, not a guard: a unit in scope is what a walk returned, whole, its record answers for every name its unit holds, and nothing reads a unit any other way.

What reaches the certifier as a unit in scope is one of three: a unit a build compiled, beside the record its walk returned; a unit the store hands back, filed with such a record (`cache::put`, `curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`); and the archived prelude, walked again by the build that embeds it (`curios-prelude/compile.rs`). `History::withhold` (`curios-verdict/src/test_support.rs`) is none of them, and neither is `already_judged` (`curios-cert/src/recheck/test_support.rs`), which mounts an empty record. Agreed with the user on 2026-11-18, lifted from the findings as a delivery of its own.

The next step is stage 1, a delivery.

## What is known

- **The rule the certifier states is the one this replaces.** `curios-cert/README.md`'s "A later walk reads the certifier's own totality record" decides that a record naming fewer definitions than its unit is not read and its unit classified from its terms, and rejects "reading a record one name at a time" and "refusing a unit whose record does not cover it, which costs a fault every hand-built environment must avoid". Both rejections rest on such records existing, and once none does neither reason holds; `curios-unit/README.md`'s "A unit is certified by construction" has `Unit::new` take a record beside a module, any record beside any module.
- **The second way of reading.** `Globals::mount` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/globals.rs`) asks `Certification::covers` of each unit and reads its record only where it covers: `classified`, `partial` and `applications` read a covering record alone; `unclassified` hands `Totals::seeded` (`obligation.rs`) an uncovered unit's items to classify from their terms; `unvectored`, `unvaried` and `unfiled` hand `file_scope_polarities` and `file_scope_variances` (`recheck.rs`) the entries no record files a vector for; `read_stands` and `stored` (`recheck.rs`) stand on no part of an uncovered unit; `verdicts_within` drops a baseline whose record does not cover its module; and `DigestedUnit` (`baseline.rs`) carries `covers` for `part_digest`, by which `curios-erase` digests a part of a unit in scope (`asked.rs`, `erased.rs`, `erased_candidate.rs`, `over.rs`).
- **No build reaches it, counted.** With `Globals::mount`, the baseline filter and `DigestedUnit::of` made to panic on a record that does not answer for every name or every former's vectors, `cargo xtask workspace test` at `61bee406e` ran 6668 tests and 28 failed, each an environment built by hand or a record withheld: `curios-cert` 18 (`scope_tests` 11, `totality_tests` 3, `adoption_tests` 2, `standing_tests` 1, `variance_tests` 1) and `curios-verdict` 10 (`believed_tests` 8, `test_support::tests` 2). No compile, recompile, stored unit, prelude root or erasure met one, and every record a build mounted files both vectors for every former. Counted; `cargo xboard report` was not run under the panics.
- **The forged record the boundary keeps forges two things.** `adoption_tests`' `a_forged_total_verdict_mounted_in_scope_admits_a_looping_proof` states the boundary's trust by a record covering every totality and filing no vector for its family, so it reaches the second way of reading as well as the lie it states.
- **The board's two withholding tickets reach their flaws only by withholding.** `admission`'s `2026_11_05_a_read_of_a_unit_with_no_covering_record…` and `2026_11_05_a_family_of_a_unit_with_no_vector_on_record…` are flaws of the recomputation the second way feeds; put without their withholding steps through `xboard probe` at `61bee406e`, the first is refused `NotTotal` at the closing term and the second `NotPositive` in `/app/X`, each by an ordinary refusal and no part of its flaw. Probed.
- **The walk's answer carries a record either way.** `Rechecked` (`curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`) holds a record beside its verdicts — an empty one where it refuses a module whole, for a redeclaration or a miscounted case, a full one where it refuses a declaration — and its readers take the record only once a refusal is ruled out: the unit's compile and the program's (`curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`), the recompile (`recompile.rs`) and the prelude's build (`curios-prelude/compile.rs`), each through `accepted`; `recheck`, `examine`, `curios-pipeline`'s `test_support` and the board's put read the verdicts alone. `recheck` (`compile.rs`) is read by tests alone.
- **One view, three types.** A unit as the certifier reads it is its module, where the module holds each name, and the record its walk filed, which three types state apart: `Baseline` (`baseline.rs`), `DigestedUnit` beside it with `covers` added, and `Mounted` (`globals.rs`) with `covered`, `fixed` and `moved`. `Erasures::part_digest` (`curios-erase/src/erased.rs`) builds a `DigestedUnit` for every part it digests, so it walks every name of the unit in scope once a part, where `DigestedUnit`'s documentation says the walk is made once a unit; not measured.
- **Histories stay the board's.** A history is what reaches what a walk files for a later walk to believe, which no single program put to the checkers can; mount, recompile and unmount are what a build does, and a unit written as Core text puts the certifier alone, which is the board's own question. Only `withhold` reaches a state no build does.

## Decided

- **The walk answers one or the other.** An accepted module, with its stated definitions, its record and, for a program, its entry, made by the walk alone; or its verdicts alone. No record of a refused walk exists.
  - Rejected: keeping `Rechecked`'s fields and its `accepted` accessor, which leaves every reader to rule the refused record out by hand.
- **A unit is made of a walk's accepted answer whole.** `Unit::new` and `Unit::read` take the accepted module with its record as one value, so a unit's module and its record are one walk's by construction, and the store's and the image's bytes are the one other door, the one the claim already names. `curios-unit` depends on `curios-cert` already, through `curios-erase`.
  - Rejected: a pair type in the mount's signature, which would move a `curios-cert` type into the unit's stored layout and change its bytes for what the constructor holds without them; the reason this specification first gave, that `curios-unit` stands on no edge to `curios-cert`, does not hold.
- **A believed record answers for every name its unit holds.** The certifier reads each mounted record per name, a totality for every definition and both vectors for every former, under that invariant stated once where it is read: an `expect` naming a miss as a construction bug, as a registry entry mounted twice already is (`scope_tests`' `a_registry_entry_mounted_twice_is_a_construction_bug`). Asking the record of each name stays the certifier's own check of the code that paired the two. A store's damaged bytes are outside the claim with a forger's.
  - Rejected: an absent entry read in the refusing direction, which would hide a construction bug of the compiler's own as a refusal.
  - Rejected: a check of coverage where the store reads a record back, a guard against bytes the claim already leaves outside.
  - Rejected: a record laid out by its unit's places so it covers by construction, for the reason `curios-cert`'s decision states, which still holds: what a walk believes would rest on the code that laid the two out.
- **One reading path.** `covers`, `covered`, `classified`, `unclassified`, `unvectored`, `unvaried`, `unfiled`, `file_scope_polarities`, `file_scope_variances`, `Totals::seeded`'s classification of what is in scope, the baseline filter, and `DigestedUnit::covers` go.
- **One view of a walked unit.** `Baseline` and `DigestedUnit` become one type, `Filed`, a unit as its walk filed it: the module, its places and its record, which `Mounted` holds beside `fixed` and `moved` and `Globals::mount` takes; the per-name reading is its methods, so the invariant is stated in one place.
- **Hand-built environments mount a walk's output.** `already_judged` walks its module with nothing in scope and mounts what the walk accepted, a fixture the walk refuses being a fixture bug; the guard's own tests go. Three tests state the claim's edge by bytes no walk wrote, through the mount's own door and nowhere else: the forged record, restated as the honest walk's record with one verdict altered and every vector kept, and `scope_tests`' two forged marks, the marked module mounted beside the record of the walk over the unmarked one.
- **`withhold` goes, and its two tickets are archived.** A record with part of it gone is bytes no walk wrote, as one with a verdict added or altered is, so no history takes the step. Once a unit is made of its walk's accepted answer whole and the certifier reads a mounted record per name, a unit in scope whose record does not answer for every name it holds is unrepresentable in the compiler, and that unit is what both flaws stood on; the tickets move to `xboard/archive/admission/` with that reason, as the archive takes a ticket whose construction no type of the trusted base can hold.
  - Rejected: restating them on the board with the step taken out, which `xboard/README.md`'s archive decision rejects: the rewrite is another history, which proves nothing of the flaw filed.
  - Rejected: deleting them, which loses the record of the flaws and the count of every flaw found, which the archive exists to keep.
- **An archived ticket is read by its header.** The loader reads an archived record's `title` and `reason` and no other field, its directory still named for its day and its title; its witnesses stay as they were filed and nothing runs them, and `REVIVED` goes. Settled with the user on 2026-11-18.
  - Rejected: holding an archived witness to failing before any checker reads it, which keeps the loader reading every word a history was ever written in, a step no board takes included, only to tell its witness unread.

## Stages

1. **The walk's answer.** `Rechecked` becomes the accepted module or the verdicts, the accepted module carrying its record (`Accepted`); its readers follow, the prelude's build among them, and `Unit::new` and `Unit::read` take the accepted module whole, the prelude's build re-consing the stated module through the accepted value, which keeps its record beside it. `recheck` moves into `curios-pipeline`'s `test_support`. `budget_tests`' `a_variance_walk_the_budget_cuts_short_is_refused_and_files_no_vector` reads no record of the refused walk, which holds none. `curios-cert/README.md`'s decision and `curios-unit/README.md`'s "A unit is certified by construction" are restated. Check: the lint; `curios-cert`'s, `curios-unit`'s, `curios-pipeline`'s, `curios-verdict`'s and `curios-prelude`'s suites; `cargo xboard report` passing; the prelude's image bytes unchanged. One commit.
2. **The archive is read by its header.** An archived ticket is its title, its day and its reason, read off its record's header and directory, beside the tickets of its part; nothing puts it, `Standing::Revived` and `Answer::unread` go, and the report still shows it `ARCHIVED` with its reason and counts the archive apart. `xboard/README.md`'s archive decision is restated. Check: `cargo xtask tools lint` and `tools test`; `cargo xboard report` passing, `elimination`'s archived ticket listed as before. One commit.
3. **One reading path.** The coverage paths go; `Baseline` and `DigestedUnit` are `Filed`, read per name under its invariant, which `Mounted` holds and `Globals::mount` takes; `already_judged` mounts a walk's output; the guard's own tests go (`totality_tests`' `a_baseline_whose_record_does_not_cover_its_module_is_no_baseline`, `a_record_that_does_not_cover_its_unit_is_not_read` and `a_unit_mounted_with_an_empty_record_is_classified_by_the_reading_walk`; `standing_tests`' `a_part_digest_reads_a_classification_where_the_certifiers_own_reads_one`, `Filed` making the two one reading; `scope_tests`' no-record half of `stored_verdicts`); the three forgeries are restated as decided; `curios-prelude`'s `every_root_restores_the_record_its_establishing_walk_filed` and `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::certification` ask per name what they asked of `covers`. Withholding goes with it, since a withheld record would now be read as a construction bug: `Step::Withhold`, the loader's `withhold` and `verdicts`, `History::withhold` and `Unit::with_certification`, with the tests that withhold — `curios-verdict`'s `believed_tests` eight, but `a_group_generic_in_a_sort_filed_partial_is_read_partial_at_prop`, which keeps its half over the kept record; its `test_support::tests` three; and `xboard`'s loader and put tests of the step — and the two tickets are archived as decided. [The soundness board](../../design/soundness/the-soundness-board.md) is restated so a believed record is a walk's output whole and a verdict dropped, added or altered alike is bytes no walk wrote; `xboard/README.md`'s history decision, `curios-verdict/README.md`'s, `xboard/src/load.rs`'s documentation, `.claude/commands/hunt-soundness.md`'s list of histories, `curios-cert/README.md`'s decision and its profile list (`totals::seeded`), `curios-core`'s `Certification` documentation, `Globals`' and `obligation.rs`' follow. Check: the lint, which certifies `/std`; the workspace's tests; `cargo xtask tools lint` and `tools test`; the forged record admitting its looping proof and its honest control refusing it; `cargo xboard report` passing, its admission part two tickets fewer and two archived; the prelude's image bytes unchanged; `cargo xtask docs build`. A figure it may move: a compile's erasure, which no longer walks a unit in scope's names for each part it digests. One commit.
