# A handed witness is followed through its superclass

**[Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md) grades a call in a callback where its callee applies it, so that "a witness handed on inside another's application, `List`'s witness applied to `Option`'s applied to a member, is graded where each applies what it is handed"; a container's witness of a concept with a superclass publishes nothing of its premise, and a family nested through it has no law known to terminate; its next step is stage 3, a ready delivery.** A summary follows a value through four constructs: a parameter handed whole to a callee, a parameter handed on at its own place to a member, a member that escapes, a call of each of its parts claiming nothing past its arguments, and a field lambda, a part of its own. A path rooted at a parameter is followed where the construct reads the parameter, and a definition applied that escapes as a member that escapes is; a witness's superclass still reaches the last by a shape the walk does not follow, a definition applied held at a field where a field lambda stands. Followed, a witness over `use Ord(T)` alone is graded as one of a concept with no superclass is.

## What is wrong today

Each is probed at `8796393a0`.

- **A call through a field that holds an applied definition reaches no part.** `List`'s witness applied to a pair's applied to a family, `obj(List({Str, D}))`, applies the pair's superclass through the slot that holds it, a path no part of the pair's witness lies at, and the family is handed nothing (`handed_within`, `curios-analysis/src/totality/grade.rs`). Over `concept Order(A: Type): pub Type { use Eql(A), ord(A, A) -> Bool, law(a: A, b: A) -> Eq()(ord(a, b), ord(a, b)) }`, with `List`'s witness over `use Order(T)` and a pair's over `use Order(A), use Order(B)`, a witness of `Order(D)` for `induct D: Type | num(Nat) | obj(List({Str, D})) end` is refused, and accepted with `use Eql(…)` written beside each container's premise.
- **The premise is written for it in eleven witnesses of `/std` and two other places.** `Ord`'s witnesses of `List`, `Option`, `Result` and the tuples of one to eight fields each take `Eql` of an element type beside `Ord` of it, with a note saying why: resolution takes a premise that matches before a premise's superclass ([Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md)), so the witness's own superclass is built from a parameter handed whole, which leaves `Ord`'s premise followed. `documentation/language.md` writes it in its derived `Ord(Tree(A))`, and `curios/src/tests/derive/ord.rs` in `Box`'s and `Rose`'s.
- **A program's witness meets it.** Over `induct Box(A: Type): Type | box(A) end` and `induct Forest: Type | none() | some(Box(Forest)) end`, each with its derived `Eql`, `satisfy (@A: Type, use Ord(A)) => Ord(Box(A)) { .. }` is accepted, and `satisfy Ord(Forest) { .. }` after it is refused, `a type in /witness~/std/Ord/Ord(/Forest) reaches the witness of Ord for head Forest, which is not known to terminate`; written `(@A: Type, use Eql(A), use Ord(A))` both are accepted. Nothing tells the witness's author which premise the family three declarations later needed.

## What it simplifies

- **`/std`'s eleven premises** and their notes go, and so do the three outside `/std`: each witness is over `use Ord(T)` alone, its superclass resolved through it.
- **The premise is no rule a program's author has to know.** A witness over `use Ord(A)` alone serves a family nested through it, written or derived, and so does one of any concept whose superclass a container builds from its element's.

## Goal

- **A definition applied to every argument its leading lambdas take, held at a field, is read as a member applied is.** Where it stands at a field of a record literal a member's leading lambdas answer, each of its parts is a part there, under the field's path, over the member's parameters and then the part's own: its call is the definition's part called with those arguments, and what it applies is what that call hands each. The leading lambdas answer it held, owing what its leading lambdas apply as they answer and nothing its parts apply, so a slot's value owes only what its definition applies when it answers.
- **Both checkers number and grade the same parts.** The summary's walk is shared, and so is `group_parts`; the certifier's typed walk records the calls a part held at a field makes where its arguments name a member of the group, as the elaborator's discovery walk does.

## Settled

- **Each rule is a construct's own reasoning at one more shape.** A summary states every application a part makes of a parameter, where every use is seen. A field handed on is seen where its callee's summary sees every use of what it is handed. A record is used by projecting a field of it, and a field lambda by applying it, so every later use of a record an applied definition answered is a call of a part of that definition, at the arguments its leading lambdas were given and others nothing is known of, as every later use of an escaping member's record is a call of the member's parts. And `D(args).s.q(more)` is `g(given).q(more)` where `D`'s record holds `g(given)` at `s`, as `W(args).s.q(more)` is the part at `[s, q]` where `W`'s does: the held parts approximate nothing.
- **The verdict moves one way.** Each rule fires where the walk would otherwise name the parameter as used otherwise, so a position that publishes today publishes the same, and one that published nothing may publish: a consumer's unknown calls of every part become calls of the parts a path reaches. The programs closing at `False` by a recursion that never descends, through a field handed on, a record stored, a record bound and applied beside the slot that holds it, a stored record read by a definition, and a path through the slot, stay refused, each beside the control it is derived from.
- **The peers leave it to the author, or reduce at the use.** Lean's derived `BEq` of a nested inductive is `partial` ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113488-general/topic/Defining.20.60BEq.60.20for.20nested.20inductive.20type.html)), its deriving handler for `DecidableEq` refuses one ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113488-general/topic/DecidableEq.20for.20nested.20inductive.20types.html)), and its derived `LawfulBEq` is refused there ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113489-new-members/topic/Adding.20an.20instance.20of.20LawfulBEq.20to.20my.20nested.20inductive.20type.html)); Idris's totality checker cannot see through `map` and leaves the author `assert_smaller` ([tutorial](https://idris-community.github.io/idris2-tutorial/Tutorial/Folds/Totality.html)); Agda and Rocq accept the recursion written out as a function of its own beside the type ([Agda's list](https://lists.chalmers.se/pipermail/agda/2019/011227.html)), and Rocq's guard checker reads a nested fixpoint by reducing the callee's body where it is met, a rule its maintainers keep relaxing ([TYPES 2025](https://msp.cis.strath.ac.uk/types2025/abstracts/TYPES2025_paper15.pdf)). A summary each definition publishes once is the reading neither needs: the decision's rejection of a method lifted beside its witness stands.

**Rejected.** The premise written wherever a witness is over a concept with a superclass, what `/std` does today: an author learns of it from a refusal at another declaration. An escape in one part confined to that part: a field lambda reads what the leading lambdas bound, so a parameter they store reaches it. A projection out of an applied witness reduced where the walk meets it, `D(args).s` unfolded to what `D`'s record holds: the walk reads the term as elaborated and asks no reduction of its driver for a path, and a definition is read through what it published. A definition at a field read as escaping as well as held: its parts' applications charged to the leading lambdas reach every field lambda through what they answer, and a record recursing through a slot that holds an applied definition, `lift(w).inner.step(s)` over `let lift(@T: Type, o: Outer(T)) -> Outer(List(T)) = Outer { inner = each(o.inner), go(_) = True/qed() }`, would be refused where it descends.

## Open

- **Whether a field read as a value is a use.** A witness whose superclass reads a field of its premise's that is no function, `mark = Mark/mark(@T) + 1` over `concept Mark(A: Type): pub Type { mark: Nat }`, names the premise where nothing applies it, and a concept with that superclass stays refused under these rules. No concept of `/std` has one; filed at retirement unless a stage meets one.

## Stages

**3.
