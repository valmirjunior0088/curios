# A handed witness is followed through its superclass

**[Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md) grades a call in a callback where its callee applies it, so that "a witness handed on inside another's application, `List`'s witness applied to `Option`'s applied to a member, is graded where each applies what it is handed"; a container's witness of a concept with a superclass publishes nothing of its premise, and a family nested through it has no law known to terminate; its next step is stage 1, a ready delivery.** A definition publishes how it applies a parameter it uses only by applying it, applying a field of it, or handing it on unchanged. A witness's superclass is used in three ways outside that list, each read by the list's own reasoning: a field of the parameter handed on, the parameter handed to an applied definition whose record is kept, and a call through a field that holds one. Followed, a witness over `use Ord(T)` alone is graded as one of a concept with no superclass is.

## What is wrong today

Each is probed at `76ec154a6`.

- **A field of a parameter handed on publishes nothing.** Over `struct Inner(A: Type): Type { step: (A) -> True }` and `struct Outer(A: Type): Type { inner: Inner(A) }`, a definition `all(e: Inner(T), s: Seq(T))` that applies `e.step` to each element of `s` publishes that it does, and `let through(@T: Type, o: Outer(T), s: Seq(T)) -> True = all(o.inner, s)` publishes nothing of `o`: an argument that is a projection of a parameter is walked as any term, where a parameter named is a use the walk does not follow (`Walk::walk_argument`, `curios-analysis/src/totality.rs`). So `let w: Outer(Doc) = Outer { inner = Inner { step(d) = match d | leaf() => True/qed() | arr(s) => through(w, s) end } }`, over `induct Doc: Type | leaf() | arr(Seq(Doc)) end`, is refused, `a proof in w reaches w, which is not known to terminate`, where it descends: `through` hands `w`'s field to `all`, which applies it below `s`.
- **A parameter handed to an applied definition whose record is kept publishes nothing.** Over `concept Order(A: Type): pub Type { use Eql(A), ord(A, A) -> Bool, law(a: A, b: A) -> Eq()(ord(a, b), ord(a, b)) }`, a witness `satisfy (@T: Type, use Order(T)) => Order(Seq(T))` fills its superclass with `Seq`'s `Eql` witness applied to the `Eql(T)` inside `Order(T)`. That application is projected nowhere, the leading lambdas of `Seq`'s `Eql` witness state nothing of a premise its field lambdas apply, and the argument is walked as any term: `Order(Seq(T))`'s witness publishes nothing of `Order(T)`, in any of its parts. A witness of `Order(Doc)` over `induct Doc: Type | num(Nat) | arr(Seq(Doc)) end`, whose `law` recurses through it, is refused, `a type in /witness~/Order(/Doc) reaches the witness of Order for head Doc, which is not known to terminate`. With the superclass gone from the concept it is accepted, and with `ord` its one field it is accepted too, nothing erased reaching the witness: so a lawless `Ord` hid it.
- **A call through a field that holds an applied definition reaches no part.** Once the two above are followed, `List`'s witness applied to a pair's applied to a family, `obj(List({Str, D}))`, applies the pair's superclass through the slot that holds it, a path no part of the pair's witness lies at, and the family is handed nothing (`handed_within`, `curios-analysis/src/totality/grade.rs`).
- **`/std` writes a premise for it in eleven witnesses.** `Ord`'s witnesses of `List`, `Option`, `Result` and the tuples of one to eight fields each take `Eql` of an element type beside `Ord` of it, with a note saying why: resolution takes a premise that matches before a premise's superclass ([Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md)), so the witness's own superclass is built from a parameter handed whole, which leaves `Ord`'s premise followed. With them a derived `Ord` of a family nested through each, and through one inside another, is accepted.
- **A program's witness meets it.** Over `induct Box(A: Type): Type | box(A) end` and `induct Forest: Type | none() | some(Box(Forest)) end`, each with its derived `Eql`, `satisfy (@A: Type, use Ord(A)) => Ord(Box(A)) { .. }` is accepted, and `satisfy Ord(Forest) { .. }` after it is refused, `a type in /witness~/std/Ord/Ord(/Forest) reaches the witness of Ord for head Forest, which is not known to terminate`; written `(@A: Type, use Eql(A), use Ord(A))`, as `documentation/language.md` writes its example, both are accepted. Nothing tells the witness's author which premise the family three declarations later needed.

## What it simplifies

- **`/std`'s eleven premises** and their notes go: each witness is over `use Ord(T)` alone again, its superclass resolved through it.
- **The premise is no rule a program's author has to know.** A witness over `use Ord(A)` alone serves a family nested through it, written or derived, and so does one of any concept whose superclass a container builds from its element's.

## Goal

- **A field of a parameter handed on is applied where its callee applies what it is handed.** Each application the callee's summary states of what it is handed is one of the field, and each of a field of it one of the longer path: `relay(o.inner, a)` publishes that `o`'s field `[0, 0]` is applied to `a`.
- **A parameter handed to an applied definition whose record is kept is applied as that record's field lambdas apply it.** A definition applied to every argument its leading lambdas take, projected nowhere the walk sees, answers a record that is used, wherever it goes, by calling a field lambda of it: the argument, a parameter or a field of one, is applied as each part under the leading lambdas states, the field lambda's own arguments claiming nothing. Where a part states nothing of it, it is a use the walk does not follow, as today.
- **A field that holds an applied definition holds that definition's parts.** Where the record a definition's leading lambdas answer holds, at a field, another definition applied to every argument its leading lambdas take, each part of that definition is a part here, under the field's path, over the definition's parameters and then the part's own: its call is the callee's part called with those arguments, and its summary is what that call hands each. So `handed`, `handed_within` and `part_calls` find it where they look a part up, and read nothing new.
- **Both checkers number and grade the same parts.** The summary's walk is shared, and so is `group_parts`; the certifier's typed walk records the calls a part held by reference makes where its arguments name a member of the group, as the elaborator's discovery walk does.

## Settled

- **Each rule is the published list's own reasoning.** A summary states every application a part makes of a parameter, where every use is seen. A field handed on is seen where its callee's summary sees every use of what it is handed, the argument for a parameter handed whole at a longer path. A record is used by projecting a field of it, and a field lambda by applying it, so every later use of a kept record is a call of a part of the definition that answered it, at the arguments its leading lambdas were given and others nothing is known of. And `D(args).s.q(more)` is `g(given).q(more)` where `D`'s record holds `g(given)` at `s`: the third rule approximates nothing.
- **The verdict moves one way.** Each rule fires where the walk would otherwise name the parameter as used otherwise, so a position that publishes today publishes the same, and one that published nothing may publish: a consumer's unknown calls of every part become calls of the parts a path reaches.
- **The three were tried together**, on 2026-11-24 over the tree that became `76ec154a6`, before `/std`'s eleven premises were written, in 165 lines of `curios-analysis/src/totality.rs`, `curios-analysis/src/totality/part.rs` and `curios-core/src/call_matrix.rs`: a derived `Ord` was accepted for a family nested through `List`, `Option`, `Result`, a tuple of one, two and three fields, a list of pairs, of triples and of lists, an option of a list and a pair of a list; six programs closing at `False` by a recursion that never descends, through a field handed on, a record stored, a record bound and applied beside the slot that holds it, a stored record read by a definition, and a path through the slot, stayed refused; and the 324 tests whose names hold `totality`, `driven`, `derive`, `callback` or `handed` passed. With the first two rules alone the list of pairs stayed refused.
- **The peers leave it to the author.** Lean's derived `BEq` of a nested inductive is `partial` ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113488-general/topic/Defining.20.60BEq.60.20for.20nested.20inductive.20type.html)) and its derived `LawfulBEq` is refused there ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/113489-new-members/topic/Adding.20an.20instance.20of.20LawfulBEq.20to.20my.20nested.20inductive.20type.html)); Agda and Rocq accept the recursion written out as a function of its own beside the type ([Agda's list](https://lists.chalmers.se/pipermail/agda/2019/011227.html)). The decision's rejection of a method lifted beside its witness stands.

**Rejected.** The premise written wherever a witness is over a concept with a superclass, what `/std` does today: an author learns of it from a refusal at another declaration. An escape in one part confined to that part: a field lambda reads what the leading lambdas bound, so a parameter they store reaches it. A projection out of an applied witness reduced where the walk meets it, `D(args).s` unfolded to what `D`'s record holds: the walk reads the term as elaborated and asks no reduction of its driver for a path. The kept record's uses charged to nothing, the slot's value passed over where its parts are published: what the applied definition's leading lambdas apply when they answer would be lost.

## Open

- **Whether the leading lambdas' own applications are published apart.** The second rule's applications are recorded where the record is answered, and every field lambda's summary states what the leading lambdas apply beside its own, so a part held by reference inherits them at arguments nothing is known of. A record whose own field is a literal, recursing through a slot that holds an applied definition, is then refused where it descends: `let lift(@T: Type, o: Outer(T)) -> Outer(Seq(T)) = Outer { inner = each(o.inner), go(_) = True/qed() }` and `w`'s `step(d) = match d | leaf() => True/qed() | arr(s) => lift(w).inner.step(s) end`. A leading part that states its own applications where a field lambda applies the parameter too would let a slot's value owe only what its definition applies when it answers. Whether that row is added, or the program filed, is decided where stage 3 lands.
- **Whether a field read as a value is a use.** A witness whose superclass reads a field of its premise's that is no function, `mark = Mark/mark(@T) + 1` over `concept Mark(A: Type): pub Type { mark: Nat }`, names the premise where nothing applies it, and a concept with that superclass stays refused under the three rules. No concept of `/std` has one.

## Stages

**1. A field handed on.** `Walk::walk_argument` records a projection of a parameter as it records the parameter. Check: the record `w` over `through` above is accepted by both checkers, and its twin over `relay(o.inner, a)`, applying the field at the value it was called with, stays refused.

**2. A kept record.** An applied definition projected nowhere hands a parameter, or a field of one, what each part under its leading lambdas applies. Check: `Order(Doc)` above is accepted by both checkers; a record that binds `same(o.inner)` and applies it in the field lambda beside the slot that holds it, looping, stays refused.

**3. A field that holds one.** `record` (`curios-analysis/src/totality/part.rs`) pushes a part for each part of the definition a field holds applied, and the certifier's typed walk records what one calls. Check: a derived `Ord` of `induct D: Type | num(Nat) | obj(List({Str, D})) end` is accepted with `/std`'s premises out; a loop through the slot stays refused; the first open question is answered.

**4. `/std` drops the premise.** The eleven witnesses. Check: `/std` builds and its tests pass; `a_derived_witness_proves_the_orders_laws` (`curios/src/tests/derive/ord.rs`) holds a family nested through a witness over `use Ord(A)` alone.
