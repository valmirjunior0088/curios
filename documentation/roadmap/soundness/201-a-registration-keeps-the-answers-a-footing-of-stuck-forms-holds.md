# A registration keeps the answers a footing of stuck forms holds

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves keeping, past an equation's registration, the answers whose footing of stuck forms still stands, where every registration clears whatever read the scope today. It is not an implementation plan: the footing is a design nobody has drawn, and whether it pays is the census's to say.

## What is missing

[A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) has a boundary keep more only where its census share reaches one percent of `/std`'s elaboration units, and its *Rejected* paragraph keeps no answer past an equation's assumption by what it read, since a footing of locals keeps little and one of the stuck forms met is the probe again. Over `/std`'s elaboration at `0fc2fc1bd`, counted in a scratch copy by the census as the decision states it, a registration's clear drops answers whose identical re-reductions take 760 352 units, 1.22 percent, an upper bound on what any footing keeps; a frame's exit drops 133 185, 0.21 percent, where retiring its clear altogether saves 39 014, so the frame's exit stays as it is. The registration's share stands above the line the decision draws, and the paragraph's premise, that a footing keeps little, is argued there and not measured.

## What is known

- **The elaborator files a footing already, for a settled spelling.** `Footing` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) is what a settled spelling or a settlement's kept window rests on beyond the frames it was read under — the solutions committed, whether a solution since moves it, the universe solver's state, the rewrites, the withdrawals and the redefinitions — and `Context::stands` asks later whether it all still stands. A footing of stuck forms would be filed on a reduct as this one is on a spelling: the stuck heads its reduction met, joined through the hits it took.
- **What each boundary clears is stated.** The reducts that may read the scope go at every boundary of it (`Caches`, the same module), one table for a judgment's reduction and one for plain reduction; a registration is one such boundary. The decision's audit, `curios-pipeline`'s `std_memo_census`, elaborates `/std` with every table cleared at every boundary and again with each answer kept as long as what it read stands, and requires one module.
- **The census is a scratch instrument.** As the decision states it: each clear moves what it drops into a side table tagged with its kind, and a term so dropped and reduced again, at its outermost reduction, credits the units it takes to that kind where its reduct comes back identical and every clear between was of that kind, over `cargo xtask profile-prelude`. Its scripts are not checked in.

## Refinement

- **The footing.** What a footing of the stuck forms an answer met is: the set of stuck heads — a local's, an opaque definition's, a neutral match's — whose reduction a case equation could move, keyed so that a registration asks of each kept answer only whether the equation names one of them; what a hit joins into the footing of the answer it serves; and what the bookkeeping costs at every step and every hit, which the decision names as the reason it keeps none.
- **The measurement.** The footing built in a scratch copy, sound by `std_memo_census` and by `/std`'s image at one path, its units read against the same copy's by the census above. At one percent or more of `/std`'s elaboration units the registration keeps more, and the decision's *Rejected* paragraph is restated to say what it keeps and by what; under it, the paragraph gains the figure and this specification is deleted.
- **What it does not reopen.** A memo outliving its declaration, a ceiling on a settlement and an assumption label per answer, each rejected by the decision for reasons the census does not touch.

## Completion and retirement

The measurement decides. A footing that pays lands with the decision restated, its parts stated in `Footing`'s module and the census figure beside the one-percent line in the decision; one that does not leaves the decision as it stands with the figure in its *Rejected* paragraph. Either way, delete this specification once nothing references it.
