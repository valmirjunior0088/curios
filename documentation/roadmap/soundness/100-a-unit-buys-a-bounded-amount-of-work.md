# A unit buys a bounded amount of work

Working specification for what a judgment's budget bounds. It is held to [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md), where one counter prices transitions, constructions and depth, and to [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md), where a compilation ends, accepted or refused for its budget, in time proportional to what was written plus what the budget counted. It closes the places where a unit buys work without bound, by the rows the budget already has, and gives the tree a measurement of what a unit buys. It prices no walk.

## What this builds on

- **The budget has three rows, charged before the work.** A transition costs one unit, a value a fold builds its logical size, and a level of guarded recursion the frame it takes, charged for each new peak (`curios-core`'s `cost`, `Reducer::spend`, each checker's `Spend::enter_level`). The folds beside a sum charge what they build: a product the collection and the nodes it distributes to (`Nat::normalize`, `curios-core/src/nat.rs`), a magnitude the summands it collects (`reduce/intrinsic/magnitude.rs`), a concatenation its operands (`reduce/intrinsic/free_monoid.rs`).
- **A walk costs its graph, and a count holds that.** A read visits a node once and a rebuild rebuilds one once a depth, which `curios`'s `tests::towers` holds by fixtures that compile at sixty lines and `tower_measurements` counts in units and node looks. A look is a term handing out its node (`take_looks`, `curios-core/src/term.rs`), sampled for each judgment as `term::looks` beside `budget::consumed` where a checker restores its budget, under the `profile` feature.
- **The gate builds what ships.** Lint and tests run with default features; what reads the instrumentation is gated on `profile`, linted by `cargo xtask instrumented lint` and taken by hand, by the command written beside it (`curios/src/tests/instrumented.rs`, `towers/measurement_tests.rs`). So a count of looks is no assertion of the gate's.
- **A comparison is losing its loop and its keys.** [A goal met again is decided alike by both checkers](102-a-goal-met-again-is-decided-alike-by-both-checkers.md) unfolds a pair of calls against itself once and assumes no goal, so both histories go with their keys. What a comparison then repeats is bounded by the terms it compares.
- **Peers.** A deterministic limit is priced one of two ways. By a meter the runtime keeps: Lean counts small allocations and adds one heartbeat for each pair of nodes its structural equality enters, "because some code … may spend a lot of time comparing" ([`expr_eq_fn.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/expr_eq_fn.cpp), [heartbeats](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/4.34.0/IO/Timing/)); Wasmtime offers fuel where a trap must be deterministic and epochs where it must be cheap ([`Config`](https://docs.wasmtime.dev/api/wasmtime/struct.Config.html)). Or by a price for each operation in the size of what it takes: Ethereum repriced the operations an attack showed underpriced ([EIP-150](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-150)), and Tezos fits its gas to benchmarks and revises it between protocols ([Delphi](https://research-development.nomadic-labs.com/smarter-contracts-thanks-to-delphi-part-12.html)). A walk met with fuel is called implementation-dependent where it is offered: Agda's `--syntactic-equality=N` ([command-line options](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tools/command-line-options.html)). rustc replaced the hard limit on constant evaluation by a lint ([#103877](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/103877)). Where unfolding is what grows, it is bounded and not priced: smalltt "only performs limited speculation" ([smalltt](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/smalltt)). Equality of two shared terms is linear in their graphs ([Sharing Equality is Linear](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.06101)).

## The gap

Every program is over `use /std/{Nat, Eq, Io};` and ends in `Io/pure(())`. Every figure is counted at `ca95d7595`, by the protocols under *Verification*; a memory figure is one run's peak resident set.

**A sum's normal form is built with no charge.** The fold of `Nat/add` (`reduce_intrinsic`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic.rs`) reduces its two operands and hands them to `Nat::sum`, which reads every summand of both and builds the sum again, a node a summand (`Nat::from_linear`), and spends nothing; `Nat/sub` and the comparisons build their residuals the same way through `Nat::cancel_common`, and `Int`'s folds through `int_sum` and `int_cancel_common`. A program drives it. Over `let f(c: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => f(c + k, k) end;`, `let t(x: Nat, y: Nat) -> Eq()(f(x, y), f(2, y)) = Eq/refl();` spends its budget at about twenty units a problem while each round reduces a sum one summand longer, and looks at 2,428,676, 24,889,890 and 220,791,260 nodes at budgets 12,500, 25,000 and 50,000, nine tenths of the last in reducing a round's two sides. Over `/std` no sum holds more than seven summands, and 161,201 are built in its elaboration and 45,934 in its certification.

**The kernel's comparison takes depth it does not pay for.** `compare` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) spends a transition where it is entered and enters no level, so the frames it nests are priced nothing, where `whnf` pays for each new peak. On `let f(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => f(k) + 2 end;` against `let h(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => 2 + h(k) end;`, `let same(n: Nat) -> Eq()(f(n), h(n)) = Eq/refl();` nests seventeen native frames a round, 9,904 bytes, for 88 units. Certifying `/std` nests a comparison at most ten deep. What drives it today is the round the other specification ends; whether anything still nests with its budget once a pair is unfolded once is not known.

**A comparison keeps a key for every goal, until the other specification removes it.** The kernel's history copies the scope's types into each goal's key, twice (`History::enter` clones the goal it inserts): on the program above its peak memory is 82 MB, 200 MB, 646 MB and 2.5 GB at budgets 25,000, 50,000, 100,000 and 200,000, and at 50,000 it enters 2,270 goals and copies 1,930,070 binder types. The elaborator's key renames a whole problem wherever it names a binder the comparison opened (`Convert::history_key`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`): the first program's memory is 76 MB, 222 MB and 862 MB at its three budgets, the keys' renamed copies. Both go with the histories, and nothing here prices them.

**Nothing measures what a unit buys.** Each of the three was met as a stall or a memory limit, not by a check. A unit's looks run from under one to twenty-two across `/std`'s declarations (`/std/Tui/Session/read`, `/std/Nat/Coprime/quotients`), and across the programs here from two, for a plain reduction that never ends, to four thousand, for the first program at budget 50,000. No fixture states a program that grows and the budget it answers inside, and no measurement reads units beside looks for one.

## Decisions

**What a walk reads is not priced.** Three things stand against a price on reading, by the node or by the walk.

- No program grows the walks a comparison makes of its own. Over nine programs built to grow, the equality at conversion's entry, `level_differences` and `mentions_rec_member` take a constant number of looks a goal at every budget, sixty-nine at most; over `/std` they and the elaborator's key are 6.2% of what elaboration spends and 4.5% of what certification does.
- A look is not the same count twice. Between two runs of one binary 193 of the 15,255 judgments of `/std`'s elaboration differ in their looks, and 252 of the 3,809 of its certification, with every judgment's units the same ([the finding](../compilation/000-findings.md)).
- What a walk enters follows how its graphs share their nodes: `Term`'s equality ends at a pair that is one allocation. Nothing holds a term's sharing the same between one elaborated in this process and one restored from a stored unit, and the budget is a fact about the program.

A walk costs its graph, so it is bounded by the nodes the budget charged when they were built; what broke that was a loop repeating a walk, and the loop is what is closed, here and in the other specification.

**A fold charges what it builds, the sum included.** A sum of `n` symbolic summands is charged the collection it reads and a node a summand before it is built, at every fold that holds a reducer: addition, subtraction and the comparisons where a summand cancels and a residual is built, on `Nat` and on `Int`. Two literals stay the scalar row's. A sum built where no reducer is in hand (`words.rs`, `successor_comparison`) spells a term its reader already holds the summands of.

**The kernel's comparison pays for its depth where a program can drive it.** It is measured once a pair is unfolded once: a comparison that nests with its budget enters a level as reduction does, and one that nests no deeper than the terms it compares is left as it is, with the count.

**The objective is held by programs that answer, and measured by hand.** A fixture of the gate states a program that grows and the budget it answers inside, with its verdict; one that a rule decides is refused as apart and not for its budget, so a loop put back fails the fixture. Beside them one measurement reads units and looks for each checker at a budget and at twice it, taken where a price or a rule of conversion changes.

## Verification

- **The programs.** Beside the three under *The gap*: `let f(c: Nat, a: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => f(c, a + k, k) end;` with `Eq()(f(x, 0, y), f(2, 0, y))`; `let g(h: (Nat) -> Nat, s: (Nat) -> Nat, c: Nat, d: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n == 0 | true => 0 | false => g(h, s, c + h(d), s(d), n / 2) end;` with `Eq()(g(h, s, x, d, y), g(h, s, 2, d, y))`; `let g(c: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n == 0 | true => 0 | false => g(c, n / 2) end;` with `Eq()(g(x, y), g(2, y))`; `let acc(h: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat, c: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => c | k + 1 => acc(h, k, c + h(k)) end;` with `Eq()(acc(h, 62, x), x)`; `let nest(n: Nat, T: Type) -> Type = match n | 0 => T | k + 1 => Option(nest(k, T)) end;` with `Eq()(nest(500, Nat), nest(500, Int))`; and `let spin(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => spin(k) end;` with `Eq()(spin(100000000), 0)`. Each claim is a declaration closed by `Eq/refl()`.
- **A declaration's units and looks.** `cargo build --release --package curios --features profile`, then `curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics --budget <N> -` with the program on standard input. The stream's `D` rows name its callsites and its `V` rows hold a judgment's `budget::consumed` and `term::looks`, filed where the next judgment restores the budget; the kernel files none for a declaration it refuses unless a later declaration reads it. A program that grows is run under a memory limit and a time limit, one budget a command, the smallest first.
- **Looks by where they are taken.** In a copy of the tree under `target/scratch/` (`git archive HEAD`): a reading of the look counter that does not reset it beside `take_looks`, and a tally for each place — the two equalities of `Convert::drain`, `identify_universe_levels` and `level_question`, `history_key`, `mentions_rec_member` in each `force`, the kernel's equality in `compare`, `rec_instances`, `History::enter` with the scope's length, and the three reductions `Convert::drain` makes of a problem — each filed as a sample where the checker restores its budget. `/std` is read with `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` and `certification <PATH>` in the copy, two runs compared row by row for what repeats.
- **The frames a round nests.** The release compiler under `gdb`, interrupted while the kernel compares: the stack pointer of two frames of `compare_telescope` a round apart, and the count of frames between them.
- **Peak memory** is the resident set `/usr/bin/time` reports for one run.
- `cargo xtask workspace lint`, which certifies `/std`, at every stage, and `cargo xtask gate` where the first three stages are in and again at completion.

## Rejected

- **A price on every look**, a meter the terms keep, as Lean keeps its heartbeats. It follows how a term is shared and does not repeat, and at a unit a look it moves `/std`'s heaviest elaboration from 1,034,898 units to 3,891,459 where its heaviest certification moves to 1,038,517: two price lists.
- **A price on the walks a comparison makes**, by the nodes each enters. No program grows them, and the count is the same meter read in fewer places.
- **A price on a history's key.** Charged as built, the two programs that grow by their keys are refused at the default budget inside seconds, the kernel's at 916 MB and the elaborator's at 251 MB; the keys go instead, and a price on what is being removed is one more thing to remove.
- **An assertion of the gate on looks.** The gate builds no instrumentation, and a look is not the same count twice.
- **Another default budget.** What is uneven is what a unit buys, two looks in a plain reduction and four thousand in the first program, not how many units a declaration has.
