# A goal met again is decided alike by both checkers

Working specification for what a comparison does where it recurs. It is held to [Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../../design/soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md), where a verdict follows neither the checker that asked nor the path it took to the question, and to [An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md), where every way the certifier accepts is a rule that is sound alone. It replaces the rule of [Conversion recurrence](../../design/soundness/conversion-recurrence.md), a goal met again assumed, by three that assume none: two recursive groups are compared as the binders they are, a pair of calls is unfolded against itself once, and no goal is assumed.

## What this builds on

- **`rec` is the only recursion form, and a group is a binder.** A `Rec` (`curios-core/src/term/shape.rs`) holds its members under one scope, each member's type and body naming every member by index, and a member's occurrence is the same node with a tail that selects it (`Term::rec_proj`). Two groups written under different names are one term, and both checkers hold two projections of one group at two universe instances to their levels before either is unfolded (`rec_instances`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`; `level_question`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`).
- **A fold's hypothesis is a binder two arms share.** Two stuck folds over one scrutinee are compared arm by arm, the predecessor and the hypothesis opened at one set of binders, so `match n | 0 => 0 | _ + 1; ih => ih + 2 end` against the same fold ending in `2 + ih` is one function to both checkers: the sum's two atoms are one `ih`.
- **The elaborator compares two bare groups as binders.** `compare_rec` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) opens every member of both at one shared label each, at the type the group states for it, and poses the types, the bodies and the tails. It is reached where two bare projections of one index meet whose skeletons part; two calls of them take the delta step, and the kernel has no such comparison.
- **Both checkers unfold two stuck calls against each other**, and programs rest on it. Two calls of one member whose spines part, and two calls of two members, are each opened one definitional step and compared again: the elaborator's delta step (`compare_same_rec_apply` and the `Apply` arm of `Convert::drain`) and the kernel's `unfolded_retry`. Counted at `ca95d7595`: certifying `/std` unfolds one member against itself 173 times, 122 ending in agreement, and two members never; the `big_nat` unit of `curios-std/src/tests/` unfolds two members twelve times, each ending in agreement. With two members never unfolded against each other `/std` does not elaborate (`/std/Hash/nat/from_count`, `/std/List/sort_by/drive_permutes`, `/std/Map/Key/bits_monotone`).
- **An unfolding inside an unfolding of the same pair decides nothing measured.** Counted at `ca95d7595`: certifying `/std` unfolds a pair of heads inside that pair's own unfolding twice, both ending apart; elaborating it does so 853 times in 3,954 unfoldings of one member, and with every one of them withheld the image is the same bytes; the nine units of `curios-std/src/tests/` hold none.
- **One pair is already refused at the door.** Two instances of one group at unequal levels reproduce themselves under every unfolding, and `rec_instances` answers them before any retry is granted. A classing asked for inside a classing is held apart the same way, inside the two atoms being compared (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/classed.rs`), and the kernel files no verdict a goal in progress stood behind (`History::assumed`).
- **No accepted program holds an equirecursive type.** `let T: Type = {Nat, T};` is refused as a recursive definition in a type position that does not terminate ([Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)), so what a comparison of two total terms can meet again is a pair of calls, never a type that is its own part.
- **Peers.** A goal assumed because it is in progress is sound only under a guard. Brandt and Henglein's fixpoint rule for recursive types asks that the proof of its premise be contractive ([Coinductive axiomatization of recursive type equality and subtyping](https://hjemmesider.diku.dk/~henglein/bib/publications/brhe98.html)). rustc's trait solver takes a cycle for coinductive only for the goal kinds it names, answers any other with overflow, and moves no result of a cycle's participant to its global cache ([coinduction](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/solve/coinduction.html), [caching](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/solve/caching.html)). A goal met again up to a substitution is a cyclic proof, sound under a global trace condition that CycleQ checks by size-change graphs, and its example 3.2 proves `Cons x xs = Nil` by a cycle with no progress ([CycleQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.12553)). Where recursion is one form in a dependently typed core, equality assumes nothing: ΠΣ replaces two definitions it is forced to identify by one fresh variable and compares what they were defined as, "this way we do not unfold recursive definitions more than once" ([ΠΣ: Dependent Types without the Sugar](https://kosmikus.org/PiSigma/PiSigma.pdf), §4), and Rocq's kernel compares two `fix` by their types, their bodies under the shared binders and then their arguments ([`kernel/conversion.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/conversion.ml)), unfolding one only where its recursive argument starts with a constructor ([Fixpoint definitions](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/V9.0.0/refman/language/core/inductive.html)).

## The gap

Every program is over `use /std/{Nat, Eq};` and is probed at `ca95d7595`.

**The two recurrence keys decide two relations, and the kernel does not answer where the elaborator accepts.** With `let f(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => f(k) + 2 end;` and `let h(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => 2 + h(k) end;`, `let same(n: Nat) -> Eq()(f(n), h(n)) = Eq/refl();` elaborates, and so does `let same: Eq()(f, h) = Eq/refl();`. The kernel spends its budget on each. Its walk enters four goals a round — the stuck fold, the sum, and the two calls twice — under a scope three binders longer than the round before, and its key copies the scope whole, so no round meets a goal again; the elaborator's key renames the binders its conversion opened and holds no context, so the second round's `f(k)` against `h(k)` is the first's and is assumed. The goal that would have to recur is closed, the two members themselves, and the same two functions written as folds are accepted by both.

**The elaborator's key holds no binder's type, so a verdict follows the order of a tuple's components.** A lookup under a binder the comparison opened reads the type its position gave it (`Binders`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`), and `Convert::history_key` renames such binders by the order they were minted in and keeps no type: `h(x)` against `h(y)` holds unread where `h`'s domain is a proposition and is two terms where it is `Nat`, and the two goals are one key. Under `use /std/Bool/{Holds};`, `let same: Eq()(((h: (Holds(true)) -> Nat, x: Holds(true), y: Holds(true)) => h(x), (h: (Nat) -> Nat, x: Nat, y: Nat) => h(x)), ((h: (Holds(true)) -> Nat, x: Holds(true), y: Holds(true)) => h(y), (h: (Nat) -> Nat, x: Nat, y: Nat) => h(y))) = Eq/refl();` elaborates and `run` stops on `the kernel refused /same`; with the two components of each tuple exchanged the elaborator refuses it as a type mismatch. The sentence that fails is [Conversion recurrence](../../design/soundness/conversion-recurrence.md)'s "It cannot move a verdict, because an opening is typeless everywhere in the elaborator", and with it the doc comment of `solve_tests`' `a_goal_assumed_by_key_collision_cannot_move_the_verdict`, whose fixture keeps its head untyped and so passes; `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`'s module documentation calls the same question open.

**The elaborator's key is an induction no rule states.** With `let f(n: Nat, x: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => x | k + 1 => f(k, x) + 1 end;` and `let h(x: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => x | k + 1 => 1 + h(x, k) end;`, `let same(n: Nat, x: Nat) -> Eq()(f(n, x), h(x, n)) = Eq/refl();` elaborates: the two are one function up to the order of their arguments, which no congruence says and an induction on `n` proves. So does the first program with `h` declared in a group beside a member it never calls, `and spare(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => spare(k) end;`. The kernel spends its budget on both.

**The kernel's rule fires on no program.** Counted at `ca95d7595`: certifying `/std` and its nine test units meets a goal again no time, where elaborating `/std` does 53 times, 44 on a problem spelled as one before it and 9 under renaming, of which 7 are level questions and 2 are assumed; with the elaborator's rule switched off the image of `/std` is the same bytes. What the kernel's rule closes is its own fixture (`a_recurring_goal_is_assumed_rather_than_unfolded_forever`), a type totality refuses in a program, and its cost is every goal's: a key holding the scope's types, and each binder's type renamed where it opens (`Local::keyed`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/scope.rs`).

Nothing is admitted by any of it. A reader meets an equation that elaborates and a kernel that spends its budget, and an equation's fate where two recursive functions meet follows the spelling of a recursion and the order two goals were met in.

## Decisions

**Two recursive groups are compared as the binders they are.** Two projections of one index of two groups are one where the groups have one shape and their members convert, each member's type and then its body at that type, every member of both opened at one shared binder. Both checkers take it before either projection is unfolded, bare or at the head of two calls, and two calls it holds are then compared by their spines at the member's telescope. It is the congruence of a binder, as two lambdas whose bodies convert under one binder are one function: no member is unfolded while two groups are compared, so nothing recurs, and the rule is sound alone. Groups are matched by position, which is what makes one index of two groups a pair.

**A pair of calls is unfolded against itself once.** What their heads and spines do not decide, two folded calls decide by one unfolding of both, as today. A pair of heads whose unfolding is being compared is, met again inside that comparison, apart. It only withholds an unfolding, so it accepts nothing; what it gives up is an equation that needs its pair unfolded twice on one path, which is an induction and is a lemma's to state. Where an unsolved metavariable is what keeps the pair's heads from being one member, the pair met again is not apart: it waits, as any problem a metavariable keeps open does, and is decided once the metavariable is.

**Apart there is a verdict of the path, and is not remembered.** A pair left apart may be one where it is met outside its own unfolding, as two atoms a nested classing leaves apart may be. So the kernel files no verdict reached across one, by the count that keeps a verdict behind a goal in progress out of its memo today, and the elaborator, which remembers no comparison's verdict, has nothing to keep.

**The path is the comparisons a pair is inside.** The kernel's walk is nested, so its path is its own recursion: a pair is recorded where both sides unfold and released when their comparison returns, and a classing compares under the pairs of the goal it belongs to. The elaborator's walk is a worklist that parks, so the pairs are carried with the problem, as the binders it is posed under are, and every comparison a run opens for a part of the problem in hand — a spine, a head, a looked-up type, a classing — is posed on that problem's pairs. A question reduction asks with no comparison in hand starts with none, in both.

**No goal is assumed.** With the two rules above nothing regresses that an assumption cut, and what an assumption accepted beyond them is an induction no guard checked. Both histories go: the kernel's key and what it costs every goal and every binder, the elaborator's key with its renaming, and the level question the elaborator asked at a recurrence, which it asks at the applied head already.

**One order in both.** One member, up to its levels, is the level question and then the spines. One index of two groups is the groups as binders and then the spines. What neither holds is one unfolding of both, and inside it the same pair is apart.

## Stages

Each lands alone, on its own check.

1. **Two recursive groups are compared as the binders they are.** The kernel gains the comparison, opening a group as `check_group` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/module.rs`) opens one, and takes it in `compare` for two bare projections before either is forced; the elaborator takes `compare_rec` at the head of two calls, in a run of its own, ahead of the delta step. Tried at `ca95d7595`, at the head of two calls in both checkers: the first program and its unapplied form are accepted by both at the default budget, the same two functions with another base case and with another step are refused by the elaborator, and `/std` certifies to the same certification image with the comparison tried seven times and holding none. Check: one test of each in both checkers under one name, mutation-checked against a comparison that holds any two groups of one shape; the two held rows and the base-case near miss in the law audit, the near miss in a step waiting for the next stage, where the kernel answers it with no regress through a classing; the first program run. May move: the bytes of `std.rkyv`, the comparison minting labels, and a budget floor a test holds.
2. **A pair of calls is unfolded against itself once.** The kernel records a pair in `unfolded_retry`, and in `compare` where two bare projections the first stage did not hold are forced; the elaborator carries the pairs with `Problem` and extends them at its three unfoldings of both sides, two bare groups of one index that are not one as binders going on to their member bodies as the kernel's do, and parks a pair met again whose level question an unsolved metavariable keeps open. Tried at `ca95d7595` with the pair recorded at the retry and at the delta steps: the argument-order program, the one with a member beside `h`, and `Eq()(g(x, y), g(2, y))` over `let g(c: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n == 0 | true => 0 | false => g(c, n / 2) end;` are refused at the default budget, `/std` elaborates and certifies to the same bytes, and the nine units pass. Not tried: a pair recorded where two bare members are forced, and a pair parked. Check: a test in both checkers under one name, mutation-checked against the rule removed under a small budget; the step near miss and the three programs as refused rows of the law audit; `std.rkyv` and `certification.rkyv` the same bytes as the stage before. May move: the budget `/std` consumes.
3. **No goal is assumed in the elaborator.** Its history goes, with the key, the renaming and the labels minted to rename into, and with the level question it asked at a recurrence; two bare instances an unsolved metavariable keeps apart are parked where they are posed. It is not the first stage, though it is what the gap's second paragraph asks: two instances of one group kept apart by unsolved metavariables unfold each other without end, and until the second stage the renamed key is what parks them (`a_recurrence_blocked_on_a_metavariable_parks_and_commits_once_it_is_solved`, `curios-elab/src/convert/conversion_tests.rs`). Tried at `ca95d7595`: with the rule off the image of `/std` is the same bytes, and with the key as written the tuple program is refused in both orders. Check: the tuple equation in both orders as refused rows of the law audit, each checker asked alone, in place of the two fixtures; the elaborator's tests of a level question at a recurrence restated at the applied head. May move: the bytes of `std.rkyv`, by the labels no longer minted.
4. **No goal is assumed in the kernel.** `Goal`, `History::enter` and what they key go with `Local::keyed`, and a classing no longer asks whether its pair is in progress. Tried at `ca95d7595`: certifying `/std` and its nine units meets no goal again, and with the rule off beside the stages above `/std` certifies and the units pass. Check: the kernel's two equirecursive fixtures restated as two groups of two shapes being apart; `kernel_memo_parity`; both images the same bytes as the stage before.
5. **Completion**, below.

Three cases [A comparison costs what it walks](100-a-comparison-costs-what-it-walks.md) describes are settled by these stages: the first decides two copies of one local member that differ, which `curios`'s `tests::inference::implicit_tests::a_local_recursive_function_elaborated_again_is_itself` holds and, with the mutation its documentation names, runs out of steps at budget 2,000,000 today and is accepted by both checkers under the rules; the second refuses its unfolding that never ends; the fourth removes the kernel's key.

## Rejected

- **One key in both checkers.** With the kernel's, the rule fires on no program and the first program waits on the budget in both: keyed as written and given no other rule, the elaborator spends its default budget on it. With the elaborator's, the kernel holds an induction: it needs every binder's type in the key and a trace condition over every cycle, and what conversion decides then follows which earlier goal a search met.
- **Never unfolding a stuck call**, as Rocq does not. `/std` rests on the single unfolding, as *What this builds on* counts.
- **Pairing members as they are met**, ΠΣ's partial bijection, so that two groups of two shapes can be one. No measured program asks for it, and position is what `compare_rec` and Rocq's kernel compare by.
- **No answer for a pair met again**, as a spent budget is none. A refusal lets a near miss be a row of the law audit, which holds no row a checker does not answer, and a nested classing is left apart already.
- **The budget alone.** It ends a regress where a verdict is at hand, in the time the budget takes.

## Non-goals

What conversion's walks are charged is [A comparison costs what it walks](100-a-comparison-costs-what-it-walks.md)'s. A rebuilt match still keeps the goal it holds (`kept_goal`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs`) once two copies of a member are compared as binders: with it off at the first stage the kernel refuses `/std/Flt/of_decimal_in`.
