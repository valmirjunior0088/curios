# A comparison costs what it walks

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves a price for what a comparison reads and keeps, so that the budget ends a comparison that grows in the time it ends any other. It is not an implementation plan.

It is held to [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md): one counter decides whether a declaration type-checks, and it prices construction as well as transitions.

## What is missing

**Conversion's own walks are not charged, so a comparison that grows is bounded in rounds and not in time or in memory.** A round of the elaborator's worklist spends a step where it dequeues a problem and what its reductions build, and nothing for comparing the two sides structurally, aligning their levels, asking whether a reduct still mentions its group or renaming a problem's opened binders for the history (`Term`'s equality, `level_differences`, `mentions_rec_member` and `history_key`, reached from `Convert::drain`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), each a walk of the term. Where every round's terms are one level larger than the last — the delta step taken on both sides of two copies of one recursive member that differ, which `curios`'s `tests::inference::implicit_tests::a_local_recursive_function_elaborated_again_is_itself` reaches with a rebuilt match taking the type it is checked at — the budget counts the rounds while the time each takes grows with the round: a declaration is refused only where its budget runs out, which at the default takes longer than ten minutes. The kernel's conversion is unpriced the same way, and its history with it: `History::enter` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) files a key for every goal it enters — the local context, the type and both terms — and spends nothing for it, so a comparison that opens a binder a round keeps a key a round larger than the last. On the program of [A goal met again is decided alike by both checkers](102-a-goal-met-again-is-decided-alike-by-both-checkers.md), at `aae5446d7`, the kernel refuses by its budget at 200,000 units and at the default exhausts a 6 GB memory cap first, one reading each. The curve is not measured. [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) prices what a step allocates and the depth it takes.

**Uncertain: a comparison that unfolds a recursive function without end takes far longer than its budget grows.** Over `let g(c: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n == 0 | true => 0 | false => g(c, n / 2) end;`, `let t(x: Nat, y: Nat) -> Eq()(g(x, y), g(2, y)) = Eq/refl();` is refused as `conversion ran out of steps` within a second at `--budget 100000` and gets no report within forty seconds at `--budget 1000000`, a thirtieth of the default: the two calls unfold to two stuck `match`es whose false arms are the same two calls one division on, no arm differs, and the elaborator's queue (`Convert::drain`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) takes one round per unfolding over sides that grow by a node each. Timed at `e19ebf500`, one reading each. Not known: what a round does in proportion to its sides that the budget does not count — the recurrence key (`history_key`) renames a whole problem, and each round's is new — nor whether the kernel's comparison shows it.

## Refinement

- **What a round is charged.** Charge each of those walks by the nodes it visits, or hold them to the nodes two sides do not share, and charge a history's key as what it builds. Check: both regresses refused within seconds at the default budget, and the budget `/std` consumes read before and after. What the walks cost over `/std` is counted before a price is chosen.
- **The unfolding that never ends.** Price what a round reads, so the default budget ends such a comparison in the time it ends any other. Check: the program refused as out of steps at the default budget within seconds.
