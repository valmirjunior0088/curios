# Soundness: findings

- **Strict positivity's and variance's readings of a type through what it computes have met no hunt.** A stuck `match` read through its arms, a datum read past the declaration's type parameters, a recursive member read by the least fixpoint of its unfolding, and two projections of one group compared at the levels that fixpoint holds each admit declarations the walks refused, on arguments [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) and [A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md) make, and no attack has been put to them. Fix: a `/hunt-soundness` round whose charters cross the readings with `Prop`, definitional K, a sort left to a caller, large elimination, a group closed over a walk's binders, Core text's definitions named before their families, and two instances of a group at two levels; an attack that holds is a ticket on the board, its fix landing next. Check: `cargo xboard report` showing no open ticket reached through the readings. Size: one hunt round.
- **The certifier admits a unit in scope whose record does not cover it, a state no build reaches, so its handling of one is exercised only by the board's withholding and by hand.** `Globals::mount` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/globals.rs`) takes any record beside a unit's definitions, classifies the items of one that does not cover them for itself (`Globals::unclassified`), and says why it takes one at all: "an environment built by hand, with no walk behind it, passes an empty record, which covers nothing a unit declares". No build mounts such a unit: a build files a unit only once it compiled (`curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`, at both `cache::put`s), with the record the certifier's walk over it filed, the prelude's units carry theirs from the same walk (`curios-prelude/compile.rs`), and `globals_after` mounts each with its own. A history reaches the state by withholding (`History::withhold`, `curios-verdict/src/test_support.rs`), which refiles a mounted unit's record with some or all of its verdicts dropped, and two fixed tickets of `admission` were found only so: a part read of a unit no record covers digesting as total, and a family with no vector on record digesting alike whatever its vector. So the certifier carries, and the board holds it to, a contract for an input only tests make, where a state no build reaches could be one no caller can state. Fix, if the investigation bears it out: a unit mounted from a store carries a record that covers it, stated in the type `Globals::mount` takes, and an environment built by hand is mounted by a constructor of its own that classifies every item from its terms, so a walk never meets a partial record and the paths that read one go; `withhold` then reaches nothing a check accepts, and the two tickets' witnesses are restated over what can still be stated, or, where nothing can, archived, as a witness whose construct left the language is, with that reason. Not yet known: which hand-built environments, the certifier's and the board's tests among them, rely on mounting a record that covers part of a unit rather than none; whether a record the store reads back, `with_apart` among its readers, can ever cover less than the unit it was filed with, a damaged or older store's included; and what the board's design then says of withholding, which it keeps as the one power over a believed record that stays inside the claim. Check: no product path constructs a partial record for a mounted unit; the two tickets' flaws unrepresentable or still refused; `cargo xboard report` passing. Size: unknown.
