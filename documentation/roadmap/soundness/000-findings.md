- **A witness goal over a constructor a closed call computes is refused outside an arm and resolved inside one.** `HeadKey::of_whnf` (`curios-core/src/witness.rs`) keys a constructor given as a function by the head its body spells, reducing nothing beneath the binder, and the two reductions spell one closed function apart: the closed machine evaluates what it substitutes (`curios-core/src/machine.rs`), where the recursive strategy, which runs wherever a refinement is registered, substitutes it as written. Under `let partial(F: (Type, Type) -> Type, S: Type) -> (Type) -> Type = (A) => F(S, A);` and a witness of `Tag((A: Type) => Box(S, A))`, the call `Tag/tag(@partial(Box, Nat))` is refused as a declaration's body, `no witness of Tag(partial(Box, Nat)) found`, and resolved in an arm of a `match` over a parameter of the same declaration; over an open term, `partial(Box, S)` with `S` a parameter, it is resolved outside an arm as well. Conversion holds the goal one with the witness's own spelling: `Eq()(partial(Box, Nat), (A: Type) => Box(Nat, A))` is proved by `Eq/refl()`. The library's witnesses answer alike, `Monad/pure(@partial(State, Nat), 3)` being refused. Probed at `7d28c0209`. Nothing is admitted by it: a witness whose signature computes its head is refused where it is declared, the goal the machine spells is refused and resolved by no other witness, and one declaration's two goals are answered by where each stands and not by which is met first, so the elaborator's one table of reducts moves neither. The same reading refuses a goal written through a definition beneath the binder: under `let f(S: Type, A: Type) -> Type = Box(S, A);`, `Tag/tag(@((A: Type) => f(Nat, A)))` reports `no witness of Tag(Box(Nat)) found`, naming the key the lookup did not compute. Check: the three probes resolve, outside an arm and inside one, and a witness declared through a head its signature computes is still refused. Size: small; it changes which goals resolve.
- **The kernel checks a tuple literal against a structure's type, and no projection reads it there.** `check` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/infer.rs`) takes a tuple literal at a `StructType` field by field, as it does at a Σ, where elaboration refuses the literal (`Error::not_a_tuple_type`), so only a term built by hand holds one: `a_structure_occurrence_at_its_declared_parameter_count_still_works` checks `(7)` at `Boxed(Nat)`. A projection out of a structure names it and reads a literal of that structure alone (`curios_core::project_literal`), so a binder stated at `Boxed(Nat)` and bound to `(7)` types `.0` at `Nat` and reduces to no literal. Nothing is admitted by it that should not be: a closed term of `Nat` is left without a value. Either the rule goes, a structure's literal being the one form its type has, or the slot reads the tuple.
- **The kernel refuses an equality between two definitions elaboration holds to be one, and nothing says so until the kernel does.** Under `use /std/{Eq, Handle, Io};` and `use /std/Handle/{close};`, the declarations `let one(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);`, `let two(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);` and `let same: Eq()(one, two) = Eq/refl();` elaborate, and the compilation ends at ``the kernel refused /same: expected `Eq(…)(one, two)`, found `Eq(…)(one, one)` ``; the same three over `(n: Nat) -> Nat = n + 1` are accepted by both checkers, which leaves the effect as what sets the two apart.
