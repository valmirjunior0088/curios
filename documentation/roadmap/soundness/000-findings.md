# Soundness: findings

- **A witness goal over a constructor a closed call computes is refused outside an arm and resolved inside one.** `HeadKey::of_whnf` (`curios-core/src/witness.rs`) keys a constructor given as a function by the head its body spells, reducing nothing beneath the binder, and the two reductions spell one closed function apart: the closed machine evaluates what it substitutes (`curios-core/src/machine.rs`), where the recursive strategy, which runs wherever a refinement is registered, substitutes it as written. Under `let partial(F: (Type, Type) -> Type, S: Type) -> (Type) -> Type = (A) => F(S, A);` and a witness of `Tag((A: Type) => Box(S, A))`, the call `Tag/tag(@partial(Box, Nat))` is refused as a declaration's body, `no witness of Tag(partial(Box, Nat)) found`, and resolved in an arm of a `match` over a parameter of the same declaration; over an open term, `partial(Box, S)` with `S` a parameter, it is resolved outside an arm as well. Conversion holds the goal one with the witness's own spelling: `Eq()(partial(Box, Nat), (A: Type) => Box(Nat, A))` is proved by `Eq/refl()`. The library's witnesses answer alike, `Monad/pure(@partial(State, Nat), 3)` being refused. Probed at `7d28c0209`. Nothing is admitted by it: a witness whose signature computes its head is refused where it is declared, the goal the machine spells is refused and resolved by no other witness, and one declaration's two goals are answered by where each stands and not by which is met first, so the elaborator's one table of reducts moves neither. The same reading refuses a goal written through a definition beneath the binder: under `let f(S: Type, A: Type) -> Type = Box(S, A);`, `Tag/tag(@((A: Type) => f(Nat, A)))` reports `no witness of Tag(Box(Nat)) found`, naming the key the lookup did not compute. Fix: a function's key is read off its body's weak-head form beneath its binders, whichever reduction spelled it. Check: the three probes resolve, outside an arm and inside one, and a witness declared through a head its signature computes is still refused. Size: small; it changes which goals resolve.
- **The kernel checks a tuple literal against a structure's type, and no projection reads it there.** `check` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/infer.rs`) takes a tuple literal at a `StructType` field by field, as it does at a Σ, where elaboration refuses the literal (`Error::not_a_tuple_type`), so only a term built by hand holds one: `a_structure_occurrence_at_its_declared_parameter_count_still_works` checks `(7)` at `Boxed(Nat)`. A projection out of a structure names it and reads a literal of that structure alone (`curios_core::project_literal`), so a binder stated at `Boxed(Nat)` and bound to `(7)` types `.0` at `Nat` and reduces to no literal. Nothing is admitted by it that should not be: a closed term of `Nat` is left without a value. Either the rule goes, a structure's literal being the one form its type has, or the slot reads the tuple.
- **The kernel refuses an equality between two definitions elaboration holds to be one, and nothing says so until the kernel does.** Under `use /std/{Eq, Handle, Io};` and `use /std/Handle/{close};`, the declarations `let one(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);`, `let two(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);` and `let same: Eq()(one, two) = Eq/refl();` elaborate, and the compilation ends at ``the kernel refused /same: expected `Eq(…)(one, two)`, found `Eq(…)(one, one)` ``; the same three over `(n: Nat) -> Nat = n + 1` are accepted by both checkers, which leaves the effect as what sets the two apart. No `False` follows, the kernel being the stricter of the two. `curios-pipeline`'s `every_let_of_the_library_reads_back_as_a_report_prints_it` writes such a proof beside every `let` of `/std` known to terminate and lists the thirteen the kernel refuses (`UNCERTIFIED`): nine run an effect, and four of `/std/Flt` (`to_str`, `significant`, `signals/scaled_prod_sum` and `signals/scaled_prod_diff`) compute, their cause not found — a division by operator beside one by call, a match with its motive written beside one without, a tuple pattern beside its projections and a type argument solved for beside one written are each converted by the kernel where they stand alone.
- **A side opened alone a step at a time costs the kernel a square of its depth where the elaborator pays its length.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** what the kernel walks at each opening. Over `let tower(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => g(tower(g, k)) end;` and `let stack(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat, top: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => top | k + 1 => stack(g, k, g(top)) end;`, `let claim(g: (Nat) -> Nat) -> Eq()(tower(g, 500), stack(g, 500, 0)) = Eq/refl();` is accepted by both checkers, the elaborator spending 45,551 units and 237,636 node looks on it and the kernel 272,916 and 12,503,400; at a depth of 1,000 the elaborator spends 87,551 and 471,450, and the kernel 1,043,416 and 49,254,386. [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) holds that two checkers given one budget cost alike, and a multiple between them that grows with the program ends in a claim one accepts and the other refuses for its budget. The kernel compares `stack`'s call by opening it alone once for each of its steps (`unfolded_retry`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`), each opening a comparison nested in the last, 2,544 bytes of stack a level, and two stack samples of it stand in a traversal of a term. Counted at `6fe03a4c9`: the units and looks are each checker's `budget::consumed` and `term::looks` samples for the claim, with a declaration after it that reads it so the kernel files its own, in the stream a profiling build files for `wonder diagnostics -`; the stack is read under `gdb`, between neighbouring frames of `compare`. Not known: what is traversed, and whether the elaborator shares what the kernel copies. Fix: once that is known. Check: the kernel's units for the two claims, doubling with the depth. Size: unknown.
