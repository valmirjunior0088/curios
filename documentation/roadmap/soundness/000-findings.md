- **Strict positivity's and variance's readings of a type through what it computes have met no hunt.** A stuck `match` read through its arms, a datum read past the declaration's type parameters, a recursive member read by the least fixpoint of its unfolding, two projections of one group compared at the levels that fixpoint holds, and a member's vector read off its group's bodies as written each admit declarations the walks refused, on arguments [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) and [A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md) make, and no attack has been put to them. Fix: a `/hunt-soundness` round whose charters cross the readings with `Prop`, definitional K, a sort left to a caller, large elimination, a group closed over a walk's binders, Core text's definitions named before their families, and two instances of a group at two levels, a member's among them, which settlement then leaves at its floors; an attack that holds is a ticket on the board, its fix landing next. Check: `cargo xboard report` showing no open ticket reached through the readings. Size: one hunt round.

With `by_their_own_type` cut to two proofs in both checkers, at `d8f53c21c`, every test of `tests::laws` passes and `/std` builds, while `x, y: {}` in the arms of `match c | true => x | false => n end` and of the same match at `y`, compared at `Eq(@F(c))` for an `F(c)` that is `{}` at `true` and `Nat` at `false`, checks without the cut and is refused under it: a part equal where it is written and the whole apart where it sits. The checkers' own suites hold the rule directly (`two_neutrals_converge_where_a_lookup_gives_them_a_type_with_one_inhabitant`, in both). Fix: a context whose goal type a reduction leaves stuck, an arm of a `Bool` match at a type that computes on its scrutinee, so a seed in it is compared where its position hands no type. Check: the cut reading fails the new context's rows at a unit, and every row keeps its side. Size: one commit.
