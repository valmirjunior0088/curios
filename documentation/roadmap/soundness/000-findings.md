# Soundness: findings

- **Only `step_var` checks that no `let`-bound local reaches a reader of the certifier by name, and only of the one name it steps.** `step_var` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/whnf.rs`) asserts it where reduction steps a local; the other readers read what they are handed as written — `compare` (`convert.rs`), `Sort::of` (`sort.rs`), the case equation's key (`Scope::refine`, `scope.rs`), the position `Certifier::record_checked` files and the arguments a call is graded on (`calls.rs`) — believed to read by value because they are reached inside the bracket `Certifier::reading` raises ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-let-is-bound-for-typing-and-read-by-value)). Handed a term by name, an equation is keyed on a spelling no reader in its arm is handed and answers nothing, a position records a name where what is decided of it reads what the term reaches, and a call is graded on a name rather than the argument it stands for. `certifier::infer::tests::binding` holds each entry's unfolding as it stands, one fixture a reader, mutation-checked against reading as written; a reader added later, or a path into a bracket that hands a term by name, is held by nothing. Fix: at each reader, on the term it is about to read — `whnf`, `Reducer::reduce_forced` and `Sort::of` past their unfolding, `compare`, `Scope::refine`, the position `record_checked` files and a graded call's arguments — a `debug_assert!` makes of the whole term, against its memoized free variables, the check `step_var` makes of one name. It sits on what the reader reads rather than in `Certifier::by_value`, so a caller that skips the unfolding is caught where its term arrives; the tests run with their checks on ([Every profile compiles optimized, and a test keeps its checks](../../design/every-profile-compiles-optimized-and-a-test-keeps-its-checks.md)) and a release build pays nothing. A violation it finds today is an entry here, or a ticket on the soundness board where a proof of `False` is built from it. Check: `cargo xtask workspace lint` and `cargo xtask workspace test`; one mutation of each kind — the unfolding dropped at `convert`'s entry, at an elimination's scrutinee, at a position and at a call's arguments — refused by the assertion in a test that reaches it; the suite's time named before and after. It lands before the first stage of [A term by name cannot reach a reader](300-a-term-by-name-cannot-reach-a-reader.md), whose type would hide what it finds. One commit.
- **Uncertain: what landed of a call in a callback graded where its callee applies it has met no hunt.** A recursion through a callback a recursive definition applies is graded where the definition applies it ([Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)): a tree's `has_zero` recursing through `List/any(children, (c) => has_zero(c))` is total (`curios`'s `a_call_in_a_callback_descends_where_its_callee_applies_it_below`), so a summary of how a definition applies a parameter admits a descent no constructor written at the call shows, and no hunt has had it for a charter. Not known: whether a parameter used in a way the walk misses, an application under a binder a summary misreads, or a bound carried past the lambda it was handed lets size-change grade a call below what it is not below, and whether that reaches `False`. Settled by: a soundness hunt with those three charters, run on the user's word as every hunt is (`.claude/commands/hunt-soundness.md`); a proof of `False` it builds is a ticket, an admission short of one an entry here, and a hunt that finds neither drops this entry. It is taken before any stage of [A call in a callback is graded where its callee applies it](302-a-call-in-a-callback-is-graded-where-its-callee-applies-it.md), each of which widens what a summary admits. Size unknown.
