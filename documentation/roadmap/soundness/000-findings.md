# Soundness: findings

- **Uncertain: a family's former is stored beside the entry that determines it.** A former is spelled from its entry (`curios_core::former_spelling`), and Core text's family form builds that spelling and states no other, yet an item holds the former as a definition of its own, its type and body beside the entry it carries (`DefinitionKind::Nominal`), and the walk judges that body against its type alone (`curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`): nothing holds it to the spelling its entry determines. So a module built by hand or forged can hold a former spelled otherwise — a projection of its own group, or a body of the former's type that is no nominal type at all — which Core text cannot write. Not known: whether the walk accepts such a former, and what a judgment that unfolds it reads. Settled by: a module carrying a family whose former is a projection of its own group, and one whose former unfolds to `Nat`, put to the walk; where either is accepted, the fix is the treatment a family's constructors have, a former made from its entry where it is read and never stored. Size unknown until then.
- **A family's constructors can repeat a tag, which only a certifier refuses.** `Constructors` (`curios-core/src/inductive.rs`) is made from any sequence of cases (`From<Vec<_>>`, `FromIterator`), and resolves a tag by first match (`Constructors::position`), so a repeat hides the case behind it from every reader of the family. The certifier refuses one twice over: a walk declares each case as a name of its own, so a repeat is refused as `Redeclared` before anything is judged, and `check_distinct_tags` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/module.rs`) refuses it as `RepeatedTag` for a family declared outside a walk, the one place it is still reached. Nothing holds it where the family is made, which is where `InductDecl::new` already holds each case's count to its arity. The fix is the same treatment: `Constructors` refuses a repeated tag where it is made, so a family that repeats one is unrepresentable and Core text's family form refuses it as it reads; `RepeatedTag` and `check_distinct_tags` then go with nothing in their place. Small: one maker and its callers, the certifier's clause and its error.
