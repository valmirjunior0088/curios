# Soundness: findings

- **Uncertain: a family's former is stored beside the entry that determines it.** A former is spelled from its entry (`curios_core::former_spelling`), and Core text's family form builds that spelling and states no other, yet an item holds the former as a definition of its own, its type and body beside the entry it carries (`DefinitionKind::Nominal`), and the walk judges that body against its type alone (`curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`): nothing holds it to the spelling its entry determines. So a module built by hand or forged can hold a former spelled otherwise — a projection of its own group, or a body of the former's type that is no nominal type at all — which Core text cannot write. Not known: whether the walk accepts such a former, and what a judgment that unfolds it reads. Settled by: a module carrying a family whose former is a projection of its own group, and one whose former unfolds to `Nat`, put to the walk; where either is accepted, the fix is the treatment a family's constructors have, a former made from its entry where it is read and never stored. Size unknown until then.
