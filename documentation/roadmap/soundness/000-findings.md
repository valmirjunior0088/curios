# Soundness: findings

- **A call the certifier types in a member's leading binder types is graded against the parameters bound so far, and the closure reads the matrix that is not square as no cycle, so the call is found and is still no edge.** `check_lambda` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/infer.rs`) types each binder's type before `bind_parameter` adds the binder, so a member's call in the type of its `k`-th leading binder reaches `record_call` with `k − 1` parameters bound and `grade` builds a `(k − 1) × arity` matrix, as it does under a field lambda's binders (`enter_field`); `curios-analysis`'s `close` drops every pair `CallMatrix::compose` cannot compose, and `decide` passes a self-call whose matrix is not square (`is_idempotent` asks `rows == columns`). So `rec /T(n: Nat, m: ((y: Type) => Nat)(/T(n, n))) -> Type = Nat` is accepted by the certifier, while the discovery walk, which opens every binder first (`open_parts`), grades the same call `2 × 2` and finds no descent. No `False` follows, since a binder's type never runs — reduction drops it at β and conversion compares it under the budget — but the reason the certifier takes a group's calls from its typing (`curios-cert`'s README, *A group's calls are the ones the certifier types*; the fourth boundary of [the soundness board](../../design/soundness/the-soundness-board.md)) is that a call it sees is an edge, and here one seen is discarded, so a later change grading a running call short fails open with no signal. Fix, a choice between two: `close` reads a pair it cannot compose, and `decide` a self-call that is not square, as claiming nothing, `Partial`, which holds every short grading to come; or a call typed before its caller's parameters are all bound is graded over the caller part's whole arity, the rows not yet bound unknown. Check: the module above refused by `NotDescending` in `/T`, as `curios-cert`'s `a_call_in_a_leading_binders_type_is_graded_over_the_callers_whole_arity` in `certifier::calls::tests`, and, for the first fix, a `curios-analysis` test of `decide` over a self-call that is not square. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: no hunt has attacked the premises a call read off a path, and a summary read through parts, add.** Both checkers grade a call through a record's field as a call of the part its path reaches, a member handed or named short of a field lambda as a call of each of its parts, and a record handed at a summarized parameter field by field (`part_calls`, `handed_member`; [Totality of the erased program](../../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md), *A call is read off a path*); they read a field of a record below the record whatever binds it (*A field is below its record*, `Shape::Smaller`); and a definition publishes a summary for each of its parts, a definition of no group and `/sys/List/map`'s and `/sys/List/fold`'s callbacks among them, composed through a callee's where it hands a parameter on (`group_applications`, `definition_applications`, `handed`, `curios-analysis`; *A call in a callback is graded where its callee applies it*). Each rests on a premise no hunt has put to the board: that the part a path reaches, and the path and part a summary records, are the part and the path the call reaches when it runs; that a field lambda's applications of a definition's parameter are never stated as its leading lambdas'; and that a record's field is a proper part of the record's value. A member handed bare and projected by its callee broke the first once before it landed, and both refusals are held by `curios`'s `tests::rules::totality_record`. Settled by a `/hunt-soundness` round with those premises as its charters, its attacks left as tests and any `False` filed as a ticket. Size unknown.
