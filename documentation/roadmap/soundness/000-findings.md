# Soundness: findings

- **The kernel holds two host calls one only as one term posed, and refuses them once either side is unfolded.** Under `use /std/{Eq, Handle, Io};` and `use /std/Handle/{close};`, the declarations `let one(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);`, `let two(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);` and `let same: Eq()(one, two) = Eq/refl();` elaborate, and the compilation ends at ``the kernel refused /same: expected `Eq(…)(one, two)`, found `Eq(…)(one, one)` ``. `let same(h: Handle) -> Eq()(one(h), two(h)) = Eq/refl();` is refused with both sides printed `handle_close(h)`, and `Eq()((h: Handle) => close(h), (h: Handle) => close(h))` with each body `handle_close` of its own binder, where `Eq()(close, close)` is accepted, probed at `242c6009f`. The kernel's `compare` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) answers two terms that are one before it reduces either; once `forced` has reduced both it hands them to `structural`, which has an arm for two intrinsics (`convert_intrinsic`) and none for two `Subterm::Foreign`, and nothing asks again whether the reducts are one term, so two calls of one row over one operand are apart. No `False` follows, the kernel being the stricter of the two. `curios-pipeline`'s `every_let_of_the_library_reads_back_as_a_report_prints_it` lists the eleven `let`s of `/std` it refuses so, the ones that run an effect (`UNCERTIFIED`). Fix: two host calls are one where their declarations are one and their operands convert, each at the type its row demands of it (`foreign_operands`, `curios-core/src/intrinsic.rs`), as an intrinsic's operands are compared at its signature's. Check: the three programs certify, the eleven leave `UNCERTIFIED`, and a kernel fixture holds two calls of two rows apart. Size: small, made a step at a time under supervision: the kernel then admits what it refused, and the rule is stated in its decision first.
- **A recursion that ends at an application of a variable is run to its end and thrown away at every step the kernel opens, a square of its depth.** `force` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) unfolds a folded recursive call until it has a value, and its doc says what it keeps: a head constructor, or a reduct carrying no member of the group, the recursion being finished. Its `Apply` arm keeps neither: where `unfold_rec_apply` has nothing more to unfold it hands back the folded call, without asking whether the reduct still names the group, which the arm beside it asks of a `match`, a variable, a metavariable and a projection. So a recursion whose answer is `g(top)`, `g` a variable, is unfolded to its last step and answered as the call it was. Conversion then opens the call one step (`unfolded_retry`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`), compares what that left, which forces it again (`forced`), and so runs what is left of the recursion once for each of its steps. Under `let walk(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat, top: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => g(top) | k + 1 => walk(g, k, top) end;`, the claim `pub let claim(g: (Nat) -> Nat, x: Nat) -> Eq()(walk(g, 4000, x), g(x)) = Eq/refl();` is accepted after 82.3 s, 20.1 s at a depth of 2,000 and 5.0 s at 1,000, where the same `walk` answering `top` in its first arm, claimed equal to `x`, takes 0.17 s, 0.10 s and 0.07 s. At a depth of 8,000 elaboration accepts the claim and the compilation ends, after 185 s, at `the kernel refused /claim: the kernel's reduction budget ran out`. Timed on top of `bdfdf9df3` through the release compiler's `wonder diagnostics`, one reading each; six stack samples of the claim at 2,000, taken under `gdb` across its twenty seconds, all stand in the kernel, in `force` beneath `forced` beneath `unfolded_retry`. Elaboration's `force_rec` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`) has the same arm and is not where the time goes. The claim the entry was filed over is the same recursion with its answer built on the way down: over `let tower(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => g(tower(g, k)) end;` and `let stack(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat, top: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => top | k + 1 => stack(g, k, g(top)) end;`, `let claim(g: (Nat) -> Nat) -> Eq()(tower(g, 500), stack(g, 500, 0)) = Eq/refl();` is accepted by both checkers, the elaborator spending 45,551 units and 237,372 node looks on it and the kernel 272,932 and 12,503,260; at a depth of 1,000 the elaborator spends 87,551 and 470,872, and the kernel 1,043,432 and 49,254,010, counted at `9cb49b9b9` as each checker's `budget::consumed` and `term::looks` samples in the stream a profiling build files. [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) holds that two checkers given one budget cost alike, and a multiple between them that grows with the program ends in a claim one accepts and the other refuses for its budget. Fix direction: `force`'s `Apply` arm keeps a reduct that names no member of the group, as its doc says and as the arm beside it does, in the kernel and in elaboration's twin; a rule of the kernel's reduction, so stated in its decision first (`.claude/rules/checking.md`). Check: the claim over `walk` at 4,000 in the time of its twin that ends at a variable, and the kernel's units for the two claims over `tower` doubling with the depth. Size: small, supervised.
- **What a typing hit recalls is kept beside the tables, and outlives their scoped entries.** `Calls::partial_terms` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/calls.rs`) remembers each term whose typing closed a group that does not descend, and a hit on a typing table counts the group again by it (`recall_partial`, asked by `infer` and `check`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/infer.rs`). The tables' entries for a term that names a local are discarded wherever the equations in force change (`Memos::begin_equations`), [a judgment that reads the scope living as long as what it read](../../../curios-cert/src/kernel/memos.rs), and the remembered term is discarded only where a declaration begins: typed again under another arm, where the group it holds may descend by what that arm establishes, the term is filed anew, and a hit on the new entry recalls what the old typing closed. The answer errs to the refusing side, a judgment said to enclose a group that does not descend, so nothing is admitted by it, and no program is known to be refused: read off the code at `80707d617`, not shown by a probe. Fix: the mark rides in the table's entry, a type or a check filed with whether its typing closed such a group, so it lives as long as the entry does, and the set goes. Check: `curios-cert`'s tests and the library's certification unchanged, and a fixture typing one term under two arms. Size: small, made a step at a time under supervision: the kernel then admits what a stale mark refused.
