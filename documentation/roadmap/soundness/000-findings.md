# Soundness: findings

- **Strict positivity's and variance's readings of a type through what it computes have met no hunt.** A stuck `match` read through its arms, a datum read past the declaration's type parameters, a recursive member read by the least fixpoint of its unfolding, two projections of one group compared at the levels that fixpoint holds, and a member's vector read off its group's bodies as written each admit declarations the walks refused, on arguments [Strict positivity, modulo polarity](../../design/theory/strict-positivity-modulo-polarity.md) and [A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md) make, and no attack has been put to them. Fix: a `/hunt-false` round whose charters cross the readings with `Prop`, definitional K, a sort left to a caller, large elimination, a group closed over a walk's binders, Core text's definitions named before their families, and two instances of a group at two levels, a member's among them, which settlement then leaves at its floors; an attack that holds is a ticket on the board, its fix landing next. Check: `cargo xboard report` showing no open ticket reached through the readings. Size: one hunt round.
- **Uncertain: whether a guard's equation should reach a form whose other binder stands beneath a binder of the form's own, and how.** `curios_analysis::could_answer` (`curios-analysis/src/case_equation.rs`) reads a part beneath a binder of the stuck form's — a lambda's body, a match's arm — as it stands and reduces nothing below it: under `match u((x) => x) < 10`, `u((x) => x + 0 * c) < 10` counts `c` and is refused `Holds` in the `true` arm, where conversion compares the two lambdas' bodies reduced and holds the forms equal at the top of an item. A proof beneath such a binder is read like any term too, since the walk asks which parts are proofs (`ReadsProofs::proof_parts`) only outside every binder of the form's — `u((x) => Nat/div(x, b, @nonzero(b, c, q)))` beside `u((x) => Nat/div(x, b, @q))`, seen by reading the walk and not yet probed — and such a refusal prints the fact with its hidden proof left out, as [A diagnostic spells what its reader can write](../../design/tools/a-diagnostic-spells-what-its-reader-can-write.md) leaves out a proof the procedure proves, so it reads as the guard itself: seen as `Holds(a / b < 5)` refused under the guard `a / b < 5`, before the filter passed over a proof at its position. Reducing there one-sided has no bound the walk can see: a body naming a recursive group, the group forced, unfolds to a body naming it again, where conversion, reducing both sides in step, stops at the first difference. Not yet known: whether any program meets the shape — none in `/std` is known to — and a reading beneath a binder that stays bounded; settled by a program that needs it, then such a reading and the compared door of `curios`'s `tests::unfolding` (`Consumed::Compared`) counted at 8, 14 and 20 definitions, in user-space instructions retired, against the walk as it stands. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: whether an arm on an expression should restate a guard that meets its key only once a definition the guard calls is unfolded, and at what cost.** An arm on an expression restates each equation in force whose settlement meets the arm's key whole in its spelling, or in the type or definition of a local it reads (`curios_analysis::meets_one_of`, read by `curios-cert`'s `Scope::restate` and `curios-elab`'s `Context::equations_read_by`), so under a guard `small(x)` whose body matches on `g(x)`, an arm on `g(x)` restates nothing and the guard's spelling, settled outside the arm, does not read `g(x)` at its case there. Restating every equation whose settlement reads a local the arm's key names reaches it, and ran `Int/DivMod/unique`, a proof `/std` held until `Int` division became Euclid's, out of the certifier's reduction budget, re-settling nearly every guard of a proof at every arm inside it. Not yet known: whether any program meets the shape — none in `/std` is known to — and a trigger between the two that reaches it within the budget; settled by a program that needs it, then such a trigger and `/std`'s build counted against the key-occurrence trigger, in user-space instructions retired. Size: unknown.
