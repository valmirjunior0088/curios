- **A recursion that ends at an application of a variable is run to its end and thrown away at every step the kernel opens, a square of its depth.** `force` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) unfolds a folded recursive call until it has a value, and its doc says what it keeps: a head constructor, or a reduct carrying no member of the group, the recursion being finished.
