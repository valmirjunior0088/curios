# Soundness: findings

- **The kernel refuses an equality between two definitions elaboration holds to be one, and nothing says so until the kernel does.** Under `use /std/{Eq, Handle, Io};` and `use /std/Handle/{close};`, the declarations `let one(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);`, `let two(h: Handle) -> Io({}) = close(h);` and `let same: Eq()(one, two) = Eq/refl();` elaborate, and the compilation ends at ``the kernel refused /same: expected `Eq(…)(one, two)`, found `Eq(…)(one, one)` ``; the same three over `(n: Nat) -> Nat = n + 1` are accepted by both checkers, which leaves the effect as what sets the two apart. No `False` follows, the kernel being the stricter of the two. `curios-pipeline`'s `every_let_of_the_library_reads_back_as_a_report_prints_it` writes such a proof beside every `let` of `/std` known to terminate and lists the thirteen the kernel refuses (`UNCERTIFIED`): nine run an effect, and four of `/std/Flt` (`to_str`, `significant`, `signals/scaled_prod_sum` and `signals/scaled_prod_diff`) compute, their cause not found — a division by operator beside one by call, a match with its motive written beside one without, a tuple pattern beside its projections and a type argument solved for beside one written are each converted by the kernel where they stand alone.
- **A side opened alone a step at a time costs the kernel a square of its depth where the elaborator pays its length.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** what the kernel walks at each opening. Over `let tower(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => g(tower(g, k)) end;` and `let stack(g: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat, top: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => top | k + 1 => stack(g, k, g(top)) end;`, `let claim(g: (Nat) -> Nat) -> Eq()(tower(g, 500), stack(g, 500, 0)) = Eq/refl();` is accepted by both checkers, the elaborator spending 45,551 units and 237,372 node looks on it and the kernel 272,932 and 12,503,260; at a depth of 1,000 the elaborator spends 87,551 and 470,872, and the kernel 1,043,432 and 49,254,010. [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) holds that two checkers given one budget cost alike, and a multiple between them that grows with the program ends in a claim one accepts and the other refuses for its budget. The kernel compares `stack`'s call by opening it alone once for each of its steps (`unfolded_retry`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`), each opening a comparison nested in the last, 2,544 bytes of stack a level, and two stack samples of it stand in `Term`'s equality under a probe of the kernel's table of reducts (`Memos::whnf`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/memos.rs`).
