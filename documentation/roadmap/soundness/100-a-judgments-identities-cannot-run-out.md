# A judgment's identities cannot run out

[`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-type-is-accepted-by-typing-it-and-reduction-is-total-on-arbitrary-terms) decides that the kernel's judgments are total and that neither shapes nor counts can abort them, and [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) decides that a memo hit is free and that what a judgment consumes is counted in units no target can move. A judgment's binder identities break all three: a hit replays the identities its computation minted, counted once per path, into a 32-bit space no budget bounds, through a counter whose width is the host's. The kernel panics where the space runs out on a native build, and on wasm32 the counter can wrap and hand out an identity already open.

## What is wrong

**A hit replays identities per path.** Every reduct the kernel remembers records the identities its computation minted, the replays of the hits inside that computation included (`Replay`, `Spend::replay_since`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/spend.rs`), and every hit adds the record to the counter (`Spend::charge_nothing`). The work is done once per node of a shared term and the count is taken once per path through it, so a `let` chain read by value whose every level names the one before it `k` times multiplies the count by `k` a level while the identities opened grow by a few. From `a0 = n`, the levels `let c = Nat/min(a / 7, 3); let d = Nat/min(a / 5, 2); let a' = a - c * 7 - d * 5;`, seventeen of them, closed by `let _within: Holds(a17 <= n) = True/proved();`, end the compilation at `binder space exhausted` (`Spend::fresh`): 90 identities opened and 4,649,045,849 replayed. `/std/time/DateTime/of_timestamp`'s printed form, with what both cases of a split state read among the scope's facts (`Reader::subtractions` and `Reader::extrema`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`), opens 377 and replays 1,045,954,252; the largest in `/std` is `/std/Map/bits/rungs`, 164 opened and 1,680,123 replayed. Counted at `68ca73258` by counters added for the runs to a copy of the tree. The typing table gave the replay up for this reason (`Kernel::infer_hit`), and the reduct tables keep it so that identities after a hit land where a memo-free run's would, which no judgment reads but through the rank [An atom is ranked by where its binder stands](../arithmetic/100-an-atom-is-ranked-by-where-its-binder-stands.md) removes, and which the first typing hit already gives up. The replay has shaped another decision: [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) gives it as the reason for "What a lookup reads, it does not reduce".

**The counter's width is the host's.** `Entropy` (`curios-utilities/src/entropy.rs`) counts in `usize`, which is 32 bits on wasm32, where `curios-js` certifies what the playground compiles. Built `--release`, with no overflow checks, a replay whose sum passes `2^32` wraps and is dropped by `seed`'s maximum, so replays can leave the counter within a few identities of the top, and the next mint wraps it to zero and hands out identities the declaration has open: a capture where the native build panics. Read off the code at `68ca73258`, not probed; no `False` is known to follow.

**The space is a limit of its own.** A local's index is a `u32` (`Mint`, `curios-core/src/names.rs`) that nothing reads but its comparison and its hash, so it is a second limit beside the budget, which no refusal names: the kernel's `Spend::fresh`, the elaborator's `Context::fresh_for` and `Context::fresh_written` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`) and the lowering's binders (`curios-text/src/into_core/context.rs`) each `expect` the counter to fit it. The printer cuts the same space in two: a render mints its own binders from `PRINTED`, `1 << 31` (`Minting`, `curios-core/src/print.rs`), and takes every local at or above it for one of its own, so a compilation's identity past `2^31`, which the replay reaches, is displayed as a printer's.

**The test that holds a memo to its semantic half runs in no step.** `kernel_memo_parity` (`curios-prelude-archive/src/restore/tests.rs`) is `#[ignore]`d, and no step of `xtask/src/steps.rs`, workflow or command runs ignored tests, so the claim the memo decision rests on is taken by name or not at all.

## Goal

A judgment mints the identities of the binders it opens and no others, counted in a width that is the same on every target and that no judgment that ends can exhaust, so the only limits a judgment meets are its budget's and the running out of identities is not a state the code can reach.

## Decisions

- **No hit mints.** Every table of the kernel's answers a hit with the answer alone, as the typing table does and as every hit already spends nothing. A hit performs no computation, so it opens no binder. The counter counts the binders the judgment opened, which reduction opens inside the units it charges for the opening (`step_func`, `is_identity`) and typing and conversion open inside nodes each charges a step, so what it reaches is bounded by the work the judgment does. The elaborator's tables replay nothing already. Lean's kernel returns a cached `whnf`, `whnf_core` or inferred type before any fresh name is drawn ([`type_checker.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp)). "What a lookup reads, it does not reduce" keeps its rule on its remaining reason, that both terms reach the lookup reduced.
- **An identity is a `u64`, and so is every counter `Entropy` keeps.** The index is only compared and hashed, so its width costs nothing it is read for: `Mint`, `Free` and `Subterm` stay 24, 32 and 176 bytes and their archived forms 31 and 107 for `Free` and `Subterm`, the archived `Mint` alone going from 18 to 22 within `Global`'s 30, counted at `68ca73258` on x86_64. A counter that only a mint advances, one at a time, holds every judgment that ends, as `std::thread::ThreadId`'s `u64` ([`id.rs`](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/blob/master/library/std/src/thread/id.rs)), Agda's `NameId` (`Word64`, [`Agda.Syntax.Common`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/Syntax/Common.hs)) and GHC's `Unique` (`Word64` since [#22010](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/-/issues/22010), where `Int` ran out on 32-bit targets) hold theirs. `Entropy` counts in `u64` for every space it mints, as `curios-core`'s cost formulas compute in `u64` regardless of the host's pointer width; an identity that indexes an arena narrows to its arena's index where it is minted (`Mint`, the `id!` macro), as it does today. The printer's binders start at `1 << 63`.
- **The counter starts over with the budget.** It stays in `Spend`, beside the units, because both start over at a declaration's boundary and both are what a judgment consumes; the reason the module documentation gives today, that a replay settles both, goes with the replay.
- **The parity runs with the suite.** `kernel_memo_parity` takes one test thread about half a minute, as a part of the library's read-back does, and holds the verdicts of the kernel and `Kernel::uncached` alike, which the first stage leaves resting on it alone once identities part after any hit.

## Rejected

- **Keeping the replay and bounding it by what the budget lets a recomputation mint**, this specification's first rule: it keeps a hit charged its computation's identities, the compounding the cost decision rejects for units, so that the kernel refuses a program the elaborator, which replays nothing, accepts within one budget; and the parity it keeps is lost at the first typing hit.
- **A refusal where the space runs out**, a fallible mint through `Reducer::fresh_binder` and `curios-analysis`'s `fresh`, at 48 sites in the kernel and the shared crates and 129 in the elaborator: a refusal for a state the representation can leave out.
- **Pricing a mint in the budget**, which makes the bound the budget's own: typing builds terms the budget prices nowhere, since the budget prices reduction, the one stage a program can drive to arbitrary cost, and a local priced alone would be the one construction typing pays for.
- **Binders the kernel names by position**, de Bruijn indices as Rocq's kernel keeps them ([`constr.mli`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/constr.mli)), which mints no name, or levels as the elaboration zoo's conversion takes a fresh variable from the context's length ([`02-typecheck-closures-debruijn`](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/elaboration-zoo/blob/master/02-typecheck-closures-debruijn/Main.hs)): a position recurs once its scope closes, and the kernel's tables over terms that name a local rest on identities that never recur within a declaration, which is why an entry that outlives its binders is unreachable rather than wrong; Lean's kernel keeps unique names for its caches the same way.
- **Capping the budget at what 32 bits hold**: a limit on a program's budget read off a representation's width.

## Stages

1. **No hit mints.** The memo decision in `curios-cert`'s README is restated first: no table replays identities, with the typing table's precedent and Lean's; `spend`'s and `memos`' module documentation and `Kernel::infer_hit`'s doc follow, and the conversion decision's "What a lookup reads, it does not reduce" is left its remaining reason. `Replay` becomes the reduct, `replay_since` and `charge_nothing` go, and the counter's half of `Spend::snapshot` with them; what a test reads of the units becomes a `#[cfg(test)]` `Kernel::consumed`, the elaborator's `Context::consumed`'s twin, and what a test reads of the identities is read by minting one on either side. `kernel_memo_parity` runs with the suite. Check: a fixture beside `a_shared_closed_term_mints_once_per_node` putting a reduct that mints at its leaf, shared twice at each of forty levels, to the whnf tables, mutation-checked against the replay; `cargo xtask workspace lint`, `format` and `test`; the chain above accepted. Tried on `68ca73258` in a copy of the tree: the workspace's suite passes, 5,254 tests, `kernel_memo_parity` passes, and the chain is accepted with 89 identities opened. Size: small, supervised, the trusted base's.
2. **An identity is a `u64`.** `Entropy` counts in one, `Mint::mint` takes one, and the `id!` and `name!` macros, `MetavarId` and `UniverseMetaId` narrow from it; `Mint`'s index, `Free::local`, `Free::local_written`, `Free::local_index` and `Spend::reserve` take one; `PRINTED` is `1 << 63`; and the `expect`s on a `u32` in the kernel, the elaborator and the lowering go with the conversions they guard. Check: `cargo xtask workspace lint`, `format` and `test`, the corpus among them since the macros reach every intermediate representation, and `cargo xtask bundle build`. The browser bundle's memory may move, where `Mint` aligns to eight bytes; the sizes on wasm32 are not measured. Size: small, mechanical across the stages.

## Verification and retirement

The chain above compiles with no `binder space exhausted`, its count the identities its binders opened. Whether the chain becomes a test turns on what its compile costs: seventeen levels take minutes, in elaboration and erasure, the kernel in neither of two stack samples. The specification is deleted once both stages land, with the references to it.

## What it leaves

[An atom is ranked by where its binder stands](../arithmetic/100-an-atom-is-ranked-by-where-its-binder-stands.md) is what makes freshness the one property a judgment reads off an identity. Until it lands, identities after any hit differ between the kernel and `Kernel::uncached`, as they already do after a typing hit, and `kernel_memo_parity` holds the verdicts to agree.
