# A term by name cannot reach a reader

**The benefit.** The certifier's reduction, conversion and classification read their terms by value, and a term that names a `let`-bound local cannot reach them. They are reached only through a door, which unfolds what it is handed. Behind the door they run over a receiver that holds the certifier borrowed and offers no binding, so nothing can make a term they hold name a `let`-bound local while they read. That holds in every build, by the code's structure. Today a flag holds it, beside an assertion only a build keeping its checks makes, and beside one that panics in every build. The project has decided to adopt it.

Its next step is stage 1, a ready delivery. It waits on nothing.

## What this builds on

- **A `let` is bound for typing and read by value** ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-let-is-bound-for-typing-and-read-by-value)). Typing walks a `let`'s tail over its binder, so a value named twice is typed once. Everything that reads a term for more than its type reads it with each `let`-bound name unfolded (`Certifier::by_value`, over `Scope::by_value`). So the reduct tables, the case equations, the recorded positions and the graded calls are keyed as a substituted `let` leaves them. An arm binds each variable its case solves the same way ([An arm is checked in a context specialized by index inversion](../../design/theory/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md)).
- **The scope keeps what it stores by value.** It holds every local's type and every `let`'s value by value. Binding over a local that stood for nothing assumes again every local whose type names it, and binds again every `let` whose value does (`Certifier::read_again`). It also restates every case equation whose settlement reads the solution (`Scope::restate`).
- **The readers neither bind nor type.** Reduction (`certifier/whnf.rs`), conversion (`certifier/convert.rs`, `certifier/convert/intrinsic.rs`), classification (`Sort::of` and the lookups beside it in `certifier/sort.rs`, `synth_neutral` among them) and the erased half (`erased_half` and `bears_proof`, `obligation.rs`) open only binders they mint. They type nothing, bind nothing, record nothing and put no case in force.
- **Reduction asks conversion, and conversion reduces.** A judgment's reduction puts four questions to its checker's conversion, each answered by plain reduction, which asks nothing ([A question reduction asks is answered by plain reduction](../../design/soundness/a-question-reduction-asks-is-answered-by-plain-reduction.md)). Conversion reduces, and both classify. So the three readers call one another, and they move behind their doors together.
- **A restricted view of the certifier has precedent.** Erasure reads the certifier through `Reader` (`certifier/reader.rs`), which offers what erasure may ask and records nothing. A binder scope is opened only through `Certifier::scoped`, the one caller of `Scope::mark` and `Scope::retract`.

## What is wrong today

- **A term is by value relative to the scope, and the scope moves.** An arm binds its solution over locals that stood for nothing (`bind_solutions`), the payload binders the certifier itself minted among them. A recursive group's tail binds its members the same way. So a term built by value before such a binding names a `let`-bound local after it: `check_arm` builds the arm's goal with `goal_at`, then binds the solution (`certifier/infer/eliminate.rs`). The scope restates what it stores across the binding; nothing else does. Erasure's remembered types once served one arm's answer in another for this reason (`Eraser::in_arm`, `curios-erase/src/eraser/eliminate.rs`).
- **A runtime flag decides whether a reader trusts its term.** `Certifier::reading` raises the `reading` field where reduction or conversion is entered from typing. While it is up, `Certifier::by_value` hands its term back as it is. `whnf`, `Reducer::reduce_forced`, `Sort::of` and `convert` unfold only while it is down. Shared reduction and the shared analyses call back into the certifier through `Reducer`, `ClosedHost`, `Driver` and `Env`, and the same flag decides whether those calls trust what they are handed. `Certifier::naming` lowers the flag for a `let`'s tail, but no path enters that bracket with the flag raised, since nothing a reader calls types.
- **Checks hold the rule.**
  - Each reader asserts that its term names no `let`-bound local (`Certifier::debug_assert_by_value`, `Certifier::debug_assert_graded`, `Scope::refine`'s assertion), but only in a build that keeps its checks.
  - `step_var` asserts it in every build, so a `let`-bound name that reached the head of a reduction would panic a release compiler: a walk that aborts, which the README counts as a defect.
  - Anywhere else such a name would be read as written. Conversion would hold two spellings of one value apart. A memo or a case equation would be keyed on a name an arm's binding moves. A position would name a binder that is closed by the time the obligations read it.
- **Plain reduction is a mode the scope carries.** `Certifier::plainly` raises `Scope`'s `plain` field while a question is answered and while a shared analysis reads a term (`Env::force`). Only readings read it: the memo tables, a case equation's settled spellings and the question points. Typing reads it once, in `record_checked`'s assertion, made in every build, that nothing is typed under plain reduction.
- **One bracket is written out twice.** Settling a case equation's reduced spelling (`Certifier::settle_refinement`) and asking whether a stuck form is an equation's scrutinee (`Certifier::asked_scrutinee`) each withhold an equation and every one inside it by four calls that must agree: `Scope::hide_refinements_from` and `Memos::set_aside`, then `Scope::show_refinements` and `Memos::hand_back`.
- **The questions are split across modules.** Three of the four questions reduction asks are conversion's functions in `convert.rs` (`asked_classes`, `asked_within`, `unread_payload`). The fourth, `asked_scrutinee`, is the certifier's, in `certifier.rs`.
- **A guard no path reaches.** `Positions::begin` raises a re-entrancy guard while an erased half is decided, so that the classification records no position or call of its own. Classification never types, so the guard suppresses nothing. The remembered half is also asked through two copies of one lookup, `Certifier::record_checked`'s and `Reader::erased`'s.
- **Three comments say a `let` is substituted.** `Env::unfold`, `Env::refined` (`certifier.rs`) and `ClosedHost::closed_body` (`certifier/whnf.rs`) say a `let` is substituted before a judgment or reduction meets its binder. The certifier has bound its `let`s instead since it began binding an arm's solutions.

## What the peers do

- **Lean 4's kernel keeps a `let`-bound variable in its local context and unfolds it where reduction meets it** (`whnf_fvar`, "zeta-reduction"). It caches weak-head forms keyed by the expression alone ([`type_checker.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp)). That is sound because a free variable's declaration never changes once made. Lean's elaborator caches only terms with no free variable: "We cache only closed terms without expr metavars" ([`WHNF.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Meta/WHNF.lean)). Rocq's kernel reads a local definition through its environment where reduction meets it ([`cClosure.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/cClosure.ml)). Neither specializes a local in place, as an arm here does, so neither needs this rule.
- **Agda builds a new context for each case it splits.** It makes a new telescope and a substitution back to the old context, and applies the substitution to the target type (`SplitClause`, [`Agda.TypeChecking.Coverage`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.6.4/docs/src/Agda.TypeChecking.Coverage.html)). What held of the old context is read again, never kept. The scope's restatements make the same move.
- **Normalization by evaluation keeps terms and values apart.** A local definition lives in the evaluation environment, so no value names one. In Idris 2, environments "map local variables to binders", a let among them "(with a value)", and values "are in head normal form" ([implementation overview](https://idris2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/implementation/overview.html); [smalltt](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/smalltt)). Those values are relative to an environment that only grows. Here the scope is specialized mid-walk, and a value held across that is stale.
- **Rocq's `EConstr` reads through its store at every node.** It wraps a term so that `kind` does not observe defined evars, reading through the evar map. `to_constr` gives the snapshot ([`EConstr`](https://docs.rocq-prover.org/v8.19/api/coq-core/EConstr/index.html)). A snapshot is stale once the store it was read against moves, and a type marking a term as already read would be such a snapshot here.
- **rustc's `EarlyBinder` guards a value behind its instantiation.** It provides `instantiate_identity` "for discharging the binder when you are 'already inside of it'" ([rustc-dev-guide](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/ty-module/early-binder.html)). A wrapper serves where the context it is relative to stays fixed for the wrapper's life, as an item's generics do.
- **GHC hands zonking a monad that cannot typecheck.** `ZonkM` is "a stripped down version of 'TcM' which is sufficient for zonking types" ([`GHC.Tc.Zonk.Monad`](https://hackage-content.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.10.3/src/GHC/Tc/Zonk/Monad.hs)). Code that must not do a thing is handed a context that cannot do it. That is the shape taken here.

## Decisions

- **The readers run over a receiver that a door makes.**
  - `ByValue` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/by_value.rs`) holds the certifier mutably borrowed, so nothing binds while it lives.
  - It offers the certifier's lookups by shared reference, and by name each capability a reading mutates: the budget, the memo tables, a case equation's settlement, a fresh binder opened, a bracket, plain reduction and the closed machine's runs.
  - It offers no typing, no binding and no recording.
  - It carries whether its reduction is plain.
  - Only its module makes one, at a door, which unfolds the terms it is handed, and a bracket hands its walk the same reading. Every door is a function of that module, so no closure handed in from outside can carry a term past the unfolding. The certifier's bracket opens and closes through one pair of steps that `Certifier::scoped` and `ByValue::scoped` share.
  - Each walk behind a door takes the receiver where it takes the certifier today, keeping its parameter's name. That is some ninety functions across `whnf.rs`, `convert.rs`, `convert/intrinsic.rs`, `sort.rs` and `obligation.rs`, each changing its receiver's type and nothing else.
  - A walk is handed only what its door unfolded, its own parts and reducts, binders it minted, and what the scope and the definitions hold, all by value.
  - It is named for the property it holds, which `Certifier::by_value` already names. `Reader`, `Certifier::read` and `Positions`' `reading` field already name erasure's view, so the reading words are taken.
- **The doors keep their names and signatures.** The doors are `whnf`, `Reducer::reduce` and `Reducer::reduce_forced` on `Certifier`, `Env::force`, `Sort::of`, `as_sort`, `carries_information`, `sort_of_intrinsic`, `synth_neutral`, `erased_half`, `bears_proof`, `Judge::sort_below`, `convert` and `Certifier::refine`. Each takes `&mut Certifier` and a term as written. So their callers do not change: typing, erasure's `Reader`, the suites (some three hundred and forty calls), `curios-elab`'s agreement tests and the law grid. `Env::force` opens a plain reading. `Certifier::refine` unfolds the scrutinee and value it is handed and resolves the scrutinee's spelling in a reading, so a case equation's key is made at the moment it is recorded. Where a door and its walk share a name, the walk's entry is the receiver's method of that name, since the crate is one namespace.
- **Each shared trait is implemented on both sides of the door.** Shared reduction and the shared analyses call back through `Reducer`, `ClosedHost`, `Driver` and `Env`. Implemented for `ByValue`, a trait trusts what it is handed, since a walk behind a door built it from what it was handed. Implemented for `Certifier`, it is a door. The receiver the shared code was handed decides which applies, so `curios-core`, `curios-analysis` and the elaborator do not change.
- **Typing stays by name, and its own by-value readings unfold where they take a term.** These are an elimination's scrutinee and goal, a `let`'s and a group's tail type, a group filed by value, a recorded position, an arm's test against a value of another case and a graded call. Each unfolds in the one function that takes the term, by `Certifier::by_value`, or by `Certifier::graded`, which keeps a variable an arm's index inversion solved. Each is made at the moment of its read, so a binding before the read cannot leave it stale.
- **Nothing asserts that a term is by value.** These go, since each checks what a door now makes so: the `reading` field, `Certifier::reading`, `Certifier::reads_by_value`, `Certifier::naming`, `Certifier::debug_assert_by_value`, `Certifier::debug_assert_graded`, `Scope::names_bound`, `Scope::names_ungraded`, `Scope::refine`'s assertion, `step_var`'s, and `record_checked`'s that nothing is typed under plain reduction.
- **Plain reduction is a mode of a reading.** The receiver carries it. `plainly` opens a plain reading over the same certifier, and the scope's probes take the mode as an argument, as the memo's tables already do. The scope keeps no mode, and typing has none to be in, so nothing can be typed under plain reduction.
- **The questions reduction asks are conversion's, in one place.** `asked_scrutinee` joins `asked_classes`, `asked_within` and `unread_payload` in `convert.rs`, each taking the receiver. The receiver offers the bracket that withholds an equation and every one inside it once (`ByValue::withholding`), and settling an equation's spelling and asking whether a stuck form is its scrutinee both open it.
- **The memo stays one component.** Its tables share their lives (`begin_declaration`, `begin_equations`, `begin_sizes`, a refused arm's `Aside`), so the by-value tables stay beside typing's. The receiver is their only caller, as `Certifier::scoped` is `Scope::mark`'s.
- **Classification records nothing because it cannot.** The receiver offers no position or call to record, so `Positions`' re-entrancy guard and `Positions::begin` go. `Positions::settle` keeps only the first undecided classification. The remembered half has one door, which `record_checked` and `Reader::erased` both ask.
- **The Π and Σ rules stay one statement for both roles.** `tuple_sort`, `func_sort` and `sort_of_binders` take the receiver that opens their binders: the `ByValue` for a lookup, the `Certifier` for a judgment. Today they take the function that establishes each part (`Establish`) the same way.

## Stages

1. **The readers run behind their doors.** Reduction, conversion and classification move together, since each calls the others.
   - Lands: `ByValue`, its doors and its brackets, `ByValue::withholding` among them. The walks of `whnf.rs`, `convert.rs`, `convert/intrinsic.rs`, the lookup half of `sort.rs`, `erased_half` and `bears_proof` take `ByValue`. The certifier's methods only readings call move onto the receiver: the memo tables' hits and stores, a case equation's settlement, `proofs_named`, the refinement probes and the closed machine's runs. `Reducer`, `ClosedHost` and `Driver` are implemented for `ByValue` in place of `Certifier`, and `Env` for both. `asked_scrutinee` joins the other questions in `convert.rs`. The Π and Σ rules take their receiver. `Certifier::refine` becomes a door.
   - Goes: the `reading` field, `Certifier::reading`, `Certifier::reads_by_value`, `Certifier::naming`, `Certifier::debug_assert_by_value`, `Certifier::debug_assert_graded`, `Scope::names_bound`, `Scope::names_ungraded`, `Scope::refine`'s assertion and `step_var`'s.
   - Restated: `ClosedHost::closed_body`'s, `Env::unfold`'s and `Env::refined`'s comments, the notes of `tests::binding` and `eliminate::scope_tests`, and the README's `let` decision with its **Evidence**.
   - Check: the verification below.
2. **Plain reduction is a reading's mode.**
   - Lands: the receiver's mode decides; `plainly` opens a plain reading; `Env::force` on `Certifier` is a door that opens one; the scope's probes take the mode as an argument.
   - Goes: `Scope`'s `plain` field, `Scope::begin_plain`, `Scope::end_plain`, and `record_checked`'s assertion.
   - Check: the verification below.
3. **Classification records nothing because it cannot.**
   - Lands: the remembered half's one door, which `record_checked` and `Reader::erased` ask.
   - Goes: `Positions`' guard and `Positions::begin`.
   - Check: the verification below, with `curios-erase`'s suites, which read through `Reader`.

## Verification

After each stage, `cargo xtask workspace lint` runs; it certifies all of `/std`. The workspace's suites run. `sys.rkyv`, `std.rkyv` and `documentation.rkyv`, which `curios-prelude`'s build writes, must digest the same as the previous stage's, built in the same checkout, since no verdict, key, reduct or position moves. From stage 1 on, a walk handed a term straight from typing does not compile. A mutation of the one constructor, handing its terms back as written, is refused by `tests::binding` and `eliminate::scope_tests`. `stored_prelude_measurements`' certification time (`timed`) may move; it is named, not retaken. The full gate runs once, after the last stage.

## Rejected

- **A term type that only reading by value makes**, the design this specification first stated. It marks how a term was made, not what the term is now. Held across an arm's binding, as the arm's goal `goal_at` builds is, such a term names a `let`-bound local and still type-checks, so the assertions would have to stay. It would also wrap every term a reader takes, the suites' calls among them: a guard type that says nothing about what a reader does.
- **A term type branded by its reading**, an invariant lifetime from a higher-ranked closure. It holds a term inside its reading as the receiver does, at the cost of a lifetime on every term a walk passes. The receiver makes that unnecessary: a walk's terms all come from its door, its own reducts and stores that hold them by value.
- **Unfolding at every entry, with no flag and no receiver.** The property would hold at each read, but nothing would stop a new walk from reading a term its entry did not unfold. Every reduction level would also walk its reduct's mentions, which the flag exists to spare.
- **Unfolding a `let`-bound name where reduction meets it**, as Lean's and Rocq's kernels do. The README's rejection stands: an arm binds a local again, and a table keyed by a spelling that names it would answer after the arm.
- **A second representation for values**, as normalization by evaluation keeps. Every reader, table and shared analysis would be written over it, and a value held across an arm's binding would be stale all the same.
- **Trusting a shared door by a flag.** The receiver's type decides at the call what the flag decides at run time.
- **Two receiver types for the two reductions**, so that plain reduction could not ask a question at all. Every walk would be compiled twice for the few places the two reductions differ, where the walk is one statement for both, as the Π and Σ rules are one statement for a lookup and a judgment.
- **Moving reduction behind its doors before conversion and classification.** Reduction asks conversion its questions, and a walk behind a door cannot hand a door the certifier it holds, so the three move together.

## Retirement

The README's `let` decision states the doors and the receiver as what holds the rule from stage 1 on. Once the three stages have landed and nothing cites this file, it is deleted.
