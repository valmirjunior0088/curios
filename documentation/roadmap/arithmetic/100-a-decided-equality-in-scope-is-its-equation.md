# A decided equality in scope is its equation

**[Equality is decided where a decider exists and is `Eq` everywhere, joined by one law](../../design/theory/equality-is-decided-where-a-decider-exists-and-is-eq-everywhere-joined-by-one-law.md) holds the bridge between the two equalities to be one lemma of the concept, no carrier stating its own, and the elaborator's own proofs cross by `/std/Nat/eq_of_eql` and `/std/Int/eq_of_eql`, public for them alone; its next step is stage 1, a ready delivery.** Stated once, a decided equality in scope — a hypothesis, a field, a conjunct either states, or a guard's true arm — is its equation by `Eql`'s law named at its type, the call a program writes. The goal's half stands: an `Eq` goal is asked as its decision and crossed by that law. With the fact's half crossing the same way, the elaborator crosses in one way at both ends, a program leaves the crossing unwritten wherever its type names the sides, and each law `Eql` and `Ord` carry has one public name.

## What is wrong today

Each is probed at `bccdd3112`.

- **A conjunct a fact states is a fact at `Nat` and `Int` alone.** The reader takes a stated conjunction apart for the comparisons it reads and for nothing else, and the fact that states a bound outright is looked for among the hypotheses and their fields, never their conjuncts (`stated_by_a_fact`, `curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`). So an omitted `Eq()(a.1, b.1)` is filled over two `{Nat, Nat}` and refused over two `{Str, Str}`, `nothing discharged`, under a hypothesis `h: Holds(a.0 == b.0 && a.1 == b.1)`, under a field `ok` stating it, and in the true arm of a guard `a.0 == b.0 && a.1 == b.1`; at a type `A` with `use Eql(A)` it is refused alike. It is filled once a local is bound to the conjunct, `let p = Bool/and/second(…)`.
- **Three names cross, where the decision has one.** `Eql/eq_of_eql` is the law. `/std/Nat/eq_of_eql` and `/std/Int/eq_of_eql` are each the proof its carrier's witness names for that law, as `Byte`'s and `Bytes`'s are, which are private; these two are `pub` because the registry's order rows name them (`OrderSyntax::eq_of_eql`, `curios-common/src/syntax.rs`), a note over each says so, and a program may call them.
- **The reader crosses carrier by carrier, at four places.** `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs` writes the carrier's lemma over the two sides and the proof where a hypothesis, a field or a conjunct states an equality (`Reader::comparison`), in an `==` guard's true arm (`Reader::guard`), where a comparison kept for last is read (`Reader::last`), and where a `!=` goal is negated (`negated`). Each then reads the equation as its two bounds (`Reader::equation`), as it reads one a fact states as `Eq`.
- **`/std` writes the crossing 90 times in 24 files**, counted off its sources by the proof each call is handed: 21 an arm's `Bool/True/qed()`, 7 `proved()`, 31 a binder in scope that states the decision, `Tuple.crs`'s seven `p1` among them, and 31 a conjunct taken at the call through `Bool/and/first` or `second`, 28 of them in `Tuple.crs`, 2 in `Map/internal/Node.crs` and 1 in `List.crs`. Three recursive calls of a carrier's own lemma, `Nat.crs`'s, `Bytes.crs`'s and `List.crs`'s, are inductions and no crossing.
- **A carrier's order lemmas are a second public path to the laws `Ord` carries.** `Nat/ord`, `Int/ord`, `Bytes/ord` and `List/ord` hold the proofs their type's `Ord` witness names, each public, so `Nat/ord/flips` and `Ord/flips` at `Nat` name one fact. What keeps a lemma public is its witness alone, declared in the parent's file, which names no private declaration of a child module. `Bytes/ord`'s `past` and its `below` subtree are no law of `Ord`: a key's encoding is ordered through them ([`prelude/`'s findings](../prelude/000-findings.md)), and `Bytes/tests.crs` reads `past`. [`curios-prelude-build`'s README](../../../curios-prelude-build/README.md) states the order lemmas as the public proofs beside each order, `Str/ord/flips` among them, which `/std` does not declare.

- **The registry and the reader.** `OrderSyntax` loses `eq_of_eql` from both its rows and from the procedure's vocabulary, the reader's four crossings become one, and that one is the function the goal's step (`equated`, `curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`) writes its own by.
- **The carriers.** `/std/Nat/eq_of_eql` and `/std/Int/eq_of_eql` are private, as `Byte`'s and `Bytes`'s are, and lose the notes that say why they are not.
- **A crossing over what is proved whole**, the 21, the 7 and the 31. Each may read `proved()` wherever its expected type names both sides, which the goal's half already admits in the shapes probed: under an arm, `| a == b => Option/some(proved())` at `Option(Eq()(a, b))`; over a hypothesis at `Str`; as `Eq/subst`'s equation, its motive and its value naming the sides; as `Eq/cong`'s under a function unification sees through, `(n: Nat) => n % 256`; and in a `let` with no type a later use pins, `let same = proved(); Eq/sym(same)`. A `let refl() = …` names no sides and is refused; it reads `proved()` only with its type given, `let refl(): Eq()(a, b) = proved()`.
- **A crossing over a conjunct**, the 28 of `Tuple.crs` and their 7 locals. Each tuple's law is handed `h: Holds(eql(a, b))`, which conversion holds to be the conjunction its `eql` states: `let p: Holds(a.0 == b.0 && a.1 == b.1) = h;` checks inside the witness, so once a conjunct a fact states is a fact, each law restates `h` once and hands every field's equation as `proved()`. ## Goal

- **One crossing.** A decided equality the reader reads — stated by a hypothesis, a field or a conjunct, decided by an `==` guard's true arm, or assumed where a `!=` goal is negated — is held as its equation, proved by `Eql`'s law, `Eql/eq` once stage 3 names it, at the equality's type over the proof the fact gives: the term a program writes, written by the one function the goal's step shares. The equation is its two bounds, by `le/of_eq`, as an equation a fact states as `Eq` is read today; the search and its certificate are unchanged.
- **A conjunct a fact states is a fact.** Each conjunct of a conjunction a hypothesis, a field or a guard's true arm states, at any depth of `&&`, is a fact of its own: what the procedure finds a bound stated by, at any type, and what the reader reads where it is a comparison. A conjunction only a reduct holds is opened by nothing but a statement that restates it.
- **One public name a law.** `OrderSyntax` holds no `eq_of_eql`; no carrier of `/std` states a public `eq_of_eql`; no carrier's order module states a public lemma restating a law `Ord` carries. What `Ord` does not carry, `Bytes/ord`'s `past` and `below`, stays public.
- **`/std` writes the crossing only where nothing names its sides.**

## Settled

- **The call names its type.** The law is written as the goal's step writes it, `Term::registered_method` applied at the carrier with the type given, so its witness slot is resolved where it is born: a candidate is checked where parking is suppressed (`check`, `curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`), and a slot left to the first argument would wait.
- **The law is no target of the vocabulary.** `Eql`'s law (`SyntaxRegistry::eql`) stands outside `EntailmentSyntax::targets()`, as it landed: a concept among the targets reorders `/std`'s build, 52 derived `Spell` witnesses meeting `call` out of scope where it was tried at `d6c96c612`.
- **`Eql`'s law stays one a program may call**, written where nothing names the sides. The work removes the other names and renames this one, never removes it.
- **The derivation writes the law in the proofs it writes**, as a program does (`equal_where_eql`, `curios-elab/src/derive.rs`); it is no crossing of the procedure's and is not moved.
- **A conjunct is a fact where its conjunction is stated, and nowhere else** (decided by the user on 2026-11-25). A conjunction is stated where the statement opens to it by its heads alone, as the procedure opens every statement it reads (`opened`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`) — a `Bool` function's call whose body is the conjunction among them. A conjunct held as the fact it states is no search: it adds no congruence and no symmetry, solves nothing, and is bounded by the layers that opening reads, as the bound's decision already reads a conjunction among the facts as its conjuncts. A conjunction only a reduct holds — a match's arm under a refinement, as `List`'s `eql` is in its arm for two conses — stays unread, the opaque conjunction that decision's rejection names; a program states one by a local whose type is the conjunction, which conversion admits.
- **A true `==` rewrites no type.** A value is still carried along the equation by `Eq/subst`, the equation left to the elaborator; no hypothesis enters conversion, the first rejection of the bound's decision.
- **A concept's law that its two values are one is `eq`** (decided by the user on 2026-11-25). Within the concept the hypothesis is its own method, so the name says it no second time: `Eql/eq_of_eql` is `Eql/eq`, and `Ord/eq_of_ord` is `Ord/eq`, the name the carriers' order modules already give that law (`Nat/ord/eq`). `Ord`'s `lt_of_ord` and `le_of_ord` relate two methods and keep both names, their short forms being the methods `lt` and `le`; `refl` stays, reflexivity's name throughout `/std`; a carrier's own lemma keeps `eq_of_eql`, its path naming no decision. `Eq` and `Eql` keep their names: the identity type's in Lean and Rocq, and `==`'s concept as `Add` is `+`'s.

**Rejected.** The two lemmas kept public as the procedure's own vocabulary, what stands today: the bridge per carrier the equality decision rejects, and the fragmentation Lean keeps, `Nat.eq_of_beq_eq_true` in its `Init.Prelude` beside `LawfulBEq.eq_of_beq`; kept only where stage 4 stops and the user chooses it over the mechanism the law would need.
