# An atom is ranked from its children's ranks

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a fact a node carries made of its children's at the cost of what it adds. Ranking an atom rebuilds the whole atom, so an atom that holds an atom is rebuilt once for itself and once for every atom around it.

**Ready for delivery**, by the stages under *Stages*; the decisions under *Decisions* are settled.

## What is wrong

`Term::rank` (`curios-core/src/term.rs`) remembers a rank per node, and computes one by `rank::ranked` (`curios-core/src/term/rank.rs`): the term rebuilt whole three times — each binder and metavariable spelled by where a walk first meets it, a metavariable's spine entries respelled under it; then each universe metavariable; then each quality metavariable — hashed, then its identities' ages listed after the hash (`Rank::Many`). The memo on the node spares an atom its own second ranking and nothing else: an atom whose operand holds an atom respells the inner one again inside its own rebuild, and lists the inner one's ages again after its own. A rank is asked where a handle is handed out (`Atoms::keyed`), so a spine of `d` atoms each holding the one below costs the square of `d` in nodes rebuilt and in keys listed.

## What is known

- **The order is decided.** [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) orders an atom by its shape, then by the age of the identities it holds, each identity read by comparison and never by its value, so that no order follows a display built and discarded, a memo's answer or a probe that opened a binder. A rank read off children's orders atoms by that rule, restated under *Decisions* in the one place it moves.
- **A rank is compared, never read** (`curios_algebra::Rank`): the crate puts atoms in order by it and hashes it, and asks nothing else of it; `curios-algebra` names no term and depends on `curios-num` alone. `Atoms::view_rank` hashes the ranks of a comparison's atoms into one key.
- **A fact a node carries is made of its children's.** The scalars are, in one post-order walk; the free-variable set is, a treap made of the largest carrying child's set with the other children's names put into it, the smaller into the larger (`Sorted::union_with`, [`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nodes-free-variables-are-its-childrens-shared)); a metavariable's spine keeps its facts and its names as it is built, so a node holding a spine reads no entry. The rank is the one fact still made of the whole term.
- **Hashing every subterm modulo renaming is a solved problem.** Maziarz, Ellis, Lawrence, Fitzgibbon and Peyton Jones summarize every subterm of a term modulo α-equivalence in O(n log² n) ([Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.02856), PLDI 2021): a node's summary is its structure, every variable a placeholder, beside a map from each free variable to a hash of the positions it holds; at an application the larger child's map is taken as it stands and each entry of the smaller is joined into it under a tag the structure carries (§4.8), and a map's hash is an aggregate of its entries' so an entry joined moves it alone (§5.2); the total is n log n map operations of log n each (Theorem 6.3), collisions are bounded (Theorem 6.7), and a rewrite recomputes the path to the root alone (§6.3). The paper hashes a free variable by its name; dropping the name from the entry leaves this rank's shape, `f(x, y)` and `f(y, z)` alike and `f(x, x)` apart. Blaauwbroek, Olšák and Geuvers decide a finer, context-sensitive relation in O(n log n) by a pass over one closed term ([PLDI 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02948)), which no fact a node carries can be made of ("Compositionality is not possible"), and McBride's co-de-Bruijn node records which of its context's variables each child uses as a thinning over the whole context ([MSFP 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04085)), of which a map keyed by minted identity is the nameless form that pays for the identities a node holds and not for its scope.
- **The identities a term holds are its free locals, its metavariables with their spines, and the universe and quality metavariables of its levels and sorts.** A node reads its own off its masked spelling (`Visit::masking_levels`, which stands children and levels down, as `Term`'s equality reads a node), a level's metavariables by the offset each stands at.
- **What the rank costs the library**, which the design is weighed against and which retires nothing, since a program pays the square above whatever `/std` pays. ## Decisions

- **A node carries its rank, made of its children's.** A summary per node, filled on first demand for the node and every node beneath it that has none, as the free-variable set is, and held where the rank is held today: the structure, with every identity a placeholder and the index of the child whose map was taken; the map from each identity, by kind and age, to the hash of its positions (`curios-utilities`' `Sorted`); and the sum of those hashes. The largest child's map is taken as it stands and each entry of every other child and of the node's own identities is joined into it under the structure as the paper's `PTJoin` (`Sorted::union_with`); the sum, and not an exclusive or, since two identities of one node can hold one position. A metavariable's node reads its spine's summary, kept beside the spine's facts and names as each entry is pushed and made again where a rewrite reads every entry, so a metavariable's rank reads no entry.
- **A rank is a shape and the identities' positions, by age.** `curios_algebra::Rank` is one key, for a term that holds no identity, or a shape — one hash of the structure and the sum — beside what breaks a tie between two of one shape (`Tie`, a trait object the caller hands over, the node's summary itself): ordered by the key, then by the tie; hashed by the key alone, so two ranks one order holds equal hash alike. Two atoms of one shape are ordered by their maps read in age order to the first entry held apart, an identity before another by its age and one identity by the hash of its positions, so `f(x, y)` and `f(y, x)` are told apart by where `x` stands; no term is read at a comparison, and a comparison costs the entries to the first held apart. A comparison atom's rank is its view's key beside a tie over the view's atoms' ranks in the view's order, where today one hash of the ranks serves.
- **The order is restated where the hash moves.** Two atoms of one shape are ordered by their identities in the order of their ages, each by the positions it holds, where today they are ordered by the ages of their identities in the order a walk first meets them, binders and metavariables before universe and quality metavariables. Both read an identity by comparison; the order moves with the hash, so `/std`'s image moves with it, named at landing. Today's shape also meets a level's universe metavariables in the order of their ids, so two spellings of one level under two renamings rank apart in shape; a level's metavariables stand at their offsets, and the shape is the α-class.
- **Both checkers rank through `Term::rank`**, as they do.
