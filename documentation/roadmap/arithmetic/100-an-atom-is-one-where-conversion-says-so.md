# An atom is one where conversion says so

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves two places where conversion tells apart two atoms it holds one. It is not an implementation plan.

It is held to [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md): no verdict and no solution follows how a term is spelled, the order it holds its operands in, or the arm it stands in.

## What is missing

**An operation that commutes by the peel is no atom of what encloses it.** `Eq()(Nat/and(x, y), Nat/and(y, x))` closes by `Eq/refl()`, and each of these is refused: `Nat/and(x, y) - Nat/and(y, x)` against `0`; `Nat/or(x, y) + Nat/xor(z, w)` against `Nat/xor(w, z) + Nat/or(y, x)`, and the same pair under `*` and under `==`; `Nat/and(Nat/or(x, y), Nat/xor(z, w))` against itself with both operands commuted, in either order; `Nat/and(Nat/and(x, y), Nat/and(y, x))` against `Nat/and(x, y)`; `Nat/eql(x, y) && Nat/eql(y, x)` against `Nat/eql(x, y)`; `Int/and(x, y) - Int/and(y, x)` against `+0`; and `Flt/min(a + b, c * d)` against `Flt/min(b + a, d * c)`. With one operand spelled alike on both sides each holds. The walk that hands out atoms (`Walk::spine`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/classed.rs`) reads through every operation the law table declares commutative and never hands the operation itself out, while its two operand orders are two spellings only the symmetric peel holds one: so a sum, a product, a difference, an idempotence or a second commutative operation over two of them pairs by an identity they do not have, and the verdict follows the order an operand was written in, which [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) rules out. Every operation declared commutative is in it, the float sum, product, `min`, `max` and fused multiply-add among them.

**Two calls of a universe-polymorphic definition under `&&`, `||` or `!=` are never one atom.** With `induct Tree(K: Type): Type | tip(K) | join(Tree(K), Tree(K)) end` and `let even(@K: Type, t: Tree(K)) -> Bool = match t | tip(_) => true | join(l, r) => even(l) && even(r) end;`, the elaborator refuses `let twice(@K: Type, l: Tree(K), held: Holds(even(l) && even(l))) -> Holds(even(l) && even(l)) = held;`, a hypothesis at a goal spelled as its own type: `type mismatch`, `inferred: Holds(even(@K, l) && even.{?u14}(@K, l))`, `expected: Holds(even(@K, l) && even.{?u5}(@K, l))`, `not compared: no operand of one side of a commutative operation is an operand of the other`. It refuses the same at two arguments, under `||`, under `!=` where both operands are such calls, with `K` a plain parameter, and at `Tree(Nat)`, where no level is the declaration's; it accepts one call beside a variable, the two calls under `+` and under `Nat/min`, the conjunction written as a nested `match`, as `/std/List/ordered` writes its own, and the whole over a type that takes no parameter. Each written call takes a level metavariable of its own, bounded from below alone and open until its declaration closes. The readers of `Bool` key an atom on its term as written, its universe instances with it: the truth table's own table (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/truth.rs`), the connective peel and the symmetric peel (`peel_commutative`, `curios-core/src/spine.rs`); the readers of `Nat` and `Int` key theirs up to universe instances (`Atoms::numeric`, `curios-core/src/atoms.rs`), which is why the sum is held. What is left is the classing, which is never asked: `may_be_one` (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/classed.rs`) reads two applications as possibly one only where their heads are one term, universe instances included, so `classable` answers no, the pair's atoms are not handed back and `convert_intrinsics` (`curios-analysis/src/conversion.rs`) ends `Outcome::Unpaired`. Asked, the elaborator's question would still answer no: it joins no two levels (`same_uncommitted`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), and these are a recursive member's, which reproduces them under every unfolding, so no value decides the pair past them.

## Refinement

- **A commutative operation as an atom.** Hand a read-through operation no fold spells one way out as an atom as well, once its operands are walked, so `Classes::of` asks its checker about two of one kind; count over `/std` what the extra questions cost before it lands. Check: the rows above in `ATOMS_HELD` of `tests::laws::written`, each checker asked alone, and an audit pass holding every generated law at a commuted spelling of a compound operand.
- **Two calls apart only in their universe instances.** A decision first, with `may_be_one` reading two heads one up to their universe instances under every choice but the first, among: the numeric carriers' licence given to `Bool`, an atom of it one term up to universe instances in the three readers, on the ground `atoms.rs` states for a number, that Core offers no elimination from a type or a level into one; a classing that answers through a level and commits it where the classed reading decides the pair, the elaborator's alone; the pair parked while a level metavariable stands in it; or a vector for a definition that answers a value, as one that answers a type has, so that the pair is a weak equation. Check: the program as a row of `curios`'s `tests::laws::written` at `&&`, at `||` and at `!=`, each checker asked alone, with the accepted spellings as its controls. The first choice is the trusted base's and small, and each of the others is a design.
