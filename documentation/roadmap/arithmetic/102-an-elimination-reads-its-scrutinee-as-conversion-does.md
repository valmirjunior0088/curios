# An elimination reads its scrutinee as conversion does

[The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) takes Coq Modulo Theory's premise, the carriers' laws decided in conversion; [Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../../design/soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md) holds that relation to congruence and transitivity; and [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) holds that no verdict follows how a term is spelled. Conversion is a congruence, so a match on a scrutinee that converts with a literal or a successor converts with that case's arm, and reduction has to reach the arm for the two to agree: Coq Modulo Theory takes an elimination modulo its theory for that reason. Here an elimination fires on what the fold leaves, so a match takes no arm on a scrutinee conversion holds equal to a literal or a successor unless the fold reaches it: a `Bool` match on a contradiction, a `Nat` match on a product of two sums.

**Ready**: the design is settled under *Design*, and stage 1 is the next step.

## What is wrong

Probed at `b5a6d9df5` with the release compiler. Each claim below is refused by `Eq/refl()`, and where the elaborator is made to accept it alone, by the kernel:

- **A `Bool` scrutinee conversion decides.** Against `2`, `match s | true => 1 | false => 2 end` at `s` each of `b && Bool/not(b)`, `b && Bool/xor(b, true)`, `(b && c) && Bool/not(b)`, `(a0 || a1) && Bool/not(a0) && Bool/not(a1)` and `(x < y) && (y <= x)`, each of which converts with `false`; and at `g(n, @p) && Bool/not(g(n, @q))`, `g` taking a proof of `0 < n`, which converts with `false` once conversion classes its two calls as one. `Eq()(match b && Bool/not(b) | … end, match Bool/not(b) && b | … end)` is refused where its scrutinees convert, the second `match` reducing and the first stuck.
- **A type that is such a match.** A binder `f: match b && Bool/not(b) | true => Nat | false => (Nat) -> Nat end` is refused at `f(1)`, "applied a non-function". `/sys`'s `Holds` is a `Bool` match, so `Holds(b && Bool/not(b))` is no `False` a `match h end` takes apart, and `Holds(b || Bool/not(b))` no `True` that `True/qed()` proves. Under the true arm of a match on `x < y`, `match c || (x < y) | true => 1 | false => 2 end` is not `1`.
- **A `Nat` scrutinee conversion decides.** `Eq()(match (a + b + 1) * (c + d + 1) | 0 => 10 | _ + 1 => 20 end, 20)` is refused, where `(a + b + 1) * (c + d + 1)` converts with its distribution, a successor.

Both checkers take an arm off the forced scrutinee alone (`reduce_match`, `curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`; `step_match`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`). The `&&` and `||` fold reads its right operand as written under a stuck left (`reduce_bool_binary`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/scalar.rs`), and every law across connectives is decided only where conversion compares (`curios-analysis`'s `connectives_agree`). The `Nat` fold leaves a product of two symbolic sums stuck, and only a comparison and a converter distribute it (`Nat::normalize`, `curios-core/src/nat.rs`), which [A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md) argues loses nothing definitional by naming those two askers and no elimination. `then_laws`' documentation (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/laws.rs`) says a law missed on an unreduced operand "is a neutral the next demand reduces"; the next demand reduces it to the same neutral.

`tests::laws`' seeds state iota at each elimination over a scrutinee that is a value already, so no audit asks an elimination about a scrutinee the theory decides. And the elaborator's procedure that proves a bound writes `Bool/holds_of_eq` over `Eq/refl()` for a decision conversion equates with `true` and reduction does not (`entailment::tautology`, `curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`): that is `Holds` of a connective conversion decides, which an elimination that reads its scrutinee reduces to `True` before the procedure is asked.

## What is known

- **Why the folds stop where they do.** A connective's right operand stays as written because reducing it made weak-head reduction of a web of predicate definitions its full normalization (`reduce_bool_binary`), and a fold that decided a connective's tree would pay the tree's diagram at every level of it; a product of two symbolic sums stays stuck because distributing it is the normal form's one quadratic step ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)). An elimination asks for one value once, where it is taken apart.
- **Why conversion alone does not answer it.** [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) rejects completing a fold or a lookup only where conversion compares: "A scrutinee inside reduction would still be decided by spelling."
- **The algebra already answers what an elimination asks.** A `Bool` connective is what the decision diagram reads (`decide_bool`), every leaf forced by the asking reduction and every node read once, and a pair it does not decide hands back the atoms a checker may class (`connectives_agree`, `connectives_agree_classed`). Against a literal, the diagram of the connective alone answers: a literal adds no atom, so the connective is `false` or `true` at every assignment exactly where its own diagram is that constant, and one read answers both. A `Nat` holding a product of two sums is what `Nat::normalize` distributes, gated by `Nat::has_stuck_product`. A `Bool` match and a structural `Nat` match are the only eliminations of a carrier conversion decides more of than the fold: every `Bool` intrinsic produces a `Bool`, `/sys`'s `Holds` is a match, a literal switch reads a literal no distribution of a symbolic product makes, and `Bytes` and `List` already peel their front in the fold.
- **The theory's decision is reduction's, the checker's classing a judgment's.** An equation's answer is split so already: plain reduction takes what the readers decide and a judgment's reduction adds what its conversion classes; the stuck fold taken again is a judgment's alone because it is classing and nothing else. The diagram and the distribution ask no checker, and the comparison fold already distributes a product in both reductions.
- **An arm no value reaches can read a scrutinee two ways.** Under an equation whose key the theory holds `false`, a judgment's reduction answers a scrutinee the readers do not hold equal to the key by its conversion, `true`, where plain reduction reads the theory's `false`; plain conversion asked there already reads it so. Neither leaves the arm, which no value reaches.
- **A prototype answers it** (`710e1c46f`, a scratch copy): at a match whose forced scrutinee is a `Bool` connective naming a local or a `Nat` holding a product of two sums, both checkers, both reductions, ask the algebra — the diagram against `false` and `true`, the distribution — and a judgment's reduction classes the atoms the diagram hands back once for both literals. Every probe above closes in both checkers; `match b && c` and `match (a + 1) * (a + 1) | 0 => 10 | _ + 1 => 20 end` against `10` stay refused; with the rule in the elaborator alone the kernel refuses. Counted over `/std`: its elaboration meets 954 such `Bool` matches and 30 such `Nat` matches and decides one, taking 60,937,834 units where it took 60,570,292 (+0.61%) and retiring 212.83 billion instructions where it retired 211.15 billion (+0.80%), interleaved, twice each; its certification takes 21,754,116 units where it took 21,646,229 (+0.50%). The distribution costs 30 of those units, and the prototype reads a scrutinee up to four times where one read serves the diagram, both literals and the atoms. `/std`'s image moves in `/std/http/Response/empty` alone, whose `Response` invariant the elaborator now proves by `True/qed()` where it wrote `Bool/holds_of_eq` over `Eq/refl()`: `Status/carries_content(…) || Bytes/len(x[]) == 0` stands under `Holds` and is `true` once its right leaf, which the fold left as written, is forced. Asking the diagram alone leaves the classed probe refused; asking the checker's whole conversion closes it and costs `/std`'s elaboration 1.04% more instructions, against 0.68% for the diagram with its atoms classed in a judgment's reduction.
- **Prior art.** Coq Modulo Theory's `ι_T` fires an eliminator where "C(ū) simplifies t": `t` is not constructor-headed and `t ↔*_T C(ū)`, decided by the theory's own procedure, with strong normalization and confluence of `βι_T` proved (Jouannaud and Strub, [*Coq without Type Casts*](https://easychair.org/publications/paper/BKQ), LPAR-21, 2017; Blanqui, Jouannaud and Strub, [*Building Decision Procedures in the Calculus of Inductive Constructions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1266), CSL 2007), the metatheory [The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) rests on; it needs the constructors free modulo the theory, which a diagram that answers agreement alone keeps. Lean's kernel computes literals natively and unfolds symbolic arithmetic by its recursive definitions, so a successor surfaces by computation and a connective's contradiction stays stuck under a match as it stays unconvertible ([Natural numbers](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/4.23.0/Basic-Types/Natural-Numbers)): reduction and conversion agree because conversion decides nothing past unfolding. Rewriting type theories reach a scrutinee one declared law at a time, under a confluence check (Cockx, Tabareau and Winterhalter, [*The Taming of the Rew*](https://research.tudelft.nl/en/publications/the-taming-of-the-rew-a-type-theory-with-computational-assumption), POPL 2021), the per-law route the decisions reject.

## Design

- **Where.** At the dispatch of a match, in both checkers, on the scrutinee as reduction forced it — after its own equations and its stuck fold were asked by the reduction that took it — where the dispatch would leave it stuck.
- **What the theory decides, in both reductions.** A `Bool` scrutinee that is a connective is read once into a diagram (`curios-algebra`'s `Formula::valuation`, `curios-core`'s `bool_value`), and the match takes the arm of the literal it is at every assignment of its atoms; a structural `Nat` scrutinee with no floor and a product of two symbolic sums is distributed, and the match takes the successor arm where the distribution has a floor. One function both reducers call, beside `another_case` in `curios-analysis` (`read_scrutinee`), answers the value the elimination reads, nothing, or the atoms the diagram could not tell apart; where no two of those may be one, the readers class the connective's nodes and read it again, as `connectives_agree` does. A value is answered only where the match then takes an arm, so no stuck form is respelled, and never a disequality: a diagram that is no constant says nothing. Like a fold, it is gated by the scrutinee's shape alone: a scrutinee that names no local is closed, and the closed machine reduces it to its value.
- **What the checker decides, in a judgment's reduction alone.** The atoms handed back, classed once by the checker's own conversion (`asked_classes`, as at a stuck fold) and read again (`read_scrutinee_classed`), under a stuck fold's two conditions: a scrutinee naming a local, and never by plain reduction. It is the third point a judgment's reduction asks its checker, and plain reduction takes the theory's answers alone, as it takes an equation's.
- **What it charges.** The diagram's steps (`Cost::decision`), the distribution (`product_bound`) and a classed spelling's terms, each charged before it is taken as the converters' asks charge them, and the classing questions the checker's conversion asks; the stuck match's reduct is remembered as any reduct is.
- **The folds' laws stand.** They decide the common term at no diagram's or distribution's cost; a term's own weak-head form still follows its spelling, which only an elimination and conversion read, and both read it as conversion does.
- **What it retires.** With every `Holds` of a connective conversion holds `true` reducing to `True`, the fill (`trivially_inhabited`) proves it before the procedure is asked, and `entailment::tautology` with its two names in the registry has nothing left to prove.

## Stages

1. **An elimination reads its scrutinee as the algebra decides it.** `Formula::valuation`, `bool_value`, `read_scrutinee` and both reducers asking it in both reductions. [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) states the rule; [A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md) names the elimination beside the comparison and the converter where a product is distributed and where the diagram is asked, and restates its rejection of reducing a tautology to `true`; [The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) names valuing a formula among `curios-algebra`'s parts; `then_laws`' and `reduce_bool_binary`'s documentation follow. Check: the seeds of iota modulo the theory — a contradiction, a tautology, comparisons by what they state and a product of two sums held, a connective the theory does not decide and a product with no floor missed — in `tests::laws::structural`, asked of each checker alone and through every audit and context; the type and `Holds` witnesses in `tests::algebra`; a test in each checker, by one name, that plain reduction takes the theory's answer; `curios-analysis/tests/driven.rs` for the shared function; `/std`'s image compared at one path and what moved named.
2. **A judgment's reduction classes the scrutinee's atoms.** The classing at the dispatch; the decision's "There are two reductions" names three points, and the kernel's `refolded`, both checkers' `plainly` and the elaborator's asking and cache documentation follow. Check: the classed seed held by both checkers, and a test in each checker that plain reduction leaves it stuck.
3. **The audit holds every law under its carrier's elimination.** `tests::laws::audit` states each `Bool` law with both sides under a `Bool` match, and each `Nat` law with its first variable the stuck product on the left and its distribution on the right, both under a structural `Nat` match; [The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) names elimination modulo the theory among the conditions its premise needs. Check: the audit holds in both checkers.
4. **The procedure that proves a bound drops its tautology rung.** `entailment::tautology` and the registry's `holds_of_eq` and `refl` go, once the rung is shown to prove nothing over `/std` and the workspace's tests. Check: the bound and entailment tests; `/std`'s image unchanged at one path.
