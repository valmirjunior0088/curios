# An elimination reads its scrutinee as conversion does

[A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) holds that an elimination reads its scrutinee as conversion does, since conversion is a congruence and [Conversion is one relation in both checkers](../../design/soundness/conversion-is-one-relation-in-both-checkers.md) needs it transitive: a match whose scrutinee converts with a literal converts with that literal's arm. Both reductions read a `Bool` connective by the decision diagram and a structural `Nat` by distribution, but conversion decides more of a connective than the diagram does: it classes the atoms a pair holds by the checker's own conversion. So a match on a connective conversion holds equal to a literal only once two of its atoms are one takes no arm.

**Ready**: the design is settled under *Design*, and stage 1 is the next step.

## What is wrong

- **A `Bool` scrutinee whose atoms conversion classes.** `match g(n, @p) && Bool/not(g(n, @q)) | true => 1 | false => 2 end` against `2`, `g` taking a proof of `0 < n`, is refused, where the connective converts with `false` once conversion classes its two calls as one. The elimination reads its scrutinee by `curios-analysis`'s `read_scrutinee`, which keys an atom on its spelling as the diagram does and hands two atoms that may be one back for a checker to class, and neither reducer classes them (`scrutinee_read`, `curios-elab/src/reduce.rs` and `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`).
- **No law is held to an elimination.** `tests::laws::structural`'s seeds state iota modulo the theory at a handful of scrutinees, and `tests::laws::audit` holds every declared law reversed, chained, at compound terms and through a solved metavariable, but states none with its sides under the elimination of its carrier. [The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) names the conditions Coq Modulo Theory's premise needs and leaves elimination modulo the theory out.
- **The procedure that proves a bound keeps a rung nothing reaches.** `entailment::tautology` (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`) writes `Bool/holds_of_eq` over `Eq/refl()` for a decision conversion equates with `true` and reduction does not. `Holds` is a `Bool` match, so the fill (`trivially_inhabited`) reduces `Holds` of such a decision to `True` and proves it by `True/qed()` before the procedure is asked, as it now proves `/std/http/Response/empty`'s invariant; once a judgment's reduction classes a scrutinee's atoms, no decision is left for the rung.

## What is known

- **The theory's decision is reduction's, the checker's classing a judgment's.** An equation's answer is split so already: plain reduction takes what the readers decide and a judgment's reduction adds what its conversion classes; the stuck fold taken again is a judgment's alone because it is classing and nothing else.
- **An arm no value reaches can read a scrutinee two ways.** Under an equation whose key the theory holds `false`, a judgment's reduction answers a scrutinee the readers do not hold equal to the key by its conversion, `true`, where plain reduction reads the theory's `false`; plain conversion asked there already reads it so. Neither leaves the arm, which no value reaches.
- **A prototype answers it** (`710e1c46f`, a scratch copy): a judgment's reduction classing the atoms the diagram hands back, once for both literals, closes the classed probe in both checkers and leaves it stuck to plain reduction; with the rule in the elaborator alone the kernel refuses. Counted over `/std`, the whole of the rule takes its elaboration 60,937,834 units where it took 60,570,292 (+0.61%) and 212.83 billion instructions where it took 211.15 billion (+0.80%), interleaved, twice each, and its certification 21,754,116 units where it took 21,646,229 (+0.50%). Asking the checker's whole conversion at the scrutinee closes the classed probe as well, at 1.04% more instructions against 0.68% for the diagram with its atoms classed.
- **Prior art.** Coq Modulo Theory's `ι_T` fires an eliminator where "C(ū) simplifies t": `t` is not constructor-headed and `t ↔*_T C(ū)`, decided by the theory's own procedure (Jouannaud and Strub, [*Coq without Type Casts*](https://easychair.org/publications/paper/BKQ), LPAR-21, 2017), the metatheory [The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) rests on.

## Design

- **What the checker decides, in a judgment's reduction alone.** The atoms `read_scrutinee` hands back, classed once by the checker's own conversion (`asked_classes`, as at a stuck fold) and read again (`read_scrutinee_classed`), under a stuck fold's two conditions: a scrutinee naming a local, and never by plain reduction. It is the third point a judgment's reduction asks its checker, and plain reduction takes the theory's answers alone, as it takes an equation's.
- **What it charges.** A classed spelling's terms, charged as the converters' asks charge them, and the classing questions the checker's conversion asks; the stuck match's reduct is remembered as any reduct is.
- **What the audit asks.** Each `Bool` law with both sides under a `Bool` match, and each `Nat` law with its first variable a product the fold leaves standing on the left and that product distributed on the right, both under a structural `Nat` match: a side the theory reads as a value reaches its arm only where the elimination reads it so.
- **What it retires.** `entailment::tautology` with the registry's `holds_of_eq` and `refl`, which nothing else reads.

## Stages

1. **A judgment's reduction classes the scrutinee's atoms.** The classing at the dispatch; the decision's "There are two reductions" names three points, and the kernel's `refolded`, both checkers' `plainly` and the elaborator's asking and cache documentation follow. Check: the classed seed held by both checkers, and a test in each checker that plain reduction leaves it stuck.
2. **The audit holds every law under its carrier's elimination.** `tests::laws::audit` states the laws as *Design* says; [The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../../design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md) names elimination modulo the theory among the conditions its premise needs. Check: the audit holds in both checkers.
3. **The procedure that proves a bound drops its tautology rung.** `entailment::tautology` and the registry's `holds_of_eq` and `refl` go, once the rung is shown to prove nothing over `/std` and the workspace's tests. Check: the bound and entailment tests; `/std`'s image unchanged at one path.
