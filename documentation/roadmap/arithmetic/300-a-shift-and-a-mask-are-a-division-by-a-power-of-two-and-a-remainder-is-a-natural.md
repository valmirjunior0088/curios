# A shift and a mask are a division by a power of two, and a remainder is a natural

**Refined.** The logic reads a right shift, a mask and a remainder on both carriers through one statement: a right shift by `k` and a mask of `k` low bits are the quotient and the remainder by `2ᵏ`, and a division has one convention, the Euclidean one, whose remainder is a natural. The next step is stage 1, a delivery.

## What is missing

**A mask is read by no proof.** Conversion and [the elaborator's procedure](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) read a right shift as the quotient by `2ᵏ` it is, through `curios-core`'s view of a division ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)), and a mask, `Nat/and(x, 15)`, as nothing beside the remainder `x % 16` it is: the procedure reads it as a free atom, and conversion holds `16 * Nat/shr(x, 4) + Nat/and(x, 15)` apart from `x` where it holds `16 * Nat/shr(x, 4) + x % 16` one with it.

**Its consumers work around it.** `/std/Map/digits` proves a byte its two nibbles — `x == 16 * Nat/shr(x, 4) + Nat/and(x, 15)`, each below sixteen — by evaluating that at all 256 bytes (`table`, through `Nat/all_below`), which a byte's range allows and nothing past it does, and its `higher`, `lower` and `joined` read the table's facts; a program holding that table checks in 449 ms [369, 468] against 240 ms [232, 254] holding one case, timed at `0476b6d8b`, seven interleaved readings of `curios wonder -` over each, median and span. `/std/Char/to_utf8` builds each byte with `/` and `%` by a power of two "under the bound it is proved to satisfy, and none is masked". [The binary64 conversion proofs](304-binary64-conversion-proofs.md) state what `/std/Flt/exact`'s `round_in` keeps, `Nat/shr(m, s)`, and drops, `m - Nat/shl(kept, s)`.

**`Int`'s right shift and remainder are unprovable, not only undecided.** `Int`'s `/` and `%` truncate toward zero and `Int/shr` floors, so `Int`'s shift is no division of its own carrier and its remainder takes the dividend's sign. Conversion holds of `%` Euclid's identity and an exact multiple, and of `Int/shr` its zero laws and count split; neither `Nat/to_int(m) % +3` nor `Int/shr(Nat/to_int(m), 2)` reduces through the widening, nor `(+0 - i) % +3` through the negation, so a lemma by `Int/view` meets a stuck term in each arm, and `i % 3 < +3`, the law grid's refused candidate, has no proof at all. `/std` floors where it divides an `Int`: `/std/time` and `/std/Io` divide through `Int/DivMod/of`, a certified floor written over `Int/view` and `Nat`'s division, at eight sites, and `/std/Int/shl/unique` states its odd mantissa on `Int/abs(m) % 2` for want of `m % 2`; the readers that truncate are `/std/time/Duration`'s `to_secs` and `to_millis`, whose docs say they round toward zero, and `/std/tests/big_num`'s test of the sign a quotient and a remainder take.

**An `Int` division by a power of two is emitted as a division.** `curios-emit` lowers a `Nat` division and remainder by a literal power of two as the shift and the mask they are ([`curios-emit`'s README](../../../curios-emit/README.md#a-division-by-a-power-of-two-is-a-shift-and-a-mask)), and `Int`'s to `i32.div_s` and `i32.rem_s` whatever the divisor, since `Int`'s division truncates where `Int/shr` floors.

## Decided

- **A mask is the remainder by a power of two, read through the view a shift is.** `curios-core`'s view of a division reads a literal mask `and(x, 2ᵏ - 1)`, in either operand order, as the remainder of `x` by `2ᵏ`, and every reader of a division takes it there and through no rule of its own: the folds rebuild what is left as a mask of its width; Euclid's recombination pairs a remainder in either spelling with a quotient's multiple in any, so `16 * shr(x, 4) + and(x, 15)` is `x`; and the procedure lifts a mask out of the facts as it lifts a remainder.
- **Every reader takes a spelling at once.** A rule that reads a shift or a mask as a division lands in conversion and in the procedure together: where conversion recombines what the procedure reads apart, a fact relating a value to its parts reduces to a tautology and the procedure loses the tie it proved from.
- **The emitter reads the same view backwards.** A division and a remainder by a literal power of two are emitted as a shift and a mask, as `Nat`'s are, so a program never picks a spelling for its speed against the one a proof reads.
- **`Int` divides Euclidean, and its remainder is a natural.** `i / d` is the quotient whose remainder lies in `[0, |d|)`, and `i % d` is the widening of that natural remainder, built so by its `/sys` row from the moment it enters Core, as `gt` and `ge` are built as their mirrors: its bound is `Nat`'s, read through the widening by the comparison's preimages. `Int/shr(i, k)` is then the quotient `i / pow(2, k)` at every `i`, a floor being Euclid's quotient by a positive divisor, so the view reads `Int`'s shift and mask as `Nat`'s; the floor law and the Euclidean split hold at `Int` and are its families, where the law decision holds them controls; `Nat/to_int` commutes with `/`, `%` and `shr`; and `Int/DivMod` is `/` and `%` themselves. Lean changed `Int`'s `/` and `%` to this convention after starting from C's, and SMT-LIB, Dafny, Agda — whose `%` answers a `ℕ` below `∣ d ∣` — and Why3's `EuclideanDivision` state it, as Boute (*The Euclidean definition of the functions div and mod*, TOPLAS 1992) and Leijen (*Division and Modulus for Computer Scientists*, 2001) argue it.

**Rejected.**

- **A mask's folds beside the division's.** A prototype at `0476b6d8b` wrote them as second rules beside the division's: it held every row, and is two statements of when a remainder folds, which the view makes one.
- **`Int`'s truncation kept, with a law for each half.** `Int/abs(i % d)` is `Int/abs(i) % Int/abs(d)` at a literal divisor and the remainder's sign is the dividend's, and `Int/shr` reaches `Nat/shr` through the sign view in two folds: `Int`'s shift and division stay two conventions, every `Int` lemma about either goes through `Int/view`, and `/std` keeps a second floor of its own in `Int/DivMod`.
- **A total division**, `x / 0 = 0` as Lean's is: a division here carries its divisor's precondition (`/std/ops/Div`'s `Ok`), which a total one would drop for every carrier to buy a law for one.

## Stages

1. **A mask is read through the view.** The same readers take `and(x, 2ᵏ - 1)` as the remainder by `2ᵏ`, conversion and the procedure in one commit: on a prototype at `0476b6d8b`, the mask's recombination in conversion alone refused `/std/Map/digits`'s `joined` and `lower`, whose facts from the table, `16 * high(x) + low(x) <= x` and its converse, conversion then reads as `x <= x`, leaving the procedure no tie between a byte and its nibbles until it reads the mask as a remainder it lifts. Check: rows of the mask's fold and of its recombination with a shift and with a quotient, in either operand order, beside the controls of a mask that is no power less one and of another width; `/std` building, its image moving only in the proofs over a mask the procedure writes — on the prototype, `higher`, `joined` and `lower` alone of those 261 declarations, printing in 362, 292 and 265 lines where they printed in 462, 505 and 365. One commit.
2. **The Map's nibbles without the table.** `/std/Map/digits`'s `whole`, `nibbled`, `higher`, `lower` and `joined` proved from the laws, `table`, `split` and `nibbles` deleted, `high` and `low` spelled as they are. Check: the Map's tests; `/std`'s check time beside stage 1's. One commit.
3. **`Int` divides Euclidean.** `curios-num`'s `Integer::div` and `rem`, and with them every constant folder sharing `scalar`; `curios-emit`'s `Int` fast paths and the fix-up after the truncating `big/div` and `big/rem`; the `/sys` rows' docs; `/std/time/Duration`'s two readers writing their rounding toward zero, and `/std/tests/big_num`'s sign test restated. Check: a value grid of the two halves at both signs of both operands against `curios-num`, through the folders, the fast path and the helpers; `curios-emit`'s `Int` tests; every program of `programs/` answering as before. One commit.
4. **`Int`'s remainder is a natural.** The `/sys` row builds `i % d` as the widening of the natural remainder, Euclid's recombination pairs it with `Int`'s quotient's multiple, the floor law and the split fold at `Int`, and the procedure reads an `Int` quotient's bounds; the law decision's `Int` bullet restated. Check: in `tests::laws::written` (*Int against Nat*), `i % 3 < +3` and `+0 <= i % 3` move from refused to held, beside `(i * 2 + 1) % 2 = 1`, `(i + 2) / 2 = 1 + i / 2` and `(2 * i + 1) / 2 = i`, which stop being controls; `tests::bounds` rows of an `Int` quotient's bounds; the audit at the new rows. One commit.
5. **`Int`'s shift and mask are read through the view.** `Int/shr(i, k)` as `i / pow(2, k)` and `Int/and(i, 2ᵏ - 1)` as `i % 2ᵏ`, in conversion and the procedure together; `curios-emit` lowers an `Int` division and remainder by a literal power of two as it lowers `Nat`'s. Check: rows of the shift's fold and recombination below zero, `Int/shr(i, n) <= i` kept a control, and `Nat/to_int` commuting with `shr`. One commit.
6. **`/std` reads the convention.** `Int/DivMod` deleted and its eight callers dividing with `/` and `%`; `/std/Int/shl/unique` stated on `m % 2`. Check: `/std`'s tests, `/std/time`'s among them. One commit.
