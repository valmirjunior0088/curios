# A stuck term is handed back as written

[A solution is the rigid side as written](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-solution-is-the-rigid-side-as-written) solves a metavariable to the term the problem writes, and `the_seventh_part_of_the_library_reads_back` fails because one is solved to a reduct: reduction unfolds a call whose unfolding is stuck, and a stuck node is rebuilt over that unfolding, so a pair derived from the node has no written side left to solve to. A recursive member's call already survives reduction folded where its unfolding sticks again (`force_rec`, `restuck`); a non-recursive definition's call is unfolded into the stuck `match` its body is, at the root and wherever a fold forces an operand, so what reduction hands back follows whether a definition names itself. This specification makes the call the weak-head form of every definition whose unfolding is stuck, in both reducers, at the root and at every operand alike, so every pair a stuck term is taken apart into holds the calls the problem wrote.

## What is wrong today

- **A call whose unfolding is stuck is unfolded.** The elaborator's `reduce_apply` and `reduce_instance` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`) and the certifier's `step_apply` and `step_instance` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/whnf.rs`) unfold a global definition's call and keep its unfolding however it ends, where `force_rec` and the certifier's `force` keep a recursive member's call folded when its unfolding sticks again. A fold forces each operand through `Reducer::reduce_forced` and rebuilds a stuck node over what that reached. Over `pub let pick(@a: Nat, h: Bool/Holds(a != 3)) -> Nat = a;`, `pick(h)` with `h: Bool/Holds(f(k) != 3)` elaborates to `pick(@#{goal = #NatType} match k | 0 => 7 | n + 1 => n end, h)` (probed at `a7e7e558f`): `a != 3` reaches Core as the intrinsic `xor` of `Eql(Nat).eql(a, 3)` with `true`, the method unfolds to the intrinsic `==`, and its fold forces `f(k)` to the `match` `f`'s body is.
- **The read-back it breaks.** `bcd72c6c7` left two `Eq/subst` endpoints of `/std/Map/internal/Node/wedge_answers` to inference, and one is solved to `digit(k, c)` unfolded through `at_place` to its `match`, which names `/std/Map/digits`'s private `high` and `low`, so the read-back declines that `let`, rightly, since a reader at `Node` writes neither. The endpoint is a root reduct: a prototype keeping the call at every operand a fold reads still solves it so.

## Context

- **What a term may be spelled as is what the readers read.** The carriers' readers know a layer by its structure and an atom by its spelling (`same_atom`, `Atoms::handle`), and the reduct is the one spelling that serves both, so a spelling handed back as written must be the one reduction itself gives. Prototyped in a scratch tree at `a7e7e558f`, by the refusals of `/std`: the whole stuck node handed back as written, 142, a fold above it reading no layer through a written call (`Nat/or(to_nat(a), to_nat(b)) < 256` loses its bound); every operand that reaches a stuck form kept as written, 107, atoms losing their identity (a motive's β-redex, proofs the bound prover found and the checkers refused); every call kept, 12, a call against its own unfolding spelled as another call or a projection (`length(s)` and `count_scalars(…)`, `Duration/to_nanos(a)` and `a.nanos`); a call kept where its unfolding ends on a stuck `match`, 2; and a fold that collapses to a kept call reducing on, 1, `/std/Set/has`'s `either` holding `is_some(x)` kept inside `||` and unfolded at the root. With that lemma split by cases, the last elaborates `pick(h)` to `pick(@/f(k), h)` and holds `tests::laws`, `tests::respelling`, `tests::inference` and `tests::runaways`, and the seventh part still declines `wedge_answers`.
- **The root and the operand are one rule.** [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) rejects an operand's reduct that "would then follow whether it was reached as an operand or at the root"; the prototypes' last refusals are that asymmetry for a call's spelling. A fold reads its operands through `reduce_forced`, which is `force_rec`, so a call kept by `force_rec`'s test is kept at every operand with no seam of the folds' own.
- **Conversion already reads the two kinds of call alike.** `Convert::compare_same_global_apply` decides two calls of one definition by their spines "so what a program's conversion accepts cannot depend on whether a definition happened to name itself", and a recursive member's folded call is unfolded where its shape is demanded or meets a differently-shaped term (`unfold_rec_apply`), in both checkers.
- **Peers.** Lean keeps a call of a definition by pattern matching folded where its `match` does not reduce (smart unfolding, [`Lean.Meta.WHNF`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Meta/WHNF.html)); it does so in the elaborator alone, and its kernel and elaborator disagree where one unfolds and the other does not ([Zulip](https://leanprover-community.github.io/archive/stream/270676-lean4/topic/Meta.2EisDefEq.20vs.2E.20Kernel.2Ewhnf.html)), so here both reducers take the rule. smalltt keeps a call's unfolding beside it and quotes the call ([glued evaluation](https://github.com/AndrasKovacs/smalltt)), two values where Curios has one term. Coq's `cbn` refolds a constant it unfolded and Agda's `DISPLAY` pragma prints a reduct as a name, each reading a reduct back to the name it came from, which the solution decision rejects.

## Goal

A call of a definition whose unfolding ends on a stuck `match` is its own weak-head form, in both reducers, at the root and at every operand a fold reads; conversion unfolds it where it meets another form, as it unfolds a recursive member's folded call. Every pair a stuck term is taken apart into then holds the calls the problem wrote, and a solution, a report's side and a refinement's key spell a definition by its name where reduction reached no head through it.

## Decided

- **The rule is the recursive member's, for every definition.** The call kept is the one reduction was asked of, as `force_rec` keeps the call it was handed: kept where its weak-head form, reached through every call its unfolding makes, is a stuck `match`, and unfolded where that form is a head — a constructor, a literal, a type former, a function, an intrinsic a fold reads through — a projection, or a call reduction leaves standing, a recursive member's folded call. Lean keeps the call of the innermost definition whose `match` is stuck and unfolds one with no match of its own; here that call is `at_place(k, c / 4, c % 4)` for `digit(k, c)`, a helper `/std/Map/digits` keeps private, so the call kept is the outermost, the one the problem wrote.
- **Layers keep their one spelling.** The folds' merging and cancelling and the spellings [A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md) lists stay as they are: the rule changes what an atom is spelled as and nothing a layer is, and a respelled layer names nothing its own atoms do not.
- **Both reducers change together**, each stage with a case put to both checkers.
- **Rejected: a stuck node handed back whole as written; every operand that reaches a stuck form kept as written; every call kept.** Each refused `/std` as Context counts, the first because the folds read their layers off the terms, the other two because the readers know an atom by its spelling.
- **Rejected: every carrier's laws decided at comparison and never written back, as `Bool`'s are.** The folds read their layers off the terms, so it needs readers that know an atom and a layer through a reducer, and no read-back turns on a layer.
- **Rejected: a written subterm looked up by its reduct when a solution is committed.** It reads a reduct back to a spelling, which the solution decision's own **Rejected** paragraph turns down.

## Open questions

- Which consumers of a weak-head form must force a kept call to see the `match` it unfolds to: postponement's read of what a stuck form waits on (`blocked_on_unsolved_metavar`), two stuck matches decomposed, a case refinement's key and probe, the inverter, the classification, the closed machine's gate.
- What the rule costs: `/std`'s build time against the record it moves, and the runaway fixtures' budgets (`curios`'s `tests::runaways`).

The suites, the laws grid and its audit, the respelling grid, `/std` and every ticket of the soundness board, under both checkers; the runaway fixtures within the budgets they state, any moved one restated with its reason.

## Retirement

[A solution is the rigid side as written](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-solution-is-the-rigid-side-as-written) restated where its fallback to a reduct no longer arises; [A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../../design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)'s rationale that a fold may not respell restated to say what a fold respells is a layer and never an atom; the rule stated where a weak-head form is owned; then this specification is deleted.
