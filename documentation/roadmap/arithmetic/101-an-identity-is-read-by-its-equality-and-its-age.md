# An identity is read by its equality and its age

**Not refined yet.** [`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts) decides that freshness is the one property a judgment reads off an identity and that a memo changes only resource verdicts, and [A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md) that what a declaration compiles to follows nothing but what it reads. The order the carriers' algebra puts atoms in hashes each identity by its value, so which proof the elaborator writes, which spelling it solves an implicit to and which monomial leads a magnitude follow how many identities were minted before, which a display, a memo miss and a probe each move.

## What is missing

An atom's rank is the structural hash of its term (`Atoms::handle`, `curios-core/src/atoms.rs`), and two folds sort by the same hash directly (`int_monomial` and `int_of_summand`, `curios-core/src/int.rs`; `read`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/magnitude.rs`). The hash reads every identity a declaration mints by its value: a binder (`Mint`, `curios-core/src/names.rs`), a metavariable (`MetavarId`), a universe metavariable and a quality metavariable. Everything else that orders identities reads them by comparison only — `Mint`'s `Ord`, every `BTreeSet<Free>` — and `clippy::iter_over_hash_type` keeps hash order out of results, so these four sites are where a counter's value reaches what a checker writes and decides.

- **The elaborator's output follows work it discards.** With the closed machine's memo dropped at the end of each run, `/std/Map/bits/within` proves one bound by another term and `/std/Int/abs/of_mul` solves an implicit to `a + b * a` where it is `a * b + a`; `within`'s proof then reads other lemmas, so the module's order (`Module::ordered`) moves it and nine `Map` declarations to other places, each the same by value. With each mismatch's display left unnormalized, `/std/Nat/Coprime/quotients` solves an implicit to the product with its factors the other way and `/std/Cli/Prepared/collect` solves one to another variable. Counted at `68ca73258`, each declaration compared by value.
- **The kernel keeps its identities by replaying them.** A remembered reduct records the identities its computation minted and replays them on every hit (`Replay`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/spend.rs`), so "every later one lands where it would have"; the table of inferred types gives the replay up because it outgrows the identity space, and from its first hit later identities land elsewhere, which the rank reads, though no verdict is known to have moved. The replay is what [A judgment's identities running out is a refusal](../soundness/100-a-judgments-identities-running-out-is-a-refusal.md) finds running past thirty-two bits.

## Design

- **A rank is a term's shape, then the age of the identities it holds.** The shape is the structural hash with every minted identity hashed alike, cached per node as the hash is. Two atoms of one shape are ordered by the identities they hold, in the order a walk meets them, each compared with the other's by kind and then by age: which was minted first. Age is what a counter that never falls already guarantees, and an identity minted between two others moves neither. For locals standing in one term, the older binder is the one whose scope encloses the other's or comes first in its telescope, since a scope is opened from its enclosing scope's body, so age orders locals by where their binders stand; a metavariable, which has no binder, is ordered the same way.
- **One order, stated once.** One function in `curios-core` states it, the four sites take it, and the identity hash serves hash tables alone. `curios-algebra`'s `Atom` carries the order's key where it carries a `u64`, naming no term.
- **The kernel replays nothing.** With no reader of an identity's value, a hit needs to mint nothing, as a typing's hit already does: `Replay` keeps the reduct alone, the README's two invariants hold by construction, and the known way past thirty-two bits is gone.
- **What it moves.** `/std`'s spellings once, in both checkers; the rationale of the compilation decision that counts identities per declaration for the rank's sake is restated for what still needs it, the stored-identity check.

**Rejected.**

- **A binder ranked by its level stored on its identity**, this specification's first statement: about 170 sites in the two checkers and the shared analyses mint binders and would each have to know their depth, a walk that opens binders it does not register in its checker's scope gives two of them one level, and a metavariable has no level.
- **Each minted identity made neutral where it is wasted** — every cache hit replaying, every display deferred, every probe accounted: a list a later walk forgets ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md) rejects state cleared field by field for the same reason), and the kernel's replay already outgrows the identity space.
- **Atoms ranked by first encounter**, as Mathlib's `AtomM` keeps them ([`Mathlib/Util/AtomM.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Util/AtomM.lean)): a normal form a fold writes would follow the order its atoms were written in, and Mathlib's `ring` looped where its atoms were numbered afresh between passes.
- **A table's identities renumbered densely before it ranks**: every reader made two-phase, its atoms collected before any monomial is built.
- **A rank by hash that reads an identity**, Lean's `Expr.quickLt`, which "first compares the hashcodes" with a free variable hashed as its unique id ([`Lean/Expr.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Expr.lean)): it is this specification's subject. Z3 met it at scale, "AST ids feed heuristic tie-breaking throughout the solver", one instance taking 17 s on one platform and 1633 s on another, and fixed the order ids are assigned in ([Z3Prover/z3#10165](https://github.com/Z3Prover/z3/pull/10165)), which here would be every site's discipline again.

## Stages

1. The order and its four sites, with `Atom`'s key; `/std` moves once. Check: below. Figures it may move: the prelude's elaboration time and allocations, and `curios`'s kernel memo measurements.
2. The kernel's replay removed, with soundness specification 100 restated for what still advances the counter. Check: `kernel_memo_parity`, whose verdicts hold with the memos off by construction, identities minted after a hit no longer landing where a memo-free run's would.
3. The decisions brought to what landed: the kernel README's two invariants, the compilation decision's counter rationale, the `atoms` module's documentation.
