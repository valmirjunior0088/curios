# Arithmetic: findings

- **A bound whose facts' products exhaust the cap is refused there, though the disequality read after them would prove it, or the linear search before them answers where it fails.** `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d` is refused with the bound of 4096 derived rows reached and only `d <= b` read; from `d <= b` alone, or from no fact, where `d = b` falsifies it, the search reaches the same bound rather than the assignment. The goals of that shape that hold — `b - (d + 1) <= b - d`, `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d + 1`, `b - d <= b - (d + 1) + 1` from `d <= b` — are proved, and so are `1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d`, one subtraction, and the entry's goal from `d < b`, in 23 rows. Re-shown on 2026-11-19 at `5b3028ed8`, each search's rows and splits printed by a temporary instrument beside the `entailment::derived` sample (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`): the two subtractions open two splits, on `d + 1 <= b` and on `d <= b`, and the products each case multiplies its facts into (`case_products`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`) hold 21 to 24 facts a case where the linear facts are 8 to 14; the linear search leaves an assignment, the search over the products spends the rest of the cap, and `linear` returns `Exhausted` there, so the disequality, read only where a search over the products left an assignment, is never read, and the linear assignment is never reported. [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) caps the search by the rows it derives, "exhausting it refuses as the reduction budget does", and asks the products as Mathlib's `nlinarith` adds them, only where linear arithmetic leaves an assignment: a stage asked where the facts alone did not settle the bound, whose running out says nothing of it. The fix the user settled on 2026-11-19: each stage — the facts and their splits, their products, the disequalities — searches under a cap of its own, a stage that runs out is passed over and the next reads what the stage before it read, the disequalities over the facts with no product where the products ran out, and the answer is the first certificate, else the assignment of the last stage that searched over no product, else exhaustion where that stage ran out. Fix: the stages in `linear` so, and the decision's cap restated as a stage's.
