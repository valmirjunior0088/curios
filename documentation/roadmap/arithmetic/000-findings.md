# Arithmetic: findings

- **Uncertain: the conversion chain classifies a pair afresh at every level of a congruence descent.** The chain (`curios-analysis/src/conversion.rs`) hands a pair's atoms out (`atoms_of`) and reads the pair classed (`classed`), each by a walk of both sides from nothing, and where that reading leaves the pair to its congruence, each operand pair is classified again from nothing; `answers` (`case_equation.rs`) does the same of a stuck form beside a key. A stuck fold classified the same way until it extended what its operands' folds filed (`Classification`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`), and a side that is a stuck reduct is its own forced form, so its classification is in its reducer's table. Not known: whether any program pays for it. Of eleven samples taken under gdb at `f7375e49e`, seven of the reversed sum of 256 `Nat/and` operations and four of the reversed sum of 256 calls of one head, none stands in a classification walk. Settled by: samples across `/std`'s elaboration (`cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration`), and a probe whose two sides differ under a spine of differences, at two sizes. Fix, where the walks show: the chain takes each side's filed classification where the side is a stuck reduct, and extends it as `atoms_within` and `refold` do. Size: unknown.

- **A counterexample spells the operands of an operation read through its definition by their reducts.** With `width` a fold over `Str/to_list(s)`, `let _: Holds(width(s) - width(t) < w) = True/proved();` is refused with `it fails at width(s) - width(t) = 0, w = 0, List/fold(@Char, @Nat, rec go: …) = …`: the whole body `width(s)` unfolds to, and again for `width(t)`, a screen of text each. A `Nat/min(width(s), width(t))` and a `width(s) / 2`, bound by a `let` or stated in the bound, report the same, where `let x = width(s);` alone reports `x = 0, w = 0`; `/std/Tui/Field/view`'s cursor bound printed so for its two `columns` calls while the terminal library was rebuilt. [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) writes what the facts invent over atoms through the spelling each atom was read from, and `a_counterexample_spells_an_atom_as_the_bound_states_it` (`curios/src/tests/bounds.rs`) holds a call to its name; the readers of an operation's cases and bounds (`Reader::definitions`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`) take its operands as the atoms of its reduct, which carry no spelling for the report (`counterexample`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/report.rs`) to write them through. Fix: an operand an operation's reader introduces keeps the spelling the operation was read from, as an atom read off a statement does. Check: that test, given the subtraction, the `min` and the quotient, finds `width(s)` named in each counterexample and no `List/fold`. Size: one commit.
