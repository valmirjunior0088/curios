# Arithmetic: findings

- **Uncertain: two powers over two spellings of one base do not convert as a product.** [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) holds a node one with another where its own reader says so. Probed at `b5a6d9df5` with the release compiler, `Nat/pow(P, y) * Nat/pow(P, z)` against `Nat/pow(D, y) * Nat/pow(D, z)` is refused by `Eq/refl()` in either order of the right side's factors, where `P` is the stuck product `(a + w + 1) * (w + a + 1)` and `D` its distribution, and so is the exponent law `Nat/pow(P, y + z) = Nat/pow(D, y) * Nat/pow(D, z)`; `Nat/pow(P, y)` against `Nat/pow(D, y)` holds, as do the product with one power times a binder, `g(P) * k(P)` against `g(D) * k(D)`, and two powers over `a + a` and `2 * a`. A power is a node of the classification, held one with another only by its operation's readers, and `P` and `D` are nodes of two operations, a product and a sum, which no reader holds one, where the linear views a comparison reads distribute `P` first; two powers alone meet at the congruence, which compares their bases by conversion. Not known: whether the classification spells a `Nat` node over a stuck product as its distribution, the canonizer the fold stops short of, and what that costs where classifying a stuck fold meets products. Settled by: that spelling built in a scratch copy, the probes above and `tests::laws` held, with `/std`'s image at one path and its units read against the same copy's; where it holds at a cost the decisions accept it is restated as a finding with that fix, and a `Nat` law stated with its first variable `P` on one side and `D` on the other, both under a structural `Nat` match, joins `tests::laws::audit`. Size: unknown.
