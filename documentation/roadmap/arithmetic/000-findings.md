# Arithmetic: findings

- **`proved()` writes a proof of a goal over a lifted remainder with the goal's split nested inside the split a disequality opened on the same comparison, so a case no value reaches is left in it and the proof does not check.** `d < (k + at) % 16` from `d <= (k + at) % 16` and `(k + at) % 16 != d` is refused with "a proof was found and did not check / that is the compiler's fault", and so is the same through a definition `g(x, y) = (x + y) % 16`, or over `x % 16` alone; over a sum, a product, `Nat/max`, a quotient or a recursion in the remainder's place it is proved, as it is from `d + 1 <= (k + at) % 16`, with no disequality to split on. Re-shown on 2026-11-19 at `5b3028ed8`, with the candidate and its check's error printed by a temporary instrument at `check` (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`): the search certifies in 102 rows over one split, the one `Reader::distinct` opens on `r <= d` for the disequality (`curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`), and the candidate is refused inside that split's false arm, in an inner arm the elaborator reports unreachable (`InUnreachableArm`, the guard `d < r` decided true), at `le/add_mono_l`'s proof argument, left pending. A remainder lifted out of the facts leaves their sum over the dividend, so a goal over it is proved in the refuting form, a split on the goal's own decision written in every arm of the certificate's splits (`Case::leaf`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/proof.rs`); under the arm where `r <= d` fails, `d < r` is decided, and the inner split has a case no value reaches. `Reader::open` refuses that nesting for two operations' guards — "a second split opened inside it would have a case no value reaches, and there its guard's fact is not `qed`'s to prove" — and joins them; the goal's split is written past it. A sum, a product or a quotient is never lifted, so its goal is never written in the refuting form, and without a disequality no split encloses the goal's. The user settled on 2026-11-19 on joining the goal's split, replacing the one table of atoms settled on 2026-11-18, which every reader of a bound already shares (`LinearViews` and `Classes`, handed to each). Fix: the refuting form splits on the goal's decision only where no enclosing arm of the certificate answers it (`answered_guard`, as `Reader::open` asks it): an arm deciding the goal true writes `qed`, one deciding it false writes the eliminated sum alone, the negation proved there by `qed`. Check: the three refusals above proved; a split on a comparison apart from the goal's still nested; the entailment suites unchanged. Size: one commit.
- **A bound whose facts' products exhaust the cap is refused there, though the disequality read after them would prove it, or the linear search before them answers where it fails.** `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d` is refused with the bound of 4096 derived rows reached and only `d <= b` read; from `d <= b` alone, or from no fact, where `d = b` falsifies it, the search reaches the same bound rather than the assignment. The goals of that shape that hold — `b - (d + 1) <= b - d`, `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d + 1`, `b - d <= b - (d + 1) + 1` from `d <= b` — are proved, and so are `1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d`, one subtraction, and the entry's goal from `d < b`, in 23 rows. Re-shown on 2026-11-19 at `5b3028ed8`, each search's rows and splits printed by a temporary instrument beside the `entailment::derived` sample (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`): the two subtractions open two splits, on `d + 1 <= b` and on `d <= b`, and the products each case multiplies its facts into (`case_products`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`) hold 21 to 24 facts a case where the linear facts are 8 to 14; the linear search leaves an assignment, the search over the products spends the rest of the cap, and `linear` returns `Exhausted` there, so the disequality, read only where a search over the products left an assignment, is never read, and the linear assignment is never reported. [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) caps the search by the rows it derives, "exhausting it refuses as the reduction budget does", and asks the products as Mathlib's `nlinarith` adds them, only where linear arithmetic leaves an assignment: a stage asked where the facts alone did not settle the bound, whose running out says nothing of it. The fix the user settled on 2026-11-19: each stage — the facts and their splits, their products, the disequalities — searches under a cap of its own, a stage that runs out is passed over and the next reads what the stage before it read, the disequalities over the facts with no product where the products ran out, and the answer is the first certificate, else the assignment of the last stage that searched over no product, else exhaustion where that stage ran out. Fix: the stages in `linear` so, and the decision's cap restated as a stage's. Check: the entry's goal proved from `d <= b` and `b != d`; refused from `d <= b` alone with `d = b` as its assignment; the `entailment::derived` sample's maximum over `/std` unchanged. Size: one commit.
