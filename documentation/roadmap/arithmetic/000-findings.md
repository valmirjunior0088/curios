# Arithmetic: findings

- **Not measured: what `/std`'s elaboration costs in time.** Counted, the budget `/std` consumes — `budget::consumed` summed over the two profiles `cargo xtask workspace lint` files, read before another build of the prelude writes over them — is 21,324,740 in elaboration and 13,021,334 in certification at `9d121c13f`, with reduction asking its checker's conversion ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)). Certifying the unit is timed, 3.5 to 3.6 s on top of `d5046c5cc` (`stored_prelude_measurements`, `curios-prelude-archive/src/restore/tests.rs`); no timed reading of its elaboration is recorded, a timed figure being a median over repeated readings on a quiet machine. Fix: take it from the stream `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` files, and state it in the decision only if it argues for something there. Check: the figure, with its commit. Size: small.
- **A stuck fold puts every two quotients, and every two remainders, under it to conversion.** The walk that hands a fold's atoms out (`atoms_within`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`) hands a quotient or a remainder out as an atom once its dividend and divisor are walked, `may_be_one` reads two stuck operations of one kind as possibly one, and each reducer's `refolded` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) then asks its conversion about every such pair, at every stuck operation over them, each question a comparison of two terms as deep as the atoms are. Over `L(0) = x0`, `L(k) = L(k - 1) % (yk + 1)` and its twin `R` over other variables, `Eq()(match L(n) <= R(n) | true => 0 | false => 0 end, 0)` is refused by `Eq/refl()` in 2.08 s [1.76–2.77] at `n = 12` and 14.55 s [14.16–14.80] at `n = 24`, where the same towers of `Nat/and` take 0.58 s [0.48–0.70] and 1.21 s [0.99–1.25]: timed, the median and span of three runs of `wonder diagnostics -` through the release compiler at `1cdbb0314`, on a machine under load. A backtrace taken under gdb stands in `classing` under `Nat::normalize` each of the two times it was taken. Fix: two quotients, or two remainders, are held one where their dividends and their divisors are, with no question put, the proof each carries being of a proposition those two state; the classification ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)) spells both before it reaches the node, and the rule is the trusted base's, since it rests on every operand `Intrinsic::algebra` leaves out being a proof. Check: the two towers as an ignored measurement, flat in `n`, and a quotient under two proofs of its divisor as a row of `curios`'s `tests::laws::written`. Size: small, made a step at a time under supervision, the rule being the trusted base's, after the campaign on the rank (the compilation and soundness findings).
- **A stuck fold walks the whole operation beneath it, at every operation of a long sum.** Each reducer's `refolded` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) hands out a stuck operation's atoms (`atoms_within`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`) and takes its fold again over its nodes (`refold`), each a walk of every node beneath the operation. A sum of `n` operations is `n` stuck sums, one inside the next, so one reduction of it walks its nodes `n` times over, and a comparison reduces it more than once. Over `x1 … xn, y1 … yn: Nat`, `Eq()(Nat/and(x1, y1) + … + Nat/and(xn, yn), Nat/and(xn, yn) + … + Nat/and(x1, y1))`, the summands reversed and none respelled, closes by `Eq/refl()` in 0.46 s [0.38–1.07] at `n = 64`, 1.92 s [1.25–3.10] at 128 and 8.35 s [5.47–11.12] at 256, and with each summand's operands swapped as well in 0.95 s [0.89–1.18], 2.50 s [2.33–2.83] and 10.75 s [10.72–10.98]: timed, the median and span of three rounds of `run -` through the release compiler on top of `0db77a9a9`, the two programs and the three sizes interleaved, on a machine under load. Two of three backtraces taken under gdb at `n = 256` stand in a walk under `refolded`, and the third in the elaboration of the infix chain. Fix: what a stuck operation's walk found, its atoms and the spelling of its nodes, is kept with its reduct, so the operation above reads its own operands and joins what they kept in place of walking them again. Check: the `summed` rows of `curios`'s `nested_operation_measurements`, whose cost per summand is then flat. Size: medium, after the campaign on the rank, which changes the same normal forms.
- **A bound over eight `abs`, or over six `sign`, is refused at the search's cap, where what both cases of each state would prove it.** `let f(a1: Int, …, a8: Int) -> Holds(a1 + … + a8 <= Nat/to_int(Int/abs(a1)) + … + Nat/to_int(Int/abs(a8))) = True/proved();` is refused as `the search for a proof ran out of its bound of 4096 derived rows`, and the same bound over six is accepted; `Holds(Int/sign(a1) + … + Int/sign(a6) <= +6)` is refused the same way, and over four is accepted, probed at `7df15083e`. The reader gives an `abs` to the search as a split and a `sign` as two, and as nothing else (`Reader::signs`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`), and the search opens each split along every path of the ones before it (`plan`, `curios-elab/src/entailment/search.rs`), so eight magnitudes are 256 cases. A truncated subtraction, a `min` and a `max` are read first through what both of their cases state, each proved by `True/qed()`; what both cases of an `abs` and of a `sign` state — `a <= |a|` and `-a <= |a|`, `-1 <= sign(a)` and `sign(a) <= +1` — conversion does not decide: `Holds(a <= Nat/to_int(Int/abs(a)))` and `Holds(Int/sign(a) <= +1)` are each a type mismatch at `True/qed()`. Fix: the four bounds as lemmas of `/std/Int/abs` and `/std/Int/sign`, a slot for each in `IntegerSyntax` (`curios-utilities/src/syntax.rs`), and `Reader::signs` admitting them beside the splits it opens. Check: the two programs over ten operations as rows of `curios`'s `tests::bounds`, and the two figures the decision records, `entailment::derived` and `entailment::written`, read again. Size: small; it adds facts to every search over an `abs` or a `sign`, so a proof the library holds over one may be written again.
- **A printed `let` generalized over levels its `let` holds at zero is not proved to be it.** `/std/Command/spawn`, printed as a report prints it and elaborated again, is generalized over nine universe levels the `let` holds at zero, and the proof by `Eq/refl()` that the printed form is the `let` asks that their maximum be zero, which elaboration does not solve for. `curios-pipeline`'s sweep lists the declaration (`UNPROVED`).
- **A classing's question is asked again across a write, and answers as it did.** Where a fold leaves an operation stuck its reducer asks which of the operation's atoms are one (`asked_classes`, in `curios-elab/src/reduce.rs` and in `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`, over `curios-analysis`'s `Classes::of`), each atom compared with the representative of every class opened before it by a conversion of its own, so a sum assembled a summand at a time is classed whole at each addition. The kernel answers a pair it has decided from the verdicts it remembers, after one transition (`Kernel::convert_hit`, in `compare`). The elaborator answers one from a table it keeps as it keeps its sorts, for as long as nothing is written (`Caches::same`, `curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`, read by `same_uncommitted_inside` in `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`): over the prelude's build it answers 14,413 of the 22,286 questions asked, counted on top of `a2cd85f5d` with the table in place, by a count added for the run. A comparison that opens a binder writes, so most askings of one pair stand either side of a write. Counted on top of `1d4a56c56`, over the prelude's build, by a count added for the run to `same_uncommitted_inside` that kept each pair asked since the budget was last restored with what it was asked under, the two write stamps (`Caches::stamps`) and the equations in force, the variables refined and the frames that hold a refined scrutinee: 6,622 pairs are asked again after a write and under the same equations, one of them answering another way, and 8,656 under other equations, one of them answering another way. Over `let acc(h: (Nat) -> Nat, n: Nat, c: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => c | k + 1 => acc(h, k, c + h(k)) end;`, `let t(h: (Nat) -> Nat, x: Nat) -> Eq()(acc(h, 62, x), x) = Eq/refl();` is refused as a mismatch under no `--budget` below about 199,900 units, and at 125 under none below about 985,800, each found by halving on top of `a2cd85f5d` with the table in place: five times the units for twice the count, where the pairs that are distinct grow fourfold. Check: the `classes` row of `runaway_measurements`, the program's budgets at 62 and at 125, and the counts over `/std`. Size: unknown.
