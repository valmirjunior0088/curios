# Arithmetic: findings

- **`proved()` finds a proof of a strict bound from its non-strict one and a disequality that does not check, where the two sides are an application.** `d < g(k, at)` from `d <= g(k, at)` and `g(k, at) != d`, with `g(x, y) = (x + y) % 16`, is refused with "a proof was found and did not check / that is the compiler's fault", while `d < b` from `d <= b` and `b != d` is proved; [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) proves it by `lt/of_le_ne`. `/std/Map/internal/Node` met the same report with a guard read through `count` unfolded into its fixpoint, and states such bounds through `strictly`, a lemma over plain variables.
- **A bound over truncated subtraction spends its derived rows before the disequality it needs is read.** `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d` is refused, the report naming only `d <= b` as read and the bound of 4096 derived rows reached, while `d < b` from the same two facts, and the goal from `d < b`, are each proved: the disequality is read last, after a search the subtraction's cases exhaust.
