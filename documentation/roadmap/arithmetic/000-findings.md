# Arithmetic: findings

- **Not measured: what reduction asking conversion costs `/std` in time.** Counted, the budget `/std` consumes — `budget::consumed` summed over the two profiles `cargo xtask workspace lint` files, read before another build of the prelude writes over them — is 21,324,740 in elaboration and 13,021,334 in certification at `9d121c13f`. No timed reading stands beside one from before reduction asked its checker's conversion ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)): a timed figure is a median over repeated readings on a quiet machine, the two builds interleaved, and none was to be had while the work landed. Fix: take it, and state it in the decision only if it argues for something there. Check: `/std`'s elaboration and its certification timed at `87189c97d` and at the commit above. Size: small.
- **An ordered comparison of two nested quotients, remainders, right shifts or `Nat/and`s costs time exponential in their depth.** `dominated` (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/compare.rs`) puts each operand the left side never exceeds against the right, and the left against each such operand of the right, each a `compare_nat` of its own that does the same one subterm down, and it keeps no verdict: two sides nested `n` deep are asked about one pair of subterms along every path that reaches it, and nothing of the walk is charged, so the budget does not end it. Over two chains that share no variable, `match L <= R | true => 0 | false => 0 end` returns in seconds at depth 8 and not within 45 seconds at depth 10, where a link is `x / (y + 1)`, `x % (y + 1)`, `Nat/shr(x, y)` or `Nat/and(x, y)`; a chain of differences merges into one and is flat, as is one of `Nat/min` and one at `Int`, which reads no such operand. A written goal over such terms pays it as well, its candidate search reducing the comparison (`curios_elab::suggest::apply_fit`). Probed at `ca95d7595`. Fix: one comparison keeps the verdict of each pair of subterms it has asked about, so a pair is compared once and the work is the product of the two sides' sizes, charged as it is done. Check: a slope measurement over the four chains, and the domination rows of `tests::laws::written` unmoved. Size: small.
