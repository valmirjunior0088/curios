# Arithmetic: findings

- **A fold past an operation's stated domain refuses a term a hypothesis made well typed.** The shared folds of `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic.rs`, with `nat.rs` and `scalar.rs` beside it, turn a narrowing, a division or a sequence read whose literal operands fall outside the domain its bound operand states into an error — `ReduceError::NatToByteAbove`, `IntToNatNegative`, `DivisionByZero`, `BytesGetOutOfBounds`, `BytesSliceOutOfRange`, `ListGetOutOfBounds`, `ListSliceOutOfRange` — and both checkers refuse the declaration whose judgment reduces one. The bound is an operand, so under a hypothesis stating the impossible the node is well typed: `let absurd(h: Holds(300 < 256)) -> False = below(Nat/to_byte(300));`, with `below(b: Byte) -> Holds(Byte/to_nat(b) < 256) = True/qed()`, is refused at `0d7d13c32` ("Nat/to_byte of 300, a value no Byte holds"), and its Core text, and a division by zero under `Holds(0 < 0)`, by the kernel's `Reduce`, where [A partial primitive is totalized by a canonical extension or it states its domain](../../design/arithmetic/a-partial-primitive-is-totalized-by-a-canonical-extension-or-it-states-its-domain.md) has the type state the domain. `NatToByteAbove`'s doc in `curios-core/src/reduce.rs`, "reaching here means a proof was admitted that should not have been", is the sentence that fails: reaching it means an absurd hypothesis is in scope. Fix, one of two: each fold declines past its domain, leaving the node neutral as `whnf` goes stuck on a shape it cannot fire, which admits only terms under an absurd hypothesis (recommended); or the error stays as a tripwire and each doc says it refuses a term under an absurd hypothesis, the refusal kept as a stated incompleteness. Check: a `curios` program test admitting `absurd`, a `curios-cert` Core text test of the narrowing and the division under their hypotheses expecting no `Reduce`, and `cargo xtask workspace lint`. Size: one commit.
