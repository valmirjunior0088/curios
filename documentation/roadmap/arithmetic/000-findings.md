# Arithmetic: findings

- **Not measured: what reduction asking conversion costs `/std` in time.** Counted, the budget `/std` consumes — `budget::consumed` summed over the two profiles `cargo xtask workspace lint` files, read before another build of the prelude writes over them — is 21,324,740 in elaboration and 13,021,334 in certification at `9d121c13f`. No timed reading stands beside one from before reduction asked its checker's conversion ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)): a timed figure is a median over repeated readings on a quiet machine, the two builds interleaved, and none was to be had while the work landed. Fix: take it, and state it in the decision only if it argues for something there. Check: `/std`'s elaboration and its certification timed at `87189c97d` and at the commit above. Size: small.
- **A stuck fold puts every two quotients, and every two remainders, under it to conversion.** The walk that hands a fold's atoms out (`atoms_within`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`) hands a quotient or a remainder out as an atom once its dividend and divisor are walked, `may_be_one` reads two stuck operations of one kind as possibly one, and each reducer's `refolded` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) then asks its conversion about every such pair, at every stuck operation over them, each question a comparison of two terms as deep as the atoms are. Over `L(0) = x0`, `L(k) = L(k - 1) % (yk + 1)` and its twin `R` over other variables, `Eq()(match L(n) <= R(n) | true => 0 | false => 0 end, 0)` is refused by `Eq/refl()` in 2.08 s [1.76–2.77] at `n = 12` and 14.55 s [14.16–14.80] at `n = 24`, where the same towers of `Nat/and` take 0.58 s [0.48–0.70] and 1.21 s [0.99–1.25]: timed, the median and span of three runs of `wonder diagnostics -` through the release compiler at `1cdbb0314`, on a machine under load. A backtrace taken under gdb stands in `classing` under `Nat::normalize` each of the two times it was taken. Fix: two quotients, or two remainders, are held one where their dividends and their divisors are, with no question put, the proof each carries being of a proposition those two state; the classification ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)) spells both before it reaches the node, and the rule is the trusted base's, since it rests on every operand `Intrinsic::algebra` leaves out being a proof. Check: the two towers as an ignored measurement, flat in `n`, and a quotient under two proofs of its divisor as a row of `curios`'s `tests::laws::written`. Size: small.
- **A stuck fold walks the whole operation beneath it, at every operation of a long sum.** Each reducer's `refolded` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) hands out a stuck operation's atoms (`atoms_within`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`) and takes its fold again over its nodes (`refold`), each a walk of every node beneath the operation. A sum of `n` operations is `n` stuck sums, one inside the next, so one reduction of it walks its nodes `n` times over, and a comparison reduces it more than once. Over `x1 … xn, y1 … yn: Nat`, `Eq()(Nat/and(x1, y1) + … + Nat/and(xn, yn), Nat/and(xn, yn) + … + Nat/and(x1, y1))`, the summands reversed and none respelled, closes by `Eq/refl()` in 0.46 s [0.38–1.07] at `n = 64`, 1.92 s [1.25–3.10] at 128 and 8.35 s [5.47–11.12] at 256, and with each summand's operands swapped as well in 0.95 s [0.89–1.18], 2.50 s [2.33–2.83] and 10.75 s [10.72–10.98]: timed, the median and span of three rounds of `run -` through the release compiler on top of `0db77a9a9`, the two programs and the three sizes interleaved, on a machine under load. Two of three backtraces taken under gdb at `n = 256` stand in a walk under `refolded`, and the third in the elaboration of the infix chain. Fix: what a stuck operation's walk found, its atoms and the spelling of its nodes, is kept with its reduct, so the operation above reads its own operands and joins what they kept in place of walking them again. Check: the `summed` rows of `curios`'s `nested_operation_measurements`, whose cost per summand is then flat. Size: medium.
