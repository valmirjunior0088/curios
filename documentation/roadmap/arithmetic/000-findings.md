# Arithmetic: findings

- **A bound stated over a local definition inside an operand reduction opens gets a proof that does not check.** Under `@_: Holds(0 < g)` and `back: Eq()(g, Nat/gcd(a, b) * g)`, `let x = a;` then `need(Nat/gcd(x, b), 1)`, against `need(a: Nat, b: Nat, @_: Holds(a <= b))`, is refused as "a proof was found and did not check, which is the compiler's fault"; written `need(Nat/gcd(a, b), 1)`, or with no `gcd` (`let x = a / g;` then `need(x, 1)` under `back: Eq()(g, (a / g) * g)`), it is proved. `Nat/Coprime/quotients` escapes it only by how it is written: its `True/proved()` reads the bound through a metavariable, so the procedure finds no stated spelling and writes its proof over the bound's reduct, and with the proposition written, `True/proved(@Holds(Nat/gcd(x, y) <= 1))`, it is refused the same way. Seen in that program with the bound read through its metavariable's solution (an experiment at `c414cd87c`): the refuting split stands over the bound as stated, and the check inside its arm infers `True` where it expects `Holds(2 <= …)` over `gcd(x, y)` opened, so the refinement the split installs does not answer the goal the arm is checked at. Not known: which spelling the arm's goal takes and why the split's equation does not answer it, its key as written, its resolved spelling or its settled reduct ([Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)). Settled by: the keys the split records beside the arm's goal, printed for this program. Check: the program elaborates, and `/std` elaborates and certifies, its image digest compared before and after.
