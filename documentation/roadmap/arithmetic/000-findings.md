# Arithmetic: findings

- **Not measured: what reduction asking conversion costs `/std` in time.** Counted, the budget `/std` consumes — `budget::consumed` summed over the two profiles `cargo xtask workspace lint` files, read before another build of the prelude writes over them — is 21,324,740 in elaboration and 13,021,334 in certification at `9d121c13f`. No timed reading stands beside one from before reduction asked its checker's conversion ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)): a timed figure is a median over repeated readings on a quiet machine, the two builds interleaved, and none was to be had while the work landed. Fix: take it, and state it in the decision only if it argues for something there. Check: `/std`'s elaboration and its certification timed at `87189c97d` and at the commit above. Size: small.
- **A stuck fold puts every two quotients, and every two remainders, under it to conversion.** The walk that hands a fold's atoms out (`atoms_within`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`) hands a quotient or a remainder out as an atom once its dividend and divisor are walked, `may_be_one` reads two stuck operations of one kind as possibly one, and each reducer's `refolded` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) then asks its conversion about every such pair, at every stuck operation over them, each question a comparison of two terms as deep as the atoms are. Over `L(0) = x0`, `L(k) = L(k - 1) % (yk + 1)` and its twin `R` over other variables, `Eq()(match L(n) <= R(n) | true => 0 | false => 0 end, 0)` is refused by `Eq/refl()` in 2.08 s [1.76–2.77] at `n = 12` and 14.55 s [14.16–14.80] at `n = 24`, where the same towers of `Nat/and` take 0.58 s [0.48–0.70] and 1.21 s [0.99–1.25]: timed, the median and span of three runs of `wonder diagnostics -` through the release compiler at `e6285da67`, on a machine under load. A backtrace taken under gdb stands in `classing` under `Nat::normalize` each of the two times it was taken. Fix: two quotients, or two remainders, are held one where their dividends and their divisors are, with no question put, the proof each carries being of a proposition those two state; the classification of [An atom is one where conversion says so](100-an-atom-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) spells both before it reaches the node, and the rule is the trusted base's, since it rests on every operand `Intrinsic::algebra` leaves out being a proof. Check: the two towers as an ignored measurement, flat in `n`, and a quotient under two proofs of its divisor as a row of `curios`'s `tests::laws::written`. Size: small once that specification's first stage has landed.
