# Arithmetic: findings

- **Uncertain: the conversion chain classifies a pair afresh at every level of a congruence descent.** The chain (`curios-analysis/src/conversion.rs`) hands a pair's atoms out (`atoms_of`) and reads the pair classed (`classed`), each by a walk of both sides from nothing, and where that reading leaves the pair to its congruence, each operand pair is classified again from nothing; `answers` (`case_equation.rs`) does the same of a stuck form beside a key. A stuck fold classified the same way until it extended what its operands' folds filed (`Classification`, `curios-analysis/src/classify.rs`), and a side that is a stuck reduct is its own forced form, so its classification is in its reducer's table. Not known: whether any program pays for it. Of eleven samples taken under gdb at `f7375e49e`, seven of the reversed sum of 256 `Nat/and` operations and four of the reversed sum of 256 calls of one head, none stands in a classification walk. Settled by: samples across `/std`'s elaboration (`cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration`), and a probe whose two sides differ under a spine of differences, at two sizes. Fix, where the walks show: the chain takes each side's filed classification where the side is a stuck reduct, and extends it as `atoms_within` and `refold` do. Size: unknown.
- **A literal-dispatch match's fallback arm states nothing of its scrutinee to the bound prover.** `match n | 0 => … | 1 => … | _ => …` lowers to a switch whose `_` arm carries no refinement, so the procedure that proves a bound from the facts in scope (`curios-elab`'s `entailment`) reads neither `n != 0` nor `n != 1` there, where the nested successor match the arms stand for would refine `n` past both. At `b04bf1f3f`, `pub let f(n: Nat, @below: Holds(n < 2)) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 10 | 1 => 11 | _ => match False/refuted() end end;` is refused with "they all hold at n = 0". Fix: the fallback arm records each literal it passed as a disequality the procedure reads, as an `==` guard's false arm does. Check: a body row in `curios`'s `tests::bounds` refuting that arm, and a control where a literal arm is missing. Size: one commit.
