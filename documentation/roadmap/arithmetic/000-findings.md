# Arithmetic: findings

- **`proved()` finds a proof of a strict bound from its non-strict one and a disequality that does not check, where a side is a remainder.** `d < (k + at) % 16` from `d <= (k + at) % 16` and `(k + at) % 16 != d` is refused with "a proof was found and did not check / that is the compiler's fault", and so is the same through a definition `g(x, y) = (x + y) % 16`, or over `x % 16` alone; over a sum, a product, `Nat/max`, a quotient or a recursion in the remainder's place it is proved, as it is from `d + 1 <= (k + at) % 16`, with no disequality to split on. [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) proves the strict bound by `lt/of_le_ne`, under the split `Reader::distinct` opens on `a <= b` where reduction decides neither order (`curios-elab/src/entailment/facts.rs`), and a remainder is read through Euclid's recombination, whose comment at the check warns that a proof naming one call two ways does not check. Not yet known: which spelling of the remainder the written proof and the split's equation each hold, which the candidate and its check's error, printed at `check` in `curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`, settle. `/std/Map/internal/Node` met the same report with a guard read through `count` unfolded into its fixpoint, and states such bounds through `strictly`, a lemma over plain variables. Size: unknown.
- **A bound over two truncated subtractions of one minuend that fails in some case spends its derived rows instead of answering where it fails, so a disequality that would make it hold is never read.** `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d` is refused with the bound of 4096 derived rows reached and only `d <= b` read; from `d <= b` alone, or from no fact, where `d = b` falsifies it, the search reaches the same bound rather than the assignment. The goals of that shape that hold — `b - (d + 1) <= b - d`, `b - (d + 1) + 1 <= b - d + 1`, `b - d <= b - (d + 1) + 1` from `d <= b` — are proved, and so are `1 <= b - d` from `d <= b` and `b != d`, one subtraction, and the entry's goal from `d < b`. [A bound that follows from the facts in scope is proved by the elaborator](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md) reads a disequality last, where the search over the others has left an assignment, and here that search never ends within the bound. Not yet known: what two subtractions of one minuend derive, the cases each splits into and their products, which `entailment::derived`, sampled where the search ends (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`), counts under an instrumented build. Size: unknown.
