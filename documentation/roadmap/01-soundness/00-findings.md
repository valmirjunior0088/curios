# Soundness: findings

Small fixes and possible bugs, worked as one pass. Each entry names where, what is wrong, the fix, its check and its size.

- **The elaborator's conversion fires tuple and struct eta whatever the goal type.** `eta_expand_tuple` and `eta_expand_struct` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) project the other side and enqueue one goal per field whether or not the goal type reduces to the literal's own tuple or struct type, keeping the field types only when it does. A literal with no fields therefore converts with anything: `convert` judges `() ≈ 1` at `Nat` (`convert/solve_tests.rs`'s `eta_at_unit_trusts_the_goal_type_label`), and a field-less struct literal answers the same way. The kernel fires tuple eta only at a non-empty Σ (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) and refuses the pair, so the two checkers part on it. No program reaches it, since elaboration compares only sides it has checked at one type, but the elaborator's answer rests on that caller invariant rather than on its own rule. Fix: where the goal type reduces to a rigid type other than the literal's — a tuple of another arity, another struct, any other former — answer `false`; an unsolved type keeps today's expansion, since the invariant then makes it the literal's own. Check: the fixture flipped to `Ok(false)` and renamed, a field-less struct beside it, and `() ≈ x` at `{}` still `true`. Size: quick.
- **The two checkers part on unit eta, and a program reaches it.** The elaborator equates any two terms at a type with no fields: `eta_expand_neutral` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) enqueues one goal per field of the goal's tuple or struct type, which is none at `{}` and at a field-less struct, and `eta_expand_tuple` does the same for `()` against a neutral. The kernel fires Σ eta only at a non-empty telescope (`compare`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) and struct eta only against a literal (`struct_eta`), though its module documentation states Σ eta without the exception and nothing says why the empty telescope is left out. So `let f(F: ({}) -> Type, r: {}, s: {}, x: F(r)) -> F(s) = x;` elaborates and `run` stops on ``the kernel refused /f: expected `F(s)`, found `F(r)` ``; `let u(r: {}) -> Eq()(r, ()) = Eq/refl();` is refused the same way, and so is the first program over a field-less `struct U` in place of `{}`, while `r` against the literal `U {}` is accepted by both. The elaborator's own rule is uneven beside it: it compares a neutral's projections at `Type` rather than at their field types, so two neutrals at `{a: {}, b: {}}` are refused where two at `{}` are accepted. Fix: the kernel fires Σ eta at the empty telescope too and struct eta between two neutrals, each at the goal's own type, which is the invariant irrelevance already stands on and the rule [Lean 4's kernel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14064) has for unit-like structures; the elaborator compares a neutral's projections at their field types; and [Eta and untyped child positions](../../design/soundness/conversion/eta-and-untyped-child-positions.md) states the rule with its fixtures. The alternative, that the elaborator stops equating neutrals at a field-less type, changes which programs are accepted, and waits on a count of what `/std` rests on it. Check: the three programs, and two neutrals at `{a: {}, b: {}}`, as rows of the two-checker matrix, accepted by both. Size: medium.
