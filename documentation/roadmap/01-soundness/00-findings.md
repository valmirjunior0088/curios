# Soundness: findings

- **The elaborator fires Π and Σ eta by the goal's type only between two terms no structural rule claims.** The kernel's `compare` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) applies both sides to fresh binders at a function type and projects both at a Σ ahead of every structural rule, whatever their shapes. The elaborator's `drain` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) dispatches on the two sides' shapes first: it fires eta by a literal, and by the goal's type (`eta_expand_neutral`) only for a pair no arm claims, so two stuck matches go to `compare_match`, two applications to `compare_apply` and two projections to `compare_proj`, each comparing children at `Type` or at a head's telescope. The two agree wherever eta ends at a field compared structurally, and part where it ends at a type that decides the goal by itself: at a record of units or a function into a unit, `match b: (_) => {{}, {}} | true => r | false => r end` against the same match over `s` is accepted by the kernel, by Σ eta and unit eta composed, and refused by the elaborator. Nothing is admitted and no program reaches the kernel with it, since the elaborator refuses first; what is lost is a program the theory accepts, refused with a type mismatch, and transitivity in the elaborator, where each match converts with `((), ())` and the two not with each other. Fix: the elaborator fires Π and Σ eta by the goal's type ahead of the structural dispatch for every pair with no flexible head, as the kernel does; that adds a step to every rigid goal at a function or a record type and may change the order metavariables are solved in, so what it costs elaborating `/std` is measured before it is taken. Check: the rows `tests::laws`' table of parted rows lists under this finding, deleted, and two stuck matches at a record of units beside `curios-elab`'s `convert::structural_tests::any_two_terms_converge_at_a_type_with_no_field`. Size: large.
- **The kernel takes a literal's eta against three shapes of neutral, and the elaborator against any term, and a program reaches the difference.** `neutral` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) is a variable, a projection or a stuck application, and the struct arms of `structural` spell the same three; the elaborator's `eta_expand_func`, `eta_expand_tuple` and `eta_expand_struct` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) fire against whatever the dispatch hands them. A stuck elimination is neither of the kernel's three, so `let t(c: Bool, s: Record, r: Record) -> Eq(@(Record))(Record { a = m.a, b = m.b }, m) = Eq/refl();`, with `m` the stuck `match c: (_) => Record | true => s | false => r end` and `struct Record { a: Nat, b: Nat }`, elaborates and `run` stops on the kernel's refusal, at the struct's own type. So do a tuple literal, a lambda and a struct literal against a stuck `match` in a stuck `match`'s arm, where no type directs the rule, and a tuple literal there against a definition that unfolds to one; at the record and the function type the kernel's eta by the goal's type accepts the same pairs. Nothing is admitted. Fix: one set in both checkers, every weak-head normal form that is not an introduction form, stated by one classifier in `curios-core` matched over `Subterm` with no wildcard; the kernel widens to it, an accepting change argued in [Eta and untyped child positions](../../design/soundness/conversion/eta-and-untyped-child-positions.md), and the elaborator narrows to it once it fires eta by the goal's type ahead of structure, the finding above. Check: the five programs compile and each near miss is refused; a literal against an introduction form of another shape is refused at a sort in both checkers; seeds in `tests::laws` whose neutral is a stuck elimination keep their side under every context. Size: medium.
- **The kernel compares two calls of one definition by their spines only after eta by the goal's type, and a program reaches it.** `compare` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) fires eta at a function and a record type ahead of `one_definition_by_its_spines`, and the pair eta hands on — each call applied to the fresh binder, or projected — is no application of a definition at its head, so the spines are never compared and both calls unfold. The elaborator's `compare_same_global_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) runs on the pair as it is posed, ahead of everything. So with `induct Z: (Nat) -> pub Prop | mk(): (0) end` and `let h(n: Nat, e: Z(n)) -> (Nat) -> Nat = match e: (_, _) => (Nat) -> Nat | mk() => (x: Nat) => x end;`, `let same(n: Nat, k: (Nat) -> Nat, p: Z(n), q: Z(n), x: Eq(@((Nat) -> Nat))(h(n, p), k)) -> Eq(@((Nat) -> Nat))(h(n, q), k) = x;` elaborates and `run` stops on the kernel's refusal: unfolded, the two proofs are a stuck `match`'s scrutinees, compared at `Type`. The same at `{Nat, Nat}`; at `Nat`, where no eta fires, both checkers accept (`tests::board::eta_tests`'s `a_definition_applied_to_two_proofs_converts_before_unfolding`). And the kernel's rule asks for the definition at the application's own head, where the elaborator's looks through a curried spine (`applied_head`), so `h(n, p)(3)` against `h(n, q)(3)` at `Nat` is refused by the kernel alone as well. Nothing is admitted. Fix: the kernel tries the spines on the pair as posed, ahead of eta by the type — congruence is sufficient at every type and never necessary, so a mismatch still hands the pair on — which is where the elaborator tries them, and through a curried spine as the elaborator does, two heads that are themselves two calls of one definition being compared by the same rule. Check: the three programs compile, as board tests beside the fixture named; a pair whose spines differ in a relevant argument is still refused; what certifying a unit costs, since every pair of calls at a function or a record type gains a spine comparison. Size: small.
- **The elaborator's rule for two calls of one definition reads the two sides as they are spelled, and a side a solved metavariable spells is not seen.** `compare_same_global_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) matches the raw spellings, taken before reduction, for two applications of one global. With `h` as above at `Nat`, `let same(n: Nat, p: Z(n), q: Z(n)) -> Eq()(h(n, p), h(n, q)) = Eq/refl();` is refused with a type mismatch: `Eq/refl()`'s implicit is solved to `h(n, p)`, the goal that follows spells that side as the metavariable, the rule does not fire, and both sides unfold to stuck `match`es whose scrutinees, the two proofs, are compared at `Type`. With the witness stated, `Eq/refl(@Nat, @h(n, p))`, the same claim compiles. The verdict follows a spelling where the two sides and their type are the same. Fix: the rule reads each side through the solutions already committed, as `solve` materializes a candidate before it analyses it. Check: the program compiles, in `curios/src/tests/inference/`; a fixture in `curios-elab`'s `convert::conversion_tests` puts the pair to `convert` with one side spelled by a solved metavariable. Size: quick.
- **The kernel types a spine's arguments under fewer heads than the elaborator, and a program reaches it.** `spine_telescope` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) reads the head's type only where the head is a variable, a universe instance of one or a `rec` projection, and compares the arguments at `Type` under any other; the elaborator's `apply_param_types` (`curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`) asks `synth_neutral` of whatever the head is, which also answers an application and a projection. So with `induct Z: (Nat) -> pub Prop | mk(): (0) end`, `let same(n: Nat, f: (Nat) -> (Z(n)) -> Nat, p: Z(n), q: Z(n), x: Eq()(f(n)(p), 0)) -> Eq()(f(n)(q), 0) = x;` elaborates, the two proofs meeting irrelevance at `Z(n)`, and `run` stops on the kernel's refusal, which compares them at `Type`; and so does the same past a projection's head, `r.0(p)` against `r.0(q)` with `r: {(Z(n)) -> Nat, Nat}`. Past a variable's own spine, `f(p)` against `f(q)`, both accept. The kernel's `synth_neutral` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/sort.rs`) answers the same two heads, a lookup, a substitution and a reduction, and `spine_telescope`'s documentation says no disagreement had asked for it. Nothing is admitted. Under a stuck elimination's head both checkers compare the arguments at `Type` and both refuse, the head's type being its motive's to state and neither reading it. Fix: `spine_telescope` asks `synth_neutral` of every head, the arguments being compared only once the two heads are, which is what makes one telescope theirs. Check: the two programs compile, as board tests; a relevant argument past each head is still refused; seeds in `tests::laws` state irrelevance through a curried and a projected head. Size: quick.
- **Neither checker compares two calls of one definition by their spines under a projection.** Both spine rules take two applications at the root of the pair. With `let h(n: Nat, e: Z(n)) -> {Nat, Nat} = match e: (_, _) => {Nat, Nat} | mk() => (1, 2) end;`, `h(n, p).0` against `h(n, q).0` is refused by the elaborator with a type mismatch, and the kernel walks it the same way: each side reduces before its head is compared, the calls unfold, and the two proofs are a stuck `match`'s scrutinees. The two checkers agree and a true equation is refused: the congruence that decides two calls of one definition before unfolding is taken at an application and at no other eliminator. Not known: how far the rule should reach — a projection alone, or every position a definition's call can be stuck under, which is comparing the unreduced spellings by their shape before either unfolds, the lazy unfolding Lean's and Rocq's conversion run on. Fix: once that is decided, in both checkers alike. Check: the program compiles. Size: unknown.
- **The zonk evidence does not see an unsolved universe level.** `Zonked::project` (`curios-core/src/module.rs`) refuses a module by two cached flags per term, an unsolved metavariable and an elaboration-only node, and a universe metavariable lives in a `Level`, which neither covers: a module declaring `held : Type(?u)` takes the evidence. Nothing is admitted by it — the kernel's walk refuses the level itself (`universe_residue`, `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`), erasure discards levels, and the elaborator refuses an unsolved level before a module exists, so only a module built by hand reaches it — but the type promises less than its name says, and a third consumer of the evidence would inherit the gap. Fix: refuse a level holding a universe metavariable in `zonked_refusal`, by a walk with `universe_metas` or by a third cached flag beside the two; the walk reads every term where the flags are read once, so its cost over `/std` is measured before it is taken. Check: the two modules `curios-cert`'s `recheck::universes_tests::a_level_holding_an_unsolved_universe_metavariable_is_refused` builds are refused by `Zonked::project`, in `curios-core`'s own tests. Size: small.
- **Index inversion's cycle guard does not see a key-shaped actual behind an instance.** `unify_index` (`curios-analysis/src/invert.rs`) declines to solve a flex arm binder against a key-shaped actual at the top of a position — `matches!(&*actual, Subterm::Var(_) | Subterm::Proj(_))` — because index refinement may have refined that key *to* the binder, and solving it back ties a reduction cycle. An instance whose head is a variable is such a key, and `Term::head_key` (`curios-core/src/term.rs`) says so by giving it the `HeadTag::Name` a bare variable gets; `judge.force` leaves one standing, `step_instance` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`) having no value for a local, so the pattern misses it and the binder is solved against it. The guard below catches only an actual whose own free variables are the arm's, which a key refined elsewhere is not. Nothing is admitted by it — a solution substitutes in one pass, and a reduction that loops is the budget's refusal — and no program reaches it, since a local is monomorphic and the elaborator mints an instance only for a polymorphic name, so only a module built by hand gets there. It is the third site of one asymmetry: the kernel's call recorder (`infer_within`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/infer.rs`) and the polarity walk (`Walk::walk`, `curios-analysis/src/positivity.rs`) each read an instance's variable head as the occurrence it is, and `Subterm::has_local_free` (`curios-core/src/term/subterm.rs`) always did. Fix: state the key shape once — a variable head, bare or under an instance — and have the guard read it through that, `Term::head_key` being where the shape is already named. Check: the inversion fixtures, and a pair whose actual is an instance over a refined key, refused rather than solved. Size: quick.
- **Uncertain: the roadmap says a board entry is graded, and the documentation rule says it carries no grade.** `documentation/roadmap.md`'s landed line for the soundness board reads "every rule that can admit a term, graded probed, argued or auditable, with its fixtures", and the entries under `documentation/design/soundness/` open their evidence with those words; `.claude/rules/documentation.md` says an entry "carries no grade: what has been found is the tickets on the board". Not known: which is intended now that what has been found is `xboard`'s tickets — whether "probed" and "argued" in an entry describe its evidence and stay, or are the grade the rule retired. Fix: once that is decided, the one sentence that is wrong. Check: none beyond reading. Size: quick.
- **Which dictionary a call uses depends on whether its key type was solved when the walk reached the premise.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether the order should matter. `elaborate_apply` attempts a `use` slot where it stands (`insert_auto_argument`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/apply.rs`), so a premise whose concept arguments are already known resolves before a later argument's type can name another dictionary: with `struct Slot(K: Type, use Key(K), V: Type)`, a value `b: Slot(Nat, use other, Str)` and `holds(@K: Type, use Key(K), @V: Type, at: Slot(K, V), key: K)`, `holds(b, 3)` takes `b`'s dictionary, while `holds(@Nat, b, 3)`, a premise over a closed type and a key pinned by an earlier argument each resolve the registered one and refuse `b` as a type mismatch (`a_key_type_fixed_before_the_slot_takes_the_registered_dictionary`). Attempting every such slot after the later arguments is not the fix: a numeral resolves eagerly (`elaborate_num_lit`), so `f(@Bool, 5, true)` over `x: Sized/Carrier(@A)` would meet a type still stuck on the witness. Fix: none taken; a slot would wait only where a later type holds the witness itself rather than a projection of it. Check: a fixture per order, stating the verdict chosen. Size: medium.
- **The kernel's conversion compares the marks of an application's arguments and of a match's arms, which none of its rules checks, and the elaborator's ignores them.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether any program reaches a disagreement. `structural` and `one_definition_by_its_spines` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) require two spines' marks to agree before comparing them, and the arm comparison requires equal arm marks, while `infer`'s application rule (`curios-cert/src/kernel/infer.rs`) checks arity and domains and never an argument's mark against its binder's, and no clause reads a constructor's registry marks, which `recheck/plicity_tests.rs` holds. `compare_apply` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) compares the argument terms alone. Elaboration rebuilds every application with its binders' marks, so the two agree on what it emits; a term built with other marks converts in one checker and falls to the unfolding retry in the other. Fix: one answer in both — the marks are checked where the application is typed, or conversion stops reading them. Check: a fixture putting two applications that differ in a mark alone to both checkers. Size: medium.
- **The telescope-instantiation entry says erasure masks by plicity.** [Telescope instantiation](../../design/soundness/introduction/telescope-instantiation.md) argues its plicity clause from "erasure masks by plicity, so the two differ in what survives", where erasure is sort-driven (`is_erasable`, `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/classify.rs`) and [plicity is part of function identity](../../design/theory/plicity-is-part-of-function-identity.md) says a mark has no run-time effect. Fix: state what the clause rests on — a mark decides which binders elaboration inserts, so it must survive conversion — and drop the erasure half. Check: the entry and the decision agree. Size: quick.
