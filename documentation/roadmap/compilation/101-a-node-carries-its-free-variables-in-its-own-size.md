# A node carries its free variables in its own size

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a read visit a node once, or pass over a subterm a fact its node carries proves it has nothing to find in. The fact a node carries of its free variables is a set copied from its children, so a node pays for every variable beneath it rather than for what it adds: a term whose every level names one more local costs the square of its depth, and a metavariable's set is rebuilt from every entry of its spine.

**Next step: delivery.** Stages 1 and 2 are ready, in order; stage 3 waits on the spine-capture and parked-wake entries of [compilation's findings](000-findings.md), the probe's other two squares.

## What is wrong

- **The fill copies.** `Subterm::free_vars_from_children` (`curios-core/src/term/subterm.rs`) fills a node's `FreeCache` (`term/frees.rs`) with the one child set it can share, or else a copy of the first carrying child's `BTreeSet<Free>` extended with the others'. So `((n + x0) + x1) + … + x{k}`, each level naming one more local, holds sets of one, two and up to k members, each copied whole from the one below.
- **A metavariable's set is rebuilt from its spine.** `Subterm::any_child_term` hands a metavariable's every spine entry to the fill, so a metavariable's set is the union of every entry's, rebuilt at every metavariable whose set is asked, where [the design rule](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) reads "a node's facts are read off its spine's, never off its entries" and `warm_scalars` reads them so; and `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`, `context.rs` and `elaborate/binding.rs` union or probe a spine's entries one by one.

Counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy holding this spec's first stage, which reads the scalars first, release with the profile feature, `wonder diagnostics -` over `n` bindings `let x{i} = a + i;` in one function, then `let g = (n: Nat) => n + x0 + … + x{n-1};` and `g(1)`: 3.69, 10.64 and 35.55 G instructions and 2.34, 5.16 and 12.78 M allocations at 1 000, 2 000 and 4 000 bindings, ×2.88 and ×3.34 a doubling, where the same bindings summed with no lambda grow ×1.87 and ×1.95. From 1 000 to 2 000 bindings the fill copies 2.02 M and then 8.03 M members.

## What is known

- **A set is the fact to keep.** Lean 4 keeps none: its `Expr.Data` packs a hash and flags, `hasFVar` among them, and a walk collects what a reader asks, remembering the nodes it visited ([`Expr.lean`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/Expr.lean), [`CollectFVars.lean`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/Util/CollectFVars.lean)); rustc keeps flags and the outermost binder a type reaches, combined from its parts where it is built ([`flags.rs`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_type_ir/flags.rs.html)); Rocq's kernel walks ([`vars.ml`](https://ocaml.org/p/coq-core/8.20.0/src/coq-core.kernel/vars.ml.html)). Here the readers ask a term's set at every scope check, by-value read and credit, and ask the terms nested in it again. Counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy, a remembered walk per read, pruned by the newest local, visits 58.5 M nodes over `profile-prelude elaboration` and 11.2 M over certification, each a hashed visit, where today's reads iterate 15.4 M and 3.8 M members; and a walk per probe leaves the substitution's square standing.
- **Merging smaller into larger is the peers' remedy.** GHC annotates each Core node with a deterministic set over integer-keyed maps, its locals alone, and inserts the smaller set into the larger ([`GHC/Core/FVs.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ghc/ghc/master/compiler/GHC/Core/FVs.hs), [`GHC/Types/Unique/DFM.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ghc/ghc/master/compiler/GHC/Types/Unique/DFM.hs)); Maziarz, Ellis, Lawrence, Fitzgibbon and Peyton Jones build every subterm's variable map smaller into larger in O(n log² n) ([Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02856)); a treap's union costs O(m log(n/m + 1)) for sets of m ≤ n members ([Blelloch and Reid-Miller](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/project/scandal/public/papers/treaps-spaa98.html), [Blelloch, Ferizovic and Sun](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.02120)). Bindlib's boxes carry a sorted list merged at every node, which is today's square ([`bindlib.ml`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rlepigre/ocaml-bindlib/master/lib/bindlib.ml)).
- **What the library fills.** Counted at `0fc2fc1bd` over `profile-prelude` in a scratch copy: elaboration copies 514 468 sets holding 3 147 229 members and certification 182 857 holding 1 131 399, at most 72 and 76; 94% hold sixteen or fewer and 99.99% sixty-four or fewer. Globals are 60% and 68% of the members copied, and the locals of a copied set alone never pass 32.
- **What the readers ask.** 88 sites in 37 files outside tests, which call nothing of the set but `iter`, `contains`, `len` and its iteration: about 38 ask one name, 9 a predicate of every member, 17 look each member up in a scope, and 25 collect a term's globals or its locals. Of the memberships asked over elaboration and certification, 96% and 100% name a local, and the newest local alone decides 58% and 60% of them.
- **Order is an output.** Readers iterate in `Free`'s order, globals before locals and locals by mint, and fold it into what they build, a solution list or a refusal's name; a scratch copy holding each representation below in ascending order kept both of `/std`'s image digests at one path.

## Settled

- **The set stays, shares, and merges smaller into larger.** A node shares its largest carrying child's set and unions the others' in, so it pays for the members its smaller children bring, each a logarithm: over the probe the members a fill takes in grow 3 287, 6 537, 13 037 and 26 037 at 250 to 2 000 bindings, ×2.0 a doubling, and the tree nodes it builds 15 475, 34 207, 75 147 and 164 003, ×2.2, where today's copies grow ×4.0 (counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy's model of the merge). Over any tree that is O(n log² n), Maziarz et al.'s bound; over a graph, two children whose equal sets were built apart pay the smaller's members in time and nothing in memory, the union handing back the larger.
- **The set is `curios-utilities`' treap at every size.** A `Sorted` set, whose shape is a function of the members it holds ([`curios-utilities`' README](../../../curios-utilities/README.md#a-persistent-maps-shape-is-a-function-of-the-keys-it-holds)), behind the node's one-word cell. The union takes the smaller operand's members the larger lacks into the larger, copying only the paths to what it adds, and hands back the larger where it adds nothing, an operand shared at its root met whole: O(m log n) for sets of m ≤ n members, the bound the merge above rests on. A slice for small sets, as Clojure's array map, Scala's `Set1` to `Set4` and Agda's one-word `VarSet` keep theirs ([`PersistentArrayMap.java`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clojure/clojure/master/src/jvm/clojure/lang/PersistentArrayMap.java), [`Set.scala`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scala/scala/2.13.x/src/library/scala/collection/immutable/Set.scala), [`VarSet.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agda/agda/master/src/full/Agda/Utils/VarSet.hs)), saves half a percent of the instructions and allocations and two to four points of peak, above, for a second representation with a union, an iteration and a threshold of its own, which copies a node's members whole below its threshold: the square this spec removes, capped at a size.
- **A metavariable's set is its spine's.** A spine counts the names its entries hold, each by the entries holding it, beside the set of them, added and taken out with an entry as its other facts are; a metavariable's fill reads that set and no entry.
- **Ascending `Free` order, held.** Iteration stays ascending, so no reader's output moves, and a reader of the locals alone starts at the first local.
- **No stored byte moves.** The cache is rkyv-skipped and filled again where a node is restored: verdicts, `/std`'s images and executable artifacts stay equal at one path.

## Stages

1. **The node's set.** `Frees` holds a `Sorted` set, filled by sharing the largest carrying child's and taking in the others' by the union. Check: the fill's members taken in and nodes built, counted over the probe at 1 000, 2 000, 4 000 and 8 000 bindings, ×2.0 and about ×2.2 a doubling; the image unchanged at one path; both halves no worse than today's in allocations, bytes and peak, and their instructions read against the table above, counted.
2. **A spine's set.** `MetavarSpine` keeps its names counted and their set beside its facts, a metavariable's fill reads it, and the readers that walked a spine's entries for their names read it. Check: a spine's set after pushes, rewrites and captures equal to a recount over its entries; a metavariable's fill visits no entry; the image unchanged at one path.
3. **The growth.** The lambda probe joins `curios/src/tests/growth.rs` as a growth held through emission (`HELD_FRONT`, `curios/src/tests/instrumented/growth.rs`). Check: `growth_slopes` holds it under `HELD_SLOPE`.
