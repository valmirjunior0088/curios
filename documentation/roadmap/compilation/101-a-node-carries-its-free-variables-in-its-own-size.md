# A node carries its free variables in its own size

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a read visit a node once, or pass over a subterm a fact its node carries proves it has nothing to find in. The fact a node carries of its free variables is a set copied from its children, so a node pays for every variable beneath it rather than for what it adds; and the rewrites that read it ask it of each name they look for at each node they enter, where the node's newest local answers alone. A term whose every level names one more local costs the square of its depth both ways, and a metavariable's set is rebuilt from every entry of its spine.

**Next step: delivery.** Stages 1 to 5 are ready, in order; stage 6 waits on the spine-capture and parked-wake entries of [compilation's findings](000-findings.md), the probe's other two squares.

## What is wrong

- **The fill copies.** `Subterm::free_vars_from_children` (`curios-core/src/term/subterm.rs`) fills a node's `FreeCache` (`term/frees.rs`) with the one child set it can share, or else a copy of the first carrying child's `BTreeSet<Free>` extended with the others'. So `((n + x0) + x1) + … + x{k}`, each level naming one more local, holds sets of one, two and up to k members, each copied whole from the one below.
- **The rewrites ask every name at every node.** `Term::substitute` (`curios-core/src/term.rs`) and `read_outside_spines` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) keep a node only where it mentions no solved name, which they learn by asking `mentions_free` of each solved name in turn, and find a variable's value by a linear search of the solutions: k names over n nodes are k·n probes, nearly all of which one comparison of the node's newest local with the oldest solved local settles.
- **Every other carrier is read by a rebuild.** `Bound::free_vars` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`) answers for a telescope, a vector of terms and a bare subterm by a traversal with no memo that rebuilds what it reads, every path of it, though each term inside holds its set. The kernel asks it of every judged item's registry telescopes on every check (`free_locals_of`, `curios-core/src/module.rs`, called from `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`), the store of every unit it files and the prelude's build of theirs (`validate_stored_identities`, called from `curios-verdicts/src/verdicts.rs` and `curios-prelude-archive/elaborate.rs`), and the lowering of every induct and struct (`curios-text/src/into_core/references.rs`, `audit.rs`).
- **A metavariable's set is rebuilt from its spine.** `Subterm::any_child_term` hands a metavariable's every spine entry to the fill, so a metavariable's set is the union of every entry's, rebuilt at every metavariable whose set is asked, where [the design rule](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) reads "a node's facts are read off its spine's, never off its entries" and `warm_scalars` reads them so; and `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`, `context.rs` and `elaborate/binding.rs` union or probe a spine's entries one by one.
- **A filled node starts a walk.** `Term::warm_frees` allocates a walk's stack before it learns the node is filled.
- **A whole set is cloned to ask one name.** `Term::free_vars` hands back an owned `BTreeSet`, which eight sites clone to test one member: the eta probes of `curios-core/src/machine.rs`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs` and `curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`, once for each binder; `curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`, `elaborate/apply.rs`, `elaborate/module.rs` and `convert.rs`'s scope check; and `curios-text/src/into_core/lowerer.rs`. An eta probe asks whether a body names the binders it just minted, newer than any local the body held, which the newest local decides.

Counted at `0fc2fc1bd`, release with the profile feature, `wonder diagnostics -` over `n` bindings `let x{i} = a + i;` in one function, then `let g = (n: Nat) => n + x0 + … + x{n-1};` and `g(1)`: 5.32, 17.14 and 61.53 G instructions and 7.43, 25.34 and 93.14 M allocations at 1 000, 2 000 and 4 000 bindings, where the same bindings summed with no lambda grow ×1.87 and ×1.95. From 1 000 to 2 000 bindings the fill copies 2.02 M and then 8.03 M members, and `Term::substitute` asks 5.0 M and then 20.0 M memberships, 10 005 of them found and 99.95% decided by the node's newest local; under gdb at 4 000, six of twelve samples stand in the fill or that substitution, under the elaborator's and erasure's `substituted_by_value`. Over `profile-prelude elaboration`, `warm_frees` allocates 4.6 M stacks for filled nodes.

## What is known

- **A set is the fact to keep.** Lean 4 keeps none: its `Expr.Data` packs a hash and flags, `hasFVar` among them, and a walk collects what a reader asks, remembering the nodes it visited ([`Expr.lean`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/Expr.lean), [`CollectFVars.lean`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/Util/CollectFVars.lean)); rustc keeps flags and the outermost binder a type reaches, combined from its parts where it is built ([`flags.rs`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_type_ir/flags.rs.html)); Rocq's kernel walks ([`vars.ml`](https://ocaml.org/p/coq-core/8.20.0/src/coq-core.kernel/vars.ml.html)). Here the readers ask a term's set at every scope check, by-value read and credit, and ask the terms nested in it again. Counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy, a remembered walk per read, pruned by the newest local, visits 58.5 M nodes over `profile-prelude elaboration` and 11.2 M over certification, each a hashed visit, where today's reads iterate 15.4 M and 3.8 M members; and a walk per probe leaves the substitution's square standing.
- **Merging smaller into larger is the peers' remedy.** GHC annotates each Core node with a deterministic set over integer-keyed maps, its locals alone, and inserts the smaller set into the larger ([`GHC/Core/FVs.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ghc/ghc/master/compiler/GHC/Core/FVs.hs), [`GHC/Types/Unique/DFM.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ghc/ghc/master/compiler/GHC/Types/Unique/DFM.hs)); Maziarz, Ellis, Lawrence, Fitzgibbon and Peyton Jones build every subterm's variable map smaller into larger in O(n log² n) ([Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02856)); a treap's union costs O(m log(n/m + 1)) for sets of m ≤ n members ([Blelloch and Reid-Miller](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/project/scandal/public/papers/treaps-spaa98.html), [Blelloch, Ferizovic and Sun](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.02120)). Bindlib's boxes carry a sorted list merged at every node, which is today's square ([`bindlib.ml`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rlepigre/ocaml-bindlib/master/lib/bindlib.ml)).
- **What the library fills.** Counted at `0fc2fc1bd` over `profile-prelude` in a scratch copy: elaboration copies 514 468 sets holding 3 147 229 members and certification 182 857 holding 1 131 399, at most 72 and 76; 94% hold sixteen or fewer and 99.99% sixty-four or fewer. Globals are 60% and 68% of the members copied, and the locals of a copied set alone never pass 32.
- **What the readers ask.** 88 sites in 37 files outside tests, which call nothing of the set but `iter`, `contains`, `len` and its iteration: about 38 ask one name, 9 a predicate of every member, 17 look each member up in a scope, and 25 collect a term's globals or its locals. Of the memberships asked over elaboration and certification, 96% and 100% name a local, and the newest local alone decides 58% and 60% of them.
- **Order is an output.** Readers iterate in `Free`'s order, globals before locals and locals by mint, and fold it into what they build, a solution list or a refusal's name; a scratch copy holding each representation below in ascending order kept both of `/std`'s image digests at one path.
- **What each representation costs the library**, counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy with stage 1's substitution and `warm_frees` (its other reads not prototyped), over `profile-prelude elaboration` and `certification`, each variant read twice and the batches interleaved; certification's readings repeat exactly, and elaboration's vary by up to 0.5% between runs of today's code.

## Settled

- **The set stays, shares, and merges smaller into larger.** A node shares its largest carrying child's set and unions the others' in, so it pays for the members its smaller children bring, each a logarithm: over the probe the members a fill takes in grow 3 287, 6 537, 13 037 and 26 037 at 250 to 2 000 bindings, ×2.0 a doubling, and the tree nodes it builds 15 475, 34 207, 75 147 and 164 003, ×2.2, where today's copies grow ×4.0 (counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy's model of the merge). Over any tree that is O(n log² n), Maziarz et al.'s bound; over a graph, two children whose equal sets were built apart pay the smaller's members in time and nothing in memory, the union handing back the larger.
- **The set is `curios-utilities`' treap at every size.** A `Sorted` set, whose shape is a function of the members it holds ([`curios-utilities`' README](../../../curios-utilities/README.md#a-persistent-maps-shape-is-a-function-of-the-keys-it-holds)), behind the node's one-word cell. The union takes the smaller operand's members the larger lacks into the larger, copying only the paths to what it adds, and hands back the larger where it adds nothing, an operand shared at its root met whole: O(m log n) for sets of m ≤ n members, the bound the merge above rests on. A slice for small sets, as Clojure's array map, Scala's `Set1` to `Set4` and Agda's one-word `VarSet` keep theirs ([`PersistentArrayMap.java`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clojure/clojure/master/src/jvm/clojure/lang/PersistentArrayMap.java), [`Set.scala`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scala/scala/2.13.x/src/library/scala/collection/immutable/Set.scala), [`VarSet.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agda/agda/master/src/full/Agda/Utils/VarSet.hs)), saves half a percent of the instructions and allocations and two to four points of peak, above, for a second representation with a union, an iteration and a threshold of its own, which copies a node's members whole below its threshold: the square this spec removes, capped at a size.
- **A metavariable's set is its spine's.** A spine counts the names its entries hold, each by the entries holding it, beside the set of them, added and taken out with an entry as its other facts are; a metavariable's fill reads that set and no entry.
- **Ascending `Free` order, held.** Iteration stays ascending, so no reader's output moves, and a reader of the locals alone starts at the first local.
- **No stored byte moves.** The cache is rkyv-skipped and filled again where a node is restored: verdicts, `/std`'s images and executable artifacts stay equal at one path.
- **The scalars answer first.** A membership asked of a local, and a probe for any of a set of names, are answered by the newest local where it decides, and by the set where it does not.

## Stages

1. **Reads by the facts.** `Term::mentions_free` answers a local by the newest local where it decides; a prepared set of names (`Names`, sorted, its oldest local held) answers `Term::mentions_any` by the newest local, then by the smaller of the two sets probing the other; `Term::substitute` and `read_outside_spines` keep a node by `mentions_any` and find a variable's value by its place among the sorted solutions; `warm_frees` returns at a filled node; each carrier's `Bound::free_vars` is the union of its terms' sets, read off their caches, and the default traversal goes. Check: a telescope over the doubling term in `every_walk_answers_a_doubling_term_in_its_own_size`; the substitution's set probes counted over the probe at 1 000, 2 000, 4 000 and 8 000 bindings, linear; the image unchanged at one path; both halves of `profile-prelude` no worse than today's in instructions, allocations, bytes and peak, counted.
2. **One accessor.** `Term::free_vars` hands out the node's set (`Frees`), a pointer's clone, in place of an owned `BTreeSet` and of `free_vars_shared`, ascending, with the locals from the least; the eight cloning sites ask `mentions_free`, and a site asking several names asks `mentions_any`. Check: `cargo xtask workspace lint`; the image unchanged at one path.
3. **A union, in `curios-utilities`.** `Sorted` gains the union above, with a combiner for a map's payloads (arithmetic 101's), and its in-order walk keeps its stack inline to a depth it states. Check: unit tests of the union's laws, of its shape as a function of its keys, of an operand handed back where nothing is added and of a walk past the inline depth; the members a union inserts and the nodes it copies counted over chained and balanced shapes.
4. **The node's set.** `Frees` holds a `Sorted` set, filled by sharing the largest carrying child's and taking in the others' by the union. Check: the fill's members taken in and nodes built, counted over the stage 1 grid, ×2.0 and about ×2.2 a doubling; the image unchanged at one path; both halves no worse than today's in allocations, bytes and peak, and their instructions read against the table above, counted.
5. **A spine's set.** `MetavarSpine` keeps its names counted and their set beside its facts, a metavariable's fill reads it, and the readers that walked a spine's entries for their names read it. Check: a spine's set after pushes, rewrites and captures equal to a recount over its entries; a metavariable's fill visits no entry; the image unchanged at one path.
6. **The growth.** The lambda probe joins `curios/src/tests/growth.rs` as a growth held through emission (`HELD_FRONT`, `curios/src/tests/instrumented/growth.rs`). Check: `growth_slopes` holds it under `HELD_SLOPE`.

## Verification and retirement

The spec retires with stage 6. What a node's fill costs and what a reader may ask go to `Frees`' and `Term::free_vars`' rustdoc, the union's contract to `Sorted`'s and to [`curios-utilities`' README](../../../curios-utilities/README.md#a-persistent-maps-shape-is-a-function-of-the-keys-it-holds), a spine's set to `MetavarSpine`'s, and the bound, with its logarithm, to the design rule's paragraph on what holds it, beside `every_walk_answers_a_doubling_term_in_its_own_size`.
