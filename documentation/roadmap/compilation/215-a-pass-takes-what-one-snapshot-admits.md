# A pass takes what one snapshot admits

Not refined yet. Five passes of the continuation optimizer rewrite one candidate a call, so its fixpoint runs a round of every pass for each candidate they hold, and a compile takes as many rounds as its program holds candidates. A sweep of any of the five moves code the corpus emits, which is what keeps them from being a finding. `split_windows`, which was the sixth, takes every region it admits in one call, and its sweep moved none.

## What is missing

`optimize` (`curios-cont/src/optimize.rs`) runs its passes in rounds until a round changes nothing, each pass reading the module whole, so a pass that rewrites one candidate and returns is drained a round at a time. The tree names the shape where it measures it, in the comment on the fixpoint's `pass!` and the doc of `fixpoint_pass_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/fixpoint.rs`), and states the rule where two passes follow it: `split_parameters` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/fields.rs`) and `eliminate_dead_parameters` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/simplify.rs`) each take every candidate one snapshot admits, decline what an earlier rewrite of the sweep has touched, and leave to later rounds only what a rewrite depends on. Five passes take one candidate a call:

- `specialize_jump_patterns`, `specialize_call_patterns` and `specialize_scc_calls` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/specialize.rs`) take the first pattern or context in node order, each under a budget of 64 clones a module. `specialize_scc_calls` gives a reason, that one clone a call keeps the fixpoint deterministic; `split_parameters` sweeps in a stated order and is as deterministic.
- `split_workers` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/fields.rs`) takes one parameter of one function.
- `uncurry_returns` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/uncurry.rs`) takes the first class it can plan.

The census's `TOML_DRIVER` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/census.rs`), sixteen lines that decode standard input and print a map's length, settles in 33 rounds and compiles in 6.69 s. Its first round leaves 15,481 live nodes and its twenty-fourth 8,579, and from the thirteenth round on a round takes 137 to 146 ms whichever pass fires in it. `specialize_jump_patterns` fires in each of the first twenty-eight. Its first call finds 117 patterns over 66 joins, other passes dissolve most of them over five rounds, and from the sixth round 23 stand at once and leave one a round: each is a resume join handed a `/sys/Result` of known tag, in `/std/Parse`'s `chain`, `label`, `lookahead`, `or` and `map`, in `/std/Toml` and in the entry. `uncurry_returns` fires in nineteen of the rounds, `split_workers` in seven and `specialize_call_patterns` in four. A program of the corpus takes at most two jump patterns and settles in 6 to 10 rounds, so the driver is what shows the shape: a round for each candidate, at what a round costs a module of its size.

With each of the five looping its one step until it finds none, the driver settles in 10 rounds, as a program of the corpus does, and compiles in 5.04 s; the loop builds a pass's analysis again for every candidate, so its first two rounds take twice what they did. What each loop moves in the text `wonder stage` prints for the driver and the corpus's twenty-six programs:

| pass looped | texts that move | the driver's rounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `specialize_jump_patterns` | the driver, `monad_async`, `parse_bindless`, `parse_manual`, `rope_push_peek` | 23 |
| `uncurry_returns` | the driver | 33 |
| `split_workers` | the driver, `walk_mirror_flat_acc` | 33 |
| `specialize_call_patterns` | the driver | 33 |
| `specialize_scc_calls` | none | 33 |
| all five | the driver and those five | 10 |

A text that moves is within 2.1 % of its size at `cont-optm` and 0.4 % at `wasm-optm`, larger for some and smaller for others. Counted on top of `653af23d2` through the release compiler, with `split_windows` sweeping as it has since, and three things added for the runs: a line at each round's close naming the passes that fired, a line at each jump pattern taken counting the patterns that stand, and each loop behind a switch read from the environment. Each program is printed by `wonder stage` at `cont-optm` and `wasm-optm` and its texts compared with those the tree prints as it stands. The two compile times are timed, each the median of seven readings of `wonder stage cont-optm` over the driver, the two interleaved on a quiet machine: 6.69 s with `split_windows` alone sweeping, its readings 6.66 to 6.73, and 5.04 s with the five loops beside it, 5.02 to 5.07. The round times are one compile's.

## Refinement

- **What a clone budget counts under a sweep.** The three specializers spend a module's 64 clones in node order on what stands when the pass is called. One clone a call spends the driver's on what each round's cleanup leaves, 28 of them over its rounds. The loop spends all 64 in the first call, on 117 patterns of which other passes would dissolve most, and takes none after it. Whether the budget is a round's, is spent on a ranking of what a clone buys, or is asked of the module's growth and not of a count, is not settled.
- **Where a specializer stands in the round.** Twenty-four of the corpus's programs take one pattern in their first round, `io/bind`'s resume handed a `/sys/Option`. Tried only in a round nothing else changed, where `split_windows` is tried, the jump loop takes 27 patterns in the driver and in twenty-three of those programs finds that one gone, and each of the twenty-three prints 363 to 4,241 bytes more at `wasm-optm`. So a pattern taken early buys what the settled module no longer offers, and a sweep stays where the pass is.
- **What an earlier rewrite of a sweep invalidates.** Read from each rewrite, and not yet run against one snapshot: two patterns of one join leave the original fewer transfers than the snapshot counts, and under two it is `inline_single_use_continuations`'; a join nested in a cloned one is copied with it, behind edges the snapshot does not hold; and a class whose call resumes into another class's return becomes that class's tail call once absorbed (`Resume::Retarget`), so two classes one snapshot holds apart are one after the first is rewritten. Each pass states what its sweep declines, as `still_admissible` does for `split_parameters`.
- **Whether the code a sweep moves is the better code.** Nothing was run. `xbench` times the corpus's workloads; the driver has no workload, and no program of the corpus is moved by `specialize_scc_calls`'s loop, though a grammar of two rules over `/std/Parse` prints 13 % less at `wasm-optm` under it. A fixture that each sweep moves, and a reading of what it runs in, come before the stage that lands the sweep.
- **What a round costs with nothing left to take.** A round in which one pass fires is about 140 ms over the driver's 8,560 live nodes. `fixpoint_pass_measurements`, read on top of `7ded6ac34`, has nine spans that are never under 9 ms a round, 127 ms between them, `cont::split_workers` at 25 ms, `cont::uncurry_returns` at 21 ms and `cont::split_parameters` at 17 ms the largest: each builds an analysis of the whole module, in most rounds to find no candidate. That is a second cost, which the sweeps leave standing in the ten rounds that remain, and whether it is this spec's is not settled.
- **Stages, once those are read**: one pass a stage, each with the corpus's texts compared and the programs it moves read; then `fixpoint_pass_measurements`'s record, whose conclusions the tools findings hold, retaken.
