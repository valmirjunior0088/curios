# The second door costs its key's graph

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) decides that a walk that only reads visits a node once. The reduction table's second door reads the two spellings it relates once per path, and that reading is not the question the door answers, which is also why it answers some spellings wrongly.

## What is wrong

The second door (`Caches::reduction_get`, `curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) answers a closed term that misses the exact table from the first key stored under the same universe-erased projection, the stored reduct carried through a substitution σ of that key's universe and sort metavariables (`AcrossUniverses`). No reduction rule reads a level, so the carried reduct is the asked term's exactly where the asked term is the stored key with σ substituted: the door answers a matching question. `AcrossUniverses::between` answers it with two flat lists, each side's levels and sorts as `universes_in_order` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`) meets them. That walk is a rewriting walk (`Visit::rewriting_universes_scoped`, no memo) read as a list of positions, and the one reading is three defects:

- **It reads per path.** A shared node is read once per path to it: a hit over a doubling key at `Type(?u)` grows fourfold with every two levels of depth. One `/std` key shares a subterm carrying universe data, a recursive group in `/std/Map/Node`'s insert, read as 1,037 levels by occurrence and 931 by a walk over the pair, and the door declines it.
- **It skips a position a rewrite may pass over.** A rewriting walk passes over a node holding no universe data, a ground `Type 0` among them, which `Mode::MaskingLevels` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`) states a comparison that aligns levels by position must not do. Once `first(Type 1, Type 0)` is stored, `first(Type 0, Type 1)` is answered `Type 1`, and with metavariables `?u` is mapped to `?v` across two positions that do not correspond. A fixture reaches it; `/std` and the corpus do not.
- **It lists levels without the structure they stand in.** A recursive group that binds its own universe parameters and the same group binding none, an opened instance whose `u` is the enclosing declaration's, list the same levels and erase alike, and the door serves the first's reduct for the second: 14 hits over `/std`, on `/std/Eq/Eq`'s group and `/sys/Option`'s, none of which moves the image.

Carrying the reduct rests on a fact nothing states. `AcrossUniverses::carried` substitutes σ through a hook that reads no universe-binder depth, where the solver's zonk shifts a solution past the binders it crosses (`substitute_universe_metas_scoped`, `curios-core/src/scope.rs`): a metavariable sent to a declaration's parameter would land unshifted under a recursive group's own universe binder, naming the group's parameter instead. It never does, because a group binding universe parameters is a published one, closed and with every level solved, so no metavariable stands under its binder; the carry is right by that fact alone.

## Context

- **What the door carries.** Counted at `68ca73258` over `/std`'s elaboration: 3,277,136 probes hit the exact table, 524,336 find nothing under the erased key, the door answers 351,126 self entries without aligning anything, aligns 25,586 times and serves 19,311, declining the rest. A served map's level entries send a metavariable to a metavariable 27,409 times, to a ground level 2,670, to a parameter 1,778 and to anything else 114; its sort entries send one to a metavariable 3,658 times, to `Prop` or `Type` 1,405 and to a parameter 277. Only 65 served hits are the stored key with its levels solved, the case the door was introduced for; what it carries is two fresh instances of one term.
- **What it saves.** With the door answering nothing past the exact table, `/std` spends 66,126,916 units where it spends 65,545,426, 0.89% more, spread over 5,876 declarations, the largest rising by 12,420 units and the heaviest not at all. Each of the corpus's 26 programs reaches the door 0 to 37 times, at most 7 levels to a key, and none meets a defect.
- **What it rests on.** By level-parametricity a reduct is a function of its key's spelling, so every universe metavariable of the key is a variable of the match whether the solver has solved it or not: 4,265 served hits name one the solver has solved or identified with another, and every one equals what a fresh reduction answers. What else a reduction reads is kept out of the reduct's levels: a definition is closed and instantiated at the key's own levels, an answer conversion gave from a level constraint leaves the reduction remembering nothing (`reduce`'s `remember`), and an instance the reduct holds is opened again at the carried levels. A term metavariable's solution is the one channel left open: 72 served hits hold one, each with a reduct holding universe metavariables its key does not.
- **What it answers.** Every door answer over `/std` reduced again with the door bypassed equals the fresh reduct, all 68,570 self entries and all served hits but 28. The 28 have one shape, a recursive group's projection the stored reduction named and the asked spelling's own reduction leaves standing: 5 are the group-binder hits above, and the other 23 a fact about how an instance is known that [the theory findings](../theory/000-findings.md) hold. The two spellings convert, and they are what moves seven `/std` bodies when the door is removed.
- **How the elaborator reads two spellings' universes elsewhere.** Conversion and a refinement key's hit both read them by one walk over the pair shared with the kernel, `Term::level_differences`, a refinement key erasing universes and deciding its levels at the read ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md), [Case equations and their key](../../design/soundness/case-equations-and-their-key.md)). The door is the one reader of two spellings' universes that does not. The walk pairs two nodes' sorts beside their levels under a reading that asks (`Families::pairs_sorts`), and `substitute_universe_metas_scoped` carries a substitution of universe metavariables as the zonk carries a solution, which is what the door needs of `curios-core`.
- **Peers.** None reuses a reduct across universe instances. Lean 4's `whnf` cache keys the exact expression and caches a closed term holding no expression metavariable, whatever level metavariables it holds, so two instances of one term are two entries ([`WHNF.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Meta/WHNF.lean)); Rocq's unfolding table keys a constant with its universe instance, hashing the constant alone and comparing the instance ([`cClosure.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/cClosure.ml)). Lean's tabled typeclass resolution keys a goal up to a renaming of its metavariables, level ones included, numbered in order of first occurrence, and reopens an answer by unification ([`SynthInstance.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Meta/SynthInstance.lean)). Tabled logic programming names the door's reuse: call subsumption answers a call from a more general one, where variant tabling answers it only from a renaming, and its soundness rests on answers being monotone under specialization ([XSB](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.5123), [subsumptive tabling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.5556)); the door's version of that premise is level-parametricity. Equality and matching over shared terms are walks over pairs of nodes, decidable in linear time ([Sharing equality is linear](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.06101)).

## Goal

The door serves a spelling the stored reduct carried through σ exactly where the asked spelling is the stored key with σ substituted for the key's universe and sort metavariables, it finds σ by one walk over the pair in the pair's graph, and it carries σ as the solver carries a solution.

## Decisions

- **The door stays, and is a matching.** Level-parametric reduction is a decided rule, so a reduct rests on its term's spelling up to its universe metavariables, and a table may state that exactly. It is the pattern a refinement key already follows: the key erases universes and the read decides the levels.
- **The walk is the one conversion reads levels by.** The door reads `Term::level_differences` under a reading of its own that names no family, reads no former as its node, sets nothing apart as curried and pairs sorts. Two spellings apart in anything but universes, a recursive group's universe context among them, end the walk.
- **σ is proposed by the pairs and accepted by the contract.** Each pair the two spellings differ in has a bare metavariable on the stored side or the hit declines, and a metavariable sent to two levels declines; the hit is served only where the stored key carried through σ is the asked spelling. That one comparison holds what the walk does not report: a metavariable the asked spelling keeps at a position where σ would move it, and a level kept as it stands that names a metavariable σ moves.
- **σ is carried as a solution is.** A level σ sends a metavariable to is stated at universe-binder depth zero and is shifted past the binders above each position it lands at, through `substitute_universe_metas_scoped`, the walk the zonk runs; a pair under a universe binder declines, a level there being its group's to read. So the carry is right by the rule that carries a solution, and rests on no fact about where groups binding levels come from.
- **The match reads spellings as written, and the door stays in `Caches`.** A solved metavariable is a variable of the match like any other, since the reduct is a function of the spelling, so the door reads no solver.
- **A query or a stored reduct holding a term metavariable goes to the exact table alone.** That closes the channel a solution opens; a self entry, which carries nothing, is served as before. It declines 72 served hits over `/std`.
- **The per-occurrence walk goes.** `universes_in_order`, `rewrite_universe_levels_scoped`, `Visit::rewriting_levels_scoped` and `Visit::rewriting_universes_scoped` have no other reader, and the three tests that hold them are restated against the walk over the pair.

## Rejected

- **Retiring the door.** It removes the mechanism and every defect above, costs 0.89% of `/std`'s elaboration spread thin, and no peer keeps one. It is rejected because the reuse rests on a decided rule this table can state exactly, and what it saves grows with every universe-polymorphic name a program uses, each use minting instances of its own.
- **A walk of the door's own beside `level_differences`.** A second statement of where two terms' universes stand against each other; the per-occurrence walk is how such a second statement drifts from the first, `MaskingLevels` stating the rule it broke.
- **A reading that reports agreeing pairs**, so that σ is checked position by position. The acceptance holds the same thing in one comparison and leaves the walk reporting what conversion asks of it.
- **Reading each pair through the solver**, as conversion's identification does. Identification commits levels and so must read them as they stand; the door commits nothing, and read through the solver it would decline a key whose metavariable is solved for an asked spelling that is a correct instance of the key as written.
- **Keys up to a renaming of their metavariables**, as Lean's typeclass tables and [hashing modulo α-equivalence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02856) key theirs. They cover the renamings, most of what the door serves, but not a metavariable sent to a ground level or a parameter, and the canonical numbering is a walk at every probe, where the erased projection is remembered on the node.
- **A table keyed by the solved spelling.** It covers the 65 hits that are a key with its levels solved, which is not what the door carries.
- **The per-occurrence walk, memoized.** Skipping a revisit on one side pairs the wrong members, as the alignment's own comment says.

## Stage

**The door matches over the pair.** In `curios-elab`: `AcrossUniverses` on the walk under a reading of its own, σ carried as a solution, the acceptance, the term-metavariable restriction, and the per-occurrence walk retired from `curios-core`. Fixtures beside `a_closed_reduct_is_served_across_universe_spellings`, each failing before: a ground sort at another position, a group binding its own levels against one binding none, and a query holding a solved term metavariable, each reduced afresh; a row in `curios-elab`'s `every_walk_answers_a_doubling_term_in_its_own_size` serving a hit over a doubling key at `Type(?u)` sixty levels deep. Check: `curios-elab`'s and `curios-core`'s tests by name, the lint, which elaborates and certifies `/std`, and `format`; the image may move where the restriction reduces afresh, and the declarations it moves are named.

## Verification

The fixtures above; `reduction::across_levels` samples the door's hits over `/std`; the counts above retaken by the protocol below show no hit served with nothing to rewrite and every served hit equal to its fresh reduct but the shape the theory finding holds.

## Retirement

Once the stage lands, `AcrossUniverses`'s documentation carries the matching and what it rests on, [A reduct states what it rests on](../theory/200-a-reduct-states-what-it-rests-on.md) no longer gives the door as its reason for the two clears at a witness's universe closing, and this specification is deleted.

## Protocol

Counted on top of `68ca73258` in a copy of the tree, by counters added for the run and not checked in: at each exit of `Caches::reduction_get` (exact hit, nothing under the erased key, self entry, served, declined), over each served map's entries by what the asked side is, at `Context::cached_reduced` whether the stored key zonked is the asked spelling, whether it holds a term metavariable or names a solved universe metavariable, and whether the reduct holds a universe metavariable the key does not, and at `Spend::restore` each declaration's units; one build with the door answering nothing past the exact table; one reducing every door answer again with the door bypassed and comparing the two; one writing each `/std` definition's name, type and body hashes, universe context and variances, with the door and without, compared line by line. The corpus figures are each program put to `wonder diagnostics` with the same counters. The doubling key's growth is a fixture's, timed once a depth, and is argued by the fixture rather than by the figure.
