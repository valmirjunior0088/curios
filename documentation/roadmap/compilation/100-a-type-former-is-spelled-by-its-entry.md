# A type former is spelled by its entry

[A nominal declaration is held as its entries](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nominal-declaration-is-held-as-its-entries) states that a former's type, body, universe context, totality and variance vector are each a function of its entry, read off it and never stored beside it, so that no module holds a former that declares another scheme than its entry. Each is stored beside the entry today, with three more parts, and nothing holds the two to each other: the certifier admits a module where they disagree, two of its tests pin the admission, and the elaborator reads a scheme from whichever copy is in hand.

Working specification for a module that states a nominal declaration once, by its entry: an item that declares a family, a record or a concept holds each name it declares a former under with that name's entry and nothing beside it, and a former, as a constructor already is, is made of its entry where it is read. Its next step is stage 1, a delivery.

## What this builds on

- **A constructor is made of its entry.** Lowering writes no constructor ([A former declares its constructors and its methods](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-former-declares-its-constructors-and-its-methods)): `Item::constructors` makes each from its family's entry by one door (`Made::constructors`, `curios-core/src/definitions.rs`) as a view no item holds, which says what it is made as (`MadeKind::Constructor`) and is no definition, a constructor's name is placed as one its item makes and the view made the first time it is read is kept (`Place::Constructor`, `Places::constructor`, `curios-core/src/places.rs`), both checkers' readers of a name in scope answer it through that place (`held`, `curios-cert/src/certifier/globals.rs`; `Defined`, `curios-elab/src/established.rs`), and the certifier defines it once its family's entry is accepted (`curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`). Nothing judges a constructor apart from its entry.
- **A former is spelled from its entry by one door.** `former_spelling` (`curios-core/src/definitions.rs`) spells a former's type and body from an entry's context, arity and result sort, for `Definition::former` and for an occurrence of the former's name, which elaboration writes as that body at the occurrence's levels (`spelled_former`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate.rs`), so no elaborated term holds a former's name ([A type former applied in full is its node](../../design/theory/a-type-former-applied-in-full-is-its-node.md); `curios-prelude`'s `no_term_of_the_prelude_names_a_type_former`). A stored former's type and body are read as the meaning of no elaborated term: only Core text writes a former's name.
- **An entry is judged whole.** `check_induct_decl` and `check_struct_decl` (`curios-cert/src/certifier/module.rs`) type every parameter, index and field as a type and require a literal sort (`check_arity`, `check_signature`, `check_declared_sort`), and the walk's boundary holds an entry's terms to their residue and to the parameters its scheme declares (`induct_residue`, `struct_residue`, `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`). These are every premise a spelled former's type and body rest on, the `InductType` and `StructType` nodes being typed off the entry the environment holds.
- **A read of a former reads its entry already.** `spelled_former` reads the entry where the name's type was read (`Context::induct_decl` records `Question::Induct`), so a former read off its entry adds nothing to what the incremental record keeps.
- **A declaration's levels are its own.** [A group binds no universe](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-group-binds-no-universe): a group's members are read under the context of the item that holds them. An entry states its own context and every occurrence of its family or record states an instance the certifier holds to that context's width (`Certifier::check_instance`).

## What is wrong

- **A former stores eight parts beside its entry.** A nominal declaration's entry is held by the definition that is its former (`DefinitionKind::Nominal`), which stores a type, a body, a universe context, an island, a proof mark and a value sort, and files a totality and a variance vector for its name. Each is a function of the entry: `Definition::former` spells the first three; the island is the entry's `module`, both being the lowering's prefix (`curios-module/src/into_core.rs`); a former is no proof; a former that takes nothing has its entry's result sort as its value's sort, which elaborated Core text states on every such record, `#{value_sort = Type} struct /Unit: Type {}`, and which a family that takes nothing, a group's member, states none of, `induct /E: Type`; a former calls nothing, so it is total; and a former outside a group holds each level as its entry does, a member of one filing no vector. `std_former_derivation` (`curios-pipeline/src/standard/tests.rs`, counted at `6a8f997d1`) spells each former of `/sys` and `/std` from its entry, 82 families, 95 records and 19 concepts, and finds the four parts it compares alike in all 196.
- **The certifier admits a disagreement.** It types a former as a definition and checks its entry as a declaration, and no judgment compares the two. `recheck::registry_tests::a_registry_and_its_type_former_may_declare_different_schemes` certifies a former declaring another scheme than its entry, in both directions, and `recheck::implied_tests::a_former_stated_otherwise_is_read_as_it_is_stated` a former stated as another proposition with a proof. No `False` comes of either, since the certifier reads a former's name by its stored definition and its node by its entry, each agreeing with itself; the second is the module a reader taking the name for the node would admit a proof of anything in. That is the coherence the decision's rationale calls a discipline of every reader, the shape of [a name declared twice](../../../xboard/tickets/admission/2026_10_06_a_name_declared_twice_means_two_things_to_the_certifier/metadata.toml) at one name.
- **The elaborator reads both.** An entry is elaborated once, and its former published as the entry spells it (`elaborate_module_nominal`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`), but a scope still answers a former's name by the definition stored for it: `reference` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate.rs`) instantiates a former's stored scheme and `spelled_former` spells the entry at that instance; `Context::carrying` writes the finished entry into the former; and the universe gate (`curios-elab/src/universes/gate.rs`) holds the former's context to its entry's and keeps a definition's scheme and an entry's in separate maps (`Schemes`).
- **Every family is a group.** The lowering writes a lone family as a group of one so that it takes the group's elaboration (the comment above `lowered.items.push(FlatItem::Rec(type_flat_items))`, `curios-module/src/into_core.rs`), and a group of formers is a group no member's type or body names: a former's domains naming its item are refused (`indexed_by_its_group`, `indexed_by_its_cycle`), and its body names its family by path. A family's name therefore unfolds to a projection out of its group, which conversion reads back as the node it builds: `Term::applied_former`, `bare_former` and `curried_former` (`curios-core/src/term.rs`), read by `Term::level_differences` under `Families::reads_formers` (`curios-core/src/variance.rs`) and by the elaborator's conversion (`bare_family` and the applied-former pair, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`), each reading a group that holds nothing.
- **A definition says what it was written as, or that it is a former.** `DefinitionKind` holds a written definition, a witness, a test, or a former with its entry (`Nominal`); a constructor is no kind of definition, and Core text's `kind` takes `Witness` and `Test` alone (`kind_read`, `curios-surface/src/core_text/item.rs`). A former is still the one name an entry declares that an item holds as a definition.
- **A concept states its record's context and labels again.** `ConceptDecl::universe_context` (`curios-core/src/concept.rs`) is its record's, held equal by the universe gate twice (`validate_universes`, `validate_definitions_instance_arities`), and `ConceptDecl::fields` is the record's field labels, a superclass field's empty, as the lowering and Core text each build them (`field_labels`, `concept_entry_read`); Core text's `concept = {…}` decorator states either apart from the record. No judgment of the certifier reads a `ConceptDecl`.
- **Core text joins any kinds into a group.** Its grammar takes "declarations of any kinds joined by `and`" (`curios-surface/src/parse/core_text.rs`, mirrored in `editors/tree-sitter/core/grammar.js`), so a family and a definition can be one item, and a lone family or record states the item its keyword does not make under `node = Let` or `node = Rec`; the program's grammar groups by kind ([Recursive groups](../../language.md#recursive-groups)).
- **Obligation (T)'s registry gate reads a family's indices through its former.** The gate walks a family's parameters and payloads and a record's parameters and fields (`curios-elab/src/totality/reach.rs`, whose `entries` walks an arity's parameters and stops at its indices); a family's index types are reached there only as positions of its former's type, so a former no longer stored would take them with it. The early net reads them off the entry as `ErasedSite::IndexOf` (`check_entry_totality`), and the certifier's (T) reads the positions `check_arity` records, indices among them.

## Peers

- **Lean 4** states a family once, as its constant's type: `InductiveVal` extends `ConstantVal`'s name, level parameters and type with counts, `numParams` and `numIndices`, its block's members and its constructors' names, and a declaration takes one list of level parameters for its mutual block, copied to each constant ([`Declaration.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Declaration.lean)). A constructor is a constant restating the block's parameters in its own type, which the kernel compares with them, refusing an argument that "does not match inductive datatypes parameters", and every occurrence of the block is held to "the parameters and universe levels of the mutual declaration" ([`inductive.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/inductive.cpp)).
- **Rocq** stores an inductive's arity several ways: `mind_arity_ctxt` with `mind_sort`, `mind_user_arity` "convertible to [mkArity (mind_arity_ctxt, mind_sort)]", its block's parameters in `mind_params_ctxt` and again inside each arity context, its constructors as written (`mind_user_lc`) and head-normalized (`mind_nf_lc`), and its universes once for the block (`mind_universes`) ([`declarations.mli`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/declarations.mli)). Its independent checker rebuilds an entry from a stored body (`to_entry`), types it again and compares the result field by field, passing some fields through untested and leaving the typing flags to a TODO ([`checkInductive.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/checker/checkInductive.ml)).
- **MetaCoq** stores `ind_type` beside `ind_indices` and `ind_sort` and makes their agreement a clause of a well-formed body, `ind_arity_eq`, with parameters and universes on the mutual block ([`EnvironmentTyping.v`](https://github.com/MetaCoq/metacoq/blob/coq-8.20/common/theories/EnvironmentTyping.v)).
- **Agda** keeps a datatype's telescope in its definition's type and counts and a sort beside it (`_dataPars`, `_dataIxs`, `_dataSort`), and a record's field telescope beside its constructor's type under "Thus, @recTel@ is redundant" ([`Base.hs`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/TypeChecking/Monad/Base.hs)).
- **GHC** caches a type constructor's arity and variables from its binders in the one function that makes it, and stores its kind as written, "equal to `mkTyConKind tyConBinders tyConResKind` only up to type synonym expansion", so that a message keeps the synonyms its user wrote; comments state the equation and no code holds it ([`TyCon.hs`](https://github.com/ghc/ghc/blob/master/compiler/GHC/Core/TyCon.hs), Note [Preserve user-written TyCon kind]).

Taken: one statement of a declaration's telescope, read wherever a former is asked for, as Lean and Agda keep theirs, and a group of declarations judged as one item, as every peer judges a mutual block. Left: a second copy held to the first by a comparison every checker runs (Rocq's checker, Lean's constructor parameters), by an equation (MetaCoq) or by a comment (Agda, GHC). Curios already has the one statement in the entry: every node is typed off it and every constructor made of it, a constructor's payload held under its family's parameters by construction rather than compared with them. The former is the copy that goes.

## Decisions

- **An item holds a nominal declaration as its entries.** A third kind of item, a nominal one, holds each name its declaration declares a former under with that name's entry, in declaration order: one for a lone declaration, several for a group, families, records or concepts but never two kinds at once, as a group is written. It holds nothing else: no former is a `Definition`, its island is its entries' module, and its scheme is the one its entries share, as a group's members share their item's: a group's entries name one another at the item's own instance, and erasure finds every name of an item at one count of sorts, so entries under two schemes are no item. Its entries are accepted together, as a group's are today, and a group's own refusals, members that do not all reach one another and a former indexed by its group, are its. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **A name an entry declares is made of it where it is read.** A former and a constructor are each made by its one door (`former_spelling`, `Made::constructors`) and placed as a name its item makes, read as a `Made` saying what it is made as, `Place::Constructor` widened to both, keeping what is made the first time the name is read. A made former is total, applies nothing, holds the entry's variance vector, binds no proof, and where it takes nothing its value's sort is the entry's result sort. Both checkers answer a name in scope through that place, `held` and `Defined` reading a former by the arm a constructor is read by. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **A definition says what was written.** `DefinitionKind` states what a definition an item holds was written as, a definition, a witness or a test, and nothing an entry declares: `Nominal` leaves it as a constructor's kind has, and a made name is read as a view no item holds, saying which it is by `MadeKind`. A held definition then states itself neither a former nor a constructor, and `Item::declared` reads what it holds as it is. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **The certifier judges a nominal item by its entries.** It accepts the entries, then defines each former and each constructor as its entry makes it, where it defines a constructor today; nothing types a former apart from its entry, so a former at another type or scheme is as unrepresentable in a module as in a term. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **The elaborator reads a former off its entry.** An entry is elaborated once ([A former declares its constructors and its methods](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-former-declares-its-constructors-and-its-methods)); once no former is stored, a scope answers a former's name from its entry, `carrying`, the gate's comparisons of a former with its entry and `Schemes`' separate maps go, and obligation (T)'s registry gate reads a family's indices off its entry as `ErasedSite::IndexOf`, as its early net does. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **Core text writes a nominal declaration as its form alone.** A group is nominal declarations of one kind or definitions, by the grammar and the tree, so a mixed group is no production; a nominal form's decorator sets its entry's own parts, its module, representation and vectors, and a group's first form the scheme its entries share, as a `let` group's first member states the group's, so the former's `type_`, `body`, `universe_context`, `island`, `proof` and `value_sort` decorators, `entry = {…}` and a nominal form's `node` are each refused as a field its node does not hold ([Core text is Core in the program's grammar](../../../curios-surface/README.md#core-text-is-core-in-the-programs-grammar-and-reads-back-as-the-term-it-writes)); and `kind` takes `Witness` and `Test`. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **A concept is its record and its superclass edges.** `ConceptDecl` keeps `supers`, read off its record's fields where they are elaborated, and its context and method labels are read off its record. Decided on 2026-11-17.
- **What a group of formers held goes with it.** `Term::applied_former`, `bare_former`, `curried_former`, `Families::reads_formers` and their readers in both conversions read only a group of formers, so they are deleted where the last such group is. Decided on 2026-11-17.

## Stages

1. **A module holds a nominal declaration as its entries.** The nominal item, its formers made and placed beside its constructors, `DefinitionKind::Nominal` gone; the lowering writing the item, and every reader of an item, erasure and both checkers' walks among them, taking it as a kind of its own; the certifier judging it by its entries; the registry gate reading a family's indices; Core text's grammar, tree, printer and reader, `editors/tree-sitter/core/grammar.js` with its committed `src/` and the Zed pin; the conversions' group readers deleted. Check: a Core text decorator on a former refused as a field its node does not hold, a mixed group failing to parse, and a former's name typed by its entry's spelling in both checkers, replacing the two certifier tests that pinned the admission; a partial index type refused under its family as an index; `std_former_derivation` deleted with the copy it compares; every verdict over `/std` and `programs/`, every pinned refusal and the emitted Wasm equal; `cargo xboard report` unchanged.
2. **A concept reads its record.** `ConceptDecl` holds its superclass edges alone; the universe gate's two comparisons of a concept's context with its record's go; Core text's `concept` decorator sets `supers` alone. No prerequisite. Check: every concept of `/std` resolves as before, every verdict and emitted module equal.

## Verification

`cargo xtask workspace lint` and the suites a stage reaches after each stage, its checks by name first, `curios-cert`'s and `curios-elab`'s among them and `curios-prelude`'s `every_unit_of_the_prelude_reads_back_from_its_core_text` for the Core text stages. Stored bytes that may change: stage 1's in every unit declaring a nominal declaration and in the prelude's archive, whose size is named, stage 2's in every unit declaring a concept; a slot's address names the compiler that framed it, so a unit stored under another layout is not found rather than misread. Every verdict, every refusal a test pins but the ones restated above and every emitted module stay equal. The full gate runs once, after the last stage.

## Rejected

- **A former materialized once where an item is made or restored**, every reader of a `Definition` left as it is, as GHC caches a type constructor's arity in the function that makes it: a group of formers cannot be stored as a group without stating its members' types and bodies, so the stored item takes a shape its form in hand does not and a proxy keeps the two in step, while in hand a former is still a `Definition` anyone can make or edit to disagree, and every family stays a group's projection.
- **Each copy kept and compared**, as Rocq's checker rebuilds an entry and compares the body it stored, or **an equation stating their agreement**, as MetaCoq's `ind_arity_eq`: a comparison every checker must run for each copy and keep in step with each new part, which is the discipline the decision rejects, and which Rocq's own checker leaves partial.
- **The former's type as the one statement**, the entry holding counts, as Lean and Agda keep a declaration: every node is typed off the entry's arity and every constructor's payload is held under its parameters by construction, which a count split off a type would turn into a comparison, as Lean's kernel compares a constructor's restated parameters with its block's.
- **A former's type kept as written for its reader**, GHC's preserved kind: a stored unit says what its items mean and not where they were written ([`curios-unit`](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-stored-unit-says-what-its-items-mean-not-where-they-were-written-or-how-they-were-come-by)), and a printer reads a node back to its former's name already.
- **One universe context held by a nominal item for its members**, as Rocq, MetaCoq and Lean hold a mutual block's: an entry is read alone wherever a registry answers a name, and a member names another at an instance the certifier holds to that member's own context, so members whose elaboration generalized them together sharing one context is the elaborator's policy rather than a fact a reader needs.
- **A former answered from the moment its entry is registered**, before its item is judged: a former is made where its constructors are, and a name the walk has not reached reads as unbound, as any item's does.
- **A mixed Core text group refused by the reader**: the grammar groups by kind as the program's does, so the form has no production rather than a rule.

## Completion and retirement

`curios-core`'s README restates its decision as an item holding a nominal declaration as its entries, with its former and constructors made of them, the rejected shapes above beside its own; `curios-cert`'s README states that a nominal item is judged by its entries and its names made of them; `curios-surface`'s README states a group by kind and a nominal form by its entries; [A type former applied in full is its node](../../design/theory/a-type-former-applied-in-full-is-its-node.md) loses its sentence on a recursive family's group projection. Once no reference to this specification remains, it is deleted.
