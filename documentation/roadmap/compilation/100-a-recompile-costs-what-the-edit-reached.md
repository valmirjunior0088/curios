# A recompile costs what the edit reached

[A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md) states that a recompile costs what its edit reached: a declaration the edit cannot have reached is neither redone, asked, decoded, copied, sorted, seeded nor stored, and five costs are bounded by something else. The code redoes no more than the edit reached and pays for everything it keeps: a respelled leaf of `/std` costs four questions of elaboration and 2.46 M allocations around them.

## What a recompile spends today

Counted at `28bcb9aba`, in allocations, with a release compiler built with `--features profile`. *One-shot*: `target/release/curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics <copy>/<file>` over a copy of `curios-std/src` holding no `.curios`, so the archived unit is the baseline, folded by `curios profile`. *Session*: `curios-wonder`'s protocol ([Measuring a question's latency](../../../curios-wonder/README.md#measuring-a-questions-latency)), its `Nat.crs` scenario over a copy of `curios-std/src`, initialized with no `workspaceFolders` so no warming check runs, opened with the leaf edit on disk and then two keystrokes held unsaved, each appending a fresh `_census_probeN`; a profile split by check. The leaf appends `pub let _census_probe(a: Nat) -> Nat = a;` to `Nat.crs`; the hub respells `xor(b, true)` as `xor(true, b)` in `/std/Bool/not`.

- **A leaf, one-shot**: 2.46 M, of which the declaration elaborated asks 4 questions. The lowering 1.16 M — its walk over every declaration 0.70 M, most of it decoding each kept declaration's lowered item out of the baseline's bytes (11 of 13 gdb samples in `Baseline::kept` under `Deferred::get`), and the baseline half restored whole 0.34 M; elaboration 0.82 M — the module sorted again 0.42 M because one item was made, recomputing every item's reach (free variables and construction heads) over the baseline's restored terms, the kept items gathered 0.26 M, decoding each, every kept declaration's trace asked 0.06 M for 102,048 questions; the certifier 0.35 M; erasure 0.07 M. Filing it: 0.45 M and a 47.2 MB slot.
- **A hub, one-shot**: 170 declarations elaborated again, 75.5 M; judging 18.7 M; 207 items erased again, 2.5 M; the module sorted again, 0.43 M; 5 void attempts.
- **A keystroke, in a session** (counted at `dad48d892`): one compile of 0.61 M — the lowering 0.22 M (its walk over every declaration 0.11 M, the exposure audit 35 K, audiences 28 K, interfaces 19 K, discovery 16 K, the edited file's parse 10 K), elaboration 0.26 M (every trace asked 56 K, the walk's setup over every declaration, the context seeded with every lowered entry among it, 44 K, the module sorted again 0.15 M), the certifier 58 K, erasure 41 K — and a store miss decided on its slot's record alone, 3.7 K. The edit's own work is a declaration of four questions.

Every row but the made declarations' own is a walk of the unit: each of the four records asked whole (the lowering's lookups, elaboration's trace, the certifier's reads, erasure's trace); the passes over the unit no record keeps (the exposure audit, audiences, interfaces, the `unused-declaration` lint, witness naming, seeding the elaborator with every lowered entry, the certifier's redeclaration check); the module ordered again; every part of the baseline materialized; and every part of the unit consed and written again. At least 501 of `/std`'s 2,822 items read `/std/Bool/not` transitively by name, against the 170 elaborated again (`wonder stage core-elab`, each name resolved by its shortest unambiguous suffix, only spellings holding a slash counted).

## What this rests on

- A declaration is a function of what it reads, never of where it is written or which declaration elaborated first ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)); a unit's items are stored in one order, given once where the module is made, and no order read off a record is stored.
- Which of a baseline's verdicts stand is the certifier's decision, from the reads its own walk filed ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)): no record another stage keeps is part of the trusted base.
- A unit compiled over a baseline is the unit a whole compile of the same text is, held to the same stored form ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md), `curios-pipeline/src/tests/incremental_tests.rs`); a unit is handed over and never copied, is stored in parts by who reads each, and says what its items mean and not how they were come by ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md)).

## Decisions

- **Each stage visits what moved, propagated through its own record.** Each record is indexed by what it read: for each name and each part of it (a signature, a body, a kind, a totality, its variances, a registry entry, a binding in an erased form), for each witness key (a witness standing there, whether one is declared there), and for whether the unit's lemmas are on offer, the declarations whose record read it. A recompile marks, from its seeds and through that index, every declaration a seed could reach, transitively. A marked declaration holds how many of the declarations it read are marked and undecided, and is decided when that count reaches zero, as Zig's `potentially_outdated` units are: where no part it read moved it stands, unasked; where one did it is found or made, and its parts are compared with what they were (`curios-elab/src/same.rs`) to flag the readers of each part that moved. A declaration never marked is the baseline's, unvisited. A declaration made again that reads something new asks for it, as the walk asks for any need today. Erasure and the certifier walk in the stored order, which already places each item after what it reads, so they mark and flag and need no count. The certifier's seeds are the items whose terms it finds are not the baseline's, and its index is of its own reads.
- **The seeds.** The declarations the lowering did not keep, the ones new to the unit, and the ones `Demand::recorded` would not keep (a place the baseline lacks, a witness not alone at its key, a poisoned reach); the names the unit gained or lost, read as absent or present; each part a record holds the digest of, of a unit in scope that moved, whose digest moved; the witness keys at which the unit declares another set of witnesses; and a declaration whose side of the offer of the unit's lemmas moved, the vocabulary's closure being computed again only where a mark reaches it. The lowering's seeds are the declarations whose text moved and the bindings the files' heads now give otherwise.
- **A record's index is made where a recompile first asks**, from the record, and kept with the unit as `Places` is; the unit compiled over it holds the index as a version with the edges of what it made replaced. A fresh process inverts each record it holds once.
- **A recompile makes a version of its baseline.** Every table a unit holds by declaration — its lowered items and the lowering's tables, its elaborated items and where it holds each name, its traces, its erased forms, its certification, and every index read off them — is a persistent map: a version shares every entry its baseline holds, replacing one costs a path, and two versions are compared by what they do not share, which is how the certifier finds the items it must judge and the store the facets it must write. A fact a pass over the unit reads today is a facet of a declaration with the reads that make it, or an aggregate over the records kept by their changes: the exposure audit a declaration's own; the `unused-declaration` lint a count of readers in the lowering's index; a module's interface a facet of its heads and of what it re-exports. The elaborator reads the lowering's entries where they stand, as it reads a scope. The stored order is the baseline's where each item made again reaches what the one it replaces reached and none was added or removed, and is given again over reaches kept with their items otherwise. Rationale: a unit is already an index of its declarations' facets handed over as that index, and Isabelle's document model holds versions the same way, "Document-content is persistent by default" and "content of common sub-structures of related document versions is shared to some extent" (Wenzel and Wolff, [Isabelle/PIDE as Platform for Educational Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.4835)); `imbl`'s `OrdMap::diff` "will avoid visiting nodes which are shared between the two sets"; an aggregate kept by its deltas is what incremental view maintenance makes of a query ([DBSP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16684)). Rejected: a unit assembled from every declaration, kept or made; a pass of word-sized comparisons over the unit as the end state, which is free at `/std`'s size and a walk of the unit all the same.
- **The persistent map is `imbl`'s**, named in `curios-utilities` alone and sealed behind a type of its own, as [one crate is the authority for one external concern](../../design/one-crate-is-the-authority-for-one-external-concern.md) requires, over `Rc` and archived through a `curios-archive` proxy as a sorted sequence. It is MPL-2.0, a file-level copyleft the workspace's Apache-2.0 takes as a dependency unmodified.
- **A unit is stored as its facets, each an object of the store named by the SHA-256 of its bytes**, and a slot holds the record and the unit's index: what every compilation after the unit restores, and for each declaration the digests of its facets. A filing writes the objects the store does not hold, in one pack named by its digest, then the slot, renamed into place, so a slot never names an object not yet written. A slot's record vouches for its index, and each object is held to its digest where it is first read. An elaborated declaration is consed alone, which is canonical by construction; the store's phase first counts what that costs, and keeps a node table for each pack, a declaration still named by the digest of its consed-alone form, where consed alone costs more than a quarter again of the embedded image's bytes or a tenth again of the allocations restoring `programs/`'s scopes. Objects are not split by part: a part's digest is computed as it is today (`same.rs`, `curios_elab::Foreign`, `curios_core::Abroad`). Rationale: Unison parses and typechecks a definition "once, and then store the results in a cache which is never invalidated" ([The big idea](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/)), and Bazel keeps a content-addressable store beside an action cache whose results name outputs by digest ([The many caches of Bazel](https://blog.engflow.com/2024/05/13/the-many-caches-of-bazel/)), where rustc "has to rewrite each file entirely in each compilation session" ([Incremental compilation in detail](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/queries/incremental-compilation-in-detail.html)). Rejected: a slot rewritten whole; each node an object of its own, a database of tens of thousands of entries per unit.
- **What no slot names is collected** where a filing finds a day has passed since the last collection: an object no slot names and unused for five days is removed, and a read renews an object's time at most once an hour, as Go's build cache trims ([`cache.go`](https://go.dev/src/cmd/go/internal/cache/cache.go)); git keeps "any object with modification time newer than the `--prune` date" for the same reason, a writer naming an object it has just written ([git gc](https://git-scm.com/docs/git-gc)).
- **A facet is found by what it was made from.** For each declaration's input to a stage — its text and scope for the lowering, its lowered form for elaboration, its elaborated terms for erasure and for judgment — the store holds every record a compile of it filed with the facet it made, and a declaration the edit reached is looked for among them before it is made, a candidate's reads compared by the digests its record holds of the parts it read, since the facet read is not in hand. The certifier looks among its own records alone. Rationale: "Once we are storing the complete result it makes sense to record many constructive traces per key" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), section 4.2.3); an edit undone, a branch checked out again and a bisection reach texts the store held before.

## What stays outside it

- **Anything smaller than a declaration.** A declaration made again is the one a whole compile makes, and reusing part of an earlier elaboration under a changed read is exact only up to the first read answered otherwise, which needs the elaborator's state at that read, as Lean 4 keeps a snapshot after a definition's header ([`Lean.Elab.DefView`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Elab/DefView.html)). A specification of its own, if a measurement asks for one.
- **A declaration parsed alone.** An edited file is parsed whole, at about 1.4 allocations a byte of `/std/Nat` at `28bcb9aba`; the largest file of `/std` is 46 KB.
- **A void attempt**, a cost of a declaration made rather than of what was reached: 5 in the hub's recompile.

## Stages

Each stage lands with this specification rewritten to the work that remains and its rationale moved to the owning document.

1. **The persistent map** in `curios-utilities`. Check: its tests — deterministic order, a diff after a replacement visiting what moved, an archive round trip.
2. **Elaboration propagates**: the trace's index, the seeds, marks and counts. Check: `incremental_tests` with a fixture for each seed kind, `std_recompile_reproduction`, `std_vocabulary_reach_reproduction`, and `std_replay_census` reporting nothing; the leaf's elaboration asks only the made declaration's questions.
3. **The certifier propagates**, over its own reads, from the items it finds changed. Check: `recheck::adoption_tests` and a mark case; [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md) restated.
4. **Erasure propagates.** Check: the erasure fixtures of `incremental_tests`.
5. **The stored order is kept** where reaches stand. Check: a respelled body sorts nothing.
6. **The lowering propagates**: its lookups indexed, its seeds from moved texts and bindings, a kept declaration's lowered item never decoded, its passes over the unit made facets or aggregates. Check: the leaf's lowering touches the edited declaration and the readers of what its heads changed.
7. **A recompile makes a version**: the unit's tables persistent, a baseline kept by default beneath the walk, the certifier's seeds found by a diff. Check: the session's leaf keystroke counted over `/std` and over the protocol's synthetic package at one, two and four times its declarations, the count not growing.
8. **The store holds objects**: the consed-alone count first, then packs, the index, verification and collection. Check: a filing after a leaf edit writes the objects that edit made and the index; a miss reads no object; two filers at once write the same objects.
9. **Facets found by what they were made from.** Check: an edit undone recompiles with nothing made.

## Verification

- **Differential**: every unit compiled over a baseline stores what a whole compile of its text stores, at every stage (`incremental_tests`, `std_recompile_reproduction`, `std_vocabulary_reach_reproduction`).
- **Census**: `std_replay_census` reports nothing differing and no declaration taken along.
- **Scaling**: the session's leaf keystroke does not grow with the package, and the one-shot leaf costs the edit's own work and the five named bounds.
