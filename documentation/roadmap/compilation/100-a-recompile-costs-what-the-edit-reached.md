# A recompile costs what the edit reached

[A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md) states that a recompile costs what its edit reached: a declaration the edit cannot have reached is neither redone, asked, decoded, copied, sorted, seeded nor stored, and six costs are bounded by something else. No stage redoes more than the edit reached or asks anything of what it did not reach: the lowering, elaboration, the certifier and erasure keep what the edit did not reach without visiting it, the stored order is revised by what was made, and a report spells by tables revised over the baseline's. A filing writes what its edit made and an index of digests. What remains is the baseline read whole where a process restores it: a respelled leaf of `/std` cost four questions of elaboration and 0.83 M allocations around them at `cb619d8ec`.

## What a recompile spends today

Counted in allocations, at the commit each row names, with a release compiler built with `--features profile`. *One-shot*: `target/release/curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics <copy>/<file>` over a copy of `curios-std/src` holding no `.curios`, so the archived unit is the baseline, folded by `curios profile`. *Session*: `curios-wonder`'s protocol ([Measuring a question's latency](../../../curios-wonder/README.md#measuring-a-questions-latency)), its `Nat.crs` scenario over a copy of `curios-std/src`, initialized with no `workspaceFolders` so no warming check runs, opened with the leaf edit on disk and then two keystrokes held unsaved, each appending a fresh `_census_probeN`; a profile split by check. The leaf appends `pub let _census_probe(a: Nat) -> Nat = a;` to `Nat.crs`; the hub respells `xor(b, true)` as `xor(true, b)` in `/std/Bool/not`.

- **A leaf, one-shot** (counted at `cb619d8ec`): 0.83 M, of which the declaration elaborated asks 4 questions, no other declaration is asked anything, and the lowering asks the lookups of the edited file's declarations alone. The lowering 0.32 M — the baseline half restored 73 K, each record and scan an object of its own and each path read where its segments lie; the tables a restored unit makes the first time a lowering asks, each over every declaration, audiences 51 K and what the lint's roots reach 29 K; the files read and compared 23 K; the records read backwards, once for the process — elaboration's and erasure's traces 40 K, the lowering's lookups 26 K, the certifier's reads 14 K; the certifier 0.30 M; the archives restored 38 K; erasure 91 K; elaboration 20 K, most of it the stored order's keys and readers made once for the process. Filing it, the address's first, writes every object: 0.55 M, a pack of 46.1 MB and a slot of 4.78 MB; over a store holding the unit, restoring the baseline costs 80 K and filing 0.10 M, a pack of 44 KB and the slot (a copy filed unedited first).
- **A hub, one-shot** (counted at `f72c8db38`): 188 declarations elaborated again, 88.7 M; 140 judged, 26.7 M; erasure 4.3 M; the module put in order by revising the baseline's, 0.21 M with the order's tables made once for the process.
- **A keystroke, in a session** (counted at `f72c8db38`): one compile of 36 K — the lowering 29 K (the files read and compared 14 K, the edited file's parse 10 K, its walk 4.5 K, the edited file's `use` declarations resolved 2.3 K), elaboration 2.3 K, the certifier 1.8 K, erasure 0.5 K — a store miss decided on its slot's record alone, 4.0 K, and the files record the session keeps with the unit, 11 K. The edit's own work is a declaration of four questions; every record is read backwards once, where the session's first check opens the file. Over the synthetic package of the scaling measurement ([Verification](#verification)), the keystroke's compile counts 40 K, 40 K and 42 K at one, two and four times its declarations, what grows being the files read and the modules passed, about fifty allocations a file, and the files record, about forty.

What the rows hold beyond the made declarations' own and the bounded costs — the files read and compared, the edited file's parse — is the baseline read whole by a process that restores it: the lowering's baseline half decoded, the tables a restored unit derives over every declaration, and the certifier's 0.30 M, of which a baseline in hand costs 1.8 K.

## What this rests on

- A declaration is a function of what it reads, never of where it is written or which declaration elaborated first ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)); a unit's items are stored in one order, given once where the module is made, and no order read off a record is stored.
- Which of a baseline's verdicts stand is the certifier's decision, from the reads its own walk filed ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)): no record another stage keeps is part of the trusted base.
- A unit compiled over a baseline is the unit a whole compile of the same text is, held to the same stored form ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md), `curios-pipeline/src/tests/incremental_tests.rs`); a unit is handed over and never copied, is stored in parts by who reads each, and says what its items mean and not how they were come by ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md)).

## Decisions

- **A recompile makes a version of its baseline.** Every table a unit holds by declaration — its lowered items and the lowering's tables, its elaborated items and where it holds each name, its traces, its erased forms, its certification, and every index read off them — is a persistent map: a version shares every entry its baseline holds, replacing one costs a path, and two versions are compared by what they do not share, which is how the certifier finds the items it must judge and the store the facets it must write. Rationale: a unit is already an index of its declarations' facets handed over as that index, and Isabelle's document model holds versions the same way, "Document-content is persistent by default" and "content of common sub-structures of related document versions is shared to some extent" (Wenzel and Wolff, [Isabelle/PIDE as Platform for Educational Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.4835)); `imbl`'s `OrdMap::diff` "will avoid visiting nodes which are shared between the two sets"; an aggregate kept by its deltas is what incremental view maintenance makes of a query ([DBSP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16684)). Rejected: a unit assembled from every declaration, kept or made; a pass of word-sized comparisons over the unit as the end state, which is free at `/std`'s size and a walk of the unit all the same.
- **The persistent maps are `curios-utilities`' own** (`Persistent` and `Ordered`, `curios-utilities/src/persistent.rs`): a treap whose shape is a function of the keys it holds, so two versions holding one set of keys are one tree. A table a unit stores is held in a map read back in a stated order — `Ordered`, by a declaration's place, or `Sorted`, by name, archived as its entries in that order (`KeyOrder`) — and never in `Persistent`, whose order follows the process; an index a walk only looks up in may be. Two versions are compared by the nodes they do not share (`Ordered::diff`): a shape that is a function of the keys is what lets two versions be walked node beside node.
- **A place is a key in hand and a rank on disk.** A unit compiled in a process holds each item at an `i64` position of an `Ordered` map, spaced apart where the module is made or restored; a recompile holds an item made again at the position of the one it replaces, and an item new to the unit at a position between its neighbours in the stored order, and gives every position again only where no position lies between them. What a unit stores names a place by its rank in the order, read off where it is filed, so a recompile stores what a whole compile stores. Rationale: a place that is a vector's index moves for every item after one inserted, so every table keyed by it — where the module holds each name, what each item erased to — would be made again for one declaration added. Rejected: an item new to the unit appended after every other, which keeps no place in the stored order, since what reads it then stands before it; ranks kept current by a tree with subtree counts, which a reader asking by place would pay a logarithm for on every read.
- **A facet is found by what it was made from.** A declaration's elaboration is found by what it was lowered to, and an item's erasure by the item, each stage's trace completed into its record where it is filed ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)); so is a verdict, the certifier's record of what judging a definition read completed with its own digest of each part read ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)).

## What stays outside it

- **Anything smaller than a declaration.** A declaration made again is the one a whole compile makes, and reusing part of an earlier elaboration under a changed read is exact only up to the first read answered otherwise, which needs the elaborator's state at that read, as Lean 4 keeps a snapshot after a definition's header ([`Lean.Elab.DefView`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Elab/DefView.html)). A specification of its own, if a measurement asks for one.
- **A declaration parsed alone.** An edited file is parsed whole, at about 1.4 allocations a byte of `/std/Nat` at `28bcb9aba`; the largest file of `/std` is 46 KB.
- **A void attempt**, a cost of a declaration made rather than of what was reached: 5 in the hub's recompile.
- **A lowering found by what it was made from.** An edit undone lowers the edited file's declarations again and nothing else, the lowering keeping every other by its text and the names it resolved; its record of lookups can be completed as the other stages' are, under a specification of its own, where a count of a checkout that moves many files asks for it.

## Stages

Each stage lands with this specification rewritten to the work that remains and its rationale moved to the owning document.

The lowering makes a version: a module the edit did not reach is passed unentered, its tables are the baseline's revised by what its walk lowered and dropped, and its passes over the unit are kept by their changes ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](../../design/compilation/a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)). Elaboration makes a version: the elaborated module's items and where it holds each name are held by place, its traces and the table of what they read of other units by name, each a version of the baseline's; its walk holds the declarations the edit could reach, grouped as the lowering's items make them (`curios_core::Declarations`), seeds the context with their lowered entries alone, and the baseline answers for every other declaration from the start (`curios-elab`'s `demand`). The certifier makes one too: it finds the items to judge by comparing the two modules by what they do not share (`curios_core::Items::changed_since`), visits those, the declarations whose filed reads reach them and the items the elaborator made again, reads every other declaration where the baseline holds it once its walk has passed it, and files the baseline's record revised where it judged or dropped a declaration (`curios-cert`'s `Changed`, `curios_core::Revision`). So does erasure: its unit's forms and its index of names are held by place, a version of the baseline's unit where the module is a version of the baseline's, the module's projection is the baseline's revised at the places that changed and is handed to the unit, and its walk visits what the elaborator made and what read it (`curios-elab`'s `ErasedOver`, `ErasedIndex`, `Projection::revised`). And the unit is one: the stored order is the baseline's revised by what was made ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)), the module is held to being finished by its items made alone (`Zonked::revised`), and what a report spells the unit by — its paths, its lexicon and where its reader stands — is the baseline's revised at what changed, made where a report first asks and once for a baseline that every unit lowered over it shares (`PreparedText::paths`, `PreparedText::lexicon`, `Reading::kept`). The store holds a unit as an index of digests and the objects it names, in packs at its slot's address, so a filing writes what its edit made and the index, and a name is read back where its archived spelling lies ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-stored-in-parts-by-who-reads-each), [`curios-verdicts`' README](../../../curios-verdicts/README.md#a-slots-objects-are-kept-in-packs-beside-it-at-its-address)). A declaration's elaboration is taken from a record the store holds of what it was lowered to, and an item's erasure and its verdict from records it holds of the item, where every part and binding read stands (`curios-pipeline`'s `candidate_tests`, `std_undo_reproduction`, `curios-cert`'s `recheck::adoption_tests`).

1. **Retire.** The counted figures retaken over a store whose records are complete, and each decision moved to its owner. Check: the scaling measurement holds.

## Verification

- **Differential**: every unit compiled over a baseline stores what a whole compile of its text stores, at every stage (`incremental_tests`, `std_recompile_reproduction`, `std_vocabulary_reach_reproduction`).
- **Census**: `std_replay_census` reports nothing differing and no declaration taken along.
- **Scaling**: the session's leaf keystroke does not grow with the package, and the one-shot leaf costs the edit's own work and the six named bounds. The package is synthetic: a `lib.crs` of `pub mod M0;` onward and modules of forty-eight declarations each, a sum over `Nat`, a value read off it and a proof by computation, so a file is one size whatever the package holds; it is opened in a fresh server on `M0.crs` and given two unsaved keystrokes, each appending a declaration, and the second is counted.
