# A recompile costs what the edit reached

[A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md) states that a recompile costs what its edit reached: a declaration the edit cannot have reached is neither redone, asked, decoded, copied, sorted, seeded nor stored, and five costs are bounded by something else. The code redoes no more than the edit reached, and no stage asks anything of what the edit did not reach; elaboration and the certifier keep what the edit did not reach without visiting it, and every other stage still pays for everything it keeps: a respelled leaf of `/std` cost four questions of elaboration and 2.41 M allocations around them at `015e4f6f7`.

## What a recompile spends today

Counted in allocations, at the commit each row names, with a release compiler built with `--features profile`; elaboration's and the certifier's figures predate their versions and may have moved since. *One-shot*: `target/release/curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics <copy>/<file>` over a copy of `curios-std/src` holding no `.curios`, so the archived unit is the baseline, folded by `curios profile`. *Session*: `curios-wonder`'s protocol ([Measuring a question's latency](../../../curios-wonder/README.md#measuring-a-questions-latency)), its `Nat.crs` scenario over a copy of `curios-std/src`, initialized with no `workspaceFolders` so no warming check runs, opened with the leaf edit on disk and then two keystrokes held unsaved, each appending a fresh `_census_probeN`; a profile split by check. The leaf appends `pub let _census_probe(a: Nat) -> Nat = a;` to `Nat.crs`; the hub respells `xor(b, true)` as `xor(true, b)` in `/std/Bool/not`.

- **A leaf, one-shot** (counted at `015e4f6f7`): 2.41 M, of which the declaration elaborated asks 4 questions, no other declaration is asked anything, and the lowering asks the lookups of the edited file's declarations alone. The lowering 1.39 M — its walk over every declaration 0.73 M, most of it decoding each kept declaration's lowered item out of the baseline's bytes, and the baseline half restored whole 0.51 M, a path for every binding its records say a lookup read; elaboration 0.39 M — the kept items gathered 0.29 M, decoding each, and the walk keeping every declaration no seed reached 36 K; the records read backwards, once for the process — elaboration's and erasure's traces 39 K, the lowering's lookups 19 K, the certifier's reads 14 K; the certifier 0.39 M, every kept item's reads and vectors copied; erasure 0.10 M. Filing it: 0.70 M and a 51.1 MB slot.
- **A hub, one-shot** (counted at `28bcb9aba`): 170 declarations elaborated again, 75.5 M; judging 18.7 M; 207 items erased again, 2.5 M; the module sorted again, 0.43 M; 5 void attempts.
- **A keystroke, in a session** (counted at `015e4f6f7`): one compile of 0.48 M — the lowering 0.24 M (its walk over every declaration 0.12 M, the `use` declarations resolved 38 K, the exposure audit 37 K, audiences 30 K, interfaces 20 K, discovery 17 K, the edited file's parse 10 K), elaboration 0.10 M (the walk keeping every declaration no seed reached 36 K, its setup over every declaration 9 K, the kept items gathered 14 K), the certifier 70 K, erasure 46 K — and a store miss decided on its slot's record alone, 4.0 K. The edit's own work is a declaration of four questions; every record is read backwards once, where the session's first check opens the file.

Every row but the made declarations' own is a walk of the unit: erasure and the lowering keeping each declaration they decide nothing of one at a time; the passes over the unit no record keeps (the exposure audit, audiences, interfaces, the `unused-declaration` lint, witness naming, the lowering's account of what it made anew and dropped); the module ordered again where an edit moves an item's place in it, every item's reach computed again; every part of the baseline materialized; and every part of the unit consed and written again. At least 501 of `/std`'s 2,822 items read `/std/Bool/not` transitively by name at `28bcb9aba`, against the 170 elaborated again (`wonder stage core-elab`, each name resolved by its shortest unambiguous suffix, only spellings holding a slash counted).

## What this rests on

- A declaration is a function of what it reads, never of where it is written or which declaration elaborated first ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)); a unit's items are stored in one order, given once where the module is made, and no order read off a record is stored.
- Which of a baseline's verdicts stand is the certifier's decision, from the reads its own walk filed ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)): no record another stage keeps is part of the trusted base.
- A unit compiled over a baseline is the unit a whole compile of the same text is, held to the same stored form ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md), `curios-pipeline/src/tests/incremental_tests.rs`); a unit is handed over and never copied, is stored in parts by who reads each, and says what its items mean and not how they were come by ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md)).

## Decisions

- **A recompile makes a version of its baseline.** Every table a unit holds by declaration — its lowered items and the lowering's tables, its elaborated items and where it holds each name, its traces, its erased forms, its certification, and every index read off them — is a persistent map: a version shares every entry its baseline holds, replacing one costs a path, and two versions are compared by what they do not share, which is how the certifier finds the items it must judge and the store the facets it must write. A fact a pass over the unit reads today is a facet of a declaration with the reads that make it, or an aggregate over the records kept by their changes: the exposure audit a declaration's own; the `unused-declaration` lint a count of readers in the lowering's index; a module's interface a facet of its heads and of what it re-exports. The elaborator reads the lowering's entries where they stand, as it reads a scope. Where an edit moves an item's place in the stored order, the order is given again over reaches kept with their items, reading no item's terms. Rationale: a unit is already an index of its declarations' facets handed over as that index, and Isabelle's document model holds versions the same way, "Document-content is persistent by default" and "content of common sub-structures of related document versions is shared to some extent" (Wenzel and Wolff, [Isabelle/PIDE as Platform for Educational Tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.4835)); `imbl`'s `OrdMap::diff` "will avoid visiting nodes which are shared between the two sets"; an aggregate kept by its deltas is what incremental view maintenance makes of a query ([DBSP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16684)). Rejected: a unit assembled from every declaration, kept or made; a pass of word-sized comparisons over the unit as the end state, which is free at `/std`'s size and a walk of the unit all the same.
- **The persistent maps are `curios-utilities`' own** (`Persistent` and `Ordered`, `curios-utilities/src/persistent.rs`): a treap whose shape is a function of the keys it holds, so two versions holding one set of keys are one tree. A table a unit stores is held in a map read back in a stated order — `Ordered`, by a declaration's place, or `Sorted`, by name, archived as its entries in that order (`KeyOrder`) — and never in `Persistent`, whose order follows the process; an index a walk only looks up in may be. Two versions are compared by the nodes they do not share (`Ordered::diff`): a shape that is a function of the keys is what lets two versions be walked node beside node.
- **A place is a key in hand and a rank on disk.** A unit compiled in a process holds each item at an `i64` position of an `Ordered` map, spaced apart where the module is made or restored; a recompile holds an item made again at the position of the one it replaces, and an item new to the unit at a position between its neighbours in the stored order, and gives every position again only where no position lies between them or the order was given again. What a unit stores names a place by its rank in the order, read off where it is filed, so a recompile stores what a whole compile stores. Rationale: a place that is a vector's index moves for every item after one inserted, so every table keyed by it — where the module holds each name, what each item erased to — would be made again for one declaration added. Rejected: an item new to the unit appended after every other, which keeps no place in the stored order, since what reads it then stands before it; ranks kept current by a tree with subtree counts, which a reader asking by place would pay a logarithm for on every read.
- **A unit is stored as its facets, each an object of the store named by the SHA-256 of its bytes**, and a slot holds the record and the unit's index: what every compilation after the unit restores, and for each declaration the digests of its facets. A filing writes the objects the store does not hold, in one pack named by its digest, then the slot, renamed into place, so a slot never names an object not yet written. A slot's record vouches for its index, and each object is held to its digest where it is first read. An elaborated declaration is consed alone, which is canonical by construction; the store's phase first counts what that costs, and keeps a node table for each pack, a declaration still named by the digest of its consed-alone form, where consed alone costs more than a quarter again of the embedded image's bytes or a tenth again of the allocations restoring `programs/`'s scopes. Objects are not split by part: a part's digest is computed as it is today (`same.rs`, `curios_elab::Foreign`, `curios_core::Abroad`). Rationale: Unison parses and typechecks a definition "once, and then store the results in a cache which is never invalidated" ([The big idea](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/)), and Bazel keeps a content-addressable store beside an action cache whose results name outputs by digest ([The many caches of Bazel](https://blog.engflow.com/2024/05/13/the-many-caches-of-bazel/)), where rustc "has to rewrite each file entirely in each compilation session" ([Incremental compilation in detail](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/queries/incremental-compilation-in-detail.html)). Rejected: a slot rewritten whole; each node an object of its own, a database of tens of thousands of entries per unit.
- **What no slot names is collected** where a filing finds a day has passed since the last collection: an object no slot names and unused for five days is removed, and a read renews an object's time at most once an hour, as Go's build cache trims ([`cache.go`](https://go.dev/src/cmd/go/internal/cache/cache.go)); git keeps "any object with modification time newer than the `--prune` date" for the same reason, a writer naming an object it has just written ([git gc](https://git-scm.com/docs/git-gc)).
- **A facet is found by what it was made from.** For each declaration's input to a stage — its text and scope for the lowering, its lowered form for elaboration, its elaborated terms for erasure and for judgment — the store holds every record a compile of it filed with the facet it made, and a declaration the edit reached is looked for among them before it is made, a candidate's reads compared by the digests its record holds of the parts it read, since the facet read is not in hand. The certifier looks among its own records alone. Rationale: "Once we are storing the complete result it makes sense to record many constructive traces per key" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), section 4.2.3); an edit undone, a branch checked out again and a bisection reach texts the store held before.

## What stays outside it

- **Anything smaller than a declaration.** A declaration made again is the one a whole compile makes, and reusing part of an earlier elaboration under a changed read is exact only up to the first read answered otherwise, which needs the elaborator's state at that read, as Lean 4 keeps a snapshot after a definition's header ([`Lean.Elab.DefView`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Elab/DefView.html)). A specification of its own, if a measurement asks for one.
- **A declaration parsed alone.** An edited file is parsed whole, at about 1.4 allocations a byte of `/std/Nat` at `28bcb9aba`; the largest file of `/std` is 46 KB.
- **A void attempt**, a cost of a declaration made rather than of what was reached: 5 in the hub's recompile.

## Stages

Each stage lands with this specification rewritten to the work that remains and its rationale moved to the owning document.

Elaboration makes a version: the elaborated module's items and where it holds each name are held by place, its traces and the table of what they read of other units by name, each a version of the baseline's; its walk holds the declarations the edit could reach, grouped as the lowering's items make them (`curios_core::Declarations`), seeds the context with their lowered entries alone, and the baseline answers for every other declaration from the start (`curios-elab`'s `demand`). The certifier makes one too: it finds the items to judge by comparing the two modules by what they do not share (`curios_core::Items::changed_since`), visits those, the declarations whose filed reads reach them and the items the elaborator made again, reads every other declaration where the baseline holds it once its walk has passed it, and files the baseline's record revised where it judged or dropped a declaration (`curios-cert`'s `Changed`, `curios_core::Revision`).

1. **Erasure makes a version**: the erased unit's forms held by place and its index by name, the walk visiting what its marks reach. Check: the erasure fixtures of `incremental_tests`, and erasure's spans flat.
2. **The lowering carries what it keeps**: a module whose text and scope stand carried whole, its declarations neither visited nor decoded; the lowering's tables versions, the declarations its items make among them, and what it lowered anew and dropped found from the edit (`PreparedText::declarations`, `lowered_anew`, `dropped_from`, each made over every declaration today); its passes over the unit — the exposure audit, audiences, interfaces, the `unused-declaration` lint, witness naming, the `use` declarations resolved — facets of a declaration or of a module, or aggregates over the records kept by their changes. Check: the lowering's spans flat across the sizes but the files read and the edited file's parse.
3. **The unit made a version**: where it holds each name, what erasure read of it and what a report spells it by carried with the edges of what was made replaced, each item's reach kept with it. Check: the session's leaf keystroke counted over `/std` and over the synthetic package at one, two and four times its declarations, the count not growing but by the files read.
4. **The store holds objects**: the consed-alone count first, then packs, the index, verification and collection, each path a pack's records hold stored once in it — the lowering's baseline half restores a path for every binding its records say a lookup read. Check: a filing after a leaf edit writes the objects that edit made and the index; a miss reads no object; two filers at once write the same objects.
5. **Facets found by what they were made from.** Check: an edit undone recompiles with nothing made.

## Verification

- **Differential**: every unit compiled over a baseline stores what a whole compile of its text stores, at every stage (`incremental_tests`, `std_recompile_reproduction`, `std_vocabulary_reach_reproduction`).
- **Census**: `std_replay_census` reports nothing differing and no declaration taken along.
- **Scaling**: the session's leaf keystroke does not grow with the package, and the one-shot leaf costs the edit's own work and the five named bounds.
