# A probe under an arm costs its own size

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a judgment not pay its scope: a metavariable's spine is minted and passed over without being walked, spelled out only where an unsolved metavariable is read at another scope, and a walk passes over what it cannot change. The elaborator spells a probe under an arm as the kernel would read it, and each spelling walks the spine of every metavariable the probe holds only to leave it standing, so a probe pays the scope its metavariables were born in, once for each entry the filter in front of the arm's equations is put to.

**Ready**: both stages are stated, and the first is the next step.

## What is wrong

**A spelling gathers what its reading does not reach.** `Context::by_value`, `erased_by_value` and `spelled_as_refined` (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`) take what to substitute from the term's free variables, which hold every binder of each metavariable's spine, and hand the list to `Term::substitute` (`curios-core/src/term.rs`), whose contract leaves a spine's bare variables standing and which walks each entry to find that out. A metavariable born under `n` locals costs each spelling `n` lookups, a list of every local definition among them and an `n`-entry walk, whatever else the probe holds. `spelled_as_refined` substitutes again until the term names no refined variable, so one that stands only in a spine runs it to its bound. Conversion's `by_value` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) gathers a candidate's local definitions from the same free set at each of its rounds, and reads them through `read_outside_spines` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`), which reads none inside an occurrence.

**The filter spells the probe again for each entry.** `could_settle` (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`) reads the probe as the kernel spells it (`Context::kernel_spelled`) for every entry `scan_settled` and `answered` put it to, since `a929d1f18`, and `kernel_spelled` files no spelling that holds an unsolved metavariable. The kernel spells its candidate once per lookup and compares it with each key as filed (`curios-cert/src/kernel/scope.rs`).

**Where it shows.** Of matches each on the `let` before it (`Growth::ChainedMatches`), each link holds three metavariables still unsolved when its arms' probes run — `/sys/Option/some`'s and `/sys/Option/none`'s `A`, and a hole — each over a spine of every local in scope. At `0fc2fc1bd`, counted, profile build: `growth_slopes` reads 3.40 instructions, 3.36 node looks and 3.37 bytes per doubling through `elaborate` from 100 links, over `HELD_SLOPE`, so the held row fails; elaboration allocates 164.8, 417.3 and 1,196.3 MB at 100, 200 and 400 links, and `refined_reduct` 75.0, 240.1 and 837.5 MB of its own from 2,079, 4,179 and 8,379 calls; at 400 links the filter's spellings of the probe take 62% of elaboration's 11.2 million node looks, and the two spellings' substitutions most of the rest. `/std`'s elaboration pays it too: 223.2 billion instructions, of which the two stages below remove 5.2%.

## What is known

- **A cache's life is not the cause.** This specification first read the square as a frame's exit clearing what a probe under the next arm reduces again. In a scratch copy at `0fc2fc1bd`, every scoped reduction-side entry filed with the deepest frame it read and kept past an exit that leaves it standing (sound: `std_memo_census` passes, `/std`'s image is unchanged at one path), and the exit's clear retired altogether (unsound), leave the row's square as it stands and take its units down 9 to 11%, linearly; over `/std`'s elaboration both save the same 39,014 units of that copy's 62,361,829 (0.063%), counted, and the bookkeeping costs 0.12% more instructions. [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) rejects keeping an answer past a frame's exit by what it read.
- **Conversion already reads a local outside every metavariable occurrence**, through `read_outside_spines`, which keeps an occurrence as it stands. The spellings read the same values through `Term::substitute`, which keeps a spine's bare variables and rewrites its other entries: two rules for one reading.
- **A prototype removes the square from the looks and the instructions.** In a scratch copy at `0fc2fc1bd`, counted: the spellings gather from the locals a term names outside its metavariable occurrences and read them through `read_outside_spines`, conversion's `by_value` aside, and `refined_reduct` spells the probe once, where the filter first needs it. `ChainedMatches` reads 2.63 instructions, 2.09 looks, 2.09 allocations and 2.48 bytes per doubling through `elaborate` and passes the held row, in a build that also reads a filled free-variable set before walking it (a compilation finding); at 400 links elaboration allocates 572.3 MB and node looks fall from 11.2 to 3.1 million. `/std`'s units (62,349,532) and image are unchanged at one path and `std_memo_census` passes. The same gathering read through `Term::substitute`, made to pass over a spine that is the identity, measures alike — on its own build, `/std`'s elaboration retires 211.6 billion instructions where the copy with the prototype off retires 223.2 billion, interleaved, twice each, and the two readings differ by 0.02% on one build — but it keeps the spellings' second rule and changes a function compilation 101 reworks.
- **What is left is compilation 101's.** With the prototype, `refined_reduct`'s own allocations are linear (15.4, 31.1 and 62.7 MB), and the free-variable sets filled under it allocate 16.6, 52.3 and 179.0 MB from 6,514, 13,014 and 26,014 fills: a node rebuilt over a metavariable copies its spine's set ([A node carries its free variables in its own size](101-a-node-carries-its-free-variables-in-its-own-size.md)).
- **No verdict and no budget unit moves, and parity stands**: the kernel meets no unsolved metavariable, and the spellings are the elaborator's reading of the kernel's.
- **Prior art.** Rocq represents "bunches of identity instances" of an evar "as a O(1) constructor" ([#16442](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/pull/16442), after [#12526](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/issues/12526)); Lean 4 keeps no spine on a metavariable occurrence, its local context standing for it ([`MetavarContext.lean`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/MetavarContext.lean)), so a substitution has nothing to walk there. Both leave an occurrence's context to the occurrence.

## Stages

1. **A spelling reads a local outside every metavariable occurrence.** `substituted_by_value` and `spelled_as_refined` read through `read_outside_spines`, and they and conversion's `by_value` gather what to read from the locals a term names outside its occurrences — off its free set where it holds no metavariable, by a walk that passes over each occurrence where it does; `spelled_as_refined`'s rounds end where one finds nothing refined. Check: `curios-elab`'s tests, a new one each for a definition read outside an occurrence and kept inside it and for a refined variable standing only inside one; `/std`'s image unchanged at one path.
2. **The filter is handed the probe spelled once.** `could_settle` takes the probe as the kernel spells it, which `refined_reduct` spells where the filter first needs it and keeps for the lookup, as the kernel keeps its candidate. Check: `growth_slopes` passes, `ChainedMatches` reading looks within a tenth of 2.0 per doubling through `elaborate`, counted; `/std`'s units and image unchanged at one path, and its elaboration instructions counted before the first stage and after this one.
