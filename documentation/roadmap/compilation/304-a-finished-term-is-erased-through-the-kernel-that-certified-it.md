# A finished term is erased through the kernel that certified it

Erasure, a crate of its own, `curios-erase`, walks the module the kernel certified, reads every type it is directed by through that kernel, files its own record of what it read, and erases the prelude where the prelude is certified. Its next step is stage 3, a ready delivery; the proof-stub investigation is independent of every other stage.

## What this builds on

- **Erasure comes after the certifier** ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)): everything the elaborator invents reaches the certifier as an ordinary term, and erasure comes after, on obligations the certifier checks. The trusted base is `curios-cert`'s dependency closure, and `curios-elab` takes `curios-cert` as a dev-dependency only, so outside tests neither checker depends on the other ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-trusted-base-is-a-crate-boundary)).
- **A reader of finished terms cannot name elaboration's state** ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-contexts-state-is-two-halves-and-a-reader-of-finished-terms-cannot-name-the-second)). `Retyping` (`curios-elab/src/context/retyping.rs`) owns a context it makes, offering the binder supply, the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget, the read trace and six entries of the engine: reduction to a head, a type's sort, whether a type is erasable, a neutral's type, an arm's refinement and entering an induct case.
- **Erasure is directed by types and types no term** ([`curios-erase`'s README](../../../curios-erase/README.md#erasure-reads-a-heads-type-off-the-finished-term)): it reads a head's type off the finished term (`Lowering::type_of`) and asks the engine for that type's head, by the reduction that accepted the term. That decision rejects the certifier's own walk because the build script erasing the prelude never runs it, a fact about where the prelude is erased.
- **Erasure is a crate of its own** (`curios-erase`), holding its erased-unit types, its recompile reasoning and its refusals (`curios_erase::Error`), and qualifying every `curios_core` and `curios_anf` name. It reads through `Retyping` and records its reads in the elaborator's vocabulary still, and `curios-elab` exports what that takes: `Retyping` and what its entries name (`InScope`, `Defined`, `Opened`, `InductCase`, `is_erasable_in`), the trace vocabulary and its digests (`Question`, `Answered`, `Made`, `Read`, `Parts`, `Foreign`, `part_digest`, `bytes_of`, `found`), the registry comparisons (`Same` and the `*_alike` functions) and `validate_universes_in`.
- **Each stage keeps what its own record says stands** ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)). The kernel files its own record of what a judgment read (`Reads`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/reads.rs`), which a recompile adopts a verdict by ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)).
- **The two checkers share what can be shared.** `curios-core` holds the intrinsic folds, the closed machine and the `Reducer` trait; `curios-analysis` holds the inversion, the erasure-shape classification and the analyses over `Env` and `Judge`. Each checker owns its weak-head reduction of open terms, its sorts and its conversion, and the kernel memoizes its reducts, sorts and inferred types ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts)).
- **The kernel has a reading door** (`curios_cert::Reader`, entered by `Kernel::read`). Each entry is a judgment the kernel makes: reduction to a head, a term's type — a neutral's, and a type former's sort — which half a value of a type is in, as the erasure obligations classify a position and remembered beside the sorts, a case put in force and an inductive arm entered as the arm is checked (`enter_case` and `enter_arm` beside `check_arm`, sharing `assume_case_value` and `bind_solutions` with it), the binders and the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget and the read record. Inside the bracket no erased position and no call is recorded. The door holds no `DefinitionKind`, which the walk reads off the certified module's items, nor an assumption at a stated universe context: a recursive group's member is bound at its type to its selection, as the kernel's own group tail binds it. What a reading spends, which erasure's tests measure, gets a test-support door when they read through it.
- **An erased form is its item's** ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#an-erased-form-is-its-items)): each item erases into a fragment on a budget restored for it, reading every other declaration by name.

## What is wrong today

**Erasure reduces through the engine that elaborates.** `curios-erase` decides what erases by the sort of each type and reads the types its walk is directed by off the terms (`lower/typed.rs`, `classify.rs`). To do it, it reduces a type to its head, asks a type's sort and whether it is a proposition, reads a neutral's type, installs a match arm's equations, and solves a match's indices through the shared inversion (`lower/eliminate.rs`), which compares two forcings by `Judge::convert_at`. Each goes through the elaborator's reducer (`Retyping`), which puts a question to the elaborator's conversion where a fold is stuck or an equation is missed. Erasing `/sys` and `/std` at `f9522a51d` puts 862 questions to conversion (671 `reduce::asked_classes`, 191 `reduce::asked_scrutinee`), drains the elaborator's conversion queue 667 times (`convert::drain`), and asks the elaborator's universe solver 4,041 consistency checks (`universe::check_consistent` and `universe::check_consistent_full`) and 56 choices (`universe::choose`) of terms whose levels elaboration settled; the shared closed machine takes 14,175 entries (`machine::reduce_closed`). Counted, by the protocol under *Verification*. The kernel certifying the same prelude answers 2,718 `convert::asked_classes` and 670 `kernel::asked_scrutinee` questions of its own, so erasure's questions are of a kind its conversion already answers.

**It cannot ask less.** A callee typed through a decision only conversion makes — `g: Pick(f(same(n)) == f(n))`, where the fold pairs atoms by spelling and conversion holds the two calls one — has a function type to both checkers and none to plain reduction, and erasure, which needs the head of every callee, record and scrutinee it eliminates, stops there at `erase: applied a non-function` (`curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests`). A reader that decides less than the reduction that accepted the term leaves erasure a type it cannot read on a program both checkers accept.

**Erasure reads and records through the elaborator.** The engine it reads through, `Retyping`, whose refusals erasure wraps and reports in the elaborator's words, its budget's exhaustion among them (`curios_erase::Error::Reading`); and its record of reads, written in the elaborator's vocabulary (`Question::Erased`, `Question::Ran`, `curios-elab/src/context/traced.rs`, with what the context's lookups note while erasure reduces) and answered by erasure's own recompile reasoning (`curios-erase/src/over.rs`, `erased_candidate.rs`).

**Erasure walks a second copy of the module.** `curios-erase/src/projected.rs` reads each declaration out of its universes the first time it is read — instances, universe contexts and nominal vectors removed and every level made ground (`curios_core::project_erased_universes`) — and erasure walks that copy, a unit in scope keeping the cells it was projected into (`Projection`, which `curios-unit` stores). The copy spares a reader that remembers nothing of certification the specialization of a polymorphic body at every use. An instance's sorts go with its levels, so at a use of a declaration that leaves its sort to its caller erasure reads the sort the declaration states, not the one the use supplies.

**The prelude is erased before it is certified, by a build kept off the kernel.** `curios-prelude-archive`'s build elaborates `/sys` and `/std` and erases them into images of uncertified units (`Uncertified::stored`), reading the library from `curios-std`; `curios-prelude`'s build restores those images and certifies them, filing the certifier's record beside them (`certification.rkyv`, `reads.rkyv`). The split exists so a certifier edit does not elaborate the prelude again, and with erasure reading through the kernel the elaborating build would reach it all the same.

**Uncertain cost: shaping a proof stub evaluates its bound's subject again.** `proof_stub` (`curios-erase/src/lower/function.rs`) reduces a slot's type to distinguish a proof from a proof-valued function and builds the latter as an applicable function of the right arity, recursively through curried results. For `Bytes/slice(go(800, x[]), 0, 10)`, where `go` appends ten bytes per recursive step (`curios`'s `tests::reduction`, `bytes_growing(800)`), the `@within` type is `Bool/Holds(0 + 10 <= Bytes/len(built))`: finding its head can run the accumulation again. Observed at `9c543476a`, counted in a scratch copy with `FUSION_CAP` lifted: 2 400 concatenations fuse, three runs of 800. A backtrace at the first concatenation of each run stands in the elaborator's `get_or_init_elaborated`, the kernel's `reading`, and `proof_stub` under `kept_operand` and `erase_let`. This locates a third evaluation; its contribution to the shipped compile remains unpriced because `type_level_sequence_cost_measurements` measures the whole compilation. Whether a placeholder can be shaped without reducing that proposition's head is also unknown. A relevance mark alone does not state a callable placeholder's arity; moving the walk to the kernel alone does not remove its reduction.

## Decisions

1. **Erasure is a crate, and the crate is erasure.** Erasure is `curios-erase`'s root and its top-level modules; no module repeats the crate's name, as `curios-cert`'s README rejects a `kernel::` namespace. `curios-erase` depends on `curios-cert`, `curios-anf`, `curios-analysis` and `curios-core`, and on nothing of `curios-elab`. It qualifies every `curios_core` and every `curios_anf` name, as `curios-emit` qualifies both representations it translates between, and `.claude/rules/rust.md` states it so.
2. **Erasure reads through the kernel that certified the term.** A reading door at `curios-cert`'s root offers a reader of certified terms the kernel's own judgments: reduction to a head, a term's type, which half a value of a type is in, a case and an arm entered as they are checked, the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget and the read record. It adds no rule, since each entry is a judgment the kernel already makes, and no verdict moves. A type is then read by the reduction that accepted the term by construction, and the reader holds no elaboration half to hide.
3. **The certifying kernel is the reader.** The driver hands the kernel that certified a unit to that unit's erasure (`certify` then `erase_unit`, `curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`), so what certification reduced and sorted is a memo erasure reads.
4. **The prelude is one crate, which erases it where it certifies it.** `curios-prelude` holds `/std` as authored, its package in `std/` beside the crate's Rust in `src/`, and the one build that makes the fixed prelude of it and `/sys`: each root elaborated, certified and erased through the certifying kernel, and stored as a certified unit in the store's own format (`Unit::stored`), its record and what judging each definition read inside it, which `with_prelude` restores as a store's unit is restored. No unit is stored uncertified, so `Unit::new` takes the certifier's record and no type stands for a unit without one. The build reaches the kernel, so a kernel edit elaborates the prelude again: the lint takes 40.3 s after an edit to `curios-erase`, which stands for a kernel edit under this decision, where a kernel edit takes 11.7 s today, and an edit of the elaborator, the frequent one, elaborates and certifies the prelude either way. Timed on `523d90358`'s tree on sixteen threads, each figure the median of three interleaved readings, which span 11.5 to 11.7 s and 39.9 to 40.4 s; to retake it, touch `curios-cert/src/lib.rs` and time `cargo xtask workspace lint`, then the same for `curios-erase/src/lib.rs`, one round unrecorded and three interleaved.
5. **Erasure files its own record.** What its reductions read, as the kernel's `Reads`, and the erased forms it read; a recompile keeps an item's erased form where that record stands, checked as a cached verdict's record is. `Question::Erased` and `Question::Ran` leave the elaborator's vocabulary for erasure's own, which `curios-unit` stores with the erased-unit types.
6. **Relevance is read off a sort until a mark states it.** Where [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) makes a binder's mark exact, `curios-erase` reads the mark where it reads a sort; nothing here waits on it.
7. **Erasure walks the module the kernel certified.** Each declaration is read as the kernel holds it, with its universes, through the kernel's environment, and an instance erases as its head: universes carry nothing at run time, so a constructed value and a record erase by their fields and a type former to the unit, as each does in the copy, where their universes are empty. `projected.rs`, the `Projection` a unit keeps and the `InScope` view the reader reads a scope through go, since the certifying kernel already holds the unit's scope.
8. **Erasure owns its refusals.** `curios_erase::Error` holds what erasure refuses a program for — the verifier's two recursion rules, named by the source names erasure minted each identity for, a natural case key past `NatCase`, and its budget's exhaustion at the term it was reading — each located at the term erasure was erasing and worded as erasure's: the program was certified, and only erasure stopped. Anything else its reader refuses of a term the kernel certified is a compiler fault, reported as a term erasure could not read with the reader's refusal beneath it, never as the kernel's verdict on the program. `curios-pipeline` reports it beside the kernel's refusal (`erasure_refusal` beside `kernel_refusal`). The elaborator's `Error` holds none of erasure's refusals and names none of its types.

**Rejected.** Reading through the elaborator's engine, as Lean's code generator infers types through `MetaM` and Rocq's extraction recomputes them through `Retyping`: the reader of a certified term would decide by the side that did not certify it. Erasure generic over the shared `Env`/`Judge` seam, which would have one implementor: the seam is for the analyses both checkers run, and erasure is not one. Folding the mark into this work: a mark states no callee's, record's or scrutinee's type, so the kernel reader is needed with it or without it, and the mark's own questions belong to the trusted base. A module named `into_anf` inside the crate, which spells the disambiguation the crate's name already makes. Keeping the build that elaborates the prelude off the kernel, with the erased half — the forms and their index, linking, evaluation, the recompile reasoning — in a crate of its own beneath `curios-erase`: a kernel edit stays at 11.7 s for one more crate, which would exist to serve a split no longer worth it. The two prelude crates kept with the archive erasing nothing, whose build would reach the kernel through `curios-unit` all the same. `curios-std` beside the prelude, a package's sources and the lines of Rust that say where they are. The certifier's record beside the images, which spared re-serializing the prelude on a certifier edit that now runs the whole build again. Projecting the module out of its universes for the reader: the certifying kernel remembers what it reduced and sorted by the terms it was handed, and a projected term is another term, so every question erasure put to the copy would miss what decision 3 hands it.

## Prior art

- **A code generator reads types through the front end.** Lean 4's LCNF decides what is irrelevant with `Meta.inferType` and `isPropFormerType` in `MetaM` ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.Types`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/Types.html)), over `CoreM` with a context of its own ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.CompilerM`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/CompilerM.lean)); Rocq's extraction computes sort families through `Retyping`, which recomputes the type of a term "known to be well-typable" without checking it ([`Retyping`](https://docs.rocq-prover.org/v8.18/api/coq-core/Retyping/index.html)); Agda's `Treeless.Erase` runs in its type-checking monad, the only checker it has ([`Agda.Compiler.Treeless.Erase`](https://hackage-content-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.7.20250701/candidate/docs/src/Agda.Compiler.Treeless.Erase.html)).
- **A verified pipeline erases from the checker's judgment.** MetaCoq's certified erasure is a single traversal of a well-typed term in a well-formed environment, deciding by the checker's theory whether a subterm is a type or a proof of a proposition, with its correctness proved (Sozeau et al., [Correct and Complete Type Checking and Certified Erasure for Coq, in Coq](https://hal.sorbonne-universite.fr/ENS-PARIS-SACLAY/hal-04077552v3), JACM).
- **Erasure by a recorded quantity reduces nothing.** Idris 2 erases 0-multiplicity arguments by the multiplicity elaboration and the case tree compiler checked ([Implementation overview](https://github.com/idris-lang/Idris2/blob/main/docs/source/implementation/overview.rst)), and a graded dependent type theory states erasure's soundness for its grades ([A Graded Modal Dependent Type Theory with Erasure, Formalized](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.29716)): the form [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) reserves.

## Open questions

- **What an instance's sort tells erasure.** At a use of a declaration that leaves its sort to its caller, the certified module states the sort the use supplies, which the copy drops, so erasure may erase there what it keeps today. Counted before stage 4 lands: the items of `/sys` and `/std` whose erased form moves when their instances keep their sorts, each a change stage 4's check then states rather than an equality it holds.
- **The record's grain.** `Reads` names a declaration read without digesting the answer, where the elaborator's trace digests each part it read, so a recompile may erase again an item today's record keeps. Measured in stage 5 by `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::incremental_tests` and the count of items an edit erases again; a finer record follows if the count moves.
- **What erasure still borrows of the elaborator once its record is its own.** The registry comparisons its recompile reasoning asks (`Same` and the `*_alike` functions), `validate_universes_in` at its boundary, and `bytes_of` and `found`, which read a store's records: each moves where both stages read it, or erasure stops asking it, before stage 5 drops the dependency.
- **The kernel reader's cost.** What erasing the prelude costs through the certifying kernel against the figures above, counted by the same protocol on stage 4 before it lands.

## Stages

3. **The prelude, one crate.** `curios-prelude-archive` and `curios-std` fold into `curios-prelude`: its Rust in `src/`, `/std` as authored in `std/`, the library's claims in `tests/claims.rs`, and one build script that elaborates each root, erases it as erasure reads today and certifies it, filing each as a certified unit in the store's format, which `with_prelude` restores. `Uncertified`, its stored form, the prelude's `Stored`, `certification.rkyv` and `reads.rkyv` go, `Unit::new` takes the certifier's record, and the three steps a recompile set on an `Uncertified` are `Unit`'s; the two profiling programs become one, which `cargo xtask profile-prelude` runs for each phase. What the split stated goes or is restated: `curios-prelude`'s *Split from the archive* and *The certifier's record is filed here, beside the images rather than inside them*; `curios-prelude-archive`'s README, its build-scoped decision moved to `curios-prelude`'s; `curios-std`'s README into `curios-prelude`'s; `curios-unit`'s *A unit is certified by construction*, which no exception qualifies; [A build script is compiled optimized](../../design/a-build-script-is-compiled-optimized.md)'s two scripts; `.claude/rules/prelude.md`, the paths `.claude/rules/curios.md` and `prelude.md` load by, and `CLAUDE.md`'s `curios-std/src`; and the reason `curios-cert`'s README, `curios-elab`'s manifest, `.claude/rules/checking.md` and `curios-analysis`'s README give for keeping the elaborator off the kernel, each restated as the independence the crate graph holds ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)) rather than a build script's rebuild. Check: every item of `/sys` and `/std` keeps the digest of its erased form and its verdict; every diagnostic the suite pins unchanged; `curios test` in `curios-prelude/std`; `cargo xtask workspace test`.
4. **The reader.** `curios-erase` reads through the kernel's door and walks the certified module, an instance erased as its head; the driver hands it the certifying kernel; `projected.rs`, `Projection` and the `InScope` view are deleted; `curios-unit` reaches `curios-cert` through `curios-erase`, and `.claude/rules/pipeline.md`'s rule, `curios-unit`'s manifest comment and its decision *Below the kernel, so a build script constructing a unit never reaches the certifier* go; erasure's budget's exhaustion becomes its own variant, worded as erasure's, and any other refusal of the kernel's while reading a compiler fault in erasure's words; the prelude's build erases each root after certifying it, through the kernel that did; `Retyping` is deleted. Erasure still writes the elaborator's trace vocabulary. Check: every item of `/sys` and `/std` erases to the form it had before this stage, but those the count under *What an instance's sort tells erasure* names; an erasure that exhausts its budget reports as erasure's, held by a test that pins its words; `tests::erasure::head_tests` and `dropped_tests` pass; the cost read by the protocol.
5. **The record.** Erasure files its own record; `Question::Erased` and `Question::Ran` leave `curios-elab`, and `curios-erase` drops its dependency on `curios-elab`. Check: `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::budget_tests`, `tests::order_tests` and `tests::incremental_tests`; `cargo tree -p curios-erase --edges normal` contains no `curios-elab`.
6. **Proof-stub reduction**, independent of the others. In a scratch copy put a `profile!` span around `proof_stub`'s reduction and attribute the erasure share of `bytes_growing(6400)`: visits, charged work and allocations under that reduction, separately from the two checkers. Use the shipped fusion cap for the cost reading; lifting it is only a separate attribution control reproducing the three walks. End with the counted contribution and either a checked way to shape the placeholder without the extra accumulation, or the reason the reduction remains necessary or its cost does not justify a change. Any fix must preserve applicable proof-valued functions, their telescope lengths and curried results; replacing all placeholders with unit is not an answer. Check: `curios`'s `tests::erasure::dropped_tests::a_proof_valued_function_in_a_kept_slot_is_applicable` with controls for different arities and curried results where the fix changes placeholder construction.

## Verification

- **The erased result does not move.** From stage 3 the images change by design — they hold `/std`'s new place and certified units, and from stage 4 forms erased through the kernel — so at each stage every item of `/sys` and `/std` is compared by the digest of its erased form against the commit before the stage, and the corpus's emitted artifacts and verdicts agree with it. A stored unit's format changes in stages 3 to 5; a unit stored by an earlier compiler lives under that compiler's address and is never read by a later one ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). Counted.
- **A head only conversion shows is read.** `curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests` hold four programs whose callee has a function type through a question to conversion: two spellings of one call, an arm's equation met at another spelling, two calls differing in a proof, a function beside its expansion.
- **What erasure asks.** In a copy of the tree under `target/`, `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` (from stage 3, the one build's profile, where the prelude is elaborated, certified and erased): the fold's `(within)` rows whose outer is `erase_unit`, read for `reduce::asked_classes`, `reduce::asked_scrutinee`, `convert::drain`, the `universe::` checks and `machine::reduce_closed`, and their kernel twins once erasure reads through the kernel. Counted.

## Retirement

Before this specification is deleted, `curios-erase`'s README states decisions 1 to 3, 5, 7 and 8 and `curios-prelude`'s decision 4, each with its rejected alternatives; `curios-elab`'s README's two-halves decision names no reader of finished terms; the independent-kernel decision says erasure reads through the certifier; [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) names `curios-erase` where it names erasure; and no reference to this filename remains.
