# A finished term is erased through the kernel that certified it

Erasure leaves the elaborator for a crate of its own, `curios-erase`, which walks the module the kernel certified, reads every type it is directed by through that kernel, files its own record of what it read, and erases the prelude where the prelude is certified. Its next step is stage 2, a ready delivery; the proof-stub investigation is independent of every other stage.

## What this builds on

- **Erasure comes after the certifier** ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)): everything the elaborator invents reaches the certifier as an ordinary term, and erasure comes after, on obligations the certifier checks. The trusted base is `curios-cert`'s dependency closure, and `curios-elab` takes `curios-cert` as a dev-dependency only, so the build that elaborates the prelude never compiles the kernel ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-trusted-base-is-a-crate-boundary)).
- **A reader of finished terms cannot name elaboration's state** ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md#a-contexts-state-is-two-halves-and-a-reader-of-finished-terms-cannot-name-the-second)). `Retyping` (`curios-elab/src/context/retyping.rs`) owns a context it makes, offering the binder supply, the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget, the read trace and six entries of the engine: reduction to a head, a type's sort, whether a type is erasable, a neutral's type, an arm's refinement and entering an induct case.
- **Erasure is directed by types and types no term** ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md#erasure-reads-a-heads-type-off-the-finished-term)): it reads a head's type off the finished term (`Lowering::type_of`) and asks the engine for that type's head, by the reduction that accepted the term. That decision rejects the certifier's own walk because `curios-elab` cannot depend on it and the build script erasing the prelude never runs it, two facts about where erasure lives.
- **Each stage keeps what its own record says stands** ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)). The kernel files its own record of what a judgment read (`Reads`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/reads.rs`), which a recompile adopts a verdict by ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)).
- **The two checkers share what can be shared.** `curios-core` holds the intrinsic folds, the closed machine and the `Reducer` trait; `curios-analysis` holds the inversion, the erasure-shape classification and the analyses over `Env` and `Judge`. Each checker owns its weak-head reduction of open terms, its sorts and its conversion, and the kernel memoizes its reducts, sorts and inferred types ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts)).
- **The kernel has a reading door** (`curios_cert::Reader`, entered by `Kernel::read`). Each entry is a judgment the kernel makes: reduction to a head, a term's type — a neutral's, and a type former's sort — which half a value of a type is in, as the erasure obligations classify a position and remembered beside the sorts, a case put in force and an inductive arm entered as the arm is checked (`enter_case` and `enter_arm` beside `check_arm`, sharing `assume_case_value` and `bind_solutions` with it), the binders and the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget and the read record. Inside the bracket no erased position and no call is recorded. The door holds no `DefinitionKind`, which the walk reads off the certified module's items, nor an assumption at a stated universe context: a recursive group's member is bound at its type to its selection, as the kernel's own group tail binds it. What a reading spends, which erasure's tests measure, gets a test-support door when those tests move.
- **An erased form is its item's** ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#an-erased-form-is-its-items)): each item erases into a fragment on a budget restored for it, reading every other declaration by name.

## What is wrong today

**Erasure reduces through the engine that elaborates.** `into_anf` (`curios-elab/src/into_anf`) decides what erases by the sort of each type and reads the types its walk is directed by off the terms (`lower/typed.rs`, `classify.rs`). To do it, it reduces a type to its head, asks a type's sort and whether it is a proposition, reads a neutral's type, installs a match arm's equations, and solves a match's indices through the shared inversion (`lower/eliminate.rs`), which compares two forcings by `Judge::convert_at`. Each goes through the elaborator's reducer, which puts a question to the elaborator's conversion where a fold is stuck or an equation is missed. Erasing `/sys` and `/std` at `f9522a51d` puts 862 questions to conversion (671 `reduce::asked_classes`, 191 `reduce::asked_scrutinee`), drains the elaborator's conversion queue 667 times (`convert::drain`), and asks the elaborator's universe solver 4,041 consistency checks (`universe::check_consistent` and `universe::check_consistent_full`) and 56 choices (`universe::choose`) of terms whose levels elaboration settled; the shared closed machine takes 14,175 entries (`machine::reduce_closed`). Counted, by the protocol under *Verification*. The kernel certifying the same prelude answers 2,718 `convert::asked_classes` and 670 `kernel::asked_scrutinee` questions of its own, so erasure's questions are of a kind its conversion already answers.

**It cannot ask less.** A callee typed through a decision only conversion makes — `g: Pick(f(same(n)) == f(n))`, where the fold pairs atoms by spelling and conversion holds the two calls one — has a function type to both checkers and none to plain reduction, and erasure, which needs the head of every callee, record and scrutinee it eliminates, stops there at `erase: applied a non-function` (`curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests`). A reader that decides less than the reduction that accepted the term leaves erasure a type it cannot read on a program both checkers accept.

**Erasure is the elaborator's in four places, not one.** The engine; the erased half of a unit (`ErasedUnit`, `ErasedItem`, `Erasures`, `ErasedBaseline`, `Projection`), which `curios-unit` stores as `curios-elab`'s; its refusals — an evaluation cycle and an initializer that performs an effect, which `curios-anf`'s verifier finds, and a natural case key past `NatCase` — variants of the elaborator's `Error` that only erasure raises, one constructor of which reads erasure's own map from the identities it minted to the source names (`Error::refused_by_verifier`, `ErasedNames`); and its record of reads, written in the elaborator's vocabulary (`Question::Erased`, `Question::Ran`, `curios-elab/src/context/traced.rs`, with what the context's lookups note while erasure reduces) and answered by erasure's own recompile reasoning (`into_anf/over.rs`, `into_anf/erased_candidate.rs`).

**Erasure walks a second copy of the module.** `into_anf/projected.rs` reads each declaration out of its universes the first time it is read — instances, universe contexts and nominal vectors removed and every level made ground (`curios_core::project_erased_universes`) — and erasure walks that copy, a unit in scope keeping the cells it was projected into (`Projection`, which `curios-unit` stores). The copy spares a reader that remembers nothing of certification the specialization of a polymorphic body at every use. An instance's sorts go with its levels, so at a use of a declaration that leaves its sort to its caller erasure reads the sort the declaration states, not the one the use supplies.

**The prelude is erased before it is certified.** `curios-prelude-archive`'s build elaborates `/sys` and `/std` and erases them into the images it stores; `curios-prelude`'s build restores those images and certifies them.

**Uncertain cost: shaping a proof stub evaluates its bound's subject again.** `proof_stub` (`curios-elab/src/into_anf/lower/function.rs`) reduces a slot's type to distinguish a proof from a proof-valued function and builds the latter as an applicable function of the right arity, recursively through curried results. For `Bytes/slice(go(800, x[]), 0, 10)`, where `go` appends ten bytes per recursive step (`curios`'s `tests::reduction`, `bytes_growing(800)`), the `@within` type is `Bool/Holds(0 + 10 <= Bytes/len(built))`: finding its head can run the accumulation again. Observed at `9c543476a`, counted in a scratch copy with `FUSION_CAP` lifted: 2 400 concatenations fuse, three runs of 800. A backtrace at the first concatenation of each run stands in the elaborator's `get_or_init_elaborated`, the kernel's `reading`, and `proof_stub` under `kept_operand` and `erase_let`. This locates a third evaluation; its contribution to the shipped compile remains unpriced because `type_level_sequence_cost_measurements` measures the whole compilation. Whether a placeholder can be shaped without reducing that proposition's head is also unknown. A relevance mark alone does not state a callable placeholder's arity; moving the walk to the kernel alone does not remove its reduction.

## Decisions

1. **Erasure is a crate, and the crate is erasure.** `curios-elab/src/into_anf.rs` becomes `curios-erase`'s `lib.rs` and its children the crate's top-level modules; no module repeats the crate's name, as `curios-cert`'s README rejects a `kernel::` namespace. `curios-erase` depends on `curios-cert`, `curios-anf`, `curios-analysis` and `curios-core`, and on nothing of `curios-elab`. It qualifies every `curios_core` and every `curios_anf` name, as `curios-emit` qualifies both representations it translates between, and `.claude/rules/rust.md` states it so.
2. **Erasure reads through the kernel that certified the term.** A reading door at `curios-cert`'s root offers a reader of certified terms the kernel's own judgments: reduction to a head, a term's type, which half a value of a type is in, a case and an arm entered as they are checked, the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget and the read record. It adds no rule, since each entry is a judgment the kernel already makes, and no verdict moves. A type is then read by the reduction that accepted the term by construction, and the reader holds no elaboration half to hide.
3. **The certifying kernel is the reader.** The driver hands the kernel that certified a unit to that unit's erasure (`certify` then `erase_unit`, `curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`), so what certification reduced and sorted is a memo erasure reads.
4. **The prelude is erased where it is certified.** `curios-prelude`'s build certifies each root and then erases it, and files the erased forms beside its record; `curios-prelude-archive`'s images hold no erased form. `curios-elab` still takes `curios-cert` as a dev-dependency only, so the build that elaborates the prelude still never compiles the kernel.
5. **Erasure files its own record.** What its reductions read, as the kernel's `Reads`, and the erased forms it read; a recompile keeps an item's erased form where that record stands, checked as a cached verdict's record is. `Question::Erased` and `Question::Ran` leave the elaborator's vocabulary, and the erased-unit types and erasure's recompile reasoning move to `curios-erase`, whose types `curios-unit` stores.
6. **Relevance is read off a sort until a mark states it.** Where [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) makes a binder's mark exact, `curios-erase` reads the mark where it reads a sort; nothing here waits on it.
7. **Erasure walks the module the kernel certified.** Each declaration is read as the kernel holds it, with its universes, through the kernel's environment, and an instance erases as its head: universes carry nothing at run time, so a constructed value and a record erase by their fields and a type former to the unit, as each does in the copy, where their universes are empty. `into_anf/projected.rs`, the `Projection` a unit keeps and the `InScope` view the reader reads a scope through go, since the certifying kernel already holds the unit's scope.
8. **Erasure owns its refusals.** `curios_erase::Error` holds what erasure refuses a program for — the verifier's two recursion rules, named by the source names erasure minted each identity for, and a natural case key past `NatCase` — beside its budget's exhaustion and what its reader refused, wrapped, each located at the term erasure was erasing. `curios-pipeline` reports it beside the kernel's refusal (`erasure_refusal` beside `kernel_refusal`), in the words the elaborator's error gave it. The elaborator's `Error` holds none of erasure's refusals and names none of its types.

**Rejected.** Reading through the elaborator's engine, as Lean's code generator infers types through `MetaM` and Rocq's extraction recomputes them through `Retyping`: the reader of a certified term would decide by the side that did not certify it. Erasure generic over the shared `Env`/`Judge` seam, which would have one implementor: the seam is for the analyses both checkers run, and erasure is not one. Folding the mark into this work: a mark states no callee's, record's or scrutinee's type, so the kernel reader is needed with it or without it, and the mark's own questions belong to the trusted base. A module named `into_anf` inside the crate, which spells the disambiguation the crate's name already makes. Projecting the module out of its universes for the reader: the certifying kernel remembers what it reduced and sorted by the terms it was handed, and a projected term is another term, so every question erasure put to the copy would miss what decision 3 hands it.

## Prior art

- **A code generator reads types through the front end.** Lean 4's LCNF decides what is irrelevant with `Meta.inferType` and `isPropFormerType` in `MetaM` ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.Types`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/Types.html)), over `CoreM` with a context of its own ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.CompilerM`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/CompilerM.lean)); Rocq's extraction computes sort families through `Retyping`, which recomputes the type of a term "known to be well-typable" without checking it ([`Retyping`](https://docs.rocq-prover.org/v8.18/api/coq-core/Retyping/index.html)); Agda's `Treeless.Erase` runs in its type-checking monad, the only checker it has ([`Agda.Compiler.Treeless.Erase`](https://hackage-content-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.7.20250701/candidate/docs/src/Agda.Compiler.Treeless.Erase.html)).
- **A verified pipeline erases from the checker's judgment.** MetaCoq's certified erasure is a single traversal of a well-typed term in a well-formed environment, deciding by the checker's theory whether a subterm is a type or a proof of a proposition, with its correctness proved (Sozeau et al., [Correct and Complete Type Checking and Certified Erasure for Coq, in Coq](https://hal.sorbonne-universite.fr/ENS-PARIS-SACLAY/hal-04077552v3), JACM).
- **Erasure by a recorded quantity reduces nothing.** Idris 2 erases 0-multiplicity arguments by the multiplicity elaboration and the case tree compiler checked ([Implementation overview](https://github.com/idris-lang/Idris2/blob/main/docs/source/implementation/overview.rst)), and a graded dependent type theory states erasure's soundness for its grades ([A Graded Modal Dependent Type Theory with Erasure, Formalized](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.29716)): the form [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) reserves.

## Open questions

- **What an instance's sort tells erasure.** At a use of a declaration that leaves its sort to its caller, the certified module states the sort the use supplies, which the copy drops, so erasure may erase there what it keeps today. Counted before stage 3 lands: the items of `/sys` and `/std` whose erased form moves when their instances keep their sorts, each a change stage 3's check then states rather than an equality it holds.
- **The record's grain.** `Reads` names a declaration read without digesting the answer, where the elaborator's trace digests each part it read, so a recompile may erase again an item today's record keeps. Measured in stage 4 by `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::incremental_tests` and the count of items an edit erases again; a finer record follows if the count moves.
- **The kernel reader's cost.** What erasing the prelude costs through the certifying kernel against the figures above, counted by the same protocol on stage 3 before it lands.

## Stages

2. **The crate, moved.** `curios-erase` is `into_anf` with its erased-unit types and its recompile reasoning, its `curios_core` and `curios_anf` names qualified, still reading through `Retyping`; its refusals leave the elaborator's `Error` for its own, which wraps what `Retyping` refuses, and `curios-pipeline` reports it; `curios-unit`, `curios-pipeline`, `curios-prelude-archive` and `curios` read it from there, and `rust.md`'s seam line names it. Check: `sys.rkyv` and `std.rkyv` byte-identical at one checkout path; every diagnostic the suite pins unchanged; `cargo xtask workspace test`.
3. **The reader.** `curios-erase` reads through the kernel's door and walks the certified module, an instance erased as its head; the driver hands it the certifying kernel; `into_anf/projected.rs`, `Projection` and the `InScope` view are deleted; the reader's refusal erasure wraps is the kernel's; `curios-prelude`'s build erases the prelude after certifying it and `curios-prelude-archive`'s images hold Core only; `Retyping` is deleted. Erasure still writes the elaborator's trace vocabulary. Check: every item of `/sys` and `/std` erases to the form it had before this stage, but those the count under *What an instance's sort tells erasure* names; `tests::erasure::head_tests` and `dropped_tests` pass; the cost read by the protocol.
4. **The record.** Erasure files its own record; `Question::Erased` and `Question::Ran` leave `curios-elab`, and `curios-erase` drops its dependency on `curios-elab`. Check: `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::budget_tests`, `tests::order_tests` and `tests::incremental_tests`; `cargo tree -p curios-erase --edges normal` contains no `curios-elab`.
5. **Proof-stub reduction**, independent of the others. In a scratch copy put a `profile!` span around `proof_stub`'s reduction and attribute the erasure share of `bytes_growing(6400)`: visits, charged work and allocations under that reduction, separately from the two checkers. Use the shipped fusion cap for the cost reading; lifting it is only a separate attribution control reproducing the three walks. End with the counted contribution and either a checked way to shape the placeholder without the extra accumulation, or the reason the reduction remains necessary or its cost does not justify a change. Any fix must preserve applicable proof-valued functions, their telescope lengths and curried results; replacing all placeholders with unit is not an answer. Check: `curios`'s `tests::erasure::dropped_tests::a_proof_valued_function_in_a_kept_slot_is_applicable` with controls for different arities and curried results where the fix changes placeholder construction.

## Verification

- **The erased result does not move.** Stage 2 is a move: `sys.rkyv` and `std.rkyv` are byte-identical at one checkout path. From stage 3 the images change by design — `curios-prelude-archive`'s lose their erased forms and `curios-prelude`'s output gains them — so every item of `/sys` and `/std` is compared by the digest of its erased form against the commit before the stage, and the corpus's emitted artifacts and verdicts agree with it. A stored unit's format changes in stages 3 and 4; a unit stored by an earlier compiler lives under that compiler's address and is never read by a later one ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). Counted.
- **A head only conversion shows is read.** `curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests` hold four programs whose callee has a function type through a question to conversion: two spellings of one call, an arm's equation met at another spelling, two calls differing in a proof, a function beside its expansion.
- **What erasure asks.** In a copy of the tree under `target/`, `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` (and, from stage 3, `certification <PATH>`, where the prelude is then erased): the fold's `(within)` rows whose outer is `erase_unit`, read for `reduce::asked_classes`, `reduce::asked_scrutinee`, `convert::drain`, the `universe::` checks and `machine::reduce_closed`, and their kernel twins once erasure reads through the kernel. Counted.

## Retirement

Before this specification is deleted, `curios-erase`'s README states decisions 1 to 5, 7 and 8 with their rejected alternatives; `curios-elab`'s README no longer holds *Erasure reads a head's type off the finished term* and its two-halves decision names no reader of finished terms; the independent-kernel decision says erasure reads through the certifier; `.claude/rules/rust.md` names `curios-erase` among the lowering seams and the crates that qualify both representations; [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) names `curios-erase` where it names erasure; and no reference to this filename remains.
