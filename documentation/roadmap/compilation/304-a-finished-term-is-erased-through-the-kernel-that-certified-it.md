# A finished term is erased through the kernel that certified it

Erasure, a crate of its own, `curios-erase`, walks the module the kernel certified at the sorts the kernel judged, reads every type it is directed by through that kernel, and erases the prelude where the prelude is certified; what is left is that it files its own record of what it read. Its next step is stage 5, a delivery that waits on one open question, what erasure still borrows of the elaborator; the proof-stub investigation is independent of every other stage.

## What this builds on

- **Erasure comes after the certifier** ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)): everything the elaborator invents reaches the certifier as an ordinary term, and erasure comes after, on obligations the certifier checks. The trusted base is `curios-cert`'s dependency closure, and `curios-elab` takes `curios-cert` as a dev-dependency only, so outside tests neither checker depends on the other ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-trusted-base-is-a-crate-boundary)).
- **Erasure is directed by types and types no term** ([`curios-erase`'s README](../../../curios-erase/README.md#erasure-reads-a-heads-type-off-the-finished-term)): it reads a head's type off the finished term (`Lowering::type_of`) and asks the kernel for that type's head, by the reduction that accepted the term.
- **Erasure is a crate of its own** (`curios-erase`), holding its erased-unit types, its recompile reasoning and its refusals (`curios_erase::Error`), and qualifying every `curios_core` and `curios_anf` name. It records its reads in the elaborator's vocabulary still, and `curios-elab` exports what that takes: the trace vocabulary (`Question`, `Answered`, `Made`, `Read`, `Parts`, `Foreign`, `HeldTrace`, `TraceRecord`, `Traces`, `Readers`) and its digests (`part_digest`, `bytes_of`, `found`, `Candidating`), the registry comparisons (`Same` and the `*_alike` functions) and the spelling a refusal is reported in (`Reading`).
- **Each stage keeps what its own record says stands** ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)). The kernel files its own record of what a judgment read (`Reads`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/reads.rs`), which a recompile adopts a verdict by ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)).
- **The two checkers share what can be shared.** `curios-core` holds the intrinsic folds, the closed machine and the `Reducer` trait; `curios-analysis` holds the inversion, the erasure-shape classification and the analyses over `Env` and `Judge`. Each checker owns its weak-head reduction of open terms, its sorts and its conversion, and the kernel memoizes its reducts, sorts and inferred types for as long as one declaration's budget lives ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#the-kernel-memoizes-its-own-evaluation-and-a-memo-changes-only-resource-verdicts), `curios-cert/src/kernel/memos.rs`).
- **A declaration that leaves a sort to its caller is judged at each sort a use can state** ([A declaration leaves a sort to its caller](../../design/theory/a-declaration-leaves-a-sort-to-its-caller.md)): the kernel judges it at every assignment of `Prop` and `Type` to the sorts it leaves open, reading its terms at that assignment (`at_every_sort`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/module.rs`), and refuses an instance that states a sort parameter (`Kernel::check_instance`), so no term it reads holds an open sort. A local group leaves none (`Error::GroupLeavesSort`). Fifty-five definitions of `/sys` and `/std` leave a sort open — the carriers and their operations, `List/map`, `Io/bind`, `Option/map` and the `Cell` and `Channel` calls among them — and twenty-one state `Prop` for one, twenty-eight times, `/std/Bool/holds` answering `Option.{Prop}(Holds(b))` (counted at `d4a405652`, in a scratch copy, from the certified prelude's printed terms).
- **Erasure reads through the kernel that certified the term** (`curios_cert::Reader`, entered by `Kernel::read`). Each entry is a judgment the kernel makes: reduction to a head, as a judgment that takes a value apart reads it, a term's type — a neutral's, and a type former's sort — which half a value of a type is in, as the erasure obligations classify a position and remembered beside the sorts, a case put in force and an inductive arm entered as the arm is checked (`enter_case` and `enter_arm` beside `check_arm`, sharing `assume_case_value` and `bind_solutions` with it), the binders and the frame bracket, the constraints a declaration's universe context states, the lookups, the budget and the read record. Inside the bracket no erased position and no call is recorded. The door holds no `DefinitionKind`, which the walk reads off the certified module's items. The driver and the prelude's build hand erasure the kernel that certified the unit (`certify_module`, then `erase_unit`, `curios-erase/src/lower.rs`), so erasure reads the unit's definitions as the walk defined them, the units in scope it mounted and the vectors it filed. A declaration that leaves sorts open erases to a form at each assignment (`ErasedForms`, `curios-erase/src/erased.rs`), a form known by its name and its sorts (`AtSorts`, `curios-erase/src/sorts.rs`), and a use reads the row and links the form at the sorts its instance states. What a reading spends is a test-support door (`Reader::consumed`).
- **The prelude is one build that reaches the kernel** ([`curios-prelude`'s README](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#the-prelude-is-one-build)): `curios-prelude`'s build script elaborates and certifies each root, erases it through the kernel that certified it and images it as a certified unit.
- **An erased form is its item's** ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#an-erased-form-is-its-items)): each item erases into a fragment on a budget restored for it, reading every other declaration by name.

## What is wrong today

**Erasure records through the elaborator.** What erasing an item read is written in the elaborator's vocabulary (`Question::Erased`, `Question::Ran`, `curios-elab/src/context/traced.rs`): the names the kernel's reader noted (`Reads`) become each question a reading of them could have met (`Asked`, `curios-erase/src/lower.rs`), and erasure's own recompile reasoning answers them (`curios-erase/src/over.rs`, `erased_candidate.rs`). So `curios-erase` depends on `curios-elab` still, for the trace vocabulary, its digests, the registry comparisons and the spelling a refusal is reported in.

**Uncertain cost: shaping a proof stub evaluates its bound's subject again.** `proof_stub` (`curios-erase/src/lower/function.rs`) reduces a slot's type to distinguish a proof from a proof-valued function and builds the latter as an applicable function of the right arity, recursively through curried results. For `Bytes/slice(go(800, x[]), 0, 10)`, where `go` appends ten bytes per recursive step (`curios`'s `tests::reduction`, `bytes_growing(800)`), the `@within` type is `Bool/Holds(0 + 10 <= Bytes/len(built))`: finding its head can run the accumulation again. Observed at `9c543476a`, counted in a scratch copy with `FUSION_CAP` lifted: 2 400 concatenations fuse, three runs of 800. A backtrace at the first concatenation of each run stands in the elaborator's `get_or_init_elaborated`, the kernel's `reading`, and `proof_stub` under `kept_operand` and `erase_let`. This locates a third evaluation; its contribution to the shipped compile remains unpriced because `type_level_sequence_cost_measurements` measures the whole compilation. Whether a placeholder can be shaped without reducing that proposition's head is also unknown. A relevance mark alone does not state a callable placeholder's arity; moving the walk to the kernel alone does not remove its reduction.

## Decisions

1. **Erasure is a crate, and the crate is erasure.** Erasure is `curios-erase`'s root and its top-level modules; no module repeats the crate's name, as `curios-cert`'s README rejects a `kernel::` namespace. `curios-erase` depends on `curios-cert`, `curios-anf`, `curios-analysis` and `curios-core`, and on nothing of `curios-elab`. It qualifies every `curios_core` and every `curios_anf` name, as `curios-emit` qualifies both representations it translates between, and `.claude/rules/rust.md` states it so.
2. **Erasure reads through the kernel that certified the term.** A reading door at `curios-cert`'s root offers a reader of certified terms the kernel's own judgments: reduction to a head, a term's type, which half a value of a type is in, a case and an arm entered as they are checked, the frame bracket, the lookups, the budget and the read record. It adds no rule, since each entry is a judgment the kernel already makes, and no verdict moves. A type is then read by the reduction that accepted the term by construction, and the reader holds no elaboration half to hide.
3. **The certifying kernel is the reader.** The driver hands the kernel that certified a unit to that unit's erasure (`certify_module` then `erase_unit`, `curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`), so erasure reads through the environment the walk built: the unit's definitions as the walk defined them, the units in scope it mounted and the vectors it filed. Its memos are not handed over: each lives one declaration's budget, and one carried from certification into erasure would make what erasure spends depend on whether certification judged the item or adopted its verdict, which a recompile changes.
4. **The prelude is erased where it is certified.** The prelude's build erases each root through the kernel that certified it, as the driver erases every unit; what the one build costs and why it reaches the kernel is [`curios-prelude`'s README](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#the-prelude-is-one-build)'s.
5. **Erasure files its own record.** What its reductions read, as the kernel's `Reads`, and the erased forms it read; a recompile keeps an item's erased form where that record stands, checked as a cached verdict's record is. `Question::Erased` and `Question::Ran` leave the elaborator's vocabulary for erasure's own, which `curios-unit` stores with the erased-unit types.
6. **Relevance is read off a sort until a mark states it.** Where [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) makes a binder's mark exact, `curios-erase` reads the mark where it reads a sort; nothing here waits on it.
7. **Erasure walks the module the kernel certified, at the sorts the kernel judged.** Each declaration is read as the kernel holds it, with its universes, through the kernel's environment. One that leaves sorts to its caller erases once for each assignment of them the kernel judged it at, each form read off the declaration's terms at that assignment as the kernel reads them, so a form is known by its declaration's name and its sorts; one that leaves none erases once. An instance erases as its head's form at the sorts it states, and a constructed value, a record, a projection and a match read their declaration's row at the sorts their instance states, so a use at `Prop` drops what the kernel classifies as a proof there and links a form erased at the same sorts. A level carries nothing at run time, so a value erases by its fields and a type former to the unit, as each does in the copy. A form identical at two assignments is one object, stored once. An item is still kept or erased again whole, its trace what erasing each of its forms asked; a program links each form it reaches, and every form of an item that computes. No copy of the module out of its universes is walked or kept, since the certifying kernel already holds the unit's scope.
8. **Erasure owns its refusals.** `curios_erase::Error` holds what erasure refuses a program for — the verifier's two recursion rules, named by the source names erasure minted each identity for, a natural case key past `NatCase`, and its budget's exhaustion at the term it was reading — each located at the term erasure was erasing and worded as erasure's: the program was certified, and only erasure stopped. Anything else its reader refuses of a term the kernel certified is a compiler fault, reported as a term erasure could not read with the reader's refusal beneath it, never as the kernel's verdict on the program. `curios-pipeline` reports it beside the kernel's refusal (`erasure_refusal` beside `kernel_refusal`). The elaborator's `Error` holds none of erasure's refusals and names none of its types.

**Rejected.** Reading through the elaborator's engine, as Lean's code generator infers types through `MetaM` and Rocq's extraction recomputes them through `Retyping`: the reader of a certified term would decide by the side that did not certify it. Erasure generic over the shared `Env`/`Judge` seam, which would have one implementor: the seam is for the analyses both checkers run, and erasure is not one. Folding the mark into this work: a mark states no callee's, record's or scrutinee's type, so the kernel reader is needed with it or without it, and the mark's own questions belong to the trusted base. A module named `into_anf` inside the crate, which spells the disambiguation the crate's name already makes. Projecting the module out of its universes for the reader: a projected term is not the term the kernel certified, and the copy reads every sort a declaration leaves open as kept, wherever it is used, which no judgment of the kernel's makes. One form for a declaration that leaves a sort open, as Rocq's extraction and Lean's compiler make one: their theories forbid a generic body to eliminate a value whose sort may be `Prop` into data but for a subsingleton, where the kernel here judges each assignment instead, so a generic body may, and a caller at `Prop` handing it a stand-in would meet a callee erased at `Type`; restricting the language to fit one form would refuse programs the kernel accepts. Forms only at the sorts some use states: a unit's erasure would depend on its successors, and the prelude's image could not be made once.

## Prior art

- **A code generator reads types through the front end.** Lean 4's LCNF decides what is irrelevant with `Meta.inferType` and `isPropFormerType` in `MetaM` ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.Types`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/Types.html)), over `CoreM` with a context of its own ([`Lean.Compiler.LCNF.CompilerM`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/CompilerM.lean)); Rocq's extraction computes sort families through `Retyping`, which recomputes the type of a term "known to be well-typable" without checking it ([`Retyping`](https://docs.rocq-prover.org/v8.18/api/coq-core/Retyping/index.html)); Agda's `Treeless.Erase` runs in its type-checking monad, the only checker it has ([`Agda.Compiler.Treeless.Erase`](https://hackage-content-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.7.20250701/candidate/docs/src/Agda.Compiler.Treeless.Erase.html)).
- **A verified pipeline erases from the checker's judgment.** MetaCoq's certified erasure is a single traversal of a well-typed term in a well-formed environment, deciding by the checker's theory whether a subterm is a type or a proof of a proposition, with its correctness proved (Sozeau et al., [Correct and Complete Type Checking and Certified Erasure for Coq, in Coq](https://hal.sorbonne-universite.fr/ENS-PARIS-SACLAY/hal-04077552v3), JACM).
- **One compiled form is safe where the theory restricts a generic body.** Rocq requires that "elimination is allowed at every ground instantiation" of a sort-polymorphic inductive, and none to `Type` at a variable sort ([Polymorphic Universes](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/V9.1.0/refman/addendum/universe-polymorphism.html)), its extraction resting on such restrictions (Rosain, Sozeau and Winterhalter, [A Ghost Sort for Proof-Relevant yet Erased Data in Rocq and MetaRocq](https://msp.cis.strath.ac.uk/types2025/abstracts/TYPES2025_paper81.pdf), TYPES 2025); Lean forbids eliminating a proof into a universe that is not `Prop` but for a syntactic subsingleton ([`propRecLargeElim`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Error-Explanations/lean___propRecLargeElim)); GHC restricts what a levity-polymorphic function may do so that one compiled form serves every representation, kinds being calling conventions (Eisenberg and Peyton Jones, [Levity Polymorphism](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/levity-polymorphism/), PLDI 2017). A monomorphizing compiler makes a form for each instance instead, which decision 7 does over the one dimension the kernel judges at each value.
- **Erasure by a recorded quantity reduces nothing.** Idris 2 erases 0-multiplicity arguments by the multiplicity elaboration and the case tree compiler checked ([Implementation overview](https://github.com/idris-lang/Idris2/blob/main/docs/source/implementation/overview.rst)), and a graded dependent type theory states erasure's soundness for its grades ([A Graded Modal Dependent Type Theory with Erasure, Formalized](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.29716)): the form [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) reserves.

## Open questions

- **The record's grain.** `Reads` names a declaration read without digesting the answer, where the elaborator's trace digests each part it read, so a recompile may erase again an item today's record keeps. Measured in stage 5 by `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::incremental_tests` and the count of items an edit erases again; a finer record follows if the count moves.
- **What erasure still borrows of the elaborator once its record is its own.** The registry comparisons its recompile reasoning asks (`Same` and the `*_alike` functions), `bytes_of`, `found` and `Candidating`, which read a store's records, and `Reading`, the spelling erasure's refusals are reported in (`curios-erase/src/error.rs`): each moves where both stages read it, or erasure stops asking it, before stage 5 drops the dependency.

## Stages

5. **The record.** Erasure files its own record; `Question::Erased` and `Question::Ran` leave `curios-elab`, and `curios-erase` drops its dependency on `curios-elab`. Check: `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::budget_tests`, `tests::order_tests` and `tests::incremental_tests`; `cargo tree -p curios-erase --edges normal` contains no `curios-elab`.
6. **Proof-stub reduction**, independent of the others. In a scratch copy put a `profile!` span around `proof_stub`'s reduction and attribute the erasure share of `bytes_growing(6400)`: visits, charged work and allocations under that reduction, separately from the two checkers. Use the shipped fusion cap for the cost reading; lifting it is only a separate attribution control reproducing the three walks. End with the counted contribution and either a checked way to shape the placeholder without the extra accumulation, or the reason the reduction remains necessary or its cost does not justify a change. Any fix must preserve applicable proof-valued functions, their telescope lengths and curried results; replacing all placeholders with unit is not an answer. Check: `curios`'s `tests::erasure::dropped_tests::a_proof_valued_function_in_a_kept_slot_is_applicable` with controls for different arities and curried results where the fix changes placeholder construction.

## Verification

- **The erased result does not move.** At each stage every item of `/sys` and `/std` is compared, at each assignment of its sorts, by the digest of its erased form against the commit before the stage, and the corpus's emitted artifacts and verdicts agree with it. A stored unit's format changes in stage 5; a unit stored by an earlier compiler lives under that compiler's address and is never read by a later one ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). Counted.
- **A head only conversion shows is read.** `curios`'s `tests::erasure::head_tests` hold four programs whose callee has a function type through a question to conversion: two spellings of one call, an arm's equation met at another spelling, two calls differing in a proof, a function beside its expansion.

## Retirement

Before this specification is deleted, `curios-erase`'s README states decisions 1 to 3, 5, 7 and 8 with their rejected alternatives, and `curios-prelude`'s decision 4; the independent-kernel decision says erasure reads through the certifier; [A proof is known by its mark](../soundness/200-a-proof-is-known-by-its-mark.md) names `curios-erase` where it names erasure; and no reference to this filename remains.
