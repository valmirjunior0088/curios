# A copy is weighed by what it emits

The next step is stage 1, ready. Every copier of `curios-cps` plans a copy within an allowance of distinct nodes ([A copy is planned within its allowance](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-copy-is-planned-within-its-allowance)), though what a node emits ranges from three WebAssembly instructions to over a hundred, and the inliner admits a callee of several call sites by what one copy holds, though it makes one per site. This specification weighs a copy by what it adds to the emitted module: each node by what its lowering emits, and a callee of several sites by all its copies together.

## What is wrong today

**A node counts one, whatever it emits.** `CopyPlan` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/clone.rs`) counts the distinct nodes of a copy's extent against the allowance its copier is given, and every copier reads that count: the inliner, contification, the SCC, call-pattern and jump-pattern specializers, `separate_loop_entries` and the rebuild's reuse versions, each budget (`CopyBudget`) charging and each priority (`CopyPriority`) ranking by nodes. Counted at `0476b6d8b` over the 28 programs of `programs/` (protocol below): a node other than an intrinsic emits 15.5 instructions on average; a `Nat` or `Int` operation, an i31 fast path beside a call into the big-number helpers, 15 to 27 (`NatLe` 15, `NatSub` 18, `NatAdd` 23, `NatMul` 27), and over reference operands at most 23 to 36; a sequence operation far more (`BytesGet` 81, at most 123, `BytesConcat` 103, `ListGet` 60, at most 87, `ListConcat` 50); a row read 4 and a row test 3. Intrinsics are 42% of the program code emitted, and the count prices a `BytesGet` at a sixth of what it emits: `/std/Str/flatten` (10 nodes, two concatenations) and `/std/Flt/digits/digits_to_str` (12 to 16) are copied into four or five sites each, and the printing of one `Flt` is 15% of `flt_hot_loop`'s module. A copy of a helper's digit reads in each arm of a sixteen-way fork, the prototype [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects, is the same understatement at its worst.

**A callee of several sites is admitted by one copy.** `inline_known_calls` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/inline.rs`) copies a callee of several call sites into each where one copy holds at most `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` (16) nodes, whatever the number of sites, and the limit's own doc names what that costs ("the next one a node larger pays the same price with nobody watching"). The corpus sits on it from both sides. `/std/Nat/of_str`'s fold step, called at `Str/fold`'s four widths of a UTF-8 scalar, is past it by a node or two, so the fold calls it per character and `parse_digits` retires 16.9% more instructions than with it copied (`curios wonder --footprint --name /std/Nat/of_str`: `of_str` 5,332 bytes beside its step's 564, against 8,583 with the step absorbed). A limit of 18, which copies that step, also copies `/std/Map/insert` (18 nodes, 62 sites), `/std/Str/follows` and `Bytes`' `Spell` witness into every test that calls them, which makes `/std`'s test program 20% smaller and its compilation 69% more expensive, the row tests' solve 2.7 times and the rebuild in place 1.7 times what they cost today, both passes [An optimizer query costs what its answer uses](200-an-optimizer-query-costs-what-its-answer-uses.md) owns.

## Goal

The allowance a copy is planned within counts what the copy adds to the emitted module, read off the emitter: what each node's lowering emits, and for a callee of several sites what all its copies emit together. The weight is a fact a test holds to the emitter; each allowance is a policy stated in that weight, its figures named by their commit.

## Decisions

- **A node weighs what its lowering emits, in average nodes.** An intrinsic weighs the instructions its lowering over reference operands emits, divided by 16 and rounded, at least one; every other node weighs one, the 15.5 instructions such a node emits on average. Reference operands are the case no later analysis improves, and the one a single emission states exactly; a weight read off the corpus's mean decides alike (corpus -3.19% either way, `/std`'s test program -3.2% and -3.0%) but is a census no test can hold.
- **The weight is stated beside the intrinsic and held by the emitter's test.** `Intrinsic::weight` sits in `curios-cps/src/intrinsic.rs` beside `operand_repr` and `result_repr`, for their reason: the optimizer asks what an operation costs without running codegen. A `curios-emit` test lowers every intrinsic over reference operands and asserts the weight stated for it, so an emitter change that moves one fails naming it.
- **Every copier plans by weight.** `CopyPlan` counts weight where it counted distinct nodes, so one extent has one count whichever pass copies it; every allowance, budget and priority reads that count, and the constants in `curios-cps/src/optimize.rs` named for nodes are named for weight. Measured with the weight at admission alone, the specializers' allowances (256) meet no callee the weight moves past them.
- **A callee of several sites is copied where one copy fits and all of them do.** A copy weighing at most `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_FLOOR` (8) is taken at every site. Past it, a callee of several sites is copied where one copy weighs at most `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT`, now 20, and its copies past the original, times its weight, at most what one copy of a callee of one site may weigh (`SCC_CLONE_NODE_LIMIT`, 256), its sites read off the sweep's `CallAnalysis::call_sites`, so every site of a callee gets one verdict a sweep. The per-copy allowance clears the corpus's hot fold steps (`/std/Nat/of_str`'s and `parse_bindless`'s own weigh 18 to 19); the floor keeps copied the helpers a walk calls per byte, however many sites a program gives them (`/std/Nat/in_range` weighs 6 with 211 sites in `/std`'s test program, `/std/Str/concat` 7 with 219, `/std/Parse/pure` 5 with 196).
- **A known function a call hands is followed as before.** A call handing a callee a function its extent applies keeps the single-site allowance out of `KNOWN_FUNCTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT`, now in weight ([A known function a call hands is followed into it](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-known-function-a-call-hands-is-followed-into-it)).

## Peers

- **A primitive weighs the code it expands to, read off the back end.** GHC charges a primop its `primOpCodeSize`, an attribute of each primop "for the purposes of calculating unfolding sizes" (default 1, a foreign call 4), plus its arguments where the primop is out of line ([`GHC.Core.Unfold`](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/-/blob/master/compiler/GHC/Core/Unfold.hs), [`GHC.Builtin.PrimOps`](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/-/blob/master/compiler/GHC/Builtin/PrimOps.hs)); Flambda 2 sizes each primitive by the native code it becomes, a tagged addition 2, a multiplication 4, a signed division with its check more, an allocation 5, a direct call 4, an indirect one 6 ([`code_size_v1.ml`](https://github.com/ocaml-flambda/flambda-backend/blob/main/middle_end/flambda2/terms/code_size_v1.ml)); GCC's `estimate_num_insns` weighs a division by a non-constant `div_mod_cost` ([`tree-inline.cc`](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/master/gcc/tree-inline.cc)); LLVM's inline cost asks the target's cost model of each instruction and charges nothing for one it rates free ([`InlineCost.cpp`](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/llvm/lib/Analysis/InlineCost.cpp)); Binaryen, whose IR is the emitted WebAssembly, measures a callee's expressions ([`Inlining.cpp`](https://github.com/WebAssembly/binaryen/blob/main/src/passes/Inlining.cpp)). Adopted: a weight per operation read off what the back end emits. Avoided: a weight tuned apart from the code it prices, GHC's attributes or Go's `inlineExtraCallCost = 57`, "benchmarked" ([`inl.go`](https://github.com/golang/go/blob/master/src/cmd/compile/internal/inline/inl.go)); a test holds each weight to its lowering instead.
- **Curios's fast path is Lean's, and Lean leaves its weight to C.** `lean_nat_add` is an always-inline C function testing both operands scalar before calling `lean_nat_big_add` ([`lean.h`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/include/lean/lean.h)), while LCNF's `Code.sizeLe` counts declarations ([`InlineCandidate`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Compiler/LCNF/Simp/InlineCandidate.html)) and clang's inliner weighs the expansion. Avoided: nothing below Curios would, Binaryen and Cranelift being good to have, and Binaryen never flexibly inlines a function holding a call (`return !hasCalls && ...`), which every `Nat` operation's slow path is.
- **A callee's copies are bounded together, above a floor.** MLton copies a non-recursive callee where `(numCalls - 1) * (size - small) <= product`, `small = 60`, `product = 320` ([`inline.fun`](https://github.com/MLton/mlton/blob/master/mlton/ssa/inline.fun), [`control-flags.sml`](https://github.com/MLton/mlton/blob/master/mlton/control/control-flags.sml)); Binaryen always inlines a body of at most 2 expressions and one of a single caller ([`pass.h`](https://github.com/WebAssembly/binaryen/blob/main/src/pass.h)); GCC caps a unit's growth by inlining, `inline-unit-growth` 40%, and lets small growth through early, `early-inlining-insns` 6 ([`params.opt`](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/master/gcc/params.opt)). Adopted: MLton's product under a per-copy allowance, and Binaryen's floor. Avoided: the product alone, which measured with `small = 8` copies large callees of few sites and grows every program 2 to 15%; and a unit-wide budget, which lets the order the inliner meets callees decide which are copied, where a callee's own copies decide it here.
- **What a copy enables is a discount.** GHC discounts an argument the callee scrutinizes or applies (`unfoldingFunAppDiscount`, Curios's known-function allowance); LLVM rewards constant arguments that simplify and the last call to a local function (`LastCallToStaticBonus`, Curios's moving of a callee of one site); Flambda 1 inlines, simplifies, and keeps the copy where `new_size - estimated_benefit <= original_size` ([`inlining_cost.ml`](https://github.com/ocaml/ocaml/blob/4.14/middle_end/flambda/inlining_cost.ml)); Chez Scheme's `cp0` integrates within a residual `score-limit` of 20 and an `effort-limit` of 200 ([`cp0.ss`](https://github.com/cisco/ChezScheme/blob/main/s/cp0.ss)). Not adopted: a literal argument's discount is the benefit model [A known function a call hands is followed into it](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-known-function-a-call-hands-is-followed-into-it) rejects for want of a weight to set it against; that reason is gone once the weight lands, and a consumer may reopen it.
- **A callee is weighed after its own callees are copied.** LLVM's inliner walks the call graph's components bottom-up. Measured, not adopted: taking calls by their callee's component, leaves first, copies `/std/Nat/in_range` into `/std/Nat/of_str`'s step before the step is weighed, which then passes 20 and gives back `parse_digits`' 16.9%; and `in_range` keeps 207 sites in `/std`'s test program, which the sweep's order does not make.
- **A hot call gets a larger allowance from a profile.** LLVM's `HotCallSiteThreshold`, Go's `inlineHotMaxBudget = 2000`, GCC's badness over estimated frequency. Avoided: Curios has no profile, and the static stand-in measured, a larger allowance at a call inside a recursive function or a cycle of continuations, admits every program's argument parsing as hot and gives back the weight's gain (corpus -0.53% where the weight alone took -3.19%, `/std`'s test program -0.07%).

## Stages

1. **All copies weighed together.** In `inline_known_calls`, a copy weighing at most `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_FLOOR` taken at every site; past it, `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` at 20 for one copy and `SCC_CLONE_NODE_LIMIT` for a callee's copies past the original; the limit's doc restated over the measured callees, the fold steps and `/std/Map/insert`, in place of `/std/State/bind`'s nine nodes. Check: inliner tests of a callee copied at four sites and refused at sixty-two at one size, of one under the floor copied at sixty-two, and of a known function's allowance unchanged; a codegen test that `/std/Nat/of_str`'s step is absorbed in `parse_digits`; the codegen fixtures sized past `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/structural/test_support.rs`) still past it.
2. **A node weighs what it emits.** `Intrinsic::weight` and its `curios-emit` test over every intrinsic; `CopyPlan` counts weight, every allowance, budget and priority in `curios-cps` reads it, and the constants named for nodes are named for weight. Check: the weight test; an inliner test refusing a callee of few nodes and heavy sequence reads that a count of nodes admits; the measurement above retaken.
