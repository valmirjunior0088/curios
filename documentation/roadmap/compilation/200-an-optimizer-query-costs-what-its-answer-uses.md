# An optimizer query costs what its answer uses

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves two places where a question the continuation optimizer asks pays for more than its answer reads: a call analysis every reader builds whole, where each consumes some of its facts, and a copy plan that enumerates a binding group's bodies before it can decline at its allowance. It is not an implementation plan. The call analysis waits on the builders under *Refinement*, what each reader pays for it being attributed under *What is known*, and the copy plan waits for a consumer: it is the documented qualification of [bounded copy admission](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-copy-is-planned-within-its-allowance), not a violation of its copied-node ceiling.

## What is missing

**Every reader builds the whole call analysis.** `origins` (`curios-cps/src/origin.rs`), `row_facts` (`row_flow.rs`) and `return_protocols` (`optimize/protocol.rs`) call `analyze_calls` and consume only `escaping`. The analysis (`analysis.rs`) also builds incoming sites, node owners, the body-only call graph and its strongly connected components, recursion closed under nested definitions and applied function references, and every function's lexical scope. Worker splitting and returned-closure uncurrying consume sites and escapes but pay for the other facts too; contification and loop-entry separation need the body graph, sites, owners and escapes, while the inliner needs the closed recursion verdict but no lexical scopes. Known-value propagation and the invariant solving over components still need their richer facts. These unused results are allocated and traversed on every query, including through several solvers in one optimizer round.

**A bounded copy enumerates before it declines.** `CopyPlan::limited` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/clone.rs`) checks the distinct-node allowance when a pending body is popped, but a visited `LetFun` first reads every defined member, inserts its identity in `nested` and queues its body; `LetCont` likewise enumerates and queues its complete group. A request with an allowance of sixteen can therefore enumerate and queue thousands of members before its seventeenth distinct node refuses the copy, and repeated call sites repeat that work. Synthetic wide-group controls establish the shape; no current consumer is known to supply wide groups often enough for it to matter, and the copied-node allowance does not price group enumeration.

## What is known

- **What each reader pays for the analysis.** Mixed, at `f0712f8e4`, over `fixpoint_pass_measurements`' `TOML_DRIVER` (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/fixpoint.rs`) in a scratch copy built with every feature and a `profile!` span around each reader's `analyze_calls`: one warm-up and seven readings, each time a median with its span, in a profiling build whose timings include its tracing and its allocation counter. A compilation builds the analysis 143 times, counted, about 5 ms each, and the builds take 757.5 ms [755.8–767.9] of `cps_optimize`'s 1,655.4 ms [1,651.5–1,674.4], about 46%: the inliner 115.1 ms over 21 builds, `origins` 113.5 over 22, contification and loop-entry separation 110.4 over 22, the shrinker's recursion verdict 69.2 over 11, known-value propagation 68.5 over 11, `row_facts` 57.5 over 12, uncurrying 57.3, worker splitting 57.0, `return_protocols` 56.3 and component specialization 55.3 over 11 each. The three readers that consume `escaping` alone take 227.3 ms [224.9–230.3], about 14%, which is the most an escape-only query saves there, less what the query costs; each reader's demand and flow solvers stand apart from these spans.
- **What a narrower builder keeps.** The two notions of recursion, the lexical scopes the verifier derives, and the treatment of nodes a round orphans: a flat scan of the live arena would count references the walk over owning bodies excludes. Dead-parameter cleanup's arena-wide escape snapshot (`optimize/dead_parameters.rs`) and its continuation-before-function phase boundary remain its own contract, not a replacement for that body-owned query.
- **What a whole test program pays for the analysis.** `curios test` in `curios-std/src` with no filter compiles the 291 tests `/std` declares as one program, where `curios-std/tests/claims.rs` compiles one a module: at `d0333400f`, release, over a cold store, the link stage takes 401.6 s against 0.4 s, 1.4 s, 1.0 s and 10.0 s for the programs of `Nat`, `Json`, `Str` and `Tui`, of 3, 10, 26 and 76 tests, the compile stage 6 s in each, timed. Of nine samples of the main thread forty seconds apart, the first two stand in `analyze_calls` under `uncurry_returns`, deep in `persistent::upsert`, and the seven from two minutes on wait on Binaryen's pass runner, which is no query of this optimizer's and is an entry of the findings file (`000-findings.md`). The test program is the compilation attribution names as paying for the whole analysis where uncurrying consumes sites and escapes alone.
- **What it does not reopen.** [A pass applies the independent plans its snapshot admits](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-pass-applies-the-independent-plans-its-snapshot-admits) rejects a general retained cache.

## Refinement

The two costs have independent consumer gates and completion checks. The next delivery is the escape-only query for `origins`, `row_facts` and `return_protocols`, measured against the existing full analysis. The whole test program of `/std` is the consumer that names a compilation paying for the full analysis under `uncurry_returns`, and its link stage is measured beside the fixpoint cohort. Other narrower builders follow only where their own attributed savings justify them. This serial improvement precedes the representation and executor work of [item tasks](302-item-tasks.md); it waits on neither that work nor the bounded-copy investigation. The measured 14% is an upper bound on the three readers' share before replacement cost, not a promised end-to-end saving or the whole analysis's 46%.

### The call analysis

- **The builders**: phase-local builders for the facts each reader needs, beginning with an escape-only query over the same owning function bodies, with no general analysis cache retained.
- **Check**: direct escape comparisons with the existing analysis on nested and orphaned references; the current recursion, scope and snapshot-deferral tests; unchanged optimized CPS and both Wasm artifacts over the fixpoint measurement's consumer cohort; and attributed analysis counts and default-release compilation cost.

### The bounded copy

- **The consumer.** It opens when attribution names a program whose compilation pays for this.
- **The direction.** Group bodies enumerated incrementally in the shared planner, keeping complete successful extents, deduplication and fresh-plan execution; no second fuel limit, and no change to which copies fit.
- **Check**: counted group-member enumeration and pending work over doubling groups, identical exact-limit plans and nested captures, and unchanged consumer artifacts and scheduling.

## Completion and retirement

A completed query states its contract in the owning builder and tests, and its remaining consumers alone stay here. Completing the call-analysis work does not release the bounded-copy work: that part remains blocked on its own consumer. Once neither part has pending work and dependent references name the implemented contracts, delete this specification.
