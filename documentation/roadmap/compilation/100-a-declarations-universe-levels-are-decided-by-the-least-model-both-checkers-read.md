# A declaration's universe levels are decided by the least model both checkers read

**Adopted by the user on 2026-11-22 and rewritten from its headline on 2026-11-23; its next step is stage 1, a ready delivery.** [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) has one counter decide whether a declaration type-checks, and the elaborator's universe solver spends a second: its consistency search, `SEARCH_BUDGET`, 200,000 visits a search, fresh at each and charged to no budget (`curios-elab/src/universes/solver.rs`). The certifier answers the same two questions of a constraint set without one, by the shared layer's forward reasoning to a least model ([`curios-analysis`'s README](../../../curios-analysis/README.md#level-entailment-is-forward-reasoning-to-a-least-model)): whether it has a model in the naturals, and what it entails. Asked by the elaborator too, those two decisions answer every question it asks of its store; the search, its cap and the cache built around it go, and a refusal names the constraints that cannot hold together.

## What is wrong today

The figures are counted at `27dfb63e0`, over the prelude's build as `cargo xtask profile prelude` files it, in a copy of the tree that notes where the cached search runs out and asks the certifier's `entails` and `satisfiable` beside each of the solver's questions.

- **A second counter decides what a declaration elaborates to.** Where the full check exhausts `SEARCH_BUDGET`, `SearchExhausted` refuses the declaration for a limit the budget does not see; where the cached entailment search, `ConsistencyCache::refuses`, exhausts it, `entails` reads the exhaustion as a refusal of the negation, so as entailed, and the weak join drops a run for want of search. The prelude's build asks the search 4,325,053 entailments, and the cached search runs out on 287 of them, in ten `/std/Map` declarations, at up to 194 branches: each of the 287 answers "entailed" where the naturals and the semilattice both answer "not entailed", so which weak pairs those ten keep, and which level an irrelevant occurrence lands on, follows the cap. The full check never runs out.
- **Each question is decided twice, and entailment as two relations.** The elaborator decides its store's consistency by a branching search over right-hand maxima, exponential in the branches and capped; the certifier by Bezem and Coquand's forward reasoning, polynomial in the set and its offsets as written (`curios-analysis/src/entail.rs`, `level_model.rs`, `satisfy.rs`). On the 63,557 checks of the prelude's build that decide a store not decided before, the two agree. For entailment the elaborator refutes the negation over the naturals, which reads consequences of their total order — `u + 1 ≤ max(u, v)` gives `u + 1 ≤ v` — and the certifier derives what the semilattice with a successor derives, the relation a generalized context is checked by; on every question the search answers without running out, they agree.
- **The weak join asks a question a pair.** `held_relations` and `relations_stand` ask, of each ordered pair of a declaration's held levels and zero, a gap a step at a time up to `GAP_CAP`, a second bound, and the join tries every pair conversion met, a repeat included: `/std`'s 2,361 joins meet 83,228 pairs, 30,724 of them distinct, `Map/internal/Node/canonical`'s 7,397 and 655, in 48,091 runs.

## Goal

- `curios-analysis`'s `LevelModel` reads a level's relations for the elaborator, and its `entails` and `satisfiable` read constraints where a caller holds them, as the elaborator's store holds them beside their origins; the certifier's verdicts do not move.
- The weak join reads a held level's relations off the least model above it — how far each other held level and zero stand below it — and drops a repeated pair before it is tried; `gap`, `GAP_CAP`, the elaborator's `entails` and `refused_as_decided` go.
- The elaborator's consistency is `satisfiable` over its store, a model a check; the search, `Potential`, `DifferenceGraph`, `SEARCH_BUDGET`, `SearchExhausted` and `ConsistencyCache` go, and `universe_context_validate` is a context's closure and `satisfiable`.
- A reported inconsistency names a least set of the store's constraints that cannot hold together, each kept only where the rest without it is satisfiable, worded from the one raised last; a speculative check asks `satisfiable` alone.

## Settled

- **Sharing the decisions costs no second opinion.** [An independent certifier re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-certifier-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md) shares "what arithmetic over the representation yields, which two copies could only disagree on through a transcription slip", and a decision procedure for one fixed theory, the semilattice with a successor, is such a function of a constraint set: the second opinion on levels stands in what each checker collects, the elaborator from its solving and the certifier from its own typing. That decision and `curios-cert`'s *The universe decisions are the shared layer's* state it.
- **Entailment is the semilattice's.** The elaborator relates held levels by the relation the certifier checks a context by, as Rocq does: Sozeau and Bezem's refinement of the same procedure answers elaboration's consistency, producing a loop, and the kernel's entailment, "correct and complete for the (in)equational theory", at about 15% over its previous checker on average ([TYPES 2025](https://msp.cis.strath.ac.uk/types2025/abstracts/TYPES2025_paper21.pdf)). One least model above a level answers every relation into it, where refuting a negation is a satisfiability run a question.
- **A model a check, nothing kept between checks.** A check reads its store whole: in the prelude's build, 63,557 checks over at most 1,953 constraints, 283 on average. Rocq keeps its model incremental, with a union-find for equalities, against a global graph that lives for a whole library; a store here lives for one declaration, and its equalities are solved by `merge_forced_equalities`.
- **A refusal names a least core.** It costs a satisfiability run a constraint, paid where a refusal is reported and nowhere else. A loop traced through the model's raises follows one part of a right-hand maximum and leaves out what bounds the others — `u + 1 ≤ max(u, v)` named without `v ≤ u` — so it can name a set that holds.
- **A repeated pair is dropped in the order met.** The model's answers are exact, so whether the store holds a pair only falls as the pairs kept before it grow: a repeat answers as its first copy did, and dropping it moves no level. Rocq keeps its weak constraints as a set of unordered pairs (`UPairSet`, `engine/univMinim.ml`), so a repeat is never tried.
- **Peers.** Lean and Agda solve level equations by unification with `max` and stop at `max(?l, ?k) = max(?u, ?v)`, which a user annotates away (TYPES 2025); Curios has no level syntax, so a store and a decision over it are its only route.

**Rejected.** The search kept and its visits charged to the declaration's budget: a second procedure for one theory, still exponential where the model is polynomial. Entailment shared alone: the search and its cap stay for consistency. A second statement of forward reasoning in the elaborator: a second engine for one rule. Entailment over the naturals, by `satisfiable` of the negation: a satisfiability run a question where one model a level answers them all, for a relation no context is checked by. The search kept to explain a refusal: a second engine for a refusal's words. A loop traced through the model: it can name a set that holds. A model kept between checks: a cache no cost has shown. Rocq's unordered set for the weak pairs: a pair's direction picks which meta `add_eq`'s shape default pins, so collapsing `(u, v)` with `(v, u)` moves which level lands where ordered deduplication does not.
