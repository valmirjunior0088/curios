# A recompile costs what the edit reached

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves a recompile whose cost is what an edit reached: a unit compiled over a baseline that passes over the declarations nothing could have moved without asking each, a declaration an edit does reach made again from the parts of it the edit left standing, and a store that holds each facet of a unit as an object of its own, written when a compile makes it and never again, shared by every slot that names it, and found again by a compile of a text the store has held before. It is not an implementation plan.

It rests on [A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md): every stage keeps a declaration by a record of what it read, so what a recompile redoes is what read the edit. What it still does for every other declaration is this specification's.

## What is missing

The figures are of a question about a copy of `/std`, compiled over the archived unit, in allocations, counted at `23c593615`. The copy is `curios-std/src` with no `.curios` beside it, asked by `target/release/curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics <copy>/Nat.crs` and folded by `curios profile <stream>`: as it is, and with `xor(b, true)` respelled `xor(true, b)` in `Bool.crs`, asked of `Bool.crs`. The rows are `into_core_unit` with `into_core_unit::baseline` and `parse` beneath it, `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over` with `order_unit_over` beneath it, `recheck_module`, `erase_unit` and `cache::put`, with the samples `demand::kept`, `demand::asked` and `erase::asked` for how many declarations a walk kept, elaborated and erased.

**A recompile walks the unit it does not redo.** With nothing edited, a unit compiled over a baseline reads every file and compares its text with the one the baseline read, restores the baseline's half whole to keep each declaration's lowering and each file's heads, 0.33 M of the lowering's 1.06 M (`into_core_unit::baseline`), asks again 99,491 questions of the traces of 2,374 declarations, 0.37 M (`demand::kept`), hands the kernel every declaration to adopt, 0.34 M, asks every item's erasure trace again, 0.06 M, and stores the unit, 0.64 M. No declaration is made again, so the floor of a recompile is its unit. What storing it costs is this section's last paragraph.

**An edit elaborates what read it, each from nothing.** Respelling `/std/Bool/not`'s body elaborates again the 168 declarations that unfolded it and none behind them, and erases 251 items again, the ones that read a binding that moved and the values computed over a body that did (`demand::asked`, `erase::asked`): 80.81 M elaborating, 17.59 M judging and 2.69 M erasing, each declaration at what it costs a whole compile. A declaration is the grain, so one whose trace fails at its last question is elaborated whole; and where anything is made again the module is put in its stored order from nothing, 0.42 M (`order_unit_over`), since the order is read off what every item reaches.

**A unit's header is parsed twice before any unit is compiled.** For the stems its package's root claims (`package_source`, `curios-package/src/layout.rs`), from the disk and outside the parse memo by design, and for whether the unit declares the file asked about (`RootSource::declares_module`): `lib.crs` is 63,068 of the 63,131 allocations the unedited question parses. A file an edit touched is parsed whole besides.

**A void attempt is forgotten.** An attempt that read a declaration not yet elaborated is made again, and the next compile of the same unit finds the same order again: a whole compile of the prelude voids 45 (the `demand::void` samples of the profile `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration <PATH>` files).

**A unit is filed whole.** A unit is filed as one file under its slot, replaced whole whenever the unit is filed (`replace`, `curios-verdicts/src/verdicts.rs`): `/std`'s is 44.13 MB, written again for an edit to one declaration. The slot holds the last unit filed under its address and nothing of the one before, so a compile of a text the store held two edits ago elaborates again what that compile elaborated. The file is laid out by reader ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-stored-in-parts-by-who-reads-each)): a recompile copies the lowered items it kept as the bytes its baseline stored and takes the baseline's erased forms as they are, and the elaborated declarations are one archive consed in one table, since they share half of what they spell. So a compile that elaborated one declaration again conses and serializes every elaborated declaration of its unit: consing `/std`'s module takes 118.7 ms (117.6 to 134.5) of the 208.0 ms (207.0 to 212.3) the unit takes to serialize, where the module, once consed, serializes in 7.6 ms (7.5 to 12.1) (`std_image_division`, timed at `a10135de5`, each the median of five readings with their span, in the test profile). One node for one structure across a unit needs a table over every node of the unit whenever one declaration is consed, and a process that restored its baseline holds none: the table is the walk.

## Refinement

### The walk

- **Whether a walk can pass over a declaration without asking its trace.** A question is asked again because its answer may have moved; where no file's text moved and no unit in scope did, none has, and where one declaration was made again the questions that can have moved are the ones about it. What decides that for a declaration ahead of its trace, and whether the kernel's adoption and erasure's keeping follow the same decision or each its own, is open. salsa's durability is the model for the first half: "If a tracked function depends (transitively) only on high durability inputs, and you change a low durability input, then we can very easily determine that the tracked function result is still valid, avoiding the need to traverse the input edges one by one" ([The red-green algorithm](https://salsa-rs.github.io/salsa/reference/algorithm.html)).
- **Whether a baseline's half is read by declaration**, as its lowered items and its erased forms already are ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-stored-in-parts-by-who-reads-each)), so that keeping a declaration's lowering restores that declaration's record and no other.
- **Whether anything smaller than a declaration is kept.** A declaration elaborated again starts from its lowered form; what of an earlier elaboration could stand under a changed read, and what would say so, is not designed.
- **Whether one declaration is parsed from where its head places it**, which bounds what an edit parses by the declaration it touched, and whether a unit's header is parsed once for both of its readers.
- **Whether an attempt can be told what it will read before it is made**, a stored order of declarations being rejected ([A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md)): the lowering knows the names a declaration writes, and elaboration reads more than those.

### The store

- **Whether a facet is an object of the store**, addressed by a digest of what it says, with a slot an index of digests: what a filing then writes, what two slots and two versions of one unit share, how an object no slot names is collected, and what the store's trust argument ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)) vouches for once a slot's bytes are no longer one file's. Unison's codebase is the model, each definition stored under the hash of its structure "in a cache which is never invalidated" ([The big idea](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/)).
- **Whether a facet is found by what it was made from**, the digest of its own input with the digests its trace read, as Go's action ID finds a package's output ([`buildid.go`](https://go.dev/src/cmd/go/internal/work/buildid.go)): a compile of a text the store has held then reads every facet that text made, in whatever order the texts came.
- **How the elaborated declarations' shared structure is held** once a declaration is an object, and found again by a recompile that consed none of it: a stored table of nodes by spelling, with each declaration a set of roots, which Agda's interface files are ([`Agda.TypeChecking.Serialise.Base`](https://hackage-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.5.1/docs/src/Agda-TypeChecking-Serialise-Base.html)); nodes as objects of their own; or a declaration's closure stored whole, at twice the bytes.
- **Whether an image writes each file's text and each path once**: a file's text lies under its scans and again where its lints and the places of its declarations point, and a path is archived as its segments wherever it occurs and interned again wherever it is restored.
- **What it is worth**, measured now that a unit is stored in parts: what a filing spends writing beside what it spends consing and serializing, and the bytes two slots hold twice.
- **What becomes of a recompile storing the bytes a whole compile stores**: a file appended to gives it up, and a store addressed by content keeps it by construction.
