# The second door costs its key's graph

Not refined yet; waits for a consumer. The reduction cache's second door aligns two spellings by a walk that reads a shared subterm once for each path to it. No program at hand gives the door a key that shares universe data, so nothing compiled today pays for it.

## What is missing

`AcrossUniverses::between` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) aligns the stored spelling's levels and sorts with the asking one's through `curios-core`'s `universes_in_order`, which reads each side by the per-occurrence walk (`the_per_occurrence_universe_walk_reads_levels_and_sorts_in_order`), so a key whose graph shares a subterm carrying universe data is walked as its tree on every hit that reaches the alignment. `Term::level_differences` names this shape — a strip of each side is "a walk per path" — and replaces it with a walk over the pair. No program at hand gives the door such a key: counted at `7d28c0209`, the alignment is reached 24,858 times while `/std` is elaborated and ten to thirty-four times in each of the corpus's twenty-six programs, and in every one the levels the per-occurrence walk reads are as many as a walk that enters each node once reads, 1.7 to a key across `/std` and thirty-two at most. It is a walk [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) is not yet true of, which nothing compiled today pays for. `level_differences` cannot stand in as it is: it answers `None` where two instances state different sorts, which the door maps (an occurrence at `Prop` beside one at `Type`), and it hands back no pair where the two sides agree, which the map reads to refuse a metavariable asked for itself and for another level. Fix: a walk over the pair that hands back every level pair and every sort pair once a node, the map built from them as it is from the two sequences. The alignment says why it reads once per occurrence, a walk that skipped a revisit on one side pairing the wrong members; a walk over the pair skips a pair of nodes it has met and never a node of one side, so that reason stands with it. The walk over a pair that exists cannot be widened by a line: `Mode::MaskingLevels` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`) stands a node's own levels down and leaves its sorts in the masked node, which is how `level_differences` finds two nodes apart in a sort apart, so the door's walk needs a mode that stands a node's sorts down beside its levels, in the traversal conversion's own comparison of levels rides on. Check: a second-door hit over a doubling term at `Type(?u)` answers in its own size, as a fixture beside `a_shared_level_walk_asks_each_level_once_per_node`, and `reduction::across_levels` samples the same hits over `/std`.

## Refinement

- **The mode.** A walk over the pair needs a node's sorts stood down beside its levels. `Mode::MaskingLevels` and `MaskedLevels` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`) stand down the levels and the children; whether sorts join them in that mode, behind what `level_differences` asks of it, or in a mode of their own, is not settled.
- **The consumer.** A program whose second-door keys share a subterm carrying universe data; `reduction::across_levels` samples the door's hits and would show one.
