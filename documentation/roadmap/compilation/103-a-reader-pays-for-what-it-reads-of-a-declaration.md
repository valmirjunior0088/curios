# A reader pays for what it reads of a declaration

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a declaration read a fact at a time — "an instantiated declaration is read by facet — its sort, its arity, one constructor, one field — and only the facet asked is instantiated" — and a compilation end in time proportional to what was written plus what the budget counted; [an item imports what it reads of another's row](../../../curios-ersd/README.md#an-item-imports-what-it-reads-of-anothers-row) so that no row is copied once for each declaration that reads it. One reader takes a row whole where it reads a fact of it, so `k` readers of a row of width `w` cost `w·k`: every judgment that asks a type alias what its value is. A row is a family's constructors, a structure's or a tuple type's fields, a function type's domains, or an erased schema.

Every stage is ready. Two readers are investigations (*What is not yet known*): a call of one method of a wide concept, and a construction of a wide family's last constructor.

### Every judgment asks a type alias's value whole

A closed definition whose value is a type is read by unfolding it, and each judgment that asks one fact of a binder typed by it — whether it is a proof, its sort, whether erasure keeps it, the register it is held in — reduces the alias and walks the value: the elaborator's proof marks at publication (`state_proof_binders` → `is_proof_in`) and totality's proof-totality check (`check_proof_totality` → `quality_of`) each walk it for its sort (`Sort::of`), the kernel's erasure obligation the same way (`erased_half` → `Sort::of` → `tuple_sort`), and erasure's register reads its width by walking its telescope (`register_shape` → `shape_walk` → `Telescope::len`). Per reader at 2,000, counted at `7c61c1a15`: the elaborator's sort walks `2w` fields and the kernel's `w`, one shared spine is cloned (`Term::unwrap_or_clone`) and none is rebuilt by consing. One reader's declaration allocates, in the elaborator and the kernel:

| Tuple width | Elaboration, MB | Certification, MB |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,000 | 1.7 | 1.0 |
| 2,000 | 3.2 | 2.2 |
| 4,000 | 6.2 | 4.3 |
| 8,000 | 14.2 | 8.6 |

An unused parameter costs as much, 14.2 and 7.8 at 8,000, so the projection is little of it. A function-type alias pays the same way, through the same judgments.

## What is known

- **The peers read a synonym's kind and a constructor's tag without the rest.** GHC reads a type synonym's kind eagerly and its right-hand side through a thunk forced where it is needed, and a data type's constructor argument types lazily, "in the hope that we may never poke on those argument types" ([`TcIface`](https://downloads.haskell.org/ghc/8.0.1/docs/html/libraries/ghc-8.0.1/src/TcIface.html)); Rocq stores a constant's relevance on it, computed once where the constant is typed (`const_relevance`, [`constant_typing.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/constant_typing.ml)); Lean's IR constructor carries its index and its field counts and nothing of its family (`CtorInfo`, [`Basic.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Compiler/IR/Basic.lean)), and its interpreter takes the IR "directly from the elab_environment", a declaration by name ([`ir_interpreter.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/library/ir_interpreter.cpp)).
## Decided

- **A closed type alias publishes the sort of its value.** A definition taking no parameter whose type is a sort states, on `Definition`, the sort its value inhabits: a sort term under its universe context, instantiated at an occurrence's universes as a family's result sort is. The elaborator writes it as the item is published, beside the proof mark; the kernel computes it where it checks the definition and refuses a definition whose stated sort is not the one it reaches, and one that states none is read by its reduct and refused nowhere, so the fact is held one way, as the proof mark is. It is the reduct's sort, never the annotation's ([`settlement_tests`](../../../curios/src/tests/universes/settlement_tests.rs)'s `a_type_named_through_a_higher_universe_solves_at_its_own`), and a reader asks it through a door of its own, as totality and the variance vectors have. Every judgment that asks a binder's sort, quality or proof-ness reads it where the type is written as the alias, before anything is reduced, in both checkers. Erasure reads its quality alone, every level being one where it reads (`curios_core::project_erased_universes`): a value at a proposition is erased with nothing walked, and any other alias is read by its reduct, as an alias that takes parameters is, which reduction builds and the budget counts. Rejected: the certifier's own record beside the elaborator's field, as a family's polarity vector is filed, which keeps two facts where one held one way serves both checkers; and a judgment's answer remembered past its declaration, which the cost decision forbids.
- **A telescope states its width.** Each `Cons` carries the length of the telescope it heads, as a count only `curios-core`'s telescope module builds, so `len` is constant time and no construction states a length its spine does not have; elsewhere a telescope is built through `Telescope::cons`. A positional slot is found by walking to it, never by listing every mark. Rejected: the width on `TupleType` and `FuncType` alone, the two formers whose readers ask it today, which leaves `len`'s other readers walking and states a sequence's length beside the sequence rather than on it.

## What is not yet known

- **What a method call pays its concept.** One call of one method of a concept of 4,000 methods costs the elaborator 353 KB against 247 at 1,000, about an allocation for each method, and the kernel and erasure the same at both widths, counted at `7c61c1a15`; at `710e1c46f` the kernel's cost and erasure's grew too, and neither does since. The elaborator's growth stands in the allocations of `Context::standing_on` itself, a parked problem's retry, 33 KB a call at 1,000 and 120 KB at 4,000; it stands there with no weak-head value filed as a key, so it is not the witness the call resolves, a structure of `w` methods. Settled by: gdb samples of the calls' elaboration at a concept of many methods, the cost attributed; a cost left is a stage here, and none left drops the item.
- **What elaboration pays a construction of a wide family.** One construction of the last constructor of a family of 4,000 costs the elaborator 73 KB and 1,461 allocations against 27 KB and 716 at 1,000, spread over the spans the profile opens, and the kernel and erasure the same at both widths, counted at `7c61c1a15`; at `b5a6d9df5` the kernel's cost grew, 47 → 120, and it does not since. Settled by: gdb samples of many constructions at a wide family, the cost attributed; a cost left is a stage here, and none left drops the item.

## Stages

7. **A closed type alias is read by the sort it publishes.** The field on `Definition`, written where the definition is published, compared where a recompile asks whether a definition moved (`curios-elab/src/same.rs`), its door, and the elaborator's sort rule answering a reference to the alias by it (`curios-elab/src/convert/sort.rs`); the kernel's check of it where it checks a definition, and its sort rule (`curios-cert/src/kernel/sort.rs`); and erasure's erasability (`is_erasable_in`), which reads a proposition off it. Stored bytes: each published definition whose value is a closed type. Check: the growths `AliasReaders` and `ArrowAliasReaders`; the higher-universe regression; a kernel test refusing a definition whose stated sort it does not reach; `/std`'s verdicts and erased forms equal. Size: three commits, the elaborator's, the kernel's and erasure's.
8. **A telescope states its width.** The count on `Cons` and `Telescope::cons`, then the readers that walk to learn a width or one slot: `marks` read at one position, erasure's register and projection. Stored bytes: every telescope. Check: the growths of stages 6 and 7 with their last width-sized walk gone, counted by `term::looks`; `tests::laws`; `/std`'s verdicts and erased forms equal. Size: two commits.

## Measuring

Each probe is a program on standard input. A reader probe declares the row and `k` functions reading it, ending in `()`, under `wonder stage ersd -` or `wonder diagnostics -`: a tuple alias `pub let T: Type = {Nat, …}` read as `pub let f{i}(x: T) -> Nat = x.0;` or not at all; a function-type alias `pub let F: Type = (Nat, …) -> Nat;` read by `pub let g{i}(_f: F) -> Nat = 0;`; a structure `pub struct S: pub Type { f0: Nat, … }` read by `pub let g{i}(s: S) -> Nat = s.f{w-1};` or by `pub let g{i}(_s: S) -> Nat = 0;`; a match `match b | c{w-1}() => 1 | _ => 0 end` over the family; `Eq/refl()` at `Eq()(Wide/c{w-1}(Nat/add(i, 1)), Wide/c{w-1}(i + 1))` over `pub induct Wide(A: Type): pub Type` of `w` constructors `c{i}(A)`; a call `C/m{w-1}(x)` of a concept of `w` methods `m{i}(A) -> A`, satisfied at `Nat`; a construction `Big/c{w-1}()`; and, the control, `pub let g{i}(x: Wide(Nat)) -> Wide(Nat) = x;`. Run each under `--profile <stream>`, fold it with `curios profile <stream>`, and read a stage's allocated bytes and allocations off the rows `elaborate_and_zonk_with_prelude`, `recheck_module` and `erase_unit` with an empty group, a declaration's off `declaration` and `certify_declaration` with its group; a reader's cost is the difference between `k` = 16 and `k` = 1 divided by 15. Counts of what a walk materialized come from temporary `curios_profile::sample!` sites in a scratch copy of the same commit: each `TupleType` `Visit::adopt` rebuilds and `Term::unwrap_or_clone` clones, and each tuple a sort walk visits; a temporary `profile!` span around `Sharing::share` and `ClosedMemo::key` counts consing. A stored unit's bytes are a library package of the declarations, judged once by `wonder diagnostics lib.crs`, its `.curios/objects` summed. Every figure is counted; none needs a quiet machine.

## Verification and retirement

Each stage holds its own growth shape under `growth_slopes`, whose every slope stays under three where the shape grows a row's width and its readers together. A stage that moves stored bytes states them and holds verdicts, erased results and executable artifacts equal; one that moves none holds `/std`'s images byte-identical at one path. A landed stage moves its contract to its owner — the published sort to [A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)'s list of what a declaration publishes — and this specification keeps the rest. Retire it once every stage has landed, both investigations are settled, and nothing references this file.
