# A reader pays for what it reads of a declaration

[Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a declaration read a fact at a time — "an instantiated declaration is read by facet — its sort, its arity, one constructor, one field — and only the facet asked is instantiated" — and a compilation end in time proportional to what was written plus what the budget counted; [an item imports what it reads of another's row](../../../curios-ersd/README.md#an-item-imports-what-it-reads-of-anothers-row) so that no row is copied once for each declaration that reads it. Seven readers take a row whole where they read a fact of it, so `k` readers of a row of width `w` cost `w·k`: an occurrence of a structure's name, an evaluation's link, a match, a comparison of two constructions or two matches, every judgment that asks a type alias what its value is, the reducers that file a type former as a key, and the anonymous schema each projecting fragment builds. A row is a family's constructors, a structure's or a tuple type's fields, a function type's domains, or an erased schema.

Every stage is ready. Two readers are investigations (*What is not yet known*): a call of one method of a wide concept, and a construction of a wide family's last constructor in the kernel.

### A structure's name is spelled from its whole entry

Elaboration makes an occurrence of a former's name what the former's body is ([`former_spelling`](../../../curios-core/src/module.rs)), and "a record's fields are no part of its former". `spelled_former` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate.rs`) clones the entry's `arity`, which for a structure is its parameters ending in its fields, and `former_spelling` opens the telescope past the parameters, the fields, for a record as for a family, so every type that names a structure costs the structure's width. Three of four gdb samples of 256 readers of a structure of 16,000 fields stand in `spelled_former`'s copy of the field telescope, in its opening of it, and in a field telescope dropped where a signature's elaboration returns; the fourth in totality's walk of a signature (`type_positions`). The cost came in with the former's node (`bf6979c37`): at `710e1c46f` both structure rows are flat.

### An evaluation links every row it reaches whole

An item is evaluated in a module of what it reaches (`evaluate`, `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/evaluated.rs`), and `Linking::add_reached` (`link.rs`) links the declaring item's rows for each row a fragment imports (`Module::link_rows`, `curios-ersd/src/link.rs`), every constructor of a family among them; `Module::taken` (`link/taken.rs`) then scans the family's list once per family the suffix names to find each constructor's position. The evaluator reads no row: nothing under `curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/` reads a family, a product or a constructor, which it holds by identity. Over the family probe, the evaluations register 1,000,000, 4,000,000 and 16,000,000 constructors and scan as many list entries at 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000.

### A match, and a comparison, read the family whole

`Constructors` finds a tag in constant time because "a scan for a tag would make every construction and every reader of one case pay the family's width" (`curios-core/src/inductive.rs`), and `Context::induct_facet` reads a sort, an arity or one constructor "and pays for no other" (`curios-elab/src/context.rs`). Four readers clone the declaration and instantiate every constructor instead:

- **The elaborator's match** (`NominalSplit::resolve`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs`) clones and instantiates the family (`instantiate_induct_decl_at`), then walks every constructor in declaration order and searches the written arms for each (`cases.iter().find`), so a match with a default pays `w` and one without pays `w` times its arms; the matrix compiler walks the constructors twice more (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/matrix.rs`).
- **Erasure's match** (`erase_induct`, `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/eliminate.rs`) clones and instantiates the family and walks every constructor, skipping each a default covers.
- **Conversion** clones the declaration to read the arity of two family types (`compare_induct_type`), one constructor's telescope of two constructions (`compare_variant`), and each written arm's constructor of two matches (`declared_at`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`).

`InductInstance::whole` defends the first two: it is "what a reader of every constructor — a match, an elimination, a derivation — takes". A match with a catch-all reads the constructors it names and no other, so the sentence holds of a derivation and of a match that writes every arm, and not of a match with a catch-all. The kernel reads by facet throughout (`InductAt`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/at.rs`), and its row for the comparison is flat.

### Every judgment asks a type alias's value whole

A closed definition whose value is a type is read by unfolding it, and each judgment that asks one fact of a binder typed by it — whether it is a proof, its sort, whether erasure keeps it, the register it is held in — reduces the alias and walks the value. Ten whole-stack gdb samples of 64 readers of a tuple alias of 8,000 `Nat` fields at `710e1c46f` stand in five judgments: the elaborator's proof marks at publication (`state_proof_binders` → `is_proof_in`, copying the shared tuple to learn it is no function type, and walking it for its sort), the elaborator's projection (`elaborate_proj` → `force_rec`), totality's proof-totality check (`check_proof_totality` → `quality_of`, walking it and filing it as a key), the kernel's erasure obligation (`erased_half` → `Sort::of` → `tuple_sort`), and erasure's register (`register_shape` → `shape_walk`, filing it as a key and dropping a copy of it). Per reader at 2,000, counted at `b5a6d9df5`: 14 tuples rebuilt by consing, 13 shared spines cloned (`Term::unwrap_or_clone`), the elaborator's sort walks `2w` fields and the kernel's `w`, and one anonymous schema of width `w`. One reader's declaration allocates, in the elaborator and the kernel:

| Tuple width | Elaboration, MB | Certification, MB |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,000 | 6.2 | 2.1 |
| 2,000 | 12.3 | 4.1 |
| 4,000 | 25.3 | 8.6 |
| 8,000 | 50.3 | 17.2 |

An unused parameter costs 38.2 and 16.4 at 8,000, so the projection is little of it. A function-type alias pays the same way, through the same judgments and erasure's walk to its codomain.

### The reducers file a weak-head former as a key, and the machine hands back a copy of it

`Term::rebuilt` is "the one place a traversal or a reduction turns a payload back into a `Term`", and "a reduction that leaves a node as it was … hands back the allocation it was given, so a value reduced again is that allocation, and a table keyed by it meets the allocation it holds" (`curios-core/src/term.rs`). The closed machine's last arm returns every remaining former — sorts, function and tuple types, tuples, structs, variants, inductive types — by `Term::from(Term::unwrap_or_clone(term))` (`curios-core/src/machine.rs`), so a shared former comes back a fresh allocation, its spine copied where it holds a telescope; and every reducer asks and files its table for a term already its own value: the machine at `ClosedMemo::remembered` before it looks at the term, the elaborator at `force_rec`'s forced cache (`curios-elab/src/reduce.rs`), the kernel at every level of `whnf_within` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/whnf.rs`), each consing the value as a key once per declaration. At 2,000 fields, 10.4 MB of each reader's 17.3 MB in the two checkers is consing, 7.1 MB of it under the closed machine's key.

### An anonymous row is materialized by each fragment that projects it

`tuple_schema` (`curios-elab/src/into_ersd/aggregate.rs`) writes a shared schema of `w` opaque fields for each fragment that projects a tuple of width `w`, and `Module::taken` writes another for each evaluation whose suffix names one. The schema states no shape, being shared by every tuple of its width, so its fields say nothing a width does not. Each projecting reader stores 116 KB of objects at 8,000 against 4 KB for a reader that leaves its parameter unused (a library of the alias and one projecting reader stores 2,715,359 bytes of objects, and with 16 readers 4,456,099; with one unused reader 2,603,126, and with 16 2,662,006).

## What is known

- **What the library pays**, at `b5a6d9df5`, over `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration` in the instrumented copy. 91 items of `/sys` and `/std` are evaluated and all 91 finish, at 27.3 MB in all, each in a module of 47 items on average and 219 at most; their links register 952 constructors and 1,027 product fields; erasure writes 505 anonymous schemas of mean width 2.1 and greatest 8. So every stage below is an asymptotic fix whose effect on the library is small, and each states the bytes it moves.
- **Stage 2, prototyped** at `710e1c46f`. An evaluation's module holding each row as an import, in a scratch copy behind a switch: `evaluate_item` 12.4, 24.8 and 49.6 MB at 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000, 234,042, 468,042 and 936,042 allocations, exactly 2.0 per doubling; no constructor registered by an evaluation. `/std`'s five prelude images byte-identical at one path, and a program printing what seven evaluated values hold, of a family with payloads, a structure, a tuple and a list of a family, erases to the same bytes and prints the same number with the switch off and on.
- **Stage 4's machine half, prototyped** at `710e1c46f`. The machine returning a sort, a function type, a tuple type, an inductive type or a structure type before it probes its memo: one tuple reader at 8,000 costs 23.0 MB in elaboration and 8.6 MB in certification against 50.2 and 17.2, with 4 rebuilds and 3 clones; over `/std`, elaboration allocates 1.5% less and erasure 4.7% less, the budget spent is the same 60,570,292 units, and the five images are byte-identical. What it leaves is the elaborator's forced cache and totality's keys, under `elaborate_proj`, `quality_of` and `register_shape`.
- **The peers cache no easy case.** Lean 4's kernel opens `whnf` with "Do not cache easy cases", returning a sort, a Π and a literal before it asks its table ([`type_checker.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp)).
- **The peers read a synonym's kind and a constructor's tag without the rest.** GHC reads a type synonym's kind eagerly and its right-hand side through a thunk forced where it is needed, and a data type's constructor argument types lazily, "in the hope that we may never poke on those argument types" ([`TcIface`](https://downloads.haskell.org/ghc/8.0.1/docs/html/libraries/ghc-8.0.1/src/TcIface.html)); Rocq stores a constant's relevance on it, computed once where the constant is typed (`const_relevance`, [`constant_typing.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/constant_typing.ml)); Lean's IR constructor carries its index and its field counts and nothing of its family (`CtorInfo`, [`Basic.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Compiler/IR/Basic.lean)), and its interpreter takes the IR "directly from the elab_environment", a declaration by name ([`ir_interpreter.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/library/ir_interpreter.cpp)).
- **The peers' matches keep a default.** Agda's case tree holds the constructors written and a `catchallBranch` ([`CompiledClause.hs`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/TypeChecking/CompiledClause.hs)); OCaml's switch holds the case counts, the cases written and a `sw_failaction` ([`lambda.mli`](https://github.com/ocaml/ocaml/blob/trunk/lambda/lambda.mli)). Rocq's kernel `Case` holds an array of branches beside its `case_info`'s arrays of each constructor's declarations and arguments ([`constr.mli`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/kernel/constr.mli)), a match paying its family by construction there; Core's `Cases::Induct` holds a default, and so does Ersd's `MatchVariant`.
- **The peers' tuples carry their size alone.** OCaml builds a tuple as `Pmakeblock(0, Immutable, shape)` and reads a field as `Pfield` by position ([`translcore.ml`](https://github.com/ocaml/ocaml/blob/trunk/lambda/translcore.ml)); a WebAssembly GC struct type is structural, so two anonymous rows of one width are one type wherever they are spelled.

## Decided

- **A record's former is spelled from its parameters.** An occurrence of a structure's name reads the entry where it stands and opens no field: the former takes the parameters, and the fields are the structure type's to state where a field is read.
- **An evaluation's module holds a row as a fragment does.** A family is held at its count with the constructors its fragments name, each at its position, and a structure's schema at its width: a linking that reads no row holds a row no linked item declares as the module's import, at what the fragment's import states (`curios_ersd::Resolution::imports_rows`), finding a position it already holds through an index kept beside the module as the constant index is; `taken` reads count, position and width through `constructor_count`, `constructor_position` and `product_width`. A program still holds every row whole, having no import. Rejected: one module shared by a unit's evaluations, extended an item at a time. [A top-level value is computed where its unit is compiled](../../design/compilation/a-top-level-value-is-computed-where-its-unit-is-compiled.md) rests on "the record is what was linked" and on "an item at a time, in a module of its own", and rejects a unit evaluated whole in one module; `Evaluator::new` indexes every statement of the module it is handed, so a shared module keeps the square unless that index is kept too; and an item that does not finish leaves rewrites in a shared module that its stored form does not hold.
- **A read of a registry entry takes the facet it reads**, through `induct_facet` and `struct_facet` in the elaborator and its retyping, or the entry by reference, as the kernel's handles already do. A match reads the constructors its arms name, ordered by their positions, and the family's every constructor only where no default stands and a missing arm must be ruled out.
- **No reducer asks or files its table for a weak-head value**, and the machine hands back the node it was given: one predicate in `curios-core` names the shapes that are their own values, the machine's last arm's, and every reducer's front door consults it before its table, the machine's, the elaborator's and the kernel's alike, so the two checkers keep one reach. A lambda keeps its self-entry, which records its η probe, and an intrinsic neutral its own. The kernel asks it after its read by value, so a `let` binder still gives way.
- **A copy taken to inspect a node is not taken.** A reader that matches on a reduct's shape reads it by reference and clones only the part it keeps.
- **A closed type alias publishes the sort of its value.** A definition taking no parameter whose type is a sort states, on `Definition`, the sort its value inhabits: a sort term under its universe context, instantiated at an occurrence's universes as a family's result sort is. The elaborator writes it as the item is published, beside the proof mark; the kernel computes it where it checks the definition and refuses a definition whose stated sort is not the one it reaches, and one that states none is read by its reduct and refused nowhere, so the fact is held one way, as the proof mark is. It is the reduct's sort, never the annotation's ([`settlement_tests`](../../../curios/src/tests/universes/settlement_tests.rs)'s `a_type_named_through_a_higher_universe_solves_at_its_own`), and a reader asks it through a door of its own, as totality and the variance vectors have. Every judgment that asks a binder's sort, quality or proof-ness reads it where the type is written as the alias, before anything is reduced, in both checkers. Erasure's erasability reads it too: a value at a proposition is erased, and one at `Type` at level zero is kept, since a type's own sort stands at level one or above, so no value of such a type is a type or a proof. Anything else is read by its reduct, which leaves a function-type alias at level one or above, and an alias that takes parameters, which reduction builds and the budget counts. Rejected: the certifier's own record beside the elaborator's field, as a family's polarity vector is filed, which keeps two facts where one held one way serves both checkers; and a judgment's answer remembered past its declaration, which the cost decision forbids.
- **A telescope states its width.** Each `Cons` carries the length of the telescope it heads, as a count only `curios-core`'s telescope module builds, so `len` is constant time and no construction states a length its spine does not have; elsewhere a telescope is built through `Telescope::cons`. A positional slot is found by walking to it, never by listing every mark. Rejected: the width on `TupleType` and `FuncType` alone, the two formers whose readers ask it today, which leaves `len`'s other readers walking and states a sequence's length beside the sequence rather than on it.

## What is not yet known

- **What a method call pays its concept.** One call of one method of a concept of 4,000 methods costs the elaborator 413 KB against 313 at 1,000, and the kernel and erasure the same at both widths; at `710e1c46f` the kernel's cost and erasure's grew too, and neither does since. A concept is a record, and its record's former is what stage 1 spells. Settled by: the run counted over stage 1, its remainder attributed; a cost left is a stage here, and none left drops the item.
- **What the kernel pays a construction of a wide family.** One construction of the last constructor of a family of 4,000 costs the kernel 120 KB against 47 at 1,000, outside every span the profile opens below `recheck_module`, and the elaborator and erasure the same at both widths. Settled by: gdb samples of many constructions at a wide family, the cost attributed; a cost left is a stage here, and none left drops the item.

## Stages

1. **A record's former opens no field.** `former_spelling` (`curios-core/src/module.rs`) opens the telescope past the parameters for a family alone, and `spelled_former` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate.rs`) spells from the entry where it stands. Check: the growth `StructReaders`; `/std`'s images byte-identical at one path. Size: one commit.
2. **An evaluation's module holds the rows it reaches as imports.** `curios-ersd`: `Resolution::imports_rows`, a family or a product imported where the fragment imports one the module does not hold, a constructor found at its position through `Module::constructor_at` and imported where the module holds none there; `Module::taken` reads count, position and width off an import. `curios-elab`: an evaluation's `Linking` holds rows as imports and links no declaring item's rows. The row decision in `curios-ersd`'s README, the module doc of `Imports`, and [A top-level value is computed where its unit is compiled](../../design/compilation/a-top-level-value-is-computed-where-its-unit-is-compiled.md) state it. Check: the growth `Values`; `curios-ersd`'s link and taken tests, a row no fragment declares imported at what its readers state and a suffix taken off an imported family; `/std`'s images byte-identical at one path; `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::incremental_tests` and `tests::budget_tests`. Size: two commits.
3. **A match reads the constructors it names.** The elaborator's `NominalSplit` and the matrix compiler read the family by facet, find each written arm by its tag once, order the arms by position, and walk the family's constructors only where no default stands; erasure's `erase_induct` does the same, instantiating the constructors its arms name. `InductInstance::whole`'s doc names a derivation and a match that writes every arm. Check: the growth `DefaultedMatches`; a test holding two matches whose arms are written in two orders to one elaborated term, which nothing holds today; `/std`'s images byte-identical. Size: two commits.
4. **No reducer asks or files a weak-head value, and none copies one.** The predicate and its front doors — the machine's `eval`, the elaborator's `force_rec` and `reduce_within`, the kernel's `whnf_within` and `reduce_forced` — the machine's last arm returning the node it was given, and the inspect-only `unwrap_or_clone` sites of the table's readers read by reference (`proof_end` and `erasable_end` in `curios-elab/src/typing.rs`, `shape_walk` in `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/classify.rs`). [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md)'s memo bullet states that a weak-head value is its own answer. Check: `/std`'s images byte-identical and its budget unmoved; `curios-cert`'s and `curios-elab`'s memo tests and `kernel_memo_parity`; the tuple reader's rebuilds and clones per reader counted none. Size: two commits.
5. **A comparison reads the facet it compares.** `compare_induct_type` the arity, `compare_variant` the constructor, and a comparison of two matches each written arm's constructor, through `declared_facet`. Check: the growth `ComparedConstructions`; `tests::laws`. Size: one commit.
6. **A shared row is stated by its width.** A shared `ProductSchema` holds its width and no field list, a shared row with a shape being one no producer can write; erasure registers one per width without materializing fields, `taken` imports one at its width, and the Cont lowering lays one out from its width (`curios-ersd/src/into_cont/layout.rs`). Stored bytes: every archived shared schema, in `/std`'s images and every stored unit; erased results, verdicts and executable artifacts equal. 7. **A closed type alias is read by the sort it publishes.** The field on `Definition`, written where the definition is published, compared where a recompile asks whether a definition moved (`curios-elab/src/same.rs`), its door, and the elaborator's sort rule answering a reference to the alias by it (`curios-elab/src/convert/sort.rs`); the kernel's check of it where it checks a definition, and its sort rule (`curios-cert/src/kernel/sort.rs`); erasure's erasability (`is_erasable_in`). Check: the growths `AliasReaders` and `ArrowAliasReaders`; the higher-universe regression; a kernel test refusing a definition whose stated sort it does not reach; `/std`'s verdicts and erased forms equal. Size: two commits.

## Measuring

Each probe is a program on standard input. The family probe declares `pub induct Big: pub Type` with `w` constructors `c{i}()` and `w` values `pub let v{i}: Big = Big/c{i}();`, ending in `()`, and is compiled by `wonder stage ersd -`. A reader probe declares the row and `k` functions reading it, ending in `()`, under `wonder stage ersd -` or `wonder diagnostics -`: a tuple alias `pub let T: Type = {Nat, …}` read as `pub let f{i}(x: T) -> Nat = x.0;` or not at all; a function-type alias `pub let F: Type = (Nat, …) -> Nat;` read by `pub let g{i}(_f: F) -> Nat = 0;`; a structure `pub struct S: pub Type { f0: Nat, … }` read by `pub let g{i}(s: S) -> Nat = s.f{w-1};` or by `pub let g{i}(_s: S) -> Nat = 0;`; a match `match b | c{w-1}() => 1 | _ => 0 end` over the family; `Eq/refl()` at `Eq()(Wide/c{w-1}(Nat/add(i, 1)), Wide/c{w-1}(i + 1))` over `pub induct Wide(A: Type): pub Type` of `w` constructors `c{i}(A)`; a call `C/m{w-1}(x)` of a concept of `w` methods `m{i}(A) -> A`, satisfied at `Nat`; a construction `Big/c{w-1}()`; and, the control, `pub let g{i}(x: Wide(Nat)) -> Wide(Nat) = x;`. Run each under `--profile <stream>`, fold it with `curios profile <stream>`, and read a stage's allocated bytes and allocations off the rows `elaborate_and_zonk_with_prelude`, `recheck_module` and `erase_unit` with an empty group, a declaration's off `declaration` and `certify_declaration` with its group, and an evaluation's off `evaluate_item`; a reader's cost is the difference between `k` = 16 and `k` = 1 divided by 15. Counts of what a walk materialized come from temporary `curios_profile::sample!` sites in a scratch copy of the same commit: the constructors `Module::link_schemas` adds and the product fields it sets, the list entries `Module::taken` scans, the width `tuple_schema` writes, each `TupleType` `Visit::adopt` rebuilds and `Term::unwrap_or_clone` clones, and each tuple a sort walk visits; a temporary `profile!` span around `Sharing::share` and `ClosedMemo::key` counts consing. A stored unit's bytes are a library package of the declarations, judged once by `wonder diagnostics lib.crs`, its `.curios/objects` summed. Every figure is counted; none needs a quiet machine.

## Verification and retirement

Each stage holds its own growth shape under `growth_slopes`, whose every slope stays under three where the shape grows a row's width and its readers together. A stage that moves stored bytes states them and holds verdicts, erased results and executable artifacts equal; one that moves none holds `/std`'s images byte-identical at one path. A landed stage moves its contract to its owner — the row decision to `curios-ersd`'s README, the weak-head rule to the cost decision's memo bullet, the published sort to [A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)'s list of what a declaration publishes — and this specification keeps the rest. Retire it once every stage has landed, both investigations are settled, and nothing references this file.
