# A recompile walks the unit it does not redo

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves a recompile whose cost is what an edit reached: a unit compiled over a baseline that passes over the declarations nothing could have moved without asking each, and a declaration an edit does reach made again from the parts of it the edit left standing. It is not an implementation plan.

It rests on [A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md): every stage keeps a declaration by a record of what it read, so what a recompile redoes is what read the edit. What it still does for every other declaration is this specification's.

**A recompile walks the unit it does not redo.** With nothing edited, a unit compiled over a baseline reads every file and compares its text with the one the baseline read, restores the baseline's half whole to keep each declaration's lowering and each file's heads, 0.33 M of the lowering's 1.06 M (`into_core_unit::baseline`), asks again 99,491 questions of the traces of 2,374 declarations, 0.37 M (`demand::kept`), hands the kernel every declaration to adopt, 0.34 M, asks every item's erasure trace again, 0.06 M, and stores the unit, 0.64 M. No declaration is made again, so the floor of a recompile is its unit. The storing is [A store that files a facet once](204-a-store-that-files-a-facet-once.md)'s.

**An edit elaborates what read it, each from nothing.** Respelling `/std/Bool/not`'s body elaborates again the 168 declarations that unfolded it and none behind them, and erases 251 items again, the ones that read a binding that moved and the values computed over a body that did (`demand::asked`, `erase::asked`): 80.81 M elaborating, 17.59 M judging and 2.69 M erasing, each declaration at what it costs a whole compile. A declaration is the grain, so one whose trace fails at its last question is elaborated whole; and where anything is made again the module is put in its stored order from nothing, 0.42 M (`order_unit_over`), since the order is read off what every item reaches.

**A unit's header is parsed twice before any unit is compiled.** For the stems its package's root claims (`package_source`, `curios-package/src/layout.rs`), from the disk and outside the parse memo by design, and for whether the unit declares the file asked about (`RootSource::declares_module`): `lib.crs` is 63,068 of the 63,131 allocations the unedited question parses. A file an edit touched is parsed whole besides, which is [A parse allocates for what it builds](../surface/201-a-parse-allocates-for-what-it-builds.md)'s.

**A void attempt is forgotten.** An attempt that read a declaration not yet elaborated is made again, and the next compile of the same unit finds the same order again: a whole compile of the prelude voids 45 (the `demand::void` samples of the profile the prelude's build files, `curios-prelude-archive/.artifacts/profile.tsv`).

## Refinement

- **Whether a walk can pass over a declaration without asking its trace.** A question is asked again because its answer may have moved; where no file's text moved and no unit in scope did, none has, and where one declaration was made again the questions that can have moved are the ones about it. What decides that for a declaration ahead of its trace, and whether the kernel's adoption and erasure's keeping follow the same decision or each its own, is open. salsa's durability is the model for the first half: "If a tracked function depends (transitively) only on high durability inputs, and you change a low durability input, then we can very easily determine that the tracked function result is still valid, avoiding the need to traverse the input edges one by one" ([The red-green algorithm](https://salsa-rs.github.io/salsa/reference/algorithm.html)).
- **Whether a baseline's half is read by declaration**, as its lowered items and its erased forms already are ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-stored-in-parts-by-who-reads-each)), so that keeping a declaration's lowering restores that declaration's record and no other.
- **Whether anything smaller than a declaration is kept.** A declaration elaborated again starts from its lowered form; what of an earlier elaboration could stand under a changed read, and what would say so, is not designed.
- **Whether one declaration is parsed from where its head places it**, which bounds what an edit parses by the declaration it touched, and whether a unit's header is parsed once for both of its readers.
- **Whether an attempt can be told what it will read before it is made**, a stored order of declarations being rejected ([A compile costs what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md)): the lowering knows the names a declaration writes, and elaboration reads more than those.
