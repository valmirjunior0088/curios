# A type former is spelled by its entry

**Not refined yet.** This specification reserves stating a nominal declaration once: a type former holds its entry, and its type, its body, its universe context and what elaboration stamps on it say again what the entry says. It is not an implementation plan.

## What is missing

A nominal declaration's entry is held by the definition that is its type former ([`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nominal-declarations-entry-is-held-by-its-type-former)), and that definition stores a type, a body, a universe context, a totality and a variance vector beside it. Each is a function of the entry. `Definition::former` (`curios-core/src/module.rs`) spells the first three; a former calls nothing, so it is total; and a former that is no member of a group holds each level as its entry does, a member of one storing no vector. `std_former_derivation` (`curios-pipeline/src/standard/tests.rs`, counted at `6a8f997d1`) spells each type former of `/sys` and `/std` from the entry it carries, 82 families, 95 records and 19 concepts, and finds every part of all 196 equal to the one the archive holds.

Nothing holds the two to each other, and each stage pays for that in its own way. The lowering writes a former's type and its entry's arity from one written telescope. Elaboration checks the type, replaces the entry's parameters with the ones the type came out with (`share_struct_params`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`), elaborates the entry's fields or constructors against them, finalizes both under one universe context (`finalize_definition`), and writes the finished entry into the former where the item leaves the walk (`carrying`); the elaborator reads a scheme from whichever of the two is in hand, so their agreement is load-bearing there. The kernel types the former as a definition and checks the entry as a declaration (`check_induct_decl`, `check_struct_decl`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/module.rs`), reads each scheme where it is authoritative, and certifies a module whose former declares another scheme than its entry (`recheck::registry_tests::a_registry_and_its_type_former_may_declare_different_schemes`). A module whose former is not its entry's spelling is a module, and no program elaborates to one.

## Refinement

- **What a former stores**: its name, its island and its entry, with the type, the body, the context and the stamps read through the entry where a reader asks, as a group's member is unfolded from the group that holds it (`RecItem`, `curios-core/src/module.rs`); or all of them materialized once where an item is made or restored, which leaves every reader of a `Definition` as it is.
- **What the elaborator elaborates**: the entry once, with the former's signature published from it, so that `share_struct_params`, the entry's half of `finalize_definition`, `carrying` and the agreement the zonk validates (`curios-elab/src/zonk.rs`) have nothing to reconcile; and what a telescope elaborated once does to where a refusal is reported and to the order a declaration's levels settle in.
- **What the kernel judges**: the entry, with a former's type answered from the entry the environment holds, so that a former at another type or scheme is as unrepresentable in a module as in a term; and what becomes of the fixture that holds the two schemes apart.
- **Constructors and method wrappers**, each a function of its owner's entry and a tag or a method's name, which generalize a universe scheme of their own today ([`curios-elab`'s README](../../../curios-elab/README.md#generated-declarations-name-their-owner)): measured first as the formers are, each spelled from its owner's entry and compared with the stored one, and decided from what that shows.
- **What it is worth**: the code that reconciles, and a disagreement neither checker has to be right about. Its bytes are uncounted: a consed module shares the terms of a former's telescope with its entry's.
