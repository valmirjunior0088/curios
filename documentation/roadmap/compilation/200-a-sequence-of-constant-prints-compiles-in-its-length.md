# A sequence of constant prints compiles in its length

A chain of constant prints is copied a link a round by the closed evaluator, because a link's continuation is copied whole where it could be named, and the cap that ends the copying is tuned to the library's write loop, which the library is shaped to stay under. The next step is a ready fix in three stages: name the continuation, retire the cap, and let the write's retry take its shape.

## What is wrong today

- **The optimized ANF of `n` constant prints doubles a print.** A program of `n - 1` lines `let () = print("line k\n")!;` closed by one more `print` holds 4 lines naming `handle_write(` in its ANF at every `n`, and 10, 22, 46, 94, 190 and 382 in its optimized ANF at `n` from 1 to 6, 6,055 at 10 and 12,454 at 16, where the pass's shared pool ends the copying; the same prints in a function's body, as `curios`'s `tests::growth` writes its `Statements` axis, hold 142, 2,296 and 23,263 at 4, 8 and 16 (counted at `e3572cdd8`, the lines of `curios wonder --stage anf -` and `--stage anf-optm -` that hold `handle_write(`).
- **A link's continuation is copied with nothing to substitute.** Each `!` erases to a call of `/std/Io`'s `bind` on the action and a function bound in the candidate's own block, the rest of the chain, which captures nothing. `reify_closure` (`curios-anf/src/optimize/evaluate/reify.rs`) names a closure over nothing only where an item binds its function (`ReifyScope::is_item_bound`), so the replacement deep-copies the continuation under an empty substitution: a function the module already holds, copied with every closed call it holds as the round found it. The round folds those calls in place and the next round folds them again in the copy, whose own links copy their suffixes in turn.
- **The cap that ends it is the write loop's weight.** `DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT` (`curios-anf/src/optimize/evaluate/budget.rs`) declines a replacement holding a description past 256 nodes, so a suffix is copied until it outweighs the cap, and its documentation sets 256 to clear the write through `/std/internal/Handle/retried`, the heaviest description a constant `print` copies. The library is shaped to stay under it: `curios-prelude-build/std/internal/Handle.crs` writes its loops "so the description a constant `print` copies stays under the evaluator's cap", `curios-prelude-build/std/lib.crs` declares `answered` apart for the same reason, and `retried` keeps a loop of its own where `/std/internal/until`, the loop every wait on the program's own state goes through, would raise the copy to 356 nodes.
- **The words for a description say the reifier declines one.** `Role::Performance` (`curios-anf/src/node.rs`) is "read where copying one would buy nothing … so the reifier declines to specialize it", and erasure's `thunk` (`curios-erase/src/eraser/intrinsic.rs`) calls the stamp "the fact the reifier's decline reads", where the reifier has copied descriptions under the cap since `8429daed1`.
- **The tests pin the arrangement, not what it protects.** `curios/src/tests/fmt.rs`'s `a_constant_prints_descriptions_are_copied_within_the_evaluators_cap` holds the loop's weight under the cap; `print_constant_args_collapses_at_anf` asserts one exact line of printed ANF and no `[lambda]` in the optimized CPS, where what it protects is the formatted bytes reaching the host write as one constant; and `a_format_past_the_evaluators_budget_still_prints` never checks that its format is past the budget, which it is: its optimized ANF still holds `/std/Fmt`'s code. Nothing pins the compounding.

## Decided

- **A closure over nothing whose function the candidate names is that function.** In a replacement that holds a description, a closure that captures nothing reifies as the function it closes, where an item binds that function or the candidate names it among its operands, either way in scope where the candidate stands: a copy under an empty substitution is the function again, and the continuation a link names is never copied. A group naming a function of the candidate's block is not closed at item level, so it is bound in the candidate's block, as `group_is_item_level_safe` already settles. Measured over a prototype on top of `e3572cdd8`, counted: the chain above holds `6n + 4` lines at 4, 16 and 64 prints, and the function's `12n - 8`, the one copy of the body the closed call of the function makes; a constant `Fmt/print` still reaches the host write as one constant; and the module text over `programs/` moves by −234 bytes in `json_round_trip` and −215 in `toml_round_trip` and in no other program.
- **The cap is retired.** Nothing past its count tells a suffix from a performance once the suffix is never copied, so a replacement holding a description is held to `MAX_REIFY_NODES` as every other is; in the measurement above the cap's retirement is included.
- **The write's retry is a wait like any other.** With no cap to stay under, `retried` waits through `/std/internal/until`, so one loop retries every attempt after a wait, at the module text the third stage measures. The wait decision gives a handle's retry a loop of its own because a watch reports what is ready already, which says why a handle needs no more than the loop gives, not why it needs a loop apart.

## Rejected and left out

- **A lower cap**, 64: no constant `print` folds (at `5763feb48`).
- **Declining the link's replacement**, as a loop's description stays the call (`ReifyScope::reaches_candidate`): it leaves the call for no gain over naming, which an item-bound function already takes.
- **Left out: the rule for every replacement, a pure one's included.** It holds the chain as well, but a pure replacement's group that names a function of the candidate's block is no longer closed at item level, so the copies it holds stay in the block as closures over it, and the module text grows by 3.8% in `json_round_trip` and 8.8% in `toml_round_trip`. Whether it removes work the later passes would not drop is filed with the first stage, as an uncertain entry of [the compilation findings](000-findings.md).

## Stages

1. **Name a description's continuation.** The rule in `reify_check` and `reify_closure`, with the functions the candidate names kept in `ReifyScope` for its replacement. Check: a test in `curios`'s `tests::growth` counting the host writes in the optimized ANF of the `Statements` axis and of the chain at the top level, at 8 and 16, at most doubling; a unit test in `curios-anf`'s `closed/tests.rs` that a description over a continuation of the candidate's block folds to one copy of its performance and none of the continuation; `curios-prelude`'s corpus tests. The rule for every replacement is filed beside it, an uncertain entry.
2. **Retire the cap.** `DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT`, `ReifyBudget::within_capped`, `a_constant_prints_descriptions_are_copied_within_the_evaluators_cap` and what only it reads (`heaviest_description_copy`, `DescriptionCopy`, `description_copy_charges`) go; `print_constant_args_collapses_at_anf` is restated over the module, every host write of the optimized ANF taking the formatted bytes as one constant, and `a_format_past_the_evaluators_budget_still_prints` checks that the evaluator left its format; `Role::Performance`, `thunk`'s comment and `Value::contains_description` say what reads the stamp; the cap's reasons leave `Handle.crs` and `lib.crs`. Check: the restated tests, mutation-checked; `curios-prelude`'s corpus tests.
3. **Fold the write's retry into `/std/internal/until`.** `retried` asks `until` for a reply that settled, waiting on a watch of its handle between attempts, `wait` suspends on the same watch, and `until` says every wait goes through it, as does [A wait is a suspension, and only the host waits](../../design/prelude/a-wait-is-a-suspension-and-only-the-host-waits.md), which retries a handle's operation in a loop of its own today and names the suspension pair in four places, where three remain: the loop, the driver and the bracket. Measured as above with the first two stages, counted: a constant `print` still reaches the host write as one constant and the chain stays linear, and the module text over `programs/` grows by 15.8 to 20.2 KB in 29 programs and by 34.3 KB in `hello_world` and `dependent_vectors`, the price of one loop for every wait. Check: the tests of `/std/Proc/Child`, `/std/tcp` and `/std/Io`, run by `curios test`; `curios-prelude`'s corpus tests; the module text over `programs/` read again.
