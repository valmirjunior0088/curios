# A sequence of constant prints compiles in its length

The closed evaluator names a description's continuation where its candidate names it (`ReifyScope::in_scope`, `curios-anf/src/optimize/evaluate/reify.rs`), so a chain of constant prints folds in its length (`curios`'s `tests::growth::a_chain_of_constant_prints_folds_in_its_length`). The cap that ended the copying before remains, tuned to the library's write loop, which the library is shaped to stay under. The next step is a ready fix in two stages: retire the cap, and let the write's retry take its shape.

## What is wrong today

- **The cap has no job, and its value is the write loop's weight.** `DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT` (`curios-anf/src/optimize/evaluate/budget.rs`) declines a replacement holding a description past 256 nodes, to keep a chain's continuation from being copied at every link, which no replacement copies now; its documentation sets 256 to clear the write through `/std/internal/Handle/retried`, the heaviest description a constant `print` copies. The library is shaped to stay under it: `curios-prelude-build/std/internal/Handle.crs` writes its loops "so the description a constant `print` copies stays under the evaluator's cap", `curios-prelude-build/std/lib.crs` declares `answered` apart for the same reason, and `retried` keeps a loop of its own where `/std/internal/until`, the loop every wait on the program's own state goes through, would raise the copy to 356 nodes.
- **The words for a description say the reifier declines one.** `Role::Performance` (`curios-anf/src/node.rs`) is "read where copying one would buy nothing … so the reifier declines to specialize it", and erasure's `thunk` (`curios-erase/src/eraser/intrinsic.rs`) calls the stamp "the fact the reifier's decline reads", where the reifier has copied descriptions under the cap since `8429daed1`.
- **The tests pin the arrangement, not what it protects.** `curios/src/tests/fmt.rs`'s `a_constant_prints_descriptions_are_copied_within_the_evaluators_cap` holds the loop's weight under the cap; `print_constant_args_collapses_at_anf` asserts one exact line of printed ANF and no `[lambda]` in the optimized CPS, where what it protects is the formatted bytes reaching the host write as one constant; and `a_format_past_the_evaluators_budget_still_prints` never checks that its format is past the budget, which it is: its optimized ANF still holds `/std/Fmt`'s code.

## Decided

- **The cap is retired.** Nothing past its count tells a suffix from a performance once the suffix is never copied, so a replacement holding a description is held to `MAX_REIFY_NODES` as every other is. Measured over a prototype on top of `e3572cdd8` with the continuation named, counted: the chain stays linear, a constant `Fmt/print` still reaches the host write as one constant, and the module text over `programs/` moves by −234 bytes in `json_round_trip` and −215 in `toml_round_trip` and in no other program.
- **The write's retry is a wait like any other.** With no cap to stay under, `retried` waits through `/std/internal/until`, so one loop retries every attempt after a wait, at the module text the second stage measures. The wait decision gives a handle's retry a loop of its own because a watch reports what is ready already, which says why a handle needs no more than the loop gives, not why it needs a loop apart.

## Stages

1. **Retire the cap.** `DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT`, `ReifyBudget::within_capped`, `a_constant_prints_descriptions_are_copied_within_the_evaluators_cap` and what only it reads (`heaviest_description_copy`, `DescriptionCopy`, `description_copy_charges`) go; `print_constant_args_collapses_at_anf` is restated over the module, every host write of the optimized ANF taking the formatted bytes as one constant, and `a_format_past_the_evaluators_budget_still_prints` checks that the evaluator left its format; `Role::Performance`, `thunk`'s comment and `Value::contains_description` say what reads the stamp; the cap's reasons leave `Handle.crs` and `lib.crs`. Check: the restated tests, mutation-checked; `curios-prelude`'s corpus tests.
2. **Fold the write's retry into `/std/internal/until`.** `retried` asks `until` for a reply that settled, waiting on a watch of its handle between attempts, `wait` suspends on the same watch, and `until` says every wait goes through it, as does [A wait is a suspension, and only the host waits](../../design/prelude/a-wait-is-a-suspension-and-only-the-host-waits.md), which retries a handle's operation in a loop of its own today and names the suspension pair in four places, where three remain: the loop, the driver and the bracket. Measured as above with the first stage, counted: a constant `print` still reaches the host write as one constant and the chain stays linear, and the module text over `programs/` grows by 15.8 to 20.2 KB in 29 programs and by 34.3 KB in `hello_world` and `dependent_vectors`, the price of one loop for every wait. Check: the tests of `/std/Proc/Child`, `/std/tcp` and `/std/Io`, run by `curios test`; `curios-prelude`'s corpus tests; the module text over `programs/` read again.

## Verification and retirement

The growth test holds; the restated tests pass; `curios-prelude`'s corpus tests pass; the module text over `programs/` is read before and after, counted, and names the commit. Once the two stages land, this file is deleted.
