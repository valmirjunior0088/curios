- **Counted figures taken through the `wasm-optm` rung, which is gone, wait to be retaken over the emitted module.** Where the rung left, each protocol that read it was restated onto `wasm` and its figures dropped: the field-shape census's static half, the casts and boxes of seven corpus programs over the Binaryen-optimized text at `447fbb0a1` (`field_shape_census`, `curios/src/tests/codegen/shapes.rs`, which now counts the emitted text); and the `br_table`s of `spines`' optimized module beside `map_wall_spines_slope` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/map_wall.rs`), counted at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`). Fix: retake each over the emitted module, by the census run, `wonder --stage wasm` and the protocol each document states, and restate it where it stood with the commit it was taken at. Check: each document holds its figure again, named by its commit and rung. Size: one commit.
- **A body written at two leaves of a match is emitted twice, since nothing takes two equal continuations of one function for one.** An arm's alternatives are each a copy of the body the arm writes, and a row a binder of its own put under several cases is an instance at each leaf ([Patterns are compiled where their types are known](../../design/surface/patterns-are-compiled-where-their-types-are-known.md)); the copies reach the optimized module and the WebAssembly whole, and the continuation optimizer (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) holds no pass that merges them. Counted at `ba4ab354e`, release, x86-64 Linux: `/std` outside its tests writes 93 arms at more than one leaf (`Compiler::leaf`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/matrix.rs`), 45 of them in proofs, and the second copy of `/std/Flt/digits/normalize`'s stray row weighs 4,130 bytes of `programs/flt_hot_loop.crs`'s WebAssembly, in every program that prints a `Flt`; a program whose last arm `| (_, none()) =>` sums forty products of its input modulo primes emits 206,091 bytes, written as alternatives or in both arms of a nested match alike, against 189,546 with the body bound once as a function. Fix: a pass after the fixpoint that keeps one continuation of each class of continuations of one function and one lineage equal up to the values, continuations and functions their extents bind, at the innermost `LetCont` enclosing every member, each member a jump to it, then the jumps forwarded and the graph shrunk and pruned once: first the classes whose members read the same values from outside, of any size; then those the inliner would leave standing as a callee of several calls, past `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` as the inliner weighs an extent ([A callee is weighed by what it emits](205-a-callee-is-weighed-by-what-it-emits.md) owns the weight), whose members read different values, each a parameter of the one kept. Classes are found by a hash computed once bottom-up and verified member by member, or by hashing modulo α (Maziarz et al., *Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence*, PLDI 2021), whichever the first commit bounds the work of. A probe of the pass, sizes by `wonder --stage wasm` and `wasm-tools parse`, run time by the user-space instructions a `curios compile`d executable retires, median of three: the corpus 0.16% smaller after the first commit and 0.27% after the second, the xbench workloads, `flt_hot_loop`, `parse_bindless`, `parse_digits` and `monad_suspend` within 0.3%; the same pass before the fixpoint grew the corpus 1.53%, and parameterized classes from two nodes saved 2.53% but moved `chain` by −11.8% and `flt_hot_loop` by +7.1%, undoing the copies the inliner made of `/std/Str/fold`'s step at each width of a UTF-8 scalar. `curios-cps`'s README states the decision with those placements rejected, and the decision's rejection of a Core node that shares a body links it. Check: `curios-cps` tests of two equal arms merged, a member nested in another's extent, a class refused where a continuation it reaches is out of scope, a second run that merges nothing, and the work over a geometric grid of nesting depth; codegen tests that the summing body is emitted once at `cps-optm` and `wasm`, and past the line that `| (some(x), none()) | (none(), some(x)) =>` over the same body is too (206,053 bytes to 191,372), each with its answers, and that a parameterized class below the line is left copied. Size: two commits, the second after the first.
- **A loop parameter every jump into it hands itself or one value from outside stays a parameter, since the invariant fixpoint takes only a function.** [A known function a call hands is followed into it](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-known-function-a-call-hands-is-followed-into-it) substitutes a function or a literal every entry of a loop hands a parameter its back edges pass on, and `known_values` (`curios-cps/src/analysis.rs`) takes nothing else from the fixpoint (`invariant_fixpoint`): "Only a function is taken from it", `resolve_atom` reading an argument that is a value from outside the fixpoint's domain as a conflict, "an unobservable runtime value". So a recursion that hands a value it was handed on unchanged keeps it as a parameter of the loop contification makes of it, passed back to itself each round. Re-shown at `82b51a065`: a program whose `all_ok(piece, wire)` asks `List/all` of a predicate calling a recursive `fed(piece, state, n)` that hands `piece` on optimizes to a loop `/fed(piece, state, n)` inside `all_ok`, its back edge handing its own `piece` and its entry `all_ok`'s. Counted at `82b51a065` by a census in `known_values`, a continuation parameter each transfer into which hands it itself or one same value, every round: 36 to 56 a program over the 28 programs of `programs/` and 61 in `/std`'s Io tests, most of them the `base` and `end` of a virtualized list window, which `/std/Str/At/floor`, `/sys/List/fold` and `/std/Str/utf8/scan_from` carry round each step of the loops every program links. With each such parameter whose value is in scope where its continuation is bound taken as that value, a prototype, the corpus's binaries (`wonder --stage wasm`, `wasm-tools parse`) are 3.32% smaller, 5,272,332 bytes to 5,097,057, every program from 0.31% (`hello_world`) to 5.16% (`parse_bindless`), and every program answers as it did; run time untimed. LLVM folds a phi whose operands are itself and one value into that value (`simplifyPHINode`), MLIR's region simplification drops a block argument every predecessor hands the same value (`dropRedundantArguments`), and GHC's static argument transformation takes such an argument out of a recursion at the source. The fix the user settled on 2026-11-19: the fixpoint itself carries a value from outside, `resolve_atom` reading it as known rather than a conflict, so a parameter every transfer hands itself, another invariant parameter or one outside value is found, and `known_values` takes such a value as it takes a function, only where it is in scope where the continuation is bound; that scope is read off the walk `continuation_scopes` makes, which holds the values beside the functions at each `LetCont` and keeps only the functions, and `Module::scopes` (`curios-cps/src/verify.rs`) hands its maps as a struct with named fields where it hands a tuple. Landed on 2026-11-19 and taken out the same day, the fix as settled, with tests of a value substituted, of one handed through another loop's invariant parameter, of two values kept and of one out of scope kept, each mutation-checked: counted at `cfc58a4e3` the corpus's binaries are 0.76% smaller, 5,132,464 bytes to 5,093,334, from 0.30% (`hello_world`) to 2.70% (`parse_bindless`), but `/std/Map/internal/Node/remove` loses its rebuild in place, its twigs copied (`List/set` where `List/overwrite` stood) and its node constructed anew where `t with Node(/1 = …)` stood, at every level. The one change inside its descent is a loop of `/std/Map/digits/parts` reading the key `k` where it was bound rather than as its window's base parameter, and the sharing analysis then reads the node as shared, by a rule not yet found; `curios`'s `rebuild::map_remove_rebuilds_its_node_at_every_level_in_a_loop_using_its_map_once`, `rebuild::a_maps_descent_writes_each_levels_twigs_in_place` and `ladder::walk_mirror_family_isolates_each_obligation` fail with it, and `structural::layout::a_list_fold_walks_its_list_through_a_window`, which pins the window's base as the fold's parameter, is restated by it. Not yet known: which rule of the sharing analysis a value read where it was bound trips, and whether the fix or the rule is what yields. Settled by tracing the sharing facts over `remove` with the fix and without, then fixing the rule or narrowing the substitution so the rebuilds hold. Fix: so, then the substitution as settled, and the decision restated to values. Check: the tests above; `remove`'s rebuild tests and the ladder's passing; the corpus's binaries smaller, restated at the fix's commit; every program's answers unchanged. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's insert does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels deep over 65,537 keys ([`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api)), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state. One reason the peers do not meet is a row's own: OCaml's tail modulo constructor fills a placeholder in a block every field of which is mutable underneath, and Koka writes a constructor context into memory it owns, where `curios-emit` declares a slot of a row's struct mutable only where some rebuild writes it, every other slot immutable so an engine reads it as the constant its construction stored (`table.rs`), so a hole makes its slot mutable for every construction of the row, and every read of that slot no constant to the engine; and a struct's slots are filled at `struct.new`, so a hole in a slot of a type no null inhabits takes a filler of that type. Settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, with the slot the loop writes declared mutable in both, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
- **A bounded copy enumerates a binding group's members before its allowance refuses it, so a wide match called from many sites is planned in work growing with their product.** `CopyPlan::limited` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/clone.rs`) checks the distinct-node allowance when a pending body is popped, but a visited `LetFun` first reads every defined member, inserts its identity in `nested` and queues its body, and a `LetCont` likewise its complete group, so a request allowed sixteen nodes enumerates every arm of a match before its seventeenth node refuses it: the documented qualification of [bounded copy admission](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-copy-is-planned-within-its-allowance), not a breach of its copied-node ceiling. `inline_known_calls` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/inline.rs`) plans a copy at each call site of a callee, so a callee whose body binds a wide group is enumerated whole once a site a round. Counted at `37afc4508` over a generated program — a family of N constructors, a `weight` matching each, and M calls of `weight` chained by `let`s in one `entry` — by gdb at the planner and at the extension that queues a `LetCont`'s bodies: 800 arms and 50 sites enumerate 441,698 members over 844 plans, 50 arms and 800 sites 441,689 over 9,845, and 800 arms and 800 sites 7,041,689 over 9,845, about 715 a plan whose allowance admits a few dozen nodes; counted at `d8f53c21c` over `programs/`, the TOML driver of `fixpoint_pass_measurements`, a JSON driver that decodes its first argument and encodes it back, and the whole `/std` test program as one program, none of 170,000 refusals had more than 60 bodies queued or 45 members recorded. Not yet known: what the enumeration costs in time beside the rest of a build, which decides between the fix below and a drop. Settled by timing the 800-by-800 program's `build` with the fix prototyped and without, interleaved, and the user's choice. Fix: the planner enumerates a group's bodies as it admits them, counted against the allowance as an effort counter is (Waddell and Dybvig, *Fast and Effective Procedure Inlining*; Guile's `peval`), keeping complete extents, deduplication and fresh-plan execution, with no second fuel limit and no change to which copies fit. Check: a `curios-cps` test of a request refused inside a wide group having enumerated no more of it than its allowance, and the copies the corpus makes unchanged. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: the optimizer's bytes grow past `growth_slopes`' bound over a program's functions and over additions nested either way, where a wide value-flow fact's bits run to its greatest number.** Over the compiler of `ba7b9b9fc`, `growth_slopes` reads the bytes `cps-optm` allocates growing 3.07 over functions and 3.18 and 3.17 over additions nested to the left and to the right, held growths no stage before emission of which read past 3.0 in bytes at `828b0e6b6`, before a wide fact's members were held as bits. A wide fact's words run from the first to the one holding its greatest number (`Members`, `curios-cps/src/value_flow.rs`), so a fact of members far apart costs the span of its solve's numbering, which grows with the program. Not yet known: whether the bits are what moved the readings, and whether readings a little over three are a square or the hash keys a run draws. Settled by counting `cps-optm`'s bytes over those growths with a fact's words held from its least word, then restating `Members` or dropping this entry. Size: unknown.
