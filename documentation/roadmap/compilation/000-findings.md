# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: a pass of `curios-anf` or below may still recognize a shape only the removed fold forms' lowering produced.** Folds, written motives and `choose` have gone from Core and the surface, and the erased `FoldNat` and `FoldSequence` forms with them. The documentation of `curios-erase`, `curios-anf`, `curios-cps`, `curios-emit` and `curios-wasm`, their READMEs included, names none of them; every variant of CPS's node, value, intrinsic-call and panic alphabets has a producer; and no fixture of theirs spells a removed construct. What fold vocabulary was left stood in `curios-core`'s, `curios-cert`'s and the suites' comments, and is gone. Not read: the code of each pass that recognizes a loop or a recursion — `contify_calls`, `separate_loop_entries`, the rebase, the inversion, `split_returns` and the specializers — against the shapes erasure makes today, the peel loop `List/fold` erases to and a recursion a program writes, for a branch only a removed form's lowering reached. Settled by: that read, each branch nothing reaches removed, or filed with its evidence where something still might. Size unknown.
- **Uncertain: a program that calls `/std/Toml`'s decoder compiles about ten times slower than at the release before, past what its size grew, nearly all of it solving `curios-cps`'s value flow.** The program `use /std/{Toml, print};` closing with `match Toml/decode("a = 1") | success(_) => print("decoded\n") | failure(_) => print("refused\n") end`, put to `curios run` as a file, timed at `1effd4217` against the `963b982ff` release, three readings interleaved, with one other compilation running on the machine: 71.1 s [70.6–71.6] of wall time, its `build` 33.3 s [33.0–33.5] and its `emit` 37.6 s [37.5–37.9], where `963b982ff` takes 7.04 s [7.02–7.05], 2.7 s and 4.2 s. The same program over `Json/decode`, or over `Map/insert`, takes about a second under both. Counted, at the same two commits: the erased term `wonder --stage anf` prints is 570,481 bytes against 574,002, the program at `cps-optm` 2,874,453 against 1,701,702 and the module at `wasm` 22,485,735 against 14,543,003, so the program grows by half and the time tenfold, in `build` and in `emit` alike. Any program that calls the decoder pays it. Sampled at `1effd4217` with `eu-stack` every 2.8 s over one compilation of that program, 27 samples: 25 stand in `ValueFlow::of` (`curios-cps/src/value_flow.rs`), solving its `dataflow::Solver` over `ValueFact` and `FlowKey`, most of them in `ValueFact`'s `join`, inserting into and cloning the `BTreeSet`s of node and function identities a fact holds; one stands in `sharing`'s `routes` under `rebuild_in_place`, and the last in Binaryen's `SimplifyGlobals`. Each reader solves the flow again: `rows_reaching`, from `optimize` and from `rebuild_in_place`, during `build`, and `Module::entry_suspension`, from `curios_emit::machine::lower`, during `emit`, which is why `emit` slowed with `build`. The value flow is this range's (`0e3c94968`), and `rows_reaching` was read off it in `f2e2b8783`. Not known: what makes one solve cost what it does — the size of the sets a join copies, the rounds the solver takes to settle, or both — and whether `/std/Toml` written with guards and runs (`1c6930793`, `d3b196635`) is what hands it so much. Settled by: a solve's rounds and the sizes of the sets it joins, counted on that program, then the cause restated as a finding or a specification. Size unknown.
