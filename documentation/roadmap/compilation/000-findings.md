# Compilation: findings

- **A committed term names a recursive group by search, and two answers of the search are no declaration's part.** `named_members` (`curios-elab/src/denoise.rs`) spells a stuck call headed by a top-level group by its member's name, found by asking which names the group goes by (`Context::member_names`): a search of the groups the unit and its scope define, by the group itself. A group that is found is noted as a read of each member's body, so the trace of the declaration that asked holds it. Two answers follow no one declaration. Where two declarations define one group, the unit's own are filed by group (`Frames::members`), each definition writing over the one before it, so the names answered are those of the one elaborated last, and which that is follows the order the walk asked for them in. And where no declaration defines the group, a local `rec`'s or an instance another declaration opened, the answer is an absence that a later text fills by declaring that group at the top level, and no trace of a kept declaration is asked it again. Either way the committed term spells the same definition, so no judgment moves; what moves is the spelling a unit stores, and with it whether a unit compiled over a baseline is stored as one compiled whole. Neither is seen in `/std`: at `7d28c0209` no two of its definitions that call themselves are one recursion, their printed bodies compared with each one's own name set aside and mutual groups not compared, and the second answer waits on a later text, which a compile of one text never meets. Fix: recognize a group by reduction's own record of the name it opened it from, as an instance of a universe-polymorphic one is recognized already (`Context::instance_of`), in place of the search. Check: a unit declaring one group under two names, and a third declaration committing a stuck call to it, stores the same bytes whichever of the two is asked for first. Size: medium.
- **A definition that answers a type runs its body's closed computations where it is declared, and a variance vector the budget cuts short is filed with nothing said.** `definition_variances` (`curios-elab/src/variance.rs`) applies a definition that answers a type at fresh binders, forces it and reads what is left, so the closed computations of its body run where it is declared, the cost a vector exists to save, and a reduction the budget refuses is read as holding every level it carries, with no error. Under `let spin(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => spin(k) end;` and `let Pick(n: Nat, _A: Type) -> Type = match n: (_) => Type | 0 => Nat | _k + 1 => Nat end;`, the declaration `pub let D(A: Type) -> Type = Pick(spin(150000), A);` takes `wonder diagnostics` 2.2 s where `pub let plain: Nat = spin(150000);`, whose check runs nothing of `spin`, takes none, and at `spin(600000)` the walk spends the declaration's whole budget and `D` is accepted in 7.4 s with nothing said. Counted at `7d28c0209` in the stream a profiling build files, `D` at `spin(100000)` spends 5,906,378 units, in `definition_variances`, where `plain` spends 3,115. What the cut vector costs a later declaration is an unfolding and no verdict of its own: it claims less, so two instances it does not hold one are unfolded and compared, and `pub let keep(x: D(Nat)) -> D(Nat) = x;` after that `D` is refused with `conversion ran out of steps between D(Nat) and D(Nat)`, the reduction that outran `D`'s budget outrunning `keep`'s. Read and not probed: a stored unit keeps the vector under any other budget, where the unfolding fits and is paid at each comparison. The levels a family holds are not filed so. `family_levels` reads a cut reduction the same way inside `sort_family_levels` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`), and `record_variances` walks the same telescopes after it, on the budget it left spent, and refuses the group: `induct F: Type | mk(x: Pick(spin(150000), List(Type))) end` is accepted with no level of its own, and the same family at `spin(175000)` and at every height tried above it, to 560,000, is refused with `reduction ran out of steps`. Timed on top of `a2c34ec1c` through the release compiler, one reading each. Fix, one of two: the definition is refused for the budget where its walk is cut, as a family is, which names the reduction where it is written and refuses a definition whose every use that reduces it runs the same budget out, as `keep` shows; or the walk reads a definition as written and forces nothing closed, so a vector costs no reduction and an unfolding is paid by the comparison that needs one. Check: `D` at `spin(600000)` refused where it is declared, or `D` at `spin(150000)` declared in the time `plain` is. Size: small either way.
