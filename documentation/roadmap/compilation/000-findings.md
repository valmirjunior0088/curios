# Compilation: findings

- **An executable's slot answers for another file once the manifest points the executable elsewhere.** The unit of an entry's own declarations is addressed by package and executable name (`curios_package::entry_slot`), not by its root file, and a hit is verified by `agrees` (`curios-verdict/src/verdicts.rs`) against the files the record names without asking that the source's own root is among them, where [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md) rests on "its own file is among the files its record names". So after an `[[executables]]` entry's `path = "x.crs"` becomes `path = "y.crs"`, the record filed from `x.crs` still agrees, `x.crs` being unchanged, and `y.crs`'s closing term is compiled over `x.crs`'s declarations: at `6b7b91bf7`, `curios run` of `prog` prints `x` where `y.crs` prints `y`, and prints `y` once `.curios/` is cleared; `curios wonder` refuses a `y.crs` that checks alone, typing its term against `x.crs`'s declarations. A history of `curios-verdict`'s test support stepping one mount from a program to Core text is answered the same way, the Core text never compiled, so a board witness written that way puts the earlier step's unit. No `False` follows: the declarations handed back were judged, and the tail is judged against them. Fix: in `agrees`, require every file the source has read by the time it is looked up (`UnitSource::reads`, which holds an entry's opened root, `RootSource::opened`) to be named in the record with the digest it has now. Check: a `curios-verdict` store test building an executable at `x.crs`, repointing it at `y.crs` and building again, expecting the entry's declarations not reused, and the history stepping a mount from a program to Core text. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: checking the bounded tower costs the square of its lines.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) lets reduction alone grow past what was written, and charges it to the budget. The bounded tower (`curios/src/tests/towers.rs`'s `Shape::Bounded`, a chain of `let`s each naming the one before it three times under a proof that the last stays below the input) is checked at 12, 24, 48 and 96 lines in 0.71, 1.58, 4.64 and 15.90 G instructions, counted at `e2719c842` through `wonder -`: ×3.50 and then ×3.69 per doubling, approaching four, as its allocated bytes do (×3.67). `tower_measurements` at the same commit reads its looks ×1.54 in the elaborator and ×1.56 in the kernel for a third more lines, where a walk visiting each node once reads at most a third more and every other tower at most 26 per cent more; its heaviest declaration's units read 43 018 and then 56 510. Main read the same looks and units at `710e1c46f`. Part of the square was the rank's, which a rank made of the children's removed ([`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nodes-rank-is-its-childrens-joined)): counted at `102ee41b4` by a scratch copy's counter, the rank rebuilt 51,794, 202,424, 799,604 and 3,177,644 nodes over the four heights ranking 612, 1,224, 2,448 and 4,896 atoms, ×3.9 per doubling, where the summaries fill 1,561 to 12,397 nodes, ×2.0; `tower_measurements` at `868e314b2` reads the bounded tower's looks 178 547 and then 248 339 in the elaborator and 105 019 and then 150 675 in the kernel, ×1.39 and ×1.43 for a third more lines, the units as before. What remains past it: the handles the proof's comparisons ask grow 3,159, 10,647, 38,583 and 146,295 over the same heights, ×3.4 to ×3.8, each a memo's answer now, asked under `compare_nat_among` nested in `Asked::verdict` (two samples under gdb at 96 lines, one in the zonk a witness resolution materializes). At `d0333400f`, release, the tower at 24, 48, 96 and 192 lines takes 0.13, 0.26, 0.67 and 1.99 s of wall and is accepted at about 85 637, 189 349, 477 836 and 1 355 216 units and at no fewer, ×2.2, ×2.5 and ×2.8 a doubling against the time's ×3.6, ×3.3 and ×3.2 past startup, and three samples at 192 lines stand in reduction: `nat_least_within`, the kernel's `whnf` under `classify::refold`, and `compare_nat_among` under `reduce_extremum`. Not known: whether the instructions follow the units as the time nearly does, which only a profile counts. Settled by: a profile of the tower at 96 and 192 lines (`cargo xtask profile`) with the heaviest declaration's units at both heights; a square the units charge in step is reduction's and drops this entry, and one they do not is restated as a finding against the rule its span departs from. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: the runaways read more nodes a unit than they did.** `runaway_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/runaways.rs`), over the same programs, reads at `41ca9548f` the plain recursion at 3.9 nodes a unit where `997cec045` read 1.7 and 1.8, its units the budget at both; the stacks at 7.7 in the elaborator and 22.1 in the kernel where they read 5.2 and 13.6; the walk at 5.6 where it read 3.8; the image at 2.3 and 3.0 where it read 1.6 and 2.1; and the classes at 3.0 where they read 2.9 — three takings at each commit, the units the same at every one. The units moved little, and down in most rows, so each unit comes with more looks than it did, and what moved is the instrument's count and not what a program pays. Not known: which of the 420 commits between moved it, and whether a charged step reads more nodes or a reader that charges nothing reads more, a hit on a remembered reduct being free. Of those commits 130 touch `curios-elab` or `curios-core`, 25 a reducer, a memo or `term.rs`, and 16 `term.rs` alone, changing what a node hands out — its free-variable set (`d8c6bc167`, `426c1beeb`), a spine's facts and names (`6192846f2`, `9a67ac6e9`), its rank (`868e314b2`) — which is what `term::looks` counts. Settled by: `runaway_measurements` over the spin, the stacks and the walk alone, bisected in a scratch copy first over the 16 `term.rs` commits and then over the nine that change a reducer or a memo, each taking in its own process, and the reader named; a reading its rule allows drops this entry, and one it does not is restated as a finding against the rule its reader departs from. Size: unknown.
