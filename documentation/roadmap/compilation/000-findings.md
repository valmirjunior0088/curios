# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: checking the bounded tower costs the square of its lines.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) lets reduction alone grow past what was written, and charges it to the budget. The bounded tower (`curios/src/tests/towers.rs`'s `Shape::Bounded`, a chain of `let`s each naming the one before it three times under a proof that the last stays below the input) is checked at 12, 24, 48 and 96 lines in 0.71, 1.58, 4.64 and 15.90 G instructions, counted at `e2719c842` through `wonder diagnostics -`: ×3.50 and then ×3.69 per doubling, approaching four, as its allocated bytes do (×3.67). `tower_measurements` at the same commit reads its looks ×1.54 in the elaborator and ×1.56 in the kernel for a third more lines, where a walk visiting each node once reads at most a third more and every other tower at most 26 per cent more; its heaviest declaration's units read 43 018 and then 56 510. Main read the same looks and units at `710e1c46f`. Part of the square was the rank's, which a rank made of the children's removed ([`curios-core`'s README](../../../curios-core/README.md#a-nodes-rank-is-its-childrens-joined)): counted at `102ee41b4` by a scratch copy's counter, the rank rebuilt 51,794, 202,424, 799,604 and 3,177,644 nodes over the four heights ranking 612, 1,224, 2,448 and 4,896 atoms, ×3.9 per doubling, where the summaries fill 1,561 to 12,397 nodes, ×2.0; `tower_measurements` at `868e314b2` reads the bounded tower's looks 178 547 and then 248 339 in the elaborator and 105 019 and then 150 675 in the kernel, ×1.39 and ×1.43 for a third more lines, the units as before. What remains past it: the handles the proof's comparisons ask grow 3,159, 10,647, 38,583 and 146,295 over the same heights, ×3.4 to ×3.8, each a memo's answer now, asked under `compare_nat_among` nested in `Asked::verdict` (two samples under gdb at 96 lines, one in the zonk a witness resolution materializes). Not known: which reader hands out the tower's atoms once per line per earlier line, and whether the budget charges it, as reduction the proof's comparisons fold, or it is work outside reduction. Settled by: the instruction count at `868e314b2` or later against `e2719c842`'s, and a profile of the tower at 48 and 96 lines (`cargo xtask profile`) naming the span whose instructions still quadruple, with the heaviest declaration's units at both heights; a square the units charge in step is reduction's and drops this entry, and one they do not is restated as a finding against the rule its span departs from. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a function type's codomain is captured by rewriting one shared spine once per metavariable.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a metavariable's spine rebuilt only where its solution reads a binder of its birth scope. `Telescope::build_marked` closes a declaration's parameters over its codomain through `capture_placed` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`), and each `Metavar` node met there has its spine rewritten entry by entry (`MetavarSpine::rewritten`, an `Ordered::insert` an entry), though every metavariable born under the one scope shares one spine version. Seen at `102ee41b4`, release: a codomain `Eq()(A, A)` over 1 026 parameters, where `A` nests 1 024 `+` operators, costs 2.6 s and a gibibyte where the same `A` as the body costs 0.24 s and 95 MB; two of four samples under gdb stand in the rewrite under the capture and one dropping those spines. Not known: whether the body form pays the same under another name (its samples stand in the free-variable sets `e2719c842` since replaced), and whether that commit's spine rewrite, which passes over the runs a capture does not reach, changes a capture that reaches every entry. Settled by: the codomain form against the body form, timed and sampled at main's tip; a square that stands is a memo of the rewrite by spine version, one that is gone drops this entry. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a stored unit whose source raised a lint holds the source's text twice in its slot.** A library of one file, `lib.crs` of 40,065 bytes, judged once by `wonder diagnostics lib.crs`, files a verdict slot of 84,152 bytes holding the file's text twice where its one function raises an unused-binder lint, and of 3,499 bytes holding none where the same file raises none, counted at `710e1c46f`; the unit already stores each source's text once among its objects (`curios_core::Anchors`, [`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md)), and a report's `Span` holds its `Source` whole (`curios-utilities/src/span.rs`), so the two copies are likely what the stored reports archive. Not known: which stored structures hold the copies, and whether a stored report needs its text where a slot is believed only while every file it read holds the text it was read as. Settled by: the slot decoded and the holders named; where a report holds them, the fix names its source by the unit's own texts, and the entry becomes a finding stating it. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a test program's back end grows faster than the tests it schedules.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md). The 291 tests `/std` declared, compiled as one test program at `b5a6d9df5`, took 588 s, 12 s of it before erasure, timed in one cold reading; the same tests as ten programs, one a subject, took 72 s side by side under nextest, which is no figure of work. Six interruptions of the one program under gdb stood three times in `curios-cont`'s optimizer (`flatten_indexed_lists`, `value_use_counts`, the row-test analysis) and three in Binaryen's `SimplifyLocals` over one function. Since `/std`'s tests are its own `tests` modules, `curios test` in `curios-std/src` with no filter compiles that program. Not known: whether any stage's work over every test exceeds the sum of its work over the modules apart, and which function `SimplifyLocals` stood in — the tail `test_program_tail` synthesizes (`curios-elab/src/test_tail.rs`) is one list literal of a pair per test, each thunk a reference to its own declaration until an optimizer inlines it. Settled by: in a scratch copy built `--release --features profile`, `curios --profile <stream> test --filter <prefix>` in `curios-std/src` for each module of tests and for all of them, one at a time over a warm store so that only the tail and the back end compile, each stage's instructions counted; a stage whose count over all the tests exceeds its modules' sum past the noise between two identical runs is a specification naming it and the function it grows in, and none drops this entry. Size: unknown.
