# Compilation: findings

- **Contify and sink each bind a continuation to keep a switch edge off a function's return, which the machine lowering lowers.** `contify_call` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/contify.rs`) binds a `return_bridge` continuation in every contified function, its body and scope two of the three nodes `CONTIFICATION_NODE_OVERHEAD` charges each loop-entry copy, so that the callee's switch edges to its return land in a continuation rather than on the call's, which may be the caller's own return; and `sink_computations` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/sink.rs`) binds an `arm` continuation where the operation it carves handed its result straight to the function's return. A switch edge to the return lands in a block of its own that returns (`lower_branch`, `curios-emit/src/machine.rs`), so both passes can retarget it as they retarget a jump. Fix: retarget a switch edge as a jump in both, drop the bridge from contify's scaffolding and its overhead to the one initial transfer, and restate `contifies_a_single_entry_tail_loop_and_bridges_switch_returns` and the extents the contify and specialize suites charge. Check: `curios-cps`'s suite and the corpus, `curios-prelude`'s tests. One commit.
- **Uncertain: what rebuilding in place costs a compile.** `rebuild_in_place` (`curios-cps/src/rebuild.rs`, its facts in `sharing.rs`) runs on every program once `optimize` settles, solving its facts twice — owning every parameter, then borrowing what the first answer leaves — with liveness kept per block and reachability asked between blocks. Seen: a scratch prototype keeping both for every node multiplied the census's TOML driver's compile several times over; the analysis in the tree was timed once, interleaved against the compiler released at `fe6820d96`, on a machine busy with other work, where one compile of the TOML driver read 7.5 s and then 10.3 s with nothing changed and `../curios-papers`' harness, from a cold store, 92 s before against 119 s after — too noisy to say what the phase costs. Not known: its share of the TOML driver's and the harness's compile. Settled by: seven interleaved rounds on a quiet machine of release builds at one commit with and without the phase, the harness compiled from a cold store; a share past a few percent makes the per-block live sets and the second solve the fix. Taken last, once compilation spec 100's other stages land. Size unknown until then.
