# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: a call's frame weight counts every value its callee's body binds, though the emitter folds them onto the locals their live ranges allow, so a recursion runs on the heap sooner than its frame needs.** [A recursion is as deep as the heap allows](../../design/compilation/a-recursion-is-as-deep-as-the-heap-allows.md) weighs a call "a fixed overhead plus one for each value its callee's body binds", a weight that "stands in for its bytes" in code Binaryen has not optimized, while [Locals are allocated by liveness](../../../curios-emit/README.md#locals-are-allocated-by-liveness) folds a function's values onto "the fewest its values' live ranges allow", so a body whose arms bind values no other arm holds weighs their sum and holds about their largest. Counted at `5bccb9ebd` in `spines`' optimized module: `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert` weighs 136 and declares 49 locals beside its parameters, and `remove` weighs 74, so thirty of an insert's levels run natively under `FRAME_BUDGET` (`curios-cps/src/frames.rs`); the one recursion seen on the heap early was a prototype over a fork of sixteen fields, which [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects, weighing 1,024, the cap a quarter of the budget sets, so that the fifth of its levels ran its heap twin. Not yet known: whether a recursion `/std` holds runs on the heap where its folded frame would not, which a count of each recursive function's weight against its emitted locals settles, and whether the weight can be read after the folding it stands in for, `bound_frames` running in the continuation IR before the emitter folds. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: an inlined or copied callee is weighed by its nodes, a `Nat` operation one of them, though its emitted form carries a fallback many times its size.** `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) counts a callee's live nodes, and each `Nat` operation is one node, emitted as an `i31` fast path beside a call into the big-number helpers. Counted at `8ed961f9c` on a prototype over a fork of sixteen fields, which [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects: its insert, each arm calling two small helpers, was 72,839 lines at `wasm-optm`, each arm holding its own copy of the helpers' digit reads with their fallbacks, and its program spent 14.5 s in `emit` against 1.1 s over the map of that commit. At `5bccb9ebd` the landed `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert` is 4,438 lines of `spines`' optimized module, which `emit`s in 1.2 s. Not yet known: whether any program of `/std` or the corpus pays for the weighing, and which pass makes each copy; settled by counting, over `/std`'s test program and the corpus, the emitted bytes each inlined or copied callee adds against the node weight that admitted it. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's insert does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels deep over 65,537 keys ([`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api)), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state; settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
- **A dead call of a total recursion stays at run time wherever the recursion becomes a loop before its claim is discharged.** `droppable_functions` (`curios-anf/src/summary.rs`) gives a certified total function a claim, droppable given what it applies of what it was handed, and a call goes only where it hands each such parameter a known function droppable at every call (`Module::may_drop_call`, `curios-cps/src/module.rs`). The optimizer makes a recursion called from one place a local continuation loop, and a continuation carries no claim, so once the loop is made nothing drops it, however dead its result: a walk over a list that applies a function it is handed, in a `let` nothing reads, stays in the optimized continuation IR as a loop that only walks, its exits resuming with nothing, where the call it was is handed a known closure. A walk that applies a field of what it is handed gets no claim at all, the summary recording an application only of a parameter itself, so `/std/Map/internal/Node/first_in`, which applies its `Key`'s `hash`, is never droppable; a `let a = first_in(l)` only the proofs of `insert` and `remove` read walked to the leftmost leaf, and hashed its key, at every level of the descent, until the node spelled `first_in(l)` only where proofs read it. Fix: the loop a function's recursion becomes carries that function's certified totality as its termination, and a loop whose result is dead goes where its body, as the optimizer has left it, does nothing a run observes — every call in it droppable, no host row, no write — so `first_in`'s loop goes once its key's encoding is resolved and dropped, which no claim stated at the function could say. LLVM splits it the same way: `mustprogress` states a loop's termination and is carried from a function to its loops, a mark a pass that cannot keep may drop since only its absence is ever assumed, and `LoopDeletion` judges the loop's side effects itself. Check: the walk above gone from the optimized continuation IR, through a field and through a parameter alike; a loop over a partial function's recursion kept; a loop whose body calls something kept. Size: one commit.
- **A rebuild of a value with no tag that a constant may be is refused, though which constant may reach it is known.** A rebuild of a value constant data may be is admitted only under a test of its tag that rules every constant's out (**Constants by tag**, `curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`), and a constant reaches a reuse version as any unique value does. A row with no tag — a product schema, a tuple — has no test to stand under, so a function handed a constant one at one call and a value nothing else holds at another rebuilds it at neither: `rebuild::tests`' `a_value_a_constant_may_be_is_rebuilt_only_under_a_test_of_its_tag` holds the untested case at none, and by the same rule a rebuild of the `Map` `/std/Map/insert` hands back, a product with no tag, stands nowhere `Map/empty()` may reach, which a program filling a map from empty is. A constant is one global every use shares, and the constants that may reach a value are the facts' already (`constant_tags`), so where no tag rules them out, a rebuild could test the value's identity against each of them and copy where it is one: the one place static uniqueness is unsure, answered at run time as Perceus and Lean answer every write, by a test, and nowhere else. Not yet known: how a rebuild names a constant bound outside its scope, a global only once emitted; what the test costs beside the rebuild it keeps, a `ref.eq` a constant that may reach it; and which rebuilds `/std` refuses for it, counted over `cargo xtask profile prelude`. Check: the untested `bump` rebuilt under the identity test, its constant call copying; the count of `Map` rebuilds in a program filling a map from empty, before and after; differential answers unchanged. Size: unknown.
- **An element a search passes over is judged shared where the search stops on it and hands it out.** `Shape::element_kept_after` (`curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`) counts a consumption of the list after a read of its element on any path, so a loop that reads an element, goes on with its list where the element is empty and answers the element where it is not, as `/std/Map/internal/Node/lone` collapses a branch left one twig, answers an element judged shared, and every map `remove` touched lost its rebuilds. The node searches for the index and reads once there (`filled_at`). Counting only the consumptions where the element is still named is unsound, since an element dead there may already have been rebuilt into on the way, as `an_element_an_update_writes_over_is_held_by_nothing_else` holds; the rule wants every construction that may reuse the element, and every consumption of a field read off it, ruled out between the read and the consumption.
