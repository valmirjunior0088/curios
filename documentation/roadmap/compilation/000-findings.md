# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: `curios-cps` and the crates below it may still hold remnants of constructs that have left the language.** Folds, written motives and `choose` have each gone from Core and the surface, and `curios-cps`, `curios-emit`, `curios-wasm` and the runtime were not re-read against the language as it stands when they went. A remnant would look like a pass whose documentation names a source shape no program can now produce (the convoy `forward_calls` was written for is one that survived only because a non-dependent `match … end(v)` can still be written), an emitter path only a removed form reached, a pattern list naming a form nothing produces any more, or a test fixture spelling a construct the surface refuses. Not known: whether any remains, of folds or of anything else. Settled by: a read of those crates, their tests and their READMEs against the language and the erased alphabet as they stand, each remnant found removed where nothing reaches it, or filed with its evidence where something still might. Size unknown.
- **Uncertain: what the fold forms' removal did to the time of a hot `List/fold`, and of a recursion through one, is unmeasured on the landed tree.** `List/fold` erases to the peel loop `/std/Bytes/fold` is written as (`erase_list_fold`, `curios-erase/src/eraser/eliminate.rs`), whose suffix CPS holds as a window, where it was a `Nat` fold over the list's length; and a loop its caller defines is contified into it (`contify_calls`, `curios-cps/src/optimize/contify.rs`), which a fold through a tree's children needs to stay a loop. Two programs read what that buys and risks: `rounds(k, xs, acc)`, which folds `(a * 31 + x) % 1000003` over a 1,000-element list `k` times, at `k = 200000`; and a rose tree `build(14, 1)`, each node `v` with children at `3v`, `3v + 1` and `3v + 2` modulo 1000003, summed by `sum(node(v, children)) = List/fold(children, v, (c, a) => (a + sum(c)) % 1000003)`. Both answer at `dc8a5442d` as at `1ecc9ddea` (counted); a prototype of the change, timed under load, read the first about 30% faster and the second even. Not known: either time on the landed tree. Settled by: both programs compiled by a `1ecc9ddea` build and by the tree, timed interleaved on an idle machine, medians of seven with their spans; a slower reading is a finding of its own. Size unknown.
