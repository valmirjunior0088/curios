# Compilation: findings

- **Branch specialization reads any literal first field as a tag.** `tagged_tuple_values` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/specialize.rs`), which `specialize_call_patterns` and `specialize_jump_patterns` read, takes a structural tuple or a row construction whose first field is a literal `Nat` and calls that field its tag, but only a variant family's row carries a tag at slot zero: a product row's first field is data, and a structural tuple has no tag. The specialization stays correct, since the literal is known, but what it reaches is arbitrary: a product whose first field is a literal is specialized, and one whose first field is computed is not. The pass names itself for [SpecConstr](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/system-f-with-type-equality-coercions-2/), whose pattern is the constructor application a callee takes apart, with no literal field selecting it: a product has one pattern, its construction, and `split_workers` (`fields.rs`) may already deliver that one. Over the corpus's twenty-six programs at `7d28c0209`, counted, the product case specializes nothing, a family's row is specialized once or twice in twenty-one, and a structural tuple once in each of three, `walk_mirror_baseline`, `walk_mirror_held_scan` and `walk_mirror_indexed`: a pair whose first field is the literal `0`, where a family's row is specialized in its place once the two cases are withheld. Withheld, those three `wasm-optm` rungs print 3.4 to 3.7% longer, 595,366 characters for 575,073 in the first, and every other program's is the text it was. Timed, each compiled program run on two million from standard input, the two arrangements interleaved, a median of nine readings with its span on a quiet machine: 1,356 ms (1,350 to 1,360) specialized and 1,349 ms (1,345 to 1,381) withheld, 1,393 ms (1,378 to 1,400) and 1,365 ms (1,354 to 1,389), 1,690 ms (1,686 to 1,691) and 1,705 ms (1,695 to 1,722), in that order of the three. So the structural case buys a twenty-eighth of three modules and no time, over the family's row it displaces, and what it keys on there is a pair's first value. Fix: key the tagged case on a family row; keep the structural and product cases only if that size is wanted, keyed on the construction and under a name that says so. Check: `specialize_tests.rs`, and the corpus's `cont-optm` rungs. Size: medium.
- **CSE's debug assertion states the one-parent rule a second way.** `debug_assert_single_owner` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/cse.rs`) counts owning links over every live node, while `Module::verify_function_body` (`curios-cont/src/verify.rs`) walks each function from its body and refuses a node reached twice. The two part on nodes no body reaches: an orphan, or a node linked from an unreachable one beside its own parent, fails the assertion and passes the verifier. Fix: factor the verifier's walk into one checker the assertion calls. Check: `cse_tests.rs` and the verifier's tests. Size: quick.
- **The reduction cache's second door reads a shared key once per occurrence.** `adapted_across_universes` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) aligns the stored spelling's levels and sorts with the asking one's through `curios-core`'s `universes_in_order`, which reads each side by the per-occurrence walk (`the_per_occurrence_universe_walk_reads_levels_and_sorts_in_order`), so a key whose graph shares a subterm carrying universe data is walked as its tree on every hit that reaches the alignment. `Term::level_differences` names this shape — a strip of each side is "a walk per path" — and replaces it with a walk over the pair. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether any program hands the door such a key; it serves terms naming no local, and a node carrying no universe data is skipped. It is a walk [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) is not yet true of. Fix: read the differing levels with `level_differences` and build the map from its pairs, declining where it answers `None`. Check: a second-door hit over a doubling term at `Type(?u)` answers in its own size, as a fixture beside `a_shared_level_walk_asks_each_level_once_per_node`, and `reduction::across_levels` samples the same hits over `/std`. Size: quick.
- **A mismatch that elaboration discards has paid for its display.** `display_mismatch` and `resolved_for_display` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) normalize a mismatch's two sides where the error is raised, and an error raised under a bracket that rolls back is thrown away with its rendering. The normalization spends the declaration's budget, warms the reduction cache and mints binders, so a walk documented as display-only runs where a program compiles: with `reduce::normalize` keeping what one display has normalized, and nothing else in the elaborator changed, about forty declarations of `/std` each spend one to four units fewer, and every one of them compiles. Counted, each declaration's `budget::consumed` sample in the profile `cargo xtask clippy` files, at `84eff3c90` and `8c8e2a378`. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** how many displays are built for errors no report shows, and whether what a display leaves behind — a warmed reduct, a minted binder — reaches the term elaboration produces. Fix: an error carries its two terms and the display is built where a report is made. Check: the counted profiles agree before and after for every declaration that compiles, and every report in `curios-pipeline/src/tests/` and the corpus is unchanged. Size: medium.
- **Erasure restates each intrinsic's operand types by hand, and nothing compares them with the roster.** `erase_intrinsic` (`curios-elab/src/into_ersd/intrinsic.rs`) erases each operand against a type its arm spells, the third place a carrier's operand types are written after `Intrinsic::signature` and `Intrinsic::algebra`. `curios-cert`'s `kernel::infer::signature_tests` holds those two to each other and does not reach this one, and a disagreement here is a miscompilation rather than an admission, since erasure runs after the kernel. The arms can read their operand types off the roster: `Intrinsic::operands` and the operands `Intrinsic::signature` states are parallel, which is how both checkers walk an intrinsic and how erasure already walks a `foreign` call's (`lower.rs`, over `foreign_operands`), and what an arm adds is the operation it binds and the operands it leaves out, a proof (`in_range`, `within`, a divisor's bound) or a type. A grid cannot hold the arms as they stand, since none exposes the type it erases an operand at. Fix: erase an intrinsic's operands by that zip, leaving out one the signature demands as a type or at a proposition, each arm keeping the operation it binds. Check: `/std`'s erased image and the corpus's `ersd` rungs are the bytes they were. Size: medium.
- **A committed term names a recursive group by search, and two answers of the search are no declaration's part.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether either arises outside a constructed case. `named_members` (`curios-elab/src/denoise.rs`) spells a stuck call headed by a top-level group by its member's name, found by asking which names the group goes by (`Context::member_names`): a search of the groups the unit and its scope define, by the group itself. A group that is found is noted as a read of each member's body, so the trace of the declaration that asked holds it. Two answers follow no one declaration. Where two declarations define one group, the unit's own are filed by group (`Frames::members`), each definition writing over the one before it, so the names answered are those of the one elaborated last, and which that is follows the order the walk asked for them in. And where no declaration defines the group, a local `rec`'s or an instance another declaration opened, the answer is an absence that a later text fills by declaring that group at the top level, and no trace of a kept declaration is asked it again. Either way the committed term spells the same definition, so no judgment moves; what moves is the spelling a unit stores, and with it whether a unit compiled over a baseline is stored as one compiled whole. Fix: recognize a group by reduction's own record of the name it opened it from, as an instance of a universe-polymorphic one is recognized already (`Context::instance_of`), in place of the search. Check: a unit declaring one group under two names, and a third declaration committing a stuck call to it, stores the same bytes whichever of the two is asked for first. Size: medium.
- **Nothing measures that a lowered form follows its recorded lookups alone.** `curios-pipeline`'s `std_replay_census` elaborates each of `/std`'s declarations again alone and reports any that comes out another way, which holds an elaboration to its trace. A lowering is kept by the lookups it recorded ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](../../design/compilation/a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)), and nothing lowers each declaration again against only what its record names to hold the record complete: `std_recompile_reproduction` holds two edits, a leaf's and a hub's, to the bytes of a whole compile. Fix direction: the census's counterpart over the lowering, each declaration lowered with its baseline answering for the lookups its record holds and for no other. Size: a measurement test.
- **Whether a proof is searched for or reused follows what a closed run left in the elaborator's context.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** what the run leaves, and what reads it. Keeping the closed machine's memo past a run, at `cccb60c1a`, changed one certificate `/std` stores and no other byte of its unit: in `/std/Map/bits/within`, one `True/proved()` of the `below` field is now the proof an earlier one of the same body found, under a cast, where with the memo dropped at the end of each run it was searched for again and proved from other facts. A hit on the memo skips whatever the skipped run did in the context beside spending, and the elaboration cache refuses an entry where a stamped write was made since its probe (`Caches::stamps_unchanged`, `curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`), which would make a run's writes decide whether a term elaborated beside it is remembered; that this is the path is not shown. Both certificates are accepted by the kernel. Fix: once that is known. Check: `within` elaborates to one term with the memo cleared at every run and kept. Size: unknown.
- **A definition's variance vector and the levels a family holds may be the budget's.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether a program reaches either, and whether a definition's walk should run its body's computations at all. `definition_variances` and `family_levels` (`curios-elab/src/variance.rs`) read a term whose reduction the budget refused as holding every level it carries and hand back what they computed past it, each saying that what is left claims less. A vector so filed refuses two instances of the definition a later declaration compares at two levels, with no word of the budget, and a family so read takes a level parameter no instance holds; a stored unit keeps either under any other budget. A family's variance vector and its polarity vector were so until each refused a walk the budget cut short (`record_variances` beside them, `positivity_vectors` in `curios-analysis/src/positivity.rs`). For a definition the walk runs the closed computations of its body, the cost a vector exists to save, so refusing there refuses a definition whose own check ran none of them. Fix: once that is known. Size: small.
- **Whether a constant `print` folds is decided by the size of the library's write loop.** The closed evaluator copies a description closure only within `DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT`, 128 nodes (`curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/budget.rs`), and the description `/std/Handle`'s `write_waiting` builds sits close under it: one more arm in the loop, or a reply read through one more bind, takes it past the cap, after which no constant `print` folds and `Tui/Session/enter` and `leave` stay in every program. What notices is `tests::fmt::print_constant_args_collapses_at_ersd`, `tests::fmt::constant_args_leave_no_formatter_in_cont` and `tests::runtime::closure_tests::folds_constant_arg_through_let_function`, none of which names the cap. The cap stops a sequencing chain's continuation, which carries its whole suffix as extent, from being copied at every level in every round, and its documentation rests it on a performance being small by construction, a few dozen nodes; the library's write is a loop that waits and resends, which is no such performance and is not a chain's suffix either, so size alone does not tell the two apart. Fix: a test that states the write's description against the cap, so an edit that crosses it fails by name; or a cap that tells a continuation carrying its program from a performance by something other than a count of nodes. Check: that test, and the three above. Size: quick for the test, medium for the cap.
- **`family_slot_probe` surveys four families.** It reads the families of the module `spines` erases to (`curios/src/tests/codegen/shapes.rs`), which listed eighteen of the library's at `447fbb0a1` and lists the four `spines` reaches at `7f2ce2cfd`. The split it reports, free against paid, was the ground for a rule its reading says is justified by code the corpus does not contain, the command-line, formatting, layout and TOML families among them, and none of those is surveyed any longer. Fix: survey the families of the library's own erased unit. Check: the probe's table lists a family no program of the corpus reaches. Size: quick.
- **A comparison `copy_growth_measurements` rests on cannot be retaken.** Its account of which instrument sees what (`curios/src/tests/codegen/structural/measurement_tests.rs`) rests on `programs/parse_digits.crs` run with the return protocol toggled and nothing else changed, and nothing toggles that pass: no flag, and no recipe beside the claim. Fix: a recipe that says how the pass is withheld, or the claim restated over something a reader can run. Check: the comparison, retaken. Size: quick.
