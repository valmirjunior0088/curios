# Compilation: findings

- **Counted figures taken through the `wasm-optm` rung, which is gone, wait to be retaken over the emitted module.** Where the rung left, each protocol that read it was restated onto `wasm` and its figures dropped: the field-shape census's static half, the casts and boxes of seven corpus programs over the Binaryen-optimized text at `447fbb0a1` (`field_shape_census`, `curios/src/tests/codegen/shapes.rs`, which now counts the emitted text); and the `br_table`s of `spines`' optimized module beside `map_wall_spines_slope` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/map_wall.rs`), counted at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`). Fix: retake each over the emitted module, by the census run, `wonder --stage wasm` and the protocol each document states, and restate it where it stood with the commit it was taken at. Check: each document holds its figure again, named by its commit and rung. Size: one commit.
- **A body written at two leaves of a match is emitted twice, since nothing takes two equal continuations of one function for one.** An arm's alternatives are each a copy of the body the arm writes, and a row a binder of its own put under several cases is an instance at each leaf ([Patterns are compiled where their types are known](../../design/surface/patterns-are-compiled-where-their-types-are-known.md)); the copies reach the optimized module and the WebAssembly whole, and the continuation optimizer (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) holds no pass that merges them. Counted at `ba4ab354e`, release, x86-64 Linux: `/std` outside its tests writes 93 arms at more than one leaf (`Compiler::leaf`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/matrix.rs`), 45 of them in proofs, and the second copy of `/std/Flt/digits/normalize`'s stray row weighs 4,130 bytes of `programs/flt_hot_loop.crs`'s WebAssembly, in every program that prints a `Flt`; a program whose last arm `| (_, none()) =>` sums forty products of its input modulo primes emits 206,091 bytes, written as alternatives or in both arms of a nested match alike, against 189,546 with the body bound once as a function. Fix: a pass after the fixpoint that keeps one continuation of each class of continuations of one function and one lineage equal up to the values, continuations and functions their extents bind, at the innermost `LetCont` enclosing every member, each member a jump to it, then the jumps forwarded and the graph shrunk and pruned once: first the classes whose members read the same values from outside, of any size; then those the inliner would leave standing as a callee of several calls, past `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` as the inliner weighs an extent ([A callee is weighed by what it emits](205-a-callee-is-weighed-by-what-it-emits.md) owns the weight), whose members read different values, each a parameter of the one kept. Classes are found by a hash computed once bottom-up and verified member by member, or by hashing modulo α (Maziarz et al., *Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence*, PLDI 2021), whichever the first commit bounds the work of. A probe of the pass, sizes by `wonder --stage wasm` and `wasm-tools parse`, run time by the user-space instructions a `curios compile`d executable retires, median of three: the corpus 0.16% smaller after the first commit and 0.27% after the second, the xbench workloads, `flt_hot_loop`, `parse_bindless`, `parse_digits` and `monad_suspend` within 0.3%; the same pass before the fixpoint grew the corpus 1.53%, and parameterized classes from two nodes saved 2.53% but moved `chain` by −11.8% and `flt_hot_loop` by +7.1%, undoing the copies the inliner made of `/std/Str/fold`'s step at each width of a UTF-8 scalar. `curios-cps`'s README states the decision with those placements rejected, and the decision's rejection of a Core node that shares a body links it. Check: `curios-cps` tests of two equal arms merged, a member nested in another's extent, a class refused where a continuation it reaches is out of scope, a second run that merges nothing, and the work over a geometric grid of nesting depth; codegen tests that the summing body is emitted once at `cps-optm` and `wasm`, and past the line that `| (some(x), none()) | (none(), some(x)) =>` over the same body is too (206,053 bytes to 191,372), each with its answers, and that a parameterized class below the line is left copied. Size: two commits, the second after the first.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's insert does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels deep over 65,537 keys ([`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api)), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state. One reason the peers do not meet is a row's own: OCaml's tail modulo constructor fills a placeholder in a block every field of which is mutable underneath, and Koka writes a constructor context into memory it owns, where `curios-emit` declares a slot of a row's struct mutable only where some rebuild writes it, every other slot immutable so an engine reads it as the constant its construction stored (`table.rs`), so a hole makes its slot mutable for every construction of the row, and every read of that slot no constant to the engine; and a struct's slots are filled at `struct.new`, so a hole in a slot of a type no null inhabits takes a filler of that type. Settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, with the slot the loop writes declared mutable in both, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
- **A rebuild of a value with no tag that a constant may be is refused, though which constant may reach it is known.** A rebuild of a value constant data may be is admitted only under a test of its tag that rules every constant's out (**Constants by tag**, `curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`), and a constant reaches a reuse version as any unique value does. A row with no tag — a product schema, a tuple — has no test to stand under, so a function handed a constant one at one call and a value nothing else holds at another rebuilds it at neither: `rebuild::tests`' `a_value_a_constant_may_be_is_rebuilt_only_under_a_test_of_its_tag` holds the untested case at none, and by the same rule a rebuild of the `Map` `/std/Map/insert` hands back, a product with no tag, stands nowhere `Map/empty()` may reach, which a program filling a map from empty is. A constant is one global every use shares, and the constants that may reach a value are the facts' already (`constant_tags`), so where no tag rules them out, a rebuild could test the value's identity against each of them and copy where it is one: the one place static uniqueness is unsure, answered at run time as Perceus and Lean answer every write, by a test, and nowhere else. How the test names a constant bound outside its scope, read in the tree on 2026-11-18: `curios-emit` interns every binding of equal constant data into one global (`hoist_consts`, held by `tests::hoist`'s `constant_tuples_hoist_to_one_interned_global`), and a function binds again at its entry each constant it reads and binds nowhere in itself (`bind_constants_again`, `curios-emit/src/machine.rs`), so a rebuild names a constant in scope by its value and mints an equal construction where none is, each the one global the constant is, and `ref.eq` against it is identity. Not yet known: what the test costs beside the rebuild it keeps, a `ref.eq` a constant that may reach it; and which rebuilds `/std` refuses for it, counted over `cargo xtask profile prelude`. Check: the untested `bump` rebuilt under the identity test, its constant call copying; the count of `Map` rebuilds in a program filling a map from empty, before and after; differential answers unchanged. Size: unknown.
- **A search that tests the head it reads on every round, contified into its caller, leaves the element it answers shared, so `remove` cannot answer `Node/lone`.** `/std/Map/internal/Node/lone`, which a remove that leaves one twig collapses its branch to, takes its list apart as `[h, ..r]` and tests each head's tag; contified into `remove`, its window is virtualized into a loop over `(base, end, length)` reading `h = List/get(base, end - length)` each round, and a remove answering `lone(l2)` copies its node and its twig list at three levels each (`map_remove_rebuilds_its_node_at_every_level_in_a_loop_using_its_map_once`, `a_maps_descent_writes_each_levels_twigs_in_place`, read at `5fe814608`, landed as `e8f5688d6`). `pick`, whose read sinks into the arm that answers and leaves the loop, keeps every rebuild, so `remove` reads `pick(l2, filled_at(l2))` through `picked_lone`, a search that computes an index and reads once. Ruled out by reading the module: the read on a later round (`Shape::read_again`), since the loop's head rebinds `base`; a window's range, since the virtualized window takes no `ListRest`; and the constant empty node `List/set` writes at the removed index, since constant data counts as unshared. The likely fact, read in the solver's sources: a borrowed parameter is a source of sharing, "never the one name an object goes by" (`sources`, `curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`), and the loop only reads `base`, so `base` is borrowed and `h`, read out of it, is shared, though the list handed in at the loop's entry is unique and dead once the loop is left — the case the module's **Borrowing** paragraph states for the caller's value, "a read-only walk the optimizer made a loop leaves its caller's value unique for the rebuild after it", and not for a part the loop hands back out. A fix that reading suggests: a part read out of a borrowed parameter judged against what each entry hands that parameter, unique where each is and is consumed after the loop only as the part rule lets a whole be. Not yet known: why `pick`, whose loop reads its list the same way, keeps every rebuild, which this reading does not explain; and so whether it is the fact, which a trace of the shared solver's sources over the remove fixture settles. Check: the two tests above over `lone(l2)`, `filled_at` and `picked_lone` deleted. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: a function's locals are numbered in the order allocation makes them, so a large one reads most of them at two-byte indices.** [Locals are allocated by liveness](../../../curios-emit/README.md#locals-are-allocated-by-liveness) folds a function's values onto the fewest locals their live ranges allow and says nothing of their order, and WebAssembly encodes a local's index in LEB128, one byte below 128: in `programs/lcg/lcg.crs`, `io/bind`, the function the program's entry comes to, declares 3,419 locals and reads or writes 9,765 of its 11,830 local accesses at two bytes. Numbered by how often each is read or written, parameters in their places, the module would take 1,751 fewer bytes of its 175,939, 1,665 of them in `io/bind`, counted at `ba4ab354e` over `wonder --stage wasm`; Binaryen's `ReorderLocals` orders a function's locals by use so. Not yet known: what the order costs in the local declarations, which group locals into runs of one type, a run split wherever two types interleave by use; settled by counting the corpus's binaries with locals numbered by use within the runs, against today's. Size: unknown.
