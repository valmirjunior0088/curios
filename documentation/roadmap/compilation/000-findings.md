# Compilation: findings

- **Closing a binder visits every entry of every spine beneath it.** `MetavarSpine::rewritten` (`curios-core/src/term/metavar_spine.rs`), which a capture reaches through `capture_placed` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`), hands every entry of a spine to the visit and refolds the spine's reach and newest local over every entry, so a binder closed over n metavariables born in a scope of n locals costs n² even where one entry changes, against the design rule's "a metavariable's spine is the persistent vector of its birth scope's locals, minted and passed over without being walked". Counted at `0fc2fc1bd` in a scratch copy, with `wonder diagnostics -` over the lambda probe of [A node carries its free variables in its own size](101-a-node-carries-its-free-variables-in-its-own-size.md): 1 005, 2 005 and 4 005 spine rewrites visit 1.00 M, 4.01 M and 16.0 M entries at 1 000, 2 000 and 4 000 bindings, each a whole spine as long as the scope, where the same bindings with no lambda visit none; under gdb at 4 000, three of twelve samples stand in `capture_placed` and `MetavarSpine::rewritten` under `elaborate_func_infer`. Fix: the spine's persistent vector keeps each subtree's reach and newest local, so a rewrite descends only into the subtrees holding a local at least as new as the capture's oldest binder, and refolds only the paths it rebuilt: an augmentation of `Ordered` (`curios-utilities/src/persistent.rs`), then the spine read through it. Check: spine entries visited over the probe's grid, linear; `/std`'s image unchanged at one path; the spine's and capture's tests. Size: two commits.
- **A wake partitions every parked problem.** `Solutions::wake_parked` (`curios-elab/src/context/solutions.rs`) takes the whole parked list and partitions it by whether each problem watches a newly solved metavariable, and `take_checking` finds a checking problem by a linear search, so n problems parked in one scope and woken one solution at a time cost n². Counted the same way over the same probe: 1 002, 2 002 and 4 002 wakes partition 0.50 M, 2.00 M and 8.00 M parked problems at 1 000, 2 000 and 4 000 bindings, and the wake's allocated bytes grow ×3.99 a doubling; under gdb at 4 000, two of twelve samples stand in parking and retrying. Fix: index the parked problems by the metavariables they watch, in parking order, so a wake reads the problems its solutions name and leaves the rest where they stand, and a checking problem by its placeholder. Check: parked problems read per doubling over the grid, linear; the image unchanged; `curios-elab`'s parking tests. Size: one commit.
