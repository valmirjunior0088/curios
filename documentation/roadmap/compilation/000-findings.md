# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: checking the bounded tower costs the square of its lines.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) lets reduction alone grow past what was written, and charges it to the budget. The bounded tower (`curios/src/tests/towers.rs`'s `Shape::Bounded`, a chain of `let`s each naming the one before it three times under a proof that the last stays below the input) is checked at 12, 24, 48 and 96 lines in 0.71, 1.58, 4.64 and 15.90 G instructions, counted at `e2719c842` through `wonder diagnostics -`: ×3.50 and then ×3.69 per doubling, approaching four, as its allocated bytes do (×3.67). `tower_measurements` at the same commit reads its looks ×1.54 in the elaborator and ×1.56 in the kernel for a third more lines, where a walk visiting each node once reads at most a third more and every other tower at most 26 per cent more; its heaviest declaration's units read 43 018 and then 56 510. Main read the same looks and units at `710e1c46f`. Part of the square is the rank's, which [An atom is ranked from its children's ranks](../arithmetic/101-an-atom-is-ranked-from-its-childrens-ranks.md) removes: counted at `102ee41b4` by a scratch copy's counter, today's rank rebuilds 51,794, 202,424, 799,604 and 3,177,644 nodes over the four heights ranking 612, 1,224, 2,448 and 4,896 atoms, ×3.9 per doubling, where that specification's summaries fill 1,561 to 12,397 nodes, ×2.0. What remains past it: the handles the proof's comparisons ask grow 3,159, 10,647, 38,583 and 146,295 over the same heights, ×3.4 to ×3.8, each a memo's answer once the rank is a node's, asked under `compare_nat_among` nested in `Asked::verdict` (two samples under gdb at 96 lines, one in the zonk a witness resolution materializes). Not known: which reader hands out the tower's atoms once per line per earlier line, and whether the budget charges it, as reduction the proof's comparisons fold, or it is work outside reduction. Settled by: the same instruction count once the rank specification has landed, and a profile of the tower at 48 and 96 lines (`cargo xtask profile`) naming the span whose instructions still quadruple, with the heaviest declaration's units at both heights; a square the units charge in step is reduction's and drops this entry, and one they do not is restated as a finding against the rule its span departs from. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a function type's codomain is captured by rewriting one shared spine once per metavariable.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) has a metavariable's spine rebuilt only where its solution reads a binder of its birth scope. `Telescope::build_marked` closes a declaration's parameters over its codomain through `capture_placed` (`curios-core/src/scope.rs`), and each `Metavar` node met there has its spine rewritten entry by entry (`MetavarSpine::rewritten`, an `Ordered::insert` an entry), though every metavariable born under the one scope shares one spine version. Seen at `102ee41b4`, release: a codomain `Eq()(A, A)` over 1 026 parameters, where `A` nests 1 024 `+` operators, costs 2.6 s and a gibibyte where the same `A` as the body costs 0.24 s and 95 MB; two of four samples under gdb stand in the rewrite under the capture and one dropping those spines. Not known: whether the body form pays the same under another name (its samples stand in the free-variable sets `e2719c842` since replaced), and whether that commit's spine rewrite, which passes over the runs a capture does not reach, changes a capture that reaches every entry. Settled by: the codomain form against the body form, timed and sampled at main's tip; a square that stands is a memo of the rewrite by spine version, one that is gone drops this entry. Size: unknown.
