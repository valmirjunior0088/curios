# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: `forward_calls` may rewrite nothing once a match can no longer be applied to the evidence its motive states.** `curios-cps/src/optimize/simplify.rs`'s `forward_calls` documents itself as the pass for the shape a convoy leaves, `match s: (x) => (e: E(x)) -> R | … end(evidence)`: once erasure has dropped the evidence, each arm binds a nullary function and jumps it to a join that only calls it, and the pass makes that call at the jump. With written motives gone that form cannot be written, and `/std` and `programs/` hold no `match … end(…)`. Not known: whether a lowering, erasure or either optimizer leaves a join whose whole body calls the function a jump hands it, from any other source. Settled by: the pass's rewrites counted over the compilations of `/std`'s tests and of `programs/`, in a scratch copy; at none, the pass and its two tests in `simplify/tests.rs` go, the corpus's optimized modules compared byte for byte without it, and its doc's convoy shape with them; at some, its doc names the shape that fires. One commit if it goes.
- **Uncertain: below Core, the compiler still carries a fold as a first-class form that Core no longer has, and what of it still earns its place is unreviewed.** With the fold drop a free-monoid match in Core is a case split alone, its cons arm binding no hypothesis, and erasure (`curios-erase/src/eraser/eliminate.rs`, the seam that once lowered a fold) states it as one peel, `SwitchNat`'s zero and successor or `Rhs::UnconsSequence`. Yet `curios-anf` keeps `Rhs::FoldNat` and `Rhs::FoldSequence` (`curios-anf/src/node.rs`) as loop forms with readers throughout: its reference semantics (`semantics.rs`), the closed evaluator (`optimize/evaluate/interpret.rs`, `budget.rs`), `verify.rs`, `print.rs`, `remap.rs`, `walk.rs`, `link/taken.rs`, `optimize/reconstruct/facts.rs` and the lowering to CPS (`into_cps/lowerer.rs`, `emitter.rs`, `census.rs`). What produces them now is `invert_structural_recursion` (`optimize/invert.rs`), which recovers a fold out of a linear structural recursion so that the shapes `rebase_monoid_recursion` (`optimize/rebase.rs`) and the CPS window rewrite (`curios-cps/src/optimize/fields.rs`, `WindowFamily`) read are reached, erasure's `List/fold` intrinsic, a `FoldNat` over the list's length (`erase_list_fold`), and its poll of a suspension's cells (`curios-erase/src/eraser/suspension.rs`), a `FoldSequence` over a sequence it builds itself. Not known: whether a loop is better stated in ANF as a fold recovered by inversion or as a self tail call the CPS optimizer contifies, which would leave inversion and the fold nodes redundant beside the monoid worker/wrapper; whether inversion and the worker/wrapper are one recognizer of a recursion over a peeled parameter; whether erasure's split forms, `SwitchNat` and `UnconsSequence`, are the ones a case split wants; and what each removal would cost in the corpus's loops, its image size and `xbench`. Settled by: a design dive over `curios-erase`, `curios-anf` and `curios-cps`, citing prior art for loop recovery, and a decision. Size unknown until then; it waits until the fold drop has landed.
