# Compilation: findings

- **Branch specialization reads any literal first field as a tag.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether the product and structural cases it specializes today pay for themselves. `tagged_tuple_values` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/specialize.rs`), which `specialize_call_patterns` and `specialize_jump_patterns` read, takes a structural tuple or a row construction whose first field is a literal `Nat` and calls that field its tag, but only a variant family's row carries a tag at slot zero: a product row's first field is data, and a structural tuple has no tag. The specialization stays correct, since the literal is known, but what it reaches is arbitrary: a product whose first field is a literal is specialized, and one whose first field is computed is not. The pass names itself for [SpecConstr](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/system-f-with-type-equality-coercions-2/), whose pattern is the constructor application a callee takes apart, with no literal field selecting it: a product has one pattern, its construction, and `split_workers` (`fields.rs`) may already deliver that one. Fix: key the tagged case on a family row, and measure what the product and structural cases specialize over the corpus today, and what `split_workers` leaves for them; keep them only if they pay, keyed on the construction and under a name that says so. Check: `specialize_tests.rs`, and the corpus's `cont-optm` rungs. Size: medium.
- **CSE's debug assertion states the one-parent rule a second way.** `debug_assert_single_owner` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/cse.rs`) counts owning links over every live node, while `Module::verify_function_body` (`curios-cont/src/verify.rs`) walks each function from its body and refuses a node reached twice. The two part on nodes no body reaches: an orphan, or a node linked from an unreachable one beside its own parent, fails the assertion and passes the verifier. Fix: factor the verifier's walk into one checker the assertion calls. Check: `cse_tests.rs` and the verifier's tests. Size: quick.
- **The reduction cache's second door reads a shared key once per occurrence.** `adapted_across_levels` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) aligns the stored spelling's levels with the asking one's through `levels_in_order`, which strips each side with `rewrite_universe_levels_scoped`, the per-occurrence level walk (`curios-core`'s `the_per_occurrence_level_walk_asks_every_occurrence`), so a key whose graph shares a subterm carrying universe data is walked as its tree on every hit that reaches the alignment. `Term::level_differences` names this shape — a strip of each side is "a walk per path" — and replaces it with a walk over the pair. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether any program hands the door such a key; it serves terms naming no local, and a node carrying no universe data is skipped. It is a walk [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) is not yet true of. Fix: read the differing levels with `level_differences` and build the map from its pairs, declining where it answers `None`. Check: a second-door hit over a doubling term at `Type(?u)` answers in its own size, as a fixture beside `a_shared_level_walk_asks_each_level_once_per_node`, and `reduction::across_levels` samples the same hits over `/std`. Size: quick.
- **The Ersd and Cont printers hand-roll their frames** (`curios-ersd/src/print.rs`, 1 069 lines; `curios-cont/src/print.rs`, 812), each walking an explicit job stack, where [depth is bought with stack](../../design/compilation/depth-is-bought-with-stack-not-with-hand-rolled-frames.md) and neither is among the decision's four exceptions. Fix: recurse under `recurse`, printing in the same order. Check: each crate's `a_deep_module_prints_without_native_stack`, renamed for what it then shows, prints its 50 000-deep module on the default test-thread stack, and every printed rung of the corpus is unchanged. Size: medium.
- **Measurement readings whose conclusions no longer follow from their figures.** The figures were retaken at `447fbb0a1` and are current; the prose around three of them was written for the figures they replaced. `death_birth_census` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/census.rs`) reads the map-spine substrate as concentrating in `/std/Map`'s `insert`/`insert_node`/`replace`, where the retake finds nothing at all under `/std/Map` in any of the fourteen programs. `family_slot_probe` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/shapes.rs`) calls `/std/Map/Node` the worst row at four slots to six and counts eight slots qualifying free, against a retake of four to five and twelve. `copy_growth_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/structural/measurement_tests.rs`) recommends emitted size as the unit for a change that copies, against a `user`-time and resident-set baseline taken at `82cb8ef7` that no longer sits beside the module sizes it is read with. Fix: restate each reading over the figures beside it. Check: every quantity a reading names appears in the block above it. Size: medium.
- **`map_wall`'s figures are not read since the map held its keys and a concatenation of two immediates stayed one.** `map_wall_spines_slope` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/map_wall.rs`) records 1 076 ns an insert at `447fbb0a1`, before a map held its keys and built two encodings an insert, and `map_wall_key_share`, whose loop folds those two, records none. Timed on a quiet machine: `programs/spines/spines.crs` compiled with `curios compile` at `4b6a05d8b` and at `f0528c1aa`, the commit that joins two immediates by arithmetic, the slope between 25 000 and 75 000 inserts a median of fifteen interleaved readings, each the least of three runs, reads 1 566 ns an insert (1 529 to 1 606) and 699 (674 to 722); a loop folding `Bytes/len(Bytes/of_nat(y))` over the same orbit, between 1 000 000 and 3 000 000 steps over eleven readings, reads 162 ns a step (160 to 164) and 16 (15 to 16), beside a control of 4. `map_wall_key_share`'s note that no `br_table` of the optimized `spines` module sits in the insert is not what `wonder stage wasm-optm` prints: two sit in `/std/Map/Node/insert`, as two sat in the `insert1` it was at `bf7e07015`. Fix: read both tests on a quiet machine and record what they print where each says what it last printed, with the share an insert's two keys are of it, and read the module's tables again. Check: the two tests, in one sitting. Size: quick.
- **Three `layout_tests` assertions hold with the defect present** (`curios/src/tests/codegen/structural/layout_tests.rs`). `a_monomorphic_slot_carries_its_own_type` asserts that the `Chain` row packs its tag into an `i8` and its `Nat` payload into an `i32`, against its own documentation and the row, which `wonder stage wasm` shows as `(field $0 i8)`, `(field $1 (ref null $row/8$/Chain))` and `(field $2 (ref null any))`; the assertion finds an `i32` because the flat display puts the whole recursion group on one line, where `Interest`'s fields supply it. `trees_leaf_rides_its_payload`'s check that no `$tuple/1` read survives cannot fail, since a family is read through its row, and `trees_constructor_payloads_stay_boxed` filters `$tuple/` declarations and never reads the `$row/` one the tree's payloads live in. Fix: assert on the named row's own fields — `Chain`'s recursive field typed as its row, its tag an `i8`, its payload the reference its carrier names — and on the tree's row reads. Check: each assertion fails when the property it names is broken by hand. Size: quick.
- **`curios-binaryen/build.rs` narrates history in two doc comments**: the replaced `BUILD_SCHEMA` constant, and the cache that once sat under `target/`. Held: `build_schema` hashes the file, so any edit discards the Binaryen cache. Fix: restate both in the present tense the next time the file changes for a reason that pays the rebuild. Check: an empty-cache build, then a cache hit from another Cargo mode or build-script fingerprint. Size: quick.
- **A mismatch that elaboration discards has paid for its display.** `display_mismatch` and `resolved_for_display` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) normalize a mismatch's two sides where the error is raised, and an error raised under a bracket that rolls back is thrown away with its rendering. The normalization spends the declaration's budget, warms the reduction cache and mints binders, so a walk documented as display-only runs where a program compiles: with `reduce::normalize` keeping what one display has normalized, and nothing else in the elaborator changed, about forty declarations of `/std` each spend one to four units fewer, and every one of them compiles. Counted, each declaration's `budget::consumed` sample in the profile `cargo xtask clippy` files, at `84eff3c90` and `8c8e2a378`. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** how many displays are built for errors no report shows, and whether what a display leaves behind — a warmed reduct, a minted binder — reaches the term elaboration produces. Fix: an error carries its two terms and the display is built where a report is made. Check: the counted profiles agree before and after for every declaration that compiles, and every report in `curios-pipeline/src/tests/` and the corpus is unchanged. Size: medium.
- **Erasure's walks have no row over a doubling term.** `curios-elab`'s `walk_tests.rs` runs the zonks, the display rendering and the sort of a type over sixty levels that each hold the one below twice, and `into_ersd/tests.rs` holds a projection chain and a curried call chain to work in their depth; no erasure walk is put to a term that shares. Every tower of `curios/src/tests/towers.rs` compiles through erasure at sixty lines, so none of those shapes reaches one. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether a walk under `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/` reads a shared term once per path — `typed.rs` states a `let`'s type with each binding substituted. Fix: put erasure's entry points to the doubling term, each row mutation-checked. Size: quick.
- **Nothing holds a hash's order out of a stored unit or a report.** [A unit is a function of what it was compiled from](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-a-function-of-what-it-was-compiled-from), and the two orders that reached what the compiler hands out are each held by a test: the frames' guards (`curios-elab/src/context/frames.rs`), in a stored unit, and a global's re-export paths (`writable_paths`, `curios-text/src/into_core.rs`), in what a report spells a name by. No step of the gate would catch the next, in a unit or in what a diagnostic prints: `curios-pipeline`'s `std_unit_reproduction` is a measurement, run by hand, as is `std_recompile_reproduction`, which holds the library compiled over a baseline to the bytes it is compiled whole. Fix: `clippy::iter_over_hash_type` over the crates between text and a stored unit, by a design decision since it configures a lint, each site it names ordered or shown to reach no output; [item tasks](200-item-tasks.md)' differential over workers holds the same claim over `/std` and the corpus once it lands, and is no substitute for a site neither compiles. Check: the lint in `cargo xtask clippy`. Size: unknown, until the lint has been run once.
- **Erasure restates each intrinsic's operand types by hand, and nothing compares them with the roster.** `erase_intrinsic` (`curios-elab/src/into_ersd/intrinsic.rs`) erases each operand against a type its arm spells, the third place a carrier's operand types are written after `Intrinsic::signature` and `Intrinsic::algebra`. `curios-cert`'s `kernel::infer::signature_tests` holds those two to each other and does not reach this one, and a disagreement here is a miscompilation rather than an admission, since erasure runs after the kernel. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether the arms can read their operand types off `Intrinsic::signature`, or a grid can hold them to it.
- **A polarity vector may be the budget's, and what reads it would be refused with no word of the budget.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether any accepted declaration files one. `positivity_vectors` (`curios-analysis/src/positivity.rs`) reads a term whose reduction its driver refused at `Mixed`, and still accepts a declaration that does not reach itself through it, since that reading only ever refuses more. The vector it hands back then holds a `Mixed` the budget put there: the elaborator writes it on the entry it publishes (`decide_polarities`, `curios-elab/src/positivity.rs`) and the certifier files it (`accept`, `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`), and a family declared after it that stores itself in that parameter would be refused as not strictly positive by an analysis that ran out of nothing, so its report would name no budget; a stored unit would keep the vector under any other budget, the budget being no part of its key ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). Between units the elaborator's vectors were so before each declaration was analysed alone; within one unit, a single analysis over all of it reported the budget. Nothing is admitted. No program was found that reaches it: `induct Wrap(A: Type): Type | wrap(Box(W12(A))) end` over twelve doubling aliases, with `induct Outer: Type | leaf() | node(Wrap(Outer)) end` after it, is refused by the kernel at `Wrap`, for the budget, at every `--budget` tried under the one `Wrap` is accepted from, so no vector of it was filed short. Fix: refuse a declaration whose analysis the budget cut short, so that a vector on record is always the declaration's own; `a_self_calling_type_level_rec_leaves_the_walk_terminating` (`curios-analysis/tests/driven.rs`) pins the acceptance that would end. Check: a family the analysis cannot finish is refused for the budget by both drivers. Size: small.
- **A declaration that reads only another's name waits for its body, so a method named at the top level cannot use its own witness's operators.** `let neq(a: Dyadic, b: Dyadic) -> Bool = Bool/not(a == b)` beside `satisfy Eql(Dyadic) { eql = eql, neq = neq }` is refused as a cycle (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/demand.rs`): the body of `neq` resolves `==` to the witness, and the witness names `neq`, of which it reads nothing but the signature, which is written out. Written in the witness's body the same method is accepted, and reads `eql` alone. So `/std`'s carriers, whose witnesses delegate to named functions, cannot state one method by the operator of another: `Dyadic/neq`, `gt` and `ge` call `eql`, `lt` and `le` by name. **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether publishing a signature ahead of its body, the refinement [item tasks](200-item-tasks.md) names for throughput, also orders this pair — the witness elaborated against the signature of `neq`, then the body of `neq` against the witness — and what the kernel is handed, since each then mentions the other's name. Fix: once that is known. Check: the pair elaborates, and `Dyadic`'s three methods are written with their operators. Size: unknown.
- **A committed term names a recursive group by search, and two answers of the search are no declaration's part.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether either arises outside a constructed case. `named_members` (`curios-elab/src/denoise.rs`) spells a stuck call headed by a top-level group by its member's name, found by asking which names the group goes by (`Context::member_names`): a search of the groups the unit and its scope define, by the group itself. A group that is found is noted as a read of each member's body, so the trace of the declaration that asked holds it. Two answers follow no one declaration. Where two declarations define one group, the unit's own are filed by group (`Frames::members`), each definition writing over the one before it, so the names answered are those of the one elaborated last, and which that is follows the order the walk asked for them in. And where no declaration defines the group, a local `rec`'s or an instance another declaration opened, the answer is an absence that a later text fills by declaring that group at the top level, and no trace of a kept declaration is asked it again. Either way the committed term spells the same definition, so no judgment moves; what moves is the spelling a unit stores, and with it whether a unit compiled over a baseline is stored as one compiled whole. Fix: recognize a group by reduction's own record of the name it opened it from, as an instance of a universe-polymorphic one is recognized already (`Context::instance_of`), in place of the search. Check: a unit declaring one group under two names, and a third declaration committing a stuck call to it, stores the same bytes whichever of the two is asked for first. Size: medium.
- **Nothing measures that a lowered form follows its recorded lookups alone.** `curios-pipeline`'s `std_replay_census` elaborates each of `/std`'s declarations again alone and reports any that comes out another way, which holds an elaboration to its trace. A lowering is kept by the lookups it recorded ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](../../design/compilation/a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)), and nothing lowers each declaration again against only what its record names to hold the record complete: `std_recompile_reproduction` holds two edits, a leaf's and a hub's, to the bytes of a whole compile. Fix direction: the census's counterpart over the lowering, each declaration lowered with its baseline answering for the lookups its record holds and for no other. Size: a measurement test.
