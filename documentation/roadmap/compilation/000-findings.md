# Compilation: findings

- **Doubled room can inflate the heap required by retained byte buffers.** [A flattening extends the payload its first piece wrote last](../../../curios-emit/README.md#a-flattening-extends-the-payload-its-first-piece-wrote-last) gives a payload room twice its run (`ForceWalk::place_first`, `curios-emit/src/rope_emitter/force_walk.rs`); an older run or view keeps that payload alive, including its unused room. Mixed at `82bfb1fb1`, Rust `1.99.1`, release with all features, Wasmtime `50.0.0`, AMD Ryzen 7 5800X3D: retaining sixteen 12.5 MiB byte buffers, each grown and read in 64 KiB steps, takes the engine heap to 1 GiB with doubling and 512 MiB with a scratch byte-only policy allocating `n + n / 8` past 1 MiB. Median peak resident memory falls from 926 MiB to 525 MiB, while whole-run time rises from 582 ms [568–597] to 729 ms [699–800] and collections from seven to 33. The same taper on one 256 MiB buffer built from fresh 64 KiB arrivals raises explicit flattening copies from 637.7 MiB to 2,332.6 MiB, resident memory from 942 MiB to 1,561 MiB and time from 583 ms [573–595] to 1,133 ms [1,116–1,153]; both engine heaps reach 2 GiB. The build-once, read-once control stays exact under both policies. Retake with the arrival count read from `Process/args`, reading the first byte after each append and checking a checksum of those reads and the final first byte, last byte and length: one buffer over 64, 128 and 256 MiB, with fresh or reused arrival payloads, and retained groups of four 50 MiB, eight 25 MiB, sixteen 12.5 MiB and thirty-two 6.25 MiB buffers, each buffer reusing a 64 KiB payload of a distinct byte; hold the group across a scalar foreign call before consuming it. Change only byte roomy-capacity arithmetic in the controls. Count payload allocations and copied bytes in the forcing helper separately from collections on plain optimized modules through `curios_native::test_support::collected`; record the engine's last heap growth and the fresh process's peak resident set separately. Time precompiled plain modules through `curios-profile`, five interleaved groups of five readings with a warmup per variant per group on a quiet machine; times are medians of group medians with all readings' span, and resident figures are medians of the plain runs' peaks. Policy choice: retain doubling as the default; alternatives for a deliberate byte-policy change are 12.5% or 50% growth past 1 MiB, or 20% past 16 MiB, each measured and trading retained room against copying and collections, with no factor consistently preferable across the controls. Check a chosen policy against both builder throughput and retained-buffer footprint, including heap-bound refusals; preserve extent claims, unsigned capacity bounds, exact host crossings and build-once allocation; match raw and optimized results for empty runs, threshold boundaries, views, shared prefixes and competing room claims, plus the emitter's rope tests and `/std`'s corpus. Size: one commit for a chosen policy, its authoritative wording and checks.
