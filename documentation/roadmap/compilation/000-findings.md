# Compilation: findings

- **Branch specialization reads any literal first field as a tag.** `tagged_tuple_values` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/specialize.rs`), which `specialize_call_patterns` and `specialize_jump_patterns` read, takes a structural tuple or a row construction whose first field is a literal `Nat` and calls that field its tag, but only a variant family's row carries a tag at slot zero: a product row's first field is data, and a structural tuple has no tag. The specialization stays correct, since the literal is known, but what it reaches is arbitrary: a product whose first field is a literal is specialized, and one whose first field is computed is not. The pass names itself for [SpecConstr](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/system-f-with-type-equality-coercions-2/), whose pattern is the constructor application a callee takes apart, with no literal field selecting it: a product has one pattern, its construction, and `split_workers` (`fields.rs`) may already deliver that one. Over the corpus's twenty-six programs at `7d28c0209`, counted, the product case specializes nothing, a family's row is specialized once or twice in twenty-one, and a structural tuple once in each of three, `walk_mirror_baseline`, `walk_mirror_held_scan` and `walk_mirror_indexed`: a pair whose first field is the literal `0`, where a family's row is specialized in its place once the two cases are withheld. Withheld, those three `wasm-optm` rungs print 3.4 to 3.7% longer, 595,366 characters for 575,073 in the first, and every other program's is the text it was. Timed, each compiled program run on two million from standard input, the two arrangements interleaved, a median of nine readings with its span on a quiet machine: 1,356 ms (1,350 to 1,360) specialized and 1,349 ms (1,345 to 1,381) withheld, 1,393 ms (1,378 to 1,400) and 1,365 ms (1,354 to 1,389), 1,690 ms (1,686 to 1,691) and 1,705 ms (1,695 to 1,722), in that order of the three. So the structural case buys a twenty-eighth of three modules and no time, over the family's row it displaces, and what it keys on there is a pair's first value. Fix: key the tagged case on a family row; keep the structural and product cases only if that size is wanted, keyed on the construction and under a name that says so. Check: `specialize_tests.rs`, and the corpus's `cont-optm` rungs. Size: medium.
- **CSE's debug assertion states the one-parent rule a second way.** `debug_assert_single_owner` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/cse.rs`) counts owning links over every live node, while `Module::verify_function_body` (`curios-cont/src/verify.rs`) walks each function from its body and refuses a node reached twice. The two part on nodes no body reaches: an orphan, or a node linked from an unreachable one beside its own parent, fails the assertion and passes the verifier. Fix: factor the verifier's walk into one checker the assertion calls. Check: `cse_tests.rs` and the verifier's tests. Size: quick.
- **The reduction cache's second door reads a shared key once per occurrence.** `adapted_across_universes` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) aligns the stored spelling's levels and sorts with the asking one's through `curios-core`'s `universes_in_order`, which reads each side by the per-occurrence walk (`the_per_occurrence_universe_walk_reads_levels_and_sorts_in_order`), so a key whose graph shares a subterm carrying universe data is walked as its tree on every hit that reaches the alignment. `Term::level_differences` names this shape — a strip of each side is "a walk per path" — and replaces it with a walk over the pair. No program at hand gives the door such a key: counted at `7d28c0209`, the alignment is reached 24,858 times while `/std` is elaborated and ten to thirty-four times in each of the corpus's twenty-six programs, and in every one the levels the per-occurrence walk reads are as many as a walk that enters each node once reads, 1.7 to a key across `/std` and thirty-two at most. It is a walk [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) is not yet true of, which nothing compiled today pays for. Fix: read the differing levels with `level_differences` and build the map from its pairs, declining where it answers `None`. Check: a second-door hit over a doubling term at `Type(?u)` answers in its own size, as a fixture beside `a_shared_level_walk_asks_each_level_once_per_node`, and `reduction::across_levels` samples the same hits over `/std`. Size: quick.
- **A mismatch that elaboration discards has paid for its display, and what the display did decides terms the unit stores.** `display_mismatch` and `resolved_for_display` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) normalize a mismatch's two sides where the error is raised, and an error raised under a bracket that rolls back is thrown away with its rendering. Elaborating `/std` builds 367 such displays, 734 sides normalized, and no report shows one. The normalization spends the declaration's budget, warms the reduction cache and mints binders, so a walk documented as display-only runs where a program compiles, and what it leaves is read. With `display_mismatch` answering the two sides as it is handed them, and nothing else changed, `/std` spends 61,276,794 units for 61,353,039 and four declarations commit other terms, `/std/Nat/Gcd/step`, `/std/Nat/DivMod/div_lt_of_lt_mul`, `/std/Nat/Coprime/quotients` and `/std/Map/bits/within`: an implicit solved to `(a / b) * b` where it is `b * (a / b)`, and in `within` another proof of one bound. Their traces differ with them; the lowering's state and every erased form are the bytes they were. The path is the mints: the `i` of `within` is minted at 186 with its displays built and at 184 with none, and the order the algebra puts atoms in reads a local's mint, which the entry on that order shows. Counted at `7d28c0209`, from samples and a switch added for the runs to a copy of the tree, the stored unit's parts digested apart. Fix: an error carries its two terms and the display is built where a report is made. Check: every report in `curios-pipeline/src/tests/` and the corpus is unchanged, and `/std`'s erased forms are the bytes they were, its elaborated module moving in the four declarations above. Size: medium.
- **Erasure restates each intrinsic's operand types by hand, and nothing compares them with the roster.** `erase_intrinsic` (`curios-elab/src/into_ersd/intrinsic.rs`) erases each operand against a type its arm spells, the third place a carrier's operand types are written after `Intrinsic::signature` and `Intrinsic::algebra`. `curios-cert`'s `kernel::infer::signature_tests` holds those two to each other and does not reach this one, and a disagreement here is a miscompilation rather than an admission, since erasure runs after the kernel. The arms can read their operand types off the roster: `Intrinsic::operands` and the operands `Intrinsic::signature` states are parallel, which is how both checkers walk an intrinsic and how erasure already walks a `foreign` call's (`lower.rs`, over `foreign_operands`), and what an arm adds is the operation it binds and the operands it leaves out, a proof (`in_range`, `within`, a divisor's bound) or a type. A grid cannot hold the arms as they stand, since none exposes the type it erases an operand at. Fix: erase an intrinsic's operands by that zip, leaving out one the signature demands as a type or at a proposition, each arm keeping the operation it binds. Check: `/std`'s erased image and the corpus's `ersd` rungs are the bytes they were. Size: medium.
- **A committed term names a recursive group by search, and two answers of the search are no declaration's part.** `named_members` (`curios-elab/src/denoise.rs`) spells a stuck call headed by a top-level group by its member's name, found by asking which names the group goes by (`Context::member_names`): a search of the groups the unit and its scope define, by the group itself. A group that is found is noted as a read of each member's body, so the trace of the declaration that asked holds it. Two answers follow no one declaration. Where two declarations define one group, the unit's own are filed by group (`Frames::members`), each definition writing over the one before it, so the names answered are those of the one elaborated last, and which that is follows the order the walk asked for them in. And where no declaration defines the group, a local `rec`'s or an instance another declaration opened, the answer is an absence that a later text fills by declaring that group at the top level, and no trace of a kept declaration is asked it again. Either way the committed term spells the same definition, so no judgment moves; what moves is the spelling a unit stores, and with it whether a unit compiled over a baseline is stored as one compiled whole. Neither is seen in `/std`: at `7d28c0209` no two of its definitions that call themselves are one recursion, their printed bodies compared with each one's own name set aside and mutual groups not compared, and the second answer waits on a later text, which a compile of one text never meets. Fix: recognize a group by reduction's own record of the name it opened it from, as an instance of a universe-polymorphic one is recognized already (`Context::instance_of`), in place of the search. Check: a unit declaring one group under two names, and a third declaration committing a stuck call to it, stores the same bytes whichever of the two is asked for first. Size: medium.
- **Nothing measures that a lowered form follows its recorded lookups alone.** `curios-pipeline`'s `std_replay_census` elaborates each of `/std`'s declarations again alone and reports any that comes out another way, which holds an elaboration to its trace. A lowering is kept by the lookups it recorded ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](../../design/compilation/a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)), and nothing lowers each declaration again against only what its record names to hold the record complete: `std_recompile_reproduction` holds two edits, a leaf's and a hub's, to the bytes of a whole compile. Fix direction: the census's counterpart over the lowering, each declaration lowered with its baseline answering for the lookups its record holds and for no other. Size: a measurement test.
- **Which proof the elaborator writes, and which spelling it solves an implicit to, follows how many names the declaration minted before.** The carriers' algebra ranks an atom by the structural hash of its term (`Atoms::handle`, `curios-core/src/atoms.rs`), and a local hashes as its mint's index (`Mint`, `curios-core/src/names.rs`), which counts the names the declaration minted before its binder was opened. The rank orders a product's factors and a form's monomials, and breaks the ties of the search that proves a bound (`curios-elab/src/entailment/search.rs`), so work that mints a name earlier in a declaration moves all three, whether or not the declaration keeps anything of it. A closed run answered from the machine's memo mints nothing where the same run made again mints its own binders: with the memo dropped at the end of each run, at `7d28c0209`, the `i` of `/std/Map/bits/within` is minted at 190 for 186, its rank moves with it, the search derives 37 rows for 39 over facts read alike up to that rank, and one of its bounds is proved by another term; `/std/Int/abs/of_mul` commits another term beside it and no other declaration does, the unit's traces, erased forms and lowering staying the bytes they were. A display built for a mismatch that is discarded mints as well, 184 for that `i` where none is built, which the entry on it counts. The elaboration cache is not the path: of the 203,295 closed runs `/std`'s elaboration makes, none changes a stamp its insert gate reads (`Caches::stamps_unchanged`, `curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`), and the gate refuses 2,265 entries with the memo kept and dropped alike. Counted from samples and notes added for the runs to a copy of the tree, the stored unit's parts digested apart. Fix: a rank reads nothing a count of earlier work moves, a local ranked by where its binder stands among those in scope. Check: `/std`'s elaborated module is the same bytes with the memo dropped at each run and kept, and with a discarded mismatch's display built and not. Size: small in code; it moves the spellings `/std` stores, once.
- **A definition's variance vector and the levels a family holds may be the budget's.** **Uncertain, and warrants investigation:** whether a program is refused by a vector or a level so filed, and whether a definition's walk should run its body's computations at all. `definition_variances` and `family_levels` (`curios-elab/src/variance.rs`) read a term whose reduction the budget refused as holding every level it carries and hand back what they computed past it, each saying that what is left claims less. A vector so filed refuses two instances of the definition a later declaration compares at two levels, with no word of the budget, and a family so read takes a level parameter no instance holds; a stored unit keeps either under any other budget. A family's variance vector and its polarity vector were so until each refused a walk the budget cut short (`record_variances` beside them, `positivity_vectors` in `curios-analysis/src/positivity.rs`). For a definition the walk runs the closed computations of its body, the cost a vector exists to save, so refusing there refuses a definition whose own check ran none of them. A program reaches the definition's walk, and its end. Under `let spin(n: Nat) -> Nat = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => spin(k) end;` and `let Pick(n: Nat, _A: Type) -> Type = match n: (_) => Type | 0 => Nat | _k + 1 => Nat end;`, the declaration `pub let D(A: Type) -> Type = Pick(spin(100000), A);` costs the elaborator 5,906,378 units, spent in `definition_variances`, where `pub let plain: Nat = spin(100000);`, whose check runs nothing of `spin`, costs 3,115; and at `spin(600000)` the walk spends the whole thirty million and `D` is accepted with nothing said. Counted at `7d28c0209`, each the `budget::consumed` samples of the declaration in the stream a profiling build files for `wonder diagnostics`. What a later declaration is then told is not shown: comparing two instances of that `D` at two levels, `Eq()(d1(Nat), d2(Nat))` over `let d1 = D; let d2 = D;` with `d2(tie(d1))` beside it, conversion unfolds both and runs out of steps itself, and the report says so. A family read so was not probed. Fix: once that is known. Size: small.
- **Whether a constant `print` folds is decided by the size of the library's write loop.** The closed evaluator copies a description closure only within `DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT`, 128 nodes (`curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/budget.rs`), and the description `/std/Handle`'s `write_waiting` builds sits close under it: one more arm in the loop, or a reply read through one more bind, takes it past the cap, after which no constant `print` folds and `Tui/Session/enter` and `leave` stay in every program. What notices is `tests::fmt::print_constant_args_collapses_at_ersd`, `tests::fmt::constant_args_leave_no_formatter_in_cont` and `tests::runtime::closure_tests::folds_constant_arg_through_let_function`, none of which names the cap. The cap stops a sequencing chain's continuation, which carries its whole suffix as extent, from being copied at every level in every round, and its documentation rests it on a performance being small by construction, a few dozen nodes; the library's write is a loop that waits and resends, which is no such performance and is not a chain's suffix either, so size alone does not tell the two apart. Fix: a test that states the write's description against the cap, so an edit that crosses it fails by name; or a cap that tells a continuation carrying its program from a performance by something other than a count of nodes. Check: that test, and the three above. Size: quick for the test, medium for the cap.
- **`family_slot_probe` surveys four families.** It reads the families of the module `spines` erases to (`curios/src/tests/codegen/shapes.rs`), which listed eighteen of the library's at `447fbb0a1` and lists the four `spines` reaches at `7f2ce2cfd`. Fix: survey the families of the library's own erased unit. Check: the probe's table lists a family no program of the corpus reaches. Size: quick.
