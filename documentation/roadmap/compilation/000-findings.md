# Compilation: findings

- **Counted figures taken through the `wasm-optm` rung, which is gone, wait to be retaken over the emitted module.** Where the rung left, each protocol that read it was restated onto `wasm` and its figures dropped: the field-shape census's static half, the casts and boxes of seven corpus programs over the Binaryen-optimized text at `447fbb0a1` (`field_shape_census`, `curios/src/tests/codegen/shapes.rs`, which now counts the emitted text); and the `br_table`s of `spines`' optimized module beside `map_wall_spines_slope` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/map_wall.rs`), counted at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`). Fix: retake each over the emitted module, by the census run, `wonder --stage wasm` and the protocol each document states, and restate it where it stood with the commit it was taken at. Check: each document holds its figure again, named by its commit and rung. Size: one commit.
- **A body written at two leaves of a match is emitted twice, since nothing takes two equal continuations of one function for one.** An arm's alternatives are each a copy of the body the arm writes, and a row a binder of its own put under several cases is an instance at each leaf ([Patterns are compiled where their types are known](../../design/surface/patterns-are-compiled-where-their-types-are-known.md)); the copies reach the optimized module and the WebAssembly whole, and the continuation optimizer (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) holds no pass that merges them. Counted at `ba4ab354e`, release, x86-64 Linux: `/std` outside its tests writes 93 arms at more than one leaf (`Compiler::leaf`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/matrix.rs`), 45 of them in proofs, and the second copy of `/std/Flt/digits/normalize`'s stray row weighs 4,130 bytes of `programs/flt_hot_loop.crs`'s WebAssembly, in every program that prints a `Flt`; a program whose last arm `| (_, none()) =>` sums forty products of its input modulo primes emits 206,091 bytes, written as alternatives or in both arms of a nested match alike, against 189,546 with the body bound once as a function. Fix: a pass after the fixpoint that keeps one continuation of each class of continuations of one function and one lineage equal up to the values, continuations and functions their extents bind, at the innermost `LetCont` enclosing every member, each member a jump to it, then the jumps forwarded and the graph shrunk and pruned once: first the classes whose members read the same values from outside, of any size; then those the inliner would leave standing as a callee of several calls, past `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` as the inliner weighs an extent ([A callee is weighed by what it emits](205-a-callee-is-weighed-by-what-it-emits.md) owns the weight), whose members read different values, each a parameter of the one kept. Classes are found by a hash computed once bottom-up and verified member by member, or by hashing modulo α (Maziarz et al., *Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence*, PLDI 2021), whichever the first commit bounds the work of. A probe of the pass, sizes by `wonder --stage wasm` and `wasm-tools parse`, run time by the user-space instructions a `curios compile`d executable retires, median of three: the corpus 0.16% smaller after the first commit and 0.27% after the second, the xbench workloads, `flt_hot_loop`, `parse_bindless`, `parse_digits` and `monad_suspend` within 0.3%; the same pass before the fixpoint grew the corpus 1.53%, and parameterized classes from two nodes saved 2.53% but moved `chain` by −11.8% and `flt_hot_loop` by +7.1%, undoing the copies the inliner made of `/std/Str/fold`'s step at each width of a UTF-8 scalar. `curios-cps`'s README states the decision with those placements rejected, and the decision's rejection of a Core node that shares a body links it. Check: `curios-cps` tests of two equal arms merged, a member nested in another's extent, a class refused where a continuation it reaches is out of scope, a second run that merges nothing, and the work over a geometric grid of nesting depth; codegen tests that the summing body is emitted once at `cps-optm` and `wasm`, and past the line that `| (some(x), none()) | (none(), some(x)) =>` over the same body is too (206,053 bytes to 191,372), each with its answers, and that a parameterized class below the line is left copied. Size: two commits, the second after the first.
- **A function's locals are numbered in the order allocation makes them, so their value types interleave and most of them are read at two-byte indices.** [Locals are allocated by liveness](../../../curios-emit/README.md#locals-are-allocated-by-liveness) folds a function's values onto the fewest locals their live ranges allow and says nothing of their order, which is the order `allocate_locals` (`curios-emit/src/locals.rs`) first needs each slot: the value types interleave, and the binary declares a run of locals at every change of type, a count and a type each, while WebAssembly encodes a local's index in LEB128, one byte below 128. Counted at `5b3028ed8` by reordering each function's `(local …)` declarations in `wonder --stage wasm`'s text, every access naming its local, and parsing with `wasm-tools parse`, the text as emitted parsing to the emitted module's bytes: `programs/lcg/lcg.crs` declares 1,777 runs of locals over its 71 functions and reads 11,454 of its 15,330 local accesses at two bytes, its 175,939 bytes; over the 28 programs of `programs/`, 5,272,332 bytes in all, numbering by use alone saves 1.97% and grows `hello_world` and `dependent_vectors` by 0.12%, one run per value type in allocation order saves 1.84%, and one run per value type, the types by their total accesses and each run by its locals' accesses, saves 2.65%, every program from 0.54% to 3.20% (`flt_hot_loop`), `lcg` to 171,277 bytes over 172 runs. Binaryen's `ReorderLocals` sorts by accesses, and its binary writer emits a function's locals grouped by type. The order the user settled on 2026-11-19: one run per value type, the types by their total accesses, each run by its locals' accesses, parameters in their places. Fix: `allocate_locals` numbers each function's slots so once they are allocated, and the decision states the order. Check: a `curios-emit` test that a function's locals are numbered by type runs and by use; the corpus's binaries smaller by the figure above, restated at the fix's commit; every program's answers unchanged and the corpus emission tests passing. Size: one commit.
- **A loop parameter every jump into it hands itself or one value from outside stays a parameter, since the invariant fixpoint takes only a function.** [A known function a call hands is followed into it](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-known-function-a-call-hands-is-followed-into-it) substitutes a function or a literal every entry of a loop hands a parameter its back edges pass on, and `known_values` (`curios-cps/src/analysis.rs`) takes nothing else from the fixpoint (`invariant_fixpoint`): "Only a function is taken from it", `resolve_atom` reading an argument that is a value from outside the fixpoint's domain as a conflict, "an unobservable runtime value". So a recursion that hands a value it was handed on unchanged keeps it as a parameter of the loop contification makes of it, passed back to itself each round. Re-shown at `82b51a065`: a program whose `all_ok(piece, wire)` asks `List/all` of a predicate calling a recursive `fed(piece, state, n)` that hands `piece` on optimizes to a loop `/fed(piece, state, n)` inside `all_ok`, its back edge handing its own `piece` and its entry `all_ok`'s. Counted at `82b51a065` by a census in `known_values`, a continuation parameter each transfer into which hands it itself or one same value, every round: 36 to 56 a program over the 28 programs of `programs/` and 61 in `/std`'s Io tests, most of them the `base` and `end` of a virtualized list window, which `/std/Str/At/floor`, `/sys/List/fold` and `/std/Str/utf8/scan_from` carry round each step of the loops every program links. With each such parameter whose value is in scope where its continuation is bound taken as that value, a prototype, the corpus's binaries (`wonder --stage wasm`, `wasm-tools parse`) are 3.32% smaller, 5,272,332 bytes to 5,097,057, every program from 0.31% (`hello_world`) to 5.16% (`parse_bindless`), and every program answers as it did; run time untimed. LLVM folds a phi whose operands are itself and one value into that value (`simplifyPHINode`), MLIR's region simplification drops a block argument every predecessor hands the same value (`dropRedundantArguments`), and GHC's static argument transformation takes such an argument out of a recursion at the source. The fix the user settled on 2026-11-19: the fixpoint itself carries a value from outside, `resolve_atom` reading it as known rather than a conflict, so a parameter every transfer hands itself, another invariant parameter or one outside value is found, and `known_values` takes such a value as it takes a function, only where it is in scope where the continuation is bound; that scope is read off the walk `continuation_scopes` makes, which holds the values beside the functions at each `LetCont` and keeps only the functions, and `Module::scopes` (`curios-cps/src/verify.rs`) hands its maps as a struct with named fields where it hands a tuple. Fix: so, and the decision restated to values. Check: `curios-cps` tests of a loop parameter handed itself and an outside value substituted, of one handed two values kept, and of one whose value is out of scope at the binding kept; the corpus's binaries smaller by the figure above, restated at the fix's commit; every program's answers unchanged. Size: one commit.
- **A dead loop is dropped where a partial loop inside it cycles through a total one, so a program that never ends prints and exits.** `eliminate_dead_loops` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/simplify.rs`) drops a loop marked terminating whose answer nothing reads, where its body observes nothing and every cycle its continuations make passes through one marked terminating, so that a partial callee contification put inside it keeps it (`DeadLoop`). `LoopWalk::cycles_past_a_terminating_loop` asks that by taking every marked continuation out and looking for a cycle among the rest, which breaks a cycle that only passes through a marked loop as surely as that loop's own recursion: a partial loop that enters a total one and resumes at its exit reads as ending. A total walk handed a function, `f(k, n)` running `go(m, acc) = match m | 0 => acc | p + 1 => go(p, k(acc)) end` from `go(n, 1)`, is marked terminating as its record says, closed over what it mentions and never over what it is handed; handed `g(n) = match h(n) == 0 | true => 0 | false => g(n + 1) end`, partial, with `h(n) = match n | 0 => 0 | k + 1 => h(k) + 1 end` total, it has `g` contified into its loop and `h`'s worker into `g`'s, and `let _ = f(g, 3); print("done")`, which never ends, prints `done` and exits 0, re-shown on 2026-11-19 at `94c579029`. With the pass off it runs forever, as it does with `g` testing `n == 0` instead, its cycle passing no marked loop, or with `g(1)` called alone. A cycle is a marked loop's own where the transfer that closes it enters the marked continuation from itself or from one its body nests. Prototyped the same day — only those back edges taken out, every marked continuation kept in the graph — the program runs forever, and the dead-loop tests of `curios-cps`'s simplifier and of `curios/src/tests/codegen/dead_loops.rs` pass unchanged, the total walks there still leaving no loop. Fix: the cycle check takes out each marked loop's own back edges and nothing else, the pass's `///` and `DeadLoop`'s restated by it. Check: the program above as a codegen test in `curios/src/tests/codegen/dead_loops.rs` keeping the walk handed `g`, beside the walks that still leave none, and the simplifier's dead-loop tests unchanged. Size: one commit.
- **A nest whose call stands in its own innermost body's tail is collapsed into a jump to the continuation the collapse removed, so a recursive performance whose answer is dropped stops the compiler.** `collapse` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/uncurry/nest.rs`) retargets every transfer of the innermost level's nodes from that level's return continuation to the collapsed function's, then rewrites each application a call's resume makes into a jump to the resume after it, `Application::after`. Where the call stands in the innermost body's tail, as a recursion's own call does, that `after` is the innermost level's return continuation, removed with the level, and the rewrite made after the retargeting names it again. `let spin(n: Nat) -> Io({}) = let m = Io/pure(n + 1)!; spin(m);`, run as `let _ = spin(0)!; print("done");` in `let entry() -> Io({})` closed by `entry()`, stops the compiler at `invalid high CPS at the close of round 4: VerifyError("~f105 references undefined or out-of-scope continuation ~k452")`, and verifying after every pass names `cps::uncurry_nests` as the pass that leaves it; re-shown on 2026-11-19 at `068d5705d`, and on the build the session started from, with `let () = spin(0)!;` alike, while `spin(n) = spin(n + 1)` with no bind inside, or `spin(0)` as the program's last term, compiles and runs forever as it should. Prototyped the same day — an application whose `after` is the innermost level's return continuation jumping to the collapsed function's instead — the program compiles and runs forever. The module's "a collapse moves bodies without rewriting them" holds only of a nest none of whose calls stands in its own innermost body. Fix: so, in `collapse`, and the module's `//!` restated by it. Check: a `curios-cps` test of a nest whose call stands in its own innermost body's tail, the module verifying after the collapse; the program above as a test that compiles; the corpus's binaries unchanged, since the old rewrite verified nowhere it fired. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's insert does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels deep over 65,537 keys ([`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api)), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state. One reason the peers do not meet is a row's own: OCaml's tail modulo constructor fills a placeholder in a block every field of which is mutable underneath, and Koka writes a constructor context into memory it owns, where `curios-emit` declares a slot of a row's struct mutable only where some rebuild writes it, every other slot immutable so an engine reads it as the constant its construction stored (`table.rs`), so a hole makes its slot mutable for every construction of the row, and every read of that slot no constant to the engine; and a struct's slots are filled at `struct.new`, so a hole in a slot of a type no null inhabits takes a filler of that type. Settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, with the slot the loop writes declared mutable in both, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
