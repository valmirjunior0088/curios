# Compilation: findings

- **Nothing lowers a declaration again against only the lookups its record names, so a record that misses one would keep a stale lowering unseen.** A lowering is kept by the lookups it recorded ([A lowering is kept where its text and the names it resolved stand](../../design/compilation/a-lowering-is-kept-where-its-text-and-the-names-it-resolved-stand.md)); `curios-pipeline`'s `std_replay_census` (`src/standard/tests.rs`) holds each of `/std`'s elaborations to its trace, and nothing holds a lowering to its record: `std_recompile_reproduction` holds two edits, a leaf's and a hub's, to the bytes of a whole compile, and `into_core_unit_over` (`curios-module/src/into_core.rs`) lowers a unit over a baseline, keeping a declaration whose every recorded name resolves as it did, with no way to lower one declaration with only its record's names in scope. Fix, in two commits: a scope that answers a record's names and no other, the lowering's own behind `curios-module`'s `test_support` (recommended, since a unit's source built from a record states the lowering's input a second time); then the census, beside `std_replay_census` as an ignored measurement, lowering each of `/std`'s declarations in that scope and comparing the lowered item and its record with what the archive holds, a name minted anew for a kept witness read as the item's own, its reading recorded with its commit. Check: the census over `/std` names no declaration that lowers another way, each it names filed as a finding of its own. It precedes scheduling from those records in [item tasks](300-item-tasks.md), and any lookup a later delivery adds to the record. Size: two commits.
- **Uncertain: a call's frame weight counts every value its callee's body binds, though the emitter folds them onto the locals their live ranges allow, so a recursion of many arms runs its fifth level on the heap.** [A recursion is as deep as the heap allows](../../design/compilation/a-recursion-is-as-deep-as-the-heap-allows.md) weighs a call "a fixed overhead plus one for each value its callee's body binds", a weight that "stands in for its bytes" in code Binaryen has not optimized, while [Locals are allocated by liveness](../../../curios-emit/README.md#locals-are-allocated-by-liveness) folds a function's values onto "the fewest its values' live ranges allow", so a body whose arms bind values no other arm holds weighs their sum and holds about their largest. Counted at `8ed961f9c`: a generic sixteen-way trie insert, each of its arms rebuilding a seventeen-slot fork, weighs 1,024, the cap a quarter of `FRAME_BUDGET` sets (`curios-cps/src/frames.rs`), against 108 for `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert`, so four of its levels run natively and the fifth of every insert runs its heap twin. Not yet known: how far the weight and the folded frame part for the recursions `/std` holds, which a count of each recursive function's weight against its emitted locals settles, and whether the weight can be read after the folding it stands in for, `bound_frames` running in the continuation IR before the emitter folds. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: an inlined or copied callee is weighed by its nodes, a `Nat` operation one of them, though its emitted form carries a fallback many times its size.** `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) counts a callee's live nodes, and each `Nat` operation is one node, emitted as an `i31` fast path beside a call into the big-number helpers. Counted at `8ed961f9c` on a prototype: a generic sixteen-way trie insert whose arms each call two small helpers compiles to an `ins` of 72,839 lines at `wasm-optm`, each arm holding its own copy of the helpers' digit reads with their fallbacks, and its program spends 14.5 s in `emit` against 1.1 s for the same program over `/std/Map`; with the arms' cold path moved into one helper, `ins` is still 61,597 lines and the `emit` 7.1 s, that helper copied into each arm. Not yet known: whether any program of `/std` or the corpus pays for it, the one seen being a shape [A map forks sixteen ways](../standard-library/100-a-map-forks-sixteen-ways.md) does not adopt, and which pass makes each copy; settled by counting, over `/std`'s test program and the corpus, the emitted bytes each inlined or copied callee adds against the node weight that admitted it. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's write pass does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels under [A map forks sixteen ways](../standard-library/100-a-map-forks-sixteen-ways.md), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state; settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
