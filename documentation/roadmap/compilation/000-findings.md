# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: a call's frame weight counts every value its callee's body binds, though the emitter folds them onto the locals their live ranges allow, so a recursion runs on the heap sooner than its frame needs.** [A recursion is as deep as the heap allows](../../design/compilation/a-recursion-is-as-deep-as-the-heap-allows.md) weighs a call "a fixed overhead plus one for each value its callee's body binds", a weight that "stands in for its bytes" in code Binaryen has not optimized, while [Locals are allocated by liveness](../../../curios-emit/README.md#locals-are-allocated-by-liveness) folds a function's values onto "the fewest its values' live ranges allow", so a body whose arms bind values no other arm holds weighs their sum and holds about their largest. Counted at `5bccb9ebd` in `spines`' optimized module: `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert` weighs 136 and declares 49 locals beside its parameters, and `remove` weighs 74, so thirty of an insert's levels run natively under `FRAME_BUDGET` (`curios-cps/src/frames.rs`); the one recursion seen on the heap early was a prototype over a fork of sixteen fields, which [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects, weighing 1,024, the cap a quarter of the budget sets, so that the fifth of its levels ran its heap twin. Not yet known: whether a recursion `/std` holds runs on the heap where its folded frame would not, which a count of each recursive function's weight against its emitted locals settles, and whether the weight can be read after the folding it stands in for, `bound_frames` running in the continuation IR before the emitter folds. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: an inlined or copied callee is weighed by its nodes, a `Nat` operation one of them, though its emitted form carries a fallback many times its size.** `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) counts a callee's live nodes, and each `Nat` operation is one node, emitted as an `i31` fast path beside a call into the big-number helpers. Counted at `8ed961f9c` on a prototype over a fork of sixteen fields, which [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects: its insert, each arm calling two small helpers, was 72,839 lines at `wasm-optm`, each arm holding its own copy of the helpers' digit reads with their fallbacks, and its program spent 14.5 s in `emit` against 1.1 s over the map of that commit. At `5bccb9ebd` the landed `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert` is 4,438 lines of `spines`' optimized module, which `emit`s in 1.2 s. Not yet known: whether any program of `/std` or the corpus pays for the weighing, and which pass makes each copy; settled by counting, over `/std`'s test program and the corpus, the emitted bytes each inlined or copied callee adds against the node weight that admitted it. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's insert does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels deep over 65,537 keys ([`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api)), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state. One reason the peers do not meet is a row's own: OCaml's tail modulo constructor fills a placeholder in a block every field of which is mutable underneath, and Koka writes a constructor context into memory it owns, where `curios-emit` declares a slot of a row's struct mutable only where some rebuild writes it, every other slot immutable so an engine reads it as the constant its construction stored (`table.rs`), so a hole makes its slot mutable for every construction of the row, and every read of that slot no constant to the engine; and a struct's slots are filled at `struct.new`, so a hole in a slot of a type no null inhabits takes a filler of that type. Settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, with the slot the loop writes declared mutable in both, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
- **A rebuild of a value with no tag that a constant may be is refused, though which constant may reach it is known.** A rebuild of a value constant data may be is admitted only under a test of its tag that rules every constant's out (**Constants by tag**, `curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`), and a constant reaches a reuse version as any unique value does. A row with no tag — a product schema, a tuple — has no test to stand under, so a function handed a constant one at one call and a value nothing else holds at another rebuilds it at neither: `rebuild::tests`' `a_value_a_constant_may_be_is_rebuilt_only_under_a_test_of_its_tag` holds the untested case at none, and by the same rule a rebuild of the `Map` `/std/Map/insert` hands back, a product with no tag, stands nowhere `Map/empty()` may reach, which a program filling a map from empty is. A constant is one global every use shares, and the constants that may reach a value are the facts' already (`constant_tags`), so where no tag rules them out, a rebuild could test the value's identity against each of them and copy where it is one: the one place static uniqueness is unsure, answered at run time as Perceus and Lean answer every write, by a test, and nowhere else. How the test names a constant bound outside its scope, read in the tree on 2026-11-18: `curios-emit` interns every binding of equal constant data into one global (`hoist_consts`, held by `tests::hoist`'s `constant_tuples_hoist_to_one_interned_global`), and a function binds again at its entry each constant it reads and binds nowhere in itself (`bind_constants_again`, `curios-emit/src/machine.rs`), so a rebuild names a constant in scope by its value and mints an equal construction where none is, each the one global the constant is, and `ref.eq` against it is identity. Not yet known: what the test costs beside the rebuild it keeps, a `ref.eq` a constant that may reach it; and which rebuilds `/std` refuses for it, counted over `cargo xtask profile prelude`. Check: the untested `bump` rebuilt under the identity test, its constant call copying; the count of `Map` rebuilds in a program filling a map from empty, before and after; differential answers unchanged. Size: unknown.
- **A search that tests the head it reads on every round, contified into its caller, leaves the element it answers shared, so `remove` cannot answer `Node/lone`.** `/std/Map/internal/Node/lone`, which a remove that leaves one twig collapses its branch to, takes its list apart as `[h, ..r]` and tests each head's tag; contified into `remove`, its window is virtualized into a loop over `(base, end, length)` reading `h = List/get(base, end - length)` each round, and a remove answering `lone(l2)` copies its node and its twig list at three levels each (`map_remove_rebuilds_its_node_at_every_level_in_a_loop_using_its_map_once`, `a_maps_descent_writes_each_levels_twigs_in_place`, read at `5fe814608`). `pick`, whose read sinks into the arm that answers and leaves the loop, keeps every rebuild, so `remove` reads `pick(l2, filled_at(l2))` through `picked_lone`, a search that computes an index and reads once. Ruled out by reading the module: the read on a later round (`Shape::read_again`), since the loop's head rebinds `base`; a window's range, since the virtualized window takes no `ListRest`; and the constant empty node `List/set` writes at the removed index, since constant data counts as unshared. Not yet known: which fact holds the head shared, which a trace of the shared solver's sources over the remove fixture settles. Check: the two tests above over `lone(l2)`, `filled_at` and `picked_lone` deleted. Size: one commit.
