# Compilation: findings

- **A probe under an arm reduces again what the arm before it reduced: an arm's exit clears every reduct that may read the scope.** Where a frame that registers a refinement leaves, `Caches::invalidate_frame_exit` (`curios-elab/src/context/caches.rs`) clears the scoped reducts and the elaborations, since one may have read its equation; a reduct that read only the frames beneath goes with them. Of matches each on the `let` before it (`ChainedMatches`), at `1a53c9734`, counted in the profile build, elaboration allocates 117.5, 257.1 and 613.3 MB at 100, 200 and 400 links, and `refined_reduct` 27.3, 78.9 and 252.3 MB of its own, its probe's operands put in weak-head normal form through every link before them again (`reduct_spelling`); `growth_slopes` reads the row's instructions at 2.89 per doubling in `elaborate` from 100 links, under `HELD_SLOPE`. Fix: a reduct of the live window kept for as long as what it read stands, as a settlement's is (`Frames::begin_reads`), filed with the deepest frame it read and answering while that frame stands, in place of the clear at a frame's exit, in every scoped table the clear reaches. Check: the row's elaboration within a tenth of 2.0 per doubling from 100 links; the prelude image unchanged. Size: the protocol of `Caches`' scoped tables, a read count on every reduction they file.
- **Uncertain: `growth_slopes`' instruction column moves between runs of one compiler.** Over six runs at `be93acf08`, counted, a nesting of parentheses reads 2.76, 1.98, 3.22, 1.98, 1.98 and 2.97 instructions per doubling in `lower`, past `HELD_SLOPE` once, which fails the measurement for a growth it holds, while its looks, allocations and bytes read 2.00 every time; a match's arms reads 5.12, 7.75 and 11.95 in `binaryen`. Not known: whose instructions the count takes there. `curios-profile`'s `Instructions` counts the threads a compile starts after it opens, so a pool thread an earlier compile's Binaryen or Cranelift started could retire work inside a later stage's window; or a stage's own work could follow the hash keys a run draws. Settled by: the count read per thread for those stages over several runs. Fix, if pool threads: a stage's instructions read on its own thread, with Binaryen's and Cranelift's read where they run. Check: six runs read one slope within a tenth for each held growth. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: an infix chain's elaboration zonks its solved metavariables through the partial chain at each step.** Elaborating `a1 + … + an` types each `+` (`elaborate_infix`), whose conversion's drain (`Convert::drain`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) materializes the solved term metavariables of what it compares (`zonk_solved_term_metas`, `curios-elab/src/zonk.rs`), and the declaration's zonk (`zonk_term`) recurses level by level through the chain. At `f7375e49e`, four of seven samples taken under gdb of the reversed sum of 256 `Nat/and` operations stand in `zonk_solved_term_metas` under `Convert::drain` under `elaborate_infix` and three in `zonk_term`, and two of four of the reversed sum of 256 calls of one head stand in `zonk_solved_term_metas`; the 256 operations take 4.24 s [2.64–4.63], timed in three rounds at a load of 45 to 57, where a tower of 40 bounded lines takes 1.17 s [0.47–2.29]. Not known: which of `drain`'s sites zonks the partial chain, whether its memo starts afresh at each step, and whether `zonk_term` walks the chain once or once a level. Settled by: samples under the `dev` profile's compiler, which keeps line tables, read at the drain frame, and the sum's node looks at two sizes beside the zonks' share. Fix, if a site zonks the chain afresh at each step: the solved spelling kept beside the term it was taken for, as [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md)'s rebuild rule keeps a node. Size: unknown.

- **Uncertain: `curios-analysis`'s walks other than the classification's run over no doubling term.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../../design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md) holds each crate that owns walks to one doubling term in `every_walk_answers_a_doubling_term_in_its_own_size`. `curios-analysis/src/walk_tests.rs` holds the classification's walks, and neither checker's table reaches the crate's positivity, totality, unfolding, specialization, inversion, variance or relevance readings: `positivity.rs` and `totality.rs` remember the nodes they meet, while `unfolding.rs`, `specialize.rs`, `invert.rs` and `erased.rs` keep no such memo. Not known: which of them walk a whole term rather than a bounded part of one, and whether any of those walks one per path. Settled by: a row for each in `curios-analysis/tests/driven.rs`, whose `test_support::Scope` and checkers supply the `Env` they read through, over a sixty-level doubling term; a row that does not answer is a finding with its fix. Size: unknown.

- **Uncertain: what the rebase's sequence monoids save, timed.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-ersd/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) makes a list, a run of bytes and a text built under their own recursion loops over accumulators. Read off the code, an element goes from a frame and three allocations to three allocations, and the first forcing of the left-leaning rope the accumulator builds grows a worklist to one entry an element. Not known: what either side takes in time, which waits on a quiet machine. Protocol: two release compilers, at `34f5e7600` and at `d8360a177`, each compile one program that builds a million elements by `List/range`, `List/zip`, `List/take` and a recursion over `Str/concat`, and reads each answer's length; five groups of five runs, the two variants interleaved inside each group and one untimed run opening each group; a variant's figure is the median of its group medians, and its span their least and greatest. Fix: the reading goes to the decision as its figure, named with its commit, and a slowdown its spans prove is a finding of its own. Check: the reading stated. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a left-leaning rope's first forcing grows its worklist to the rope's depth.** `$<carrier>/force` (`curios-emit/src/rope_emitter.rs`) descends through `ForceWalk::push_uncached_node` (`rope_emitter/force_walk.rs`), which goes left and pushes every right child on an `$elems` worklist grown by doubling, so a rope that leans left — what `List/fold`'s builders and the rebase's accumulators build — holds one entry an element at its deepest, where a right-leaning one holds about one. Not known: whether the worklist costs anything a program notices beside the three allocations an element the rope already took. Fix, if it does: copy a flat right child in place at its offset and descend left without pushing it, which keeps the worklist small on either lean. Check: forcing a fold-built list of a hundred thousand elements grows no worklist past its first size, counted. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a large run's room may want a taper.** [A flattening extends the payload its first piece wrote last](../../../curios-emit/README.md#a-flattening-extends-the-payload-its-first-piece-wrote-last) gives a payload room twice its run (`ForceWalk::place_first`, `curios-emit/src/rope_emitter/force_walk.rs`), so a run read, grown and read again holds as much room as run for as long as any run over its payload is held. SpiderMonkey grows a flattened string by 12.5% past 1 MiB and Erlang a binary by 20% past 16 MB, bounding a large run's room for a few more copies an element. Not known: whether a Curios program holds a run large enough for the room to cost it — a heap that must grow, or an allocation the engine refuses where the exact run would have fit. Protocol: one program that grows a `Bytes` to 256 MiB in 64 KiB arrivals and reads its first byte after each, run under `curios_runtime::test_support::collected` once as the walk stands and once with the doubling replaced by a 12.5% taper past 1 MiB, the peak heap read off the engine's last growth record and the collections counted. Fix, if the doubled run's heap is the one that grows: a taper past the threshold the reading names, in `ForceWalk::place_first`. Check: both runs' peak heaps and collections stated, counted. Size: unknown.

- **Uncertain: a type the proof totality check reads is reduced again after its declaration reduced it.** `checked_proof_positions` (`curios-elab/src/totality.rs`) asks `proof_position` once per distinct zonked recorded type, inside the declaration's span but after it publishes and its budget is restored (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`), and the closed machine reduces the type again at what elaborating the term first cost. At `997cec045`, with each charge noted at its site and summed by the spans open around it (the protocol of [compilation spec 210](210-a-tail-step-of-a-closed-walk-keeps-no-frame.md)), `Fmt/render` over a format of `n` characters is charged about 325 units a character three times: under `apply::elaborate_apply`, under `check_proof_totality`'s `totality::proof_position`, and under `erase_unit`. Every `Fmt/render` site of `/std` pays all three, and the 93 sites that spell `/std`'s text through `Fmt` since `5b4818c6f` raised `/std`'s elaboration from 62,151,113 units to 64,683,107 (counted, `cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration`'s `budget::consumed`). Not known: whether the declaration's closed answers are gone when the check runs, or its type is asked under another key or demand than the application's own `format_type_with(Str, Fmt/parse(s))`; erasure is a judgment with a budget of its own, so its third reduction is not part of this. Settled by: the closed memo's hit or miss at `proof_position` for that type, with the tables the declaration holds listed at the check's entry. Fix, if the answers are gone: the check run over the declaration's lasting answers, which [A reduction step costs what it builds](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md) lets live the whole declaration. Check: the three charges above retaken, the second gone; `/std`'s units; the audit `std_memo_census`. Size: unknown until then.

- **Uncertain: `growth_slopes` can refuse a held growth on one run's noise.** Over the compiler of `e983a9a82`, parentheses' lowering grew 3.01 by instructions in one run of two, past `HELD_SLOPE`, and under 2.0 in the other, its allocations and bytes reading 2.00 in both (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/growth.rs`). The test states the cause it guards against: the instructions a run's drawn hash keys move in a stage that grows little. Not known: how far a held row's instruction slope moves between runs. Settled by: the held rows' slopes over ten runs, counted. Fix, if they straddle the limit: each held row's instructions taken as the least of several counts at each size, which a run's keys can only raise. Check: ten runs pass. Size: small.

- **`FUSION_CAP`'s census names a grain that is gone, and no commit.** `FUSION_CAP`'s rustdoc (`curios-core/src/free_monoid.rs`) chooses the cap of 64 from a census of the concatenations `normalize_concat`'s two `merge` closures fuse: 207 in the fixed prelude, "all bit-grain", and 210 in the cross-stage corpus, "207 bit-grain, 3 `List`, 0 byte-grain", beside an accumulation reproducer at n = 800 whose program it does not name. The bit grain is gone since the commit that removed it, so no retake can give those readings, and none names the commit it was taken at or that it is counted. The argument may still hold — it needs only that what the corpus fuses is a few generators — but its figures are not ones a reader can retake. Fix: the census taken again, each fusing concatenation counted by carrier with its largest operand, through a note in each `merge` closure of a scratch copy, over a prelude build (elaboration and certification of `/sys` and `/std`), over `curios`'s tests and over the reproducer alone; the table rewritten with the readings, their commit and `counted`, and the protocol stated precisely enough to rebuild the note, the reproducer's program named. Check: the table's figures stated with their commit; `cargo xtask docs build`. Size: one commit.

- **`curios-unit`'s README rejects the constraint positions the code keeps.** Its stored-form decision ([A stored unit says what its items mean, not where they were written or how they were come by](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-stored-unit-says-what-its-items-mean-not-where-they-were-written-or-how-they-were-come-by)) rejects "a universe constraint that keeps its position in memory and loses it in a slot, or takes the position of the use that instantiated its scheme", since "an inconsistency would be located by how its unit was compiled". The code does both. A declaration's own constraints keep their extents while its unit is in hand and lose them where it is consed (`UniverseContext::unplaced`, `curios_core::Item::shared`): `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert`'s group, compiled in a process, holds 6 of its 8 constraints placed, and restored from a store none. And since `ac663f240` a scheme's constraint restated at a use takes the extent of the occurrence that instantiated it (the instantiation loop of `curios-elab/src/universe_solver.rs`). Neither locates a report by how a unit was compiled, since a restated constraint never carries its scheme's own position, so the sentence is what is stale. Fix: the rejection restated as the rule the code holds — a constraint is placed where the elaboration reading it raised it, its own declaration's while that declaration elaborates and an occurrence's where a scheme is restated, and a slot holds none — with the reason it no longer has dropped. Check: `cargo xtask docs build`. Size: one commit.
