# Compilation: findings

- **A committed term names a recursive group by search, and two answers of the search are no declaration's part.** `named_members` (`curios-elab/src/denoise.rs`) spells a stuck call headed by a top-level group by its member's name, found by asking which names the group goes by (`Context::member_names`): a search of the groups the unit and its scope define, by the group itself. A group that is found is noted as a read of each member's body, so the trace of the declaration that asked holds it. Two answers follow no one declaration. Where two declarations define one group, the unit's own are filed by group (`Frames::members`), each definition writing over the one before it, so the names answered are those of the one elaborated last, and which that is follows the order the walk asked for them in. And where no declaration defines the group, a local `rec`'s or an instance another declaration opened, the answer is an absence that a later text fills by declaring that group at the top level, and no trace of a kept declaration is asked it again. Either way the committed term spells the same definition, so no judgment moves; what moves is the spelling a unit stores, and with it whether a unit compiled over a baseline is stored as one compiled whole. Neither is seen in `/std`: at `7d28c0209` no two of its definitions that call themselves are one recursion, their printed bodies compared with each one's own name set aside and mutual groups not compared, and the second answer waits on a later text, which a compile of one text never meets. Fix: recognize a group by reduction's own record of the name it opened it from, as an instance of a universe-polymorphic one is recognized already (`Context::instance_of`), in place of the search. Check: a unit declaring one group under two names, and a third declaration committing a stuck call to it, stores the same bytes whichever of the two is asked for first. Size: medium.

- **The closed evaluator unrolls the library's write loop a level a round, and hands the next stage thirty times what it keeps.** `/std/Handle`'s `write_waiting` is a description that calls itself from its own continuation, on a short write and after a poll, and its copy fits the cap a description is copied under (`DESCRIPTION_COPY_NODE_LIMIT`, `curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/budget.rs`), so each round of `evaluate_closed_terms` folds the closed call inside the last round's copy into another copy, for all eight rounds. Counted on top of `7ded6ac34` through the release compiler: `programs/hello_world.crs`, one `print`, has 638 functions at `ersd-optm`, 209 of them `io/bind`, 144 `/std/Handle/write_waiting` and 68 `/std/Handle/poll`; a program that reads once and prints twice has 1,250, and `programs/monad_io.crs` 1,934. Read as text, hello world is 257,115 bytes at `ersd`, 757,332 at `cont` and 23,203 at `cont-optm`, where eight lines name the loop as at `ersd`: the copies are deleted by the stage they are handed to. The rule that keeps a recursive value's dictionary from being unrolled (`ReifyScope::escapes_knot`, `curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/reify.rs`) reads a knot's initializer and not a function that calls itself, and `curios`'s `a_constant_prints_descriptions_are_copied_within_the_evaluators_cap` holds the loop under the cap so that a constant `print` folds. Check: hello world's function count at `ersd-optm`, and the three collapses of `curios`'s `tests::fmt`. Size: medium.
- **`split_windows` virtualizes one region a call, and each region costs two rounds of every pass.** `split_windows` (`curios-cont/src/optimize/fields.rs`) rewrites the widest region it admits and returns, and the fixpoint tries it only in a round nothing else changed (`optimize`, `curios-cont/src/optimize.rs`), so a module's regions are taken a round apart, with the round between spent on `fold_intrinsic_identities` folding what the split minted. `split_parameters`, beside it, states what the pass departs from: one split a call leaves convergence to a round of every pass per split, where a sweep applies what one snapshot admits. Counted on top of `653af23d2` through the release compiler, a line printed at each round's close for the runs: twenty-four of the corpus's twenty-six programs hold four to six regions and settle in 15 to 20 rounds, eight to twelve of them these, `programs/lcg/lcg.crs` its four over rounds 8 to 15 of 16; `programs/hello_world.crs` and `programs/dependent_vectors.crs` hold none and settle in 6; and the census's `TOML_DRIVER` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/census.rs`) holds six, rounds 31 to 42 of its 43. With the pass's one step looped until it admits none, the widest searched again on the module as each split leaves it, every one of those twenty-seven programs prints at `cont-optm` and at `wasm-optm` the bytes it printed, the twenty-four settle in 9 or 10 rounds and the driver in 33, and `curios-cont`'s tests pass. Size: small. The five other passes that take one candidate a call are `214-a-pass-takes-what-one-snapshot-admits.md`'s.
