# Compilation: findings

- **Uncertain: a call's frame weight counts every value its callee's body binds, though the emitter folds them onto the locals their live ranges allow, so a recursion runs on the heap sooner than its frame needs.** [A recursion is as deep as the heap allows](../../design/compilation/a-recursion-is-as-deep-as-the-heap-allows.md) weighs a call "a fixed overhead plus one for each value its callee's body binds", a weight that "stands in for its bytes" in code Binaryen has not optimized, while [Locals are allocated by liveness](../../../curios-emit/README.md#locals-are-allocated-by-liveness) folds a function's values onto "the fewest its values' live ranges allow", so a body whose arms bind values no other arm holds weighs their sum and holds about their largest. Counted at `5bccb9ebd` in `spines`' optimized module: `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert` weighs 136 and declares 49 locals beside its parameters, and `remove` weighs 74, so thirty of an insert's levels run natively under `FRAME_BUDGET` (`curios-cps/src/frames.rs`); the one recursion seen on the heap early was a prototype over a fork of sixteen fields, which [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects, weighing 1,024, the cap a quarter of the budget sets, so that the fifth of its levels ran its heap twin. Not yet known: whether a recursion `/std` holds runs on the heap where its folded frame would not, which a count of each recursive function's weight against its emitted locals settles, and whether the weight can be read after the folding it stands in for, `bound_frames` running in the continuation IR before the emitter folds. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: an inlined or copied callee is weighed by its nodes, a `Nat` operation one of them, though its emitted form carries a fallback many times its size.** `MULTI_SITE_INLINE_LIMIT` (`curios-cps/src/optimize.rs`) counts a callee's live nodes, and each `Nat` operation is one node, emitted as an `i31` fast path beside a call into the big-number helpers. Counted at `8ed961f9c` on a prototype over a fork of sixteen fields, which [`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api) rejects: its insert, each arm calling two small helpers, was 72,839 lines at `wasm-optm`, each arm holding its own copy of the helpers' digit reads with their fallbacks, and its program spent 14.5 s in `emit` against 1.1 s over the map of that commit. At `5bccb9ebd` the landed `/std/Map/internal/Node/insert` is 4,438 lines of `spines`' optimized module, which `emit`s in 1.2 s. Not yet known: whether any program of `/std` or the corpus pays for the weighing, and which pass makes each copy; settled by counting, over `/std`'s test program and the corpus, the emitted bytes each inlined or copied callee adds against the node weight that admitted it. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: the rejection of a constructor context's destination gives a rope's reason, and it also holds out a row.** [A recursion closed by a monoid is a loop](../../../curios-anf/README.md#a-recursion-closed-by-a-monoid-is-a-loop) rejects "a destination with a hole, OCaml's tail modulo constructor and Koka's constructor contexts" because "a rope node states its length where it is built, and that length is the part not yet built"; a row states no length, and under [the rebuild in place](../../../curios-cps/README.md#a-construction-beside-a-dying-value-of-its-row-is-rebuilt-in-place) its hole is the slot a descent writes. A descent that rebuilds one slot a level, as a trie's insert does, keeps a frame, a guard and a return a level where a destination would make it a loop that writes once. Not yet known: whether a program pays enough for those frames to want the loop, `/std/Map`'s descent being about five levels deep over 65,537 keys ([`Map` is a trie forking sixteen ways, and its shape is its API](../../../curios-prelude/README.md#map-is-a-trie-forking-sixteen-ways-and-its-shape-is-its-api)), and whether rows are held out for a reason the paragraph does not state. One reason the peers do not meet is a row's own: OCaml's tail modulo constructor fills a placeholder in a block every field of which is mutable underneath, and Koka writes a constructor context into memory it owns, where `curios-emit` declares a slot of a row's struct mutable only where some rebuild writes it, every other slot immutable so an engine reads it as the constant its construction stored (`table.rs`), so a hole makes its slot mutable for every construction of the row, and every read of that slot no constant to the engine; and a struct's slots are filled at `struct.new`, so a hole in a slot of a type no null inhabits takes a filler of that type. Settled by timing such a descent as written and as a hand-written loop in a scratch copy, with the slot the loop writes declared mutable in both, then restating the paragraph's reason or reopening the destination for rows. Size: unknown.
- **A dead call of a total recursion stays at run time wherever the recursion becomes a loop before its claim is discharged.** `droppable_functions` (`curios-anf/src/summary.rs`) gives a certified total function a claim, droppable given what it applies of what it was handed, and a call goes only where it hands each such parameter a known function droppable at every call (`Module::may_drop_call`, `curios-cps/src/module.rs`). The optimizer makes a recursion called from one place a local continuation loop, and a continuation carries no claim, so once the loop is made nothing drops it, however dead its result: a walk over a list that applies a function it is handed, in a `let` nothing reads, stays in the optimized continuation IR as a loop that only walks, its exits resuming with nothing, where the call it was is handed a known closure. A walk that applies a field of what it is handed gets no claim at all, the summary recording an application only of a parameter itself, so `/std/Map/internal/Node/first_in`, which applies its `Key`'s `hash`, is never droppable; a `let a = first_in(l)` only the proofs of `insert` and `remove` read walked to the leftmost leaf, and hashed its key, at every level of the descent, until the node spelled `first_in(l)` only where proofs read it. Fix: the loop a function's recursion becomes carries that function's certified totality as its termination, and a loop whose result is dead goes where its body, as the optimizer has left it, does nothing a run observes — every call in it droppable, no host row, no write — so `first_in`'s loop goes once its key's encoding is resolved and dropped, which no claim stated at the function could say. LLVM splits it the same way: `mustprogress` states a loop's termination and is carried from a function to its loops, a mark a pass that cannot keep may drop since only its absence is ever assumed, and `LoopDeletion` judges the loop's side effects itself. Check: the walk above gone from the optimized continuation IR, through a field and through a parameter alike; a loop over a partial function's recursion kept; a loop whose body calls something kept. Size: one commit.
- **A rebuild of a value with no tag that a constant may be is refused, though which constant may reach it is known.** A rebuild of a value constant data may be is admitted only under a test of its tag that rules every constant's out (**Constants by tag**, `curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`), and a constant reaches a reuse version as any unique value does. A row with no tag — a product schema, a tuple — has no test to stand under, so a function handed a constant one at one call and a value nothing else holds at another rebuilds it at neither: `rebuild::tests`' `a_value_a_constant_may_be_is_rebuilt_only_under_a_test_of_its_tag` holds the untested case at none, and by the same rule a rebuild of the `Map` `/std/Map/insert` hands back, a product with no tag, stands nowhere `Map/empty()` may reach, which a program filling a map from empty is. A constant is one global every use shares, and the constants that may reach a value are the facts' already (`constant_tags`), so where no tag rules them out, a rebuild could test the value's identity against each of them and copy where it is one: the one place static uniqueness is unsure, answered at run time as Perceus and Lean answer every write, by a test, and nowhere else. How the test names a constant bound outside its scope, read in the tree on 2026-11-18: `curios-emit` interns every binding of equal constant data into one global (`hoist_consts`, held by `tests::hoist`'s `constant_tuples_hoist_to_one_interned_global`), and a function binds again at its entry each constant it reads and binds nowhere in itself (`bind_constants_again`, `curios-emit/src/machine.rs`), so a rebuild names a constant in scope by its value and mints an equal construction where none is, each the one global the constant is, and `ref.eq` against it is identity. Not yet known: what the test costs beside the rebuild it keeps, a `ref.eq` a constant that may reach it; and which rebuilds `/std` refuses for it, counted over `cargo xtask profile prelude`. Check: the untested `bump` rebuilt under the identity test, its constant call copying; the count of `Map` rebuilds in a program filling a map from empty, before and after; differential answers unchanged. Size: unknown.
- **A search that tests the head it reads on every round, contified into its caller, leaves the element it answers shared, so `remove` cannot answer `Node/lone`.** `/std/Map/internal/Node/lone`, which a remove that leaves one twig collapses its branch to, takes its list apart as `[h, ..r]` and tests each head's tag; contified into `remove`, its window is virtualized into a loop over `(base, end, length)` reading `h = List/get(base, end - length)` each round, and a remove answering `lone(l2)` copies its node and its twig list at three levels each (`map_remove_rebuilds_its_node_at_every_level_in_a_loop_using_its_map_once`, `a_maps_descent_writes_each_levels_twigs_in_place`, read at `5fe814608`). `pick`, whose read sinks into the arm that answers and leaves the loop, keeps every rebuild, so `remove` reads `pick(l2, filled_at(l2))` through `picked_lone`, a search that computes an index and reads once. Ruled out by reading the module: the read on a later round (`Shape::read_again`), since the loop's head rebinds `base`; a window's range, since the virtualized window takes no `ListRest`; and the constant empty node `List/set` writes at the removed index, since constant data counts as unshared. The likely fact, read in the solver's sources: a borrowed parameter is a source of sharing, "never the one name an object goes by" (`sources`, `curios-cps/src/sharing.rs`), and the loop only reads `base`, so `base` is borrowed and `h`, read out of it, is shared, though the list handed in at the loop's entry is unique and dead once the loop is left — the case the module's **Borrowing** paragraph states for the caller's value, "a read-only walk the optimizer made a loop leaves its caller's value unique for the rebuild after it", and not for a part the loop hands back out. A fix that reading suggests: a part read out of a borrowed parameter judged against what each entry hands that parameter, unique where each is and is consumed after the loop only as the part rule lets a whole be. Not yet known: why `pick`, whose loop reads its list the same way, keeps every rebuild, which this reading does not explain; and so whether it is the fact, which a trace of the shared solver's sources over the remove fixture settles. Check: the two tests above over `lone(l2)`, `filled_at` and `picked_lone` deleted. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: a call's freedom from effects crosses into the continuation IR as a claim every rewrite must keep true, though the IR states what each of its nodes observes and a dead loop is now judged on its body.** A function crosses as `Droppable`, the certifier's termination met with `curios-anf`'s effect summary (`droppable_functions`, `curios-anf/src/summary.rs`), and every pass that copies, substitutes or restates what a call does maps or restates that claim — `Droppable::mapped`, `rewrite_atoms`, `shrink`'s census of what claims name, `uncurry_returns` and `uncurry_nests`, the inliner and the cloner — about forty-seven sites of `curios-cps` outside its tests. One reader of the claim stands above the crossing: `curios-anf`'s rebase (`rebase_one`, `curios-anf/src/optimize/rebase.rs`) leaves a recursion closed by a deciding first operand, `a && f(t)`, as written where the claim says it may be dropped, since placement in the IR then runs it as a tail call only where the first operand does not decide — a decision made in one stage on what the next reads, so a reading of the IR's bodies that refused such a recursion would leave it neither rebased nor placed, a loop lost rather than an answer wrong. Termination is the one half the code cannot answer, which the checkers prove; the other is in the IR already, each intrinsic's `IntrinsicEffect` and each cell, channel, host row and closure call a node of its own, which `eliminate_dead_loops` reads a loop's body by, termination alone carried (`Function::terminates`, `Continuation::terminates`). Read so for every call, bottom-up over the call graph and over the values a body applies of what it was handed, the claim would be the code's at each round and nothing would map it. LLVM splits it that way: the frontend states `mustprogress`, and `FunctionAttrs` infers a function's memory effects and `willreturn` from its IR, as Flambda reads its primitives' effects in its own. [An unread call is dropped by what it hands](../../design/compilation/an-unread-call-is-dropped-by-what-it-hands.md) rejects "CPS deriving purity again after inlining" as a second lattice the two stages' summaries would drift apart in; `curios-anf`'s summary stays whatever this decides, `prune`, `invert` and the eager verifier reading it, so the question is the crossing alone, and the dead loop already reads a body in the IR. Not yet known: which calls of `/std`'s test program and the corpus the claims admit that a reading of the bodies would refuse — a group tied through cells, whose initializer the ANF verifier holds to performing nothing, is read in the IR through a forcing function that polls a cell and takes from a channel (`lower_knot`, `curios-anf/src/into_cps/lowerer.rs`), and a recursive witness may be such a group — and which the reverse, a walk applying a field of what it is handed among them, counted over both at `cps-optm`; and what a fixpoint over the call graph costs a round. Size: unknown.
