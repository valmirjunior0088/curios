# Compilation: findings

- **Contify and sink each bind a continuation to keep a switch edge off a function's return, which the machine lowering lowers.** `contify_call` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/contify.rs`) binds a `return_bridge` continuation in every contified function, its body and scope two of the three nodes `CONTIFICATION_NODE_OVERHEAD` charges each loop-entry copy, so that the callee's switch edges to its return land in a continuation rather than on the call's, which may be the caller's own return; and `sink_computations` (`curios-cps/src/optimize/sink.rs`) binds an `arm` continuation where the operation it carves handed its result straight to the function's return. A switch edge to the return lands in a block of its own that returns (`lower_branch`, `curios-emit/src/machine.rs`), so both passes can retarget it as they retarget a jump. Fix: retarget a switch edge as a jump in both, drop the bridge from contify's scaffolding and its overhead to the one initial transfer, and restate `contifies_a_single_entry_tail_loop_and_bridges_switch_returns` and the extents the contify and specialize suites charge. Check: `curios-cps`'s suite and the corpus, `curios-prelude`'s tests. One commit.
