# Tools: findings

- **`wonder --name` does not say whether the certifier found a definition total.** The declaration query (`curios-wonder/src/named.rs`, `described`) answers what a declaration is declared as, its type, its members, its witnesses, where it stands and its text, and nothing of the record the certifier made for it, though what becomes of the definition's code turns on that record: a function of a total definition is marked total below Core (`curios_anf::Function::total`), and an unread call of it may be dropped (`curios_cps::Droppable`). So a program cannot ask why an unread computation in it is kept: in `let execute() -> Try(Io, Str, {}) = let arguments = Proc/args()!; let unused = List/fold(arguments, 0, (x, a) => a + 30019); …`, the unread fold is dropped from the optimized CPS (`wonder --stage cps-optm -`) where `execute` prints nothing and kept where it prints through `/std/print` or `Fmt/print`, which `execute` being recorded `Partial` would explain and no query confirms. Fix: `--name` reports a definition's verdict, `Total` or `Partial`, read off the unit that declares it (`Unit::certification`, `Certification::totality`). Check: a `curios-wonder` test that `--name` reports `Total` of a definition whose recursion descends and `Partial` of one whose recursion does not. One commit.
