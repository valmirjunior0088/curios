# Tools: findings

- **No fixture makes a rung panic after `wonder stage` handed one on.** `stage` (`curios-wonder/src/stage.rs`) hands the asked rung to its asker where the driver emits it, so a panic in a later rung leaves it answered, and its tests hold the hand-off against a later refusal alone: no program is known that makes a later rung panic, and the query has no seam to put one in. Fix: a fixture over a program whose emission panics, once one is known, asserting the earlier rung on standard output. Size: quick.
- **Three measurements record a reading that names no commit.** A recorded figure names the commit it was taken at, so a reader can tell which code it prices. The reading beside each of these does not: `launcher_guard_positive_control` (`curios/src/bundle/tests.rs`), the first reading of `string_walk_ladder_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/ladder.rs`), and the `user` figure that closes `split_return_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/structural/measurement_tests.rs`), retaken as `copy_growth_measurements` beside it says the pass is withheld. Fix: each is retaken and names its commit. The ladder's and the split return's are timed by hand, outside their tests, over native binaries; `launcher_guard_positive_control` is counted, the figures on record a macOS build's, and its failing image is a second build of the launcher. Check: every recorded reading names a commit. Size: a quiet machine for the two timed readings and a build of the failing launcher for the third.
- **The coordination probes do not reach their scripted terminal resizes.** `listing_host` and `assert_listing` (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/test_support.rs`), shared by `coordination_measurements` and `host_boundary_measurements`, supply 100 down/up pairs followed by `q` and require three screen clears. Replaying that script at `eb40b82e9` and `e9af482cc` produces one clear: `/std/Tui`'s size ticker sleeps for 100 ms, while `MockHost::wait` advances synthetic timers only when no watched input is ready, so the session quits before the ticker queries either scripted resize. Fix: give the script an idle interval or explicit resize events, retain the resize assertions, then retake both owning records. Check: both ignored measurement tests complete and observe the two resizes and session restoration. Size: a small host-script change and a quiet measurement sitting.
- **Two profiling probes disclaim the counting allocator their dependency installs.** The reproduction prose of `coordination_measurements` and `host_boundary_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/{coordination,host_boundary}.rs`) says their test binary has no counting allocator and folded memory columns are zero. `curios-profile` installs its global allocator under `enabled`, which the probes' `profile` feature enables; the folded stream carries nonzero allocation counters. Fix: correct the current-instrumentation prose against `curios-profile/README.md`; keep dated readings tied to their measured revisions. Check: the descriptions agree with the allocator installed by the feature and the stream they fold. Size: quick.
