# Tools: findings

- **Uncertain: a manifest states its repeated declarations in two shapes and two numbers.** `curios-package`'s manifest (`Package::dependencies`, `Package::executables`, `Package::foreign`, `Umbrella::catalog`, `curios-package/src/manifest.rs`) reads an executable and a foreign provider as rows of an array of tables, `[[executables]]` named in the plural and `[[foreign]]` in the singular, and a dependency and an umbrella's catalog pin as entries of a table keyed by package name, `[dependencies]` and `[catalog]`, as a row's `[foreign.answers]` and `[foreign.browser.static]` are keyed tables; `members` is an array of paths ([The manifest](../../usage.md#the-manifest)). So a writer learns two spellings of one kind of declaration, and one misremembered is refused as an unknown key. Seen: nothing states which shape or number a repeated declaration takes, and the peers part both ways — Cargo keys `[dependencies]` by name and names a row by what one is, `[[bin]]` and `[[example]]` ([Cargo Targets](https://doc.rust-lang.org/cargo/reference/cargo-targets.html)), where TOML's own example names its rows in the plural, `[[products]]` ([TOML 1.0, Array of Tables](https://toml.io/en/v1.0.0#array-of-tables)). Not known: the convention every repeated declaration follows — rows named by what one row is, as `[[dependency]]` and `[[executable]]` would be, where a repeated name needs a refusal of its own; or tables keyed by the name an entry is told apart by, where TOML itself refuses a key written twice, so the grammar holds the uniqueness a row has to be checked for; or rows where an entry has no key and keyed tables where it has one — with what each costs `curios pin`, which writes a dependency's `hash` at its key (`curios-package/src/pin.rs`), and a refusal, which stands at the row or key it is about. Settled by surveying how the peers' manifests state a repeated declaration and why, and reading each shape against what the manifest's refusals and `curios pin` stand on; it ends as a convention stated in `curios-package`'s README and a ready entry applying it to every key, or a specification where that takes three commits. Size unknown.
- **Uncertain: `.curios/` lays out one store as nine entries side by side, a unit's slot in two of them.** [Where things go](../../usage.md#where-things-go) lists `native/<package>/<name>` and `browser/<package>/<name>/`, what `compile` emits, named by target at the top beside `documentation/<package>/`, while `payloads/<target>/<slot>` puts the target under the kind; a unit's slot is kept twice, its record and index at `verdicts/<slot>` and its packs at `objects/<slot>/<pack>`, one address in two trees, though [A slot's objects are kept in packs beside it, at its address](../../../curios-verdict/README.md#a-slots-objects-are-kept-in-packs-beside-it-at-its-address) has the packs beside the slot; and the half `CURIOS_CACHE` moves (`sources`, `foreign`, `verdicts`, `objects`, `payloads`, `compiler`) stands interleaved with the half that always stays (`native`, `browser`, `documentation`), told apart by the reference's table alone ([The store goes where a manifest governs, and nowhere it was not pointed](../../../curios-package/README.md#the-store-goes-where-a-manifest-governs-and-nowhere-it-was-not-pointed)). Not known: whether a slot can be one directory holding its record, its index and its packs without moving what its decision rests on — a filing that writes its pack and then renames the slot into place, a collection that finds what no slot names, a restore that probes one address; whether what a command emits nests by its kind and its target, `executables/<target>/<package>/<name>` beside the documentation; whether the two halves are two directories, so the variable moves one whole; and, past those, whether `.curios/` as a whole is laid out as one rule would lay it out, which may mean a rework of the store's layout throughout. Settled by an investigation of the whole of `.curios/` against the decisions that shape it — the store's place, the slot and its packs, collection, the payload family, `curios pin`'s staging — and against how the peers lay out what a build keeps and what it emits, as Cargo's `target/`, Zig's cache and its install prefix, Lake's `.lake` and Go's build cache do; it reports a layout and the moves to it, and ends as a specification of the restructuring, a ready entry where it takes two commits, or dropped where the layout holds. Size unknown.
- **Uncertain: what a file writes is read off its subject's whole lowering on every check.** `PreparedText::texts` (`curios-module/src/into_core/mention.rs`), which both transports read a file's texts by, took 9 ms of each check's 21 ms of stating its answers (`placed`) over `curios-prelude/std/Nat.crs` in a copy of the `std` package at `289149bbe`, timed by spans added for the reading in `curios-wonder`'s README keystroke scenario, one session of ten keystrokes; a check has paid it since its answers were read off the lowering. Seen: it builds the unit's module bodies over every declaration's record (`Bodies::of`, once a lowering), and a recompile makes a new lowering each keystroke, so a check of one file reads the library's. Not yet known: whether `Bodies::of` is the cost, or the canonical spelling of each module's file `lies` asks for, or reading the restored half's records; settled by spans inside `texts` over the same scenario. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: a name answers no walk it came in by.** A bare name a `use` brought in answers the path it is exposed at and the one it is declared at, and the `use` line answers its own names where it is written (`curios_core::Imports::lines`), but nothing answers which `use` line a name in a declaration was reached through, as Agda's "why in scope" does. Seen: the lowering knows the `use` a bare name resolved through where it resolves it (`Context::note_binding_use`, `curios-module/src/into_core/context.rs`) and keeps none of it with the declaration. Not yet known: whether a reader asks it, a name's exposed path saying already which module it came from; settled by a consumer that asks. Size: one record beside what the lowering mints, and one fact in a place's answer.
- **Uncertain: a body's `use` lines are held to no shape.** Nothing reports two groups of one body that say what one says, or a `use` standing where the library writes none ([A lint is an exact finding read off the compilation](../../design/tools/a-lint-is-an-exact-finding-read-off-the-compilation.md)). Seen, counted over `curios-prelude/std` and `programs/` at `d8f53c21c`: every plain `use`, 560, stands before its body's first declaration; every `pub use` stands there, 41, after the `mod` line whose module its path reads, 173, or right after the family or the concept whose namespace it re-exports, 51; no two groups of one body write one path at one visibility; 274 side-by-side pairs share only a root, `pub use /sys/{let Bool};` beside `pub use /sys/Bool/{True, False, xor, eql, Holds};`, which no group joins, a group's members being names ([*Imports and re-exports*](../../language.md#imports-and-re-exports)); and one pairs a `use` with a `pub use` of one path, an import and a re-export (`curios-prelude/std/Str.crs:6`). Probed at `5d2f73550`: an item that reads a name a later `use` imports is refused, the name not in scope, and so is a body that declares a name a `use` imports, wherever the two stand; so a plain `use` moved earlier — into its body's header, or onto an earlier group of its path — changes nothing a program the compiler accepts means, its path's head standing after the line that binds it. A `pub use` is placed by where it stands as well: its names are documented on its module's page there (`document_unit`, `curios-module/src/into_core.rs`), so two of one path apart may be two places on a page, and join only side by side. Not yet known: whether the rules — a plain `use` in its body's header, a `pub use` in one of the three places the library writes one, and two groups of one path and one visibility joined — are lints of a group of the written form, stated in `curios-surface` beside the formatter as functions of a body's items and placed by the lowering, which reads each body's items with their places and keeps its lints by body as it keeps `unused-import`'s, so every report reads them; or the formatter's, which can join two groups side by side on any text it parses and can move none across an item without the compiler's word. Size: the group's place in the lowering, three rules and their tests, and the lint decision restated to hold a group of the written form.
- **Uncertain: a lint's identity is one closed enum of the bottom crate, and no report or diagnostic names it.** `LintKind` (`curios-core/src/lint.rs`) names every kind of every stage, `unused-dependency` among them, which only `curios-wonder` decides, and nothing in production reads a kind but its name (`LintKind::name`); it is archived with every unit's lints, so a lint added to any stage edits the crate every stage builds on, rebuilds the workspace and moves the stored format, and what its closedness buys, one list of the names, no test holds `documentation/usage.md`'s lint table to. A lint's block opens on its words and closes on `[lint]` ([A command narrates on one transcript and reports in blocks](../../design/tools/a-command-narrates-on-one-transcript-and-reports-in-blocks.md)), and the server's diagnostic holds its message alone (`curios-wonder/src/server.rs`), so the name the table documents a lint by is shown nowhere: not as the protocol's `code`, nor its row as `codeDescription`, nor the unused family under the `Unnecessary` tag an editor fades code by ([LSP 3.17](https://microsoft.github.io/language-server-protocol/specifications/lsp/3.17/specification/)). Peers write a lint's identity on its report's line: Biome `lint/correctness/noUnusedImports`, GHC `[-Wunused-imports]`, ESLint the rule's id, Ruff `F401`. Not yet known: whether each group names its kinds beside its rules, the archive storing the name and a test holding the table to every group's kinds, each name unique across the groups, or the enum gains a level per group; whether the name joins the tag as a word, `[lint unused-import]`, a tag's words being one to three and `/`, `:` and `|` each holding a meaning in the language, rather than the tag naming the stage that decided a lint, which it is the same whichever; and what `codeDescription` links to, the table being a file of the repository. Size: the vocabulary and its seven constructions, the archive, the table's test, the report's tag and the server's diagnostic.
- **Uncertain: the server answers less than a check's records hold.** It advertises formatting, definition, hover and a document's symbols beside its diagnostics (`curios-wonder/src/server.rs`), and the record a check leaves of a file (`Placed`, `curios-wonder/src/named.rs`) holds what more of the protocol asks, with nothing compiled at the request: every term's range, nested, which `selectionRange` expands a selection along; the type each written binder was opened at, which `inlayHint` writes beside a binder written without one; a concept's witnesses, which `implementation` answers (`Described::witnesses`); where a `mod` line declares a module, which `documentLink` links a `mod` or a `use` line by; each text's range, which `foldingRange` folds; and the fold's progress, which `wonder` narrates and `$/progress` would report while a check runs. Seen: semantic tokens, completion and the places a declaration is written are tools/303's, 304's and 305's, and a lint's name in a diagnostic is the finding of a lint's identity's. Not yet known: which of them a reader in an editor asks for, and whether an inlay hint's type is the one spelled where the binder is written; and what the server would need for a quick fix (`codeAction`), a lint's way out being prose and no edit, for a call's signature (`signatureHelp`), and for the workspace's symbols, each left out until a consumer asks. Size: a handler and a test each, over the records as they stand.
