# Tools: findings

- **The retained reading at a release is not the release's.** [A reading records what it was taken under, and nothing derived from it](../../../xbench/README.md) has a reading carry the compiler it is about; `xbench/src/reading_03.rs`, the newest, names `curios 0.16.12 (a819aab3e)`, two commits before the bump that cut `release/0.16.13`, so a consumer that pins that release, as the paper does, shows figures of a compiler the release is not and spends a sentence saying so. The fix: take a release's reading in the commit exactly preceding its bump, so that the subject the reading names is the tagged commit's parent and the two differ by the version alone, under the sitting the README states. Its check: `git rev-parse --short release/<v>^` is the hash the newest reading's subject names. Size: one commit per release, the reading itself, landed before the bump.
