- **A stream is read through the fold's summary and `awk` alone, where a trace viewer would lay it out as a timeline.** `curios profile <STREAM>` prints the fold's summary (`fold`, `curios-profile/src/fold.rs`), and a question it does not answer is asked of the rows by hand, as `curios-profile`'s README does with `awk`; the rows hold what a timeline needs — each span's entry and exit with its time, its fields and the allocator's readings, and each event (`curios-profile/src/trace.rs`). Check: a `curios-profile` test writing a stream of nested spans, an event and an open span, its events in order and paired; `documentation/usage.md`'s `profile` row states the flag. Size: one commit.

- **An operator form drops the proof its node holds, so a report prints a division the compiler refuses when it is pasted.** `intrinsic_form` (`curios-surface/src/spelling.rs`) writes `NatDiv`, `NatRem` and `IntDiv` as `/` and `%` and `IntRem` as `Int/rem(a, b)`, each without its proof of a nonzero divisor, and nothing proves it again where the reader stands, where a method projected off a dictionary is written as its operator only where the procedure that proves a bound from the facts in scope proves each dropped proof again (`proved_again`, `curios-elab/src/written.rs`). Fix: an intrinsic's operator is written without its proof only where `proved_again` proves the proof again, as a method's is, and otherwise as the call with its proof, `Div/div(a, b, @proof)`. Check: a test of the report over that program, its division written as the call, and one where the divisor's bound is a fact in scope, written as the operator. Size: one commit.
