# Tools: findings

- **No fixture makes a rung panic after `wonder stage` handed one on.** `stage` (`curios-wonder/src/stage.rs`) hands the asked rung to its asker where the driver emits it, so a panic in a later rung leaves it answered, and its tests hold the hand-off against a later refusal alone: no program is known that makes a later rung panic, and the query has no seam to put one in. Fix: a fixture over a program whose emission panics, once one is known, asserting the earlier rung on standard output. Size: quick.
- **Four measurements record a reading that names no commit.** A recorded figure names the commit it was taken at, which is how a reader tells one the code has moved past, and twenty-seven measurements' readings do. The reading beside each of these does not: `launcher_guard_positive_control` (`curios/src/bundle/tests.rs`), the first reading of `string_walk_ladder_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/ladder.rs`), `fixpoint_pass_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/fixpoint.rs`), whose reading the next entry holds, and the `user` figure that closes `split_return_measurements` (`curios/src/tests/codegen/structural/measurement_tests.rs`), retaken as `copy_growth_measurements` beside it says the pass is withheld. Fix: each is retaken and names its commit. The ladder's and the split return's are timed by hand, outside their tests, over native binaries; `launcher_guard_positive_control` is counted, the figures on record a macOS build's, and its failing image is a second build of the launcher. Check: every recorded reading names a commit. Size: a quiet machine for the two, the next entry for the third, a build of the failing launcher for the fourth.
- **`fixpoint_pass_measurements` records a fixpoint of eleven rounds, and concludes from them what forty-three contradict.** Its reading on record (`curios/src/tests/instrumented/fixpoint.rs`) has `cont_optimize` at 634 ms of a 949 ms compile over 11 rounds, names no commit, and concludes that no pass sets the round count and that the cost left is inside passes. Taken in release with every feature on top of `7ded6ac34`, one reading alone on a quiet machine, it is 8,247 ms of 8,818 ms over 43 rounds, `cont::split_workers` 1,154 ms, `cont::inline_known_calls` 996 ms and `cont::uncurry_returns` 950 ms the largest lines. By the doc's own reading of its `fired` column, a pass whose `fired` tracks `rounds` being drained a round at a time, three passes set that count: `cont::specialize_jump_patterns` fires in twenty-eight of the rounds, `cont::uncurry_returns` in nineteen, and `cont::split_windows` in six with a round between each. `split_windows` sweeps since, which takes ten of the rounds, and the other two are `documentation/roadmap/compilation/214-a-pass-takes-what-one-snapshot-admits.md`'s; `coordination_measurements` beside it reads as its record does. Fix: the reading is retaken, timed as a median with its span and naming its commit, and its two conclusions are written again from the fired column. Check: the measurement's table. Size: a quiet machine.
- **A report shows a term as the compiler holds it where two binders of one concept reach a superclass by paths of one length.** Witness resolution takes the projection whose type meets the goal (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`, its second step), so inside a function with its own `use Key(J)`, written under a declaration's `use Key(K)`, a `Hash(J)` goal is answered by the inner binder. The translation reads a concept's edges as the concepts they reach, with no arguments (`FieldSpelling::Super`, `curios-core/src/print.rs`), and restores a superclass path only where it is the one of its length in scope (`restorable`, `curios-text/src/from_core.rs`); the projection it would otherwise have to write is of a hidden field with no label, so the whole term is declined. No `let` of `/std` holds such a term since `4fe2620dc`, where a solved metavariable keeps the name it was handed, so `curios-pipeline`'s sweep lists none. A program does: inside `pub let outer(@K: Type, use Key(K), k: K) -> Nat`, the local `let inner(@J: Type, use Key(J), j: J) -> Nat = let p: Eq()(Hash/hash(j), x[]) = Eq/refl(); 0;` reports its mismatch as `Eq(@Bytes)(((x).0).0(j), ((x).0).0(j))` under `a term above is shown as the compiler holds it`, where the same `inner` at the top level reads `Hash/hash(j)`, probed at `3258d8744`. Fix direction: an edge holds the arguments its concept is reached at, so `restorable` passes over a route to the concept at another argument as it passes over a binder of it (`apart`). Check: the program above reports `Hash/hash(j)`. Size: small; it changes what a unit's lexicon stores, and is tried in a copy of the tree before it is stated as the fix.
- **`documentation/usage.md` gives the language server as answering what `wonder` answers, and it answers one query of four.** `wonder`'s table has `server` as "the same questions over the language server protocol". `serve` (`curios-wonder/src/server.rs`) declares text synchronization and document formatting: it publishes a document's diagnostics, goals and lints, which is `diagnostics`, and formats it, which is `curios format`'s answer and no query of `wonder`; `tests`, `cost` and `stage` are asked of it by no request, and the request loop hands every method but `Formatting` to `reply_unhandled`. Fix: the row says what the server answers, a document's diagnostics as it is edited and its formatting. Check: by reading, against the capabilities `serve` declares. Size: quick.
