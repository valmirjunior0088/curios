# Every lint has one owner, which reads what elaboration typed

**The benefit.** A lint that reads a type reports what no resolution zero can, and the first two such findings are exact: an action bound and never run, which today compiles, runs and does nothing without a word, and a `_` binding `{}`, which lets a result that stops being `{}` pass silently. And one owner deciding every lint, from facts the stages that know them record, keeps each new lint one rule in one crate, where the four lints of today are already spread over four crates. The project has decided to adopt it: the owner and the record are the foundation, and each later lint is a rule over the record, and a fact where the record lacks one.

The next step is stage 1, a delivery. It waits on nothing.

## What is missing

**A lint reads no type.** [A lint is an exact finding read off the compilation](../../design/tools/a-lint-is-an-exact-finding-read-off-the-compilation.md) holds four lints, "and nothing else", each a zero of name resolution decided where names resolve, before anything is typed. A finding only a type states has nowhere to be decided. The first is a bug class the peers with lazy actions report and Curios cannot: probed at `9688a9e33`,

```text
use /std/{print};

let _ = print("never printed\n");
print("ran\n")
```

runs, prints `ran` and nothing else, and `wonder` and `lint` report nothing: an `Io` value bound to `_` is a description nothing runs.

**A lint is decided in four crates.** `curios-module`'s lowering decides `unused-import`, `unused-binder` and `unused-declaration` (`Lowerer::flush` and its siblings in `curios-module/src/into_core/lowerer.rs`) and holds the `Lint` type (`curios-module/src/lint.rs`); `curios-wonder` decides `unused-dependency` over the package (`curios-wonder/src/lint.rs`); `curios-pipeline` drops the `unused-binder` lints elaboration credited, at five call sites (`curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs` and `recompile.rs`, through `PreparedText::credit` and `credit_reused`); and `curios-unit` hands a reused declaration its baseline's credits (`credited_in`, `curios-unit/src/unit.rs`). A typed lint added the way the credit was would put a fifth crate's worth of lint logic inside elaboration, the shape in which lints grow across every owner of a run's state.

## What this builds on

- **The lowering already keeps a per-declaration record.** `flush` decides `unused-binder` from the written binders a declaration minted (`candidates`), the identities a reference resolved to (`used`), and whether a written goal stands anywhere in it (`saw_goal`); `unused-import` and `unused-declaration` read the selectors resolution went through and the unit's reachability (`PreparedText`'s `reachability`). Each is a zero over facts resolution collected, decided in the same breath.
- **Elaboration already records a fact for a lint, per declaration, and a recompile reuses it.** A proof the elaborator writes from the facts in scope reads written binders; `Credits` (`curios-elab/src/context/credits.rs`) keys each by its declaration and its place among the declaration's written binders (`LintedBinder`), and the trace a declaration files keeps them (`Made::credited`, `curios-elab/src/traces.rs`), so a compile that takes the declaration from its baseline credits it with nothing elaborated.
- **Every Core term carries where it was written** (`curios_core::Extent`, `curios-core/src/extent.rs`, identity-irrelevant): an anchor naming the declaration, the offsets in its text and the stamp of that text. A lint already stands at one, so a stored lint is placed in the text its file is read as now.
- **The elaborator can prove an arm is never taken.** `dead_guard` (`curios-elab/src/typing.rs`) reads, by reduction, an arm whose guard is always another case or contradicts an equation an enclosing arm put in force, and today words an error raised in such an arm; entailment refutes an empty proposition from the facts in scope (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`).

## What the peers do

Three shapes, by where a lint is detected:

- **Inside each phase, one registry for identity and output.** GHC warns from its renamer, typechecker and desugarer, OCaml numbers warnings its typer raises, Agda and Gleam warn as they check. A fact is free where it is computed, and every phase grows lint logic: the spread this specification removes.
- **One lint owner over a typed representation and a query interface.** rustc's lint passes run after the other analyses, early over the expanded AST and late over HIR with the typing results, all registered in one `LintStore` ([`rustc_lint`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/rustc_lint/)); Clippy is hundreds of lints in the same passes and no phase change. Kotlin's K2 reports diagnostics only in a checkers stage over fully resolved FIR ([`fir-basics.md`](https://pigweed.googlesource.com/third_party/github/JetBrains/kotlin/+/49b2ac1b100c5042a0e98582713938062efa6a31/docs/fir/fir-basics.md)). Go's analysis framework gives each analyzer the package's syntax and type information and lets it export facts that travel to the packages importing it ([`analysis.go`](https://go.googlesource.com/tools/+/HEAD/go/analysis/analysis.go)), as a stored unit carries its lints here. Even rustc keeps a back door for a fact one phase alone knows: its resolver buffers `unused_imports` and the early pass emits them ([`check_unused.rs`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/stable/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_resolve/check_unused.rs.html)).
- **A record a phase writes, which linters read.** Lean's linters run after each command over its syntax and the info trees elaboration left ([unused variables](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Linter/UnusedVariables.html)); Scalafix's semantic rules read SemanticDB, a record the compiler writes beside its output ([Scalafix](https://scalacenter.github.io/scalafix/docs/rules/overview.html)).

What is taken: one owner (rustc, Kotlin), facts recorded where they are known and decided elsewhere (rustc's buffer, Lean's info trees, SemanticDB), facts that travel with the unit (Go). What is set aside: detection in every phase (GHC); linting as a second type check, which typescript-eslint pays as "generally as slow as type checking that same project" ([typed linting](https://typescript-eslint.io/troubleshooting/typed-linting/performance)), where elaboration has decided the facts already; and heuristic rules that need options and ignore lists, as Lean's unused-variables linter carries, which the decision's exactness forbids.

## Settled

- **One owner, the crate `curios-lint`.** It decides every lint, the four of today among them, and holds `Lint`, its kinds, its reports and its order. It sits over `curios-module` and `curios-elab`, which do not depend on each other; `curios-pipeline` asks it for a unit's lints once the unit has elaborated, and `curios-wonder` for the package's.
- **A stage records, the owner decides.** The lowering records what resolution established of each declaration; elaboration records what it typed of each declaration's written sites; neither decides a lint or words one.
- **A site is keyed by where it was written.** A site a lint may be about — a written binder, a `_` among them — is named in the lowering's record by its extent, which elaboration reads off the Core term it lowered to; the owner joins the two records on it. The lowering binds values of its own with no name — the action a destructuring `!` takes apart, and what a statement `action!;` binds — so elaboration records a fact at every binding it meets with no name, and only one the lowering's record names as written is linted. A recompile over a baseline keeps a declaration's text, so its extents, and its facts with them.
- **Elaboration's facts live in the declaration's trace**, beside `credited` (`Made`), so a declaration taken from its baseline brings them, as it brings its credits.
- **A fact is decided or absent.** A type still a metavariable when the declaration closes, a classification the reduction allowance ran out on, a declaration elaboration refused: no fact, and no lint. The record holds, of a binding, its class — `{}`, an action, or nothing recorded. Exactness stands as the decision states it: nothing to configure, no suppression but changing the program, each message saying how.
- **A lint's question never changes a verdict.** What elaboration computes for the record runs under an allowance of its own, separate from the declaration's budget, rolls back what it solved, and credits nothing; running out is no fact. The allowance is a constant beside `DEFAULT_BUDGET`, the least power of ten above the most any binding of `/std`, `programs/` and the test corpus spends to be classified, measured in stage 2 and held by a test classifying them all within it. *What is known* shows why: a question asked under the declaration's budget spent it, and refused two declarations.
- **A lint is the same however its unit was reached.** A unit compiled now, one restored from the store, the prelude's units read from its archive and a declaration a recompile takes from its baseline report the same lints: the record lives in each declaration's trace and the decided lints are stored with the unit, so asking again costs nothing and no answer depends on what happened to be compiled. The owner runs wherever a unit is made — the prelude's build among them — and never under `lint` alone. A compiler with a lint an older one lacks never reads that one's units as complete, since the store addresses everything it holds by the compiler that wrote it (`.curios/compiler`). A unit read from Core text holds no lints, as today: no lowering made it. Rejected: reporting only what a run elaborated, whose answer moves with the store; and a `lint` that elaborates every unit it reports on again, which costs the closure at each ask and answers at another cost than the server.
- **Reported as today.** `wonder` reports a lint, the server publishes it as a warning, `run`, `compile` and `test` never mention it, and only `lint` turns it into an exit code ([`lint`](../../usage.md#lint)).

## The first lints

- **`unrun-action`: an action nothing runs.** A `let` whose whole pattern is `_`, or one name nothing reads, binding an action is a description the program never performs: run it with `!`, or delete it. An action is a value whose type reduces to `Io` (`curios_core::Intrinsic::IoType`), or to a struct of one field holding an action — `Try` over `Io` among them, the form `/std`'s fallible operations answer in — read off the type's structure, so no stage names a prelude declaration for it. A parameter is no such site: it is a slot the function's type asks for, and a name it leaves unread is `unused-binder`'s, given up to `_`. Nor is a part of a pattern, which takes apart a value built already. A binder nothing reads that holds an action is reported as this lint and not as `unused-binder`: one finding a site, the more precise one, which joins a fact of the lowering with one of elaboration. The report names the binding and says what it holds — "`x` is an action this program never runs" — and how to change it — "run it with `!`, or delete it". It is Rust's `#[must_use]` on futures ("futures do nothing unless you `.await`", [`must_use`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/attribute.must_use.html)), typescript-eslint's [`no-floating-promises`](https://typescript-eslint.io/rules/no-floating-promises) and GHC's `-Wwrong-do-bind` ([GHC-08838](https://errors.haskell.org/messages/GHC-08838/)). Rust exempts `let _ = future;` as a deliberate drop; here a description has no effect until it is run, so a bound one nothing runs is deleted with nothing lost. Rejected: every value of a type with a `Monad` witness, which reports a `List`, an `Option` or a `Result` bound to `_` — data, not a description; and the monad of the region a binding stands in, which a `let` with no `!` does not know: a `!` reads its region off the region's expected type where it stands (`elaborate_bang`), and carrying that type to every binding is a consumer's to argue for.
- **`unit-wildcard`: a `_` that binds `{}`.** Written `()`, the pattern checks the value is `{}`, so a result that stops being one is refused where `_` would discard whatever it became: Clippy's [`ignored_unit_patterns`](https://rust.googlesource.com/rust/+/28c53f2eef1c8b9ffe2f2fd7e9fac89f646ea0e2/clippy_lints/src/ignored_unit_patterns.rs). `()` is accepted wherever `_` binds `{}`, a lambda's parameter (`(()) => 1`) and a payload (`some(())`) among them, probed at `9688a9e33`. It is not GHC's `-Wunused-do-bind` ([GHC-81995](https://errors.haskell.org/messages/GHC-81995/)), which reports a result a `do` block discards: here the `{}` is bound by a written `_` and asked to be named. A site is a written `_` binding a value of `{}` in a `let`'s pattern, whole or part, a lambda's parameter, a payload or a tuple's component; a statement `action!;` binds nothing written, and a final arm's `| _ =>` binds nothing either, taking what the arms before it left. The report says what the `_` holds — "this `_` binds `{}`" — and how to change it — "write `()`, which refuses a value that stops being `{}`".

**Considered and left out.** An arm the elaborator proves is never taken, by reduction or by the facts in scope — Dafny's warning on a proof that leaned on contradictory assumptions ([proof dependencies](https://dafny.org/blog/2023/10/27/proof-dependencies/)), rustc's [`unused_comparisons`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/rustc_lint/types/static.UNUSED_COMPARISONS.html). The probe found 177 in the library, most of them in proofs over `Nat`, `Str/Valid` and `Map/bits`, and the ones read are the arms exhaustiveness makes a program write and a refuted proof discharges — `Option/get`'s `| none() => match ok end` — so a lint would report what the author must write; in tests, the eight it found by reduction match on a value the elaborator computed, whose dead arm is the test's failure. It becomes a lint only where the language lets an arm the facts refute go unwritten, which is a question for the language and not for this specification. A written proof argument entailment would have found, and an explicit implicit inference would have chosen, report a deletion that may document intent, and are left out until a consumer argues otherwise.

## What is known

- **Counted at `9688a9e33`**, in a scratch copy (`git archive` into `target/scratch/<name>`) built with `--features profile`, with two temporary `note!`s: one at `open_arm` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs`) asking `dead_guard` and `entail` of `False` under the arm's refinements, one in `elaborate_let` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`) at each binding with no name, with its type reduced; over `profile-prelude` (`curios-prelude/profile.rs`), which elaborates `/sys` and `/std` as the prelude's build does, `curios --profile <stream> lint` in a copy of `curios-prelude/std` holding no store, which elaborates `/std`'s test modules over the archived prelude, and `curios --profile <stream> wonder` over every program of `programs/` and the programs decoding JSON and TOML.
- **Arms.** In the library, 7,300 openings of an arm probed and 177 arms never taken, by the facts at 171 and by reduction at 7, the ones read cases exhaustiveness asks for and a refuted proof discharges; in `/std`'s test modules, 98 openings probed and 8 arms never taken, all by reduction; in the corpus, one. A control, an arm `| n > 10 =>` under `Holds(n < 5)`, was found by the facts; one `| n < 0 =>` over a `Nat` was found by neither.
- **Bindings with no name.** In the library, 140 of `{}` and 23 of `Io(…)`; in `/std`'s test modules, fifteen of `{}` and four of `Io(…)`; in the corpus, twenty-nine of `{}`. Every `Io` one read is a binding the lowering made, of the action a destructuring `!` takes apart — `let () = kept(r, now)!;` among them — or a statement `action!;` runs; the corpus writes `let _ = …!;` twenty-eight times, twenty-five printing a program's result, two stepping a `State` and one yielding. No written `_` binds an action nothing runs.
- **A question can cost what a declaration has.** The probe's `entail` of `False`, asked under the declaration's budget, spent it: two declarations of `Str/tests/decomposition.crs` were refused for `budget spent`.

## Stages

1. **The owner.** Create `curios-lint`; move `Lint`, its kinds, `LintedBinder`, reporting and ordering into it; the lowering records, per declaration, its written binders and their uses and whether a goal stands, the selectors and what resolved through them, and the unit's reachability and reached mounts, in place of the lints it decides now; the owner decides the four lints, `unused-dependency` among them, and applies credits, from elaboration or from a reused baseline, in one call where five stand. What a unit stores for its successors and its readers is the owner's lints, as it stores the lowering's now. Check: every lint test by name unchanged — `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::lint`, `curios-module`'s and `curios-wonder`'s lint suites and the reports `curios`'s suites pin — a unit's lints the same compiled now, restored from the store and taken from a baseline over a recompile, `/std`, `programs/` and the test corpus lint clean, and the store's format version raised where an archived type moved.
2. **The record.** Elaboration records, per declaration in its trace, the class of what each binding with no name and each written binder binds — `{}`, an action, or nothing — reduced at the declaration's close under the record's own allowance, measured and named as *Settled* states, and a declaration taken from its baseline brings its record; the owner reads it. Check: `curios-elab` tests that a `_` of `{}`, of `Io(T)`, of `Try(Io, E, T)` and of anything else records its class, that a binding the lowering made records one the owner does not lint, that a metavariable left open and a refused declaration record nothing, and that the record's reduction leaves the declaration's budget as it found it; `curios-pipeline`'s incremental suites with a declaration reused over a baseline keeping its record; and `curios-prelude`'s `every_authored_source_is_lint_clean` reading the typed lints of the prelude's units from the record its archive holds, as it reads their credits now.
3. **`unrun-action`.** Check: a `_` holding an `Io` and one holding a `Try` over `Io`, a named binder nothing reads holding one (this lint, not `unused-binder`), an action run with `!`, an action passed on as a value, a parameter of an action's type, a `List` and a pure value bound to `_`, and a statement `action!;`, each reported or not as the lint states; `/std`, `programs/` and the test corpus lint clean.
4. **`unit-wildcard`.** The corpus's `let _ = …!;` of a `{}` rewritten to `let () = …!;` in the same change, and every other site `/std`, `programs/` and the test corpus hold. Check: a `_` of `{}` in a `let`, a part of a `let`'s pattern, a lambda's parameter and a payload reported, one of anything else, a statement `action!;` and a final arm's `| _ =>` not; `/std`, `programs/` and the test corpus lint clean.

Each stage's contract lands in its owner as it lands: the decision restates which stage decides a lint and what a lint may read, its **Rejected** paragraph's "walking elaborated terms instead of the lowering" restated as what is still rejected, a lint re-deriving what a stage decided; `usage.md`'s `lint` lists the kinds; `curios-lint`'s README states its mission and `curios-module`'s gives up the lints.

## Retirement

Once the four stages have landed, the decision, `usage.md` and the crates' READMEs state the owner, the record and the lints, and nothing references this specification, it is deleted. A later lint is a rule in `curios-lint` and, where the record lacks its fact, a fact elaboration records, filed as its own work when a consumer argues for it.
