# A declaration is asked through one selector

Adopted: `curios wonder` asks about one declaration through one selector, `--name <PATH>`, and every view of a declaration is an option beside it — `--name <PATH> --witnesses`, `--name <PATH> --footprint`, `--name <PATH> --stage <STAGE>` — so a reader asking what one declaration became, weighs or reads as at a rung gets that declaration and nothing of the library's. Today `--witnesses <PATH>` is a selector of its own, `--cost` lists every named function of a program, and `--stage <STAGE>` prints a program whole at its rung. Settled with the user on 2026-11-18, and restated the same day from its objective alone.

What a program holds of a declaration past Core is read here off a fact each function and continuation carries: the function of the declaration's text it was made of, its lineage, stamped where erasure mints it and kept by every copy, as a function's role already is. Today that relation is a spelling, the hint a dump prints, which no reading can trust (**What is wrong today**).

The next step is stage 1, a delivery.

## What is wrong today

- **A view beside another query is dropped.** `--exports` requires `--name` (`Query::augment_args`, `curios/src/cli.rs`), and clap waives a requirement whose target conflicts with an argument that is present (`is_missing_required_ok`, clap_builder 4.6.7), which `--name` does with every query through their group: `curios wonder --at 3:5 --exports app.crs` answers `--at` and exits 0, as `--tests`, `--cost`, `--stage core` and `--declarations` beside `--exports` answer their own query, and only `--exports` alone is refused.
- **The selectors and the listing.** `--name <PATH>` answers a declaration's block, and `--witnesses <PATH>` that block and the witnesses it takes part in ([`wonder`](../../usage.md#wonder)), the block's pointer to them spelled `curios wonder --witnesses <PATH>` (`listing`, `curios-wonder/src/ask.rs`), which no test holds. `--cost` states one outcome a hint, `survived`, `specialized <n>` or `absorbed` (`curios-cps/src/survey.rs`): counted at `61bee406e` over a program of four declarations reading `n` from its arguments — `square`, `sum_squares` folding `square` over `List/range(0, n)`, the tail-recursive `count` adding `sum_squares(p)` for each `p` below `n`, and `execute` — it answers 191 rows, ten of them the program's and every one of those `absorbed`. `--stage <STAGE>` prints its rung's module whole (`Stage`'s `Display`, `curios-pipeline/src/stage.rs`), rooted at the program's entry from `anf` on. `documentation/usage.md`'s surface counts `wonder`'s six queries, eight today, and [A place in a file is answered from the declaration that writes it](../../design/tools/a-place-in-a-file-is-answered-from-the-declaration-that-writes-it.md) names `wonder --witnesses` among them.
- **The `core` rung of a library checks every unit with no store.** `unit_stage` (`curios-wonder/src/stage.rs`) states that a unit restored from a store holds its lowering without its items, "so the unit is lowered again in this process with no store", and reads it through `Fold::units` with no cache, which compiles every unit of the fold whole: `wonder --stage core` over a library's file narrates its `check` and no `store`, and over `curios-prelude/std/Bool.crs` elaborates and certifies all of `/std` to print its lowering, where `Fold::lowered_unit` (`curios-pipeline/src/standard.rs`) lowers the last unit alone over the ones before it, as `--at` over the same file does.
- **What a function was made of is a spelling.** A function's hint is the only trace of the declaration it came from, and the survey counts hints (`descendants`, `curios-cps/src/survey.rs`), whose module documentation opens on "a function carries the name of the declaration it descends from". It does not: a function bound by a local `let` takes its binder's bare name (`/twice`'s local `step` prints `~f188$step`, and `/std/Nat/descend/descend`'s local loop `go`, `go/1` and `go@w`, counted at `61bee406e`), so `Fate::name`'s "the declaration's fully qualified name, or an anonymous function's derived `owner/n`" is false of its rows; the suffixes minted for a crossing's step and retry (`/step`, `/again`, `curios-erase/src/eraser/crossing.rs`), a computed value's thunks (`/force`, `/init`, `curios-anf/src/into_cps/`) and a lambda (`owner/n`, `derived_hint`, `curios-erase/src/eraser/walk.rs`) read as path segments, while `/std/Io/step` and `/std/Str/utf8/step` are declarations; and the heap twin of a function is hinted `heap/<hint>` (`heap_named`, `curios-cps/src/frames.rs`), which `fates` never lists, reading only the names before the optimizer. [A printer states each fact once, where it is bound](../../design/tools/a-printer-states-each-fact-once-where-it-is-bound.md) keeps hints display-only so that "behaviour [stops] growing back onto spellings", and the survey's fates are such a behaviour.
- **Inlining leaves no trace of the function it inlined.** The inliner lands the callee's body on the call node unnamed (`curios-cps/src/optimize/inline.rs`), while contification moves a function's hint onto the loop it makes of it (`contify.rs`): over the program above, `/count` stands at `cps-optm` as the loop `~k1206$/count` inside a copy of `io/bind`, and `/square`, `/sum_squares` and `/sum_squares/1`, two, two and one functions at `cps`, as nothing, though the program computes them.
- **The emitter hands back no function's size.** The Wasm writer encodes each body into a buffer of its own before it writes it (`write_code`, `curios-wasm/src/writer.rs`) and hands back the module's bytes alone.

## What this builds on

- **A fact of where a function came from is stamped at its birth and kept by every copy.** `curios_anf::Function::role` is "stamped where it is born, and kept by every copy, since it is a fact of where the function came from that no later shape recovers"; `total` is lowered onto `curios_cps::Function::terminates`, which every copy keeps and contification moves onto its loop (`Continuation::terminates`). Neither is printed.
- **Erasure knows the declaration it is erasing.** Every definition and made name is bound through `Eraser::erase_binding` (`curios-erase/src/eraser/binding.rs`), and the functions minted inside it are minted under its owner (`with_owner`, `walk.rs`), in an order the erasure of the item fixes.
- **The survey reads the graphs either side of the optimizer and instruments no pass**, a decision of its module: `Stage::CpsOptm` is observed after the optimizer, the rebuild in place and the frame bound (`lower_from_anf`, `curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`), so the graph it reads holds every copy, twin, rebuild and guard.
- **The emitter names a function's Wasm functions by one rule**, `func/{index}$hint` from the CPS function's index and hint (`function_name`, `direct_name`, `wrapper_name`, `curios-emit/src/machine/structurize.rs`), the index its identity and the hint display.
- **A declaration's items are its text's.** A unit's anchors give what each declaration's text lowered to, which `--at` and `--name` already read (`curios-wonder/src/named.rs`), and a unit keeps every declaration's text, which `--name` prints.
- **A query is one option at most, and the subject is the one positional argument.** `--name` answers off the unit, without compiling a program; `--name` and `--at` answer two questions, each where the other cannot (`curios-wonder/src/named.rs`), probed on 2026-11-18 over a file one of whose declarations is refused.

## Prior art

- **A dump narrowed to one function.** rustc filters its MIR dumps by function (`-Zdump-mir=<filter>`), and LLVM what it prints (`-filter-print-funcs`).
- **One name asked, many facts answered.** Idris 2's `:di <name>` dumps a name's multiplicity, erasable arguments, compiled version, runtime-linked terms and size-change graph, beside the whole-file `--dumpcases` and `--dumpanf` ([Debugging the compiler](https://idris2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/reference/debugging.html)).
- **Where inlined code went.** LLVM tells it two ways: a pass remark, `-Rpass=inline` printing `'foo' inlined into 'bar'` as the inliner decides (clang's [`optimization-remark.c`](https://llvm.googlesource.com/llvm-project/clang/+/refs/heads/master/test/Frontend/optimization-remark.c)), and a location every instruction carries, whose `inlinedAt` names the call it was inlined through ([`DILocation`](https://llvm.org/docs/LangRef.html#dilocation)).
## Decided

- **`--name <PATH>` is the one selector, and a view stands beside it.** With no view it answers as it does today, the block then the declaration's whole text as its author wrote it; with a view, the block then the view. A view is one of `--witnesses`, `--exports`, `--footprint` and `--stage`, one at most, and each conflicts with every query but `--name`, a relation the parse states once, so a view beside another query is refused where the line is parsed rather than dropped. `--stage` alone stays the query that prints a program whole. `--at` takes no view: it is the hover's question, answered with the record [The editor is answered at a position](101-the-editor-is-answered-at-a-position.md) makes of a place.
  - Rejected: `--at` as a second selector taking the views (the user, 2026-11-18).
  - Rejected: one block for `--name` and `--at`, the facts a compilation established of the declaration: `--name` would answer a refused subject's own declarations from the lowering, a second question under a name that asks for what was elaborated.
  - Rejected: a selector of its own, `--of <PATH>`, beside `--name`, a second word for one declaration.
  - Rejected: a rung and a path joined in one value, `--stage cps-optm:<PATH>`, a separator to learn.
- **`--witnesses` is a view**, answering as it does today under the selection, and the block's pointer to it is spelled `--name <PATH> --witnesses`.
- **A function carries its lineage.** A `curios_common::Lineage` — the path a declaration is declared at, and the function of its erasure the code was made of, by the order erasure mints them — is stamped by erasure on every function it mints inside a declaration's binding, kept by every copy the erased optimizer and the CPS optimizer make, as `role` and `terminates` are, lowered onto the CPS function, given by the CPS lowering to every continuation it makes while lowering a function's body, moved onto the loop contification makes, and kept by a heap twin and by a continuation the frame bound lifts to a function. A function erased from no declaration a path names — a witness's, the entry's — and a continuation a pass makes carry none. Hints are unchanged, and display-only again. An erased form and the prelude's images hold it, so their stored bytes change; no verdict changes, and no emitted module, since nothing emits it.
  - Rejected: reading the relation off hints, which a local binder, a derived suffix and a twin each break, and which makes a behaviour of a spelling.
  - Rejected: the linker's map of each function to the names its item declares (`evaluating`, `curios-erase/src/link.rs`): it holds an item, not the declaration of a group a function was written in, and only the functions of an item's evaluation, the totality reading it serves.
  - Rejected: a source location on every node, LLVM's `inlinedAt`, which would place straight-line code inlined into a caller: every rewrite of every pass would carry it, and no consumer but this view asks for it; a capability of its own should a trap naming its source or a profile attributing inlined code ask.
  - Rejected: passes recording what they inlined, LLVM's remarks: an instrumented pass, which the survey's module decision declines.
- **`--footprint` takes `--cost`'s place**, asked of a selected declaration alone, the program-wide listing going with `--cost`: a row for each function of the declaration's text, by its lineage and labelled by its hint at `cps`, stating what became of it — `not linked` where the program holds nothing of it, `pruned before CPS` where the erased optimizer dropped it, `kept`, `copied <n>`, `inlined into <caller>` where a continuation of its lineage stands in a function of another, named by that function's hint, and `absorbed` where nothing of its lineage stands — with a row for its heap twin where the frame bound made one; beside each, its bytes in the emitted module, as the writer measures each body, found by the emitter's naming rule and never by parsing a name, and its rebuilds, the `Rebuilt` values it holds. Only a program subject asks it. It is named for what it answers, what the compiled program holds of the declaration and what that weighs.
  - Rejected: sizes after Binaryen: Binaryen's rewrite is below what the compiler controls, and the view answers what Curios made of the declaration (the user, 2026-11-18).
  - Rejected: a frame's weight: `frame/enter` stands at the guarded call, names no callee and weighs the heaviest a call may reach (`IntrinsicCall::FrameEnter`, `curios-cps/src/node.rs`), and the weight is the budget's stand-in for bytes (`frame_weight`, `curios-cps/src/frames.rs`), not a measurement; the twin's row says the declaration's frames may move to the heap, and `--stage cps-optm` under the selection prints its guards.
  - Rejected: a field of `--name`'s block, which would compile a program to answer a question asked off the unit.
  - Rejected: keeping the listing beside the view, almost every row of it the library's.
  - Rejected: reasons in a row, why a pass inlined or copied, which a pass would have to record as it decides.
- **`--stage <STAGE>` under a selection prints, at its rung, the declaration's part.** At `text`, its text parsed and reprinted; at `core`, the items the subject's own declaration lowered to, a unit in scope keeping no lowering, so a declaration of one is refused there with the command that asks it of its unit's file; at `core-elab`, its items in its unit's elaborated module, spelled as Core text spells them; from `anf` to `cps-optm`, the functions and continuations of its lineage, each as the binding subtree it is, under the chain of functions it stands in, which [A printer states each fact once, where it is bound](../../design/tools/a-printer-states-each-fact-once-where-it-is-bound.md) takes as a rule, so a capture reads as bound by the function named above it; at `wasm`, the Wasm functions the emitter made of them. Alone `--stage` prints the program whole, as today.
- **A library's `core` rung is its lowering**, read through `Fold::lowered_unit` over the store, as its documentation says.
