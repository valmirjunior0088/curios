# A cabinet holds one object a unit, and what its projects reach

A unit's place in the cabinet is addressed by the compiler that filed it (`unit_slot`, `entry_slot`, `payload_slot`, `curios-package/src/cabinet.rs`), and a payload's by the engine besides. So every compiler build files a new place for every unit it compiles, and nothing removes the places no compiler files at any more. `unit_slot`'s `///` says the address is "what bounds the cabinet — a project has as many slots as it has units, forever", which holds for one compiler. A unit a project no longer has keeps its place as well, and a layout a move leaves behind stays whole, since no compiler knows it.

Counted on 2026-11-20:

- With `main` at `67c89c1c3`, the main checkout's `/std` cabinet holds its test unit at two addresses, filed 37 minutes apart by two compiler builds, of 97,073,784 and 97,091,621 bytes; the two records name the same 268 files.
- The curios-papers project's cabinet holds 148M in the layout `d1b828364` and `e35c93f80` moved away from, which no compiler reads. Of the nine units filed there, on 11-13, 11-15 and 11-17 by three compiler builds, six were dead before the move.
- The compilers of `dc1f25fa4` and `19d53645f`, which differ in the CPS optimizer alone, file `/std`'s test unit as one byte-identical pack of 88,879,924 bytes beside index files that differ, and the cabinet holds the pack twice, at two addresses.

The words are uneven besides. *Slot* names this place, a row's field position in the back end, and a registry's slot. *Object* names a content-addressed piece of a stored unit where a compiler's *object* is the compiled unit itself. *Verdict* names a checker's judgment, a test's outcome, and the cabinet's family and file. `curios-verdict`'s `//!` still calls a unit's place a one-file slot, which it has not been since `e35c93f80`.

What a compile made of a declaration is kept as a candidate, found by the facet and the input it was made from (`curios_common::candidate_prefix`), and the certifier takes the verdict a candidate holds wherever every part it read digests now as it did (`stored`, `curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`). Nothing in a candidate's name or its making says which compiler made it: what holds a candidate to its compiler is the address its packs stand at, which names the compiler (`Verdicts::candidates`, `curios-verdict/src/verdicts.rs`). And the grace that collects an address is also what keeps the candidates of a text that comes back, which `curios-common/src/candidates.rs` and [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md) promise an edit undone, a branch checked out again and a bisection.

The next step is stage 1.

## Decided

Settled by the user on 2026-11-20.

- **The cabinet's words.** A unit as the cabinet keeps it is an *object*, as a compiler's compiled unit is (C's `.o`, OCaml's `.cmo`, Rocq's `.vo`, Lean's `.olean`): a directory `objects/<address>/` holding its `unit` file, its record ahead of its index, beside its packs. A content-addressed piece of an object is a *blob*, as git's, restic's and Bazel's are, and a *pack* is a file of blobs. *Slot* is left to the back end's field positions and a registry's slots. *Verdict* is left to a checker's judgment and to what keeps one as it was filed, the crate `curios-verdict` and its handle `Verdicts`, the sense [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md) uses; a test's outcome is an *outcome*. `.claude/rules/documentation.md`'s vocabulary table states each.
- **An object's address belongs to `curios-verdict`.** `unit_slot`, `entry_slot`, `payload_slot` and the two schema tags move from `curios-package`, `curios-verdict` being their only reader, beside the record they pair with ("A slot is addressed, and a hit is verified"). `curios-package`'s "One subsystem, one owner" is restated to what it owns: where `.curios/` and its cabinet are, its families' paths, the package's own families, and the compiler's identity.
- **The compiler is named by the record, not the address.** An object is addressed by what its unit is: its mounts or its executable, what it declares, its kind, and the archived root it stands in for. Its record names the compiler whose verdict it holds. A read by any other compiler is a miss, neither a hit nor a baseline, since a baseline's verdicts are believed too ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). A compiler change therefore replaces the object, and its filing writes only the blobs the address lacks. A payload is addressed by its package, executable and target, and names its compiler and engine in its frame. Two compilers alternating on one project each recompile the unit at every switch, and the blobs they share stay written. Peers: rustc's incremental cache writes the compiler's version into its files' header, discards a mismatch and overwrites it ([`file_format.rs`](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/blob/master/compiler/rustc_incremental/src/persist/file_format.rs)), Lake mixes the toolchain into every module's trace and rebuilds its one `.olean` over it ([`Lake.Build.Trace`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lake/Build/Trace.html)), and Ninja keeps one result per output. The peers that key by compiler each need a second mechanism: Go a clock ([`cache.go`](https://go.dev/src/cmd/go/internal/cache/cache.go)), ccache and sccache a size cap, Bazel's disk cache a size or an age ([remote caching](https://bazel.build/remote/caching)), Cargo's target directory an outside tool ([cargo-sweep](https://github.com/holmgr/cargo-sweep)).
- **A candidate is taken where a record of the running compiler names it.** The packs of an address hold what every compiler filing there wrote, and a candidate is found by what it was made from, so a compile takes one only where a record the object keeps, naming the running compiler, names its blob, those records being read where the handle opens the address. A candidate's name ends in the digest of what it holds (`curios_common::candidate_name`), so a blob such a record names is the bytes that compiler filed, whoever wrote the pack it stands in, and a pack another compiler merged or rewrote meanwhile changes nothing. The stages ask for candidates as they do, and `curios-verdict`'s packs answer through the records. The compiler in a candidate's name is rejected: every stage that names or looks one up would be handed a fact only the cabinet knows, and the prelude image, built before the binary that embeds it, cannot name its compiler.
- **No clock.** An object keeps its own record and the one it replaced, and the blobs those two name; a blob neither names is taken by no compile from that filing on. A pack is rewritten without what neither names where that is at least half its blobs, and otherwise where the packs' geometric merge rewrites it, as restic's prune leaves a partly used pack alone up to its `--max-unused` ([Removing backup snapshots](https://restic.readthedocs.io/en/latest/060_forget.html)): every byte the dropping rewrites is paid for by a byte that died, where dropping at every filing would rewrite the pack the unit was first filed in, its largest, at nearly every filing of an editing session. Two writers alternating at one address, two checkouts sharing a cabinet or two compilers, lose nothing, each compiling over the candidates its own record names, and a third's blobs go out of reach at the next filing. A filer naming a blob that a filing beside it drops stays a miss and never a belief. A candidate so reaches one filing back: an edit undone before the next filing finds what was made of the text it brings back, and a branch checked out again or a bisection past that is judged again. This revises [What no object names is collected five days after a collection first finds it so](../../../curios-verdict/README.md#what-no-object-names-is-collected-five-days-after-a-collection-first-finds-it-so), whose grace and daily interval served those alternating writers and a candidate's reach past one filing, and whose rejection of a time renewed by reads held of blobs, never of addresses; and the reach `curios-common/src/candidates.rs` and Cached verdicts state, with the latter's rejection of a verdict kept for the baseline's text alone.
- **What a project no longer reaches goes.** A filing in a project's own cabinet keeps the objects of every unit the project reaches, and takes away every other object: every package its governing root holds, each member an umbrella enumerates or the one package, each one's library and executables in both kinds, and what each reaches, since a run's fold reaches its own package's graph alone (`order`, `curios-package/src/graph.rs`). It keeps the payloads of those executables and of their test subjects, and the sources and foreign modules their manifests pin. An entry of `.curios/` or of its cabinet that no family of the running compiler holds is taken away too, so a layout a later move leaves behind is collected without a migration written for it.

## Open

- **A shared cabinet.** A run against `CURIOS_CABINET` knows its own project's roots and no other's. Either each project's run files what it reaches under a name for its root, and a collection keeps the union of the projects whose root still exists, as Nix keeps a closure while its root link resolves ([gcroots](https://nixos.org/manual/nix/stable/package-management/garbage-collector-roots)); or a shared cabinet is collected within each address alone, the last decision not holding there.

## Stages

1. **The words, and the address's owner.** The renames, and the three addresses with their schema tags moved into `curios-verdict`, with no behaviour moved.
2. **The compiler into the record.** The addresses lose the compiler, and the payload's loses the engine; the record and the payload's frame name them, and a read by another compiler is a miss. An object keeps the record it replaced beside its own, and a candidate is taken where one of them naming the running compiler names it. Both schema tags advance.
3. **No clock.** `Packs::collect` runs at every filing over what the object's two records name, and rewrites a pack half of which they leave unnamed; `GRACE`, the interval and the `collection` file go. The crate README's collection decision, `curios-common/src/candidates.rs`'s `//!` and Cached verdicts are restated.
4. **What a project reaches.** The collection from a project's roots, the unknown entries of `.curios/` and of its cabinet, and the shared cabinet as *Open* settles.

## Verification

- Stage 1: the suite and the docs build stand, and no file of `curios-package`, `curios-verdict` or `curios-wonder` writes *slot* or *verdict* in a sense `.claude/rules/documentation.md`'s vocabulary table retires.
- Stage 2: a cabinet two compiler identities file one unit into in turn holds one object and each pack once; another compiler's object is no hit and no baseline; and a candidate only another compiler's record names is not taken. Each is mutation-checked.
- Stage 3: alternating filings of two texts of one unit keep both texts' blobs and take each other's candidates; a third text's filing leaves the first's blobs named by no record and taken by no compile, and drops them from a pack once half of it is unnamed; counted, the blobs a run of one-declaration edits rewrites stay within a constant multiple of the blobs that died and the merges'.
- Stage 4: a package an umbrella stops enumerating, an executable a manifest drops, and an entry of `.curios/` no family holds are gone after the next filing, while a sibling member's objects stay; the curios-papers cabinet, filed by the compiler of this stage, holds its project's objects and nothing else.

## Retirement

When stage 4 lands, its decisions live in `curios-verdict/README.md` and `curios-package/README.md`, what a candidate reaches in `curios-common/src/candidates.rs` and Cached verdicts, and this file is deleted.
