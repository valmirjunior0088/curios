# A cabinet holds one object a unit, and what its projects reach

An object is collected within its own address alone, and only where a day has passed since the address was last collected: a blob no record names stays five days (`GRACE`, `INTERVAL`, `curios-verdict/src/packs.rs`). Nothing removes an object no project reaches any more: a unit a project no longer has keeps its object, and a layout a move leaves behind stays whole, since no compiler knows it.

Counted on 2026-11-20: the curios-papers project's cabinet holds 148M in the layout `d1b828364` and `e35c93f80` moved away from, which no compiler reads. Of the nine units filed there, on 11-13, 11-15 and 11-17 by three compiler builds, six were dead before the move.

The grace that collects an address is also what keeps the candidates of a text that comes back, which `curios-common/src/candidates.rs` and [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md) promise an edit undone, a branch checked out again and a bisection.

The next step is stage 3.

## Decided

Settled by the user on 2026-11-20.

- **No clock.** An object keeps its own record and the one it replaced, and the blobs those two name; a blob neither names is taken by no compile from that filing on. A pack is rewritten without what neither names where that is at least half its blobs, and otherwise where the packs' geometric merge rewrites it, as restic's prune leaves a partly used pack alone up to its `--max-unused` ([Removing backup snapshots](https://restic.readthedocs.io/en/latest/060_forget.html)): every byte the dropping rewrites is paid for by a byte that died, where dropping at every filing would rewrite the pack the unit was first filed in, its largest, at nearly every filing of an editing session. Two writers alternating at one address, two checkouts sharing a cabinet or two compilers, lose nothing, each hitting or compiling over a record of its own, and a third's blobs go out of reach at the next filing. A filer naming a blob that a filing beside it drops stays a miss and never a belief. A candidate so reaches one filing back: an edit undone before the next filing finds what was made of the text it brings back, and a branch checked out again or a bisection past that is judged again. This revises [What no object names is collected five days after a collection first finds it so](../../../curios-verdict/README.md#what-no-object-names-is-collected-five-days-after-a-collection-first-finds-it-so), whose grace and daily interval served those alternating writers and a candidate's reach past one filing, and whose rejection of a time renewed by reads held of blobs, never of addresses; and the reach `curios-common/src/candidates.rs` and Cached verdicts state, with the latter's rejection of a verdict kept for the baseline's text alone.
- **What a project no longer reaches goes.** A filing in a project's own cabinet keeps the objects of every unit the project reaches, and takes away every other object: every package its governing root holds, each member an umbrella enumerates or the one package, each one's library and executables in both kinds, and what each reaches, since a run's fold reaches its own package's graph alone (`order`, `curios-package/src/graph.rs`). It keeps the payloads of those executables and of their test subjects, and the sources and foreign modules their manifests pin. An entry of `.curios/` or of its cabinet that no family of the running compiler holds is taken away too, so a layout a later move leaves behind is collected without a migration written for it.

## Open

- **A shared cabinet.** A run against `CURIOS_CABINET` knows its own project's roots and no other's. Either each project's run files what it reaches under a name for its root, and a collection keeps the union of the projects whose root still exists, as Nix keeps a closure while its root link resolves ([gcroots](https://nixos.org/manual/nix/stable/package-management/garbage-collector-roots)); or a shared cabinet is collected within each address alone, the last decision not holding there.

## Stages

3. **No clock.** `Packs::collect` runs at every filing over what the object's two records name, and rewrites a pack half of which they leave unnamed; `GRACE`, the interval and the `collection` file go. The crate README's collection decision, `curios-common/src/candidates.rs`'s `//!` and Cached verdicts are restated.
4. **What a project reaches.** The collection from a project's roots, the unknown entries of `.curios/` and of its cabinet, and the shared cabinet as *Open* settles.

## Verification

- Stage 3: alternating filings of two texts of one unit keep both texts' blobs and take each other's candidates; a third text's filing leaves the first's blobs named by no record and taken by no compile, and drops them from a pack once half of it is unnamed; counted, the blobs a run of one-declaration edits rewrites stay within a constant multiple of the blobs that died and the merges'.
- Stage 4: a package an umbrella stops enumerating, an executable a manifest drops, and an entry of `.curios/` no family holds are gone after the next filing, while a sibling member's objects stay; the curios-papers cabinet, filed by the compiler of this stage, holds its project's objects and nothing else.

## Retirement

When stage 4 lands, its decisions live in `curios-verdict/README.md` and `curios-package/README.md`, what a candidate reaches in `curios-common/src/candidates.rs` and Cached verdicts, and this file is deleted.
