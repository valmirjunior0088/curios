# A lint is decided by the stage that knows its last fact

**The benefit.** Every binder lint is decided in `curios-lint` from facts two stages record only for it — the lowering's zeros of a written binder, and the credits and site classes elaboration keeps in each declaration's trace record — so each fact a lint needs leaves the stage that knows it, is kept across recompiles, and is joined again by every reader that decides: `Unit::new`, the pipeline's `Findings::decided` for a refused program, and the prelude's lint-clean test, each with a copy of its own, the pipeline's wording a declaration's sites without the classes its record holds. Deciding each lint in the stage that establishes its last fact, and keeping the decided lint in that stage's record of the declaration, leaves nothing to join: the lowering decides what resolution alone decides, elaboration decides what needs a proof or a type when it files the declaration's record, and every reader gathers what the records hold. GHC, OCaml and rustc's resolver and liveness pass decide a warning in the phase that knows it, and rustc keeps a query's diagnostics with its result. The project has decided to adopt it.

The next step is stage 1, a delivery. It waits on nothing.

## What is wrong today

- **A refused program words its declarations' sites without a class.** `Findings::decided` (`curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`) reads the classes `Observed` holds, the final term's alone, since a declaration's stand in its record, so a refused program reports no `unit-wildcard` in a declaration and tells a binder of `{}` to write `_`. Its credits are whole: a refused recompile joins those of every record its walk holds to the context before handing the refusal out (`elaborate_and_zonk_over`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`), a second statement of what a record concluded, which a record holding its lints makes unneeded.
- **A refused library reports none of its lints.** Probed at `f7afc7555`: a library whose `lib.crs` writes `use /std/{Nat, Bool};` and reads only `Nat` reports `unused import` at `Bool` under `curios lint`, and with a declaration of it refused reports `0 lints, 1 error`. A unit's lints are decided where the unit is made (`Unit::new`), and a refused unit is made nowhere: the fold of a library answers its refusal alone (`Fold::units`, `curios-pipeline/src/standard.rs`; `Diagnosed::refused`, `curios-wonder/src/diagnostics.rs`), where a refused program hands its lints beside its verdict (`Checked`).
- **The facts a lint is decided from are joined three times.** `curios_lint::lints` takes what was read and what each site binds as two closures, so each caller states what a credit and a class are: `Unit::new` (`curios-unit/src/unit.rs`) from the traces and the final term's `Observed`, `Findings::decided` from `Observed` alone, and `every_authored_source_is_lint_clean` (`curios-prelude/src/tests.rs`) from the archive's traces.
- **Elaboration's record holds another stage's inputs.** `Concluded::credited` and `Concluded::classes` (`curios-elab/src/traces.rs`) and `Observed` (`curios-elab/src/context/sites.rs`) exist for `curios-lint` alone, and a store's candidate adopts its credits into the context (`credit_adopted`) only for them to be handed out again.
- **The rejection the owner rests on does not hold.** [A lint is an exact finding read off the compilation](../../design/tools/a-lint-is-an-exact-finding-read-off-the-compilation.md) rejects "a stage deciding a lint, which spreads lint logic across every stage that knows a fact, as GHC's warnings spread across its renamer, typechecker and desugarer", and `curios-lint`'s README argues "A lint joins facts two stages know halves of, which neither can join alone". Two stages know a lint's facts, and the later one can be handed the earlier's half, as the lowering already hands elaboration `curios_core::Minted` beside the module and an `Unresolved` under a binder for elaboration to report. GHC's failure is a phase that runs twice reporting twice ([#23569](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/-/issues/23569)), where a stage here decides a declaration once and keeps the decision in the record a recompile reuses. One owner did not remove the spread: both stages still record lint-shaped facts, and the owner adds the join.

## What this builds on

- **`Minted`** (`curios-core/src/names.rs`) is what the lowering hands elaboration beside the module: per declaration, persistent over a baseline, read once where elaboration seeds its solver, and never on the `Definitions` every later stage and the certifier read.
- **`Unresolved`** (`curios-core/src/unresolved.rs`) is a report one stage makes and another reports, in `curios-core`, which both reach.
- **A declaration's trace record is filed whenever an attempt stands** (`demand.rs`), its refused items included, with its credits (`credited_in`) and its sites' classes (`classes_in`) decided there; a recompile keeps it with the declaration, and a store's candidate is adopted with it.
- **A lint placed when a record is made stays placed wherever the record is reused.** A term archives its extent (`curios-core/src/term.rs`), so a lowered item's digest, which a store's candidate is found by (`lowered_digest`, `curios-elab/src/candidate.rs`), holds every extent; and a declaration lowered again is elaborated again (`curios-pipeline/src/recompile.rs`).
- **The lowering keeps its binder zeros per declaration** (`LoweredDeclaration::zeros`, `Unused`, `curios-module/src/into_core/lowered.rs`), by definition and place, renamed with a witness's name (`curios-module/src/into_core/witness.rs`), and its import and declaration zeros per module body in its baseline half, `unused-declaration`'s reachability revised over a baseline (`Reachability`).

## What the peers do

GHC, OCaml, Agda and Gleam warn as they check; rustc declares every lint once and decides each where its facts are — the resolver buffers `unused_imports` ([`check_unused.rs`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/stable/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_resolve/check_unused.rs.html)), liveness decides `unused_variables` ([`liveness.rs`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_mir_transform/liveness.rs.html)) — and keeps the diagnostics a query emitted beside its cached result, emitted again when the result is reused ([`QuerySideEffects`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/stable/nightly-rustc/rustc_query_system/query/struct.QuerySideEffects.html)). Kotlin K2 reports only in a checkers phase over resolved FIR ([`fir-basics.md`](https://pigweed.googlesource.com/third_party/github/JetBrains/kotlin/+/49b2ac1b100c5042a0e98582713938062efa6a31/docs/fir/fir-basics.md)), and Lean's linters read the info trees elaboration left ([unused variables](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Linter/UnusedVariables.html)): a phase of their own pays where linters are many and open, which Curios's six exact lints are not. **What is taken:** the decision where the fact is (GHC, rustc), the kept result (rustc). **What is set aside:** a phase that decides twice (GHC's desugarer), and an owner that joins records kept for it.

## Settled

- **Each lint is decided and worded by the stage that establishes its last fact.** The lowering decides `unused-import` and `unused-declaration`, a dead private module folding its declarations as now; elaboration decides `unused-binder`, `unused-let` and `unit-wildcard`; `curios lint`'s package pass decides `unused-dependency` (`curios-wonder/src/lint.rs`). The kinds, their wording, where each stands and `usage.md`'s table do not change.
- **The vocabulary is `curios-core`'s**, as `Unresolved` is: `Lint`, `LintKind` and its names, the one phrasing every `unused-*` lint shares, `Lint::report` and `Lint::ordered`, in `curios-core/src/lint.rs`. `curios-lint` is deleted.
- **The lowering hands elaboration what no written name reads, beside the module, as `Minted`.** A per-declaration table in `curios-core` holds each written binder and `_` no written name reads, and each `let` that runs no action none of whose binders one reads, by definition and place, with its spelling and extent; a declaration holding a written goal holds none. It takes the place of `Zero::{Binder, Wildcard, Let}` and `Unused`, kept per declaration and renamed with a witness as they are, and is never put on `Definitions`, which the certifier reads and Core text spells.
- **Elaboration decides when it files a declaration's record**, from the declaration's entry of that table, the credits its proofs wrote and its sites' classes, and keeps the lints in the record (`Concluded::lints`) in place of `credited` and `classes`. A store's adopted record brings its lints, so `credit_adopted` goes. The entry's final term is decided where its elaboration closes. Credits and classes never leave elaboration.
- **Elaboration hands out every lint it holds, whatever it concluded.** `Observed` becomes the final term's lints and those of every record the walk's traces hold — made now, adopted or kept from the baseline — taken before a refusal is returned, so a refused unit reports what an accepted one would.
- **Every reader gathers.** `Unit::new` stores the lowering's lints and `Observed`'s in reading order (`Lint::ordered`), and the pipeline's `Findings` gathers the same for a refused program and a refused library, handed beside the refusal; `every_authored_source_is_lint_clean` reads the lints the archive's units store. No reader decides.
- **What the store holds changes, and nothing a program is judged or runs as.** The stored bytes that change are each trace's `Concluded`, holding lints where it holds credits and classes, and the lowering's baseline half, holding lints per module body and the unread table per declaration where it holds zeros; the store's `SCHEMA` (`curios-package/src/store.rs`) is raised with each. Every verdict, certification, erased and emitted program stays what it is.

## Stages

1. **Elaboration decides what a proof or a type decides.** The unread table beside `Minted`, handed by the pipeline wherever `Minted` is; elaboration's decision at filing and at the final term's close; `Concluded::lints`; `Observed` the lints, those of every record the walk holds taken where a refusal now joins their credits; `Unit::new`, `Findings` and the prelude's test gather; `Concluded::{credited, classes}`, `credit_adopted` and `curios_lint::lints` go. `curios-lint`'s `unused_binder`, `unused_let` and `shadowed_parameter` suites become the lowering's, asserting what its table holds, and `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::lint` asserts what elaboration reports. Check: those suites; the incremental suites with a declaration kept over a baseline and one adopted from a store's candidate keeping their lints; `tests::lint::a_kept_declarations_proof_reads_its_hypothesis_beside_a_refusal`, and a refused program's declaration binding `{}` to `_` reported as `unit-wildcard`; the lint-clean tests; `classification_allowance_measurements`; `SCHEMA` raised.
2. **A refused unit reports its lints, as a refused program does.** A unit's compile hands its findings beside its refusal — the lowering's lints and `Observed`'s, gathered as `Findings::of_text` gathers a program's — and the fold of a library hands them on (`Fold::units`), so `wonder`, the server and `curios lint` report them beside the errors. Check: a test in `curios-wonder` — a library with an import nothing reads and a refused declaration — reporting `unused-import` beside the refusal, which fails before the change; `every_test_module_lints_clean`.
3. **`curios-lint` goes.** `unused_dependencies` and its suite move to `curios-wonder`, and the crate, its README and its rows in the workspace manifest and `.claude/rules/pipeline.md` are deleted. Check: `cargo xtask workspace lint`; `curios lint`'s suite in `curios-wonder`.

Each stage's contract lands in its owner as it lands: the decision restates who decides a lint — the stage that establishes its last fact, in its own record — with its **Rejected** paragraph naming an owner that joins records kept for it, and dropping "a stage deciding a lint"; `curios-module`'s README gives up "the zeros of resolution the lints are decided from" for the lints it decides and the table it hands on; `Minted`'s doc stops being "the whole of what the lowering hands elaboration"; `.claude/rules/pipeline.md`'s lint line names the two stages and the vocabulary.

## Retirement

Once the three stages have landed, the decision, the crates' READMEs and the rules state who decides each lint, and nothing references this specification, it is deleted. A later lint is decided by the stage that establishes its last fact, and a fact one stage knows and a later one needs travels beside the module, as the unread table does.
