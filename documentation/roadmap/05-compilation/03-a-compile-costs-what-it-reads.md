# A compile costs what it reads

Working specification for making what a compilation does follow what its declarations read, at every stage it runs: an edit redoes, stage by stage, the declarations that read what it changed and no other, and a compile pays for the declarations of its scope it reads, not for the scope. The first is the minimality of a build, with early cutoff, at the grain of a declaration; the second is the same rule read from the other side, a scope being an input like any other. Parsing, storing and restoring are stages it runs, and a unit's boundary is no limit of the rule.

It rests on [A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md): a record of reads decides nothing while anything but reads reaches a declaration. [Item tasks](04-item-tasks.md) needs this, for the cells its workers write and the records that say which of them may run together.

## What this builds on

- **The `Cache` seam and the stored-unit format** (`curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`, `curios-unit`): a product-supplied policy the fold consults, and a serialized unit that already crosses processes, read in place before anything of it is restored (`curios_archive::access`).
- **A stored unit is a baseline** ([A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md)): a unit compiled over one is stored as the bytes it is compiled whole.
- **A declaration is the unit of work** ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)): lowered in identities of its own, elaborated on demand in a state of its own, an attempt that read a declaration not yet finished made again after it (`Demand`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/demand.rs`), judged by the kernel alone, and stored in one order.
- **The accessors that record a need** (`Context::assumption`, `definition_body`, `var_reduct`, `induct_decl`, `struct_decl`, `concept`, `witness_unfinished`, `curios-elab/src/context.rs`): every read of another declaration's name, body or registry entry passes one of them, and no memo outlives the declaration that filled it, so no answer reaches a declaration without its read.
- **The certifier records what it read.** `curios_core::Reads` holds, per definition, the items whose type, universe scheme or registry entry a judgment read and the bodies it asked for. Every unit files them, and no stage reads them.
- **A witness is named by its module, its concept and the key its signature is spelled at** (`curios-core/src/witness.rs`), so whether a witness stands at a key is read off a unit's names.
- **An erased form is its item's, and a program links what it reaches** ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#an-erased-form-is-its-items), `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/erased.rs`, `link.rs`).
- **Elaboration and erasure read a scope where it stands** (`InScope`, `curios-elab/src/context/in_scope.rs`): a context asks the units in scope where its own stores hold nothing under a name, and is answered by reference.
- **A stored elaborated module holds no position** ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-stored-unit-says-what-its-items-mean-not-where-they-were-written-or-how-they-were-come-by)): one node for each structure as it is spelled (`curios_core::Sharing`), which is what lets a recompiled unit be held to the bytes of a whole one.
- **A unit is handed over, never copied** ([`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-is-handed-over-never-copied)): its lowered state, its elaborated module, its erased forms and its certification are each held by pointer, a unit compiled over a baseline keeps the baseline's own erased form for every item it does not erase again, and where a unit holds each name (`curios_core::Places`) is made once and kept with it. Elaboration reads the declarations a recompile keeps where the baseline holds them (`Established::keeping`, `curios-elab/src/established.rs`).
- **A file is parsed once for each text a process holds of it** (`parsed`, `curios-text/src/root_source.rs`), and the lowering reads its predecessors as layers, copying none (`Scoped`, `curios-text/src/into_core/scoped.rs`).

## The gap

What a question about `/std` costs, compiled over the archived unit, in allocations. Counted at `01fc36712`, as *Verification* states; a count moves in its fifth digit from one run to the next.

| What a compile does | What it follows today | Nothing edited | `/std/Bool/not`'s body respelled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restores the prelude | both images, whole | 1.02 M | 1.02 M |
| Parses | every file of the unit, once for each process | 76.37 M | 76.37 M |
| Lowers | the unit | 2.66 M | 2.66 M |
| Decides what to redo | every declaration of the unit | 0.73 M | 0.71 M |
| Elaborates | what reaches the edit | 0.15 M | 105.94 M |
| Judges | what reaches the edit | 0.02 M | 29.08 M |
| Erases | what reaches the edit | 0.01 M | 5.21 M |
| Stores | the unit | 0.60 M | 0.77 M |

**A recompile walks the unit it does not redo.** With nothing edited, a unit compiled over a baseline lowers every module again, compares every lowered declaration with the baseline's (`invalidated`, `curios-pipeline/src/recompile.rs`, whose comparison walks both sides of each, `Term::equal_modulo_metas`), puts the items it keeps and the ones it made again back into one module and orders it (`reassemble`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`, `Module::ordered`), and serializes all of it. Most of what the lowering costs beyond its parse is the documentation record (`document`, 1.62 M), built for every question and read by the two commands that document.

**A cell is tied to a text.** Every occurrence of a term holds a position and the source it lies in (`Span`, `curios-utilities/src/span.rs`), so a declaration lowers to another form wherever an edit above it moves it, two lowered forms are compared up to their positions, an elaborated declaration a recompile keeps points at lines that have since moved, and a stored unit holds the text of every file it was lowered from.

**An edit redoes everything that reaches it.** Respelling `/std/Bool/not`'s body elaborates and judges 1,260 declarations again, 135.02 M allocations between the two checkers. The closure `invalidated` takes is over names an item reaches, whatever it read of them, and transitive, since nothing says where a re-elaborated item came out the same.

**Nothing records what the elaborator read.** What elaboration read and left no name for — a body unfolded while a candidate was tried, a key at which no witness stands, whether a proof was offered the unit's lemmas — is in no term, and elaboration adds 3,018 edges to the 6,488 the lowered forms of `/std`'s declarations name, 1,881 of them to a witness, counted at `0283c7242`. Whether the prover's vocabulary reads a declaration is read off the baseline's elaborated graph (`vocabulary_reach`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`), so an edit that changes what the vocabulary reads leaves a reused declaration elaborated under the other answer.

**A reused verdict rests on a closure the certifier does not check.** The kernel passes a reused declaration over where its terms are the mounted ones (`recheck_over`, `Judged::of`), and which declarations are reused is the pipeline's closure: one cut short files the baseline's verdict beside a declaration it no longer describes, the finding in [`01-soundness`](../01-soundness/00-findings.md).

**A unit is stored and restored whole.** Filing serializes all of a unit, hash-consed in one table, and a hit decodes all of it. Every process validates and restores both of the prelude's images before its first declaration, the erased form of every item of the library among them: about half of the stacks sampled inside `restore_archives` stand in erased forms (sixteen samples, timed, at `c98e44af9`), where `programs/hello_world.crs` links 570 of them.

**The kernel and the lowering bring in the whole scope.** `Globals::of` copies each registry of every predecessor and maps each of its definitions, and the kernel copies the result again (`Kernel::seed`): 0.05 M allocations of a question about `programs/hello_world.crs`. The lowering lists every path of every unit in scope for a table only a report reads (`writable_paths`, `curios-text/src/into_core.rs`), 0.04 M. A unit compiled over a baseline hands the kernel a copy of every item it keeps (`Module::restricted`, `Globals::mount`), and erasure keys every unit in scope again for each compile (`Projected::over`, `curios-elab/src/into_ersd/projected.rs`).

**A unit is addressed by the units before it, and believed whole or not at all.** A slot names every unit placed ahead of it in the fold and its record vouches for what each contained. Over a package depending on `alpha` and on `beta`, neither naming the other, an edit to `alpha` compiles `/beta` again, whole; under an umbrella one of whose members reaches `beta` after `alpha` and another `beta` alone, `/beta` is compiled once for each and filed in two slots; and a unit compiled against an edited dependency is compiled whole, a baseline being offered only after the units it was compiled after.

**A program links what computes, observable or not.** A program links every declaration in its scope that computes at initialization, with what each reaches, for the optimizer's prune to drop the ones nothing observes: `programs/hello_world.crs` links 570 items, 136 of them because they compute.

**A question about a program redoes the program.** The entry is no unit (`Fold::check`, `curios-pipeline/src/standard.rs`): nothing of it is kept or filed, so each question lowers, elaborates, judges and erases every declaration it holds. Asked about `programs/walk_mirror_indexed.crs`, 111 lines, that is 0.21 M allocations elaborating and 0.16 M judging its 14 declarations, whichever was edited.

**The file under edit is parsed whole.** The library's 167 files parse in 76.37 M allocations, 96 a byte, and a session parses a file again for every text it holds of it; the finding in [`03-surface`](../03-surface/00-findings.md), which bounds what a keystroke costs once nothing else follows the file.

**A void attempt is forgotten.** An attempt that read a declaration not yet elaborated is made again, and the next compile of the same unit finds the same order again: a whole compile of the prelude voids 43.

## Prior art

- **The goal has a definition.** Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones: "A build system is minimal if it executes tasks at most once per build and only if they transitively depend on inputs that changed since the previous build" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), definition 2.1), and under early cutoff, "When it executes a task and the result is unchanged from the previous build, it is unnecessary to execute the dependent tasks" (section 2.3). The tree today is the paper's Make for what an edit dirties: a static over-approximation, read before anything runs.
- **A trace of what was read decides what is rebuilt.** A verifying trace is "to record the values/hashes of dependencies used last time, and if something has changed, the key is dirty and must be rebuilt" (section 4.2.2). A restarting scheduler, which the elaborator's is, "is also not minimal in the sense that a task may start, do some meaningful work, and then abort" (section 4.1.2).
- **Reads are revalidated in the order they were made.** rustc's try-mark-green: "it is important that we visit each node in `reads(Q)` in same order as they occurred in the original compilation" ([Incremental compilation](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/queries/incremental-compilation.html)). A small query over a large one is a firewall: "even if the result of the monolithic query changes (which it is very likely to do) the small projections can still mostly be marked as green" ([Incremental compilation in detail](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/queries/incremental-compilation-in-detail.html)).
- **A result is known by a hash of what it says.** rustc: "Each time a new query result is computed, the query engine will compute a 128 bit hash value of the result", so that marking "can just compare the (already loaded) previous fingerprint to the fingerprint of the new result" ([Incremental compilation in detail](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/queries/incremental-compilation-in-detail.html)). Unison: "Each Unison definition is identified by a hash of its syntax tree", so "we can parse and typecheck definitions once, and then store the results in a cache which is never invalidated" ([The big idea](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/)).
- **A position is relative to what holds it.** rustc: "Changing the contents of a function may require redoing part of the compilation process for another function in another file", answered by a flag that "encodes local spans relative to the span of an item" ([rust-lang/rust#84762](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/84762)). Roslyn's green node "tracks its *width* but not its *absolute position*" ([Persistence, facades and Roslyn's red-green trees](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/archive/blogs/ericlippert/persistence-facades-and-roslyns-red-green-trees)).
- **A file has a summary a body's edit does not move.** rust-analyzer's `ItemTree` "condenses a single `SyntaxTree` into a "summary" data structure, which is stable over modifications to function bodies", under the invariant that "typing inside a function's body never invalidates global derived data" ([Architecture](https://rust-analyzer.github.io/book/contributing/architecture.html)).
- **The peers cut off the same way.** Skyframe: "If a node is invalidated, but upon rebuild, it is discovered that its new value is the same as its old value, the nodes that were invalidated due to a change in this node are 'resurrected'" ([Skyframe](https://bazel.build/reference/skyframe)). salsa backdates: "even though the inputs changed, the output didn't" ([The red-green algorithm](https://salsa-rs.github.io/salsa/reference/algorithm.html)).
- **Every stage is keyed, not one.** Sixty's build library "keeps track of what dependencies a task used when it was executed (much like Shake) in a table, i.e. what keys it fetched and what the values were", over a key for each parsed module, each resolved module and each definition's type ([Query-based compiler architectures](https://ollef.github.io/blog/posts/query-based-compilers.html)).
- **What is known not to have changed is not revalidated.** salsa's durability: "If a tracked function depends (transitively) only on high durability inputs, and you change a low durability input, then we can very easily determine that the tracked function result is still valid, avoiding the need to traverse the input edges one by one" ([The red-green algorithm](https://salsa-rs.github.io/salsa/reference/algorithm.html)). A unit whose own digest stands is such an input here: every read of it stands with it.
- **A read of another unit carries what it read.** GHC's `Usage` records, for a module of the same package, the "Entities we depend on, sorted by occurrence name and fingerprinted", and "if we don't use anything from X it won't appear in our Usage" ([`GHC.Unit.Module.Deps`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/ghc-9.10.1/docs/src/GHC.Unit.Module.Deps.html)). Go stamps a package with "the action ID, which is a hash of the inputs to the action that produced the packages or binary, and the content ID, which is a hash of the action output", and "we use the content IDs to compute the next action ID" ([`buildid.go`](https://go.dev/src/cmd/go/internal/work/buildid.go)).
- **A declaration is the grain, in a proof assistant too.** iCoq "tracks fine-grained dependencies between Coq definitions, propositions, and proofs, and only checks those proofs affected by changes between two revisions" ([Çelik, Palmskog and Gligoric](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~gligoric/papers/abstracts/CelikETAL17iCoq.txt), ASE 2017). Leino and Wüstholz cache a verifier's results per procedure ([Fine-grained Caching of Verification Results](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/fine-grained-caching-verification-results/), CAV 2015). Lean's own incremental elaboration reuses a prefix: "If all the syntax inspected up to a certain point is unchanged, we can assume that the old state can be reused" ([`Lean.Language.Lean`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Lean/Language/Lean.html)).
- **The peers split a signature from a body by declaring the body opaque.** Rocq's `.vos` is "similar to `foo.vo` except that all opaque proofs are skipped in the compilation process" ([Compiled interfaces](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/V9.0.0/refman/practical-tools/coq-commands.html)), and under Lean's module system "Changes to files that affect only non-exported information (e.g. proofs) will not trigger rebuilds outside of these files" ([The Module System](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/4.24.0/The-Module-System/)). No body is opaque here, so which bodies a declaration unfolded is recorded, never declared.
- **A lookup is a read whatever it finds.** Kotlin's incremental compiler keeps "for each file … the ABI it produces, and the names (not full declarations) that were looked up during its compilation" ([The Dark Secrets of Fast Compilation for Kotlin](https://blog.jetbrains.com/kotlin/2020/09/the-dark-secrets-of-fast-compilation-for-kotlin/)), and in Swift "The "depends" sets are tracked by instrumenting lookups in the compiler" ([Dependency analysis](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift/blob/main/docs/DependencyAnalysis.md)). GHC "identifies orphan modules, and visits the interface file of every orphan module below the module being compiled" for the instance no name leads to ([Separate compilation](https://ghc.gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/doc/users_guide/separate_compilation.html)). Curios's orphan rule and its keys spelled before elaboration are what let a goal's read be one key, found or absent.
- **A scope is read as it is asked for.** rustc's metadata is "A list of lazily-decoded values, with the added capability of random access" ([`rustc_metadata::rmeta`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/src/rustc_metadata/rmeta/mod.rs.html)). In Lean's `.olean` files "Lean terms and values are represented just as they are in memory", so a file "can be directly memory-mapped" ([Elaboration and compilation](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Elaboration-and-Compilation/)), and rkyv, which the store is written with, reads an archive with "just a pointer offset and a cast" ([Zero-copy deserialization](https://rkyv.org/zero-copy-deserialization.html)).
- **Code is collected from roots, over summaries.** rustc's collector: "Lazy strategy means that items will only be instantiated when actually used" ([`rustc_monomorphize::collector`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/nightly/nightly-rustc/rustc_monomorphize/collector/index.html)). ThinLTO's bitcode "is augmented with a compact summary of the module", and "Fast and efficient whole-program analysis is then performed on the combined summary index" ([ThinLTO](https://clang.llvm.org/docs/ThinLTO.html)).

## Decisions

1. **A declaration has a cell for each stage, and a file has a scan.** A declaration's lowered form; what it elaborates to, with the registry entries, vectors and totality it publishes and, where it is a witness, its key; the certifier's verdict on it; its erased form; and the summary of what evaluating it does. A file's scan is its items, each with where it is written and a digest of its text, the names its module binds, and what it exports again. One task writes each, once, and reads another's through its cells alone.
2. **A cell holds no position in a file.** A position in a term is an extent of the declaration that wrote it — an anchor and two offsets from where the anchor's text begins — and holds no source; a file's scan says where each anchor sits. A report resolves an extent where it is made, against the scans in hand, and one whose anchor's text has changed since is reported at the declaration, never at a line it does not lie on. So a declaration an edit moves lowers, elaborates and stores to what it did.
3. **A cell is held, never copied.** A unit is an index of its declarations' cells and is handed over as that index: what a recompile keeps is the baseline's own cell, and what a session keeps, a store restores and a fold hands on is one unit.
4. **A cell is identified by what it says.** A digest of its content, its positions left out and its binders' names kept, computed once for each node: what "the same cell" means wherever a record is stored, across units and across processes. In one process two cells are compared as terms are, by pointer, then by hash, then by structure.
5. **Every read goes through one environment, and is recorded.** A read is a question — a declaration's signature, apart from its body; a registry entry; whether a witness stands at a key; a name looked up in a module; whether the unit's lemmas were on offer — and the identity of its answer, found apart from absent. A task's trace is its questions in the order it asked them. One type holds a trace and one rule says whether it stands, for every stage.
6. **A cell is reused where what it was made from stands.** Its own input is unchanged — an item's text for a lowered form, the lowered form for an elaboration, the elaborated declaration for a verdict and for an erased form — and every question of its trace is answered as it was, asked again in the order it was made, each one's declaration settled first. A cell made again and found the same dirties nothing. The rule does not ask which unit an answer is in.
7. **A trace is its reader's own, stored with its cell.** The lowering's for a lowered form, the elaborator's for what the declaration elaborated to, the certifier's for its verdict, as `Certification` holds it, and erasure's for the erased form.
8. **The certifier decides which of its verdicts stand.** It is handed the baseline whole and adopts a verdict where the declaration's terms are the baseline's and every cell its own filed reads name is unchanged, a declaration's verdict among them. No closure is handed in, and [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)' per-item argument is restated over the certifier's record in the change that makes it so.
9. **Whether a proof was offered the unit's lemmas is one question of its declaration's trace.** The walk elaborates what the vocabulary reads before it offers anything, whole or over a baseline, so a declaration settled on the other side of that line from its trace is elaborated again, and no graph is consulted.
10. **A file's scan is a function of its text, and a file that stands is not parsed.** A file whose text is the one its scan was made from is neither read into a tree nor walked: discovery, the export view and the unit-wide lints are decided from scans and cells.
11. **A declaration's lowered form is a function of its item's text and the lookups it made.** Every name it resolves is a question of its trace, a glob the interface it read. What it writes beside its terms — its mints, the imports it used, the mounts it reached, its lints, a test, a foreign row — is part of its cell.
12. **A scope and a baseline are read where they stand, at every stage.** The kernel's environment reads through to the units as elaboration's and erasure's scope do, a baseline is one more layer beneath the unit's own stores, and a report spells a name of a unit in scope by that unit's own paths, made where the report is: nothing of a scope or a baseline is copied, replayed, validated or keyed again.
13. **A program links what it reaches and what is observable.** Its entry's imports, transitively, and every declaration in scope whose evaluation at initialization something can observe, with what each reaches. Whether a declaration's top-level statement is observable is decided once, where its unit is certified, since totality is the certifier's: a summary is a cell of a declaration — an exported function's behaviour, and what a callee's resolution reads of an exported value — composed as the optimizer's own summary composes them. The prune then keeps what is reached or observable, as it did, so what a program runs at initialization does not follow what it links.
14. **The program asked for is a unit.** Its declarations are cells, compiled over a baseline as any unit's are, and its final term is one more; a session keeps it and a project files it.
15. **A unit is stored and restored by cell.** Reading a declaration reads what it reaches of its unit's file and no other declaration, and filing a unit writes the cells that were made, one node still standing for one structure.
16. **A unit is addressed by who it is.** Its slot names the compiler, its mounts and the prefixes it declared, and no unit before it. What it read of another unit is in its cells' traces, by digest, so a unit after an edited dependency is believed cell by cell.
17. **A unit's documentation record is built where it is asked for.** By the commands that document, from the unit's stored tables and its sources; no unit carries one. The prelude's records are an image of their own, made by the build that lowers its roots and restored by nothing that compiles.

## Open questions

- **How a unit's file is laid out.** One archive read by part and by declaration through one kept pool of shared nodes serves reading and leaves a write whole; a table of nodes with each cell a set of roots serves both. Which, is decided on how an image divides between scans, lowered, elaborated and erased forms and records, which is not measured.
- **What restoring by cell costs conversion.** Two cells restored apart from one consed image share their nodes only where the restoration keeps one pool, and conversion's first test is a pointer.
- **How the parser's cost divides** between its boxed actions, its memo's entries and backtracking, which decides the fix; the finding in [`03-surface`](../03-surface/00-findings.md).
- **A position copied from another declaration.** An elaborated term may hold an extent of the declaration whose signature it was instantiated from. Whether such an extent is ever what a report is placed by is not known.

## The components

| Component | Treatment | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verdicts | Adopted by the certifier from the baseline by its own reads; its environment a view | Certifier |
| Positions | An extent of a declaration in every term; resolved where a report is made | Positions |
| Scans | One for each file, by its text; a standing file not parsed | Lowering |
| Lowered forms | One for each declaration, by its item's text and its lookups; unit-wide passes over scans and cells | Lowering |
| Documentation, path table | Built where asked for | Lowering |
| Reads | Recorded where the environment is asked, in order, hits as misses, stored with the cell | Records |
| Invalidation | By trace, revalidated in order, stopping where a cell comes out the same; `invalidated` and `vocabulary_reach` are deleted | Records |

| Builds | Compile a moved unit over its baseline, as a question does | Records |
| The store | Written and read by cell; a digest for each node | Storage |
| Addresses | A slot is its unit's own; a read of another unit carries a digest | Units |
| Observability | A summary cell for each declaration, made where its unit is certified; a program links the observable ones | Linking |
| The entry | A unit, its final term a cell | Program |

## Stages

Each lands alone, on its own check, and says what it expects to move; a move outside that is a finding.

1. **Certifier.** Decision 8, and decision 12 for the kernel. Expected to move: what the kernel judges of a recompile, and what it costs before a compile's first declaration.
2. **Positions.** Decision 2. Expected to move: a stored unit's bytes, by the positions and sources its lowered module no longer holds, and nothing a report says.
3. **Lowering.** Decisions 10, 11 and 17, and decision 12 for the path table. Expected to move: a recompile's parse, its lowering and what deciding costs, a stored unit's bytes, by its scans, traces and the record it no longer carries, and what restoring the prelude costs.
4. **Records.** Decisions 5 to 7 and 9, and decision 12 for erasure's view. Expected to move: a stored unit's bytes, by its traces; what elaboration costs, by the recording; which declarations a recompile redoes; and what a build compiles of a moved unit.
5. **Storage.** Decisions 4 and 15. Expected to move: what filing and restoring cost, and every stored byte.
6. **Units.** Decision 16, and decision 6 across units. Expected to move: every slot's address, and which declarations an edit to a dependency compiles.
7. **Linking.** Decision 13. Expected to move: a stored unit's bytes, by its summaries, what a program links, and every row of a program's compile behind its erasure.
8. **Program.** Decision 14. Expected to move: which declarations a question about a program redoes, and what a question files.

## Verification

- **The cost rows.** `cargo xtask profile programs/hello_world.crs --profile release` builds the compiler and files the program's rows. For the library, a copy of `curios-std/src` with no `.curios` beside it, and `target/release/curios --profile <stream> wonder diagnostics <copy>/Nat.crs`, folded by `curios profile <stream>`: as it is; with `pub let _census_probe(a: Nat) -> Nat = a;` appended to `Nat.crs`; and with `xor(b, true)` respelled `xor(true, b)` in `Bool.crs`, asked of `Bool.crs`. The rows are `restore_archives`, `parse` over its modules, `into_core_unit` less it, `compile_unit_over` less its children, `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over`, `recheck_module`, `erase_unit`, `cache::baseline`, `cache::put`, and for a program `compile_entrypoint`, `elaborate_and_zonk`, `recheck` with `recheck_module` beneath it, `erase_unit`, `link_program`, `writable_paths` and `lower_from_ersd`, each by its allocations. Counted. Their durations are a second measurement, timed, the median of repeated readings on a quiet machine, and no stage quotes one it has not taken so.
- **What a row's own time is.** Where a span's children do not account for it, the release compiler is run under `gdb -batch -ex run -ex bt` and interrupted at a delay stepped across the span, one stack a run, and the stacks are counted by the function that stands beneath the span. Timed, and good for naming a span's halves, no finer.
- **Reproduction.** `std_unit_reproduction` and `std_recompile_reproduction` after every stage that changes what a unit stores: a unit compiled over a baseline is the bytes it is compiled whole.
- **Certifier.** A module handed to the certifier over a baseline with a dependency changed and its dependent presented unchanged is judged again where it read the dependency, held in `curios-cert/src/recheck/`; `budget_tests` holds a program to the budget it takes whatever its scope holds; the hub's `recheck_module` row and a program's `recheck` row are retaken.
- **Positions.** A declaration moved by an edit above it and then refused by a later pass is reported at its new line, and every report of `curios-pipeline/src/tests/` and the corpus is what it was.
- **Lowering.** A unit lowered over a baseline is the unit lowered whole, its lints and reports with it, held by `incremental_tests` over an edited body, an added declaration, a `pub use` moved and a glob whose module gains a member; a file whose text stands is not parsed, held by the `parse` rows of a question in a process that had read nothing; the pages a library's build writes are the ones it wrote from the record its unit carried, held by `curios/src/tests/document.rs`.
- **The trace is complete.** An ignored measurement beside `std_replay_census`: each declaration of `/std` elaborated against the declarations its trace names and no other gives the declaration the library holds, and the same of its lowered form against its lookups. Counted.
- **Invalidation.** A recompile over a baseline redoes exactly the declarations whose recorded reads changed, held by fixtures over a signature change, a body change that comes out the same, a witness added at a key a goal found empty, and a declaration moved into what the prover's vocabulary reaches; and the hub's rows are retaken.
- **Storage.** The image's division, as bytes of each part archived alone and as `restore_archives`' stacks; what `hello_world` and the unedited question read of each part; the `verdicts::put` and `restore_archives` rows before and after.
- **Units.** Over a package depending on two packages that do not name each other, an edit to either leaves the other reused; under an umbrella two members that reach one package after different others file it once; and an edit to a dependency's body that no declaration of its dependent unfolded redoes none of them. Held by tests in `curios/src/tests/packages.rs`.
- **Linking.** An ignored measurement over the prelude: every declaration the whole-program summary calls observable, the stored one does too, and the count the other way is its precision. `hello_world`'s rows are retaken. Counted.
- **Program.** A question about a program after one of its declarations' bodies is edited redoes that declaration and what read the edit, held by a fixture; the `walk_mirror_indexed` rows are retaken.
- **The void attempts.** The `demand::void` samples of the profile the prelude's elaboration files, `curios-prelude-archive/.artifacts/profile.tsv`. Counted.

## Rejected

- **salsa.** At 0.28.5, which describes itself as experimental, with no 1.0; its database model would own the term representation, and its invalidation would sit beside the store's trust argument rather than under it. The graph here has thousands of nodes and obligations — budgets, deterministic identities, charged caches — that a small component states directly.
- **A record and a rule for each stage**, the lowering's by text and interfaces, the elaborator's by its reads, erasure's by a closure: one rule kept in agreement by being read five times.
- **The certifier's reads as the elaborator's record.** They are what its judgment of the finished terms read, and a key found absent, a candidate tried and dropped and a lemma offered and not applied are in no finished term.
- **The names an elaborated declaration reaches as the record.** It is what a unit's items are ordered by, and it holds no read that left no name.
- **A closure the pipeline hands the certifier**, narrowed by the elaborator's record: the trusted base would rest on a record the certifier did not make.
- **A source held as one allocation for a path and a text**, so that two positions' sources compare by pointer wherever a text stands: every cell still holds a text, a declaration an edit moves is still another form, and the archive's reader needs an `unsafe` adapter to keep the invariant.
- **Positions moved onto the new text where a declaration is kept**: a walk of every kept declaration of an edited file, for a form that should not have said where it was written.
- **A lowered form reused a file at a time**, a file that changed lowered whole and its declarations compared with the baseline's: the comparison this removes, paid for the file an author is in.
- **A digest compared inside a process.** Where a baseline is in hand it holds the cell itself, and a term's comparison ends at a pointer or a cached hash; a digest is what a record holds.
- **A cell archived on its own.** What a consed image collapses is between definitions — `/std` is 30,635 distinct structures — so cells that shared no node would store each of them once for every declaration that spells it.
- **A unit addressed by the closure of what it depends on.** The closure needs an order no fold gives twice, and a unit after an edited dependency is still compiled whole.
- **A stored order of declarations**, Excel's calc chain. A reused declaration's reads are settled before it is examined, in the order its trace gives, so only a declaration made again can meet one unfinished, and a whole compile has no baseline to learn an order from.
- **Linking every declaration in scope and pruning**, or every one that computes: a program's link, its first analysis and its first prune walk what the library reaches, to drop nearly all of it for every program.
- **Evaluating a top-level value only where it is read.** It is the language's meaning that moves, a declaration whose initialization is observable running or not by what a program names; OCaml links a library's module only where it is referenced, "If this flag is not given, unreferenced modules are not linked in" ([`-linkall`](https://ocaml.org/manual/5.3/native.html)), and that is the choice.
- **The documentation record kept on the unit and reused a page at a time.** Every question about a library would build or copy what two commands read, and every process that compiles would restore the prelude's.
- **The entry left a program beside the units.** Its declarations are the ones an author edits most, and nothing of them would be kept.

## Completion and retirement

Done when every stage's cell for a declaration is written once and reused where what it read stands, within a unit and across units; a recompile parses, lowers, elaborates, judges, erases and stores the declarations whose reads changed and no other; a compile restores what it reads of its scope; a program links what it reaches and observes; and the program asked for is compiled as a unit is. The rule, its rationale and what is rejected go to a design decision under `documentation/design/compilation/`; the environment's, the store's and the linker's contracts are in the owning crates' READMEs and rustdoc, and `.claude/rules/`' area routing names them.
