# A declaration is a function of what it reads

Working specification for making what a declaration compiles to depend on its own text and on what it reads of other declarations, and on nothing else: not the order declarations elaborate in, not how many elaborated before it, not whether its unit was compiled whole or over a baseline. As a check, elaborating any one declaration alone against the finished unit gives the declaration the unit holds. One environment every read goes through, and a record of those reads, are how the property is built and then used: the record says what an edit redoes, orders the kernel, and schedules the work [item tasks](03-item-tasks.md) run in parallel, whose contract that every number of workers gives the same bytes is this property read once more.

It needs [the rule that no memo outlives its declaration](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md), [identities no other compilation could mint](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-carries-no-identity-another-compilation-could-mint) and [the certifier's record](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-later-walk-reads-the-certifiers-own-totality-record-never-elaborations-stamp). It changes how the elaborator moves from one declaration to the next, and what a read of a declaration not yet finished means.

## What this builds on

- **Three computations of the item graph**: the lowering's sort (`curios-text/src/into_core/order.rs`), the kernel's `dependency_order` (`curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`), and the recompile closure `invalidated` (`curios-pipeline/src/recompile.rs`).
- **The `Cache` seam and the stored-unit format** (`curios-pipeline/src/compile.rs`, `curios-unit`): a product-supplied policy the fold consults, and a serialized unit that already crosses processes.
- **The certifier's record and reads.** Each unit carries `curios_core::Certification`: per definition, its totality and what judging it read (`curios_core::Reads`) — the types, universe schemes and registry entries it consulted and the bodies it asked for — a group's members sharing the group's reads and a declaration's entry filed with its type former. Positivity, one judgment over the whole declaration set, is attributed to no item. `curios-pipeline`'s `the_prelude_reads_along_the_graph_its_definitions_reach` holds the reads inside the elaborated graph.
- **An item can be undone, and elaborated apart from its unit.** `ItemMark::undo` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/recovery.rs`) takes a refused item out of every store a later item reads, and `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over` elaborates a set of items against the rest of their unit replayed as a predecessor.
- **A top-level definition writes its type** ([Top-level definitions](../../syntax.md#top-level-definitions)), so its universe levels are all a signature leaves open.

## The gap

**A declaration elaborated alone is not always the one the compile produced.** Of `/std`'s 2,170 items, 1,856 can be elaborated apart from what is generated with them, and 8 of those differ from the archived unit's, counted at `87189c97d` by the replay under *Verification*. `/std/tcp/Socket/close_raising` and `/std/tcp/Listener/close_raising` take one universe parameter where the archive holds none. `/std/Nat/le/mul_mono_r`, `/std/Nat/div_mod/div_lt_of_lt_mul`, `/std/Nat/Divides/trans` and `/std/Nat/Divides/mul` differ in how an inferred implicit is spelled, `@ap * k` for `@k * ap`. `/std/Char/to_ascii_upper` and `/std/Str/At/onward` differ in a proof the elaborator wrote. The last six follow the number of unrelated declarations elaborated first: `mul_mono_r` is spelled one way after none, another after one, and as the archive holds it after five.

**Four reads are answered by what has elaborated so far.**

- **The witness table.** `resolve_witness` (`curios-elab/src/resolve.rs`) answers a rigid key with no entry `Resolution::Missing`, and the goal is deferred, retried after every later item and reported once the unit has elaborated. Over `/sys` and `/std`, 162 goals defer and the sweeps defer them again 5,679 times, counted at `87189c97d`. A declaration that closes with a goal pending has that goal's levels made ground (`UniverseSolver::finalize`'s `pending`), which is what decides the two `close_raising`; and a goal that never resolves is reported after its item has finished, which retracts the item and every kept item that reaches it (`Survivors::retract`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/recovery.rs`).
- **Whether a head is a monad.** `lift_on_check` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/binding.rs`) wraps an action in `lift` only where `is_monad` finds a `Monad` witness registered under each head. With `let run(n: Nat) -> Io(Nat) = boxed(n);` over a `Box` whose `satisfy Monad(Box)` and `satisfy Lift(Box, Io)` are declared after `run`, the program is refused, `type mismatch`, `Box(Nat)` against `Io(Nat)`; with them declared before, it is accepted.
- **What the bound prover may write.** `in_scope` (`curios-elab/src/entailment.rs`) lets a proof form apply only names assumed in the context so far, so what the elaborator proves for an item of `/std` follows which of its vocabulary has elaborated.
- **Where a witness is ordered.** `witness_dep_nodes` (`curios-text/src/into_core/order.rs`) orders a use after every witness row of a concept it dispatches through and drops the edge where it deadlocks, so whether a witness precedes its use follows what else the unit declares.

**A definition and a witness that reach each other are refused by the kernel, and not by name.** With `let describe(b: Box) -> Str = Show/show(b);` and a `satisfy Show(Box)` whose `show` calls `describe`, the elaborator accepts both and the kernel refuses `/describe`, ``unbound name `/witness@0` ``. `check_witness_cycles` names a cycle between two witnesses only.

**A cell is written more than once.** A witness goal resolved late solves a metavariable inside an item already finished, which the module's zonk closes after every item. An inductive's telescopes keep a metavariable until a later witness registers where a struct's are zonked as its definition finalizes ([Theory: findings](../02-theory/00-findings.md)). Polarity vectors and totality stamps are written onto finished declarations by the passes that run after every item (`check_positivity`, `record_totality`).

**More than reads flows in.** One binder, metavariable and universe counter serves a unit, in the lowering and in elaboration, so a local's identity counts the declarations before it; an atom's rank is its structural hash (`curios-core/src/atoms.rs`), a product's factors are ordered by rank, which `curios-algebra/src/product.rs` states as "a monomial's factor order is a hash, and a hash is not a value", and a product rebuilt from its normal form is spelled in that order. A deferred goal is retried under the budget of the item it is retried after, and the passes after the items spend one budget between them.

**The fold is a line.** `Predecessors` are every unit before this one rather than what this one depends on, and `curios-package` has the dependency graph and flattens it into that line.

**Every compile re-seeds all its predecessors.** Erasure projects every predecessor's Core and elaboration re-seeds every definition, `Globals::of` copies each registry and maps each definition once per predecessor unit, and the kernel copies the result again (`Kernel::seed`) and recomputes positivity over every declaration in scope. Compiling `programs/hello_world.crs`, `compile_entrypoint` takes 533 ms (528 to 545): elaboration 22, the kernel 31 of which positivity is 19, erasure 81, and the back half 385, which is [item tasks](03-item-tasks.md)'s. Timed, the median of nine compiles under a release `profile` build at `87189c97d`. An item-level recompile pays the same floor for each question: `std_recompile_closure_census`'s leaf edit, whose closure is one item, recompiles in 1.3 s, the median of five readings that all read 1.3 s.

**The item graph is computed three times, each over names.** An edit to one item of `/std` reaches 39.2 items on average through the reverse closure `invalidated` takes, 5 at the median and 1,449 for `/std/ops/Cmp`, where the kernel's recorded body reads name 5.8 with the item itself, 1 at the median. `/std/Bool/not`'s body, the census's hub, reaches 1,123 items and is read by 50. Counted at `87189c97d` over the restored prelude.

**The kernel walks a module.** `verdicts_within` declares every registry entry before any item and accepts each after all of them, and decides positivity and reconciles the carried variance vectors once the walk is over, so no verdict is a declaration's own before the walk ends. An item-level recompile hands it the reassembled module and rests on its skipping by name ([The certifier confirms what it skips](../01-soundness/02-the-certifier-confirms-what-it-skips.md)).

**A signature is not final before its body.** `finalize_definition` settles a written signature's universe context with the constraints its body raised (`finalize_universe_metas(interface, internal)`), and the surface has no syntax for a level, so Lean's rule — the signature alone fixes the universe parameters — is not available.

**A proof body is not opaque.** Eliminating `Accessible` into data reduces the proof, and a relevant `match` on an `Eq` proof reduces it to `refl` ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../../design/theory/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)). A later item may need any body.

## Prior art

- **A build is correct when recomputing any task from the finished store gives what the store holds.** Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones define it so, "the result of recomputing the corresponding task matches the value stored in the result", for a task that "just computes a single output, in a side-effect-free way, using a callback (fetch) to find the values of its dependencies" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), definition 3.1 and section 3.2). They classify what a build does when a task needs one not yet built: Excel "aborts computation of C, moves D before C in the calc chain, and resumes the build starting with D", and Shake "suspends their execution until the dependencies are brought up to date". And what a later build redoes: a verifying trace records "the values/hashes of dependencies used last time", and under early cutoff a task whose "result is unchanged from the previous build" stops the rebuild, since then "it is unnecessary to execute the dependent tasks". The tree today is the paper's Make: a topological order over dependencies read before anything runs, and a static over-approximation of what an edit dirties.
- **A language whose declarations need not precede their uses is checked by queries.** rust-analyzer's account of [three architectures](https://rust-analyzer.github.io/blog/2020/07/20/three-architectures-for-responsive-ide.html) keeps the snapshot-after-headers design for languages with a "declaration before use" rule, and answers the rest by demand, where "we compensate for the deficit of laziness with incrementality". Curios's reference lets a witness follow the code that resolves through it ([Witness resolution](../../syntax.md#witness-resolution)).
- **The peers that check in parallel fix a statement without its proof.** Lean 4.19 elaborates theorem bodies in parallel ([lean4#7084](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/pull/7084)), for a theorem outside a `mutual` block, and its module system stops exporting a definition's body, for the reason it gives: "Changes to files that affect only non-exported information (e.g. proofs, comments, and docstrings) will not trigger rebuilds outside of these files" ([Source Files and Modules](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Source-Files-and-Modules/)). Rocq processes only opaque proofs asynchronously, and needs the theorem's statement without looking at its proof ([Asynchronous and Parallel Proof Processing](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/v9.1/refman/addendum/parallel-proof-processing.html)).
- **A worker that waits can deadlock.** rustc's parallel front end holds its output to the serial one's — its binaries "are expected to be the same no matter which front-end is being used" — and says of its threads that "there are some known bugs, including deadlocks" ([Faster compilation with the parallel front-end in nightly](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2023/11/09/parallel-rustc/)).
- **GHC reads every instance head before it checks any body**, which [A universe level settled before its evidence is in](../02-theory/02-levels-settled-before-their-evidence.md) cites: what a head gives is its key.

## Decisions

1. **A declaration is a function of what it reads.** What a declaration publishes — its key where it is a witness, its signature, its body, its registry entries with their vectors, its totality, the kernel's verdict and its erased form — is determined by its lowered text and by the cells it read of other declarations. Elaborated alone against an environment holding every other declaration as the compile published it, it publishes the same.
2. **The declaration is the unit of work.** A definition, a recursive group, a `struct`, `induct` or `concept` with every item the lowering generates from it, a witness. A unit remains what names, privatizes and caches: its mounts, its manifest, its record and its slot in the store are unchanged in role, and the store stays per unit. Units are not scheduled; a declaration in one reads the declarations of another, so the package graph is honoured without a scheduler of its own.
3. **An answer does not depend on when it is asked.** The witnesses of a concept are known by key before a question about that concept is answered. A key is a cell of its witness, computed from the witness's elaborated signature when first asked for, and a question about a concept — a goal, whether a head is a monad, a report's survey of declared edges — reads every key the unit's witnesses of that concept have. A lookup that finds no key reports no witness where it is asked.
4. **A read of a declaration not yet finished voids the attempt.** What the attempt wrote is undone, the declaration it needed is elaborated first, and the attempt is made again. An attempt is never suspended to run another inside it, and never waits on one. An attempt that read an incomplete answer is void whatever it went on to conclude.
5. **A cycle is refused by its members.** A declaration demanded while it is being demanded is a cycle, through a written name, a chosen witness or a key alike. It is refused at its first member in source order, naming every member.
6. **A cell is written once**, by the declaration that owns it, before any other declaration reads it.
7. **Nothing but reads flows in.** A declaration's identities — binders, metavariables, universe levels — count from zero for that declaration, in its lowering and in its elaboration; its solver, its caches and its budget are its own; and each pass over it spends a budget restored for it.
8. **A signature is published with its body.** A declaration that reads another's type reads it once that declaration's elaboration has finished. Publishing a signature earlier is a refinement for declarations whose signature provably fixes its own universe context: over `/sys` and `/std` the graph admits 6.2 times one worker's throughput on 8 workers with it and 7.4 times without, and 8.0 against 11.8 on 16, counted in allocations at `87189c97d` as *Verification* states.
9. **A body another declaration needs is read, never withheld.** No body is treated as opaque.
10. **One environment, and every read goes through it.** A declaration is a record of cells. Every read of another declaration is a request for one of those cells, or a question to the witness index, and is recorded: a signature read apart from a body unfolded, a key found apart from a key absent. That record is the one item graph, which scheduling, the kernel's order and invalidation are read off.
11. **The kernel judges a declaration.** Its verdict is a cell of the declaration it judged, with what the judgment read.

## The components

| Component | Treatment | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The driver | Items keep their lowered positions and the module keeps the lowered order; elaboration order becomes demand order. Demanding an item first demands every pending item its lowered form reaches. An attempt that recorded a need is undone, the need demanded, and the item tried again | 2 |
| Witness resolution | Against an index of every witness the unit declares, under the concept its lowered head names, each with a key cell; a key is computed at an item boundary by elaborating the signature and undoing it. A lookup answers from a registered witness, needs the witness where its key is known and it is pending, needs the keys where the concept's are incomplete, and otherwise reports no witness. The deferred store, its sweeps, `finalize`'s `pending` and the retraction of finished items are deleted | 2 |
| Refusal recovery | An item whose demand reaches a refused declaration is withheld, as today. A void attempt is undone as a refused one is and poisons nothing | 2 |
| The bound prover | A vocabulary name is in scope for an item where a predecessor declares it or it is lowered before the item, and demanded where it is still pending. What it rests on once the lowering's order goes is under *Open* | 2, then 4 |
| Whole-module passes | A declaration is zonked, its goal reports collected, and its polarity and variance vectors, its totality stamp and its erasure obligations decided when it finishes, each pass on a budget restored for it. What is about the whole set stays after the items: the entry, and the concept registry's check | 3 |
| Identities | The lowering's counters start at zero for each source declaration, and `Minted` and the table of unbound names are per declaration | 3 |
| Elaboration context | An environment — published definitions, registries, the witness index, totality, mounts, and what the unit accumulates for its lints, obligations and goal reports — and a per-declaration state — frames, solutions, universe solver, caches, fresh names, budget — replaced whole at each declaration | 3 |

| Invalidation | By recorded reads, revalidated in the order they were made, stopping where a re-elaborated cell comes out equal | 4 |

## Stages

Each lands alone, on its own check, and says what it expects to move; a move outside that is a finding.

1. **The gate.** The replay under *Verification* becomes `curios-pipeline`'s `std_replay_census`, a measurement beside `std_recompile_closure_census`, over declarations rather than items, so a type former replays with what is generated from it. Nothing else moves.
2. **Complete answers.** Decisions 3 to 5. Expected to move: the two `close_raising`, to one universe parameter; the programs under *The gap*, accepted in either order or refused as a cycle; nothing else of `/std`. [A universe level settled before its evidence is in](../02-theory/02-levels-settled-before-their-evidence.md)'s acceptance is this stage's.
3. **Isolation.** Decisions 6 and 7, in three landings: the passes that write a cell run when its declaration finishes; the lowering mints per declaration; the elaboration context is an environment and a per-declaration state. Expected to move: the bodies of the `/std` items whose spelling or proof followed the unit-wide counter, once, and no scheme.
4. **The recorded graph.** Decisions 10 and 11 within a unit. The elaborator records its reads; the lowering's sort, `dependency_order` and `invalidated` are replaced by the recorded graph; the kernel judges each declaration as it is published; a recompile over a baseline revalidates in read order; and [cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/admission/cached-verdicts.md)' per-item argument is restated over recorded reads in the same change. The critical path is retaken over the elaborator's reads.
5. **One environment across units.** `Established`, `Globals`, `Resumed` and `Predecessors` become views of it, so a unit's prologue costs what the unit reads.

## Verification

- **The replay.** For each declaration of `/std`, `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over` with its closure that declaration as the unit's sources lower it and its reused items the archived unit less it, and each definition's universe context, type, body and totality and each registry entry compared with the archived unit's; then again with unrelated authored definitions lowered before it added to the closure, which elaborate first. Counted. Until stage 1 a declaration is one item, and the constructors, method wrappers, concepts and formers that elaborate only with what is generated from them are left out.
- **The differential.** Before and after each stage: every prelude item's universe context, type and body digested, `curios-prelude-archive`'s `universe_parameter_census`, and the module each program of `programs/` compiles to. A difference outside what the stage expects is a finding, never a fixture update.
- **Stage 2.** `curios`'s `tests::universes` holds both programs of [A universe level settled before its evidence is in](../02-theory/02-levels-settled-before-their-evidence.md) in both orders; `tests::concepts` holds the monad program in both orders and the definition and witness that reach each other, refused naming both. The replay reports no declaration that differs by a universe parameter.
- **Stage 3.** `curios-pipeline/src/tests/incremental_tests.rs` asserts over its fixtures what the replay measures over `/std`, with and without unrelated declarations first, and the replay reports nothing under any number of them.
- **Stage 4.** A recompile over a baseline redoes exactly the declarations whose recorded reads changed, held by fixtures over a signature change, a body change, and a witness added, and `std_recompile_closure_census` is retaken for its hub.
- **Stage 5.** The compile of `programs/hello_world.crs` is retaken as *The gap* states it.
- **The graph's figures.** The nodes are the restored prelude's items and an edge is a name `Module::reaches`; an edge is the interface's where a definition's type or a registry entry reaches the name, and the body's otherwise; body readers are the items whose `Certification::reads` hold the name among their bodies. A declaration's weight is the allocations its `declaration` span made in `curios-prelude-archive`'s build under `profile`. Throughput on n workers is the summed weight over the finishing time of a list schedule that hands the lowest-positioned ready item to a free worker; with the signature first, an item is two tasks, its signature waiting on the signatures its interface reaches and its body on those and on the bodies it reads.
- **The deferral count.** `curios-prelude-archive`'s build script, which elaborates `/sys` and `/std` and is built unoptimized, run under a debugger with a breakpoint on `Solutions::defer_witness` that prints its callers: `attempt_witness_goal` and `typing::retry_one` are goals deferred for the first time, and `retry_deferred_witnesses` is a sweep deferring one again.

## Open

- **What the bound prover may use once the lowering's order is gone.** Its vocabulary is a read the source does not write, and two items that each could use the other's lemma cannot both go first, so availability needs a rule that is a function of the program: today it is the lowering's order, which stage 2 keeps and stage 4 deletes. Reachability does not settle it alone, since neither of two unrelated lemmas reaches the other.
- **What the kernel's barrier keeps.** Positivity is one judgment over every declaration in scope, recomputed whole on each compile, where a family's variance is read from the vector its unit carries ([Compilation: findings](00-findings.md)); registry entries are declared before the walk and accepted after it. Whether a declaration's polarity vector is a cell the certifier files, read as variance is, is a decision about the trusted base, taken before stage 4.

## Design decisions this overturns or corrects

- [A universe level is implicit, cumulative, and settles by where it came from](../../design/theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-came-from.md): a goal still pending when its consumer closes, and its rejection of elaborating a missing witness first. Stage 2.
- [Concepts resolve with global coherence](../../design/surface/concepts-resolve-with-global-coherence.md) and [Witness resolution](../../syntax.md#witness-resolution): the end-of-module sweep, and a rigid key with no entry yet deferring. Stage 2.
- [A stored unit is a baseline for an item-level recompile](../../design/compilation/a-stored-unit-is-a-baseline-for-an-item-level-recompile.md): a deferred goal retried and agreement short of equality, at stages 2 and 3; invalidation by recorded reads, at stage 4.
- [`curios-unit`'s README](../../../curios-unit/README.md): every counter starting at zero for a unit, at stage 3; *Predecessors are borrowed, per stage*, with `Predecessors`' own documentation, at stage 5.
- [Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/admission/cached-verdicts.md): the per-item argument restated over recorded reads. Stage 4.
- [A module is a compilation unit, and the prelude is an environment](../../design/compilation/a-module-is-a-compilation-unit-and-the-prelude-is-an-environment.md): a compilation stops being units folded over one dependency order. Stage 5.

## Rejected

- **salsa.** Before 1.0 with breaking releases, it removed its experimental parallel feature in 0.25.0; its database model would own the term representation, and its invalidation would sit beside the store's trust argument rather than under it. The graph here has thousands of nodes and obligations — budgets, deterministic identities, charged caches — that a small component states directly.
- **Lean's signature rule**, that a signature alone fixes a declaration's universe parameters: it needs level syntax the surface does not have.
- **Opaque proof bodies**, the reason Lean's theorem bodies and Rocq's proofs parallelize freely: Curios reduces proofs where it eliminates `Accessible` and matches on `refl`.
- **Publishing a signature speculatively and rolling back dependents** when the body disagrees: the answer would be deterministic and the work would not, and a dependent's diagnostics would be computed against a signature that never existed.
- **Elaborating the unfinished declaration inside the attempt that needs it**, Shake's suspension: it needs a context per declaration before any answer becomes complete, and under several workers it leaves the cell another worker holds, which waits.
- **Waiting on the task that holds a cell**: a worker that waits needs a deadlock detector, which is what rustc's parallel front end lists open.
- **Ordering uses after their witnesses in the lowering**, with more or harder edges: which witness a use chooses is a typing fact the lowering cannot know, so the edges over-approximate and deadlock.
- **Clearing a shared context between declarations**, field by field: a field added later flows from one declaration to the next unless its author remembers the list, where a state replaced whole cannot carry one.
- **Renumbering a declaration's identities as its elaboration begins**, in place of minting them per declaration: every table keyed by a lowered identity — unbound names, a level's origin — would follow the renaming.

## Completion and retirement

Done when every declaration of `/std` and of the corpus is the declaration its replay gives, every read of one declaration by another goes through the environment and is recorded, the item graph is computed once, and the kernel judges each declaration as it is published. The rule, its rationale and what is rejected are a design decision under `documentation/design/compilation/`; the environment's contract is in the owning crates' READMEs and rustdoc, and `.claude/rules/`' area routing names it.
