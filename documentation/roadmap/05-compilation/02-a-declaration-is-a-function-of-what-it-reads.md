# A declaration is a function of what it reads

Working specification for making what a declaration compiles to depend on its own text and on what it reads of other declarations, and on nothing else: not the order declarations elaborate in, not where any of them is written, not how many the unit or its scope holds or what they cost, not whether its unit was compiled whole or over a baseline. Two checks state it. Elaborating any one declaration alone against the finished unit gives the declaration the unit holds; and a unit accepts a declaration at every budget the declaration alone is accepted at, and stores the same items wherever its named declarations are written.

It needs [the rule that no memo outlives its declaration](../../design/soundness/a-reduction-step-costs-what-it-builds.md), [identities no other compilation could mint](../../../curios-unit/README.md#a-unit-carries-no-identity-another-compilation-could-mint) and [the certifier's record](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-later-walk-reads-the-certifiers-own-totality-record-never-elaborations-stamp). What the property buys — an edit that redoes what read it and no more, a compile that costs what it reads, work on several workers — is [A compile costs what it reads](03-a-compile-costs-what-it-reads.md) and [item tasks](04-item-tasks.md), which both need this whole.

## What this builds on

- **A unit's items elaborate on demand, and an answer does not depend on when it is asked** ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). An item elaborates after what it reads, an attempt that read a declaration not yet elaborated — its name, its body, a witness's key or a registry entry — is void and made again, declarations that need one another are refused by name, and a witness is known before it elaborates by the key its signature spells (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/demand.rs`, `curios-elab/src/concept/spelled.rs`).
- **A declaration's identities and its state are its own** (the same decision). `curios_core::Minted` holds what each source declaration's lowering minted, a declaration being an item with the items generated from it; `Context::attempt` replaces the elaboration state where an attempt begins; and a finished item is published into the context zonked, as a unit in scope is replayed.
- **An item can be undone, and elaborated apart from its unit.** `ItemMark::undo` and `ItemMark::void` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module/recovery.rs`) take a refused item and a void attempt out of every store another item reads, and `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over` elaborates a set of items against the rest of their unit replayed as a predecessor.
- **The elaborator writes a declaration's cells once, each on a budget of its own** (the same decision). `settle_published` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) decides a declaration's polarity vectors, holds its variance vectors to its final telescopes, stamps its totality and decides its erasure obligations as it is published; no pass runs over a unit after its items, a recompile writes none of a reused declaration's cells, and erasure and the replay of a scope's witnesses restore their budget for each item and each witness. `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::budget_tests` hold a unit to the budget one of its declarations takes.
- **A proof's lemmas are a function of what the vocabulary reads** (the same decision). The walk asks for the declarations of the prover's vocabulary first, which brings in every declaration they read, with no lemma of the unit on offer, and offers all of them from then on (`Demand::all`, `Context::proof_may_apply`); compiled over a baseline, it asks with them for what the vocabulary reads through a reused item (`vocabulary_reach`, `curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`).
- **A unit's items are stored in one order** (the same decision). `curios_core::Module::ordered` puts a module's items each after the items it reaches and otherwise by name, once, where the elaborator makes the module, whole or over a baseline; the certifier and erasure walk the items as stored, and neither sorts.
- **The lowering hands a unit's items on as they are written** (the same decision). It sorts nothing and refuses no cycle: the elaborator's walk asks for each declaration when it is read, refuses the ones that need one another by name (`Error::declaration_cycle`), and reports refusals in the order they are written.
- **The kernel judges a declaration, and files its polarity vector** (the same decision). `verdicts_within` (`curios-cert/src/recheck.rs`) checks an item, classifies it and holds its erased positions, then accepts the registry entries it declares, their positivity, their variance and their sizing each on a budget restored for it and over those entries alone. Each unit carries `curios_core::Certification`: per definition, its totality and what judging it read, and per family, the polarity vector a later walk composes through ([Cached verdicts](../../design/soundness/cached-verdicts.md)). The walk that files it decides where every declaration of a module stands before it judges any, and writes its environment once ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../../design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)).
- **A top-level definition writes its type** ([Top-level definitions](../../syntax.md#top-level-definitions)), so its universe levels are all a signature leaves open.

## The gap

Nothing of the design is left to land. What is left is to read the property over the library once more, by the measurements *Verification* names, before this specification is retired.

## What bounds the design

**A signature is not final before its body.** `finalize_definition` settles a written signature's universe context with the constraints its body raised (`finalize_universe_metas(interface, internal)`), and the surface has no syntax for a level, so Lean's rule — the signature alone fixes the universe parameters — is not available.

**A proof body is not opaque.** Eliminating `Accessible` into data reduces the proof, and a relevant `match` on an `Eq` proof reduces it to `refl` ([`Prop` is strict, proof-irrelevant, and definitionally K](../../design/theory/prop-is-strict-proof-irrelevant-and-definitionally-k.md)). A later item may need any body.

**A witness is named by where it is written.** `WitnessId` is the declaring module and an ordinal among that module's witnesses, so a witness moved past another in its module is another declaration, and so is every declaration that resolves through it ([findings](00-findings.md)). The order of decision 13 and its check hold for a unit's named declarations wherever they are written, and for its witnesses in the order each module writes them.

## Prior art

- **A build is correct when recomputing any task from the finished store gives what the store holds.** Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones define it so, "the result of recomputing the corresponding task matches the value stored in the result", for a task that "just computes a single output, in a side-effect-free way, using a callback (fetch) to find the values of its dependencies" ([Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), definition 3.1 and section 3.2). They name what a void attempt is, *restarting*: "whenever a task calls fetch on an out-of-date key dep, abort the task, and switch to building the dependency dep", which is "not minimal in the sense that a task may start, do some meaningful work, and then abort" (section 4.1.2).
- **The industrial restarting scheduler asks nothing to wait.** Bazel's Skyframe hands a function `null` for a dependency that is not built, "the function itself is expected to return `null`", and "the dependent node is evaluated, and then the original node builder is invoked again" ([Skyframe](https://bazel.build/reference/skyframe)).
- **A language whose declarations need not precede their uses is checked by queries.** rust-analyzer's account of [three architectures](https://rust-analyzer.github.io/blog/2020/07/20/three-architectures-for-responsive-ide.html) keeps the snapshot-after-headers design for languages with a "declaration before use" rule, and answers the rest by demand, where "we compensate for the deficit of laziness with incrementality". Curios's reference lets a witness follow the code that resolves through it ([Witness resolution](../../syntax.md#witness-resolution)).
- **The peers' kernels judge a declaration as it is added.** Lean's reference says so: "Before new inductive types or definitions are added to the environment by the command or term elaborators, they must be checked by the kernel" ([Elaboration and Compilation](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Elaboration-and-Compilation/)), and since 4.17 the kernel's environment is held apart from the elaborator's so that the two may run beside each other ([lean4#5145](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/pull/5145), [lean4#6368](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/pull/6368)). Agda checks "that the datatypes in the mutual block containing the given declarations are strictly positive", a block at a time, and adds "information about positivity and recursivity of records to the signature" ([`Agda.TypeChecking.Positivity`](https://hackage-origin.haskell.org/package/Agda-2.6.0/docs/Agda-TypeChecking-Positivity.html)).
- **The peers that check in parallel fix a statement without its proof.** Lean 4.19 elaborates theorem bodies in parallel ([lean4#7084](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/pull/7084)), for a theorem outside a `mutual` block, and its module system stops exporting a definition's body, for the reason it gives: "Changes to files that affect only non-exported information (e.g. proofs, comments, and docstrings) will not trigger rebuilds outside of these files" ([Source Files and Modules](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Source-Files-and-Modules/)). Rocq processes only opaque proofs asynchronously, needs the theorem's statement without looking at its proof, and signals universe inconsistencies late ([Asynchronous and Parallel Proof Processing](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/v9.1/refman/addendum/parallel-proof-processing.html)).
- **A worker that waits can deadlock.** rustc's parallel front end holds its output to the serial one's — its binaries "are expected to be the same no matter which front-end is being used" — and says of its threads that "there are some known bugs, including deadlocks" ([Faster compilation with the parallel front-end in nightly](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2023/11/09/parallel-rustc/)).

## Decisions

1. **A declaration is a function of what it reads.** What a declaration publishes — its key where it is a witness, its signature, its body, its registry entries with their vectors, its totality, the kernel's verdict and its erased form — is determined by its lowered text and by the cells it read of other declarations. Elaborated alone against an environment holding every other declaration as the compile published it, it publishes the same.
2. **The declaration is the unit of work.** A definition, a recursive group, a `struct`, `induct` or `concept` with every item the lowering generates from it, a witness. A unit remains what names, privatizes and caches: its mounts, its manifest, its record and its slot in the store are unchanged in role.
3. **An answer does not depend on when it is asked.** The witnesses of a concept are known by key before a question about that concept is answered: a key is read off its witness's signature as it is written, when the unit begins, and registration holds the elaborated signature to it.
4. **A read of a declaration not yet finished voids the attempt**, whatever is read: a name, a witness key, a registry entry, a verdict. What the attempt wrote is undone, the declaration it needed is elaborated first, and the attempt is made again. An attempt is never suspended to run another inside it, and never waits on one.
5. **A cycle is refused by its members**, once, by the elaborator: a declaration demanded while it is being demanded is a cycle, through a written name or a chosen witness alike, refused at its first member as written, naming every member.
6. **A cell is written once**, by the declaration that owns it, as that declaration finishes and before any other reads it: its vectors, its totality and its erasure obligations with its terms. No pass over a unit writes a declaration's cell, and a recompile writes none of a reused declaration's.
7. **Nothing but reads flows in.** A declaration's identities — binders, metavariables, universe levels — count from zero for that declaration, in its lowering and in its elaboration; its solver, its caches and its budget are its own; each judgment of it, in either checker and in erasure, spends a budget restored for it; and what bringing a scope in costs is spent on no declaration's.
8. **A signature is published with its body.** A declaration that reads another's type reads it once that declaration's elaboration has finished. Publishing a signature earlier is a refinement for declarations whose signature provably fixes its own universe context: over `/sys` and `/std` the graph admits 6.2 times one worker's throughput on 8 workers with it and 7.4 times without, and 8.0 against 11.8 on 16, counted in allocations at `87189c97d` as *Verification* states.
9. **A body another declaration needs is read, never withheld.** No body is treated as opaque.
10. **The kernel judges a declaration.** Its item, then what the item declares: the registry entry accepted, its positivity and its variance decided over the declaration's own families, its totality classified, its erased positions held. Each verdict is the declaration's own when its judgment ends.
11. **The certifier files a declaration's polarity vector**, as it holds its variance vector, and a later judgment reads it there. One answer for both vectors: a filed vector stands as a cached verdict does, and judging a declaration reads the vectors of the families it mentions, never every declaration in scope. The vector an entry carries stays elaboration's, which no judgment of the kernel reads.
12. **A proof the elaborator writes applies the vocabulary, and the vocabulary applies none.** The prover's vocabulary and every declaration it reads, by a name it writes or a witness its elaboration resolves to, are elaborated before the rest of their unit and are given no lemma of it; every other declaration is given all of them, each finished by then. Which lemmas a declaration has is then a function of what the vocabulary reads, and of nowhere anything is written, and no proof the elaborator writes makes one declaration wait on another. Of the 144 lemma applications the elaborator wrote into 57 declarations of `/std`, none is in a declaration the vocabulary reaches; counted at `0283c7242` as *Verification* states.
13. **A unit's items are stored in one order, read off what they name**: every item after the items its elaborated form reaches, and where that leaves a choice, by name. The module is put in that order once, where it is made, whole or over a baseline, and every later walk takes it: the kernel, which refuses an item that names a later one as unbound, and erasure. No stage sorts again, and the lowering hands its items on as written.

## Verification

- **Budget independence.** `curios-pipeline/src/tests/budget_tests.rs`, each fixture finding the smallest budget one declaration is accepted under, by doubling and then halving to within a sixteenth, and putting many to it, so none states a figure the library's growth would move: sixteen independent families are checked, by the elaborator and the kernel together, under the budget one of them is; a program is checked under the budget it takes once its scope holds a unit of witnesses it never asks for; and sixteen values, each at a type of its own that costs to read, erase under the budget one of them does.
- **Order independence.** `curios-pipeline`'s `tests::order_tests`: a unit and the same named declarations written in another order store the same elaborated module, record and erased arena.
- **The replay.** `curios-pipeline`'s `std_replay_census`, an ignored measurement: for each declaration of `/std`, `elaborate_and_zonk_unit_over` with its closure that declaration as the unit's sources lower it and its reused items the archived unit less it, compared with the archived unit's; then again with unrelated authored definitions added to the closure, which elaborate first. Counted.
- **Whole and over a baseline.** `curios-pipeline/src/tests/incremental_tests.rs` holds a unit compiled over a baseline to the bytes it is compiled whole, and `std_recompile_reproduction` the standard library.
- **The differential.** Before and after each stage: by name, every prelude item's universe context, type, body and totality, every registry entry with its vectors, and the certifier's record, digested; `curios-prelude-archive`'s `universe_parameter_census`; and the module each program of `programs/` compiles to. A difference outside what the stage expects is a finding, never a fixture update.
- **The graph's figures.** The nodes are `/std`'s declarations, an item with the items generated from it. An edge is a name `Module::reaches` gives for an item of one, declared by another: lowered, over the module `into_core_unit` gives for the library's tree, and elaborated, over the restored prelude's. A lemma application the elaborator wrote is a name of `SyntaxRegistry::entailment`'s `targets` the elaborated declaration reaches and the lowered one does not; the vocabulary reaches a declaration where one of those names' declarations does, through any number of elaborated edges; and a declaration's place as written is `Minted::from`'s. For decision 8, a declaration's weight is the allocations its `declaration` span made in `curios-prelude-archive`'s build under `profile`, and throughput on n workers is the summed weight over the finishing time of a list schedule that hands the lowest-positioned ready item to a free worker; with the signature first, an item is two tasks, its signature waiting on the signatures its interface reaches and its body on those and on the bodies it reads. Counted.

## Rejected

- **Lean's signature rule**, that a signature alone fixes a declaration's universe parameters: it needs level syntax the surface does not have.
- **Opaque proof bodies**, the reason Lean's theorem bodies and Rocq's proofs parallelize freely: Curios reduces proofs where it eliminates `Accessible` and matches on `refl`.
- **Publishing a signature speculatively and rolling back dependents** when the body disagrees: the answer would be deterministic and the work would not, and a dependent's diagnostics would be computed against a signature that never existed.

## Completion and retirement

Done when a unit accepts a declaration at every budget the declaration alone is accepted at, in the kernel as in the elaborator and in erasure; a unit stores the same items wherever its named declarations are written; every declaration of `/std` is the declaration its replay gives; no pass over a unit writes a declaration's cell; and the items are ordered once. The rule, its rationale and what is rejected are [A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md), which each stage extends; the contracts are in the owning crates' READMEs and rustdoc, and `.claude/rules/`' area routing names what a change to a declaration's judgments must also inspect.
