# Every crate decides visibility at each hop

Held to [Visibility is decided at each hop](../design/visibility-is-decided-at-each-hop.md): a name is private or `pub` and every module private, a crate root names its API and never globs, a record's fields are all `pub` and an owner's all private, and a scoped modifier sits only on a method narrower than its type. `curios-archive-derive`, `curios-cranelift` and `curios-js` keep it, and `curios-elab` and `curios-erase` nearly do; every other crate departs from it, and no row of the lint table holds any of it.

## What is wrong today

Counted at `f5bfe7f60`; every figure is `counted`. Clippy's are the distinct spans of `cargo clippy --workspace --exclude 'x*' --all-targets --message-format=json -- --cap-lints warn -W clippy::<lint>`, which reports a site once for each target it is compiled in; the jig's crates and `pub(super)` are counted by a text search of each crate's tracked `.rs` files for a line that opens with a scoped modifier outside an `impl` or `trait` block.

- **27 crate roots export by glob**, 214 `pub use x::*` lines. A glob exports every `pub` name below it, hides the unused ones from rustc's dead-code analysis, and turns a respelling to `pub` into an export: 14 crate-private structs, among them `curios-cert`'s `Held` and `Over`, would join their crate's API if respelled today.
- **755 items carry `pub(crate)`**: 669 in the shipped crates by `redundant_pub_crate` (377 functions, 144 structs, 48 imports, 46 enums, 34 constants, 12 type aliases, 7 traits, a module) and 86 in the jig's. The densest crates are `curios-emit` (136), `curios-cps` (130), `curios` (72) and `curios-cert` (71); `curios-erase` holds 5.
- **926 items carry `pub(super)` or `pub(in …)`**, which no lint reports. The densest crates are `curios-cert` (227), `curios-surface` (158), `curios` (100), `curios-core` (96) and `curios-module` (90); `curios-erase` holds 5.
- **328 fields carry a scoped modifier**: 265 in 94 structs of 20 shipped crates by `field_scoped_visibility_modifiers`, and 63 in the jig's (`xtask/src/tree.rs` 20, `xbench/src/reading.rs` 13, `xboard/src/ticket.rs` 11). Of the 99 shipped structs either field lint names, 67 give every field their own reach, a `pub(crate)` field on a `pub(crate)` struct, and are a respelling; 32 are a record or an owner to choose. The densest files are `curios-emit/src/emission.rs` (22), `curios-anf/src/remap.rs` (13), `curios-cps/src/analysis.rs` (12), `curios/src/tests/laws/structural.rs` (11) and `curios-cps/src/optimize/clone.rs` (10); the ten in `curios-prelude/src/sys_module/` are compiled into its build script too.
- **Eight structs mix `pub` fields with others** (`partial_pub_fields`): `Source` (`curios-common/src/span.rs`), `Minted` and `Imported` (`curios-core/src/names.rs`), `InductArm` (`curios-core/src/term/shape.rs`), `BlockData` (`curios-emit/src/frame.rs`), `Loaded` (`curios-module/src/into_core.rs`), `Pinning` (`curios-package/src/pin.rs`) and `Indexed` (`curios-unit/src/indexed.rs`). `InductArm`'s rustdoc says its body and marks are sealed behind `InductArm::new` so the two "can never drift apart", and its `pub body` can be reassigned from any crate; none does.
- **A method may carry a modifier no narrower than its type**: a `pub(crate)` method on a type that stays in its crate, as four of `Opened`'s are in `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`. How many there are elsewhere is known once a crate's root names its API, since a glob carries out of the crate types that will stay in it.
- **The two converted crates hold leftovers.** `curios-elab`'s root names `declaration_input` and `lowered_digest` for `curios-pipeline`'s tests alone, and five facade clusters write their `pub use` before their `use`: `curios-elab/src/lib.rs`, `convert.rs`, `context.rs` and `entailment.rs`, and `curios-erase/src/lib.rs`.
- **The lint table holds none of it.** Neither field lint nor `redundant_pub_crate` is configured, and the table denies warnings, so a row cannot stand ahead of its sites.

## Goal

Every crate keeps the decision, the jig's included: its root names its API, no item but a method narrower than its type carries a scoped modifier, and every struct is a record or an owner. `field_scoped_visibility_modifiers`, `partial_pub_fields` and `redundant_pub_crate` stand at `warn` in `Cargo.toml`'s `[workspace.lints.clippy]` and in `editors/zed/Cargo.toml`'s table, the decision names `redundant_pub_crate` beside the two field lints, and every lint step of the gate passes.

## Settled

- **The rows at `warn`**, as the table's others are; `warnings = "deny"` makes a hit an error.
- **`redundant_pub_crate` is all that holds the items.** `pub(super)` on an item, a glob in a root's `pub use` and a method no narrower than its type are held by review; the workspace writes no check of its own for them.
- **A crate is taken from the top of the dependency graph down**, the jig's first and the leaves last, so a root names its API once every crate that reads it has shed its dead code: rustc's dead-code analysis skips an exported name, so a list named first would keep a name its readers stop reading.
- **A crate's root is named before any of its names is respelled `pub`**, since under a glob a respelling is an export. The root names what another crate's production code reads. A name only tests read, in its crate or another, is reached under the crate's `test_support`: moved there where nothing in production reads it, and named there where the crate's own production does. A name nothing reads once the glob is gone is deleted.
- **A member reaches as far as its type**: a method keeps a scoped modifier only where it is narrower than the type's reach, and a field keeps none.
- **A field some readers may touch and others may not is an owner's**, whose fields none touch and whose operations are its methods. What a sibling module does with an owner's fields moves onto the owner as a method, and an accessor stays only where the value itself is what the sibling needs, as a memo's key. `ParserState` and `ParserError` (`curios-parse`) are read by six siblings for the span or the progress between two states, the rest of the input, the character before, a literal prefix and the ranking of two failures, each an operation of theirs; `into_cps/lowerer.rs` calls the builder of `Emitter`'s `module` (`curios-anf/src/into_cps/emitter.rs`) 144 times, and those calls become `Emitter`'s methods. An `impl` block outside its type's module subtree moves under it: `ForeignBindings`'s in `curios-native/src/plugin.rs` and `Verdicts`'s in `curios-verdict/src/payload.rs`. A struct none of whose fields holds an invariant is a record instead.
- **The eight mixed structs.** Owners: `Source`, whose line starts cache its `text`; `InductArm`, whose marks parallel its body's binders; `Pinning`, whose `directory` is its manifest's; `Indexed`, whose index names exactly the objects it holds; `Loaded`, whose tree is parsed from its source. Records: `Minted`, whose shared entries only make its archive smaller; `Imported`, whose spelling is read off fields that hold no invariant; `BlockData`, none of whose fields depends on another.
- **The other judgment structs.** Owners: `ParserState` and `ParserError` (`curios-parse`); `Emitter`, `RowLayout`, `ConstructionTemplate` and `Mark` (`curios-anf`); `CopyPlan` (`curios-cps`); `EmissionBody`, whose escapes are derived from its blocks and tail, and `ForceWalk` (`curios-emit`); `Grader` and `SizeContext` (`curios-analysis`); `Unseen`, `Shared`, `Lowerer` and `Aliases` (`curios-module`); `Running`, `ForeignBindings`, `MachineCode` and `Compiled` (`curios-native`); `Verdicts` and `Taken` (`curios-verdict`); `Store` (`curios-pipeline`'s tests). Records: `ParsedCli` (`curios/src/cli.rs`), the flags clap parsed, and `SysModule` (`curios-prelude/src/sys_module/constructors.rs`), whose `hoisted` is a mode flag.
- **The `test_support` records are records**: the nine structs holding the 26 fields sit in `#[cfg(test)]` modules of their own crate, and eight already give every field their own reach.

## Stages

1. **The crates, one at a time, from the top down**: `xtask`, `xbench`, `curios`, `curios-wonder`, `curios-verdict`, `curios-pipeline`, `curios-prelude`, `curios-unit`, `curios-package`, `curios-erase`, `curios-module`, `curios-cert`, `curios-browser`, `curios-elab`, `curios-surface`, `curios-emit`, `curios-anf`, `curios-analysis`, `curios-wasm`, `curios-native`, `curios-cps`, `curios-core`, `curios-parse`, `curios-document`, `curios-algebra`, `curios-abi`, `curios-num`, `curios-common`, `curios-archive`, `curios-unicode`, `curios-profile`, `curios-print`, `curios-binaryen`. In each, the root names its API; each item's scoped modifier gives way to private or `pub`, the facades deciding reach; each method keeps one only where narrower than its type; each struct becomes a record or an owner; and what nothing reads once the root names its API is deleted. Check, per crate: `cargo xtask format`, then `cargo clippy --workspace --exclude 'x*' --all-targets --keep-going -- --cap-lints warn -W clippy::field_scoped_visibility_modifiers -W clippy::partial_pub_fields -W clippy::redundant_pub_crate`, which reports nothing but those three lints and none of them in the crates taken; the jig's crates by the same run over `--package 'x*'`, and `curios-native` by `cargo xtask native lint` too. After each layer — the jig's and `curios` to `curios-pipeline`, `curios-prelude` to `curios-analysis`, and the rest — `cargo xtask workspace lint` and `cargo xtask workspace test`.
2. **The three rows in both lint tables**, with the decision's sentence naming `redundant_pub_crate`. Check: every lint step of the gate — `workspace lint`, `tools lint`, `native lint`, `instrumented lint`, `zed lint` — and `zed test`.

The next step is a ready delivery: Stage 1's first crate.

## Retirement

Once the rows hold, the decision's sentences are the code's state; nothing references this specification, and it is deleted.
