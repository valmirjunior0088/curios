# The packed carrier has one grain

Working specification for retiring `Bits`, the bit-grained packed carrier, from the language, the host boundary and the toolchain, leaving `Bytes` as the one packed carrier and the compiler's internals named after it. It is a removal: nothing `Bits` does is rebuilt elsewhere. Neither `/std` nor the surface names `Bits` any more; what is left is the carrier below them, and the internals' names.

## What this builds on

- **One packed carrier at two grains.** `curios_num::Binary` is a run of bits read at a `Grain`, `B` an atom a bit and `X` an atom a byte (`curios-num/src/binary.rs`), and every packed intrinsic takes the grain as data: `BinType(Grain)`, `Bin(Grain, Binary)`, `BinLen`, `BinEql`, `BinGet`, `BinSlice`, `BinAppend`, `BinConcat`, `BinReplicate`, `BinAnd`, `BinOr`, `BinXor`, and `BinReinterp`, the one that crosses grains (`curios-core/src/intrinsic.rs`). `/sys/Bits` and `/sys/Bytes` are the two instantiations (`bin_ops` and `regrain_op`, `curios-text/src/sys_module.rs`); `/std/Bytes` is the library over the byte grain, and nothing in `/std` reads `/sys/Bits`, whose row in the roster of `/sys`'s readers (`SYS_READERS`, `curios-prelude-archive/src/tests.rs`) names none.
- **The surface writes one packed literal**, `x[…]` ([`syntax.md`](../syntax.md), *Packed literals*), and its tree holds no grain: lowering writes `Grain::X` (`lower_bin_literal`, `curios-text/src/into_core/lowerer.rs`), and `curios-text/src/from_core.rs` answers no text for a packed form or pattern at `B`.
- **Two run-time representations per grain.** A short run rides an `i31` whose layout is the grain's (`ImmediateLayout::of`, `curios-emit/src/immediate.rs`: three bytes, or twenty-six bits), and a longer one is a `$rope/bin` whose length field counts the grain's atoms; each grain has its own force, exact, embed, box, norm, slice and read helpers (`bytes_*` and `bits_*` in `curios-emit/src/table.rs`, `$bits/force` walking in bit units).
- **The host boundary carries both.** `WireLeaf::Bits` and `WireType::Bits` (`curios-abi/src/host/store.rs`, archived in a unit and in a stored payload's record of its foreign rows, `curios-verdicts/src/payload.rs`), the wire vocabulary the parser reads (`curios-text/src/parse/top_level.rs`), the runtime's `Bits` binding type (`curios_runtime::Bits`), and the JavaScript harness's `Bits` and `List(Bits)` codecs (`curios-js/src/harness.js`).
- **The carriers' algebra is declared per carrier.** `curios-algebra`'s law table states concat, length and equality rows for `Packed(Grain::B)` beside `Packed(Grain::X)`'s, the inverse pair between the grains, and `Boolean` as `Bits`'s element (`curios-algebra/src/law.rs`); a word over a packed run compares bit by bit at `B` (`curios-algebra/src/word.rs`). The law grid under `curios/src/tests/laws/` states no row at `B`, since `/std` no longer spells the carrier to state one in: `at_the_bit_grain` (`generated.rs`) filters them, and the grid's spellings answer `unreachable!` there.

## The gap

**`Bits` has no consumer.** Neither `/std` nor the surface nor any program under `programs/` names it; a program can still take a bit run across the host boundary through a `Bits` row, and nothing else.

**Its cost is spread through the toolchain.** At `a274348aa`, before `/std/Bits` was removed, `git grep -c -E "Grain::B|\bBits\b|bits_" -- '*.rs'` counted 648 lines across seventeen crates, counted: `curios` 200, `curios-emit` 192, `curios-core` 54, `curios-text` 44, `curios-ersd` 38, `curios-runtime` 29, `curios-elab` 26, `curios-cert` 13, `curios-abi` 12, `curios-num` 11, `curios-cont` 9, `curios-algebra` 9, `curios-pipeline` 6, `curios-prelude-archive` 2, `curios-verdicts` 1, `curios-profile` 1, `curios-analysis` 1. Beside them: a wire type in the host ABI and the JavaScript harness; and a compile-time `Binary` kept bit-granular for both grains, so every byte-grain operation asserts an alignment only `Bits` can break (`Binary::len`, `Binary::byte`, `Binary::to_bytes`).

**The two-grain machinery names the carrier twice.** What serves both grains is spelled `Bin` — the intrinsics, `$rope/bin`, `emit_bin_len`, `LoadAs::Bin`, `Carrier::Packed(Grain)` — and the surface says `Bytes`. With one grain left the split stands for nothing.

## Prior art

- **Erlang keeps both, as a type and its aligned subtype.** "A bitstring is a sequence of zero or more bits, where the number of bits does not need to be divisible by 8. If the number of bits is divisible by 8, the bitstring is also a binary"; the default unit of a binary segment is 8, and building or matching one that is not byte-aligned raises an error ([Bit Syntax](https://www.erlang.org/doc/system/bit_syntax.md)). Erlang's bit syntax serves protocol parsing at the bit level, a consumer Curios does not have.
- **Lean 4 keeps a byte buffer and a fixed-width bit vector, apart.** `ByteArray` and `BitVec` are sibling modules of its core library ([Lean API](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/navbar.html)), and `BitVec n` is a bit vector of a width its type states, whose proofs `bv_decide` automates ([Lean 4.12.0](https://lean-lang.org/blog/2024-10-3-lean-4120)) — a fixed-width word for reasoning about machine arithmetic, not a variable-length bit string. Curios's bit-level reasoning goes through `Nat`'s bitwise operations, which are already intrinsics.

## Decisions

1. **`Bytes` is the one packed carrier.** No bit-granular carrier remains in the language, `/sys`, `/std`, the host ABI or the run-time representation. A program that needs a bit string later gets one as a `/std` type built over `Bytes` with its length beside it, not as a second carrier.
2. **It is a removal.** Nothing `Bits` does is ported to `Bytes`. A test that names `Bits` is one of three kinds, and each is treated by its kind: a test of the bit grain itself is deleted with it — its law rows, the regrain intrinsic, the `i31` layout at twenty-six bits; a test over every carrier keeps its other carriers and loses its `Bits` case — printing, charges, the law audit, capacity, the host boundary; and a test of machinery the grains share whose one fixture is a `Bits` program keeps its subject, its fixture rewritten over `Bytes`, or over `List(Bool)` where it needs an element of one bit. Every test of the third kind has been rewritten, and every test whose program named `/std/Bits` treated, since none compiles without it; the tests that still name the grain are listed under *The tests left*, each with its kind and stage.
3. **The surface keeps `Bytes` and `x[…]`.** The internals take the surface's name once one grain remains, the whole family that spells the packed carrier `Bin`: the intrinsics (`BinType`, `Bin`, `BinLen`, `BinEql`, `BinGet`, `BinSlice`, `BinAppend`, `BinConcat`, `BinReplicate`, `BinAnd`, `BinOr`, `BinXor`), `BinSegment` and `Literal::Bin`, `BinPattern` and `MatchPattern::Bin`, `FreeMonoid::Bin`, `Constant::Bin`, `SequenceGrain::Bin`, `BinChunk`, `BinRest` and `BinLevel`, `Repr::Bin`, `Carrier::Bin` and `Carrier::Packed`, `LoadAs::Bin`, `$rope/bin`, and the emitter's `bin_*` helpers — about 1,600 spellings at `1a8c6cedf`, `git grep -o -E "\bbin_[a-z_]+|\bBin[A-Z][A-Za-z]*|Bin\(" -- '*.rs'`, counted — and every other variant that names the packed carrier beside its sibling carriers: `FieldShape::Packed` (`curios-ersd/src/schema.rs`), `IntrinsicForm::Packed` (`curios-core/src/print.rs`) with its reader in `curios-text/src/from_core.rs`, and the law grid's `Value::Packed` (`curios/src/tests/laws/semantics.rs`). `packed` stays where it describes the payload's form — `packed_bound`, `packed_bytes`, a packed run — and `curios-num`'s `Binary` keeps its own name, the payload's representation and not the carrier. `Radix::Bin` (`curios-text/src/nat.rs`) is a numeral's base and is no part of the family.
4. **Bit-level work goes through `Nat`.** `Nat`'s `and`, `or`, `xor`, `shl` and `shr`, and `Byte`'s conversions to and from `Nat`, are what a program has; no bit accessor is added, since nothing asks for one.
5. **`Binary` becomes byte-granular.** Its windows are kept at a bit offset and a bit length for both grains (`curios-num/src/binary.rs`), and with `B` gone nothing reads one at a bit: the byte grain's readers take `bit_length` for a charge, a comparison or an offset scaled by `Grain::bits`, and the charge comes out in bytes already — `packed_bound(Grain::X, bits)` is `Cost::packed_bytes(bits / 8)` (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/cost.rs`) — so a window kept in bytes charges the same units. What only an unaligned window reaches goes with it: the alignment assertions of `len`, `byte` and `to_bytes`, and the bit-by-bit walks `concat`, `to_packed_bytes`, `and`, `or` and `xor` fall back to. `Binary` is archived, so its layout change advances the store's `SCHEMA` and rebuilds the prelude's images; the emitted code does not change, since a `Bytes` rope already counts bytes.
6. **`Grain` is removed outright.** Past choosing between the two grains, nothing reads it but `other`, which only `BinReinterp` calls, `bits`, which is 8 at the byte grain, and debug text — the erasure evaluator's spine key (`d{grain:?}`, `curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/spine.rs`), `curios-ersd`'s test support strings and `curios-emit`'s immediate tests. `curios-abi`, `curios-runtime` and `curios-js` never name it.

## Verification

- **Nothing of the grain is left.** `git grep -n -E "Grain::B|WireLeaf::Bits|WireType::Bits|/std/Bits|/sys/Bits|\bb\["` over the tree finds nothing but this specification until it is retired; after the rename, `\bGrain\b` and `\bBin[A-Z]` find nothing either.
- **What `Bytes` does is unchanged.** Every program under `programs/` and every corpus unit compiles and prints what it printed at `a274348aa`, the commit before `/std/Bits` was removed — each program run with `1000` as its argument and on standard input (`trees` with `10`, which grows exponentially in it), and each corpus unit through `curios test`. The erased form of every `/std` item that survives is the one it was there: each item's erased form printed by `curios-ersd`'s printer under the names it declares, dumped by a scratch test over `curios_prelude_archive::with_prelude` in a copy of the tree, built for both commits at one path, since `std.rkyv` holds absolute paths. Counted.
- **The cost goes.** `/std`'s elaboration units (`cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration`'s `budget::consumed` in all) went from 59,662,520 at `a274348aa` to 59,265,023 at the commit that removed `/std/Bits` and its witnesses, counted. ## Design decisions this overturns or corrects

- [`syntax.md`](../syntax.md), *Foreign declarations*: the wire vocabulary without `Bits`. Carrier.
- [`curios-abi`'s README](../../curios-abi/README.md), the `WireType` decision: its vocabulary without `Bits`, and its rejection of `Bits` carrying its own length dropped with the type. Carrier.
- [A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md): one immediate packed form. Carrier.

## Rejected

- **`Bits` kept as a carrier for a consumer that may come.** It costs every reader of a packed value a grain to dispatch on and the host boundary a wire type, for a program no one has written; a bit string a program needs later is a `/std` type over `Bytes` (decision 1).
- **Porting `Bits`'s tests and fixture to `Bytes`.** A test of the bit grain tests what is gone; what a test of shared machinery tested keeps its subject under a fixture of another carrier (decision 2).
- **Renaming `Bytes` to `Bin`.** The internals' name would reach about 1,070 spellings in `/std` and 910 in the programs and the compiler's tests, and either leave the functions named for bytes — `Str/to_bytes`, `Str/of_bytes`, `Flt/to_le_bytes` — describing a `Bin`, or rename them away from the vocabulary their standards use; `Byte` and `Bytes` read as element and container as `Char` and `Str` do.
- **Respelling the literal `b[…]`, or giving it no letter.** `b[` would rewrite every `x[` in the tree and every pattern to mean what `x[` means; a bare `[…]` typed by its expected type gives one bracket two meanings and leaves a packed literal unable to elaborate without one.
- **`Grain` kept as a one-variant extension point.** Nothing reads a second grain, and a type with one inhabitant states nothing a reader of it can use (decision 6).
- **A bit accessor on `Nat` or `Byte`.** Nothing asks for one (decision 4).

## Completion and retirement

Decisions 1 and 5 go to [`curios-num`'s README](../../curios-num/README.md), which states what a packed run is and how `Binary` holds one; the documents listed above are corrected as their stages land.
