# The packed carrier has one grain

Working specification for retiring `Bits`, the bit-grained packed carrier, from the language, the library, the host boundary and the toolchain, leaving `Bytes` as the one packed carrier and the compiler's internals named after it. It is a removal: nothing `Bits` does is rebuilt elsewhere.

## What this builds on

- **One packed carrier at two grains.** `curios_num::Binary` is a run of bits read at a `Grain`, `B` an atom a bit and `X` an atom a byte (`curios-num/src/binary.rs`), and every packed intrinsic takes the grain as data: `BinType(Grain)`, `Bin(Grain, Binary)`, `BinLen`, `BinEql`, `BinGet`, `BinSlice`, `BinAppend`, `BinConcat`, `BinReplicate`, `BinAnd`, `BinOr`, `BinXor`, and `BinReinterp`, the one that crosses grains (`curios-core/src/intrinsic.rs`). `/sys/Bits` and `/sys/Bytes` are the two instantiations (`bin_ops` and `regrain_op`, `curios-text/src/sys_module.rs`), and `/std/Bits` and `/std/Bytes` the library over them.
- **The surface spells the grain by a letter glued to the bracket** ([`syntax.md`](../syntax.md), *Packed literals*): `b[…]` builds `Bits` and `x[…]` builds `Bytes`, in literals and in patterns, with a fold's `; ih` over either.
- **Two run-time representations per grain.** A short run rides an `i31` whose layout is the grain's (`ImmediateLayout::of`, `curios-emit/src/immediate.rs`: three bytes, or twenty-six bits), and a longer one is a `$rope/bin` whose length field counts the grain's atoms; each grain has its own force, exact, embed, box, norm, slice and read helpers (`bytes_*` and `bits_*` in `curios-emit/src/table.rs`, `$bits/force` walking in bit units).
- **The host boundary carries both.** `WireLeaf::Bits` and `WireType::Bits` (`curios-abi/src/host/store.rs`, archived), the runtime's `Bits` binding type (`curios_runtime::Bits`), and the JavaScript harness's `Bits` and `List(Bits)` codecs (`curios-js/src/harness.js`).
- **The carriers' algebra is declared per carrier.** `curios-algebra`'s law table states concat, length and equality rows for `Packed(Grain::B)` beside `Packed(Grain::X)`'s, the inverse pair between the grains, and `Boolean` as `Bits`'s element (`curios-algebra/src/law.rs`); a word over a packed run compares bit by bit at `B` (`curios-algebra/src/word.rs`).

## The gap

**`Bits` has no consumer.** Inside `/std`, `/std/Bits` (180 lines) is read by its own `Hash` and `Map/Key` witnesses and by `Bytes/to_bits`, and by nothing else; no program under `programs/` names it. What kept it was `/std/BigNat`'s binary naturals, and `Nat` has been unbounded at run time since; their certified arithmetic survives only as a test fixture, `curios-std/src/tests/big_nat` (2,667 lines), which is `Bits`'s largest user.

**Its cost is spread through the toolchain.** At `1a8c6cedf`, `git grep -c -E "Grain::B|\bBits\b|bits_" -- '*.rs'` counts 647 lines across sixteen crates, counted: `curios` 200, `curios-emit` 192, `curios-core` 54, `curios-text` 44, `curios-ersd` 38, `curios-runtime` 29, `curios-elab` 26, `curios-cert` 13, `curios-abi` 12, `curios-num` 11, `curios-cont` 9, `curios-algebra` 9, `curios-pipeline` 6, `curios-prelude-archive` 2, `curios-verdicts` 1, `curios-profile` 1. Beside them: the `b[…]` syntax in the parser, the lowering, the printers and three editors; a wire type in the host ABI and the JavaScript harness; a design decision of its own ([A `Bits` encoding is injective by its extent](../design/standard-library/a-bits-encoding-is-injective-by-its-extent.md)); and a compile-time `Binary` kept bit-granular for both grains, so every byte-grain operation asserts an alignment only `Bits` can break (`Binary::len`, `Binary::byte`, `Binary::to_bytes`).

**The two-grain machinery names the carrier twice.** What serves both grains is spelled `Bin` — the intrinsics, `$rope/bin`, `emit_bin_len`, `LoadAs::Bin`, `Carrier::Packed(Grain)` — and the surface says `Bytes`. With one grain left the split stands for nothing.

## Prior art

- **Erlang keeps both, as a type and its aligned subtype.** "A bitstring is a sequence of zero or more bits, where the number of bits does not need to be divisible by 8. If the number of bits is divisible by 8, the bitstring is also a binary"; the default unit of a binary segment is 8, and building or matching one that is not byte-aligned raises an error ([Bit Syntax](https://www.erlang.org/doc/system/bit_syntax.md)). Erlang's bit syntax serves protocol parsing at the bit level, a consumer Curios does not have.
- **Lean 4 keeps a byte buffer and a fixed-width bit vector, apart.** `ByteArray` and `BitVec` are sibling modules of its core library ([Lean API](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/navbar.html)), and `BitVec n` is a bit vector of a width its type states, whose proofs `bv_decide` automates ([Lean 4.12.0](https://lean-lang.org/blog/2024-10-3-lean-4120)) — a fixed-width word for reasoning about machine arithmetic, not a variable-length bit string. Curios's bit-level reasoning goes through `Nat`'s bitwise operations, which are already intrinsics.

## Decisions

1. **`Bytes` is the one packed carrier.** No bit-granular carrier remains in the language, `/sys`, `/std`, the host ABI or the run-time representation. A program that needs a bit string later gets one as a `/std` type built over `Bytes` with its length beside it, not as a second carrier.
2. **It is a removal.** Nothing `Bits` does is ported to `Bytes`. A test that names `Bits` is one of three kinds, and each is treated by its kind: a test of the bit grain itself is deleted with it — its law rows, the regrain intrinsic, the `i31` layout at twenty-six bits; a test over every carrier keeps its other carriers and loses its `Bits` case — printing, charges, the law audit, capacity, the host boundary; and a test of machinery the grains share whose one fixture is a `Bits` program keeps its subject, its fixture rewritten over `Bytes`, or over `List(Bool)` where it needs an element of one bit. The third kind guards what has nothing to do with bits: `curios`'s `a_recursive_call_read_twice_is_evaluated_once` (`tests/reduction.rs`), a detector of the closed machine's record of a forced call, and `curios-analysis`'s `add_raw_is_accepted_only_because_arms_refine_the_scrutinee`, which holds match refinement in the size order, are two. At `1a8c6cedf` 81 test functions name `Bits`, `b[` or `Grain::B`, 33 of them by a name that speaks of bits, packing or a grain; the library stage sorts all of them and lists each with its kind.
3. **The surface keeps `Bytes` and `x[…]`.** The internals take the surface's name once one grain remains, the whole family that spells the packed carrier `Bin`: the intrinsics (`BinType`, `Bin`, `BinLen`, `BinEql`, `BinGet`, `BinSlice`, `BinAppend`, `BinConcat`, `BinReplicate`, `BinAnd`, `BinOr`, `BinXor`), `BinSegment` and `Literal::Bin`, `BinPattern` and `MatchPattern::Bin`, `FreeMonoid::Bin`, `Constant::Bin`, `SequenceGrain::Bin`, `BinChunk`, `BinRest` and `BinLevel`, `Repr::Bin`, `Carrier::Bin` and `Carrier::Packed`, `LoadAs::Bin`, `$rope/bin`, and the emitter's `bin_*` helpers — about 1,600 spellings at `1a8c6cedf`, `git grep -o -E "\bbin_[a-z_]+|\bBin[A-Z][A-Za-z]*|Bin\(" -- '*.rs'`, counted. `curios-num`'s `Binary` keeps its own name, the payload's representation and not the carrier. The literal's letter stays `x`: `b` becomes an ordinary identifier everywhere, and the tight junction is `x[`'s alone.
4. **Bit-level work goes through `Nat`.** `Nat`'s `and`, `or`, `xor`, `shl` and `shr`, and `Byte`'s conversions to and from `Nat`, are what a program has; no bit accessor is added, since nothing asks for one.
5. **Size-change termination stays.** Its rationale names `/big_nat/add/raw`, `raw_assoc` and `raw_comm` as the corpus that needs it (`curios-analysis/src/totality.rs`, [Totality of the erased program](../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md)); the engine keeps its tests, whose call matrices are built by hand, and the rationale is restated over `/std`'s own. At `1a8c6cedf`, a scratch build whose `group_totality` notes every group it accepts that no parameter shrinks in on every self-call, and every accepted group of more than one member, finds five in `/std` and no proof among them, counted: `/std/List/sort_by`'s `merge` descends on either list by arm, the shape `add/raw` had; and `Cli`'s `check/problems` with `check/beneath`, `parse_command` with `chosen`, `variables` with `variables_of`, and `Tui`'s `asked` with `all` close only across their two members, the closure `raw_comm` needed. The first pair is reached by a type — `Cli/WellFormed` is `Clear(check/problems(command, version))`, a proposition every `Cli` holds a proof of — so a structural rule would leave a declared command line's type unformed, which is the reason the rationale states.
6. **`Binary` becomes byte-granular.** Its windows are kept at a bit offset and a bit length for both grains (`curios-num/src/binary.rs`), and with `B` gone nothing reads one at a bit: the byte grain's readers take `bit_length` for a charge, a comparison or an offset scaled by `Grain::bits`, and the charge comes out in bytes already — `packed_bound(Grain::X, bits)` is `Cost::packed_bytes(bits / 8)` (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/cost.rs`) — so a window kept in bytes charges the same units. What only an unaligned window reaches goes with it: the alignment assertions of `len`, `byte` and `to_bytes`, and the bit-by-bit walks `concat`, `to_packed_bytes`, `and`, `or` and `xor` fall back to. `Binary` is archived, so its layout change advances the store's `SCHEMA` and rebuilds the prelude's images; the emitted code does not change, since a `Bytes` rope already counts bytes.
7. **`Grain` is removed outright.** Past choosing between the two grains, nothing reads it but `other`, which only `BinReinterp` calls, `bits`, which is 8 at the byte grain, and debug text — the erasure evaluator's spine key (`d{grain:?}`, `curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/spine.rs`), `curios-ersd`'s test support strings and `curios-emit`'s immediate tests. `curios-abi`, `curios-runtime` and `curios-js` never name it.

## Stages

Each leaves a tree that builds and passes its checks, in the order that keeps what remains reachable: the library before the syntax that writes its values, the syntax before the carrier it lowers to.

1. **Library.** `/std/Bits` and everything in `/std` that names it, the `big_nat` fixture and `Bits`'s cases in the library's tests, the `Bits` encoding decision, and size-change termination's rationale restated; every test that names `Bits` sorted into decision 2's kinds, and those of the third rewritten over another carrier here, so the stages after delete only what tests the grain. Check: `curios-std`'s test units and the workspace tests; `/std`'s elaboration units (`cargo xtask profile-prelude elaboration`'s `budget::consumed`) before and after, counted.
2. **Syntax.** `b[…]` out of the parser, the lowering, both printers and the editors; `syntax.md`'s packed literals stated for `x[…]` alone. A `b[` left in a program then reads as the name `b` beside a list, and meets the refusal any two juxtaposed terms do: "two terms stand side by side with nothing that joins them: a call is written `f(a, b)`, its arguments in one parenthesized list". No refusal of its own is added. Check: `curios-text`'s parse and print tests, the corpus's canonical-format tests, `editors/grammar`'s `npm test` with `src/` regenerated, `editors/vscode`'s tests.
3. **Carrier.** `/sys/Bits`, `BinReinterp`, and `Grain::B` through `curios-core`, both checkers, erasure, the continuation IR, the emitter, the runtime, the ABI, the JavaScript harness and the algebra; `Grain` keeps its one variant until stage 4. Check: the workspace tests, `curios`'s `tests::laws` and its audit, `tests::host`, `curios-js`'s tests through `cargo xtask bundle`, the store's `SCHEMA` advanced, the prelude's images rebuilt. The website's copies of the harness and of the rustdoc are generated, and follow the release that carries this stage (`../valmirjunior0088.github.io` pins `releaseArtifacts.tag`).
4. **Rename.** `Grain` removed, the internals named after `Bytes`, and `Binary` byte-granular (decisions 6 and 7). Check: the workspace tests, with the codegen and emitter tests that match `$rope/bin` or `bin_len`, and the test support that prints a grain, following the rename; the store's `SCHEMA` advanced; the emitted Wasm of every program under `programs/` the same as before the stage apart from the names, counted by comparing the modules with names stripped.

## Verification

- **Nothing of the grain is left.** `git grep -n -E "Grain::B|WireLeaf::Bits|WireType::Bits|/std/Bits|/sys/Bits|\bb\["` over the tree finds nothing but this specification until it is retired; after stage 4, `\bGrain\b` and `\bBin[A-Z]` find nothing either.
- **What `Bytes` does is unchanged.** Every program under `programs/` and every corpus unit compiles and prints what it printed before stage 1, and the erased form of every `/std` item that survives is the one it was: the prelude's erased images compared item by item at one path (`std.rkyv` holds absolute paths), counted.
- **The cost goes.** The stage-1 commit states `/std`'s elaboration units before and after; stage 3's states `curios-emit`'s and `curios-core`'s line counts before and after, and the `git grep` count of the gap above taken again. Counted.

## Design decisions this overturns or corrects

- [A `Bits` encoding is injective by its extent](../design/standard-library/a-bits-encoding-is-injective-by-its-extent.md): deleted, with the encoding it decides. Library.
- [Totality of the erased program](../design/soundness/totality-of-the-erased-program.md): its rejection of a structural rule keeps its reason and takes `/std`'s own witnesses, `Cli/check/problems` above all (decision 5). Library.
- [`syntax.md`](../syntax.md), *Packed literals* and the grain-letter rule: one packed literal. Syntax.
- [`curios-abi`'s README](../../curios-abi/README.md), the `WireType` decision: its vocabulary without `Bits`, and its rejection of `Bits` carrying its own length dropped with the type. Carrier.
- [A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md): one immediate packed form. Carrier.

## Rejected

- **`Bits` kept as a carrier for a consumer that may come.** It costs every reader of a packed value a grain to dispatch on and the host boundary a wire type, for a program no one has written; a bit string a program needs later is a `/std` type over `Bytes` (decision 1).
- **Porting `Bits`'s tests and fixture to `Bytes`.** A test of the bit grain tests what is gone; what a test of shared machinery tested keeps its subject under a fixture of another carrier (decision 2).
- **Renaming `Bytes` to `Bin`.** The internals' name would reach about 1,070 spellings in `/std` and 910 in the programs and the compiler's tests, and either leave the functions named for bytes — `Str/to_bytes`, `Str/of_bytes`, `Flt/to_le_bytes` — describing a `Bin`, or rename them away from the vocabulary their standards use; `Byte` and `Bytes` read as element and container as `Char` and `Str` do.
- **Respelling the literal `b[…]`, or giving it no letter.** `b[` would rewrite every `x[` in the tree and every pattern to mean what `x[` means; a bare `[…]` typed by its expected type gives one bracket two meanings and leaves a packed literal unable to elaborate without one.
- **`Grain` kept as a one-variant extension point.** Nothing reads a second grain, and a type with one inhabitant states nothing a reader of it can use (decision 7).
- **A bit accessor on `Nat` or `Byte`.** Nothing asks for one (decision 4).

## Completion and retirement

Done when `Bytes` is the only packed carrier and the internals are named after it. Decisions 1 and 6 go to [`curios-num`'s README](../../curios-num/README.md), which states what a packed run is and how `Binary` holds one, and decision 3's literal to [`syntax.md`](../syntax.md); the documents listed above are corrected as their stages land.
