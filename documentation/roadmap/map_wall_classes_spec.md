# The map wall falls by classes, not by symptom

## Status

Refined working specification, not started. It sequences five steps; each is independently landable and independently measurable, and the reshape that ends the sequence deliberately comes last. Produced by the read-only investigation of 2026-08-19 (stage dumps of `programs/spines/spines.crs` at commit `3b94e1fa`, source verification of every claim below, and the survey in the appendix's sources). Revised the same day after a claim-by-claim source check: step 4's refinement pass now leads the schedule while steps 1 and 2 land, step 3 is named the disposable hedge it is, step 1 keeps only its two cheap price points (the other two are parked in the appendix), step 5 states the `List`-rope residual its measurement must be judged against, and the decomposition's two naming slips are corrected. Every figure in this file carries its method inline; when a step lands, its figure moves into an ignored probe beside the code per the measurement discipline, and this file records only what the figure decided.

## Why it exists

Run 07 of the benchmark harness put `/std/Map` 81× off Rust and 10.6× off Perceus-compiled Lean on `spines` — 2.8 µs per insert against Lean's 0.26 and Rust's 0.035 — the worst relative position Curios has held in any table the harness has produced ([07_RESULTS.md](../../benchmarks/07_RESULTS.md)). A probe had already ruled the collector out (0.1 collections per 1000 inserts, `spines_collection_decomposition`). What remains is the insert path itself, and the decomposition below shows it is not one deficit but the product of two: a trie with 1-bit fan-out (~17 dependent levels at the 65 536-key plateau) multiplied by a per-level cost that is itself ~20–50× what a native level costs.

The premise of this specification is that neither factor is a Map bug. Every cost in the wall belongs to a *class* with consumers beyond Map — `Str` walks, the `Json` and `Toml` codecs, and `BigNat`, whose limbs live on the `Bits` substrate, so the entire `BigFlt` roadmap inherits the same floor. The precedent is the closed machine's: extend the class so the honest source spelling is fast, rather than bless one library type the way Lean blesses its strings ([Evaluating a closed term is representation, not judgment](../design/toolchain/evaluating-a-closed-term-is-representation-not-judgment.md)). The sequence therefore fixes the classes first and reshapes `/std/Map` last, into the shape the fixed classes reward.

## The deficit, decomposed

Measured 2026-08-18, x86-64 dev box, release `curios run`, min-of-5, slope between two Ns so compile and startup cancel; probes printed output identical to `/std/Map`'s at every N as the correctness check. Re-verified against emitted code at `3b94e1fa` on 2026-08-19.

- `/std/Map` insert costs 3567 ns and get 1692 ns. A local trie with the identical 1-bit walk but an arithmetic byte source costs 467 ns per insert; the same trie reading through `Bytes/get` costs 1367 ns.
- **~60% is the per-element read protocol.** Every `Bytes/get` emits `call $bytes/read` (leaf/view/node dispatch); on a node-rooted value — which every append-built value is — the node arm unconditionally calls `$bytes/force` even when the cache is filled, so a bit test costs two calls in a serial dependency chain beside a cache-missing fork load (~53 ns per fork). Binaryen at its current optimize-2/shrink-1 setting (`curios-binaryen/src/lib.rs`) inlines none of the 15 call sites.
- **~25% is the two-walk insert**: a full `lookup` descent, then a second full descent in `insert_node` or `replace`, the latter non-tail with real frames, rebuilding ~17 four-field forks.
- **Key construction scatters, the first read gathers**: `BinAppend` allocates three objects per element (`emit_rope_append` in `curios-cont/src/into_wasm/code_emitter.rs`), so `Bytes/of_nat` spends 48 ns scattering a plateau key's two bytes into six heap objects and 106 ns flattening them back on first read.
- Not the collector, not `of_nat`'s arithmetic (2%), not the division by 9 alone (4%).

The classes, named: (1) sequence reads pay an out-of-line dispatch per element; (2) element-wise construction scatters and the first read gathers; (3) small values of packed carriers are boxed; and beside them the library-local facts that the insert walks twice and the trie is 17 levels deep because its fan-out is 1 bit.

## Step 1 — a read costs a read

Attack class 1 at its two cheap price points, cheapest first, composable with each other. The class's two dearer attacks — known-flat dataflow and payload-carrying windows — are parked in the appendix, where the reason is stated: neither is spines-critical, and the windows one cannot reach Map at all.

- **In-helper cache probe.** `$bytes/read`'s node arm (`curios-cont/src/into_wasm/rope_emitter.rs`) checks the `cache` field inline and answers off it, calling `$bytes/force` only when null. A few emitted instructions; halves the call count on the hot path of every walk over an already-forced rope. Same change for `$bits` and `$list`.
- **Leaf fast path at the call site.** `BinGet`/`ListGet` emission gains an inline "leaf → `array.get_u`, else call" split. Before writing it, run the free experiment: Binaryen's shrink level and partial-inlining options may produce the same split from the existing module — a datapoint, not a fix, since the invariant should not live in a third-party heuristic.

Beneficiaries: Map's `bit`/`crit`/`eql` path, every `Str` walk the window pass cannot admit, `Json`/`Toml` decoding, `List` indexing, `BigNat` limb access.

## Step 2 — a known length builds flat

Attack class 2 at the two sites the code names, stated precisely because half of the naive phrasing is already built. The packed half is the site `curios-text/src/into_core/lowerer.rs` documents: a non-constant atom lowers to `BinAppend` "with no carve-out", even where the literal's length is statically known — give those literals the flat construction the `List` chunk already has, one leaf with an exact array, no node-rooted result, no gather on first read. The list half is *not* the spread-free literal — `curios-cont/src/into_wasm/expr_emitter.rs` already emits that as a single exact-length `array.new_fixed` leaf, non-constant elements included — but the spread spellings a wide fork's rebuild must use: `[..pre, c, ..post]` lowers to a `ListConcat` of rope nodes whose first read gathers, so step 5's child arrays need a rebuild form that is flat at birth. That half is not a tidy: it is the prerequisite for step 5, because the only flat, dynamically-indexable storage reachable from source is the `List` rope, and a wide node's child array is worthless if constructing it costs a node tree and reading it costs step 1's undone dispatch.

Beneficiaries: every encoder (`Json`, `Toml`, `Nat/to_str`) and the wide node. Not the key builder: `Bytes/of_nat` scatters through a *recursive* spread-append no literal lowering reaches, which is why the measurement section charges the key's 154 ns to step 4.

## Step 3 — the library pair, an interim hedge

Two `Map.crs`-local changes, independent of the compiler steps — and named for what they are: a hedge, not a lasting layer. Both halves are superseded by step 5, whose insert is a different algorithm (bitmap update and child splice, not a crit descent) and whose nibble-indexed forks replace the mask, and the ~25% the first half attacks is measured against seventeen levels, so it shrinks with the depth. Land this only if steps 1, 2 and 4 run long or step 5 slips, take the interim win, and plan its disposal at reshape time — otherwise the single-walk discipline folds into step 5's design and this step writes no code. What landing it would validate meanwhile is real: the pair-returning descent exercises the return protocol exactly where step 5's wider walk will.

- **Single-walk insert (~25%).** Descend once rebuilding by `bit(k, tc)`, decide hit or miss at the leaf, compute `crit` there, and wedge on the way back up at the first fork whose crit sits below the new one — crit positions increase strictly downward, so the attachment point is identifiable during the return. The return protocol already delivers the uniform pair this needs (`curios-cont/src/cps/protocol.rs`); only a variant-shaped return would hit the rejected variant-width class.
- **Mask forks (~10% bound).** Store a byte index and a mask instead of a bit number, so the per-level test drops the `div 9 / rem 9 / sub / shr` chain to an and-with-mask, with mask 0 encoding the length-discriminator branch. Canonicity unaffected: index and mask are determined by the key set exactly as the bit number is.

## Step 4 — a small packed value rides the i31

Attack class 3 by extending the repo's own precedent — the variant collapse describes itself as "OCaml's int-versus-block split, expressed per family instead of globally" ([A variant collapses when nothing needs to distinguish it](../design/toolchain/a-variant-collapses-when-nothing-needs-to-distinguish-it.md)) — to the packed carriers: a `Bytes`/`Bits` value whose payload and length fit ~26 bits rides the i31 instead of a rope.

What it buys, on this workload and beyond: every `spines` key (1–3 bytes) becomes a register value; `Bytes/of_nat` becomes pure arithmetic with zero allocation and no force ever; `bit()` becomes shift-and-mask; `Bytes/eql` becomes an `i32` compare; and `BigNat` gains a small-number fast path without touching `BigNat.crs`, which the `BigFlt` sequence inherits. Feasibility is better than it first looks: constructor fields already store `(ref null any)`, so heap storage is unaffected, and the change lives in the packed-op emitters and the rope helpers' entries. The honest cost is breadth — every packed-op emitter gains an immediate case, and an operation that leaves the immediate range boxes on the way out. This is the largest single swing in the sequence and, for the spines objective, its centerpiece: it attacks the key's build, bit tests, and equality at once, where steps 1 and 2 each attack one cost. It is also the step most deserving of its own refinement pass before implementation — so that pass leads the schedule, running while steps 1 and 2 land, rather than waiting on their re-measure. The question the pass must settle first: the variant collapse is admitted only where a single `ref.test` reconstructs everything the tag said, and `Nat`/`Int` already ride the i31 — a small `Bytes` riding it too changes which families pass that admission, so the collapse's analysis must learn that a packed payload can be immediate before any packed value becomes one.

## Step 5 — the map takes its width

Reshape `/std/Map`'s trie to qp-style fan-out: a fork holds a 16-bit (or 32-bit, if 5-bit nibbles measure better) occupancy bitmap and its present children in a flat `List`, indexed by `popcnt` of the masked bitmap — `/sys/Nat/popcnt` already exists through every stage. Depth at the 65 536-key plateau drops from ~17 dependent levels to ~4–5, and with steps 1–2 landed a child access is an inline flat read. The imperative literature sizes the shape's intrinsic merit: qp tries are smaller and 10–30% faster than crit-bit *in C*, where a level is already cheap; here each avoided level also avoids the per-level protocol, so the depth ratio is worth proportionally more.

Ordering constraint, stated as the reason this step is last: a wide node indexed through today's `List` pays `$list/read` dispatch per child and hands the depth win straight back. Two Map rewrites would be churn, so the reshape happens once, in this form, after its prerequisites.

The floor this shape accepts, stated so the step's measurement is judged against it rather than quietly absorbing it: children live in the `List` rope, so even with steps 1–2 landed a child access keeps a tag test and a `ref.cast` that a raw wasm array would not pay. That residual is the price of reusing a class that pays off everywhere else instead of minting a carrier for one consumer — the rejection below records the trade — and the step's probe should name it.

The design detail the reshape must settle: the current 9th presence bit per byte generalizes to a per-nibble end-of-key branch (a 17th slot, or leaf-before-subtrie ordering); either preserves the shorter-key-first structural order.

**Rejected shapes, so they stay rejected:**

- **`fork16` as a struct** (16 child fields plus a `Node/empty` filler): needs no compiler work, but a struct field needs a constant index, so every descent pays a 16-way `br_table`; sparse maps pay ~4.5× node memory; and a 17-ary constructor is hostile to any future certification. Recorded as the shortcut deliberately not taken.
- **A dedicated flat child carrier** (a raw wasm array type reachable only by the map's forks): deletes the rope's tag test and cast per child access — the residual stated above — but mints a parallel storage abstraction for one consumer, against the convention that prefers extending an existing ownership boundary, and its wins are otherwise exactly what steps 1–2 already deliver to every `List` consumer. Rejected with its cost priced, so the reshape's probe can say what staying on the rope cost.
- **HAMT/CHAMP**: CHAMP shows canonical-form hash tries exist, but they need a hash, make canonicity conditional on collision handling, and lose the sorted structural order `Toml/encode` publicly promises.
- **Balanced trees** (AVL, red-black, weight-balanced): shape depends on rotation history — breaks the canonicity contract outright, beside costing 3–114× in the extensional setting Rocq measured.
- **Deterministic treaps / zip trees**: canonical, but hash-dependent and expected-depth deeper than the current trie.
- **Hybrid small-map representations**: threshold canonicity is delicate for what it buys.
- **An intrinsic Map carrier**: Lean-style blessing — it would grow the trusted base with map laws against the kernel-enumeration discipline, and the `Bool`-cannot-move-below-the-prelude precedent marks the boundary. The whole sequence exists to make this unnecessary.

## What the reshape must preserve

- **Canonicity is API, not implementation.** `Toml/encode` promises "one deterministic document in the map's structural order", and same-entries-same-shape is what makes map equality structural. The qp shape keeps both unconditionally: bitmap and children are determined by the key set.
- **The `Key` boundary stays.** Byte-string identity with write-side injectivity — stated where it can be checked — is the right interface, and the survey's one system with the same requirement (Rocq/CompCert, canonical binary tries) converged on the same contract. Step 4 removes most of the runtime cost of the boundary encoding without touching the concept.
- **Proofs, if they come, bind to a view.** Nothing in the repo proves anything about `Node` today, so the 1-bit shape's proof economy is a premium with no collector. If a certified-Map campaign arrives, its laws should be stated against `entries`/get-extensionality rather than node anatomy — the library-level analogue of the free-monoid/rope decoupling — so the runtime shape stays free to be wide. Worth recording as a design decision at reshape time.

## Measurement

Each step lands with an ignored probe beside the code carrying its command, date, and figure, in the `stored_prelude_measurements` pattern. The decomposition above gives each step a predicted band to check against: step 1 attacks the ~60% protocol share, step 3's first half — if the hedge lands at all — the ~25% second walk, step 4 the 154 ns key build-and-force plus the key's share of the read chain, step 5 divides whatever per-level cost remains by ~3–4 and answers for the `List`-rope residual it chose to keep. The harness's `spines` table is the end-to-end check; the honest target is the Lean/OCaml cluster, since the Rust row is an imperative hash map no canonical persistent structure will meet.

## Appendix — classes deliberately outside this campaign

- **The rest of class 1: known-flat dataflow and payload-carrying windows.** A cont-optm analysis tracking "this rope value has been read, hence forced and cached" and rewriting later reads on the same value to a dispatch-free flat read — aligned with the origins/demands machinery and the open dataflow-campaign items — and the missing member of `curios-cont/src/cps/fields.rs`'s `[base, offset, length]` window record, the forced payload, whose addition completes the `Str/fold` family. Parked from step 1 because neither is spines-critical: the dataflow pass is real machinery aimed at a share the two cheap price points already halve, and the window completion cannot reach Map at all — a key stored whole into a leaf is a hostile use, correctly declined. Both rejoin whenever the dataflow campaign resumes.
- **Typed heap fields (class 4).** Every fork descent pays a `ref.cast` from `(ref null any)`; monomorphic-at-erasure fields could carry their concrete ref type. The variant-collapse decision already names field representation as its successor's subject. Broad, independent of this sequence, and not spines-critical once steps 1–5 land — it belongs to its own campaign with its own census.
- **Rebuild walks (class 5).** `replace`/`insert_node` keep real frames and rebuild a spine per insert. Lean's cure (Perceus in-place reuse) is unavailable under wasm-GC; the engine-side analogue is [A young value dies free](generational_nursery_spec.md), already specified as its own line. A tail-recursion-modulo-constructor transform would turn rebuild walks into loops — real but modest here, in tension with the mutability-behind-atomicity law at the IR level even though the emitter already knot-ties `RecInit` and the rope's `mut` fields below it. Both stay parked: allocation measured near-free on this workload and the collector measured absent.
- **The key boundary as an accessor concept (class 6).** Replacing `Key/to_bin -> Bytes` with an indexable bit source is the fusion Appel and Leroy recommend for string-keyed tries, but the injectivity a `Key` owes is unprovable for arithmetic encodings under unary elimination — the same wall `Map.crs` already records — and step 4 removes most of the encoding's runtime cost for numeric keys with no proof obligation. Deferred indefinitely; reinstate only if a key type appears whose encoding cost survives step 4.

**Survey sources** (2026-08-19): [Appel & Leroy, Efficient Extensional Binary Tries, JAR 2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10817-022-09655-x) and [its Coq development](https://github.com/xavierleroy/canonical-binary-tries) — the extensionality requirement, the 3–114× balanced-tree penalty, Patricia rejected only for non-extensionality, and the fuse-the-conversion remark; [Tony Finch's qp tries](https://dotat.at/prog/qp/blog-2015-10-04.html) — 4–5-bit popcount Patricia, smaller and 10–30% faster than crit-bit in C; [Steindorfer & Vinju, CHAMP, OOPSLA '15](https://michael.steindorfer.name/publications/oopsla15.pdf) — canonical hash tries and their limits; [Okasaki & Gill's big-endian Patricia](https://hackage-content.haskell.org/package/containers-0.8/docs/Data-IntMap.html) — 1-bit branching is affordable where a level is a word compare; [Lean's `Std.TreeMap`](https://lean-lang.org/doc/api/Std/Data/TreeMap/Raw/Basic.html) — weight-balanced ordered tree whose speed rests on machine-int compares plus Perceus reuse.
