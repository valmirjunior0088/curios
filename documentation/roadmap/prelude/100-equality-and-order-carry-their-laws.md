# Equality and order carry their laws

**Adopted by the user on 2026-11-22; its next step is stage 1, a ready delivery.** `==` decides equality and `<` a total order wherever a type has them: `Eql` and `Ord` carry their laws as fields, as `Div` carries its precondition, so generic code and its theorems rely on what an operator says rather than taking the law as a hypothesis, and a type that cannot meet the laws has no witness and names its comparisons, as `Flt` names `Flt/eql` and `Flt/identical`. Curios states a partial operation's domain where the operation is, rather than giving it a total face that answers junk or traps ([A partial primitive is totalized by a canonical extension, or it states its domain](../../design/arithmetic/a-partial-primitive-is-totalized-by-a-canonical-extension-or-it-states-its-domain.md)), and a lawless `==` is that face over a concept.

## What is wrong today

- **`Eql` and `Ord` carry no law.** `ops.crs` says a witness of `Eql` "owes no proof that its answer agrees" and `Ord.crs` that "`Ord` itself carries no law"; a theorem over a witness that needs one takes it as a hypothesis, `Ord/Flips` in `List/sort`'s `ordered` and `sorted`, and `Map/Key` proves `refl`, `flips` and `transitive` of its own beside it. Two witnesses of `Eql` decide no `Eq`: `http/Field/Name`'s ignores ASCII case, and `Flt/internal/Dyadic`'s compares the values of a representation it keeps unnormalized on purpose. `/std` holds 25 written and 57 derived witnesses of `Eql` and 22 written and 11 derived of `Ord` (counted at `eb8f8c8bf`).
- **`<` needs a second witness that no derivation writes.** `<`, `<=`, `>` and `>=` dispatch through `Cmp`, four methods no law ties to `Ord`, and `{ .. }` derives `Ord` but no `Cmp`: `Month`, `Ordering`, `{Nat, Nat}` and `{}` have an `Ord` and no `Cmp`, so `<` is refused on each, `no witness` of `Cmp` (probed at `eb8f8c8bf`). `Flt`'s `Cmp` is IEEE's: `+nan.0 <= +nan.0` is `false`, and `0.0 <= -0.0` and `-0.0 <= 0.0` are both `true`.

## Goal

- `Eql(A)` holds `eql` and two laws: `eq_of_eql`, that `a == b` gives `Eq()(a, b)`, and `refl`, that `a == a`.
- `Ord(A)` holds `use Eql(A)`, `ord`, `lt` and `le`, and five laws: `flips`, `Ord/Flips(ord)`; `transitive`, `Ord/Transitive(Ord/le(ord))`; `eq_of_ord`, that `ord(a, b)` answering `eq` gives `Eq()(a, b)`; and `lt_of_ord` and `le_of_ord`, that `lt(a, b)` is `Ordering/is_lt(ord(a, b))` and `le(a, b)` is `Ordering/is_le(ord(a, b))`.
- `Cmp` is gone: `<` and `<=` dispatch through `Ord`'s `lt` and `le`, and `a > b` and `a >= b` are `b < a` and `b <= a`, their operands evaluated in the order written.
- `Flt` has no comparison operator: `Flt/lt`, `Flt/le`, `Flt/gt` and `Flt/ge`, IEEE's comparisons, are called by name, as `Flt/eql` is.
- `{ .. }` writes the proofs of a derived `Eql`'s and `Ord`'s laws, and each written witness proves its own.
- A theorem over a witness takes the laws from it: `List/sort`'s `ordered` and `sorted` lose their `flips`, and `Map/Key`'s `refl`, `flips` and `transitive` are removed for `Ord`'s, read through its superclass. `List/sort_by` and `List/Pairwise` keep theirs: each is handed a function, and `Ord/Flips` and `Ord/Transitive` are that function's laws.

## Settled

- **The laws are the concepts' own fields.** A lawful companion, as Lean's `LawfulBEq` is beside `BEq` and `Std.TransCmp`, `Std.OrientedCmp` and `Std.LawfulEqCmp` beside `Ord`, leaves the operator lawless wherever the companion is absent, the face Curios refuses; Mathlib's `LinearOrder`, Isabelle's `linorder`, Rocq's `OrderedTypeAlt` and Agda's `IsStrictTotalOrder` bundle their laws, as Curios does here.
- **A type with a narrower contract states it in its witness, once one needs to.** `Div`'s `Ok(A) -> Prop` is the precedent, and `Flt`'s `Ok(_) = Bool/True` declares its division total in the witness itself. A domain field for `Eql` or `Ord` waits for a consumer, and none exists.
- **`lt` and `le` stay methods.** The totality guard reads a `Nat` comparison as the intrinsic it reduces to (`curios-analysis/src/totality/guard.rs`), so `Nat`'s witness names `Nat/lt` and `Nat/le`; read off `ord` instead, `i < n` would be `Ordering/is_lt(Nat/ord(i, n))`, stuck at every bound. A derived witness writes `lt(a, b) = Ordering/is_lt(ord(a, b))`, whose agreement is `Eq/refl()`. Mathlib's `LinearOrder` keeps `lt`, `le` and `compare` as fields under laws tying them the same way.
- **`>` and `>=` are `<` and `<=` swapped**, as Haskell's `Ord` documents `x > y = y < x` and Swift's `Comparable` asks for `<` alone; IEEE's comparisons obey the swap, so `Flt`'s named ones keep it. A report spells `a > b` back as `b < a`, the form the term holds, which elaborates back to it; Core keeps no mark whose one reader is a report.
- **A carrier's lemmas its own witness reads are spelled through its named functions.** A lemma whose type is written `a <= b` at `Nat` resolves `Ord(Nat)`, and once that witness's law reads the lemma the pair is refused as a declaration cycle, as [theory/302](../theory/302-a-definition-and-a-witness-that-read-each-other-are-one-group.md) states and `Dyadic` meets today (probed at `eb8f8c8bf`). The lemmas `Nat`'s, `Int`'s, `Byte`'s, `Bytes`'s, `Char`'s, `Str`'s and `Bool`'s witnesses read write `Nat/le(a, b)` where they wrote `a <= b`, one type by conversion, so their callers keep the operators, and they write the operators again once theory/302 lands.
- **`Ord`'s reflexivity is `flips`'s**: at `a = b`, `ord(a, a)` is its own flip, so `eq`; with it, `eq_of_ord` and `eq_of_eql`, `ord(a, b)` answers `eq` exactly where `a == b`. Rocq's `OrderedTypeAlt` states the same pair, `compare_sym` and `compare_trans`.
- **A derived proof under a hypothesis writes an arm per constructor.** Over `h: Holds(a == b)`, a proof matching `a` and then `b` writes one arm of `b` in each arm of `a`, the pairs of differing constructors closed by the contradiction `h` reduces to there, as a case the facts refute is left unwritten (probed at `eb8f8c8bf`: without `h`, the same match is refused, `no arm matches`). It splits `h` by `Bool/and/first` and `second` written out, since the facts in scope read no conjunct of a conjunction over a witness's method ([the decision's **Rejected**](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md)).
- **A derived `flips` and `transitive` go through `by_tag`'s and `lexicographic`'s laws**, proved once in `/std/Ord`: an arm per constructor, `by_tag`'s laws taking the tags' equality as the hypothesis the facts refute where they differ, where an arm per pair is quadratic, 144 for `Month`.
- **`Map`'s `Eql` compares its entries**, as today: its `eq_of_eql` follows from `try_get/injective`, two maps alike at every key are one, with `of_listed` and `listed` reading `to_list` as `try_get`.
- **`Hash` takes no law**: once `==` decides `Eq`, equal values encode alike by congruence.
- **`http/Field/Name` is stored lowercase**, as HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 send a name and Rust's `http::HeaderName` stores one, so its `Eql` derives; `Dyadic` drops its `Eql`, `Ord` and `Cmp` for the functions it calls by name.
- **A lawful order on floats is at hand**: `Flt/total_key` and `Flt/total_key_injective` give IEEE's total order as `Nat/ord` reads a key, and `Flt/eq_of_identical` gives `Toml`'s float case, so a type wrapping `Flt` has an `Ord` and an `Eql` from proofs that exist.

**Rejected.** A lawful companion beside each concept, `Ord/Lawful` as Lean splits them. The laws as hypotheses, as today: every theorem restates them, and no generic code can rely on `==`. `<` read off `ord` alone: it blocks every bound over `Nat`. `Cmp` kept beneath `Ord`, as Rust's `PartialOrd` is beneath `Ord`: a second derivation for every ordered type, and a lawless `<=` on `Flt`. Decisions returning evidence, Idris's `DecEq` and Agda's `Dec`: `==` stays the `Bool` that conversion folds and bounds are stated in. A count of what the derived proofs cost `/std`'s build: no choice waits on it, and the figures the stages move are named where they land.

## Stages

**1. `Cmp` folded into `Ord`.** `lt` and `le` methods of `Ord`, written by the derivation; `Cmp` deleted and `>` and `>=` swapped; each carrier's `ord` its own witness reads calling its comparisons by name; `Flt`'s comparisons named. Check: `<` on a derived type compiles; `<` on two floats is refused as `==` is; `a > b` evaluates `a` first.

**2. `Eql`'s laws.** The two fields; their proofs written by the derivation and by the 25 written witnesses; `Name` stored lowercase and `Dyadic`'s witness dropped. Check: a witness whose `eql` breaks either law is refused where it is declared; a derived witness of a recursive family, of a nested one and of one with a proof field is accepted.

**3. `Ord`'s laws.** The five fields; `Ord/lexicographic`'s and `Ord/by_tag`'s laws proved once, for the derivation and the written lexicographic witnesses; the carriers' lemmas their witnesses read spelled through their named functions; the 22 written witnesses' proofs. Check: a witness that breaks `flips` is refused where it is declared; a derived `Ord` of a recursive family is accepted.

**4. The theorems take the laws.** `List/sort`'s `ordered` and `sorted` lose their `flips`, and `Map/Key`'s `refl`, `flips` and `transitive` are removed for `Ord`'s. Check: `/std` builds, and its tests pass as restated.
