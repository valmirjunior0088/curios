# Equality and order carry their laws

**Adopted by the user on 2026-11-22; its next step is stage 1, a ready delivery.** `==` decides equality and `<` a total order wherever a type has them: `Eql` and `Ord` carry their laws as fields, as `Div` carries its precondition, so generic code and its theorems rely on what an operator says rather than taking the law as a hypothesis, and a type that cannot meet the laws has no witness and names its comparisons, as `Flt` names `Flt/eql` and `Flt/identical`. Curios states a partial operation's domain where the operation is, rather than giving it a total face that answers junk or traps ([A partial primitive is totalized by a canonical extension, or it states its domain](../../design/arithmetic/a-partial-primitive-is-totalized-by-a-canonical-extension-or-it-states-its-domain.md)), and a lawless `==` is that face over a concept.

## What is wrong today

- **`Ord` carries no law.** `Ord.crs` says "`Ord` itself carries no law"; a theorem over a witness that needs one takes it as a hypothesis, `Ord/Flips` in `List/sort`'s `ordered` and `sorted`, and `Map/Key` proves `refl`, `flips` and `transitive` of its own beside it. `/std` holds 22 written and 11 derived witnesses of `Ord` (counted at `3c4346ab6`).

## Goal

- `Ord(A)` holds five laws beside `ord`, `lt` and `le`: `flips`, `Ord/Flips(ord)`; `transitive`, `Ord/Transitive(Ord/le_by(ord))`; `eq_of_ord`, that `ord(a, b)` answering `eq` gives `Eq()(a, b)`; and `lt_of_ord` and `le_of_ord`, that `lt(a, b)` is `Ordering/is_lt(ord(a, b))` and `le(a, b)` is `Ordering/is_le(ord(a, b))`.
- `{ .. }` writes the proofs of a derived `Ord`'s laws, as it writes a derived `Eql`'s, and each written witness proves its own.
- A theorem over a witness takes the laws from it: `List/sort`'s `ordered` and `sorted` lose their `flips`, and `Map/Key`'s `refl`, `flips` and `transitive` are removed for `Ord`'s, read through its superclass. `List/sort_by` and `List/Pairwise` keep theirs: each is handed a function, and `Ord/Flips` and `Ord/Transitive` are that function's laws.

## Settled

- **The laws are the concepts' own fields.** A lawful companion, as Lean's `LawfulBEq` is beside `BEq` and `Std.TransCmp`, `Std.OrientedCmp` and `Std.LawfulEqCmp` beside `Ord`, leaves the operator lawless wherever the companion is absent, the face Curios refuses; Mathlib's `LinearOrder`, Isabelle's `linorder`, Rocq's `OrderedTypeAlt` and Agda's `IsStrictTotalOrder` bundle their laws, as Curios does here.
- **A type with a narrower contract states it in its witness, once one needs to.** `Div`'s `Ok(A) -> Prop` is the precedent, and `Flt`'s `Ok(_) = Bool/True` declares its division total in the witness itself. A domain field for `Eql` or `Ord` waits for a consumer, and none exists.
- **A carrier's lemmas its own witness reads are spelled through its named functions.** A lemma whose type is written `a <= b` at `Nat` resolves `Ord(Nat)`, and once that witness's law reads the lemma the pair is refused as a declaration cycle, as [theory/302](../theory/302-a-definition-and-a-witness-that-read-each-other-are-one-group.md) states and `Nat`'s, `Int`'s and `Byte`'s `ord` meet already, each calling its `lt` by name. The lemmas `Nat`'s, `Int`'s, `Byte`'s, `Bytes`'s, `Char`'s, `Str`'s and `Bool`'s witnesses read write `Nat/le(a, b)` where they wrote `a <= b`, one type by conversion, so their callers keep the operators, and they write the operators again once theory/302 lands.
- **A law is proved where its method is stated.** A method written in the witness, as every derived one and `Map`'s and the tuples' are, has its law written there, recursing through the witness where the method does. A method that is a named function, as a carrier's is, has its law a lemma over that function, which the witness names: inside its own body a witness's methods are opaque, so an induction recursing through it meets the named function in its hypothesis and the witness in the call it makes, refused as a type mismatch (probed at `a1c0126b9`). A proof of one term over other witnesses' laws, or by reduction, is written in place, as `Map/Key`'s witnesses write theirs. A carrier's `eq_of_eql` is the proof its witness names and no program's: a program crosses between `==` and `Eq` by `Eql/eq_of_eql`, the one bridge [Equality is decided where a decider exists and is `Eq` everywhere, joined by one law](../../design/theory/equality-is-decided-where-a-decider-exists-and-is-eq-everywhere-joined-by-one-law.md) has. `Nat`'s and `Int`'s stay public as the elaborator's own vocabulary, which is elaborated before any witness ([the finding](000-findings.md)). Lean's `Nat.instLawfulBEq` and Agda's `≤-isTotalOrder` are records of lemmas stated over the plain relation, as a carrier's witness is here.
- **`Ord`'s reflexivity is `flips`'s**: at `a = b`, `ord(a, a)` is its own flip, so `eq`; with it, `eq_of_ord` and `eq_of_eql`, `ord(a, b)` answers `eq` exactly where `a == b`. Rocq's `OrderedTypeAlt` states the same pair, `compare_sym` and `compare_trans`.
- **A derived proof under a hypothesis writes an arm per constructor.** Over `h: Holds(a == b)`, a proof matching `a` and then `b` writes one arm of `b` in each arm of `a`, the pairs of differing constructors closed by the contradiction `h` reduces to there, as a case the facts refute is left unwritten (probed at `eb8f8c8bf`: without `h`, the same match is refused, `no arm matches`). It splits `h` by `Bool/and/first` and `second` written out, since the facts in scope read no conjunct of a conjunction over a witness's method ([the decision's **Rejected**](../../design/arithmetic/a-bound-that-follows-from-the-facts-in-scope-is-proved-by-the-elaborator.md)). Each arm closes by matching each payload's equality to `refl()` and answering `Eq/refl()`, as `/std/Eq`'s own `cong` is proved, so a payload typed over an earlier one, a proof field among them, needs nothing more, and no congruence is chained (probed at `a1c0126b9`). At a struct and at a tuple, whose pattern binds projections and so no variable an equation solves, the proof is a congruence written over field variables in a helper, taking an equation for each field and matching each in turn, applied at the projections, eta closing it, the form both checkers accept (probed at `30a577452`). The law is applied outside the helper, at the projections, a field being below its record: applied at the helper's own binders it is graded as a call at fresh arguments, and a family nested through the record would not descend through it (probed at `3c4346ab6`). The derivation writes that helper, and a written witness writes its own, until [A record is its fields wherever an equation is read](../theory/100-a-record-is-its-fields-wherever-an-equation-is-read.md) lets a field's equation be solved and the helper goes.
- **A derived `flips` and `transitive` go through `by_tag`'s and `lexicographic`'s laws**, proved once in `/std/Ord`: an arm per constructor, `by_tag`'s laws taking the tags' equality as the hypothesis the facts refute where they differ, where an arm per pair is quadratic, 144 for `Month`.
- **`Map`'s `Eql` compares its tries**, node for node (`Node/same`): a trie is a function of its entries (`Node/canonical`), so two maps hold the same keys at equal values exactly where their tries are alike. The values' method is applied at a leaf's own payload, below the map, so a family nested through a map, as `Toml` is, descends through it, where a comparison of the listings a fold builds places nothing below the map; and its `eq_of_eql` is a structural proof over the nodes, a map being one with another of the same root.
- **`Hash` takes no law**: once `==` decides `Eq`, equal values encode alike by congruence.
- **A lawful order on floats is at hand**: `Flt/total_key` and `Flt/total_key_injective` give IEEE's total order as `Nat/ord` reads a key, and `Flt/eq_of_identical` gives `Toml`'s float case, so a type wrapping `Flt` has an `Ord` and an `Eql` from proofs that exist.

**Rejected.** A lawful companion beside each concept, `Ord/Lawful` as Lean splits them. Every law's proof written in its witness, whatever its method: an induction cannot recurse through a witness whose method is a named function, a proof reading its carrier's lemmas typed through the operators meets the same declaration cycle inside the witness as beside it, and every reader of the witness would be tied to how the law is proved, the read a proof's withheld body exists to prevent ([A declaration is a function of what it reads](../../design/compilation/a-declaration-is-a-function-of-what-it-reads.md)). The laws as hypotheses, as today: every theorem restates them, and no generic code can rely on `==`. Decisions returning evidence, Idris's `DecEq` and Agda's `Dec`: `==` stays the `Bool` that conversion folds and bounds are stated in. A count of what the derived proofs cost `/std`'s build: no choice waits on it, and the figures the stages move are named where they land.

## Stages

**1. An `Eq` goal decided at a lawful carrier.** The bound procedure asks an `Eq()(a, b)` goal at a carrier with a lawful `Eql` as `Bool/Holds(a == b)` and answers through `Eql/eq_of_eql` ([arithmetic's finding](../arithmetic/000-findings.md)). Check: an implicit `Eq()(List/length([1, 2, 3]), 3)` is filled, and one at `Flt`, whose `==` is no equality, is not.

**2. `Ord`'s laws.** The five fields; `Ord/lexicographic`'s and `Ord/by_tag`'s laws proved once, for the derivation and the written lexicographic witnesses; the carriers' lemmas their witnesses read spelled through their named functions; the 22 written witnesses' proofs. Check: a witness that breaks `flips` is refused where it is declared; a derived `Ord` of a recursive family is accepted.

**3. The theorems take the laws.** `List/sort`'s `ordered` and `sorted` lose their `flips`, and `Map/Key`'s `refl`, `flips` and `transitive` are removed for `Ord`'s. Check: `/std` builds, and its tests pass as restated.
