- **A key past three bytes costs a map's insert more than twice a short key's.** `Hash/hash` of a `Nat` of `2^20` or more, of an `Int` as large and of a `Str` past three bytes is a `Bytes` on the heap, built an append a byte, where a shorter one rides the `i31` ([A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md)); a branch reads its digit through `$bytes/read`, and a leaf's key is encoded again where it is compared. Timed at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`), each workload a program in the shape of `spines` built by the release compiler, seven interleaved rounds, the slope between 200,000 and 1,000,000 inserts, median and span over the rounds: `spines`' keys 226.6 ns [221.7, 231.8] an insert, the same keys times a thousand and seven 548.8 [544.8, 563.3], and `"key/"` followed by the number 772.8 [769.6, 787.5]. The routes, none timed: a key read through a prepared form `Key` supplies, a digit reader with a law to `Hash/hash`, which changes a public concept; the emitter keeping a recursion's unchanged `Bytes` argument resolved, as a loop keeps the run it last resolved; or `Hash/hash` building one flat run. Settled by timing each over the map, text keys beside the numbers, and taking the one that costs the fewest declarations.
- **Uncertain: a union, a filter, an intersection and a difference of maps insert entry by entry, where one walk of both tries would build them.** Each is a `Map/fold` over one map's entries inserting into the map it builds (`curios-prelude-build/std/Map.crs`): `union(a, b)` inserts each of `b`'s entries into `a`, each a walk from `a`'s root that, where a twig is empty or a leaf holds another key, encodes a key it meets to learn where the two part; `filter(m, pred)` builds from the empty map what is already a trie of the shape it builds; and `intersection(a, b)` and `difference(a, b)` are filters of `a` asking `has(b, k)` at each entry, a walk of `b` from its root an entry. `rekey` keeps inserting, its trie's shape being another dictionary's. Okasaki and Gill's merge of two big-endian Patricia trees descends both at once in time linear in the two sizes (*Fast Mergeable Integer Maps*, 1998; Haskell's [`Data.IntMap`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/containers/docs/Data-IntMap.html)); a branch here holds the position it forks at and no prefix, so a merge reads a twig's prefix off its first key's encoding, as `Node/insert` reads where a key parts from a branch, and two branches at one position merge twig by twig. The laws are stated (`Map/try_get/of_union`, `of_filter`, `of_intersection`, `of_difference`), and extensionality (`Map/try_get/injective`) makes any two implementations that meet them one map. Not yet known: whether a program spends its time there — `Map/union`'s one consumer in `/std` is `Set/union`, and no workload of `xbench` or program of `programs/` calls either. Settled by a program a union's insertions dominate, then a merge with its soundness built in its own recursion, as `Node/insert` builds its outcome's, and a filter that empties a branch's twigs without inserting, the four laws proved of them and the program timed against the fold; or by dropping this entry. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: no codec of `/std` proves that what it writes it reads back.** `Json`, `Toml`, `Html`, `http/Url`, `time/rfc3339` and `time/iso8601`, and `Flt`'s decimal and hex text each say in prose that `decode` reads back what `encode` writes, and each holds it by examples in its `tests` module; none states it as a theorem, though each is a total function over certified text and the claim is a closed equation, `decode(encode(v)) = success(v)`. `time/Date`'s `of_days` and `to_days` are inverse by prose alone too. EverParse proves in F* that each parser it generates inverts its serializer and that every message has one encoding, for the TLS, Bitcoin and CBOR formats (USENIX Security 2019; PulseParse and EverCDDL, 2025), and Narcissus derives such pairs in Coq. Open: what a proof through `Parse`'s combinators costs, whether a round-trip law for each combinator (parser and printer proved inverse a piece at a time, Narcissus's shape) makes each codec's theorem a composition, or whether each codec is proved by hand. Settled by proving one small codec, `time/rfc3339`'s date, through the combinators and measuring what it took.
- **Uncertain: `Tui/View`'s size index is met by `View/fit` in every widget, so it constrains little a program writes.** `View(w, h)` carries its size in its type, so that two views join only where their sizes agree and a screen is the terminal's size by construction (`curios-prelude-build/std/Tui/View.crs`). But a `Pane` is handed its size only as the program runs, so every layout and widget in `/std` (`Pane/rows`, `cols`, `paragraph` and `window`, `border`, `Field`, `Listing`) ends in `View/fit`, which crops or pads at run time, and its folds carry a dependent pair `{h: Nat, view: View(w, h)}` to keep the index while they stack. What the index still refuses is a hand-written `beside` or `above` of two views whose sizes differ, which `fit` would otherwise pad. ratatui renders into a `Rect` and clips; Brick sizes a widget by a policy, `Fixed` or `Greedy`, and crops; neither puts a size in a type. Open: whether the index earns its folds and `Point` proofs, or whether an unindexed view, fitted once where the loop draws the frame (`Frame(w, h)` kept for the diff `render` takes), gives the same screens with the same guarantee. Settled by writing one widget and one test program each way and comparing what each refuses and what each costs to write.
- **`Cli/Parsed` walks a list by hand where `/std/List` names the walk.** `option_slots`, `required_slots` and `optional_slots` are maps, `joined` a concatenation, and `Has`, `HasIn` and `HasCommand` each a membership proposition written as a walk beside the lookup that walks the same list again (`curios-prelude-build/std/Cli/Parsed.crs`); `presented` is a type-valued function under a lowercase name, where `Carrier`, `Lookup` and `Slots` are capitalized. Written over `List/map`, a spread and `List/any`, `Parsed`'s index still reduces at a closed declaration: `/std` checked with `Cli`'s tests over a scratch copy so rewritten, and was refused once a membership was inverted. Fix: the three maps `List/map`, `joined` a spread, the three memberships `Bool/Holds(List/any(…))`, and `presented` renamed `Presented`. Check: `Cli`'s tests pass and no walk of `Parsed` restates a `/std/List` function. Size: one commit.
- **Uncertain: every write is two host calls.** Since `3c9262483`, `write_waiting` (`curios-prelude-build/std/internal/Handle.crs`) calls `handle_write`, which takes the whole message and hands on what the handle takes now, and then `handle_flush` to wait while anything stays held, because the write's reply says nothing of what it left: a write the handle took whole crosses the boundary twice. Open: whether that matters to a program that writes in a loop. Settled by a print loop's host calls and time at `3c9262483~1` and after; where it does, `handle_write` answers whether anything stays held, a status rather than a count, and the flush follows only then.
- **Uncertain: no type a program declares can key a `Map` or fill a `Set`.** `Map/Key` is written for `Bool`, `Byte`, `Bytes`, `Int`, `Nat` and `Str` alone and has no derivation, so a `Date`, a tuple or an enumeration derives `Hash` and `Ord` and still keys no `Map`. A derivation could not take a derived `Hash` for the key's encoding: `Hash/tagged` frames each part behind its four-byte length, so two encodings order by their parts' lengths before their contents, `"b"` below `"ab"` where `Str/ord` puts `"ab"` first, and `monotone` fails. FoundationDB's tuple layer and the memcomparable key formats of MyRocks and CockroachDB encode a tuple so that its bytes sort as its fields do, each variable-length part ended by an escaped terminator. Open: whether `Hash`'s derived encoding becomes order-preserving or a derived `Map/Key` encodes on its own, and what its proofs cost the derivation that [Equality and order carry their laws](100-equality-and-order-carry-their-laws.md) extends. Settled by a derived `Map/Key` sketched for a two-field struct against both encodings, once the `concept-laws` branch, which extends the derivation, has landed. Size: unknown.
- **Four carriers each state the bridge from `==` to `Eq`, which `Eql`'s law states once.** `Byte`, `Bytes`, `Int` and `Nat` each declare `eq_of_eql(a, b, h: Bool/Holds(a == b)) -> Eq()(a, b)`, where [Equality is decided where a decider exists and is `Eq` everywhere, joined by one law](../../design/theory/equality-is-decided-where-a-decider-exists-and-is-eq-everywhere-joined-by-one-law.md) joins the two equalities by the one law `Eql` carries. Fix: the law's lemma stated once on `Eql`, each carrier's copy dropped and its callers reading the concept's. Taken once the `concept-laws` branch, which gives `Eql` its laws, has landed. Check: no carrier declares its own bridge, and `/std` builds. Size: one commit.
