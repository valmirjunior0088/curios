- **A key past three bytes costs a map's insert more than twice a short key's.** `Hash/hash` of a `Nat` of `2^20` or more, of an `Int` as large and of a `Str` past three bytes is a `Bytes` on the heap, built an append a byte, where a shorter one rides the `i31` ([A field is declared at the carrier its shape names](../../design/compilation/a-field-is-declared-at-the-carrier-its-shape-names.md)); a branch reads its digit through `$bytes/read`, and a leaf's key is encoded again where it is compared. Timed at `5bccb9ebd` (landed as `231acaf5d`), each workload a program in the shape of `spines` built by the release compiler, seven interleaved rounds, the slope between 200,000 and 1,000,000 inserts, median and span over the rounds: `spines`' keys 226.6 ns [221.7, 231.8] an insert, the same keys times a thousand and seven 548.8 [544.8, 563.3], and `"key/"` followed by the number 772.8 [769.6, 787.5]. The routes, none timed: a key read through a prepared form `Hash/Key` supplies, a digit reader with a law to `Hash/hash`, which changes a public concept; the emitter keeping a recursion's unchanged `Bytes` argument resolved, as a loop keeps the run it last resolved; or `Hash/hash` building one flat run. Settled by timing each over the map, text keys beside the numbers, and taking the one that costs the fewest declarations.
- **Uncertain: a union, a filter, an intersection and a difference of maps insert entry by entry, where one walk of both tries would build them.** Each is a `Map/fold` over one map's entries inserting into the map it builds (`curios-prelude-build/std/Map.crs`): `union(a, b)` inserts each of `b`'s entries into `a`, each a walk from `a`'s root that, where a twig is empty or a leaf holds another key, encodes a key it meets to learn where the two part; `filter(m, pred)` builds from the empty map what is already a trie of the shape it builds; and `intersection(a, b)` and `difference(a, b)` are filters of `a` asking `has(b, k)` at each entry, a walk of `b` from its root an entry. `rekey` keeps inserting, its trie's shape being another dictionary's. Okasaki and Gill's merge of two big-endian Patricia trees descends both at once in time linear in the two sizes (*Fast Mergeable Integer Maps*, 1998; Haskell's [`Data.IntMap`](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/containers/docs/Data-IntMap.html)); a branch here holds the position it forks at and no prefix, so a merge reads a twig's prefix off its first key's encoding, as `Node/insert` reads where a key parts from a branch, and two branches at one position merge twig by twig. The laws are stated (`Map/try_get/of_union`, `of_filter`, `of_intersection`, `of_difference`), and extensionality (`Map/try_get/inj`) makes any two implementations that meet them one map. Not yet known: whether a program spends its time there — `Map/union`'s one consumer in `/std` is `Set/union`, and no workload of `xbench` or program of `programs/` calls either. Settled by a program a union's insertions dominate, then a merge with its soundness built in its own recursion, as `Node/insert` builds its outcome's, and a filter that empties a branch's twigs without inserting, the four laws proved of them and the program timed against the fold; or by dropping this entry. Size: unknown.
- **Uncertain: no codec of `/std` proves that what it writes it reads back.** `Json`, `Toml`, `Html`, `http/Url`, `time/rfc3339` and `time/iso8601`, and `Flt`'s decimal and hex text each say in prose that `decode` reads back what `encode` writes, and each holds it by examples in its `tests` module; none states it as a theorem, though each is a total function over certified text and the claim is a closed equation, `decode(encode(v)) = success(v)`. `time/Date`'s `of_days` and `to_days` are inverse by prose alone too. A closed instance is a theorem already: `Result/map(Parse/run(rfc3339/date(), "2024-02-29"), iso8601/write_date)` is `Result/success("2024-02-29")` by `Eq/refl()`, the checker running the parser over the literal, and `Parse/run(Parse/pure(a), s)` is `Result/success(a)` by `Eq/refl()` for every `a` and `s` (both checked on standard input at `1fb7b00e9`), so each example a codec's `tests` module holds could be an equation the build checks. No lemma of `/std` states a parser's `run` or the round trip of a number's text. A parser is a function from a position to a position and a value (`Parse/Of`), so `bind`, `map` and `pure` unfold, and the text primitives read `Str/At/try_next` at a position whose evidence is erased: the general theorem needs a statement of each primitive over those positions and one of `iso8601`'s `fixed` over a number's padded text, neither written. EverParse proves in F* that each parser it generates inverts its serializer and that every message has one encoding, for the TLS, Bitcoin and CBOR formats (USENIX Security 2019; PulseParse and EverCDDL, 2025), and Narcissus derives such pairs in Coq. Open: what a proof through `Parse`'s combinators costs, whether a round-trip law for each combinator (parser and printer proved inverse a piece at a time, Narcissus's shape) makes each codec's theorem a composition, or whether each codec is proved by hand. Settled by proving one small codec, `time/rfc3339`'s date, through the combinators and measuring what it took.
- **Uncertain: `Tui/View`'s size index is met by `View/fit` in every widget, so it constrains little a program writes.** `View(w, h)` carries its size in its type, so that two views join only where their sizes agree and a screen is the terminal's size by construction (`curios-prelude-build/std/Tui/View.crs`). But a `Pane` is handed its size only as the program runs, so every layout and widget in `/std` (`Pane/rows`, `cols`, `paragraph` and `window`, `border`, `Field`, `Listing`) ends in `View/fit`, which crops or pads at run time, and its folds carry a dependent pair `{h: Nat, view: View(w, h)}` to keep the index while they stack. What the index still refuses is a hand-written `beside` or `above` of two views whose sizes differ, which `fit` would otherwise pad. ratatui renders into a `Rect` and clips; Brick sizes a widget by a policy, `Fixed` or `Greedy`, and crops; neither puts a size in a type. Open: whether the index earns its folds and `Point` proofs, or whether an unindexed view, fitted once where the loop draws the frame (`Frame(w, h)` kept for the diff `render` takes), gives the same screens with the same guarantee. Settled by writing one widget and one test program each way and comparing what each refuses and what each costs to write.
- **`OffsetDateTime` keys no map.** Its `Ord` compares the moment first, then the local date-time, then the offset, while its derived `Hash` encodes the date-time and then the offset (`curios-prelude-build/std/time/OffsetDateTime.crs`), so `satisfy Hash/Key(OffsetDateTime) { .. }` is refused, its `Ord` "not the one a derivation writes". Fix: its `Hash` written in its order's sequence, the moment's encoding and the date-time's delimited and the offset's last, and its key written over its order's fold, which is the derived shape's, through `Hash/Key/then` and `last`. Check: a map keyed by two offset date-times of one moment at two offsets, and a third later, walks in `Ord` order. Size: one commit.
- **`Option` and `List` key no map.** Their `Hash` is written in the derived shape, an `Option`'s position and then its payload and a list's elements each delimited (`curios-prelude-build/std/Option.crs`, `List.crs`), and their `Ord` is the order those encodings stand in, `none` first and a list lexicographic with a proper prefix first, yet neither writes a `Hash/Key`, so neither keys a map, and a derived key over a payload of either is refused for want of one (`curios/src/tests/derive/key.rs`'s `a_payload_with_no_key_is_reported_against_the_payload` holds `Option(Nat)` as that payload). Fix: a key for each under `use Hash/Key(A)`, an `Option`'s `mono` by the cases of its two values and a `some` against a `some` by its payload's own, and a list's by a recursion over both lists, two conses through `Hash/delimited/ord` and the elements' own; the test's payload moves to a type that orders and encodes and keys nothing. Check: maps keyed by `Option(Str)` and by `List(Nat)` walk in `Ord` order. Size: one commit.
