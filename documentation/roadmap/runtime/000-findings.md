# Runtime: findings

- **A map whose keys are past an immediate sits at the collector's knee by sixty-five thousand entries.** [The heap is sized ahead of its churn](../../../curios-runtime/README.md#the-heap-is-sized-ahead-of-its-churn) takes its sixteen mebibytes from `chain` and `spines`, and `spines`' keys are immediates. Timed at `7f2ce2cfd` on a quiet machine: `programs/spines/spines.crs` with its map a `Map(Bytes, Nat)` keyed by `Bytes/of_nat(y * 251)`, three bytes and an immediate, by `Bytes/of_nat(y * 70001)`, five bytes, and by `Str/to_bytes(Nat/to_str(y * 70001))`, ten, each compiled and run in the test process as `spines_collection_decomposition` runs its own (`curios/src/tests/codegen/churn.rs`), the slope between two sizes a median of five readings, each the least of three runs. Between 4 000 and 12 000 inserts the three read 562 ns an insert (538 to 612), 1 688 (1 688 to 1 775) and 1 962 (1 938 to 2 188); between 25 000 and 75 000, where the live set is five times that, 590 (572 to 600), 8 012 (7 948 to 8 068) and 16 220 (16 026 to 16 518). So an insert past an immediate costs five and eight times as much at the larger size, and one at an immediate costs the same. It is collection. Counted at `7d28c0209`, each program run once at each size in a test process and the engine's `Begin GC` records counted by a `log` logger set for the process, as `curios-runtime`'s `test_support::collected` counts them since `3b4401026`: the stock program and the three-byte keys are collected 0, 0, 1 and 7 or 6 times at 4 000, 12 000, 25 000 and 75 000 inserts, the five-byte keys 0, 2, 5 and 103 times, and the ten-byte keys 1, 4, 10 and 204 times, the heap growing to thirty-two mebibytes in the last run of each of those two. Between the two larger sizes that is one collection in ten thousand inserts at an immediate, two in a thousand at five bytes and four in a thousand at ten. Fix: the decision's floor is restated over a map of text, the commonest key, and the heap sized for it. Check: the three programs' slopes and collection counts at both sizes, under the size chosen. Size: a measurement on a quiet machine and a constant; the size chosen rewrites the floor the README states.
