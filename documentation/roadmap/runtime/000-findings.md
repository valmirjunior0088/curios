# Runtime: findings

- **A map whose keys are past an immediate sits at the collector's knee by sixty-five thousand entries.** [The heap is sized ahead of its churn](../../../curios-runtime/README.md#the-heap-is-sized-ahead-of-its-churn) takes its sixteen mebibytes from `chain` and `spines`, and `spines`' keys are immediates. Timed at `7f2ce2cfd` on a quiet machine: `programs/spines/spines.crs` with its map a `Map(Bytes, Nat)` keyed by `Bytes/of_nat(y * 251)`, three bytes and an immediate, by `Bytes/of_nat(y * 70001)`, five bytes, and by `Str/to_bytes(Nat/to_str(y * 70001))`, ten, each compiled and run in the test process as `spines_collection_decomposition` runs its own (`curios/src/tests/codegen/churn.rs`), the slope between two sizes a median of five readings, each the least of three runs. Between 4 000 and 12 000 inserts the three read 562 ns an insert (538 to 612), 1 688 (1 688 to 1 775) and 1 962 (1 938 to 2 188); between 25 000 and 75 000, where the live set is five times that, 590 (572 to 600), 8 012 (7 948 to 8 068) and 16 220 (16 026 to 16 518). So an insert past an immediate costs five and eight times as much at the larger size, and one at an immediate costs the same. It is collection. Counted at `7d28c0209`, each program run once at each size in a test process and the engine's `Begin GC` records counted by a `log` logger set for the process, the capture in `curios-profile` hearing none of them, which the tools findings hold: the stock program and the three-byte keys are collected 0, 0, 1 and 7 or 6 times at 4 000, 12 000, 25 000 and 75 000 inserts, the five-byte keys 0, 2, 5 and 103 times, and the ten-byte keys 1, 4, 10 and 204 times, the heap growing to thirty-two mebibytes in the last run of each of those two. Between the two larger sizes that is one collection in ten thousand inserts at an immediate, two in a thousand at five bytes and four in a thousand at ten. Fix: the decision's floor is restated over a map of text, the commonest key, and the heap sized for it. Check: the three programs' slopes and collection counts at both sizes, under the size chosen. Size: a measurement and a constant, once the counter counts.
- **The scripted host reports readiness a slot did not ask for, and the test of that promise passes either way.** `handle_poll`'s row promises masks "each within the interest it asked for plus `ERR` and `HUP`" (`curios-abi/src/host/ops.rs`), a promise beyond the row's types, which [the decision](../../design/runtime/a-host-operation-is-a-foreign-declaration-whose-types-are-its-whole-contract.md) leaves to each host's own tests. `MockHost::handle_poll` (`curios-runtime/src/mock_host.rs`) answers `READ`, beside `WRITE` where it was asked, for a scripted stream or a connection with a chunk due and for a serial port, whatever the slot asked; `WRITE` for a connecting socket and `READ` for an ended child likewise. So a slot asking `WRITE` alone of a scripted stream is answered `READ | WRITE`. `a_poll_answers_one_mask_for_each_handle` (`curios-runtime/src/mock_host/tests.rs`, and its twin in `curios-runtime/src/os_host/tests.rs`) asks the two output streams for `WRITE`, which neither host answers with `READ`, and asserts each mask within `Poll::READINESS`, every bit a poll may report, so it holds neither host to the interest; the native host is held by `slots_naming_one_descriptor_are_answered_each_for_its_interest`. Read at `7d3487f2f`. Fix: the scripted host answers each slot within its interest, `ERR` and `HUP` aside, and both tests assert that of a slot asking one direction of a handle ready for the other. Check: the two tests, and `curios-std`'s, which run under the scripted host. Size: quick.
- **The host's table still states what no reader asks: four operand measures.** `WireOperand::measure` (`curios-abi/src/host/crossing.rs`) is what `HostOp::admit` reads of an operand a row's requirement names, and the table's one requirement, `handle_poll`'s `SameLength`, names a `Vec<Handle>` and a `Vec<Poll>`. The measures of `u64`, `Vec<u8>`, `Vec<Vec<u8>>` and `StdioMode` were read by the reply contracts `80a63651f` removed: they are still stated, the trait still documents "a count's value" and "a mode's code" as what a requirement reads, and `host_rows` (`curios-runtime/src/engine.rs`) still takes every operand's measure for every call. Read at `7d3487f2f`. Fix: the four measures are removed with the sentence, the default `None` standing for an operand no requirement reads. Check: `cargo xtask clippy`, and `curios-abi`'s, `curios-runtime`'s and `curios-prelude-archive`'s tests. Size: quick.
