# Arithmetic: findings

- **Uncertain: a direction in which the checkers could part on a guard over local definitions does not reproduce.** The kernel records a guard over local definitions with them substituted, and the elaborator records the spelling that names them, with the kernel's beside it as an alias no settlement reads, so under `let n = Byte/to_nat(c); match Nat/in_range(n, 0, 0x7F)` the kernel's reduct would answer `Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F` where the elaborator's does not. Two programs written to meet it are accepted by both checkers at `9d121c13f`: in the `true` arm, `let _: Holds(Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F) = True/qed()`, and over `Nat/in_range(n, 1, 0x7F)`, `let _: Holds(1 <= Byte/to_nat(c)) = Nat/le/of_in_range(Byte/to_nat(c), 1, 0x7F, True/qed()).low`. So is the opposite direction's program, `Nat/le/of_in_range(m, 240, 244, True/qed())` under `let n = m + 0; match Nat/in_range(n, 240, 244)`. Not known: whether a third spelling parts them, or whether only the procedure that proves a bound meets it (`curios`'s `tests::bounds` states the shape with `False/refuted()`). Investigate before any fix: read what each checker records for the guard and which spellings each probes.
- **Not measured: what reduction asking conversion costs `/std` in time.** Counted, the budget `/std` consumes — `budget::consumed` summed over the two profiles `cargo xtask clippy` files, read before another build of the prelude writes over them — is 21,324,740 in elaboration and 13,021,334 in certification at `9d121c13f`. No timed reading stands beside one from before reduction asked its checker's conversion ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)): a timed figure is a median over repeated readings on a quiet machine, the two builds interleaved, and none was to be had while the work landed. Fix: take it, and state it in the decision only if it argues for something there. Check: `/std`'s elaboration and its certification timed at `87189c97d` and at the commit above. Size: small.
- **`peel_int_pair` builds two terms before it asks whether either is its shape** (`curios-core/src/spine.rs`), where `peel_nat_pair` tests the intrinsics first. The peels' agreement is asserted, so a debug build pays the two allocations for every pair the list is asked. Fix: gate on the intrinsics, as `nat_shaped_intrinsic` does. Check: the `spine` tests and the law grid. Size: quick.
- **Uncertain: refusing an equation between two calls of a recursive function can stall the elaborator, or end it on a segmentation fault.** `classes` (`curios-elab/src/convert/intrinsic.rs`) asks `same_uncommitted` (`curios-elab/src/convert.rs`) about each two atoms a pair hands back, which is a comparison of its own and classes again where its own pair hands atoms back, and nothing ends the nesting. At `e19ebf500`, `let t(x: Nat, y: Nat) -> Eq()(Nat/pow(x, y), Nat/pow(2, y)) = Eq/refl();` gets no report within 45 seconds, and neither does the same claim over a local `let f(c: Nat, n: Nat) -> Nat = match n == 0 | true => c | false => f(c, n / 2) * f(c, n / 2) end;`, nor one over a body that multiplies or adds two different calls; over a body whose call stands alone, beside a literal, or is added to itself, the claim is refused at once, and so is `Eq()(Nat/pow(x, y), Nat/pow(x, z))`. Where the second base is an implicit to solve, `lemma(Nat/pow(x, y), Eq/refl())` under `lemma: (@b: Nat, n: Nat, e: Eq()(n, Nat/pow(2, b))) -> Nat`, `run` and `wonder diagnostics` end on a segmentation fault, the stack spent about four thousand rounds of `convert_intrinsic`, `classes`, `same_uncommitted`, `convert_under` and `Convert::drain` deep. Not known: whether what each round classes is the two calls one unfolding further, standing unpaired in the arms of the stuck `match`es the calls unfold to; whether the reduction budget is charged along the way and would end it; and why one program stalls where the other overflows. Fix, once that is known: a comparison asked while classing classes nothing itself, as a question inside a question asks nothing ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)), or the nesting is charged and runs under `recurse`. Check: each program above refused with a mismatch, as a `curios/src/tests/respelling.rs` fixture. Size: small.
- **A remainder against a side with a floor decides under `<` and not under `<=`.** `dominated` (`curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/compare.rs`) reads a remainder's strict dominator, its divisor, through `Comparison::through_dominator` (`curios-algebra/src/order.rs`), which concludes only where the divisor is at most the other side. So `x % 8 < y + 8` reduces to `true` and `x % 8 <= y + 7` stays stuck, and `Eq()(x % 8 <= y + 7, x % 8 < y + 8)` is refused, though it is the held seam `x <= y` against `x < y + 1` at `x % 8` and `y + 7`; `x % 8 <= 7` and `7 <= y + 7` each reduce to `true`, so the order conversion decides is not transitive there. Fix: a side strictly below its dominator is at most whatever the dominator's predecessor is at most, so where the divisor against the other side decides nothing, compare it against that side's successor and read `<=` there as `<=` here. Check: the three rows held in `tests::laws::written`'s `Nat comparisons`, and a block of `dominators_upper_bound_every_closed_instantiation`. Size: small.
- **`Int`'s product is associative by conversion and the law table does not say so.** `Eq()((i * j) * k, i * (j * k))` closes by `Eq/refl()`, and `TABLE` (`curios-algebra/src/law.rs`) declares `Associativity` for `Product` at `Natural` and not at `Integer`, so the row is neither generated nor audited at `Int`. Fix: declare it. Check: `tests::laws::generated` and `tests::laws::audit`. Size: quick.
