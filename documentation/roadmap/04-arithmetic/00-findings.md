# Arithmetic: findings

- **The law grid's refused atom row is a sum, and a sum's verdict does not move with the order binders are declared in.** `ATOMS_REFUSED` (`curios/src/tests/laws/written.rs`) states two stuck `match`es summed against the same two swapped, at two binder orders, and its comment, `ATOM_BINDERS`'s and `curios-core`'s `reduce/intrinsic/forced.rs` say such a pair would hold or fail with the order its binders were declared in. A sum keeps its summands as they were written, so the row is refused at every binder order and held at every one once both sides are written in one order; the dependence on binder order shows where operands are held in hash order — a product, `==`, `!=` — which no row states. Fix: the first stage of [An atom is one where conversion says so](01-decided-by-spelling-or-cap.md#an-atom-is-one-where-conversion-says-so) holds the row, and its sweep replaces the two fixed orders. Check: the sweep. Size: with that stage.
- **Uncertain: the second direction in which the checkers part on a refinement key does not reproduce.** [What reduction reads by spelling](01-decided-by-spelling-or-cap.md#what-reduction-reads-by-spelling) says that under `let n = Byte/to_nat(c); match Nat/in_range(n, 0, 0x7F)` the kernel's reduct answers `Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F` and the elaborator's does not. Two programs written to meet it are accepted by both checkers at `67b3e8f86`: in the `true` arm, `let _: Holds(Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F) = True/qed()`, and over `Nat/in_range(n, 1, 0x7F)`, `let _: Holds(1 <= Byte/to_nat(c)) = Nat/le/of_in_range(Byte/to_nat(c), 1, 0x7F, True/qed()).low`. Not known: whether a third spelling parts them, whether only the procedure that proves a bound meets it (`curios`'s `tests::bounds` states the shape with `False/refuted()`), or whether the sentence describes a difference the elaborator's escalation has since closed. Investigate before any fix: read what each checker records for the guard and which spellings each probes. The first direction, `let n = m + 0`, reproduces as "the kernel refused".
