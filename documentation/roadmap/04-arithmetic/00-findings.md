Two programs written to meet it are accepted by both checkers at `67b3e8f86`: in the `true` arm, `let _: Holds(Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F) = True/qed()`, and over `Nat/in_range(n, 1, 0x7F)`, `let _: Holds(1 <= Byte/to_nat(c)) = Nat/le/of_in_range(Byte/to_nat(c), 1, 0x7F, True/qed()).low`. Not known: whether a third spelling parts them, whether only the procedure that proves a bound meets it (`curios`'s `tests::bounds` states the shape with `False/refuted()`), or whether the elaborator's escalation has closed the difference. Investigate before any fix: read what each checker records for the guard and which spellings each probes. The first direction, `let n = m + 0`, reproduces as "the kernel refused".
