# Arithmetic: findings

- **Uncertain: a direction in which the checkers could part on a guard over local definitions does not reproduce.** The kernel records a guard over local definitions with them substituted, and the elaborator records the spelling that names them, with the kernel's beside it as an alias no settlement reads, so under `let n = Byte/to_nat(c); match Nat/in_range(n, 0, 0x7F)` the kernel's reduct would answer `Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F` where the elaborator's does not. Two programs written to meet it are accepted by both checkers at `9d121c13f`: in the `true` arm, `let _: Holds(Byte/to_nat(c) <= 0x7F) = True/qed()`, and over `Nat/in_range(n, 1, 0x7F)`, `let _: Holds(1 <= Byte/to_nat(c)) = Nat/le/of_in_range(Byte/to_nat(c), 1, 0x7F, True/qed()).low`. So is the opposite direction's program, `Nat/le/of_in_range(m, 240, 244, True/qed())` under `let n = m + 0; match Nat/in_range(n, 240, 244)`. Not known: whether a third spelling parts them, or whether only the procedure that proves a bound meets it (`curios`'s `tests::bounds` states the shape with `False/refuted()`). Investigate before any fix: read what each checker records for the guard and which spellings each probes.
- **Not measured: what reduction asking conversion costs `/std` in time.** Counted, the budget `/std` consumes — `budget::consumed` summed over the two profiles `cargo xtask clippy` files, read before another build of the prelude writes over them — is 21,324,740 in elaboration and 13,021,334 in certification at `9d121c13f`. No timed reading stands beside one from before reduction asked its checker's conversion ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)): a timed figure is a median over repeated readings on a quiet machine, the two builds interleaved, and none was to be had while the work landed. Fix: take it, and state it in the decision only if it argues for something there. Check: `/std`'s elaboration and its certification timed at `87189c97d` and at the commit above. Size: small.
- **`peel_int_pair` builds two terms before it asks whether either is its shape** (`curios-core/src/spine.rs`), where `peel_nat_pair` tests the intrinsics first. The peels' agreement is asserted, so a debug build pays the two allocations for every pair the list is asked. Fix: gate on the intrinsics, as `nat_shaped_intrinsic` does. Check: the `spine` tests and the law grid. Size: quick.
- **An operation that commutes by the peel is no atom of what encloses it.** `Eq()(Nat/and(x, y), Nat/and(y, x))` closes by `Eq/refl()`, and each of these is refused: `Nat/and(x, y) - Nat/and(y, x)` against `0`; `Nat/or(x, y) + Nat/xor(z, w)` against `Nat/xor(w, z) + Nat/or(y, x)`, and the same pair under `*` and under `==`; `Nat/and(Nat/or(x, y), Nat/xor(z, w))` against itself with both operands commuted, in either order; `Nat/and(Nat/and(x, y), Nat/and(y, x))` against `Nat/and(x, y)`; `Nat/eql(x, y) && Nat/eql(y, x)` against `Nat/eql(x, y)`; `Int/and(x, y) - Int/and(y, x)` against `+0`; and `Flt/min(a + b, c * d)` against `Flt/min(b + a, d * c)`. With one operand spelled alike on both sides each holds. The walk that hands out atoms (`Walk::spine`, `curios-core/src/reduce/intrinsic/classed.rs`) reads through every operation the law table declares commutative and never hands the operation itself out, while its two operand orders are two spellings only the symmetric peel holds one: so a sum, a product, a difference, an idempotence or a second commutative operation over two of them pairs by an identity they do not have, and the verdict follows the order an operand was written in, which [A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md) rules out. Every operation declared commutative is in it, the float sum, product, `min`, `max` and fused multiply-add among them. Fix: hand a read-through operation no fold spells one way out as an atom as well, once its operands are walked, so `Classes::of` asks its checker about two of one kind; count over `/std` what the extra questions cost before it lands. Check: the rows above in `ATOMS_HELD` of `tests::laws::written`, each checker asked alone, and an audit pass holding every generated law at a commuted spelling of a compound operand. Size: about a day.
- **Euclid's recombination pairs a quotient with a remainder by the identity of their dividends, which two spellings of one dividend do not have.** Under `f: (Nat) -> Nat, a: Nat, b: Nat`, `let p: Holds(2 * (f(a + b) / 2) + f(a + b) % 2 <= f(a + b)) = True/qed()` is refused as a mismatch between `True` and `True`, and `Nat/lt/add_mul_lt(f(a + b) / 2, f(a + b) % 2, f(a + b) / 2 + 1, 2, True/qed(), True/qed())` is refused against `Holds(f(a + b) < 2 * (f(a + b) / 2) + 2)`, the statement it proves, as a mismatch between two types that print alike; the first is refused at `Int` the same way. With `f(a)` in place of `f(a + b)`, or with the dividend bound once by a `let`, each holds, and `Eq()(2 * (f(a + b) / 2) + f(a + b) % 2, f(a + b))` holds by `Eq/refl()`. Each written `a + b` reaches its concept through a witness metavariable of its own, solved to the one witness and not yet substituted where the sum is folded, and reduction opens neither inside the stuck call: the two dividends are two terms to the fold (`Nat::recombine` and `int_recombine`, `curios-core`), the substitution the conversion chain makes before it reads a pair (`Driver::prepare`, `curios-elab/src/convert/intrinsic.rs`) comes after the fold, which is not taken again, and a decision reduced for `Holds` never reaches the chain. The same pairing misses two spellings conversion holds one: `Eq()(2 * (f(a + b) / 2) + f(b + a) % 2, f(a + b))` is refused by `Eq/refl()`, where `Eq()(f(a + b) / 2, f(b + a) / 2)` holds. A pair's atoms are classed ([A term is one where conversion says so](../../design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)), but a quotient and a remainder are two atoms of two kinds, and the dividend inside each is handed out as none. The kernel is handed substituted terms, so the statements in one spelling are the elaborator's alone to refuse; the procedure that proves a bound writes its proof with solved metavariables substituted for this reason. Fix: hand a quotient's and a remainder's dividend out to be classed, and take a fold again where substituting or classing respelled an operand it pairs by identity. Check: the statements above as held rows of the law grid (`curios/src/tests/laws/`), each checker asked alone. Size: about a day.
