# A universe level settled before its evidence is in

Working specification for one property of [a universe level that is implicit and cumulative](../../design/theory/a-universe-level-is-implicit-cumulative-and-settles-by-where-it-came-from.md): the elaborator commits a level, equal to another or at a value, only where nothing that comes later can tell — at a position conversion compares nothing at, or at a terminal sort, which cumulativity lifts. Four commitments are made elsewhere today. Two instances of a definition are equated at a level its value does not hold; a signature's input is settled through a bound its body put on it; a result is settled at its floor at a level conversion compares; and a type alias's body level is settled at zero. Each refuses a program no assignment of levels explains, and a level has no syntax to say otherwise.

The four are one specification because they lean on one another: the first's refusal through `!` exists only where the third has decided an action's level, and settling a level at its least value is harmless exactly where the first stops comparing it. It meets [A subsumption blocked on a metavariable waits as a subsumption](03-blocked-subsumption.md) and waits on nothing: that specification's third decision keeps the level shortcuts from a problem asked at subsumption, which would accept the first program below and none of the others.

## What this builds on

- **Two instances of a definition apart in a level its value does not hold are one term, and both checkers say so by unfolding.** The kernel compares two calls of one definition by their spines and levels (`one_definition_by_its_spines`, `curios-cert/src/kernel/convert.rs`) and, where that decides nothing, forces both (`by_the_type`). The elaborator's structural path reduces both sides. `let first(x: Both(Io)) -> Io(Nat) = x.0`, over `let Both(F: (Type) -> Type) -> Type = {F(Nat), F(Type)}`, certifies and runs with `x.0` at `Io.{1}(Nat)` and its declared type at `Io.{0}(Nat)`.
- **A bare former's levels are equal only under eta.** At a function type the kernel applies both sides to the same binders before either head is read (`eta_function`). At any other type it compares two lambdas' annotations (`structural`'s `Func` arm) and two projections of one group at every level (`rec_instances`).
- **Four places commit two levels equal.** `identify_universe_levels` (`curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`) is asked by `drain` ahead of reduction for two sides differing only in levels, by `compare_same_global_apply` once two spines of one polymorphic global have converted, and by `level_question`, at a recurrence and in `drain`'s arm for two applied projections of one group. It commits a pair where either level is undecided, and declines, inserting nothing, on two unequal ground levels and on a pair under a universe binder. A pair at a position a nominal family is irrelevant in is a weak equation, which the declaration's closing joins where it can.
- **A family carries a vector, and the walk that aligns two terms' levels reads it** ([A nominal type's levels compare by variance](../../design/soundness/a-nominal-types-levels-compare-by-variance.md)): `variance_vectors` (`curios-analysis/src/variance.rs`) computes it over each constructor's and field's reduct, and `Term::level_differences` (`curios-core/src/term.rs`) sets apart the pair at an irrelevant position of a nominal node and of a family's name applied in full.
- **How a declaration's levels close.** `finalize_definition` and the group's path (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) hand `UniverseSolver::finalize` the levels of the signature as its interface, those of a terminal sort apart (`result_sort_only_metas`), and the body's as internal. `finalize` joins the weak equations, minimizes every level of provenance *inferred* the constraints reach, a `Type` written outside an input position among them, settles an interface occurrence at its principal lower bound or its recorded floor, and generalizes what is left.
- **The kernel types a type as written** ([`curios-cert`'s README](../../../curios-cert/README.md#a-type-is-accepted-by-typing-it-and-reduction-is-total-on-arbitrary-terms)): a function type's sort and a tuple type's are their parts' sorts as typing reads each, above a part's reduct where the part is a call typed by its codomain or an occurrence at a level its value drops. Decision 6 has the elaborator size a former the same way.
- **What the prelude holds**, counted at `e19ebf500`. By `curios-prelude-archive`'s `family_variance_inventory`: 160 families, 59 of them carrying 104 universe parameters, 63 irrelevant and 41 invariant, the invariant ones being `/std/Cli`'s 37, `Monad`'s one and `Lift`'s three. By its `universe_parameter_census`: `/std/Cli`'s `claim`, `place`, `run_cluster`, `step`, `field`, `fill`, `parse`, `decode`, `drive` and `main` take no universe parameter and its readers `str`, `nat`, `int` and `flt` are `Value.{0}`, where `Arg`, `option`, `usage`, `help` and `select` take four. Over the whole elaboration of `/sys` and `/std`, `identify_universe_levels` is asked 28,185 times and identifies 4,090 pairs of sides, equating 3,379 pairs of levels and recording 1,730 weak, by its samples `identify::equated` and `identify::weak`. Of the equated pairs 3,313 come from `drain`'s shortcut, 54 through `level_question` and 12 from `compare_same_global_apply`, read by a debugger breaking on the insertion in `curios-prelude-archive`'s build script and printing its caller.

## The gap

Stated as it stands at `e19ebf500`, ahead of every stage below; a stage's entry says which of these verdicts it moved.

| A level stands | Conversion compares it | Committed today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at a position its family is irrelevant in, the family applied in full | no | a weak equation; settled |
| at an instance of a definition whose value does not hold it, or in a bare former's binder | no: by unfolding, and under eta | **an equation**; settled |
| at a declaration's terminal sort | lifted by cumulativity | its least value |
| in an input, at a parameter's `Type` or a level its family is invariant in | yes | the declaration's parameter, **unless the body bounds it** |
| in a result, at a tuple type's field, a former's argument or a level its family is invariant in | yes | **its floor** |
| in a type-valued definition's body alone, at any of those | yes, once unfolded | **zero** |

**Two instances are equated at a level the value does not hold.** A bare former passed as a family:

```crs
use /std/{Nat, Io};
let small(n: Nat) -> Io(Nat) = Io/pure(n);
let through(F: (Type) -> Type, x: F(Nat), y: F(Type)) -> Nat = 0;
let probe(n: Nat) -> Nat = through(Io, small(n), Io/pure(Nat));
```

It is refused, `this Type would need to be strictly below itself`, `1 ≤ 0`, in either order of `x` and `y`, and is accepted with `(A) => Io(A)` in `Io`'s place; `List` and an alias, `let Act(A: Type) -> Type = Io(A)`, are refused alike. Passing `Io` where `(Type) -> Type` is wanted equates its level with the family's domain, a function type's domains being compared by conversion (`subsume_telescope`); `F(Type)` puts that domain above zero; and `small(n)`'s `Io.{0}(Nat)` against `F(Nat)`, which is `Io(Nat)` at the instance passed, is two spellings apart in a level alone, so `drain`'s shortcut commits the instance to zero before `Io` unfolds to a former that carries none. The bound that refuses the commitment is in the store when it is made, and nothing asks.

It is no property of a former. An ordinary function is refused the same way, and accepted as `(A, x) => const_nat(A, x)`:

```crs
use /std/{Nat, Eq};
let const_nat(A: Type, x: A) -> Nat = 0;
let witness() -> Eq()(const_nat(Nat, 0), const_nat(Nat, 0)) = Eq/refl();
let through(G: (A: Type, x: A) -> Nat, p: Eq()(G(Nat, 0), G(Nat, 0)), q: Eq()(G(Type, Nat), G(Type, Nat))) -> Nat = 0;
let probe() -> Nat = through(const_nat, witness(), Eq/refl());
```

The commitment reaches `!` through a transformer, and there the bound arrives after it. With `large(n: Nat) -> Try(Io, Str, Type) = Try/pure(Nat)` and `huge(n: Nat) -> Try(Io, Str, Type)` binding `large(n)!` before answering `Try/pure(Type)`, the action's `Io` sits at one decided level and the region's a level above: checking the action, `Try`'s node is compared parameter by parameter, the shortcut meets the region's bare `Io` against the action's and commits the region's level to the action's, and the region's payload then contradicts it, `2 ≤ ?u`. Over `Io`, `Try/bind` and `Monad/bind` written out are accepted, one side there being a metavariable's solution and both compared reduced, and so is `!` over `(A: Type) => Io(A)`. `Try(Option, Str, Type)` is refused in all three spellings, for two reasons. Under `!` the two bare `Option`s meet as `Try`'s parameter, as the two `Io`s do, and identification commits the region's level to the action's. Under `Try/bind` and `Monad/bind` written out the base monad is an implicit solved from the action, and that refusal is no identification's and none of the four commitments: the solution is committed at the instance it was read off, which decision 8 ends. The refusal under `!` needs the action's level to have been decided: with `let large_at(K: Type, k: K, n: Nat) -> Try(Io, Str, K) = Try/pure(k)`, whose level is its caller's, `huge` binding `large_at(Type, Nat, n)!` is accepted. `large`'s is decided by the third commitment below.

So one pair of types has three verdicts: accepted where both levels are decided, the shortcut declining; accepted where the two sides arrive in spellings apart in more than levels, as `first`'s do; and refused where they arrive apart in levels alone with one still open.

**A signature's input is settled through a bound its body put on it.** `Value` carries a type, so its level is its caller's to choose:

```crs
use /std/{Str, List, Result};
pub struct Value: pub Type { A: Type, read: (Str) -> Result(Str, A) }
let all(v: Value, xs: List(Str)) -> Result(Str, List(v.A)) =
    let read_with(w: Value, s: Str) -> Result(Str, w.A) =
        Result/map_failure(w.read(s), (reason) => Str/concat("bad: ", reason));
    List/traverse(xs, (s) => read_with(v, s));
```

`all` elaborates at `Value.{0}`, and so cannot be handed a `Value` whose `A` is `Type`. With the local definition alone, with `List/traverse` alone, or with a `!` in its place, the same function keeps `Value`'s level as a parameter. The local definition's call equates its `Value`'s level with `v`'s, and where the declaration closes, the weak equations its `Result`s and `List`s left are joined and the merge of what they force leaves that level in one class with levels of the signature that stand at irrelevant positions, one of which represents it. The `Monad` witness for `(A) => Result(E, A)` requires the error type's level to be at most the payload's. A witness's levels close with the declaration that resolves it, so that condition is live when `settle` runs over the signature's occurrences: the class takes the error type's level as its principal lower bound, the error type's settles at its floor, zero, and `Value`'s level is zero with it. Following a bound down is what settles `Io(List(Str))` at zero, so neither the bound nor the order is the defect. What separates the two is the position the level stands at: `Io`'s reduct drops its level, and `Value`'s holds it. `/std/Cli`'s `field` and `fill` are the library's instances.

**A result is settled at its floor at a level conversion compares.**

```crs
let small() -> {A: Type, a: A} = (Nat, 0);
let big() -> {A: Type, a: A} = (Type, Nat);
let pick(b: Bool) -> {A: Type, a: A} = match b | true => small() | false => big() end;
```

`small` is `{A: Type, a: A}` at zero and `big` at one, and `pick` is refused, `1 ≤ 0`. `() -> List(Type)` over `[Nat]` and over `[Type]` is refused alike, `2 ≤ 1`, and so is `Value { A = Nat, … }` beside `Value { A = Type, … }`. Written as a parameter's type each keeps its level as the declaration's: `takes(p: {A: Type, a: A})` takes one universe parameter. A `Type` written outside an input position is minimized, and an occurrence settles at its recorded floor. That takes nothing from a caller at a terminal sort, where cumulativity lifts the result, or at a level its family is irrelevant in, where conversion compares nothing. A tuple type's field, a former's argument and a level its family is invariant in are compared for equality, and the least level there is one choice among levels no rule relates. `/std/Cli`'s readers are the library's instances: a spec holding `nat` beside `reader(@Type, "T", …)` is refused where it is built.

**A type alias's body level is settled at zero.**

```crs
let Holder(v: Value) -> Type = {held: Value, tag: Nat};
let hold(v: Value) -> Holder(v) = (held = v, tag = 0);
```

`Holder` elaborates to `(v: Value.{u}) -> Type.{1}` with the body `{held: Value.{0}, tag: Nat}`, and `hold` to `(v: Value.{0}) -> Holder.{0}(v)`: nothing in the source ties the body's `Value` to the parameter's, a level only a body mentions is minimized, and `hold` of a `Value` whose `A` is `Type` is refused. `let Types: Type = List(Type);` settles the same way, at `List.{1}(Type)`. A definition unfolds, so the level is one conversion compares. `/std/Cli`'s `Claim(spec: List(Arg)) -> Type = {arg: Arg, put: (Str) -> Slots(spec)}` is the library's instance: `claim`'s four levels are committed to its body's zeros where its tuple is checked, `place`, `run_cluster` and `step` follow, and with `Claim`'s field spelled through the spec, `arg: Elem(spec)` over `let Elem(@A: Type, _: List(A)) -> Type = A;`, all four keep their levels. So `help` prints a spec whose reader answers a type and `parse` refuses it.

## Prior art

- **Harper and Pollack** ([*Type checking with universes*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(90)90108-T), TCS 89, 1991). A definition's scheme is principal when it "must capture all valid types" of its definiens, and their completeness is stated for principal definitional contexts (§6). A level settled where a caller could have chosen it leaves a scheme that captures fewer.
- **Sozeau and Tabareau** ([*Universe Polymorphism in Coq*](https://sozeau.gitlabpages.inria.fr/www/research/publications/Universe_Polymorphism_in_Coq.pdf), ITP 2014). "The general principle guiding us is that the use of constants should be transparent, in the sense that the system should behave exactly the same when using a constant or its body" (§3). Equating two instances' universes can be "too restrictive, as unfolding would only add a (trivial) constraint", and "The issue also comes up with universes that appear equivariantly". "To ensure that we make the least commitment and generate most general constraints, there are two options. Either we find a static analysis that tells us for each constant which constraints are to be generated for a self-unification with different instances, or we do without that information and restrict ourselves to unifications that add no constraints" (§4.2). They set the first aside, "deciding covariance would amount to do βδ-normalization", and implement the second: "we allow identifying universes if at least one of them is flexible. This might lead to overly restrictive constraints on fresh universes, but this is the price to pay for automatic inference of universe instances". Of minimizing: "this might only endanger generality, not consistency. In particular, for level variables that appear only in types of parameters of a definition (a very common case), this does not change anything" (§4.3).
- **Rocq** ([reference manual, *Universe polymorphism*](https://rocq-prover.org/doc/master/refman/addendum/universe-polymorphism.html)). "The semantics respect the fact that definitions are transparent, so indistinguishable from their bodies during conversion": the first-order rule "tries to short-cut unfolding by comparing the arguments directly", fires "only when unifying the universes results in instantiating a so-called flexible universe variables", and "if such an equation of applicative terms fail due to a universe comparison not being satisfied, the terms are unfolded". Its unifier gathers "the universe constraints that would make term1 and term2 equal" and adds them where the graph takes them, "Otherwise fallback to unfolding" ([`pretyping/evarconv.ml`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/pretyping/evarconv.ml), the first of `ise_try evd [f1; f2; f3]`, which catches `UniverseInconsistency`). Of the commitment it records, "Lub constraints … correspond to unification of two levels which might not be necessary if unfolding is performed" ([`engine/univProblem.mli`](https://github.com/rocq-prover/rocq/blob/master/engine/univProblem.mli)). Minimization "is applied only to fresh universe variables"; "User-named universes and the anonymous universe implicitly attached to an explicit `Type` are considered rigid for unification and are never minimized"; and of its own example the manual says "the more general definition would keep the fresh level". `Private Polymorphic Universes` "removes universes which appear only in the body of an opaque polymorphic definition from the definition's universe arguments", so a transparent definition keeps them. `Universe Minimization ToSet` collapses a universe nothing bounds, and Iris's std++ unsets it in its telescopes: "At some point, Coq will decide that you don't really need all those universes and `Set` is good enough for some of them. (But it's never good enough..)" ([stdpp#19](https://gitlab.mpi-sws.org/iris/stdpp/issues/19)).
- **Lean 4** has no cumulativity, and tries two calls of one constant by their levels and arguments before it unfolds them: in its kernel, "We try to check if their arguments are definitionally equal. If they are, then t_n and s_n must be definitionally equal, and we can skip the delta-reduction step" ([`kernel/type_checker.cpp`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/kernel/type_checker.cpp)), and in its elaborator under a checkpoint a failure rolls back (`tryHeuristic`, [`Meta/ExprDefEq.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/master/src/Lean/Meta/ExprDefEq.lean)).
- **Agda** computes a polarity for every definition, functions included, and compares nothing at an argument a definition is constant in: `compareWithPol Nonvariant cmp x y = return ()` ([`TypeChecking/Conversion.hs`](https://github.com/agda/agda/blob/master/src/full/Agda/TypeChecking/Conversion.hs)). Its levels are arguments, so that is a definition's irrelevant level left uncompared.

## Decisions

Taken before the stage each binds.

1. **A definition carries which of its levels its value holds.** `Definition` gains `variances`, one entry per universe parameter, beside `totality` and for its reason. Where the definition answers a type, it is read off its body applied at as many fresh binders as its type's outer telescope holds and forced, by the walk `variance_vectors` runs: a level the reduct mentions where conversion compares it is invariant and every other is irrelevant, a parameter's annotation and the result sort going unread as they do for a family. A lambda left in the reduct is read with its binders' annotations, which the kernel compares where two lambdas meet at no function type, and what the walk cannot see through marks every level it carries. Any other definition is read as written, every level its body mentions beneath its own binders invariant, so identification commits there as it does today: the walk forces each closed part of what it reads, which in a type sees through an alias and in a function's body runs its closed computations, the cost a vector exists to save. It is Sozeau and Tabareau's first option narrowed to irrelevance, which a conservative walk decides with no normal form past the body's head. It is computed once, where the definition's levels have closed, and its unit carries it. A recursive member carries none: both checkers compare two projections of one group at every level. The elaborator alone reads it. The kernel compares two instances of a definition by their spines and levels and unfolds where that decides nothing, so a vector claiming an irrelevance its body denies costs a kernel refusal and admits nothing.
2. **Identification commits where the value holds the level, and records a weak equation where it does not.** `Term::level_differences` reads a definition applied in full by one spine as it reads a family's name applied in full. A function short of its application is neither committed nor called one term: a bare or partly applied instance apart in a level its vector calls irrelevant, a family's name among them, two bare or partly applied projections of a former, and two lambdas apart in a binder's annotation, which is what a bare instance unfolds to and so is read as one, whichever of the two a reduction left standing. There the shortcut and the same-global rule decline, inserting nothing, as they decline on two unequal ground levels. The pair goes on to reduction, and at its function type eta applies both sides, where the applied spellings are identified by the vector. Once the run holds the two sides one, each pair it left is a weak equation, so a signature holds one instance where it can, as it did under the equation. Where no type directs the caller, at a recurrence and between two applied projections of one group, a curried head keeps every level, as the kernel compares it there. So at a level the vector reads, the shortcut refuses nothing unfolding accepts, whichever of the commitment and its evidence comes first. Its cost is the kernel's to state: the elaborator's arm for two lambdas reads no annotation at any type, where the kernel reads them at a position that hands it none, so two lambdas apart there in an annotation alone, which the equation held together, reach the kernel joined where the weak equation can hold and are refused by it where it cannot.
3. **A signature's level settles only where every position it stands at is irrelevant or the terminal sort.** Where a declaration's levels close, each domain of its signature and a terminal that is no sort are read as they are written, by the alignment conversion reads two terms by: a part is aligned with itself one level up, so every level it carries is one pair, set apart by the position it stands at as decision 2 sets it apart between two terms. A level in a compared pair is held: with every level joined to it, it is the declaration's parameter with its bounds, neither settled nor minimized, a `Type` written outside an input position among them, in a value as in a type. The positions are read off the signature and followed by class, so which member of a class survives decides nothing. A family passed as an argument is a function short of its application there, a lambda read by its body and a bare former by its vector, since a parameter is compared at its function type, where both checkers apply the two sides before reading either; read with its binders' annotations, every signature naming `Try(Io, …)` would hold `Io`'s level and take a parameter no caller needs. Nothing is reduced, an applied definition answering by the vector decision 1 gave it: what a declaration keeps is what conversion would be asked to equate between two instances of it, by the one reading of which positions those are, at the cost of what is written. That holds a level a reduct would have dropped, in an argument a definition's value never reads, reads only as a number, or projects a part out of that does not hold it, [as a concept's method does out of a witness](00-findings.md); it costs a parameter and commits nothing. A level the reading does not mark settles as it does today, at its argument's level or its floor: nothing compares it, so the least level takes nothing from a caller and keeps a signature from carrying a level for each occurrence.
4. **A type-valued definition's body level is its parameter where its body holds it.** Where a declaration's terminal is a sort, its body is read as decision 3 reads a signature's part, before its levels close: as it is written, a binder's annotation unread, being the signature's to hold, and a group member's as a `let`'s is, its siblings standing as free names. A level only the body mentions that the reading marks joins the interface, held, a `Type` the body writes among them: an abbreviation then behaves as its body does, `{A: Type, a: A}` keeping its level for a caller written out or named, and as a `struct` of the same content does. The level of the result sort alone is none of them. A body restates the sort it answers at wherever it says what it answers, in a match's motive, and cumulativity lifts the result there as at the signature's terminal. Every other body level is minimized as it is, nothing being able to observe it.
5. **Each stage is read before the next.** Every definition's scheme and type are printed at the two commits and compared by name. A count of parameters alone has hidden a moved signature.
6. **A type former is sized by its parts as typing read them.** A function type's sort and a tuple type's are the join of their parts' sorts as `check_is_sort` hands each back, a Π into a proposition and a record of propositions being one, which is where the kernel types them. Sized from its parts' reducts a former sat below a part whose written type is above its reduct's, a closed match typed by its motive, a call typed by its codomain, an occurrence at a level its value drops, and the kernel refused the sort the elaborator declared. Decision 3 holds such a level wherever conversion compares it as written, a witness's among them, so the two checkers land in one sort by sizing alike and not by a settlement that puts them there; a level left to settle settles for economy alone.
7. **A family's level is its callers' where a parameter is typed at it, where its sort stands, or where an instance holds it.** Where a family's group closes, the walk its vector is computed by reads its index types, payloads, fields and targets while their levels are open. A level it marks is held as decision 3 holds a signature's, a bound on it being its callers' to meet. A level of a telescope it does not mark, that no parameter is typed at and the sort does not stand at, is one no two instances differ in: it is minimized as a body's is, so no occurrence of the family mints a level for it and no signature naming the family carries one. A family is read by its parts' reducts, as its vector is: both checkers hold the vector to that walk, and it runs once a group.
8. **A solved implicit takes a family at an occurrence of its own.** A candidate read off a problem's rigid side that is a family, its name with no argument, the bare projection of its group or a lambda over a nominal type, is committed at a fresh instance of that family, and each family among the type's parameters with it, as the family a metavariable under an application imitates already is. The fresh instance is paired with the one it was read off as two occurrences are, by an equation where the family holds the level and by a weak one where it does not. A type's instance is the one its problem compares. A family is a function, and what stands at a level it does not hold is each application's to choose: committed at the instance it was read off, the implicit is a family of the one occurrence that solved it, and the monad of a small action sequences small payloads alone.
9. **A weak equation decides no level the declaration holds.** Where a declaration's levels close, the levels it holds are read first, and a weak pair is joined only where the store then says of those levels, one against another and each against zero, what it said before. A weak equation is recorded at a position nothing reads, so joined to a held level it decides for every caller what no checker asked. A small action sequenced into a region holds the region's payload to zero, the two instances of the monad meeting where it is irrelevant; two arms answering at their own constructors' levels make those levels one through the declared result's. The pair that is dropped leaves an occurrence that settles from its own bounds, at the join of the held levels below it. The levels a concept's method wrapper is issued at are held the same way, each being one of its concept's parameters.

**What this reverses in the universe decision.** It rejects "minimizing only the occurrences unfolding erases, which conversion observes": after decision 2 conversion does not observe them, and decision 3 is that rule with the compared positions generalized, where they are settled today. It rejects "reconstructing a level's role at finalization from an occurrence flag or a record of discharged bounds", the surviving member of an identified pair being arbitrary: decision 3 reads a position off the signature's term and holds the whole class. And its provenance *inferred* loses a `Type` written at a compared position of a signature or of a type-valued body.

## Stages

1. **The fixtures and the readings.** Landed, and no verdict moved. The programs under *The gap* are held at today's verdicts with their controls: in `curios`'s `tests::universes::instance_tests` the ordinary function passed bare and `first`, in `tests::universes::settlement_tests` the three producers, the alias with its recursive twin and `/std/Cli`'s parser, and in `tests::concepts::monad_tests` a transformer over a bare nominal former in every spelling and `large_at`. `universe_parameter_census` prints each definition's constraints and type after its count, and `identify_universe_levels` samples the pairs it equates and the pairs it records weak; both read as *What this builds on* states.
2. **The vector**, decision 1, with nothing reading it. Landed, and no signature moved: `universe_parameter_census` prints the 2,458 lines it printed at stage 1, and `kernel_disagreements` reports none over 2,433 items. `curios-analysis` exposes the walk as `held_levels`, which reports a level by its head, a metavariable as a parameter; `Definition` carries `variances`, which `item_unchanged` compares and the stored unit's schema tag moves for; and `curios-elab`'s `definition_variances` is the driver. ## Rejected

- **Declining a commitment the store refuses**, which is what Rocq and Lean do. It accepts the bare former and the ordinary function, whose refusing bound is in the store first, and not `!`, whose bound arrives after; it decides consistency at each commitment, which `UniverseSolver::add_constraint` is built not to; and it leaves a verdict following the order of a declaration's evidence.
- **A bound tagged by where it came from**, an occurrence's own argument or a witness's side condition, which the earlier form of this specification leaned to. A floor survives the substitution that discharges it with no origin (`record_floor`), and the producers and the alias lose their level with no witness in sight.
- **Reading a bound only once the level it names has settled.** An `Io` over a payload at zero stops following its payload down, which is what settling an occurrence at its argument is for.
- **Covariant nominal levels**, `Value.{u} ≤ Value.{v}`. It lets a `struct`'s producer be lifted and reaches neither the tuple type, the list nor `all`, and it needs subsumption to reach nominal types in both checkers.
- **Reading a signature by its parts' reducts**, with the walk that computes a vector. It holds exactly what reduction leaves, and it costs what the signature's computations cost to run, a bound over a closed scan being run to learn that it names no level; and it is a second statement, beside the alignment's, of which positions conversion compares.
- **Leaving a `Type` in a value to be minimized**, a constructor's payload or a literal's component, so that no definition carries a level of its own into a value. A parameter's own sort already reaches one through an implicit argument, `Any/any(S)` storing `Type u` for an `S: Type u`, so the rule would close nothing and leave the property an exception.
- **Holding only an occurrence's level in a type-valued body**, a `Type` written there staying minimized. It leaves the paradox's carrier and every fixture over it as they were, and it leaves an abbreviation behaving unlike its body: `{A: Type, a: A}` written out keeps its level for a caller and named it does not, where a `struct` of the same content takes the level.
- **A vector the kernel reads.** Unfolding is its rule already, so a vector would be one more thing for it to hold for nothing it accepts.
- **A vector on a recursive member.** Two projections of one group reproduce themselves under every unfolding, and both checkers compare their every level.
- **Tying a body's occurrence of a family to the parameter's**, as `Claim`'s author meant. The source states no such tie, and one read off a name would hold two unrelated occurrences together.
