# Standard-library invariants, part 2: universes the elaborator does not record

Working specification for the universe constraints a written type implies and the elaborator never records, which the kernel does not see because it types a type's reduct rather than the type as written. Stage 1 has run, and its findings below replace the hypothesis it tested: the refusals come from how finalization settles a level, not from one declaration shape, and none of them is a soundness gap. A first attempt at the settlement then found three more causes behind it, and the stages are planned from all of them. The part stands because the kernel's reading of written types is still owed.

## What this builds on

- **How a type former is levelled.** `/sys`'s formers are definitions — `List(T: Type) -> Type = ListType(T)`, built by `pub_fn` in `curios-text/src/sys_module.rs` — archived as `List.{u,v} : (T: Type u) -> Type u = (T: Type u) => ListType(T)` with the constraint `v ≤ u`. The result is the argument's level; `v` occurs in neither the type nor the body. `Io`, `Cell` and `Channel` are the same. The surface has no syntax for a level, so every level is inferred.
- **How the kernel types a type.** `infer_type` (`curios-cert/src/kernel/infer.rs`) reduces the type with `reduce_forced` and infers the reduct. Its documentation gives the reason: an application of a former types at the former's promised codomain, while its reduct is the minimal level the constructor size condition needs, and typing the written spelling cost the standard library twelve items to that condition and one to a projection through `/std/Fmt`. A closed type's arguments are evaluated on the way, which is why the kernel's `infer` is memoized per node.
- **How the elaborator sizes a type.** Its sort of a tuple type (`elaborate_tuple_type`), a Π (`elaborate_func_type`) and a declaration's domains (`telescope_sizing`) is `Sort::of` (`curios-elab/src/convert/sort.rs`), which reduces first. Both checkers therefore size a type from its reduct.
- **How the elaborator settles levels.** `finalize_definition` (`curios-elab/src/elaborate/module.rs`) splits a declaration's levels into its interface — the metas of its type — and its internal levels — the metas of its body, plus `result_sort_only_metas`, every meta of the type's terminal that no binder domain mentions. `UniverseSolver::finalize` (`curios-elab/src/universe_solver.rs`) minimizes the internal ones, a level with no lower bound going to zero, and generalizes every other unsolved level in the connected component. An occurrence of a polymorphic declaration mints its instance levels with the same role a written `Type` in a signature gets (`Context::instantiate_assumption`).
- **How a level gets its role.** A universe meta is `Generalizable` or `Flexible` (`curios_core::UniverseRole`). Lowering mints a written `Type` in an input position `Generalizable` (`input_type`, `curios-text/src/into_core/lowerer.rs`) and every other written `Type` `Flexible`; `UniverseSolver::instantiate` mints an occurrence's instance levels `Generalizable` too, so finalization cannot tell a use's level from a written one. Two levels identified alias one onto the other: `default_shape_equal` solves a flexible one toward a generalizable one and takes a fixed arbitrary direction for a same-role pair, and `merge_forced_equalities` keeps the lower meta id.
- **How a witness goal waits.** A goal whose key has no entry yet defers (`Context::deferred_witness_goals`) and is retried between items, so it can resolve after the declaration that raised it has finalized; `/std`'s module order makes that routine — `Io` precedes `Handle` and `Async`. The scheduler orders a concept's witnesses before an item that dispatches through it by operator or method wrapper, as a soft edge dropped where it deadlocks, and deliberately not for `!` (`witness_dep_nodes`, `curios-text/src/into_core/order.rs`).
- **How conversion treats instance levels.** Two spellings that differ only in levels are decided by committing the levels equal, before either side unfolds (`identify_universe_levels`, `curios-elab/src/convert/neutral.rs`); only two unequal ground levels fall through to unfolding.
- **How solving spells a solution.** A flexible side is solved by the rigid side's reduct, by its written spelling when the reduct is a `stalled_unfolding`, and by its written spelling when the reduct does not re-check (`solve_at_birth`, `curios-elab/src/convert.rs`).

## The gap

**A written type can violate levels its reduct hides.**

```
use /std/{List, Nat, Io};

pub let pair_of(x: List(Nat)) -> Io({List(Nat), List(Nat)}) =
    Io/pure((x, x));
```

elaborates to `pair_of : (x: List.{u,v}(Nat)) -> Io.{0,0}({List.{u,v}(Nat), List.{u,v}(Nat)})`: `Io` at level 0 applied to a type that, as written, sits at `Type u`, with `u ≤ 0` never recorded, and `v` a parameter nothing uses. The reduct, `IoType` over two `ListType(Nat)`, is well levelled, which is why the kernel certifies it. Inferring written types instead of reducts refuses 46 `/std` declarations.

**`!` is refused at a large payload type.** In an arm,

```
pub let walk(x: Result(Str, Type), n: List(Str)) -> Result(Str, Type) =
    match n
    | [] => x
    | [_, .._] =>
        let t = x!;
        Result/success(t)
    end;
```

is refused — "this Type would need to be strictly below itself", `?u1+1 ≤ 0`, pointing at the `Type` in `x`'s type — and in a flat body, `let t = x!; Result/success(t)`, it is refused too, as `inferred Result(Str, ?)`, expected `Result(Str, Type)`. `Result/bind(x, (t) => Result/success(t))` passes in both places. `/std/Cli` met it: a walk over a specification-indexed family is large, because its constructors bind an argument that holds a `Type`, and it binds through `Result/bind` instead.

## Findings

Taken on `bb28989d`. **To retake them**, patch `infer_type` to infer `type_` itself instead of `reduce_forced(type_)`, gated on an environment variable so `curios-prelude`'s build still certifies, and widen `kernel_disagreements`' per-verdict line to the full error; then `cargo test -p curios-prelude-archive --all-features --lib -- --ignored kernel_disagreements --nocapture` with the variable set walks `/std` typing written types, and `curios wonder diagnostics` certifies a probe the same way. A declaration's archived scheme — type, universe context and raw terms — is read off `with_prelude`'s items. Neither driver is checked in.

**The 46.** 39 are `Oversized`: a family's constructor or field domain written at a level above the family's own — `Json`, `Html`, `Toml`, `Test`, `Vec`, `Command`, `http/Request`, `http/Response`, most of `Async`, `Cli` and `Tui/Layout`. 7 are `Mismatch`: `Async/poll_ready`, `Async/expire_sleepers`, `Tui/drive`, `Parse/many0`, `Parse/sep_by0`, `Toml/build/inline_fold`, `Toml/values/array_parse`. All are one class.

**An occurrence's level floats above its lower bound.** `List(Nat)` checks `Nat : Type 0` against a fresh `Type ?u`, which records `0 ≤ ?u`. Where the occurrence stands in a signature or a family's domain, `?u` counts as interface and is generalized, so the written type sits at a parameter while both checkers size the enclosing type from the reduct, where the occurrence is gone. `Json.{u,v,w,x} : Type 0` holds two `List` occurrences in its constructors, each at a generalized `u` above the family's 0; `poll_ready` carries sixteen parameters and `Tui/drive` twenty-eight. Rocq and Agda settle the same level at its lower bound (see stage 2).

**A level occurring nowhere is kept.** `List`'s `v` is one: joined to `u` by one constraint, it is generalized as a parameter, and every occurrence of `List` mints it. This inflates counts and causes no refusal.

**The hypothesis.** "A former's result level floats free of its body" is the second finding; it causes none of the refusals. Solving `List`'s `v` away leaves `pair_of` at `(x: List.{u}(Nat)) -> Io.{0}({List.{u}(Nat), …})`, still written at `Type u` under an `Io` at 0.

**`!`.** The refusal is not `!`'s: `Monad/bind(x, (t) => Result/success(t))` at `Result(Str, Type)` is refused with the same `?u1+1 ≤ 0`. `/std/Monad/Monad` is `.{u,v,w,x,y} : (M: (Type u) -> Type v) -> Type max(u,v,w+1,x+1,y+1)` with `w, x, y ≤ u`, the levels of `pure`'s `@A` and `bind`'s `@A` and `@B`. `Result`'s witness is `(@E: Type u) -> Monad.{v, max(u,v), 0, 0, 0}((A) => Result.{u,v}(E, A))`: the three method levels occur only in its goal, so `result_sort_only_metas` counts them determined, and having no lower bound they go to zero. `bind` through that witness takes `A, B : Type 0` only. The flat body meets the same refusal through its parked region.

**`Parse`'s old combinators.** Taken from `d5d24a63`, with the since-removed `Nat/Lt/le_succ_of_lt(…, ok)` written `ok`, they elaborate, certify and run as a standalone module on this tree; `many0` is `Parse.{u,0,u}(List.{u,0}(A))`, `A`'s level carried into the occurrence. The divergence recorded earlier no longer reproduces; the current `many0` and `sep_by0` are in the class above.

**The two written-spelling commits.** A stalled unfolding and `solve_at_birth`'s fallback both commit a written spelling. An argument check bounds an occurrence's level from below only, so a written spelling's level is at least its reduct's: committing it can raise a metavariable's level and never lower it — a possible spurious refusal, never an admission. Once an occurrence sits at its lower bound, the two are equal and neither path raises one. No program was written.

**No closed term of `False`.** By the same monotonicity a written type overstates its level and never understates it, and the kernel reads a written level only where it infers an application's type, where an overstatement can only refuse. A struct over `List(Type)` is placed at `Type u+1`; the retraction `wrap(A) = S { [A] }`, `el(s) = match s.l …` that Hurkens' paradox needs certifies only there; the kernel typing written types refuses the one probe declaration with a floating occurrence, `Io` over two `List(Type)`, as `expected Type.{u+1}, found Type.{v}` — an overstatement, refused.

**What a first attempt at stage 2 found.** Settling every occurrence at its least solution took `/std`'s contexts from 4 472 universe parameters to 519 and the 46 written-type refusals to none, but it zeroed a witness's method levels, and the fixes that followed each met another cause:

- *Which levels are a caller's choice is not recorded.* The attempt reconstructed it at finalization — an occurrence flag, a record of the constant lower bounds the store discharges, the written levels an occurrence was aliased to — and it broke on the alias direction: whether an occurrence or the written `Type` it was identified with survives as the representative is arbitrary, and settling the occurrence settled the choice.
- *A concept's method levels are generalized apart from its family's domain.* `Monad` carries five universe parameters and `Lift` eleven, one per method binder. Pinned at zero they refused `!`; left generic, a witness's scheme carries maxima over them — `Try`'s `Lift(Io, …)` witness took `@M: (Type.{max(u,y,z,u1,w1)}) -> …` and nine levels — and a use relating that domain to one level meets a disjunction, `u ≤ max(…)`, which stall closure answers by guessing: `tcp/Socket/close_raising` and its `Listener` twin lost `u` and the kernel refused them.
- *A deferred goal resolves after its declaration's scheme closed.* `Io`'s `satisfy Read(Async, Input)` resolves `Read(Async, Handle)` in its body only once `Handle`'s witness registers; finalized generic in the unapplied `Async`'s level, it met a witness monomorphic at zero.
- Rocq's condition for minimizing, "an atomic universe (i.e. not an algebraic max() universe)", tried against the second cause, left occurrences whose lower bound is a maximum floating above their families — six `Cli` families — since Curios's levels are algebraic where Rocq's are not.

The prior art settles each. Rocq keeps a user-written universe apart from the fresh ones: "No user-introduced universe (i.e. coming from a user-written anonymous Type) gets touched by this, only the fresh universes generated for each global application" (`dev/doc/universes.md`), and `choose_canonical` keeps a rigid universe as an equivalence class's representative over a flexible one; it keeps an inferred `Set ≤ u` apart so minimization can read it. Lean's `Pure (f : Type u → Type v)` declares `pure {α : Type u}`, the method level tied to the family's domain, and Rocq's manual writes its own monad example the same way, `monad@{i}` with `unit : forall (A : Type@{i}), A -> m A`. Lean and Rocq require an instance to be declared before its use; GHC admits a later one by checking every instance head before any binding (`tcTyClsInstDecls`, then `tcTopBinds`, then `tcInstDecls2` in `GHC/Tc/Module.hs`), which works because a head carries no universe level, where a Curios witness's scheme depends on its body.

## Stages

1. **The investigation.** Landed: the findings above.
2. **A level's role decides how it settles.** Three steps, each landing with `/std` certifying, the parameter census retaken, and a stop on any refusal or census change it does not explain:
   - **2a. Levels carry a role.** Landed. A solver meta carries a `Provenance` — `Chosen`, `Occurrence` or `Inferred` — the first and last read off the `UniverseRole` a seed or `fresh` supplies, which keeps the stored-unit format where it was, and the second minted by `instantiate` alone; `default_shape_equal` and `merge_forced_equalities` keep the greater member of an identified pair. The census moved from 4 472 parameters to 4 478 and the written-type refusals stayed at 46: the mutual-atom merge kept the lower meta id, so a class of occurrence levels that held one inferred level was minimized whenever the inferred id was the lower. It now keeps an occurrence, and generalizes it until 2c settles it at its floor — `Toml/build`'s `walk`, `apply_header`, `apply_assign` and `apply_aot` gain one parameter each and `Try`'s `Monad` witness two.
   - **2b. A chosen level bounded only from above by one chosen level is that level.** Landed. `UniverseSolver::identify_bounded_choices` runs before generalizing. It occurs in input positions alone, and cumulativity already lets a caller pass anything smaller, so identifying it with its bound costs no caller a program; it stops a witness implementing a method at a level below its family's domain. `Monad` takes two parameters, as Lean's does, and `Lift` seven — its two `Monad` superclasses shed three each, and its own `lift`, bounded by both families' domains, keeps a level of its own — and no `/std` witness was refused. `!` sequences a large payload: `Result`'s `Monad` witness names `Monad`'s domain alone, which its `(A: Type) => Result(E, A)` leaves to the caller. The rule also took the spec's first hypothesis: a `/sys` former's body level, written at its binder and bounded only by its result level, is identified with it, so `List`, `Io`, `Cell` and `Channel` take one parameter each and an occurrence of one mints one level; no Rust site spelled a former's arity. The census moved from 4 478 parameters to 3 279 with no declaration gaining one; the written-type refusals stayed at 46, which are 2c's.
   - **2c. An occurrence settles at its recorded floor.** Landed. `UniverseSolver::finalize` settles an occurrence level at its principal lower bound, or at zero where a constant bounded it from below — a floor recorded as the store discharges `0 ≤ u`, at insertion or by substitution, which is Rocq's `Set ≤ u` — and leaves one nothing bounds from below, as an unapplied former's is; a level neither the type nor the body mentions is settled the same way, after the occurrences, since settling one at a chosen level's bound makes that level the one the signature names — Rocq's restriction of a context to the universes its terms use; a still-deferred witness goal is made ground, its levels settled and every level a settlement lands on with them, chosen ones included, since the witness it finds can only be pinned to a level the goal already fixes and the language keeps its promise that a witness may be declared after its use; `result_sort_only_metas` reads only a terminal that is a sort. The 46 written-type refusals went to none, and `pair_of` and `Json` take no parameter. The census moved from 3 279 parameters to 815, and from 771 polymorphic declarations to 450. Seventeen declarations gained a parameter, for one reason: `result_sort_only_metas` read any terminal's levels as a result sort's and zeroed them, a caller's choice included — `Async/Future/new` and `Async/Channel/new` took their `A` at `Type 0` alone through the `Io` they return and `Async/map` one of its two, `Cli`'s argument constructors pinned `Arg`'s four `Value` levels, `Toml`'s `Parse(Bytes, Toml)` constants and `build/build_empty` pinned `Toml`'s own, and two witnesses their heads'. The ground rule has a price: `tcp/Socket/close_raising` and its `Listener` twin take their `A` at `Type 0`, since their `!` lifts `Handle/close`'s `Io`, at zero, through `Lift(Io, Io)`, whose identity body makes its two sides one level, while the `Try` witness that premise belongs to elaborates after them; a generic declaration using a witness declared later in its unit is pinned the same way, which `a_goal_deferred_past_its_declaration_settles_at_its_least_levels` states beside the control that declares the witness first. Since the baseline stage 2 started from, `/std` elaboration went from 121.4 s to 93.1 s, peak 450.7 MiB to 380.2 MiB, and certification from 40.5 s to 34.4 s, peak 142.9 MiB to 126.5 MiB. [Part 3](03-shared-term-costs-spec.md)'s type-level claims stayed within a fifth of their budgets — `split_once` at 30.1 s against 28.2, `trim` at 29.7 against 27.9 — and `index_of` in a type still does not finish in 300 s.

   Rejected: reconstructing a level's role at finalization, which the first attempt did and which breaks on alias direction; Rocq's atomic-bound condition, which leaves an occurrence floating wherever its lower bound is a maximum; keeping method levels generic and solving their disjunctions harder, a cost that grows with every concept; minimizing only the occurrences of definitions that unfolding erases, since conversion observes those levels; equating an occurrence's level with its argument's at application, incomplete under cumulativity when one level bounds several arguments; requiring a witness before its use, as Lean and Rocq do, which breaks `syntax.md`'s promise and `/std`'s module order to solve what the deferred-goal rule already does; checking witness signatures before any body, as GHC checks instance heads, since a Curios witness's scheme depends on its body; fixing a scheme from the signature before the body, as Lean does for theorem headers, which needs level syntax for a body to narrow it; McBride's displacement (Favonia, Angiuli and Mullanix, POPL 2023), one level per definition displaced at each use, which sets cumulativity aside and would replace the solver and the kernel's entailment; sizing by the written spelling in both checkers, which keeps every floating level and pays the size condition; resolving the `/sys` formers to their intrinsics, which leaves aliases and nominal occurrences; declaring the reduct the truth, which leaves the kernel accepting a declared type it never typed; settling only the levels a deferred goal names, which left `close_raising` at `A`'s level, where the constraint its late witness needed reached no scheme and the kernel refused it; retrying a declaration's own deferred goals before its scheme closes, which resolves nothing, since a goal defers only when no witness answers its key and none registers while one declaration elaborates; elaborating a missing witness on demand, pausing the declaration that needs it, which makes item elaboration re-entrant; and holding a declaration's scheme open until its goals resolve, which gives the items between it and its witness its levels rather than instances of them.
3. **The kernel types the written type.** `infer_type` infers the type as written, which after stage 2 lands in the sort its reduct does, so the size condition loses nothing and the 46 go to zero; its documentation is rewritten to say so. The per-node `infer` memo is measured with and without it, and kept unless certification without it stays within a fifth of its budget, since [part 3](03-shared-term-costs-spec.md)'s last stage extends it.
4. **`!` at a large type.** Stage 2b fixed it: `Monad`'s method levels are its domain, so no witness pins them. The arm program, the flat body and `Monad/bind` at `Result(Str, Type)` become fixtures, `Result/bind` their control, and `/std/Cli`'s walks use `!`.

## Verification

- `pair_of`'s signature carries no universe parameter, `Json` none, `List` one and `Monad` two; `Monad/bind` at a large payload elaborates.
- The parameter census is retaken after each of 2a, 2b and 2c, and every change it shows is one the step explains.
- The two-checker fixtures and `kernel_disagreements` stay clean, and `/std` certifies with the kernel typing written types.
- The type-level claims [part 3](03-shared-term-costs-spec.md) benchmarks keep their budgets.
- The prelude build is measured before and after stages 2 and 3, naming the stage.

## Retirement

Record the role model as a design decision under `documentation/design/language/`, with its prior art and the alternatives rejected above; record the level contract in `curios-elab`'s and `curios-cert`'s documentation and the universe entries of [the soundness perimeter](../../design/language/the-soundness-perimeter.md); replace the roadmap entry with a checked summary, verify that nothing references this filename, and delete it. Three follow-ups are recorded rather than taken here: the scheduler's witness edges extended to `!` and `use` dispatch, so fewer goals defer past their declaration and the ground rule's price shrinks to witness cycles — `a_goal_deferred_past_its_declaration_settles_at_its_least_levels`' first count turns to one — together with a check that a late resolution constrains no level its declaration already closed, reported at that declaration rather than left to the kernel, and a refusal that names the late witness where `rewrap` at `Box(Type)` now reads `inferred: Type, expected: ?`; `identify_universe_levels` committing levels equal where unfolding alone would decide, which Rocq answers with weak `ULub` constraints; and Rocq-style variance for a nominal type's universe parameters.
