## Goal

- **A measurement names its figures through one harness.** It stays an ignored test beside the code it measures, reaching its crate's test support; each figure it names carries a name, a unit and its readings, and the harness prints it, so no measurement prints a table of its own or states a protocol in prose.
- **Each instrument is read one way.** Time, allocations and looks off the recorder's trace, a phase of a measurement being a span the harness opens; a magnitude off the trace's samples; a count a checker hands back as the value it returns; and instructions by the harness at a phase's boundaries alone, as [`curios-profile`'s README](../../curios-profile/README.md#four-instruments-because-time-and-bytes-cannot-tell-waste-from-bad-inputs-and-neither-sees-everything-a-stage-does) keeps them off spans.
- **The recorder reads what its program hands it.** At every span boundary it reads the counters its program hands it, the look count among them, read without resetting, where it reads the allocator, and as it leaves out of the allocator's readings what its own callbacks took ([`curios-profile`'s README](../../curios-profile/README.md#each-thread-counts-what-it-takes-while-someone-reads-and-a-program-installs-the-allocator)).
- **One runner takes a measurement by name.** `cargo xbench measure <NAME>`: the executable is the one of the workspace's test executables, as cargo names them (`--message-format=json`), that lists the name among its ignored tests (`--list --ignored`), a name unique across the workspace as the 48 are; a group is an invocation of its own, so each group draws its own hash keys, its own addresses and its own restore of the prelude; inside one, the harness runs each variant round-robin, an untimed round first; every reading is kept, and the record is filed as `xbench/records/<NAME>/NN.toml` from what the run printed, nothing where it fails.
- **`xbench` owns every record.** One format, a sitting of one measurement at one commit: its platform, its pins and each figure's readings by group; the contest one measurement among them, each contestant's retired instructions counted beside its time; and the report comparing two records of a measurement by the decision's method, each figure as far as its unit transports.
- **A bound is a test where its figure repeats.** A bound a measurement holds on a figure that is one of the compiler's own counts, and that repeated exactly over every reading of its record, is a test of the suite asserting the bound — at most, or within a stated span — and never the figure; where its record shows the figure moving, the bound is dropped to the record. An instruction count is never such a figure: `Instructions` (`curios-profile/src/instructions.rs`) opens no count where the kernel grants none, so the suite could not hold it wherever it runs.
- **A document's figure cites its record.**

- **`xbench` owns records, and the harness is its library.** Its timing already records what it was taken under and nothing derived from it, interleaves its contestants within groups, reads a figure as the median of group medians with their span, and calls a difference only where two spans are disjoint: the decision's method, kept.
