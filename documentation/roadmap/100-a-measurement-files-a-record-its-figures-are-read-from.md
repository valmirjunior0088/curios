## Goal

- A measurement stays an ignored test beside the code it measures, reaching its crate's test support, and runs its workload under the recorder: its figures are named, each `counted` or `timed` with its unit, and read off its trace — spans, counters and samples — and its instruction count, by one harness, never printed by a table of its own.
- One runner takes a measurement by name under the decision's protocol: its readings taken in groups, its variants interleaved within each, every reading kept, and a timed figure read on a machine its record names.
- `xbench` owns every measurement: one record format, a sitting of one measurement at one commit; its corpus contest one measurement among them, each contestant's retired instructions counted beside its time; and its report comparing two records of a measurement by the decision's method, a counted figure across two machines where a timed one is refused.
- A bound a measurement held is a test of the suite where its figure repeats exactly, asserting the bound — at most, or within a stated span — and never the figure, and is a reading of its record where it does not.
- A document's figure cites its record.

## Settled

- **A measurement lives beside its code.** As Go keeps a benchmark in its package's tests and runs it through its test tool, a measurement stays where it reaches what it measures; moving the 48 into `xbench` would export every crate's test support to it.
- **`xbench` is reworked to own records.** Its timing already records what it was taken under and nothing derived from it, interleaves its contestants within groups, reads a figure as the median of group medians with their span, and calls a difference only where two spans are disjoint: the decision's method, kept. What changes is its record, widened from a contest's timings to any measurement's figures, the contest's `timings/NN.toml` migrated to it once; and its reading of each contestant, which gains the instructions the contestant retires, read by `Instructions` opened before each execution, whose count inherits into the process it starts.
- **A count compares as far as what it counts is the same.** The compiler's own counts — reduction units, looks, settlements, and allocations under one allocator — compare across any two machines, retired instructions across machines of one architecture running one build, and a timed figure across none: a comparison refused for a machine, a state or a pin that differs refuses only the figures that cross it, and a measurement prefers the compiler's counts where they answer its question.
- **A measured run is a run** (decided by the user on 2026-11-23). Nothing is fixed for it that another run leaves drawn: a count that moves between runs is read with its span and gated nowhere, as a time is.
- **A record leaves its test on standard output** (decided by the user on 2026-11-23). The harness prints it as lines under a fixed prefix, as `go test -bench` and `cargo bench` print theirs, and the runner files what that run printed, so a run that fails files nothing and no path is spelled twice.
- **A profile is read, a record is cited** (decided by the user on 2026-11-23). `cargo xtask profile` reads one run's trace for whoever looks, and a measurement files a record a document cites: a figure quoted anywhere is a record's, never a profile's summary.
- **Nothing is shared with `xboard`.** Its witnesses go to the checkers at every run and fail where they disagree with their ticket; a record is a historical fact a report reads.

**Rejected.** The measurements moved into `xbench` as its instruments, rustc-perf's and LLVM LNT's shape, which crosses every crate's test support. Measurement records in a second format beside `xbench`'s timings, two shapes of record and two readers. The contest kept timed alone, which leaves every sitting on another machine without a comparison. The hash seed fixed for a measured run, a configuration no user runs, or every map of the compiler hashed with fixed keys, 325 mentions over a cause no reading shows, which a hash of an address (`curios-elab/src/context/traced.rs`) escapes anyway. A record written to a file, its path spelled in the harness and the runner and a failed run's predecessor filed as its own. A bound asserting its figure, a snapshot every unrelated change breaks.

## Open questions

- **How looks and instructions reach a trace.** `curios-core` counts looks a thread at a time and hands them out by resetting them (`take_looks`, `curios-core/src/term.rs`), and `curios-profile`, which the recorder is, reaches `curios-common` alone of the compiler's crates; and an instruction count read at every span boundary is a system call a span, which [`curios-profile`'s README](../../curios-profile/README.md#four-instruments-because-time-and-bytes-cannot-tell-waste-from-bad-inputs-and-neither-sees-everything-a-stage-does) rejects. The candidate: the recorder takes counters its program hands it, read where it reads the allocator, a look count among them read without resetting; and the harness reads `Instructions` at the measurement's own phases alone, written into the trace as a counter of the measurement's.
- **What the runner is.** `cargo xbench measure <NAME>`, beside `collect`, which the contest becomes one case of.
