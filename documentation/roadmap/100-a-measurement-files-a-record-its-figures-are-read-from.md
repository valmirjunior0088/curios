## Goal

- **A measurement is the bench's.** It lives in `xbench`, a function the bench runs by name, and drives the compiler through its public entries and the test support a crate keeps for another's tests, as `xboard` drives the checkers; the code it measures keeps only its spans. Each figure it names carries a name, a measure and its readings, and the harness prints it, so no measurement prints a table of its own or states a protocol in prose.
- **Each instrument is read one way.** Time, allocations and looks off the recorder's trace, a phase of a measurement being a span the harness opens and a stage of a compile the span its owner opens over it; a magnitude off the trace's samples; a count a checker hands back as the value it returns; and instructions by the harness at a phase's boundaries alone, as [`curios-profile`'s README](../../curios-profile/README.md#four-instruments-because-time-and-bytes-cannot-tell-waste-from-bad-inputs-and-neither-sees-everything-a-stage-does) keeps them off spans.
- **`xbench` owns every record.** One format, a sitting of one measurement at one commit: its platform, its pins and each figure's readings by group; the contest one measurement among them, each contestant's retired instructions counted beside its time; and the report comparing two records of a measurement by the decision's method, each figure as far as its measure transports.
- **A bound is a test where its figure repeats.** A bound a measurement holds on a figure that is one of the compiler's own counts, and that repeated exactly over every reading of its record, is a test of the suite asserting the bound — at most, or within a stated span — and never the figure; where its record shows the figure moving, the bound is dropped to the record. An instruction count is never such a figure: `Instructions` (`curios-profile/src/instructions.rs`) opens no count where the kernel grants none, so the suite could not hold it wherever it runs.
- **A document's figure cites its record.**

## Settled

- **The bench hosts every measurement** (decided by the user on 2026-11-25). A measurement reaches its crate's test fixtures, not its private code, so it moves into the bench once the fixtures it shares with the suite are the owning crate's test support, the path a crate keeps for another's tests; the bench runs it as a child of its own binary, a group a process, and nothing of it is left in the compiler's crates or their test builds. Costs and surveys alike, so a measurement has one home.
- **Each stage's owner opens its span** (decided by the user on 2026-11-25). The ANF optimizer, Binaryen's optimize and Cranelift's compile and precompile each open a span over their work, and a compile's stages are read off those and the spans the other stages already open; the fold's preamble before a compile's text stage opens none, no pass running there; a stage's instructions are not read, since a span carries none.
- **A test that asserts what it compares is a check** (decided by the user on 2026-11-25). `curios-pipeline`'s seven reproductions of `/std` stay ignored tests beside their code, each ignore reason opening `check:` with its cost, and file no record; the bench refuses a sitting that reads no figure.
- **A profile is read, a record is cited** (decided by the user on 2026-11-23). `cargo xtask profile` reads one run's trace for whoever looks, and a measurement files a record a document cites: a figure quoted anywhere is a record's, never a profile's summary.
- **A figure is one compiler's.** A measurement reads the compiler it is built in, and a document argues from what that compiler does, never from its distance to code a change replaced: a comparison of two compilers needs a second build kept beside the first, and its baseline is the replaced one [`.claude/rules/documentation.md`](../../.claude/rules/documentation.md) drops. The eight standing today — five in [`curios-cps`'s README](../../curios-cps/README.md), one in [`curios-emit`'s](../../curios-emit/README.md), the six builds of [`curios-profile`'s](../../curios-profile/README.md#every-build-carries-the-instrumentation) and one in [A value costs when it is kept, not when it is named](../design/compilation/a-value-costs-when-it-is-kept-not-when-it-is-named.md) — are dropped, or restated as a reading of today's compiler where the decision's argument needs one.
- **Which figures repeat is read off their records.** Each figure's record states whether its readings agree; that, not an investigation by hand, decides where a bound is lifted into the suite.
- **Every record is taken in one sitting before landing** (decided by the user on 2026-11-24 and 2026-11-25). A record names the commit its sitting was taken at, and a sitting refuses a tree a commit does not hold, so the records are taken once the code is done and the branch is rebased onto `main`, counted and timed alike, in one session on the machine quieted, and each names a commit `main` will hold; a check of a measurement before then runs it once and reads its lines, filing nothing.
