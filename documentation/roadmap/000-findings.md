# Findings

- **`curios-ersd`'s test scaffolding is compiled into every build.** `.claude/rules/rust.md` keeps `pub mod test_support` in five crates, "each behind a `test-support` feature because another crate's tests read it", and four gate theirs (`curios-runtime`, `curios-core`, `curios-utilities`, `curios-analysis`). `curios-ersd/src/lib.rs` declares `pub mod test_support;` under no gate and its `Cargo.toml` states no such feature, seen at `6d5f6611b`. So the digest of a module's shape, the census's verdict on a field and the weight of a description's copy are public names of the crate as it ships, and what only they read (`description_copy_charges`, `curios-ersd/src/optimize/evaluate/closed.rs`) is compiled where nothing reads it. Fix: the module and what only it reads stand behind a `test-support` feature the crate states, taken by the crate's own tests and by the dev-dependencies of the three crates whose tests read it, `curios`, `curios-elab` and `curios-pipeline`. Check: `cargo tree -p curios --edges normal -e features` names no `test-support` of `curios-ersd`, and the suite passes. Size: quick.
