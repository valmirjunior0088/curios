# The refinement key

**Assumes.** An equation is a claim about one term, and only a term structurally equal to the scrutinee may be refined to its case value

**Status.** **probed**. `curios-cert`'s `recheck::tests::a_case_equation_does_not_refine_an_occurrence_at_another_universe_instance` is the regression, with `a_case_equation_still_refines_the_occurrence_it_scrutinized` as the mutation-checked control. The kernel's key is the scrutinee itself, structural equality being the under-approximation of definitional equality that costs nothing to justify. The elaborator's conversion keys the same way, held by `curios-elab`'s `convert::tests::value_conversion_does_not_identify_distinct_type_payloads`; its refinement store still keys through `project_erased_universes`, and that copy is **open** at Core, reachable from no surface program.

**The premise that a universe argument cannot affect computation is false, and `Type u` is the counterexample.** Core has no eliminator *over* levels, which is what makes the premise plausible — but `Type u` embeds a level *in a term*, and a definition carrying its parameter into a constructor payload therefore reduces to genuinely different values at two instances. A key that projects levels away through `project_erased_universes` — written for the Core-to-Ersd hand-off, where levels really are irrelevant, and rebuilding every `Type` payload at one canonical ground level — read as a quotient by definitional equality identifies `Type 0` with `Type 1`, the universe hierarchy's whole content. For `induct E : Type 3 | wrap(T : Type 2) end`, an index-only `induct Q : (e : E) -> Type 0`, and `f<u | u + 1 <= 2> : (x : Nat) -> E = (x) => match x | 0 => wrap(Type u) | _ => wrap(Type 0) end`, a kernel keyed that way certified `coerce : (x : Nat, q : Q(f<0>(x))) -> Q(f<1>(x))` with zero refusals whenever its body passed through an arm scrutinizing `f<0>(x)`: the arm recorded its equation under `f(x)`, and the unrelated stuck term `f<1>(x)` probed onto it and was refined to `wrap(T)`, a case value it was never shown to have. The same coercion with the arm removed was refused as a `Mismatch`, the kernel's own verdict that the two indices are distinct types.

**No surface program reaches the open copy, and that is a construction rather than a census.** The elaborator mints its own levels, and `UniverseSolver::finalize` mints a parameter only from a declaration's *interface*: a level reachable only through the body is minimized to a constant, no occurrence being able to choose a value for one. The pair this defect needs is two occurrences of one definition at differing instances whose *values* differ, and only a level carried into a payload can make a value differ — `Type u` embedded in a term. Such a level is body-only by construction, so it never becomes a parameter and the pair has no surface spelling at all. `curios`'s `tests::universes::a_body_carried_level_is_minimized_rather_than_generalized` is the fixture, asserting the parameter count at a definition storing a bare sort against a definition whose level rides a `Type`-sorted parameter as the control that generalization is live in the same program. A Core-level route or a future generalization rule would reach the store's copy; nothing at the surface can.

**What the two sides do not share is *argument* reduction, and only the elaborator has it.** Its store keys on `shallow_scrutinee` — universe instances erased and solved metavariables materialized, arguments exactly as written — because reducing arguments to record a key makes registering a fact cost the fact's own subject: `10 <= Bytes/len(built)` would evaluate a hundred-thousand-step accumulation on the way *into* the arm that exists to avoid evaluating it. `canonical_scrutinee` is the escalation a probe reaches only after the written spelling missed, so what matches is the written spellings plus the reduced ones and nothing more. The kernel records the written spelling and computes the reduced one at most once per equation and only when a probe has already missed; keying the written form alone refuses a small number of prelude items, each a decided proposition that no longer collapses to `True`, so the reduced spelling is not optional. The reduction that would otherwise run once per arm — over a scrutinee mentioning a local, which no memo may hold — runs only when something demands it, and `curios`' `scrutinee_refinement_measurements` holds what that costs. The obligations the deferral creates are [case equations inside an arm](case-equations-inside-an-arm.md)'s, since they are about the equation's scope rather than about the key.

**The elaborator registers a guard in the shape the reducer will present it, operands included, and the rule deciding how far that goes is *rewriting, never evaluating*.** The head's half was always there — `spine_whnf` re-registers a concept-dispatched scrutinee as the intrinsic it resolves to — and the operands' half went unnoticed while every proposition `/sys` states was written in the guard's own operands; a window's bound, `Le(s + l, len b)` instantiated at a call as `Le(0 + n, len b)`, is the first whose probe form is a fold away rather than a spelling away, and without it no window bound could be discharged by the guard that established it. What reaches both without evaluating: `spine_whnf` opens application layers and stops, and `reduce_intrinsic` under a reducer that returns its operands untouched performs the half of a fold that acts on operands already in hand. Both are bounded by the term, so a guard over an expensive subject stays free to register — the property `shallow_scrutinee` exists to keep, and the line was drawn by measurement: a single canonicalization of `n < 256` over an inferred implicit consumed an entire declaration's step budget, and every further attempt then failed instantly on the spent counter, so no memo could rescue it. It is the admitting direction — more refinements fire — on the same premise as the rest of this entry, and the kernel re-checks either way, so an over-firing refinement fails closed at certification. `curios`'s `tests::numeric::a_guard_discharges_a_window_bound_stated_over_a_sum` is the fixture, with a guard over a *different* window as its control: registration must collapse two spellings of one comparison and must not collapse two comparisons. Solved metavariables are materialized into the key for the same reason, since a key holding one metavariable probed with another, identically solved, silently did not fire while the same term with the implicit written out did; `tests::matching::an_inferred_implicit_does_not_break_a_refinement_key` is the regression, re-mutation-checked when its shape moved to a concept dispatch, since a first replacement passed with the mechanism removed and was discarded.

**The elaborator's conversion identifies levels instead of erasing them.** A value fast path comparing whole goals through the projection accepts `wrap(Type 0) ~ wrap(Type 1)` at a relevant family while `curios_cert::convert` refuses the identical goal — two constructor values differing in a relevant payload, the premise's counterexample spelled with no definitions at all, and the first disagreement the goal-level conversion differential found. `identify_universe_levels` commits every differing level pair of two otherwise-identical spellings as the same `Conversion` equality constraint the structural path emits for a type's vectors, and declines with nothing inserted when a pair is two unequal ground levels — this entry's **Assumes** applied to the elaborator's half, since after the commitment the two spellings are one term. `value_conversion_does_not_identify_distinct_type_payloads` is the regression, carrying the differential's two verdicts; `value_conversion_does_not_unfold_terms_differing_only_by_universes` is the control, asserting that acceptance arrives with the identification that justifies it; both are mutation-checked against the projection comparison.

**Two more readers of the projection sit in reduction, and they survive the premise's falsification for a reason of their own.** `Nat::cancel_common` and `compare_nat`'s shared-inner shortcut both match summands under `project_erased_universes`, because two occurrences of a polymorphic name are independently instantiated and a syntactic match would leave `n <= n` undecided for any `n` mentioning one. That makes reduction a second reader of the premise this entry records as false. What actually holds these two up is narrower and is about the carrier rather than about erasure: Core offers no elimination from a type or a level into a `Nat`, so two summands differing only in their instances denote one number however they are spelled. `summands_cancel_across_a_universe_instance_and_not_across_an_argument` records the equation that buys — `g<0> + 1` and `g<1> + 1` decide **equal**, which the ordinary structural comparison refuses because it reaches the differing levels — with distinct arguments under one instance as the control that the key collapses levels and not terms. A null result, and a conditional one: any intrinsic reading a level or a sort as a count turns that fixture into a witness.
