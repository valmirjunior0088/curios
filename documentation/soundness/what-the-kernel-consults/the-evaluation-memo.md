# The evaluation memo

**Assumes.** A remembered reduct is the kernel's own pure function of the definition store, and replaying one changes what a judgment spends but never what it decides

**Status.** **argued**, in the four invariants the component states for itself, and **probed** at the interlock between a live case equation and a remembered reduct. The reason it is not merely a performance note is that a wrong reduct is a false definitional equation — which conversion believes, and which congruence with substitution carries to `False` by the route [Intrinsic fold laws](../per-term-rules/intrinsic-fold-laws-and-the-free-monoid-peel.md) already records. *Keys are valid by construction*: an unfold entry is keyed by a name whose body is closed, and `invalidate` is called whenever a name is overwritten, so validity never rests on the store being append-only; a whnf entry is stored only for a local-free term, which is what makes its key scope-independent, and the test is applied inside the component rather than at the call sites that would have to remember it. *They are consulted at every reduction level, not only at the crate's boundary*: `whnf_within` probes on entry and stores on exit, so a scrutinee, an application's head and each turn of `force`'s loop are served by the same table, and what makes reaching further safe is that `Memos::storable` gates the lookup as well as the store. *The term-keyed tables live exactly as long as a budget does*, cleared wherever it is restored — which is also why they are stored against that budget alone and charge the compilation's retention allowance nothing; the name-keyed unfold table outlives a declaration and is the one that pays. *A store cannot charge*: `Memos` hands back a `Replay` and applying one to the budget and the entropy counter is `Spend`'s job, so nothing can both remember and charge twice.

**What a hit costs is now part of the assumption, and it is weaker than it was.** A term-keyed hit spends no steps; only the name-keyed unfold hit is charged what its computation consumed. Minted identities are replayed either way, so the entropy trajectory is unchanged. What that gives up is exhaustion points: a free hit can only *reduce* what a judgment spends, so it can only turn an exhaustion refusal into an acceptance, and a semantic refusal is budget-independent and cannot move. The former assumption — that a hit costs what recomputation would, so the whole trajectory was bit-identical either way — priced work the kernel had not performed, and compounded: recorded costs that include other recorded costs made a structure the memos evaluate linearly exponential to charge. The second invariant is what keeps the surviving property deterministic, since a free hit on a table that outlived a declaration would make one declaration's spend depend on which declarations were walked before it. **Not covered:** an `unfold` record is measured over a computation that may itself have taken free term-keyed hits, so it can record less than the same body costs cold, and which declaration first unfolds a name decides what every later one is charged for it. The direction is undercharging, so it can only accept

**The load-bearing half of the first invariant is about intermediates, and the interlock is now probed while the general claim is not.** `storable` asks whether the *term being stored* is local-free; what the argument needs is that reduction of a local-free term never *encounters* a local-bearing stuck form along the way, which is what makes it impossible for a memoized reduct to depend on an equation later retracted. That claim is stated in `Scope::refine`, one component over. What stands behind the pair is `kernel_memo_parity`, which runs the whole prelude with the memos on and off and requires identical verdicts — the semantic ones, which is now the property it is *for* rather than an accident of a corpus where nothing exhausts — and `Kernel::uncached`, which exists so that test can be written; both are whole-prelude, so what they establish is coverage by corpus, the same standard [Across the perimeter](../across-the-perimeter.md) declines to accept for conversion.

**Attacked at the interlock, and the result was null.** `kernel::whnf::tests::a_case_equation_reaches_the_reduct_and_not_the_memos` is the fixture the paragraph above used to ask for: two terms reduced inside an arm refining a binder, then again after it retracts. The open one is what the equation answers — `1` inside the arm, the stuck successor outside — and the retraction assertion is what a leaked entry would fail; the closed one crosses the *other* gate, since `machine_admissible` declines the closed machine while any equation is live, so its inside reduct comes from the recursive strategy and the outside call is served by the entry that strategy stored, where the machine would otherwise have run. Both routes reach what a kernel that never entered the arm reaches, and the whole sequence agrees with `Kernel::uncached`. Mutation-checked, and the mutation is the exact one the argument turns on: dropping the local-free test from `storable` makes the arm's answer for the open term outlive the arm, and this fixture is the only one that sees it — the two module fixtures that also move under it are resource assertions.

**What the fixture does not establish** is the general claim, which is about intermediates rather than about the two sets being disjoint at one live equation. It shows the boundary holding where an equation is assumed and a memoizable term is reduced beside it; it does not show that no reduction of a local-free term can reach a local-bearing stuck form, which is a property of every reduction rule rather than of a fixture. The rules were read once against it — substitution, delta into a closed body, iota binding a closed payload, and `step_func`'s eta probe, whose fresh binders are minted unique and so match no recorded key — and a global with no body (`Definition::value` is `None` for a `foreign` declaration or a deliberately opaque name) is the one local-free term that can stick, which `Scope::refine` then declines to record. Declining to record is the safe direction. That reading is an audit, not a probe, and it is what a rule taught to reduce under a binder would invalidate in silence

**That half now carries more weight than it did, and the exposure grew without the argument changing.** `whnf_within` probes and stores at *every* reduction level rather than only at the crate's two `Reducer` methods, so the intermediates the paragraph above is about are no longer a handful per judgment — a scrutinee, an application's head, each turn of `force`'s loop and every other internal call now presents a key. Nothing in the argument moved: the same `storable` test gates every one of them, on the lookup as well as the store, which is precisely why reaching further was safe to do. But an argument that rests on a claim held in another component and tested only by whole-prelude parity is now relied on far more often, and the missing test above is the one that would close it. What motivated the reach is not soundness at all: the kernel charged 5.3× the elaborator for the identical reduction at the identical depth, so a program the elaborator accepted was refused for exhaustion by the kernel with no disagreement about any rule — `curios`' `str_literal_cost_measurements` records it
