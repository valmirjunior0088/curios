# The certifier's totality record

**Assumes.** A totality classification a walk reads for a definition already in scope was reached by the certifier's own walk over the unit holding that definition, closed over everything the definition mentions, and filed with that unit — `curios_core::Certification`, which only `curios_cert::certify_module` makes

**Status.** **probed**, at each way a later walk can be handed a classification: a record covering its unit, an empty one, and one naming fewer definitions than its unit holds. Obligations (T) and (V) close over what an erased position mentions, and what it mentions runs out of the environment as readily as out of the module being walked — `partial_definitions` says seeding from the environment is load-bearing rather than an optimization — so a `Total` on a partial definition already in scope lets a proof reaching it pass (V). That classification is the certifier's own. `Globals::of` and `Globals::mount` seed the non-total set from a unit's record where the record covers the unit, and otherwise hold the unit's items as unclassified, which `partial_definitions` classifies from their terms exactly as it classifies an item it judges. Nothing on that route reads the totality elaboration stamped on a definition. `recheck::totality_tests::a_carried_totality_stamp_is_ignored_where_the_certifiers_record_classifies` mounts an honest record beside a library whose stamp lies and requires the proof reaching the lie refused exactly as the same library judged fresh refuses it; `a_unit_mounted_with_an_empty_record_is_classified_by_the_reading_walk` mounts the lying library with an empty record, as an environment built by hand is; and `a_record_that_does_not_cover_its_unit_is_not_read` mounts it with a forged record naming the lying definition `Total` and nothing else, which a reader taking a record one name at a time would believe.

**What holds a record to its unit.** It is a field of the `Unit` it classifies, and a `Unit` is made only by certifying an `Uncertified` one with the record its walk left, so no unit in scope lacks one; it is filed, addressed and found as the unit is — [Cached verdicts](../admission-without-judgment/cached-verdicts.md) answers for that, and the address names the compiler binary whose certifier made it. A unit the pipeline compiles carries the record of the walk that judged it (`curios-pipeline`'s `certification_tests::a_compiled_unit_files_the_certifiers_record_of_every_definition`), a stored one brings it back (`curios-verdicts`'s `a_reused_unit_brings_its_certification_back`), and an item-level recompile joins the baseline's classifications for the items it reused to its own walk's (`an_item_level_recompile_keeps_the_baselines_record_for_what_it_reuses`) on the per-item argument Cached verdicts makes: a reused item's classification is closed over what it mentions, and nothing it mentions is in the invalidation closure. The fixed prelude's records are a constant of `curios-prelude`'s build, made by the walk that certified its images from an empty environment and that fails the build on any refusal, and attached where that crate lends the units (`the_restored_prelude_carries_a_record_covering_every_definition`). A record is read only where it covers its unit: one naming fewer definitions than the unit holds was not made by a walk over that unit, so none of its entries is known to be the closure it claims. A unit mounted with a record that does not cover it — an empty one, for an environment built by hand — is classified by the reading walk, which costs that walk the unit's termination analysis and admits nothing.

**The stamp is still written, and the kernel reads it only as a disagreement.** Elaboration's `record_totality` still stamps each definition, and the elaborator still seeds its own classification from the stamps of what it inherits. The kernel reads a stamp only on an item it judges, comparing it after the closure against its own closed verdict: `a_totality_stamp_contradicted_only_by_the_closure_is_refused` is the refusal, and `an_honest_stamp_on_a_definition_reaching_a_partial_one_is_accepted` is the control that a `Partial` classification stays a classification rather than an error. A wrong stamp on a judged item is a disagreement between the two checkers; a wrong stamp on a carried one is read by nothing the kernel decides. Nor by anything below it: the erased representation's termination flag, which the optimizer's pruning and rebasing and the Cont lowerer rest on when they drop or reorder a call, is marked from the same records where the sealed program meets them — every unit's in scope and the entry's own — and erasure itself marks nothing. `curios-elab`'s `into_ersd::tests::erasure_marks_no_function_total_whatever_its_stamp` and `a_record_marks_the_functions_its_total_definitions_erased_to` hold the two halves, and `curios-pipeline`'s `certification_tests::a_sealed_program_marks_its_functions_from_the_certifiers_records` holds every mark on a compiled program to a record classifying it `Total`. The elaborator's classification keeps a premise of its own: `classify_module` treats a name neither defined nor inherited as total, on the argument that every name a zonked module mentions is defined in it or in a unit it was elaborated against. That is fail-open, and it now bears on the elaborator's verdict alone.

**A recorded `Partial` may be the budget's, and a recorded `Total` cannot be.** The size-change engine reads an argument's shape through its driver's reduction, and a read the budget refuses answers *unread* — `unfolded_shape` and the guard read in `curios-analysis`'s `totality` — so a group whose descent shows only behind an unfolding classifies `Partial` under a budget that dies inside that read, where a larger one classifies it `Total`. Nothing demands descent of a group erasure keeps, so where nothing after the read spends, the group is accepted and classified `Partial`, the record is filed with the unit, and Cached verdicts hands it to a later compile under any budget. It is the refusing direction, a `Partial` classification only ever withholding a use, and it is an incompleteness that outlives the invocation that produced it. No fixture produces one: it needs the declaration's remainder to run dry inside the read and not before it, and `curios`'s budget ladder, which puts a type-level recursion descending by division to every rung, met only refusals naming the budget. The other direction does not exist. A group is `Total` when every idempotent call path descends, and its calls are the applications the walk typed: a typing the budget refuses refuses the item rather than dropping the call, and a refused read removes a decrease and never an edge, so running out cannot manufacture a `Total` — which is what an optimization resting on the verdict would need it not to do.
