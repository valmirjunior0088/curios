# An elimination at an ambient goal

**Assumes.** An arm inhabits the goal as written in the enclosing context once that goal is specialized by the most-general unifier of its case

**Status.** **probed**, in both checkers, at the rule's two heads and at its refusals.

**Why a second result form exists.** A motive is a closed family, checked under fresh binders outside every arm — and that is the one obligation the arm rule does not need. Both checkers already specialize the arm's *context* by index inversion (see `documentation/design/language/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md`): the kernel substitutes the unifier into body and goal and shadows every local whose type it touches, the elaborator refines the head and each variable index in the arm's frame. Under that rule the goal can be stated once, where it was written, and taken at each case by the same substitution — `T[x := c(ȳ), ī := targets]` in `Γσ` — with no family to close. The closed family fails exactly where a hypothesis's type mentions the scrutinee: `/std/Str/Valid/drop`'s `d : from(s, b)` no longer matches the position it occupies once `s` becomes a fresh binder. That mismatch was the whole reason the elaborator synthesized a convoy, a Π over such hypotheses applied back to the originals, and the ambient form retires it. `MatchResult::Ambient` in `curios-core/src/term/shape.rs` is the form; `MatchResult::at` is the substitution, shared by every consumer.

**The rule, as the kernel states it.** `check_motive` infers the goal's type in the ambient context and reads its sort, which is what the large-elimination guard consumes; `check_arm` takes the goal at the case's targets and value and substitutes the unifier's solutions as it does for a family; `infer` reads the elimination's type as the goal itself. Soundness is the specialization rule's: substitution preserves typing, the unifier is most general so every instance of the case factors through it, and definitional K licenses the deletion rule (`index-inversion-and-k.md`). Nothing new is trusted; what is removed is a check the family form needed only because it was a family. The elaborator's arm does what the kernel's `shadow` does: every local whose type mentions a substituted variable is re-assumed at the substituted type (`shadow_specialized` in `curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs`), so a hypothesis typed by the scrutinee is a tuple, or a witness at the case, wherever it is read — including in a metavariable solution parked and retried outside the arm's frame, which the frame's refinements alone would not reach.

**Substitution carries the form to an expression scrutinee, and the rule is closed under it.** Elaboration builds the form over a variable, which a case is substituted for. The kernel then substitutes a `let` value into its tail and reduction contracts a redex, so the same match reaches the arm rule with an expression at its head and the goal substituted alongside. For that shape the goal's *syntactic occurrences* of the head expression stand for the case — `T[v ↦ c(ȳ)]`, the with-abstraction reading — which is a fixed operation on the spelling and so a certification contract, where a case equation is not: an occurrence the spelling does not show is one the rule does not touch, and the arm is then simply refused. Sound for the reason with-abstraction is: when the head computes to a constructor the elimination reduces to that arm, whose goal has the head's spelling replaced by what the head is. The kernel's `an_ambient_result_over_an_expression_replaces_its_occurrences` puts both halves to it: a goal naming the expression, taken at the case in the arm and inhabited there by a hypothesis stated at the expression through the case equation the arm records, and one that does not name it, the same goal in every arm.

**One precondition, stated rather than assumed.** A free-monoid fold keeps a family: its induction hypothesis is the fold at the tail, typed at the result at the tail, and once the head has been substituted away an ambient goal has no tail to be taken at. The kernel refuses with `KernelError::AmbientFold`; the elaborator never builds one.

**Evidence.** `curios-cert/src/kernel/infer/eliminate/ambient_tests.rs`: an ambient elimination whose goal mentions a hypothesis typed by the scrutinee, accepted, beside the closed family stating the same thing, refused as not a motive — the pair that is the form's reason to exist; a lying ambient goal refused as the mismatch it is; an ambient result over an expression read by occurrence; an ambient fold refused. The elaborator's side is `curios/src/tests/matching/motive_tests.rs::a_hypothesis_typed_by_the_scrutinee_needs_no_convoy` and `tests::soundness::a_proof_over_a_dependent_hypothesis_descends`, the program the synthesized convoy used to hide from the size-change walk.
