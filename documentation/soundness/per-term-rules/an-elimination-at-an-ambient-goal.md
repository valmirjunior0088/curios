# An elimination at an ambient goal

**Assumes.** An arm inhabits the goal as written in the enclosing context once that goal is specialized by the most-general unifier of its case

**Status.** **probed**, in both checkers, at the rule's two heads and at its refusals.

**Why a second result form exists.** A motive is a closed family, checked under fresh binders outside every arm — and that is the one obligation the arm rule does not need. Both checkers already specialize the arm's *context* by index inversion (see `documentation/design/language/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md`): the kernel substitutes the unifier into body and goal, the elaborator records it as refinements in the arm's frame, and both re-assume every local whose type it touches. Under that rule the goal can be stated once, where it was written, and taken at each case by the same substitution — `T[x := c(ȳ), ī := targets]` in `Γσ` — with no family to close. The closed family fails exactly where a hypothesis's type mentions the scrutinee: `/std/Str/Valid/from_bad`'s `w : from(Scan/bad(), x)` no longer matches the position it occupies once `x` becomes a fresh binder. That mismatch was the whole reason the elaborator synthesized a convoy, a Π over such hypotheses applied back to the originals, and the ambient form retires it. `MatchResult::Ambient` in `curios-core/src/term/shape.rs` is the form; `MatchResult::at` is the substitution, shared by every consumer.

**The rule, as the kernel states it.** `check_motive` infers the goal's type in the ambient context and reads its sort, which is what the large-elimination guard consumes; `check_arm` takes the goal at the case's targets and value and substitutes the unifier's solutions as it does for a family; `infer` reads the elimination's type as the goal itself. Soundness is the specialization rule's: substitution preserves typing, the unifier is most general so every instance of the case factors through it, and definitional K licenses the deletion rule (`index-inversion-and-k.md`). Nothing new is trusted; what is removed is a check the family form needed only because it was a family. Which locals are re-assumed, and at what type, is one shared rule both checkers' arms apply, `retyped` in `curios-analysis/src/specialize.rs`: every local whose type mentions a variable the case's solution solves, reading through local definitions, at the substituted type, in every arm and for either result form. So a hypothesis typed by the scrutinee is a tuple, or a witness at the case, wherever it is read — including in a metavariable solution parked and retried outside the arm's frame, which the frame's refinements alone would not reach. The elaborator once re-typed by the goal's own substitution instead, only at an ambient goal and even on a clash, and so accepted an unreachable arm the kernel refused.

**The form reaches an expression scrutinee two ways, and the rule is closed under both.** Elaboration builds it wherever a motive is elided in a checked position, over a variable, which a case is substituted for, or over an expression as written. Substitution carries a variable's form to an expression too: the kernel substitutes a `let` value into its tail and reduction contracts a redex, so the same match reaches the arm rule with an expression at its head and the goal substituted alongside. For an expression head the goal's *syntactic occurrences* of it stand for the case — `T[v ↦ c(ȳ)]`, the with-abstraction reading — which is a fixed operation on the spelling and so a certification contract, where a case equation is not: an occurrence the spelling does not show is one the rule does not touch, which the arm meets only through the case equation it records, keyed as [The refinement key](../what-the-kernel-consults/the-refinement-key.md) states, and the arm is refused where that equation does not fire. Sound for the reason with-abstraction is: when the head computes to a constructor the elimination reduces to that arm, whose goal has the head's spelling replaced by what the head is. The kernel's `an_ambient_result_over_an_expression_replaces_its_occurrences` puts both halves to it: a goal naming the expression, taken at the case in the arm and inhabited there by a hypothesis stated at the expression through the case equation the arm records, and one that does not name it, the same goal in every arm.

**One precondition, stated rather than assumed.** A free-monoid fold whose arm reads its induction hypothesis keeps a family: the hypothesis is the fold at the tail, typed at the result at the tail, and once the head has been substituted away an ambient goal has no tail to be taken at. The kernel refuses one with `KernelError::AmbientFold`; the elaborator never builds one. The rule every reader of a fold states is the same one — the hypothesis is assumed exactly when the arm reads it, and only a family types it — so a case split, whose arm reads none, takes the ambient form as a `Bool` elimination does. Sound by subject reduction: the split's reduct `arm(h, t, fold(t))` never contains the fold at the tail, so nothing needs the type an ambient goal cannot give it. The family's other precondition, that its motive reach the scrutinee only through its binder (`KernelError::FoldMotiveCapturesScrutinee`), protects the hypothesis's type and binds exactly the same folds.

**Evidence.** `curios-cert/src/kernel/infer/eliminate/ambient_tests.rs`: an ambient elimination whose goal mentions a hypothesis typed by the scrutinee, accepted, beside the closed family stating the same thing, refused as not a motive — the pair that is the form's reason to exist; a lying ambient goal refused as the mismatch it is; an ambient result over an expression read by occurrence; an ambient fold that reads its hypothesis refused — mutation-checked, since admitting it fails that test — and an ambient case split checked at each case, with an arm that misses the goal refused beside it. The elaborator's side is `curios/src/tests/matching/motive_tests.rs` — `a_hypothesis_typed_by_the_scrutinee_needs_no_convoy`, the case split whose goal holds a proof about its scrutinee at `Bytes`, `List` and `Nat`, and the fold that reads its hypothesis refused at the same goal — and `tests::soundness::a_proof_over_a_dependent_hypothesis_descends`, the program the synthesized convoy used to hide from the size-change walk. The shared re-typing is `tests::perimeter::index_tests::an_unreachable_arm_retypes_no_local`, the clash the elaborator once accepted and the kernel refused, and the arms of `tests::inference::implicit_tests` whose implicit reads a hypothesis the case re-types: at the ambient goal, under a written motive, from inside an index, and over a `let` of the scrutinee.
