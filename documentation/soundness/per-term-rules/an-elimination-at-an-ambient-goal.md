# An elimination at an ambient goal

**Assumes.** An arm inhabits the goal as written in the enclosing context once that goal is specialized by the most-general unifier of its case

**Status.** **probed**, in both checkers, at the rule's precondition and at its two refusals.

**Why a second result form exists.** A motive is a closed family, checked under fresh binders outside every arm — and that is the one obligation the arm rule does not need. Both checkers already specialize the arm's *context* by index inversion (see `documentation/design/language/an-arm-is-checked-in-a-context-specialized-by-index-inversion.md`): the kernel substitutes the unifier into body and goal and shadows every local whose type it touches, the elaborator refines the head and each variable index in the arm's frame. Under that rule the goal can be stated once, where it was written, and taken at each case by the same substitution — `T[x := c(ȳ), ī := targets]` in `Γσ` — with no family to close. The closed family fails exactly where a hypothesis's type mentions the scrutinee: `d : Utf8(s, b)` no longer matches the position it occupies once `s` becomes a fresh binder. That mismatch was the whole reason the elaborator synthesized a convoy, a Π over such hypotheses applied back to the originals, and the ambient form retires it. `MatchResult::Ambient` in `curios-core/src/term/shape.rs` is the form; `MatchResult::at` is the substitution, shared by every consumer.

**The rule, as the kernel states it.** `check_motive` infers the goal's type in the ambient context and reads its sort, which is what the large-elimination guard consumes; `check_arm` takes the goal at the case's targets and value and substitutes the unifier's solutions as it does for a family; `infer` reads the elimination's type as the goal itself. Soundness is the specialization rule's: substitution preserves typing, the unifier is most general so every instance of the case factors through it, and definitional K licenses the deletion rule (`index-inversion-and-k.md`). Nothing new is trusted; what is removed is a check the family form needed only because it was a family.

**Two preconditions, stated rather than assumed.** The scrutinee must be a *variable* with a binder to substitute at: an expression's occurrences in the goal could only be read through a case equation, which the inversion record decided is an elaboration convenience and not a certification contract. The kernel refuses otherwise with `KernelError::AmbientOverExpression`, before anything else about the elimination is checked; the elaborator builds the form only over a variable head and leaves an expression scrutinee to occurrence abstraction, as before. And a free-monoid fold keeps a family: its induction hypothesis is the fold at the tail, typed at the result at the tail, and once the head has been substituted away an ambient goal has no tail to be taken at. The kernel refuses with `KernelError::AmbientFold`; the elaborator never builds one.

**Evidence.** `curios-cert/src/kernel/infer/eliminate/ambient_tests.rs`: an ambient elimination whose goal mentions a hypothesis typed by the scrutinee, accepted, beside the closed family stating the same thing, refused as not a motive — the pair that is the form's reason to exist; a lying ambient goal refused as the mismatch it is; an ambient result over a constructor value refused at the precondition; an ambient fold refused. The elaborator's side is `curios/src/tests/matching/motive_tests.rs::a_hypothesis_typed_by_the_scrutinee_needs_no_convoy` and `tests::soundness::a_proof_over_a_dependent_hypothesis_descends`, the program the synthesized convoy used to hide from the size-change walk.
