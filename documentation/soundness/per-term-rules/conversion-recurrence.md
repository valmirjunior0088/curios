# Conversion recurrence

**Assumes.** Absence of finite disagreement is sufficient for equality

**Status.** **argued** (the fixpoint on (T), recorded with its twin in [Definitional proof irrelevance](definitional-proof-irrelevance.md)); auditable only otherwise, and inert in the kernel — the only thing that reaches the rule there is `kernel::convert::recursion_tests`, where `a_recurring_goal_is_assumed_rather_than_unfolded_forever` reaches it on two spellings of a recursive type equation and `a_recurrence_does_not_excuse_a_finite_disagreement` is the control a blanket accept would fail. The elaborator's key is **probed** at its cross-branch collision: the collision is real and demonstrated verdict-preserving — the closing paragraph is the account.

**The *key* is where this rule admits, and the kernel's has been audited.** A history hit on two goals that are not the same goal is the one way absence-of-disagreement becomes absence-of-a-comparison. The kernel's goal records the local context, the type and both terms, each renamed by position, and omits the two pieces of ambient state a comparison also reads: the case equations assumed inside an arm, which the reducer consults at stuck heads, and the item's assumed universe constraints, which entailment discharges against. Neither omission can bite today, and for a structural reason rather than a designed one — conversion mutates neither set, and a history is created per top-level conversion, so both are constant for its lifetime and cannot differ between two colliding entries. That is a property of what conversion happens *not* to do, so teaching it to consult a case equation — the built-in convoy's natural next step — would make the key incomplete without anyone touching the key. The equation set itself now has its own entry, [Case equations inside an arm](../what-the-kernel-consults/case-equations-inside-an-arm.md), which is where the argument for its scoping lives.

**"Records the local context" is true of the key and weaker than it reads.** A grounded comparison opens both scopes at one shared set of opaque binders and *assumes every one of them at `Type`*, a stand-in `ground_scope` justifies by the claim that a binder's recorded type feeds only this key, identically on both sides. So in exactly the positions grounding reaches, the context component of the key is a run of `Type`s carrying no information about what the binders really were, and what separates two goals there is the terms alone. Nothing observed today turns on it, since the binders are the same opaque identities on both sides and are renamed by position anyway. It is recorded because the entry's own argument is about what the key can tell apart, and a reader checking that argument against the code will find a context that discriminates in the nested case and not in the grounded one

**The grade above is the kernel's, and the copy that fires is keyed by a different rule.** The elaborator has its own key and it is not the same one: it renames the labels *it* minted, in mint order, and does not record the local context at all. For a strictly nested walk the two schemes coincide, since the binders in scope at a goal are exactly the path to it — but the elaborator's walk is not nested. It carries a worklist and it parks goals, so two comparisons reached under different binder types can be renamed onto one fingerprint. Nothing about the kernel's inertness bounds the elaborator's copy — inertness is a fact about the goals that arrive, and these are two different populations. Two goals distinct under their binder types, colliding on one elaborator history key, are assumed rather than compared. `curios-elab`'s `convert::solve_tests::two_goals_distinct_under_their_binder_types_share_one_history_key` builds the two body goals through the same telescope walk the drain dispatches to, one under a `Nat` binder and one under a `Bool`, and they rename onto one fingerprint whose second arrival hits the history; instrumenting the drain's hit branch while the probe was written showed the same skip inside a real run, with the assumed goal a genuinely false equation in the refusing half.

What the collision cannot do is move a verdict, and the reason is the openings' uniformity rather than the key. An opening is typeless everywhere in the elaborator — `Context::fresh` mints a bare label, no rule assumes a type for it, and `synth_neutral` and `apply_param_types` answer `None` for one — so every rule behind the history guard treats two openings alike, a collided goal is its twin under an injective relabeling of openings, and any finite disagreement it hides is also the twin's, whose own children surface it. `a_goal_assumed_by_key_collision_cannot_move_the_verdict` holds both directions: aliased definitions accept, and a disagreement hidden in the assumed goal still refuses through its twin's spine child. A null result on soundness — and the uniformity it rests on is an inventory of exactly `ground_scope`'s kind, held by these fixtures at the demonstrated site and by nothing else: a rule taught to read an opening's type invalidates it in silence

**Two instances of one recursive group are a level question, and neither rule sees them.** The population the paragraph above anticipated arrived: a signature whose declared type spells `/std/List/zip` at the levels of its type parameters while its body carries the same group at the levels of its list arguments, related only by cumulativity. The kernel's context-carrying key never collided on it — every unfolding added two opaque binders to a context the key copies whole — so the walk grew until the host died with the budget barely spent; the elaborator's key collided on the second round and assumed the pair, never committing the level equality its own license for level-differing spellings demands, so the declaration finalized with two universe parameters where the program wrote one level. Both halves now decide the pair before either recurrence rule is reached. The kernel decides two projections of one group that agree modulo levels by their level vectors under the item's hypotheses — `rec_instances`, held by `two_instances_of_one_recursive_group_convert_when_their_levels_are_equal_under_the_hypotheses`, `two_instances_of_one_recursive_group_at_unequal_levels_are_refused_without_unfolding` (which records the mutation: without the head rule it exhausts a small budget) and `two_calls_of_one_recursive_group_with_different_spines_still_retry_through_an_unfolding` — over a skeleton that keeps every level's position, the opposite of the projection [The refinement key](../what-the-kernel-consults/the-refinement-key.md) refuses as a key. The elaborator identifies the levels at the applied head and, where a term metavariable kept the head undecided until the pair recurred, at the recurrence: `level_recurrence` identifies a recurring pair whose sides agree modulo levels, refuses one whose levels are unequal ground levels, parks one that only an unsolved metavariable keeps from being a level question, and assumes only a genuine cycle — held by the two fixtures sharing the kernel's names, `a_recurrence_whose_sides_differ_only_in_levels_is_identified_rather_than_assumed` and `a_recurrence_blocked_on_a_metavariable_parks_and_commits_once_it_is_solved`, with `a_signature_instantiating_one_recursive_definition_twice_certifies_with_its_levels_identified` putting the program itself to the compile path. What is unchanged is the control population: `a_recurring_goal_is_assumed_rather_than_unfolded_forever` and its control in the kernel, `two_goals_distinct_under_their_binder_types_share_one_history_key` and `a_goal_assumed_by_key_collision_cannot_move_the_verdict` in the elaborator, whose sides differ in terms and carry no universe data. A pair an unsolved metavariable *inside* the group keeps from the head decision is not left to the unfolding either: the unfolding runs, since it is what solves the metavariable, and the two heads are put to the walk as a problem of their own, decided by the same level question once the metavariable is solved — the `(Rec, Rec)` arm asks it before the member-wise comparison, whose sorts would otherwise commit the levels whatever the bodies then decide — so a pair the unfolding decides structurally without ever recurring (a literal count reduces both matches to their zero arms) still leaves with its levels identified, held by `two_instances_kept_apart_only_by_a_metavariable_identify_their_levels_once_it_is_solved`, whose second half pins that two distinct holes park the head problem without committing.
