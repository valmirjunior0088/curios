# Eta and untyped child positions

**Assumes.** Eta at Π and Σ preserves equality, and comparing an untyped child at `Type` forfeits rules rather than adding them

**Status.** **argued** and **probed** at both rules, at their composition, and at the stand-in the grounded comparison opens its binders with; what is left **argued** is the caller-side fence the last paragraph names, the one configuration where the stand-in is the more permissive of the two. Conversion is type-directed, so eta is what converts a function with its expansion and a pair with its projections without either side being in that shape; `kernel::whnf::tests` covers both spellings, with `eta_contracts_a_function_that_only_forwards` and `eta_declines_when_the_head_mentions_the_binder` on the contracting side. The second clause reads as a weakness and is a guard: an application spine's arguments, a stuck elimination's motive and arms under their opaque binders, and a struct type-former's parameters are compared at `Type` rather than at the types their head assigns, which *forfeits* type-directed eta and goal-level irrelevance in those positions. A forfeited rule cannot admit — it is why the kernel's own irrelevance never fires — so the clause moves only toward refusal, and a refusal is a disagreement, which is the signal the second checker exists to produce.

**Both rules are probed in both directions, and their composition is reachable only at Σ.** Eta at Π and at Σ each accept their expansion and each refuse a near miss — an expansion that drops its binder, and one that swaps its components — so a comparison answering *true* at every Π and every Σ would satisfy the accepting rung and fail those two. The composition is unreachable at Π, structurally: conversion tries irrelevance *before* it dispatches on the goal type's shape, and Π into a proposition is a proposition whatever it quantifies over, so the goal is discharged whole at the top and eta never opens a binder — `a_function_into_a_proposition_is_discharged_before_eta` is that accepting rung, with a relevant codomain refused beside it so the discharge cannot read as conversion giving up on function types. At Σ a record stays relevant as soon as one component is, irrelevance does not preempt eta, and eta is what hands the proof component to a `Prop`-sorted goal: `hands_a_records_proof_component_to_irrelevance` runs both rules in one equation, with `still_compares_a_records_relevant_component` holding the other component to a comparison, so the acceptance is not eta declining to look.

**The forfeiture is executable, and a nominal struct's eta is not part of it.** Two proofs of one proposition standing as arguments of an opaque head are compared at `Type`, irrelevance is never asked, and the kernel refuses a program the elaborator accepts: `a_grounded_argument_forfeits_irrelevance` is the two-checker matrix's instance of *the kernel refusing what the elaborator accepts*, recorded conversion incompleteness. What grounding forfeits is *type-directed* eta and *goal-level* irrelevance, the two rules conversion reaches by asking what the goal type is; a nominal struct's eta is neither. It reads the literal's own declaration, so it fires from the structural comparison, which is exactly where a grounded pair lands, and it skips a `Prop`-sorted field by classifying the declaration's field type. `a_nominal_structs_eta_is_not_forfeited_there` is the sharpest spelling: every field of the struct is a proposition, the field walk therefore compares nothing at all, and the literal is equated with the neutral on the strength of the neutral restriction alone. What establishes that the neutral inhabits the struct is not that restriction, which excludes only non-neutrals and not neutrals of the wrong type, but the callers: every grounded pair is the corresponding children of two parents already shown convertible, so their types agree by typing — argument comparison runs only after the two heads were accepted, and every other grounded position pairs children of one shape. That is a property of the callers rather than of the rule, and it is written in neither place.

**The stand-in is sound because of its value, not because nothing reads it.** Opening two scopes at shared opaque binders assumes each of them at `Type` regardless of what it really is. `ground_scope`'s own justification — that a binder's recorded type feeds only the conversion history's context key — is false: `synth_neutral` answers a `Var` with `Kernel::type_of`, which is that same recorded type, so it reaches `Sort::of`, and `Sort::of` is what `compare` asks before *every* goal, because the proof-irrelevance test is the first rule tried. What holds the stand-in up is that `Type` is the least informative answer `Sort::of` can return for a binder: irrelevance fires on `Sort::Prop` and on nothing else, and eta dispatches on the goal type's own shape rather than on a binder's, so both accepting rules are keyed on a positive answer the stand-in cannot produce. `curios-cert`'s `kernel::convert::tests::a_binders_stand_in_type_decides_a_goal_the_way_a_relevant_type_does` is the grid: one goal at each type the binder could really carry — `Prop`, a relevant `Type 3`, the `Type 0` stand-in, and a non-sort — against two side-pairs, distinct and convertible-but-not-identical, and the stand-in's row matches the relevant-sort row in both halves. `a_grounded_motive_binder_carries_the_stand_in_rather_than_its_real_type` reaches the same conclusion through `ground_scope` itself, on two stuck `bool_match`es whose motive bodies index a family at its own parameter, and pins the counterfactual beside it: the same binder assumed at `Prop` discharges the goal by irrelevance, so the verdict does move and it moves toward refusal. It is mutation-checked against the one substitution that would make grounding *add* the rule — assuming the opaque binders at `Prop` rather than at `Type` makes it fail naming the discharged index actuals — and carries a beta-redex control, so the refusal is a relevant sort deciding `u ≠ v` rather than the grounded scope declining to compare its bodies. The argument from the value is the durable one: an inventory of readers is falsified by any new call site, and a claim about the stand-in's value by nothing short of changing the stand-in.

**One row is not a forfeiture, and its fence is the callers'.** A binder whose real type is not a sort at all leaves `Sort::of` nothing to decode, so the typed opening refuses the whole certification with `NotASort` while the stand-in classifies it `Type 0` and goes on to accept. There grounding is strictly *more* permissive than the truth, and nothing in `ground_scope` fences it: what does is that `infer`'s `check_motive` types a motive under its real binders before any comparison grounds it, so a motive using a `Bool`-typed binder as a type never reaches here — the same shape as the struct-eta restriction above, resting on callers and written in neither place. **Missing test:** that fence.
