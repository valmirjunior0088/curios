# A fold motive binds its scrutinee

**Assumes.** A free-monoid fold's induction hypothesis is typed at the motive opened at the tail, and nothing else in the arm reaches the scrutinee's occurrence in it

**Status.** **probed**, in both checkers, from the surface and by construction.

**The hole was context specialization leaking into the hypothesis.** A fold's cons arm is checked with the scrutinee specialized to the cons value — substituted and shadowed in the kernel, refined in the elaborator's frame — which is what lets an arm-local hypothesis reduce at the case. The induction hypothesis is assumed inside that same arm, at the motive opened at the tail. A motive that *names* the scrutinee instead of binding it, `match n : (_) => Eq(n, 0)`, has that occurrence specialized as well, so the hypothesis reads `Eq(k + 1, 0)`, the arm's own goal, and `| k + 1; ih => ih` closes the induction. `all_zero(1) : Eq(1, 0)` then certifies. Verified while the hole was open: the surface program compiled, ran, and printed, and so did the same shape over a `List` fold, over an expression scrutinee read through its case equation (`match twice(n) : (_) => Eq(twice(n), 0)`), and over a `let` alias of the scrutinee. A recursor without context specialization has no such hole — its successor arm is checked at arbitrary `k` with the outer `n` untouched, which is exactly why `refl` would not have typed the base arm there — so the rule is the price of the specialization the inversion record decided on, not a defect of motives.

**The rule is syntactic, and that is its whole reach.** A fold's motive may not mention its scrutinee: for a variable, a free occurrence; for an expression, the spelling standing whole. The kernel tests `motive.body().mentions_term(scrutinee)` in `check_free_monoid` and refuses with `KernelError::FoldMotiveCapturesScrutinee`; the elaborator tests the same in the three fold paths of `curios-elab/src/elaborate/match_.rs` (`refuse_captured_scrutinee`) and additionally reads a defined local through its definition, because a `let` alias is a definition the reducer reads through in the arm, while the kernel sees the alias substituted away by then. An occurrence hidden behind a definition the reducer would *not* read whole — a respelling — is one the case equation cannot fire on either, which is why a syntactic test is exact here where it would be an approximation elsewhere. The elaborator's copy does not read a metavariable's spine, which names every local in scope and is an occurrence of none of them; a solution that does capture the scrutinee is met by the kernel's copy, which runs post-zonk. An elided motive over a bare variable closes over that variable and captures nothing; one over an expression is solved by occurrence abstraction, which refuses an occurrence it cannot abstract, so only a written motive reaches the rule.

**Evidence.** `curios/src/tests/perimeter/fold_tests.rs` holds the four surface routes above as `rejected_by` fixtures and a binding control that folds, all in the perimeter corpus put to both checkers; the elaborator refuses first, so the kernel's column is `NotAsked` there. `curios-cert/src/recheck/elimination_tests.rs::a_fold_motive_that_captures_its_scrutinee_is_refused` puts the hand-built module to the kernel alone, with `a_fold_motive_that_binds_its_scrutinee_types_the_hypothesis_at_the_tail` as the control that is refused at the successor arm as the mismatch it is, and `a_fold_motive_that_binds_its_scrutinee_is_accepted_at_an_inhabited_goal` as the one that certifies.
