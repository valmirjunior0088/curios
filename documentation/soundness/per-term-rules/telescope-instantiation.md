# Telescope instantiation

**Assumes.** A dependent telescope is opened at the actuals already checked, in order, and plicity is part of a function type's identity

**Status.** **probed incidentally, never directly.** One assumption serving five typing rules — an application's arguments, a projection's field, and the three literal forms — and four conversion sites: binder comparison, tuple eta, nominal argument comparison, and struct eta. Each opens the rest of a telescope at what it has already accepted, and each is sound only because the earlier positions were established first; tuple eta opens at the *left* side's projection and says so in its own doc, nominal comparison at the left instance's actuals. Nothing states the shared rule, so nothing holds the four spellings to it, and a fifth added later would be judged by whichever neighbour its author read. The plicity clause is the half most obviously able to admit: conflating an explicit binder with an implicit one would let a value be applied through the wrong convention, and erasure masks by plicity, so the two differ in what survives. It is guarded by hand in three places — conversion's function and function-type arms, and subsumption — each with a comment, and no row saying why the guard has to be there
