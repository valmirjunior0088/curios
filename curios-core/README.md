# curios-core

The Curios core language: the term representation both checkers build on — what a term *is*, with no rule that judges one — including the intrinsic folds, the closed machine and the linear views a comparison is read through. The judgments live on either side: `curios-analysis` holds the rules both checkers run, `curios-cert` the kernel that only checks, `curios-elab` everything that makes the surface language work, and all three depend on this crate with none of those dependencies reversing ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)). The carriers' algebra is `curios-algebra`'s, over abstract atoms: this crate says which operation each intrinsic is (`Intrinsic::algebra`) and which terms are one atom (`atoms`), reads terms into the algebra's views, and rebuilds terms from what it concludes ([The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../documentation/design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md)). Local architecture belongs to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### A name is an identity, not a record

**Decision.** A compiler name distinguishes one binding from another and renders for a human, and nothing else: behaviour never branches on a name's characters, prefix or collation order, and a fact known where a name was built is carried as a value, never recovered by taking the name apart. The vocabulary — `Free`, `Global`, `Mint`, `WitnessId` — belongs to `names.rs`'s rustdoc, and the property is upheld by the absence of accessors: a `Free` yields no text but its display hint (`Free::hint`), read to mint a fresh binder's hint and to spell a diagnostic, so behaviour from spelling would need a new method on a name type.

**Rationale.** A spelling that carries structured facts — a namespace path, global against local, minted against authored, a minted binder's hint, an owner and its family — is an undocumented wire format between stages, each consumer deserializing it with a parser of its own that is sound only under an invariant stated elsewhere. Such a parser misfires — a `#` test for minted locals is defeated by a `#`-bearing global and discards cache entries a prelude build needs — and a constructor's runtime tag read as its alphabetical rank renumbers, when a case is renamed, the tags of every case it sorts past.

**Rejected.** Fixing the readers, which leaves the encoders and invites the next parser; a reserved sigil namespace, one wire-format field for another; a flat `Global { qualifier, disambiguator }`, whose `qualifier` would read as module plus name for an authored global and as the declaring module for a witness.

**Rejected — de Bruijn levels for free variables.** Bound variables are indices and stay so; identifying opened binders by level would make context-dependence structural, and typed names already deliver every correctness benefit while the risk concentrates in the least forgiving parts of the tree. Conversion's `history_key` renames minted opening labels in mint order to collapse successive rounds of an unfolding cycle, and mint order is not depth order — siblings at equal depth share a level — so whether the schemes are equivalent cannot be settled by reading, and a false history hit is unsoundness. Parked work freezes a local frame and reapplies it at a possibly different depth, which identities make free and levels would require rebasing, and the lowerer would track depth through decision-tree compilation. Neither the mutation stamp nor the linear scan is an objection: `Sort::of_in` threads its opened binders rather than assuming them into the `Context`, but a level is the context's depth plus a position, readable without mutation, and the scan holds one entry per telescope binder. Reopen on a concrete problem, settling the `history_key` question first.

### A foreign call is typed through the signature an intrinsic is

**Decision.** `foreign_signature` states a host call's operands and result in the `Signature` vocabulary `Intrinsic::signature` uses: a returning row's operands `Operand::At` their wire types and its result `Produced::Fixed(Io(…))` over its wire results, and the one diverging row, exit, a type operand ahead of its `Byte` producing `Io(A)`. Both checkers walk that signature with the operand handling they share with intrinsics, and erasure drops the type operand as it drops an intrinsic's. The row is read through the identity the term carries — the roster's for a builtin, the declaration's own for a user's `foreign` — so nothing written beside it reaches the type.

**Rationale.** A rule of each checker's own reading a signature off the term let a term say what its row did not and two checkers disagree; typed as an intrinsic, a call has one statement and one walk ([A host operation has one contract, checked at both ends](../documentation/design/effects/a-host-operation-has-one-contract-checked-at-both-ends.md)).

**Rejected.** A result-type field on the foreign term, which admits a type for a returning row and none for a diverging one; a dedicated `Exit` intrinsic, a compiler exception for what a diverging row describes.

### The algebra's demand boundaries are Core's

**Decision.** When the algebra runs is Core's: a symbolic product distributes only at the normalization gates; reading a Boolean formula stops at the eight-atom cap without forcing the rest of the input; a stuck connective keeps its right operand as written; atom arguments are forced only where a pair conversion decided nothing, once for every reader of the pair (`force_atoms`), and a pair still undecided keeps the spelling it was handed; a window measures its operands only as far as the seam walk reaches. The algebra reports the work it would do before doing it, and Core charges it. Refinement keys keep their written spellings and the dual and successor probes; the canonical linear view reads a comparison where one is asked for and rebuilds no key.

**Rationale.** Demand, spelling and cost change together when a rule runs eagerly, and a guard's refinement is keyed on its written spelling ([A law is decided where it neither respells nor invents](../documentation/design/arithmetic/a-law-is-decided-where-it-neither-respells-nor-invents.md)), so the boundaries stay where the terms are.
