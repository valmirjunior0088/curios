# curios-core

The Curios core language: the term representation both checkers build on — what a term *is*, with no rule that judges one — including the intrinsic folds, the closed machine and the linear views a comparison is read through. The judgments live on either side: `curios-analysis` holds the rules both checkers run, `curios-cert` the kernel that only checks, `curios-elab` everything that makes the surface language work, and all three depend on this crate with none of those dependencies reversing ([An independent kernel re-checks what the elaborator accepts](../documentation/design/soundness/an-independent-kernel-re-checks-what-the-elaborator-accepts.md)). The carriers' algebra is `curios-algebra`'s, over abstract atoms: this crate says which operation each intrinsic is (`Intrinsic::algebra`) and which terms are one atom (`atoms`), reads terms into the algebra's views, and rebuilds terms from what it concludes ([The carriers' algebra stays in conversion](../documentation/design/arithmetic/the-carriers-algebra-stays-in-conversion.md)). Local architecture belongs to the crate rustdoc.

## Design

### A name is an identity, not a record

**Decision.** A compiler name distinguishes one binding from another and renders for a human, and nothing else: behaviour never branches on a name's characters, prefix or collation order, and a fact known where a name was built is carried as a value, never recovered by taking the name apart. A local's hint is what its author called it: a member written without a name and a binder the compiler introduces have none, so no stage mints a spelling to stand for either, and a hint's presence is the fact that source can write the name (`Free::nameable`). The vocabulary — `Free`, `Global`, `Mint`, `WitnessId` — belongs to `names.rs`'s rustdoc, and the property is upheld by the absence of accessors: a `Free` yields no text but its display hint (`Free::hint`), read to mint a fresh binder's hint and to spell a diagnostic, so behaviour from spelling would need a new method on a name type.

**Rationale.** A spelling that carries structured facts — a namespace path, global against local, minted against authored, a minted binder's hint, an owner and its family — is an undocumented wire format between stages, each consumer deserializing it with a parser of its own that is sound only under an invariant stated elsewhere. Such a parser misfires — a `#` test for minted locals is defeated by a `#`-bearing global and discards cache entries a prelude build needs — and a constructor's runtime tag read as its alphabetical rank renumbers, when a case is renamed, the tags of every case it sorts past.

**Rejected.** Fixing the readers, which leaves the encoders and invites the next parser; a reserved sigil namespace, one wire-format field for another; a flat `Global { qualifier, disambiguator }`, whose `qualifier` would read as module plus name for an authored global and as the declaring module for a witness.

**Rejected — de Bruijn levels for free variables.** Bound variables are indices and stay so; identifying opened binders by level would make context-dependence structural, and typed names already deliver every correctness benefit while the risk concentrates in the least forgiving parts of the tree. Conversion's `history_key` renames minted opening labels in mint order to collapse successive rounds of an unfolding cycle, and mint order is not depth order — siblings at equal depth share a level — so whether the schemes are equivalent cannot be settled by reading, and a false history hit is unsoundness. Parked work freezes a local frame and reapplies it at a possibly different depth, which identities make free and levels would require rebasing, and the lowerer would track depth through decision-tree compilation. Neither the mutation stamp nor the linear scan is an objection: `Sort::of_in` threads its opened binders rather than assuming them into the `Context`, but a level is the context's depth plus a position, readable without mutation, and the scan holds one entry per telescope binder. Reopen on a concrete problem, settling the `history_key` question first.

### A nominal declaration's entry is held by its type former

**Decision.** What an `induct`, a `struct` or a `concept` declares — its parameters, its constructors or fields and its sort, and for a concept its resolution metadata with the record its witnesses are values of — is one `Nominal`, held by the definition that is the declaration's type former (`DefinitionKind::Nominal`). A `Module` holds no registry beside its items: an entry is read at the place its name is declared (`Module::nominal`), every stage reads an item's entries off its formers (`Item::nominals`), and what each of an item's names is declared as is in its outline, so a stored module says which names are formers without restoring one.

**Rationale.** Held in registries beside the items, an entry and its former are two facts under one name with nothing holding one to the other: a former with no entry, an entry no item declares and one name in two registries are each a module, and every stage that can meet one carries a check, a reconciliation or a refusal for it. Held by the former they are unrepresentable. An item's entries travel with it — restored with it, compared with it where a baseline is asked whether a declaration stands, undone with it where it is refused — and a name declares at most one nominal because it declares one definition.

**Rejected.** The entry as a payload of the kind beside registries the lowering keeps in step, which keeps the second copy; registries derived once for a module and held on it, which a unit read by item restores whole to build.

### A foreign call is typed through the signature an intrinsic is

**Decision.** `foreign_signature` states a host call's operands and result in the `Signature` vocabulary `Intrinsic::signature` uses: a returning row's operands `Operand::At` their wire types and its result `Produced::Fixed(Io(…))` over its wire results, and a row declared `-> Never`, as exit is, a type operand ahead of its own producing `Io(A)`. Both checkers walk that signature with the operand handling they share with intrinsics, and erasure drops the type operand as it drops an intrinsic's. The row is the one its declaration states, carried on the term, so nothing written beside it reaches the type.

**Rationale.** A rule of each checker's own reading a signature off the term let a term say what its row did not and two checkers disagree; typed as an intrinsic, a call has one statement and one walk ([A host operation is a foreign declaration whose types are its whole contract](../documentation/design/runtime/a-host-operation-is-a-foreign-declaration-whose-types-are-its-whole-contract.md)).

**Rejected.** A result-type field on the foreign term, which admits a type for a returning row and none for a diverging one; a dedicated `Exit` intrinsic, a compiler exception for what a diverging row describes.

### The algebra's demand boundaries are Core's

**Decision.** When the algebra runs is Core's: a symbolic product distributes only at the normalization gates — the chain before its peels, the comparison, a power's exponent, a structural `Nat` match's scrutinee, the elaborator's linear views, a magnitude's operand, and the classification reading two nodes of one bucket; reading a Boolean formula stops at the eight-atom cap without forcing the rest of the input; a stuck connective keeps its right operand as written; a pair's atoms are handed out to be classed only where conversion decided nothing of the pair, and read back one spelling to a class for every reader at once (`curios-analysis`'s `atoms_of` and `classed`), with which atoms are one left to the checker that asks, and a pair still undecided keeps the spelling it was handed; a stuck operation's atoms are handed out the same way where a checker's reducer asks, the fold runs once more over one spelling to a class, and its result is kept only where it is no longer that operation (its `atoms_within` and `refold`); a window measures its operands only as far as the seam walk reaches. The algebra reports the work it would do before doing it, and Core charges it. A refinement is recorded under its written spelling, asked there first with the dual and successor probes, and answers what conversion holds equal to it; the canonical linear view reads a comparison where one is asked for and rebuilds no key.

**Rationale.** Demand, spelling and cost change together when a rule runs eagerly, and a guard's refinement is recorded under its written spelling, which it meets for one lookup where another spelling costs a settlement and a question ([A term is one where conversion says so](../documentation/design/arithmetic/a-term-is-one-where-conversion-says-so.md)), so the boundaries stay where the terms are.

### A node's free variables are its children's, shared

**Decision.** A node carries the set of its free variables, filled where first asked and handed out as a pointer's clone (`Frees`): `curios-utilities`' treap at every size, whose shape is a function of the names it holds, made of the largest carrying child's set with the other children's names put into it, the smaller into the larger (`Sorted::union_with`). A node that adds nothing to its largest child's set holds that set itself, a metavariable's is its spine's, which the spine counts as it is built and rewritten, and a reader asking a local is answered first by the newest local a node keeps (`Term::mentions_free`, `Term::mentions_any`). Iteration is ascending in `Free`'s order, globals first.

**Rationale.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../documentation/design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md): a set copied from its children makes a node pay for every name beneath it, the square of its depth in a term whose every level names one more local, where merged smaller into larger it pays for the names its smaller children bring, a logarithm apiece — O(n log² n) over a tree, the bound Maziarz, Ellis, Lawrence, Fitzgibbon and Peyton Jones give every subterm's variable map ([Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02856)), and GHC's free-variable annotation inserts the smaller set into the larger the same way ([`GHC/Types/Unique/DFM.hs`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ghc/ghc/master/compiler/GHC/Types/Unique/DFM.hs)). The readers ask a term's set at every scope check, by-value read and credit, and again of the terms nested in it, so the set is the fact a node keeps and each ask costs its answer. One persistent structure at every size is the shape every other persistent table here has, unioned by what its operands do not share; Bindlib's boxes, a sorted list merged at every node, are the square this removes ([`bindlib.ml`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rlepigre/ocaml-bindlib/master/lib/bindlib.ml)).

**Rejected.** No set, the scalars' flags passing over what holds no local and a remembered walk collecting a set where a reader asks, as Lean 4 keeps none ([`Expr.lean`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover/lean4/master/src/Lean/Expr.lean)): each ask walks its term's graph, the readers ask the terms nested in one again, and a rewrite asking at every node walks at every node. A sorted slice while a set is small and the treap past a size, as Clojure's array map and Scala's `Set1` to `Set4` keep small collections: a second representation with a union and an iteration of its own, which copies a node's members whole below its threshold: the square, capped at a size.

### A node's rank is its children's, joined

**Decision.** A node carries what it is ranked by among the atoms of a reading (`Ranked`, `term/rank.rs`), filled where first asked as its free-variable set is: its structure, every identity — a free local, a metavariable, a universe or quality metavariable — a placeholder; a map from each identity, by kind and age, to a hash of the positions it holds (`Sorted`); and the wrapping sum of those hashes. The largest child's map is taken as it stands and every other child's entries and the node's own identities are joined into it under the structure, as Maziarz, Ellis, Lawrence, Fitzgibbon and Peyton Jones join position trees under a structure's tag ([Hashing Modulo Alpha-Equivalence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02856), §4.8); a metavariable's is its spine's, which the spine keeps as it is built and rewritten, each entry's positions placed at its rank. `curios_algebra::Rank` reads the structure and the sum as one key and the map as the tie: two atoms of one key are ordered by their maps read in age order to the first entry held apart, an identity before another by its age and one identity by the hash of its positions, and no term is read at a comparison. Both checkers rank through `Term::rank`.

**Rationale.** [Every stage but reduction costs the graph it is handed](../documentation/design/compilation/every-stage-but-reduction-costs-the-graph-it-is-handed.md): respelling the whole atom, memoized per node, rebuilt an inner atom once per enclosing atom and listed its identities' ages again after the outer's — the square of a sum spine's depth, 655,613 to 41,945,085 nodes over depths 256 to 2 048 and 3,177,644 at 96 lines of the bounded tower, counted at `102ee41b4` — where a summary made of the children's fills each node once and joins the smaller map into the larger, n log² n over a tree, the bound the paper gives. The sum and not the paper's exclusive or, since two identities of one node can hold one position, two universe metavariables at one offset of one level. A tie read off the two maps compares what the summaries hold and walks nothing, and `f(x, y)` and `f(y, x)` part at `x`'s positions. The prelude ranks 80,573 atoms in elaboration and 26,785 in certification; `cargo xtask profile-prelude` at `868e314b2` against `b5a6d9df5`, counted, has elaboration allocate 30,712 MB in 278.7 million allocations where it allocated 30,552 MB in 281.9 million, peaking at 317.7 MiB where it peaked at 289.6, and certification 7,462 MB in 68.8 million where it allocated 7,248 MB in 69.0 million, peaking at 160.5 MiB where it peaked at 149.3: the rebuilds' allocations are gone, and the summaries kept on the nodes beneath each ranked atom are the tenth the peak gained. The build, timed in user seconds over interleaved rebuilds on a loaded machine at `102ee41b4`, took 104.9 and 107.5 under the rebuilt rank and 106.5 and 106.3 under the summary: the library pays nothing for the square it never had.

**Rejected.** The ages listed after the key, in the order a walk first meets them: an atom inside an atom lists the inner one's ages again, the square in keys. A tie walked over the two terms to the first identity held apart, passing over pairs one allocation or one hash: a rank that reads the terms at every comparison, and each node's own structure kept or masked again for the walk, where the maps already hold the answer. The paper's exclusive or, which cancels two identities at one position. A hash table for the map, which a node two atoms hold cannot share. `Atom` generic over the caller's order, a parameter on every type of a crate that names no term, where one trait object keeps its dependency on `curios-num` alone.

### A place is a key in hand and a rank on disk

**Decision.** A unit compiled in a process holds each item at an `i64` position of an `Ordered` map, spaced apart where the module is made or restored (`Items`); a recompile holds an item made again at the position of the one it replaces, and an item new to the unit at a position between its neighbours in the stored order, and gives every position again only where no position lies between them (`Items::version`). What a unit stores names a place by its rank in the order, read off where it is filed, so a recompile stores what a whole compile stores.

**Rationale.** A place that is a vector's index moves for every item after one inserted, so every table keyed by it — where the module holds each name, what each item erased to — would be made again for one declaration added.

**Rejected.** An item new to the unit appended after every other, which keeps no place in the stored order, since what reads it then stands before it; ranks kept current by a tree with subtree counts, which a reader asking by place would pay a logarithm for on every read.

### Solving and satisfiability part company here

**Decision.** A `UniverseContext` is data. Inferring the instance a program meant is the elaborator's universe solver's; deciding whether the resulting constraint set is satisfiable is each checker's own — the elaborator's by its solver, the kernel's by its own procedure in `curios-cert`.

**Rationale.** Inferring an instance is inference over that data, so it sits with the elaborator; deciding satisfiability admits a program, so each checker decides it for itself.

### A fold's arithmetic is Core's, and how far its operands reduce is the checker's

**Decision.** The intrinsic folds are the boundary case of what a term is, and `Reducer` draws the line: what `2 + 2` folds to belongs here, while how far an operand reduces before a fold sees it is a strategy each checker supplies for itself.

**Rationale.** What a fold computes is arithmetic on the representation, which is Core's; how far an operand reduces first is a strategy, and each checker has its own.

### Two untrusted modules live here for their consumers

**Decision.** `print` and `builders` live in this crate, though neither is trusted: `print` because `Term`'s `Display` is used throughout the elaborator's error paths, `builders` because neither the surface lowering nor the elaborator, which both build terms with it, owns what a term is.

**Rationale.** Neither can admit a bad program: no judgment calls a builder, and printing decides nothing.
